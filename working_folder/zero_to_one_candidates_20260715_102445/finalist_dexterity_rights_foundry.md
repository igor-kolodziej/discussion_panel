# Zero-to-One Candidate: Dexterity Rights Foundry

**Canonical business specification: v1, frozen 2026-07-15 Europe/Warsaw for independent simulation.**

Candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses to test.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Dexterity Rights Foundry owns and repeatedly licenses multimodal expert demonstrations of difficult cable, connector, clip, and soft-material manipulation tasks that robotics teams need but cannot obtain from generic robot datasets.

## Founder Fit

The founder can combine ML/data engineering, biosignals, experimental design, and access to electrical-installation task knowledge. The family installation business can supply unpaid discovery, safe task mock-ups, and technician recruitment, but it does not count as independent paid proof. Contractors can provide capture engineering and performer work without requiring a full robotics lab.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is a physical-AI, humanoid, industrial-robotics, or teleoperation-data team training manipulation models for cable routing, connector mating, strain relief, clips, plugs, flexible conduit, and other deformable or precision tasks.

Generic datasets overrepresent rigid pick-and-place. A new task, gripper, embodiment, or cell version needs successful demonstrations, near-failures, recoveries, and consistent semantic segmentation. Current workarounds are internal teleoperation on scarce robot hardware, synthetic data, broad data-collection vendors, and bespoke technician sessions. These are expensive, exclusive, and often not reusable across teams.

## Product Wedge

A 30-day licensed “Dexterity Task Pack” for one narrow task family:

- synchronized egocentric RGB-D/video, hand/arm kinematics, task-state events, tool/object geometry, force proxies, and optional eye gaze or forearm EMG;
- demonstrations by technicians and novices, including successful execution, controlled failures, and recovery sequences;
- standard action segmentation, quality flags, metadata, and a LeRobot-compatible export;
- a customer evaluation subset and conversion notebook for the customer's embodiment;
- a non-exclusive commercial licence; the startup retains ownership of base recordings, performer assignments, metadata schema, and retargeted derivatives.

The first catalogue pack covers three variants of cable insertion and connector seating on a portable bench mock-up.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Robot companies can buy cameras and teleoperation labour, but the scarce unit is legally clean, consistently labelled expert manipulation across task variants and failure recovery. Small teams want useful data now without assembling technicians, capture rigs, performer rights, and QA. A reusable catalogue changes project economics: the same owned recording base can generate multiple licences and embodiment-specific derivatives instead of resetting to zero for every customer.

## Underused Momentum

- Open physical-AI stacks such as Isaac GR00T and LeRobot reduce the cost of consuming outside datasets.
- Robotics investment creates more teams than there are internal expert-demonstration operations.
- Electrical and installation work contains commercially relevant dexterity tasks accessible in Poland at lower capture cost.
- Commodity cameras, hand tracking, and biosignal equipment make a useful human-centred capture rig possible before owning robots.

## Beachhead Market

European and North American robotics teams publicly working on humanoid, mobile-manipulator, or industrial dexterity and already using Isaac/LeRobot-compatible workflows. The first task family is low-voltage connector and flexible-cable handling on safe bench mock-ups; exclude high-voltage work and tasks requiring access to proprietary customer factories.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- First evaluation licence: 25,000–40,000 PLN per standard task pack for one team and one internal evaluation/use scope.
- Annual catalogue licence: 150,000–400,000 PLN for several task families, updates, and derived formats.
- Embodiment conversion or new task variant: 30,000–100,000 PLN, accepted only when base rights remain reusable.
- First-pack gross-margin hypothesis: 35%–50% because capture setup is front-loaded.
- Re-licence gross-margin hypothesis: 75%–90%; blended scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 65%; operating-margin hypothesis: 28%–38%.
- No buyer-specific exclusive collection is accepted unless priced high enough to fund creation of a separately owned catalogue asset; otherwise the compounding thesis disappears.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build a named list of 100 robotics companies and labs from NVIDIA/LeRobot ecosystems, accelerator portfolios, robotics conferences, GitHub projects, job postings, and companies publicly hiring teleoperation/data staff.
- Send a downloadable 60-second sample, data card, exact licence terms, and a fixed 25,000 PLN evaluation offer to a manipulation or data lead.
- Conversion event: paid licence, signed non-exclusive rights, defined evaluation metric, and written permission to retain the base/derivative corpus.
- First ten: founder-led technical outbound to teams whose public demos show cable, connector, soft-material, or precision assembly tasks; no distributor or introduction is assumed.
- First 100: publish catalogue metadata and benchmark tasks, distribute format converters through open-source robotics communities, and recruit specialist technicians whose task packs expand search demand.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1, not yet proven:** startup ownership of signed performer IP/data assignments, raw and processed base recordings, standard task ontology, and reusable non-exclusive licensing rights. The first paid licence accepting retained rights proves commercial control; three independent licences of related packs prove that it is more than a custom service.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resource: a legally clean, technically useful expert-task corpus and a buyer that accepts retained rights.

Bootstrap:

1. Use the family installation context only to map tasks, construct safe mock-ups, and recruit paid demonstrators under explicit assignments.
2. Spend below 30,000 PLN on a portable capture rig: existing computer/phone where possible, depth/egocentric cameras, hand tracking, lighting, calibration, storage, contractor capture help, and optional rented EMG/eye tracking.
3. Capture one 50–100-trajectory sample pack before building a general platform.
4. Approach 100 named robotics teams with a fixed evaluation licence. A buyer can first inspect a watermarked sample and data card.
5. Within 12 months, prove control with three paid licences, at least two task packs, all performer rights, reproducible QA, and one re-licence of previously captured base data.

An unknown founder can secure the resource because the technicians are locally recruitable, the tasks are safe and portable, rights can be purchased directly, and a customer need not grant factory or robot access to evaluate the pack.

## Distribution Advantage

Searchable task metadata, open format converters, and sample benchmarks make the catalogue discoverable where robotics engineers already work. Each customer conversion request expands formats and evaluation evidence for the next customer. Technician supply in Poland creates a cost and task-access advantage, while the direct catalogue relationship remains with the startup.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with cable and connector task families. Expand to:

1. plumbing, harness, hose, textile, packaging, and maintenance manipulation;
2. paired novice/expert/failure-recovery datasets;
3. retargeting and quality models across human demonstrations and robot embodiments;
4. capture kits used by contracted technician cells in other regions;
5. later proprietary wearable capture hardware informed by which signals buyers actually value.

Broad data-collection vendors can reproduce a task after they see demand. The defence must be earlier ownership of dense variations, expert supply, failure/recovery coverage, cross-customer evaluation history, and licences that make the catalogue standard.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers a strong technical workforce, lower capture cost, and direct access to skilled electrical/industrial trades. EU GDPR and performer-rights discipline can make data cards and provenance more reliable. This is a supply and execution advantage, not a legal barrier; the catalogue must compound faster than foreign collectors can recreate it.

## Compounding Advantage

Every task pack adds owned human movement, optional EMG/gaze, error-recovery trajectories, performer skill labels, object geometry, and conversion results. Re-licensing spreads fixed capture cost; customer feedback improves quality and retargeting. Ordinary delivery builds proprietary datasets/IP, advanced robot-learning AI/ML, wearable capture expertise, real-time HCI, access to skilled engineers/technicians, and robotics distribution.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash while the founder remains employed, with distributions capped at 5%. The strong case reinvests 100%. Capital funds new owned task families, capture QA, technician supply, secure storage, and retargeting models—assets that increase catalogue value and recurring licensing margin.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–20: 15 buyer interviews; select one connector/cable task; sign performer and licence templates.
- Days 21–60: build the portable rig and capture a 50–100-trajectory sample with success, failure, and recovery.
- Days 61–90: produce data card, benchmark notebook, watermarked sample, and named 100-account list.
- Days 91–180: 100 technical outreaches, 15 evaluations, five proposals, and one 25,000–40,000 PLN paid non-exclusive licence.

The founder remains employed and limits work to five hours daily. Family-company involvement is unpaid discovery/supply support and does not count as paid customer proof.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Tranche 1: maximum 36,000 PLN before paid proof — legal/IP 4k, cameras/tracking/calibration 13k, paid performers 5k, capture contractor 6k, storage/compute 3k, outreach/contingency 5k.
- Tranche 2: cumulative 65,000 PLN after one paid licence and technical acceptance, for second pack and improved capture.
- Tranche 3: up to 100,000 PLN only after two independent payers or a paid re-licence with positive contribution.
- Family capital excluded until repeat demand and viable unit economics.
- Maximum pre-proof loss: 36,000 PLN; much of the rig is reusable/resalable.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after at least five independent paying customers or contracted annual licences, one paid re-licence of existing base data, positive pack-level contribution, a repeatable technical-outbound signal, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If external licensing fails, reusable assets include the portable multimodal capture rig, performer contracts, task ontology, signal-processing code, and owned recordings. They can support a different research or HCI candidate only after a new evaluation; the founder does not assume bespoke data services will meet the wealth gate.

## Critical Evidence

- Physical-AI workflows explicitly consume real and simulated teleoperation data.
- NVIDIA describes high-quality demonstrations and system access as constraints.
- Existing competitors validate demand while increasing the need for narrow owned rights.

## Critical Hypotheses

- A standard pack created without customer robot access has 25,000–40,000 PLN value.
- Retained rights survive procurement.
- Cable/connector tasks transfer across embodiments.
- Re-licensing rather than bespoke capture becomes a meaningful share of revenue.
- Twenty-four global annual customers support the conservative wealth case.

## Kill Criteria

- No paid non-exclusive licence within 180 days after 100 qualified contacts and at least five technical evaluations.
- All serious buyers require exclusive on-robot data and reject retained base rights.
- Customer evaluations show the human-centred pack cannot improve a relevant model or reduce data-collection time.
- Pack gross margin remains below 40% after the third sale or no base data is re-licensed within 12 months.
- Capture quality requires more than the 100,000 PLN founder-funded cycle before paid proof.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- Maximum 36,000 PLN before proof; maximum 100,000 PLN founder capital in 12 months.
- Founder: experiment design, ML/data, sales, five hours daily.
- One part-time capture/robotics engineer.
