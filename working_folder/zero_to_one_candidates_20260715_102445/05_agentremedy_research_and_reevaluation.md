# AgentRemedy Rail: Decision-Critical Research and Advisory Re-evaluation

This is an advisory re-evaluation of the exact candidate after official-source research. It is not a validation score.

## Discovery-Stage Disclosure

The business is at idea/planning/discovery stage; no pilot, legal opinion, contract, proprietary dataset, or control proof is assumed. Missing post-launch evidence is judged through the proposed proof path, while uncontrolled legal and trust dependencies remain penalised.

## Research Result

Official sources do not establish that AgentRemedy is illegal. They establish a narrow structural tension:

- If AgentRemedy takes possession of remedy funds or initiates transfers, Polish/EU payment-services analysis becomes material; KNF says exclusions are narrow.
- If a separate insurance contract or risk-transfer promise is introduced, assisting with claims can become insurance distribution.
- Keeping funds in a vendor-controlled account and acting as a technical evidence/adjudication provider may reduce payment-service exposure, but it also weakens the claimed control point: the vendor remains able to reject or bypass the remedy decision.

The candidate correctly budgets for a legal memo, but the official-source check cannot resolve the national-law classification or prove vendor willingness to grant meaningful delegated authority.

Sources retrieved 2026-07-15: [KNF](https://www.knf.gov.pl/dla_rynku/procesy_licencyjne/platniczy/informacje_ogolne/zakres_uslug_platniczych), [PSD2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32015L2366), [IDD](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32016L0097), [EIOPA Q&A](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/qa-regulation/questions-and-answers-database/1794-1869-2440-definition-customer-context-insurance-distribution_en).

## Re-evaluation Diagnosis

The new research strengthens the feasibility of a no-custody technical structure but exposes a control dilemma rather than resolving it. If the startup controls funds and claims, regulatory exposure rises; if the vendor controls payment and final approval, the Tier-1 claim route is weaker and can be copied or bypassed. Combined with unproved six-figure annual willingness to pay from small vendors, this is too much unresolved authority/trust dependence for an independent panel in the current run.

## Overall Score

**65/100 advisory.** The legal research improves risk clarity but lowers control-point strength and durability relative to the first 67/100 assessment. This score is not gate-eligible.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 today; unresolved Tier-1 aspiration.** Strongest copy risk: an established PSP, insurer, or observability vendor combines regulated payment capability with claim administration, while a no-custody AgentRemedy remains a technical contractor whose decisions the vendor can override.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass on arithmetic, sensitive on commercial reality.** The conservative table still clears 5m PLN, but annual fee, delegation, claim volume, and margin are unproved.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail for panel readiness in this exact version.** Spending 8,000 PLN on a legal memo is reachable, but a gate-eligible paid proof still needs three uncertain facts to align: legally safe structure, vendor-prepaid remedy economics, and enough delegated authority/data rights to create control. The first 180 days can test them, but the exact current candidate is not ready to claim that a paid pilot is credible without the counsel result.

## Fatal Objections

1. The legal-safe version and the control-rich version may be different businesses.
2. An unknown founder must win customer-remedy trust from both a vendor and its end users before owning relevant outcome history.
3. Small vendors may prefer direct refunds and existing payment rails to a 120,000+ PLN annual administrator.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A real remedy event, rather than monitoring, creates valuable labelled agent-action failure and recovery outcomes.

## Recommendation

kill

AgentRemedy is discarded for this run. A future reconsideration would require a materially different regulated partner position, buyer, or control structure—not a renamed no-custody claims desk.
