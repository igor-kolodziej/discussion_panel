# Research and Sources

Retrieval date for all links: **2026-07-15**. Numerical business-model assumptions not established by a source are labelled as hypotheses in the finalist briefs.

## Cross-Candidate Evidence

### Poland's business-services beachhead

- ABSL reports 2,081 business-services centres and 488,700 employees in Poland; 90% of centres use AI for cognitive tasks, 74% use intelligent automation, and 78% increased staff training. This supports a concentrated buyer list for CatchPool AI, but not willingness to pay. [ABSL, 2025 sector report announcement](https://absl.pl/en/news/p/absl-report-polands-business-services-sector-grows-value-and-strategic-importance)
- The EU AI Act requires providers and deployers to take measures ensuring a sufficient level of AI literacy. This supplies organisational attention but does not create a budget for a particular vendor. [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689), [European Commission Q&A](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers)
- OECD work toward common AI-incident reporting describes fragmented terminology and the importance of comparable incident information. This supports the value of structured AI failure records, not demand for bounties. [OECD, 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/02/towards-a-common-reporting-framework-for-ai-incidents_8c488fdb/f326d4ac-en.pdf)

### Human-AI and agent competition

- Workforce AI assessment is already crowded: [Workera](https://www.workera.ai/platform/ai-agent/skills-assessments), [Mercer AI Agility](https://www.mercer.com/solutions/talent-and-rewards/talent-assessment/ai-agility-framework/), and [CodeSignal agentic assessments](https://codesignal.com/agentic-assessments/). This is why no pure assessment finalist was retained.
- Visa has published a Trusted Agent Protocol for agentic commerce, evidence that autonomous commerce is moving toward identity and action-verification infrastructure. It does not prove small vendors will outsource remedies. [Visa Trusted Agent Protocol](https://developer.visa.com/capabilities/trusted-agent-protocol/overview)

## AgentRemedy Rail

### Evidence

- Autonomous booking and purchasing actions create observable transactions, confirmations, cancellations, and monetary remediation events. The Visa protocol above and emerging agentic-commerce infrastructure establish the event class, not the proposed business model.
- Consumer redress, payment-services, insurance-distribution, and client-money rules may apply depending on structure. Legal classification is therefore a first-gate deliverable, not an assumption.
- KNF explains that payment activity generally involves taking possession of transaction funds, while a technical provider that never possesses the funds can fall outside the payment-service list; exclusions must be interpreted narrowly. This supports a vendor-controlled account design but does not classify the proposed adjudication flow. [KNF payment-services scope](https://www.knf.gov.pl/dla_rynku/procesy_licencyjne/platniczy/informacje_ogolne/zakres_uslug_platniczych)
- PSD2 defines a payment transaction broadly as placing, transferring, or withdrawing funds and notes that providers limited to technical services do not hold client funds. [Directive (EU) 2015/2366](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32015L2366)
- The Insurance Distribution Directive defines insurance distribution broadly when an insurance contract exists, including assisting with claim administration. AgentRemedy proposes no insurance contract or pooled risk, but only counsel can determine whether its promise, adjudication, and remuneration create a regulated activity under Polish implementation. [Directive (EU) 2016/97](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32016L0097), [EIOPA Q&A on group arrangements](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/qa-regulation/questions-and-answers-database/1794-1869-2440-definition-customer-context-insurance-distribution_en)

### Decision-critical hypotheses

- A small autonomous-agent vendor will prepay 10,000–25,000 PLN for a capped remedy programme and delegate first-line adjudication within a defined amount.
- The service can be structured as vendor-funded fulfilment/administration rather than insurance, risk pooling, indemnity, or regulated custody.
- Covered action logs can be exported without platform integration during the paid pilot.
- A vendor-controlled payment instruction can provide a credible remedy to end users while leaving AgentRemedy with enough delegated authority to own the event.

## CatchPool AI

### Evidence

- Poland has a large, concentrated business-services buyer universe and high reported AI adoption, per ABSL.
- Current incident-reporting and AI-governance products generally sell reporting, risk registers, or compliance workflow. The proposed differentiated event is a verified payout tied to corrected work, not another dashboard.

### Decision-critical hypotheses

- An operations or transformation leader will fund a 10,000 PLN employee bounty pool plus a 15,000 PLN pilot fee.
- The net prevented loss or rework can be evidenced within six weeks without exposing sensitive client data to the startup.
- Employees will report valuable catches without materially gaming the reward system.

## TiltLock

### Evidence

- PokerStars lets players save their own hand histories, enabling user-controlled post-hand data ingestion without scraping other players. [PokerStars hand-history help](https://www.pokerstars.com/help/articles/save-hand-histories/239828/)
- PokerStars prohibits tools that provide real-time advice or share/datamine hands, making the compliance boundary material. TiltLock must never recommend poker actions and must operate only as a user-precommitted session-control tool. [PokerStars third-party tools policy](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/room/prohibited/), [PokerStars terms](https://www.pokerstars.com/tos/)
- PokerStars itself offers cooling-off and responsible-gaming controls, demonstrating user familiarity with self-exclusion but also incumbent substitution risk. [PokerStars responsible gaming](https://www.pokerstars.com/about/responsible-gaming/)
- Session-alert and tilt-adjacent tools already exist. [PokerCharts session alerts](https://pokercharts.com/en/features/session-alerts), [Noeva physiology for trading](https://noeva.io/)
- Pseudonymised physiological data remains personal data; research reuse requires explicit governance and truly anonymised derivatives where claimed. [EDPB pseudonymisation guidance announcement](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-adopts-pseudonymisation-guidelines-and-paves-the-way-to-improve-cooperation-with_en)

### Decision-critical hypotheses

- At least 10 of 100 directly recruited serious players will pay a 499–699 PLN founding-cohort fee before software exists.
- Users will authorise an OS-level local lock and consistently wear a supported sensor.
- State-based self-binding reduces losses enough to retain users at 99–139 PLN per month.
- Platform policies permit a non-strategic, user-authored session-control companion; written legal/policy review is required before public launch.

## Dexterity Rights Foundry

### Evidence

- NVIDIA describes high-quality demonstrations as scarce and teleoperation data as constrained by access to systems. [NVIDIA GR00T-Mimic](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/advancing-humanoid-robot-sight-and-skill-development-with-nvidia-project-gr00t/)
- NVIDIA's physical-AI workflow explicitly includes collecting real and simulated teleoperation data. [Isaac GR00T](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/gr00t), [end-to-end workflow](https://docs.nvidia.com/learning/physical-ai/gr00t-e2e-workflow/latest/index.html)
- Commercial collection competitors already exist, including [Phi Robotics](https://www.phirobo.com/), [Tactum Labs](https://tactumlabs.com/), and [Operant](https://www.operantdata.com/). Competition makes retained task rights and a narrow expert-task catalogue essential.

### Decision-critical hypotheses

- A robotics buyer will pay 25,000–40,000 PLN to license a standard sample pack whose base data is retained and can be licensed again.
- Affordable capture can produce trajectories useful enough for a customer's existing Isaac/LeRobot workflow without access to the customer's robot.
- Cable and connector manipulation data transfers across multiple embodiments well enough to justify non-exclusive purchase.

## RingRelay

### Evidence

- Oura says it has sold more than 5.5 million rings, establishing a material installed base for one ring family. [Oura company announcement](https://ouraring.com/blog/oura-ring-4-ceramic-announcement/)
- Oura relies on a sizing kit and exact ring size, supporting the mismatch problem. [Oura sizing guidance](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025590653-Tips-for-Determining-Your-Oura-Ring-Size)
- Oura's limited warranty and non-user-replaceable battery create a device-condition uncertainty in used sales. [Oura warranty](https://ouraring.com/terms-and-conditions), [Oura battery guidance](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046218953-Ring-Battery-Tips)
- Back Market already provides broad refurbished-device distribution and warranty, so a generic refurbisher proposition is not defensible. [Back Market quality standards](https://www.backmarket.com/en-us/quality)
- EU battery rules increase lifecycle and replaceability pressure from 2027, but do not automatically grant a small reseller parts access. [Regulation (EU) 2023/1542](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj/)

### Decision-critical hypotheses

- Exact model/size reservations produce at least five paid deposits before inventory purchase.
- A standardised charge-retention and identity/lock test can predict unacceptable returns.
- A one-swap guarantee and buyback quote support a 28% gross margin and inventory turns fast enough to avoid cash lock-up.

## Generator-Stage Research Boundary

The evidence above supports the existence of buyers, events, regulations, infrastructure, or competitors. It does not prove willingness to pay, signed rights, margins, accuracy, retention, or control. Those are explicitly staged hypotheses for paid discovery and downstream evaluation.
