## Finalist Selection

Five shapes survive the generator's founder-wealth and reachability screens:

1. **AgentRemedy Rail** — vendor-funded, capped remedies for autonomous-agent mistakes; controls a prepaid claim and payment event.
2. **CatchPool AI** — employer-funded bounties for verified AI-agent error catches; controls a recurring internal correction and payout workflow.
3. **TiltLock** — physiology-aware, user-precommitted loss controls for serious online poker; controls the direct self-binding workflow and accumulates paired behavior/physiology outcomes.
4. **Dexterity Rights Foundry** — retained-rights multimodal demonstrations for difficult robot manipulation; owns the base corpus and licences task packs.
5. **RingRelay** — model-and-size-specific smart-ring recommerce with one-swap certainty; controls exact inventory, buybacks, and device diagnostic history.

## Deliberate Main-Agent Improvement Record

The raw versions above remain preserved in this funnel. The following changes were made before advisory evaluation.

### AgentMistake Reserve → AgentRemedy Rail

- Replaced an ambiguous “reserve” or risk-pooling proposition with a capped vendor-funded customer-remedy programme: each vendor's money covers only that vendor's approved remedies.
- Added a mandatory pre-sale Polish/EU legal classification memo and a kill criterion for insurance, payment-services, or client-money licensing exposure.
- Narrowed the initial buyer to small autonomous booking, purchasing, and workflow-agent vendors whose actions have visible monetary consequences but who cannot fund a full claims team.
- Made the control point contractual: prepaid programme balance, exclusive first-line claim route for covered actions, event-log access, delegated adjudication band, and retained anonymised taxonomy rights.

### Internal AI incident reporting → CatchPool AI

- Added a real economic event: a client-funded bounty is paid only after a corrected AI action is verified against a pre-agreed loss or risk rubric.
- Narrowed the beachhead to Polish/CEE business-services centres using generative AI in document-heavy workflows, supported by ABSL's published installed base.
- Added anti-gaming controls: invited workflows, capped pools, duplicate merging, severity evidence, manager sign-off above a threshold, and rewards for prevention rather than raw report count.
- Turned data collection into ordinary delivery: prompt/action/correction/root-cause/outcome records are needed to adjudicate and prevent repeat payouts.

### PokerTilt Physiology Lab → TiltLock

- Replaced a retrospective coaching programme with user-authored, pre-session stop rules enforced by a local desktop companion.
- Kept all analysis non-strategic and separated from poker decision advice; the product reads the user's own permitted hand-history files and device data to detect state, then applies only user-precommitted session controls.
- Made physiological data an optional compounding Tier-2 asset, not the sole economic moat; the subscription works even if a user declines research reuse.
- Added a six-week paid founding cohort before software build, followed by a local prototype that can be tested below the 40,000 PLN first tranche.

### Generic dexterity-data service → Dexterity Rights Foundry

- Narrowed capture to cable routing, connector mating, clips, and soft-material tasks reachable through the founder's family electrical-installation context.
- Required signed performer assignments and retained, non-exclusive base-recording and derivative rights before a task is accepted.
- Standardised one reusable task-pack schema rather than selling open-ended bespoke teleoperation labour.
- Made first proof a paid licence to an existing sample pack; if every buyer demands exclusive collection on its own robot, the shape is killed.

### FitSwap Rings → RingRelay

- Replaced speculative inventory buying with exact model/size reservations and five paid deposits before the first ten-unit inventory purchase.
- Added a one-swap guarantee, battery/charging/identity test, and a contracted buyback price to create repeat inventory flow.
- Narrowed the initial scope to one ring family and the most liquid sizes; expansion waits for measured sell-through and return rates.
- Made the strategic asset the device-specific failure, fit, battery, and resale dataset plus direct wearable-buyer distribution—not generic recommerce data.

## Why Held Shapes Were Not Padded Into the Finalist Set

- **PlatformOverride** and **DriverSleep Renewal Rail** may be revisited only if a platform or fleet independently confirms paid workflow routing.
- **AccessInput Uptime Rail** is socially meaningful and founder-accessible, but current economics depend on unproven support load and household budgets.
- **WearLoop Authorised Returns Cell** could accumulate valuable hardware knowledge, but an unknown founder cannot claim control until a brand signs a mandate and consigns spares.
