# Wave 4 Research and Sources

Sources retrieved 2026-07-15. Evidence is separated from hypotheses. Candidate generation only — not validated.

## PulsePhantom Bench

- IEC 63203-402-3 defines terminology, a measurement protocol, and an accuracy test for heart-rate wearables using PPG. It does not endorse this product. [DIN/IEC listing](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-en-iec-63203-402-3/380191722)
- A 2025 dynamic wrist-phantom study reproduced PPG waveforms and varied skin tone and vessel depth, reporting up to 77.8% signal degradation and supporting controlled wearable testing. This is technical evidence, not market demand or transferable IP. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41210553/)
- Earlier work explains that human tests lack repeatability and are effort-intensive, and demonstrates optoelectronic PPG simulation. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31565494/)
- WhaleTeq already markets an advanced PPG heart-rate simulator, proving a commercial category and strong copy/competition risk. [WhaleTeq product material](https://www.whaleteq.com/data/download/files/1667782891129476448.pdf)
- Hypotheses: three European wearable teams prepay 8,000 PLN; a useful beta can be built below 100,000 PLN; cartridges/software create recurring margin; 500–800 labs/teams are reachable at the proposed pricing.

## HarnessCell

- IPC states IPC/WHMA-A-620 is the industry-consensus process/acceptance standard for cable and wire-harness assemblies, and training/certification is available. [IPC standard overview](https://www.ipc.org/meet-your-standards), [IPC certification](https://www.ipc.org/ipc-certifications)
- Rapid no-MOQ/high-mix harness supply already exists: Loombotic advertises about seven-day delivery and instant quotes; MiniProto advertises no MOQ/two-week delivery; OurPCB Poland advertises 24–72-hour prototypes; multiple Polish manufacturers serve prototype/low-volume work. [Loombotic](https://loombotic.com/), [MiniProto](https://www.miniproto.com/), [OurPCB Poland](https://ourpcb.pl/wiazki-kablowe/)
- Competition establishes the pain and also directly weakens a speed-only thesis. The proposed defensibility must come from a narrower robotics/wearable configuration, traceability economics, fixtures, revision history, and data-enabled quality—not generic rapid production.
- Hypotheses: 60 CEE robotics/wearable teams form a reachable recurring base; an unknown founder wins a paid 5,000+ PLN NRE/order in 180 days; 20%–25% operating margins survive labour, components, certification, scrap, and acquisition.

## NeuroPort Uptime Library

- BioSemi states that its supplier stopped producing the MK2 connector; MK2 electrode sets cease availability from autumn 2026, while official upgrade/trade-in/repair routes exist. This creates timing but also a strong manufacturer substitute. [BioSemi transition FAQ](https://www.biosemi.com/faq/Upgrade_to_Active2.5_or_upgrade_to_Active3.htm)
- BioSemi publicly prices research accessories in the hundreds to thousands of euros, showing costly interconnect/sensor components. [BioSemi pricing](https://www.biosemi.com/faq/prices.htm)
- Brain Products says the BrainAmp family has contributed to thousands of publications and documents multiple connectors/adapters; it and EASYCAP already sell cross-system adapters, proving both installed use and incumbent supply. [Brain Products BrainAmp](https://www.brainproducts.com/solutions/brainamp/), [Brain Products/EGI adapters](https://pressrelease.brainproducts.com/egi_adaptors/), [EIB64-DUO](https://pressrelease.brainproducts.com/eib64-duo/)
- Hypotheses: publication methods create a practical prospect list; one lab prepays a research-only uptime audit/reservation; 250 labs sustain 20,000–60,000 PLN annual contracts; third-party adapter/loaner liability and rights remain manageable.
