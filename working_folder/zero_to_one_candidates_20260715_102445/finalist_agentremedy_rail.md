# Zero-to-One Candidate: AgentRemedy Rail

Candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses to test.

## One-Sentence Thesis

AgentRemedy Rail lets small autonomous-agent vendors sell trust they cannot yet earn alone by pre-funding a capped customer-remedy programme whose claims, evidence, and payouts create a proprietary AI-action failure and recovery dataset.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build log ingestion, evidence extraction, anomaly triage, adjudication support, and failure-taxonomy ML while employed. The first delivery is manual and software-assisted; it does not require manufacturing, clinical authority, or a complete platform. Contract, payments, and regulatory classification require fixed-fee legal help.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a 5–50-person European vendor whose agent books travel, purchases services, changes reservations, or executes business workflow actions with direct monetary consequences. A mistake creates refunds, support escalation, chargebacks, and customer distrust. Current workarounds are case-by-case founder support, disclaimers, manual refunds, and blanket human approval that destroys the agent's speed advantage.

The buyer wants a credible promise: “If our covered agent action causes a defined loss, there is a fast, funded remedy with an evidence trail,” without hiring a 24/7 claims team or transferring open-ended risk.

## Product Wedge

A 90-day, vendor-specific programme:

- the vendor prepays a 10,000–25,000 PLN remedy balance that remains attributable only to its covered customers;
- a 12,000–20,000 PLN setup and administration fee defines covered actions, exclusions, evidence, remedy bands, and response times;
- the startup receives relevant action logs and customer evidence, performs first-line adjudication up to a capped amount, and triggers an approved refund, service credit, or replacement;
- the vendor receives a root-cause and prevention report; the contract grants the startup rights to retain a de-identified failure taxonomy and derived models, never customer identity or reusable raw transaction content without a separate lawful basis.

The startup neither pools risk across vendors nor promises payment from its own balance sheet.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Agent vendors are building identity, permission, and transaction protocols, but small vendors still lack the operational remedy layer that makes autonomous action tolerable to a cautious buyer. A generic monitoring dashboard tells the vendor that something failed; a funded, timed remedy changes the end customer's downside. The transaction and adjudication route also produces harder-to-copy labelled data: intended action, actual action, claimant evidence, fault class, remedy, and recurrence.

## Underused Momentum

- Agentic booking and commerce are creating machine-initiated actions with objective receipts and logs.
- Trust infrastructure is emerging around agent identity and permission, leaving post-action redress as a missing operational layer.
- Small vendors can pre-fund a capped liability more easily than they can obtain bespoke insurance or employ a claims function.

## Beachhead Market

European autonomous travel-booking, appointment-booking, procurement, and transaction-execution startups with 5–50 staff, at least 100 monthly agent actions, and transaction values below 5,000 PLN. Exclude regulated investment, medical, credit, and high-value financial actions during the POC.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Pilot: 12,000–20,000 PLN setup/admin fee plus vendor-funded 10,000–25,000 PLN remedy balance. The remedy balance is not revenue.
- Recurring: 6,000–20,000 PLN monthly platform/admin fee depending on action volume, plus 8%–15% administration fee on remedies paid; no underwriting spread.
- Pilot delivery hypothesis: 70%–80% gross margin on fees after legal-template amortisation and analyst time.
- Scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 78%; operating-margin hypothesis: 22%–35% after compliance, support, payments, and engineering.
- Contribution proof: the first pilot must show positive fee contribution after all adjudication labour and payment charges; vendor remedy funds are excluded from margin.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build a named list of 100 agent vendors from Product Hunt launches, accelerator directories, G2 categories, agent marketplaces, and public product demos. Qualify only products that execute a customer-visible action.
- Send a founder-led “remedy readiness test” showing three concrete failure scenarios from the vendor's own public workflow and a fixed-price 90-day offer.
- Conversion event: signed pilot, paid setup invoice, prepaid remedy balance, log-access clause, adjudication delegation, and derived-taxonomy rights.
- First ten: five Polish/CEE or EU vendors reachable in compatible time zones, then five UK/EU vendors through public founder email and LinkedIn. No introductions are assumed.
- First 100: publish an anonymised quarterly “Agent Action Remedy Index,” integrate with two common agent logging stacks, and offer a standard trust badge whose verification requires an active funded balance and SLA.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1, not yet proven:** a vendor contract that routes all covered remedy claims through AgentRemedy, a prepaid vendor-specific balance, delegated adjudication within fixed bands, action-log access, and durable rights to a de-identified cross-vendor error/remedy taxonomy. A legal memo and one paid contract are required before claiming control.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resource: one vendor's funded remedy event, relevant logs, and contractual rights.

Bootstrap:

1. Spend up to 8,000 PLN on a Polish/EU legal classification memo and contract design. Kill or restructure if the proposed flow is insurance, risk pooling, payment services, or regulated client-money custody.
2. Use the founder's own Stripe/PSP account only for fee billing; hold remedy funds using a structure approved by counsel, preferably a vendor-controlled balance with payment instructions rather than startup custody.
3. Build a manual claim portal, evidence packet, severity rubric, and auditable decision log for under 8,000 PLN in tools and contractors.
4. Offer 100 named vendors a 90-day paid pilot. One signed, funded pilot proves access; three independent vendors and at least 20 adjudicated claims prove repeatability.

An unknown founder can secure this because the offer reduces a live support burden, has a fixed cap, needs no celebrity trust, and can be trialled without replacing the vendor's product.

## Distribution Advantage

Every active programme creates a customer-facing assurance page and claim entry point. The trust badge links end users back to AgentRemedy, while integrations into common logging tools reduce implementation friction for the next vendor. Aggregate failure reports target agent founders through technical communities rather than broad consumer advertising.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with low-value booking and purchasing remedies. Expand into:

1. multi-vendor benchmarks for failure frequency and remedy cost;
2. prevention models and pre-action guardrails trained on adjudicated outcomes;
3. standard programme terms for vertical agent categories;
4. later partnerships with licensed insurers or payment firms only after proprietary loss data exists.

Monitoring vendors can copy a form; they cannot instantly recreate funded programme history, claimant relationships, adjudicated loss curves, integration switching cost, or a recognised remedy credential.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland supplies low-cost technical and multilingual operations and access to the EU regulatory environment. The initial EU scope supports standard consumer-language templates and GDPR governance. Localisation is operational rather than a formal barrier; the defence must be accumulated data and embedded claim routing, not “being Polish.”

## Compounding Advantage

Each paid claim labels intent, action, error mechanism, evidence quality, remedy cost, customer response, and preventive change. More cases improve pricing, triage, fraud detection, SLA design, and vendor selection; better performance makes the programme more credible and harder to replace. Ordinary delivery builds advanced AI/ML capability, proprietary behavioural/transaction data, real-time human-agent review interfaces, and distribution among agent vendors.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

During employment, conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax business cash available for distribution in integrations, security, claim operations, legal templates, and acquisition; optional founder distributions are capped at 5%. The strong case reinvests 100%. Reinvestment expands covered action categories and proprietary loss history rather than funding unrelated product development.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–21: legal classification memo; 20 buyer interviews; three programme designs.
- Days 22–45: manual portal, evidence schema, contract, and one vendor-specific failure teardown.
- Days 46–90: 100-account outreach, 15 discovery calls, five written proposals.
- Days 91–180: secure one signed pilot, setup payment, and vendor-funded remedy mechanism; deliver at least five real or controlled claims with a positive contribution margin.

Paid proof target: one 12,000–20,000 PLN paid setup plus funded remedy balance. Founder time: no more than five hours daily, with contracted legal help and a part-time claim analyst only after payment.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Tranche 1, before paid proof: maximum 28,000 PLN — legal 8k, security/contract review 4k, prototype/tools 6k, outreach/travel 4k, contingency 6k.
- Tranche 2, after paid pilot and successful delivery: up to a cumulative 60,000 PLN for integration, audit logging, and contractor operations.
- Tranche 3, only after three independent payers and positive contribution: up to the founder's 100,000 PLN total.
- Family capital: excluded until repeat demand and a documented acquisition channel exist.
- Maximum pre-proof loss: 28,000 PLN plus founder time.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder leaves employment only after at least five independent active paying vendors, contracted recurring fee revenue covering 1.5× planned monthly payroll and infrastructure, positive claim-level contribution economics, a repeatable outbound or integration channel, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If vendors will not pre-fund remedies, the legal memo, error taxonomy, evidence packet, adjudication tooling, and agent-vendor buyer map can support a narrower paid failure-analysis product. That fallback is not treated as the same scalable company and would require new evaluation. No customer-identifiable data is reused outside contracted purposes.

## Critical Evidence

- Emerging agent identity and transaction protocols show the event class is real.
- A manual funded programme can be sold before a complete platform.
- The founder can build the data and triage layer; fixed-fee specialists cover law and payments.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Programme structure avoids regulated insurance/payment activity.
- Vendors value a funded remedy promise at 6,000–20,000 PLN monthly.
- One contract can grant both claim routing and de-identified taxonomy rights.
- Claim frequency is high enough to create data but low enough to maintain service economics.

## Kill Criteria

- Counsel concludes the POC requires an insurance, payment-institution, or client-money licence unavailable within 12 months and 100,000 PLN.
- Fewer than five serious discovery calls from 100 qualified vendors or no paid funded pilot within 180 days.
- Pilot claim labour consumes more than 50% of fee revenue with no automation path.
- Every serious buyer refuses delegated adjudication or retained derived-taxonomy rights.
- The remedy promise does not improve conversion, retention, support cost, or customer recovery enough to justify the fee.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- Six-month maximum before proof: 28,000 PLN; 12-month founder-capital ceiling: 100,000 PLN.
- Founder: product, ML, sales, and integration, up to five hours daily.
- Fixed-fee commercial/regulatory lawyer.
- Part-time claim analyst only after funded pilot.
- Security/privacy contractor for a bounded review after payment.
