# Zero-to-One Candidate: PulseReturn Rail v1

Post-pivot discovery specification — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses unless cited.

## One-Sentence Thesis

PulseReturn Rail becomes the specialist EU returns cell for independent smart-ring, sports-watch, and recovery-band brands, routing every returned device through serialised diagnostics, disposition, refurbishment/recommerce, and failure intelligence so warranty cost funds a proprietary intelligent-wearable failure dataset.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build diagnostics software, serial/condition data, automated evidence, and outbound while employed. A contract electronics-quality technician handles paid batches; fixed-fee counsel covers consumer, privacy, WEEE/battery shipping, insurance, and brand contracts. The company begins with non-clinical devices and no live biosignal retention.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is an independent wearable brand with 20,000–100,000 hypothesised annual EU shipments, meaningful returns, and no specialist owned EU depot. Generic 3PL triage or whole-unit replacement can discard recoverable devices and hide fit, charging, battery, firmware, contact, and optical failure patterns. Current alternatives are large lifecycle operators, distributors/3PLs, local repair shops, return-to-origin, or internal handling.

## Product Wedge

- 15,000 PLN Failure Batch Autopsy for 30–50 already-returned devices: secure reset, serial/firmware/condition, battery/charging, functional, contact/optical checks appropriate to the model, disposition, recoverable-value estimate, and engineering report.
- After quantified value: 5,000–15,000 PLN monthly minimum plus 120–250 PLN per processed device, parts/freight, and 15%–30% share of realised recommerce recovery.
- The brand/merchant owns refund and warranty decisions. PulseReturn applies a pre-agreed matrix and does not insure the warranty or claim manufacturer authorisation.
- Customer-identifiable data are deleted/isolated. Contract permits retention of de-identified device condition, failure, repair, and outcome derivatives.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Large lifecycle operators optimise broad electronics fleets; small wearable brands may fall between generic return handling and an OEM-scale depot. A ring/band-specific cell can determine whether an expensive replacement is necessary and turn returned inventory into usable stock and engineering feedback. The gap is real only if brands lack comparable current service and produce enough volume.

## Underused Momentum

EU consumer guarantees make repair/replacement an existing cost, and circularity/right-to-repair policy increases pressure for recovery. Wearable exemptions and product-specific rules mean no mandatory independent-wearable repair market is assumed. The same momentum benefits large incumbents.

## Beachhead Market

One non-clinical PPG ring/watch/band family sold in the EU by an independent brand, with 30+ existing returned units in Europe, accessible firmware/reset documentation, and no hazardous battery repair in the first scope. Exclude medical devices, consumer-facing warranty contracting, and brands requiring manufacturer-authorised repair claims.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Batch 15,000 PLN; recurring brand ACV hypothesis 400,000 PLN: 120,000 PLN minimum + 225,000 PLN for 1,500 returns at 150 PLN + 55,000 PLN reports/recovery share.
- Target 55%–62% gross margin after technician time, rent, tools, secure storage, insurance, losses, shipping support, parts, data handling, and inventory depreciation; 25%–30% operating margin at scale.
- Brand prepays/monthly minimum and consigns parts/chargers. PulseReturn does not finance consumer refunds or speculative inventory.
- If a brand has fewer than roughly 1,000 annual routed units, a higher minimum or pooled standard diagnostics must preserve contribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build 100 named brands from crowdfunding alumni, wearable app listings, product databases, trade shows, public EU shipping/warranty pages, and hardware/operations job posts.
- Qualify shipment/return volume, current EU depot, replacement cost, reset/tool access, and batch location before proposal.
- Conversion event: paid batch, custody, processing authority, signed data/disposition terms, and actual baseline costs.
- First ten through direct founder outreach; no 3PL partnership. First 100 only after a repeatable brand cohort, with technical reports/referrals and direct contracts.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1:** paid/minimum-volume return-routing mandate for a defined SKU/territory, device custody, disposition authority, consigned spares/tools, and rights-clean failure derivatives. Owned diagnostics and serialised cross-brand outcomes compound as Tier 2. No control exists today; one batch does not equal a mandate.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Qualify 40 brands and choose one model with an existing EU return batch.
2. Buy universal ESD/battery/charging/test/security equipment only after five disclose real economics.
3. Sell the 15,000 PLN batch before model-specific fixtures or warehouse commitment.
4. Use contract technicians and rented secure space; no sealed-battery repair unless separately proven safe/economic.
5. Within 12 months/100,000 PLN, process 500 devices for two independent brands, sign two paid routed streams, receive consigned spares/tools, document recovered/avoided cost, and retain contractual failure derivatives.

## Distribution Advantage

The brand universe is finite/public and outreach can use model-specific cost logic. A successful batch can convert directly into routed volume. Distribution fails if qualified brands already use comparable lifecycle operators or will not trust a new depot.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with one PPG model, expand across rings/bands/watches, then hearables, EEG headbands, smart glasses, and intelligent wearables. Use cross-brand evidence to improve failure prediction, parts stocking, repairability feedback, and automated disposition.

B2X, MTTC, Metarvo, generic 3PLs, OEM depots, and brands can add the same diagnostics. PulseReturn must win small-brand economics, faster implementation, and a dense wearables-specific failure corpus before incumbents care.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides lower technical/warehouse cost and central EU shipping. EU consumer/circularity rules create operational relevance. Neither blocks established European networks.

## Compounding Advantage

Every routed return adds serial/firmware/battery/charging/fit/contact/failure/repair/recommerce outcomes, device hardware expertise, brand relationships, technician access, diagnostics IP, inventory knowledge, and distribution. This builds advanced AI/ML and intelligent-wearable capability without selling consumer biosignals.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment and cap optional distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds diagnostics, secure cells, quality systems, parts, automation, insurance, and EU hubs only against contracted volumes.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 20 brand/repair interviews; 100-account list; compliance/insurance scope.
- Days 31–75: qualify 40 brands, obtain five real cost/volume disclosures, send 20 fixed batch offers.
- Days 76–120: one paid 15,000 PLN batch and custody.
- Days 121–180: deliver quantified report and secure one paid country/SKU routing trial or a second independent batch.

Founder remains employed/five hours daily. No permanent site or inventory precedes payment.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 35,000 PLN: bench/security/tools 12k; technician 6k; rent/storage 4k; legal/insurance/WEEE/battery review 6k; outreach/shipping 4k; contingency 3k.
- Up to 65,000 PLN after paid batch and positive contribution.
- Up to 100,000 PLN after two independent brands and one routed stream.
- Family capital excluded until repeat routed demand, positive contribution, and acquisition economics.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Test | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified independent-brand denominator | 100-list/40 qualifications | Kill if fewer than 30 plausible recurring brands |
| Incremental batch value | Paid batch with baseline | Kill if recovery/avoided cost does not exceed fee |
| Routing trust/control | Paid SKU/country contract | Batch consulting alone is failure |
| Diagnostic/tool/spare access | Contract before routing | Exclude models without safe access |
| Fully loaded margin | Time, losses, rent, support | Kill below 45% gross |
| Incumbent gap | Compare current depot economics | Kill if mature operators already win target niche |
| Wealth robustness | Bottom-up brand cohorts | Fail if 30 × 400k is implausible |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after five independent routed brands, six months positive contribution, one renewal/volume expansion, repeatable acquisition, technicians independent of founder, recurring gross profit above 1.5× burn, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Tools can be resold at uncertain value. Reusable assets include diagnostics, model teardowns, failure taxonomy, secure workflows, technicians, and brand outreach. All customer/consumer data follow contract deletion; a generic 3PL is not the fallback.

## Critical Evidence

EU rules establish an existing repair/replacement event; major companies establish a lifecycle-service market. No evidence establishes the independent-wearable gap, volumes, ACV, margin, or data rights.

## Critical Hypotheses

One brand pays; a batch shows >15k value; two route returns; 30 brands can each create 400k ACV; sealed devices still permit valuable diagnosis/recommerce; failure derivatives improve economics.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than 30 qualified recurring brands among 100 accounts.
- No paid batch after 20 qualified proposals by day 120.
- No paid routed SKU/territory after a successful batch by month 12.
- Existing depot already provides comparable evidence/recovery.
- Gross margin below 45% or founder labour scales with units.
- Brand refuses required tools/spares/data/disposition authority.
- Conservative founder wealth falls below 5m.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

35,000 PLN pre-proof; 100,000 PLN year one. Founder software/data/sales; contract electronics-quality technician; part-time secure logistics after routing; fixed-fee legal/insurance/environmental review.
