# Zero-to-One Candidate: SpeechBridge Workline

Wave-3 candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses unless a source is cited.

## One-Sentence Thesis

SpeechBridge Workline gives professionals with dysarthria or progressively changing speech a persistent business-call number that learns their speech, transparently restates unclear phrases in a chosen synthetic voice, and can add a trained human captioner for important calls—turning successful work conversations into a compounding personalised speech-to-intent system.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build the AI orchestration, correction workflow, consent ledger, call-quality analytics, and browser softphone while retaining corporate employment. A contract speech-language therapist reviews onboarding and communication design; a contract telephony engineer integrates a compliant EU carrier only after deposits. The product does not diagnose, treat, impersonate, or make emergency-call promises.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The initial customer is an English- or Polish-speaking professional, freelancer, founder, or job seeker whose dysarthria makes high-stakes remote calls exhausting, frequently misunderstood, or avoided. Their economic problem is not imperfect transcription in general: it is losing control of a client, interview, sales, or team conversation because the other party cannot follow them quickly enough.

Current workarounds include repeating themselves, asking a colleague or family member to interpret, typing in chat, using generic captions, avoiding calls, or assembling speech-recognition and synthetic-voice tools. These break conversational flow, expose dependence, and do not preserve a work identity or learn from corrections across calls.

## Product Wedge

- A prepaid 90-day Workline programme at 900 PLN for five design customers: dedicated browser-call identity, personalised phrase pack, live captions, push-to-restatement, post-call correction review, and up to two human-supported important calls.
- The system never silently changes the speaker's words. The user explicitly triggers or approves synthetic restatement; the call opening discloses AI assistance and any human captioner.
- Important-call fallback is scheduled, not guaranteed on demand. Emergency, medical-decision, regulated legal-advice, and covert-call use are excluded.
- After pilot: 249–349 PLN/month subscription plus metered human assistance; business/team plans can sponsor an employee without gaining access to content or personal speech data.
- Default retention minimises raw audio. User-approved corrections, acoustic embeddings, phrase preferences, and outcome labels are contractually portable/exportable; optional research consent is separate from service consent.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Assistive speech products are commonly framed as transcription or daily communication tools, while the user's willingness to pay is concentrated around economically consequential moments. A persistent work number can sell successful call completion—not generic accessibility—then learn exactly which phrases, people, accents, and contexts create failures. The narrow wedge can be more reliable and accountable than a general model without requiring general speech recognition from day one.

## Underused Momentum

Voiceitt validates demand for non-standard-speech recognition, Google reports that Project Relate required more than one million speech samples, and Apple provides on-device Personal Voice. These advances reduce component cost while revealing the unresolved problem: generic platforms can generate or recognise speech, but do not own a cross-platform work-call identity, correction history, explicit intervention protocol, and outcome record for an individual professional.

## Beachhead Market

Polish and European English-speaking professionals with acquired or progressive dysarthria who already make at least four economically important remote calls per month and can use a browser softphone. The first programme excludes people who need a medical device, emergency reliability, uncontrolled inbound PSTN access, or continuous human interpretation.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Discovery programme: 900 PLN prepaid for 90 days.
- Standard subscription hypothesis: 249 PLN/month; Professional 349 PLN/month with scheduled human-supported minutes; employer-sponsored seat 499 PLN/month including administration and privacy controls.
- Scaled blended ARPU hypothesis: 249–299 PLN/month, 70%–75% gross margin after telephony, inference, support, captioner time, refunds, and payment fees; 25%–32% operating margin after acquisition, accessibility QA, security, and product development.
- Human assistance is metered or capped; if customers require continuous 1:1 interpretation, the software margin and founder-wealth model fail.
- No revenue depends on selling identifiable speech. Research licences, if ever offered, require separate opt-in, a documented public-benefit protocol, and pricing independent of the subscription case.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build a 100-person/organisation target list from Polish and European dysarthria, Parkinson's, motor-neurone-disease, stroke, cerebral-palsy, and disability-employment communities; contact individuals only through public opt-in channels and organisations with a request to circulate a paid opportunity.
- Offer a 20-minute private call-friction interview followed by the fixed 900 PLN 90-day programme; do not ask a charity or clinic to endorse the product.
- Conversion event: payment, consented baseline call, at least one real work-call objective, and agreement to measure correction rate, fallback use, completion, and repeat use.
- First ten: founder-led outreach, two public accessibility demos, and referrals from paying users. Pay fixed referral fees only after a completed paid programme.
- First 100: documented before/after call outcomes, search content around work with dysarthria, employer accommodation budgets, and product-led referrals using the member's persistent Workline identity. No unspecified clinical partnership is required.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed compounding Tier 2:** the member's persistent work-call identity plus longitudinal, consented mapping from their non-standard speech and context to intended phrases, corrections, intervention choices, and call outcomes. The right is secured by a direct customer contract that permits service improvement for that member and de-identified aggregate model improvement while preserving deletion/export rights. It is not currently proved, is vulnerable to platform copying, and becomes defensible only if users make enough consequential calls and switching would discard materially useful personal history.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Recruit five customers through direct/community outreach and collect 900 PLN each before telephony build.
2. Deliver the first calls with a browser room, off-the-shelf captions, explicit user-controlled restatement, and scheduled human fallback; measure where the workflow—not only the model—prevents failure.
3. Store only consented correction pairs, phrase/context tags, intervention choices, and outcome measures under a plain-language data agreement.
4. Build the dedicated-number/softphone integration only after three customers complete four meaningful calls and at least two request renewal.
5. Within 12 months and 100,000 PLN, reach 30 paying members, 1,000+ consented meaningful calls, at least 10 members with six months of history, and a measured personalisation advantage over a reset baseline.

An unknown founder can reach individuals without institutional endorsement, sell a bounded programme, and assemble the first workflow from existing speech, telephony, and captioning components. The control point is disproved if personal history does not improve outcomes or retention.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial list is accessible through public condition and disability-employment communities, relevant search terms, webinars, and direct user referrals. Each successful member story can reduce trust cost, while the persistent number can create receiver-side familiarity. Distribution compounds only if paid member referrals and employer sponsorship become measurable channels; community goodwill alone is not a moat.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with user-controlled important calls, not general communication. Expand to inbound Workline calls, multilingual phrase models, team meetings, interview practice, employer accommodation plans, and communication continuity as speech changes. Later, the same real-time correction/intervention system can support other non-standard speech and high-noise human–AI interfaces.

Voiceitt, Apple, Google, Microsoft, carriers, and accessibility platforms can copy individual features. SpeechBridge wins only if the cross-platform identity, high-stakes workflow, longitudinal individual model, outcome evidence, and trusted intervention protocol improve faster than a user could recreate them inside one platform.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Begin with Polish/English bilingual phrases, EU-hosted data, GDPR-grade consent/deletion, explicit synthetic-speech disclosure, and local employer-accommodation documentation. Poland supplies a lower-cost supervised-service and engineering base for an EU-wide English product. Regulation and localisation improve trust but are not treated as an exclusionary barrier.

## Compounding Advantage

Ordinary delivery accumulates rare consented speech-to-intent corrections, longitudinal within-person speech change, phrase/context failure patterns, intervention choices, and call outcomes. This builds advanced speech AI/ML, real-time assistive HCI, proprietary datasets/IP, research relationships earned after user proof, and access to speech-language and accessibility specialists. The asset compounds only while the subscription itself produces positive contribution.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

The conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash while the founder remains employed and cap optional distributions at 5%; the strong case reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds accessibility QA, telephony redundancy, personalisation research, security, multilingual models, and channels—never speculative raw-data acquisition disconnected from paying use.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 25 problem interviews, five recorded-with-consent call walkthroughs, data/privacy terms, landing page, and a list of 100 reachable people/communities.
- Days 31–60: sell five prepaid 900 PLN programmes; kill or redesign if fewer than three pay after 30 qualified offers.
- Days 61–120: deliver at least 30 meaningful calls through browser rooms with captions, user-controlled restatement, and scheduled human fallback; measure completion, correction, latency, support time, and willingness to reuse.
- Days 121–180: obtain at least three renewals at 249+ PLN/month, two paid referrals or one employer-sponsored seat, and evidence that personal history beats a reset baseline on repeated phrases.

The founder remains employed and works no more than five hours daily. The first paid proof requires no carrier partnership or clinical endorsement.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 25,000 PLN: privacy/accessibility legal review 5k; therapist/captioner review and delivery 5k; prototypes/inference/telephony 6k; participant travel/equipment/accommodations 3k; outreach/content 3k; contingency 3k.
- Up to 40,000 PLN only after prepaid customers begin delivery and contribution time is measured.
- Up to 100,000 PLN only after three renewals, at least two independent referral sources or an employer seat, positive contribution excluding founder labour, and a measurable personalisation lift.
- Family capital remains excluded until multiple independent recurring customers, repeatable acquisition, and positive contribution economics.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after at least 100 independent recurring members or equivalent contracted employer seats, positive fully loaded contribution for six months, monthly logo retention above 95% as a hypothesis-confirmation threshold, one repeatable channel with under-12-month payback, support time below the modelled cap, recurring gross profit covering 1.5× business burn, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If subscriptions fail, reusable assets include consent/data-contract templates, accessibility research, real-time intervention UI, personal speech evaluation tooling, call-quality analytics, and relationships with paid users and specialists. Identifiable speech is deleted/exported according to the contract; it is not repurposed to rescue the business. A generic AI transcription consultancy is not the fallback.

## Critical Evidence

Existing products and projects establish that non-standard-speech recognition is technically valuable and data-intensive, and that personal synthetic voices are becoming platform features. They do not establish SpeechBridge willingness to pay, telephony reliability, retention, market size, or a durable control point.

## Critical Hypotheses

Five professionals prepay 900 PLN; important-call completion creates renewal at 249+ PLN/month; longitudinal history measurably improves outcomes; human help stays bounded; a persistent identity resists free substitution; and 4,000 global paying members are reachable without heroic CAC.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than three prepaid programmes after 30 qualified offers by day 60.
- Fewer than three subscription renewals or no paid referral/employer seat by day 180.
- Personal history shows no meaningful outcome or correction improvement over a reset baseline after six months.
- Fully loaded gross margin remains below 55% or continuous human support is necessary.
- Users prefer existing/free tools and will not sustain at least 249 PLN/month.
- Privacy, consent, disclosure, or telephony requirements prevent a lawful bounded product within 100,000 PLN.
- Bottom-up evidence makes 4,000 conservative subscribers implausible, causing founder wealth below 5m PLN.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

25,000 PLN before paid proof; maximum 40,000 PLN in the first 180 days and 100,000 PLN in year one. Founder product/AI/sales; contract telephony engineer only after payment; contract speech-language therapist/accessibility reviewer; scheduled captioners paid per delivered session; fixed-fee privacy counsel.
