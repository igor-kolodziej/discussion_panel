# Zero-to-One Candidate: TiltLock

**Canonical business specification: v1, frozen 2026-07-15 Europe/Warsaw for independent simulation.**

Candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses to test.

## One-Sentence Thesis

TiltLock is a physiology-aware self-binding companion for serious online poker players: before a session the player chooses stop rules, then a local app detects loss-of-control states and enforces only those precommitted limits while building a uniquely paired physiology–decision–financial-outcome dataset.

## Founder Fit

The founder has biosignal, neuroscience, ML, behavioural, and poker interest, and can build signal-quality checks, personalised state models, and a desktop prototype. The first cohort uses off-the-shelf chest straps or watches and manual analysis, avoiding hardware manufacture and operator partnerships. Direct-to-consumer sales can be performed outside corporate hours.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first customer is a serious online cash-game or tournament player who plays at least 40 hours monthly, studies strategy, owns a wearable or will use a chest strap, and has experienced revenge play, fatigue, broken stop-loss rules, or session extension that damaged bankroll.

Solvers improve decisions when calm; they do not stop the player from continuing when arousal, fatigue, and losses degrade self-control. Current workarounds are willpower, static stop-loss notes, platform cooling-off tools, bankroll trackers, coaches, and generic breathwork. They are either too blunt, retrospective, or disconnected from the user's actual physiological state and self-authored rules.

## Product Wedge

Founding product: a paid six-week “A-Game Lock” programme at 599 PLN.

1. User exports only their own hand histories and supplies supported HR/HRV and sleep data.
2. The user labels tilt, rule breaches, and session context; the service identifies personal risk signatures after play, not poker strategy errors.
3. Before each later session, the user selects a binding rule such as a maximum duration, stop-loss, mandatory break, or full cooldown.
4. A local desktop prototype monitors authorised hand-history updates, session duration, and BLE physiology. It never advises an action, bet, or table choice. When the user's own rule fires, it blocks approved poker clients/domains or locks the session for the precommitted period.
5. The user receives a behavioural report: rule compliance, state transitions, and bankroll exposure avoided.

The app works as a paid self-control product even if the user declines research reuse.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Serious players already spend on tools that improve expected value, yet the financially costly failure often happens after they know what good play is. The overlooked product is not another coach but a commitment device that becomes harder to override precisely when self-control falls. Pairing objective game events with physiology and user-labelled state creates a personalised intervention loop that static platform limits cannot match across sites.

## Underused Momentum

- Players can export their own hand histories.
- Commodity wearables expose continuous heart-rate and recovery data without custom hardware.
- Local software can enforce user-authored OS controls without cooperation from the poker operator.
- Online poker communities already pay for tracking, solvers, coaching, and bankroll tools.

## Beachhead Market

English- and Polish-speaking serious online poker players using desktop clients that permit personal hand-history export, beginning with cash-game players because session boundaries and loss rules are easier to define. Exclude minors, players seeking treatment, and any promise to diagnose addiction or improve strategic win rate.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Founding cohort: 599 PLN for six weeks; user supplies a supported wearable or rents a chest strap for a 150 PLN refundable deposit.
- Subscription after cohort: 99–139 PLN monthly or 999–1,299 PLN annually.
- Optional team/coaching dashboard later: 2,500–8,000 PLN annually per coaching stable, only with player control and consent.
- Cohort gross-margin hypothesis: 55% after founder/analyst time and support.
- Software gross-margin hypothesis: 85%; operating-margin hypothesis: 25%–35% after support, acquisition, privacy, and engineering.
- Value proof: user-level reduction in rule-breaking bankroll exposure and session overrun; no claim that the product makes users profitable.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- First list: 100 named or permissioned prospects from Polish and English poker forums, public Discord communities, Reddit communities, coaching-stable communities, poker newsletters, and search terms around tilt/stop loss. Follow each community's promotion rules.
- Offer: a 20-minute private “tilt loss map” call plus a fixed 599 PLN six-week founding programme; no free custom analysis.
- Conversion event: payment, supported device confirmation, consented data import, and a signed acknowledgement that the product is non-strategic and user-directed.
- First ten: direct community posts and one-to-one outreach to players already discussing tilt; five payments are required before buying or renting any sensors.
- First 100: publish aggregated “state-to-rule breach” findings, use affiliate agreements with independent mental-game coaches capped at 20% of first-year gross profit, and retain the direct billing/data relationship.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed compounding Tier 2, not yet proven:** the installed self-binding workflow, user-authored enforcement permissions, longitudinal personal baseline, direct billing relationship, and contracted rights to de-identified/anonymised cross-user derivatives. Users can leave and incumbents can build controls, so the control point is not Tier 1. Its defensibility depends on intervention outcomes, personal calibration, trust, and cross-platform continuity compounding faster than a generic tracker.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resource: paying players, their own permitted hand histories, wearable data, rule labels, enforcement permission, and lawful research rights.

Bootstrap:

1. Sell five 599 PLN deposits before purchasing six Polar-class chest straps or commissioning software.
2. Deliver the first ten cohort users with manual post-session analysis, a standard protocol, and existing OS blocking tools.
3. Build a local-only prototype that reads the user's own file updates and BLE stream; transmit only derived session features unless the user explicitly opts into research.
4. Obtain a written gaming-platform policy/legal review confirming that non-strategic user-authored session control is permissible for supported clients.
5. Twelve-month proof: 100 paying users, 50 eight-week completers, at least 60% month-three paid retention, and a useful de-identified paired dataset from separately consenting users.

An unknown solo founder can recruit users directly, demonstrate the product on their own data, and deliver without operator APIs or clinical endorsement.

## Distribution Advantage

The beachhead has concentrated communities and coaches whose economic interest is player longevity, but the business does not rely on influencer access. User-generated precommitment templates and aggregated findings can spread through search and communities. Cross-platform control is the distribution wedge against site-specific responsible-gaming tools.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with online poker. Expand only after retention proof to:

1. coaching-stable licences with player-controlled data;
2. trading and esports sessions where behaviour and outcomes are machine-readable;
3. a general biosignal commitment SDK for high-stakes digital work;
4. optional custom ring/earable integration after data identifies the minimum useful signals.

The moat is not heart rate. It is the longitudinal mapping from an individual's state to actual rule breach, the tested intervention timing, local enforcement permissions, trust, and cross-domain outcome corpus.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides low-cost product development and an accessible poker community, while an English product can sell globally. EU privacy discipline and local processing can differentiate a sensitive-data product. There is no durable geographic barrier; the defence must be outcome data, personal calibration, and product trust.

## Compounding Advantage

Each consenting session aligns physiology, sleep, game tempo, losses, self-labelled state, chosen rule, intervention, override attempt, and outcome. More longitudinal data improves personalised state detection, intervention timing, sensor selection, and false-positive reduction. Ordinary subscription delivery builds biosignal intelligence, behavioural data, real-time HCI, AI/ML capability, wearable integration expertise, direct distribution, and potential proprietary IP.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

In conservative and expected cases, 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution is reinvested while the founder remains employed; optional founder distributions stay at or below 5%. The strong case reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds sensor integrations, local security, community acquisition, behavioural science, and personalised models that improve retention and gross margin.

- Days 22–60: deliver first manual cohort using users' own exports and supported wearables; no automated strategic output.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after at least 300 active paying users from multiple independent channels, positive contribution after support and refunds, three consecutive cohorts or months showing recurring retention, a repeatable CAC below first-year gross profit, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If poker willingness to pay fails, the founder retains only lawfully reusable signal-quality tooling, local BLE/desktop integration, consent architecture, and intervention research—not user raw histories outside their permissions. A move to trading, esports, or clinical behaviour is a new candidate requiring re-evaluation.

## Critical Evidence

- Users can export their own hand histories.
- Platform policies clearly distinguish prohibited real-time strategic advice, creating a testable non-strategic boundary.
- Existing session alerts and platform cooling-off tools validate the problem while showing substitution risk.
- Off-the-shelf wearables remove hardware capital from proof.

## Critical Hypotheses

- A serious player pays 599 PLN for a state-control programme and 99–139 PLN monthly thereafter.
- User-authored enforcement is valuable rather than too restrictive.
- Physiology adds predictive value over loss, time, and behaviour alone.
- The product remains permitted because it provides no poker advice.
- 6,000 global subscribers can be acquired without influencer dependence or heroic CAC.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than five paid deposits after compliant access to 100 qualified prospects.
- No platform-policy-safe implementation for local non-strategic enforcement.
- Fewer than 70% of cohort sessions have usable sensor/hand-history data.
- No reduction in rule-breaking exposure or users routinely disable the app when it matters.
- Month-three paid retention below 40% after 50 users, or support consumes more than 30% of revenue.
- Acquisition cost exceeds first-year gross profit with no organic/referral improvement after three tested channels.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- Maximum 24,000 PLN before paid proof; 100,000 PLN in the first 12 months.
- Founder: ML, biosignals, product, community sales, five hours daily.
- Part-time desktop engineer after deposits.
- Fixed-fee gaming-policy/privacy lawyer.
- Mental-game coach as paid adviser, not required gatekeeper or exclusive channel.
