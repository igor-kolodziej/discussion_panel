# Wave 3 Advisory Evaluations

Applied with `$evaluate-zero-to-one`. These scores are diagnostic only and do not satisfy any validation gate.

## Neutral Discovery-Stage Disclosure

The business is at idea/planning/discovery stage. No pilots, contracts, proprietary datasets, customer outcomes, or control-point proof are assumed unless explicitly documented. The evaluation asks whether the proposed validation path can produce missing evidence quickly and affordably, rather than treating absence of post-launch proof as evidence that an operating company failed. Implausible assumptions, weak economics, speculative access, and dependencies that cannot be tested are still penalised.

## HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail

**Exact candidate:** `finalist_hololens_uptime_exit.md`

**Advisory score:** 64/100  
**Recommendation:** `kill`  
**Founder Wealth Gate:** fail  
**Reachability Gate:** marginal pass  
**Proposed control point:** temporary Tier 1 inventory during a bounded EOL window; enduring expansion control falls to Tier 3 unless separately proved.

### Evidence State

- Official: HoloLens 2 manufacturing has ended, stock is exhausted globally, and Microsoft gives a defined security-servicing horizon.
- Official/public acquisition source: Microsoft partner and deployment materials can seed a prospect list, but do not prove active small fleets or budgets.
- Hypothesis: used units can be acquired/tested below 6,000 PLN, a qualified customer will pay 15,000 PLN within 180 days, and a shared reserve pool can support 60,000–180,000 PLN annual contracts.
- Hypothesis: the business can escape a shrinking installed base into 80 multi-vendor sites at 120,000 PLN annually without becoming a bespoke migration/integration service.

### Gate Diagnosis

The 180-day test is bounded and staged: one diagnostic unit, a fixed audit, a deposit before pooled inventory, and a customer configuration test fit within 35,000 PLN while the founder remains employed. The enterprise procurement and security cycle make it marginal, not impossible.

The conservative wealth case does not clear on the HoloLens business itself. It assumes a later multi-vendor continuity company whose recurring need, control point, inventory economics, and customer denominator are not evidenced by the initial EOL wedge. HoloLens inventory is physically controllable but depreciating and openly purchasable; after the EOL interval, the enduring business is migration expertise plus ordinary test hardware. That expansion is a different, service-heavy shape rather than a demonstrated compounding consequence of the wedge.

### Strongest Objections

1. The scarce resource is temporary, reproducible secondary-market inventory; paid reservations reduce speculation but do not create durable scarcity.
2. The first buyer population is finite and uncounted. The 80-site conservative case depends on successor-platform demand rather than the documented HoloLens problem.
3. Enterprise trust, secure configuration handling, shipping, and compatibility work may prevent paid proof inside six months or create low-margin bespoke delivery.
4. Integrators and device-management providers can purchase the same units and fold continuity into an existing relationship.

### Falsification Path

The proposed 100-account qualification, paid audit/deposit, shared-pool utilisation, and paid successor migration can falsify the operational thesis cheaply. They cannot make the current Founder Wealth Gate pass without replacing the wealth model with evidence for an enduring control point. Because that replacement would materially change the business shape, the candidate is killed rather than panelled.

## SpeechBridge Workline

**Exact candidate:** `finalist_speechbridge_workline.md`

**Advisory score:** 78/100  
**Recommendation:** `pursue`  
**Founder Wealth Gate:** provisional pass with a decision-critical denominator/CAC risk  
**Reachability Gate:** pass  
**Proposed control point:** compounding Tier 2 if longitudinal personal history measurably improves outcomes and retention; otherwise Tier 3.

### Evidence State

- Official/company evidence: non-standard-speech recognition products exist; Google reports a million-plus-sample research effort; Apple provides personal synthetic voice. This validates need/technical momentum and intensifies substitution risk.
- Hypothesis: at least three of 30 qualified prospects prepay, at least three renew, personal history beats a reset baseline, and scheduled human fallback stays bounded.
- Hypothesis: 4,000 global members sustain 249 PLN/month at 70% gross and 25% operating margin, creating 6.723m PLN founder equity at 75% ownership and a 3× operating-profit value.

### Gate Diagnosis

The 180-day proof path is unusually direct for assistive technology: the buyer is the user, the offer is prepaid, the first service uses browser rooms and existing components, and no clinic, insurer, carrier integration, or endorsement is required. The 25,000 PLN pre-proof cap, five-hours-daily constraint, explicit exclusions, and renewal/referral tests make Reachability credible.

The wealth arithmetic is internally consistent and does not double-count distributions. Four thousand global subscribers is not inherently heroic relative to the broad condition population, but the addressable subset—professionals making frequent economically important calls, able to pay roughly 3,000 PLN annually, and not satisfied by platform tools—is unquantified. The wealth gate is therefore a provisional pass based on a credible global narrow-market path, not established demand. The candidate must kill itself if cohort evidence cannot extrapolate to 4,000 members with acceptable CAC/support.

### Strongest Objections

1. Apple, Google, Voiceitt, carriers, meeting platforms, or OS accessibility layers can copy captions, voice generation, and personalised recognition; only measurable longitudinal outcome improvement and persistent identity can elevate control above Tier 3.
2. The user may prefer typed chat, ordinary captions, an interpreter, or a free platform feature rather than pay 249 PLN every month.
3. Human fallback, privacy support, and individual onboarding may make the apparent software subscription a labour-intensive assistive service.
4. A persistent number creates familiarity but not strong lock-in if number portability, model export, or ethical deletion lets the user leave; defensibility must come from better service, not hostage data.
5. Condition-community access is reachable but delicate; direct acquisition economics and referral rates remain unknown.

### Founder-Wealth and Control Arithmetic

Conservative founder wealth: 4,000 × 249 PLN × 12 = 11.952m PLN revenue; 25% operating margin = 2.988m PLN profit; 3× = 8.964m PLN company value; 75% founder = 6.723m PLN. The buffer above 5m is sufficient only if margin and ownership survive support and acquisition costs. At the same terms, approximately 2,975 subscribers produce 5m PLN founder equity; a lower 15% operating margin would require roughly 4,960, showing sensitivity rather than hidden certainty.

### Falsification Path

- Demand: 30 qualified offers → at least three prepaid programmes by day 60.
- Recurrence: at least three renewals plus a paid referral or employer seat by day 180.
- Control: within-person history must beat a reset baseline after repeated calls and six months.
- Economics: fully loaded gross margin must rise above 55% and support must not require continuous 1:1 labour.
- Scale: channel cohorts must support a credible bottom-up route to 4,000 members without over-12-month payback.

The candidate is panel-eligible because it passes both hard gates at discovery-stage credibility, makes missing evidence explicit, and can falsify the decisive assumptions before large capital. The advisory score is not validation.

## Routing Decision

- HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail: killed; do not panel.
- SpeechBridge Workline: freeze the exact candidate as a canonical version and submit to two independent simulated judges.
