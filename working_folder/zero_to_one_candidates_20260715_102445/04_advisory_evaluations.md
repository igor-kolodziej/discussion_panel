# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluations

These are advisory evaluations under `$evaluate-zero-to-one`; they are not simulated-panel scores, real-chat scores, confirmations, or validation gates.

## Neutral Discovery-Stage Disclosure Applied to Every Candidate

The business is at the idea/planning/discovery stage. No pilots, contracts, proprietary datasets, customer commitments, control-point proof, margins, or retention are assumed unless explicitly documented. The evaluation judges whether the proposed validation path can produce the missing evidence quickly and affordably rather than treating absence of post-launch proof as evidence that an operating company failed. Implausible assumptions, weak economics, speculative access, and dependencies that cannot be tested remain penalised.

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: AgentRemedy Rail

## Diagnosis

The core insight is strong: a funded remedy can change customer downside in a way monitoring cannot, and the adjudicated event would create unusually useful agent-failure data. As written, however, legal structure and vendor delegation are simultaneous existential dependencies, while willingness to pay at recurring six-figure annual fees is unsupported. The proof path is bounded and intelligently puts legal classification first, but it cannot establish a Tier-1 claim route until a vendor both funds it and grants delegated adjudication and derived-data rights.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence establishes emerging agent identity/transaction infrastructure and a need for comparable incident reporting. The buyer's remedy budget, legal classification, claim frequency, fee level, log access, conversion lift, and retained rights are hypotheses. The discovery-stage plan correctly treats them as tests, but a research memo—not product work—must eliminate the regulatory veto before panel use.

## Overall Score

**67/100.** Weighted sum 133 across total weight 20; `10 × (133 / 20) = 66.5`, rounded to 67.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 today; Tier-1-shaped only if contracted and prepaid.** Strongest copy risk: a payment provider, insurer, or agent observability vendor can add a vendor-funded claims administrator after one legal/vendor contract. Catch-up becomes difficult only after AgentRemedy owns material adjudicated history and an accepted credential.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass on planning logic, with high sensitivity.** The scenarios include customers, price, revenue, gross and operating margin, capital, ownership, dilution, reinvestment, value, and timing without double-counting programme balances. The conservative 80-vendor case reaches 5.07m PLN founder equity at 3× operating profit and 80% ownership, leaving little cushion if annual fee, margin, or multiple is lower.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass conditionally.** A 12,000–20,000 PLN setup payment within 180 days at 28,000 PLN pre-proof risk is compatible with employment. The condition is that the legal memo supports a non-regulated structure and a vendor can fund/delegate the event without a licence; those are tested sequentially rather than assumed.

## Expansion Check

The low-value agent-action beachhead is coherent and prevention/insurance adjacencies are logical. Competitor entry is substantial because payments, insurance, and observability incumbents already possess trust and distribution. The EU/Poland angle lowers operating cost but does not prevent entry.

## Fatal Objections

1. The proposed remedy flow may become regulated insurance, payment services, or client-money custody.
2. A small vendor may not delegate adjudication or pay 120,000+ PLN annually before the programme has brand trust.
3. One contract may not simultaneously grant claim routing, log access, and durable cross-vendor taxonomy rights.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A prepaid remedy event produces more defensible customer value and labelled agent-failure outcomes than generic AI monitoring.

## Recommendation

research further

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CatchPool AI

## Diagnosis

CatchPool makes AI-error reporting economically concrete and has an unusually cheap manual paid-proof path. The weakness is structural: an internal bounty remains discretionary, can be copied by an employer or governance platform, and may produce reward gaming instead of scarce workflow control. The corpus can compound, but the client owns the underlying work and can terminate the feed before switching costs become meaningful.

## Evidence and Assumptions

ABSL supports the installed buyer base and high AI adoption; OECD supports fragmented incident learning. Employer willingness to pre-fund bounties, employee response, avoided-loss measurement, annual fee, renewal, data rights, and 90-workflow reach are hypotheses. The six-week pilot tests participation and value efficiently but cannot by itself prove durable routing.

## 16-Area Scorecard

| Area | Score | Weight | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Core insight | 8 | 1 | Paying for verified catches is more behaviourally credible than asking for reports |
| Customer pain and willingness to pay | 6 | 2 | Hidden errors matter; a distinct bounty budget is uncertain |
| Business model and economics | 6 | 2 | Pilot economics are clear; recurring value and adjudication load are hypotheses |
| Profitable domination potential | 6 | 1 | Can win a workflow niche but employer internalisation is easy |
| Beachhead quality | 8 | 1 | Polish business-services centres are concentrated and specific |
| Expansion path | 7 | 1 | Evaluations and intervention triggers follow logically |
| Asymmetric defensibility | 6 | 1.5 | Accepted-correction corpus helps, but access is revocable and non-exclusive |
| Local or regional advantage | 7 | 0.5 | Strong local buyer density, limited formal defence |
| Strength of advantage | 6 | 1 | Better incentive design, not yet a hard control point |
| Timing and underused momentum | 8 | 0.5 | Rapid deployment creates real near-miss pressure |
| Repeatable distribution | 6 | 2 | Named outbound is feasible; enterprise repeatability is unproved |
| Durability and profit retention | 5 | 1.5 | Internal teams and governance vendors can replicate the programme |
| Founder fit and minimum people stack | 8 | 0.5 | Strong fit and bounded delivery |
| Risk clarity and first-test quality | 8 | 0.5 | Fast paid test with anti-gaming measures |
| Founder wealth creation potential | 7 | 2 | Denominator works on paper; annual price and margin remain sensitive |
| Reachability and capital efficiency | 8 | 2 | Paid pilot is possible below 32k PLN without integration |

## Overall Score

**67/100.** Weighted sum 133; `10 × (133 / 20) = 66.5`, rounded to 67.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 if a workflow mandate and derived-data clause are signed; otherwise Tier 3.** Strongest copy risk: the employer or an existing AI-governance vendor adds rewards to an incident form and retains all data. The proposed corpus and employee trust do not yet create enough switching cost to overcome that one-process-change risk, so the overall score remains below 80.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass on scenario completeness, with material commercial sensitivity.** The conservative 90-workflow, 120,000 PLN annual-fee case reaches 5.18m PLN founder equity at 3× operating profit and 80% ownership. The 4.3% Polish-centre denominator is not heroic, but account value, operating margin, and non-founder delivery need paid evidence.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The 15,000 PLN paid fee plus client-owned 10,000 PLN pool can be sold and delivered in six weeks with 32,000 PLN maximum pre-proof loss while employed. Privacy acceptance, buyer budget, and employee behaviour are critical but tested in one bounded pilot rather than requiring a complete product.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is focused and locally dense. Expansion into evaluations and intervention triggers is coherent, but incumbents can enter from governance, RPA implementation, or internal quality operations. The local advantage improves acquisition, not durability.

## Fatal Objections

1. The bounty is an optional programme, not a mandatory economic event or externally controlled workflow.
2. Reward gaming, duplicate reports, and subjective avoided-loss estimates can consume the value created.
3. The employer can internalise the programme and stop the proprietary data feed with little switching cost.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Verified correction payouts can surface higher-information human-AI failure examples than passive incident reporting.

## Recommendation

kill

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: TiltLock

## Diagnosis

TiltLock has the cleanest founder-controlled proof path and strongest founder-domain fit. Its counterintuitive insight is credible: the expensive failure is often inability to stop, not lack of strategy knowledge, and user-precommitted enforcement can act when willpower fails. The main risks—policy compatibility, willingness to pay, intervention benefit, sensor adherence, retention, and CAC—are independent but can be tested sequentially below the capital cap. Defensibility is Tier 2 rather than a monopoly control point, so success depends on longitudinal calibration and trust compounding quickly.

## Evidence and Assumptions

PokerStars documents user-owned hand-history export and prohibits real-time strategic advice; existing session alerts, cooling-off tools, and adjacent physiology products validate the category and substitution risk. Price, conversion, physiological lift, override behaviour, retention, policy interpretation, and the 6,000-subscriber denominator are hypotheses. At discovery stage, the five-deposit/manual-cohort sequence is an appropriate way to test them rather than evidence of a failed operating company.

|---|---:|---:|---|
| Core insight | 8 | 1 | Self-binding at the moment of impaired control is a distinct job-to-be-done |
| Customer pain and willingness to pay | 7 | 2 | Financial pain is direct; actual payment/retention remain unproved |
| Business model and economics | 7 | 2 | Subscription can scale; support and CAC are decisive unknowns |
| Profitable domination potential | 7 | 1 | Serious poker is a focused global niche with adjacency |
| Beachhead quality | 8 | 1 | Users, data source, event, and exclusion boundary are specific |
| Expansion path | 8 | 1 | Poker can seed a broader high-stakes digital self-control engine |
| Asymmetric defensibility | 7 | 1.5 | Longitudinal baseline and outcomes compound but are user-portable/revocable |
| Local or regional advantage | 6 | 0.5 | Poland helps build and seed; global distribution is required |
| Strength of advantage | 7 | 1 | Cross-platform personalised enforcement beats static controls if effective |
| Timing and underused momentum | 7 | 0.5 | Wearables and local processing enable it; category timing is not externally forced |
| Repeatable distribution | 7 | 2 | Communities/search/coaches are concrete, but policy and CAC are untested |
| Durability and profit retention | 7 | 1.5 | Software margins are attractive; platform features and trackers can copy basics |
| Founder fit and minimum people stack | 10 | 0.5 | Exceptional biosignal, ML, behaviour, and interest fit |

| Founder wealth creation potential | 7 | 2 | 6,000 global users clears the gate but retention/CAC determine plausibility |
| Reachability and capital efficiency | 9 | 2 | Paid proof is directly founder-controlled at 24k PLN maximum risk |

## Overall Score

**75/100.** Weighted sum 149; `10 × (149 / 20) = 74.5`, rounded to 75.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** Strongest copy risk: a poker tracker, operator, or generic focus-blocking app adds user-set cooldowns and wearable thresholds through routine product work. Catch-up is harder only if TiltLock accumulates trusted longitudinal intervention outcomes, superior false-positive control, and cross-platform user history faster than competitors.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass.** All three scenarios state subscribers, pricing, revenue, margins, capital, ownership, reinvestment, valuation, and timing without double-counting data or retained earnings. The conservative case reaches 5.70m PLN founder equity at 6,000 users and 25% operating margin; this is global rather than heroic national penetration, but it is sensitive to month-three retention and first-year CAC.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Five paid deposits and ten 599 PLN users can be pursued directly within 180 days and 24,000 PLN while employed. Policy review, manual delivery, and one local prototype are sequenced; no operator partnership, clinical claim, full product, or custom hardware is required.

## Expansion Check

Poker is narrow enough to learn state-to-financial-outcome relationships and large enough to test subscription demand. Trading/esports expansion is plausible but cannot compensate if poker retention fails. Platform controls and tracker entry are credible; local processing, trust, and cross-platform continuity are the defence rather than Poland itself.

## Fatal Objections

None identified at planning stage, provided policy review occurs before real-time launch and the manual cohort is killed on the stated payment, adherence, intervention, and retention thresholds.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A user-authored commitment device aligned to the person's own physiological state can create value precisely when ordinary self-control is least reliable.

## Recommendation

pursue

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Dexterity Rights Foundry

## Diagnosis

This is the strongest finalist because the paid product and strategic asset are the same owned corpus, rather than a service that hopes data will later become valuable. The narrow cable/connector task supply is founder-accessible, the retained-rights test is explicit, and the first licence can be sold before a robotics platform or expensive robot fleet exists. Competition and technical usefulness are serious risks, but the proof plan tests both within one pack and one non-exclusive licence.

## Evidence and Assumptions

NVIDIA documents the role and scarcity of high-quality demonstrations; multiple commercial collectors validate both demand and competition. Buyer price, transfer across embodiments, evaluation impact, licence acceptance, re-licensing, and margins remain hypotheses. The discovery-stage plan does not assume them and kills the shape if customers require exclusive on-robot work.

## Founder Wealth Gate

## Expansion Check

Cable/connector tasks form a credible beachhead and trade adjacencies are numerous. Large data collectors can enter, but a catalogue of difficult variations with retained rights and customer evaluation history can expand outward faster than a broad collector moves inward. Poland's skilled-trade supply is a real operational advantage.

## Fatal Objections

None identified at planning stage, provided the exact non-exclusive retained-rights and technical-utility tests remain kill gates. The most dangerous caveat is that buyers may only pay for exclusive data on their own embodiment, which would destroy the model.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The company should own narrowly specialised human-demonstration rights that can be licensed repeatedly, not merely coordinate custom data collection.

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: RingRelay

## Diagnosis

RingRelay has a practical paid-proof path and can build real wearable hardware knowledge, but the control point is local working inventory that any well-capitalised marketplace or OEM can purchase. The wealth case depends on scaling a labour- and capital-intensive operation to 16,000 annual cycles while maintaining 28% gross margin and subtracting inventory debt. The exact-size guarantee is useful, yet it is an execution advantage rather than an asymmetric asset strong enough for this run.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Oura's installed base, exact sizing, battery uncertainty, and broad recommerce competition are evidenced. Reservation conversion, 28% gross margin, 45-day turns, diagnostic predictiveness, return rate, buyback supply, and the 16,000-cycle denominator are hypotheses. The first 30 cycles test reachability well but do not solve the capital and copy risks.

## 16-Area Scorecard

| Area | Score | Weight | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Core insight | 7 | 1 | Exact-size liquidity insurance is a real specialist gap |
| Customer pain and willingness to pay | 7 | 2 | Used-device savings and fit uncertainty are tangible |
| Business model and economics | 6 | 2 | Returns, warranty, debt, and labour threaten margin |
| Profitable domination potential | 5 | 1 | Local niche can be won but is hard to defend at scale |
| Beachhead quality | 7 | 1 | One model and three sizes is appropriately narrow |
| Expansion path | 7 | 1 | Other wearables and repair are logical but add complexity |
| Asymmetric defensibility | 5 | 1.5 | Inventory and diagnostics are purchasable/copyable |
| Local or regional advantage | 7 | 0.5 | Fragmented Polish listings help initial consolidation |
| Strength of advantage | 5 | 1 | Guarantee and buyback are operational features |
| Timing and underused momentum | 7 | 0.5 | Installed base and upgrades create supply |
| Repeatable distribution | 7 | 2 | Exact-size search pages and reservations are concrete |
| Durability and profit retention | 5 | 1.5 | Competition and working capital can compress profit |
| Founder fit and minimum people stack | 7 | 0.5 | Good wearable fit, weaker retail-operations fit |
| Risk clarity and first-test quality | 9 | 0.5 | Deposits-before-inventory is disciplined |
| Founder wealth creation potential | 5 | 2 | Conservative case barely clears 5m and is volume/debt sensitive |
| Reachability and capital efficiency | 9 | 2 | Ten sales fit 25k PLN and employment |

## Overall Score

**65/100.** Weighted sum 129; `10 × (129 / 20) = 64.5`, rounded to 65.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 for each owned ring, Tier 3 as a durable business control point.** Strongest copy risk: a marketplace, OEM, or specialist reseller buys the same sizes and adds a swap/buyback policy through one inventory decision and routine process change. No credible switching cost yet offsets this, so the score is below 80.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The table is complete and subtracts net debt, but the conservative result is only 5.05m PLN at 16,000 annual cycles, 14% operating margin, and a 2.5× multiple. Small adverse changes in returns, inventory finance, labour, or valuation drop founder equity below 5m; reaching 44 cycles per day from a narrow specialist category is too sensitive to qualify as conservative.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Five deposits and ten completed sales are possible within 180 days and 25,000 PLN while employed. Inventory purchases are staged after demand and much capital is recoverable, although recoverability at cost is not assumed.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is disciplined, but expansion adds brands, parts, repair authority, and inventory complexity. Broad recommerce and OEMs can enter cheaply. Poland improves initial sourcing, not long-run advantage.

## Fatal Objections

1. Founder Wealth Gate fails under modest downside to a barely clearing conservative case.
2. The business requires working-capital and fulfilment scale without a hard-to-copy resource.
3. The core guarantee can be copied by a marketplace or OEM through routine inventory and policy changes.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Paid exact-size reservations before inventory purchase are an excellent cold-start mechanism for fragmented wearable SKUs.

## Recommendation

kill
