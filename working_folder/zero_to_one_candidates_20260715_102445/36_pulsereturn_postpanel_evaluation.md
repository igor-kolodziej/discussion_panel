# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PulseReturn Rail v1 After Panel

Applied with `$evaluate-zero-to-one` to the exact canonical candidate and both complete independent verdicts. Advisory only.

## Diagnosis

PulseReturn is more reachable and contractable than the standalone fixture, but the panel identifies a narrow customer intersection that the wealth model treats as a broad conservative base. A customer must have enough EU returns to support roughly 400,000 PLN annual value, yet lack an established depot and trust a new company with warranty-adjacent custody, tools, spares, data, and disposition. The incumbent can copy the differentiated diagnostic layer inside an existing logistics relationship before a cross-brand corpus compounds. The paid batch can be a useful service, but no credible route from that service to durable, high-margin control and 5m PLN founder wealth remains as written.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Evidence: warranty repair/replacement is real; lifecycle operators already bundle RMA, triage, repair, warehouse, refurbishment, and recovery.
- Hypotheses: 30 qualifying brands, 1,500 returns/equivalent per brand, 400k ACV, 55% gross, 25% operating, batch-to-routing conversion, derivative-data advantage.
- Independent verdicts: 72 and 64; both identify the same denominator, mandate durability, physical-margin, trust, and incumbent-copy failures.

## Overall Score

Weighted points: 116.5 / 20; overall **58/100**.

## Control Point

**Conditional Tier 2, present Tier 3/none.** A routed contract and custody create operational control, but no exclusive/multi-year/irrevocable right is proposed and existing 3PL relationships dominate. Strongest copy risk is one wearable-specific technician/process added by the current lifecycle provider.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The arithmetic is complete, but the “conservative” case depends on 30 brands simultaneously meeting volume, service-gap, trust, and ACV conditions that are unsupported. Expected/strong cases multiply the same denominator. Physical-service margin and ownership buffers are inadequate against modest downside.

## Reachability Gate

**Marginal pass for a paid batch, not for durable control.** One 15,000 PLN batch can be attempted within 180 days/35k/employment. It does not prove recurring routing; custody and tool/spare authority introduce a second sales/trust gate.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is coherent, but incumbents already span device categories and infrastructure. New wearable types add rather than remove operational complexity.

## Fatal Objections

1. The conservative qualified-brand denominator is unproved and structurally narrow.
2. Batch-to-routing requires a second high-trust sale and may leave only a report service.
3. Incumbents can copy the diagnostic layer inside an existing custody contract.
4. Fully loaded margins are aggressive for model-specific physical operations.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Future-lab data is most credible when generated by a profitable, controlled recurring transaction or workflow; however, this run found no reachable customer/control configuration that also clears founder wealth.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Do not pivot again within wearable returns, reverse logistics, test fixtures, or EOL inventory. The same customer-trust, purchasable-process, and denominator objections now recur across multiple versions.
