# StableGuard Research After Structural Pivot

Retrieval date: 2026-07-15. This research establishes that organised backing, bankroll provision, performance monitoring, and hand-history review exist; it does not prove the buyer denominator, budget, or willingness to route a bankroll decision.

- Vanguard Stable publicly states that it supplies bankroll, daily coaching, structure/accountability, and reviews player applications. [Vanguard Stable](https://vanguardstable.com/)
- PokerStaking publicly offers full MTT/Spin & Go staking, coaching, player groups, and performance monitoring. [PokerStaking](https://pokerstaking.com/)
- Standard Backing states that it has operated since 2008, reviews applications/stats, requests hand histories, and offers backing and cashout/profit arrangements. [Standard Backing](https://standardbacking.com/)
- FirstFund describes a professional online-tournament fund and claims more than 1,000 players brought to stable income. The claim is company-supplied and not independently audited, but it shows at least one operator positions itself at meaningful player scale. [FirstFund](https://firstfund.pro/en)
- PokerStars recognises private backing agreements but does not settle them and imposes gameplay/transfer rules. This supports an off-platform stable workflow while showing that platform-level enforcement is unavailable. [PokerStars staking/backing rules](https://www.pokerstars.com/help/articles/rmt-staking-escrow-backing/155985/)
- PokerStake confirms that tournament staking contains deposits, event confirmation, result verification, and winnings allocation. This is adjacent transaction infrastructure but not the same as long-term stable backing. [PokerStake FAQ](https://www.pokerstake.com/faq)

## Research Consequence

The buyer and workflow exist, but no primary or reliable source establishes 100 reachable stables, average stable size, software budget, or current loss from rule breaches. These remain explicit hypotheses. StableGuard must therefore prove both a paid workflow mandate and a credible denominator before independent simulation; it cannot borrow the original consumer market size.
