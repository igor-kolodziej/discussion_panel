# PulseReturn Rail Research

Sources retrieved 2026-07-15. Research tests the pivot; it is not validation.

## Regulatory and Timing Evidence

- The EU repair directive must be transposed/applied by member states from 31 July 2026, but manufacturer repair duties attach to product categories with specific reparability requirements; this does not establish that small wearables must use an independent depot. [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/directive-repair-goods_en)
- EU consumer rules already require sellers to provide repair or replacement for non-conforming goods. This confirms an existing warranty-cost event. [European Commission sustainable consumption](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/consumers/consumer-rights-and-complaints/sustainable-consumption_en)
- Current EU battery-rule consultations explicitly discuss wearable-device exemptions. Circularity is momentum, not a guaranteed repairability mandate for the target devices. [European Circular Economy Stakeholder Platform](https://circulareconomy.europa.eu/platform/en/news-and-events/all-news/public-consultation-adding-exemptions-rule-portable-batteries)

## Competitive Evidence

- B2X markets integrated hardware lifecycle operations including RMA, reverse logistics, triage, repair, warehousing, inventory, fulfilment, refurbishment, and asset recovery, backed by a partner network. [B2X](https://www.b2x.com/)
- MTTC markets European repair, refurbishment, trade-in, and pre-owned device services for smart-device customers. [MTTC](https://mttc.eu/)
- Metarvo markets diagnostics, repair, refurbishment, and technology-brand recovery programmes. [Metarvo](https://www.metarvo.fi/)

These providers establish real budgets and also demonstrate that the visible operational bundle is already available. PulseReturn's only plausible gap is specialist PPG/ring/band diagnosis for brands too small for large operators, combined with faster failure intelligence and recovered value.

## Missing Decision-Critical Evidence

- No reliable public count of independent brands with 20,000–100,000 annual EU shipments and no specialist depot was found.
- No source establishes 1,500 annual returns/brand, 120–250 PLN processing price, 55% gross margin, a 15,000 PLN batch-autopsy budget, or 30 reachable recurring brands.
- Sealed/waterproof devices may be uneconomic to repair; a diagnosis/recommerce result may not cover the operating cost.
- Brand return data, spares, firmware tools, secure-reset authority, and disposition rights are contractual dependencies that must be granted by each customer.

## Research Decision

The shape has stronger control than selling test equipment, but competition and the unsupported small-brand denominator make the Founder Wealth Gate the decisive question. A complete candidate should be evaluated conservatively; circularity regulation must not be treated as a mandate.
