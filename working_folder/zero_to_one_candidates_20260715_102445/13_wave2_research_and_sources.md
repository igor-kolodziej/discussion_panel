# Wave 2 Research and Sources

All sources retrieved 2026-07-15. Sourced facts establish denominators, legal momentum, incumbent capability, or workflow existence—not willingness to pay or control.

## GymSignal Media Rail

- IAB Polska reports Polish DOOH spend of 257.2m PLN in 2024, up 36.1%, supporting advertiser momentum. [IAB Polska](https://raportstrategiczny.iab.org.pl/digital-out-of-home/)
- Quividi documents privacy-preserving audience/attention measurement, proving technical feasibility and incumbent capability. [Quividi privacy](https://quividi.com/privacy/)
- Vistar documents publisher-estimated impression concepts and programmatic DOOH infrastructure. [Vistar glossary](https://help.vistarmedia.com/hc/en-us/articles/226101528-Ad-platform-glossary)
- EuropeActive reports 67,515 European fitness clubs in 2025. [EuropeActive](https://www.europeactive.eu/blog/press-corner-4/strong-growth-in-members-and-revenues-for-european-health-fitness-market-in-2025-140)
- Hypotheses: independent gyms will grant exclusive rights for a 300–500 PLN monthly minimum; local/vertical advertisers will prepay 6,000–10,000 PLN; 300 sites can average 3,000 PLN monthly gross bookings at 22% operating margin.

## CharacterContinuity Rail

- PARP reports 824 active Polish game producers/publishers, 14,568 workers, and 450+ annual platform-wise releases; the EU study reports about 5,000 EU studios. [PARP 2025](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/publications/publication/the-game-industry-of-poland---report-2025), [European Commission study](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/study-european-video-games-sector)
- Steam requires disclosure of pre-generated and live-generated AI content and guardrails for live generation. [Steam content survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey?language=english)
- The 2025 SAG-AFTRA game agreement codifies consent, disclosure, usage reporting, and compensation for vocal digital replicas. This demonstrates contract feasibility, not applicability to Polish actors. [SAG-AFTRA agreement](https://www.sagaftra.org/contracts-industry-resources/interactive/2025-interactive-media-video-game-agreement)
- ElevenLabs already operates a voice marketplace with 10,400+ creators and reports more than $22m earned, making generic voice supply non-defensible. [ElevenLabs Voice Library](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-library), [creator payout announcement](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/22-million-earned-by-voice-creators-on-elevenlabs)
- EU AI Act Article 50 creates synthetic-content marking/transparency obligations but does not grant this company rights. [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689)
- Hypotheses: actors accept narrow category-exclusive options; one studio pays 15,000–25,000 PLN for an update-heavy character; 200 active character licences can average 45,000 PLN retained annual revenue.

## WorkScore Passport

- Directive (EU) 2024/2831 requires rights around automated decisions and, from national implementation, portability of ratings/reviews including direct transmission to a requested third party. Member States must transpose by 2 December 2026. [Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ%3AL_202402831)
- The Council cites 28.3m platform workers in the EU, establishing a large denominator. [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-platform-workers-in-the-eu/)
- Hypotheses: multi-platform workers pay 299 PLN annually before a severe incident; acquisition through public worker groups remains economical; APIs/national implementation permit useful transfer; 20,000 EU members are reachable without union/platform distribution.

## ScopeBand

- Statistics Poland reports 346,200 construction microenterprises in 2024, providing a large Polish denominator but not the number with material change-order leakage. [Statistics Poland](https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/en/defaultaktualnosci/3317/8/12/1/activity_of_enterprises_with_up_to_9_persons_employed_in_2024.pdf)
- Buildertrend and BuildBook already combine change orders, approvals, budgets, invoices, and payments, proving the workflow and strongest copy risk. [Buildertrend](https://buildertrend.com/project-management/construction-change-order-software/), [BuildBook](https://buildbook.co/pricing)
- Hypotheses: a one-button wearable captures otherwise unbilled verbal work; three contractors pay 2,500 PLN pilots; 1,200 contractors can generate 6.84m PLN revenue without support destroying a 30% operating margin.
