# Zero-to-One Evaluation: VoiceAccess Release Gate

Applied with `$evaluate-zero-to-one` to the pivot and current research. Discovery-stage disclosure applies. This is advisory only.

## Diagnosis

VoiceAccess correctly turns platform incumbents from substitutes into buyers and replaces thousands of consumer subscriptions with a finite B2B prospect list. The recurring release event and task-grounded benchmark are stronger than the original Workline control point. However, research shows that voice-agent release testing, disability-participant recruitment, and non-standard-speech APIs already exist as separately purchasable capabilities, while the European Accessibility Act does not mandate this specific test. The concept therefore risks becoming a specialist testing service whose conservative wealth path depends on unsupported annual contract value and customer count.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Official evidence: EU and Polish accessibility rules are in force and cover relevant service categories, but do not require VoiceAccess's test or confer certification authority.
- Company evidence: Polish banks deploy voicebots at scale; Evalgent, Coval, and VoiceEval sell voice-agent testing; Alana sells lived-experience testing access; Voiceitt sells a non-standard-speech API backed by proprietary data.
- Hypotheses: one of 60 vendors pays 12,000 PLN within 180 days, accepts reusable benchmark rights, and later routes releases at 60,000–180,000 PLN annually.
- Missing: complete founder-wealth scenarios, a sourced 40-customer denominator, channel conversion, participant rights economics, recurring release demand, and automated-vs-human delivery cost.

## Overall Score

Weighted points: 136.5 across 20 total weight; `10 × (136.5 / 20) = 68.25`, rounded to **68/100**.

## Control Point

**Compounding Tier 2 if proved; Tier 3 today.** Reusable benchmark rights and routed releases can become meaningful, but the strongest copy risk is an established voice-agent evaluation platform contracting a non-standard-speech panel or Voiceitt adding release-regression reporting around its API. Each ingredient is already commercially available; only accumulated, rights-clean cross-release history might make catch-up slow.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The pivot lacks the required conservative, expected, and strong scenarios. Its new hypothesis of 40 customers at 300,000 PLN annual value is unsupported, above the initial stated annual range, and not tied to a bottom-up buyer/channel model. Productised margin and founder ownership are also unspecified.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, with sales-cycle risk.** A public vendor list, outside-in sample, 12,000 PLN prepaid sprint, direct participant contracting, and 30,000 PLN pre-proof limit fit the formal constraints. One paid sprint can be sought before production data, carrier access, certification, or a full platform.

## Expansion Check

Polish non-standard speech is a credible niche; adjacent languages and voice interactions are logical. Yet broad testing platforms move inward through a participant contract more easily than this narrow company moves outward through enterprise distribution. EU localisation helps execution, not exclusion.

## Fatal Objections

1. The proposed test is not mandated, and a recurring budget owner is unproved.
2. Voice evaluation, lived-experience participant recruitment, and non-standard-speech technology are already purchasable layers.
3. No credible conservative 5m PLN founder-wealth model exists as written.
4. Bespoke participant delivery may scale with labour and prevent the claimed product margins.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A profitable operating workflow should generate rights-clean human-interaction data as an unavoidable by-product rather than asking a data buyer or accessibility budget to fund speculative corpus creation.

## Recommendation

**kill**

The same testing-service shape should not be cosmetically refined. Return to candidate generation with the salvageable by-product insight: sell an independently valuable recurring output and instrument ordinary delivery to own the future-lab asset.
