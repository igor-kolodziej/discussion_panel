# Zero-to-One Candidate: StableGuard Bankroll Control Rail v1

Post-pivot candidate for advisory re-evaluation only — not validated. All business-model numbers are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

StableGuard lets professional poker backing stables protect deployed bankroll by making objective player risk-rule compliance a required input to the next funding or continued-backing decision, while ordinary delivery builds a cross-stable behaviour–intervention–financial-outcome dataset.

## Founder Fit

The founder has ML, biosignal, behavioural, analytics, and poker interest. A manual pilot needs structured data and workflow design rather than a full platform. Legal/privacy help and one paid stable-operations adviser are bounded contractors. The founder remains employed and works no more than five hours daily.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is a 20–200-player online-poker backing stable that allocates bankroll, coaches players, and requires hand-history or result reporting. A backed player who breaks agreed drawdown, game, duration, or review rules can lose the stable's capital and coaching investment. Current control is delayed spreadsheets, chat, coach review, and trust. The stable wants earlier objective evidence and a repeatable review before issuing more bankroll, not poker strategy advice.

## Product Wedge

An eight-week, 10–20-player paid pilot:

- stable and player record risk rules before play;
- players authorise import of their own hand histories/session summaries; the stable exports its bankroll ledger;
- StableGuard flags objective breaches and assembles evidence;
- the stable contract requires the evidence/review record before the next bankroll tranche or continued-backing decision;
- the stable retains final authority and all funds; StableGuard never holds or transfers bankroll;
- start with behaviour-only rules. Optional wearable physiology enters only as an A/B feature after it proves incremental prediction and receives separate consent;
- no bet, hand, table, or strategy recommendation is produced.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The original consumer blocker sold restraint to the person likely to reject it under impaired judgment. Backing stables already have an aligned economic interest and a consequence they control: future bankroll and contract continuation. Small stables can enforce rules manually but lack a cross-player evidence standard, coach queue, and outcome benchmark.

## Underused Momentum

Public backing organisations already advertise bankroll, coaching, accountability, performance monitoring, applications, and hand-history review. User-owned hand-history export and off-platform backing contracts permit a manual pilot without operator API access. Commodity biosignals are optional future inputs rather than a prerequisite.

## Beachhead Market

English-speaking online MTT and Spin & Go stables with 20–200 active contracted players, formal bankroll rules, and exportable hand histories/results. Exclude informal one-person staking, unlicensed gambling operation, minors, real-time strategy, and startup custody of funds.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Pilot: 15,000–25,000 PLN for eight weeks and 10–20 players.
- Recurring: 2,000 PLN average annual fee per active player, with a 30,000 PLN annual minimum per stable; volume bands 1,200–3,000 PLN per player.
- Pilot gross-margin hypothesis: 55% after review labour and legal-template amortisation.
- Scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 80%; operating-margin hypothesis: 30%–38% after support, security, sales, and engineering.
- Value test: reduction in objective rule-breach bankroll exposure and coach review time; no promise of player profitability.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Compile 100 public backing stables, staking organisations, and coaching-for-profit groups from application pages, player sponsorships, poker forums, Discords, and staking marketplaces.
- Send owners a fixed 15,000 PLN pilot based on their public rules and a mock breach-evidence packet.
- Conversion event: paid invoice, at least ten enrolled players, authorised hand-history access, stable ledger export, and an MSA making the StableGuard review record a required input to a real next-funding/continued-backing decision.
- First ten: direct owner/coach outreach and referrals generated by paid pilot case studies; no celebrity introduction is assumed.
- First 100: standard rule templates, imports for common trackers/ledgers, and a cross-stable risk benchmark. Direct billing and data contracts remain with StableGuard.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed contracted Tier 2:** the stable MSA routes compliance evidence and a required review step before the next bankroll/continued-backing event for enrolled players, while StableGuard retains lawful de-identified breach/intervention/outcome taxonomies. It is not Tier 1 because the stable owns funds and final authority and can replace the provider; switching cost must compound through longitudinal player baselines, workflows, and cross-stable benchmarks.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resource: one paid stable mandate, ten players, a bankroll ledger, objective rules, decision outcomes, and derived-taxonomy rights.

1. Interview 15 owners/coaches from a 100-account list.
2. Obtain a fixed-fee gaming/privacy/contract review and player notice.
3. Sell one paid pilot before integration.
4. Deliver with CSVs, a secure evidence store, manual review, and stable-controlled decisions.
5. Six-month proof: one paid stable, ten players, three objective breach events, one real funding/review decision routed through StableGuard, and positive pilot contribution.
6. Twelve-month proof: three independent paying stables, 100 active player-months, one renewal, and a useful retained de-identified taxonomy.

No operator partnership, clinical claim, custom hardware, or bankroll custody is required. An unknown founder can contact public organisations and test one bounded workflow without asking them to replace poker software.

## Distribution Advantage

The direct buyer list is publicly discoverable, and common rule/ledger templates reduce sales friction across similar stables. Each paid stable creates references, player migration continuity, and benchmarks useful to the next stable. This is an acquisition thesis, not yet proven proprietary distribution.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with objective rule compliance and coach review. Expand to personalised risk baselines, stable portfolio allocation, intervention experiments, and—only if it adds lift—optional wearable state. Adjacent prop-trading academies or esports teams require separate validation and do not rescue poker failure. The defence must be contracted workflow, longitudinal player outcomes, and cross-stable benchmarks, not a blocker feature.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland supplies low-cost development and access to European poker communities. GDPR-grade minimisation, player transparency, and local processing may improve trust. The business sells globally and has no formal Polish barrier; local execution is not itself a moat.

## Compounding Advantage

Every routed event links a pre-agreed rule, actual session, breach, coach review, funding/contract response, later compliance, and bankroll outcome. The longitudinal cross-stable taxonomy improves rule calibration, triage, review time, and stable portfolio risk. Ordinary delivery builds behavioural/financial data, human-computer intervention workflows, AI/ML, optional biosignal intelligence, direct distribution, and proprietary IP.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution during employment and cap optional distributions at 5%. The strong case reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds secure imports, coach workflow automation, evidence quality, acquisition, and longitudinal models.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 15 owner/coach interviews, legal/privacy memo, rule and player-notice templates.
- Days 31–60: manual evidence prototype and 100-account outreach.
- Days 61–120: ten qualified calls and five fixed proposals.
- Days 121–180: one 15,000–25,000 PLN paid pilot, ten players, three breaches, one real bankroll/review decision, and positive contribution.

Founder time remains below five hours daily. Paid evidence is an invoice and routed decision, not a letter of interest.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof: maximum 35,000 PLN — legal/privacy 6k, prototype/security 8k, paid adviser 4k, outreach/travel 5k, contractor delivery 5k, contingency 7k.
- Up to cumulative 65,000 PLN only after paid delivery and positive contribution.
- Up to 100,000 PLN only after three independent payers or recurring renewal/acquisition evidence.
- Family capital excluded until repeat demand and viable economics.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Fast test | Kill/narrow response |

| Reachable buyer denominator | Build and qualify 100 named stables | Kill if fewer than 50 meet the 20-player/formal-workflow threshold |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after five independent paying stables or equivalent contracted recurring demand, positive contribution, one renewal, repeatable owner acquisition, recurring gross profit covering 1.5× planned burn, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If stables do not buy, retain only lawfully reusable hand-history parsers, rule ontology, consent/security architecture, and de-identified research permitted by contract. Do not relabel optional coaching analytics as the same business. Any move to trading/esports requires new evaluation.

## Critical Evidence

Public stable sites show bankroll provision, coaching, accountability, application review, performance monitoring, and hand-history use. PokerStars recognises private backing but does not settle it, supporting an off-platform workflow while limiting account-level control.

## Critical Hypotheses

- At least 50 reachable stables have 20+ active players and formal bankroll rules.
- A stable pays at least 15,000 PLN and makes the review record a required decision input.
- The 2,000 PLN annual per-seat price is below protected bankroll/coach value.
- Objective breaches occur often enough to prove value but not so often that the stable's model is broken.
- Longitudinal baselines and benchmarks create switching cost beyond a manual spreadsheet.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than 50 genuinely qualified buyers in a 100-account mapping.
- Fewer than five owner calls or no paid pilot within 180 days.
- No payer routes a real next-bankroll or continued-backing decision through the record.
- Players or counsel reject core data processing, or platform rules prohibit the required imports.
- Pilot cannot show positive contribution or measurable protected exposure/coach-time value.
- Real denominator, price, margin, or retention makes the conservative founder stake below 5m PLN.
- A stable can recreate and replace the entire workflow after one pilot without losing data continuity or outcomes.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- 35,000 PLN maximum before paid proof; 100,000 PLN in 12 months.
- Founder at five hours daily; fixed-fee lawyer; bounded poker-operations adviser; engineer after payment.
- No clinical staff, custom hardware, operator integration, fund custody, or unspecified partnership.
