# Zero-to-One Candidate: GymSignal Media Rail

**Canonical business specification: v1, frozen 2026-07-15 Europe/Warsaw for independent simulation.**

Wave-2 candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

GymSignal acquires exclusive digital-media rights inside independent gyms, sells prepaid campaigns to fitness-adjacent brands, and measures anonymous attentive exposure plus offer conversion at the edge—building a privacy-preserving human-display dataset and a hard-to-recreate vertical media network.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build edge vision, experiment design, campaign analytics, and low-cost screen/sensor operations. Venue and advertiser selling are learnable founder-led processes; a commission salesperson is added only after paid proof. No clinical claim, custom hardware, or identity recognition is required.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The paying customer is a local/regional meal-prep, supplement, physiotherapy, recovery, sports-app, apparel, or fitness-service brand. It wants concentrated access to people already spending on fitness and evidence beyond estimated footfall. Current choices are broad social ads, influencer deals, posters, or national DOOH networks with weak independent-gym inventory and limited action attribution.

The supply customer is an independent gym with 600+ members. It wants incremental revenue without selling ads, managing hardware, or exposing member identities.

## Product Wedge

- Three gyms grant GymSignal six-month exclusive rights to sell digital screen inventory, with a 300–500 PLN monthly minimum guarantee and revenue share.
- A brand prepays 6,000–10,000 PLN for a four-week campaign across the three sites.
- GymSignal installs used commercial screens and edge cameras; only aggregate head-pose/dwell/attentive-view events are emitted. No face template, identity, raw video retention, or emotion inference.
- Each creative carries a trackable QR/NFC/coupon for a trial, consultation, class, or offer; the report combines verified play, anonymous attentive seconds, and conversion.
- The campaign is optimised by time/site/creative, not by identifying people.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Independent gyms have valuable vertical attention but are too fragmented for brands to buy efficiently. Attention measurement is commodity; exclusive venue inventory and campaign/conversion history are not. Aggregating a single context lets GymSignal sell a more useful audience than generic local screens while giving venues found revenue.

## Underused Momentum

Polish DOOH spend is growing, privacy-preserving edge measurement is commercially available, and Europe has 67,515 fitness clubs. Local fitness brands increasingly need alternatives to saturated social/influencer channels. None of this proves the specific campaign economics.

## Beachhead Market

Independent Warsaw gyms with 600–3,000 members, existing reception/training-floor screen locations, and no exclusive media contract. Initial advertisers must sell a trackable fitness-adjacent offer within 10 km or nationally online. Exclude health-outcome targeting and biometric identification.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Campaign: 6,000–10,000 PLN for three sites/four weeks; later 1,500–5,000 PLN per site/month depending on traffic and exclusivity.
- Venue economics: 300–500 PLN monthly minimum plus 20%–30% of site booking revenue above the guarantee.
- Hardware: 2,000–3,500 PLN installed per site using used/refurbished screen, edge computer/camera, mount, and connectivity; depreciated over 24 months.
- Scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 60%–68% after venue share, hardware depreciation, installation, connectivity, and campaign fulfilment; operating margin 22%–30% after sales.
- Advertiser value test: comparable or better qualified-offer cost than its existing local/social channel, with attention used for optimisation rather than vanity reporting.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- First venue list: 60 Warsaw independent gyms from public maps, club directories, and social pages; sell a minimum guarantee and six-month exclusivity, not a speculative partnership.
- First advertiser list: 50 local/regional brands with public paid offers and geographies matching signed gyms.
- Conversion event: three signed venue contracts, one prepaid 6,000–10,000 PLN campaign, approved creative, and offer-tracking method before further installation.
- First ten advertisers: direct founder outreach using actual three-site campaign results; venue members are never sold as leads.
- First 100: category-specific campaign templates, case studies, and a self-serve booking calendar; venue referrals earn a limited installation credit while GymSignal retains inventory and advertiser contracts.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1:** prepaid/guaranteed six- to twelve-month exclusive venue media rights, renewal options tied to minimum revenue, physical screen custody, and advertiser order flow. The sensing model is not the control point. Twelve-month proof is 20–30 reserved venues and ten independent paying advertisers before the 100,000 PLN cap.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resources are venue inventory and one advertiser order.

1. Sign three venues with a capped minimum guarantee; contracts start only when the first campaign begins.
2. Sell a prepaid campaign using site photos/member traffic bands and a prototype report before buying more than three installations.
3. Use commodity privacy-preserving edge components and manually schedule creatives.
4. Produce verified play, aggregate attention, and offer conversion; pay venue guarantees from campaign cash.
5. Add sites only against prepaid advertiser demand or signed venue options, reaching 20–30 controlled sites within 12 months and 100,000 PLN.

An unknown founder can secure independent venues because the venue receives guaranteed income and no sales workload, while the first advertiser buys a small, trackable vertical campaign rather than trusting a national network.

## Distribution Advantage

Exclusive gym inventory attracts fitness advertisers; more advertisers raise venue yield and make exclusivity easier to renew. Venue density lets a brand buy Warsaw fitness attention in one order. Conversion benchmarks and creative learning lower campaign risk for the next advertiser.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Dominate independent Warsaw gyms, then add other Polish cities and adjacent controlled contexts such as climbing gyms and boutique studios. Later sell programmatic access only while retaining direct venue rights and first-party campaign data. Hardware and models remain replaceable. Catch-up requires signing comparable exclusive sites, installing them, and rebuilding cross-campaign response history.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland has growing DOOH spend, dense urban fitness supply, and fragmented independent venues underserved by global networks. Privacy-by-design edge measurement fits EU expectations. The defence is contracted local inventory and advertiser liquidity, not regulatory arbitrage.

## Compounding Advantage

Each campaign adds venue/time/creative attentive-view patterns and offer conversions without retaining identity. That improves inventory pricing, scheduling, creative selection, sales proof, and site acquisition. Ordinary delivery builds edge AI/ML, behavioural attention data, real-time HCI, sensing hardware expertise, capital, venue distribution, and advertiser distribution.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment and cap optional distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Cash buys only sites supported by demand, sales capacity, edge reliability, and proprietary campaign benchmarks.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 20 venue and 20 advertiser interviews; privacy/legal review; sample campaign/report.
- Days 31–60: sign three conditional exclusive venue contracts and collect one advertiser deposit.
- Days 61–100: install three sites and run the four-week campaign.
- Days 101–180: obtain one advertiser renewal or second independent payer, measured offer conversions, venue retention, and positive campaign contribution.

Paid proof is prepaid advertiser cash, not a venue letter. Founder remains employed and batches sales/installation within five hours daily.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 28,000 PLN: legal/privacy 4k; three installations 10.5k; venue guarantees 4.5k; creative/sales 4k; contingency 5k.
- Up to 60,000 PLN after one paid delivered campaign and positive contribution.
- Up to 100,000 PLN only after two advertisers, renewal/repeat demand, and at least ten controlled venues.
- Family capital excluded until repeatable advertiser economics.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Test | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Venue exclusivity | Three signed conditional contracts | Kill if venues demand high guarantees/no exclusivity |
| Advertiser WTP | Prepaid campaign | Kill after 50 qualified offers and no payer |
| Offer conversion/renewal | Campaign result and second order | Drop attention vanity metrics; kill if no economic signal |
| Privacy acceptability | Edge architecture/legal review | No identity/raw video; kill if venue/member trust fails |
| Site economics | Fully loaded contribution | Install only against demand |
| Copy/durability | Renewal options and advertiser repeat | Kill if rights cannot extend or inventory churns cheaply |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after 20+ controlled sites, at least five independent paying advertisers, positive fully loaded contribution, advertiser renewal or repeat ordering, a repeatable venue/advertiser acquisition signal, operations not dependent on founder visits, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Screens are resalable. Reusable assets include edge privacy tooling, anonymous attention experiments, venue/advertiser maps, and campaign workflow. No member-identifiable footage exists to repurpose. A move to another venue category requires new evaluation.

## Critical Evidence

Polish DOOH is growing; anonymous edge attention measurement exists; Europe's gym base is large. These facts support timing and denominator, not demand for this network.

## Critical Hypotheses

Three gyms grant low-cost exclusivity; one advertiser prepays; attention plus conversion beats existing channels; venue yield reaches 3,000 PLN monthly; 300-site sales/operations retain 22% margin.

## Kill Criteria

- No three exclusive conditional venues within 60 days.
- No prepaid advertiser after 50 qualified prospects or within 120 days.
- No advertiser repeat/renewal or credible qualified-offer economics by day 180.
- Fully loaded campaign gross margin below 40% or venue guarantees exceed sustainable yield.
- Privacy review requires identity/biometric processing or venues reject the edge design.
- Rights cannot be renewed/optioned and competitors can displace sites with one offer.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

28,000 PLN pre-proof; 100,000 PLN first-year founder cap. Founder handles ML/analytics/sales; installer paid per site; privacy lawyer fixed fee; commission salesperson only after repeat demand.
