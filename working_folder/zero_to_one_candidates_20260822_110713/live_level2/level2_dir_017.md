# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-017

Lead: `RAW-FPE-08`, qualified secondary-packaging capacity option. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Secondary packing/repacking/serialization is licensed manufacturing requiring an authorized GMP site; the coordinator cannot hold medicines. URPL recorded 10,541 national/European-procedure products, 3,414 EC authorizations and 26,127 variations in 2025, but these overstate distinct buyers. Polish CMOs already offer packaging, serialization, cold chain and QP services. Medicine shortages are real, yet public evidence does not identify secondary packaging as a frequent root cause.

## Proposed Transaction

A licensed CMO issues a conditional term sheet; the MAH buys a 15–40k PLN readiness study covering variation pathway, artwork/materials, serialization, audit, quality agreement and QP workflow. Only after quality approval and regulatory changes does an exact capacity option activate. A 30–50k PLN proof can fund a study and term sheet, not full qualification.

## Acquisition Route

Secure one narrow-format CMO term sheet, then approach smaller generic, parallel-import or specialty MAHs through supply-chain/quality owners. Sell a paid dossier rather than an abstract subscription.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Packaging-specific bottleneck frequency; CMO willingness to reserve; variation class/timing; dossier reuse across products; printed-material lead time; validation burden; willingness to pay pre-approval; service credits; local versus regional budget; trust without regulated custody. General shortage evidence is insufficient.
