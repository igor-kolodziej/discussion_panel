# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-016

Lead: `RAW-RSA-08`, shared manufacturer repair-and-parts reserve. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Directive (EU) 2024/1799 allows subcontracting but leaves responsibility with the manufacturer/importer/distributor and covers only Annex-II consumer goods from 31 July 2026 after transposition. Poland’s appliance market is large, but substitutes are mature: 300+ service-point networks, fixed-price repairs from 599 PLN and existing consignment warehouses. Cross-brand pooling is not intrinsically novel; only smaller importers with enough installed failures but too little density for a captive network are plausible.

## Proposed Transaction

One importer consigns 20 named parts and shares authorized compatibility data; two technicians accept dispatch SLAs; routed jobs trigger a 75–175 PLN administration fee plus technician/parts charges. A test readiness fee is 1.5–4k PLN/importer/month. The importer approves scope and retains statutory liability. A 90-day proof must show three jobs, wrong-part/repeat-visit rates and reservation renewal.

## Acquisition Route

Target smaller Annex-II brands through APPLiA/service ecosystems; obtain 12–24 months of anonymized failures and price one regional reserve against current courier/ad-hoc replacement cost.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Exact covered catalogue/transposition; failure volume; data/manual/IP access; stock traceability; incumbent response; obsolete-stock risk; technician density; whether pooling reduces rather than duplicates inventory; permissible parts. History and current substitutes make route-around severe.
