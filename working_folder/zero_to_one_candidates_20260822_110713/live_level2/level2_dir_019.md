# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-019

Lead: `RAW-RSA-03`, one-year grid-connection deadline rescue investor. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

For conditions issued after 30 April 2026, producers have 12 months to conclude a connection agreement; earlier conditions retain two-year validity. The 2026 reform adds 60 PLN/kW application advances, 30–60 PLN/kW security and permit milestones. URE recorded 4,897 refusals covering 107 GW in 2025, which proves scarcity but not a unique expiry pipeline. A 40–100k PLN budget fits only sub-scale, single-gap cases.

## Proposed Transaction

A dedicated SPV buys an option or minority stake in the genuine project company and releases capital against verified milestones. It never buys naked “grid capacity.” Diligence must cover cap table, conditions/agreement, operator correspondence/consent, land, permits, security, liens and a documented exit. A 0.5–1 MW proof may use 5–20k option consideration, 15–35k diligence, 15–30k security and 15–40k permit/design work.

## Acquisition Route

Source owner-authorized screens through renewable permitting/grid consultants, law firms, surveyors, land agents and small EPC/developer pipelines. Cheapest proof: screen 20–30 projects and identify one 60–120 days from expiry with one fundable milestone before investing.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Unique sub-1 MW pipeline; soluble versus fatal blockage; change-of-control consent; post-curtailment value; paid advances/security; permit feasibility; investor protection and exit; collective-investment perimeter; adverse selection. This is capital/legal intensive even when the statutory premise is valid.
