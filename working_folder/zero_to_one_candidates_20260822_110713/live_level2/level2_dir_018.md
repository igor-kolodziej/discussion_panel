# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-018

Lead: `RAW-RSA-05`, serialized photovoltaic take-back route. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Poland had 1,636,673 mostly-PV microinstallations at year-end 2025, but present waste flow is claimed at only about 2,000 t/year and is not independently audited. From 9 October 2025, government guidance says affected end users rather than producers bear disposal cost, weakening the original payer premise. Reported owner fees around 1.5–2 PLN/kg yield only 30–40 PLN for a 20 kg module. Recyclers already advertise nationwide pickup and pallet/KPO workflows.

## Proposed Transaction

An installer/owner orders collection; a BDO-verified carrier moves directly to an authorized recycler; the coordinator owns reusable A-frames and a provenance layer but no waste storage. Charge fixed 600–1,500 PLN/stop plus carrier/treatment pass-through. Route economics likely require 4–8 t/day or a higher small-lot fee.

## Acquisition Route

Use the family installer and two peers to aggregate damaged, warranty-return and repowering panels for four weeks, get two recycler quotes and run one prepaid day with rented frames. This tests count/weight/KPO/damage and contribution before buying equipment.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Payer by cohort; current captured share; 30–60 day tonnes; BDO role; waste codes by damage/chemistry; difficult-module acceptance; value of serial mapping beyond KPO; installer willingness; serial visibility; frame economics. Large stock is not current flow.
