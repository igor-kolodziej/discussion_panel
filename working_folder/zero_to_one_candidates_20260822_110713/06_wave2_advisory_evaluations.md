Framework: `holistic-11-v1`. Model/effort: `gpt-5.6-sol` / `high`. Each exact frozen candidate was assessed by a fresh isolated evaluator that read only the evaluation skill, founder profile, neutral framework, the candidate, its own Level-2 dossier and its own fact-closure report. Evaluators did not receive shadow artifacts, reserve candidates, generator reasoning, validation thresholds, previous scores, another evaluator's report, historical rejection rhetoric or validator feedback. Advisory scores are diagnostic only and satisfy no validation gate.

## Canonical routing decision

No Wave 2 candidate is a `strong opportunity`; all six have the exact diagnosis `decision-critical research`. Public fact closure has already exhausted the relevant public evidence and leaves the decisive premise private. The resolving tests require contracts, third-party commitments, external filings, paid professional work, customer funds or founder capital. Those actions are approval-gated and were not performed.

Some reports use “panel-ready” narrowly to mean that a dossier is sufficiently specified for an advisory discussion. The orchestration contract routes from the complete diagnosis, not that phrase: a `decision-critical research` candidate must resolve and be freshly re-evaluated before it can enter the independent simulated panel. Accordingly, **zero candidates advance to simulated judging**. No panel score exists, no real Zero to One chatbot was contacted, and no feedback was reused.

## Regeneration stopping decision

No third live discovery wave is started. The contract permits another wave only when the rejection record identifies a materially untested mechanism/control class or structural transformation. Wave 2 already produced 50 raw hypotheses across 15 distinct economic families, including assigned-payment title, contingent recovery, payment-security completion, transaction-gating records, regulated source-of-truth custody, long-lived product custody, exclusive powered-site options, reserved expiring capacity, recovered-input title/offtake, circular hardware title, owned inventory, prepaid reusable assets, metered outcome equipment, buyer-facing prime fulfillment and deposit-return value capture.

The remaining bottleneck is not an omitted structural family. Across the strongest live shapes it is the same class of unavailable private evidence: counterparty authorization, paid acceptance, complete contribution economics and resistance to an accessible incumbent route-around. Launching another public generation wave without a named untested mechanism would violate the stopping rule and risk laundering advisory language back into generation. The reserve remains excluded; the matched shadow arm remains non-routing and had no influence on this decision.
