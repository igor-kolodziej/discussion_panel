# Regeneration Wave 2 — Incumbent-Attacker Report

Accessed: 2026-08-22. Ten unscored hypotheses spanning construction, renewable energy, EV infrastructure, cross-border commerce, logistics, packaging, retail, personal services, payments, and custom manufacturing.

## W2-IAT-01 — Short-duration mixed-brand tool packs

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Construction and renewable installation / owned-asset rental.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant rents complete, mixed-brand installation tool packs to small electrical, HVAC, and solar crews for one-to-nine-day jobs, with a paid same-day swap commitment.
- **Loss bearer:** Small contractors losing billable crew hours when a tool fails or a short project requires equipment they do not own.
- **Payer:** Installation contractor.
- **Beneficiary:** Crew, contractor, and project customer.
- **Authority holder:** Entrant controls the rental fleet; the contractor controls workplace use and worker authorization.
- **Distributor:** Founder-led direct sales through the family renewable firm’s installer and wholesaler relationships.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains serial-number, utilization, damage, job-type, seasonality, and willingness-to-pay records.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears utilization, residual-value and ordinary equipment-failure risk; renter bears misuse and an agreed deductible.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as equipment owner and lessor.
- **First paid event:** A contractor pays for a reserved job pack and deposit before collection.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Serial-numbered fleet, accumulated demand history, deposits, repeat rental contracts, and route-level availability.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Offer a predefined photovoltaic/electrical pack to installers near Ostrów Wielkopolski, initially sourcing equipment only after a prepaid reservation; approximately **2,000–8,000 PLN** for a demonstrator and insurance, or **15,000–35,000 PLN** for two owned packs.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q8/S8:** Hilti’s surge-tool service is restricted to active fleet customers, limited to Nuron equipment, subject to fleet-size constraints, and not offered for fewer than ten working days—leaving a directly addressable short-duration and mixed-brand gap. [Hilti temporary-tool pricing](https://www.hilti.pl/content/hilti/E3/PL/pl/business/business/equipment/zarzadzanie-flota/maszyny-zast_pcze---cennik.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q8/S9:** The incumbent core offer is a bundled long-term fleet arrangement covering maintenance, repairs and theft protection through fixed monthly fees. [Hilti Fleet Management](https://www.hilti.pl/content/hilti/E3/PL/pl/business/business/equipment/fleet-management.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Insurable theft rate, achievable daily price, equipment certification requirements, seasonal utilization, and whether renters accept deposits.
- **Constraint class:** Capital equipment, insurance, occupational safety.
- **Lawful structure:** Written rental contract, inspection log, manufacturer-compliant maintenance, declared deductibles, and appropriate equipment/liability insurance.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Unsafe tools, falsified inspection records, unauthorized firmware, hidden damage charges, or access to incumbent fleet systems.

## W2-IAT-02 — Solar-inverter warranty exchange merchant

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Renewable-energy equipment / title-taking exchange and recovery.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant buys a failed eligible inverter or controller from an installer, supplies an owned replacement, and retains title to the failed unit and any lawful repair, warranty, or resale recovery.
- **Loss bearer:** Installer bearing repeat visits and reputational damage, and system owner losing generation during the claims process.
- **Payer:** Installer pays the net exchange price after the agreed failed-unit credit.
- **Beneficiary:** Installer and photovoltaic system owner.
- **Authority holder:** Property owner authorizes replacement; qualified electrician controls electrical work; manufacturer controls warranty acceptance.
- **Distributor:** Direct offers through the founder’s family-accessible renewable installer and nearby installation firms.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains serial-number, fault-code, model, documentation-completeness, repair-cost and recovery-outcome data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears title, authenticity, repair and recovery risk; qualified installer bears installation workmanship risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as purchaser of the failed unit and seller of the replacement.
- **First paid event:** An installer pays an exchange invoice for a named model and transfers the failed unit under a signed title document.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Replacement inventory, recovered units, serial-level failure history, installer contracts, and model-specific recovery procedures.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Obtain one documented failed unit through the family firm, secure a paid replacement reservation, and source one interchangeable unit; approximately **12,000–30,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q7/S7:** An adjacent Huawei warranty policy promises repair or replacement within fourteen calendar days after receipt, requires complete product submission, and transfers replaced parts or products to Huawei—evidence that title, completeness and downtime terms matter. This source is not inverter-specific. [Huawei Poland warranty policy](https://consumer.huawei.com/pl/support/warranty-policy/), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Actual commercial-inverter warranty terms, transferability, availability of lawful replacement stock, grid-setting requirements, failure concentration by model, and recovered-unit resale demand.
- **Constraint class:** Electrical safety, warranty assignment, inventory authenticity.
- **Lawful structure:** Explicit title transfer, manufacturer-specific eligibility check, qualified electrical installation, accurate refurbished-product disclosure, and ordinary VAT invoicing.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Impersonating the original purchaser, submitting unauthorized claims, counterfeit parts, deceptive compatibility, bypassing grid protection, or unsafe installation.

## W2-IAT-03 — Inspection-guaranteed refurbished EV chargers

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** EV infrastructure / refurbishment, resale and bounded performance guarantee.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant purchases used AC chargers, refurbishes them through qualified technicians, and sells installed units with a contractual allowance covering one UDT re-test if the initial inspection fails for an entrant-controlled defect.
- **Loss bearer:** Small hotel, clinic, workplace, or housing site exposed to stranded equipment, failed-inspection cost and commissioning delay.
- **Payer:** Property owner or tenant installing the charger.
- **Beneficiary:** Property operator and authorized drivers.
- **Authority holder:** UDT controls the inspection; the property owner and relevant electrical parties authorize installation and connection.
- **Distributor:** Renewable installers and electricians acting as disclosed sales agents, plus founder-led direct outreach to destination sites.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains charger provenance, test measurements, defect, refurbishment, inspection and resale records.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears inventory and covered inspection-failure risk; installation contractor bears workmanship risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as charger owner, merchant and guarantee issuer.
- **First paid event:** Customer places a deposit on a serial-numbered refurbished charger and installation package.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Charger stock, test fixtures, serial-level inspection history, guarantee reserve and installer channel contracts.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Source one traceable used charger, obtain a conditional customer deposit through the family installation channel, and commission a qualified bench test; approximately **8,000–25,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q4/S5:** UDT lists the initial technical inspection fee at **1,554.01 PLN**, establishing a concrete cost attached to inspection readiness. [UDT inspection fees](https://www.udt.gov.pl/uslugi-udt/elektromobilnosc/zgloszenie-urzadzenia-do-badania/badanie-techniczne-wstepne/oplaty-za-badanie), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q4/S6:** EIPA’s public register is populated from operator-supplied data, and UDT disclaims its current accuracy; the entrant’s asset history would instead come from units it owns and refurbishes. [EIPA register](https://eipa.udt.gov.pl/search/), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Availability and provenance of used chargers, inspection treatment after refurbishment, model-specific spare parts, guarantee loss frequency, and product-liability insurance terms.
- **Constraint class:** Regulated electrical equipment, product liability, working capital.
- **Lawful structure:** Traceable purchase documents, qualified refurbishment, truthful specification and compatibility statements, compliant installation, and independent UDT inspection.
- **Prohibited reliance:** False certification, tampered safety systems, cloned serial numbers, concealed refurbishment, or claims of guaranteed UDT approval regardless of site conditions.

## W2-IAT-04 — Micro-batch importer and packaging-obligation principal

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Cross-border commerce / importer-of-record and inventory merchant.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant purchases a small foreign product batch into its own title, imports and documents it as the Polish principal, and resells compliant landed lots to microretailers.
- **Loss bearer:** Microretailer currently exposed to fragmented freight, customs, packaging records and BDO mistakes.
- **Payer:** Polish microretailer buying a landed domestic lot.
- **Beneficiary:** Retailer and foreign microbrand seeking a Polish route to market.
- **Authority holder:** Customs and environmental authorities; the entrant holds importer and introducer responsibilities for its transaction.
- **Distributor:** Founder-owned niche catalogue and direct retailer outreach.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains supplier terms, product classification, packaging-mass records, sell-through and retailer reorder data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears inventory, customs, tax, compliance and supplier-quality risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as importer, inventory owner and domestic seller.
- **First paid event:** Retailer pays a deposit against a specified landed batch.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Supplier agreements, product dossiers, packaging-mass history, domestic inventory title and retailer reorder contracts.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Select a non-food, non-cosmetic, non-electrical product, secure a retailer deposit, and import one documented micro-batch; approximately **10,000–30,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q3/S3:** PARP lists registration, annual fees, BDO numbers on documents, packaging-mass records, recycling/product-fee duties, education duties and reporting for entities introducing packaged products. [PARP obligations guide](https://www.parp.gov.pl/attachments/article/85271/CRMSP_2023_23-10-2023.pdf), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q3/S4:** Polish regulations specify product fees per kilogram by packaging material, including **2.70 PLN/kg for plastics**, **0.70 PLN/kg for paper and cardboard**, and **0.30 PLN/kg for glass or wood**. [Packaging product-fee regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/2683/text.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Product-specific conformity duties, customs classification, minimum supplier orders, retailer deposit appetite, returns and unsold-inventory exposure.
- **Constraint class:** Customs, tax, environmental compliance, working capital.
- **Lawful structure:** Entrant genuinely purchases and imports the goods, registers where required, retains mass and customs evidence, and sells under clear domestic terms.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Nominal importer arrangements, false packaging weights, undervaluation, missing conformity documents, or shifting statutory duties through sham contracts.

## W2-IAT-05 — Carrier-of-record for awkward e-commerce parcels

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Logistics / freight resale backed by owned reusable crates.
- **One-sentence transaction:** A merchant buys a fixed-price, door-to-door awkward-item shipment from the entrant, which supplies an owned reusable crate and purchases the underlying parcel or LTL capacity in its own contractual chain.
- **Loss bearer:** Merchant suffering carrier rejection, dimensional surcharges, damage, returns and unrecoverable shipping fees.
- **Payer:** E-commerce merchant or installer.
- **Beneficiary:** Merchant and end buyer.
- **Authority holder:** Entrant controls acceptance and crate specifications under its contract; underlying carrier controls linehaul safety rules.
- **Distributor:** Direct outreach to sellers of mirrors, lamps, sports equipment, long installation components and similar awkward goods.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains exact dimensions, lanes, damage modes, carrier outcomes and crate-cycle records.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears quote and agreed transit-liability risk; subcontracted carrier bears liability allocated under the carrier contract.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as the merchant-facing freight contractor and invoice issuer.
- **First paid event:** Merchant pays for one declared shipment before crate dispatch.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Reusable crates, freight contracts, lane economics, damage history and merchant shipping contracts.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Build three adjustable crates and sell a prepaid shipment for a nearby merchant before committing to additional stock; approximately **8,000–20,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q2/Q6/S2:** GLS Point limits parcels to 31.5 kg and a circumference-plus-longest-side ceiling; non-deliverable parcels are returned while the original fee is not refunded. [GLS Poland price list](https://gls-group.com/PL/media/downloads/gls_cennik_a4_maj_2025_a4_RGB_online.pdf), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q11/S12:** InPost’s courier schedule allows dimensional verification, nonstandard and oversize charges to accumulate, with heavier volumetric parcels reclassified to pallet service. [InPost courier price list](https://inpost.pl/sites/default/files/docs/regulaminy/cennik-uslug-inpost-kurier-obowiazujacy-od-29052025-2056314.pdf), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Freight-forwarder insurance price, carrier contract minimums, crate return economics, damage frequency and profitable lane density.
- **Constraint class:** Transport liability, insurance, packaging engineering.
- **Lawful structure:** Disclosed freight-forwarding or carrier contract, accurate declarations, written subcontracting terms, cargo insurance and compliant packaging.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Understated dimensions, undeclared dangerous goods, hidden carrier substitution, unsafe packaging, or unauthorized carrier labels.

## W2-IAT-06 — Serialized reusable packaging rental

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Packaging and circular commerce / deposit-backed asset loop.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant rents serialized reusable mailers or crates to local online merchants per dispatch cycle, retains title, and operates the customer deposit and return process.
- **Loss bearer:** Merchant paying repeatedly for single-use packaging and managing packaging records; entrant bears non-return and damage.
- **Payer:** Merchant pays the cycle fee; recipient may fund a disclosed refundable deposit.
- **Beneficiary:** Merchant and repeat customer.
- **Authority holder:** Entrant controls the packaging assets; merchant retains all statutory duties that cannot lawfully be transferred.
- **Distributor:** Founder-led direct sales to local repeat-purchase merchants.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains cycle count, loss, return time, merchant volume and packaging durability data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears asset loss, cleaning and reverse-logistics risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as packaging owner, lessor and deposit administrator.
- **First paid event:** Merchant prepays a thirty-day batch lease for serialized packaging.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Packaging pool, serial and return history, deposit balances, merchant integration and accumulated cycle economics.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Offer a prepaid batch to one repeat-purchase merchant and acquire approximately 100 units after contracting; **5,000–15,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q3/S3:** Packaged-product introducers face BDO registration, packaging records, recycling or product-fee obligations and reporting. [PARP obligations guide](https://www.parp.gov.pl/attachments/article/85271/CRMSP_2023_23-10-2023.pdf), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q3/S4:** Statutory fees vary materially by packaging material and mass, making verified cycle and material records commercially relevant. [Packaging product-fee regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/2683/text.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Legal treatment of rented packaging in each flow, return rate, cleaning requirements, customer deposit acceptance and reverse-shipping cost.
- **Constraint class:** Packaging law, waste classification, consumer deposits, sanitation.
- **Lawful structure:** Genuine lease with retained title, transparent deposit terms, documented cleaning, continued merchant compliance, and licensed end-of-life treatment when an item becomes waste.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Calling waste a product to evade controls, promising automatic BDO exemption, false environmental claims, or unlicensed waste handling.

- **Control/compounding asset:** Inventory title, authenticated supplier history, buyer lists, realized-price data and repeat liquidation rights.

## W2-IAT-08 — Employer-funded expiring appointment capacity

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Physiotherapy and personal services / capacity options and wholesale credits.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant buys cancellable weekly options on otherwise expiring appointment blocks and sells prepaid same-week service credits to local employers.
- **Loss bearer:** Independent provider loses revenue from empty slots; employer bears avoidable employee waiting and absence costs.
- **Payer:** Employer purchasing the credit pack.
- **Beneficiary:** Employee and provider.
- **Authority holder:** Provider retains all clinical or professional authority; employee chooses and consents; entrant controls only contracted capacity and payment.
- **Distributor:** Founder-led direct sales to SMEs, with an entrant-owned booking and credit channel.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains capacity, redemption and commercial records while providers retain clinical records.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears unused-credit and capacity-option risk; provider bears professional-service liability.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as wholesale capacity-option buyer and employer-credit seller.
- **First paid event:** Employer prepays a monthly pack of named service credits.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Capacity-option contracts, employer agreements, redemption history and an owned local demand pool.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Contract options with two independent providers and sell one prepaid pack to a Warsaw SME; approximately **3,000–12,000 PLN** for option fees, contracts and payment operations.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q9/S10:** Booksy charges **145 PLN net monthly**, plus **35 PLN per additional user**, and its optional Boost charges **45% net of the completed first visit** from an acquired customer. [Booksy Biz pricing](https://biz.booksy.com/pl-pl/cennik), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Employer benefit-tax treatment, provider appetite for option contracts, credit expiry rules, health-data boundaries and employee utilization.
- **Constraint class:** Health privacy, professional independence, employment benefits, consumer terms.
- **Lawful structure:** Provider remains independent, entrant processes only necessary booking data, employer receives no clinical data, and expiry/refund terms are transparent.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Clinical outcome claims, medically inappropriate steering, unauthorized health-data access, fake bookings or provider misclassification.

## W2-IAT-09 — Purchase of already-disputed card receivables

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Payments and events / receivable acquisition.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant purchases an already-filed, documented card-dispute receivable from a small event or experience merchant and retains the lawful recovery proceeds.
- **Loss bearer:** Merchant facing the disputed principal, evidence workload and processor fees.
- **Payer:** Commercial value is paid through the discount between the receivable’s face value and purchase price; the acquirer or issuer remits proceeds if recovery succeeds.
- **Beneficiary:** Merchant receives immediate, certain liquidity.
- **Authority holder:** Issuer and card network decide the dispute; merchant grants the entrant an assignment or narrowly drafted recovery mandate.
- **Distributor:** Direct merchant outreach through accountants, payment consultants and entrant-owned acquisition.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains consented transaction evidence, dispute type, submission and recovery-outcome records.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears the purchase-price and recovery-cost risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as legal owner of assignable proceeds or exclusive recovery principal.
- **First paid event:** The first assigned recovery is remitted to the entrant; the merchant received the agreed purchase price at assignment.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Claims book, merchant contract clauses, dispute-outcome data and specialized evidence procedures.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Acquire a small, fully documented post-dispute claim under a counsel-reviewed assignment; approximately **5,000–20,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q10/S11:** Stripe Poland lists a **90 PLN dispute-received fee**, another **90 PLN** for a manually countered dispute—refunded only when won—and a Smart Disputes charge of **30% of recovered value** on won cases. [Stripe Poland pricing](https://stripe.com/en-pl/pricing), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Assignability under processor terms, merchant evidence quality, data-transfer permissions, recovery duration, licensing boundaries and attainable purchase discount.
- **Constraint class:** Contract assignment, payment rules, privacy, possible debt-servicing regulation.
- **Lawful structure:** Post-loss acquisition only, case-specific assignment or mandate, minimal PCI exposure, truthful evidence and legal review of processor terms.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Fabricated evidence, coached false statements, unauthorized account access, pre-loss insurance without authorization, or concealing the assignee’s role.

## W2-IAT-10 — Custom-order merchant of record

- **Sector / economic mechanism:** Custom furniture and light manufacturing / prime contracting and milestone risk assumption.
- **One-sentence transaction:** The entrant sells a fixed-specification custom order to the end buyer, subcontracts production to a workshop, documents milestone acceptance, and bears the buyer-facing refund and card-dispute risk.
- **Loss bearer:** Small workshop currently exposed to ambiguous custom specifications, marketplace fees, refunds and chargebacks.
- **Payer:** End customer.
- **Beneficiary:** Customer obtains one accountable counterparty; workshop receives milestone-based payment.
- **Authority holder:** Customer approves specification and milestones; workshop controls safe production; entrant controls the customer contract, payment and remedy process.
- **Distributor:** Entrant-owned quote flow, search acquisition and direct outreach to business buyers.
- **Information holder:** Entrant retains specifications, revisions, acceptance evidence, workshop performance and customer history.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant bears chargeback, refund, rework and subcontractor-performance risk; workshop bears allocated workmanship and safety liability.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as disclosed merchant of record and prime contractor.
- **First paid event:** Customer pays a design and specification deposit to the entrant.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Customer contracts, standardized specification records, acceptance evidence, workshop performance history and repeat buyer relationships.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Sell one small, tightly specified order using an existing workshop quote and maintain a rework reserve; approximately **5,000–18,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q10/S11:** Stripe’s dispute pricing creates a directly measurable cost for merchants, while original processing fees are generally not returned after refunds. [Stripe Poland pricing](https://stripe.com/en-pl/pricing), accessed 2026-08-22.
  - **Q1/Q5/S1:** Marketplace selling can also carry category commission, delivery, promotion and subscription costs, giving workshops a reason to move a controlled custom transaction. [Allegro seller fees](https://help.allegro.com/pl/fees/pl), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Customer acquisition cost, cancellation treatment for custom goods, workshop defect rates, rework reserve size and payment-provider acceptance of the model.
- **Constraint class:** Consumer law, product safety, payment processing, subcontractor performance.
- **Lawful structure:** Disclosed prime contract, precise specifications, fair custom-order cancellation terms, staged acceptance, compliant invoices and written workshop subcontract.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Hidden merchant identity, unfair non-refundable terms, fabricated acceptance, unsafe products, misleading origin or deceptive compatibility.
