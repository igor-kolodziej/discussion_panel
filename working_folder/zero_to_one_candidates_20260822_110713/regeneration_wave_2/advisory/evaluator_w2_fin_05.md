## Evidence calibration

### Verified

- Polish law recognizes waste dealers that trade without taking physical possession.
- Dealer/broker BDO registration is required.
- A non-possessing dealer does not automatically assume statutory waste-management responsibility.
- Article 101a creates professional construction-waste segregation obligations.
- A 2026 Ostrów Wielkopolski procurement places construction-waste removal cost on the contractor.
- A local container-service route already serves companies and provides fraction-sensitive quotations.
- Historical Orli Staw tariffs accepted relevant construction-waste codes and displayed materially different charges by composition.

### Interpreted

- Rejection, downgrade, repeat haulage, quotation effort, and project delay create meaningful contractor pain.
- Public procurements can provide a founder-accessible list of named projects and contractors, but not evidence of purchase intent.
- Historical price differences show severe contamination sensitivity directionally; they do not establish current local economics.
- Processor-specific photographs and settlement history could improve quotation accuracy and risk selection over time.

### Counsel-required

- Exact title-transfer point.
- Correct KPO representation of the principal/dealer.
- Article 101a responsibility allocation.
- VAT, invoicing, and tax treatment of negative-value waste transactions.
- Insurance and contractual allocation of rejection, contamination, and misdescription risk.

### Unknown

- Two processors’ current willingness to quote and reserve dependable capacity for a new dealer.
- Contractor willingness to prepay the complete processor, haul, contingency, and coordination amount.
- Current gate, haul, waiting, downgrade, and rejection terms.
- Positive reconciled contribution per batch.
- Serviceable repeat-customer denominator.
- Repeat batch frequency and achievable acquisition cost.
- Availability and price of suitable insurance.
- Whether container companies would refer business rather than retain the customer.

### Contradicted

The earlier implication that GZUK pricing was local to Ostrów Wielkopolski is contradicted: the referenced operator is in **39-103 Ostrów**, outside the beachhead. Those tariffs cannot support local pricing or capacity.

No evidence directly contradicts the candidate’s indispensable premise; it remains private and unresolved.

## Principal commercial and execution risks

- **Strongest route-around:** An incumbent container provider can bundle container, haulage, and disposal, quote the contractor directly, and retain the customer relationship.
- **Distribution failure:** Contractors refuse full prepayment or find the principal’s conditional, specification-heavy process less convenient than established container service. Proposed container-company referrals are particularly fragile because those firms may see the entrant as a competitor.
- **Economic sensitivity:** Against the modeled 1,300 PLN batch contribution, historical ten-tonne gate costs ranged from 115 PLN for clean concrete to 1,690.50 PLN for contaminated concrete and 9,200 PLN for insulation-heavy mixed mineral material. A downgrade can eliminate several batches’ contribution.
- **Execution risks:** Misclassification, contamination, processor rejection after haulage, unavailable capacity, incomplete KPO handling, insufficient insurance, daytime dispatch conflicts with employment, and dependence on reliable carriers.
- **Competitive durability:** Processor rules, photographs, and realized-margin data can improve decisions but are not exclusive. Processors, carriers, and container companies can copy the checklist or transact directly.

## Lawful role boundary

The candidate must remain a BDO-registered, non-possessing dealer/principal with movement strictly:

`contractor site → BDO-registered carrier → permitted processor`

It must not operate a yard, unload, store, consolidate, physically re-sort, or reclassify waste. It must not claim to assume the contractor’s Article 101a segregation duty. Written Polish waste-law advice is required before the first transaction for title, KPO, responsibility, VAT, invoicing, and insurance.
