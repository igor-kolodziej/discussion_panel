## Exact Candidate

Build a Polish authorized-representative and transaction operator that reaches commercial energy-efficiency projects through installers before the owner's first binding project commitment, screens eligibility, commissions the required independent audit, prepares and administers the URE filing, preserves completion evidence, and arranges lawful monetization of issued property rights. The project owner keeps 85%–90% of net realized proceeds after explicitly defined third-party transaction costs; the company retains 10%–15% only after a successful sale. No eligibility, issuance, timing, volume or certificate price is guaranteed.

The first wedge is not generic white-certificate consulting. It is a pre-commitment installer channel for repeatable 30–50 toe commercial projects where the owner would otherwise fail to file in time.

## Customer, Payer And Trigger

- Contracting customer: the project owner undertaking an eligible energy-efficiency investment.
- Acquisition controller: the installer that sees the project before a binding order or works start.
- Immediate fee source: the owner's realized certificate-sale proceeds.
- Ultimate economic payer: the purchaser of the property rights and, in turn, obligated energy-market participants.
- Paid trigger: property rights are issued and sold; the company deducts agreed costs and its contractual share before remitting the balance.

## Contract And Risk Boundary

1. Owner and operator sign the authorization and proceeds-sharing agreement before the first binding commitment.
2. An independent qualified auditor prepares the technical audit.
3. The application is filed before project commencement under the current URE requirements.
4. The owner executes and documents the project.
5. The operator completes the notice, registry and sale workflow under counsel-reviewed tax, client-money and agency terms.
6. The owner bears project execution risk; the operator bears only capped preparation expenditure stated in the agreement.

The company does not claim to be URE, TGE, the registry, a broker, an obligated party or the independent auditor unless it actually holds the required role.

## First Paid Proof And Maximum Loss

- Through the family renewable installer, identify five projects before any binding order.
- Reject projects below the economic threshold unless lawful aggregation and shared audit work make them viable.
- Obtain one fixed audit quote and one counsel-reviewed agreement.
- Seek one signed 30–50 toe owner authorization with capped preparation spend.
- Initial capital at risk: only the agreed audit, legal and dossier costs; no speculative portfolio of already-committed projects.

## Economics — Planning Assumptions, Not Guaranteed Proceeds

For an illustrative 50-toe project:

- at 1,800–2,500 PLN per toe, gross rights proceeds are 90,000–125,000 PLN;
- a 15% share yields 13,500–18,750 PLN revenue;
- modeled audit, dossier and transaction cost is 6,000–7,000 PLN;
- modeled gross profit is 7,500–11,750 PLN.

Public evidence verifies a transaction with the owner retaining 90% and the contractor retaining 10% of net proceeds. The dossier's founder-wealth cases are scenario assumptions and depend on repeat installer-originated volume, audit costs, issuance duration, certificate price and the ability to avoid financing long working-capital cycles.

## Acquisition And Compounding Control

- First channel: the founder's family installer and adjacent HVAC, industrial-process, insulation and controls installers.
- Expansion: standardized pre-commitment screens, auditor coverage and installer white-label workflows.
- Compounding asset: early project flow, documented eligibility patterns, auditor throughput, URE exception handling and realized issuance/sale data.
- Incumbent route-around: owners and installers can use KAPE, TAURON or other end-to-end providers, aggregate small projects, perform the work internally or use substitute compliance routes.

## Closed Facts And Indispensable Unknown

URE permits an authorized representative, publishes an authorization form, and reported 1,330 issued certificates covering 195,851.216 toe with an estimated aggregate value near 434 million PLN in 2025. An official 2026 tax record documents a 90/10 net-proceeds arrangement. Public evidence does not establish a founder-accessible repeat flow of still-uncommitted 30–50 toe projects or complete transaction costs.

> The indispensable premise is that installer channels repeatedly expose eligible, uncommitted projects whose owners accept the proceeds share and whose project-specific audit and processing costs preserve positive contribution.

Stop if installer files do not contain pre-commitment projects, owners reject the proceeds share, binding audit quotes erase contribution, authorizations are unusable, or actual processing and financing duration makes expected contribution unattractive.
