## Exact Candidate

Operate as a counsel-reviewed, BDO-registered non-possessing waste dealer and contractual principal for narrowly specified, source-separated construction and demolition fractions around Ostrów Wielkopolski. Before quoting a batch, obtain two current processor acceptance offers with exact waste code, contamination specification, capacity window and downgrade terms, plus a direct-haul carrier price. The contractor prepays the processor/gate allowance, transport and coordination margin. The company purchases the specified batch on agreed title terms, directs it from site to processor without a yard or fleet, and bears only the expressly priced title, price, downgrade and contamination risks that counsel and insurance permit.

The first unit is one prepaid, ten-tonne-class batch with photographs, source attestation and processor acceptance—not a general skip-hire or waste-broker marketplace.

## Customer, Payer And Trigger

- Customer and prepayer: construction or demolition contractor that already bears removal cost.
- Processor: licensed receiver that controls acceptance, downgrade and gate price.
- Carrier: direct-haul contractor operating under the required transport and BDO documentation.
- Paid trigger: contractor accepts a specification-specific all-in quote and prepays before dispatch.

## Contract And Risk Boundary

1. Counsel confirms the non-possessing dealer/principal role, title point, Article 101a implications, KPO workflow, invoicing and liability allocation.
2. Contractor remains responsible for lawful source segregation, coding inputs and loading under explicit warranties.
3. Processor provides written batch specification and acceptance capacity.
4. Carrier moves the load directly; the company does not hold waste or operate a yard.
5. Prepayment and contingency cover processor, haul and downgrade exposure before collection.
6. Photographs, weight tickets, KPO, contamination events and realized settlement are retained.

## First Paid Proof And Maximum Loss

- Secure two current processor quotes and one carrier quote before any customer commitment.
- Quote one clean, narrow fraction from a named works site.
- Require contractor prepayment sufficient to cover the conservative gate, haul and contingency case.
- Use no owned vehicle, container or yard.
- Founder capital at risk is limited to counsel, BDO setup, insurance and any expressly capped batch difference after prepayment.

## Economics — Planning Assumptions, Not Current Local Prices

The dossier's illustrative ten-tonne batch models 4,300 PLN contractor revenue, but current processor, haul, rejection and downgrade quotes were not obtained. Historic Orli Staw tariffs show why live pricing is indispensable: ten tonnes of clean concrete was mechanically 115 PLN at the 2020 tariff, contaminated concrete 1,690.50 PLN, and insulation-heavy mixed mineral material 9,200 PLN. Those figures are historical and not executable offers.

Any founder-wealth path requires repeat route density, reliable processor allocation, contamination control, prepaid cash conversion and contribution that survives container-service substitutes. It cannot be inferred from historic gate-price spreads.

## Acquisition And Compounding Control

- First customers: named local contractors on projects where tender documents place removal cost on them.
- Supply: two or more processors per accepted fraction and contracted direct-haul carriers.
- Compounding asset: processor-specific acceptance rules, contamination photos, realized downgrade economics, contractor/source reliability and dense scheduled routes.
- Incumbent route-around: local container providers already offer rapid, fraction-sensitive service and can quote established customers directly.

## Closed Facts And Indispensable Unknown

Polish law distinguishes a non-possessing dealer, and a 2026 local procurement puts construction-waste removal cost on the contractor. The prior GZUK tariff belongs to 39-103 Ostrów, not Ostrów Wielkopolski, and cannot support local economics. No current public evidence establishes two processors' acceptance capacity, contractor prepayment or a positive reconciled local batch margin.

> The indispensable premise is that two processors provide dependable direct-haul routes and contractors prepay enough for repeated batches to retain contribution after transport, contamination and downgrade costs.

Stop if counsel rejects the structure, two processors will not quote, contractors prefer incumbent containers at lower all-in prices, prepayment is refused, or the first reconciled batches show non-positive expected contribution.
