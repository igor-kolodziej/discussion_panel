## Exact Candidate

Sell Polish stationary-battery importers, private-label distributors and integrators a managed readiness batch and long-lived passport-custody operation for covered industrial batteries above 2 kWh. The responsible economic operator signs the Article 77 written authorization and retains legal responsibility. The company maps manufacturer data, captures commissioning events through installers, creates up to ten provenance-preserving records, implements public/restricted access, supplies open JSON/CSV export, and then charges prepaid annual custody plus per-passport and lifecycle-update fees. It contractually prohibits data resale and unrelated analytics and maintains backup, migration and business-failure continuity.

This is managed multi-brand data operations, authorization control and lifecycle custody—not commodity QR hosting, conformity certification or a claim to be a notified body.

## Customer, Payer And Trigger

- Customer and payer: the economic operator that places the finished covered battery on the EU market.
- Channel: installers, renewable wholesalers and storage integrators that can identify the responsible brand/import chain and capture commissioning events.
- First paid trigger: 60% advance for one fixed readiness batch covering one or two models and up to ten records.
- Recurring trigger: acceptance of the batch and prepayment for annual custody; later passport batches are prepaid or invoiced monthly.

## Contract And Risk Boundary

1. Obtain explicit written authority for creation, storage and defined updates.
2. State that accuracy, completeness and currency responsibility remains with the placing operator.
3. Record source, author, time and reason for every change.
4. Separate role-based public and restricted fields.
5. Process data only for custody; no resale or unrelated model training/analytics.
6. Provide documented exports, identifier continuity, backup, incident response and migration.
7. Never alter manufacturer-originated data without provenance and authority.
8. Exclude conformity certification and unsupported upstream-data reconstruction.

## First Paid Proof And Maximum Loss

- Use the family installer to map one complete brand-to-importer-to-installation chain.
- Identify the responsible placing operator for one model.
- Obtain written authorization before production work.
- Deliver up to ten live or pre-production records within six weeks.
- Charge 8,000–12,000 PLN and require at least 60% in advance.
- Founder-funded first-proof cost: 6,000–18,000 PLN; first-year product-spend ceiling: 80,000 PLN, with at least 20,000 PLN retained until paid renewal and workload are observed.

## Economics — Planning Assumptions With Public Competitive Anchors

- Proposed readiness batch: 8,000–12,000 PLN.
- Proposed production onboarding: 12,000–18,000 PLN.
- Proposed annual custody minimum: 9,600–24,000 PLN.

Commodity software is advertised from free to 149 EUR per month for 2,000 passports, while a managed provider advertises 5,000 EUR per organization annually, 50 EUR activation and 10 EUR annual maintenance per live industrial passport. The exact candidate must earn the managed-service premium through data reconciliation and custody. The dossier's expected founder-wealth case reaches about 27.95 million PLN in year 7, but assumes 220 paying operators, 12.15 million PLN revenue, 20% operating margin and 76% ownership; the Polish payer denominator and these conversion volumes are unverified.

## Acquisition And Compounding Control

- First route: family installer → responsible importer/private-label distributor → one paid batch.
- Expansion: white-label commissioning capture for installers and wholesalers; common OEM-document and BMS mappings; nearby EU importer portfolios.
- Compounding asset: authorized lifecycle records, validated model templates, supplier mappings, commissioning/repair/ownership/recycling events, integrations and exception patterns.
- The control is operational and contractual, not data enclosure; open export and prohibited secondary use limit lock-in.
- Incumbent route-around: OEM portals, self-service tools, Siemens and other horizontal vendors, ERP extensions and spreadsheets.

## Closed Facts And Indispensable Unknown

EU rules require passports for covered industrial batteries from 18 February 2027, contemplate another operator acting under written authorization, and require continuing availability, access control, integrity and interoperability. Managed implementation work and a managed commercial price point exist publicly. No source proves that an accessible Polish responsible operator will authorize and pay this independent company or supply the necessary upstream and BMS data.

> The indispensable premise is that a responsible placing operator pays for continuing managed custody above commodity software pricing because source-data reconciliation, commissioning capture and lifecycle updates require real operations.

Stop if no placing operator grants authority, three accessible importers insist on OEM hosting, mandatory upstream data cannot be obtained, buyers accept only commodity hosting prices, manual work stays excessive, or paid annual custody does not follow a successful batch.
