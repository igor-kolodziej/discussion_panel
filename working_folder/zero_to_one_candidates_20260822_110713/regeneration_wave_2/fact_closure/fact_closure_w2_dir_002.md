## Closure finding

Public evidence confirms a lawful, commercially used proceeds-sharing transaction. An official 2026 tax record describes a municipality receiving 90% of net certificate-sale proceeds while the authorized contractor retained 10% net. URE permits the project entity or its authorized representative to apply, and publishes an authorization form.

The evidence also exposes stronger route-arounds than the dossier established. KAPE and TAURON already provide end-to-end service; TAURON aggregates projects below 10 toe, handles the application, and defers settlement until certificates are obtained. Obligated parties can also use substitute payments and other compliance routes.

The indispensable premise remains unclosed because no public source establishes that a founder-accessible installer channel repeatedly produces still-uncommitted 30–50 toe projects, that owners in that channel will accept the proposed share, or that project-specific audit and processing costs leave positive contribution.

## Exact public facts closed

### Payer denominator and compliance budget

URE reported for the **2024 obligation year**:

- **34,903.516 toe** from redeemed certificates;
- **954,130,276.18 PLN** in substitute payments;
- **24,134.5 toe** from non-refundable support programs.

In 2025, URE issued **511 redemption decisions** covering **79,017.214 toe** for obligations from 2022–2025. It also reported that **37 entities** offered non-refundable programs in 2025. These are actual compliance-route observations, but they do not disclose the number of obligated entities or the amount they spent purchasing certificates. [URE 2025 activity report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16226/SprawozdaniezdzialalnosciPrezesaUREw2025r.pdf)

URE separately issued **1,330 certificates representing 195,851.216 toe** in 2025 and estimated the resulting rights at approximately **434 million PLN**. The associated applications dated from 2017–2025. Issuance, redemption and substitute-payment figures therefore represent different cohorts and cannot be combined into a single serviceable-market denominator. [URE 2025 activity report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16226/SprawozdaniezdzialalnosciPrezesaUREw2025r.pdf)

**Closed interpretation:** obligated market participants are the ultimate economic payers, but their demand for purchased rights is reduced by lawful substitute-payment, direct-project and support-program routes.

**Still unknown:** the count of active obligated buyers, annual certificate-purchase expenditure, bid depth, and buyer concentration.

### Direct willingness-to-pay evidence

An official Ministry of Finance tax record describes:

- an owner authorizing the contractor to apply;
- the contractor appearing on and selling the certificates;
- customary exchange, clearing and brokerage costs deducted from sale proceeds;
- **90% of net proceeds paid to the municipal owner**;
- **10% net retained by the contractor**, with VAT treatment addressed in the record;
- payment to the owner after the sale.

This is direct evidence that an owner accepted percentage compensation, but it is one transaction structure rather than a general market rate. [Ministry of Finance Eureka record](https://eureka.mf.gov.pl/informacje/podglad/678930)

A public procurement record separately requested an end-to-end white-certificate service and required bidders to quote a **percentage commission**. The accessible record did not disclose the winning percentage. [Public procurement record](https://platformazakupowa.pl/transakcja/991840)

### Authority, title and market access

URE states that an application may be made by the entity planning the investment or by an entity it authorizes. URE publishes planned-project forms and an example authorization. [URE application materials](https://bip.ure.gov.pl/bip/efektywnosc-energetyczn/swiadectwa-efektywnosci/1146%2CSwiadectwa-efektywnosci-energetycznej.html)

TGE lists PMEF, PMEF-year and PMEF_F rights as traded property-right products. The accessible page displayed trading sessions and registry/access links but did not expose an executable PMEF_F price or bid/offer depth. [TGE property-rights market](https://tge.pl/prawa-majatkowe-rpm)

KAPE states that sale requires certificate-registry membership and brokerage access. It offers identification, audit, application and sale in a success-fee model, with the complete process commonly taking several to more than twelve months. [KAPE FAQ](https://kape.gov.pl/faq)

### Substitute and incumbent route-around

TAURON publicly offers to:

- perform the audit and certificate procedure;
- combine same-type projects to reach 10 toe;
- include projects individually below 10 toe;
- settle after certificates are obtained;
- serve business customers nationally.

This means aggregation and deferred audit economics are already available from a major incumbent. A new provider cannot rely on a 30–50 toe targeting floor or success-fee timing as unique features. [TAURON Business](https://www.tauron.pl/dla-biznesu/audyty/audyt-efektywnosci-energetycznej)

Other direct route-arounds are owner self-filing, KAPE’s end-to-end service, brokerage-supported sale, and obligated parties’ alternative compliance routes.

### Price, cost, capital and sensitivity

No executable TGEeff quote was exposed by the official TGE page. The best public price anchor remains URE’s rounded 2025 estimate:

- 434 million PLN ÷ 195,851.216 toe ≈ **2,216 PLN/toe**.

A 2026 commercial price aggregator reported approximately **5,803 PLN** for an enterprise energy-efficiency audit. Its page failed to render when opened, and the quoted service is not demonstrably identical to a project-specific white-certificate audit. It is therefore only a weak cost proxy, not a quotation. [Commercial audit-price listing](https://cenauslug.pl/budowa-i-remont/audyt-efektywnosci-energetycznej-przedsiebiorstwa/biala-podlaska)

Using the 2,216 PLN/toe price anchor:

| Project | Gross proceeds | 10% fee | Fee less 5,803 PLN audit proxy | 15% fee | Fee less 5,803 PLN audit proxy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 toe | 66,480 PLN | 6,648 PLN | 845 PLN | 9,972 PLN | 4,169 PLN |
| 50 toe | 110,800 PLN | 11,080 PLN | 5,277 PLN | 16,620 PLN | 10,817 PLN |

These residuals precede dossier preparation, clarification work, registry, brokerage, referral, financing, cancellation and overhead costs. The public 10% transaction therefore makes a normal 30-toe project potentially marginal if the audit proxy is directionally representative.

Because KAPE reports a several-to-more-than-twelve-month process, any provider fronting audit costs also assumes material work-in-progress financing. Exact capital needs remain dependent on binding quotes, concurrent project count, rejection frequency and contractual recovery of costs.

## Cheapest resolving test

Use one cooperating installer’s current quotation pipeline before orders are signed:

1. Obtain equipment specifications, baseline consumption, proposed order terms and implementation dates for live candidate projects.
2. Have an independent qualified auditor perform a fixed-price eligibility screen and quote the complete audit work.
3. For a suitable 30–50 toe project, present the owner with a transparent mandate showing both the proceeds split and every cost deduction.
4. Obtain a signed, commencement-conditional mandate and authorization before the implementation contract or material purchase.
5. Record the same fields for subsequent installer opportunities through a complete sales cycle.

This directly tests channel timing, project frequency, owner acceptance, audit cost and contribution without first financing a portfolio or building software.

## Lawful-structure boundary

- No application may depend on backdating or concealing a binding implementation commitment or material purchase.
- The project owner, beneficiary, authorized applicant and rights holder must be disclosed consistently.
- Rights must be recorded and sold through the lawful registry and brokerage route.
- The agreement must define audit costs, transaction deductions, partial issuance, cancellation, sale authority, price approval and remittance timing.
- Customer funds must not be commingled.
- Audit calculations and professional responsibility must remain with a competent auditor.
- The fiduciary title structure, VAT treatment, public-aid interaction and accounting flow require Polish legal and tax advice for the proposed company’s exact facts.
