## Closure finding

Public evidence confirms a real, time-sensitive KSC workflow, permits an authorized person to submit the registry application, shows broad cybersecurity outsourcing, and confirms that IT-partner white-label delivery is already commercially offered.

It does **not** establish the indispensable premise: there is no public denominator for already-classified medium private entities, no observed willingness to pay for recurring evidence custody, no public confirmation that an external operator may hold the necessary S46 incident roles, and no usable insurance or out-of-hours delivery quotation. A self-service KSC product priced at **199–499 PLN net per month** is also a substantially cheaper route-around than the proposed **2,500 PLN monthly** managed retainer.

The concept therefore remains commercially possible but publicly unverified.

## Remaining private premises

- How many already-classified medium clients each accessible MSP or law firm can name.
- Whether those clients currently rely on scattered email, spreadsheets and MSP tickets.
- Whether management will buy quarterly maintenance rather than only the initial filing.
- Whether buyers will prepay 7,500 PLN for a quarter of managed custody.
- Whether an external operator can receive the required S46 role and incident access.
- Whether clients and MSPs will provide recurring evidence feeds and source attestations.
- Actual hours required for onboarding, monthly custody and an incident package.
- An MSSP’s price, availability commitment and liability allocation for out-of-hours intake.
- Cyber/E&O premium, exclusions, limits and requirements for incident-evidence custody.
- Frequency of serious incidents within the intended customer cohort.
- Permissible commercial arrangements with counsel, auditors and insurance distributors.
- Whether partner compensation leaves the modeled contribution after security and availability costs.

## Cheapest resolving test

Before building software:

1. Obtain one narrow written Polish-counsel opinion covering external S46 account roles, proxy limits, incident submission authority, GDPR roles and referral structure.
2. Obtain fixed quotations from one MSSP for out-of-hours intake and from an insurer or broker for the exact evidence-custody scope.
3. Through one MSP, present five already-classified medium clients with two written offers:
   - 8,000 PLN registry dossier and approved filing workflow;
   - the same setup plus a prepaid quarter of evidence custody at 2,500 PLN/month.
4. Deliver one paid engagement manually, preserving time records, evidence volume, approval delays and vendor-response effort.
5. Run a tabletop incident using the client’s actual MSP evidence and require the client or counsel—not the operator—to make the reporting decision.

A paid recurring order, the counsel opinion and the two delivery quotations would close the indispensable premise more directly than additional national market estimates.

## Lawful-structure boundary

- Accept only clients whose KSC applicability and category have already been confirmed by management or qualified counsel.
- Registry preparation and authorized filing may be sold within a narrow written mandate.
- Do not sell external S46 incident submission until the permitted roles and authority are confirmed in writing.
- The client retains incident classification, legal conclusions, statutory responsibility and final approval.
- Preserve originals and timestamps; use segregated tenants, least-privilege access, immutable activity logs and two-person approval for external submission.
- Do not promise out-of-hours response before a named provider has accepted contractual responsibility.
- Treat paid law-firm referrals, mixed incident evidence, retention periods and insurance adequacy as `counsel-required`.
