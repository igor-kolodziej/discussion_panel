# Fixed fact-closure cohort

These six dossiers are fixed; reserve dossiers are not substitutes.

1. **W2-L2-007 — Exclusive options over underused grid-connection headroom**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** An owner of a technically credible site will grant enforceable short-term exclusivity, and a qualified developer will pay a non-refundable reservation while funding operator-facing costs.
   - **Why it remains:** Connection scarcity is directly evidenced by 6,259 refused applications representing nearly 42.4 GW, while 49 cable-pooling agreements and 12 completed shared connections establish a real transaction path. The founder can secure a contractual option before risking development-scale capital.

2. **W2-L2-002 — White-certificate proceeds for energy-efficiency projects**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** Installer channels can repeatedly identify uncommitted 30–50 toe projects whose owners accept the proceeds share and whose audit costs preserve positive contribution.
   - **Why it remains:** URE reported 1,330 certificates covering 195,851 toe and approximately 434 million PLN of rights in 2025; a documented commercial structure already uses a 15% success fee. The family installer can expose projects before the binding-commitment deadline.

3. **W2-L2-005 — KSC registry and incident-evidence operator**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** Already-classified medium entities will buy recurring evidence custody—not merely one-time registration—through MSP or counsel channels at workable security and out-of-hours delivery costs.
   - **Why it remains:** Registration, 14-day updates, incident deadlines and management exposure are binding, while cybersecurity outsourcing is established behavior. A proxy-supported, paid registry workflow can be proved manually before building software.

4. **W2-L2-001 — Purchase of signed construction warranty retentions**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** A repeatable supply of accepted, assignable and debtor-acknowledged claims exists whose sellers accept discounts sufficient to cover legal work, funding and realized deductions.
   - **Why it remains:** The purchased receivable is an identifiable asset with a contractual maturity and direct debtor payment event. The structure can start with a small, unleveraged portfolio sourced through installer and accountant relationships, then compound through actual debtor and loss history.

5. **W2-L2-009 — Principal buyer of separated demolition fractions**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** Two processors will provide dependable direct-haul acceptance routes and contractors will prepay enough for repeated batches to retain contribution after contamination, transport and downgrade costs.
   - **Why it remains:** Source segregation is mandatory, and published commercial tariffs demonstrate different economics for separated and mixed loads. The first paid batch requires no yard or fleet and creates processor-specific acceptance and realized-margin data.

6. **W2-L2-006 — Stationary-battery passport custodian**

   - **Indispensable premise to close:** A responsible placing operator will authorize and pay for managed, long-lived custody above commodity software pricing because upstream-data reconciliation, commissioning capture and lifecycle updates require continuing operations.
   - **Why it remains:** Qualifying industrial batteries require passports from 18 February 2027, and written authorization of another operator is expressly permitted. Each accepted batch can convert into prepaid annual custody while building reusable model mappings and lifecycle workflows.

# Ordered reserve

1. **W2-L2-013 — Metered electric drying capacity for grain stores**  
   The loss and prevailing tonne-point price are evidenced, but viable repetition still depends jointly on site power, delivered kWh per tonne-point, seasonal utilization, grain-quality liability and fleet financing.

2. **W2-L2-008 — Employer-funded expiring appointment capacity**  
   The underlying absence burden is material and a pilot is inexpensive, but both provider wholesale supply and employer prepayment remain unobserved, alongside unresolved payroll, privacy and local-liquidity mechanics.

3. **W2-L2-014 — Custom-order merchant of record**  
   Staged payments and meaningful furniture-order values support the cash structure, but willingness to choose the entrant over a workshop, repeat purchasing, remedy costs and founder-independent delivery remain unknown.

4. **W2-L2-004 — Delegated KSeF issuance desk**  
   Delegation and mandatory issuance are established, but inexpensive ERP and gateway substitutes already cover transmission; separate willingness to pay for staffed exception handling has not been evidenced.

5. **W2-L2-010 — Solar-inverter warranty exchange merchant**  
   The installed base and replacement prices are substantial, but fast manufacturer RMA, transfer restrictions, grant conditions and uncertain recovery value materially narrow the eligible exchange event.

6. **W2-L2-012 — Owned roof-material lifting cells rented by the day**  
   The founder can acquire the asset and existing rental supply validates the category, but the modeled price requires unusually large avoided crew or alternative-rental costs, while utilization, classification and insurance remain unresolved.
