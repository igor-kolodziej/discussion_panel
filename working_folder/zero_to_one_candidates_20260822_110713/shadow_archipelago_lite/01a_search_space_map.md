- Parentage or a shared delivery primitive was not treated as sufficient for merging. In particular, D-001/X-001, D-002/X-002, D-003/D-015/X-003, D-019/D-031/X-004, D-032/X-005, and D-033/X-006 retain distinct clusters because their losses, paid triggers, transaction owners, or payer sets differ.
- The inversion pairs I-001/D-007, I-002/D-008, I-004/D-025, I-005/D-026, and I-006/D-027 remain sibling clusters because each inversion deliberately changes payer, timing, transaction unit, or acquisition route.
- D-006 and D-028 share a food-integrity direction but are not merged: one monitors supplier-product relationships, while the other gates individual production releases.
- D-001, X-001, D-011, and D-018 share a renewable-project preflight direction but represent four separately purchased stages: permit packet, installation dispatch, tender, and grid application.
- D-003, D-015, and X-003 share freight-event evidence, but retrospective invoice dispute, dock detention collection, and proactive container evidence are separately owned transactions.
- The sole merge is I-003 into CL-018 with D-009. All six required comparison fields coincide; the warranty adds bounded provider liability and calibration history without changing the underlying audit transaction. A stricter interpretation that treats liability as a separate transaction would yield 48 clusters.

## Independent anti-overmerge audit and final map

## 1. Audit findings

### DIR-001 — Renewable-project preflight

Challenge sustained. The four raw concepts should not remain in one direction:

- `D-001` is an installer-paid permit/interconnection packet transaction owned by EPC operations and acquired through installers and electrical designers.
- `D-011` is an EPC-paid tender workspace owned by bid management and acquired through distributors and tender advisers.
- `D-018` is a developer/installer-paid grid-application decision owned by the connection-application team and acquired through installer audits.
- `X-001` is an installer-paid pre-dispatch readiness transaction owned by an operations scheduler.

Their paid triggers, transaction owners, routes, and compounding assets materially differ. Preserve `DIR-001` for `D-001`; split `D-011`, `D-018`, and `X-001` into new stable directions `DIR-032`, `DIR-033`, and `DIR-034`. Existing cluster IDs remain unchanged.

### I-003 merged into CL-018 with D-009

Challenge rejected; the merge remains justified.

`I-003` and `D-009` have the same customer/loss, importer-or-shipper payer, per-invoice audit and confirmed-credit trigger, logistics-procurement/finance owner, consultant/outsourced-finance route, and contract-audit asset. The warranty adds bounded provider liability and calibration history but does not create a materially different payer, paid transaction, owner, or route. Retain `I-003` as `merged` in `CL-018`.

Corrected result: 48 raw concepts, 47 effective clusters, 34 directions, 47 retained records, and one merged record.
