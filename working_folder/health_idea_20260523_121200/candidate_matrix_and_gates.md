## Selected Finalist For Real Gate

Selected candidate: IVF Genetic Add-On Consent Rail.

Why selected: it is the only candidate with a simulated score above 90 while matching the requested genetics domain. The control point is stronger when narrowed to a signed, mandatory pre-add-on intake workflow inside one IVF clinic, plus pre-reserved clinical geneticist review capacity for escalation cases. It is not a lab affiliate, test seller, genetic diagnosis product, or generic report.

Simulated Zero To One score for exact finalist wording: 92/100.

Control point tier: B+ initially, A- only if the pilot makes the workflow mandatory for all eligible couples before non-reimbursed carrier screening or PGT decisions.

Strongest simulated caveats:

- IVF clinics may resist an unknown founder unless the first pitch is framed as doctor-time reduction, consent quality, and ethical add-on governance rather than genetics advice.
- Clinics and labs can copy forms; the defensibility must come from cross-clinic workflow memory, clinic-specific consent maps, structured family-history intake data, and reserved reviewer capacity.
- The founder must avoid crossing into genetic counseling, treatment recommendation, or IVD/medical-device software claims.
- Willingness to pay must be proven with either clinic-paid pilot fees or transparent patient-paid decision-prep fees, not verbal praise.

Profitability veto assessment: passes. Plausible gross margin above 60% if geneticist review is used only for escalations, acquisition is targeted to official provider lists, delivery starts concierge/manual, and clinics pay per routed couple or monthly for a mandatory workflow.
