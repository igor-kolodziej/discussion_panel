Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: Approved Commercial Insurance Payout Lockbox.

One-sentence thesis: acquire or collect only already-approved, non-litigated commercial insurance payout receivables where the insurer has acknowledged the payout amount or approved tranche, the business claimant signs assignment or payment direction, and the founder can collect near-term cash from the insurer instead of funding disputed claims or legal battles.

Exact buyer: Polish SMEs, landlords, fleet operators, warehouses, small manufacturers, property managers, restoration contractors, equipment repairers, and brokers dealing with commercial property, equipment, fleet, business interruption, cargo, or liability-related claims where the insurer has already approved a payout or progress-payment tranche but cash timing, paperwork, payment direction, or contractor settlement is still blocking work.

Acute trigger: a company has an insurer-approved payout, settlement letter, repair approval, loss-adjuster recommendation, or approved progress-payment tranche, but payment has not arrived or must be directed to a contractor/vendor before repairs, reopening, handover, or cash-flow recovery can proceed. The founder rejects disputed liability, underpaid-damage fights, personal-injury claims, consumer motor claims, medical claims, litigation, and cases where the insurer has not acknowledged the payable amount.

Control point: the founder controls value only when all of these are present:

- claim number, policyholder/insured identity, insurer contact, and commercial/non-consumer status;
- insurer approval, settlement letter, approved invoice, repair authorization, payout schedule, or written acknowledgement of payable amount/tranche;
- no active dispute over liability, coverage, fraud, total amount, beneficiary, mortgage/pledge/lien, sanctions, or policy exclusion;
- legal/accounting review that assignment or payment direction is allowed under the policy and claim type;
- signed assignment, collection mandate, or direct payment instruction from the claimant and, where required, contractor/vendor;
- insurer/debtor acknowledgement of payment instruction or assignment before any capital is advanced;
- bank-account verification, invoice/VAT handling, lien/mortgage/pledge clearance, and anti-fraud checks;
- written rule that the founder does not adjust claims, negotiate coverage, advise on insurance law, or finance disputed claims.

6-month proof:

1. Sign referral/payment-split terms with 3 insurance brokers, restoration contractors, commercial repair firms, loss-adjuster networks, or property managers that already see approved but unpaid claims.
2. Review 20 approved-payout opportunities.
3. Reject any case without insurer acknowledgement, clean beneficiary/payment path, assignability/payment-direction clarity, and near-term collection.
4. Sign one assignment, collection mandate, or payment-direction agreement over an approved commercial payout/tranche.
5. Obtain insurer acknowledgement of assignment/payment direction before advancing funds or doing collection work.
6. Complete one collection or directly paid contractor/vendor settlement within 60 days.
7. Prove at least 5,000-15,000 PLN gross profit/fee after legal/accounting review, referral fee, payment/admin costs, and default reserve.
8. Document time-to-cash, insurer response path, rejected-case reasons, and whether the same channel can route a second approved payout.

First acquisition mechanism:

Broker / contractor pitch:
"When a business client has an approved insurance payout or repair tranche but cash has not landed, send the settlement letter and payment path. I only take non-disputed approved commercial claims. If the insurer acknowledges assignment or payment direction, I can buy the receivable, collect for a success fee, or route direct payment to the contractor."

Claimant pitch:
"If the insurer has already approved the payout but timing or payment direction is blocking repairs or cash flow, I can offer a conditional cash-out or collection mandate. I do not handle disputes, appeals, injury claims, or underpayment fights. I proceed only after the insurer acknowledges the payable amount and payment path."

Economics:

- Target approved payout/tranche: 40,000-250,000 PLN.
- Founder advance or purchase price where used: 80-93% of acknowledged payout depending on timing, debtor clarity, and costs.
- Alternative success-fee model: 5-15% of collected cash where no advance is needed.
- Legal/accounting/admin/referral/reserve: 2,000-10,000 PLN per case.
- Target gross profit/fee: 5,000-25,000 PLN per completed case.
- Collection timing target: 15-60 days after insurer acknowledgement.
- No capital outlay without insurer/debtor acknowledgement and clean payment direction.

Payback comes from one assigned or directed approved payout, not speculative factoring. The POC fails if insurers refuse acknowledgement, assignments are blocked, approved payouts are too small, or clean non-disputed commercial cases are rare.

Copy risk:

Factoring firms, claims-management firms, lawyers, brokers, banks, repair contractors, and insurers already exist. The wedge is not broad claims finance. It is a narrow lockbox for already-approved commercial insurance payouts with debtor acknowledgement before capital exposure, sourced through brokers/contractors who see the cash-flow problem before banks or generic factors. Once the claimant signs assignment/payment direction and the insurer acknowledges the payment path, competitors cannot copy that specific payout.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup controls a named payable receivable or payment direction from an insurer. It does not sell a claims dashboard, consulting report, marketplace, broker lead list, or legal dispute service.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- No consumer claims, personal injury, medical claims, prescription/Rx, health claims, disputed claims, litigation funding, or coverage disputes.
- No claim adjustment, insurance legal advice, loss assessment, underpayment negotiation, or fraud investigation by the founder.
- Licensed insurance/legal/accounting professionals review assignment, payment direction, tax/VAT, liens, beneficiary rights, and policy restrictions.
- Insurer/debtor acknowledgement is required before any advance.
- The claimant remains responsible for truthful claim information and policy duties.
- Contractors/restoration firms remain responsible for repair quality, permits, warranties, and handover.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed and unconfirmed ideas:

- Not CarrierProof or Approved Carrier Credit Lockbox: no parcel/shipping credits or carrier claims.
- Not Warranty Retention Release Lockbox: debtor is a commercial insurer with an approved payout or tranche, not a construction customer holding retention.
- Not PromoLeak: no retailer deductions or trade-promotion accounting.
- Not DORA, KSC, AI Act, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, CBAM, EUDR, GreenTender, HeatQuiet, or HeritageDoor: no evidence desk, compliance memo, platform listing rescue, health diligence, municipal event rail, import evidence, noise dispute, or salvage asset.
