## Selected Candidate

**PFASFree FoodPack Substitute Run Lockbox**

One-sentence thesis: Food-contact packaging converters and private-label/QSR suppliers facing post-12 August 2026 PFAS order blockers will pay to reserve a qualified substitute material allocation or converter run slot, funded by buyer deposits, before a named packaging PO, reorder, onboarding, or production run is lost.

### Why It Clears Simulation

The prior PPWR evidence pack failed at 83 because the founder controlled only a packet. This version controls a commercial substitute path:

- named customer request and blocked packaging family;
- current PO, run, reorder, onboarding, or private-label approval;
- supplier allocation or converter run-slot option for a substitute material/packaging family;
- buyer deposit or converter prepayment before the founder commits nonrefundable cash;
- food-contact/packaging reviewer and lab route for no-go boundaries;
- first accepted sample path, substitute PO, customer release, or clean no-go.

The first proof is not a memo. It is a buyer-funded supply/run reservation or replacement PO path for a packaging job that would otherwise stall.

### Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof: passes only if the founder can secure signed supplier/converter options, buyer deposits, and one substitute run or accepted sample path.
- One vendor/consultant copy risk: partially passes. Material suppliers and converters can copy the idea, but cannot copy a specific optioned allocation and buyer-funded run once controlled.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: passes only for runs or POs above 250,000 PLN where a 15,000-60,000 PLN fee/spread is small.
- Vague partnerships/goodwill: passes only with signed option/allocation terms, not "we know suppliers."
- Regulated authority: passes only by keeping labs, suppliers, converters, food-contact reviewers, lawyers, and customers responsible for formal compliance decisions.

Simulated score: **88.2 / 100**

## 6-Month POC

1. Build a target list of 80 Polish/CEE food-contact packaging converters, co-packers, QSR/private-label suppliers, and packaging material reps with greaseproof, molded fiber, coated paperboard, wrap, bakery, QSR, pizza, and takeout packaging exposure.
2. Sign 3-5 substitute material or converter run-slot option agreements where the supplier/converter will reserve named material/run capacity only after buyer deposit.
3. Close 3 paid live customer/run files where a named PO, reorder, onboarding, private-label approval, or production run is blocked by PFAS evidence or substitution concerns.
4. Produce 2 accepted substitute sample paths, customer-progress outcomes, replacement POs, lab-needed no-gos, or buyer-approved switch plans.
5. Complete 1 buyer-funded substitute run, allocation, or PO release.
6. Collect 100,000-300,000 PLN while keeping gross margin above 55% after reviewer, lab coordination, supplier option costs, and contractor work.

## Kill Conditions

- Material suppliers and converters refuse option/allocation terms without large nonrefundable commitments.
- Customers will not pay deposits before lab results or full formal food-contact/legal review.
- Most cases are simple declaration evidence or lab testing, not substitute material/run bottlenecks.
- Existing packaging suppliers can solve the switch directly with no role for the founder.
- Substitute materials have poor availability, high MOQ, bad pricing, weak performance, or customer rejection.
- Reviewers/labs/legal boundaries consume the margin.
- Average run value falls below 250,000 PLN or fee/spread falls below 15,000 PLN.
- The work turns into packaging brokerage, compliance consulting, legal opinion, lab brokerage, or generic supplier search.
