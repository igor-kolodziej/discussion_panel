## Search Frame

The remaining possible upgrade is a harder control point: named AI-security reviewer capacity reserved under contract, plus prepaid partner slots tied to current buyer deadlines.

This round tests whether controlled scarce specialist capacity and prepaid live slots are strong enough to move the near-passing RAGProbe branch above the real gate. The startup is not selling generic AI governance, broad red teaming, or a dashboard. It is selling a bounded AI red-team annex slot backed by named reviewers and current partner/client payment.

## Lead Candidate

**RAGRedTeam Reviewer Slot Bank**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It addresses the main Round 152-153 objections by adding named expert credibility and formal reviewer capacity rather than asking buyers to trust an unknown founder's evidence judgment.
- The hard object is a prepaid slot backed by contracted scarce reviewer time and a live buyer deadline, not a speculative report.
- The channel buyer is already trusted by the client: appsec boutiques, SOC2 consultants, and fractional CISOs keep the relationship and route only the AI-specialist slot.
- The artifact is a bounded independent test annex with reviewer sign-off, trace package, rework/no-go findings, and scope caveats, not broad legal AI governance.
- Proof is cash/control: reviewer capacity contracts, partner slot purchases, current client authorizations, accepted annexes, repeat slots, and gross margin after reviewer costs.

### Internal Objections

- This may be viewed as a brokered expert-service capacity lane rather than a startup.
- AI-security vendors and appsec firms can copy if demand is obvious.
- Named reviewers may consume margin and limit throughput.
- The founder must avoid false assurance and must exclude high-risk AI, formal certification, legal opinions, and production-destructive agent tests.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point is materially stronger than the prior RAGProbe and RAGModule evidence-file variants.
