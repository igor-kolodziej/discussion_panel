Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
REACH Formaldehyde Furniture PO Release Lockbox

One-sentence thesis:
Polish and CEE furniture, joinery, wood-panel, fit-out, and private-label suppliers facing buyer blocks before the 6 August 2026 EU REACH formaldehyde restriction will pay for a named PO/SKU release lockbox that controls the buyer request, product-family evidence, sample chain, lab or reviewer path, and buyer-progress/no-go outcome.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE manufacturers, exporters, contract-furniture suppliers, joinery suppliers, cabinet makers, flat-pack brands, office-furniture suppliers, private-label furniture suppliers, fit-out contractors, DIY/private-label board-product suppliers, and furniture distributors with a named EU buyer, retailer, developer, hotel group, public/private framework customer, or marketplace asking for formaldehyde-emission evidence before confirming, shipping, renewing, onboarding, listing, or paying for an indoor furniture or wood-based article order.

Acute trigger:
A buyer, retailer, framework customer, developer, hotel group, marketplace, or procurement team pauses a PO, reorder, tender award, shipment schedule, framework renewal, listing, vendor onboarding, or invoice/payment step because the supplier cannot tie its current furniture or wood-based article evidence to the EU formaldehyde restriction that applies after 6 August 2026. Existing E1, CARB, TSCA, F-star, supplier declaration, panel, adhesive, coating, laminate, or chamber-test documents may exist but are scattered, outdated, not SKU-mapped, not final-article mapped, not accepted by the buyer, or do not show whether testing or a clean no-go is needed.

Control point:
Signed mandate over a named buyer request and PO/reorder/framework/shipment/SKU family; current buyer wording; commercial consequence; product-family BOM and material structure; supplier declarations and test reports; supplier RFI trail; sample identity and chain notes where testing is needed; lab or qualified reviewer route; case chronology; buyer response wording; missing-evidence/no-go list; and the right to coordinate the release path until buyer progress, lab-needed escalation, or clean stop. The startup never certifies compliance or claims that the product meets the restriction.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Five prepaid live order files, each tied to a named buyer request and at least 250,000 PLN equivalent of PO, reorder, framework, shipment, onboarding, listing, or invoice value at stake. At least three files obtain a lab, qualified reviewer, or buyer-accepted test/evidence path. At least two files produce buyer progress, accepted evidence path, accepted test path, narrowed deficiency list, replacement evidence request, payment/order progress, lab-needed no-go, or clean stop. At least one file produces a sample-chain/lab-result milestone or buyer acknowledgement that the proposed evidence/test path is acceptable before further shipment or production. Collect 70,000-180,000 PLN in fixed case fees, urgent review fees, test-path coordination fees, or buyer-funded release retainers, excluding unpaid success-fee receivables.

6-month POC:
Handle 18-35 live PO/SKU/formaldehyde release files across 10-20 exporters, manufacturers, distributors, or fit-out suppliers. Put 15,000,000-60,000,000 PLN of order, framework, shipment, listing, onboarding, or invoice value under mandate. Produce 20+ objective outcomes: buyer-accepted evidence path, accepted test path, lab result returned, narrowed deficiency list, buyer progress, shipment/order/payment progress, no-go, or stop decision. Establish 3-5 repeat channels among furniture-export advisers, contract-furniture reps, REACH/furniture labs, buyer-compliance advisers, trade associations, or QA consultants. Collect 450,000-1,100,000 PLN revenue with gross margin above 60% after reviewer, lab coordination, sample-chain, translation, contractor, and secure-intake costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with Polish/CEE furniture exporters and advisors in indoor furniture, office furniture, kitchen/bathroom cabinets, joinery, flat-pack furniture, fit-out packages, and wood-based interior products. The opening ask is: "Show one current buyer request where formaldehyde evidence is blocking a PO, reorder, framework, listing, shipment, or payment. I do not certify compliance. I map the SKU family, supplier evidence, sample/test path, missing items, and buyer response so the case becomes buyer-progress, lab-needed, or clean no-go before you ship or lose the order."

Economics:
Charge 12,000-20,000 PLN for a single-SKU or simple product-family triage, 25,000-45,000 PLN for urgent buyer-release files across multiple SKU families or a high-value framework/order, plus transparent reviewer, translation, sample-chain, and lab coordination costs where needed. Add a 1-3% release bonus only when tied to documented order/payment/listing progress and where attribution is clean. The buyer pays because a delayed or lost 250,000-2,000,000 PLN order, framework renewal, hotel/furniture package, private-label onboarding, or shipment is more expensive than a fast evidence/test/no-go path.

Copy risk:
TIC labs, REACH consultants, furniture QA teams, buyer-compliance advisers, chemical consultants, trade associations, and large furniture groups can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is transaction-specific control: signed mandate over the named buyer request, SKU/BOM evidence room, supplier RFI chronology, sample-chain route, lab/reviewer path, buyer wording, no-go discipline, and repeated accepted/deficient pattern memory by furniture family and buyer requirement.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup has the supplier mandate, current buyer request, PO/SKU file, source documents, lab or reviewer route, sample-chain chronology, and buyer response log for a named blocked order, another consultant or lab cannot copy that exact release path without the supplier moving the case and documents away from the already-paid operator. Incumbents can compete for future files, but they do not control the current buyer wording, response chronology, and paid release workflow once the startup is authorized.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live commercial order files with a named buyer request, named SKU/product family, source evidence, and commercial consequence. The output is buyer progress, buyer-accepted evidence or test path, lab/sample milestone, narrowed deficiency list, lab-needed no-go, or clean stop decision tied to a specific PO/reorder/framework/shipment/listing/payment. It does not sell generic REACH readiness, a dashboard, a supplier database, a report library, lab introductions, or broad furniture compliance consulting.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, REACH compliance opinions, formaldehyde certification, laboratory testing, product safety certification, customs advice, market-surveillance representation, buyer warranty wording, or product-design approval. Furniture manufacturers, importers, placing-on-market parties, suppliers, REACH/legal advisers, accredited labs, QA teams, and buyers remain responsible for legal obligations, test methods, final claims, declarations, product acceptance, and sale decisions. Reject cases with no named buyer request, no commercial consequence, missing BOM authority, pressure to claim compliance without evidence, unclear product identity, suspicious supplier reports, no sample identity where testing is required, medical/device/PPE/textile cases outside the target scope, disputed buyer claims, counterfeit documents, or requests to evade law.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it is not financial-sector ICT supplier evidence. It is not REDBlocked because it is not Amazon connected-device listing rescue. It is not GreenTender EPREL because it is not lighting energy-label evidence for public tenders. It is not CBAM, EUDR, PPWR/PFAS packaging, CPSC eFiling, KSeF/AP payment release, PromoLeak, Data Act, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack, Amazon Vendor Central, or a marketplace evidence desk. The buyer, trigger, controlled artifact, and proof are a named indoor furniture or wood-based article PO/SKU release file tied to the EU REACH formaldehyde restriction and a buyer-accepted evidence/test/no-go path.

Strongest anticipated objections:
TIC labs, furniture QA teams, REACH consultants, and large buyers may already handle formaldehyde evidence directly. Many suppliers may only need ordinary E1/CARB/TSCA report collection, not a paid release lockbox. Existing evidence may not map to final articles, SKU families, or EU Appendix 14 test conditions. Buyers may demand a lab result, legal opinion, or their own lab before accepting any path. Lab capacity, sample identity, chamber testing time, and reviewer availability can break urgent timelines. Missing or suspicious supplier reports cannot be fixed. The founder may lack furniture, REACH, and lab credibility without named reviewers. Gross margin can collapse if every case needs custom BOM review, supplier chasing, translations, sample handling, lab coordination, and buyer calls. The opportunity may spike around August 2026 and then normalize as suppliers update standard portfolios and buyers standardize requirements.
