# Real Working Validation: Pediatric Sleep-EEG Slot Yield Rail

## Evaluator Summary

The evaluator considered the idea a plausible narrow service wedge, but not a strong venture. It accepted that pediatric sleep EEG has real preparation friction, no-sleep risk, late-cancel risk, and low-capital POC testability. The core failure was value capture: many Polish sleep EEG slots are priced too low for the proposed per-case economics, the buyer pool narrows sharply after filtering for meaningful premium pediatric sleep EEG volume, and standby slot recovery is structurally harder than a normal last-minute appointment.

## Strongest Objections

1. The target market may be too small after filtering for private labs with meaningful pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG volume, measurable failed-slot pain, willingness to add a third-party processor, and willingness to pay instead of improving internal reminders.
2. The assumed slot economics are uneven. Some premium home, night, or long EEG slots can be worth 800-1,200+ PLN, but many in-clinic pediatric sleep EEG slots are closer to 320-520 PLN, making an 80-160 PLN routed-case fee too high.
3. Labs already have cheap substitutes: SMS reminders, WhatsApp templates, receptionist calls, mandatory prepayment, cancellation fees, failed-exam fees, better PDFs, and booking software automation.
4. The workflow touches minors and health data, which adds GDPR and trust friction even if the startup stays out of diagnosis, interpretation, and medical advice.
5. Parent questions can easily drift into clinical territory around medication, fever, seizures, sleep-deprivation details, anxiety, breastfeeding, or sleep aids.
6. The standby list is less valuable than expected because replacement families must match location, appointment type, age, sleep window, clinical requirements, preparation state, transport availability, and payment readiness.
7. Measurement would be noisy in a 30-day pilot unless clinics already track baseline failed sleep rate, no-show rate, late cancellations, rescheduled cases, technician idle time, lost revenue, and parent complaints.
8. Part-time execution conflicts with live appointment operations, parent checks, exception routing, standby matching, cancellation response, and clinic coordination.

## Useful Lesson

The evaluator recommended narrowing to premium, long, night, home/mobile, or VEEG slots worth 800+ PLN, but still scored the current idea far below gate. Do not continue health/EEG appointment-yield variants unless there is a much stronger payment-control point and a larger acute buyer pool.
