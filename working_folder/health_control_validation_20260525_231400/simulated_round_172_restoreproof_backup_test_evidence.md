## Chosen Candidate

Idea name: RestoreProof Cyber-Insurance Backup Test Evidence Bench

One-sentence thesis: A broker- and MSP-routed restore-test evidence bench for SMEs whose cyber-insurance bind, renewal, ransomware sublimit, premium, or exclusion decision is blocked because the underwriter asks for proof that backups are isolated/immutable and have actually restored.

Exact buyer: Cyber-insurance brokers, managing general agents, and MSPs serving 50-500 employee SMEs where a live underwriter, carrier, or MGA request asks for tested backup restore evidence, backup isolation/immutability evidence, recovery-test documentation, or backup-system proof before quote, bind, renewal, ransomware coverage, or premium/exclusion decision.

Acute trigger: A named cyber-insurance application, renewal, bind, subjectivity, conditional quote, ransomware sublimit, funds-transfer exclusion, premium increase, customer-contract requirement, or underwriter follow-up is stuck because the insured cannot show recent, dated, credible evidence that critical backups can be restored and are protected from ordinary ransomware compromise.

Control point: A paid broker/MSP-routed live case with the exact underwriter request, renewal deadline, insured approval, current backup platform or MSP backup service details, agreed sample restore scope, MSP/IT-owner technical contact, no-go criteria, and written permission to produce a buyer-safe restore evidence artifact. The controlled artifact is a bounded dated restore test with logs/screenshots/exception notes, not a questionnaire response.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

- Two broker or MSP routing agreements.
- At least eight prepaid live underwriter-subjectivity cases or two prepaid case blocks.
- Five completed dated restore-test evidence artifacts.
- Three underwriter/broker progress outcomes: subjectivity cleared, follow-up reduced, quote/bind/renewal advanced, ransomware exclusion reconsidered, or clean no-go accepted.
- Gross margin above 55 percent after backup/technical contractor costs.
- Rejection record for cases with no recent backup, no MSP authorization, false statement pressure, or full remediation need.

6-month POC:

- Handle 35-60 paid live cases through 5-10 brokers/MSPs.
- Generate 250,000-600,000 PLN revenue from fixed fees and prepaid case blocks.
- Document 20+ underwriter progress or clean no-go outcomes.
- Maintain sub-5-business-day turnaround for evidence-ready cases.
- Build a private library of underwriter restore-test wording, accepted artifact formats, backup-platform evidence patterns, no-go reasons, broker response language, and contractor runbooks for M365, Google Workspace, Veeam, Datto/Kaseya, Acronis, Cove/N-able, Rubrik, Cohesity, and common MSP backup stacks.

Economics:

- Narrow restore-test evidence case: 5,000-9,000 PLN.
- Multi-system or urgent renewal case: 10,000-18,000 PLN.
- Prepaid broker/MSP case block: 30,000-70,000 PLN for 5-8 cases.
- Technical contractors paid per test/witness/review.
- Brokers and MSPs pay because one stuck renewal, ransomware exclusion, premium load, or customer-contract requirement can cost far more than the fee, and because collecting restore proof under deadline is tedious, risky, and interrupts higher-value brokerage or MSP work.
- Gross margin target: 55-70 percent after reviewer/technical contractor costs and broker/MSP margin.

Copy risk:

Brokers, MSPs, backup vendors, cyber consultants, vCISOs, insurers, and GRC platforms can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-case qualification, broker/MSP white-label routing, repeat underwriter wording memory, accepted artifact formats, restore-test runbooks by backup platform, fast no-go discipline, and prepaid case blocks that assign the current renewal queue before competitors react. MSPs can run restores but are often conflicted when self-verifying their own backup service; brokers need a credible operational artifact without touching client systems.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once a broker or MSP signs a paid case block, routes a live underwriter subjectivity, obtains insured approval, and the startup schedules the restore test with the MSP/IT owner, the startup controls the immediate case workflow, evidence artifact, timestamps, exception register, and broker response package for that renewal. A competitor can build a similar service later, but cannot instantly take the already-routed paid cases or the dated restore-test artifacts in progress.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

It accepts only live cyber-insurance blockers with an exact request for tested backup/restore evidence. The output is an operational artifact created from a bounded restore test: sample scope, backup-platform evidence, restore log or screenshot extract, timestamped result, exception/no-go register, insured approval line, broker-ready response, and remediation handoff where the test fails. It does not sell broad cyber readiness, insurance advice, dashboards, policy templates, or generic questionnaire help.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- No insurance advice, legal advice, warranty of coverage, false attestation, claim handling, incident response, security certification, penetration test, full backup architecture design, or admin access without MSP/IT-owner authorization.
- Brokers remain responsible for insurance advice and carrier communication.
- MSPs, backup vendors, and insured IT owners remain responsible for systems, backups, restoration, security controls, and remediation.
- The startup coordinates or witnesses bounded tests and prepares truthful evidence artifacts approved by the insured and broker/MSP.
- Avoid patient data, raw regulated health data, and highly sensitive personal data in test samples.

Duplicate risk:

Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: no regulated-finance vendor evidence and no DORA/ICT procurement. Not CyberBind as previously tested: not broad cyber-insurance subjectivity packaging across MFA, EDR, patching, IR, and backups. This is narrower and harder-control: a live underwriter backup-restore evidence blocker, broker/MSP-routed paid case block, and an actual dated restore-test artifact. Not RAGProbe, AI security, NIS2/KSC, EAA, CBAM, REDBlocked, PromoLeak, or health/Rx/clinical.

Strongest anticipated objections:

- Brokers and MSPs may already be able to produce backup evidence themselves.
- Many clients will lack real tested backups, so cases become remediation/no-go rather than evidence generation.
- Underwriters may still decline, surcharge, exclude ransomware, or request more evidence.
- Backup-platform access is sensitive and MSPs may refuse an outside reviewer.
- The founder needs credible backup/BCDR contractors immediately.
- Evidence quality varies by platform and restore scope.
- Misstatement risk is severe if the insured wants to exaggerate recovery capability.
- The business could remain a narrow white-label service unless broker case blocks repeat and accepted underwriter artifact formats compound.
