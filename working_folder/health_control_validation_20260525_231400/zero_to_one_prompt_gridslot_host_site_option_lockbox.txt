Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: GridSlot Host-Site Option Lockbox.

One-sentence thesis: secure signed options over exact Polish commercial host sites that already have valid grid-connection conditions, grid-connection agreements, or credible documented spare connection capacity, then resell or assign the host-site project option to C&I solar, battery-storage, EV-charger, or energy-service developers only after a buyer/developer deposit is paid.

Exact buyer: Polish/CEE C&I solar installers, battery-storage developers, EV charging operators, energy-service companies, and industrial energy developers that need scarce grid-connected host sites with real connection documentation, site-owner consent, roof/land/parking rights, and enough commercial load or export/charging potential to justify fast diligence.

Source seller: commercial property owners, factories, logistics parks, retail parks, farms, warehouses, parking operators, or project holders that have valid connection conditions, a connection agreement, spare transformer/power capacity evidence, or an unused grid/project path but are not going to build before expiry, fee increase, internal budget cancellation, tenant change, or management distraction.

Acute trigger: Poland's grid capacity is scarce and connection conditions/projects can expire or become more expensive under tightening rules against unused "ghost" projects. A site owner or project holder has usable connection documentation or spare-capacity evidence but no plan, budget, or execution team. A developer wants a de-risked host-site option before competitors discover it.

Control point: the founder controls value only when all of these are present:

- exact site address, land/roof/parking/transformer area, ownership/lease authority, and decision-maker;
- valid OSD/DSO connection conditions, connection agreement, preliminary power/capacity letter, transformer capacity evidence, or other specific grid documentation reviewed by an energy lawyer/engineer;
- written site-owner option or exclusivity agreement covering the relevant roof/land/parking/equipment area, diligence access, document access, and assignment/substitution mechanics;
- confirmation that the founder is not selling grid-connection rights directly if they are non-transferable; the asset is the controlled host-site/project option and buyer diligence access;
- buyer/developer non-refundable or partly refundable deposit tied to that exact site before founder pays meaningful option consideration;
- legal review of lease/option, OSD consent/assignment limits, corporate authority, land registry/tenant issues, and tax/VAT;
- written rule that developers remain responsible for engineering, permits, grid applications, PPA/lease, energy law, construction, financing, and final OSD approvals.

6-month proof:

1. Build a source list of 100 commercial sites/project holders through energy accountants, PV installers, facility managers, property managers, industrial brokers, and failed PV/storage/EV leads.
2. Review 20 site opportunities with some connection/spare-capacity documentation.
3. Reject any site without real owner authority, valid grid documentation, assignment/lease path, buyer demand, or clear commercial use case.
4. Sign one conditional host-site/project option where founder payment is contingent on buyer/developer deposit and legal/engineering review.
5. Obtain one buyer/developer deposit of at least 10,000-30,000 PLN for that exact site option.
6. Complete one option assignment, referral/success-fee closing, or back-to-back project-option transfer.
7. Prove at least 10,000-40,000 PLN gross profit/fee after option payment, legal/engineering review, travel, documents, referral fees, and risk reserve.
8. Document whether the same source channel can produce a second site.

First acquisition mechanism:

Site-owner pitch:
"You have connection/spare-capacity documentation or an unused energy project path that may expire or never get built. I can option the site and bring a developer only if the legal, grid, and commercial path is real. You get an option payment or success fee without funding the project."

Developer pitch:
"I have a specific host site with owner option, grid-connection/spare-capacity documentation, site access, basic load/export context, and a short deadline. Deposit reserves diligence on the exact site; you remain responsible for engineering, OSD, permits, lease/PPA, and construction."

Economics:

- Site-owner option payment: 2,000-15,000 PLN initially, often conditional on developer deposit.
- Legal/engineering/document review: 3,000-12,000 PLN.
- Buyer/developer deposit: 10,000-30,000 PLN for exclusivity/diligence on a credible exact site.
- Closing/success fee or option spread: 20,000-80,000 PLN on a high-quality site, with a 10,000-40,000 PLN first-transaction gross-profit target.
- No material founder payment without valid documentation, legal/engineering review, owner authority, and developer deposit.

Payback comes from one controlled site-option transaction, not consulting. The POC fails if connection conditions cannot be legally monetized through a host-site option, if developers will not pay deposits before full diligence, or if most sites have unusable/expired grid documentation.

Copy risk:

PV installers, energy brokers, CPOs, storage developers, property brokers, and RES project developers already originate sites. The wedge is not generic lead generation. It is controlling overlooked exact sites with valid grid/spare-capacity documentation before the project expires or becomes more expensive, using signed site-owner exclusivity plus buyer deposits before capital outlay. Once the site owner signs the option and a developer deposits against that site, incumbents cannot copy that specific opportunity.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup controls a signed option/exclusivity over a named host-site/project path and a developer deposit. It does not sell a database of grid leads, site scouting report, energy audit, brokerage list, marketplace, dashboard, or consulting memo.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- The founder does not provide energy-law advice, engineering design, grid application services, construction, financing, PPA legal advice, permitting, electrical works, or guarantee of OSD approval.
- Energy lawyers and engineers review connection documents and assignment/option mechanics.
- Developers perform full technical, legal, commercial, and grid diligence before closing.
- Site owners retain responsibility for truthful documents, authority to grant site rights, tenant/landlord constraints, and building/land issues.
- The transaction is structured around host-site/project option control, not illegal resale of non-transferable grid-connection conditions.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed and unconfirmed ideas:

- Not BatteryFit Retrofit Pack: this does not qualify PV battery retrofits for installers or consumers; it controls scarce grid-connected host-site options for developers.
- Not Data Act Machine Data Liberation Desk: no machine telemetry or energy-savings memo.
- Not DORA, KSC, AI Act, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, CBAM, EUDR, GreenTender, PromoLeak, HeatQuiet, HeritageDoor, or physical-inventory arbitrage: no evidence desk, platform case, municipal event rail, import/tender packet, noise dispute, salvage lot, or generic used-equipment trade.
