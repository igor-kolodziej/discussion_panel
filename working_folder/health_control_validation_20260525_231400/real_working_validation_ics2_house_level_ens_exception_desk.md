## Evaluator's Best Version

Only accept cases with:

- partner-routed current shipment batch;
- actual missing-data, warning, rejection, or filing-readiness issue;
- shipper/importer authorization or partner authority;
- documents available;
- prepaid fee;
- no tariff-classification decision required;
