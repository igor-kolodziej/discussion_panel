## Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof exists: signed mandates, supplier acknowledgements, credit notes, refunds, and accepted offsets.
- Copy risk is meaningful, but a signed account-period file with supplier acknowledgement cannot be copied midstream.
- CAC/payback is plausible only through accountant/procurement/PE channels and hard minimum balances; small single-site cylinder accounts must be rejected.
- No vague partnership dependence if the first cases start from buyer-held statements and account authority.
- Legal/provider boundary is clean only if the startup never transports, stores, inspects, fills, sells, or certifies cylinders and never handles hazardous goods.

## Kill Conditions

- Supplier statements rarely show balances above 75,000 PLN.
- Suppliers refuse to discuss files with customer-authorised third parties.
- Most balances disappear after rental, loss, damage, contamination, return-delay, account-debt, or minimum-charge deductions.
- Customer records lack serials, delivery slips, return slips, entity authority, or deposit schedules.
- Accountants or procurement teams can resolve clean files in under two hours after seeing the checklist.
- The work requires physical cylinder handling, hazardous-goods logistics, supplier negotiation over liability, or legal collection.
