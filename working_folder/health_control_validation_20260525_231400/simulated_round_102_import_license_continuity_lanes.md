## Current Source Notes

F-gas import pressure is current and document/portal based:

- Regulation (EU) 2024/573 applies since 11 March 2024 and covers fluorinated greenhouse gases and products/equipment containing or relying on them: `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj`
- The European Commission F-gas Portal manages HFC quota, import/export licensing, and compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/573: `https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases/f-gas-portal_en`
- European Commission equipment/product guidance states that an import licence, via valid F-gas Portal registration as importer of pre-charged equipment/product, is required for customs procedures except temporary storage, and the HFC quantity threshold for quota coverage in imported products/equipment is 10 t CO2e: `https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases/stakeholder-obligations/f-gases-equipment-and-products_en`
- Regulation (EU) 2024/573 links the F-gas Portal to the EU Single Window Environment for Customs, supporting shipment-level customs checks.

This is not CBAM: the domain is F-gas import licensing/quota/portal/declaration evidence for HFCs and pre-charged equipment, not carbon-emissions certificates or authorised CBAM declarant status.

## Search Implication

This candidate is deliberately CBAM-like in structure but not in domain:

- broker-first;
- current import/shipment file;
- official portal/licence/quota/declaration evidence;
- importer remains legally responsible;
- founder produces a broker-branded continuity packet and escalation map;
- no broad climate compliance, no legal opinion, no customs declaration filing.
