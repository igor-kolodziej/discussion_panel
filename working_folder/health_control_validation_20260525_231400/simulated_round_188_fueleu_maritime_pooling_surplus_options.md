## Search Frame

Recent recovery, residual, partner-slot, appsec, and invoice-credit ideas failed because signed mandates did not create enough control against incumbent discretion or copyability. This round tests a compliance-credit style object where the law itself creates ship-specific compliance balances, a database workflow, verifier recording, and a hard annual deadline.

The 2025 reporting-year pooling deadline has passed as of 2026-05-29. Therefore this candidate is framed only as a 2026 reporting-year forward option and deposit product, with final pool recording by 30 April 2027.

Regulatory facts checked before simulation:

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 applies FuelEU Maritime to commercial ships above 5,000 GT calling at EU ports.
- Article 21 permits pooling of ship compliance balances, prevents a ship's balance from being included in more than one pool for the same reporting period, requires companies to register pooling details and allocation in the FuelEU database, and requires the selected verifier to record definitive pool composition and allocation by 30 April of the verification period.
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/394 provides database functionality for pooling and for company/verifier interaction.

## Finalist Review

### FuelEU Maritime Pooling Surplus Option Mandates

Why it advances:

- The control object is more concrete than a document pack: named ships, responsible companies, projected ship-specific compliance balances, a selected verifier, a FuelEU database workflow, and a hard statutory deadline.
- A ship's balance cannot be included in more than one pool for the same reporting period, so a signed seller-side no-double-sale option over named surplus ships can control a specific scarce compliance object before it is marketed broadly.
- The buyer pain is monetary: deficit operators can avoid penalties or a more expensive late purchase by reserving surplus early.
- First proof is cash movement, not interest: buyer deposits and seller option fees against named 2026 compliance-year surplus, plus verifier precheck.
- The founder does not need to be a verifier, legal adviser, ship manager, fuel seller, or database operator; the product is a controlled commercial option package routed through accredited verifiers and the responsible companies.

Strict objections:

- Maritime compliance, bunker, ETS, FuelEU pooling, and carbon-market incumbents are closer to shipowners than an unknown Warsaw founder.
- Projected 2026 surplus may be uncertain until verified 2027 data exists.
- The commercial option may not be enforceable or trusted enough unless drafted by maritime counsel and accepted by the responsible companies.
- The FuelEU database records only companies/verifiers, so the startup's control exists in private contracts and deposits rather than direct database ownership.
- Forward buyer deposits may be hard to win when 2026 pool prices and surplus/deficit volumes are still forming.
- The founder may be viewed as a generic broker unless exclusivity over named surplus ships and deposited buyer reservations are real.
