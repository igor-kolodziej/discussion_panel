Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: Merchant Center Suspension Release Lane.

One-sentence thesis:
Build an agency-routed Google Merchant Center suspension release desk for e-commerce merchants whose Shopping ads/free listings are stopped, controlling exclusive paid current-case flow from PPC/feed agencies, merchant admin authorization, evidence workspaces, and prepaid live suspension cases before doing any remediation.

Exact buyer:
Google Ads/PPC agencies, feed-management agencies, Shopify/WooCommerce/PrestaShop agencies, and e-commerce operators whose Google Merchant Center accounts are suspended or restricted for misrepresentation, account quality, destination mismatch, product data, policy, identity, business information, or review/appeal issues.

Acute trigger:
When Merchant Center is suspended or restricted, Shopping ads and free listings can stop or degrade. For a merchant with meaningful Google Shopping revenue, every day blocked means lost sales, wasted agency retainer value, stranded ad budget, and pressure on the PPC agency that cannot fix policy/feed/site trust issues quickly. Bad premature appeals can also create cooldowns or repeated denials.

Transferable control point:
- Exclusive or priority routing agreement with 2-4 PPC/feed agencies that manage Google Shopping spend and already receive Merchant Center suspension cases.
- Merchant authorization for named accounts, including read/write Merchant Center access, website/feed/CMS access where needed, appeal-review permission, and data-processing terms.
- Prepaid current cases: each live suspension pays an upfront diagnostic/release package before remediation begins.
- Case workspace per merchant: issue timeline, Merchant Center account-level issues, feed errors, website trust evidence, business identity, contact/shipping/returns/payment consistency, policy-page evidence, change log, appeal draft, review timing, and final outcome.
- Case-memory database of issue patterns, accepted evidence, appeal timing, platform responses, and no-go patterns, built from authorized cases.
- Payment split with agencies where the agency owns the merchant relationship but routes the case to the founder as specialist desk.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- Agency routing agreements naming the founder as the specialist desk for Merchant Center suspensions, with referral/payment split and non-circumvention.
- 8-15 merchant authorizations for live suspended/restricted accounts.
- 8-15 prepaid current cases, each with a fixed diagnostic/release fee and optional success bonus.
- Account-access and data-processing terms limiting the founder's role to evidence remediation, feed/site/account consistency, and appeal preparation.
- First 3-5 completed outcomes: reinstated, materially improved, or documented no-go with evidence.

6-month proof:
1. Recruit 20 Google Shopping/PPC/feed agencies and identify 2-4 with recurring Merchant Center suspension pain.
2. Sign exclusive or priority routing with at least 2 agencies that already manage affected merchant accounts.
3. Receive 15-30 live current cases and accept only cases with merchant authorization, meaningful blocked revenue, and a plausible remediation path.
4. Collect 40,000-120,000 PLN from prepaid diagnostics/release packages and optional success bonuses.
5. Complete 12+ cases with documented issue taxonomy, change logs, review submissions, timelines, and outcomes.
6. Prove at least 5 successful reinstatements or restriction removals, plus a clear no-go screen that prevents bad-fit cases.
7. Show gross margin above 60% after contractors, agency split, tools, legal/data terms, and account QA.
8. Show repeatability by receiving new cases from the same agencies without cold outreach.

First acquisition mechanism:
Agency pitch: "Your Google Shopping clients blame you when Merchant Center suspensions stop revenue, but these cases consume specialist policy/feed/site trust work and repeated appeal discipline. Route current suspensions to us under a prepaid specialist desk. You keep the client and ad budget relationship; we handle the evidence workspace, change log, review package, and appeal timing."

Merchant pitch through agency: "We do not guarantee Google approval or create new accounts to evade policy. We run a structured suspension release process: identify Merchant Center account issues, feed/site/business mismatches, policy-page gaps, identity and trust problems, make documented fixes, and submit a disciplined review only when the account is ready."

Economics:
- Prepaid diagnostic/release package: 3,000-8,000 PLN per live suspension depending on revenue, platform complexity, SKU volume, and number of prior denials.
- Optional success bonus: 5,000-20,000 PLN for reinstatement or material restriction removal, only where permitted by agency/customer terms.
- Agency share: 15-30% of collected fees or reciprocal case routing.
- Target first 6 months: 15-30 accepted cases, 40,000-120,000 PLN collected, 60%+ gross margin, 20,000-60,000 PLN owner contribution if case scope is bounded.
- Payback for merchant: one recovered Shopping channel can repay the fee in days or weeks for merchants with real ad-driven revenue.
- No work begins without prepaid current case and account access. No generic audit reports sold to non-suspended merchants.

Copy risk:
Generic PPC agencies, feed tools, and policy consultants exist, but most do not have concentrated case memory across live suspensions. The founder's first defensible wedge is signed current-case routing from agencies plus a growing database of issue patterns, accepted evidence, appeal timing, and no-go signals. Another consultant can copy the service description, but not the signed agency case lane, authorized current cases, and accumulated outcomes before those cases are processed.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The control point is not broad Google knowledge. It is agency-owned current suspensions, merchant authorizations, prepaid case intake, and case-memory compounding from repeated outcomes. Once an agency routes live cases and merchants authorize the desk, a competing consultant would need to displace an active specialist process during an urgent suspension rather than merely advertise a similar service.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business owns a paid current-case lane with signed agency routing, merchant authorization, account access, evidence workspaces, change logs, appeal submissions, and outcome memory. It does not sell generic audits, a SaaS dashboard, lead brokerage, or marketplace introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not guarantee reinstatement, evade Google enforcement, open replacement accounts to circumvent policy, misrepresent merchant identity, fabricate reviews/documents, or submit appeals before fixes are made. Merchants remain responsible for truthful business, product, pricing, shipping, returns, contact, and legal claims. Agencies remain ad-account managers unless separately agreed. Data-processing, password/MFA, account access, contractor confidentiality, and liability terms are documented. Cases involving counterfeit goods, fraud, illegal products, deceptive claims, controlled goods, or unresolved ownership disputes are rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk. REDBlocked was an Amazon connected-device marketplace evidence rescue desk. This candidate uses Google Merchant Center / Google Shopping, routes through PPC/feed agencies that control ad-budget relationships, and focuses on account/feed/site trust, product data, business identity, and Merchant Center review workflows. The control point is agency-owned current suspension flow plus merchant authorization, not Amazon listing evidence.

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: there is no bank-regulatory renewal evidence buyer.

This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack: there is no investor diligence or health-claim review.

This is not TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot: it is not municipal event routing.

This is not HeritageDoor Suites: it has no physical property or hospitality asset.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether agencies will route enough current cases and allow specialist ownership instead of trying to solve them internally.
- Whether Google review outcomes are too opaque or random to make outcomes repeatable.
- Whether the founder can avoid becoming a generic PPC/feed/CRO/web-development contractor.
- Whether merchants will pay upfront after already losing revenue and possibly being burned by prior suspension consultants.
- Whether case memory compounds enough to create a durable advantage, or whether public checklists/tools commoditize fixes.
- Whether the desk becomes ethically risky if merchants pressure it to hide identity, claims, or product issues.
