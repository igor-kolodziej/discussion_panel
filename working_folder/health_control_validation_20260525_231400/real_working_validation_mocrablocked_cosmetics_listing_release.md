## Zero To One Verdict

Zero To One called this one of the stronger blocked-listing release ideas because it combines a real regulatory change, urgent Amazon cash pressure, agency distribution, and a bounded SKU-specific deliverable. It accepted that the product should be sold as live Amazon / retailer / importer evidence-response execution, not broad MoCRA compliance.

It still failed because defensibility is moderate, many cases cross into regulatory judgment, and FDA/MoCRA consultants or Amazon agencies can copy simple cases. The evaluator said the idea is credible as a narrow specialist desk, but not close to a confirmed `85+` business under the founder constraints.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Many apparent MoCRA blockers are actually claims, ingredient, drug/cosmetic classification, SPF, acne, anti-dandruff, skin-lightening, GMP, lab testing, labeling, mercury, or prohibited-product-policy issues.
2. Amazon may reject even correct evidence because of screenshots, name mismatches, bundled SKUs, unclear facility identity, brand-owner mismatch, or claims that do not match the evidence.
3. FDA registration/listing is not FDA approval, and sellers may ask for certificates or assurance that do not exist.
4. Hard cases need qualified professionals for label legality, safety substantiation, adverse-event process, toxicology, color additives, fragrance allergens, or claim cleanup.
5. Agencies can internalize simple workflows once they see the checklist.
6. Case supply may be lumpy unless agencies already have repeated cosmetics blockers.
7. Seller source evidence will often be fake, incomplete, mismatched, outdated, or impossible to reconcile.
8. The founder needs a named reviewer/provider bench from day one to avoid overclaiming MoCRA expertise.

## Useful Narrowing From Validator

The best version is:

> White-label MoCRA/FDA evidence response desk for Amazon agencies handling blocked US cosmetics ASINs.

POC constraints:

- Sign three agencies before building anything else.
- Accept only live blockers with request text, ASIN/SKU, label, ingredients, facility/product-listing evidence, and seller authorization.
- Require `2,000-3,500 PLN` paid triage before deeper work.
- Reject at least 25-35% of cases to protect margin and credibility.
- Continue only if at least two agencies send five or more paid cases each within six months.

## Lesson

Agency-routed platform evidence can still be commercially credible, but this branch does not clear the confirmed-idea gate unless the founder controls something harder than a live response packet. MoCRA is more promising than generic product-compliance variants, but it remains a specialist desk with moderate defensibility and significant regulatory-boundary risk.
