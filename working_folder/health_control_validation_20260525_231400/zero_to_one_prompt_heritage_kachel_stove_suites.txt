Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
HeritageKachel Complete Tiled-Stove Suites

One-sentence thesis:
A controlled-inventory business that buys or options complete pre-war tiled-stove suites before demolition or renovation fragments them, then sells numbered, documented, project-ready stove-tile suites to premium heritage renovations through architects, stove fitters, boutique developers, hotels, and restoration firms.

Exact buyer:
Premium interior architects, heritage renovation firms, stove fitters, boutique developers, HNW apartment/villa owners, boutique hotels, premium property managers, and restoration-minded joinery or masonry firms working on Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, Gdansk, Sopot, and other Polish or CEE pre-war apartments, villas, townhouses, boutique hotels, and adaptive-reuse interiors. The first buyer wants an authentic tiled-stove focal point, either decorative or rebuilt by a qualified stove fitter, not a generic antique tile.

Acute trigger:
A pre-war apartment, villa, institutional building, hotel, or townhouse is being gutted, modernized, inherited, sold, or cleared, and a complete tiled stove is about to be demolished, split into loose tiles, damaged by crews, discarded, or sold as fragments. On the demand side, a premium renovation wants one coherent original stove suite for a reception room, dining room, library, boutique-hotel lobby, apartment salon, or heritage interior, but loose tiles and reproduction pieces do not provide the same visual continuity, patina, proportions, maker/style consistency, or documented origin.

Control point:
Written first-refusal, option, or purchase agreement over a complete tiled-stove suite before dismantling; site photos and video of the intact stove; numbered disassembly map; titled possession of original stove tiles, cornices, plinths, crowns, relief/decorative tiles, doors, vents, frames, cleanout elements, visible cast-iron parts, and recoverable trim; condition records for cracks, glaze loss, soot, missing pieces, overpaint, and previous repairs; palletized storage by numbered layer; and buyer reservation deposits for full suites rather than loose tile sales.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three written first-refusal or pickup agreements with demolition crews, estate-clearance operators, renovation firms, small developers, building administrators, antique dealers, or stove fitters; two complete tiled-stove suites physically controlled or optioned with title path, site photos, numbered disassembly maps, and condition records; one qualified stove fitter/restorer willing to inspect or receive buyer handoffs on paid terms; two buyer deposits of 3,000-10,000 PLN for full suites from architects, restorers, developers, hotel owners, or HNW project managers; one paid handoff or reservation contract; no restoration work started before buyer deposit.

6-month POC:
Control 8-15 complete tiled-stove suites; secure 8-12 buyer deposits; complete 4-8 paid handoffs; collect 120,000-500,000 PLN in raw-suite sales, reservation deposits, and restorer-handoff fees; maintain gross margin above 45% after acquisition, careful dismantling, transport, cleaning, palletized storage, photography, and breakage allowance; build a private catalog by city, period, tile color, relief pattern, count, height, width, completeness, functional/decorative suitability, missing pieces, restoration path, buyer profile, and sale outcome; get at least three repeat gatekeepers to ask for future suites before they specify reproduction.

First acquisition mechanism:
Supply side: build a list of 150 demolition crews, gut-renovation contractors, estate-clearance firms, antique dealers, stove fitters, building administrators, boutique developers, and renovation project managers in Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, Gdansk, Sopot, and heritage-heavy towns. Pitch: "Call before a complete tiled stove is broken or split. I pay fast for full intact suites, document them on site, and arrange careful removal."

Demand side: build a list of 120 interior architects, heritage renovators, stove fitters, boutique hotel designers, boutique developers, premium property managers, and HNW renovation project managers. Pitch only controlled suites: "Reserve a complete numbered original tiled-stove suite from one property, with site photos, disassembly map, condition notes, and stove-fitter handoff. Full-suite deposits only; no loose-tile sourcing."

Economics:
Acquire or option complete suites for 2,000-10,000 PLN depending on rarity, access, and seller urgency. Spend 3,000-12,000 PLN on careful dismantling, transport, cleaning, condition recording, packaging, storage, and breakage allowance. Sell raw documented suites for 15,000-45,000 PLN and rarer/restorer-ready suites for 40,000-100,000+ PLN through buyer-selected stove fitters or restoration partners. The startup captures spread by preserving the complete coherent suite before the demolition/salvage market fragments it into low-value loose tiles.

Copy risk:
Antique dealers, architectural salvage yards, stove fitters, heritage renovators, and demolition-connected restorers can copy pieces. The defense is upstream first-look density, fast cash/pickup reliability, full-suite documentation discipline, numbered disassembly maps, buyer deposits before restoration, gatekeeper memory of which colors/styles sell, and controlled inventory of exact suites before competitors can access those properties.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup signs the pickup/option, documents the intact stove, dismantles and numbers the suite, controls titled possession, and takes a full-suite buyer deposit, competitors cannot sell that exact coherent stove suite. They can source other loose tiles or reproduction stoves, but they cannot recreate the original property-level set, site provenance, condition record, and reservation contract already controlled for that buyer.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup controls titled or optioned physical suites and sells full, numbered, documented stove-tile sets with buyer deposits. It does not sell renovation advice, interior design, stove-building services, heating installation, chimney work, software, a salvage marketplace, generic sourcing, a database, or brokered introductions. Cases without full-suite control and buyer deposit discipline are rejected.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not certify heating safety, build functional stoves, approve chimneys, provide construction supervision, provide conservation-law advice, provide fire-safety advice, install heating systems, or guarantee functional rebuild. Qualified stove fitters, chimney professionals, conservation officers, architects, engineers, owners, and contractors remain responsible for functional reconstruction, chimney/fire safety, building permissions, conservation constraints, and installation decisions. The startup may sell suites for decorative-only use when functional restoration is not approved. Material with unclear title, protected heritage restrictions, unsafe removal conditions, suspected theft, or incomplete provenance is rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not HeritageDoor Suites because it does not control internal doors, frames, casings, hinges, handles, locks, or door-suite rhythm. The controlled unit is a complete original tiled-stove suite with numbered tiles and stove components, sold as a focal architectural object or stove-fitter handoff. It is not HeritageWindow Cremone Hardware because it does not sell window operating hardware. It is not HeritageRadiator because it does not sell heating radiators or plumbing-dependent cast-iron sets. It is not StormTile, hotel FF&E, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Demand may be too narrow and lumpy. Reproduction stove tiles and newly built tiled stoves are substitutes. Existing stove fitters and antique dealers may already control the best supply. Missing or cracked tiles, soot damage, glaze loss, bad prior repairs, overpaint, or careless dismantling can destroy value. Buyers may want inspection by a stove fitter before paying deposits. Functional rebuild requires chimney, fire-safety, building, and stove-fitter decisions outside the startup's role. Storage, breakage, numbering, cleaning, and transport can eat margin. Some suites may be too taste-specific, too common, too incomplete, or too hard to fit into modern interiors. The idea can drift into generic salvage unless it sells only complete property-level suites with deposits.
