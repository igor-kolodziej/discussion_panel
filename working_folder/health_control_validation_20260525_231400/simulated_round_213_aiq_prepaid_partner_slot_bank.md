## Why It Passes Internal Simulation

- The strongest prior AIQ shape failed at 82 because partner routing was not hard enough. This version requires prepaid minimum ticket capacity and non-circumvention before a partner gets the slot.
- The payment proof is cash upfront, not interest or a maybe-ticket.
- The partner channel solves direct trust/CAC; the founder is the white-label bench behind existing SOC2/fractional-CISO/privacy adviser relationships.
- The scope is bounded to ordinary embedded B2B SaaS AI buyer questionnaires, excluding high-risk AI, legal classification, clinical, HR, credit, biometric, law-enforcement, model validation, DPIA-only, and formal AI Act opinions.
- It can be part-time if slots are limited and reviewers are contracted per ticket.

## Internal Cap Check

- Concrete 6-month control proof: yes. Prepaid slot contracts plus ticket delivery and partner/buyer-progress outcomes.
- Copyable by one consultant or hire: partly, but prepaid slot ownership, partner-specific memory, and white-label urgency make it harder than direct consulting.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: credible if partners prepay and reviewer costs are bounded.
- Vague partnerships: avoided by requiring prepaid slots, minimum use, and no free routing promises.
- Regulated/legal scope: manageable only with strict exclusions and partner/lawyer/DPO ownership of final legal positions.
