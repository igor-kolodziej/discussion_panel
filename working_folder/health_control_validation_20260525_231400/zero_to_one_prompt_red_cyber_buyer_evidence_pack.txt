Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: RED Cyber Buyer Evidence Pack.

One-sentence thesis:
Provide a fixed-scope, partner-routed evidence-response pack for 20-150 person connected-device vendors whose EU B2B buyers, distributors, OEM customers, installers, retailers, or public procurement teams are blocking orders or onboarding over RED cybersecurity / EN 18031 / connected-product security evidence requests.

Exact buyer:
EU/CEE connected-product manufacturers, importers, distributors, and product vendors with 20-150 employees selling non-Amazon B2B or channel-distributed radio/connected products: industrial IoT gateways and sensors, smart building devices, EV chargers, smart locks/access control, CCTV/security equipment, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth products, connected appliances, smart meters, building automation devices, retail IoT devices, and other radio equipment where a buyer asks for cybersecurity, update, vulnerability, support-period, or RED/EN 18031 evidence before purchase, distributor onboarding, tender acceptance, OEM design-in, or framework renewal.

Channel buyer:
CE/RED labs, product-compliance consultancies, embedded-security reviewers, IoT distributors, industrial automation integrators, and B2B procurement/security consultants that already receive buyer evidence requests but do not want to assemble fast buyer-ready response packs.

Acute trigger:
Since RED cybersecurity requirements for relevant radio equipment apply from 1 August 2025, buyers and distributors can ask vendors for evidence that connected devices have appropriate cybersecurity, privacy, fraud-protection, vulnerability/contact, update, and support-process controls. A procurement or distributor gate may ask for EN 18031 mapping, Declaration of Conformity consistency, RED 3.3(d)/(e)/(f) exposure, evidence boundaries, firmware/update process, default-password/password-policy evidence, vulnerability contact, data/security statements, test/lab status, and what is safe to claim now. The vendor often has scattered technical docs, supplier PDFs, firmware notes, old CE files, cloud/app notes, support emails, and incomplete policies but no buyer-ready response.

Transferable control point:
- Live buyer, distributor, OEM, installer, retailer, tender, or procurement evidence request tied to a current order, onboarding, design-in, tender, or renewal.
- Product evidence folder: CE/DoC files, radio module info, BOM/product variant mapping, firmware/update notes, app/cloud/data-flow notes, security statements, vulnerability/support contacts, manuals, supplier documents, test/lab records, and prior buyer responses.
- Partner routing agreement with CE/RED lab, compliance consultancy, embedded-security reviewer, or distributor.
- Prepaid fixed-scope evidence pack before work begins.
- Reviewer lane: paid per-case RED/CE/product-security issue-spotting, not formal conformity assessment unless separately handled by an authorized party.
- Case memory by buyer request wording, product type, missing evidence, safe/unsafe claim, reviewer flag, buyer follow-up, accepted pack format, and lab-escalation trigger.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 2-3 partner routing agreements with CE/RED labs, product-compliance consultancies, embedded-security reviewers, IoT distributors, or industrial automation integrators.
- 3-6 live buyer evidence cases with vendor authorization and product folders.
- Prepaid pack fees for each live case.
- NDA/data-processing terms for product/security documents.
- Reviewer terms for flagged RED/cyber evidence sections.
- First 2-3 completed buyer-ready packs with documented buyer follow-up, order/onboarding progress, lab escalation, or no-go outcome.

6-month proof:
1. Build a target list of 60 CE/RED labs, product-compliance consultancies, embedded-security reviewers, IoT distributors, industrial automation integrators, and connected-device vendors in Poland/CEE/EU.
2. Sign 2-3 partner routing agreements with organizations that already see RED cyber / EN 18031 / connected-product security buyer requests.
3. Close 8-15 prepaid live buyer evidence packs at 8,000-30,000 PLN each.
4. Complete packs within 5-7 business days using existing vendor evidence plus reviewer issue-spotting.
5. Prove 100,000-300,000 PLN collected revenue, >60% gross margin after reviewers/partner share/tools, and 40,000-150,000 PLN owner contribution if scope remains fixed.
6. Show at least 5 cases where the pack leads to buyer follow-up reduction, distributor onboarding progress, tender response, OEM procurement progress, order release, lab escalation, or documented no-go.
7. Capture repeat patterns across product type, buyer wording, evidence gaps, and reviewer flags.
8. Reject any case requiring formal certification, legal opinion, pentest, conformity assessment, or open-ended engineering remediation unless separately routed to the appropriate partner.

First acquisition mechanism:
Partner pitch: "Your connected-device clients now get buyer/procurement questions about RED cyber, EN 18031, firmware updates, vulnerability contact, support periods, and safe claims. You keep testing, certification, legal, or advisory work. I take the overflow evidence-response layer: assemble existing product documents into a buyer-ready pack, flag what is unsafe to answer, draft supplier/vendor questions, and route lab/security/legal escalation back to you."

Vendor pitch through partner: "If a distributor, OEM, installer, retailer, tender, or procurement team is asking for RED cyber / connected-product security evidence, send the live request and one product family folder. We turn what you already have into a 5-7 business day response pack: answerable now, missing evidence, unsafe claim, reviewer flag, lab/security/legal escalation, and buyer-ready wording."

Economics:
- Fixed-scope request interpretation and evidence-gap memo: 4,000-8,000 PLN.
- Full buyer evidence pack for one product family / buyer request: 8,000-18,000 PLN.
- Complex multi-variant or OEM/distributor onboarding pack: 18,000-30,000 PLN.
- Partner share: 20-35% where routed by lab/compliance/security/distributor partner.
- Reviewer cost: 1,000-5,000 PLN per case depending on RED/CE/security complexity, paid only on flagged sections.
- Buyer value: protects an order, distributor onboarding, OEM design-in, framework renewal, tender, or product launch where delay can be worth far more than the pack fee.
- No free generic readiness reports. No software build before repeated paid packs.

Copy risk:
Labs, compliance consultants, cyber firms, and Big Four can copy generic RED/CRA advice. They cannot instantly copy a signed partner lane, live buyer requests, product-specific evidence folders, accepted buyer wording, reviewer-boundary memory, and product-category case patterns from the first paid packs. The first moat is not a template; it is partner-routed live procurement evidence memory.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder targets overflow work that labs/compliance/security partners do not want to staff as a fixed-scope rapid-response product. Once a partner routes a live buyer request and the vendor authorizes the product folder, another consultant would have to displace an active evidence-response process during a procurement or onboarding deadline. Each case creates reusable buyer wording, safe-claim, evidence-gap, and escalation memory.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls live buyer evidence requests and product folders routed through partners and produces fixed-scope buyer-ready response packs under strict boundaries. It does not sell generic compliance readiness, SaaS dashboards, product certification, pentesting, legal opinions, marketplace leads, or app reports.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not certify RED/CRA compliance, issue Declarations of Conformity, perform conformity assessment, provide legal advice, conduct formal pentests, make false security claims, or guarantee buyer acceptance. CE/RED labs, notified bodies, legal advisers, and security testers handle formal testing, conformity, legal, and technical remediation when needed. Vendor management remains responsible for final statements and product compliance. Cases involving unsafe products, fabricated documents, unsupported supplier claims, illegal surveillance, medical claims, or unresolved product ownership are rejected or escalated.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk. REDBlocked activates after Amazon Seller Central compliance triggers for connected baby monitors and indoor cameras through Amazon account-health/reinstatement agencies. RED Cyber Buyer Evidence Pack excludes Amazon and marketplace reinstatement entirely. It serves B2B product vendors facing buyer/distributor/OEM/procurement/tender evidence blockers, routed through CE/RED labs, compliance consultancies, embedded-security reviewers, and distributors.

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: the buyer is not a SaaS/vendor responding to a bank ICT third-party-risk questionnaire. The evidence domain is connected radio-product cybersecurity under RED/EN 18031 and adjacent CRA reporting readiness.

This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack, TraceFaktura, CBAM, EUDR, ICS2, KasaSerwis, MailDomain, or HeritageDoor Suites.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether live non-Amazon B2B buyer requests are frequent enough before CRA main obligations in 2027.
- Whether CE/RED labs and compliance consultancies already absorb this work or will route overflow.
- Whether the founder can credibly coordinate RED/cyber evidence without being a lab, lawyer, notified body, or security tester.
- Whether buyers require formal testing/certification rather than an evidence pack.
- Whether product evidence folders are too messy or supplier-controlled to produce a useful response in 5-7 business days.
- Whether this is too close to REDBlocked unless the channel and buyer are kept strictly non-marketplace.
