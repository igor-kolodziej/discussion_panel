## Selected Round 200 Candidate

**GPSR Responsible-Person Label Continuity Book Buyout**

### Thesis

Option or buy a small EU Responsible Person / authorised-representative provider book serving non-EU sellers of low-risk non-food, non-CE consumer products whose marketplace listings and packaging already name the provider. Do not sell generic GPSR readiness. The startup only proceeds where the seller provider signs an option, customers approve a successor mandate, current annual renewals/prepayments clear before closing, and product categories stay inside a strict low-risk filter.

### Control Point

- Signed option over a named retiring/overloaded RP micro-provider book.
- Customer contracts and written RP/successor mandates.
- Existing RP contact address/inbox/workflow.
- Product file custody and request log.
- Customer-approved renewal/payment-direction transfer before seller payout.
- SKU/category exclusion list to reject electronics, baby/safety products, toys, PPE, medical, cosmetics, food, supplements, batteries, e-mobility, connected devices, and other high-risk or harmonized products.

### 60-Day Proof

- One provider option signed.
- At least 20 customer-approved successor mandates.
- At least 60,000 PLN in annual renewal/prepaid continuity fees collected or escrowed before closing.
- At least 200 low-risk SKUs transferred into a controlled file index.
- First marketplace/listing or buyer continuity acknowledgement showing the successor RP details were accepted or the listing remained live after the transition.
- Insurance/legal template review completed.

### Economics

- Customer fee: 1,200-4,000 PLN/year base per seller plus SKU/file bands and authority-notice handling fees.
- Initial book: 40-80 customers, 100,000-250,000 PLN annual recurring revenue if the provider already has paid clients.
- Acquisition: low upfront option plus seller earnout tied only to retained collected renewals; target under 100,000 PLN cash at risk.
- Gross margin target: 65-75% after address/inbox operations, reviewer checks, insurance, legal-template amortization, and contractor admin.
- Cash conversion: annual prepayment before seller payout; no inventory, no fund custody, no success-fee disputes.

### Why It Might Beat Prior Failures

- Unlike Amazon evidence desks, it controls the named provider-of-record role and renewal cash, not a one-off response packet.
- Unlike generic micro-acquisitions, the current product listings/labels already point to the provider, so handoff creates urgency and switching friction.
- Unlike customs, tax, insurance, or crypto ideas, the first proof can be customer-approved continuation and prepayment without moving regulated funds or advising on legal eligibility.
- Unlike bulky inventory, there is no storage/logistics drag.

### Internal Caps Applied

- Capped below 95 because legal/product-safety liability and incumbent AR/RP providers are real.
- Capped below 92 because the first clean seller book may be scarce and customer transfer may be slower than expected.
- Capped below 90 unless the product-category filter is strict and the founder refuses categories requiring deep technical, lab, or legal judgment.
- Not capped below 87 because the control point is stronger than an evidence desk: signed provider-book option, customer-approved successor mandate, current renewal cash, file custody, and existing external listing/label dependence.
