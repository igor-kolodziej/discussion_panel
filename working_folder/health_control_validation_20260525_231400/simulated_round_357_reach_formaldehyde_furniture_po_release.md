## Current Source Check

- EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2023/1464 amends REACH Annex XVII for formaldehyde and formaldehyde releasers. It states that, after **6 August 2026**, articles cannot be placed on the market if formaldehyde released under Appendix 14 test conditions exceeds **0.062 mg/m3 for furniture and wood-based articles** or **0.080 mg/m3 for other articles**. Source: `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1464/oj/eng`
- ECHA's consolidated Annex XVII restrictions repeat the formaldehyde restriction and furniture/wood-based article limit. Source: `https://echa.europa.eu/documents/10162/1046d94e-f527-0bbb-7435-ad9eb040bea7`
