Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Please cap the score below 85 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, assigned, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Cap below 87 if CAC, payback, gross margin, cash conversion, or owner earnings are not credible. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality. Penalize generic clinics, apps, reports, dashboards, marketplaces, coaching, patient-data businesses, and prescription-drug businesses without asset/payment-flow control.

Scored idea:

Adult Diabetes Group R NFZ Realizator Continuity Buyout options or acquires a controlling stake in a retiring/closing micro-company that already has an NFZ contract for zaopatrzenie w wyroby medyczne, already realizes adult diabetes Group R CGM/FGM/pump-consumable orders, and already has eZWM access, supplier accounts, order history, settlement routines, phone/domain/mailbox, and active monthly patient reorder flow.

The founder does not prescribe, issue e-zlecenia, diagnose, provide clinical product advice, advertise medicines, sell prescription medicines, or claim clinical-provider status. The acquired/optioned realizator entity remains the regulated transaction party for reimbursed medical devices. Authorized clinicians issue e-zlecenia. The founder buys continuity of the existing realizator/order-flow entity, not a generic patient lead list.

Buyer / payer:
- Adult insulin/CGM/FGM/pump users, especially 50-60+ patients and caregivers, who need monthly reimbursed device continuity.
- The acquired NFZ-contracted medical-device realizator entity, whose owner wants succession/earnout instead of closure.
- Potential later acquirers: larger medical-device shops, diabetes distributors, or pharmacy/medical-device chains that value a retained recurring Group R patient book plus settlement history.

Acute trigger:
A small diabetes-device shop/realizator owner is retiring, closing, or unable to digitize monthly recurring fulfillment. If the entity closes, patients must re-route e-zlecenia, stock continuity breaks, phone trust disappears, and recurring NFZ/co-pay transaction flow is lost.

Control point:
The asset is not a partner agreement. It is direct continuity control over the existing contracted realizator entity and its transaction flow:
- signed option/SPA over a controlling stake or asset-plus-entity continuity structure reviewed by counsel;
- existing NFZ zaopatrzenie w wyroby medyczne agreement, valid place of service, and eZWM access under the entity;
- phone/domain/mailbox/Google profile/call routing and old-owner handoff;
- active adult Group R patient reorder book with recent monthly order history;
- exact SKU/device/reorder history and supplier accounts/terms;
- starter inventory and serial/batch records where needed;
- bank/settlement workflow, accountant files, NFZ settlement reports, and receivables visibility;
- existing staff/owner transitional service agreement so regulated routines are not interrupted;
- non-compete/non-solicit around the acquired book where lawful;
- first post-option eZWM-pulled orders and settlement evidence under the controlled entity.

The hard control is: controlled contracted entity + active eZWM order realization + settlement/payment flow + old-owner handoff.

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 5,000-15,000 PLN refundable/creditable option fee for 60-90 day exclusivity over a micro realizator entity;
- 10,000-20,000 PLN legal/NFZ/GDPR/accounting diligence, including whether share purchase/control change, management agreement, or asset transfer is the clean route;
- 10,000-25,000 PLN starter inventory / supplier deposits only for already-active adult Group R SKUs;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN CRM, phone/mailbox continuity, order calendar, access logging, and reconciliation;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN owner transitional services and staff/operator training;
- no large closing cash until pulled orders, fulfilled orders, settlement proof, and contribution margin are proven.

Required proof:
1. Signed exclusivity/option over one NFZ-contracted medical-device realizator entity with adult Group R order history.
2. Legal memo confirming the proposed control route does not break NFZ/eZWM/place-of-service obligations.
3. Owner handoff of phone/domain/mailbox/Google profile and active reorder calendar.
4. 75 active adult Group R patients verified by recent order history, not raw contacts.
5. 40 patient/caregiver continuity authorizations or re-confirmations with GDPR-compliant language.
6. 35 actual eZWM-pulled adult Group R orders under the controlled/optioned entity during the POC.
7. 30 fulfilled/delivered orders and first post-option settlement/accounting evidence.
8. Contribution margin above 30% after product cost, courier, returns, call handling, owner earnout, stock buffer, and settlement timing.
9. No prescription medicine sale/advertising, no clinical advice, no false patient lock-in.

Economics:
Revenue comes from retained gross margin on recurring reimbursed Group R medical-device orders, lawful patient co-pay/top-up where applicable, and compliant non-Rx accessories only when routed as product fulfillment rather than clinical advice.

Payback logic:
The founder avoids buying a generic shop upfront. The founder options a micro contracted entity, proves 30+ fulfilled monthly orders and first settlement evidence, then pays a low fixed closing amount plus seller earnout from retained gross margin. The first 75-150 active recurring adult diabetes-device users should show whether owner earnings can exceed a small operator salary with low paid CAC.

Why this is not a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace:
This is a controlled regulated transaction-flow acquisition. It does not sell advice, content, coaching, dashboards, or prescription medicines. A competitor cannot copy the specific book without acquiring the same entity, phone/domain trust, eZWM workflow, supplier history, and old-owner handoff.

Copy risk:
Large online medical-device shops and pharmacies can compete for future patient choice. The wedge is narrower: control of a specific live entity/book, current-month eZWM execution, phone trust, exact SKU continuity, and settlement/payment history. Copy risk remains high if only contacts transfer. It is materially lower only if active monthly order flow continues under the controlled entity.

Regulatory and data guardrails:
- Authorized clinicians issue e-zlecenia; founder does not.
- Controlled/acquired NFZ-contracted realizator remains the regulated transaction party for reimbursed medical devices.
- No prescription medicine sale, advertising, reservation, delivery, or clinical advice.
- No false lock-in: patient choice remains clear.
- GDPR/health-data handling around PESEL/order/device data must be reviewed.
- Mail-order realization only where legally permitted for the exact product and executed from the scheduled/contracted place of service.
- Product support questions go to clinicians, manufacturers, or the contracted/acquired realizator's proper support channel, not the founder personally.

Main risks acknowledged:
- NFZ contract/control-change transferability or notification requirements may kill the deal.
- Share/entity acquisition brings inherited liabilities.
- The seller may not have enough active adult Group R patients or clean records.
- Patients retain choice and can switch.
- Working capital and supplier spread may compress margins.
- GDPR/health-data handling is serious.
- Product support must not drift into medical advice.
