## Strongest Objections

1. Brokers and MSPs may already do this with screenshots, exports, backup evidence, EDR reports, MFA scope notes, and renewal support.
2. Many cases require implementation, not packaging.
3. Misstatements in insurance applications or underwriter responses can create severe coverage, legal, and trust risk.
4. SME willingness to pay splits sharply; low-premium SMEs may reject 5,000-25,000 PLN fees.
5. Underwriters may still decline, surcharge, exclude, or ask more questions.
6. Evidence collection can become messy across MSP exports, admin-console screenshots, backup logs, vulnerability reports, EDR coverage, MFA exceptions, and training records.
7. Trust is a real barrier because the startup sees sensitive security gaps, admin screenshots, endpoint coverage, backup architecture, privileged access, and scan findings.
8. Insurers, MGAs, brokers, and underwriting platforms may standardize intake.
9. Broker/MSP economics can compress because brokers want margin and MSPs may prefer to sell remediation.
10. The business can drift into generic cyber consulting if it accepts broad readiness, remediation, audit, or vCISO work.

## Evaluator's Best Beachhead

Cyber-insurance brokers and MSPs serving 100-500 employee SMEs.

Best cases:

- live underwriter subjectivity;
- bind or renewal deadline within 30 days;
- specific controls requested;

Avoid:

- missing core controls;
- no MSP/IT owner;
- low-premium micro-SME cases;
- claims disputes;
- breach or incident response;
- clients asking the startup to make weak controls sound compliant.

## Lesson

This branch is good but likely capped below the current gate. Broker-routed evidence with a live commercial deadline is not enough if natural channel partners can internalize the work and many cases require actual implementation. Do not retest cyber-insurance variants unless the control point becomes an assigned payment stream or a broker-owned live case book with prepaid repeats that materially changes defensibility.
