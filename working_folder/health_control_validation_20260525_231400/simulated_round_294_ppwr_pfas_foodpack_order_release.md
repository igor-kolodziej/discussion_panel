## Current Fact Check

Official EU sources confirm that Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste entered into force on 11 February 2025 and generally applies from 12 August 2026. EUR-Lex Article 5(5) states that from 12 August 2026 food-contact packaging shall not be placed on the market if PFAS concentrations are at or above the listed thresholds, unless another Union act already prohibits the packaging. The Commission packaging-waste page also flags restrictions on PFAS in food-contact packaging from the same application window.

## Selected Candidate

**PPWR PFAS FoodPack Order Release Pack**

### Buyer

Polish/CEE food-contact packaging converters, importers, co-packers, private-label food suppliers, and food brands with live EU customer requests around packaging placed on the market after 12 August 2026.

Best first cases involve grease-resistant or contact-sensitive packaging where customers are plausibly worried about PFAS: bakery papers, wraps, molded fiber bowls/trays, pizza/QSR packaging, paperboard, food-service board, cup sleeves/lids, takeout packaging, and private-label food-contact packs.

### Acute Trigger

A named retailer, brand owner, QSR customer, co-packer, or distributor pauses or conditions a Q3 2026 purchase order, packaging run, reorder, item onboarding, private-label approval, or supplier declaration because it needs PPWR/PFAS evidence before accepting food-contact packaging after the August application date.

### Transferable Control Point

The startup controls the exact commercial release file:

- customer request;
- PO/run/reorder at risk;
- packaging SKU and BOM;
- material supplier declarations;
- existing PFAS/non-intentional-use evidence;
- relevant FCM declarations;
- available test reports or lab sample path;
- supplier RFI;
- no-go/substitute branch if evidence is not supportable;
- customer-facing response matrix and status chronology.

The startup does not certify PPWR compliance, run chemical testing, issue food-contact opinions, or give legal advice. FCM/packaging reviewer and lab contacts remain named external reviewers/providers where needed.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid live order-release cases from at least two companies.
2. Every case includes a named customer request, SKU/packaging family, PO/run/reorder or onboarding consequence, and decision-maker.
3. One food-contact/packaging compliance reviewer on paid per-case terms.
4. One lab route for cases requiring confirmatory testing, with the lab remaining provider of record.
5. At least two customer-progress outcomes: order/run released, customer narrows follow-up, evidence accepted for a defined batch/date, substitute path chosen, or clean no-go accepted before production spend.
6. 35,000-90,000 PLN collected in fixed fees.
7. Reject cases without customer pressure, BOM access, supplier cooperation, or authority to stop unsupported claims.

### Economics

Charge:

- 8,000-14,000 PLN for a 5-business-day triage and customer response matrix;
- 15,000-28,000 PLN for a 10-business-day order-release pack with supplier RFI, reviewer check, and lab-route coordination;
- 30,000+ PLN only for multi-SKU private-label or QSR batches.

The buyer pays because a 10,000-25,000 PLN release/no-go file is small compared with a paused packaging run, lost private-label order, retailer onboarding delay, or scrapped nonconforming inventory.

### Copy Risk

Packaging consultants, FCM lawyers, labs, material suppliers, and larger converters can copy the category. The case-level defense is timing and control of the exact customer request, BOM, supplier evidence, reviewer notes, and response chronology. The longer-term asset is accepted wording, recurring material-risk patterns, supplier responsiveness, lab turnaround memory, no-go triggers, and buyer-request variants by packaging type.

### Why This Is Not A Service/Report/App/Broker

The proof is not a generic compliance report or readiness checklist. It is a prepaid commercial order-release packet for a named customer request and packaging run, with explicit authority to say "cannot support this claim; substitute or test before shipment." The output changes a live commercial decision: proceed, proceed with limited evidence, test, substitute, or stop.

### Duplicate-Risk Distinction

This is not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs: the buyer, statute, artifact, and trigger are different. GreenTender serves lighting distributors in public tenders through EPREL/energy-label evidence. This serves food-contact packaging suppliers facing PPWR PFAS order/run release before an August 2026 placing-on-market restriction.

This is not REDBlocked, DORA, CBAM, Data Act, BioSignal, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, or TraceFaktura. It is also not a marketplace listing rescue, buyer questionnaire sprint, recovery mandate, or physical lot.
