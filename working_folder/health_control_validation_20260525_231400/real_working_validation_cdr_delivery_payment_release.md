## Validator Summary

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Incumbents already sit in the workflow and are likely the first call for blocked retirements, invoices, or payment release.
2. Missing evidence may be impossible to create; honest outcomes will often be paid no-go or supplier rework.
3. A delivery packet can be mistaken for assurance, project rating, or corporate claim approval.
4. Case flow may be sparse because durable CDR issuance volumes are still small relative to long-term purchase commitments.
5. Corporate buyers may not trust an unknown Warsaw solo founder with contracts, invoices, registry serials, lab data, MRV reports, and claim-sensitive documents.
6. Payment-release attribution is messy because suppliers, registries, finance teams, or existing advisers may solve the issue independently.
7. CDR pathways differ too much across biochar, DAC, enhanced weathering, mineralization, bio-oil, and BECCS.
8. Suppliers may resist sharing lab data, feedstock records, chain-of-custody proof, registry evidence, and audit details.
9. Carbon markets are reputationally fragile, so buyers will avoid anything that looks like a lightweight approval stamp.
10. The six-month POC is too optimistic without one strong reseller or marketplace channel.

## Useful Narrowing From Validator

Best first niche:

**CDR resellers or marketplaces with repeated delivery exceptions where registry serials, retirement requests, delivery statements, invoices, and buyer evidence requirements do not line up.**

Accept only cases with:

- named tonnes, serials, project, or batch;
- signed mandate;
- invoice, payment, or retirement blocker;
- source-document owners identified;
- buyer acceptance criteria in writing;
- no assurance or claims-advice expectation;
- clear no-go authority.

The evaluator suggested a realistic POC of 2-3 channel partners, 3-6 paid cases, 50,000-180,000 PLN revenue, 2 release/payment/retirement outcomes, 2 no-go/rework outcomes, and one repeat reseller batch. Even then, this remained far below gate.

## Lesson

Do not continue carbon/CDR delivery, claim-risk, registry, or payment-release variants in this run. Current cash plus a signed mandate still fails where the market is small, trust-heavy, global, and already controlled by specialist incumbents.
