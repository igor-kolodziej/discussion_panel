## Current Timing Notes

Polish grid-connection capacity is a current bottleneck. Public and legal sources describe insufficient grid capacity, connection refusals, "ghost" projects holding capacity, and 2026 Energy Law changes intended to reduce speculation and shorten the validity of connection conditions. The useful candidate must not sell connection paperwork or legal advice. It must control a real host-site/project option with valid conditions and a buyer/developer deposit.
