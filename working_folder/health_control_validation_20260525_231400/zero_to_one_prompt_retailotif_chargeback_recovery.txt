Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
RetailOTIF Chargeback Recovery Mandate.

One-sentence thesis:
EU/CEE CPG, beauty, household, food, and DIY vendors will pay a success-linked recovery desk for non-promo retailer operational deductions when cash has already been short-paid for ASN, appointment, pallet, label, routing, shortage, delivery-window, or OTIF chargebacks, because the proof is buried across retailer portals, EDI logs, warehouse records, carrier documents, and remittance statements.

Exact buyer:
The buyer is a Polish/CEE mid-market vendor selling recurring B2B goods to large retailers, discounters, drugstores, DIY chains, supermarkets, marketplaces, or distributor-retail hybrids. Categories include beauty, personal care, household, FMCG, packaged food, pet, DIY, home goods, and private-label manufacturing. The economic buyer is the CFO, finance controller, supply-chain lead, key account director, or owner whose receivables are being short-paid by retailer operational chargebacks.

Acute trigger:
The retailer has already deducted or short-paid invoices for operational reasons: ASN mismatch, EDI error, missing/desynced despatch advice, late/early delivery, delivery-window miss, DC appointment issue, pallet format, carton label, routing-guide violation, shortage claim, POD mismatch, BOL/CMR issue, carton/pallet count discrepancy, incorrect packaging, missing COA/document, or OTIF scorecard penalty. The desk rejects promotional deductions, trade spend, rebates, MDF, pricing disputes, retailer media, general invoice disputes, and broad finance cleanup.

Control point:
The startup controls the live deduction queue: signed recovery mandate, retailer portal/export access where allowed, remittance statement, debit memo/chargeback register, invoices, purchase orders, EDI/ASN logs, warehouse picking/packing records, pallet/carton labels, appointment logs, POD/BOL/CMR, carrier or 3PL records, delivery confirmation, dispute-window deadlines, evidence packet, retailer case IDs, reversal tracker, win/loss reason table, and success-fee right tied only to actual reversed credits or recovered cash.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign at least five recovery mandates with vendors that each have at least 50,000 PLN in candidate non-promo operational deductions within open or recently closed dispute windows. File at least 50 evidence-backed retailer disputes across the mandates. Produce at least one accepted reversal, credit memo, payment, deduction cancellation, or written retailer acknowledgement, and issue the first success-fee invoice or collect the first recovery fee. Reject any mandate where usable PO/ASN/POD/warehouse/carrier evidence is missing.

6-month POC:
Close 20-40 vendor mandates, process 1,000,000-4,000,000 PLN of candidate operational deductions, recover or reverse at least 300,000-1,200,000 PLN, collect 80,000-300,000 PLN in fees, and maintain at least 60% gross margin after data cleanup and reviewer/operations costs. At least five vendors must send a second deduction batch, and at least three 3PLs, EDI providers, or retail finance advisers must refer cases. Track win/loss by retailer, chargeback code, evidence type, dispute age, and root cause.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with mid-market vendors selling to retailers known for operational deductions, plus 3PLs, retail EDI providers, CPG fractional CFOs, and sales-finance consultants. The outreach line is: “If your retailer has already deducted cash for non-promo delivery, ASN, pallet, label, shortage, or OTIF chargebacks, I will recover only evidence-backed claims and charge only from reversed credits or recovered cash plus a small setup fee for messy exports.” Use 3PL and EDI partners to access evidence quickly without becoming a logistics consultant.

Economics:
Charge a 2,000-8,000 PLN setup fee only for messy exports or minimum-case setup, plus 15-30% of recovered or reversed cash. Do not charge for a leakage report alone. One 100,000 PLN deduction queue with 25,000-50,000 PLN recoverable can justify the first engagement. Founder work is deduction classification, evidence matching, retailer case filing, dispute tracking, and win/loss learning, not warehousing, transportation, accounting, legal claims, or supply-chain consulting.

Copy risk:
AP-audit firms, retailer deduction specialists, SupplyPike-like tools, EDI providers, 3PLs, freight auditors, retailer consultants, brand finance teams, and key account teams can copy the workflow. The defense is a narrow CEE retailer operating memory: chargeback-code mapping, evidence packets by retailer, dispute-window discipline, accepted proof patterns, 3PL/EDI evidence routes, and signed current deduction queues with success-fee rights.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the vendor signs the recovery mandate, grants access to the deduction register and evidence sources, and the startup files current disputes with retailer case IDs, competitors cannot control that exact claim queue, evidence packet, or success-fee right before the current dispute window unless the vendor terminates the mandate. Incumbents can compete for future retailer deduction work, but not the specific filed claims already under paid control.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It does not sell a deduction report, dashboard, benchmarking database, consulting retainer, marketplace, audit template, or brokered introduction. It accepts only already-deducted non-promo operational chargebacks and produces a specific cash artifact: filed evidence-backed disputes, retailer case IDs, accepted reversals, credits, payments, cancellation notices, no-go reasons, and recovery-fee invoices tied to actual cash or credit.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No legal advice, no false evidence, no alteration of delivery records, no general debt collection, no retailer relationship negotiation, no promotion/rebate/media disputes, no accounting opinion, no transportation operations, no guaranteed recovery, and no root-cause remediation unless separately routed to qualified operators. The vendor, 3PL, carrier, EDI provider, accountant, lawyer, and retailer remain responsible for official records, truthful submissions, transport facts, contracts, accounting, and commercial relationship decisions.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from PromoLeak because it excludes promotions, rebates, MDF, retailer media, pricing leakage, trade spend, and commercial deduction analysis. The trigger is non-promo operational chargebacks for delivery, ASN, pallet, label, appointment, routing, shortage, or OTIF issues, and the proof is a retailer case reversal or credit tied to logistics/EDI evidence. It is distinct from Amazon Vendor Central recovery because it focuses on non-Amazon retail and CEE operational chargeback queues. It is distinct from DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, GreenTender EPREL, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, and health/Rx/clinical routes.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Retail operational deduction recovery is crowded and may be too close to AP-audit or PromoLeak. Many deductions are valid because the vendor, warehouse, carrier, EDI provider, or 3PL actually made the error. Retailer portals and dispute outcomes are opaque and outside founder control. Evidence may be missing, late, or controlled by a 3PL that has little incentive to help. Vendors may internalize the checklist after one recovery. Retailer relationships are sensitive, and key account teams may resist aggressive disputes. The idea only works if the first proof is current signed mandates, usable evidence, filed disputes, first reversed cash or credits, and repeatable win/loss memory by retailer and deduction type.
