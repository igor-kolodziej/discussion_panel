Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

KSeF Payable-Acknowledged Funding Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:

Polish suppliers now have some invoices that buyers already acknowledge as payable, but payment scheduling or receivable funding is still blocked by KSeF number, UPO, FA(3), correction, QR/visualization, access, PO, GRN, WZ, or buyer-AP matching evidence; the startup accepts only these payable-acknowledged cases and converts the final KSeF/AP blocker into scheduled payment, factor funding, reserve release, accepted correction, or clean no-go.

Exact buyer:

Polish SME and mid-market B2B suppliers with invoices above 100,000 PLN to large domestic buyers, public-sector entities, manufacturing groups, distributors, retail chains, construction groups, logistics firms, and shared-service centers. Secondary channel buyers are factoring brokers, invoice-finance providers, outsourced CFOs, and accounting offices whose clients have already delivered goods or services and have written buyer AP/procurement/receiving acknowledgement that the invoice or delivery is payable, but payment-run inclusion or invoice financing is blocked by a KSeF/AP evidence defect.

Acute trigger:

The supplier has written buyer AP, procurement, receiving, or treasury text saying the invoice, delivery, milestone, or receivable is accepted/payable in substance, but cannot be paid, scheduled, confirmed to a factor, financed, or released from reserve until the KSeF number, UPO/acceptance evidence, QR/visualization, two-step access link, FA(3)-compatible correction, buyer NIP, PO/order/GRN/WZ mapping, receipt tie-out, attachment/reference handling, or correction route matches the buyer's AP process.

Control point:

Signed payable-invoice release mandate; exact invoice and receivable value; buyer AP/procurement/receiving acknowledgement that the invoice or underlying delivery is payable subject to KSeF/AP evidence completion; factor or finance-provider hold notice where relevant; KSeF number, UPO, XML, rejection/error log, missing-KSeF diagnosis, QR/visualization/access evidence, or correction history; PO/order/GRN/WZ/delivery references; seller accountant or invoicing-system contact; buyer AP contact; seller-approved response path; and a release ledger showing AP acceptance, payment-run inclusion, scheduled payment, actual payment, factor advance, reserve release, accepted correction, or written no-go.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Eight screened cases and four prepaid mandates from at least three suppliers or two factoring/accounting channels. Every accepted case must have invoice value above 100,000 PLN, written buyer acknowledgement that the invoice or delivery is payable in substance, and a documented KSeF/AP evidence condition blocking payment scheduling or financing. At least two cases produce buyer AP acceptance, payment-run inclusion, scheduled payment, actual payment, factor funding, reserve release, accepted correction, or written no-go. First 40,000-90,000 PLN collected. No case is accepted without seller accountant involvement when tax judgment, correction legality, VAT treatment, or invoice cancellation/reissue is required.

6-month POC:

Screen 70-120 payable-acknowledged invoice blockers, accept 25-45 paid cases, release or no-go 3,000,000-10,000,000 PLN of invoice/funding value, collect 220,000-550,000 PLN in fees, document 18+ AP acceptance/payment/funding/correction/no-go outcomes, secure at least two repeat referral channels from factoring brokers, accounting offices, outsourced CFOs, or receivables-finance providers, keep gross margin above 65%, and reject at least 40% of cases where the buyer acknowledgement is weak, the real blocker is delivery dispute, price dispute, buyer cash delay, tax/legal uncertainty, commercial setoff, fraud, insolvency, or ordinary debt collection.

First acquisition mechanism:

Start with factoring brokers, invoice-finance providers, outsourced CFOs, accounting offices, and Polish suppliers that sell repeatedly to large domestic buyers. The outreach asks for one current invoice above 100,000 PLN where the buyer already acknowledged the invoice or delivery as payable but will not schedule payment, confirm the receivable to a factor, include it in a payment run, or release funding until KSeF number, UPO, FA(3), QR/visualization, PO/GRN/WZ, receipt, correction, or access evidence is fixed. The first channel pitch is: send only payable-acknowledged KSeF blockers that are too small and urgent for your senior people but large enough to justify a fixed fee.

Economics:

Charge 7,000-14,000 PLN prepaid for a single payable-acknowledged invoice release file, 15,000-25,000 PLN for urgent multi-document cases or finance-provider release cases, and 0.5-1.0% of released, newly scheduled, funded, or reserve-released value where attribution is clear. Fixed fees dominate because payment timing attribution can be disputed. No financing, factoring, invoice purchase, tax advisory, ERP implementation, debt collection, or litigation is required.

Copy risk:

Accounting offices, ERP vendors, KSeF software providers, outsourced CFOs, factoring brokers, factors, AP automation vendors, debt collectors, and buyer AP teams can copy pieces. The narrow defense is control over the live payable-acknowledged invoice case: buyer acknowledgement, KSeF evidence, correction history, factor/funding blocker, seller mandate, accountant contact, buyer AP path, and release ledger. The longer-term defense is a private library of buyer-specific accepted KSeF evidence patterns, factor confirmation requirements, correction routes, payment-run conditions, no-go causes, and channel trust.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

The startup does not own KSeF expertise or factoring relationships generally. It controls a specific payable-acknowledged invoice release case after mandate: invoice value, buyer acknowledgement, KSeF/AP blocker, seller finance authority, accountant-approved correction path, factor or funding hold where relevant, buyer AP contact thread, and fee right. Accountants, ERP vendors, and factors can solve broad KSeF or financing workflows, but they do not automatically control the supplier's urgent payment-release file once this mandate and response path are active.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The product is a controlled payable-invoice release case. It starts only when a named invoice above 100,000 PLN has written buyer acknowledgement and a specific KSeF/AP condition blocking payment scheduling or financing. The output is AP acceptance, payment-run inclusion, scheduled payment, actual payment, factor advance, reserve release, accepted correction, or written no-go. It is not KSeF readiness consulting, tax advisory, ERP support, AP chasing, debt collection, factoring brokerage, a dashboard, or a marketplace for accountants.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

The startup does not provide tax advice, legal advice, VAT opinions, accounting services, debt collection, financing, factoring, invoice purchase, ERP implementation, or official certification of KSeF compliance. The seller remains issuer of record and is responsible for invoice truth, tax treatment, corrections, customer communication, and payment terms. The seller's accountant, tax adviser, ERP vendor, legal counsel, factor, or buyer AP team approves correction legality, VAT treatment, financing terms, and any disputed contractual issue. Cases involving fraud, fake delivery, disputed quantity, disputed price, insolvency, sanctions, litigation, tax avoidance, or buyer nonpayment unrelated to KSeF/AP acceptance are rejected or routed out.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

This is not TraceFaktura because TraceFaktura concerned municipal animal-care all-event invoice evidence and a lower-gate payment routing rail. This is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retailer promotion deductions. It is not generic KSeF support because cases require written buyer acknowledgement that the invoice or delivery is payable in substance and a specific KSeF/AP blocker preventing payment scheduling or receivable funding. It is not DORA, CBAM, Data Act, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, GreenTender, Supplement Stack Safety, or an evidence-only compliance desk.

Strongest anticipated objections:

Clean payable-acknowledged cases may be rare because competent suppliers, accountants, and factors should already resolve them. Buyer acknowledgement may still hide PO mismatch, delivery dispute, budget approval, setoff, tax issue, or cash delay. Accountants, ERP vendors, KSeF providers, and factoring brokers are natural incumbents. Factors and buyers may ignore a third-party coordinator unless the supplier works through its official finance channel. The strongest pain may be temporary during the 2026 KSeF transition. Success-fee attribution can be disputed. The offer can decay into generic AP chasing unless intake stays strict.
