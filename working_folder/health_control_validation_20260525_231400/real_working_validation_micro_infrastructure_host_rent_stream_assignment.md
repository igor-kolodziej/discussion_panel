## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator accepted that the asset class is real: small installed-machine rent streams can be awkward, under-monetized, and too small for institutional finance. It also accepted that payer acknowledgement before capital deployment is the right proof object.

The idea failed because each stream is tied to a specific site contract, operator, uptime/access obligations, property ownership, termination rights, assignment language, and payment process. The evaluator saw this becoming bespoke contract cleanup or micro-factoring rather than a scalable rent-stream bank.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Operators may refuse assignment, require novation, pay only the contracting owner, prohibit bank-direction changes, or tie payments to continuing host obligations.
2. Host payments are not always passive; landlords may still need to maintain access, electricity, servicing, insurance, signage, complaint handling, and site condition.
3. Many streams are too small; 1,000 PLN/month does not leave enough absolute gross profit after contract review, KYC, operator acknowledgement, monitoring, counsel, and failed files.
4. Owners may not sell at the discount required for attractive economics unless they have a real trigger such as property sale, closure, inherited contract, accounting cleanup, or quick-cash need.
5. Revenue-share streams are risky because they depend on footfall, uptime, reporting, pricing, maintenance, and operator discretion.
6. Legal structure can drift into regulated lending, factoring, debt collection, payment services, investment intermediation, or securities offerings.
7. Payer acknowledgement may take weeks and break the 60-day proof.
8. Acquisition does not scale cleanly because visible machines do not reveal owner identity, rent amount, assignment clause, payment history, termination rights, or willingness to sell.

## Evaluator's Best Narrowing

The evaluator said the best version would be:

**Operator-Acknowledged Host-Rent Lockbox Desk**

Only accept:

- fixed rent, not variable commission;
- minimum 2,000 PLN/month for buyout candidates;
- clean 6-12 month payment history;
- clear owner authority;
- written operator contract;
- no maintenance or site dispute;
- operator acknowledgement before capital commitment;
- payments only to host account, regulated escrow, or counsel-approved structure.

Best first target:

- parcel lockers and ATMs on small commercial properties.

Avoid first:

- EV chargers with usage-based economics;
- vending commissions;
- telecom/microcell contracts with complex technical/site obligations;
- anything needing new operator consent before the owner understands the benefit.

## Lesson

Do not continue tiny rent-stream or local-site cashflow variants unless the stream is materially larger, payer acknowledgement is standardized, and the host obligation can be separated cleanly from payment. Case-level cash control is not enough when every file is contract-specific, low-ticket, and legal-heavy.
