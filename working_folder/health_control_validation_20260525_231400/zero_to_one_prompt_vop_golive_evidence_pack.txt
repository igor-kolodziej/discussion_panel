Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
VoP Go-Live Evidence Pack

One-sentence thesis:
A 5-business-day vendor-side response pack for CEE payment-software, name-matching, core-banking, fraud, and account-verification vendors whose PSP customer go-live, procurement, pilot expansion, or renewal is blocked by Verification of Payee evidence, test-result, API, latency, exception-handling, or scheme-conformance questions.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized CEE payment vendors, account-verification providers, IBAN/name-matching vendors, core-banking/payment processors, open-banking providers, fraud/risk vendors, and fintech infrastructure suppliers selling Verification of Payee or payee-verification-related functionality to banks, payment institutions, e-money institutions, processors, credit unions, or PSP technology stacks.

Acute trigger:
A PSP customer has a live Instant Payments Regulation / Verification of Payee implementation, procurement, go-live, pilot expansion, renewal, or scheme testing deadline and asks the vendor for evidence around matching logic, response codes, API behavior, latency, availability, audit logs, exception handling, data protection, scheme rulebook fit, false-positive/false-negative handling, batch/bulk-payment handling, and operational support. The commercial consequence is delayed go-live, stalled procurement, failed test cycle, or lost PSP contract.

Control point:
Signed go-live evidence mandate with the live PSP question list or test feedback, contract/procurement/go-live deadline, vendor API specification, response-code matrix, matching-threshold explanation, anonymized test cases, latency/availability logs, support model, exception-handling workflow, audit/logging evidence, data-protection/security artifacts, customer-specific response tracker, and reviewer notes. The startup controls the active response file and evidence room for that PSP customer process.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three prepaid live PSP go-live/procurement packs at 15,000-45,000 PLN each; each has a named PSP customer or procurement, a go-live/procurement/renewal value above 150,000 PLN or a strategic PSP logo, a written VOP/payee-verification evidence request or test-feedback list, vendor authorization to review product/test artifacts, one payment-scheme/API reviewer available on paid per-case terms, and at least one pack submitted to a PSP project/procurement/security/payment-ops owner. At least one case must produce PSP test-cycle progress, procurement progress, accepted answer/gap split, remediation scope, pilot continuation, renewal progress, or clean no-go.

6-month POC:
Complete 8-15 paid packs, collect 180,000-500,000 PLN in fees, keep gross margin above 60% after payment-scheme/API and data/privacy reviewer costs, secure 2-3 repeat channels from payment consultants, core-banking integrators, fintech lawyers, PSP implementation partners, or VOP/name-matching vendors, and build a private library of PSP request wording, response-code disputes, latency/availability evidence, batch/bulk-payment edge cases, matching-threshold explanations, false-positive/false-negative objections, and accepted answer patterns.

First acquisition mechanism:
Map CEE payment vendors and fintech infrastructure suppliers with public bank/PSP customers, Instant Payments or VOP positioning, IBAN/account-name verification, fraud/risk, open-banking, or core-payment products. Send precise outbound and partner messages: "If a PSP customer is blocking VOP go-live, procurement, or pilot expansion on matching logic, response-code, test-result, latency, exception-handling, or scheme-evidence questions, I turn your existing artifacts into a PSP-ready answer/gap pack in 5 business days. No legal opinion, no certification, no scheme guarantee."

Economics:
Charge 15,000-22,000 PLN for a narrow one-PSP answer/gap pack, 22,000-35,000 PLN for API/test-heavy or multi-stakeholder packs, and 35,000-45,000 PLN for urgent go-live/procurement cases with multiple payment flows or batch/bulk-payment edge cases. Reviewer cost is 3,000-10,000 PLN per case depending on payment-scheme/API and privacy/security flags. The vendor pays because a 150,000+ PLN PSP contract, strategic bank logo, pilot expansion, or go-live date is blocked by answerable evidence, not by abstract compliance interest.

Copy risk:
Payment consultants, EPC scheme specialists, core-banking vendors, payment processors, bank implementation partners, lawyers, and larger regtech firms can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-case focus, CEE PSP wording memory, response-code and test-feedback taxonomy, fast vendor-side evidence-room assembly, and partner overflow from firms that prefer implementation, legal, certification, or core-platform work.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the vendor signs the mandate, prepays, and shares the PSP evidence request, test feedback, API spec, response-code matrix, logs, exception-handling workflow, and reviewer notes, the startup controls the active go-live response file for that PSP process. Another provider must displace the customer-specific tracker, answer/gap split, evidence room, test-feedback response, remediation handoff, and PSP answer workflow under deadline pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live VOP/payee-verification PSP go-live, procurement, pilot-expansion, renewal, or test-feedback blockers. The output is a go-live artifact used in an active PSP process: response matrix, evidence index, API/test-log evidence, response-code map, matching-threshold explanation, exception workflow, audit/logging evidence, remediation handoff, and no-go notes. It is not generic payments consulting, legal advice, certification, scheme adherence assurance, implementation, dashboard, database, marketplace, or brokered expert introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, regulatory compliance certification, EPC scheme adherence certification, payment-service outsourcing, production implementation, security audit, privacy opinion, or guarantee of PSP approval. PSPs, vendors, payment consultants, lawyers, auditors, scheme participants, and implementation partners remain responsible for legal interpretation, scheme adherence, production testing, contractual commitments, go-live approval, security/privacy decisions, and payment operations. Cases involving live payment execution, funds movement, fraud incident response, sanctions, AML decisions, or customer account actions are excluded or routed to qualified parties.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is adjacent to DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because both serve vendors selling into regulated finance, but the trigger and proof artifact are different. DORA is ICT third-party-risk and operational-resilience evidence for regulated-finance renewals. VoP Go-Live Evidence is payment-product implementation evidence for Verification of Payee go-live/procurement/testing: matching logic, response codes, API behavior, latency, false positives/negatives, exception handling, and PSP test feedback. It is not REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
PSPs, banks, core-banking vendors, payment processors, EPC scheme specialists, lawyers, and payment consultants may already own this work. A solo unknown founder needs named payment-scheme/API reviewer credibility. Vendors may lack real logs, test artifacts, or clear matching logic, turning the pack into a gap memo. PSP approval and production test progress are outside founder control. Some questions may require legal, privacy, security, or scheme interpretation rather than evidence assembly. The buyer pool may be small and relationship-heavy. The idea may be considered too close to DORA because it is another vendor-side regulated-finance evidence pack.
