This round tests a different confirmed high-scoring pattern: a prepaid live decision case where the buyer is a capital allocator/acquirer, the channel owns deal flow, and the output changes an investment, LOI, price, or no-go decision.

## Current Source Notes

Current M&A and AI diligence commentary shows growing buyer concern around AI-specific risks in acquisitions:

- 2026 M&A commentary from law firms and diligence advisers repeatedly flags AI use, IP/data provenance, third-party model dependencies, privacy/security, compliance, and transaction-risk allocation as deal issues.
- Search-fund and small-acquisition diligence processes are compressed and cost-sensitive, making fixed-scope technical risk reviews more attractive than large-firm diligence.
- This round does not rely on legal advice; the founder provides technical/commercial risk mapping and routes legal/IP/privacy issues to buyer counsel.

The hard-control unit is not a report sold to the open market. It is a paid, broker/searcher-routed live data-room case with buyer authorization, target evidence access, and a decision deadline.

## Search Implication

This candidate is deliberately not a generic report:

- it only activates on a live acquisition/investment target;
- the case is routed by a broker, searcher group, angel syndicate, or micro-PE deal source;
- the acquirer prepays;
- the target data room is the controlled asset;
- output must affect LOI, price, condition, escrow, technical no-go, or post-close plan.
