## Advanced Candidate

**MoCRABlocked Amazon Cosmetics Listing Release Desk**

### Thesis

EU/CEE cosmetics brands and private-label sellers exporting to the US are now exposed to FDA MoCRA facility-registration, product-listing, responsible-person, label, ingredient, and adverse-event evidence requests inside Amazon, retailer, 3PL, and importer workflows. The fastest wedge is not broad cosmetics compliance. It is a live ASIN/SKU release file for sellers whose US listing, FBA/inventory movement, retail onboarding, or wholesale PO is blocked by a named MoCRA/FDA evidence request.

### Buyer

Amazon account-health, reinstatement, and marketplace-compliance agencies serving EU/CEE cosmetics sellers; secondarily private-label cosmetics brands, distributors, and beauty importers with US sales or retail onboarding blocked by MoCRA-related evidence.

### Acute Trigger

A named Amazon US ASIN/SKU, retailer onboarding item, 3PL/importer handoff, or wholesale PO is blocked by a request for FDA MoCRA facility registration, cosmetic product listing, responsible-person/contact details, label/ingredient evidence, manufacturer/facility status, adverse-event process, or related documentation.

### Transferable Control Point

Signed seller or agency mandate over the exact ASIN/SKU case; product label and ingredient files; manufacturer/filler/facility information; FDA Cosmetics Direct submission status or prepared submission file; responsible-person/contact evidence supplied by the seller or qualified provider; Amazon/retailer case ID; agency resale terms; and a strict submit/cannot-submit/escalate decision. The startup controls the case file and fee right for that live commercial blocker, not FDA legal status.

### 60-Day Signed/Filed/Paid Proof

- Three Amazon/account-health or marketplace-compliance agencies sign referral or resale terms.
- Six paid live ASIN/SKU cases are closed under written seller authorization.
- At least three cases produce FDA submission confirmation, corrected product-listing/facility evidence, Amazon/retailer progress, FBA/listing progress, retail onboarding progress, or clean no-go escalation.
- At least `30,000 PLN` is collected in setup/case fees.
- No accepted case lacks a live commercial blocker, named product, seller authorization, and available source truth.

### Economics

Initial pricing:

- `2,000-3,500 PLN` triage and submit/cannot-submit decision.
- `6,000-12,000 PLN` for one ASIN/SKU release dossier.
- `15,000-40,000 PLN` for a multi-SKU brand sprint.
- 25-35% agency resale/referral margin where routed through agencies.

Gross margin can exceed 60% if the founder standardizes the FDA/Amazon evidence crosswalk and pays specialist reviewers only for edge cases.

### Copy Risk

FDA/MoCRA consultants, cosmetics regulatory advisers, Amazon agencies, product-safety consultants, labeling consultants, and US responsible-person providers can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-case control: Amazon/agency case flow, exact ASIN/SKU evidence memory, FDA Cosmetics Direct/product-listing artifacts, agency resale relationships, and a CEE seller/supplier communication workflow before general consultants productize the Amazon-specific release desk.

### Internal Caps Applied

This barely clears internal simulation because it has a live commercial blocker, measurable platform/retailer progress, and a narrow FDA/Amazon case artifact. It would fall below the gate immediately if positioned as broad MoCRA readiness, FDA legal advice, cosmetic safety substantiation, label review, a product-registration agency, or generic Amazon compliance.

### Strongest Anticipated Objections

1. FDA/MoCRA consultants and Amazon agencies may already solve the easy cases.
2. Hard cases need truthful source data, correct facility/product facts, label/ingredient records, adverse-event process, and possibly a qualified US responsible person or regulatory consultant.
3. Amazon/retailer acceptance remains outside founder control.
4. Cosmetics claims can drift into drug claims, SPF, acne, anti-dandruff, medical/wellness claims, safety substantiation, allergen, labeling, or state-law issues outside scope.
5. Seller documents may be false, incomplete, mismatched, or impossible to reconcile.
6. Case values may be too small unless the buyer has meaningful US listing revenue or multi-SKU retail onboarding.
7. The MoCRA transition pain may standardize as cosmetics agencies and sellers learn the process.
8. The founder needs a credible reviewer/provider bench from day one.
