## Useful Narrowing

Best beachhead:

B2B SaaS/API vendors with 1-10 enterprise customers on usage-based or hybrid contracts, where annual or quarterly true-ups are contractually defined but not operationally billed.

Accept only cases with signed customer contract, explicit overage/minimum-commit/true-up clause, named customer, usage logs tied to the contract metric, prior invoice history, CFO approval, no informal waiver, no delivery dispute, and at least 25,000 PLN candidate recovery per customer.

Reject ambiguous clauses, weak logs, strategic accounts where billing would harm retention, product downtime/service-credit issues, privacy/data-quality defects, customer-success waiver history, likely churn damage, legal disputes, and anything resembling debt collection.
