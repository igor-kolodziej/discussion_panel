## Lead Candidate: Methane27 Importer Report Workpaper Desk

### Buyer

CEE fossil-energy importers, smaller coal/coke/fuel traders, LNG/natural-gas importers, crude-oil or petroleum-product importers where covered, and customs/energy compliance advisers serving importers with Regulation (EU) 2024/1787 Article 27 reporting obligations.

### Acute Trigger

The second annual EU methane-regulation importer report is due by 31 May 2026 for the 2025 reporting period. Importers need to assemble origin, producer/exporter, import volume, supply-chain, monitoring/reporting/verification, and methane-abatement information from internal import records and non-EU suppliers. Competent-authority submission, board/compliance signoff, supplier response gaps, and future public methane transparency profiles create a deadline-driven workpaper need.

### Control Point

Importer-authorized report workpaper with:

- importer mandate and named responsible person;
- customs/import records, invoices, contracts, CN/TARIC/cargo identifiers where available;
- producer/exporter/origin mapping;
- Article 27 / Annex IX field checklist;
- supplier RFI templates and response log;
- available producer MRV / abatement / emissions information;
- missing-field and no-go log;
- competent-authority submission support packet for the importer's own submission;
- reviewer issue-spotting from energy/compliance counsel where needed.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid importer or adviser-routed workpaper cases at 15,000-45,000 PLN each.
2. At least one report workpaper used in a competent-authority submission or correction response.
3. At least one non-EU supplier response obtained or a documented clean no-go for unavailable supplier information.
4. One energy/compliance reviewer on paid per-case terms.
5. Repeat field map covering origin, producer/exporter, import volumes, MRV/equivalence, abatement evidence, transport path, missing supplier evidence, and authority follow-up.

### Internal Simulated Score

**88 / 100**

Reason for advancing: the deadline is unusually concrete and current, the proof can be tied to official submission workpapers, and the buyer's avoided risk is higher than ordinary evidence desks.
