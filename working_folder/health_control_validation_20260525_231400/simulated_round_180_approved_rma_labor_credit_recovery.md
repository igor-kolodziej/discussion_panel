## Lead Candidate: Approved RMA Labor-Credit Recovery Mandate

### Buyer

Polish and CEE PV installers, heat-pump installers, refrigeration service companies, security/low-voltage integrators, EV-charger installers, and other contractor-distributors that have recurring approved warranty/RMA cases but weak back-office follow-through on manufacturer/distributor credit notes, labor reimbursements, return credits, freight credits, and portal deadlines.

### Acute Trigger

The installer has already repaired or replaced equipment, received an RMA number, filed a warranty case, sent a defective part back, or received distributor/OEM acknowledgement, but the expected credit memo, labor reimbursement, replacement offset, freight credit, or account balance correction has not arrived. The missing credit ties up cash and masks job profitability.

### Control Point

The startup controls only active, account-evidenced recovery files:

- signed installer mandate over named RMA/warranty/credit cases;
- distributor/OEM account statement or portal export;
- RMA numbers, serial numbers, installation/commissioning dates, product registrations, claim windows, photos, service reports, return labels, and shipment proof;
- payor acknowledgement, claim status, or portal evidence showing an eligible reimbursement/credit path;
- submitted follow-up packet and payment/credit memo trail;
- first recovered credit, account offset, or reimbursement invoice.

It rejects disputed warranty interpretation, consumer complaints, failed repairs, litigation, chargebacks, insolvency, and cases where no payor acknowledgement or rule-based eligibility path exists.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Five installers or service firms sign recovery mandates covering at least 40 named RMA/warranty/credit cases.
2. At least 150,000 PLN of claimed credits or reimbursements are documented through distributor/OEM statements, RMA portals, service records, or written payor acknowledgements.
3. Two distributor/OEM payor paths are confirmed in writing for credit follow-up.
4. At least 40,000 PLN of credit memos, account offsets, or reimbursements are recovered.
5. At least three installers pay fixed screened-ledger fees or agree to success fees from recovered credits before total spend exceeds 100,000 PLN.

### 6-Month POC

Handle 20-35 installer ledgers; accept 250-500 named claim lines; recover or credit 300,000-900,000 PLN; collect 80,000-220,000 PLN in fixed and success fees; maintain gross margin above 65% after admin reviewers; build a private recovery map by OEM/distributor, portal, serial evidence, claim window, return proof, and credit memo failure mode; reject at least 35% of cases as no-acknowledgement, warranty-disputed, consumer-facing, stale, or technical.

### Economics

Charge a 1,500-5,000 PLN screened-ledger fee for the first account review plus 15-25% of recovered credits or 400-2,500 PLN per recovered RMA line, depending on claim value and complexity. The fee is credible when installers are carrying old credits, replacement costs, labor reimbursements, or account offsets they already earned but have not collected. The model avoids inventory, receivable purchase, client-fund custody, and warranty adjudication.

### Copy Risk

Distributors, OEMs, installer ERPs, warranty automation tools, outsourced admin providers, and accountants can copy parts. The defense is narrow case ownership: signed mandates over exact RMA ledgers, portal histories, distributor account trails, return proof, claim-window evidence, and credit memo follow-up patterns. Longer-term advantage comes from repeated payor-path memory across fragmented CEE distributors and installers, not from generic warranty advice.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

Once the installer signs the mandate and provides the RMA ledger, portal exports, serial evidence, account statements, and distributor/OEM contact path, the startup controls those exact pending credit cases. A distributor, OEM, accountant, or admin contractor can offer a similar process later, but cannot collect or reassign the already-authorized claim queue without the installer moving the mandate and exposing the same account evidence again.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup does not sell warranty consulting, repair services, technical diagnosis, consumer advocacy, product registration software, dashboards, or introductions. It accepts only named RMA/warranty/credit lines where an installer or service firm already has account evidence and a payor path. The output is recovered cash, credit memo, account offset, or documented no-go for that exact credit line.

### Boundaries

The startup does not decide warranty eligibility, certify repairs, inspect equipment, perform installation work, represent consumers, provide legal advice, hold funds, handle disputes, or guarantee manufacturer outcomes. OEMs, distributors, authorized service providers, installers, engineers, and lawyers remain responsible for warranty interpretation, technical assessment, repair quality, product safety, and legal claims. Reject consumer complaints, clinical/medical equipment, disputed warranty coverage, missing serial identity, unsafe installation evidence, fraud flags, chargebacks, insolvency, export-control or sanctions concerns, and any case requiring litigation or regulated claims handling.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not BatteryFit because it does not sell retrofit advice or battery economics to installers. It is not HeatQuiet because it does not handle noise complaints or heat-pump siting. It is not Solar Inverter Replacement Options, GridFund, BESSGuard, PV GridTrip, or other energy-procurement ideas because the founder is not advising project replacements or funding release. It is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, 3PL, freight audit, PSP reserve, KSeF, DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, ShowComms, LeaseClear, or any platform evidence desk. The payment event is payor-acknowledged RMA/warranty/labor credit recovery for contractor accounts.
