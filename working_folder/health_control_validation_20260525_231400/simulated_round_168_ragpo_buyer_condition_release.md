## Lead Candidate

**RAGPO Buyer-Condition Release**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It improves the 84-scoring RAGProbe branch by requiring a written buyer condition and a named commercial release path before intake.
- The startup is not asking a buyer to trust generic AI security claims; it is answering a specific buyer condition through the partner of record.
- Proof is PO/pilot/payment/procurement progress, accepted lower-risk deployment scope, or clean no-go, not just a nice evidence packet.
- Cases with formal pentest, certification, high-risk AI, regulated decisioning, live production data, or broad remediation are rejected.

### Internal Objections

- It may still cap below 85 because AI security incumbents, appsec firms, and GRC platforms are strong.
- Buyer conditions may require remediation or formal review, not a bounded release packet.
- Partner-routed trust lowers CAC but gives partners a path to internalize.
- The line between evidence and false assurance must be narrow.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because it is the only near-miss branch with a materially stronger cash/payment control point than the prior prompt.
