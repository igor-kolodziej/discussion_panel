## Internal Simulation For Selected Candidate

**Idea:** RAGTrace Buyer Holdback Release Desk

**Simulated score:** 89/100

**Why it clears the simulated gate**

- The control point is a live buyer condition tied to a named payment, PO, renewal, milestone, or go-live acceptance event.
- The 60-day proof requires signed vendor authorization, buyer condition text, escrow/holdback/milestone value, test access, paid cases, and buyer condition releases or documented no-go outcomes.
- The artifact is not a generic report. It is a bounded evidence trace package that can release a specific buyer security condition or formally prove the vendor cannot safely proceed.
- CAC is plausibly low through SOC2, fractional-CISO, implementation, and appsec partners who already receive these blocked buyer conditions.
- Cash conversion is strong if cases are prepaid because the vendor has an immediately blocked deal or payment.
- Prior real validation showed the base buyer-security-evidence branch scored 84 and could rise to 88-90 if proof of partner routing, buyer-progress outcomes, and margin arrived. This version makes those outcomes the entry condition and first proof.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Cap would fall below 82 if the business accepts broad AI security testing without written buyer condition text and blocked economic value.
- Cap would fall below 85 if it becomes ordinary appsec subcontracting or partner slot packaging.
- Cap would fall below 87 if cases are not prepaid, reviewer cost exceeds 40%, buyer releases cannot be documented, or remediation consumes the delivery.
- Cap would fall below 82 for high-risk AI, clinical, HR, credit, insurance decisioning, biometric, law-enforcement, autonomous adverse decisioning, legal AI Act opinions, or formal certification work.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

The Zero To One evaluator may still view this as a trust-heavy appsec service with incumbents able to copy it. The prompt therefore must be explicit that only live buyer-held-money cases are accepted, the startup does not certify or remediate, and first proof is buyer condition release or cash/milestone movement.
