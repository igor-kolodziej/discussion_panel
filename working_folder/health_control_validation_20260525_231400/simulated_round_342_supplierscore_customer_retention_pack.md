## Finalists

1. **EcoVadis score rescue for SME suppliers**: strongest because the buyer trigger is an existing customer account, the platform status is visible, and the buyer has a commercial reason to pay before a renewal or PO is harmed.
2. **IntegrityNext automotive supplier release**: strong platform trigger but narrower and more automotive-quality-adjacent.
3. **EcoVadis corrective-action evidence sprint**: useful but score improvement may take too long and depends on platform scoring logic.
4. **Responsible sourcing portal rescue**: broader, weaker, more likely to become generic ESG consulting.
5. **EcoVadis multi-site evidence normalization**: stronger for groups, but less acute unless a named customer deadline exists.
6. **Customer-approved score rescue lockbox**: best synthesis, merging live customer request, platform workspace, current evidence, and buyer-progress outcome.

## Selected Candidate

**SupplierScore Customer Retention Pack**

### Thesis

Polish/CEE SME suppliers are increasingly pushed into EcoVadis, IntegrityNext, Sedex, SAQ, and similar customer-risk platforms by larger customers. Broad ESG reporting is crowded, but the ugly urgent wedge is narrower: a named customer has paused onboarding, renewal, preferred-supplier status, a framework agreement, or a PO until the supplier's score, reassessment, corrective-action upload, or evidence file moves from red/yellow/expired to acceptable or to a clean no-go.

### Control Table

| Field | Forced answer |
|---|---|
| Buyer | 50-300 employee Polish/CEE manufacturing, packaging, components, maintenance, logistics, chemicals-lite, food-ingredient, or industrial-service suppliers selling to large EU/UK customers |
| Acute trigger | Written customer request, supplier-portal red/yellow status, expiring reassessment, missing document flags, corrective-action deadline, or customer procurement note tied to a named account |

| First acquisition mechanism | Targeted outbound to suppliers visible in large-customer supply chains plus partner referrals from accountants, fractional procurement/quality people, and small ESG boutiques |
| Gross margin/payback | 8k-18k PLN single customer/platform pack; 18k-40k PLN urgent multi-site or multi-customer sprint; reviewer/accountant help scoped only for edge cases; target >60% gross margin |
| Copy risk | ESG consultants, EcoVadis advisers, accountants, procurement consultants, and platforms can copy parts |
| Why incumbents cannot copy before control | Once the founder holds the signed mandate, exact customer request, platform workspace, evidence chronology, upload register, and buyer follow-up, that case is controlled; the compounding asset is cross-customer portal wording, missing-evidence patterns, accepted upload structures, and no-go boundaries |
| Why not service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker | It starts only from a live customer/platform blocker and ends in submitted evidence, customer/platform progress, or paid no-go; no generic ESG report, dashboard, marketplace, or consultant referral |

### Internal Cap Audit

- Concrete 6-month control proof: yes, if three paid live customer-platform cases produce platform submission and at least two buyer/platform progress outcomes.
- Consultant/copy risk: material, but the live customer-platform rescue wedge, no-go discipline, and repeated accepted-evidence memory keep the initial simulation above the gate.
- CAC/payback/margins: credible if outbound targets suppliers with named enterprise customer pressure and charges prepaid fixed fees.
- Partnership dependence: helpful but not required; first cases can be direct from customer-pressure signals and supplier lists.
- Legal/regulated-data risk: moderate ESG/HR/environmental boundaries; avoid assurance, legal opinion, carbon calculations without source, and green claims.

## Simulated Zero To One Score

**88.2 / 100**

Rationale: The idea combines a live customer revenue gate, visible external platform status, immediate willingness to pay, low capital requirements, and a focused data/evidence workflow. It is materially sharper than generic ESG consulting because the buyer is not buying sustainability virtue; the buyer is trying to keep a named customer, PO, onboarding, or preferred-supplier status from slipping. The score is not higher because ESG consultants and platform advisers are credible substitutes, the founder must avoid assurance/legal/carbon-calculation claims, and some cases will reveal real missing practices rather than document gaps.

## Strongest Simulated Objections

1. EcoVadis and ESG consultants already exist; the startup must avoid being another score-improvement consultant.
2. Some scores cannot be fixed quickly because the supplier lacks policies, management systems, workforce records, environmental data, supplier controls, or corrective actions.
3. Platform scoring and customer acceptance are outside founder control.
4. Buyers may resist paying if the customer deadline is soft or the account is not material.
5. Customer-request formats vary across EcoVadis, IntegrityNext, Sedex, SAQ, proprietary portals, and customer procurement teams.
6. Evidence handling may include sensitive HR, wage, accident, environmental, supplier, and governance records.
7. Accountants, ESG boutiques, procurement consultants, and platforms can copy the method.
8. The six-month proof needs real buyer/platform progress outcomes, not just submitted files.
