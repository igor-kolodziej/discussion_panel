## Zero To One Summary

Zero To One said the pain is legitimate: EDGAR Next compliance is required, Login.gov credentials are required, the right filer roles matter, and non-enrolled filers need Form ID access to regain filing ability. It still scored the idea 78 because the strongest disruption was around the 2025 EDGAR Next transition, because filing agents and securities lawyers already own most trust, and because the buyer pool is fragmented.

## Strongest Objections

1. The EDGAR Next transition spike may be temporary; one-time enrollment chaos should fade.
2. Filing agents, financial printers, securities lawyers, issuer-service vendors, paralegal teams, and corporate secretaries are natural incumbents.
3. Trust friction is high for an unknown Warsaw founder handling EDGAR access, CIKs, delegated entities, CCC/passphrase paths, and filing deadlines.
4. Legal boundaries are easy to cross because access blockers often sit near filing obligations, lateness, amendments, insider obligations, disclosure, or issuer status.
5. Some blockers are not operational and may involve corporate authority, delinquency, issuer-status disputes, sanctions, fraud, or enforcement issues.
6. Official SEC resources are free, so buyers pay only for urgent named filing blockers.
7. Buyer pool is fragmented across Section 16 insiders, small issuers, FPIs, funds, Form D issuers, and Rule 144 sellers.
8. Filing agents may fear client leakage and prefer internal handling or white-label subcontracting.
9. Outcomes depend on SEC Form ID review, account-admin action, Login.gov, filer signatures, notarization/identity steps, and delegated-entity approvals.
10. The business can degrade into low-margin EDGAR account-admin support unless every case starts with named CIK, named filing, written blocker, and prepaid mandate.

## Evaluator Advice

The best version is a white-label EDGAR Next access-resolution desk for filing agents, IR firms, law-firm support teams, financial printers, broker affiliate-sale desks, and company-secretarial providers with named CIKs and named filing deadlines. Accept only cases with a written access/delegation/admin blocker, existing counsel or filing agent responsible for content, EDGAR dashboard/account evidence where authorized, and a prepaid fixed fee. Reject disclosure drafting, legal analysis, XBRL/tagging, delinquency strategy, issuer-status disputes, enforcement/sanctions/fraud issues, no named filing deadline, and cases where only legal interpretation creates value.

## Lesson

Official hard-access gates are not enough when the disruption is mostly transitional and trust sits with lawyers or filing agents. Future candidates need live current cash or status control plus a stronger reason incumbents cannot absorb the work.
