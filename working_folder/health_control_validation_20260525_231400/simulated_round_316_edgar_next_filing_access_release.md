## Selected Candidate

**EDGAR Next Filing-Access Release Desk**

## Internal Score: 88.1

Why it clears simulation:

- Official access transition creates a binary gate: filers must be enrolled/granted access and delegated correctly to file.
- Proof is objective: EDGAR dashboard status, delegated authority, Form ID/access grant, and first accepted accession/filing.
- The buyer has current financial/reputational risk: late SEC filings, blocked Form 6-K/20-F/Form D/Section 16/144 filings, broker restrictions, or exchange compliance issues.
- The founder can operate through filing agents, IR firms, securities counsel overflow, or company secretaries while staying out of disclosure drafting and legal advice.
- The control point is an active filing-access workroom, not an advisory memo.

## Internal Concerns

- Securities lawyers, EDGAR filing agents, corporate secretaries, brokers, and compliance vendors already own trust.
- This may be a transition spike after 2025 rather than a durable company.
- Legal/disclosure boundaries are dangerous; the startup must not prepare filing content or advise on securities obligations.
- Unknown Warsaw founder has credibility issues with SEC filers and access credentials.
- Many official resources are free.

## 60-Day Proof Standard

- 5 prepaid cases tied to named filings or deadlines.
- 3 accepted access/delegation/Form ID/account-admin outcomes or clean no-go decisions.
- 2 actual accepted EDGAR filings/accessions after the access fix.
- 70,000-150,000 PLN collected.
- At least one filing agent, IR firm, broker, or securities-counsel channel sends a second case.
- Rejection log above 50% for legal advice, disclosure drafting, XBRL/tagging, issuer delinquency, exchange compliance disputes, fraud, and cases without a named filing.

## Why This Is Not A Duplicate

- Not SAM/NCAGE: SEC filer access and accepted EDGAR filings, not government vendor registration.
- Not SupplierPortal or factor release: no customer AP or receivables.
- Not DORA/security evidence: official SEC portal access and filing authority.
- Not legal or filing-agent business: only access/delegation/admin evidence for named filings, with filing content and legal obligations left to counsel/filing agents.
