2. Many EUDAMED data issues are actually regulatory issues that the founder must not decide.

4. EUDAMED registration does not prove MDR/IVDR compliance or marketability.
5. The deadline pressure may normalize over 6-18 months as ARs and software vendors catch up.

7. Pricing only works for urgent, messy, commercial-release-linked batches; clean small batches may be done by junior RA staff.
8. AR/regulatory partners can internalize the field map and process after a few batches.
9. Trust, not demand, is the real 60-day bottleneck.
10. The work can easily drift into medical-device consulting, PRRC/AR responsibility, notified-body liaison, clinical evidence, PMS/vigilance, or legal interpretation.

## Lesson
