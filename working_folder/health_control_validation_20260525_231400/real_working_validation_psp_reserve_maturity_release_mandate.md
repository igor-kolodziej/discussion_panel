## Validator Summary

The validator accepted that PSP reserves, payout holds, rolling reserves, and closed-account balances are real cash-flow pain points. It also accepted that a narrow evidence-backed reserve-release operator could be useful for clean cases.

However, it scored the idea as a good cash-recovery micro-business rather than a defensible company. The key reason was that the signed mandate does not create durable control over the money or decision. It is revocable administrative access around a support/risk process where the PSP still controls timing, release, threshold changes, and final decisions.

## Strongest Objections Captured

- PSPs may be contractually right to hold funds even when the merchant believes the reserve is mature.
- Clean cases may auto-release or be handled by ordinary merchant support follow-up.
- Inbound cases are likely to be adverse-selection traps involving fraud flags, prohibited goods, refund spikes, chargebacks, KYC mismatch, legal holds, or angry merchants.
- Unknown-founder trust is a severe bottleneck because merchants must share sensitive PSP dashboards, payout exports, KYC, chargeback data, fulfillment proof, and support threads.
- The control point is weaker than stated: the merchant can revoke the mandate, the PSP may refuse to engage directly, and lawyers/accountants/agencies/internal finance can take over the same file.
- The business can collapse into low-margin support-ticket chasing if outcomes depend on reminders and luck rather than tight repeatable playbooks.
- Success-fee attribution is disputed when funds release after a scheduled review or rolling-reserve maturity.
- Legal-boundary risk is real, especially around legal interpretation, debt collection, regulated complaints, and quasi-representation.

## Lesson

Payment-reserve recovery has real urgency, but Zero To One treats the mandate as weak control because the processor keeps discretion and merchants retain/revoke the file. Do not keep testing broad PSP support-ticket recovery variants unless the next version controls a stronger payment event, receivable, titled balance, assignment, or channel that cannot be replaced by existing finance/legal/payment consultants.
