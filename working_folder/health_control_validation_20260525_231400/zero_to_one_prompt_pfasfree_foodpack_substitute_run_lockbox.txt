Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
PFASFree FoodPack Substitute Run Lockbox

One-sentence thesis:
Food-contact packaging converters, co-packers, and private-label or QSR suppliers facing post-12 August 2026 PFAS order blockers will pay to reserve a qualified substitute material allocation or converter run slot, funded by buyer deposits, before a named packaging PO, reorder, onboarding, or production run is lost.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE food-contact packaging converters, co-packers, private-label food suppliers, QSR packaging suppliers, food-service packaging distributors, and food brands with named greaseproof, molded-fiber, coated paperboard, bakery, wrap, pizza, QSR, tray, bowl, bag, or takeout packaging runs at risk. The first buyer must have a named customer request, PO, reorder, private-label onboarding, or production run above 250,000 PLN equivalent where PFAS evidence, substitution, or post-12 August 2026 acceptance is blocking commercial progress.

Acute trigger:
A retailer, QSR customer, food brand, co-packer, distributor, or private-label buyer pauses or conditions a packaging run, reorder, onboarding, PO, or product launch because the current food-contact packaging uses a grease barrier, coating, paper treatment, molded-fiber furnish, adhesive, ink, laminate, or supplier declaration that may not satisfy the buyer's PFAS requirements under the post-12 August 2026 PPWR/PFAS environment. The buyer needs a credible substitute material or run path, not another broad readiness memo.

Control point:
A signed mandate over the named packaging customer/run; current PO, run, reorder, onboarding, or approval consequence; packaging BOM or material structure; current supplier declaration/evidence; customer request text; substitute supplier or converter option over named material, MOQ, lead time, sample path, and run slot; buyer deposit or converter prepayment before any nonrefundable commitment; reviewer/lab escalation route; customer response chronology; and either an accepted substitute sample path, replacement PO, reserved run, lab-needed no-go, or clean stop decision.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three paid live order/run files, each with a named customer request and at least 250,000 PLN equivalent of packaging order, reorder, onboarding, or production-run value at stake. At least two substitute material or converter run-slot options signed, each contingent on buyer deposit and bounded by named packaging families. At least two buyer or converter deposits/prepayments collected before supplier commitments. At least one accepted substitute sample path, customer-progress outcome, replacement PO, reserved run, lab-needed no-go, or clean stop decision. Collect 60,000-180,000 PLN in fixed fees, option fees, spreads, or buyer-funded run-reservation fees.

6-month POC:
Handle 12-25 live substitute-run files across 6-12 converters, co-packers, QSR/private-label suppliers, or food brands. Put 8,000,000-35,000,000 PLN of packaging PO/run/reorder/onboarding value under mandate. Secure 8+ substitute material or converter run-slot options and 8+ buyer-funded deposits or prepayments. Produce 10+ customer-progress outcomes: accepted substitute sample path, replacement PO, buyer-approved switch plan, reserved run, lab-needed no-go, or clean stop decision. Complete 3-5 buyer-funded substitute runs or allocated material paths. Collect 350,000-1,000,000 PLN revenue with gross margin above 55% after reviewer, lab coordination, supplier option, and contractor costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with packaging reps, food-contact packaging converters, co-packers, QSR/private-label suppliers, and material distributors handling greaseproof, molded-fiber, coated paperboard, bakery, wrap, pizza, tray, bowl, bag, and takeout packaging. The opening ask is: "Show one named PO, customer request, or run where PFAS uncertainty may stop the order. I do not certify compliance. I reserve a substitute material/run path only if your buyer funds the option or prepayment, then we return an accepted switch path, lab-needed route, or clean no-go."

Economics:
Charge 10,000-20,000 PLN for fast substitute-run triage, 20,000-60,000 PLN for a buyer-funded multi-SKU or high-value run lockbox, plus transparent option fees, allocation spreads, or success fees only when tied to a replacement PO, reserved run, or accepted switch path. Costs are founder operations, supplier/run-slot sourcing, reviewer and lab coordination, secure BOM/document handling, sample tracking, buyer response preparation, and contractor support. The buyer pays because losing or delaying a 250,000-2,000,000 PLN packaging run, QSR supply order, retailer onboarding, or private-label launch is much more expensive than a buyer-funded substitute path.

Copy risk:
Packaging material suppliers, converters, food-contact lawyers, packaging consultants, labs, QSR packaging teams, and large distributors can copy pieces. The defense is transaction-specific control: signed mandate over a named customer/run, current buyer request, substitute supplier/converter option, buyer-funded deposit, sample/run chronology, and repeated no-go/switch taxonomy by packaging family and customer wording.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup holds the mandate, current PO/run file, buyer request, substitute allocation option, and buyer deposit for a named packaging family, another consultant or supplier cannot copy that specific run path without displacing the already-reserved material or converter slot. Incumbents can compete for future substitutions, but not the current buyer-funded optioned path without the customer and converter switching.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell generic PPWR readiness, maintain a supplier marketplace, certify PFAS compliance, or write reports. It accepts only live named packaging orders/runs with commercial consequence and controls a substitute material or converter run path funded by buyer deposits. The output is a reserved run, accepted substitute sample path, replacement PO, lab-needed route, or clean stop decision.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, food-contact compliance opinions, PFAS certification, laboratory testing, product safety certification, regulatory representation, packaging design approval, quality release, or final customer compliance claims. Food-contact lawyers, packaging compliance reviewers, testing labs, material suppliers, converters, co-packers, brand owners, and customers remain responsible for formal compliance, safety, declarations, test methods, material performance, customer acceptance, and legal decisions. Reject cases with missing BOM, no supplier authority, no customer request, no commercial consequence, pressure to claim compliance without evidence, clear testing requirement where the buyer refuses testing, suspicious declarations, unknown material structure, high-risk food-safety issues, medical/infant/regulated product claims, counterfeit or unauthorized material, or requests to evade law.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not the prior PPWR PFAS FoodPack Order Release Pack because the controlled object is not a release/no-go evidence packet. It is a buyer-funded substitute material or converter run lockbox. It is not GreenTender EPREL, CBAM, DORA, REDBlocked, Data Act, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, BioSignal, Supplement Stack Safety, Amazon Vendor Central, KSeF, CPSC eFiling, DRS/Kaucja, ESPR NoDestroy, or any marketplace evidence desk. The buyer, trigger, control point, and proof artifact are a named food-contact packaging run and buyer-funded substitute allocation.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Existing material suppliers and converters may solve substitutions directly. Buyers may not pay deposits before lab evidence, food-contact legal review, sample testing, shelf-life testing, machine trials, sealing trials, grease-performance tests, or price approval. Substitute materials may have high MOQs, long lead times, worse performance, higher cost, or limited availability. The founder may lack packaging and food-contact credibility without named reviewers and supplier relationships. Many cases may simply need declarations or testing, not a substitute run. The business can drift into packaging brokerage, compliance consulting, lab brokerage, or supplier search. Customer acceptance remains outside the founder's control. The August 2026 wave may normalize after suppliers update portfolios. Gross margin can collapse if sampling, lab coordination, supplier RFIs, converter trials, customer calls, and no-go cases consume too much time.
