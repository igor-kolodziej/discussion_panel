## Search Frame

The recent search failures point to one rule: ordinary service, evidence, documentation, and micro-acquisition shapes are capped unless the founder controls a live transaction substrate. The round therefore focuses on controlled books/entities where proof is current cash movement, accepted orders, payment direction, regulated settlement, titled stock, or buyer deposits.

The lead candidate is health-adjacent, so it is only eligible because the control point is unusually hard and the boundaries keep licensed/authorized parties in their roles. The startup does not diagnose, prescribe, interpret clinical data, issue e-zlecenia, sell prescription drugs, or provide clinical advice.
