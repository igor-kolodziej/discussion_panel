Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
GxP Vendor Qualification Release Pack

One-sentence thesis:
Life-sciences companies increasingly block software vendors at procurement, renewal, pilot, or go-live until supplier qualification, Annex 11, Part 11, data-integrity, validation-support, security, hosting, change-control, and audit evidence is buyer-ready; the wedge is a vendor-side release pack for live regulated-customer blockers.

Exact buyer:
20-200 employee EU/CEE SaaS, software-product, automation, analytics, LIMS, eQMS, CMMS, MES-adjacent, lab-data, quality-workflow, maintenance-workflow, and document-workflow vendors selling to pharma, biotech, CDMO, CRO, medtech manufacturers, regulated laboratories, or GMP/GDP/GxP operations.

Acute trigger:
A named life-sciences buyer has written that procurement, renewal, pilot, go-live, supplier approval, vendor qualification, audit closure, or a purchase order is blocked until the vendor provides a qualification or evidence response covering one or more: supplier questionnaire; vendor audit request; Annex 11 / computerized-system evidence; 21 CFR Part 11 / electronic-records/e-signature evidence; data integrity and audit trail evidence; validation-support package; SDLC/change-control/release evidence; hosting/cloud/subprocessor evidence; backup/restore, access control, incident, retention, export, or deletion evidence; quality agreement or supplier file inputs.

Control point:
The startup controls a signed vendor-side release file: exact buyer request; named customer, opportunity, renewal, PO, pilot, or go-live consequence; existing vendor evidence room; product and deployment scope; GxP impact assumptions stated by buyer/vendor; Annex 11/Part 11/GAMP-style topic matrix where relevant; evidence index and response pack; buyer-safe no-go split: answerable now, product remediation needed, validation/QA owner needed, legal review needed, or unsafe to claim.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days: one retained GxP/CSV/vendor-qualification reviewer; three prepaid vendor mandates at 12,000-35,000 PLN each; at least two live buyer requests processed; at least two buyer-facing packs submitted; and at least one hard outcome such as buyer QA/procurement acknowledgement, narrowed follow-up, supplier approval progress, audit/CAPA closure progress, PO/renewal/go-live progress, or accepted no-go.

6-month POC:
12-20 paid files; 7+ buyer acknowledgements, supplier-approval progress events, narrowed follow-ups, renewal/procurement/go-live progress events, or accepted no-go outcomes; 3 repeat/referral sources; 220,000-600,000 PLN revenue; 60%+ gross margin after reviewer cost; private evidence-pattern library across at least 6 software categories and 20 buyer request types.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 100 EU/CEE software vendors with public pharma, biotech, lab, CDMO, medtech, or regulated manufacturing customers. Search for vendors advertising LIMS, eQMS, QMS, CMMS, MES-adjacent workflows, electronic signatures, audit trails, lab automation, quality workflows, maintenance, calibration, document control, training, data capture, and analytics in life sciences. Build a partner list of 30 GxP/CSV consultants, QA freelancers, pharma IT consultants, medtech QA consultants, and validation contractors.

Send a narrow offer: "If a named pharma/lab/medtech customer is blocking procurement, renewal, pilot, or go-live on vendor qualification, Annex 11, Part 11, or validation-support evidence, we turn your existing facts into a buyer-ready release pack. No validation, no certification, no legal opinion, no patient data."

Economics:
4,000-7,000 PLN paid triage; 12,000-35,000 PLN standard buyer-response pack; 35,000-75,000 PLN urgent multi-customer or multi-product sprint if evidence exists and reviewer capacity is locked. Reviewer cost is 2,500-10,000 PLN per file.

Value capture is protecting a life-sciences customer, renewal, pilot, go-live, or purchase order that can be worth far more than the pack fee.

Copy risk:
GxP consultants, CSV consultants, validation contractors, QA freelancers, pharma IT consultants, eQMS/LIMS vendors, and life-sciences advisory firms can copy the workflow.

The defense is not generic Annex 11 or Part 11 knowledge. It is a vendor-side live blocker lane, strict no-go discipline, and accumulated buyer-request/evidence-response memory across underprepared EU/CEE software vendors.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
They can copy the checklist, but they cannot work a specific case before the founder controls the signed vendor mandate, exact buyer request, opportunity/renewal/go-live consequence, vendor evidence room, reviewer-backed response matrix, no-go boundary, and submission chronology.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is a named buyer-release pack for a live procurement/renewal/go-live blocker. It is not broad GxP consulting, validation execution, audit certification, software implementation, marketplace, dashboard, or generic document report.

The deliverable is a buyer-submission pack tied to one customer request and one commercial consequence.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No validation certification. No claim that the vendor, product, or buyer process is compliant. No formal computer system validation execution for the regulated buyer. No clinical, diagnostic, patient, genetic, or personal health data handling. No legal, regulatory, QA release, batch-release, clinical, or medical-device advice. No invented SOPs, backdated controls, fake audit trails, or false Part 11/Annex 11 statements.

Product remediation, validation protocol execution, quality agreement negotiation, legal review, and formal QA approval stay with qualified providers and the buyer/vendor.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not DORA: buyer is a life-sciences software vendor and gatekeeper is pharma/medtech/lab supplier qualification, not regulated-finance ICT third-party-risk renewal.

Not BioSignal: this is not investor diligence, health-claim review, or biosignal evidence. It is vendor-side regulated-customer procurement evidence for software suppliers and excludes patient/clinical data.

Not REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, CMMC, HeritageDoor, or PromoLeak.

Strongest anticipated objections:
GxP/CSV consultants and validation contractors already exist. Unknown founder credibility is weak without a named GxP/CSV reviewer. Many blockers require product changes, real validation work, QA ownership, or buyer-side validation, not packaging. Buyer acceptance and supplier approval remain outside control. Handling life-sciences evidence creates confidentiality and misrepresentation risk. Some cases drift into clinical/patient data, medical device validation, legal review, or formal QA approval and must be rejected. Direct outbound may miss hidden live buyer blockers. The idea could look like a DORA clone unless the launch is tightly tied to life-sciences vendor qualification and software validation-support evidence.
