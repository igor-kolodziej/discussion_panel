## Finalist Review

### RAGProof Partner Slot Blocks

Why it advances:

- It upgrades the prior 84-scoring RAGProbe branch by changing the first control point from a routing agreement to prepaid partner slot blocks.
- The buyer/payor is the trusted appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO partner, not the unknown founder's cold end customer.
- It keeps formal security/legal ownership with the partner and uses the startup as a narrow AI/RAG/agent test module.
- First proof is current cash before delivery: two partners buy slot blocks, then route live buyer blockers into those slots.
- It preserves the strongest part of RAGProbe: generated test traces for prompt injection, source isolation, data leakage, tool permissions, logging, and human review, not questionnaire answer packaging.

Strict objections:

- It can still be seen as an AI/appsec service.
- Appsec firms can hire AI security reviewers and internalize the module.
- Slot blocks may be hard to sell before proof.
- False assurance risk must be tightly bounded.
- If product failures require remediation, the startup must no-go or route work back to the partner.
