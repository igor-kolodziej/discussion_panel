## Materially Different Candidate

`CardioRoute Holter/ABPM Fleet-Book Buyout`

This candidate buys or options a retiring, overloaded, or under-digitized Polish Holter ECG / ABPM micro-operator's existing B2B fleet-book. It controls titled physical diagnostic devices and the clinic route/payment handoff, not patient data, diagnosis, reporting, or clinical interpretation.

## Buyer

- small private cardiology clinics;
- POZ/private primary-care clinics with cardiology referrals;
- occupational-medicine clinics serving aging workers;
- local diagnostic clinics that already sell paid Holter/ABPM slots but suffer device overflow, failure, missing cuffs/cables, or admin delays.

## Acute Trigger

Paid Holter/ABPM slots are delayed or canceled because a recorder, cuff, cable, charger, docking station, software station, cleaning cycle, courier route, or admin handoff breaks. Clinics lose appointment revenue and patient trust, especially among 50-60+ patients and occupational exam populations where cardiac/blood-pressure monitoring demand is recurring.

## Control Point

The acquired asset is the B2B fleet-book:

- titled Holter ECG and ABPM devices;
- cuffs, cables, electrodes/accessories, chargers, docks, cleaning supplies, shipping cases;
- serial files, service records, CE/instruction files, cleaning logs, and accessory compatibility map;
- transferable software station or readout workflow where legally/technically transferable;
- booking phone/domain/mailbox;
- clinic contacts and seller-endorsed handoff;
- deposit/payment terms, route/courier notes, and recurring clinic usage history;
- prepaid clinic overflow/loaner commitments before close.

## Two-Month Proof

Required:

1. One signed conditional acquisition option under 100,000 PLN over a named micro-operator's fleet-book.
2. Six to ten working Holter/ABPM devices checked by serial number, accessories, service files, cleaning logs, and legal/technical transfer status.
3. Five clinics or occupational-medicine buyers sign continuity/overflow agreements.
4. At least `25,000 PLN` prepaid rental/deposit cash clears before close.
5. One live week of overflow/failure-cover rentals is completed.
6. Founder does not access diagnostic interpretation or patient-report content.
7. Licensed clinics/doctors remain provider of record.

## Why It Scores Above Prior Health Ideas

- Titled physical devices and accessories are harder control than a renewal calendar.

- Copy risk is reduced because the asset is a named local fleet-book and seller handoff, not generic device rental from scratch.
