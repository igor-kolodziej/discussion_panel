## Main Simulated Objections

1. Seller supply is the bottleneck: the founder needs a small app vendor with clean IP, real Data Center customers, weak cloud path, and willingness to carve out the tail.
2. Atlassian Marketplace transfer, licensing, app vendor terms, customer data, security obligations, and DPAs may be complex.
3. Enterprise customers may prefer known Atlassian Solution Partners or demand vendor-level security and support.
4. App maintenance can become deeper than expected: Java/Atlassian API changes, security patches, compatibility, migration scripts, customer data export, and production incidents.
5. One app is a cash-flow asset, not a company, unless repeat seller sourcing is proven.
6. Customers may use the handoff as a signal to migrate away.
7. Some apps may have no future because Data Center is winding down; the tail could decay faster than expected.
