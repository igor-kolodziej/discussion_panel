Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality.

Scored idea:

SomnoConsent is a Poland/CEE biosignal rights company that signs exclusive mandates with small, private, retiring, or under-monetized sleep labs to convert named archived PSG/HSAT recordings into explicitly patient-consented commercial validation assets. It does not assume old pseudonymised health data can simply be sold. The POC proves a lawful conversion machine: lab-controlled reconsent outreach, explicit patient consent for algorithm validation/research/commercial health-AI testing, secure inventory of raw EDF/scoring metadata, and refundable inspection deposits from sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, CPAP, wearable, and medtech buyers.

The control point is the exclusive right to convert named dormant sleep archives into consented validation cohorts before competitors can access those same recordings. Example mandate: "Lab A, 2017-2025, about 1,200 PSG/HST nights, named devices, scorer labels, patient-contact channel controlled by lab, no competing commercial reconsent/data-buyer mandate for 180 days, small option fee plus revenue share, no raw export before DPO/legal approval and patient consent."

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 20,000-30,000 PLN for Polish/EU health-data lawyer and DPO route memo, consent language, controller/processor structure, withdrawal handling, and data-room terms.
- 10,000-20,000 PLN for lab mandate/option fees to 2-3 private labs.
- 15,000-40,000 PLN for patient incentives and lab-controlled outreach to 500-1,000 former patients.
- 10,000-20,000 PLN for EDF/HST metadata inventory, secure storage, extraction QA, and redaction pipeline.
- 5,000-10,000 PLN for targeted buyer outreach.

Required POC proof:
1. Two signed exclusive lab mandates naming archive scope, device systems, approximate record count, reconsent authority, commercial-readiness rights, and no-competing-mandate window.
2. DPO/lawyer memo naming lawful paths, no-go paths, GDPR Article 6/9 basis, data-transfer limits, withdrawal handling, and secure-processing controls.
3. First 50-150 explicit patient consents for commercial validation/research/testing access, with no raw export before legal gates.
4. Metadata-only inventory of at least 500 records.
5. One or two refundable buyer inspection deposits of 5,000-15,000 PLN for secure feasibility review of the named consented cohort, not just LOIs.

Buyer: sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, CPAP, digital-health, clinical-trial tooling, and wearable companies that need independent PSG/HST comparison data before certification planning, investor diligence, clinical-validation design, enterprise procurement, or strategic partnership.

Beachhead: adult obstructive sleep apnea and sleep-staging validation cohorts from private Polish/CEE PSG and home sleep-test labs using mainstream devices and human scorer labels.

Hidden insight: most people think the scarce asset is a big hospital dataset. The actionable scarce asset is the exclusive consent-conversion right over messy, legally retained private-lab archives before they enter any broad data catalog or are cleaned by a better-funded intermediary. Small labs are not data vendors; AI companies need real-world PSG/HST validation data; the founder can bridge the gap if the POC proves lab exclusivity, patient consent response, and buyer deposits before serious capital is spent.

Why this is not just a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace: the foundational asset is signed exclusive mandates plus patient-level consented conversion rights over named archives. Service work exists only to make that asset legally usable. If another company wants the same cohort, it cannot buy one vendor contract, one dashboard, or one equipment purchase; it must win different labs or wait out the exclusivity.
