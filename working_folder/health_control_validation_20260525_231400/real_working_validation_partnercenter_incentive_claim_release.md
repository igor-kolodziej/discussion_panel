## Strongest Objections

1. Microsoft distributors, partner-development managers, partner marketing agencies, CSP operations consultants, accountants, and partner-ops freelancers already help with incentives, co-op claims, and payouts.
2. Some claims cannot be fixed if the root cause is program ineligibility, wrong partner ID, missed window, invalid POE, wrong customer association, unsupported campaign, tax/bank profile failure, or licensing/program-rule mismatch.
3. Partner Center access is sensitive and creates trust friction for an unknown founder.
4. Microsoft support can be slow and opaque, which weakens success-fee cash conversion.
5. Many partners may have too little visible claim value to justify a 4,000-10,000 PLN setup fee.
6. The work can drift into generic partner-ops consulting: licensing, co-sell strategy, designations, marketplace setup, campaign planning, customer association cleanup, tax/bank setup, or FinOps.
7. Proof-of-execution disputes can be messy when activity dates, campaign periods, invoices, deliverables, screenshots, attendee lists, assets, and program rules do not line up.
8. Success-fee triggers are hard to define around paid cash, reinstated eligibility, corrected statement, accepted POE, future payout trace, or unlocked claim.
9. Partners may blame the founder for valid Microsoft denials unless fixed no-go fees are prepaid.
10. Microsoft native tooling and support may improve and reduce demand over time.

## Evaluator's Best Beachhead

Small Microsoft CSP/MSP/ISV partners with 50,000-300,000 PLN equivalent in visible but stuck incentives, co-op/MDF, or marketplace payouts.

Best first cases:

- claim value visible in Partner Center, statement, distributor report, or marketplace payout report;
- claim ID or support-case ID exists;
- deadline or expiry exists;
- POE, customer-association, or payout mismatch is document-fixable;
- partner lacks a dedicated partner-ops person;
- fixed fee is prepaid.

Avoid:

- "help us find incentives";
- no visible claim value;
- pure eligibility disputes;
- bank/tax profile identity issues;
- licensing or legal advice;
- fake POE or questionable customer association;
- partners under 30,000 PLN claim value.

## Lesson

Portal recovery queues are still being capped when natural ecosystem incumbents already sit close to the money. Signed claim control is not enough if the platform, distributor, specialist consultant, or account manager is the obvious workflow owner. Continue only with current-money ideas where the founder controls an asset or payment stream that incumbents cannot see or absorb before proof.
