## Strongest Objections Captured

- Key charts, bitting records, master-key hierarchy, authorization lists, and restricted blank access are sensitive security assets.
- Manufacturer, dealer, or restricted keyway control may not transfer to the startup or its contractor.
- Customers may use the transition to rekey or switch to a known local locksmith.
- Seller books may be messy: incomplete charts, old paper records, unclear authorization lists, residential/commercial mix, obsolete keyways, or unsupported systems.
- Per-order economics may be too low after authorization checks, support, contractor coordination, custody/security, and admin time.
- Incumbent locksmiths are the natural acquirers because they can perform the work and keep full margin.
- Scope creep into emergency lockouts, door repairs, access-control admin, CCTV/alarm changes, safe work, and security advice is dangerous.
- Liability from one wrong master level, unauthorized duplicate, chart leak, or bad authorization check can exceed fee revenue.
