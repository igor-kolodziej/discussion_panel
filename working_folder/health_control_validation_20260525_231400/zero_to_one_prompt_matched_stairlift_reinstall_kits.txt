Idea: Matched Stairlift Reinstall Kit Bank.

Acquire complete used stairlift systems from estate clear-outs, renovations, downsizing families, care-home refits, and demolition contractors before they are scrapped or split. Keep each as a matched kit: rail sections, chair, motor/gearbox, controls/remotes, batteries, charger, brackets, photos, measurements, removal notes, service history, and condition record. Sell project-ready low-cost reinstall kits through qualified installer partners to families preparing homes for 50-60+ spouses/parents or soon-elderly owners who need fast mobility adaptation.

The startup does not give medical advice, disability assessment, or install unless using qualified installers. It controls complete titled kits, pickup rights, measurement records, installer handoff, and paid reservations.

Buyer: adult children and 50-60+ homeowners adapting homes after injury, surgery, falls, or parent move-in; stairlift installers needing cheaper used-kit options; care coordinators/OTs who refer families; estate clearers and renovation crews removing old lifts.

Acute trigger: a spouse/parent cannot safely use stairs. New stairlifts may be expensive or slow. Used systems are often discarded because buyers cannot verify completeness, fit, or installer readiness. A complete measured kit with installer review can become a fast paid install.

Control point: complete titled matched kit, rail/staircase measurements, photos, handedness, landing constraints, bracket count, removal notes, service papers, first-refusal pickup rights, installer review, and paid reservation.

2-month proof:
1. signed first-refusal pickup agreements with 3 supply sources;
2. 3 complete titled stairlift kits acquired;
3. installer condition notes for all kits;
4. measurement/fit record and photo pack for each kit;
5. 3 paid reservations before refurbishment/delivery;
6. one completed paid handoff/install through qualified installer partner;
7. 25k+ PLN collected/prepaid;
8. 35%+ gross margin after removal, storage, inspection, batteries/parts, transport, installer handoff, warranty/returns, liability reserve;
9. no medical advice or unqualified installation.

Not a marketplace: it owns complete physical kits with fit records and installer-ready handoff. Risks: safety/liability, curved rail mismatch, battery/motor failure, buyer trust, lumpy supply, competition from new stairlift dealers.
