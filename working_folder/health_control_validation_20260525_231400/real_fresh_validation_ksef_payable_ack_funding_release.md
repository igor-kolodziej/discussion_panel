## Fresh Zero To One Summary

The fresh evaluator agreed that the idea is timely and commercially sharper than generic KSeF consulting because it targets named, high-value, already-payable invoices and a cash-release outcome. It accepted that mandatory KSeF and FA(3) transition create real invoice/AP evidence friction.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Clean payable-acknowledged cases may be rarer than assumed; many apparent cases will be delivery disputes, price disputes, missing approvals, budget delays, setoffs, tax uncertainty, or buyer cash management.
2. Buyer acknowledgement may be weaker than it looks: AP, procurement, receiving, treasury, and factors may all have different acceptance/funding requirements.
3. Accountants, KSeF software vendors, ERP integrators, outsourced CFOs, factors, and factoring brokers are structurally close to the invoice, XML, correction, financing, and AP process.
4. Free Ministry of Finance KSeF tools commoditize part of the evidence retrieval layer.
5. The no-tax/no-legal/no-accounting boundary is fragile because clients will ask for correction-route, cancellation/reissue, VAT-sensitive, or buyer-refusal judgment.
6. Success-fee attribution will be disputed unless the trigger is very tightly defined.
7. The six-month POC target is ambitious for an unknown part-time Warsaw founder because channel trust with factors and accountants takes time.
8. The pain may be transition-heavy, not permanent; KSeF workflows may normalize within 6-18 months.
9. Case control is useful for getting paid on one case, but it is not yet a company asset.
10. A real moat would require accumulated buyer-specific evidence patterns plus trusted recurring channel flow, neither of which is proven at idea stage.

## Fresh Evaluator's Stronger Version

The evaluator said the strongest version would start with factoring/funding-release cases rather than ordinary supplier payment chasing, because factors already think in evidence, reserve release, confirmation, debtor acceptance, and receivable eligibility.

Suggested 60-day acceptance gate:

- written buyer acknowledgement from AP/procurement/receiving/treasury;
- named blocker tied to KSeF/AP/factor evidence;
- seller accountant or finance owner available within 24 hours;
- buyer AP contact thread already open.

Suggested kill criterion:

- kill or pivot if the founder cannot get 4 prepaid cases, at least 2 resolved/no-go outcomes, and one repeat channel willing to send a second case in 60 days.

## Lesson

KSeF can pass a working chat when framed tightly, but fresh validation sees it as a temporary, manual, service-heavy transition desk. Do not continue KSeF variants without actual live case proof, recurring factor/accounting channel ownership, or a materially stronger payment stream.
