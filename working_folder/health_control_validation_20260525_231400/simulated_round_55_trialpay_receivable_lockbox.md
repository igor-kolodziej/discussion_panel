## Characteristics Of The Best Rated Ideas So Far

The strongest ideas in this search were not patient-facing health products. They shared these traits:

- B2B payment flow or operational continuity, not consumer trust transfer.
- An existing book/asset is acquired or optioned before buildout.
- The control point is hard: assigned invoice/receivable, customer-approved account transfer, source code/access custody, titled inventory, phone/domain/mailbox, prepaid slots, or reserved capacity.
- The founder avoids diagnosis, prescribing, clinical interpretation, patient-data resale, and regulated provider claims.
- First cash is a gate before closing, not a later hope.
- The POC produces one verifiable transaction: prepaid renewal, collected receivable, live support ticket resolved, completed batch, or title-clean resale.
- Copy risk is reduced by locking a specific lot/book/payment stream before incumbents can react.

Failed ideas were usually capped because they depended on consumer trust transfer, low-margin coordination, a licensed partner owning the valuable work, speculative inventory demand, or a generic service wrapper.

## Material Pivot

Move from health-service and equipment continuity into a legally assigned B2B receivable/payment-flow control point inside clinical trials.

`TrialPay Site Receivable Lockbox Book Buyout`

This acquires or options the customer book and open receivable workflow of a small clinical-trial finance/admin specialist serving Polish private research sites. It targets GLP-1/obesity, addiction, cardiometabolic, neurology, psychiatry, sleep, EEG, and biosignal-heavy studies only as therapeutic/operational niches; it does not sell, advertise, prescribe, reserve, or distribute medicines.

## Buyer

- independent Polish clinical-trial sites;
- private clinics running sponsored studies;
- small site-management organizations;
- PIs/sites with delayed sponsor/CRO payments, unpaid pass-through costs, query-dependent milestones, and weak finance operations.

## Acute Trigger

Clinical-trial sites often wait months for sponsor/CRO payment and must reconcile visit milestones, EDC/eCRF status, invoice portals, pass-through costs, screen-fail fees, startup fees, closeout fees, patient reimbursements, and query holds. Payment delays strain site cash and can slow enrollment or operations.

## Control Point

The acquired/optioned asset is not a generic factoring website. It is a payment-control and receivable-recovery book:

- signed client book from a retiring or overloaded clinical-trial accountant/admin;
- open invoice ledger and aging schedule;
- CTA/budget/payment-term extracts;
- sponsor/CRO AP contacts and portal workflows;
- source-document checklist for billable milestones, without collecting unnecessary patient data;
- claim evidence pack for completed visits, startup/closeout fees, screen fails, unscheduled visits, and pass-through costs;
- assignment of selected undisputed receivables or debtor-notified collection mandate;
- lockbox/payment-instruction letter where debtor accepts or acknowledges the assignment;
- success-fee and optional micro-advance economics;
- seller handoff to sites and AP contacts.

The hard control is a signed receivable assignment/collection mandate plus debtor/CRO acknowledgement and first collected cash.

## Two-Month POC Under 100,000 PLN

Budget:

- `5,000-15,000 PLN` refundable/creditable option over a retiring/overloaded specialist's site-payment admin book;
- `10,000-20,000 PLN` legal review of assignment, anti-assignment clauses, CTA/budget terms, data-minimization, tax/VAT, and collections wording;
- `5,000-15,000 PLN` accounting/clinical-trial admin contractor for ledger cleanup and evidence packs;
- `0-50,000 PLN` optional micro-advance only against one debtor-acknowledged receivable; initial wedge can be success-fee only to conserve capital;
- small CRM, secure file, and bank/lockbox setup.

Required proof:

1. Signed conditional option or revenue-share acquisition over one specialist's clinical-trial payment admin book with at least 3 active site clients.
2. At least `150,000 PLN` in open, undisputed, documented site receivables identified across sponsor/CRO debtors.
3. Signed collection mandate or assignment over at least `50,000 PLN` of receivables.
4. Debtor/AP/CRO acknowledgement or written payment status confirmation for at least one assigned/mandated receivable.
5. First collected cash of at least `25,000 PLN` within 60 days.
6. No clinical decision-making, no patient recruitment, no medicine sale, no Rx advertising, no patient-data monetization.
7. Only minimum redacted visit/milestone evidence is handled; patient identifiers are excluded unless legally necessary and covered by site-controlled processing terms.

## Economics

Revenue:

- `8-15%` success fee on recovered aged/underpaid amounts;
- `1-4%` payment-admin fee on current study-payment flows;
- optional `2-6%` discount/micro-advance spread where assignment is clean and debtor has acknowledged;
- recurring monthly fee for payment ledger, invoice package, query-to-cash, and AP chase workflow.

Payback logic:

- first cash can arrive from an already-earned receivable rather than new customer demand;
- capital is mostly legal/admin unless optional advances are used;
- gross margin can exceed `60%` on success-fee recovery after contractor/legal allocation if claims are undisputed and evidence is strong;
- founder avoids financing broad receivable portfolios until debtor acknowledgement and collection proof exist.

## Why This Is Not A Service/Report/Dashboard/Marketplace

The founder is not selling generic receivables software or consulting. The asset is an acquired/optioned specialist book plus assigned/mandated receivables and debtor-notified payment flow. The POC only passes if cash is collected from a real sponsor/CRO debtor.

## Copy Risk

Generic factoring companies can finance invoices, but usually do not parse clinical trial CTAs, EDC-triggered milestones, screen-fail rules, pass-throughs, query holds, closeout fees, and sponsor/CRO payment portals. CRO payment platforms serve sponsors and CROs, not necessarily small sites chasing unpaid amounts. The founder's defense is narrow specialization plus assigned receivables and AP acknowledgements, not software novelty.

## Primary Caveats

- CTAs may prohibit assignment or require sponsor/CRO consent.
- Some claims may be disputed, query-held, or insufficiently documented.
- A founder must avoid handling unnecessary patient data and must use site-controlled/redacted evidence packs.
- It can become generic factoring if it lacks clinical-trial payment expertise and debtor acknowledgements.
- Under `100,000 PLN`, the founder should start with success-fee recovery and only use micro-advances after clean assignment and debtor acknowledgement.
- There may be AML/KYC, tax, interest/usury, debt-collection, and financial-services boundary questions requiring legal review.
