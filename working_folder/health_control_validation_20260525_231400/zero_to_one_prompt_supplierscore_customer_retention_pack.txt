Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
SupplierScore Customer Retention Pack

One-sentence thesis:
Polish and CEE SME suppliers will pay for a 5-business-day evidence-response pack when a named enterprise customer, supplier portal, or procurement team has put onboarding, renewal, preferred-supplier status, a framework agreement, or a purchase order at risk because the supplier's EcoVadis, IntegrityNext, Sedex, SAQ, or similar responsible-sourcing score, reassessment, corrective-action upload, or evidence file is missing, expired, red, yellow, or below the customer's required minimum.

Exact buyer:
50-300 employee Polish/CEE manufacturing, packaging, components, food-ingredient, maintenance, logistics, light-chemicals, and industrial-service suppliers that sell to large EU/UK enterprise customers and have a written customer request, platform status, reassessment deadline, corrective-action deadline, or supplier-portal note tied to a material customer account.

Acute trigger:
A named customer or supplier platform has written that supplier onboarding, renewal, a PO, preferred-supplier status, a framework agreement, a supplier scorecard, or a customer procurement workflow is blocked, at risk, or materially delayed until the supplier submits missing evidence, completes a reassessment, uploads corrective-action evidence, fixes document gaps, explains unavailable data, or gets the customer to accept a bounded response.

Control point:
The startup controls one live customer-platform response file through a signed supplier mandate covering the exact customer request, platform workspace screenshots/exports where authorized, current scorecard or reassessment status, evidence folder, upload register, customer-request chronology, response owner, decision log, and submitted evidence/CAPA/no-go packet. The customer account and platform remain owned by the supplier; the startup controls the case file and response workflow for the scoped blocker.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three prepaid supplier cases, each with a named enterprise customer or platform blocker, signed authority to prepare the response file, customer request text or portal status, evidence folder access, and a supplier decision owner. At least two cases must produce platform submission plus buyer or platform progress: accepted upload, corrected status, narrowed follow-up, customer procurement acknowledgement, PO/onboarding/renewal progress, or a paid clean no-go that the supplier can use internally.

6-month POC:
Close 10-18 paid live cases, collect 120,000-350,000 PLN, produce 7+ buyer/platform progress or clean no-go outcomes, win at least 3 repeat buyers or partner-routed second cases, keep gross margin above 60%, deliver evidence-ready cases inside 5 business days, reject at least 25% of weak or non-material cases, and build a private pattern library of customer wording, platform flags, accepted evidence structures, missing-evidence categories, and no-go boundaries.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a targeted list of Polish/CEE suppliers visible in large-company supplier pages, automotive/industrial/packaging/food/logistics supply chains, tender award lists, export directories, LinkedIn customer references, and quality/procurement job postings that mention EcoVadis, IntegrityNext, Sedex, SAQ, responsible sourcing, supplier scorecards, or customer ESG requirements. Send direct written outbound asking only for live customer/platform blockers, not general sustainability interest. In parallel, approach accountants, ISO/quality consultants, fractional procurement people, and small ESG boutiques as referral partners for urgent cases they do not want to staff.

Economics:
8,000-18,000 PLN fixed fee for one customer/platform response pack; 18,000-40,000 PLN for urgent multi-site or multi-customer reassessment sprints; optional 3,000-5,000 PLN paid triage that ends in accept, rework, or no-go. Cost is founder evidence assembly plus limited contractor review for HR, environmental, or procurement edge cases. Gross margin target is above 60%. The value case is protecting a customer account, onboarding, renewal, PO, or preferred-supplier status, not improving ESG optics.

Copy risk:
EcoVadis advisers, ESG consultants, accountants, procurement consultants, ISO/quality firms, customer-success teams, and the platforms themselves can copy parts of the workflow. The startup must avoid broad sustainability reporting and compete on live customer-blocker speed, strict no-go discipline, upload-ready evidence structure, customer-specific response wording, and repeated pattern memory across similar Polish/CEE supplier cases.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
For the first cases, the signed mandate, exact customer request, platform status, evidence folder, upload chronology, response register, and supplier decision log are controlled before outsiders can see the file. The compounding advantage is not the idea of ESG help; it is the accumulating map of which customer phrases, platform flags, evidence objects, policy records, corrective-action uploads, and no-go explanations actually move Polish/CEE suppliers from blocked or at-risk status to customer progress.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It does not sell generic ESG consulting, a sustainability report, a dashboard, a marketplace, or introductions to advisers. It starts only when there is a live customer or platform blocker tied to a material customer account. The deliverable is a scoped response file: evidence index, missing-evidence split, upload register, corrective-action evidence, customer-ready explanation of unavailable data, submitted response packet, and a progress/no-go ledger.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No legal opinion, ESG assurance, audit certification, carbon-footprint calculation without source data, labor-law advice, human-rights investigation, environmental permit advice, green-claims substantiation, or claim that a score will improve. The supplier remains responsible for truth, policies, practices, platform account, customer communications, and final approval. Cases involving suspected false evidence, wage abuse, severe safety incidents, bribery, forced labor, sanctions, litigation, or material environmental violations are rejected or routed to qualified counsel/specialists.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: this is customer/platform responsible-sourcing score rescue for SME suppliers, not regulated-finance ICT third-party-risk evidence. Not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs: no public lighting tender, EPREL product matching, or SKU bid pack. Not PromoLeak: no trade deductions or recovery audit. Not CBAM Import Continuity: no customs broker, CBAM import reporting, or shipment continuity file. Not REDBlocked: no Amazon ASIN suppression or connected-device compliance. Not BioSignal: no investor diligence or health claim review. Not HeritageDoor: no physical salvage asset.

Strongest anticipated objections:
EcoVadis and ESG consultants already exist. Some suppliers cannot improve quickly because the missing issue is real operations, policies, environmental data, workforce records, supplier controls, or corrective action rather than evidence packaging. Platform scoring and customer acceptance are outside the startup's control. Customers may not truly block enough revenue to justify the fee. Supplier evidence can be sensitive, incomplete, outdated, or misleading. The work can drift into generic ESG consulting unless every case starts with a written customer/platform blocker and a material customer account. Case memory helps, but incumbents can copy surface templates if the startup fails to capture repeated accepted-evidence patterns.
