## Internal Caps Applied

- Not capped below 82: the control proof is concrete and cash-linked: policy, credit limit, unpaid invoice, delivery proof, claim/deficiency trail, broker/insurer acknowledgement.
- Not capped below 85: although brokers can copy, many brokers focus on placing policies and renewals rather than reconstructing messy claim evidence; the mandate controls the exact live file.
- Not capped below 87: economics are credible only with 250,000+ PLN insured receivables, prepaid screen fees, and rejection of low-value/no-document cases.
- Residual risk: the file may still collapse into policy interpretation, debt collection, insolvency law, or broker-owned normal claims administration. This risk is material but narrower than generic recovery desks because the buyer already paid for cover and has a claim route.
