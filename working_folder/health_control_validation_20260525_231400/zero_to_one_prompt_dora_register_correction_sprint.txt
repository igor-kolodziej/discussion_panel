Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
DORA Register Correction Sprint

One-sentence thesis:
A 7-business-day row-level correction sprint for small Polish/CEE payment institutions, insurers, leasing groups, fintech lenders, electronic-money institutions, and other smaller regulated financial entities whose DORA ICT third-party Register of Information is rejected, incomplete, internally unauditable, or blocked before board, group, auditor, KNF, or supervisory submission.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized regulated financial entities in Poland/CEE: payment institutions, electronic-money institutions, insurance intermediaries or undertakings, leasing/financing groups, fintech lenders, small banks or cooperative banking groups, investment firms, and regulated fintechs that must maintain DORA ICT third-party information but lack a clean row-level register. The buyer is the compliance, risk, operations, procurement, outsourcing, audit, or board owner who has real contracts and existing vendors but a messy register.

Acute trigger:
The entity has a live DORA Register of Information problem: internal audit flags missing ICT service rows; group compliance asks for corrected templates; board signoff is blocked; KNF or another supervisor asks questions; external auditors cannot trace rows to contracts; critical/important-function classification is inconsistent; LEI/entity identifiers, contract dates, subcontractors, service locations, exit fields, cloud details, or termination fields are missing; or the entity submitted once but needs correction/re-submission or annual refresh before the next control deadline.

Control point:
Prepaid live correction case with the entity's current register export/template, ICT vendor list, contracts and amendments, outsourcing/procurement files, cloud/subprocessor records, critical/important-function mapping, responsible owners, service country/location data, subcontractor and chain data where available, exit/termination fields, missing-field log, evidence room, accountable owner signoff path, and upload-ready corrected register file. The startup controls the row-level evidence index, source-to-register mapping, gap/no-go log, owner question list, reviewer notes, and corrected file package for that active board/audit/supervisory event.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three regulated financial entities or two entities plus one DORA/cyber/audit partner sign prepaid live register correction cases at 20,000-80,000 PLN each; every case has an existing register file or template, real ICT contracts, named internal owner, board/audit/group/supervisory deadline, and source documents; at least two cases produce accepted internal audit progress, board-ready file, group-compliance acceptance, supervisor-response package, or upload-ready corrected register; one partner routes a second case; gross margin stays above 60% after DORA/legal-boundary/cyber reviewer costs; fewer than 25% of files drift into legal advice, contract renegotiation, full DORA program work, GRC implementation, or vendor remediation.

6-month POC:
Deliver 15-35 paid register correction sprints; collect 500,000-2,000,000 PLN in prepaid case fees and urgent correction fees; document 12+ board/audit/group/supervisory progress outcomes, 8 no-go/escalation logs, and 5 repeat or annual refresh requests; build a private taxonomy of register row defects by entity type, ICT service category, contract evidence, missing field, subcontractor issue, LEI/entity identifier issue, cloud/service-location ambiguity, critical/important-function mapping gap, exit/termination field gap, and accepted correction wording; keep turnaround under 7 business days for evidence-ready cases; keep legal/compliance conclusions with the entity and its advisers.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target small regulated financial entities and partner channels already close to them: boutique DORA consultants, cyber firms, auditors, outsourcing/procurement advisers, fintech lawyers, fractional compliance officers, and core-banking/payments consultants. Pitch only live register blockers: "If your DORA Register of Information rows do not tie cleanly to contracts, vendor/service records, subcontractor details, entity identifiers, cloud locations, exit fields, or owner signoff, I turn the existing file and contract folder into a row-level corrected package in seven business days. No legal opinions, no DORA certification, no GRC implementation."

Economics:
Charge 20,000-35,000 PLN for a narrow register defect correction sprint under 50 ICT service rows, 35,000-80,000 PLN for multi-entity or multi-template correction, and 80,000-150,000 PLN for urgent group/audit/supervisory response packages with many vendors. Pay legal-boundary, DORA, cyber, or outsourcing reviewers only for flagged sections. Buyers pay because a broken register creates board, audit, supervisory, and management risk, while a full consulting or GRC implementation can be slower and more expensive than a targeted correction sprint.

Copy risk:
Big Four firms, DORA consultants, GRC tools, law firms, cyber firms, auditors, outsourcing advisers, and internal compliance teams can copy pieces. The defense is speed, row-level artifact discipline, local Polish/CEE financial-sector wording, small-entity affordability, partner overflow, and accumulated correction memory across actual register defects. The first wedge is not broad DORA readiness; it is an urgent correction sprint for live register files that advisers and internal teams do not want to clean manually.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
After the entity signs the prepaid live correction case and shares its register export, ICT contracts, vendor list, owner map, deadline, and evidence folder, the startup controls the active row-level correction package, gap/no-go log, reviewer notes, and board/audit/supervisory response file for that event. A large consultancy or GRC vendor can sell an alternative project, but it must displace an in-flight corrected register package while the deadline is already active.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only prepaid live register correction blockers with an existing register file/template, contracts, source evidence, and a deadline. The output is an operational compliance artifact: corrected Register of Information rows, source-to-row evidence index, missing-field log, owner question list, reviewer notes, signoff path, and upload-ready/board-ready file package. It is not a generic DORA readiness report, dashboard, GRC implementation, legal memo, audit, marketplace, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, DORA certification, supervisory guarantees, outsourcing-contract negotiation, audit opinion, GRC-tool implementation, ICT risk assessment, cybersecurity remediation, contract redlines, or compliance signoff. The financial entity, management body, compliance/risk/audit owners, lawyers, auditors, DORA advisers, ICT owners, and supervisors remain responsible for legal conclusions, register submission, criticality classification, contractual adequacy, and final signoff. Cases where contracts or owners do not support a truthful register row are marked gap/no-go rather than papered over.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because that confirmed idea serves ICT/software vendors responding to bank/insurer/payment-customer renewal questionnaires and supplier evidence requests. This idea serves regulated financial entities themselves and fixes their own DORA Register of Information rows for board, audit, group, KNF, or supervisory events. The control point, buyer, payment event, and proof artifact are materially different: corrected register rows and source-to-row evidence for the regulated entity, not vendor-side buyer evidence for a supplier renewal. It is not REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, Data Act, CBAM, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Large DORA consultancies, GRC tools, law firms, auditors, cyber firms, and internal compliance teams may already own this work. Many financial entities may have submitted first registers already, making the remaining market cleanup and annual refresh rather than urgent first-time panic. Small entities may still prefer their existing auditor, lawyer, or DORA adviser. Register corrections can require legal interpretation, contract remediation, criticality classification, outsourcing-policy decisions, or vendor outreach outside scope. Supervisory acceptance remains outside founder control. Sensitive ICT contracts and outsourcing data create trust and security barriers. The idea may be too close to the confirmed DORA vendor-side evidence pack unless it stays strictly financial-entity register correction. The workflow can become generic DORA consulting unless it accepts only live register files with deadlines and source evidence.
