## Internal Simulation

Score: **88.2 / 100**

### Why It Clears The Simulation Gate

- The asset is not an opinion, report, dashboard, or generic audit. It is a named cash deposit, bank guarantee, insurance guarantee, surety bond, standby LC, or guarantee-limit blockage tied to a public/private contract event.
- The proof is hard: beneficiary release instruction, authority acknowledgement, bank/insurer cancellation, freed guarantee line, refunded bid deposit, reduced bond amount, scheduled refund, bank statement, or written no-go.
- The buyer has a current cash/capacity reason to pay: blocked guarantee limits constrain new bids, new contracts, working-capital lines, or bonding capacity.
- The founder does not need to litigate entitlement if intake is restricted to expired tenders, accepted handovers, signed no-claim protocols, documented step-down dates, or beneficiary acknowledgements.
- CAC can be efficient through banks, surety brokers, bid-writing agencies, public-procurement consultants, accountants, project managers, and CFOs at contractors or IT/facility suppliers.
- Capital need is low. Work requires document intake, schedule tracking, beneficiary/bank routing, finance-ops contractors, and legal boundary review for rejects.

### Internal Cap Pressure

- Cap pressure from incumbent procurement admins, surety brokers, lawyers, and contractor CFO teams.
- Cap pressure from disputes: defect claims, unresolved acceptance, pending penalties, subcontractor claims, and ambiguous guarantee wording can turn the work into legal collection.
- Cap pressure from small deposits and automatic tender refunds.
- Cap pressure from bank/authority response time.

The candidate still clears because the intake can reject disputed or low-value files and focus only on high-value named guarantees where release or step-down should already be mechanically available.

## 60-Day Control Proof

- 5 signed mandates over named guarantees/deposits.
- 1,500,000-6,000,000 PLN equivalent in blocked guarantee capacity or deposits screened.
- 750,000-3,000,000 PLN accepted after exclusions.
- 3 beneficiary/authority release acknowledgements, refund approvals, or step-down confirmations.
- 2 bank/insurer cancellations, guarantee-limit releases, cash refunds, or scheduled releases above 150,000 PLN each.
- 40,000-120,000 PLN fees collected or invoiced.
- 40%+ rejection/no-go rate for disputed, premature, low-value, or legally unclear files.

## Rejection Rules

Reject cases involving:

- active defects, penalties, claims, withheld acceptance, insolvency, litigation, arbitration, or payment disputes;
- ambiguous entitlement to release or reduction;
- missing guarantee instrument, contract, acceptance protocol, release date, or beneficiary identity;
- cash deposits below 50,000 PLN or guarantee capacity below 150,000 PLN unless bundled;
- requests to pressure a beneficiary on disputed performance;
- public-procurement legal advice, claim drafting, tender appeals, or contract interpretation beyond document routing.

## Why It Is Not Duplicate

Not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, factoring reserve, AP credit cash-out, construction retention recovery, grant reimbursement, customs/import continuity, DORA, REDBlocked, GreenTender, TraceFaktura, HeritageDoor, or CBAM.

The distinctive control point is a signed mandate over a named tender/performance/warranty guarantee, bid deposit, surety bond, standby LC, or guarantee-line blockage where a beneficiary/authority/bank/insurer release action converts into freed capacity or refunded cash.
