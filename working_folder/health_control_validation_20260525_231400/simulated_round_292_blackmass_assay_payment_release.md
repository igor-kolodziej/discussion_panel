## Lead Candidate

**BlackMass Assay Payment-Release Lockbox**

### Why It Advances

The control point is materially harder than a recovery mandate or evidence desk because the buyer's money is attached to a specific lot and a specific acceptance issue. The startup only accepts cases with:

- named material lot or batch;
- seller/offtaker contract, PO, invoice, or statement;
- written buyer/offtaker hold, haircut, rejection, or clarification request;
- independent or source assay documents where available;
- weight/moisture/impurity/mass-balance/chain-of-custody file;
- licensed-party transport/waste documents handled by the responsible parties;
- clear no-go boundaries for legal, waste, transport, fraud, sanctions, or safety issues.

### Buyer / Seller

The paying customer is a small or mid-sized Polish/CEE battery recycler, pre-processor, battery-scrap trader, dismantler, production-scrap holder, or industrial seller that has already sold or tendered a black-mass, cathode/anode scrap, battery-production scrap, or battery-material lot to an offtaker/refiner but has a current payment hold, assay dispute, moisture/impurity haircut, mass-balance question, chain-of-custody gap, or accepted-lot documentation issue.

### Control Point

- Signed lot-payment release mandate.
- Exact lot ID, batch ID, weigh tickets, sample references, photos where lawful, shipment references, invoice, PO/offtake reference, and buyer/offtaker issue text.
- Assay reports, sampling method notes, moisture/impurity data, mass-balance file, material description, prior correspondence, and responsible-party transport/waste records.
- Independent reviewer issue spotter for assay/mass-balance logic.
- Seller-approved response packet to the buyer/offtaker.
- Buyer/offtaker acceptance, corrected haircut, released holdback, partial payment, payment schedule, or clean written no-go.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Four signed mandates over named lots with at least 2,000,000 PLN total held, haircut, or disputed payment value.
2. At least two lots have independent lab assay or offtaker assay documents plus weigh/moisture/mass-balance evidence.
3. One battery/recycling materials reviewer retained on a paid per-case basis.
4. One buyer/offtaker acceptance, corrected settlement statement, released holdback, partial payment, or explicit written no-go.
5. At least 40,000 PLN collected through fixed lot-review fees, release fees, or success fees.
6. Every case touching illegal shipment, unclear waste authority, missing source truth, suspected fraud, sanctions, unsafe handling, or legal dispute is rejected.

### Economics

Use a 5,000-12,000 PLN paid lot-screen fee for material values above 250,000 PLN, 12,000-30,000 PLN for an urgent full release packet, and 1-3% of released holdback, corrected haircut, or released payment value. The 100,000 PLN budget is enough for secure document handling, reviewer retainers, targeted sourcing, and early case delivery because the startup does not buy inventory, finance claims, or handle material.

### Copy Risk

Battery recyclers, scrap traders, refiners, labs, waste brokers, environmental consultants, accountants, and in-house commercial teams can copy generic checklists. They cannot copy a signed mandate over a specific lot, the seller's evidence room, buyer/offtaker issue thread, release response, and fee right once controlled. The longer-term defense is a private acceptance/no-go taxonomy by offtaker, material type, assay pattern, moisture/impurity issue, mass-balance gap, and document defect.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The product is a controlled payment-release case for a named material lot. The first proof is buyer/offtaker acceptance, released payment, corrected settlement, or a no-go that prevents further wasted effort. The startup does not match buyers and sellers, list inventory, trade material, issue assays, provide dashboards, or produce generic compliance reports.

### Boundaries

The startup does not own, buy, sell, broker, transport, store, process, sample, classify, export, import, or certify waste batteries, black mass, hazardous waste, or battery materials. It does not issue assays, recycling-efficiency verification, recycled-content verification, legal opinions, waste-shipment advice, transport documents, sanctions/origin opinions, or environmental compliance certifications. Licensed operators, labs, waste brokers, transporters, recyclers, refiners, customs/waste advisers, and legal counsel remain responsible for regulated actions and decisions.

### Duplicate Risk

- Not BatteryFit: this is not PV battery retrofit economics for installers.
- Not Battery Passport or Battery Regulation due-diligence consulting: it handles named lot payment release only after a commercial offtaker issue.
- Not BDO DPR/EDPR allocation: no statutory recycling-document allocation, no BDO document trading, no producer-obligation compliance.
- Not UCO/recycling route buyout: no physical route, no collection logistics, no material brokerage.
- Not RMA/warranty credit recovery: no OEM warranty or service campaign.
- Not generic AP/credit memo recovery: the control object is assay/mass-balance acceptance for a named battery-material lot.
- Not BioSignal, REDBlocked, DORA, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, or other confirmed themes.
