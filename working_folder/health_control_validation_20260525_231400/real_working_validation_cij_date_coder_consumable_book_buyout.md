## Zero To One Judgment

Zero To One treated this as a strong recurring-revenue micro-acquisition idea, especially because it avoids random inventory and requires a seller-endorsed reorder book, transferred payment direction, exact printer/fluid compatibility map, and prepaid current-cycle orders before closing.

The idea still failed because OEMs, authorized distributors, compatible-fluid suppliers, and online suppliers are serious competition. The evaluator also emphasized compatibility/warranty/QA risk, seller scarcity, customer churn after handoff, gross-margin pressure, emergency support creep, and inventory/supplier-term risk.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. OEMs and authorized distributors can recapture customers by arguing official fluids protect print quality, safety, warranty, uptime, and production performance.
2. Compatibility mistakes can destroy trust immediately through bad codes, clogged printheads, quality holds, rejected batches, downtime, safety issues, or warranty disputes.
3. Retiring sellers with clean transferable books may be rare; many may have messy records, stale customers, personal trust, or informal supplier terms.
4. Customers may approve one transition order and later switch back to OEM channels or cheaper online suppliers.
5. Gross margin can collapse under price transparency because many compatible consumables are available online.
6. Emergency support creep is dangerous: plants may call for line-down printer faults, not just consumable reorders.
7. Inventory risk remains around shelf life, storage, wrong SKUs, slow movers, leaks, customer churn, and supplier discontinuation.
8. Supplier terms may not transfer from the retiring reseller.
9. Food, pharma, cosmetics, and quality-sensitive plants may require QA approval for supplier, SKU, SDS, batch, packaging, or fluid-source changes.
10. The first book may be too small to support owner earnings unless reorder value, margin, density, and support limits are strong.

## Useful Narrowing

Zero To One said the best first acquisition is a retiring CIJ consumable reseller with 20-40 active production customers, 60+ mapped printers, and repeat orders of known exact SKUs.

Only proceed if:

- seller provides 24-month order history;
- printer/SKU compatibility map exists or can be built;
- 20+ customers approve payee transfer;
- 50,000+ PLN prepaid current-cycle orders clear before seller payout;
- same SKUs and sources continue after handoff;
- seller does warm phone/email handoff;
- inventory exposure stays under prepaid demand;
- support scope excludes printer repair unless separately contracted.

Even with proof of 40 active customers, 250,000-500,000 PLN annualized run-rate, gross margin above 25%, compatibility exceptions below 5%, handling time below 20 minutes/order, repeat orders after first transferred cycle, and one second seller source, Zero To One estimated the score would rise only to 78-82.

## Decision

Reject. Do not continue commodity or semi-commodity B2B consumable books unless the founder controls something stronger than seller trust, compatibility memory, and current-cycle orders.
