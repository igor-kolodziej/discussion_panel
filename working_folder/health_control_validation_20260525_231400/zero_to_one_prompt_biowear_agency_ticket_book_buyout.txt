Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 2-month POC, part-time. Cap below 85 without a hard control point securable in 2 months. Cap below 87 if CAC, payback, margins, cash conversion, or owner earnings are not credible.

Idea: BioWear Regulated-Listing Ticket-Book Buyout.

Acquire or option a small Amazon/Allegro account-health, reinstatement, or e-commerce compliance agency's regulated-product ticket book covering connected wellness, sleep, recovery, baby/sleep monitoring, HRV, temperature, and non-medical biosignal wearables.

The acquired/optioned asset is the live channel, not a generic consulting offer:
- client mandates for current or recent regulated-product evidence tickets;
- agency inbox/tagged ticket history;
- seller contact list and signed permission to transfer/escalate;
- support scripts and response templates;
- current blocked or at-risk listings;
- payment direction or white-label resale agreement;
- agency owner handoff;
- exclusive 6-12 month routing of connected wearable/RED/GPSR evidence tickets;
- reviewer/lab escalation bench.

The startup does not certify, test, sell devices, handle user health data, validate health claims, give legal advice, or guarantee reinstatement. It owns the regulated-listing case-flow and document-response operating layer.

Seller:
- small marketplace agency, Amazon account-health consultant, Allegro/e-commerce consultant, or CE/product-compliance agency that handles occasional regulated connected-device tickets but does not want to build a specialist desk.

Economic customer:
- connected wellness/sleep/biosignal device seller with a blocked ASIN/listing/payment/ad/inventory flow.

Acute trigger:
The acquired agency already has clients or ticket history where channel revenue is blocked by CE/DoC rejection, GPSR responsible-person/manufacturer/safety info, RED or RED cybersecurity evidence, app/cloud/data-flow questions, battery/charger/manual/label mismatches, wellness vs medical claim wording, supplier-document mismatch, or missing model traceability.

Control point:
- signed option/exclusive routing agreement over the agency's regulated connected-device ticket lane;
- direct seller approvals or agency white-label authority;
- payment direction or collected prepaid fees before close;
- current live tickets assigned to the founder;
- agency handoff and support inbox/ticket history;
- reviewer/lab bench;
- case-memory database built from inherited + new cases.

No assigned ticket flow, no deal.

2-month proof:
1. One signed option or exclusive routing agreement with a small agency over all connected wellness/wearable/RED/GPSR evidence tickets for 6-12 months.
2. At least 3 live or recent tickets assigned with seller permission or white-label agency authority.
3. At least 2 clients pay the new workflow directly or through an agreed agency payment split.
4. 25,000+ PLN collected/prepaid before final close.
5. One dossier submitted to Amazon/Allegro/channel and either unblocked, progressed, escalated, or killed as impossible.
6. One RED/CE/product-safety reviewer and one privacy/app-data reviewer contracted per-case.
7. 60%+ gross margin after agency split, reviewers, and founder time.
8. Case-memory database with inherited ticket type, evidence requested, documents available, supplier defects, response wording, and outcome.

First acquisition:
- Contact 50 Amazon/Allegro/e-commerce agencies and ask specifically for regulated-product ticket lanes they do not want to staff.
- Offer a non-cash-heavy structure: 20-35% of collected gross revenue to the agency for transferred clients plus a small option fee only after the first paid ticket.
- Accept only if the agency routes all connected wearable/RED/GPSR blocks exclusively for 6-12 months.
- Reject ordinary referral partnerships because they do not create control.

Economics:
- 7,500-12,000 PLN full dossier per assigned case.
- 15,000-30,000 PLN multi-ASIN seller sprint.
- Agency receives 20-35% of collected revenue.
- Reviewer costs capped to flagged sections.
- Gross margin target: 60%+ after agency/reviewer costs.
- Cash conversion is stronger than a generic desk because the first cases and payment flow are assigned before close.

Why this is not a generic evidence desk:
The prior weaker version was a desk selling into agencies. This version buys or options the agency's actual case-flow and payment path. The business only starts if a signed agency route, assigned tickets, and collected prepaid cash exist. Otherwise it is rejected.

Duplicate risk:
Closest confirmed analogue is REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk. This variant is different because it is an acquisition/option model controlling an existing agency's regulated-product ticket flow and payment direction, focused on connected wellness/sleep/biosignal wearables. If that is not different enough, treat it as a REDBlocked expansion rather than a new idea.
