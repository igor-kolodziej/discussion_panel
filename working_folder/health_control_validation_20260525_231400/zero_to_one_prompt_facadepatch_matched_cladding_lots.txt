Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
FacadePatch Matched Cladding Repair Lots

One-sentence thesis:
Control exact matched exterior facade and cladding repair lots before they are discarded or fragmented, then sell them with buyer deposits to property managers, facade contractors, insurers, boutique developers, and building owners facing active repair cases where visible mismatch, long OEM lead time, or full-elevation replacement would be expensive.

Exact buyer:
Commercial property managers, premium multifamily administrators, facade contractors, insurers or insurer-side repair coordinators, boutique developers, hotel/retail building owners, and building owners with active facade, cladding, stone, brick, terracotta, ceramic tile, rainscreen, spandrel, or special-brick repair needs. The best first buyers have a current damage, maintenance, insurance, tenant-opening, warranty, or facade-restoration case where a small exact matched lot avoids visible mismatch or larger replacement scope.

Acute trigger:
A building has damaged or missing exterior elements, but the original material is discontinued, long-lead, batch-specific, visually hard to match, or unavailable from the original supplier. The buyer needs a small exact lot now because mismatch damages the elevation, slows tenant opening, complicates insurance repair, or forces a larger replacement than the damaged area.

Control point:
Seller option or title over an exact matched repair lot with quantity, dimensions, profile, finish/color, batch/code markings where available, source chain, condition photos, storage/pickup terms, and pickup deadline; plus buyer deposit, conditional PO, or written reservation from a named active repair case before final lot purchase. Installation remains with the buyer's chosen facade contractor, glazier, stone mason, or installer.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three seller options over exact matched facade/cladding repair lots; three buyer deposits or conditional POs for named active repair cases before final lot purchase; one paid handoff of a lot to a buyer-selected facade contractor, property manager, or building owner; at least 50,000 PLN of buyer deposits or conditional orders; lot documentation for every controlled lot covering quantity, dimensions, profile, color/finish, batch/markings, condition, photos, source chain, storage/pickup terms, and installation boundary.

6-month POC:
Control 12-20 matched repair lots; secure 10 buyer deposits or conditional POs; complete 5 paid handoffs; collect 150,000-450,000 PLN revenue with gross margin above 45% before founder salary; build a private source and demand map by material, facade system, dimensions, profile, color/finish, urgency, and buyer type; kill if fewer than 25% of controlled lots receive buyer deposits within 45 days or if installation, warranty, code, fire, anchoring, or weatherproofing objections dominate.

First acquisition mechanism:
Source lots from demolition firms, facade contractors, stone yards, tile distributors, glaziers, developers, building administrators, insurers' repair networks, overstock yards, and contractors holding leftover crates from completed projects. Demand outreach goes to property managers, facade contractors, insurer repair coordinators, building owners, and boutique developers with a concrete offer: "Reserve an exact matched repair lot for your active facade repair before the remaining material is split, scrapped, or lost."

Economics:
Option small lots for 500-3,000 PLN and buy only after buyer deposit or conditional PO. Typical acquisition plus pickup/staging cost is 2,000-20,000 PLN. Resale or reservation price is 8,000-60,000 PLN depending on material, scarcity, quantity, active repair value, and avoided mismatch or full-elevation replacement. Buyer deposit funds pickup and prevents speculative storage. Target 30-60% gross margin.

Copy risk:
Facade contractors, property managers, demolition firms, stone yards, tile distributors, glaziers, and salvage dealers can copy the general idea. They cannot copy a specific optioned lot, buyer reservation, pickup window, source-chain file, and deposit once controlled. The longer-term advantage is source timing and exact-match memory: which buildings/materials are being stripped, which lots have batch markings, and which property managers or contractors pay for visible continuity.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup has a seller option or title file, lot card, pickup window, buyer deposit or conditional PO, and active repair-case handoff path, a competitor cannot deliver that same exact material without taking over the option or convincing both seller and buyer to break the pending transaction. They can source other lots later, but not the controlled exact batch.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup sells controlled title/options to exact repair lots with buyer deposits and paid handoffs. It does not provide facade design, engineering, installation, certification, insurance adjustment, renovation management, a searchable marketplace, or generic introductions. The proof artifact is a paid transfer or reservation of a controlled lot for a named repair case.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No structural certification, fire certification, weatherproofing guarantee, anchoring design, facade engineering, installation, conservation opinion, insurance adjusting, code compliance opinion, product certification, or warranty claim handling. Buyer-selected qualified contractors remain responsible for inspection, installation, suitability, anchoring, waterproofing, fire/code compliance, and final acceptance. Reject safety-critical, uncertifiable, unknown-origin, damaged, stolen, contaminated, asbestos-risk, fire-risk, or no-document lots.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not HeritageDoor because this is commercial and premium building exterior repair supply for active facade/cladding repair, not matched pre-war internal door suites for interior heritage renovations. Not hotel FF&E because it is palletized exact repair lots with buyer deposits, not bulky generic furniture liquidation. Not StormTile roof lots because the trigger is facade/elevation mismatch and active property-manager/facade-contractor repair, not generic roof-tile salvage. Not GreenTender, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, or health/Rx/clinical routes.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Facade contractors and original suppliers may already know where replacement materials sit. Exact lots may be rare, hard to identify, damaged, incomplete, badly stored, undocumented, or unsuitable. Installation and code boundaries can kill cases even if the visual match is good. Some active repairs require certified system components rather than salvage or overstock. Property managers may prefer replacement with new material if warranty and liability are clearer. Storage, breakage, transport, measurement errors, and color mismatch can destroy margins. Buyer deposits may be hard before installer inspection. The business may become another salvage operation if buyer-deposit-first discipline slips.
