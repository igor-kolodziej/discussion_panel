## Zero To One Judgment

Zero To One treated the idea as a real and fairly acute automotive supplier pain. It agreed that IMDS rejections tied to PPAP, ISIR, launch, engineering change, or customer release can block revenue and that accepted MDS IDs are more concrete than a compliance report.

The idea still failed because the work is already served by IMDS specialists, automotive quality consultants, and internal quality teams. The evaluator also emphasized that the founder cannot invent missing upstream material truth and that many blocked cases require lab, supplier, engineering, or compliance escalation outside the startup's control.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Good IMDS consultants and automotive quality firms already exist, especially around IATF, PPAP, APQP, and supplier quality.
2. Missing upstream material truth cannot be manufactured by workflow skill; if supplier declarations, SDSs, BOM truth, or material certificates are absent or contradictory, the case stalls.
3. An unknown solo founder is not credible without a named experienced IMDS/material-compliance reviewer.
4. Customer-specific OEM and Tier 1 requirements differ, so rejection patterns may be less reusable than the simulation assumed.
5. Some cases require lab/material analysis, supplier escalation, legal/compliance decisions, BOM correction, or engineering-change alignment outside scope.
6. Larger suppliers may already have quality, engineering, or compliance teams that handle IMDS internally.
7. IMDS access, customer portal authority, supplier authorization, and data-handling terms can slow case start.
8. Rejection outcomes can be opaque, so the offer must sell progress and source-truth triage rather than guaranteed acceptance.
9. Part-number volume can explode unless pricing is tied to part family, BOM complexity, and source-evidence quality.
10. Case flow may be lumpy unless consultant channels can repeatedly feed live blocker cases.

## Useful Narrowing

Zero To One said the best version is an "IMDS blocker release pack for named PPAP/ISIR part numbers" with:

- live request or rejection text;
- named part numbers;
- a PPAP, ISIR, customer approval, launch, or payment consequence;
- supplier authorization;
- available BOM, SDS, material certificate, and supplier declaration evidence;
- a paid IMDS reviewer;
- prepaid case fees;
- explicit refusal to invent missing source truth.

It suggested stronger pricing of 2,000-4,000 PLN for triage, 5,000-10,000 PLN per part family, and 12,000-25,000 PLN for urgent launch sprints. It also suggested a stronger POC of 12+ paid cases, 50+ part numbers, 10 accepted MDS IDs or measurable customer-progress outcomes, one consultant channel that sends 3+ cases, gross margin above 60%, and fewer than 30% of cases collapsing because source truth is missing.

Even with that stronger proof, Zero To One estimated the score would rise only to 82-84. This remains below the current gate.

## Decision

Reject. Do not continue ordinary accepted-portal or evidence-release desks unless the control point is materially stronger than a paid submission case and the proof is current cash, payment direction, assigned recovery, titled asset control, or another hard asset the founder can legally control inside 60 days.
