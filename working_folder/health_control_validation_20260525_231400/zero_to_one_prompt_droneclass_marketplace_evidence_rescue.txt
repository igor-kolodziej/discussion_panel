Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
DroneClass Marketplace Evidence Rescue Desk

One-sentence thesis:
EU drone sellers experience class-label and conformity rules as urgent marketplace or retail SKU blocks, while account-health agencies understand platform appeals but not EASA C0-C6 evidence; the wedge is an agency-routed response desk for live drone compliance requests.

Exact buyer:
Primary channel buyer: Amazon account-health agencies; marketplace-compliance agencies; ecommerce reinstatement consultants; EU/CEE drone ecommerce agencies; specialist drone retailers/importers that handle platform and retailer compliance internally.

Economic buyer: EU/CEE private-label or imported consumer/prosumer drone sellers whose ASIN/SKU/listing/retailer PO is suppressed, at risk, or blocked by C-class/CE/DoC/manual/remote-ID/geo-awareness evidence requests.

Acute trigger:
A live marketplace, retailer, distributor, or account-health request blocks an ASIN/SKU/PO/onboarding/inventory movement until the seller provides drone compliance evidence such as EU Declaration of Conformity referencing the correct drone model/class; C0-C6 class identification label evidence; CE marking and product photo evidence; serial/model matching; EASA information notice/manual evidence; remote identification, geo-awareness, height/low-speed mode, sound-power, or class-specific feature evidence where relevant; notified-body / EU-type examination route where relevant; truthful no-go where the seller lacks manufacturer evidence or the drone cannot support the claimed class.

Control point:
The startup controls one live platform/retailer response file: exact request text and deadline; ASIN/SKU/model/serial scope; supplier/manufacturer documents; product photos and label evidence; DoC/model/class matching matrix; class-feature checklist; supplier RFI; agency/customer response wording; submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed/no-go decision; platform case chronology.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days: one retained drone/EU product-compliance reviewer familiar with EASA UAS class evidence; three agency or retailer-channel partners willing to route live drone document blocks; three prepaid cases at 4,000-12,000 PLN each; at least two submitted response dossiers; and at least one hard outcome such as marketplace acknowledgement, accepted upload, narrowed follow-up, listing/onboarding progress, lab/notified-body escalation, or paid no-go.

6-month POC:
20-35 paid live cases; 10+ accepted uploads, narrowed follow-ups, listing/PO/onboarding progress events, lab escalations, or paid no-go outcomes; 5 repeat/referral agencies or retailer/importer partners; 180,000-500,000 PLN revenue; 60%+ gross margin after reviewer costs; private case library by request wording, drone class, missing documents, supplier country, evidence submitted, and outcome.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 60 Amazon/account-health/marketplace agencies and 30 EU drone retailers/importers. Recruit one drone compliance reviewer and one CE/product-safety reviewer on paid per-case terms. Send a narrow partner offer: "Your next drone ASIN/SKU compliance block goes to us. We turn the request, product photos, DoC, class-label and supplier evidence into a submit/no-go/lab-needed response within 48-72 hours. You keep the client relationship and margin."

Ask for live or recent drone compliance cases, not general calls.

Economics:
1,500-3,000 PLN paid request triage; 4,000-12,000 PLN for one ASIN/SKU response dossier; 12,000-30,000 PLN for multi-SKU seller sprint; 25-35% channel margin for agencies.

The seller pays to protect blocked or at-risk marketplace/retail revenue, not for generic compliance education.

Copy risk:
Copy risk is real from drone compliance consultants, CE consultants, notified bodies/labs, marketplace agencies, product-safety firms, and drone distributors.

The defense is narrow platform-case memory: actual request wording, document gaps, DoC/class mismatch patterns, supplier RFIs, submitted response structures, no-go patterns, and agency trust.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
They can copy the category, but they cannot work a specific live case before the founder controls the agency/seller mandate, exact marketplace/retailer request, ASIN/SKU/model scope, supplier evidence folder, reviewer-backed submit/no-go matrix, response chronology, and agency channel relationship.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is a live listing/PO/onboarding rescue packet for a named drone SKU blocker. It is not broad drone compliance consulting, legal advice, certification brokerage, operator training, a dashboard, a database, or a marketplace of labs.

The deliverable is a submission-ready response dossier and no-go/lab-needed decision tied to a specific platform or retailer request.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No drone certification. No class label creation. No notified-body representation. No operator flight advice. No CE/DoC invention. No legal advice or market-surveillance defense. No modification/retrofit claims unless documented by manufacturer and qualified reviewer. No false C0-C6, remote-ID, geo-awareness, sound-power, or class-feature claims.

Reject DIY drones, counterfeit labels, mismatched DoCs, missing manufacturer authority, unsafe products, and authority enforcement cases needing counsel.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not REDBlocked: REDBlocked is Amazon connected baby-monitor/indoor-camera evidence under RED cyber/GPSR/CE triggers. DroneClass is drone-specific EASA/UAS class-identification and Declaration-of-Conformity evidence for C0-C6 product claims.

Not BatteryFit or Amazon button-cell evidence: this is not battery safety, UN38.3, or button/coin-cell evidence.

Not GreenTender, CBAM, DORA, Data Act, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, or PromoLeak.

Strongest anticipated objections:
It may be too close to REDBlocked because both use marketplace-agency routing and live product-compliance blocks. Drone compliance consultants, CE consultants, labs, notified bodies, and distributors already understand the product. Many cases require manufacturer documents the seller cannot obtain. Hard cases may need formal certification, lab/notified-body action, or legal counsel. Amazon/marketplace outcomes remain opaque. Case volume may be narrow if generic CE/GPSR agencies already handle most drone requests. Seller willingness to pay may be weaker if the drone SKU has low revenue or is easy to replace. The founder needs a named drone/EU product-compliance reviewer before selling.
