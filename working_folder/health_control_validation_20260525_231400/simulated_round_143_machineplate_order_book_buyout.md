## Selected Candidate

## Why It Can Clear Simulation

This avoids the common traps:

- not a marketplace, dashboard, report, or advisory service;
- not a regulated medical/legal/financial workflow;
- not bulky inventory;
- not a route list without payment direction;
- not dependent on a licensed provider as core value;
- not a generic engraver startup from scratch.

The asset is a live, customer-specific B2B order stream: recurring machine builders, panel builders, and factories that already reorder rating plates, serial plates, valve tags, asset tags, and control-panel labels from a retiring supplier because the supplier holds exact artwork, serial templates, material specs, hole positions, finishes, fonts, logos, and reorder history.

## Internal Cap Notes

Why not higher:

- The market is fragmented and copyable if the founder only buys equipment or a generic website.
- Customers can switch to other engravers if artwork handoff and seller trust are weak.
- Gross margin can be killed by small orders, quoting time, rework, rush shipping, and production quality failures.
- The first seller must be unusually clean: repeat B2B customers, current POs, no custom chaos, and subcontractable production.

## Required Proof

- Signed option over a named retiring supplier's phone/domain/mailbox, customer files, artwork/template archive, quote history, current POs, and payment-direction rights.
- 20 named B2B customers with order history in the last 12 months.
- 10 customer-approved billing/support transfers.
- 25 current/recent SKUs with reusable templates and material specs.
- 30 delivered orders through an outsourced production partner or seller transition.
- First 40,000+ PLN collected under transferred payment direction.
- Gross margin above 45% after production, shipping, rework allowance, seller earnout, and admin time.
