Idea name: TachoArchive Download-Compliance Book Buyout. One-sentence thesis: Acquire or option tiny retiring transport-admin and telematics-installer books that already perform recurring tachograph and driver-card download/archive/reminder work for small EU/CEE cross-border carriers, but close only after customers approve payment transfer and current-month cash is collected. Exact buyer: Economic customers are small Polish/CEE cross-border carriers with 3-50 vehicles or driver cards that need recurring tachograph-data download, DDD archive custody, reminder, and inspection-ready export admin. Acquisition sellers are retiring or overloaded transport accountants, tachograph-data clerks, telematics installers, fleet-admin micro-providers, and small software resellers with active recurring customers. Acute trigger: Tachograph data is recurring, not a one-off install. Driver-card downloads, vehicle-unit downloads, remote-download device checks, archive retention, reminders, infringement exports, and inspection-ready DDD custody repeat monthly or quarterly. The 1 July 2026 EU LCV tachograph expansion adds more small fleets needing ongoing admin after installation. Control point: Signed seller option plus customer-approved transfer of the live recurring book: vehicle/driver-card register, download calendar, DDD archive, remote-download portal/admin credentials where lawful, SIM/device inventory if any, reminder templates, customer contracts, support inbox, phone/domain, issue history, and direct billing/payment direction. 60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof: One seller option; 20 customer approvals or 80 vehicle/driver-card units transferred; 30,000 PLN current-month or quarterly renewal cash collected before acquisition close; archive custody test; credential rotation; seller endorsement; written boundaries excluding legal advice, tachograph manipulation, workshop calibration, installation, and authority representation. 6-month POC: Control 2-3 micro-books totaling 200-400 active vehicle/driver-card units; collect 120,000-300,000 PLN gross revenue; keep gross margin above 60%; keep admin handling below 20 minutes per active unit per month after transition; source two sellers through the same path; keep churn below 15% after reconsent. First acquisition mechanism: Build a list of 150 retiring, small, or overloaded Polish/CEE transport accountants, tachograph-data clerks, telematics installers, fleet-admin freelancers, and small tachograph-software resellers. Offer a no-cash-upfront option: seller endorses transfer, only transferred/prepaid customers count toward closing value, and seller receives fixed multiple or earnout on retained monthly gross margin. Economics: Charge 69-149 PLN per vehicle or active driver-card unit per month. A 150-unit book at 99 PLN/month is about 14,850 PLN monthly gross revenue. Portal/software/storage/contractor admin costs below 35-40% of revenue. Seller consideration paid from retained recurring cash after customer reconsent. Copy risk: Telematics platforms, tachograph workshops, transport accountants, fleet-compliance firms, and software vendors can offer similar recurring admin. The defense is acquisition of the exact small customer book, seller endorsement, historical archive custody, customer reconsent, payment direction, and recurring reminder/download calendar before competitors know those accounts are movable. Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream: Before closing, the founder controls a signed option over a specific seller book and only pays for customers who approve transfer and redirect payment. Competitors cannot copy the already-signed seller endorsement, transferred archive, customer approvals, payment direction, and current-month collected cash for that exact micro-book. Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker: The startup does not build tachograph software, sell a dashboard, broker workshops, or write compliance reports. It acquires customer-approved recurring admin/payment books and proves current cash before paying for them. Not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, or HeatQuiet.
