Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
BESSGuard Warranty Commissioning Passport

One-sentence thesis:
A distributor-recognized commissioning and warranty-release passport for Polish/CEE commercial battery energy storage installations where final installer margin, preferred-installer status, extended warranty handling, replacement eligibility, or priority support is blocked until the distributor gets a clean BESS-specific evidence packet.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE commercial BESS distributors, battery/inverter wholesalers, manufacturer reps, warranty teams, and specialist BESS installers selling or supporting 20 kWh-2 MWh commercial, agricultural, industrial, retail, and community-scale battery systems. Best first buyers are distributors and warranty teams handling multiple installer partners, early warranty disputes, grid-code setting mistakes, battery/inverter mismatch, missing telemetry setup, poor handover files, or repeated support tickets after commissioning.

Acute trigger:
A BESS distributor or warranty team is delaying, downgrading, or manually reviewing installer benefits because new commercial battery installations arrive with incomplete commissioning evidence: inverter/BMS pairing, firmware versions, serial numbers, SoH baseline, battery rack photos, ventilation/clearance photos, protection settings, grid-code settings, EMS/export-limit settings, thermal placement, cable/protection documentation, telemetry portal connection, alarm screenshots, fire-separation evidence supplied by the installer, customer handover, and first operating snapshot. The economic gate is warranty handling, preferred-installer status, reserve/final-margin release, replacement eligibility, faster RMA triage, rebate eligibility, or priority distributor support.

Control point:
Signed distributor or warranty-channel recognition of a narrow BESSGuard packet for named installer jobs, plus installer authorization, project list, model/serial map, commissioning checklist, photo set, settings exports/screenshots, telemetry/BMS portal snapshot, firmware/version evidence, customer handover confirmation, exception log, reviewer notes, and accepted/rework/no-go status. The startup controls the BESS commissioning evidence workflow for the distributor-recognized gate, not the electrical installation, fire design, grid connection, warranty decision, or product certification.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
One BESS distributor, wholesaler, warranty team, or manufacturer rep signs a written pilot recognizing the BESSGuard packet as a condition or fast-track for one economic gate such as preferred-installer status, priority warranty review, final margin/rebate release, RMA triage, or installer ranking. Five commercial BESS jobs from at least two installers are prepaid at 2,500-8,000 PLN per job; every job includes serials, model map, photos, settings/firmware evidence, telemetry/BMS screenshot, SoH or baseline status where available, handover proof, and exception log; at least three packets are accepted, fast-tracked, or returned with specific rework items by the distributor; one installer submits a second job; gross margin stays above 60% after contractor electrical/BESS review.

6-month POC:
Secure two distributor or warranty-channel recognition pilots; process 40-80 commercial BESS commissioning packets across 8-15 installers; collect 180,000-500,000 PLN in packet and channel fees; document at least 25 accepted/fast-tracked/reworked/no-go packets; get at least four installers to submit repeat jobs; reduce distributor manual review time or support back-and-forth by at least 30% on accepted packet classes; maintain a private taxonomy of BESS commissioning gaps, battery/inverter mismatch, firmware/settings issues, grid-code/export-limit mistakes, telemetry setup failures, SoH baseline problems, thermal/clearance photo gaps, and handover defects.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a target list of Polish/CEE commercial BESS distributors, inverter/battery wholesalers, manufacturer reps, warranty teams, and specialist BESS installers. Pitch only the economic gate: "If you are paying warranty/support cost because installer job files are messy, I will run a fixed BESS commissioning passport pilot for five commercial jobs. You define the fields that fast-track warranty review, installer ranking, final margin/rebate release, or RMA triage; installers pay per job; qualified electrical/BESS reviewers handle flagged technical checks; you keep the warranty decision."

Economics:
Charge installers 2,500-4,000 PLN for evidence-ready small commercial systems, 5,000-8,000 PLN for larger or multi-inverter systems, and 8,000-15,000 PLN for urgent warranty/RMA/rework sprints. Charge distributors 5,000-15,000 PLN for pilot setup only after they recognize a concrete gate. Contractor review costs are 800-3,000 PLN per packet depending on system complexity. The buyer pays because rejected warranty claims, slow RMA triage, battery degradation disputes, repeated support tickets, installer ranking, and final margin/rebate release are economically meaningful.

Copy risk:
OEMs, distributors, inverter vendors, battery vendors, electrical engineers, fire-safety specialists, commissioning tools, and installer software can copy pieces. The defense is not a form. It is distributor-recognized economic-gate ownership, accepted/reworked packet memory, BESS-specific gap taxonomy, installer repeat submissions, and channel trust around warranty/RMA triage.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a distributor signs the recognition pilot, names the eligible jobs, and installers prepay for BESSGuard packets tied to warranty fast-track, installer ranking, final margin/rebate release, RMA triage, or preferred status, the startup controls the accepted evidence workflow for those jobs. A generic consultant, OEM tool, or installer app would need to displace the already-recognized packet, exception log, reviewer path, and distributor feedback loop while current jobs are waiting for gate acceptance.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live commercial BESS jobs tied to a distributor or warranty economic gate. The output is an operational release artifact: model/serial map, commissioning evidence index, photo set, settings/firmware snapshot, telemetry/BMS evidence, SoH/baseline status where available, customer handover proof, exception log, reviewer notes, and accepted/rework/no-go status. It is not generic installer training, electrical design, fire certification, warranty outsourcing, SaaS, a dashboard, a marketplace, or brokered introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not perform electrical installation, fire design, grid-connection certification, product certification, conformity assessment, warranty adjudication, legal advice, insurance advice, fire-code compliance opinions, or guarantees of safety, performance, acceptance, RMA, or warranty outcome. Licensed electricians, installers, designers, fire specialists, distributors, OEMs, DSOs/OSDs, insurers, property owners, and warranty teams remain responsible for installation, safety, compliance, grid settings, fire requirements, product selection, and final decisions. Cases with unsafe installation signs, missing core evidence, active fire incidents, disputed electrical design, suspected fraud, or pressure to approve unsafe work are stopped and routed to qualified professionals.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not the confirmed Distributor-backed Heat-Pump Commissioning Passport because the buyer, trigger, equipment, payment event, and proof artifact are different. BESSGuard concerns commercial battery storage warranty/RMA/preferred-installer gates, battery/inverter/BMS settings, telemetry, SoH baseline, firmware, thermal/clearance evidence, and storage-specific handover. It is not BatteryFit because it does not qualify PV prosumer battery retrofits for sales. It is not HeatQuiet because there is no acoustic siting or complaint evidence. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Distributors and OEMs may already have commissioning forms, warranty portals, or installer certification programs. Electrical/fire/grid boundaries are serious, and a packet cannot fix unsafe installation or missing professional certification. Commercial BESS volume may be too small for a standalone business in the first six months. Installers may resist paying for an extra packet unless the distributor gate is real. Distributors may like the workflow but refuse to tie it to money, status, or warranty speed. OEMs, BESS consultants, and electrical engineers can copy the packet once the fields are known. The idea risks looking like a duplicate energy commissioning passport unless the first pilot proves a storage-specific warranty/RMA economic gate.
