## Strongest Objections

1. The deadline delay weakens near-term urgency. EUDR applies from 2026-12-30 for large/medium operators and 2027-06-30 for micro/small operators, so many Polish SMEs will say they have time. Only live buyer requests matter.

2. Buyer requests may demand actual compliance, not a better evidence pack. The startup can organize evidence but cannot manufacture DDS reference numbers, supplier chain mapping, legality evidence, geolocation/source data, role clarity, or upstream truth.

3. Role mapping is easy to get wrong. Operator/downstream/trader/SME/non-SME/importer/exporter distinctions and simplified obligations create legal boundary risk.

4. EUDR consultants and software vendors are crowded. The startup must stay on fast buyer-blocked SME cases, not EUDR readiness.

5. The founder may get stuck between buyer, exporter, upstream supplier, and legal adviser without authority over acceptance or source data.

6. Reference numbers may not exist yet in many 2026 pre-deadline cases, weakening the DDS/reference-number handoff promise.

7. Product classification and SKU mapping can become messy fast across multiple wood species, composite boards, veneers, plywood, MDF/HDF, imported components, packaging wood, mixed suppliers, and changing batches.

8. Sensitive supplier data may not be shared because source, geolocation, producer, and reference-identifier data can be commercially sensitive.

9. Buyer acceptance is hard to prove; a delivered pack is not enough. The POC needs portal progress, PO release, onboarding progress, buyer correction, reduced follow-up, or explicit acceptance of evidence structure.

10. The moat is useful but not deep. Buyer wording, supplier gaps, reference-number patterns, and case memory help, but larger consultants and software vendors can build similar libraries.

## Interpretation For Next Rounds

Regulatory buyer-release evidence can score better than physical salvage or cyber broker cases, but it still falls well below the real gate when timing is delayed and legal/supplier authority is outside founder control. Do not keep iterating EUDR unless the control point changes from evidence organization to a stronger assigned order, payment, or contractual routing right.
