## Lead Candidate

**OTP Sender-ID Traffic Book Buyout**

### Buyer

Ecommerce brands, marketplaces, SaaS vendors, fintech-adjacent firms, logistics operators, appointment platforms, delivery networks, and B2B services that already send OTP, login, fraud, delivery, order, appointment, or service notifications through a small SMS/CPaaS reseller, agency, or integrator.

### Acute Trigger

The current reseller is retiring, closing, losing supplier terms, suffering deliverability failures, failing sender-ID registration, or being repriced by upstream CPaaS providers. The customer cannot tolerate OTP/login/order messages failing, but switching providers risks broken sender IDs, template approvals, delivery routes, whitelisting, webhooks, invoices, and support continuity.

### Control Point

- seller option over active transactional messaging customers only;
- 12-month traffic and gross-margin logs;
- sender IDs, short codes, phone numbers, WhatsApp/SMS templates where applicable;
- customer LOAs and billing-transfer approvals;
- upstream CPaaS/supplier terms;
- technical route handover;
- direct payment direction to the founder/new entity;
- first current-month or next-cycle traffic billed under the new payee before seller payout.

### Internal Score Rationale

This barely clears the simulated gate because it is not a generic CPaaS reseller launch. It buys or options a current critical traffic stream and proves control through customer-approved billing transfer plus live traffic under known sender identities before close. The asset is digital, compact, current, and payment-producing inside 60 days.

Main internal doubts:

- CPaaS incumbents are strong;
- SMS gross margins can be thin;
- customers may use the handoff to switch direct;
- trust/security around OTP traffic is sensitive;
- deliverability/support can become operationally heavy.

Gate decision: advance to working Zero To One validation.
