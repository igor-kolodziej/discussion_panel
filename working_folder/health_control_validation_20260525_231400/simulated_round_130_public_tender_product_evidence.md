## Lead Candidate: CableTender CPR Fire-Class Bid Packs

### Buyer

Polish/CEE cable distributors, electrical wholesalers, low-voltage/telecom cable importers, and electrical contractors bidding public-sector, hospital, school, rail, municipal, office, and industrial tenders where cable fire-reaction class and Declaration of Performance evidence are required.

### Acute Trigger

A live tender asks for exact cable evidence such as CPR Euroclass (for example B2ca/Cca/Dca/Eca), smoke class, flaming droplets, acidity class, CE marking, Declaration of Performance, AVCP/certification evidence, manufacturer datasheet, installation/environmental restrictions, and exact SKU equivalence. The tender team must decide quickly which catalog SKUs can be bid and which are disqualified or need supplier documents.

### Control Point

One live tender plus one distributor catalog/SKU export turned into a bid evidence pack:

- tender requirement extraction;
- SKU shortlist/rejected list;
- CPR class and DoP match matrix;
- CE/DoP/certificate/datasheet links;
- supplier/brand/part-number alias map;
- missing evidence and supplier RFI;
- bid/no-bid risk memo;
- strict "evidence only, no design/spec substitution" boundary.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three paid bid packs at 2,000-6,000 PLN each for live or recently closed tenders.
2. At least two buyers provide catalog/SKU exports or price lists.
3. At least one tender team uses the pack to bid, no-bid, request supplier evidence, or correct a submission.
4. One electrical/cable standards reviewer available per pack.
5. A reusable matrix across tender language, CPR class, DoP, SKU aliases, supplier evidence, and buyer outcome.

**89 / 100**

Reason for advancing: it is close to the proven GreenTender revenue-linked public-tender mechanics, but materially different in product evidence regime, buyer risk, source documents, and proof artifacts.
