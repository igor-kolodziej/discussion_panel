## Selected Candidate

Idea name: RestoreProof Cyber-Insurance Backup Test Evidence Bench

Simulated score: 88.5 / 100

Reason for passing simulation gate: The selected version materially changes the failed CyberBind shape. It is not broad cyber-insurance evidence packaging. It accepts only cases where an underwriter, broker, MGA, or carrier has asked for tested backup/restore evidence before bind, renewal, ransomware coverage, funds-transfer coverage, or premium/exclusion decision. The first proof is a dated restore test artifact generated from existing backups with MSP/IT owner involvement and insured approval.

## Internal Cap Check

- Concrete 6-month control proof: yes; broker-routed paid cases, backup-platform access through MSP/IT owner, dated restore artifacts, underwriter progress outcomes.
- Copy risk: serious, but mitigated by broker case blocks, white-label role, evidence format library, and refusal to sell broad consulting. Does not cap below 85.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: plausible if sold through brokers/MSPs and paid upfront per case or as prepaid case blocks. Does not cap below 87.
- Vague partnership dependence: partially dependent on brokers/MSPs, but first proof requires paid case blocks and named renewal blockers, not interviews.
- Legal/clinical/regulated-data authority: no health/patient data; no legal advice; no insurance advice; insured and broker approve statements; MSP/IT owner performs or authorizes technical actions.

## Kill Criteria

- Brokers will not pay or prepay for case blocks.
- MSPs refuse backup-platform access or say they can do all restore evidence themselves.
- More than 40% of leads lack recoverable backups and become remediation/no-go.
- Underwriters do not accept or progress cases based on dated restore artifacts.
- Average delivery time exceeds 5 business days for evidence-ready cases.
- Gross margin falls below 55% after backup/technical contractor costs.
