Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
LC Forwarder Overflow Ticket Book

One-sentence thesis:
White-label a prepaid LC discrepancy overflow desk behind freight forwarders, export accountants, chambers/export desks, and trade-finance advisers that already have exporter trust, taking only already-routed live tickets with written bank discrepancy notices and producing payment release, accepted resubmission, applicant waiver, narrowed discrepancy, discounting release, or clean no-go.

Exact buyer:
Freight forwarders, export accountants, chambers/export desks, and trade-finance advisers serving Polish/CEE SME exporters that occasionally use documentary credits for machinery, industrial goods, food, chemicals, furniture, building materials, and other B2B exports. The partner is the commercial buyer or paid channel because it already has the exporter relationship and wants a specialist overflow lane for stressful LC discrepancy cases without hiring a full-time documentary-credit expert.

Acute trigger:
A partner's exporter client has shipped goods or presented documents under a named letter of credit, and the advising/presenting/issuing bank sends a written discrepancy/refusal/reserve notice that blocks payment, discounting, or document acceptance. The partner needs to keep the client relationship but does not want its freight, accounting, or export-support team to improvise LC discrepancy choreography under deadline.

Control point:
Signed partner overflow agreement; partner-approved ticket pricing; exporter consent for the specific file; named LC and amendments; full presented document set; written bank discrepancy/refusal/reserve notice; bank deadline and response channel; source-document owners at forwarder, carrier, insurer, chamber, inspection body, exporter, and applicant where authorized; named LC/documentary-credit reviewer; partner-branded or co-branded corrected-document tracker, waiver/resubmission chronology, response log, outcome file, and no-go record.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Two to three partner overflow agreements; five prepaid live tickets routed through partners, not cold direct outreach; every ticket has exporter consent, named LC, written bank discrepancy notice, full document set, and prepaid fixed fee; one retained LC/documentary-credit reviewer visible in partner materials; at least two objective outcomes: bank-accepted correction, applicant waiver, accepted resubmission, payment release, discounting release, narrowed discrepancy, or paid clean no-go; 80,000-160,000 PLN collected; one partner sends a second live ticket.

6-month POC:
Sign 5-8 partner overflow agreements across forwarders, export accountants, chambers/export desks, and trade-finance advisers. Process 20-40 prepaid live LC discrepancy tickets with 30,000,000-100,000,000 PLN equivalent payment value under mandate. Achieve 12-20 objective outcomes: payment release, accepted resubmission, applicant waiver, bank acceptance, discounting release, narrowed discrepancy, or clean no-go. Collect 500,000-1,300,000 PLN in partner/client fees. Build a private case library by partner type, bank wording, discrepancy type, correction route, waiver path, response timing, no-go reason, and payment outcome. Maintain gross margin above 60% after reviewer, secure document handling, and partner-share costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target forwarders, export accountants, chambers/export desks, and trade-finance advisers with a narrow offer: "When your exporter client receives a bank discrepancy notice after presentation, keep the relationship and route the overflow ticket to me. You stay client-facing and keep freight/accounting/advisory roles. I run the document chronology, correction tracker, waiver/resubmission path, reviewer issue-spotting, and outcome record under your brand or with your approval." The first sale is not to exporters generally; it is a paid overflow lane for partners who already receive urgent calls.

Economics:
Partner bills the exporter or approves direct billing. Ticket price is 15,000-30,000 PLN for a standard live discrepancy file and 30,000-55,000 PLN for urgent multi-document, multi-party, or applicant-waiver files. The startup keeps 60-80% after partner share, or charges the partner a wholesale fixed fee. Optional 1-2% outcome fee is used only when payment release or discounting release is clearly tied to the ticket chronology. The fee is rational because a six- or seven-figure export payment can be delayed by a documentary mismatch, and the partner avoids hiring a full-time LC specialist for lumpy cases.

Copy risk:
Forwarders, export accountants, chambers, trade-finance advisers, banks, export clerks, documentary-credit consultants, and lawyers can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is already-routed paid partner tickets, fast response under partner brand, a named reviewer bench, accumulated discrepancy-response memory across multiple small partners, and active control over live ticket chronology before any competitor sees the file.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a partner routes a paid live ticket, the exporter consents, the LC file and discrepancy notice are in the workroom, the reviewer is assigned, and the partner-branded correction/waiver/resubmission chronology is active, a competitor cannot copy that specific ticket without the partner and exporter switching mid-deadline. Incumbents can copy the category, but not the signed partner queue and already-paid LC files in motion.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is an operating queue for already-routed live LC discrepancy tickets. The deliverable is not training, a report, software, a database, or a brokered introduction. Each ticket ends with bank response, corrected-document/resubmission chronology, applicant waiver path, payment or discounting release evidence, narrowed discrepancy list, or clean no-go. The startup does not sell general LC consulting or introduce exporters to forwarders or banks.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not act as bank, freight forwarder, carrier, insurer, chamber, inspection body, customs broker, lawyer, sanctions adviser, escrow agent, guarantor of payment, documentary-credit issuer, or applicant. It does not interpret disputed legal rights, certify document truth, forge or backdate documents, advise on sanctions/export controls, handle cargo/title disputes, pressure buyers, guarantee bank acceptance, or file customs documents. Banks, exporters, buyers/applicants, forwarders, carriers, insurers, chambers, inspection bodies, customs brokers, trade-finance advisers, and counsel remain responsible for their roles.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM Import Continuity, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs, Supplement Stack Safety, BatteryFit, or HeatQuiet. The control point is a partner-routed paid LC discrepancy ticket queue and payment-release chronology, not a regulated-finance customer evidence response, Amazon product evidence response, investor healthtech diligence, heritage physical lot, municipal invoice rail, retailer deduction recovery, import continuity file, machine-data evidence memo, public tender product mapping, supplement-safety review, PV retrofit economics, or heat-pump noise packet.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Partners may internalize the checklist after a few cases. Some forwarders and export accountants already have experienced document staff or bank contacts. Banks and applicants still control acceptance and payment. Many discrepancies require applicant waiver or clean no-go rather than a correction. Serious cases can involve fraud, forged documents, sanctions, backdating pressure, cargo/title disputes, legal interpretation, or buyer refusal. A named LC reviewer is mandatory, and reviewer cost can compress margins. Partner-branded delivery can trap the startup as hidden subcontract labor. Case volume may be lumpy because LC use is occasional for many SMEs.
