## Gate Decision

Advance **Home Pediatric Sleep/Video-EEG Micro-Lab Acquisition** to real Zero To One working-chat validation first because it is inside the requested EEG/biosignal domain.

If it fails the real working-chat gate, advance **Stoma/Urology NFZ Micro-Shop Buyout** as the next simulated-pass candidate.

## Why This Passed Simulation

- It changes EEG from a generic test service into a micro-operator acquisition.
- The control point is not the ability to perform EEG. It is a signed option over the existing demand surface: phone, domain, Google profile, referral-doctor relationships, active booking queue, EEG/VEEG device, caps/consumables, technician handoff, and interpreting-doctor relationship.
- The two-month proof can be hard and observable: signed owner option, at least one device transferred or escrowed, 20-30 active bookings retained, 2 referral sources retained, 10-15 paid tests completed.
- Competitors can buy EEG equipment, but they cannot copy the exact acquired referral queue and owner-endorsed transition.

## Critical Caveats For Real Gate

- If the founder only buys equipment, the score collapses to 80-82.
- Medical authority remains with technicians/neurologists; the founder must operate scheduling, logistics, acquisition, and evidence/admin workflow only.
- Pediatric/home EEG creates trust and data-protection sensitivity; patient/parent re-consent and clean transfer notices are mandatory.
- A one-lab book may be small. Owner earnings depend on paying a conservative acquisition price and proving that home/sleep/video EEG carries materially better economics than commodity in-clinic EEG.

## Backup Pass: Stoma/Urology NFZ Micro-Shop Buyout

Buyer: stoma/urostomy patients and caregivers, with payer partly or fully NFZ.

Trigger: new stoma discharge or renewal of a monthly/12-month e-zlecenie.

Control point: buy or option the legal entity / enterprise of a small NFZ-contracted medical shop, including phone, domain, eZWM process, inventory, customer book, and signed monthly delivery authorizations. The startup does not prescribe; authorised clinicians issue orders and the contracted shop executes them.

Two-month proof:

- binding shop/company option under 80,000 PLN upfront plus earnout;
- 70-100 active recurring patients verified;
- 40 re-authorized deliveries;
- first NFZ settlement;
- GDPR/medical-data transition notices and patient-choice language.

Economics: recurring monthly order flow from stoma/urology supplies can support meaningful revenue even with a small patient book; acquisition-led CAC is the advantage.

Copy risk: online incumbents exist, but they cannot instantly copy a signed local phone/book/owner-handoff packet.
