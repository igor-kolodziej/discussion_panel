## Strongest Objections

1. The pain may be annoying but not expensive enough. Plants may tolerate weak documentation unless a named customer repeatedly refuses waiting-time, rejected-load, pump-overtime, or quantity charges.
2. Driver adoption is the real product. Muddy sites, poor signal, rushed unloads, impatient contractors, missing signatures, and driver resistance can break the live ledger.
3. Photos and timestamps do not prove the hardest disputes, especially concrete quality, water added on site, curing conditions, structural cracks, quantity calculations, workmanship, weather, or site preparation.
4. Existing batching software, fleet tracking, electronic proof-of-delivery, ERP modules, mobile forms, and QC systems are closer substitutes than the pitch admits.
5. The prepaid pilot price may be too ambitious for an unknown part-time founder without construction-sector trust.
6. Plants may resist creating an audit trail if it can expose their own lateness, weak waiting claims, ticket mismatch, or batch mistakes.
7. "Invoice packet accepted" is a weak metric unless it shows a charge paid that was previously disputed, faster payment, fewer back-and-forth messages, or customer demand for the packet format.
8. Part-time founder availability is mismatched with early, fast, field-pressure concrete delivery unless the system requires no real-time founder intervention.
9. A 6-month result of three plants, 150 pours, and 50,000 PLN revenue would prove someone will pay, but not defensibility or scale without renewals and repeated charge recovery.

## Lesson

Do not advance generic field-event ledgers unless the controlled event has a more unavoidable payment gate than "better evidence." The real evaluator discounted "live source ledger" when the buyer pain was not obviously large, repeated, and monetizable.

Next search should return to harder transaction assets: assigned acknowledged claims, legally controlled payment accounts, prepaid case flow from an existing channel, exact titled lots matched to buyer deposits, or live release blockers where the buyer cannot complete a transaction without the controlled asset.
