Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
UK PSTI Connectable-Product PO/Listing Release

One-sentence thesis:
A 48-72 hour agency-routed release desk for UK retail, distributor, marketplace, and importer PO/listing/onboarding/stock holds where connected consumer products need a truthful UK PSTI Statement of Compliance plus bounded product-security evidence, model/SKU traceability, role mapping, and buyer-ready response artifacts before revenue can move.

Exact buyer:
EU, CEE, and UK importers, distributors, private-label brands, marketplace operators, and compliance or retail agencies serving connected children's toys, smart locks, alarms, smoke detectors, home automation devices, smart security products, connected appliances, and other consumer connectable products with a named UK buyer, retailer, importer, distributor, or marketplace request.

Acute trigger:
A UK retailer, marketplace, importer, distributor, or buyer blocks a purchase order, listing, vendor onboarding, stock release, shipment acceptance, or account continuation because the product lacks a usable PSTI Statement of Compliance, UK role evidence, vulnerability-reporting contact, security update period evidence, default-password evidence, model/SKU match, or manufacturer/importer/distributor traceability.

Control point:
The startup controls only a paid live release case: a buyer request, the affected SKU/model list, supplier/manufacturer documents, portal or email evidence of the hold, a prepaid case fee, a bounded evidence room, a drafted response pack, and a go/no-go record. It does not certify the product, test security, act as manufacturer/importer/distributor, or promise buyer acceptance.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign two agency or distributor routing agreements, take six prepaid live cases at 8,000-35,000 PLN each, collect buyer requests and SKU files for every case, deliver four buyer-progress or no-go outcomes, receive at least one repeat agency case, and show over 60% gross margin after paying a PSTI/product-security reviewer on hard cases.

6-month POC:
Run 40-80 paid live cases, generate 400,000-1,200,000 PLN revenue, produce at least 25 buyer-progress or no-go outcomes with documented reasons, build a reusable accepted-format library for statements/evidence rooms/model mapping/support-period/vulnerability-contact responses, and prove repeat routing from at least four agencies, importers, or distributors.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound only to UK/EU retail compliance agencies, Amazon and marketplace agencies, product-compliance consultants, toy/smart-home distributors, and CEE importers already handling UK retail or marketplace requests. The pitch is limited to live blocked POs/listings/onboarding/stock holds, not general compliance readiness.

Economics:
Charge 8,000-12,000 PLN for a narrow Statement of Compliance and role/model pack, 15,000-25,000 PLN for a full PO/listing/onboarding release pack, and 25,000-45,000 PLN for urgent multi-SKU or stock-hold cases. Expected gross margin is 60-75% after specialist reviewer costs because cases are document/evidence assembly and buyer-response work, not lab testing or product redesign.

Copy risk:
Compliance consultants, product-safety firms, TIC labs, marketplace agencies, and retailer compliance teams can copy parts of the workflow. The defensible wedge is not the template; it is agency-routed live blockers, category-specific case memory, accepted buyer response patterns, fast triage of manufacturer truth gaps, and repeated handling of the same connected-device SKU/model evidence problems.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Before an incumbent reacts, the founder can control the prepaid live case, the exact buyer request, the SKU/model files, the supplier/manufacturer evidence, the drafted Statement of Compliance response, the go/no-go record, and the agency routing relationship for that blocked revenue event. A generic consultant cannot recover the same case without buyer request access, seller authorization, and manufacturer evidence already routed into the desk.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is a narrow paid release desk for live UK PSTI-triggered commercial holds, with a specific buyer request and SKU file at intake, a prepaid case fee, a deliverable response packet, and an outcome record. It does not sell generic advice, a readiness report, software, a database, introductions, or a marketplace.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No legal advice, no product certification, no security testing, no vulnerability assessment, no false product-security claim, no acting as manufacturer/importer/distributor/UK responsible party/retailer, no product redesign, no personal-data processing beyond ordinary business contacts, no guarantee of buyer acceptance, and immediate no-go where manufacturer facts or evidence are missing or inconsistent.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Adjacent to REDBlocked, but materially different. REDBlocked is an EU Amazon Seller Central connected baby-monitor/indoor-camera evidence rescue desk under EU RED Cyber/CE/GPSR triggers. This idea is a UK PSTI Statement of Compliance and product-security evidence desk for UK retailer, importer, distributor, marketplace, PO/listing/onboarding, and stock-release holds across broader connected consumer products, with different legal artifact, buyer channel, geography, role mapping, and revenue gate.

Strongest anticipated objections:
UK PSTI has been in force since 2024, so the deadline panic may already have passed. Many compliance consultants, labs, retailer compliance teams, and agencies can handle PSTI paperwork. Hard cases depend on manufacturer truth, firmware/security facts, support-period policies, vulnerability-reporting contacts, and product redesign outside the founder's control. The market may be too narrow for a standalone company. UK retail access may be hard for a Warsaw solo founder. Buyers may view this as a one-off compliance service rather than a durable wedge. The similarity to REDBlocked may make it more like a product-line extension than a distinct zero-to-one business.
