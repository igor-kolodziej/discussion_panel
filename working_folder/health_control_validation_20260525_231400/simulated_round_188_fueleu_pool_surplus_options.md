## Factual Check Used Internally

Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 Article 21 allows compliance balances of two or more ships to be pooled. Companies register the intention to pool in the FuelEU database, all participating companies validate pool details, and by 30 April of the verification period the selected verifier records the definitive pool composition and allocation. Private pricing between companies is left to commercial agreements.

## Finalist Review

### FuelEU Pool Surplus Options

Why it advances:

- FuelEU pooling is a formal legal mechanism, not a made-up offset market.
- The control object is a named ship's compliance balance allocation in a pool, backed by ship/company approvals, a selected verifier, and database registration later in the verification period.
- Private pricing is allowed between companies, so a founder can structure a commercial option/deposit around future surplus/deficit matching without becoming the verifier.
- First proof is a signed commercial option plus buyer deposit for a named surplus/deficit match, not a memo.
- The 2025 compliance year has already created operational awareness; 2026 compliance planning can start before the 2027 reporting/allocation window.

Strict objections:

- Maritime compliance buyers are relationship-heavy and already served by verifiers, maritime lawyers, DNV/LR-style advisers, fuel suppliers, and specialist platforms.
- The founder does not control final verified balances or database acceptance.
- Deposits before verified balances may be hard to secure.
- The market may consolidate quickly around existing verifiers and maritime pooling platforms.
- Founder location and credibility are weak without a maritime compliance contractor.
