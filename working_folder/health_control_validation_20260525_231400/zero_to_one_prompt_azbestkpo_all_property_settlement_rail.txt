Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: AzbestKPO All-Property Settlement Rail

One-sentence thesis:
Sell a prepaid all-property evidence routing pilot to asbestos-removal contractors so every gmina-funded property removal produces the BDO/KPO, photo, protocol, weight, owner, landfill, and invoice-line packet needed for contractor payment and gmina/fund settlement.

Exact buyer:
Licensed asbestos-removal/waste contractors serving multiple Polish gminas under seasonal removal, collection, transport, and disposal contracts, especially contractors that must process lists of private properties and settle actual removed quantities with gminas or public environmental funds.

Acute trigger:
Each property job creates evidence at the crew moment: owner/property ID, date, photos, removed material quantity, BDO/KPO reference or exported KPO PDF supplied by the responsible party, transport/vehicle, landfill or waste recipient evidence, completion protocol, and invoice-line linkage. Missing or inconsistent evidence can delay contractor invoice acceptance, gmina reimbursement/fund settlement, or final contract acceptance. The contractor cannot reliably reconstruct all property evidence after crews leave.

Control point:
A signed paid pilot requiring all current jobs for two named gminas to be routed through the evidence rail first, plus written gmina packet-format acknowledgement/correction, plus a live property-event ledger and invoice packet generator for current-month work.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. 5,000-15,000 PLN prepaid pilot from one asbestos-removal contractor.
2. All-property routing mandate for at least two named gminas, or one gmina with at least 20 scheduled property removals.
3. Property list/work-order import and field capture flow for scheduled removals.
4. Gmina packet-format acknowledgement, field correction, or usable feedback on the proposed property settlement packet.
5. 10-30 live property events captured with photos, property IDs, dates, crew/vehicle, owner confirmation where available, KPO number/status or exported KPO PDF supplied by the responsible party, actual weight/quantity, landfill/recipient evidence, protocol, and invoice-line linkage.
6. At least one invoice, gmina, or fund settlement packet generated from live captured events.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build a list of 40-60 asbestos-removal contractors and public contract/gmina program targets from BIP/procurement notices, gmina asbestos-program pages, WFOŚiGW program references, and award documents; interview 8-12 contractors/admins about settlement friction.
Month 2: close one prepaid all-property routing pilot and get packet-format feedback from at least one gmina contact through the contractor's existing contract channel.
Month 3: capture at least 10-30 live property removals and generate the first settlement packet.
Months 4-6: expand to 2-3 contractor/gmina work packages, 75+ captured property events, 3+ generated settlement packets, at least 30,000-60,000 PLN collected, and documented evidence that missing-field catches, packet generation, or reduced reconstruction time matter to the buyer.

First acquisition mechanism:
Use public BIP/procurement and gmina asbestos-removal program data to contact contractors with named gmina/property-list context. The pitch is not "waste software." It is: "For your current gmina asbestos jobs, every property event becomes a complete KPO/photo/protocol/weight/invoice packet before month-end reconstruction." Accept only pilots where the contractor can mandate crew/admin usage for all selected properties.

Economics:
The first pilot fee should be 5,000-15,000 PLN, funding setup and manual QA. Post-pilot pricing can be 1,500-4,000 PLN/month during active season plus 50-150 PLN per property packet or a small percentage of documented invoice value, depending on volume and contract size. The value comes from avoiding delayed acceptance, missing-field rework, staff reconstruction time, and fund/gmina settlement friction. Manual QA is acceptable during proof; do not build broad SaaS before a contractor proves current work-package value.

Copy risk:
BDO tools, waste consultants, environmental software, generic field-service apps, spreadsheets, and contractor admins can copy pieces. The defense is not forms or OCR. The defense is entering the contractor's live all-property workflow before invoice settlement, learning exact gmina/fund packet rules, and owning the source ledger for current jobs.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific workflow/payment event:
Once a contractor routes all selected gmina properties through the rail and the gmina has corrected or acknowledged the packet format, a later competitor cannot recreate the original timestamped property events, missing-field catches, photos, KPO references, weight/protocol links, or gmina-specific settlement memory for that work package. They can only compete for future work packages.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business is not selling environmental advice, a static report, a generic dashboard, or waste brokerage. It controls a paid live event-routing mandate inside an existing contractor's work package and produces settlement packets from source events captured during current jobs.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The contractor and licensed waste chain remain responsible for asbestos removal, worker safety, transport, disposal, BDO obligations, KPO issuance/status, landfill transfer, legal compliance, and environmental reporting. The founder does not handle waste, transport waste, broker waste, issue KPOs as the responsible party, certify asbestos work, advise on asbestos safety, provide legal/environmental opinions, or represent gmina/fund acceptance. The founder may capture contractor-supplied evidence, organize exported documents, flag missing fields, and generate packet drafts for contractor review.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, or BioSignal because it is not a compliance evidence desk for finance, platform unblock desk, or investor diligence pack. It is closest in structure to TraceFaktura's all-event municipal contractor rail, but the payment event, documentation objects, buyer, and risk boundaries are materially different: hazardous asbestos property removals with BDO/KPO, weight, landfill, protocol, and gmina/fund settlement evidence, not animal pickup/shelter/vet invoice events.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Contractors may already manage settlement adequately with spreadsheets, BDO exports, and admin staff.
Gmina payment friction may be weaker than assumed or handled informally.
Seasonal timing may make a 60-day proof hard if no active property list exists.
Field crews may resist capture unless contractor management enforces all-property routing.
Unit economics may be too small if property volumes are low or contractors have thin margins.
The founder must avoid becoming an environmental consultant, BDO operator of record, asbestos safety adviser, or waste broker.
BDO or environmental software incumbents could add packet workflows if demand is visible.
