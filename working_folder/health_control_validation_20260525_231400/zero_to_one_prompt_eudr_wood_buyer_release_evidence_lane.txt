Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: EUDR Wood Buyer-Release Evidence Lane.

One-sentence thesis: provide a prepaid, case-specific evidence lane for Polish/CEE wood and furniture suppliers whose EU buyers, distributors, retailers, marketplaces, or tender customers are asking for EUDR traceability, DDS/reference-number handoff, supplier/source facts, or role-mapping evidence before confirming a PO, listing, onboarding, framework supply agreement, tender, or shipment schedule.

Exact buyer: Polish/CEE furniture makers, timber suppliers, plywood/board/flooring/door/joinery manufacturers, pallet/packaging suppliers, wood-product traders, and SME exporters with EU B2B buyer relationships. The first economic buyer is a Polish wood/furniture exporter too small for enterprise EUDR software but large enough to lose meaningful German, Dutch, Nordic, Austrian, French, retail, marketplace, or distributor orders.

Acute trigger: a named EU buyer, marketplace, distributor, retailer, contractor, importer, or tender customer asks the Polish supplier for EUDR evidence before confirming an order, listing, onboarding, 2027 framework supply, tender, or shipment schedule. The buyer request is not generic "be ready for EUDR." It asks for current order-release artifacts such as supplier chain and wood species/product mapping, DDS reference numbers or simplified declaration identifiers from upstream sources where applicable, role mapping in the supply chain, batch/SKU/order mapping to upstream reference numbers, country of production and supplier details, geolocation or source documentation where the Polish supplier is upstream/operator for imported or produced material, recordkeeping file for first downstream buyer obligations, and a clear statement of what is known, missing, not applicable, or must remain the operator's responsibility.

Control point: the founder controls the case only when all of these are present:

- a current written buyer request, tender question, onboarding checklist, PO condition, or supplier-portal evidence request;
- supplier/customer authorization to assemble the evidence pack for that named case;
- access to product/SKU/order/batch/supplier files;
- upstream supplier mandate or email thread requesting DDS/reference identifiers and product/source facts;
- prepaid fixed case fee from the Polish supplier before delivery;
- agreement that the customer/operator remains legally responsible for due diligence and any DDS/simplified declaration submission;
- right to retain anonymized case taxonomy: buyer wording, product type, missing evidence, supplier response, reference-number handoff, and final buyer acceptance or pushback.

The founder does not act as operator, customs declarant, legal adviser, auditor, certifier, deforestation-risk assessor, or EUDR Information System submitter unless separately lawfully appointed and reviewed. The initial wedge avoids submission and focuses on order-release evidence organization.

6-month proof:

1. Build a target list of 120 Polish furniture, wood-product, timber, board, flooring, doors, pallet, packaging, and joinery exporters with visible EU B2B/retail/marketplace buyers.
2. Build a redacted sample buyer-release pack using official European Commission EUDR roles and due-diligence fields with dummy supplier data.
3. Contact 80 exporters and 20 export/accounting/customs/quality advisors with a narrow prompt: "Send only live buyer requests, not readiness curiosity."
4. Close at least 4 prepaid current buyer-release cases at 6,000-18,000 PLN each.
5. Collect at least 30,000 PLN before delivery.
6. For each case, assemble product/SKU/batch/supplier maps, role assumptions, upstream reference-number request trail, missing-evidence register, buyer response draft, and escalation boundaries.
7. Obtain at least one written buyer acceptance, buyer correction, supplier-portal progress event, or reopened order discussion showing the pack moved the order/listing/tender forward.
8. Reject any case where the buyer only wants generic policy text, no current order is affected, or the supplier expects the founder to certify legality, negligible risk, or DDS correctness.

First acquisition mechanism:

Exporter pitch:
"If a German/Dutch/Nordic buyer is asking for EUDR traceability before confirming your order, send the exact buyer request and one SKU/batch. I turn your existing supplier, product, and DDS/reference-number evidence into a buyer-ready response pack, clearly separating known facts, missing upstream data, non-applicable fields, and items your legal/compliance adviser must approve."

Advisor/channel pitch:
"Your client is not ready for full EUDR software, but has one buyer request blocking a PO. Send the live request. I handle document assembly and supplier RFI drafting so you keep advisory/accounting/customs work."

Economics:

- 6,000-9,000 PLN for one SKU/order/buyer request with limited supplier chain;
- 10,000-18,000 PLN for multi-SKU or multi-supplier buyer-release pack;
- 3,000-6,000 PLN annual/order refresh once prior case memory exists;
- no free readiness audits; only paid current buyer cases.

Founder handles evidence mapping and drafting. Optional forestry/EUDR reviewer is paid only on flagged cases. Translation and secure document storage are scoped costs. Two prepaid cases can cover setup/reviewer costs, and one blocked EU order or framework buyer relationship can justify the fee if order value is material.

Copy risk:

EUDR consultants and software vendors can copy generic templates. They cannot instantly copy the named buyer request, current SKU/batch/supplier file, upstream reference-number email trail, order-specific buyer response, accepted or pushed-back wording memory by buyer type and product class, and channel relationships with Polish SME wood/furniture exporters that do not want enterprise EUDR software. The compounding asset is not a regulation summary; it is the map of buyer wording, supplier evidence gaps, product/SKU role questions, reference-number handoff patterns, and what specific EU buyers accept or reject.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup only works on live buyer/order/tender/listing requests with a prepaid fee and named product files. It does not sell broad EUDR readiness, a dashboard, a supplier database, legal opinions, audits, certification, or marketplace matching. The controlled unit is a current buyer-release case plus case-memory taxonomy.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- The customer/operator remains responsible for EUDR compliance, due diligence, DDS or simplified declaration submission, and legal conclusions.
- Lawyers, EUDR consultants, auditors, customs advisers, and forestry experts handle legal/technical approval where needed.
- The founder does not state that a product is deforestation-free, legally produced, or negligible-risk unless the customer/adviser provides that statement.
- The founder does not submit into the EUDR Information System in the initial wedge.
- Pack wording separates known evidence, missing evidence, non-applicable fields, and adviser/operator decisions.
- Sensitive supplier data is handled under NDA/DPA where needed.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

- Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: no ICT supplier risk evidence for financial-sector renewals.
- Not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: no Amazon account-health or connected-device compliance.
- Not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack: no healthtech/investor claim diligence.
- Not PromoLeak Recovery Desk: no retailer deduction recovery.
- Not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs: no EPREL public lighting bid pack.
- Not TraceFaktura municipal animal-care invoice evidence: no municipal event invoice ledger.
- Not HeritageDoor Suites: no physical salvage inventory.
