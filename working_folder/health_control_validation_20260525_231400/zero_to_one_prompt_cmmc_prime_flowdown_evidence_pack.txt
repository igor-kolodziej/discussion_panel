Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CMMC Prime-Flowdown Evidence Pack

One-sentence thesis:
A vendor-side fixed-fee evidence-response pack for CEE/EU defense, aerospace, electronics, software, machining, robotics, drone, simulation, and industrial suppliers whose US prime, defense customer, supplier portal, bid, PO, onboarding, or renewal is blocked by CMMC / NIST SP 800-171 / DFARS / FCI-CUI evidence requests.

Exact buyer:
CEE and EU-adjacent defense, aerospace, electronics, software, engineering, machining, components, materials, robotics, drone, simulation, test equipment, and industrial suppliers that sell to US defense primes, US subsidiaries, prime contractors, or defense-adjacent integrators and receive a live CMMC / NIST SP 800-171 / DFARS / FCI-CUI evidence request tied to a subcontract, renewal, PO, onboarding, bid, or supplier portal.

Best first buyers have one or more US prime/customer relationships, no mature US compliance team, and a current written request with a clear contract consequence.

Acute trigger:
A US prime or defense customer asks for CMMC status, Level 1/2 self-assessment evidence, SPRS score/status, affirmation readiness, NIST SP 800-171 control evidence, SSP/POA&M files, CUI/FCI boundary, subcontractor/cloud/MSP evidence, incident-reporting process, access-control evidence, or proof that the supplier can continue on a PO, bid, renewal, or onboarding path.

Control point:
Prepaid live prime-request case with:
- current written prime/customer request, supplier portal item, contract clause, PO condition, or bid/onboarding blocker;
- supplier authorization and evidence room;
- scope/boundary map for FCI/CUI exposure using contract/customer-provided materials only;
- evidence index for Level 1/2 self-assessment support, SPRS/affirmation readiness, SSP/POA&M gaps, subcontractor/cloud/MSP facts, incident process, access/MFA/logging/vulnerability/backups, and unsafe answers;
- named CMMC RPO/consultant or NIST 800-171 reviewer on paid per-case terms;
- no CMMC certification claim, no legal/export-control advice, no C3PAO assessment, no final SPRS/affirmation submission unless the qualified responsible party handles it.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid live CMMC/prime-flowdown cases at 15,000-40,000 PLN each.
2. At least two different primes/customers represented by incoming requests.
3. One CMMC RPO/consultant or NIST 800-171 reviewer contracted per case.
4. Two contract-progress outcomes: prime portal item accepted, bid/onboarding proceeds, PO not blocked on evidence, customer narrows follow-up, or supplier cleanly no-go's a contract before overcommitting.
5. 60,000-120,000 PLN collected.
6. Reusable case taxonomy across prime wording, supplier type, FCI/CUI boundary, evidence gaps, unsafe claims, reviewer notes, and customer pushback.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build a target list of 100 CEE/EU suppliers with visible US defense-prime, aerospace, drone, robotics, electronics, machining, simulation, software, or industrial customer relationships; recruit one CMMC RPO/consultant or NIST 800-171 reviewer; build a redacted sample response pack from dummy prime request wording; and contact suppliers with a live-request-only offer.

Month 2: close three prepaid cases from suppliers with current prime/customer requests. Build FCI/CUI boundary maps, evidence indexes, self-assessment support files, SSP/POA&M gap split, unsafe-answer flags, and reviewer notes.

Month 3: secure at least two contract-progress outcomes and document no-go cases: no live prime request, no contract consequence, export-control/legal issue dominates, CUI boundary cannot be established from customer materials, no evidence room, requires full MSP/cyber remediation before any useful response, or no budget.

Months 4-6: complete 10-18 paid live prime-flowdown cases, collect 180,000-450,000 PLN, keep gross margin above 60% after reviewer cost, get one defense/aerospace adviser or CMMC partner sending two cases, and build case memory by prime wording, supplier type, FCI/CUI boundary, evidence requested, unsafe answer pattern, reviewer note, and customer pushback.

First acquisition mechanism:
Supplier pitch:
"If a US prime, defense customer, supplier portal, bid, PO, or renewal is asking for CMMC, NIST SP 800-171, DFARS, SPRS, Level 1/2, SSP/POA&M, or FCI/CUI evidence, send the exact request and your existing evidence folder. I turn it into a buyer-ready response pack: what you can safely answer now, what needs technical/security remediation, what needs legal/export-control review, what needs management approval, and what should not be answered without a qualified adviser."

Partner pitch:
"You keep implementation, MSP remediation, C3PAO assessment, legal/export-control, and long CMMC projects. I handle urgent CEE/EU supplier evidence collation and prime-response overflow for companies that have a live contract blocker but are not ready for a broad consulting project."

Economics:
Charge 15,000-25,000 PLN for a focused Level 1/FCI or limited prime-flowdown evidence response, 25,000-40,000 PLN for a Level 2/NIST 800-171 SSP/POA&M evidence pack and reviewer pass, and 8,000-15,000 PLN for annual refresh or changed-since-last-prime-response update. Reviewer costs are scoped per case. The buyer pays because a defense subcontract, PO, onboarding, renewal, or strategic prime relationship is at risk.

Copy risk:
CMMC RPOs, C3PAOs, MSPs, cyber consultants, law firms, and prime supplier-risk teams can copy pieces. The defense is CEE/non-US supplier focus, live prime-flowdown response speed, bilingual evidence packaging, RPO/reviewer bench, prime wording memory, and case memory by supplier type and FCI/CUI boundary. The startup is not trying to replace CMMC consultants; it owns urgent vendor-side evidence packaging and routes implementation/certification/legal work.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the supplier routes a live prime/customer request, signs the prepaid case, grants evidence-room access, and uses the startup's response pack under a bid, PO, onboarding, or renewal clock, another provider must displace an active evidence workflow. The controlled unit is the exact prime request, contract consequence, supplier evidence folder, FCI/CUI boundary map, reviewer notes, response wording, and customer progress or no-go outcome.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live prime/customer-request cases with prepaid fees and named evidence folders. The output is a buyer-ready response pack for a specific bid, PO, onboarding, supplier-portal item, or renewal blocker, not a CMMC readiness report, legal memo, certification, dashboard, marketplace, broad GRC app, or brokered expert call.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, export-control advice, CMMC certification, C3PAO assessment, formal SPRS/affirmation submission, MSP remediation, security certification, investment advice, or representation to the US government or primes. The supplier remains responsible for truthful statements, management affirmation, security controls, contract interpretation, customer communications, legal/export-control decisions, and remediation. CMMC RPOs, C3PAOs, MSPs, cyber specialists, and legal/export-control counsel handle flagged work where needed. The startup does not request or store CUI unless a lawful handling structure is explicitly in place; early cases use contract/customer-provided scope and evidence descriptions rather than raw sensitive technical data when possible.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack. DORA serves software vendors selling to regulated finance customers under EU ICT third-party-risk pressure. This serves CEE/EU defense and industrial suppliers responding to US defense prime CMMC/NIST/DFARS flowdown requests. The buyer trigger, customer channel, evidence map, reviewer bench, proof artifact, and contract language are different.

It is not Part-IS aviation supplier evidence, KSC/NIS2, AI Act, CBAM, REDBlocked, Data Act, GreenTender, PromoLeak, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, or Supplement Stack Safety.

Strongest anticipated objections:
- CMMC consultants, RPOs, MSPs, C3PAOs, law firms, and prime supplier-risk teams already serve this market.
- Unknown Warsaw solo founder may lack trust for defense-prime evidence and sensitive supplier data.
- CEE/EU suppliers with live US CMMC flowdown requests may be too sparse or hard to find.
- Many cases require real cybersecurity remediation, MSP work, legal/export-control review, C3PAO assessment, or management affirmation rather than an evidence pack.
- FCI/CUI boundary questions can become legal/security decisions outside founder authority.
- The startup may not be allowed to handle CUI or sensitive defense technical data without a stronger compliance structure.
- Prime acceptance remains outside founder control.
- The business may become generic cyber compliance project management if not limited to live prime-flowdown blockers.
