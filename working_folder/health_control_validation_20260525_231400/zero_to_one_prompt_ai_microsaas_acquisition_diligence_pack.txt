Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: AI MicroSaaS Acquisition Diligence Pack.

One-sentence thesis:
Provide a prepaid, broker/searcher-routed diligence pack for live acquisitions of AI-enabled microSaaS, AI automation tools, and small AI software agencies, using NDA data-room access to expose LLM cost/margin risk, wrapper depth, vendor dependency, data/IP provenance gaps, security/privacy issues, customer concentration, and post-close technical debt before LOI, price, escrow, or no-go decisions.

Exact buyer:
Search funds, micro-PE funds, angel acquirers, solo searchers, SaaS acquirers, small M&A brokers, acquire.com-style buyers, software-holding companies, and operator-investors evaluating sub-10m PLN or small lower-middle-market AI-enabled software targets. Targets include AI microSaaS apps, AI customer-support bots, AI automation agencies, no-code/low-code AI tools, vertical SaaS with AI features, Shopify/WordPress/Chrome AI apps, and small productized AI service businesses.

Acute trigger:
The buyer has a live acquisition or investment decision with a compressed diligence window. The seller claims AI differentiation, sticky ARR, proprietary workflows, automation margin, or defensible model/data assets. The buyer needs to know before LOI/closing whether the business is a thin LLM wrapper, dependent on one model/vendor/API, exposed to token-cost margin compression, using customer data unsafely, lacking data/IP rights, overstating automation, hiding manual service labor, facing churn/support debt, or requiring expensive rebuild after close.

Transferable control point:
- Signed routing agreement with 2-4 micro-M&A brokers, search-fund communities, operator-investor groups, SaaS acquisition advisers, or angel syndicates.
- Prepaid live diligence case for a named target before LOI, exclusivity, price renegotiation, closing, or no-go.
- Buyer authorization and target/NDA data-room access: product demo, revenue cohort exports, Stripe/payment data where available, customer concentration, churn/refunds, LLM/API invoices, infrastructure logs, vendor contracts, code/repo access if permitted, prompt/workflow samples, customer data-flow notes, privacy/security documents, support tickets, and founder claims.
- Fixed-scope output: buyer-ready risk memo, red/yellow/green matrix, evidence gaps, AI-depth classification, LLM cost/margin model, vendor dependency map, data/IP/privacy/security flags, post-close rebuild/support estimate, and diligence questions for counsel/technical reviewer.
- Case memory by target type, AI claim, revenue quality, token-cost pattern, vendor dependency, manual-labor hidden ratio, data/IP gap, and acquisition outcome.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 2 broker/searcher/adviser routing agreements for paid current diligence cases.
- 3-5 prepaid live acquisition diligence cases.
- NDA/data-processing terms and buyer authorization for each target data room.
- Optional contractor/reviewer terms for code/security/privacy review modules where needed.
- First 2-3 completed decision memos with documented buyer outcome: LOI terms changed, price adjusted, escrow/condition added, deeper specialist review triggered, post-close plan revised, or no-go.

6-month proof:
1. Build a target list of 50 searchers, micro-PE buyers, software acquirers, M&A brokers, SaaS acquisition communities, and operator-investor groups that see AI/SaaS deals.
2. Sign 2-4 routing agreements or preferred-review arrangements.
3. Close 8-15 prepaid live diligence cases at 8,000-30,000 PLN each.
4. Collect 80,000-300,000 PLN revenue with >70% gross margin after contractor/reviewer modules.
5. Complete each pack in 3-7 business days after data-room access.
6. Show at least 5 cases where the memo materially affects a buyer decision: LOI/no-go, price, earnout, escrow, representations, condition, technical rebuild reserve, or post-close plan.
7. Build a repeatable AI acquisition-risk taxonomy across LLM cost/margin, wrapper depth, data/IP provenance, vendor dependency, privacy/security, manual labor, customer concentration, and churn/support debt.
8. Reject deals where buyer only wants generic market research, legal advice, full code audit, or a valuation opinion.

First acquisition mechanism:
Broker/searcher pitch: "AI claims are now appearing in small software deals, but most acquirers cannot tell in one week whether the target is a durable AI product or a fragile wrapper with hidden API costs, manual service labor, data/IP exposure, and post-close rebuild risk. Route one live target under NDA. I return a fixed-scope buyer memo that changes LOI, price, escrow, conditions, or no-go."

Buyer pitch: "Before you sign LOI or close, send the live data room and claims you are underwriting. We do not provide legal advice, valuation, or a full code audit. We identify the AI-specific deal risks that can destroy owner earnings: model/API dependency, token costs, manual labor hidden behind automation, data rights, privacy/security gaps, customer concentration, churn/refunds, support debt, and rebuild risk."

Economics:
- Fixed-scope live diligence memo: 8,000-15,000 PLN for simple microSaaS/AI app targets.
- Extended pack with LLM cost model, data-flow review, revenue-quality review, and limited code/security reviewer module: 15,000-30,000 PLN.
- Broker/adviser referral share: 10-25%.
- Contractor/reviewer cost: 1,000-6,000 PLN only for optional code/security/privacy modules.
- Buyer value: a single memo can justify a no-go, 5-15% price reduction, escrow/earnout condition, rebuild budget, or post-close operating plan on a deal worth hundreds of thousands to millions of PLN.
- No free generic teardowns. No broad AI consulting. No work without a live target, buyer authorization, NDA/data room, and prepaid fee.

Copy risk:
Tech diligence firms, fractional CTOs, cyber firms, M&A lawyers, and AI consultants exist. The wedge is not generic tech diligence; it is fast, fixed-scope, buyer-side AI microSaaS acquisition diligence with broker/searcher-routed live cases and a growing private taxonomy of AI-specific small-deal failure patterns. Incumbents can copy a checklist, but cannot copy signed deal-flow channels, current NDA data rooms, target-specific evidence, and outcome memory before the buyer makes the decision.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder targets small deals where Big Four/large tech-DD firms are too expensive and generic fractional CTOs may lack AI-specific M&A pattern memory. Once a broker/searcher routes a live deal and the buyer prepays, another reviewer would have to displace an active diligence process under the transaction clock. Each case compounds deal-specific pattern memory around AI claims, revenue quality, model dependency, and post-close risk.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls prepaid live diligence cases tied to named acquisition decisions and NDA data-room access. The output is not generic AI advice or market research; it is a decision artifact for LOI, price, escrow, condition, no-go, or post-close plan. Software may later support evidence intake, but the wedge is broker-routed paid case flow and proprietary deal-pattern memory.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not provide legal advice, tax advice, valuation opinion, fairness opinion, investment advice, cybersecurity certification, formal privacy assessment, or full code audit. Buyer counsel handles reps/warranties, IP ownership, privacy terms, employment issues, and legal risk allocation. Security/privacy/code specialists handle modules where needed. The founder reviews only authorized data-room materials and flags evidence gaps, risks, and follow-up questions. Sensitive customer data is minimized, redacted where possible, and handled under NDA/data-processing terms.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack. BioSignal evaluates healthtech/wearable/digital-biomarker claims for investors and accelerators. AI MicroSaaS Acquisition Diligence Pack evaluates live acquisition targets in AI-enabled software and small AI agencies, with broker/searcher-routed NDA data-room access and deal-term impact.

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk, TraceFaktura, CBAM, EUDR, RED Cyber, ICS2, F-Gas, KasaSerwis, MailDomain, or HeritageDoor Suites.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether searchers/micro-acquirers see enough AI software deals with budgets for paid specialist diligence.
- Whether brokers will route live data rooms before the founder has credentials.
- Whether buyers will trust an unknown solo founder for acquisition diligence.
- Whether existing tech diligence firms, fractional CTOs, M&A counsel, or AI consultants already satisfy the need.
- Whether target data-room access is deep enough to verify LLM costs, data/IP provenance, customer concentration, and hidden manual labor.
- Whether the output becomes a generic report rather than a decision-changing artifact tied to LOI, price, condition, or no-go.
