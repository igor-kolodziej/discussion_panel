Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: FBA Stranded-Lot Removal Buyout With Buyer Deposits.

One-sentence thesis: buy or option exact Amazon FBA stranded, suppressed, aged, or required-removal inventory lots only after a downstream B2B buyer has paid a deposit and a seller-approved removal order sends the named lot to a controlled 3PL, turning a disposal/storage-fee deadline into a back-to-back inventory resale.

Exact buyer: EU/CEE Amazon sellers with non-regulated, non-expired, non-counterfeit, documented FBA inventory that is no longer economically worth rescuing inside Amazon, plus B2B liquidation buyers, wholesalers, outlet operators, and off-Amazon resellers willing to deposit against exact SKU/FNSKU lots after manifest review. Initial categories exclude food, supplements, cosmetics, medical devices, prescription/Rx products, children's products, batteries, electronics requiring unresolved CE/RED evidence, dangerous goods, and products with unresolved safety or IP claims.

Acute trigger: a seller has FBA inventory that is stranded, suppressed, aged, unfulfillable, tied to account closure, or under a required removal/disposal/liquidation deadline. The seller is facing storage fees, removal fees, disposal loss, or locked capital, and does not want to spend more time on account-health appeals, listing fixes, external warehousing, or one-by-one liquidation. The founder only acts when the seller chooses inventory monetization rather than listing reinstatement.

Control point: the founder controls the opportunity only when all of these are signed or evidenced:

- Seller Central inventory report showing exact marketplace, ASIN/FNSKU/SKU, quantity, condition, location/status reason, and removal/disposal/fee deadline where available.
- Seller invoices or supply-chain records proving the seller owns the goods and can transfer title.
- Written exclusions for counterfeit, IP-disputed, unsafe, expired, recalled, dangerous, prescription, medical, children's, food/supplement/cosmetic, battery, and unresolved CE/RED/GPSR categories.
- Seller title-transfer or conditional option over the exact lot, conditional on buyer deposit and Amazon removal feasibility.
- Seller-approved removal order to a founder-controlled or buyer-approved EU 3PL, or a 3PL receiving plan ready before removal.
- Downstream buyer purchase order or deposit tied to the exact manifest before the founder funds the seller or removal.
- VAT/invoice treatment, warehouse receiving proof, and inspection/shortage adjustment rules.
- Written rule that the founder does not certify product safety, compliance, IP clearance, consumer resale legality, or Amazon reinstatement.

6-month proof:

1. Sign referral terms with 3 Amazon account-health agencies, FBA prep centers, or ecommerce accountants that already see stranded inventory cases.
2. Build a buyer list of 50 EU/CEE liquidation buyers, wholesalers, outlet operators, and off-Amazon resellers by category.
3. Review 20 stranded/aged/removal inventory manifests.
4. Reject any lot with regulated product risk, unresolved IP/safety claims, weak title, uncertain removal path, missing invoices, poor resale demand, or less than 10,000 PLN realistic net spread.
5. Sign one conditional option/title-transfer agreement over an exact lot.
6. Obtain one downstream buyer deposit of at least 20% of the agreed B2B resale price for that exact manifest.
7. Place the removal order to controlled 3PL or buyer-approved 3PL and confirm received quantities.
8. Complete one resale with at least 10,000 PLN gross profit after seller payment, removal/3PL/transport fees, inspection, shortages, payment fees, accounting/legal review, and risk reserve.
9. Prove whether the same agency/3PL channel can route a second paid case.

First acquisition mechanism:

Agency / prep-center pitch:
"When a seller has stranded or required-removal FBA inventory that is not worth another appeal, send the manifest before disposal or storage fees eat it. I only take non-regulated, clean-title lots. You get a written referral fee or revenue share if a buyer deposits and the lot closes."

Seller pitch:
"If you are done trying to fix this FBA inventory, I can make a conditional buyout offer. I only proceed if the manifest, title, removal path, and buyer deposit are clean. You recover cash instead of paying storage/removal/disposal or spending weeks finding buyers."

Buyer pitch:
"This is a named FBA lot with manifest, seller invoice/title path, category exclusions, removal-to-3PL plan, inspection adjustment rules, and a short deadline. Deposit reserves the exact lot; you remain responsible for downstream resale channel, compliance, and consumer-facing claims."

Economics:

- Target lot landed cost basis: 40,000-250,000 PLN.
- Founder cash to seller: usually 10-35% of landed cost, structured as conditional option, partial upfront plus closing balance, or seller upside share where possible.
- Removal, 3PL, transport, inspection, payment, accounting/legal, and risk reserve: 3,000-20,000 PLN depending on lot size.
- B2B resale price: 25-55% of landed cost where the category has visible off-Amazon demand.
- Target gross profit: 10,000-40,000 PLN per closed lot.
- No seller funding unless buyer deposit, title path, removal path, and risk exclusions are already confirmed.

Payback comes from one controlled lot transaction, not generic consulting. The POC fails if clean non-regulated lots are rare, if sellers expect too much of the upside, if buyers refuse deposits before 3PL receipt, or if Amazon removal timing is too uncertain.

Copy risk:

Liquidators, auction sites, 3PLs, FBA prep centers, and Amazon agencies already exist. The wedge is not "liquidate Amazon inventory." It is controlled access to exact stranded/removal lots before disposal or broad liquidation, routed by agencies and prep centers that see seller distress first, with title/removal/deposit paperwork signed before capital exposure. Once a seller signs the conditional title/option, removal path, and a buyer deposits against the exact manifest, incumbents cannot copy that specific lot.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup controls title or a conditional title option over a named inventory lot, the removal-to-3PL path, and a downstream buyer deposit. It does not sell listing reinstatement, account-health consulting, a liquidation marketplace, a dashboard, or an inventory report.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- The founder does not help reinstate listings, appeal account-health cases, certify compliance, clear IP disputes, certify product safety, or provide legal advice.
- Account-health agencies handle appeals only if the seller wants reinstatement; this product begins when the seller chooses liquidation/resale instead.
- 3PLs handle receiving, counting, storage, palletization, and shipment.
- Buyers remain responsible for downstream resale legality, product claims, consumer labeling, warranties, tax/VAT treatment, and channel restrictions.
- Legal/accounting review covers title transfer, VAT/invoices, referral fees, shortage adjustments, and seller/buyer terms.
- Excluded categories are not waived for attractive economics.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed and unconfirmed ideas:

- Not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: this does not unblock ASINs or prepare RED/GPSR/CE evidence. It monetizes exact stranded/removal inventory after the seller has chosen not to keep fighting the platform block.
- Not DORA, CBAM, EUDR, GreenTender, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, or HeatQuiet: no evidence pack, compliance memo, health diligence, municipal event rail, or dispute packet.
- Not Factory Control-Spares, Forklift Option Buyout, HeritageDoor, StormTile, or HeritageRadiator: inventory is platform-held ecommerce stock under removal/disposal pressure, not industrial spares, lease-end equipment, or architectural salvage.
- Not CarrierProof or Approved Carrier Credit Lockbox: no receivable or parcel claim collection.
