# Simulated Round 75: GeneBlocked IVD/DNA Kit Marketplace Evidence Rescue Desk

Date: 2026-05-26

## Why This Pivot

Round 73 failed because a biosignal buyer questionnaire was not durable enough control. Round 74 failed because recurring workplace-testing route books may not exist in transferable form and retainers may be weak.

Round 75 returns to the highest-scoring confirmed pattern: a live platform/channel revenue block. It applies that shape to genetics/IVD/DNA/sample-collection kits rather than connected cameras or general medtech.

## Candidate

`GeneBlocked IVD/DNA Kit Marketplace Evidence Rescue Desk`

A 48-72h evidence-response desk for EU/CEE marketplace-compliance agencies, e-commerce agencies, and small importers/sellers of at-home DNA, paternity, nutrigenetic, microbiome, fertility, STI, lateral-flow, sample-collection, and other consumer-facing IVD or quasi-IVD kits whose Amazon, Allegro, eBay, Shopify payment provider, reseller, or B2B distributor channel is blocked by a live compliance-document request.

The desk does not sell genetic tests, process samples, interpret results, advertise medical claims, certify products, give legal advice, act as a notified body, or touch patient/customer samples or genetic data. It only triages the evidence that can honestly be supplied for a live sales-channel block and routes formal IVDR/GDPR/legal/lab issues to qualified advisors.

## Buyer

Primary channel buyer:

- Amazon/Allegro/e-commerce account-health and marketplace-compliance agencies;
- small EU/CEE importers or sellers of consumer-facing test kits;
- Shopify/payment-risk consultants helping sellers with blocked checkouts or product claims;
- CE/IVDR/lab advisors who do not want fast marketplace-response work.

Economic buyer:

- seller/importer with a live listing suppression, document request, payment hold, reseller-onboarding block, or distributor evidence request tied to a revenue channel.

## Acute Trigger

A real channel asks for:

- CE/IVDR status, Declaration of Conformity, importer/economic-operator data, EU authorised representative, UDI or registration evidence where applicable;
- IFU/labelling language evidence, sample-collection instructions, product images, intended-use wording, and claims boundary;
- proof that a test is professional-use, self-test, RUO, wellness, or non-medical where honestly applicable;
- GDPR/privacy/data-flow statement, lab/provider role, sample shipping, retention, deletion, or genetic-data handling evidence;
- GPSR/product safety or marketplace safety docs;
- proof that the seller is not making prohibited disease, treatment, fertility, ancestry, nutrition, or genetic-health claims beyond evidence.

## Control Point

The hard control is the live blocked channel ticket:

- Seller Central/Allegro/payment-provider/reseller evidence request text;
- blocked ASIN/listing/product page/order flow/payment hold;
- agency client relationship or seller-paid fixed fee;
- supplier/manufacturer/lab evidence folder;
- buyer-ready response dossier submitted to the channel or reseller;
- case memory of what evidence is accepted, rejected, escalated to lab/legal/IVDR counsel, or killed as unsafe to claim.

No live blocked channel or reseller request means no project.

## Deliverable

For one SKU/listing/channel block:

- request interpretation;
- submit / cannot-submit / counsel-needed / lab-needed decision;
- CE/IVDR/DoC/label/IFU inconsistency check;
- claim-boundary table;
- seller/product evidence index;
- supplier/lab RFI in Polish/English/Chinese-friendly English;
- channel response wording;
- do-not-claim list;
- escalation map to IVDR, privacy, lab, payment-risk, or marketplace counsel;
- handoff memo for the agency or seller.

## 2-Month Proof

Required proof:

1. 50 marketplace/e-commerce/account-health agencies contacted and 10 CE/IVDR/privacy/lab advisors contacted.
2. 4 live or recent blocked IVD/DNA/test-kit channel cases reviewed.
3. 2 paid cases at 5,000-12,000 PLN, or one multi-SKU sprint above 15,000 PLN.
4. At least 15,000 PLN collected or prepaid.
5. One response submitted to a real channel/reseller/payment provider and either unblocked, progressed, or honestly escalated/killed as impossible.
6. One IVDR/CE reviewer and one privacy/data-protection reviewer available on paid per-case boundaries.
7. 60%+ gross margin after reviewer costs.
8. Written rule that the desk handles documents only and never touches samples, results, consumers, prescriptions, diagnoses, or genetic data.

## First Acquisition Mechanism

- Build a target list of marketplace account-health, reinstatement, product-compliance, CE/IVDR, and e-commerce agencies.
- Ask for live or recent blocked test-kit listings or reseller document requests, not general calls.
- Produce one redacted sample dossier from public marketplace/product claims.
- Let agencies keep 25-35% margin as channel owners.
- Route formal legal, CE/IVDR, lab, and privacy work to qualified reviewers or partners.

## Economics

- 2,000-3,000 PLN triage only.
- 5,000-12,000 PLN full SKU/channel response dossier.
- 15,000-30,000 PLN multi-SKU seller sprint.
- 25-35% agency margin.
- Reviewer costs should stay under 25-35% of gross revenue because reviewers cover flagged evidence boundaries only.

Willingness to pay comes from blocked marketplace revenue, payment holds, stuck inventory, or reseller/distributor onboarding.

## Why It Is Not A Generic Report Or Marketplace

Reject if there is no live blocked listing, payment hold, reseller evidence request, or distributor onboarding block.

The desk is not a genetic-test seller, lab, telemedicine service, consumer app, interpretation service, affiliate storefront, or compliance newsletter. It is a document-response layer for blocked channel revenue.

## Copy Risk

The model is copyable by marketplace agencies, IVDR consultants, privacy lawyers, and CE/lab advisors.

The wedge is narrower:

- urgent blocked sales-channel tickets;
- DNA/IVD/test-kit category focus;
- agency-routed distribution;
- cross-border supplier/lab evidence RFIs;
- marketplace-response memory;
- explicit escalation rather than pretending to certify or validate.

`88 / 100`

## Simulated Rationale

This clears simulated scoring because it has a live revenue-block control point, strong urgency, low capital requirements, fast cash conversion, agency-led CAC, and genetics/IVD specificity while avoiding genetic data, sample handling, diagnosis, prescribing, or direct test sales.

The score is not higher because:

- sellers may lack valid evidence;
- platforms may reject the category regardless of documents;
- IVDR and genetic-data rules are trust-heavy;
- the category may be smaller than connected-device compliance blocks;
- advisors/agencies can copy the workflow;
- the first cases may become "do not sell this claim" rather than unblock.

## Gate Decision

Advance to real working-chat Zero To One validation.

If it passes, fresh-validate the identical wording. If it fails below 82, pivot away from evidence desks and back to titled physical assets or assigned payment flow.
