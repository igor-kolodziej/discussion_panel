Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

Cloud Marketplace Private-Offer Payout Release Mandate

One-sentence thesis:

A signed-mandate payout-release desk for B2B SaaS vendors whose AWS Marketplace, Microsoft commercial marketplace/Azure Marketplace, or Google Cloud Marketplace private-offer revenue is already won but missing, delayed, short-paid, offset, unreconciled, or trapped behind marketplace, channel, tax, fee, or report mismatch.

Exact buyer:

Small and mid-sized B2B SaaS vendors, ISVs, data-product vendors, security tools, developer tools, analytics tools, and enterprise software companies with marketplace private offers or channel private offers above 100,000 PLN equivalent. The economic buyer is the founder, CFO, controller, RevOps lead, marketplace operations owner, alliances lead, or head of finance responsible for turning accepted private offers into cash.

Acute trigger:

An enterprise buyer has accepted, transacted, or paid a cloud marketplace private offer, channel private offer, CPPO-style transaction, or annual marketplace renewal, but the seller's expected payout does not match the seller's deal model or month-close records. The blocker is not winning the customer. It is a live cash/reconciliation file: missing disbursement, delayed payout, short remittance, unexpected marketplace fee, tax withholding, reseller/channel margin mismatch, wrong listing/SKU mapping, wrong contract date, wrong bank or legal entity, missing invoice reference, support-case loop, or report mismatch across offer, order, invoice, payout, and bank deposit.

Control point:

Signed recovery mandate for a named marketplace transaction or payout batch, plus delegated portal access or exports, private-offer/order IDs, buyer PO or contract evidence, listing/SKU data, invoice records, marketplace disbursement reports, tax/fee/withholding table, channel/reseller margin evidence where relevant, bank deposit records, support-case IDs, communication authority, submitted reconciliation packet, payout tracker, no-go memo, and success-fee right. The startup controls the assembly and pursuit of that named payout file through the seller's authorized marketplace or channel route.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

- Three signed recovery mandates over named private-offer or marketplace-payout files above 100,000 PLN equivalent each.
- At least five named transactions or payout lines with offer/order IDs, expected-vs-received payout math, and portal/report evidence.
- At least two marketplace, cloud-provider, distributor, reseller, or channel support cases acknowledged with case IDs.
- One corrected payout, credit memo, scheduled disbursement, written platform/channel explanation that changes the seller's books, or paid no-go determination.
- One collected intake fee, recovery fee, or success-fee invoice.
- Reusable issue matrix by marketplace, private-offer type, report source, fee/tax/channel path, rejection reason, support wording, and final payout evidence.

6-month POC:

Handle 20-30 qualified payout files across 8-12 SaaS vendors, submit 12-18 complete reconciliation/support packets, produce 6+ corrected payouts, credits, scheduled disbursements, or paid no-go determinations, release or correctly schedule at least 1.5-3.0 million PLN equivalent in seller cash, collect 150,000-300,000 PLN in fees, and document repeat patterns by cloud marketplace, private-offer structure, channel route, report mismatch, support escalation path, and corrected payout proof. Continue only if at least two partner channels send repeat files and gross margin remains above 70% after accounting/tax reviewer and contractor costs.

First acquisition mechanism:

Partner-routed outbound through cloud marketplace GTM agencies, AWS/Azure/GCP marketplace consultants, channel private-offer advisers, fractional CFOs for SaaS, RevOps consultants, startup finance operators, cloud alliance consultants, CPPO/channel partners, and SaaS founder communities. The ask is specific: "send only accepted marketplace private offers or renewals where the seller expected cash and the payout, report, fee, tax, channel margin, or bank deposit does not reconcile." Direct outreach can target SaaS companies that publicly announce marketplace availability, cloud co-sell wins, enterprise marketplace transactions, or marketplace-led procurement.

Economics:

Charge 4,000-8,000 PLN for intake/no-go and 6-12% of recovered, corrected, or newly scheduled payout, capped per file, or 10,000-25,000 PLN fixed for high-value non-disputed files. A single 100,000-1,000,000 PLN marketplace transaction can justify the fee if cash is already won but not correctly disbursed or reconciled. Founder work is document intake, payout math, report mapping, discrepancy classification, support packet assembly, follow-up routing, no-go decisions, and partner coordination. Accountants, tax advisers, legal counsel, and revenue-recognition owners remain responsible for accounting, tax, legal, and revenue-recognition judgments.

Copy risk:

Cloud marketplace GTM agencies, marketplace operations consultants, Tackle/Clazar/Labra-style tooling providers, RevOps consultants, fractional CFOs, channel partners, distributors, accountants, and internal finance teams can copy the workflow. The defense is strict live-file qualification, fast transaction-level payout reconstruction, repeat support-case wording, partner channels that see stuck payout files but do not want contingency recovery work, and case memory across cloud marketplace, private-offer structure, fee/tax/channel path, rejection reason, support route, and final payout proof.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once the startup has the signed mandate, transaction IDs, private-offer records, portal exports, disbursement reports, bank deposit evidence, submitted reconciliation packet, support cases, and success-fee right, a competitor cannot work that exact payout file unless the seller switches mid-case and recreates the packet. The practical control is over a named current cash file and the seller-authorized support route, not over generic marketplace knowledge.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The output is not a cloud marketplace launch plan, GTM consulting, revenue dashboard, SaaS spend audit, accounting report, broker introduction, or generic reconciliation template. The proof is a corrected payout, released cash, credit memo, scheduled disbursement, platform/channel explanation that resolves the accounting treatment, or paid no-go determination for a named private-offer payout file.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

No tax advice, legal advice, accounting opinion, revenue-recognition opinion, contract renegotiation, buyer dispute, collections pressure, or guaranteed platform outcome. Reject files where the enterprise buyer has not accepted or paid, the underlying contract is disputed, fraud is alleged, the seller lacks authority, the portal evidence is missing, the expected payout is only a sales forecast, or the problem requires legal or tax interpretation before the payout path can be pursued. Accountants, tax advisers, auditors, legal counsel, cloud marketplace teams, distributors, and channel partners remain responsible for their regulated or provider roles. Access must use least-privilege exports or delegated access approved by the seller.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

This is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central deduction recovery, Partner Center incentive recovery, SaaS usage true-up, retailer chargeback recovery, DORA evidence, or generic cloud marketplace enablement. The distinct control object is a seller-side cloud marketplace private-offer payout file where an enterprise sale has already happened and the proof is corrected cash, scheduled disbursement, credit, or a paid no-go outcome.

Strongest anticipated objections:

- Many apparent discrepancies are normal marketplace fees, tax withholding, payment timing, reseller/channel margin, FX, contract terms, revenue-recognition rules, or reporting lag.
- Serious SaaS vendors with large marketplace revenue may already have marketplace operations, finance, RevOps, cloud alliance, accountant, or platform-support capacity.
- Cloud marketplace GTM agencies and tooling vendors can add payout troubleshooting once they see repeated cases.
- Platform and channel support can be slow, opaque, or unresponsive, limiting turnaround and success-fee attribution.
- Public signals for stuck payout files are weak, so acquisition depends on partner channels and precise qualification.
- The market may be too narrow if most vendors have only one or two marketplace transactions per year.
- The workflow can drift into tax, accounting, legal, revenue-recognition, or contract interpretation unless no-go boundaries are enforced.
- Sellers may resist sharing portal exports, bank deposits, buyer PO data, and private-offer details with an unknown solo founder.
