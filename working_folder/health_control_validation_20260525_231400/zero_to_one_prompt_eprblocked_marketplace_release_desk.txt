Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
EPRBlocked Marketplace Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A white-label release desk for Amazon, Kaufland, Cdiscount, Allegro, TikTok Shop, and marketplace-compliance agencies handling EU marketplace sellers whose listings, FBA/inventory movement, or account compliance are blocked by country/category EPR and product-responsibility evidence requests.

Exact buyer:
Marketplace account-health agencies, Amazon reinstatement/compliance agencies, EU marketplace agencies, and ecommerce compliance agencies serving sellers with live EPR/product-responsibility requests. The economic payer is the seller or brand with revenue-producing listings, inventory, or marketplace expansion blocked by EPR evidence in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Poland, or other EU markets.

Acute trigger:
A marketplace asks for proof of packaging, WEEE, battery, textile, furniture, paper, or other country/category producer-responsibility registration, authorized representative/provider evidence, EPR registration number, French UIN, German LUCID/VerpackG evidence, WEEE/battery registration, take-back arrangement, category classification, or marketplace country/category mapping. Listings are suppressed, at risk, blocked from expansion, or inventory movement is delayed until acceptable evidence is submitted.

Control point:
Signed agency-routed live-case mandate with marketplace request text, seller account/case screenshots, country/category/SKU list, sales-country map, product category evidence, existing registration/provider documents, invoices or scheme receipts, responsible-producer role notes, missing-evidence matrix, provider/escalation path, marketplace submission wording, response log, and clean no-go rules. The startup controls the live release case file and evidence-submission package, not the seller's legal producer obligations.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three marketplace agencies route at least one live case each; ten prepaid live cases at 3,000-15,000 PLN each; every case has a blocked listing, inventory, expansion, or account-health/compliance warning tied to EPR/product-responsibility evidence; at least five cases involve more than one country or category rather than commodity single-country packaging admin; at least five cases produce accepted evidence, SKU/listing progress, inventory/compliance progress, provider registration progress, or clean no-go; gross margin above 60% after agency margin, EPR provider fees, and reviewer costs.

6-month POC:
Handle 35-80 paid cases through 5-10 agencies; collect 180,000-500,000 PLN in gross fees; keep average turnaround under 5 business days for evidence-ready cases; get at least three agencies to send 5+ cases; produce 20+ accepted/progress outcomes or clean no-go decisions; build a private country/category evidence taxonomy by marketplace, request wording, EPR stream, registration document, response pattern, and rejection reason.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 80 marketplace agencies, Amazon account-health consultants, EU compliance agencies, and ecommerce service providers. Offer a white-label escalation desk: "When a seller's EU marketplace listing or inventory is blocked by EPR evidence, send the request, SKU/country/category map, and existing provider docs. I return the marketplace-ready evidence matrix, missing-evidence route, submission wording, and clean no-go. You keep the client relationship and 25-35% margin."

Economics:
Charge 3,000-5,000 PLN for narrow evidence-ready cases, 5,000-9,000 PLN for multi-country or multi-category cases, and 9,000-15,000 PLN for urgent high-revenue listings or messy provider-document cases. The seller pays because marketplace revenue or inventory movement is blocked. Agency margin is 25-35%. The startup does not become the EPR scheme, producer, legal adviser, tax adviser, or marketplace guarantee provider.

Copy risk:
EPR providers, tax advisers, marketplace agencies, Amazon service providers, compliance platforms, and seller consultants can copy parts. The defense is narrow live-release focus, agency distribution, request wording memory, evidence taxonomy by marketplace/country/category, accepted/rejected response history, and fast routing between seller, agency, EPR provider, and marketplace case.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once an agency routes a live seller case, shares the marketplace request, case screenshots, SKU/country/category map, existing registrations, provider documents, and seller authorization, the startup controls the active release file and submission package for that marketplace case. A generic EPR provider would need to displace the agency relationship, the case timeline, the evidence matrix, and the response log under listing/inventory pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It accepts only live marketplace listing, inventory, expansion, or account-health blockers tied to EPR/product-responsibility evidence. The output is a case artifact used in an active marketplace workflow: evidence matrix, missing-document route, provider/escalation request, country/category/SKU map, submission wording, response log, and no-go notes. It is not broad EPR readiness, generic registration admin, a dashboard, a database, marketplace software, or brokered introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, tax advice, producer-responsibility legal opinions, EPR scheme services, official representative-of-record service, product safety certification, or guarantee of marketplace acceptance. Sellers, producers, importers, authorized representatives, EPR schemes/providers, tax/legal advisers, and marketplaces remain responsible for role determination, registration, fees, declarations, take-back obligations, official filings, and legal compliance. Cases involving false registrations, forged documents, counterfeit goods, unsafe products, sanctions, fraud, or unclear seller authority are declined or routed to qualified providers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked because it does not handle connected-device RED/CE/GPSR cybersecurity or Amazon baby-monitor/camera evidence. EPRBlocked handles marketplace product-responsibility registration evidence by country/category across packaging, WEEE, batteries, textiles, and related EPR streams. The buyer channel is similar marketplace agencies, but the control artifacts, trigger, provider routing, and proof of progress are materially different. It is not DORA, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Single-country EPR registration is already served by EPR providers, tax advisers, Amazon service-provider networks, and marketplace compliance agencies. Agencies may already handle common LUCID/UIN/WEEE/Battery evidence internally. Many cases may require actual registration or fee payment before any response can work, which is outside a desk's control. Marketplace outcomes are opaque and not guaranteed. Legal role determination can be sensitive. Fees may be too low if provider chasing and country/category classification consume time. The idea may be too close to existing marketplace compliance services unless it proves repeated agency-routed live release cases with accepted evidence outcomes.
