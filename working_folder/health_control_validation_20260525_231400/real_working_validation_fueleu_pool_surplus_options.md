## Validator Summary

Zero To One accepted that FuelEU pooling is a real legal/commercial mechanism and that named surplus/deficit matching is directionally intelligent. It failed the idea because the market is already visible, platformized, relationship-heavy, and served by maritime verifiers, compliance platforms, bunker advisers, lawyers, and specialist consultants.

The evaluator said the startup lacks the unfair access needed to control meaningful surplus before incumbents: privileged surplus supply, buyer demand, verifier trust, maritime legal authority, or platform liquidity.

## Strongest Objections Captured

- Existing FuelEU pooling marketplaces and managed-service providers already offer surplus/deficit matching, pricing transparency, legal workflow support, and access to many vessels.
- Market pricing may compress because surplus supply appears abundant in early signals, reducing buyer willingness to pay deposits or spreads.
- The option is over an expected compliance balance, not a finalized allowance. Routes, fuel mix, verifier treatment, charterer behavior, eligibility, and data corrections can change the balance.
- Counterparty trust is the scarce asset: ship authority, data rights, pooling rights, owner/charterer allocation, and pool-collapse terms are complex.
- Small operators may be underserved but also slow, low-data, hard to forecast, or outside scope.
- Incumbents already sit close to the data and relationships, so they can attack both surplus and deficit sides.
- Legal/regulatory characterization and escrow/deposit design need maritime counsel.
- Transaction-specific option control expires and does not create a durable company unless the founder has exclusive CEE surplus access or a strong maritime partner.

## Evaluator's Best Narrowing

The evaluator suggested a seller-side research-only wedge:

CEE FuelEU surplus discovery and pre-April pool-readiness desk for small in-scope ship managers.

Better proof would be:

- 10 named in-scope ships reviewed for likely surplus/deficit;
- 3 surplus holders sign non-exclusive monetization mandates;
- 1 maritime lawyer or FuelEU adviser signs off on the structure;
- 1 marketplace, verifier-adjacent adviser, or bunker desk agrees to receive the package;
- 1 buyer-side reservation or paid advisory fee above 20,000 PLN.

Even then, this would be a test, not a confirmed idea.

## Search Lesson

Formal regulatory pooling rights are not enough when established platforms already own liquidity and trust. Future entitlement/certificate ideas need a genuinely proprietary seller-side channel or current buyer cash that cannot simply route through the incumbent marketplace.
