Date: 2026-05-27

## Pivot From Round 96

KasaSerwis Authorized Fiscal Device Book Buyout failed working-chat validation at `70 / 100`.

Reason to pivot: the evaluator treated the authorized fiscal technician/provider as the operating core. This round looks for hard-control acquisition targets where a Warsaw solo founder can control the account, customer authorization, payment direction, and renewal cash without relying on a licensed specialist as the scarce asset.

## Current Source Notes

Official transfer rails exist for many digital infrastructure accounts:

- ICANN domain-transfer materials describe domain-holder transfer procedures and registrar-transfer policy: `https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/registrars/transfers-en`
- NASK describes .pl domain transfer / authinfo procedures for the Polish registry ecosystem: `https://nask-registrars.pl/en/transfer_domain_pl`
- Microsoft Partner Center documents customer subscription/billing-owner transfer between CSP partners: `https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/customers/transfer-subscriptions`
- Google Workspace Admin Help documents transfer of paid Google Workspace subscriptions between Google and resellers: `https://support.google.com/a/answer/7643791`

The hard-control pattern is not "do IT support." It is buying or optioning an existing renewal/admin/payment book from a retiring small web agency, hoster, MSP, or reseller, with customer authorizations and current renewal cash before closing.

## Search Implication

This candidate deliberately avoids a broad MSP rollup. The real asset must be a signed, customer-approved renewal/admin/payment book:

- customer remains the registrant/tenant owner;
- founder controls billing and support continuity only after consent;
- seller payout depends on current-month/quarter renewal cash;
- bespoke rebuilds, app development, and broad helpdesk work are excluded unless prepaid.
