Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
Lender-Routed BBC Deficiency Release Bench.

One-sentence thesis:
Small ABL lenders, factors, commercial-finance brokers, and turnaround advisers can route borrowers with written borrowing-base certificate deficiencies to a narrow paid desk that turns the exact lender exception into a corrected borrower-certified submission, accepted draw package, or clean no-go before availability, overadvance, or covenant pressure worsens.

Exact buyer:
The routing buyer is a small or mid-market asset-based lender, invoice factor, receivables finance company, commercial-finance broker, turnaround adviser, or collateral-monitoring boutique that sees repeated borrower reporting defects but does not want to become the borrower bookkeeping team.

The economic payor is the borrower with an active ABL, factoring, inventory-backed, or borrowing-base facility whose current draw, availability, collateral eligibility, overadvance cure, or lender relationship is blocked by written deficiency text.

Best first borrowers have 5,000,000-80,000,000 PLN revenue, repeated AR or inventory borrowing-base reporting, a controller or bookkeeper who can certify facts, and enough blocked availability that a 10,000-35,000 PLN urgent case fee is rational.

Acute trigger:
A lender or factor sends written deficiency text that blocks or reduces a draw, cuts eligible collateral, imposes a reserve, threatens overadvance/default discussion, or requires corrected reporting before further funding. Typical deficiency text covers AR aging mismatch, unapplied cash, credit memos, contra accounts, concentration caps, cross-aged debtors, ineligible foreign or affiliate receivables, inventory roll-forward mismatch, slow-moving inventory, proof-of-shipment gaps, debtor disputes, borrowing-base certificate math, collateral schedule tie-outs, or missing support for an exception.

The desk accepts only current draw or availability blockers. It rejects generic reporting cleanup, covenant advisory, turnaround consulting, field-exam crisis work, fraud suspicion, and bookkeeping remediation without a named lender/factor exception.

Control point:
Signed borrower mandate for a named lender/factor deficiency ticket; routing note or referral terms from the lender, factor, broker, adviser, or collateral-monitoring partner; the exact lender deficiency text; current borrowing-base template; latest submitted borrowing-base certificate; AR aging, inventory report, cash-application export, sales/credit memo/open-item support, shipment/proof-of-delivery support where relevant; borrower certification owner; reviewer notes; correction log; submission timestamp; lender/factor response; accepted package, narrowed exception, restored availability, scheduled draw, or clean no-go.

The startup does not certify collateral truth, value inventory, opine on legal eligibility, decide covenant status, perform field exams, provide audit opinions, provide legal advice, or negotiate lender rights. The borrower certifies facts and the lender/factor decides availability.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Two lenders, factors, commercial-finance brokers, or turnaround advisers sign referral or routing terms for deficiency tickets they do not want to staff internally. Six borrowers sign paid deficiency-release mandates tied to written lender/factor exceptions and current availability or draw consequences. At least 300,000 PLN of blocked or reduced availability is under active correction files. At least 75,000 PLN is collected in fixed fees before delivery. At least two cases produce objective outcomes: lender accepts a corrected borrowing-base package, releases or schedules a draw, narrows an ineligible/reserve item, confirms a clean no-go, or gives a written next-step list that prevents further wasted borrower submissions.

6-month POC:
Secure 5-8 repeat routing partners among small ABL lenders, factors, finance brokers, turnaround advisers, and collateral-monitoring boutiques. Handle 35-70 paid deficiency tickets. Collect 450,000-1,200,000 PLN in fixed case fees and urgent surcharges. Produce 20-40 objective outcomes: accepted corrected BBC, restored or scheduled availability, reserve narrowed, deficiency cured, lender next-step list accepted, or clean no-go where books/collateral cannot support the requested draw. Build a private taxonomy by lender/factor template, ERP/export source, deficiency category, ineligible logic, required support, accepted correction pattern, and no-go cause. Keep gross margin above 60% after ABL/factor reviewer costs, secure storage, and contractor data-prep work. Reject at least 30% of weak, fraudulent, legal, or broken-book cases.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with lenders and brokers, not distressed borrowers. Build a target list of Polish/CEE and EU small factors, receivables financiers, ABL lenders, commercial-finance brokers, turnaround advisers, and collateral-monitoring operators that serve SMEs but lack a formal borrower reporting cleanup bench.

Partner outreach:
"When a borrower sends a broken borrowing-base certificate and your team has already written the deficiency, send the borrower to me with the exact exception text. I do not decide eligibility or certify collateral. I turn the borrower facts and your template into a corrected borrower-certified package or a clean no-go, so your credit team stops receiving bad resubmissions."

Borrower outreach through partner:
"Your lender/factor has already named the reporting defect blocking availability. Send the exact deficiency, template, aging/inventory/export files, and borrower certification owner. We produce a corrected submission package or a clean no-go. We do not promise a draw and we do not certify collateral truth."

Economics:
Standard urgent deficiency package: 10,000-18,000 PLN prepaid for one written deficiency ticket and one corrected submission cycle.

Complex multi-ledger or inventory/AR package: 18,000-35,000 PLN prepaid where blocked availability exceeds 750,000 PLN or where lender deadline is inside five business days.

Partner economics: 10-20% referral share, wholesale pricing, or fixed partner fee where lawful and disclosed.

Reviewer cost: 2,000-8,000 PLN per case for a senior ABL/factor reviewer depending on complexity. Data preparation contractors handle exports, schedules, tie-outs, and packet assembly under borrower approval.

No success-fee-only cases. No speculative "find savings" reports. The fee is justified by current liquidity value, avoided failed resubmissions, and lender relationship protection.

Copy risk:
Lenders, factors, accountants, borrower CFOs, field exam firms, collateral-monitoring vendors, and turnaround advisers can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is narrow routing into current deficiency tickets, accepted correction memory by lender/factor template, and fast execution before month-end or draw-deadline pressure. Once the startup controls a specific borrower mandate, lender deficiency text, data room, correction log, and submission timetable, a competitor cannot take over that exact draw event without restarting authorization and evidence intake.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
The controlled object is not general ABL consulting. It is a named borrower deficiency ticket with written lender/factor exception text, a current draw or availability consequence, borrower certification owner, lender template, specific exports, submission deadline, and fee right. A lender can choose another provider next time, but once the borrower mandate and ticket file are active, the immediate draw event is controlled by the desk until accepted, narrowed, or killed.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The desk accepts only live deficiency tickets with written lender/factor text and current availability consequences. Each case ends in a borrower-certified corrected package, lender/factor response, restored or scheduled draw, narrowed exception, or clean no-go. It does not sell dashboards, generic reporting cleanup, bookkeeping, a database, software, field exams, financing introductions, lender brokerage, or advice memos.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The borrower remains responsible for all collateral facts, receivable validity, inventory existence, shipment status, title, eligibility, covenant compliance, and borrowing-base certification. The lender or factor remains responsible for credit decisions, reserves, availability, ineligible treatment, default rights, and funding decisions. Accountants, lawyers, field examiners, appraisers, and auditors remain responsible for bookkeeping remediation, legal interpretation, inventory valuation, audit opinions, fraud investigation, and field examination.

Reject cases with fraud suspicion, fabricated invoices, disputed collateral ownership, missing books, insolvency/workout negotiations, legal disputes with lender, covenant default advice, inventory valuation dispute, debtor concentration fight requiring credit judgment, sanctions issues, litigation, receivable collectability disputes, related-party concealment, or any request for the startup to certify facts instead of packaging borrower-certified evidence.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because there is no regulated-finance ICT supplier evidence, bank vendor questionnaire, operational-resilience pack, subcontractor list, exit plan, audit-rights response, or DORA renewal. The buyer trigger is a borrowing-base deficiency blocking borrower liquidity under an ABL/factoring facility.

This is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retailer deductions, promotions, retail media, chargebacks, pricing leakage, or trade-spend accruals. It is not KSeF/AP invoice release because it does not depend on buyer invoice acceptance, service entry, Polish e-invoice status, payable acknowledgment, or factorable-invoice confirmation. It is not BorrowingBase Certificate Book Buyout because it does not acquire a seller's recurring reporting book; it uses lender/factor-routed current deficiency tickets. It is not GreenTender, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, CBAM, Data Act, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack, Amazon recovery, LC discrepancy, guarantee-line release, construction retention, or physical-lot control.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Lenders and factors may not want to refer borrowers to an unknown outside desk, especially if borrower reporting quality reflects credit risk. Borrowers may prefer their accountant, controller, fractional CFO, or lender's normal support process. Field exam firms, collateral-monitoring vendors, turnaround advisers, and finance-ops consultants can internalize the workflow. Many deficiency tickets reveal broken books, fraud risk, collateral disputes, ineligible receivables, stale inventory, or actual liquidity distress, not a packaging problem. Borrower-certified facts create liability if the desk appears to validate collateral. Lender templates and reserve logic vary enough to make each case bespoke. Month-end and draw-deadline spikes are hard for a part-time founder. Pricing only works when blocked availability is material and source data exists. Partner channels may send only messy adverse-selection cases. The idea becomes generic finance-ops consulting if intake accepts reporting cleanup without written deficiency text and current draw consequences.
