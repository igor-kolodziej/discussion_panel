## Internal Simulation For Selected Candidate

**Idea:** EEvidence Addressee Appointment Release Desk

**Simulated score:** 88/100

**Why it clears the simulated gate**

- The proof artifact is harder than a report: written designation/appointment of an EU legal representative or designated establishment through a qualified provider/law-firm route, plus internal order-intake runbook and customer-ready proof.
- The deadline is close enough to create commercial urgency before EU procurement, enterprise security review, or EU launch.
- The target segment is narrower than all SaaS: non-EU or cross-border providers of communications, hosting, storage, cloud, collaboration, domain/DNS, email, messaging, and account platforms that actually fall into the e-Evidence service-provider universe and already sell into the EU.
- The startup can reject legal advice and order-response handling while still owning the execution workflow for appointment readiness.
- Acquisition can ride privacy/security/procurement lawyers, DPOs, EU reps, and compliance consultants who do not want to package the operational evidence layer for smaller providers.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Cap below 82 if the startup acts as legal representative, evaluates order lawfulness, touches content production decisions, or handles law-enforcement orders directly.
- Cap below 85 if it sells generic e-Evidence readiness without a live procurement/customer/EU-launch blocker.
- Cap below 87 if qualified appointment providers/law firms will not sign referral/resale routes or if buyers can solve it with one law-firm email.
- Cap below 87 if customer proof is only a memo rather than signed appointment evidence, request-intake ownership, escalation contacts, and board/management authorization.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

Zero To One may still view this as legal/compliance coordination owned by law firms and privacy consultants. The real test is whether the official appointment artifact and narrow live customer blocker make it materially stronger than a generic compliance evidence desk.
