## Selected Candidate

**BDO DPR/EDPR Allocation Lockbox**

### Internal Gate Review

Buyer:
Packaging recovery organisations, environmental-compliance operators, and producers/importers that need material-specific 2026 DPR/EDPR documents for packaging recovery/recycling obligations or product-fee exposure.

Acute trigger:
For 2026 periods, DPR/EDPR applications and issuance are in the BDO Confirmation Module. Buyers need valid material-specific allocations, while smaller recyclers/exporters/source entities may have surplus document potential and weak monetisation/admin capacity.

Transferable control point:
The startup only controls a transaction after a source signs an exclusive allocation mandate over a named material, quarter, source entity, mass range, and recipient-designation workflow; a buyer signs a deposit/take-or-pay reservation for the same stream; and payment direction/lockbox terms are acknowledged by both sides. BDO account holders remain responsible for lawful BDO actions.

60-day proof:
One source-side mandate for 50-300 tonnes, one buyer deposit/reservation for part of the same stream, verified BDO registration/source authority, payment-direction acknowledgement, environmental-law boundary review, and first BDO Confirmation Module application/designation action or a written no-go.

Economics:
Use deposit-funded allocation fees or a 5-15% spread. The first stream must plausibly generate 8,000-25,000 PLN gross profit or fee without inventory capital. Six-month proof requires 4-8 streams, 300-1,500 tonnes under mandate/reservation, 120,000-400,000 PLN gross fee/spread, and at least two repeat buyers/sources.

Copy risk:
High category-level copy risk from recovery organisations and waste brokers. The defense is not knowledge; it is signed source exclusivity, buyer deposit, payment direction, and acceptance history for exact named streams.

Boundaries:
The startup does not certify recycling, calculate obligations, issue documents, use another party's BDO account, act as recovery organisation, advise on product-fee law, or create waste/mass evidence. Sources, buyers, and their compliance advisers retain those responsibilities.

### Internal Objections

- Incumbent recovery organisations may already have better direct source relationships, compliance trust, and price discovery.
- Legal transferability must be checked carefully; exclusivity and payment direction cannot become unauthorised BDO account use, environmental advice, or document issuance.
- Unknown-founder trust may make buyer deposits hard.
- If the BDO module improves transparency, spreads may compress fast.
- Source-side fraud/fake mass/document pressure is a serious kill risk.
