# Simulated Round 148: Partner-Program Monetary Claim Queues

Date: 2026-05-29
Real browser gate: working Zero To One >=85, fresh Zero To One >=85

## Search Frame

Recent rounds failed because current-cash claims were either already owned by strong recovery incumbents, too close to legal/tax/customs interpretation, or dependent on missing source evidence. This round searches inside monetary portal queues where the buyer has already earned, been allocated, or can identify a specific payment/credit/incentive, and the founder can control the active claim packet, portal case, and success-fee mandate within 60 days.

The target shape is not audit software or generic consulting. It is a signed claim queue with current money, portal artifacts, case IDs, and first cash/credit movement.

## Lead Candidate

**PartnerCenter Incentive Claim Release Desk**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- Current cash is the starting point: incentive statements, co-op/MDF allocations, marketplace payouts, rebate lines, or support-case acknowledgements already exist.
- The founder controls a signed claim mandate, Partner Center evidence exports, claim IDs, support cases, proof-of-execution files, and recovery fee before doing work.
- The 60-day proof is cash or official portal movement, not a report: accepted claim, reinstated eligibility, corrected statement, support-case acknowledgement, paid incentive, or clean no-go.
- It avoids legal/tax advice by treating tax/bank profile failures as accountant-routed exclusions.
- It is materially different from PromoLeak and Amazon Vendor Central recovery: the claim queue is partner-program incentive money inside Microsoft Partner Center, not retailer deductions or Amazon remittances.
- The buyer can be reached through public Microsoft partner directories, CEE cloud communities, LinkedIn partner roles, ISV/CSP groups, and small distributors' downstream partners.

### Internal Objections

- Microsoft distributors, partner-development managers, partner-ops consultants, and accountants can help with many claims.
- Some blocked payouts are caused by tax/bank profile, eligibility, licensing, customer-association, or program-rule problems outside founder control.
- Partner Center access is sensitive and must be tightly permissioned.
- Many partners may not have enough claim face value to justify the fee.
- This is not defensible if it becomes generic partner-ops consulting instead of a narrow monetary claim queue.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the proof object is a controlled current-money claim queue.
