## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator treated this as a real capacity-control variant rather than just another RAG testing service. It agreed that small appsec, SOC2, and fractional-CISO partners may face AI-security evidence requests before they can justify hiring an internal AI-security specialist, and that prepaid capacity tied to live partner deadlines is commercially plausible.

The score fell below the prior RAGProbe and RAGModule variants because "reviewer slot bank" shifts the control point away from repeatable module IP and toward scarce expert scheduling. The evaluator viewed that as a brokered expert-services lane unless the startup clearly owns the test method, report-annex structure, trace library, caveat language, and partner workflow.

## Strongest Objections

1. The model can collapse into expert brokering if the startup mainly matches partners with reviewers.
2. The reviewer, not the founder, owns the credibility at 25,000-75,000 PLN slot prices.
3. Margins can compress badly if reviewer time overruns.
4. Named reviewer availability is hard to guarantee inside 7-business-day windows.
5. Partners may bypass the startup and contract directly with reviewers after one successful slot.
6. Larger AI-security vendors are more credible when buyers ask for independent AI red-team evidence.
7. Short bounded tests create false-assurance risk unless caveats are explicit.
8. Tool-using agent tests create sandboxing and side-effect risk.
9. Partner wholesale economics may become awkward at 60,000-90,000 PLN end-client prices.
10. A slot bank is weaker than a module bench because it sells access to scarce humans instead of process IP.

## Evaluator's Repositioning

The evaluator said not to lead with reviewer slots. The stronger positioning would be a reviewer-backed standardized RAG/agent security annex module, with reserved capacity only as the fulfillment engine. That is still below the current real gate because the AI-security branch remains capped by incumbent credibility, reviewer cost, false-assurance risk, and partner bypass.

## POC Rules Suggested By Evaluator

Within 6 months:

- partners do not bypass reviewers directly.

## Lesson

Stop testing AI-security variants in this run. The near-passing RAGProbe branch was strongest when it owned the evidence artifact; attempts to solve credibility through white-label partner lanes or reviewer capacity lowered the score. Future candidates must come from a different hard-control category: current cash recovery, direct payment direction, titled scarce lots with buyer deposits, or a non-AI regulatory trigger with a sharper live economic gate than ordinary evidence packaging.
