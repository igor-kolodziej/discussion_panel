Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
RAGTrace Buyer Holdback Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A payment-release desk for B2B SaaS vendors whose enterprise buyer is currently withholding a PO, renewal expansion, milestone payment, go-live acceptance, or security approval until a specific RAG/agent security evidence condition is answered with bounded test traces rather than policy text.

Exact buyer:
20-200 person B2B SaaS vendors with a deployed or pilot-stage RAG assistant, enterprise search feature, document-processing AI workflow, customer-support copilot, internal knowledge assistant, or limited tool-using agent. The vendor has a live enterprise buyer, channel partner, or implementation customer that has written a specific AI-security condition into procurement, security review, go-live acceptance, renewal expansion, milestone acceptance, or payment release. The first market excludes credit, insurance, HR decisioning, biometrics, clinical workflows, law enforcement, autonomous adverse decisions, regulated-finance model-risk work, and any case requiring formal certification or legal AI Act classification.

Acute trigger:
The buyer or implementation customer states in writing that payment, PO issue, renewal expansion, pilot conversion, go-live, milestone acceptance, or security approval is blocked until the vendor shows evidence for one or more bounded concerns: prompt injection, cross-tenant or cross-source leakage, sensitive source exposure, retrieval isolation, unsafe tool permissions, action approval, logging, human review, model-provider retention settings, data deletion, AI incident handling, or buyer-safe failure handling. The vendor has money or an acceptance event stuck now, not a general desire to improve AI security.

Control point:
Signed payment-release mandate with the SaaS vendor and, where available, the appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO/implementation partner already involved in the buyer review. The mandate includes the buyer's written condition, blocked economic event and value, deadline, authorized product scope, sandbox or demo account, synthetic tenant/source setup when needed, RAG corpus/sample documents, tool/action permission map, model/provider settings, logging access, human-review workflow evidence, no-go rules, and allowed buyer response format. The startup controls the scoped test plan, execution traces, evidence room, rework/no-go log, buyer-safe response wording, and release evidence for that specific holdback.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Six paid live holdback cases are signed at 18,000-55,000 PLN each with written buyer condition text and named blocked economic value. At least four cases include partner co-signoff or partner routing from a SOC2 consultant, fractional CISO, appsec boutique, AI implementation agency, or security-questionnaire firm. At least three cases produce a documented buyer condition release, narrowed follow-up, PO/security approval movement, milestone acceptance, go-live approval, paid invoice, or formal no-go/remediation decision accepted by the vendor. The startup collects at least 120,000 PLN within 60 days, documents buyer outcome evidence for every delivered case, and keeps external reviewer cost below 40% of revenue.

6-month POC:
Deliver 35-70 live holdback-release cases; collect 900,000-2,400,000 PLN in prepaid case fees; document 25+ buyer condition releases, narrowed follow-ups, payment/milestone/PO movements, or vendor-accepted no-go decisions; secure 8+ repeat routing partners; keep average delivery under 8 business days for bounded evidence-ready products; maintain gross margin above 60%; build a private library of buyer condition language, accepted trace formats, rejected trace formats, no-go triggers, retrieval-isolation failures, tool-permission failures, logging gaps, and buyer-safe response patterns.

First acquisition mechanism:
Contact SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, appsec boutiques, security-questionnaire firms, AI implementation agencies, and procurement-response consultants with a narrow offer: "When your SaaS client has buyer-held money or go-live blocked by a RAG/agent security evidence condition, route only that live holdback. I will not replace your audit, legal, or remediation work. I run bounded authorized traces and return a buyer-safe release/rework/no-go packet in under eight business days." The founder also targets SaaS founders and revenue leaders posting or discussing stalled enterprise AI security reviews, but only accepts cases with written buyer condition text and blocked economic value.

Economics:
Charge 18,000-28,000 PLN for one-feature RAG/source-isolation holdback cases, 30,000-45,000 PLN for multi-source RAG or customer-support copilot cases, and 45,000-75,000 PLN for urgent multi-feature or tool-using agent holdbacks tied to large renewals or implementation milestones. Cases are prepaid because they address buyer-held money, payment release, or go-live acceptance. External AI-security reviewer cost is budgeted at 6,000-20,000 PLN per case depending on scope. The vendor pays because a 20,000-60,000 PLN release packet is rational when a 150,000-1,500,000 PLN deal, milestone, expansion, or invoice is stuck.

Copy risk:
AI-security firms, appsec boutiques, penetration testers, red-team providers, GRC platforms, SOC2 consultants, AI governance firms, and internal security teams can copy pieces. The defense is not a generic method; it is controlling live written holdback cases, partner-routed urgency, buyer condition language, fast scoped test recipes, release/no-go outcome memory, and evidence formats accepted by actual enterprise buyers in small-SaaS RAG and agent procurements.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the vendor signs the mandate, provides the buyer's written condition, authorizes the sandbox, shares the relevant RAG/source/tool/logging scope, and prepays for the holdback release case, the startup controls the evidence traces and buyer response package for that specific deadline. A larger appsec or AI-security incumbent can compete generally, but it has to displace an already-authorized live case while the buyer condition is waiting and the partner or vendor has already committed to the response path.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business refuses general AI security reviews, readiness assessments, dashboards, questionnaires, marketplace matching, broad consulting, and nice-to-have reports. It accepts only live buyer-held-money or go-live acceptance cases with written buyer condition text, blocked economic value, vendor authorization, and bounded test scope. The deliverable is an evidence-backed release/rework/no-go packet containing test plan, trace evidence, source-isolation checks, tool-permission checks, logging/human-review evidence, buyer-safe wording, and the outcome record for the named holdback event.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, AI Act classification opinions, DPIAs, SOC2 certification, ISO 42001 implementation, formal penetration-test certification, model validation, safety certification, production incident response, or product remediation. It does not guarantee buyer approval. Partners, vendors, lawyers, DPOs, security officers, auditors, enterprise buyers, and implementation owners remain responsible for legal compliance, security obligations, privacy, remediation, procurement decisions, and formal assurance. Production testing requires written authorization and safe limits; destructive tests, real money movement, external email sending, privileged admin actions, and unbounded production data access are declined or routed to qualified firms.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it does not handle regulated-finance ICT third-party-risk, DORA clauses, exit plans, subcontracting, audit rights, or operational resilience renewal evidence. It is not REDBlocked because it is not Amazon connected-device compliance or product-listing release. It is not AIQ Partner Ticket Book or RAGProbe Buyer Security Evidence Sprint because the entry condition is a written buyer holdback tied to payment, PO, milestone, renewal expansion, or go-live acceptance, and first proof is buyer condition release, payment movement, or accepted no-go rather than evidence packet delivery alone. It is not VoiceAI Article 50, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, MoCRA, CPSC eFile, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Appsec and AI-security incumbents have stronger credibility and can sell broader assurance. Buyers may demand formal penetration testing, certification, legal AI classification, SOC2 evidence, ISO 42001 controls, privacy review, model validation, or remediation outside scope. A short trace packet can create false assurance if scope limits are weak. Test failures often require engineering work before any buyer release occurs. Sensitive prompts, logs, source documents, model settings, and customer data create trust and security burdens. Tool-using agents can cause side effects unless testing is sandboxed and tightly authorized. Partners may internalize the playbook after seeing several cases. The business may be constrained to a narrow set of non-regulated SaaS vendors with mature enough test environments and live enterprise buyers. Outcome attribution can be messy because a buyer may release the holdback for reasons beyond the packet.
