## Chosen Candidate

Idea name: PayBand Buyer Release Pack

One-sentence thesis: A vendor-side 5-business-day evidence response for ATS, HRIS, payroll, recruiting, and compensation SaaS vendors whose EU enterprise renewals or procurements are blocked by live EU Pay Transparency Directive readiness requests.

Exact buyer: 20-250 employee EU/CEE ATS, HRIS, payroll, compensation, recruiting CRM, job-board, and recruitment-agency software vendors selling to employers with EU workforces.

Acute trigger: A live customer, procurement team, HR/legal team, or works-council-influenced buyer asks before renewal/procurement: show how your product supports salary-range disclosure, salary-history-ban workflows, employee pay-info request handling, objective pay criteria records, gender-pay reporting exports, audit logs, and multi-country readiness for the 7 June 2026 transposition deadline.

Control point: The live buyer request, product screenshots/test tenant, workflow notes, export samples, configuration boundaries, existing security/privacy docs, and a fixed buyer-ready response pack split into supported now, configurable, roadmap, legal/customer-owned, and unsafe-to-claim items.

60-day proof: Three vendors pay fixed fees tied to live buyer requests, five real customer threads are assigned, two enterprise buyers accept the packet as resolving or advancing EUPTD readiness evidence, and one renewal/procurement moves forward.

6-month POC: Sell 10-15 packs at 10,000-24,000 PLN; create a private library of buyer questions, accepted answer patterns, workflow evidence, screenshots, unsupported-claim warnings, and country-delta notes; convert 3 vendors to annual refresh retainers.

Economics: 10,000-24,000 PLN per live buyer pack, 6,000-12,000 PLN annual refresh, 60-75% gross margin after HR/employment-law reviewer issue spotting. One saved enterprise renewal or procurement can justify the fee.

Copy risk: HR law firms, HR consultancies, payroll vendors, and product teams can copy. The wedge is side selection and urgency: employer-side advisors tell employers what to do; this helps smaller vendors answer real customer buyer gates without overclaiming product capabilities.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream: Once the vendor assigns the live buyer thread and product evidence, the founder controls the evidence intake, response chronology, buyer objection log, and accepted wording for that renewal/procurement blocker.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker: It is sold only against a live customer/procurement request and delivers a buyer-ready evidence response tied to a concrete renewal or procurement, not generic employer compliance consulting or software.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits: No legal advice, no equal-pay certification, no employer compliance assurance, no pay-gap calculations as final legal reporting, no works-council negotiation. Employment-law reviewers issue-spot only; vendor counsel and customer counsel decide legal positions.

Duplicate risk: Not DORA; different regulation, buyer counterparty, artifact, and HR/payroll workflow. Not TraceFaktura, REDBlocked, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, or HeritageDoor.

Strongest anticipated objections: HR/payroll vendors may already be preparing; customer requests may not be urgent enough; member-state transposition may be delayed or fragmented; buyers may need product changes not evidence; law firms/HR consultants can copy; unknown founder lacks HR/legal credibility; this may be a compliance service unless accepted buyer-request memory compounds.
