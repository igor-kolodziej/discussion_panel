Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
AIQ Prepaid Partner Slot Bank

One-sentence thesis:
Sell prepaid, minimum-commitment white-label AI-questionnaire response slots to SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, privacy/security questionnaire firms, and DPO/privacy advisers that already control trusted B2B SaaS client relationships but need a reserved AI evidence bench for live enterprise buyer AI sections.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, virtual security teams, privacy/security questionnaire outsourcing firms, DPO/privacy consultants, and boutique SaaS security/privacy advisers serving 20-150 person B2B SaaS vendors with ordinary embedded AI features. The paying buyer is the partner, not the SaaS vendor, unless the partner explicitly approves direct billing. Best first partners already handle security/privacy questionnaires for 3-20 SaaS clients and are starting to see enterprise buyer AI sections that they do not want to handle as legal AI Act classification, ISO 42001, model validation, or full AI governance work.

Acute trigger:
Partners are receiving AI-specific enterprise buyer questions on live SaaS deals, renewals, pilots, and procurement approvals, but those tickets are sporadic, deadline-driven, and awkward to staff internally. Normal security/privacy questionnaire work covers SOC2, subprocessor, access-control, encryption, incident, privacy, and vendor-risk evidence; the new AI sections ask for model inventory, model-provider/subprocessor disclosure, customer-data training or no-training proof, retention/logging settings, prompt and output handling, RAG source controls, eval/monitoring evidence, human review, opt-out, AI incident path, model-change control, and buyer-specific answer wording.

Control point:
Prepaid partner slot agreement covering a fixed number of AI-questionnaire tickets or response hours; minimum monthly or quarterly ticket commitment; partner-branded delivery rules; non-circumvention/client-confidentiality terms; client authorization workflow; evidence-intake checklist; reviewer-cost pass-through rules; escalation/no-go boundaries; and a current ticket ledger. For each ticket, the startup controls the partner-routed AI answer matrix, evidence-room index, model/subprocessor table, data-flow map, no-training proof index, retention/logging evidence index, RAG/source-control evidence, eval/monitoring summary, human-review evidence, buyer response wording, and no-go/escalation log under the partner's trusted client relationship.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign three prepaid partner slot agreements with minimum commitments totaling at least 120,000 PLN cash collected or contractually due; reserve capacity for at least 18 AI-questionnaire tickets; process at least eight live partner-routed tickets with named SaaS clients, buyer questionnaires, deal value/deadline, and evidence-access authorization; produce at least five partner-accepted deliveries, buyer-progress outcomes, narrowed follow-ups, approved lower-risk deployment scopes, or clean no-go decisions; get at least two partners to use a second slot; and keep gross margin above 65% after AI security, privacy, and governance reviewer costs.

6-month POC:
Secure 8-12 prepaid partner slot customers; reserve 80-160 ticket slots; deliver 60-120 partner-routed tickets; collect 700,000-1,800,000 PLN from prepaid slots, direct-bill tickets approved by partners, and renewal slot agreements; document 40+ partner-accepted or buyer-progress outcomes plus 15 clean no-go/out-of-scope decisions; get at least six partners to use 5+ tickets; keep sub-5-business-day turnaround for evidence-ready tickets; maintain gross margin above 65%; and build a private partner memory base of buyer AI question patterns, accepted answer formats, evidence gaps, no-training proof, model/subprocessor disclosures, RAG/source controls, eval evidence, legal/high-risk escalation triggers, and partner-specific style rules.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 150 SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, virtual security teams, security questionnaire firms, DPO/privacy consultants, and boutique privacy/security advisers serving B2B SaaS. Pitch prepaid capacity, not free referrals: "Reserve five white-label AI-questionnaire response slots for your SaaS clients this quarter. You keep the client relationship and final adviser role. I return partner-branded answer matrices, AI evidence-room indexes, no-go/escalation notes, and buyer wording in five business days for evidence-ready tickets. Unused slots roll once, but the slot bank is prepaid because capacity is reserved."

Economics:
Partners prepay 30,000-60,000 PLN for five-ticket quarterly slot banks, 8,000-14,000 PLN for additional evidence-ready single-product tickets, 14,000-25,000 PLN for multi-feature, RAG, or agent tickets, and 25,000-40,000 PLN for urgent high-value renewal or procurement sprints. Direct-bill SaaS clients pay 10,000-30,000 PLN only when the partner approves. Reviewer costs are 2,000-8,000 PLN per ticket from AI security, privacy, or governance reviewers, passed through or built into slot pricing. The partner pays because reserved capacity protects its client relationship, prevents embarrassing template answers, avoids hiring a specialist for sporadic AI questions, and lets the partner keep broader advisory work.

Copy risk:
SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, privacy lawyers, GRC platforms, AI governance consultants, AI implementation agencies, and security questionnaire outsourcers can copy pieces. The defense is prepaid partner slot ownership, partner-specific delivery memory, accepted/rejected buyer wording, evidence-room taxonomy, no-go discipline, reviewer bench, and becoming the behind-the-scenes AI questionnaire capacity for partners before they can justify hiring internally.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a partner prepays for a slot bank, signs non-circumvention and delivery terms, assigns current AI-questionnaire tickets, and authorizes client evidence access under its trusted relationship, another consultant cannot control those prepaid slots or active ticket workflows without displacing the partner's chosen white-label bench. Competitors can sell future AI questionnaire services, but not the already prepaid partner slot capacity, ticket ledger, delivery history, style guide, evidence room, and buyer-response sequence.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business is a prepaid partner capacity bank for live AI-questionnaire tickets, not generic AI readiness, legal compliance, ISO implementation, model validation, SaaS, dashboards, templates, a marketplace, or introductions. The output is a partner-branded deal-release artifact used in an active enterprise buyer workflow: answer matrix, evidence-room index, model/subprocessor table, data-flow map, no-training proof, retention/logging evidence, eval/monitoring summary, human-review evidence, access-control evidence, RAG/source-control evidence, buyer response wording, and no-go/escalation log. No prepaid partner slot and no live buyer questionnaire means no ticket.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, AI Act classification opinions, DPIAs, high-risk AI conformity assessment, ISO 42001 implementation, SOC2 certification, privacy legal opinions, model validation, safety certification, clinical/HR/credit/biometric/law-enforcement review, or guarantees of buyer approval. Partners, vendors, lawyers, DPOs, security officers, AI governance specialists, auditors, and enterprise buyers remain responsible for legal compliance, privacy, security, contractual terms, product changes, and procurement decisions. Cases involving regulated high-risk AI, autonomous adverse decisions, clinical data, credit/insurance/HR decisioning, biometrics, law enforcement, unclear training claims, missing evidence, or buyer demands for legal opinions are declined or routed back to the partner's qualified advisers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it is not regulated-finance ICT third-party-risk under DORA and the paying buyer is a prepaid SOC2/fractional-CISO/privacy partner buying white-label AI-questionnaire capacity. It is distinct from the failed AIGate direct procurement pack because the hard control point is prepaid partner slot capacity and active partner ticket ledgers, not direct cold sales to SaaS vendors. It is distinct from the failed AIQ Partner Ticket Book because this version requires prepaid minimum-capacity commitments rather than non-binding or case-by-case partner routing. It is distinct from REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, KSC/NIS2, and health/Rx/clinical routes.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Partners may refuse prepaid commitments and prefer case-by-case subcontracting. Good SOC2 consultants and fractional CISOs can internalize the workflow once ticket volume is visible. GRC platforms may add AI questionnaire automation that handles common questions. Partners may fear client-stealing, liability, embarrassment, or evidence-handling risk. Vendors may lack real evidence for no-training, retention, evals, RAG sources, logging, human review, opt-out, or model-change controls. Enterprise buyers may ask for legal classification, DPIA, model validation, or product remediation outside scope. Sensitive architecture and log access create trust friction even through partners. The business may still become low-margin white-label questionnaire labor unless prepaid slot pricing, strict no-go filters, and repeated accepted-answer memory hold.
