## Current Source Notes

Polish Ministry of Finance materials state Poland has roughly 2.5 million fiscal cash registers and that online systems register cash-register service technicians. Current regulation requires mandatory technical reviews not less often than every 2 years, performed by an authorized/service-qualified party. A prior BiznesOferty listing showed a functioning fiscal cash-register/computer-equipment shop with Posnet and Novitus authorization, annual revenue around 660,000 PLN and profit around 280,000 PLN, though the listing price was above the user's 100,000 PLN limit. The candidate must therefore be structured as a seller-financed book option/current-month cash transfer rather than a full cash purchase.
