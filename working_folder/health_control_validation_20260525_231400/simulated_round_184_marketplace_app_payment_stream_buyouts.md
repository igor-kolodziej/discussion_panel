## Search Pivot

The recent failures show that a mandate over someone else's decision process is weak. This round tests a harder digital asset: an existing marketplace app listing with current subscription billing, source/control transfer, customer notification, and first payout under the new owner before seller payout.

The internal bar is strict: no generic app building, no brokered introductions, no dashboards, no diligence reports, no consulting sprints. The only acceptable shape is a signed option over a named listing/payment stream plus app-store billing control and live subscriber revenue.

## Lead Candidate

**Shopify Ops App Continuity Buyout**

### Internal Score Rationale

This barely clears the simulated gate because the founder is not selling an app idea, build service, support desk, or app acquisition advisory. The hard proof is control of an already-installed Shopify app listing, source/update channel, active merchant subscription billing, seller endorsement, customer continuation notice, and first app-store payout before seller payout.

The strongest niche is boring operational apps that merchants use daily but do not want to re-platform: order tags, packing slips, local invoicing exports, delivery notes, simple returns labels, inventory alerts, product feed cleanup, B2B price lists, store admin utilities, and low-risk workflow helpers. Exclude checkout/payment apps, customer-data-heavy marketing apps, privacy-sensitive tracking apps, AI apps, and apps requiring high uptime or deep custom support.

Main internal doubts:

- micro-SaaS/app acquisition markets are mature;
- good apps may be too expensive or not for sale;
- app transfer and billing handoff can be messy;
- support creep and platform API updates can erase margin;
- one app is a cash-flow asset, not a company, unless repeat acquisition works.

Gate decision: advance to working Zero To One validation.
