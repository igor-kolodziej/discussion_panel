## Lead Candidate

**REDLab EN18031 Slot Lockbox**

### Buyer

Amazon/account-health agencies, marketplace-compliance agencies, EU product-compliance agencies, and CEE connected-device importers or private-label sellers whose revenue-producing connected devices are blocked by RED cybersecurity evidence requests and cannot be solved honestly with existing supplier PDFs.

### Acute Trigger

A live marketplace, retailer, distributor, or buyer request requires RED cybersecurity / EN 18031 evidence, a product-security test report, or a credible lab-needed path before listing, inventory movement, private-label onboarding, or PO release. REDBlocked-style response packets are insufficient when the seller lacks actual test evidence or the supplier documents are mismatched, stale, or not product-specific.

### Control Point

- written lab or specialist-lab MOU reserving a limited number of RED/EN 18031 cybersecurity pre-test, gap-review, or test-report slots;
- agency ticket-routing agreement;
- seller/importer prepaid slot deposit;
- exact product model, sample, supplier file, app/firmware/accessory scope, and Amazon/retailer request;
- chain-of-custody and sample receipt;
- lab quotation/test plan/report path;
- agency payment split and evidence boundary.

### Internal Score Rationale

This only clears the simulated gate because it changes the control point from "better evidence desk" to "reserved regulated test capacity plus prepaid sample lane." It is materially different from REDBlocked if the proof is a lab slot, sample receipt, and test path rather than an Amazon response dossier.

Internal risks remain high:

- labs may not reserve slots to an unknown founder;
- labs and agencies can bypass the founder once connected;
- not all cases need testing;
- actual testing cost and turnaround can break 60-day cash conversion;
- the idea can look like lab brokerage unless sample custody, agency routing, and repeated case memory are real.

Gate decision: advance to working Zero To One validation because it is the strongest remaining attempt to convert a previously confirmed evidence wedge into a harder capacity/payment-control asset.
