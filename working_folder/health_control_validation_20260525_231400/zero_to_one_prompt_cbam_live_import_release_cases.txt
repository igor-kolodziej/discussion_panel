Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CBAM Live Import-Release Case Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A broker-routed 48-72h live CBAM import-release case desk for Polish customs brokers and freight forwarders whose SME steel, aluminium, fertiliser, cement, hydrogen, or electricity importer clients have a current MRN/declaration/shipment blocked or at risk because CBAM authorised-declarant status, timely application evidence, mass-threshold/exemption evidence, TARIC document codes, or declarant/importer role evidence is missing or inconsistent.

Exact buyer:
Polish customs brokers, indirect customs representatives, freight forwarders, and SME importers of CBAM-covered goods that already have a current shipment, MRN/declaration, PUESC/customs workflow, or import booking after the 2026 definitive CBAM regime began. The first buyer is the broker or forwarder who has the import documents and wants a fast release/progress/no-go packet for a named importer and shipment.

Acute trigger:
A live import flow is blocked or at risk: customs validation asks for CBAM declarant status or application evidence; the importer or indirect representative crossed the unified mass threshold but status/application evidence is unclear; the customs declaration needs correct CBAM TARIC/document-code evidence; role allocation between importer and indirect customs representative is unresolved; the broker cannot prove that a timely application allows temporary continuation; or the shipment should be marked clean no-go because the importer did not apply in time and cannot import CBAM goods until status is obtained.

Control point:
Prepaid broker-routed live shipment case with MRN/declaration draft or rejected declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, CN/TARIC lines, mass calculations, importer identity, indirect-representative role, PUESC/CBAM portal status or application evidence where available, prior CBAM import history, threshold/exemption evidence, document-code issue list, supplier and goods description, broker deadline, and customs communication. The startup controls the release/progress/no-go packet, issue checklist, evidence index, broker response wording, and escalation map for that active import event while the broker/customs representative remains declarant or representative of record.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Two customs brokers or freight forwarders sign paid routing terms; five to eight live CBAM import-release cases are prepaid at 5,000-25,000 PLN each; every case has a current shipment, MRN/declaration draft or customs validation issue, importer authorization, CBAM-covered CN/TARIC scope, mass/threshold data, and broker deadline; at least two cases produce accepted declaration progress, shipment release, accepted document-code correction, accepted application/status evidence, accepted threshold/exemption treatment, or clean no-go before more demurrage/customs time is wasted; one broker routes a second case; gross margin stays above 60% after customs/CBAM reviewer costs; no case is accepted if it requires legal representation, customs filing as declarant, authorised-declarant status creation from scratch, or supplier-emissions verification.

6-month POC:
Handle 40-100 live CBAM import-release or no-go cases through 5-10 broker/forwarder partners; collect 400,000-1,500,000 PLN in case fees, urgent fees, and partner-wholesale fees; document 25+ release/progress/no-go outcomes, 10+ accepted document-code/status/threshold corrections, and 5 broker partners routing repeat cases; build a private taxonomy by CN/TARIC line, goods category, importer role, indirect-representative setup, mass-threshold issue, PUESC/application status, customs validation wording, document-code correction, release outcome, and no-go reason; keep turnaround under 72 hours for evidence-ready cases; keep fewer than 20% of cases drifting into broad CBAM consulting, authorised-declarant application projects, customs representation, supplier-emissions work, or legal advice.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target Polish customs brokers and freight forwarders that handle repeated SME steel and aluminium import flows. Pitch only live release blockers: "If a CBAM-covered shipment is blocked or at risk because status, timely application, threshold/exemption, TARIC document-code, importer/representative role, or evidence is unclear, send the declaration draft/rejection, invoice, packing list, CN/TARIC lines, mass data, importer authorisation, and CBAM portal/application evidence. I return a 48-72h release/progress/no-go packet under your brand. I do not act as declarant, give legal advice, or create authorised status from scratch."

Economics:
Charge brokers 5,000-8,000 PLN wholesale for narrow document-code/status/threshold triage, 10,000-18,000 PLN for a full live shipment release/progress packet, and 18,000-35,000 PLN for urgent multi-line or demurrage-sensitive cases. Brokers can resell to importers or include the desk as an emergency CBAM escalation product. The importer pays because the fee is small relative to delayed goods, demurrage, lost production, customer penalties, or inability to import above the threshold.

Copy risk:
Customs brokers, CBAM consultants, freight forwarders, Big Four firms, customs-law firms, and CBAM software vendors can copy pieces. The defense is broker-routed live cases, fast shipment-specific issue memory, accepted customs-document-code and status evidence patterns, local Polish PUESC/KOBiZE/Gov.pl workflow familiarity, and strict no-go discipline. The startup must become the broker's emergency CBAM release desk, not another readiness consultant.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
After the broker signs routing terms, sends the live MRN/declaration file, importer authorization, status evidence, shipment documents, customs validation issue, and deadline, the startup controls the active release/progress/no-go packet for that import event. A CBAM consultant or broker can build a similar method, but cannot instantly own the specific evidence room, issue map, and broker-branded response already in progress for that shipment.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only prepaid live import-release cases with a shipment/declaration/MRN or customs validation issue. The output is an operational customs-support artifact: issue map, CBAM status/application/threshold/document-code evidence index, broker response wording, release/progress/no-go packet, and escalation note for that import event. It is not a generic CBAM readiness memo, software dashboard, declarant application service, emissions-reporting service, marketplace, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not act as customs broker, customs representative, authorised CBAM declarant, legal adviser, tax adviser, emissions verifier, accredited verifier, PUESC filer of record, or declarant of record. Customs brokers, indirect representatives, importers, legal advisers, tax advisers, CBAM authorities, customs authorities, and accredited verifiers remain responsible for declarations, legal interpretations, authorisation, filings, emissions evidence, and release decisions. Cases where the importer lacks timely application/status and is above threshold are marked no-go or referred to proper advisers rather than papered over.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not CBAM Import Continuity Desk because the confirmed idea is a broker-branded continuity/exposure memo for repeated SME importers before CBAM becomes a live customs release problem. This idea accepts only live shipment/declaration/MRN/customs-validation blockers after the definitive regime and produces release/progress/no-go packets tied to actual import events. The buyer channel is still brokers, but the control point, payment event, proof artifact, and urgency are materially different: current shipment release or no-go, not pre-emptive continuity triage. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
This may be too close to the confirmed CBAM broker-continuity idea. Customs brokers and freight forwarders may internalize the workflow quickly because live declarations are already their job. Hard cases require authorised-declarant status, legal/customs decisions, PUESC portal action, or authority processing outside founder control. Importers who failed to apply by the deadline cannot be fixed by a packet. Supplier emissions evidence and CBAM certificate planning are outside scope but clients may ask for them. Case volume may be short-lived as brokers standardize TARIC/document-code and status workflows. Mistakes can create serious customs/legal risk. The business can drift into broad CBAM consulting unless it accepts only live shipment release/progress/no-go cases.
