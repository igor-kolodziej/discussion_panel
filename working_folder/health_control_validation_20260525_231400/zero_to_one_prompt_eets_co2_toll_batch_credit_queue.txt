Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
EETS CO2 Toll Batch Credit Queue

One-sentence thesis:
CEE fleets are still overpaying or overspending on German truck tolls when newer vehicles remain billed in Toll Collect CO2 class 1/default status; instead of cold-selling fleets one by one, the startup runs partner-routed batch correction queues for EETS/fleet-card advisers, transport accountants, leasing offices, truck dealers, and fleet bookkeepers that already control toll statements and client trust.

Exact buyer:
Primary economic buyer and channel: EETS/fleet-card advisers, transport accountants, fleet bookkeepers, truck leasing offices, used-truck dealers, and transport back-office firms serving Polish/CEE fleets with regular German mileage.

End payer or beneficiary: Polish/CEE hauliers, truck fleets, truck lessors, and rental fleets with Euro VI vehicles, meaningful German toll exposure, and vehicles still billed or reported as CO2 class 1/default or with unconfirmed toll-relevant vehicle data.

Acute trigger:
A fleet, accountant, EETS adviser, leasing office, or dealer sees German toll invoices, Toll Collect statements, EETS exports, fleet-card statements, or vehicle onboarding records showing newer trucks still at CO2 class 1/default or showing vehicle-data gaps. The correction matters because German toll cost is current cash, monthly margin, lease pricing, route profitability, customer fuel/toll surcharges, or buyer TCO on a sale/lease.

Control point:
The startup accepts only partner-routed or fleet-authorized batches with named vehicles, toll statements, VIN/registration lists, first-registration dates, visible class 1/default or data issue, German mileage exposure, CoC/CIF/registration evidence path, portal or guided-submission authority, official submission/status tracking, and a credit/savings attribution ledger.

The startup does not buy toll claims, finance refunds, represent fleets in fines, make legal/tax determinations, guarantee refunds, operate as an EETS provider, hold toll money, or handle disputed violations. It operates the document-and-status queue: screen vehicles, reject non-qualifying files, assemble evidence, prepare or guide portal submissions, track official statuses, and document corrected class, credit, refund, offset, or future rate delta.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign two partner batch agreements or one partner agreement plus three direct fleet mandates; screen at least 150 named vehicles; accept at least 40 vehicles into correction queues; collect at least 45,000 PLN in setup, vehicle, or batch fees; obtain client authorization for every submitted vehicle; document at least one official CO2 class update or vehicle-data correction; document at least one corrected statement, credit, refund, offset, or measurable future-rate delta; and reject at least 30% of screened vehicles as non-qualifying, missing evidence, low-mileage, or uneconomic.

6-month POC:
Run 700-1,500 vehicles through screening; submit 250-600 qualified corrections; collect 250,000-700,000 PLN revenue; document at least 75 official updates and 25 monetary credits, refunds, offsets, or recurring rate-delta statements; keep average accepted vehicle handling time under 35 minutes after intake; keep gross margin above 65% after partner share and contractor help; get at least three partners to route second batches; and maintain a no-go log for pre-2019 vehicles, missing CoC/CIF paths, low German mileage, already-correct class, and unresolved ownership/document issues.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with transport accountants, fleet bookkeepers, EETS/fleet-card advisers, truck leasing offices, used-truck dealers, and transport back-office firms that already receive German toll statements or client VIN lists. The partner offer is: "Send a batch of vehicles still showing CO2 class 1/default or vehicle-data issues. We screen, reject weak vehicles, assemble the evidence queue, prepare or guide submissions, track official statuses, and return a credit/savings ledger under your client relationship."

Direct outreach is limited to fleets with visible German exposure and likely post-2019 Euro VI trucks. Direct fleet cases are used mainly to prove workflow and examples for partners.

Economics:
Charge partners or fleets a 2,500-8,000 PLN batch setup fee plus 150-450 PLN per accepted vehicle, or 15-25% of documented first-year savings/credits where attribution is clean. Partners can keep 20-35% where they own the client. A strong batch has 30-100 vehicles, enough qualifying vehicles to absorb document-chasing overhead, and clear monthly German toll exposure. The model dies if most cases are one-off low-mileage vehicles, if retroactive credits are rare and future savings cannot be charged on, or if evidence retrieval exceeds the fee.

Copy risk:
EETS providers, fleet-card companies, truck dealers, leasing firms, transport accountants, transport associations, Toll Collect self-service guides, fleet software vendors, and toll advisers can copy the checklist. The defense is narrow execution speed and controlled batch memory: partner-routed queues, accepted/rejected vehicle profiles, CoC/CIF retrieval paths, document mismatch patterns, official submission statuses, correction timelines, fee attribution records, and partner trust from repeated batches.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
They can copy the category, but they cannot retroactively own a signed partner batch, client authorizations, evidence-room folder, submission tracker, fee agreement, no-go register, and credit/savings ledger for named VINs already routed to the startup. The startup's control is case-level and batch-level, not market-level; it must compound through repeat partner queues before incumbents add the work internally.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell a toll-cost audit, marketplace, dashboard, generic fleet consulting, or broker introduction. It accepts only named vehicle batches with current toll statements and correction authority, then produces official submission-ready evidence, status tracking, no-go decisions, and documented class updates, credits, refunds, offsets, or recurring rate deltas.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Toll Collect, EETS providers, fleets, lessors, vehicle owners, dealers, and official portals remain responsible for formal registrations, submissions, corrections, toll collection, and official decisions. The startup does not handle fines, violations, litigation, tax treatment, refund financing, payment services, claim purchase, vehicle homologation, forged documents, or disputed ownership. It rejects vehicles without truthful source evidence, unclear authority, low German mileage, or unresolved legal/tax/fine issues.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, or a marketplace evidence desk. It is closest to the failed direct CO2TollClass Refund Mandate, but the control point is materially different: partner-routed batch queues with current client authorizations and repeated statement access, not cold fleet-by-fleet mandates.

Strongest anticipated objections:
EETS and fleet-card providers can internalize the work. Toll Collect self-service may be enough for competent fleets. Many vehicles correctly remain class 1 or lack qualifying documents. Retroactive refunds may be uncertain, making future-savings attribution harder. Partner margin can compress economics. Vehicle-by-vehicle evidence chasing can become low-margin admin. The opportunity may be a transition wave unless repeat partner queues extend into broader vehicle-data/toll correction work. Cold direct fleet acquisition is noisy because hauliers receive many toll, fuel, factoring, and compliance offers.
