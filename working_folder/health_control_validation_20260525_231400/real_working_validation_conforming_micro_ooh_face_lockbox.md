## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator accepted that the "exact face" insight is real: some advertisers want one specific school-run corner, clinic cluster, commuter route, retail strip, or neighborhood entrance that large OOH networks do not sell cleanly.

The idea failed because the evaluator saw three structural problems: legal ambiguity, low-ticket local ad sales, and operational fragmentation. It framed the business as potentially workable as a disciplined Warsaw/CEE micro-OOH operator, but not as a strong startup.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Many apparent faces will not be legally usable because owner consent may not override city rules, building rules, heritage restrictions, road-safety restrictions, tenant lease limits, community rules, or local landscape resolutions.
2. Low ticket size creates sales friction: a 4,000 PLN clinic campaign can still require calls, creative changes, landlord coordination, printer coordination, install checks, invoice follow-up, and removal.
3. Buyers may not trust the media value without standardized reach, footfall, proof-of-posting, monitoring, visibility, or obstruction data.
4. Local agencies can bypass the founder after seeing an attractive wall, fence, or window unless the site option is exclusive and enforceable.
5. Major OOH networks and local operators already own advertiser trust and media-buying relationships.
6. Static OOH still requires print production, installation, weatherproofing, landlord access, damage checks, vandalism handling, removal, insurance, electricity where relevant, and site photos.
7. Campaign demand is lumpy around openings, events, school intake, and seasonal promotions.
8. Part-time execution is mismatched with local, physical, deadline-driven campaign operations.

## Evaluator's Best Narrowing

The evaluator said the better first wedge would be:

**Clinic / private-school / franchise-node micro-OOH in Warsaw**

Accept only faces where:

- owner authority is documented;
- no new permit is needed, or legal/use basis is reviewed;
- the face is already used for signage/advertising or clearly allowed;
- visibility is proven with photos/video;
- install/removal path is known;
- buyer pays before print/install;
- campaign value is at least 4,000 PLN;
- the first use is static, not digital.

Better 60-day proof:

- 15 mapped candidate faces;
- 5 legally reviewed site options;
- 3 prepaid campaigns;
- 1 posted campaign;
- 1 repeat site or repeat buyer;
- true gross margin after owner share, legal review, print, install, removal, and checks.

## Lesson

Do not continue OOH, parking, local-site, booth, or other exact-location arbitrage unless the asset has much higher ticket size, clearer legal transferability, and lower two-sided field-sales burden. Exact site control is not enough when every site requires separate legal verification, buyer sale, installation, and monitoring.
