## Simulated Pass Candidate

### HazmatBlocked FBA Evidence Release Desk - 91

One-sentence thesis: a white-label evidence-response desk for Amazon FBA agencies and sellers whose ASINs, inbound shipments, or existing inventory are blocked by dangerous-goods/hazmat review, producing upload-ready SDS/exemption/battery-document packets within 24-48 hours.

Buyer: Amazon FBA operations agencies, account-health agencies, reimbursement/stranded-inventory agencies, and 7-8 figure FBA sellers of non-drug consumer products with hazmat-sensitive categories: cosmetics, cleaning products, art/craft supplies, liquids, gels, aerosols, batteries, power banks, electronics accessories, toys with batteries, and chemical-containing kits.

Acute trigger: Amazon flags an ASIN or inventory for dangerous-goods review, blocks inbound shipment creation, strands inventory, restricts FBA sale/removal, or requests Safety Data Sheet, dangerous-goods exemption sheet, battery exemption sheet, lithium-battery test summary, or related supplier/manufacturer document matching. Seller revenue or inventory movement is blocked now.

Transferable control point: priority agency routing plus prepaid live cases. The desk controls current Seller Central request text/screenshots, ASIN/category facts, supplier/manufacturer documents, product images/labels, evidence packet assembly, reviewer boundary notes, response wording, and case-memory about what Amazon accepts/rejects.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- Two agency routing agreements with 25-35% agency margin or referral fee.
- Five prepaid live hazmat/DG review cases from real Seller Central requests.
- Case intake authorizations and document-handling terms.
- A dangerous-goods/SDS reviewer or consultant on paid per-case terms.
- At least one submitted response packet and one accepted/cleared, escalated, or rejected-with-reasons outcome.
- A case log of request wording, document type, supplier-document mismatch, submission path, and outcome.

First acquisition mechanism: target Amazon agencies and FBA operations consultants that already handle stranded inventory, shipment creation errors, Account Health, and Seller Central case escalation. Ask for one live or recent hazmat review where a seller has SDS/exemption/battery documents but the ASIN remains blocked or the seller does not know what to submit.

Gross margin and payback logic: initial pricing can be 1,500-3,000 PLN for triage and 4,000-8,000 PLN for a full response dossier, with 25-35% kept by the agency. Reviewer cost is scoped per case. Gross margin is credible because the seller is protecting blocked FBA inventory/revenue and the work is document triage, not lab testing or logistics.

Copy risk: Amazon agencies, dangerous-goods consultants, SDS writers, FBA tools, and seller ops consultants can do pieces. The defense is agency-routed live case flow, fast response, narrow Amazon DG evidence memory, and repeated accepted/rejected pattern knowledge by product/document type.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured: once the agency sends current prepaid cases and the desk has the Seller Central request, ASIN facts, supplier docs, and seller permission, a competitor cannot copy those live cases. Broader competitors can chase future cases, but they cannot recreate this case history and outcome memory without doing the work.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker: it is a live evidence-response desk for current blocked ASINs/inventory. It does not sell a generic software dashboard, general Amazon consulting, or a directory of SDS providers. The output is a submission-ready packet for a specific Seller Central request plus clear submit/cannot-submit/escalate boundaries.

Reasons this could still fail:
- Agencies may already solve simple SDS/exemption cases themselves.
- Amazon outcomes are opaque and cannot be guaranteed.
- Some cases require real manufacturer SDS correction, lab data, UL/lithium documents, or product redesign outside founder control.
- The founder must not create fake SDS, classify dangerous goods as a legal certifier, or promise transport compliance.
- Case volume may be too episodic unless agencies aggregate demand.
- If the work becomes generic Amazon support, the differentiation disappears.

Decision: advance this candidate to real Zero To One validation.
