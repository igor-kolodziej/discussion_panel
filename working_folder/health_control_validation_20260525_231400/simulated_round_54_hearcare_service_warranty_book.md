## Materially Different Candidate

`HearCare Service/Warranty Book Buyout`

This candidate buys or options a retiring independent audioprosthetist/hearing-aid shop's existing aftercare and warranty service book serving 50-60+ and elderly customers. It controls phone/domain/customer handoff, device serials, warranty/repair calendars, earmold files, service history, loaner inventory, and consumable purchase flow.

The founder does not perform audiology, diagnose hearing loss, fit medical devices, or claim clinical provider status. Execution is done by a licensed audioprosthetist/audiologist partner and manufacturer repair channels.

## Buyer

- existing hearing-aid users aged 50-80;
- families helping older parents;
- customers whose local independent hearing-aid shop is closing or owner is retiring;
- customers needing warranty handling, repair, loaner device, earmold replacement, batteries, filters, domes, tubes, cleaning, and NFZ/private renewal reminders.

## Acute Trigger

When a hearing aid, earmold, charger, receiver, tube, or battery/consumable supply fails, the customer cannot wait weeks or rebuild their history from scratch. A retiring shop holds the device serials, settings/aftercare notes, warranty status, earmold files, manufacturer contacts, and trusted phone number.

## Control Point

- customer consents and seller-endorsed handoff;
- phone/domain/mailbox;
- device serials and warranty/repair calendars;
- earmold files, impressions/files where transferable, and size/fit records;
- service notes, manufacturer repair-channel notes, and appointment history;
- consumable purchase history for batteries, filters, domes, tubes, wax guards, cleaning kits;
- small loaner device/accessory inventory;
- manufacturer/wholesaler terms where transferable;
- prepaid aftercare/repair/consumables before close.

## Two-Month Proof

Required:

1. One signed conditional acquisition option over a named retiring audioprosthetist/shop service book.
2. Clean consent/data-transfer and manufacturer-warranty transfer review.
3. Licensed audioprosthetist/audiologist partner agreement.
4. At least 75-150 active service customers verified, with device serials and warranty/repair records.
5. At least 25 customers prepay aftercare, repair handling, consumables, or loaner membership.
6. At least `25,000 PLN` prepaid cash clears before close.
7. One completed repair/loaner/earmold/consumables service cycle.

## Why It Scores Higher Than Compliance Renewal

- The acquired book contains customer-specific device serials and service history, not only a renewal calendar.
- Hearing-aid aftercare is recurring and urgent when the device fails.
- Physical loaner inventory and earmold/service records create control beyond generic coordination.
- Existing customer handoff reduces CAC.
- The founder avoids clinical fitting/diagnosis by using licensed execution partners.

## Primary Caveats

- Chains and large hearing-aid retailers can compete.
- Manufacturer warranty/service rights may not transfer cleanly.
- Customer consents and data handling are mandatory.
- B2C trust may be harder than B2B renewal.
- If the founder cannot secure a licensed audioprosthetist partner, the idea fails.
- If the book has stale customers or no prepaid aftercare conversion, it is just goodwill.
