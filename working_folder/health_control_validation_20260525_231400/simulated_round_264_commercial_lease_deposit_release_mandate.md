## Search Premise

The recent failures show that a signed mandate is weak when a platform, authority, lab, buyer, or provider has opaque discretion. This round looks for recoverable business cash where the debtor can acknowledge a specific balance and where the startup can reject disputed cases before doing work.

The selected candidate focuses on matured commercial lease deposits from closed or assigned business locations. The test is not broad lease auditing, legal debt collection, tenant representation, property management, or litigation. It only accepts clean files where the tenant has a lease/deposit proof, move-out or assignment evidence, no known active defect/setoff dispute, and a landlord/property-manager acknowledgment or near-term acknowledgment path.

## Selected Candidate

### Idea Name

LeaseDeposit Clean-Exit Release Mandate.

### Thesis

Multi-site operators often leave matured commercial lease deposits, cash guarantees, or overpaid landlord balances behind after store closures, relocations, assignments, or old-entity wind-downs. The startup accepts only clean, non-disputed deposit files, secures a signed mandate and payment direction, obtains landlord/property-manager acknowledgment, and gets paid from released cash.

### Buyer

Franchise groups, retail chains, gyms, restaurants, clinics, private schools, coworking operators, light-industrial tenants, logistics tenants, and office tenants with 5-200 current or former locations and weak closed-site administration.

The best first channel is not cold tenants. It is accountants, outsourced CFOs, franchise consultants, tenant brokers, restructuring advisers, property managers for tenants, and retail operations managers who know which locations closed, assigned, downsized, or moved.

### Acute Trigger

A premises was vacated, assigned, downsized, merged, sold, or closed. The lease deposit, cash guarantee, rent overpayment, reconciled utility/service-charge balance, or landlord-held account balance should have been returned after handover, final reconciliation, or defect/setoff period, but no one has chased it to cash.

### Transferable Control Point

- Signed recovery mandate and limited authority to communicate with the landlord/property manager about the named premises.
- Lease, annexes, deposit clause, deposit payment proof, handover protocol, assignment/termination documents, final invoice/reconciliation, and tenant payment direction.
- Screened no-dispute rules: no active litigation, no unresolved defect claim, no unpaid rent admission, no contested damage/setoff, no insolvency/legal conflict, no missing tenant authority.
- Landlord/property-manager acknowledgment of the balance, release condition, rejection reason, or near-term reconciliation path.
- Success fee or fixed screening fee before broad archive work begins.
- Payment direction to the tenant's account or approved account only; the startup does not custody client money.

### 60-Day Proof

- Eight signed mandates from at least two multi-site operators or channel partners.
- Twenty-five named former premises screened from lease/deposit evidence.
- Six landlord/property-manager written acknowledgments of a deposit, cash guarantee, credit, reconciliation status, or release condition.
- At least 100,000 PLN in acknowledged or release-ready balances.
- At least 40,000 PLN released, scheduled, credited, or cleanly classified as no-go.
- At least 15,000 PLN collected in fixed screening and success fees.
- One repeat channel partner sends a second batch.
- Rejection log proves discipline: disputed defects, legal claims, unclear authority, insolvency, missing proof, or balances too small are rejected early.

### Internal Cap Application

The candidate clears simulation only as an acknowledged-cash release mandate with strict clean-file screening and first released cash. It would fail the simulation gate if it becomes lease-audit consulting, rent negotiation, legal debt collection, property management, landlord dispute work, generic AP cleanup, or success-fee chasing without debtor acknowledgment.

### 6-Month POC

- Build three repeat channels: one accountant/outsourced CFO, one retail/franchise adviser, and one tenant broker/restructuring contact.
- Screen 150-300 former premises across 10-25 operators.
- Accept 40-80 clean deposit/reconciliation files.
- Obtain 25+ landlord/property-manager acknowledgments.
- Release, schedule, credit, or classify 500,000-2,000,000 PLN in balances.
- Collect 120,000-400,000 PLN in fixed screening and success fees.
- Keep gross margin above 65% after document review, channel referral fees, admin, and legal-template review.
- Maintain rejection rate above 40% if needed; a high rejection rate is healthy because disputed lease claims are not the product.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Build a target list of 120 accountants, outsourced CFOs, retail franchise consultants, tenant brokers, restructuring advisers, and property managers who serve multi-site tenants.

Partner pitch:

"When a client closes stores or transfers premises, landlord deposits and reconciled balances often stay open in old files. I only handle clean no-dispute deposit releases: lease proof, handover proof, payment proof, landlord acknowledgment, and payment direction. You keep accounting/advisory work; I recover the named balances or classify no-go."

Operator pitch:

"Send your last three years of closed/assigned premises. I screen only deposit and landlord-balance files where lease proof and handover evidence exist. No legal disputes, no rent negotiation, no defect fights. Fee only after a clean screen or released cash."

### Economics

- 750-2,500 PLN fixed screen per accepted premises batch or per 5-10 premises, credited against success fee where appropriate.
- 12-25% success fee on released, scheduled, or credited cash where attribution is documented.
- 2,000-8,000 PLN fixed fee for large operators that want an old-location deposit register and no-go classification before success-fee work.
- Channel referral share: 10-25% of collected fees.

The buyer pays because a single commercial deposit can be 10,000-150,000 PLN, the cash may already be contractually due, internal teams are focused on active stores, and old leases sit across email, accounting, and property folders.

### Copy Risk

Lawyers, lease auditors, tenant brokers, property managers, accountants, outsourced CFOs, and internal finance teams can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is specific file control: signed mandate, tenant authority, named premises, lease/deposit evidence, landlord acknowledgment, payment direction, and a repeat channel that sends old-location batches.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

Once the startup has the tenant mandate, the lease/deposit file, landlord/property-manager thread, acknowledgment, release schedule, and payment direction for a named premises, a competitor cannot instantly control that same cash-release file without the tenant revoking and restarting the process. Incumbents can compete for future batches, but not for the already-authorized release queue.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The product is not a lease audit, legal demand letter, rent negotiation, property-management service, dashboard, marketplace, or generic refund search. It accepts named clean deposit files and gets paid from released cash, scheduled release, credited balance, or paid no-go classification. No signed mandate, no lease/deposit proof, no landlord acknowledgment path, no sale.

### Boundaries

No legal advice, debt collection, litigation, rent negotiation, defect dispute, insolvency claim filing, tax advice, property management, escrow custody, landlord representation, or guarantee of recovery. Disputed cases go to lawyers or are rejected. Client money moves directly from landlord/property manager to the tenant or tenant-approved account.

### Duplicate-Risk Distinction

This is not the failed PSP reserve, utility deposit, construction retention, warranty bond, carrier credit, supplier rebate, OTA recovery, or pallet-pool recovery shapes. The debtor is a landlord/property manager under a named commercial lease file, not a platform, processor, carrier, retailer, pooler, supplier, or construction beneficiary. The proof artifact is a landlord-acknowledged matured lease deposit or balance release with tenant payment direction.

It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

### Strongest Anticipated Objections

1. Many apparent deposits are validly withheld for damage, unpaid rent, service-charge true-up, restoration obligations, missing handover documents, or setoff.
2. Landlords and property managers may ignore an unknown third-party mandate or insist on dealing only with tenant legal/finance teams.
3. Lawyers, tenant brokers, lease auditors, accountants, and internal finance can chase clean deposits.
4. Aged files may have missing leases, payment proofs, annexes, handover protocols, or authority.
5. Success-fee attribution can be disputed if the landlord would have released the deposit after scheduled reconciliation anyway.
6. The business can become low-margin document chasing if batch screening is not tight.
7. Some tenants may be embarrassed by weak closed-site administration and reluctant to share files.
8. Average recoverable balances may be too small outside retail, logistics, and larger office/clinic sites.
9. Cross-border leases and insolvency cases can create legal complexity.
10. The founder must avoid disputed cases even when the apparent cash amount is tempting.

## Decision

Advance LeaseDeposit Clean-Exit Release Mandate to real Zero To One validation. It passes internal simulation at `88.1` only as a clean acknowledged-cash release workflow with strict rejection of disputed legal/property-management cases.
