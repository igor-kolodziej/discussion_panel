## Lead Candidate: KaucjaMark SKU Release Pack

### Buyer

Polish/CEE beverage producers, beverage importers, craft breweries, cider/soft-drink/water/juice/functional-beverage brands, private-label beverage suppliers, and distributors whose named SKUs must be accepted by Polish retailers, distributors, marketplaces, or operators under the active Polish deposit-refund system.

### Acute Trigger

A retailer, distributor, marketplace, private-label customer, operator, packaging supplier, or internal sales team blocks or delays a SKU because deposit-system artifacts are missing, inconsistent, or not retail-ready: operator membership/status, deposit mark/logo, covered packaging classification, EAN/SKU master data, product-card fields, label artwork, transition stock split, deposit value, invoice/deposit line handling, buyer response, or sell-in date.

### Control Point

Prepaid named-SKU release case with:

- current written retailer/distributor/operator/marketplace/SKU-master-data request, rejection, or PO/listing condition;
- SKU/EAN/product-card file, packaging type/volume, label/artwork file, packaging supplier or printer contact, operator status/evidence, and existing product master data;
- buyer-approved response packet: SKU/EAN deposit field map, label/artwork evidence, operator/status evidence, transition stock note, known/missing items, and escalation list for legal/accounting/operator approval;
- right to retain anonymized case memory by packaging type, retailer request wording, operator issue, EAN/data-field issue, artwork rejection, and progress outcome.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Five prepaid named-SKU release cases from at least three beverage producers/importers/distributors.
2. At least two current retailer/distributor/marketplace/operator requests represented.
3. Two retail progress outcomes: product master accepted, listing progressed, PO released, corrected artwork approved for print, operator evidence accepted, stock transition plan accepted, or buyer narrows follow-up to a non-DRS issue.
4. 40,000-100,000 PLN collected.
5. One packaging/artwork reviewer and one accounting/legal/operator escalation contact on per-case terms.
6. Case memory across at least 20 SKUs/EANs.

### Economics

Charge 6,000-9,000 PLN for one SKU family / simple importer listing blocker, 10,000-20,000 PLN for multi-SKU retailer release pack, and 3,000-8,000 PLN for annual or new-SKU refresh once prior operator/artwork/master-data files exist. The buyer pays because a retailer listing, sell-in date, private-label order, distributor onboarding, or imported SKU revenue is blocked.

### Copy Risk

Deposit-system operators, packaging consultants, artwork agencies, accountants, ERP/EDI consultants, lawyers, and retailer supplier teams can solve pieces. The defense is not legal knowledge; it is current SKU release speed, retail buyer wording memory, operator/artwork/master-data issue taxonomy, Polish/English supplier/importer communication, and repeated progress outcomes by retailer/distributor/packaging type.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not PromoLeak because it is not recovery of retailer deductions, trade promotions, retail media charges, or remittance leakage. It is pre-sale SKU/listing release under the Polish deposit-refund system.

This is not GreenTender EPREL because there is no public lighting tender or EPREL product energy-label evidence. It is not CBAM, DORA, REDBlocked, EUDR, KSeF, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Data Act, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, or Supplement Stack Safety.
