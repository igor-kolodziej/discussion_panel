## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator treated the idea as stronger than an F-gas evidence desk because the control point is transactional: named shipment/order, importer mandate, tCO2e file, quota-holder option, portal trail, and payment sequence. It accepted that F-gas authorisation coverage can be a real shipment-level blocker for HVAC, heat-pump, and refrigeration equipment importers.

The idea failed because the evaluator saw a trust-heavy, two-sided, legally sensitive transaction desk. Quota holders may transact directly, customs brokers can internalize the workflow, the F-gas Portal already has official matching paths, and many real shipment blockers will be caused by importer registration, classification, labelling, customs, supplier data, or legality problems rather than lack of authorisation supply.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Quota holders and authorisation managers may prefer direct deals with importers or existing consultants.
2. The F-gas Portal already includes official paths and matchmaking, weakening the value of a middle desk.
3. The founder may accidentally become an authorisation manager or regulated portal actor without the right legal structure.
4. Many blocked shipments are not authorisation-supply problems; they are importer registration, classification, labelling, supplier-data, customs, or legality problems.
5. Portal timing, corrections, counterparties, payment, broker checks, and customs timing may not fit urgent shipment deadlines.
6. Importer registration is a hard gate; matching authorisations alone does not solve invalid registration.
7. Trust is non-trivial because both sides share shipment value, quota terms, pricing, refrigerant data, and portal status.
8. Transaction spreads can compress if authorisation pricing is transparent or brokers learn the playbook.
9. Customs brokers are both the best channel and likely future competitors.
10. The 60-day proof target was too aggressive for an unknown founder without warm broker/F-gas consultant access.

## Evaluator's Best Narrowing

The strongest beachhead would be:

> Customs-broker-routed HVAC, heat-pump, and refrigeration equipment importers with named shipments above 150,000 PLN and verified refrigerant/tCO2e data.

Suggested proof:

- 1-2 broker or F-gas consultant channels.
- 1 quota-holder or authorisation-manager option.
- 1-2 importer mandates.
- 1 named shipment with verified tCO2e file.
- 1 accepted/pending portal transaction or documented no-go.
- 15,000-50,000 PLN collected.

## Lesson

A hard regulatory-use right is stronger than a document packet, but it still fails when the founder must source both sides of a specialized market and the obvious channel partners can internalize the transaction. Avoid more F-gas variants unless there is already exclusive broker-routed importer demand or quota-holder access before the round begins.
