## Lead Candidate

**PPWR Lab-Chain PO Release Lockbox**

### Why It Advances

- Prior PPWR/PFAS evidence validation came close at 83 and the validator's best narrowing was live food-contact PFAS release/no-go for named post-12-August-2026 orders.
- This version adds a harder control point: the startup controls the named PO/run release file plus physical sample chain, lab order/status, supplier evidence, buyer conditional acceptance text, and no-go/substitute decision trail.
- It does not sell general PPWR readiness, legal opinions, lab brokerage, or unsupported compliance packaging.
- The first proof is not "interest"; it is prepaid live cases, buyer conditional acceptance paths, samples sent to labs, lab results or written lab-needed no-go, and order/run release or substitution decisions.

### Buyer

Polish/CEE food-contact packaging converters, co-packers, private-label food suppliers, food-service packaging distributors, and food brands whose customer has paused or conditioned a post-12-August-2026 PO, production run, reorder, item onboarding, or private-label packaging approval until a named PFAS evidence or lab-result condition is satisfied.

### Acute Trigger

A retailer, QSR, co-packer, distributor, brand owner, or private-label customer writes that a named packaging SKU/family cannot be accepted, reordered, onboarded, or produced for post-12-August-2026 placing on the market until the supplier provides acceptable PFAS evidence, a specific test report, supplier batch declaration, material-structure proof, or substitute path.

### Control Point

- Signed case mandate and prepaid fee over the named PO/run.
- Customer conditional acceptance wording.
- Packaging SKU/family and BOM/material structure.
- Batch/roll/lot sample chain-of-custody where testing is needed.
- Lab quotation/order/status/result, with lab remaining provider of record.
- Supplier declarations and RFI chronology.
- Food-contact and PFAS evidence folder.
- Reviewer issue-spotting notes.
- Buyer response matrix and outcome log.
- Explicit no-go/substitute authority when evidence or tests cannot support the claim.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Four prepaid live cases from at least three companies.
2. Every case has named customer request text, PO/run/reorder or onboarding value, packaging SKU/family, batch/sample source, and buyer decision deadline.
3. At least two cases include customer-written conditional acceptance criteria such as a specific lab result, supplier batch declaration, material structure, or substitute proof.
4. At least three sample chains or evidence-room packets are opened.
5. Two lab orders or lab-needed no-go decisions are documented.
6. Three measurable outcomes occur: PO/run release, customer narrowed follow-up, lab-confirmed pass/fail path, substitute path accepted, or clean no-go before production spend.
7. 50,000-120,000 PLN collected.

### 6-Month POC

- 15-30 paid live cases.
- 250,000-700,000 PLN revenue.
- 10+ measurable buyer outcomes.
- 5+ lab-result or lab-needed no-go files.
- Two repeat channels from packaging sales reps, converters, consultants, food-contact reviewers, or small labs.
- Gross margin above 55% after reviewer and sample/logistics costs.
- Kill if customer acceptance always requires full legal opinion, if labs/consultants absorb the workflow, or if no-go/lab-needed cases cannot be monetized.

### Economics

Charge 12,000-18,000 PLN for triage plus buyer conditional-acceptance mapping; 20,000-35,000 PLN for a sample-chain/lab-route PO release lockbox; 40,000+ PLN for multi-SKU retailer/QSR batches. Lab fees are passed through or paid by the client. The client pays because paused packaging runs, retailer onboarding delays, scrapped inventory, and missed private-label delivery windows can exceed the case fee.

### Copy Risk

Food-contact lawyers, packaging consultants, labs, material suppliers, large converters, and retailer compliance teams can copy pieces. This remains the main risk. The case-level defense is control of the named PO/run, customer condition, sample chain, lab order/status, supplier evidence trail, buyer chronology, and no-go/substitute decision before others can step in.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The controlled unit is a prepaid PO/run lockbox tied to physical samples and a buyer's conditional acceptance route. The outcome is commercial: PO/run released, buyer requirement narrowed, lab path opened, substitute accepted, or clean no-go before production spend. It is not a general PPWR report, readiness checklist, dashboard, marketplace, lab introduction, or broad packaging consulting project.

### Duplicate Risk

This is related to the failed PPWR PFAS FoodPack Order Release Pack but materially harder because it requires named conditional customer wording, sample chain-of-custody, lab order/status or lab-needed no-go, and a specific PO/run lockbox. It is not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs, REDBlocked, DORA, CBAM, Data Act, PromoLeak, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, BatteryFit, or TraceFaktura.
