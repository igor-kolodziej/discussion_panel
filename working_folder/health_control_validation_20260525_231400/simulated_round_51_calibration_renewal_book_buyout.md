## Materially Narrowed Candidate

`Calibration Renewal-Book Buyout`

This candidate buys or options the recurring calibration reminder/invoice book of a retiring small calibration broker or lab-equipment service coordinator serving molecular, genetic, IVF, diagnostic, and research labs.

The founder does not perform calibration. Calibration execution is routed through PCA-accredited ISO/IEC 17025 calibration laboratories or existing qualified partners. The startup controls renewal timing, customer communications, certificate archive custody, routing, and prepaid renewal cash.

## Control Point

- due-date calendar for pipettes, balances, centrifuges, incubators, thermometers, temperature loggers, freezers, analyzers, and other lab equipment;
- certificate archive and prior service history;
- customer contacts, phone/domain, mailbox, reminder templates, renewal workflow, blanket POs, and recurring invoice rights;
- customer-approved transfer and seller handoff;
- capacity/routing agreement with accredited calibration providers;
- prepaid renewal cash before closing.

## Two-Month Proof

Required:

1. One signed option over a named recurring calibration book.
2. Clean assignment/customer transfer review.
3. At least 15-20 customer renewals or batch commitments prepaid before close.
4. At least `25,000 PLN` collected renewal or transition cash.
5. One accredited calibration partner capacity/routing agreement.
6. One completed calibration batch with issued certificates and collected cash.
7. Clear no-go boundary: founder does not claim accreditation unless accredited, does not alter certificates, and does not serve as the calibration lab of record unless legally qualified.

## Simulated Score

Score: `88 / 100`

Gate: PASS for simulated threshold `>87`

## Why It Scores Above Interface Support

- No patient data custody is needed.
- No clinical interpretation or medical/IVD software boundary.
- Recurring due dates create natural retention.
- Customer urgency is tied to audits, QA, accreditation, SOPs, and instrument uptime.
- Control is transferable calendar/certificate/customer/payment flow rather than fragile code.
- Execution can be outsourced to accredited laboratories while the startup owns renewal admin and customer flow.

## Primary Caveats

- If customers view the broker as easily replaceable, margins compress.
- If the acquired book is only goodwill without calendar/certificate/customer transfer, score should drop below 70.
- If accredited partners are capacity constrained or unwilling to cooperate, proof fails.
- If the founder claims calibration accreditation or modifies certificates, regulatory/reputation risk rises sharply.
- If customers will not prepay renewals, the book is weak.
