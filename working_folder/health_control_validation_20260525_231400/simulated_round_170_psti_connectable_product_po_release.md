## Lead Candidate

**UK PSTI Connectable-Product PO/Listing Release**

### Internal Simulated Score

89 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The trigger is current UK retail/marketplace revenue: PO, onboarding, listing, or stock release blocked by missing PSTI Statement of Compliance or narrow product-security evidence.
- The artifact is statutory and concrete, not a generic compliance memo.
- The first buyer can be agencies and distributors already holding the seller relationship.
- It is materially different from REDBlocked because it is UK PSTI Statement of Compliance and retailer/importer/distributor duty, not EU RED Cyber/GPSR Amazon baby-monitor/camera evidence.
- Cases can be killed cleanly when manufacturer facts are missing or the product would require real security remediation.

### Internal Objections

- It is adjacent to REDBlocked and may be treated as an expansion rather than a new company.
- UK PSTI has been in force since 2024, so the first deadline panic may be gone.
- Compliance consultants, labs, product-safety firms, and retailer compliance teams can copy pieces.
- Hard cases require manufacturer truth, firmware/security facts, support-period policy, vulnerability-disclosure process, or product redesign outside founder control.
- A UK-retail focused channel may be harder for a Warsaw founder than Polish/CEE channels.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the prompt makes the duplicate-risk distinction explicit.
