## Candidate Advanced To Real Validation

Idea name: UFI/PCN SKU Release Desk

One-sentence thesis: release blocked EU hazardous-mixture SKUs by assembling the responsible entity's formula/SDS/label file, routing qualified CLP review, generating or validating UFI/PCN submission evidence, and delivering buyer-facing proof only for live order, listing, or private-label blockers.

Exact buyer: Polish/CEE importers, private-label sellers, distributors, ecommerce brands, cleaning-product sellers, automotive-chemical importers, hobby/resin/adhesive sellers, fragrance/candle brands, refill-product brands, and industrial-mixture suppliers with hazardous mixtures sold into EU retail, marketplace, distributor, Horeca, or private-label channels.

Acute trigger: A retailer, distributor, marketplace, private-label customer, 3PL, customs-adjacent broker, or chain buyer blocks a named SKU because the UFI is missing from the label/SDS, the Poison Centre Notification proof is missing or inconsistent, the label UFI does not match formula/SDS records, the responsible legal entity is unclear, or a formula/label change requires updated evidence before shipment/listing.

Control point: Signed SKU release mandate; named SKU/EAN/order/listing; buyer blocker text; responsible legal entity confirmation; supplier/formulator formula or mixture-in-mixture evidence; current SDS and label artwork; qualified CLP/SDS reviewer route; UFI generation or validation trail; PCN submission or update evidence where the responsible entity submits; label/SDS alignment pack; buyer-facing release packet; accepted listing/order release, shipment approval, or written no-go.

60-day proof:

- Six prepaid SKU release cases.
- Four complete formula/SDS/label/reviewer files accepted for processing.
- Four UFI/PCN submission/update evidence packets prepared with the responsible entity.
- Two retailer, distributor, marketplace, 3PL, or private-label releases, or written acceptance/no-go outcomes.
- 40,000-120,000 PLN collected.
- At least 40% of inbound cases rejected because source formula, responsible-entity authority, qualified review, or legal classification boundary is missing.

6-month POC:

- 40-90 paid SKU release cases.
- 25-60 complete UFI/PCN/label/SDS files processed.
- 15-35 buyer/listing/order releases or clean no-go outcomes.
- 250,000-800,000 PLN startup revenue.
- Three repeat channels such as packaging agencies, SDS/CLP consultants, marketplace agencies, private-label buyers, or import accountants.
- Gross margin above 60% after qualified CLP/SDS reviewer costs, secure formula handling, translations, and channel commissions.

First acquisition mechanism: Target packaging-label agencies, SDS translators, small CLP consultants, marketplace agencies, private-label brokers, import accountants, and distributors that see urgent SKU blockers but do not want to operate the UFI/PCN evidence workflow. Direct outreach only to brands with written buyer/listing blocker text and named SKUs.

Economics: Charge 6,000-12,000 PLN for a simple evidence-ready SKU, 12,000-25,000 PLN for multi-country or mixture-in-mixture cases, and urgent premiums for order value above 150,000 PLN. The buyer pays because a live SKU, listing, private-label order, or shipment is blocked and the output is accepted evidence or a clean no-go, not a generic compliance memo.

Copy risk: CLP/SDS consultants, chemical compliance firms, packaging agencies, labs, marketplace agencies, and large distributors can copy. The defense is current SKU mandate control, repeated accepted/rejected blocker memory, fast packaging/label/SDS/PCN coordination, trusted reviewer bench, and channel relationships with agencies that do not want to own the full workflow.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/database/marketplace/broker: The unit is a named SKU release file tied to buyer blocker text and containing formula/SDS/label/UFI/PCN evidence, qualified review trail, responsible-entity submission proof, buyer-facing packet, and order/listing release or no-go. It is not generic chemical compliance consulting, SDS translation, a dashboard, database, marketplace, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries:

- The startup does not classify mixtures, certify safety, provide legal advice, act as importer/downstream user/responsible entity, submit false information, or place products on the market.
- The responsible legal entity and qualified CLP/SDS reviewer own classification, PCN submission, label approval, and legal marketability.
- Reject biocides, pesticides, pharmaceuticals, controlled substances, medical products, explosives, weaponized chemicals, high-risk vape/e-liquid cases, missing formula/source data, suspicious supplier documents, or requests to bypass classification.
- Reject cases where a lab test, legal opinion, reformulation, supplier disclosure, or responsible-entity change is required before useful evidence can exist.

Duplicate risk:

- Not REDBlocked, GPSR responsible person, MoCRA cosmetics, EUDAMED, PPWR/PFAS, EPRBlocked, DRS/KaucjaMark, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, or Amazon button-cell release.
- The distinctive control point is EU hazardous-mixture UFI/PCN/label/SDS evidence for a named blocked SKU, with qualified CLP review and responsible-entity submission proof.
