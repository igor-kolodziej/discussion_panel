Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
AccessLock RED PO Release Desk.

One-sentence thesis:
EU/CEE smart-lock and wireless access-control importers, distributors, and private-label suppliers will pay for a fast evidence-response desk when a retailer, hotel group, installer network, locksmith wholesaler, property buyer, or tender authority blocks a named purchase order, listing, shipment, or bid on RED cybersecurity, CE, GPSR, app/cloud security, update-support, or supplier-document evidence.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE importers, distributors, private-label brands, locksmith wholesalers, access-control installers, hotel-door-lock suppliers, and B2B ecommerce sellers of connected smart locks, wireless lock cylinders, digital door handles, BLE/NFC access keypads, hotel-room locks, garage/gate controllers, wireless cabinet locks, and no-camera access-control devices. The economic buyer is the importer owner, distribution head, product/compliance manager, sales director, or installer-network lead with a live buyer request blocking a current order or listing.

Acute trigger:
A named buyer has written that a SKU, product family, tender response, B2B catalog listing, hotel project, retail listing, or shipment cannot proceed until the seller provides acceptable evidence around RED/CE documents, EN 18031 or RED cybersecurity exposure, Declaration of Conformity consistency, GPSR product-safety data, app/cloud data flows, firmware/update-support process, vulnerability contact, product labels, model mapping, or supplier technical-file gaps. The seller wants to protect current PO value, launch timing, tender eligibility, or channel revenue, not buy broad compliance consulting.

Control point:
The startup accepts only live buyer-condition cases with named SKUs and written request text. The controlled artifact is the access-control SKU release dossier: buyer request interpretation, SKU/model map, supplier document index, Declaration of Conformity inconsistency check, test-report/model-match screen, RED cybersecurity exposure map, EN 18031 applicability and restricted-clause warning where relevant, app/cloud/update/vulnerability-contact evidence request, GPSR/product-safety document screen, supplier RFI, submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed decision, buyer-safe response wording, and escalation route to a lab, notified body, CE consultant, cybersecurity reviewer, or lawyer where needed.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign at least three paid cases covering at least five named access-control SKUs or one multi-SKU product family. Each case must include written buyer request text, product/SKU identifiers, seller authorization, supplier documents, and a current commercial consequence above 50,000 PLN in blocked PO, tender, listing, or launch value. At least two cases must produce buyer progress: PO release, listing acceptance, narrowed rework request, accepted lab escalation, supplier correction, buyer-approved no-go, or written confirmation that the seller cannot honestly submit the requested evidence.

6-month POC:
Close 20-35 paid access-control evidence cases from at least eight importers/distributors/installer networks or three repeat channel partners. Document at least 12 buyer-progress outcomes and reject at least 25% of unsuitable cases. Build reusable case memory around access-control product families, supplier-document mismatch types, EN 18031/RED exposure patterns, app/cloud/update evidence gaps, buyer wording, lab-needed triggers, and buyer-safe response language. Keep gross margin above 55% after RED/CE reviewer, product-security reviewer, translation, and case-management cost.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with Polish/CEE access-control distributors, locksmith wholesalers, hotel-lock suppliers, marketplace agencies with B2B hardware sellers, CE/RED consultants that do not want emergency buyer-response work, and product-testing labs that prefer receiving pre-sorted cases. Outreach asks for one live blocked SKU or tender condition, not a general compliance call: "If a buyer is holding a smart-lock/access-control PO or listing because RED/CE/cybersecurity evidence is unclear, I turn the request and supplier files into a submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed response dossier in five business days."

Economics:
Pricing is 3,000-5,000 PLN for request interpretation and submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed triage, 8,000-18,000 PLN for a single-SKU response dossier, and 15,000-35,000 PLN for a multi-SKU buyer/tender sprint. A distributor can justify the fee when 50,000-500,000 PLN of PO value, tender eligibility, launch timing, or channel revenue is blocked. Capital needs are low: reviewer time, secure document handling, translation/RFI templates, and targeted outbound. The founder does not buy inventory, run tests, certify products, or become importer of record.

Copy risk:
CE/RED labs, notified bodies, product-compliance consultants, access-control OEMs, importers, cybersecurity advisers, and larger distributors can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is narrow case memory and channel habit: access-control-specific buyer request patterns, model-mismatch taxonomy, supplier RFI wording, EN 18031/RED exposure triage, app/cloud/update evidence gaps, buyer-safe response language, and lab-needed triggers. Once the founder controls a live buyer condition and seller authorization for a named SKU, the current release case is not freely copyable before the response window closes.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the founder holds the seller's paid mandate, written buyer condition, named SKU files, supplier documents, reviewer route, response deadline, and buyer-safe dossier path for a specific blocked order/listing/tender, another consultant or lab cannot control that exact case before the current buyer response is delivered unless the seller terminates the mandate. Incumbents can compete for future cases or formal testing, but not the live response file already under paid control.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It does not sell generic CE consulting, a dashboard, a marketplace, lab lead generation, certification, legal opinions, cybersecurity assurance, or broad compliance readiness. It accepts only live, written, named-SKU buyer blockers and produces a purchase-order/listing/tender release artifact: request interpretation, SKU/model evidence map, supplier RFI, submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed decision, buyer-safe response wording, reviewer notes, escalation path, and buyer outcome tracking.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No certification, no Declaration of Conformity signing, no legal advice, no importer-of-record role, no product-safety certification, no cybersecurity assurance, no penetration testing, no physical-security rating, no lock-grade claims, no warranty guarantees, no promise of buyer acceptance, and no lab/notified-body substitution. Labs, notified bodies, CE/RED consultants, cybersecurity reviewers, lawyers, OEMs, importers, and buyers remain responsible for formal conformity, testing, legal interpretation, product changes, technical-file completeness, app/cloud security, labels, instructions, and final acceptance. Cases with missing source truth, suspicious supplier documents, false model mapping, required testing, unsafe claims, physical-security certification issues, or legal classification disputes are escalated or rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from REDBlocked because it is not an Amazon baby-monitor or indoor-camera reinstatement desk and does not start from Seller Central ASIN suppression. The buyer trigger is a B2B purchase order, tender, retail listing, hotel/access-control project, locksmith wholesale listing, or installer-channel request for smart-lock and wireless access-control SKUs. The proof artifact adds access-control-specific app/cloud/update/vulnerability-contact, model-mapping, and physical-security boundary issues. It is also distinct from DORA, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, GreenTender, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, Supplement Stack, and health/Rx/clinical routes.

Strongest anticipated objections:
This may still be seen as another compliance evidence desk. Labs, CE consultants, notified bodies, product-safety advisers, cybersecurity reviewers, OEMs, and larger distributors may already handle serious cases. Simple cases may be too easy to justify the fee, while hard cases require real testing, technical-file repair, firmware/update commitments, labeling changes, product redesign, supplier documents, or legal review outside founder control. Buyer acceptance remains outside founder control. The market may be smaller than connected cameras because access-control buyers can use established OEMs. Founder credibility requires named RED/CE and product-security reviewers. The business must avoid duplicating the already confirmed Amazon connected-device evidence desk by staying focused on B2B access-control PO/tender/listing release.
