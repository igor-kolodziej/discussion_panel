## Lead Candidate: VoiceAI Article 50 Release Evidence Pack

### Buyer

EU/CEE and EU-facing B2B AI voice-agent, customer-service AI, sales-call AI, call-center automation, and conversational-AI vendors with live enterprise, regulated-industry, public-sector, or BPO customer deployments where legal/procurement/security teams are asking for AI interaction disclosure, human handoff, audit/logging, and Article 50 transparency evidence before go-live, renewal, or expansion.

### Acute Trigger

A customer launch, renewal, expansion, or tender is blocked by a written request such as:

- prove callers/users are informed they interact with AI;
- show where disclosure appears in scripts, IVR, chat UI, emails, transcripts, or help pages;
- document when a human handoff occurs;
- show logs or test calls proving disclosures fire;
- identify provider/deployer responsibilities without legal overclaiming;
- provide instructions for the deployer/customer's own transparency obligations;
- confirm synthetic voice/content boundaries and escalation rules.

### Control Point

Vendor-side live release pack tied to one customer deployment:

- buyer request text;
- product flow, call scripts, IVR/chat UI, emails, transcripts, and disclosure copy;
- test-call/test-chat logs;
- human-handoff rules and escalation evidence;
- deployer instruction sheet;
- provider/deployer responsibility map for issue spotting only;
- model/vendor disclosure and known limitations;
- incident/complaint routing note;
- gap split: safe to ship, product change needed, legal review needed, customer/deployer decision needed, unsafe to answer.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid live release packs at 10,000-30,000 PLN each.
2. At least two packs tied to written enterprise, regulated-industry, public-sector, or BPO customer blockers.
3. At least one launch, renewal, pilot expansion, or tender step progresses after the pack.
4. One AI governance/legal issue-spotting reviewer on paid per-case terms.
5. One reusable evidence checklist across AI voice disclosure, handoff, test logs, deployer instructions, and synthetic content boundaries.
