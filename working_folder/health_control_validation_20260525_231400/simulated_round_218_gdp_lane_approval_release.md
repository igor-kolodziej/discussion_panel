## Lead Candidate: GDP Cold-Chain Lane Approval Release Desk

### Buyer

Small and mid-sized Polish/CEE GDP couriers, specialty cold-chain logistics providers, pharma 3PL subcontractors, depot operators, temperature-controlled packout providers, and logistics arms of distributors whose pharma, CRO, biotech, wholesaler, or specialty-care customer will not approve a named route, lane, renewal, tender, or shipment program until the provider submits a bounded temperature-control and GDP evidence file.

### Acute Trigger

A customer QA, procurement, or supply-chain owner blocks a lane, route, shipment program, subcontractor approval, contract renewal, or tender award because the provider has not produced enough evidence around route risk, temperature control, logger calibration, vehicle/fridge qualification, thermal packaging, SOPs, training, deviation handling, subcontractors, handoff points, or test-run data.

### Control Point

Prepaid live lane approval case with:

- exact customer QA/procurement request and deadline;
- named route/lane or customer shipment program;
- vehicle/fridge/packaging/logger IDs and calibration certificates where available;
- route risk notes, handoff points, transport mode, seasonal assumptions, and lane timing;
- SOPs, training records, deviation/excursion process, subcontractor list, and escalation contacts;
- one or more lane test runs or dry-run logger records when needed;
- GDP/cold-chain reviewer issue spotting for flagged sections;
- customer-ready lane file with no-go/escalation notes;
- strict boundary that the startup does not act as wholesaler, logistics provider, QA approver, GDP certifier, medicine seller, medicine carrier, or legal adviser.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid live lane approval cases at 12,000-35,000 PLN each.
2. At least two include real logger/test-run evidence rather than only SOP paperwork.
3. One named GDP/cold-chain QA reviewer on paid per-case terms.
4. One customer QA/procurement progress outcome: lane accepted, conditional approval, tender/customer next step, shipment program unblocked, or written clean no-go.
5. At least 50,000 PLN collected.
6. Repeatable case taxonomy: product temperature band, route type, packaging/loggers, customer request wording, evidence gaps, reviewer flags, customer acceptance/no-go reason.

### Economics

Charge 6,000-10,000 PLN for paid lane triage and submit/cannot-submit/no-go decision; 12,000-22,000 PLN for a standard lane approval file; and 25,000-35,000 PLN for urgent multi-route or tender-critical packs with logger dry runs and reviewer issue spotting. The fee is justified only when a real pharma/CRO/biotech/wholesaler customer contract, tender, renewal, subcontractor approval, or shipment program is blocked. Expected gross margin is 60-70% after reviewer, logger rental, admin, and template costs if cases are tightly filtered.

### Copy Risk

GDP consultants, large 3PL QA teams, packaging vendors, temperature-monitoring vendors, validation firms, and pharma logistics specialists can copy the workflow. The defense is not a GDP checklist. It is live lane-case control, prepaid customer deadlines, actual logger/test-run files, CEE provider focus, reviewer-bounded no-go discipline, and accumulated customer-request/outcome memory by lane type.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Broker

The unit is a named customer lane approval case with prepayment, route evidence, logger/test-run artifacts, reviewer flags, and a customer QA outcome. The startup does not introduce logistics providers to pharma customers, run shipments, sell monitoring hardware, build a dashboard, or write generic GDP reports. It assembles and controls the live lane evidence file for a current approval blocker.

### Legal And Provider Boundaries

The startup does not sell, prescribe, dispense, store, transport, import, export, or advertise medicines. It does not hold wholesale distribution authorization, certify GDP compliance, validate packaging, approve QA release, handle patient data, or interpret legal obligations. Licensed wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers, logistics providers, depots, QA owners, GDP advisers, and customers remain responsible for regulated activities, contracts, shipments, product quality, release decisions, and final approvals. Cases involving patient-specific data, clinical advice, blood/plasma, controlled substances, falsified evidence, unclear authorization, or medicine handling by the startup are rejected.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not PharmaFridge Cold-Chain Compliance Book Buyout, which tested recurring pharmacy fridge/logger calibration renewal books and failed below gate. This candidate is customer-contract lane approval for logistics providers, with route-specific logger evidence and customer QA progress as proof.

It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, Part-IS Aviation, KSC/NIS2, CPSC eFile, EUDR, or Amazon/marketplace evidence. The buyer, trigger, controlled artifact, and payment event are materially different: a named pharma cold-chain transport lane or shipment program blocked by customer QA approval.
