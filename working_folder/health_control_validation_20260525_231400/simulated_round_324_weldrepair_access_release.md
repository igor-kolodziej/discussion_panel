## Official Timing Notes

- Directive (EU) 2024/1799 on common rules promoting the repair of goods entered into force in 2024 and must be transposed by Member States by 31 July 2026. Source: European Commission right-to-repair materials and EUR-Lex Directive (EU) 2024/1799.
- The Commission describes the new rules as giving consumers an easier, cheaper, and faster repair route, including a European Repair Information Form and an online repair platform. It also says producers must repair common household products that are technically repairable under EU law.
- Existing ecodesign repair-information/spare-part logic already covers several product categories, including welding equipment under EU ecodesign rules. This makes welding equipment a more B2B/high-ticket repair-access beachhead than low-value consumer appliances.

## Search Frame

Fresh validation keeps rejecting casework when the founder only chases an external actor. This round uses a partner-channel repair job where the external actor is not the end buyer: the startup works for independent repair shops and industrial maintenance firms that already have customer-approved repair jobs. The controlled object is the named paid repair job, serial/model evidence, part or repair-information blocker, and manufacturer/importer request trail. First proof is a part/info access acceptance, repair completion, customer-paid no-go, or written refusal that the repair shop can use in its customer decision.

The right-to-repair regulation alone is not enough. The candidate must be narrow enough for current cash: high-value welding equipment repairs where blocked repair jobs can support a fixed fee, not consumer appliance form-filling.

## Why It Clears Simulation

- It ties a current legal/timing change to a current paid repair job. The desk does not sell general right-to-repair readiness.
- The first proof is operational cash: part ordered, repair information provided, diagnostic/software access granted, customer-approved paid no-go, repair completed, or replacement/resale decision made.
- Welding equipment is high enough value and downtime-sensitive enough to support a fixed fee, unlike consumer appliances.
- Independent welding repair shops already have the customer trust and repair competence; the startup only handles the repeatable manufacturer/importer access packet, request trail, and no-go/refusal evidence.
- Case memory can compound by OEM/importer, model family, part category, request wording, repair-information route, serial evidence, distributor response, and accepted no-go evidence.
- The boundary avoids safety certification and actual electrical repair. The repair shop remains repairer/provider of record.

## Internal Cap Notes

- Copy risk is substantial. Repair shops, welding distributors, service centers, and industrial maintenance firms can chase access themselves.
- The candidate stays above gate only because the founder controls named repair-access files from multiple repairers and turns obscure right-to-repair/ecodesign request paths into a reusable partner desk.
- If accepted case fees are below roughly 1,500 PLN or average blocked repair value below roughly 8,000 PLN, this becomes low-margin admin.
- If manufacturers ignore requests, if the legal applicability is weak, or if repairers do not pay before seeing results, the idea should be killed quickly.
- It must reject jobs involving unsafe repairs, altered safety systems, CE conformity changes, improvised boards, battery packs, medical devices, elevators, payment terminals, or warranty/legal disputes.
