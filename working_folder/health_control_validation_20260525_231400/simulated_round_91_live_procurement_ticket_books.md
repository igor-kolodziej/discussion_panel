## Current Regulatory Timing Notes

- EU AI Act transparency obligations remain a current 2026 issue. The European Commission published draft guidelines on AI transparency obligations on 2026-05-08 and states that from 2026-08-02 people in the EU must be informed when interacting with AI systems or exposed to certain AI-generated/manipulated content.
- On 2026-05-07 the Council and Parliament reached a provisional agreement that would delay stand-alone high-risk AI rules to 2027-12-02 and product-embedded high-risk rules to 2028-08-02, while also setting 2026-12-02 as a new deadline for transparency solutions for artificially generated content.
- Therefore the candidate should not rely on high-risk AI obligations alone. It should focus on live buyer/procurement transparency, chatbot disclosure, AI-generated content labeling, AI use inventory, and deployer/provider boundary evidence.
