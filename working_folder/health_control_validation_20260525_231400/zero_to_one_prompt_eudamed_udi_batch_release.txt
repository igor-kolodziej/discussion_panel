Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
EUDAMED UDI Batch Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
From 28 May 2026, the first EUDAMED modules are mandatory, creating an immediate data-operations bottleneck for manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, and system/procedure-pack producers; the wedge is an AR/regulatory-consultant-routed batch desk that cleans, structures, and tracks UDI/device-registration batches for named launch, PO, shipment, or distributor-release cases while leaving all medical-device regulatory responsibility with the manufacturer, authorised representative, PRRC, notified body, and counsel.

Exact buyer:
Authorised representatives, PRRC/regulatory consultants, medtech regulatory-affairs boutiques, and small medical-device manufacturers/importers that already have valid conformity and source documents but are behind on EUDAMED Actor, SRN, Basic UDI-DI, UDI-DI, device-registration, legacy-device, or system/procedure-pack data tasks. The first buyer should be an authorised representative or regulatory consultant with multiple client batches and no appetite to hire temporary data staff.

Acute trigger:
EUDAMED's first four modules became mandatory from 28 May 2026. A manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, or system/procedure-pack producer has devices or SPPs that must be registered, newly placed on the market, continued after the transition, submitted to a distributor, released for shipment, supported in a tender, or cleaned up for an AR/regulatory file. The buyer does not need broad MDR advice; it needs a controlled data batch assembled from existing source truth and reviewed by the responsible regulatory actor before submission or commercial release.

Control point:
A signed AR/regulatory/manufacturer batch mandate over named device families, Basic UDI-DIs, UDI-DIs, SRNs, actor records, certificates where applicable, Declarations of Conformity, risk class, legacy/regulation status, issuing-entity identifiers, manufacturer/importer/AR relationships, procedure-pack data where applicable, EUDAMED draft/export/import files, portal submission chronology, reviewer comments, error logs, distributor or launch deadline, and commercial progress marker. The startup controls the data-operations batch and submission log; the manufacturer, authorised representative, PRRC, notified body, legal counsel, and competent authority retain regulatory responsibility and sign-off.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three AR/regulatory consultant or manufacturer batch mandates are prepaid or partner-guaranteed at 8,000-45,000 PLN each; at least 60 UDI-DI or equivalent device/SPP records are processed; every batch has source documents supplied by the responsible actor; at least two batches receive AR/PRRC/regulatory reviewer acceptance for portal submission or correction; at least one batch is submitted, accepted, corrected, or converted into a documented clean no-go; at least one buyer records commercial progress such as new-SKU launch readiness, distributor PO release, shipment release, tender evidence acceptance, or removal of a specific EUDAMED data blocker. No classification, conformity assessment, clinical, PMS/vigilance, or legal opinion work is performed by the startup.

6-month POC:
Process 15-40 batches across 5-12 AR/regulatory/manufacturer channels; clean or structure 600-2,000 UDI-DI/device/SPP records; produce 30+ accepted submission/correction/no-go outcomes; document 10+ launch, distributor, shipment, tender, or commercial-progress outcomes; collect 250,000-900,000 PLN in batch fees and urgent release fees; retain three repeat AR/regulatory partners; keep gross margin above 60% after contractor data operations, secure intake, translation, and reviewer costs; maintain a private data-error taxonomy by device type, actor role, UDI field, source-document mismatch, portal error, and reviewer correction.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target authorised representatives, PRRC consultants, MDR/IVDR regulatory boutiques, small notified-body-adjacent consultants where allowed, and small manufacturers/importers already warning clients about the 28 May 2026 EUDAMED mandatory-use change. Pitch: "If you have client EUDAMED UDI/device-registration batches piling up, I run the data-room, field mapping, error log, draft import/export files, and correction tracker under your review. You keep regulatory judgement and client ownership; I take the structured data work and deadline ops."

Economics:
Charge 8,000-15,000 PLN for a small clean batch with up to 20 UDI-DI/device records; 18,000-45,000 PLN for multi-device urgent batches, legacy/regulation mixed batches, SPP batches, or distributor/launch-linked cases; 2,000-8,000 PLN per correction cycle or urgent commercial-release add-on; partner discount of 20-30% for repeat AR/regulatory channels. Costs are secure intake, field mapping, document comparison, portal/import file prep, language cleanup, data contractors, and paid regulatory reviewer time. The buyer pays because a missed or messy EUDAMED batch can block launch, distributor release, tender evidence, shipment readiness, or regulatory file closure.

Copy risk:
Authorised representatives, PRRCs, regulatory-affairs consultants, QMS software vendors, UDI consultants, notified-body-adjacent consultants, and manufacturers' RA teams can copy the workflow. The defense is current batch control, partner-channel trust, speed during a live mandatory-use transition, data-error memory, and a repeatable evidence-to-field mapping process for small manufacturers that are too small for enterprise RA software rollouts.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once an AR/regulatory partner or manufacturer assigns a prepaid batch, shares the source documents, grants portal/data-operation access under their review, and ties the work to a launch, PO, shipment, tender, or correction deadline, another operator cannot copy that specific data room, source-document chronology, reviewer comments, field mapping, error log, and submission clock without replacing the active partner-reviewed case. Incumbents can chase future batches, but they do not control the already-assigned record set and deadline.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup only accepts named EUDAMED batches with source documents, responsible-actor review, and a commercial or portal deadline. The proof is structured UDI/device/SPP records, reviewer-accepted field mapping, submission/correction logs, portal progress, and commercial-release progress. It is not a generic MDR report, software dashboard, public database, marketplace, regulatory hotline, certification offer, medical-device consultancy, or referral broker.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not classify devices, assign regulatory status, certify devices, validate clinical claims, perform conformity assessment, act as manufacturer/importer/authorised representative/PRRC/notified body, provide legal advice, provide PMS/vigilance services, handle patient data, interpret clinical evidence, decide whether a device may be placed on the market, or guarantee EUDAMED acceptance. Manufacturers, authorised representatives, PRRCs, notified bodies, lawyers, competent authorities, importers, and distributors remain responsible for regulatory truth, sign-off, and market decisions. Cases lacking source documents or requiring classification, conformity, clinical, safety, PMS/vigilance, legal, or false-document work are rejected or routed to qualified professionals.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked because it is not Amazon account-health, connected-device RED/CE/GPSR evidence, or marketplace reinstatement. It is not DORA, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, or any recovery mandate. The buyer is an AR/regulatory/manufacturer channel, the trigger is mandatory EUDAMED Actor/UDI/device-registration use from 28 May 2026, the control point is a named data batch under responsible-actor review, and the proof artifact is EUDAMED submission/correction/commercial release progress.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Regulatory-affairs consultants and authorised representatives already own the trust relationship and may internalize the data work. Many manufacturers will lack clean source data, Basic UDI-DI logic, certificates, DoC consistency, issuing-entity identifiers, actor role clarity, or legacy/regulation status. EUDAMED upload progress does not prove MDR/IVDR compliance. The founder must not drift into device classification, conformity, clinical, PMS/vigilance, legal, importer, AR, or PRRC responsibilities. Direct manufacturer acquisition is weak without AR/regulatory channels. The window may narrow once EUDAMED workflows are normalized. Confidential medical-device files require secure handling. Some cases will become messy regulatory remediation rather than bounded data operations.
