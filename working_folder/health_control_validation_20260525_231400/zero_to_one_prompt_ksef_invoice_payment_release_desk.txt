Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
KSeF Invoice Payment Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A current-cash release desk for Polish B2B sellers, CFOs, accountants, and AR teams whose customer payments are stuck because an invoice or invoice batch is not cleanly accepted, retrieved, matched, corrected, or paid under Poland's mandatory KSeF structured-invoice workflow.

Exact buyer:
Polish B2B suppliers, wholesalers, service companies, equipment sellers, construction subcontractors, SaaS/local-service vendors, and their accountants or CFOs with unpaid invoice batches above 100,000 PLN where a customer AP team, shared-service center, municipality, large enterprise, or procurement department has paused payment because the invoice lacks a valid KSeF number, cannot be retrieved in KSeF, does not match the PO/GRN/vendor master, has FA(3)/XML/KSeF validation issues, needs a correction path, or is missing the KSeF receipt/visualization/QR evidence the buyer requires.

Best first buyers are sellers with 10-200 B2B invoices per month, a few large customers, messy ERP/accounting workflows, customer AP tickets already open, and a finance person who can involve the accountant or ERP provider quickly.

Acute trigger:
A real customer payment is delayed or refused after the 2026 KSeF rollout because the buyer says the invoice is not visible, valid, matchable, payable, or correct in KSeF. The seller has delivered the goods or service and has commercial AR, but cash is blocked by KSeF number, XML, buyer access, correction, PO/GRN, vendor-master, split-payment/reference, or portal evidence friction.

Control point:
Signed invoice-release mandate for one live unpaid invoice batch with:
- buyer AP rejection, ticket, email, or payment-hold notice;
- invoice register and unpaid AR amount;
- KSeF numbers, UPOs, FA(3)/XML files, rejection/error messages, or missing-number log;
- PO, GRN, delivery, acceptance, vendor-master, bank-account, split-payment, and buyer-reference evidence where relevant;
- accountant and ERP-provider contact path;
- correction/resubmission/evidence-room plan;
- buyer AP response log and payment-release tracker.

The startup does not need to become the tax adviser or ERP integrator. It controls the urgent cross-party file: what the buyer says blocks payment, what KSeF evidence exists, what the accountant/ERP provider must fix, what the seller must resend, and what AP needs to release cash.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid invoice-release cases at 4,000-18,000 PLN each, each tied to unpaid AR above 100,000 PLN.
2. At least one case includes a buyer AP payment-hold notice or ticket that explicitly references KSeF visibility, KSeF number, structured invoice, XML, correction, PO/GRN/vendor match, or invoice retrieval.
3. At least one invoice batch is corrected, reissued, rematched, retrieved, accepted by buyer AP, paid, or given a written payment date after the release pack.
4. One accountant/KSeF reviewer and one ERP/integration troubleshooter are available per case on paid contractor terms.
5. A repeat taxonomy of blockers: missing KSeF number, wrong buyer NIP, buyer cannot retrieve invoice, rejected FA(3)/XML, wrong PO/GRN reference, missing correction path, offline/online date mismatch, duplicate invoice, wrong vendor bank/master data, missing QR/visualization, or split-payment reference mismatch.
6. One accountant, ERP integrator, outsourced CFO, or AP shared-service contact routes a second case.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build an intake checklist for live unpaid KSeF invoice batches; recruit one KSeF/accounting reviewer and one ERP/XML troubleshooter; create a target list of 80 accountants, CFO advisers, ERP integrators, outsourced finance teams, B2B suppliers, construction subcontractors, and wholesalers with large-customer AR exposure.

Month 2: close three prepaid cases. Accept only files with delivered goods/services, unpaid AR above 100,000 PLN, a named buyer AP blocker, invoice/KSeF evidence, and a named accountant or ERP contact. Deliver a release pack: blocker map, KSeF evidence index, correction/resubmission checklist, AP reply package, buyer-reference map, and no-go/escalation notes.

Month 3: prove one commercial outcome: AP accepts the invoice, buyer retrieves it, correction is accepted, invoice is rematched to PO/GRN, payment date is issued, payment is released, or a clean no-go is documented before the seller wastes more time.

Months 4-6: complete 12-30 paid cases, collect 100,000-300,000 PLN in fees, keep gross margin above 65% after reviewer/ERP-contractor costs, secure 2-4 repeat channels, and build a private taxonomy of KSeF payment blockers by buyer type, ERP, AP workflow, and correction path.

First acquisition mechanism:
Buyer pitch:
"If a customer will not pay because a KSeF invoice is not visible, valid, matched, corrected, or payable, send the AP rejection, invoice register, KSeF numbers/UPO/XML/errors, PO/GRN references, and accountant/ERP contact. I return a payment-release pack: blocker map, evidence index, correction/resubmission path, AP reply package, and escalation/no-go log. I do not provide tax advice, accounting certification, or ERP implementation."

Channel pitch:
"You keep tax, accounting, and ERP responsibility. I take the ugly live AR cases where customer cash is blocked by KSeF invoice matching, retrieval, correction, XML, buyer AP, or payment-reference friction."

Economics:
Charge 4,000-7,500 PLN for a narrow one-buyer/one-batch release case, 7,500-12,000 PLN for multi-invoice or ERP/XML-heavy cases, and 12,000-18,000 PLN for large-customer AP cases involving many invoices, corrections, or buyer-reference mismatches. Optional success fee of 1-3% of released AR only where the accountant and contract permit it, but the core model is prepaid fixed fees. Contractor reviewer/troubleshooter cost is 500-3,000 PLN per case. The buyer pays because 100,000-1,000,000+ PLN of delivered-work AR is delayed by invoice evidence friction.

Copy risk:
Accountants, ERP vendors, KSeF software vendors, tax advisers, outsourced CFOs, and AP consultants can copy pieces. The defense is not generic KSeF implementation; it is fast control of the live unpaid invoice file, buyer AP blocker, seller/accountant/ERP correction path, KSeF evidence room, and payment-release log. Case memory accumulates across AP objections, ERPs, buyer shared-service behaviors, correction paths, XML errors, and PO/GRN/vendor-master mismatches.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the seller signs a release mandate, prepays the case, provides the AP hold notice, shares the unpaid invoice batch, KSeF evidence, buyer references, accountant contact, and ERP contact, the startup controls the active cash-release workflow. Another provider must displace the live AP conversation, evidence map, correction checklist, buyer response log, and payment-release tracker under time pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live unpaid invoice batches with a named buyer payment blocker. The output is a payment-release artifact used in an active AP/correction workflow: blocker map, KSeF evidence index, correction/resubmission checklist, buyer AP reply package, payment-release tracker, and no-go/escalation log. It is not a KSeF implementation project, accounting office, tax opinion, ERP integration product, dashboard, marketplace, generic broker, or invoice-financing product.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide tax advice, accounting certification, VAT classification, legal advice, official tax representation, ERP implementation, KSeF software, invoice financing, debt collection, or a guarantee of payment. The seller, accountant, ERP provider, tax adviser, and buyer AP team remain responsible for tax treatment, invoice issuance, official KSeF submission, accounting records, payment approval, and contractual decisions. Cases involving fake invoices, undelivered goods/services, disputed performance, buyer insolvency, tax fraud, bank-account fraud, sanctions, or true commercial dispute are declined or escalated.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not TraceFaktura because it does not create municipal animal-care operational invoice evidence. It handles live Polish B2B AR blocked by mandatory KSeF structured-invoice payment friction. It is not PromoLeak because it is not retailer deduction recovery. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, BioSignal, GreenTender, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
- Accountants, ERP vendors, and KSeF software providers may already solve many invoice problems.
- Some AP holds may be buyer bureaucracy or commercial dispute disguised as KSeF friction.
- Many issues may be trivial once the seller learns the workflow, reducing repeat revenue after the rollout stabilizes.
- Legal/tax/accounting boundaries are sensitive because invoice correctness, VAT treatment, KSeF submission, and corrections are official records.
- The founder may struggle to get enough trust to access invoices, XML, AP tickets, buyer references, bank details, and ERP/accountant contacts.
- Buyer AP teams may not cooperate with an outside coordinator.
- If cases require ERP implementation rather than evidence/correction coordination, delivery becomes slow and low-margin.
- The opportunity may fade after 2026 if the market normalizes and KSeF errors become routine internal work.
