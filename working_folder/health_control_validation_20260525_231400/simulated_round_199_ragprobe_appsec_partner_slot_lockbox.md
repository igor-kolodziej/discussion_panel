### Idea

### Why It Clears The Simulated Gate

- It directly addresses the evaluator's prior RAGProbe objection: unknown founder trust. The buyer relationship stays with appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO partners.
- It avoids the prior RAGModule weakness by requiring prepaid or partner-guaranteed slot blocks, written outcome reporting, current buyer blockers, and direct buyer-progress/no-go artifacts.
- It rejects dangerous tool-using, production, regulated, or formal-assurance work and tests only bounded sandbox environments.
- The proof is cash/control first: partner slot contracts, paid modules, accepted annexes, repeat partner routing, and buyer-progress outcomes.
- The artifact is more than a report: reproducible prompt-injection traces, source-isolation checks, tool-permission screenshots/logs, logging/human-review evidence, a rework/no-go table, and partner-branded buyer-safe annex wording.

### Main Internal Objections

- Appsec partners can internalize the playbook after several modules.
- Larger AI-security firms have stronger credibility for formal red-team or pentest work.
- Sensitive test access and false-assurance boundaries remain serious.
- Buyer acceptance remains outside the startup's control.
- The business fails if partner slots are soft referrals rather than paid/guaranteed current cases.
