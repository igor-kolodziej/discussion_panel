## Simulated Pass Candidate

### Cloud Marketplace Private-Offer Remittance Recovery - 89

One-sentence thesis: a success-fee recovery mandate for small EU/CEE B2B SaaS vendors whose accepted AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud marketplace private offers have payout, fee, tax, discount, refund, metering, customer-commit, or remittance mismatches that can be documented and disputed through marketplace portals.

Buyer: 10-150 person B2B SaaS/ISV vendors in Poland/CEE and EU-adjacent markets with at least one cloud marketplace listing and accepted private offers, but no mature marketplace operations or finance-ops team. Best first buyers have 250,000 PLN+ annual marketplace GMV or one six-figure private offer where payout timing, marketplace fees, tax, customer discount, renewal, metering, or cloud-commit drawdown is not matching the sales contract or portal reports.

Acute trigger: seller closed a private offer through AWS Marketplace, Microsoft commercial marketplace, or Google Cloud Marketplace, but expected remittance is short, delayed, misclassified, refunded, taxed/withheld unexpectedly, not tied to the right offer, not consuming the customer's committed cloud spend, or inconsistent across portal exports and bank receipts. The cash is already associated with a closed order, not speculative pipeline.

Transferable control point: signed recovery mandate, seller authorization, marketplace seller-portal reports, private-offer terms, offer/order IDs, customer PO/contract evidence, payout/remittance statements, bank receipt evidence, tax/fee line items for accountant review, support-case submissions, and first accepted correction, credit, or payout.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:

- 3 signed recovery mandates from SaaS vendors with live private-offer/remittance issues.
- Portal/report access or exported evidence for at least 10 private offers.
- At least 500,000 PLN in candidate mismatch value screened, with only documented non-speculative claims accepted.
- 5 marketplace support cases or finance tickets filed with structured evidence.
- At least one marketplace acknowledgement, corrected report, payout trace, credit memo, or recovered remittance.
- First 20,000-70,000 PLN collected in setup fees, minimum fees, or success fees.

First acquisition mechanism: target EU/CEE SaaS vendors that publicly list AWS/Azure/GCP marketplace availability, Microsoft co-sell/private offers, AWS Marketplace private offers, Google Cloud marketplace procurement, or enterprise customer cloud-commit language. Outreach goes to founders, CFOs, revenue operations, alliances, and channel leads with a narrow ask: "Do any accepted private offers have payout, fee, tax, discount, refund, metering, or customer-commit mismatch against your contract, portal reports, or bank remittance?"

Gross margin and payback logic: charge a 4,000-12,000 PLN minimum/setup fee for report normalization and claim register creation, plus 10-20% of recovered credit, corrected remittance, or previously stuck payout. Large private offers make one recovery worth tens of thousands of PLN. Founder work is structured report reconciliation, claim evidence, portal cases, and follow-up; accountants and cloud-marketplace specialists are paid only for edge review.

Copy risk: cloud GTM consultants, RevOps contractors, marketplace agencies, accountants, cloud alliances consultants, and larger ISV finance teams can do pieces of the work. The defense is narrow EU/CEE small-ISV focus, recovered-cash pricing, private-offer/remittance case memory, fast report normalization, and refusal to become generic cloud marketplace consulting.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream: once the startup has the signed mandate, private-offer IDs, portal exports, bank remittance evidence, customer PO/contract evidence, support cases, and recovery workflow, competitors cannot act on that exact claim without the vendor switching. The asset compounds through mismatch taxonomy by marketplace, offer type, fee/tax/refund pattern, support-case wording, and payout outcome.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/generic broker: the output is not a marketplace-readiness report, RevOps dashboard, listing service, or brokered intro. The proof is an accepted correction, payout trace, credit, recovered remittance, corrected marketplace report, or documented no-go reason for a closed private offer. The startup is paid for cash recovery and payment clarity tied to already-sold offers.

Reasons this could still fail:

- The eligible EU/CEE private-offer seller pool may be small.
- Larger ISVs already have finance operations, cloud alliances, or marketplace agencies.
- Many apparent mismatches may be valid fees, taxes, refunds, timing delays, or contract misunderstandings.
- Marketplace support outcomes can be opaque and slow.
- Portal access and commercial contract data are sensitive.
- Cloud GTM consultants can copy the workflow.
- Success-fee cash conversion may lag if corrections take weeks or months.
- The idea can drift into generic marketplace listing, co-sell, or RevOps consulting.

Decision: advance this candidate to real Zero To One validation.
