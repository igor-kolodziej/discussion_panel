Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: KSeF AP Payment-Acceptance Release Desk.

Exact buyer: Polish B2B suppliers, wholesalers, manufacturers, service firms, and subcontractors selling to larger companies or public-sector buyers where one or more named invoices are unpaid because the buyer's AP/procurement team says the KSeF invoice, KSeF number/UPO, PO match, buyer master data, correction invoice, split-payment fields, invoice visualization, or source-system export is not acceptable for payment.

Acute trigger: The supplier has already issued or attempted to issue an invoice, but cash collection is blocked by a concrete buyer AP status: rejected KSeF invoice, missing KSeF number/UPO, PO mismatch, incorrect buyer/seller NIP or branch data, wrong payment/split-payment field, correction invoice mismatch, buyer portal mismatch, or buyer email saying the invoice cannot be booked or paid until the KSeF/AP issue is fixed. The desk does not handle vague KSeF readiness or ordinary unpaid invoices without a documented AP/KSeF acceptance blocker.

Control point: The startup signs a paid mandate over a named unpaid invoice batch and works only where the supplier provides buyer AP/procurement rejection evidence or payment-status evidence. The desk coordinates the technical/accounting evidence path: KSeF ID/UPO/status, FA(3) XML/visualization, PO/contract/protocol references, buyer master data, accountant-approved correction or reissue decision, and buyer-ready response packet. The founder does not unilaterally decide tax/accounting meaning; the supplier's accountant approves any correction, reissue, or semantic field change.

Exactly what is signed, assigned, or prepaid within 60 days:

- signed paid mandate for a specific unpaid invoice set, not an open-ended support relationship;
- buyer AP/procurement rejection email, portal status, payment hold note, or accounting-status screenshot;
- invoice list with values, due dates, KSeF IDs/UPOs where available, PO/contract/protocol references, and buyer contact path;
- accountant-approved correction/reissue/field-change decision when invoice content changes;
- buyer-ready acceptance packet sent back to AP/procurement;
- first documented payment movement, buyer acceptance, rebooking, or written next-step confirmation before month 2 ends.

6-month proof:

- 8-12 paid mandates from suppliers with named AP/KSeF payment blockers;
- at least 5 mandates tied to invoices worth 100,000+ PLN each or cumulative batches above 250,000 PLN;
- at least 3 cases where buyer AP accepts/rebooks the invoice, confirms the fix, or releases payment after the packet;
- 60,000-120,000 PLN revenue from fixed fees and/or modest success fees;
- reusable error map across buyer AP systems, KSeF status issues, ERP exports, PO/contract mapping, and correction-invoice paths;
- one accountant/tax reviewer and one KSeF/XML specialist on paid terms;
- no case accepted where the real issue is debtor insolvency, commercial dispute, quality dispute, litigation, or missing performance.

First acquisition mechanism: Start with suppliers that sell to large enterprises, municipalities, hospitals, utilities, retailers, manufacturers, and construction buyers where payment depends on AP acceptance. Use targeted outbound to finance managers, owner-operators, accountants, and industry groups with the message: "If a buyer is holding payment because the invoice cannot be booked through KSeF/AP, send the rejection/status and invoice list; I return an accountant-approved buyer acceptance packet." Also ask accounting offices and ERP integrators for only cases where a named buyer payment is blocked, not generic month-end KSeF errors.

Economics: Charge 4,000-15,000 PLN fixed per payment-release case depending on invoice value and complexity, with optional 1-3% success fee on released cash only where legally and commercially appropriate. The fee is credible when 100,000-1,000,000 PLN of receivables are delayed by an invoice acceptance issue. Costs are a KSeF/XML specialist, accountant/tax reviewer for semantic changes, and founder coordination. Gross margin is attractive only if the desk rejects insolvency, legal disputes, construction-performance disputes, open-ended ERP repair, and ordinary collections.

Copy risk: Accountants, ERP vendors, AP consultants, and debt collectors can copy pieces. The wedge is the combination of named buyer payment status, KSeF/AP technical evidence, accountant-approved correction path, and direct cash-release framing. Once the founder controls the signed mandate and buyer AP case history for a named invoice set, competitors cannot collect that exact case. Longer-term advantage comes from a buyer/AP error memory: what specific buyers, portals, fields, KSeF statuses, visualizations, and correction paths actually move invoices from rejected to bookable/payable.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker: It is not KSeF readiness, XML validation, or debt collection. It activates only on named invoices already blocked by a buyer AP/KSeF acceptance issue and gets paid for moving that specific payment case. It produces a buyer acceptance packet, accountant-approved correction/reissue path, and payment-status trail, not generic advice or software.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- no tax advice, legal advice, accounting filing, or invoice-content decision without the supplier's accountant/tax reviewer;
- no debt collection, litigation, insolvency, or commercial-dispute cases;
- no guarantee that the buyer will pay; the promised output is AP/KSeF acceptance movement and documented next step;
- no KSeF credential sharing; access uses exported XML/status evidence or proper delegated authorization only;
- no handling of customer bank funds unless a separate legal/payment structure exists;
- reject cases where the buyer is using KSeF as a pretext for quality, delivery, performance, or cashflow disputes.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

- Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: this is invoice cash-release for suppliers, not regulated-finance ICT evidence.
- Not REDBlocked: no marketplace ASIN/listing compliance or Amazon agency channel.
- Not BioSignal Claim-Risk: no healthtech investor diligence.
- Not PromoLeak: no FMCG trade deduction audit or retail chargeback recovery; this is KSeF/AP invoice acceptance before payment.
- Not prior KSeF Month-End Acceptance Rail: this is not an accounting-office exception queue. It is supplier-side, named unpaid invoice batches with buyer AP rejection/status and cash release as the economic trigger.
