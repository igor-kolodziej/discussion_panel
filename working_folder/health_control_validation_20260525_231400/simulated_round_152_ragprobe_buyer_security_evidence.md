## Lead Candidate

**RAGProbe Buyer Security Evidence Sprint**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It upgrades Round 151 from answer packaging to actual bounded technical evidence.
- The artifact is controlled and concrete: test plan, synthetic prompt set, RAG source isolation checks, prompt-injection traces, tool-permission tests, logs, pass/rework/no-go findings, and buyer-ready evidence.
- The partner channel solves trust and distribution better than direct sales.
- It remains deal-linked: no live buyer request and deadline, no ticket.
- It avoids high-risk AI legal classification and full model validation by focusing on non-regulated RAG/agent security evidence for ordinary SaaS features.

### Internal Objections

- AI security vendors and application-security firms are natural incumbents.
- Some failures require product remediation, not evidence.
- Testing must be scoped tightly to avoid false assurance.
- Sensitive test access and logs create security obligations.
- Partner margins may compress if every sprint needs senior AI security review.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control artifact is stronger than a questionnaire response.
