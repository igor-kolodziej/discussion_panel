## Lead Candidate: EcoIT Public Tender Evidence Packs

### Why It Advances

- The buyer has a visible current revenue event: a public tender deadline for laptops, monitors, desktops, thin clients, printers, or classroom IT equipment.
- The founder can acquire public tenders and public certification data without waiting for platform, authority, debtor, or upstream supplier decisions.
- The artifact is concrete: SKU eligibility matrix, official certification links/screenshots, manufacturer letter checklist, missing-evidence RFI, and bid/no-bid summary.
- It avoids GreenTender's lighting domain by using IT hardware tender language and official evidence sources such as TCO Certified, EPEAT, ENERGY STAR, EU energy labels where applicable, manufacturer warranty/support evidence, security/TPM/OS lifecycle proof, and accessibility/support fields.
- It has low capital intensity and high gross margin. Each pack can be produced manually before building software.
- Distribution can start with evidence-led outbound: public tender plus reseller catalog plus a specific missing or mismatched certification requirement.

### Internal Objections

- This is adjacent to GreenTender and must be killed if it is just "GreenTender for a different category" without distinct buyer economics and evidence complexity.
- IT resellers and distributors may already have tender desks and manufacturer portals that produce these documents.
- Some requirements may be simple manufacturer PDFs, making the fee too low.
- The best IT resellers may have direct vendor bid support from Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft, Cisco, or distributors.
- Some public tenders are written around exact known models; evidence mapping may not change the bid decision.
- Broadening across many hardware categories can dilute the graph. The first niche should be laptops, monitors, desktops, thin clients, and printers for Polish education/public-sector tenders.
