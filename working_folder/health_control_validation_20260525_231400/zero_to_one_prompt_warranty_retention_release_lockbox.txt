Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: Warranty Retention Release Lockbox.

Exact buyer: Polish/CEE small B2B subcontractors, fit-out suppliers, installation firms, and specialist trade contractors that have completed commercial or public-sector work and have matured retention, warranty deposit, or final-payment balances where the debtor has already acknowledged the obligation or release condition.

Acute trigger: A warranty-retention amount or final release has matured after acceptance, warranty-period expiry, defect-removal confirmation, or final protocol, but the general contractor, developer, facility owner, or public buyer is slow-paying. The seller has paperwork and acknowledgement but does not want to spend owner time chasing a small-to-medium receivable.

Control point: The startup only takes cases with a concrete existing payment obligation. It signs an assignment or collection mandate over a named acknowledged receivable, gets debtor acknowledgement or written payment-status confirmation, and directs payment to a founder-controlled lockbox or client escrow account before doing work. No speculative disputed claims, no litigation funding, no broad factoring.

Exactly what is signed, titled, assigned, or prepaid within 60 days:

- signed assignment or collection mandate for one named retention/final-payment receivable;
- underlying contract, invoice, retention schedule, acceptance protocol, warranty expiry or defect clearance evidence;
- debtor acknowledgement, payment-status email, accounting confirmation, or written statement that the balance is payable subject only to administrative release;
- direct payment instruction to lockbox/client account or signed success-fee mandate;
- first collected cash from at least one debtor before month 2 ends.

6-month proof:

- 8-15 signed mandates or assignments;
- at least 3 debtor-acknowledged claims collected;
- 100,000-250,000 PLN gross receivables processed;
- 15,000-40,000 PLN gross fees or spread collected;
- zero active litigation cases;
- no case funded without debtor acknowledgement;
- median collection under 45 days from debtor acknowledgement;
- repeat referral channel from two accountants, quantity surveyors, construction bookkeepers, or small contractor advisors.

First acquisition mechanism: Build a narrow outbound/referral list through construction accountants, quantity surveyors, contract administrators, and small trade contractor networks. The pitch is not "factoring" or "legal recovery"; it is "send only matured retention/final-release balances where the debtor has acknowledged the payable amount and you want the admin/payment chase removed." First cases are mandate/success-fee only or very small discounted purchases after debtor acknowledgement.

Economics: Charge 8-20% of collected cash depending on age, documentation, and whether the founder advances any capital. Prefer success-fee mandates at first to avoid balance-sheet loss. If buying claims, pay only after debtor acknowledgement and cap single exposure below 25,000 PLN. Gross margin is high if cases are administrative follow-up; the business is killed if it becomes legal debt collection, disputed construction claims, or litigation.

Copy risk: Lawyers, debt collectors, factors, and accountants can copy the category. The defensibility is not category monopoly; it is control over exact assigned receivables and a repeatable small-claim release process. Once the founder has debtor acknowledgement and payment direction for a named claim, competitors cannot collect that specific cash. Long-term edge comes from a referral channel that filters only payable acknowledged retentions, not contested claims.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker: It controls a named receivable/payment direction and gets paid from collected cash. It does not sell software, reporting, legal memos, generic recovery advice, or a marketplace of claims. Cases without assignment/mandate plus debtor acknowledgement are rejected.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- no legal advice, litigation, court enforcement, insolvency claims, or disputed-defect claims;
- use a licensed lawyer/debt-collection advisor only for document templates and boundary review;
- if the debtor denies the obligation or raises defect/offset claims, the case is rejected or returned;
- no consumer debt and no patient/health data;
- comply with assignment, data-processing, AML, and debt-collection rules before buying any claim;
- no claim purchase above the founder's defined exposure limit.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

- Not PromoLeak: no FMCG trade deductions, retail-media charges, or brand recovery audit.
- Not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Data Act, or TraceFaktura: no compliance evidence pack, no marketplace listing rescue, no investor diligence, no import/customs documentation, no energy or municipal event ledger.
- It is closest to generic receivable collection/factoring, but the intended wedge is narrower: matured, debtor-acknowledged retention/final-payment balances with direct payment control and no litigation.
