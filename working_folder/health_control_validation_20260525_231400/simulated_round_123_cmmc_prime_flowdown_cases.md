## Lead Candidate: CMMC Prime-Flowdown Evidence Pack

### Buyer

CEE and EU-adjacent defense, aerospace, electronics, software, engineering, machining, components, materials, robotics, drone, simulation, test equipment, and industrial suppliers that sell to US defense primes, US subsidiaries, prime contractors, or defense-adjacent integrators and receive a live CMMC / NIST SP 800-171 / DFARS / FCI-CUI evidence request tied to a subcontract, renewal, PO, onboarding, bid, or supplier portal.

### Acute Trigger

A US prime or defense customer asks for CMMC status, Level 1/2 self-assessment evidence, SPRS score/status, affirmation readiness, NIST SP 800-171 control evidence, SSP/POA&M files, CUI/FCI boundary, subcontractor/cloud/MSP evidence, incident-reporting process, access-control evidence, or proof that the supplier can continue on a PO, bid, renewal, or onboarding path.

### Control Point

Prepaid live prime-request case with:

- current written prime/customer request, supplier portal item, contract clause, PO condition, or bid/onboarding blocker;
- supplier authorization and evidence room;
- scope/boundary map for FCI/CUI exposure using contract/customer-provided materials only;
- evidence index for Level 1/2 self-assessment support, SPRS/affirmation readiness, SSP/POA&M gaps, subcontractor/cloud/MSP facts, incident process, access/MFA/logging/vulnerability/backups, and unsafe answers;
- named CMMC RPO/consultant or NIST 800-171 reviewer on paid per-case terms;
- no CMMC certification claim, no legal/export-control advice, no C3PAO assessment, no final SPRS/affirmation submission unless the qualified responsible party handles it.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid live CMMC/prime-flowdown cases at 15,000-40,000 PLN each.
2. At least two different primes/customers represented by incoming requests.
3. One CMMC RPO/consultant or NIST 800-171 reviewer contracted per case.
4. Two contract-progress outcomes: prime portal item accepted, bid/onboarding proceeds, PO not blocked on evidence, customer narrows follow-up, or supplier cleanly no-go's a contract before overcommitting.
5. 60,000-120,000 PLN collected.
6. Reusable case taxonomy across prime wording, supplier type, FCI/CUI boundary, evidence gaps, unsafe claims, reviewer notes, and customer pushback.

### Economics

Charge 15,000-25,000 PLN for a focused Level 1/FCI or limited prime-flowdown evidence response, 25,000-40,000 PLN for Level 2/NIST 800-171 SSP/POA&M evidence pack and reviewer pass, and 8,000-15,000 PLN for annual refresh or changed-since-last-prime-response update. The buyer pays because a defense subcontract, PO, onboarding, renewal, or strategic prime relationship is at risk.

### Copy Risk

CMMC RPOs, C3PAOs, MSPs, cyber consultants, law firms, and prime supplier-risk teams can copy pieces. The defense is CEE/non-US supplier focus, live prime-flowdown response speed, bilingual evidence packaging, RPO/reviewer bench, prime wording memory, and case memory by supplier type and FCI/CUI boundary. The startup is not trying to replace CMMC consultants; it owns urgent vendor-side evidence packaging and routes implementation/certification/legal work.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack. DORA serves software vendors selling to regulated finance customers under EU ICT third-party-risk pressure. This serves CEE/EU defense and industrial suppliers responding to US defense prime CMMC/NIST/DFARS flowdown requests. The buyer trigger, customer channel, evidence map, reviewer bench, proof artifact, and contract language are different.
