# Simulated Round 351: Conforming Micro-OOH Face Lockbox

Date: 2026-05-31 Europe/Warsaw

## Search Posture

Round 350 failed because third-party payout control was not actually transferable. Fresh-chat failures before it also punished public-process execution, copyable evidence packs, payment-release casework, and incumbent-owned data. This round tests a more local, asset-specific control object: exact legal outdoor-advertising faces where the founder controls the site right before selling the campaign.

This is deliberately not a billboard marketplace, media agency, new-permit development project, or speculative OOH buildout. It accepts only existing small-format or wall/window/fence/facade advertising faces with owner consent, current or prior legal-use evidence, and a buyer insertion order or deposit before material capex.

## Source Checks

Outdoor advertising legality is local and contentious in Poland. Warsaw's landscape-resolution work and enforcement record show that illegal or nonconforming ad devices are a live issue, and city materials emphasize rules for advertising devices, signs, and visual order. The simulation treats that legal friction as the reason to control only pre-vetted conforming faces, not to speculate on new permits.

## Selected Candidate

**Conforming Micro-OOH Face Lockbox**

One-sentence thesis: Small brands, franchises, clinics, schools, events, and local OOH agencies will prepay for exact legal street-level advertising faces in constrained Polish city nodes when the founder controls the landlord/permit/use right before campaign capex.

### Why It Clears Simulation

The control point is not a case file, checklist, or payment exception. It is an exact physical and legal advertising location:

- owner or manager consent for the exact wall, window, fence, pylon, facade, or ground face;
- current or prior legal-use evidence such as existing sign documentation, building-owner approval, municipal no-objection path, lease terms, or proof the face is a permitted/on-premise/conforming display;
- exclusive short-term option or lease assignment for campaign use;
- buyer insertion order, deposit, or prepaid reservation before printing, installation, lighting, or other capex;
- proof of first posted campaign or a clean legal/site no-go.

This gives a better "before incumbents copy" story than previous service ideas. A competitor can sell other outdoor inventory, but cannot sell the exact corner-facing wall, storefront window, fence line, or small legal face after the founder has the owner consent and buyer-funded campaign right.

### Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof: passes if actual faces are under option/lease and first buyer IOs are prepaid.
- One vendor or consultant copy risk: partially passes. OOH incumbents can copy the general playbook, but not the exact site right after it is controlled.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: passes only if the first proof uses buyer deposits before capex and static/small-format placements, not expensive speculative digital screens.
- Vague partnerships/goodwill: passes only with signed site options and signed buyer IOs, not landlord interest.
- Legal/provider limits: passes only by rejecting new-permit speculation, illegal faces, traffic-safety conflicts, heritage/building disputes, and sites without documented owner authority.

Simulated score: **88.4 / 100**

## 6-Month POC

1. Source 30-60 existing small legal ad faces from retiring micro-OOH operators, landlords, property managers, small shopping strips, gyms, clinics, schools, car washes, storage sites, industrial fences, and local service buildings.
2. Accept only sites with owner authority and documented legal/use basis before sales.
3. Sign 10-20 short exclusive options or campaign-use leases.
4. Sell exact faces to local agencies, franchises, clinics, schools, event promoters, property developers, and service businesses using location photos, audience context, traffic/pedestrian proxy, dimensions, install path, and legal-use evidence.
5. Take deposits or prepaid insertion orders before printing/install spend.
6. Target 8-15 campaigns, 80,000-250,000 PLN revenue, 40,000-125,000 PLN gross margin, and at least 5 repeat site renewals or repeat buyers.

## Kill Conditions

- Fewer than 10 legally usable faces can be optioned after 60 serious site-owner conversations.
- Landlords will only provide vague interest, not written owner consent or a campaign-use option.
- Buyer deposits do not arrive before printing/install/capex.
- City, road, heritage, building, traffic-safety, or landscape-rule risk makes most faces unusable.
- OOH agencies say they already have enough legal micro inventory and do not need exact-site sourcing.
- Gross margin falls below 45% after rent share, print/install, removals, legal review, sales commissions, and vacancy.
- First campaigns are mostly one-off low-ticket local ads with no repeat renewal path.
