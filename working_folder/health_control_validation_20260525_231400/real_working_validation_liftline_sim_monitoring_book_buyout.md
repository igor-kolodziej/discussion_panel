- Sellers may not own a clean transferable book; records may be messy or embedded in maintenance contracts.

- Safety liability leaks even with boundaries.
- Failed test calls can require field service: signal, antenna, old modules, battery, routing, locked cabinets, or configuration.
- Lift maintainers, alarm centers, and intercom installers can copy the operating model for new customers.
- 100,000 PLN budget is thin for 400-800 accounts unless seller financing is mostly earnout-based.
- Part-time execution is mismatched with safety-adjacent recurring operations.
