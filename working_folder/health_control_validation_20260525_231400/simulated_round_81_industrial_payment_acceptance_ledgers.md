# Simulated Round 81: Industrial Payment-Acceptance Ledgers

Created: 2026-05-27 Europe/Warsaw

## Search Frame

This round pivots outside health and away from generic route books. It looks for boring B2B payment events where a solo founder can control the evidence stream, payment packet, customer acknowledgement, or transferred cashflow inside 60 days under 100,000 PLN.

The filter is hard: no dashboards, marketplaces, reports, lead lists, advisory services, or speculative receivables. A candidate only matters if a signed mandate, assigned claim, titled asset, customer-approved payment transfer, or prepaid current case is created before the incumbent can copy the exact flow.

## Lead Candidate

**ReadyMix Pour Acceptance Ledger** clears simulated `>87` because it is not a route-book acquisition or a generic app. The buyer signs that every pour for two named contractors/jobs must route through the founder's capture packet during the pilot. The packet is created at the moment payment evidence is still fresh: delivery ticket, batch number, truck time, site photos, slump/temperature if used, waiting time, pump notes, lab cube/sample IDs, rejected-load reason, and contractor/client signature.

The hard-control proof is concrete enough for 60 days:

- one signed prepaid pilot with a ready-mix plant or pump operator;
- two named contractors/jobs under all-pour routing;
- at least 30 live pours captured;
- at least one invoice packet accepted by a customer, or one rejected-load/waiting-time/accessorial charge paid using the packet;
- at least 10,000 PLN collected in pilot/packet fees;
- no product build beyond mobile capture, structured storage, OCR, PDF packet generation, and manual QA.

## Strongest Simulated Objections

- Concrete plants, batch systems, construction ERPs, and inspection/QC software already exist; the wedge must stay in small plants and disputed invoice packets, not broad construction management.
- Field adoption can fail if drivers and site staff will not capture data before leaving the site.
- If the plant's customers already sign clean delivery tickets and pay without friction, willingness to pay falls sharply.
- Defect disputes can become technical/legal expert matters; the founder must stay out of engineering certification and legal claims.
- Repeatability depends on finding plants with enough small/medium pours, paper/WhatsApp evidence, and real invoice friction.

## Why This Is Not A Duplicate

- It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, PromoLeak, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, TraceFaktura animal-care, parcel claims, or heritage-door inventory.
- The closest pattern is TraceFaktura's live event ledger, but the buyer, event object, payment proof, and economics are materially different: private concrete producers/pumpers, delivery tickets and pour acceptance, B2B construction invoices, rejected loads, waiting time, and material/QC evidence.
- It is not a municipal-service invoice packet. It is a plant-side payment-acceptance rail for private construction-material delivery events.

## Decision

Write the prompt and submit ReadyMix to real working-chat Zero To One validation. Treat simulated score `89` as a pass.
