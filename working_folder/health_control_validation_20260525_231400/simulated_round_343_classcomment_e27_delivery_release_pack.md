## Selected Idea

**ClassComment E27 Delivery Release Pack**

### One-Sentence Thesis

IACS UR E27 has turned cyber-resilience evidence for on-board systems into a class/shipyard acceptance blocker, and small marine OT equipment suppliers need a fast, reviewer-backed comment-response pack when a named delivery, FAT/SAT, payment, or vendor approval is stuck.

### Exact Buyer

Polish/CEE/EU marine equipment and automation suppliers with 10-150 employees selling on-board systems or equipment into shipyards, marine system integrators, or newbuild/refit programs.

Initial focus:

- automation panels and control systems;
- alarms and monitoring systems;
- remote monitoring gateways;
- navigation/communication interfaces;
- propulsion/energy-management controls;
- fire/safety monitoring electronics;
- sensor gateways and networked bridge/engine-room equipment.

### Acute Trigger

A written class society, shipyard, owner, or system-integrator comment has blocked one of:

- FAT/SAT acceptance;
- delivery sign-off;
- vendor-list approval;
- milestone invoice;
- final payment;
- commissioning handoff;
- type-approval or project-specific acceptance path.

The comment must explicitly concern cyber-resilience, IACS UR E27, ship cyber rules incorporating E27, network interfaces, account/access control, update/patch handling, vulnerability handling, logging, secure configuration, remote access, hardening, backup/recovery, SBOM/software inventory, or security documentation for on-board systems.

### Transferable Control Point

The startup controls a signed supplier mandate over one specific comment response:

- exact class/shipyard/system-integrator comment thread;
- named vessel/project/order and equipment model;
- equipment category and interface list;
- existing manuals, architecture notes, software/firmware inventory, update process, access-control notes, hardening guidance, vulnerability-contact process, support-period statement, remote-access statement, and test/FAT records;
- reviewer-backed E27 response matrix;
- source-evidence index;
- submission wording and chronology;
- written no-go route when the product requires engineering remediation, class/type-approval work, legal review, or formal certification.

### 60-Day Signed/Titled/Assigned/Prepaid Proof

Within 60 days:

- one retained marine cyber or class-facing OT reviewer;
- two partner channels willing to route live comment overflow: marine system integrator, marine cyber consultant, shipyard QA subcontractor, or equipment distributor;
- three prepaid supplier mandates at 18,000-45,000 PLN each;
- at least two response packs submitted against real class/shipyard comments;
- at least one hard outcome: comment closed, revised comment accepted, FAT/SAT released, delivery accepted, payment scheduled, or written no-go accepted by the buyer/channel.

### 6-Month POC

- 12-18 paid live comment cases.
- 6+ accepted comment closures, delivery/FAT/SAT releases, payment releases, or accepted no-go outcomes.
- 3 repeat referral partners.
- 250,000-600,000 PLN revenue.
- 55%+ gross margin after reviewer payments.
- Reusable comment library covering 30+ comment patterns across 5+ equipment families.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Use trigger-based outbound and partner routing:

1. Build a target list of 80 CEE/EU marine OT equipment suppliers with public shipyard, class, offshore, ferry, naval, or commercial-vessel references.
2. Build a parallel list of 25 marine system integrators, marine cyber consultants, electrical/automation subcontractors, and shipyard QA/documentation overflow providers.
3. Send a narrow offer: "If a class, yard, owner, or integrator cyber comment is blocking FAT, delivery, or payment for an on-board system, we turn the existing product evidence into a reviewer-backed E27 response pack. No certification claims, no pentest, no legal advice, no generic readiness."
4. Accept only written live comments tied to named projects and commercial blockers.

### Economics

Pricing:

- 6,000-9,000 PLN paid triage for comment scope, document sufficiency, and no-go decision.
- 18,000-45,000 PLN fixed fee for one equipment/project comment-response pack.
- 55,000-90,000 PLN for multi-equipment urgent sprint only if evidence is clean and reviewer capacity is locked.

Cost:

- reviewer: 4,000-12,000 PLN per case depending on complexity;
- founder: evidence mapping, response chronology, supplier RFI, and pack assembly;
- secure storage/tooling: minor during POC.

Payback:

- one blocked equipment delivery, FAT/SAT milestone, or marine project payment can justify the fee;
- partner channels keep 20-30% if they route the case and retain the client relationship.

### Copy Risk

Copy risk is real:

- class societies;
- marine cyber consultancies;
- OT security firms;
- marine system integrators;
- large equipment OEMs;
- shipyard QA/documentation teams.

The best defense is not generic E27 knowledge. It is a controlled live-comment intake lane plus cross-case memory of what class/yard/integrator comments actually say for small suppliers.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

They can copy the generic checklist, but they cannot work a particular case before the founder controls:

- the supplier's signed mandate;
- the exact class/shipyard comment;
- the equipment evidence folder;
- the reviewer-backed response matrix;
- the submission chronology and no-go boundary;
- the partner/channel relationship for that live ticket.

Long-term defensibility is weaker than physical ownership, but stronger than generic documentation work because every accepted response adds class/yard-specific comment memory for an obscure, high-stakes niche.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

It is a live acceptance-release packet for a named equipment/project blocker. The product is not a readiness report or advisory memo. It ends in a submission-ready response matrix, source-evidence index, reviewer notes, supplier RFI, and comment chronology tied to a specific FAT/delivery/payment/vendor-approval event.

It does not introduce buyers and sellers. It does not list consultants. It does not build software before paid cases. It rejects generic readiness, broad security audits, pentests, certification, legal interpretation, and open-ended remediation.

### Boundaries And Legal/Provider Limits

- No certification.
- No class approval guarantee.
- No representation as class society, surveyor, notified body, legal counsel, or formal assessor.
- No pentesting unless routed to a qualified provider under a separate engagement.
- No cyber remediation unless a qualified contractor is separately retained.
- No false claims about compliance, support periods, patching, access control, logging, SBOM, or vulnerability handling.
- Reject cases involving sanctions, naval classified information, export controls, unsafe equipment, falsified documents, undisclosed remote access, or requests to backdate evidence.

### Duplicate Risk Against Existing Confirmed Ideas

Not DORA: buyer is a marine equipment supplier, gatekeeper is class/shipyard/system integrator, and the artifact is an E27 project-comment release pack rather than a regulated-finance renewal evidence pack.

Not REDBlocked: channel is maritime class/yard acceptance for on-board equipment, not Amazon Seller Central ASIN reinstatement for connected consumer devices.

Not CBAM/Data Act/GreenTender/Supplement/BioSignal/HeritageDoor: no customs import continuity, machine-data liberation, public tender evidence, supplement safety, investor diligence, or reclaimed physical interiors.

### Strongest Anticipated Objections

1. Marine cyber and classification expertise may be too specialized for an unknown solo founder without a named reviewer.
2. Class societies, shipyards, system integrators, and OEMs may already have preferred documentation and cyber reviewers.
3. Some comments require real engineering remediation, secure development changes, firmware updates, architecture changes, or formal type approval, not packet assembly.
4. Final acceptance remains outside founder control.
5. Case volume may be lumpy because only suppliers selling into newbuilds/refits subject to E26/E27 pressure will feel acute pain.
6. Partner channels may internalize after seeing the response matrix.
7. Sensitive marine OT documents create trust friction.
8. The Warsaw solo-founder fit is weaker than local regulated-finance or Amazon-agency work unless partner routing works.
