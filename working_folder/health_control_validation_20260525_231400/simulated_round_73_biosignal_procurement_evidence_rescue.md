# Simulated Round 73: BioSignal Buyer-Block Evidence Rescue Desk

Date: 2026-05-26

## Why This Pivot

The latest failures show a repeated split:

- Physical inventory ideas have hard control, but demand is lumpy and compatibility/refurbishment risk caps scores.
- Health and Rx-adjacent ideas fail when the transaction rail is owned by a clinic, pharmacy, realizator, or patient-trust relationship.
- Generic biosignal consulting and evidence reports are too copyable unless they attach to a live decision gate.

The strongest confirmed pattern in the archive is not "write a report." It is a deadline-bound evidence-response product tied to a live blocked transaction, as in DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack and REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk.

Round 73 adapts that pattern to the user's requested focus: EEG, wearables, biosignals, remote monitoring, medical AI, and healthtech procurement.

## Candidate

`BioSignal Buyer-Block Evidence Rescue Desk`

A 7-business-day paid response service for EU/CEE wearable, EEG, sleep, remote-monitoring, digital-biomarker, and medical-AI vendors whose live hospital, insurer, pharma/CRO, employer-health, or enterprise pilot/procurement/renewal is blocked by a buyer evidence request.

The trigger must be concrete: a named buyer has sent a questionnaire, evidence checklist, DPA/security request, AI/MDR/GDPR question set, clinical validation request, integration review, or safety-claim challenge that is delaying a pilot, renewal, procurement approval, or commercial rollout.

The startup does not certify, practice medicine, validate clinical claims, run trials, give legal advice, act as a notified body, or guarantee procurement approval. It organizes the vendor's existing evidence into a buyer-ready response pack and flags what must go to counsel, security, regulatory, or clinical reviewers.

## Buyer

- 10-150 person EU/CEE healthtech vendors with wearable, EEG, sleep, remote-monitoring, digital-biomarker, or medical-AI products.
- Vendors trying to close hospital, insurer, pharma/CRO, employer-health, or regulated-enterprise pilots.
- Founders, COOs, commercial leads, security leads, quality/regulatory leads, and CTOs facing a live buyer block.

## Acute Trigger

A buyer says the procurement, renewal, or pilot cannot proceed until the vendor answers evidence questions around:

- clinical validation and intended use;
- wellness vs medical-device boundary;
- MDR/MDCG software classification exposure;
- AI Act high-risk classification and human oversight;
- GDPR/DPA and health-data handling;
- cybersecurity, uptime, access control, incident response, and subprocessors;
- integration with hospital or enterprise systems;
- safety, claims, and buyer-risk language.

## Control Point

The hard control is not a database or generic template. It is the combination of:

- live buyer questionnaire or evidence request;
- named buyer deadline and blocked commercial process;
- vendor-paid fixed-fee rescue contract;
- temporary custody of the vendor's evidence room under NDA;
- access to product, security, validation, DPA, and integration documents;
- buyer-ready response pack submitted into the actual procurement/pilot workflow;
- case memory of accepted answers, rejected claims, missing-evidence patterns, and reviewer escalations.

No live buyer block means no project.

## 2-Month Proof

Pass only if the founder can show:

1. 30 interviews with EU/CEE biosignal, wearable, EEG, remote-monitoring, or medical-AI vendors that have live or recent buyer evidence blocks.
2. 3 paid live cases at 8,000-18,000 PLN each.
3. At least 25,000 PLN collected or prepaid.
4. One response submitted to a real buyer and either accepted/unblocked or converted into a clear buyer-facing gap/remediation path.
5. One regulatory/quality reviewer, one cyber/security reviewer, and one clinical-methodology reviewer available on paid per-section terms.
6. 70%+ gross margin after reviewer costs.
7. Less than 7 business days from document intake to response pack for the first full case.

## First Acquisition Mechanism

- Build a list of 80 EU/CEE healthtech vendors from public hospital pilots, insurer pilots, pharma/CRO digital-endpoint programs, accelerators, EU digital-health competitions, and remote-monitoring vendor pages.
- Send precise outbound asking only for live or recent blocked buyer questionnaires.
- Build a partner-referral list of boutique medtech regulatory consultants, cyber/DPA advisors, healthtech accelerators, and CRO digital-endpoint teams. They keep formal advisory/remediation work; the desk handles urgent evidence collation and response packaging.
- Publish one redacted sample "buyer-block response pack" using public claims and a synthetic buyer checklist.

## Economics

- First paid pack: 8,000-18,000 PLN.
- Urgent multi-buyer sprint: 18,000-35,000 PLN.
- Annual evidence refresh before next buyer review: 6,000-12,000 PLN.
- Reviewer costs should stay below 20-30% of revenue because reviewers only handle flagged sections.
- Cash conversion is credible because the vendor is protecting a pilot, renewal, investor-backed enterprise sale, insurer rollout, or pharma/CRO contract that can be worth far more than the pack.

## Copy Risk

Copy risk is real. Regulatory firms, privacy lawyers, cyber consultants, and CROs can offer parts of the work.

The differentiated wedge is narrower:

- only live blocked procurement/pilot evidence requests;
- biosignal/EEG/wearable/remote-monitoring specificity;
- 7-business-day response;
- buyer-safe wording rather than broad compliance advice;
- case memory across recurring buyer objections and accepted answer structures;
- referral positioning as overflow/escalation for formal advisors, not a substitute for them.

## Why It Is Not A Generic Report

Reject if:

- no named buyer;
- no blocked procurement, renewal, pilot, or enterprise rollout;
- the vendor only wants general readiness;
- the work becomes certification, legal opinion, clinical validation, or a broad compliance roadmap;
- the output is not submitted to a real buyer.

The business is a paid transaction-unblock workflow, not a dashboard, marketplace, generic diligence memo, app, database, or advisory retainer.

## Simulated Rationale

This clears the simulated gate because it has a concrete 2-month control proof, urgent B2B willingness to pay, low capital requirement, fast cash conversion, and a control point tied to a live blocked transaction rather than general consulting.

The score is not higher because:

- formal buyers may demand legal/regulatory/cyber certification, not a response pack;
- vendors may lack evidence, causing many cases to become "cannot answer";
- CAC may be hard if live blocks are rare or hidden;
- established medtech regulatory/cyber firms can copy the offer;
- health-data and medical-device boundaries require careful liability control.
