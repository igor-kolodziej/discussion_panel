Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

EcoIT Public Tender Evidence Packs

One-sentence thesis:

Polish IT hardware resellers lose time, margin, or bid opportunities when public-sector laptop, desktop, monitor, thin-client, printer, and classroom-device tenders require model-specific environmental, energy, accessibility, warranty, lifecycle, and security evidence; the wedge is a 24-72 hour tender-to-SKU evidence pack tied to one live bid decision.

Exact buyer:

Polish and CEE IT hardware resellers, VARs, office-equipment dealers, education-device suppliers, and small-to-mid distributors that bid into public tenders for laptops, desktops, monitors, thin clients, printers, multifunction devices, and classroom IT equipment but do not have mature product-evidence operations across many vendors.

Acute trigger:

A public-sector tender, framework mini-competition, school/university procurement, hospital/admin office IT purchase, or municipal equipment tender is open and the reseller must prove that proposed SKUs satisfy evidence requirements such as TCO Certified, EPEAT, ENERGY STAR, EU energy-label or energy-use information where applicable, manufacturer warranty and service terms, repairability/spare-parts declarations, lifecycle/support period, TPM/secure boot/OS support, accessibility documentation, recycled-content or packaging evidence, ISO page-yield evidence for printers, or equivalent manufacturer statements.

Control point:

The startup controls the active tender evidence file for one named tender and one reseller catalog or price list: tender requirement extraction, SKU shortlist, official certification/database links, manufacturer evidence links, screenshot archive, model/part-number alias map, evidence-gap table, supplier RFI, substitution/no-bid list, and bid-ready response wording. It does not control the tender authority, manufacturer certification, reseller pricing, or bid submission.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Within 60 days, three resellers prepay for at least one live tender pack each; each case includes tender URL/deadline, reseller SKU list or target vendors, and written permission to build the evidence file. At least two packs produce a documented bid/no-bid, substitution, missing-evidence correction, manufacturer RFI, or buyer-question response. One reseller buys a second pack or a monthly tender-monitoring add-on.

6-month POC:

Deliver 25-60 paid packs across 8-15 resellers; normalize 5,000-20,000 hardware SKUs or model aliases; document 15+ bid decisions, substitutions, buyer-answer responses, or clean no-bids; get 3 resellers to buy at least 4 packs or a recurring monitor; keep turnaround under 72 hours for evidence-ready tenders; keep gross margin above 65%; build a private Polish IT tender evidence graph of tender phrases, certification sources, manufacturer evidence pages, model aliases, and accepted answer formats.

First acquisition mechanism:

Monitor e-Zamowienia, BZP, TED, and Polish public procurement notices for live IT hardware tenders. Build a list of 80 Polish IT resellers and office-equipment dealers with public catalogs or known public-sector activity. Send evidence-led outbound using one live tender and one concrete model/evidence observation, for example: "This school laptop tender asks for TCO/EPEAT/ENERGY STAR and three-year on-site service proof. Your catalog has plausible models, but the part-number/certificate mapping is not bid-ready. I can return a 48-hour SKU evidence pack for this tender."

Economics:

Charge 1,500-3,000 PLN for a small single-lot pack, 3,000-6,000 PLN for multi-lot or multi-vendor tenders, and 800-3,000 PLN per month for monitoring only tenders that match the reseller catalog with evidence gaps already identified. First packs take 4-10 founder hours plus optional 300-1,000 PLN reviewer cost for procurement or IT-hardware edge cases. Repeat packs become faster after catalog normalization. The fee is small relative to a public IT bid, avoided no-bid, avoided rejected offer, or better substitution decision.

Copy risk:

Large IT distributors, vendor bid desks, tender consultants, procurement tools, and the reseller's own tender staff can copy parts. The defense is local Polish tender-language memory, reseller-specific catalog normalization, model/part-number alias history, official certificate lookup patterns, accepted buyer answer formats, and speed on live tenders where the reseller cannot wait for several vendor portals and email chains.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once a reseller prepays and sends the tender, SKU list, vendor priorities, and deadline, the startup controls that tender's evidence matrix, screenshot archive, certification/source links, RFI trail, substitution list, and response wording under a short deadline. A manufacturer or generic tender consultant can provide pieces, but they do not already own the reseller-specific cross-vendor map and tender-specific missing-evidence file for that bid.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The unit is a named public tender and reseller SKU list with a bid-ready evidence pack: requirement extraction, SKU eligibility matrix, official certification links, screenshot archive, missing-evidence log, manufacturer RFI, substitution/no-bid list, and response wording. It is not generic IT bid consulting, tender alerts, certification consulting, procurement representation, software, dashboard, database sale, marketplace, or vendor introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

The startup does not submit bids, provide legal procurement advice, certify products, alter manufacturer evidence, guarantee tender acceptance, make environmental claims beyond source evidence, or represent a manufacturer. Resellers remain responsible for pricing, availability, bid submission, formal declarations, product selection, and buyer communications. Cases requiring legal protest strategy, custom certification, false equivalence claims, or unsupported environmental claims are rejected or routed to qualified advisers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

This is adjacent to GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs because both use public tenders and product evidence. It is materially different in buyer, product category, evidence surface, and graph: IT hardware resellers and public laptops/desktops/monitors/printers using TCO Certified, EPEAT, ENERGY STAR, manufacturer warranty/service, OS/security, accessibility, page-yield, and lifecycle evidence, not lighting distributors using EPREL and lighting-spec evidence for LED and street-lighting tenders. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, Supplement Stack Safety, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, or any health/Rx route.

Strongest anticipated objections:

Many IT resellers already have tender departments and manufacturer bid-support portals. Vendor databases may make simple evidence retrieval cheap. Large distributors and manufacturers may provide ready-made declarations. Some public tenders are effectively written around known models, so the pack may not change the bid decision. The idea may be too close to GreenTender unless IT hardware evidence complexity and reseller willingness to pay are proven. Pricing may be hard for small dealers if a pack only saves admin time rather than unlocking a real bid. Broad IT hardware scope could become generic bid support unless the first niche stays limited to public education and office-device tenders with repeated certification and model-alias evidence gaps.
