Date: 2026-05-29
Real browser gate: working Zero To One >=85, fresh Zero To One >=85

## Search Frame

Recent AI rounds failed because they were too close to legal AI Act compliance, model-risk validation, or product redesign. This round tests a narrower commercial blocker: small B2B SaaS vendors with ordinary embedded AI features are receiving AI sections inside enterprise security/procurement questionnaires, and deals stall because the vendor has no evidence room for model inventory, data flows, retention, training/no-training claims, evals, human review, and AI policy enforcement.

The target is a deal-release evidence pack, not AI Act legal compliance, ISO 42001 implementation, model validation, DPIA, or voice-agent transparency.

## Lead Candidate

**AIGate Procurement Evidence Pack**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The trigger is a live customer deal or renewal, not vague AI Act readiness.
- The buyer already has a specific questionnaire and deadline; the founder controls the response packet for that active deal.
- It avoids high-risk AI, regulated model validation, clinical/HR/credit/biometric decisions, Article 50 voice-agent deployment, legal compliance opinions, and ISO implementation.
- The artifact is a concrete procurement evidence room and answer set: AI feature inventory, data-flow map, model/subprocessor table, no-training evidence, retention/logging evidence, eval/monitoring summary, human-review points, access controls, incident path, and exact buyer response wording.
- The first acquisition route can use SaaS founders, SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, privacy consultants, and AI implementation agencies that are now seeing AI sections in security questionnaires.

### Internal Objections

- Vanta, Drata, SOC2 consultants, AI governance firms, privacy lawyers, and cyber firms can copy pieces.
- Some buyers will ask for legal AI Act classification, DPIA, high-risk assessment, contract clauses, or product changes outside scope.
- Sensitive evidence access creates trust friction.
- The idea only works for live revenue blockers; generic readiness packs should be rejected.
- The score depends on proving buyer-progress outcomes, not producing nice documents.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the scope is materially narrower than prior failed AI model-risk and Article 50 variants.
