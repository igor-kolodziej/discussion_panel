## Internal Strict Simulation

### Why It Clears The Simulation Gate

- **Concrete 6-month control proof:** one signed seller option, source custody, machine map, customer transfer approvals, first support cash, and one resolved compatibility/update ticket.
- **Current payment stream:** customers prepay continuity before seller payout; the startup does not buy goodwill without current cash.
- **Non-generic asset:** exact posts and machine maps cannot be copied by a new consultant without customer access, source files, and machine-specific history.
- **Urgent trigger:** CAM updates, controller swaps, machine moves, Windows/CAM upgrades, urgent repeat-job programs, and retiring-maintainer loss can block production quickly.
- **Founder fit:** data/software diligence, contractor coordination, structured intake, repository hygiene, support-history analysis, and fixed-scope maintenance suit a Warsaw solo founder.
- **Margin:** annual continuity and fixed update tickets can keep >60% contribution margin if bespoke CAM programming, machine crashes, field commissioning, and safety-critical claims are excluded.

### Internal Objections

1. The right seller is rare: active paid customers, clean source rights, reusable support, seller trust, and willingness to accept earnout must coincide.
2. The founder must attach a senior CAM/post reviewer before close; otherwise diligence is fake.
3. Customers may fear the handoff and ask machine OEMs, CAM resellers, or local CNC programmers instead.
4. Support can become open-ended CNC programming, toolpath debugging, machine parameter work, post rewrites, field commissioning, or crash liability.
5. Source ownership may be informal across old employers, machine dealers, CAM resellers, freelancers, and customers.
6. Machine-specific work can create production damage risk if output is not constrained and customer-owned testing remains mandatory.
7. One acquired book is a proof point, not yet a scalable acquisition machine.

This clears the simulated gate because the first proof is controlled payment transfer plus source custody, not interest or documentation. The main risk is seller/customer scarcity, but the transaction sequence is strong enough to justify real validation.
