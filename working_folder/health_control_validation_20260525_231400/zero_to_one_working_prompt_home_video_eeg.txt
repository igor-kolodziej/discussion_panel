Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Please cap the score below 85 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, uniquely produced, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality.

Scored idea:

Home Pediatric Sleep/Video-EEG Micro-Lab Acquisition is a micro-operator acquisition business in Warsaw/Mazovia neurodiagnostics. It does not try to build a new EEG clinic from scratch or sell a generic EEG booking marketplace. It options or acquires a tired small home/sleep/video EEG operator's demand surface and continuity assets, then standardizes scheduling, technician dispatch, equipment custody, and referral-doctor handoff while licensed clinicians/technicians remain responsible for medical work.

Target seller:
- a small private EEG/home EEG/video EEG operator, pediatric neurodiagnostic technician-owner, or small clinic sub-brand with low digital maturity;
- owner wants exit, reduced admin, or fewer home visits;
- has a working phone number, website/domain or Google profile, recurring referrals, active patient bookings/waitlist, EEG/VEEG device, caps/electrodes/consumables, technician know-how, and reading neurologist relationship.

Buyer / payer:
- parents of children who need sleep EEG, home EEG, or video EEG because the child cannot cooperate in a standard clinic setting, has suspected epilepsy/events, or needs a specialist decision;
- pediatric neurologists, psychiatrists, developmental clinics, and private EEG clinics that need overflow home/sleep EEG capacity;
- payer is mostly private parents or private clinics/referral sources, not NFZ.

Hidden insight:
Ordinary in-clinic EEG is a commodity test. The scarce asset is the owner-endorsed local queue for hard pediatric/home/sleep EEG cases: phone, Google profile, referral doctors, active bookings, technician trust, device/cap compatibility, reading neurologist relationship, and dispatch routine. Competitors can buy EEG equipment, but they cannot copy the exact acquired referral queue and owner handoff in 60 days.

Control point:
The founder signs a conditional option or acquisition agreement over:
- phone number, domain/website, Google Business profile and local listings where transferable;
- active booking queue/waitlist and referral-source introductions;
- patient/parent re-consent for data/contact transfer and new service agreements;
- title or escrowed transfer of at least one EEG/VEEG kit, caps, consumables, camera/accessory set, and dispatch kit;
- technician handoff/training and, if possible, continued part-time technician contract;
- reading neurologist / interpreting physician relationship;
- standard pricing, scheduling, cancellation, report-delivery, and medical-record custody workflow.

The founder does not diagnose, interpret EEGs, practice medicine, or advertise medical claims. The startup owns scheduling, acquisition, logistics, patient/parent consent workflow, equipment custody, billing, and admin evidence. Licensed/qualified medical professionals remain responsible for test performance and interpretation.

2-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 40,000-70,000 PLN option/acquisition budget for one small operator's phone/domain/book/equipment/handoff, with part of price as earnout after bookings transfer;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN for legal/GDPR/patient-transfer documents, service terms, medical-record/process boundaries, and insurance review;
- 10,000-20,000 PLN for equipment safety check, caps/electrodes/consumables, repairs, software/export setup, and dispatch case;
- 5,000-10,000 PLN for scheduling/billing workflow and targeted referral-source outreach;
- reserve 5,000-10,000 PLN for technician/neurologist retainer deposits.

Required POC proof:
1. One signed conditional option/acquisition agreement with a named micro-lab/operator covering phone/domain/listing, referral-source handoff, active bookings, equipment, and owner transition support.
2. Title transfer or escrowed access to at least one usable EEG/VEEG kit plus caps/consumables after technical/safety check.
3. At least 20-30 active booked or rebookable patients/waitlist entries verified, with patient/parent re-consent or clean transfer notices where required.
4. At least 2 referral sources retained or introduced by the seller.
5. At least 10-15 paid home/sleep/video EEG tests completed or prepaid through the acquired channel in 60 days.
6. Written role-boundary memo: founder/startup handles logistics/admin/equipment/payment; qualified technicians/clinicians handle medical testing/interpretation.

Economics:
The purchase only closes if existing bookings and referral flow support payback. In-clinic EEG is lower-priced and less attractive; home EEG, sleep EEG, pediatric cooperation cases, and video/event EEG are higher-friction and can command higher private prices. The target is a small book doing 20-30 tests/month, where acquisition-led CAC is near-zero and owner earnings come from better scheduling, dispatch utilization, outsourced interpretation, and reducing owner admin.

Initial pricing logic:
- do not compete on cheap commodity in-clinic EEG;
- focus on home/sleep/video pediatric cases where parents pay for convenience/speed and referral doctors need reliable execution;
- charge private-pay package prices high enough to cover technician dispatch, equipment amortization, interpretation, admin, and cancellation risk.

Why this is not a generic clinic/service/marketplace:
The foundational asset is a signed option over a live micro-operator demand surface: phone, local search listing, referral relationships, active queue, equipment, technician workflow, and reading-doctor relationship. The founder is not buying "ability to do EEG"; the founder is buying an owner-endorsed queue and operational custody over a narrow neurodiagnostic workflow.

Main risks acknowledged:
- If the founder only buys equipment, the score should drop sharply.
- Medical trust and pediatric parent trust are sensitive; seller handoff is mandatory.
- Licensed professionals own the medical work; the startup must not become an unlicensed medical provider.
- Pediatric data and medical records require careful GDPR/medical-record handling.
- A one-operator book may be too small or owner-dependent.
- Referral sources may not transfer despite seller endorsement.
- EEG equipment and accessories must be safe, legal, compatible, and serviceable.
- Larger clinics could copy home EEG, but cannot copy an acquired phone/referral/book packet instantly.
