Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: HazmatBlocked FBA Evidence Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A white-label evidence-response desk for Amazon FBA agencies and sellers whose ASINs, inbound shipments, or existing inventory are blocked by dangerous-goods/hazmat review, producing upload-ready SDS, exemption, and battery-document packets within 24-48 hours.

Exact buyer:
Amazon FBA operations agencies, account-health agencies, reimbursement/stranded-inventory agencies, and 7-8 figure FBA sellers of non-drug consumer products with hazmat-sensitive categories: cosmetics, cleaning products, art/craft supplies, liquids, gels, aerosols, batteries, power banks, electronics accessories, toys with batteries, and chemical-containing kits.

Acute trigger:
Amazon flags an ASIN or inventory for dangerous-goods review, blocks inbound shipment creation, strands inventory, restricts FBA sale/removal, or requests a Safety Data Sheet, dangerous-goods exemption sheet, battery exemption sheet, lithium-battery test summary, or related supplier/manufacturer document matching. Seller revenue or inventory movement is blocked now.

Control point:
Priority agency routing plus prepaid live cases. The desk controls current Seller Central request text/screenshots, ASIN/category facts, supplier/manufacturer documents, product images/labels, evidence packet assembly, reviewer boundary notes, response wording, and case-memory about what Amazon accepts, rejects, or escalates.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Two agency routing agreements with 25-35% agency margin or referral fee.
2. Five prepaid live hazmat/dangerous-goods review cases from real Seller Central requests.
3. Case intake authorizations and document-handling terms.
4. A dangerous-goods/SDS reviewer or consultant on paid per-case terms.
5. At least one submitted response packet and one accepted/cleared, escalated, or rejected-with-reasons outcome.
6. A case log of request wording, document type, supplier-document mismatch, submission path, and outcome.

6-month POC:
Month 1: recruit one dangerous-goods/SDS reviewer, create intake and evidence-boundary templates, build a 60-agency target list, and produce one anonymized sample dossier from public or historical request patterns.
Month 2: sign two agency routing agreements and complete the first two prepaid live cases.
Month 3: complete five prepaid live cases and record at least one concrete Amazon outcome: cleared classification, request for corrected document, escalation to manufacturer/lab, or documented rejection reason.
Months 4-6: complete 15-25 paid cases through 3-5 agency sources, keep delivery under 48 hours for triage and 72 hours for full packets, maintain gross margin above 60% after reviewer and agency fees, and build a private case-memory library by category, document type, mismatch, submission path, and outcome.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target Amazon agencies and FBA operations consultants that already handle stranded inventory, shipment creation errors, Account Health, and Seller Central case escalation. Ask for one live or recent hazmat review where a seller has SDS, exemption, battery, label, or supplier documents but the ASIN remains blocked or the seller does not know what to submit. The agency keeps the client relationship and margin; the desk handles the specialist evidence packet.

Economics:
Initial pricing can be 1,500-3,000 PLN for triage and 4,000-8,000 PLN for a full response dossier, with 25-35% kept by the agency. Reviewer cost is scoped per case. Gross margin is credible because the seller is protecting blocked FBA inventory/revenue and the work is document triage, not lab testing, transport, warehousing, or legal representation. Multi-ASIN seller sprints can price higher if documents repeat across a product family.

Copy risk:
Amazon agencies, dangerous-goods consultants, SDS writers, FBA tools, and seller operations consultants can do pieces. The defense is not generic Amazon knowledge. The defense is agency-routed live case flow, fast response, narrow Amazon dangerous-goods evidence memory, and repeated accepted/rejected pattern knowledge by product, document type, mismatch, and submission path.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific case flow:
Once the agency sends current prepaid cases and the desk has the Seller Central request, ASIN facts, supplier documents, and seller permission, a competitor cannot copy those live cases. Broader competitors can chase future cases, but they cannot recreate the current case history, agency routing, and outcome memory without doing the work.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is a live evidence-response desk for current blocked ASINs, shipments, or inventory. It does not sell a generic software dashboard, general Amazon consulting, or a directory of SDS providers. The output is a submission-ready packet for a specific Seller Central request plus clear submit/cannot-submit/escalate boundaries.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The desk does not create fake SDS, alter manufacturer documents, certify dangerous-goods classification, provide legal advice, operate as a carrier/warehouse, transport dangerous goods, test products, provide lab reports, or guarantee Amazon approval. Manufacturers, suppliers, labs, and qualified dangerous-goods/SDS professionals remain responsible for source documents and formal classifications. The desk may organize seller-supplied evidence, spot mismatches, request corrected documents, draft Seller Central response wording, and route lab/manufacturer-required cases out of scope.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, BioSignal, or TraceFaktura. It is closest in channel to REDBlocked because both use Amazon agency-routed urgent evidence cases, but the control event is materially different. REDBlocked handles connected-device RED/CE/GPSR listing compliance for baby monitors and indoor cameras. HazmatBlocked handles FBA dangerous-goods/hazmat classification, SDS/exemption/battery documentation, inbound shipment blocks, stranded inventory, and FBA inventory movement. The case artifacts, reviewer expertise, buyer trigger, and outcome memory are different.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Agencies may already solve simple SDS or exemption cases themselves.
Amazon outcomes are opaque and cannot be guaranteed.
Some cases require real manufacturer SDS correction, lab data, UL/lithium documents, or product redesign outside founder control.
The founder must not create fake SDS, classify dangerous goods as a legal certifier, or promise transport compliance.
Case volume may be episodic unless agencies aggregate demand.
Seller willingness to pay may be lower if the inventory value is small.
If the work becomes generic Amazon support rather than dangerous-goods evidence response, differentiation disappears.
