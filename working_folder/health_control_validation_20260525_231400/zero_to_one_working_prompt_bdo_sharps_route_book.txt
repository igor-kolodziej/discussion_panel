Scored idea:

BDO Sharps/Medical-Waste Route-Book Buyout acquires or options a small environmental-compliance or clinic-admin operator's BDO/KPO medical-waste admin route serving private injectable-procedure clinics, diabetes/obesity clinics, aesthetic clinics, vaccination points, dental offices, labs, and small treatment rooms.

The founder does not transport, collect, store, neutralize, or physically handle medical waste unless separately licensed. A licensed medical-waste collector/transporter/disposal operator remains responsible for pickup, transport, confirmation, and disposal. The startup controls clinic-facing admin, container placement, pickup scheduling, KPO/BDO checklisting, QR/container logs, exception handling, route book, and collector coordination.

This is GLP-1/insulin/injection-adjacent without selling, prescribing, advertising, dispensing, or handling prescription drugs.

Buyer:
- private injectable-procedure clinics;
- diabetes/obesity/endocrinology clinics generating sharps and medical waste;
- aesthetic medicine clinics;
- vaccination points;
- dental offices;
- small labs and outpatient treatment rooms;
- clinic managers/practice owners who are responsible for BDO/KPO medical-waste documentation but do not have a strong back-office.

Acute trigger:
The clinic generates sharps and medical waste and must keep BDO/KPO documentation, classify waste codes correctly, schedule pickups, avoid overfilled/expired containers, reconcile mass discrepancies, and respond when a licensed collector rejects or modifies KPO data. Injection volume from GLP-1/insulin/aesthetic/vaccination clinics increases the nuisance, but the service never touches medicines.

Control point:
The acquired/optioned asset is the route/admin book:
- named clinic waste-owner contacts and BDO responsible-person workflow;
- signed container/pickup/admin agreements;
- container QR/label map by room/site;
- waste code checklist and mass/pickup cadence history;
- KPO/BDO precheck and exception log;
- pickup calendar and collector confirmation workflow;
- licensed collector cooperation terms and route windows;
- phone/domain/mailbox/WhatsApp continuity;
- prepaid clinic admin fees or monthly retainers.

The hard control point is the clinic-side BDO/KPO route book plus recurring pickup/admin payment flow. The licensed collector owns physical waste transport/disposal; the startup owns the admin/call-routing/documentation layer and recurring clinic account.

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 5,000-15,000 PLN option/revenue-share handoff over a small environmental/clinic-admin route book;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN legal/environmental compliance review and role separation memo;
- 5,000-20,000 PLN compliant container purchase/placement float where allowed, labels/QRs, scales if appropriate, cabinet/signage inventory;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN BDO/KPO SOP, checklist, CRM, phone/mailbox, and admin setup;
- 5,000-20,000 PLN licensed collector coordination deposit or route-retainer;
- remaining cash reserved for refunds, replacements, audits, and insurance.

Required proof:
1. Signed route/book option or revenue-share handoff with at least 5 active small medical-waste-generating clinic accounts.
2. Written cooperation agreement with one licensed collector/transporter/disposal operator covering pickup windows, KPO confirmation workflow, and non-transport role separation.
3. At least 10 clinic contracts, including 3 paid pilots within 14 days.
4. At least 15,000 PLN monthly recurring admin/pickup-coordination fees contracted or 25,000 PLN prepaid/collected over 60 days.
5. 10-20 QR/container placements or verified existing containers mapped to rooms/sites.
6. One completed pickup cycle with KPO/BDO entries reconciled and collector confirmation/exception workflow documented.
7. Zero startup physical handling or transport of medical waste unless licensed.
8. Kill if clinics say their collector already handles BDO/KPO admin well enough for no incremental fee.

Economics:
- 400-900 PLN per clinic/month for BDO/KPO admin route, container monitoring, pickup coordination, exception handling, and documentation readiness;
- 75-150 PLN per pickup admin fee where monthly retainer is not used;
- margin on compliant container/label/signage placement where lawful;
- seller earnout only from retained gross margin.

Gross-margin logic:
The startup is not buying trucks or incinerators. Variable cost is admin time, clinic calls, container/label costs, CRM/BDO checklisting, and collector coordination. Gross margin can exceed 50% if collector fees are passed through separately or billed directly by the licensed collector. Payback is driven by recurring clinic retainers and low working capital.

Why this is not a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace:
The startup acquires a route book and recurring BDO/KPO admin payment flow. It is not selling a dashboard, report, coaching, or generic compliance advice. The POC only passes if clinics pay, containers/routes are mapped, and one real pickup cycle is reconciled.

Copy risk:
Collectors can offer admin service, and environmental consultants can manage BDO. The wedge is clinic-specific route density, container QR map, phone/admin continuity, small-clinic workflow, and a licensed collector partnership where collectors prefer the startup as clinic-facing admin rather than doing small-account handholding themselves.

Regulatory and legal guardrails:
- Startup does not physically collect, transport, store, neutralize, or handle medical waste unless separately licensed.
- Licensed collector/transporter/disposal operator handles regulated physical execution.
- Clinic/generator legal responsibility for BDO/KPO cannot be falsely transferred.
- Public wording avoids prescription-drug names and medicine claims.
- No controlled-substance destruction handling in the first POC.
- Legal review must confirm role separation, container placement limits, BDO authority/delegation, insurance, and liability caps.

Main risks acknowledged:
- If licensed collectors already include BDO/KPO admin for free, willingness to pay collapses.
- Environmental consultants and collectors can copy the service.
- Legal role separation is mandatory so the founder does not become an unlicensed waste transporter/collector.
- Clinic waste volumes may be too low for high retainers.
- BDO responsibility may remain with the generator/clinic, so the service cannot promise full liability transfer.
- If the acquired route book has only contacts and no paid pickup/admin cadence, it becomes cold B2B compliance sales.
