## Zero To One Summary

Zero To One verified that the urgent CBAM import-release premise is real. It cited the 2026 definitive regime and Polish guidance that importers above the 50-tonne annual mass threshold who missed the 31 March 2026 application path cannot import CBAM goods until authorised status is obtained. It still scored the idea 82 because the wedge is close to the confirmed CBAM Import Continuity Desk, brokers already own the transaction, and customs/legal boundaries are dangerous.

## Strongest Objections

1. The idea may be too close to the confirmed CBAM Import Continuity Desk. The distinction is real but narrow.
2. Customs brokers and indirect customs representatives already control the declaration, TARIC/document codes, customs system response, and release path.
3. Some cases are hard no-go, not rescue, especially where an above-threshold importer missed the application path.
4. Legal/customs-representation boundaries are dangerous around document-code decisions, importer role, indirect representation, threshold interpretation, sanctions, false codes, and customs disputes.
5. Portal and authority timing is outside control; authorisation can take a long time.
6. Threshold proof can be messy across declarations, CN/TARIC lines, importers, and representation structures.
7. Many blockers will actually be supplier-emissions, default-value, certificate strategy, or annual-declaration problems, which this desk must reject.
8. The 2026 spike may fade after brokers and importers standardize status checks, threshold ledgers, and document-code handling.
9. Unknown-founder trust is a problem with customs documents, import values, supplier identities, PUESC/CBAM portal status, broker drafts, and delivery deadlines.
10. Success proof can be ambiguous because release progress may be caused by the broker, authority, warehouse, importer, or timing.

## Evaluator Advice

The best version is broker-led intake for named shipment, customs warehouse release, import lane, customer order, or declaration blockers. Accept only cases with written broker/customs/warehouse blocker, CBAM-covered CN/TARIC lines, invoice and packing list, prior declarations, mass-threshold ledger, PUESC/CBAM portal or application/status evidence, broker declaration draft or code issue, importer/broker mandate, and prepaid fixed fee. Reject generic readiness, emissions data, annual filing, legal/customs representation, sanctions, false-code issues, emissions verification, importer-of-record disputes, and cases without a named import event.

## Lesson

Even a strong current CBAM gate does not clear when the idea sits too close to an existing confirmed wedge and when brokers/customs representatives can own the transaction. Avoid further CBAM variants unless the control point is plainly outside the broker/import-continuity workflow.
