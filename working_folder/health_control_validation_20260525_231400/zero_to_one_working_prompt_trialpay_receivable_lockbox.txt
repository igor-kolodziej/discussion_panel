Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Please cap the score below 85 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, assigned, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Cap below 87 if CAC, payback, gross margin, cash conversion, or owner earnings are not credible. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality. Penalize generic clinics, apps, reports, dashboards, marketplaces, coaching, patient-data businesses, and prescription-drug businesses without asset/payment-flow control.

Scored idea:

TrialPay Site Receivable Lockbox Book Buyout acquires or options the customer book and open receivable workflow of a small clinical-trial finance/admin specialist serving Polish private research sites.

It is not a clinic, not a patient-recruitment agency, not a prescribing platform, not a pharmacy, not a telemedicine service, not a medical-data marketplace, and not a generic factoring website. It does not sell, advertise, prescribe, reserve, distribute, or route medicines. Licensed clinical-trial sites, investigators, sponsors, CROs, clinics, and pharmacies remain responsible for clinical care, investigational products, pharmacy handling, and patient work. The startup only controls B2B payment recovery/admin around already-earned clinical-trial receivables.

Therapeutic focus:
- GLP-1/obesity and cardiometabolic trials;
- addiction/smoking/alcohol-use/psychiatry trials;
- neurology, sleep, EEG, and biosignal-heavy studies;
- genetics/biomarker/sample-heavy trials.

The therapeutic focus is used only to specialize payment reconciliation. The founder never markets a medicine, claims clinical benefit, recruits patients, or handles prescribing.

Buyer:
- independent Polish clinical-trial sites;
- private clinics running sponsored studies;
- small site-management organizations;
- PIs/sites with delayed sponsor/CRO payments, unpaid pass-through costs, query-dependent milestones, and weak finance operations.

Acute trigger:
Clinical-trial sites can wait months for sponsor/CRO payment and must reconcile visit milestones, EDC/eCRF status, invoice portals, pass-through costs, screen-fail fees, startup fees, closeout fees, patient reimbursements, and query holds. Delayed or underpaid study receivables strain site cash and distract coordinators from enrollment and operations.

Control point:
The acquired/optioned asset is a payment-control and receivable-recovery book:
- signed client book from a retiring or overloaded clinical-trial accountant/admin specialist;
- open invoice ledger and aging schedule;
- CTA/budget/payment-term extracts;
- sponsor/CRO AP contacts and payment-portal workflows;
- source-document checklist for billable milestones, without collecting unnecessary patient data;
- claim evidence pack for completed visits, startup/closeout fees, screen fails, unscheduled visits, and pass-through costs;
- assignment of selected undisputed receivables or debtor-notified collection mandate;
- lockbox/payment-instruction letter where debtor accepts or acknowledges the assignment/mandate;
- success-fee and optional micro-advance economics;
- seller handoff to sites and AP contacts.

The hard control is a signed receivable assignment/collection mandate plus debtor/CRO/AP acknowledgement or written payment-status confirmation and first collected cash.

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 5,000-15,000 PLN refundable/creditable option over a retiring or overloaded specialist's site-payment admin book;
- 10,000-20,000 PLN legal review of assignment, anti-assignment clauses, CTA/budget terms, data-minimization, tax/VAT, and collection wording;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN accounting/clinical-trial admin contractor for ledger cleanup and evidence packs;
- 0-50,000 PLN optional micro-advance only against one debtor-acknowledged receivable; initial wedge can be success-fee only to conserve capital;
- small CRM, secure file, and bank/lockbox setup.

Required POC proof:
1. Signed conditional option or revenue-share acquisition over one specialist's clinical-trial payment admin book with at least 3 active site clients.
2. At least 150,000 PLN in open, undisputed, documented site receivables identified across sponsor/CRO debtors.
3. Signed collection mandate or assignment over at least 50,000 PLN of receivables.
4. Debtor/AP/CRO acknowledgement or written payment-status confirmation for at least one assigned/mandated receivable.
5. First collected cash of at least 25,000 PLN within 60 days.
6. No clinical decision-making, no patient recruitment, no medicine sale, no Rx advertising, no patient-data monetization.
7. Only minimum redacted visit/milestone evidence is handled; patient identifiers are excluded unless legally necessary and covered by site-controlled processing terms.

Economics:
- 8-15% success fee on recovered aged/underpaid amounts;
- 1-4% payment-admin fee on current study-payment flows;
- optional 2-6% discount/micro-advance spread where assignment is clean and debtor has acknowledged;
- recurring monthly fee for payment ledger, invoice package, query-to-cash, and AP chase workflow.

Payback logic:
First cash can come from an already-earned receivable rather than new customer demand. Capital is mostly legal/admin unless optional advances are used. Gross margin can exceed 60% on success-fee recovery after contractor/legal allocation if claims are undisputed and evidence is strong. Founder avoids financing broad receivable portfolios until debtor acknowledgement and collection proof exist.

Why this is not a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace:
The founder is not selling generic receivables software or consulting. The asset is an acquired/optioned specialist book plus assigned/mandated receivables and debtor-notified payment flow. The POC only passes if cash is collected from a real sponsor/CRO debtor.

Copy risk:
Generic factoring companies can finance invoices, but usually do not parse clinical trial CTAs, EDC-triggered milestones, screen-fail rules, pass-throughs, query holds, closeout fees, and sponsor/CRO payment portals. CRO payment platforms serve sponsors and CROs, not necessarily small sites chasing unpaid amounts. The founder's defense is narrow specialization plus assigned receivables and AP acknowledgements, not software novelty.

Regulatory and legal guardrails:
- Do not sell, advertise, prescribe, reserve, distribute, or route prescription medicines or investigational products.
- Do not recruit patients or provide clinical services.
- Do not handle unnecessary patient data; use redacted milestone/payment evidence where possible.
- Legal review must reject receivables with anti-assignment blocks, disputed claims, patient-reimbursement custody issues, unclear VAT/tax treatment, or collection/licensing issues.
- Do not advance capital unless assignment is clean and debtor/AP acknowledgement exists.

Main risks acknowledged:
- CTAs may prohibit assignment or require sponsor/CRO consent.
- Claims may be disputed, query-held, or poorly documented.
- Some sponsors/CROs may ignore assignments or delay payment anyway.
- Under 100,000 PLN, the founder should start with success-fee recovery and only selectively micro-advance.
- Generic factoring firms, law firms, SMOs, CRO payment vendors, and site accountants can compete.
- Handling clinical-trial documentation creates confidentiality and data-protection risk even when patient identifiers are minimized.
- If there is no real admin specialist book to option, no debtor acknowledgement, or no first collected cash, the idea fails.
