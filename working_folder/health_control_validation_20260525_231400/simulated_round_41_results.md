## Gate Decision

No Round 41 candidate clears simulated `>87`. No real Zero To One validation is allowed for these wordings.

## Fatal Objections Extracted

- Health/pharma software workflows are useful but usually controlled by clinics, pharmacies, EMRs, PSPs, or existing compliance vendors.
- Direct GLP-1/addictive-drug commerce is not acceptable without licensed sellers/providers and creates Rx advertising and medicine-sale risk.
- Data-rights ideas keep failing when legal access, buyer prepayment, and trust are conditional rather than owned.
- 50-60 / pre-elderly ideas score only when tied to a forced transaction; otherwise they become planning, care navigation, or professional services.
- Medical-device inventory ideas need sealed/traceable status, MDR distributor discipline, clinician trust, and enough repeated demand; prior dental/neurodevice versions did not prove that.

## Round 42 Pivot

Keep the user-requested territories, but change the business shape:

- From clinic/pharmacy workflow to buyer-controlled payment, escrow, or reimbursement hold.
- From raw health data to owned operational archive/export rights where the buyer is the clinic itself, not a medtech data buyer.
- From generic 50-60 planning to legal/financial release events for people aged 50-60.
- From GLP-1 products to illicit regulated-product payment/claims/brand-protection rights where the startup can own a case mandate or assigned recovery fee.
