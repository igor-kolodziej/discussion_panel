Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: VoP Bulk Payment Exception Release Desk.

One-sentence thesis:
Build a signed exception-ticket desk for payment bureaus, ERP/AP integrators, treasury consultants, and PSPs whose corporate clients now face Verification of Payee / beneficiary-name mismatch warnings in bulk payment flows, controlling prepaid current exception batches, customer authorization, supplier confirmation packets, master-data correction evidence, and repeat partner routing without ever moving funds.

Exact buyer:
Payment service bureaus, ERP/accounting/payment-file integrators, AP automation vendors, treasury consultants, payroll bureaus, and PSP implementation teams serving corporate customers that process supplier, payroll, tax, rent, fleet, loan, or other bulk payment files and are hit by payee-name/IBAN verification exceptions, no-match warnings, close-match warnings, blocked approvals, or customer-support surges.

Acute trigger:
Verification-of-payee regimes and instant-payment obligations make beneficiary-name accuracy operationally visible. When bulk payment files produce no-match or close-match warnings, corporate AP/treasury teams may pause vendor payments, payroll, refunds, rent, or urgent supplier runs. The payment bureau or ERP partner gets blamed even if the root cause is dirty beneficiary master data, outdated supplier legal names, inconsistent trading names, wrong account holders, or missing confirmation evidence.

Transferable control point:
- Signed priority ticket-routing agreement with payment bureaus, ERP/AP partners, treasury consultants, or PSP implementation teams that already receive current beneficiary-verification exceptions.
- Customer authorization for a named current exception batch, including permitted access to the exception list, beneficiary master-data fields, supplier contacts, evidence documents, and correction workflow.
- Supplier confirmation packet for each exception: legal name, trading name, IBAN/account holder evidence where lawfully obtainable, invoice match, authorized contact, callback log, and customer approval record.
- Master-data correction log: before/after beneficiary fields, evidence source, approval, timestamp, partner/customer sign-off, and unresolved/no-go reason.
- Prepaid current exception batches before work begins.
- Outcome memory by bank/PSP/country/supplier type: which mismatches are fixable, which names require supplier-side correction, which cases should remain blocked, and what evidence reduces repeated warnings.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 2-3 partner routing agreements with payment bureaus, ERP/AP implementers, payroll bureaus, treasury consultants, or PSP implementation teams.
- 10-25 customer-authorized current exception batches, each tied to an actual upcoming or paused payment run.
- Prepaid batch fees before processing.
- Data-processing/access terms limiting the founder to evidence collection, supplier confirmation, master-data correction workflow, and exception documentation.
- First 5-10 completed batches with documented resolved/no-go outcomes and partner sign-off.

6-month proof:
1. Interview 30 payment bureaus, ERP/AP integrators, treasury consultants, payroll bureaus, and PSP implementation teams exposed to beneficiary-name verification exceptions.
2. Sign 2-3 priority-routing agreements with partners that already handle current corporate payment exception queues.
3. Accept 20-50 prepaid current exception batches from corporate customers through those partners.
4. Collect 60,000-180,000 PLN in prepaid batch fees and urgent-run surcharges.
5. Resolve or document no-go outcomes for at least 500 beneficiary exceptions across supplier, payroll, refund, rent, and vendor payment files.
6. Prove at least 70% of accepted exceptions are closed with customer-approved master-data updates, supplier-side corrections, or documented hold/no-go decisions.
7. Prove gross margin above 60% after contractor verification calls, data handling, partner share, tooling, legal/data terms, and QA.
8. Show repeatability: at least one partner sends 5+ batches without cold outreach.

First acquisition mechanism:
Partner pitch: "Your corporate clients blame your payment file, ERP setup, or payment bureau when verification-of-payee warnings slow payment runs. We take the exception queue under your brand or as specialist desk: supplier confirmation, evidence packet, master-data correction log, and documented no-go decisions. You keep the client and payment relationship; we clear current exception batches for a prepaid fee."

Corporate customer pitch through partner: "We do not move funds or decide whether you should pay. We document beneficiary-name/IBAN exceptions, contact suppliers through approved channels, collect evidence, prepare master-data corrections, and return a signed exception file so your AP/treasury team can approve, hold, or reroute payments with a documented audit trail."

Economics:
- Prepaid batch fee: 2,000-8,000 PLN for small/medium batches, 8,000-25,000 PLN for urgent or high-volume corporate payment runs.
- Optional urgent-run surcharge for payroll, rent, tax, supplier-critical, or same-week payment runs.
- Partner share: 15-30% of collected fees.
- First 6-month target: 20-50 accepted batches, 60,000-180,000 PLN collected, 60%+ gross margin, and 30,000-90,000 PLN owner contribution if scope is bounded.
- Payback for customer: avoided payment-run delay, supplier escalation, payroll panic, fraud-risk uncertainty, and internal AP/treasury rework.
- No speculative audits. No unpaid data cleanup. No work without a current exception batch, customer authorization, and prepaid fee.

Copy risk:
ERP vendors, banks, PSPs, and AP teams can build generic validation tools, but they cannot copy partner-owned current exception queues, customer authorizations, supplier confirmations already in progress, and batch-level outcome memory before those cases are cleared. The wedge is a live operational exception desk attached to payment partners, not a generic AP cleanup checklist.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder starts where partners have an immediate queue and reputational pain. Once the partner routes a customer-authorized batch and the founder begins supplier confirmations and correction logs, another provider would have to take over an active payment exception workflow mid-run. The acquired advantage is the signed partner lane, current batches, confirmation records, and outcome memory.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls prepaid current exception batches and returns signed correction/no-go workpapers tied to real payment runs. It is not a dashboard, lead marketplace, or generic AP audit. The output is an operational release file: exception list, evidence, supplier confirmation, corrected master-data fields, unresolved holds, and partner/customer sign-off.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not hold, move, initiate, approve, or advise on payment execution. The corporate customer remains responsible for payment decisions, supplier validation policy, fraud controls, and bank/PSP instructions. Banks/PSPs remain payment providers of record. Supplier calls follow approved callback procedures and do not request sensitive bank changes unless explicitly authorized by the customer. Data-processing, confidentiality, least-privilege access, retention, contractor QA, and audit logging are documented. Suspected fraud, sanctions, impersonation, disputed invoices, or legal conflicts are escalated back to the customer/partner as no-go cases.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: it is not bank-vendor regulatory renewal evidence. It is operational exception clearing for beneficiary verification in payment runs.

This is not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: there are no marketplace listings or Amazon cases.

This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack: there is no health/investor-diligence component.

This is not TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot: it is not municipal event documentation.

This is not HeritageDoor Suites: it has no physical property or renovation asset.

This is not a generic AP master-data cleanup service: only current verification-of-payee exception batches with partner routing, customer authorization, supplier confirmation, and prepaid release work are accepted.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether payment partners already solve exceptions internally or through existing vendors.
- Whether partner routing exists before broad Verification of Payee exception volumes become acute in the target market.
- Whether corporate customers will allow an unknown third party into beneficiary/payment master data.
- Whether data-protection, fraud-control, callback, and liability requirements make the process too slow for urgent runs.
- Whether enough exceptions are complex enough to pay for but simple enough to resolve without bank/PSP changes.
- Whether the work becomes low-margin manual supplier chasing rather than a repeatable exception desk.
