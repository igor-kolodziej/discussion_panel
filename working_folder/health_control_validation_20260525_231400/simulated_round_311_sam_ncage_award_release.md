## Search Pivot

This round tests a harder current-payment/procurement control object: a named U.S. federal, NATO, defense-prime, grant, subcontract, or vendor-onboarding event blocked because a Polish/CEE entity cannot get or maintain the exact entity identifiers and active registration status required by the buyer.

The commercial event is not "register in SAM someday." It is a live award, onboarding, payment, bid, or subcontract blocked by UEI/SAM/NCAGE/CAGE validation, renewal, physical-address mismatch, entity-name mismatch, banking/POC data, or inactive status. The proof is active registration, buyer/procurement acceptance, or clean no-go.

Sources checked:

- SAM.gov states registration is needed to bid on federal contracts and apply for federal assistance, assigns a Unique Entity ID during registration, and registration can take up to 10 business days to become active.
- SAM.gov says registration and UEI are free and points users to official assistance resources, which is a major copy/price objection.
- SAM.gov external resources link to the DLA CAGE portal and the NCAGE request tool for international entities.
- SAM.gov entity checklist says non-U.S. entities must request an NCAGE code before starting SAM.gov registration.
