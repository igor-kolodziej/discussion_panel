Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
AI Model-Risk Renewal Evidence Pack

One-sentence thesis:
A vendor-side renewal evidence pack for AI workflow vendors selling to banks, insurers, payment institutions, and regulated fintechs whose renewal, pilot, procurement, or production approval is paused by a live AI governance, model-risk, data, human-oversight, or hallucination-control review.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized B2B AI vendors selling AI-assisted document extraction, call summarization, customer-service copilots, AML alert triage support, collections assistance, fraud-investigation workflow support, complaint routing, internal knowledge search, or workflow automation to banks, insurers, PSPs, EMIs, and regulated fintechs in Europe.

Best first buyers already have one regulated-finance customer, a renewal or paid pilot above 200,000 PLN or EUR-equivalent, a named AI governance/model-risk/security/procurement request from the customer, and enough product evidence to answer if it is organized quickly.

Acute trigger:
A bank, insurer, PSP, EMI, or regulated fintech pauses a renewal, procurement, production rollout, pilot expansion, or vendor approval because the AI vendor cannot yet answer the customer's AI governance/model-risk review: model and system inventory, use-case boundary, provider/deployer role map, data flows, training/fine-tuning/RAG sources, evaluation results, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, logging, monitoring, change control, incident reporting, subcontractors, security/privacy handoff, and prohibited/high-risk-use boundaries.

Control point:
Signed renewal-evidence mandate with:
- the live customer AI/model-risk/procurement request;
- renewal, pilot, PO, or expansion value and deadline;
- customer use-case description and production boundary;
- product architecture, model/system inventory, prompt/RAG/fine-tuning/data-flow map where relevant;
- evaluation, QA, red-team, error-rate, hallucination-control, fallback, and human-review evidence where it exists;
- logging, monitoring, incident, change-management, access-control, and data-retention evidence;
- security/privacy/DPA/subprocessor handoff documents;
- no-go/escalation log for legal, regulatory, model-validation, credit, insurance, HR, biometric, clinical, or automated-decision issues;
- customer-specific response matrix and evidence room.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid renewal-evidence cases at 15,000-60,000 PLN each.
2. Each case has a live regulated-finance customer request and renewal, pilot, or procurement value above 200,000 PLN or EUR-equivalent.
3. At least one case produces a submitted response matrix and evidence room used in the customer's AI governance/model-risk/procurement process.
4. At least one case gets customer acceptance, next-stage approval, renewal progress, pilot expansion, production-review progress, or a clean no-go before wasting more sales time.
5. One AI governance/model-risk reviewer and one technical AI/evaluation reviewer available per case on paid contractor terms.
6. A repeat taxonomy of customer blockers: unclear AI role map, no use-case boundary, missing model inventory, undocumented RAG sources, weak eval evidence, hallucination-control gaps, missing human oversight, no incident/change process, weak logging, subprocessor uncertainty, or prohibited/high-risk-use ambiguity.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build a strict intake checklist for regulated-finance AI renewal blockers; recruit one AI governance/model-risk reviewer and one technical AI/evaluation reviewer; map 80 AI workflow vendors selling into banks, insurers, PSPs, EMIs, regtech buyers, and regulated fintechs.

Month 2: close three prepaid cases. Accept only vendors with a real customer request, existing product evidence, renewal/procurement value above 200,000 PLN or EUR-equivalent, a named buyer deadline, and no unresolved legal/clinical/credit/insurance/HR/biometric automated-decision dependency. Deliver the customer-specific response matrix, evidence index, product-use boundary, no-go/escalation log, and customer answer package.

Month 3: document whether at least one case moves commercially: customer accepts the evidence room, advances the renewal, unblocks a pilot, schedules production review, approves a limited use case, or issues a clean no-go.

Months 4-6: complete 8-18 paid packs, collect 150,000-600,000 PLN in fees, keep gross margin above 60% after reviewers, secure 2-3 repeat channels from AI security reviewers, model-risk consultants, venture-backed AI vendors, or regulated-finance procurement advisers, and build a private taxonomy of regulated-finance AI governance blockers by use case and evidence artifact.

First acquisition mechanism:
Buyer pitch:
"If a bank or insurer is pausing renewal because your AI product cannot answer model-risk or AI governance questions, send the customer request, renewal deadline, product boundary, model/data-flow materials, evals, logs, oversight controls, incident/change process, and security/privacy handoff docs. I return a customer-specific response matrix, evidence room, no-go/escalation log, and renewal answer package. I do not certify AI Act compliance, provide legal advice, or validate regulated credit/insurance decisions."

Channel pitch:
"You keep legal, regulatory, model validation, and security ownership. I take the live regulated-finance renewal case where an AI vendor has customer money at risk and needs its existing model-risk evidence organized into a buyer-usable response."

Economics:
Charge 15,000-25,000 PLN for a narrow single-use-case response pack, 25,000-40,000 PLN for a multi-artifact bank/insurer renewal pack, and 40,000-60,000 PLN for urgent production-review or multi-stakeholder model-risk cases. Reviewer cost is 3,000-12,000 PLN per case depending on complexity. The buyer pays because a six-figure renewal, bank pilot, production approval, or procurement process is blocked by buyer trust evidence, not because they want generic AI compliance advice.

Copy risk:
AI governance consultants, law firms, Big Four firms, ISO 42001 consultants, security auditors, model-risk specialists, and internal product/security teams can copy pieces. The defense is strict focus on live regulated-finance customer requests, fast evidence-room assembly, customer-specific response matrices, model-risk objection memory, and refusal to sell broad AI compliance programs.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the AI vendor signs the mandate, prepays the case, shares the live customer request, renewal deadline, product evidence, model/data-flow materials, evals, logs, and customer stakeholders, the startup controls the active renewal evidence file. Another provider must displace the customer-specific response matrix, evidence room, reviewer notes, no-go log, and renewal answer workflow under deadline pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live regulated-finance AI renewal/procurement blockers with named customer requests and contract value at risk. The output is a renewal artifact used in an active customer process: response matrix, evidence room, use-case boundary, model/data-flow map, eval/oversight/logging evidence index, incident/change-control handoff, and no-go/escalation log. It is not an AI governance program, legal memo, certification, ISO implementation, model-validation service, dashboard, database, marketplace, or brokered consultant introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, AI Act compliance certification, regulated model validation, credit/insurance underwriting approval, clinical validation, biometric assessment, HR/employment scoring review, security certification, or a guarantee of customer approval. Lawyers, bank model-risk teams, security teams, privacy officers, model validators, and the AI vendor remain responsible for legal classification, regulated decisioning, formal validation, security, privacy, procurement approval, and production use. Cases involving automated credit/insurance decisions, clinical use, biometric identification, employment scoring, law enforcement, materially false claims, missing core evidence, or unsafe autonomous decisioning are declined or escalated.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not the DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack. DORA handles ICT third-party-risk and operational-resilience evidence for regulated-finance vendor requests. This handles AI system behavior and model-risk evidence for regulated-finance AI workflow renewals: use-case boundary, model/system inventory, data flows, evals, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, logging, monitoring, incident/change control, and AI governance response. The buyer context overlaps, but the trigger, control artifact, reviewer skill, objections, and proof evidence are materially different.

It is not REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, BioSignal, GreenTender, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
- Banks and insurers may already route AI governance reviews to their own model-risk, security, privacy, legal, and procurement teams.
- AI governance consultants, law firms, Big Four teams, ISO 42001 consultants, and security auditors can offer broader and more credible support.
- A solo Warsaw founder may lack credibility with regulated-finance AI and model-risk stakeholders unless senior reviewers are attached.
- Many vendors will not have enough underlying evidence; the pack cannot invent evals, logs, human oversight, or model controls.
- Some use cases drift into regulated credit, insurance, HR, biometric, clinical, or automated-decision domains outside scope.
- Customer approval remains outside the founder's control.
- The work may become generic AI compliance consulting if it is not tied to a live customer renewal request.
- The idea is adjacent to DORA because it is another regulated-finance vendor evidence response, so it must prove AI model-risk is an independent repeated blocker.
