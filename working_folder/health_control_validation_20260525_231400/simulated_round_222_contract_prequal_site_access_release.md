## Search Premise

Round 221 failed because transferred ad-site cash still became a local operator rollup. Round 222 searches for live B2B customer portal holds where a supplier already won or is close to work, but site access, PO activation, onboarding, or invoice ability is blocked by a concrete prequalification evidence workflow. The lead avoids financial/legal/health portals and focuses on industrial contractor HSE/vendor prequalification.

Internal scoring caps applied only for simulation:

- cap below 82 without concrete 6-month control proof;
- cap below 85 if one vendor contract, equipment purchase, consultant, or hire can copy it;
- cap below 87 if CAC, payback, margins, cash conversion, or owner earnings are weak;
- cap below 87 if dependent on vague partnerships/goodwill/interviews;
- cap below 82 if clinical/legal/patient/regulated-data authority is unresolved.

## Lead Candidate: ContractorPrequal Site-Access Release Desk

### Buyer

CEE maintenance, installation, industrial cleaning, electrical, mechanical, scaffolding, crane/lifting, HVAC, telecom field, facility, and specialty contractors that have already won or are close to customer work but cannot start, dispatch workers, activate a PO, pass onboarding, or invoice because an industrial customer or third-party portal has not accepted contractor prequalification evidence.

### Acute Trigger

A customer, plant, project owner, or third-party prequalification portal blocks site access, onboarding, PO activation, job start, renewal, or invoice approval until the contractor clears specific HSE, insurance, training, licensing, policy, incident-rate, subcontractor, equipment, or worker-document requirements. Delay burns crew time, mobilization slots, and customer trust.

### Control Point

Signed release mandate for a named customer/job/portal; exact portal item list or customer condition text; account access under contractor authorization or contractor-provided screenshots/exports; insurance certificates; HSE policies; training matrix; worker certificates; equipment inspection certificates; licence/registration documents; subcontractor list; prior incident/near-miss records where available; nonconformity/correction tracker; submitted response chronology; reviewer no-go list; and customer/portal progress ledger.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Eight prepaid live site-access or onboarding release cases at 6,000-25,000 PLN.
2. Every case has written customer/portal condition text, named job/PO/site, deadline, blocked value, and contractor authorization.
3. Five cases produce portal progress, accepted document set, site-access restoration, PO activation, worker roster clearance, invoice path restored, or clean no-go.
4. Two referral sources among industrial accountants, insurance brokers, HSE consultants, staffing coordinators, or equipment rental/service networks send repeat cases.
5. At least 80,000 PLN collected and gross margin above 60% after HSE reviewer/document ops costs.
