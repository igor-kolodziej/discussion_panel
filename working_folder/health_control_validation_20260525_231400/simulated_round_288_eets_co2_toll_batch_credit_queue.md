## Lead Candidate

### Idea Name

EETS CO2 Toll Batch Credit Queue.

### Internal Simulated Score

88.4 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It uses a real official payment/savings surface already validated in Round 286: German truck toll CO2 class correction for vehicles defaulted to class 1.
- The control point is materially stronger than a direct fleet mandate: partner-signed batch queue, client authorizations, exact VIN/toll statement folders, official submission IDs, and credit/savings attribution.
- It borrows trust from transport accountants, EETS/fleet-card advisers, leasing offices, truck dealers, and fleet bookkeepers that already see statements and clients.
- The first proof is not a report: official CO2 class update, corrected statement, refund/credit, or documented rate delta on named vehicles.
- Capital need is low; work is document retrieval, evidence mapping, portal-guided submission, status tracking, and no-go filtering.

### Internal Objections

- EETS providers, fleet-card companies, truck dealers, leasing companies, and transport accountants can internalize the checklist once demand is obvious.
- Toll Collect self-service remains a substitute for competent fleets.
- Many vehicles will correctly remain class 1, and many files will lack CoC/CIF or qualifying evidence.
- Retroactive refunds may be less reliable than future savings, making fee capture harder.
- Partner economics can compress margins if the partner owns the client and expects 20-40%.
- Manual vehicle-by-vehicle document chasing can destroy margin unless batches are large and no-go rules are strict.
- This may still be a transition-wave cashflow wedge unless it expands into recurring fleet toll-data correction, vehicle onboarding, and other toll statement corrections.

## Gate Decision

Advance **EETS CO2 Toll Batch Credit Queue** to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above `87` and the control point materially strengthens the Round 286 near miss.
