## Zero To One Judgment

Zero To One treated this as a solid recovery-mandate idea with real cash pain, clear evidence objects, and measurable outcomes. It agreed that the proof is stronger than a report because the desired outcome is a credit memo, corrected invoice, offset, or refund.

The idea still failed because 3PL contracts vary heavily, many apparent overbillings are valid but misunderstood, exports may be messy or incomplete, and brands may avoid claims that damage the warehouse relationship. The evaluator described it as a good niche business rather than a defensible confirmed idea.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Many "overbillings" are valid charges that brands misunderstand because 3PL invoices include packaging, storage, receiving, returns, minimums, project labor, oversized handling, disposal, and accessorial fees beyond base pick-pack.
2. 3PL contracts and invoices vary heavily by order, unit, SKU, carton, pallet, bin, cubic storage, returns, kitting, project labor, packaging tables, minimums, and account fees.
3. 3PL relationship risk is real because the warehouse holds inventory and controls daily fulfillment.
4. WMS/order/return/storage exports may be messy, inaccessible, overwritten, or not mapped cleanly to invoice lines.
5. 3PLs may resist credits even with evidence by citing contractual interpretation, corrected WMS data, minimums, verbal approvals, packaging substitutions, or their own storage snapshots.
6. Large brands may already reconcile internally.
7. Small brands may not have enough recoverable value to justify a paid screen.
8. Ecommerce ops consultants, freight-audit firms, accountants, 3PL-switch advisors, and spend-management tools can copy the workflow.
9. Recovery can drift into 3PL-switch consulting, contract renegotiation, RFP management, or warehouse-move work.
10. The simulated recovered-value target was too aggressive; 2-5% confirmed recoverable credits on screened spend is more realistic than 12.5%.

## Useful Narrowing

Zero To One said the best first niche is EU/CEE ecommerce brands with 600,000-5,000,000 PLN annual 3PL spend, messy invoices, and contract-renewal or 3PL-switch pressure within 6 months.

Best first claim types:

- wrong contract/rate-card application;
- duplicate charges;
- storage count mismatches with snapshot evidence;
- pick-pack or return count mismatches where order IDs and invoice units match cleanly;
- unauthorized project labor, kitting, or packaging fees where written approval is missing.

Only accept cases where:

- 12 months of invoices are available;
- contract/rate card is available;
- WMS/order/return/storage exports exist;
- monthly 3PL spend exceeds 50,000 PLN;
- brand approves every claim before submission;
- relationship-risk context is clear;
- paid screen is collected before success-fee recovery.

Even with proof of 8+ mandates, 1,000,000 PLN+ screened spend, 50,000 PLN+ recovered credits/refunds/offsets, one channel partner sending 3+ prospects, 65%+ gross margin, and minimal relationship damage, Zero To One estimated the score would rise only to 78-81.

## Decision

Reject. Do not continue operational invoice recovery unless the next version controls a proprietary payment stream or customer-approved recurring invoice flow, not merely claim packages against invoices.
