Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
BDO DPR/EDPR Allocation Lockbox

One-sentence thesis:
The 2026 move of Polish DPR/EDPR packaging-recycling documents into the BDO Confirmation Module creates a narrow opening to control named document-allocation streams with source-side exclusivity, buyer deposits, and payment direction before recovery organisations and brokers can capture the exact source/buyer match.

Exact buyer:
Packaging recovery organisations, environmental-compliance operators, and producers/importers that need material-specific 2026 DPR or EDPR documents to satisfy packaging recovery/recycling obligations or reduce product-fee exposure.

Acute trigger:
For periods from 2026, applications and issuance of DPR and EDPR documents are handled through the BDO Confirmation Module. Buyers need valid material-specific documents by source, quarter, mass, and recipient, while smaller recyclers, exporters, collectors, or packaging-waste source entities may have surplus document potential but weak buyer access, admin capacity, or payment confidence.

Control point:
The startup only accepts a case when it can control a named allocation stream: source-side exclusive allocation mandate for a specified material, quarter, BDO source entity, mass range, and recipient-designation workflow; buyer-side deposit or take-or-pay reservation for the same material/quarter/mass; anti-circumvention terms; and acknowledged payment direction or lockbox terms.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
One source-side mandate over 50-300 tonnes of 2026 DPR/EDPR document potential for one material stream; verified source BDO registration and authority; one buyer deposit or take-or-pay reservation for at least part of the named stream; written payment-direction or lockbox acknowledgement by buyer and source; environmental-law boundary review; and the first BDO Confirmation Module application, recipient-designation action, buyer acceptance, or written no-go reason.

6-month POC:
Control 4-8 named streams across 2-4 materials and quarters, with 300-1,500 tonnes under source mandate or buyer reservation; complete 3-6 paid allocations or documented no-go outcomes; collect 120,000-400,000 PLN gross allocation fee/spread; prove two repeat buyers or sources; keep legal/compliance review costs below 20% of gross revenue; reject suspicious, disputed, fake, or unclear-entitlement streams.

First acquisition mechanism:
Source-side outreach to small recyclers, exporters, collectors, and waste entities: "Do not list generic DPR/EDPR supply. Give me a short exclusive over one named 2026 material/quarter stream; I bring a deposit-backed buyer and payment releases after buyer verification and BDO recipient acceptance." Buyer-side outreach to recovery organisations and obligated producers: "I have a named 2026 DPR/EDPR source stream with material, source, quarter, mass range, BDO registration check, source exclusivity, and payment-direction terms; reserve it only if it fits your obligation."

Economics:
Charge an allocation fee, a source-funded monetisation fee, or a 5-15% spread between buyer price and source payout. No speculative purchase of documents. Buyer deposits fund the transaction. A first stream must support 8,000-25,000 PLN gross fee/spread; a six-month POC needs enough repeat named streams to support 120,000-400,000 PLN gross revenue with low capital exposure.

Copy risk:
High at the category level. Packaging recovery organisations, waste brokers, and environmental-compliance firms can buy DPR/EDPR documents directly and have stronger compliance trust. The narrow defense is transaction-specific control: once a source signs exclusivity for a named material/quarter/mass and a buyer deposits against that stream, competitors cannot use that exact allocation without breaking the signed terms.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
The protected asset is not the idea of buying DPR/EDPR documents. It is the exact source-authorised material/quarter/mass stream, buyer reservation, anti-circumvention term, payment-direction acknowledgement, and BDO recipient/application workflow record. Incumbents can compete for the next stream, but not the stream already signed and reserved.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
No revenue is counted for advice, training, listings, introductions, obligation calculations, or software. The deliverable is a controlled allocation/payment event for a named statutory document stream. The proof is source exclusivity plus buyer deposit plus BDO workflow action or written no-go, not a compliance memo or marketplace match.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Registered recyclers/exporters/waste entities remain responsible for lawful recycling records, waste mass evidence, DPR/EDPR applications, issuance, and BDO account actions. Buyers and their compliance advisers remain responsible for obligation calculations, product-fee exposure, document acceptance, and legal interpretation. The startup does not issue documents, certify recycling, calculate obligations, use another party's BDO account, falsify mass, act as a recovery organisation, or provide environmental/legal advice. Suspicious KPO records, unclear entitlement, disputed mass, fake document pressure, sanctions/fraud indicators, or requests to create evidence are rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because the control point is a Polish statutory packaging-recycling document allocation and payment stream, not ICT supplier evidence for regulated finance. Not REDBlocked because there is no Amazon account-health case or connected-device evidence response. Not PromoLeak because this is source/buyer allocation of statutory DPR/EDPR streams, not retailer deduction recovery. Not CBAM Import Continuity Desk because it does not triage import documents or carbon-border exposure. Not TraceFaktura because it is not a municipal operational event ledger. Not HeritageDoor because there is no reclaimed physical inventory.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Incumbent recovery organisations may already control the best sources and buyers; environmental-law boundaries may make exclusivity/payment-direction structures hard; buyer deposits may be difficult for an unknown solo founder; source-side fraud or bad mass records can create serious risk; BDO module transparency may compress spreads; the business may be viewed as waste-document brokerage with limited defensibility; six-month repeatability depends on finding multiple clean, monetisable, under-served streams rather than one lucky allocation.
