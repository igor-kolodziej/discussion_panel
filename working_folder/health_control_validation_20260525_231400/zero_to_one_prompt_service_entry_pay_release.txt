Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
ServiceEntry Pay-Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
CEE suppliers that already performed enterprise service, maintenance, engineering, shutdown, field, or project work will pay to release invoices when buyer AP has written that payment cannot move until a service-entry sheet, goods receipt, work confirmation, completion record, or PO-line acceptance state is created and accepted.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE industrial maintenance firms, engineering-service suppliers, automation contractors, facilities-service vendors, machinery-service firms, shutdown contractors, field-service subcontractors, equipment-service providers, and specialist B2B service suppliers selling to large enterprise, energy, industrial, telecom, FMCG, logistics, retail, and multinational customers. The economic buyer is the owner, CFO, controller, AR lead, sales director, project manager, or service operations lead responsible for cash already earned under a named PO, work order, or service contract.

Acute trigger:
The supplier has delivered the work and issued or prepared an invoice, but the customer's AP, procurement, SAP/Ariba/Coupa/Oracle portal, shared-service center, or requester has written that payment cannot release because a service-entry sheet, goods receipt, work confirmation, completion record, service acceptance, PO-line match, or requester approval is missing, rejected, incomplete, or stuck with a named business approver.

Control point:
Signed limited mandate over one named customer, PO/work order, and invoice queue; written buyer blocker text; PO and invoice list; delivery/service completion evidence; site signoff, work reports, timesheets, completion photos, acceptance protocols, tickets, or service logs where already truthful and available; customer requester and approver map; authorized portal visibility where allowed; AP ticket trail; escalation chronology; clean no-go rules; and customer-ready release packet mapped exactly to the missing service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, or acceptance state.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Six prepaid cases tied to named enterprise customers and invoices above 150,000 PLN each; at least 1,500,000 PLN of unpaid invoice value under mandate; at least 60,000 PLN collected in prepaid and explicitly success-tied fees; four buyer-accepted service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, invoice-acceptance, payment-scheduled, remittance, narrowed-follow-up, or clean no-go outcomes; rejection log above 40% for commercial disputes, missing delivery, poor performance, buyer cash delay, insolvency, fraud review, tax/legal issues, weak invoice value, or cases without written buyer blocker text.

6-month POC:
Handle 25-60 payment-release files across 15-35 suppliers; put 8,000,000-25,000,000 PLN of invoice value under mandate; collect 300,000-950,000 PLN in fees; achieve 18+ accepted service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, invoice-acceptance, payment-scheduled, remittance, narrowed-follow-up, or clean no-go outcomes; build private case memory by buyer group, ERP/procurement system, service-entry wording, PO-line mismatch, approver role, evidence item, AP ticket path, elapsed time, and accepted packet format; source repeat channels from fractional CFOs, industrial accountants, SAP/Ariba consultants, ERP implementers, field-service software consultants, and industrial sales agencies; maintain gross margin above 60% after AP-ops contractor, ERP/portal specialist, document QA, and secure workflow costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound to CEE service suppliers that visibly sell to large industrial, energy, telecom, retail, logistics, FMCG, or multinational customers and whose projects create high-value service invoices. The ask is only for live written blockers: "If a large customer says AP cannot pay because SAP, Ariba, Coupa, Oracle, or the requester has not accepted the service-entry sheet, goods receipt, work confirmation, completion record, or PO line, send the blocker text and invoice list. I turn it into an acceptance-release packet, approver chronology, portal correction trail, and paid/remittance/no-go outcome." Partner outreach goes to fractional CFOs, industrial accountants, AR outsourcing firms, SAP/Ariba consultants, ERP implementers, and industrial service advisers that see trapped service invoices but do not want to chase buyer acceptance states.

Economics:
Charge 8,000-18,000 PLN prepaid for one named customer and invoice queue under 400,000 PLN, 18,000-40,000 PLN for urgent or multi-PO cases, and 2-6% success fee only where the customer has written that a specific service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, or acceptance state blocks payment and attribution can be captured through before-state text, submitted packet, buyer acknowledgement, payment schedule, or remittance. Reject single invoices below 150,000 PLN unless bundled with a repeat customer queue. Contractors are paid for portal/AP/service-entry operations and document QA, not for legal claims, technical certification, or debtor pressure.

Copy risk:
Supplier finance teams, controllers, AR teams, project managers, ERP consultants, SAP/Ariba/Coupa specialists, customer-success teams, accounts-receivable outsourcers, collections agencies, and buyer AP/procurement teams can solve parts of this workflow. The defense is narrow case qualification, fast acceptance-state packet assembly, repeated buyer/ERP/approver memory, and active control over the named PO-line, service-entry, goods-receipt, AP ticket, evidence chronology, and remittance proof once the mandate is signed.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the supplier signs the mandate, prepays, grants authorized portal visibility or AP correspondence, provides the PO, invoice queue, buyer blocker text, completion evidence, requester map, and customer contact trail, the startup controls the active payment-release file and response chronology for that named buyer acceptance state. A consultant can compete for future cases, but cannot control the same SES/GR/AP ticket and paid-invoice proof without the supplier switching mid-blocker and restarting the evidence trail.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell generic AR outsourcing, collections, procurement consulting, supplier onboarding, ERP implementation, an AP dashboard, a service-management tool, or introductions to buyer AP contacts. It accepts only named invoice or PO blockers where the customer's written blocker identifies a missing service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, completion, or PO-line acceptance state. The output is an operational acceptance-release file: corrected portal state, requester packet, approver chronology, AP escalation file, accepted SES/GR/work-confirmation state, payment schedule, remittance confirmation, paid invoice, narrowed follow-up, or clean no-go.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Do not act as a collection agency, lawyer, tax adviser, accountant of record, payment institution, buyer representative, safety inspector, engineering certifier, or work-performance expert. Do not pressure disputed debtors, negotiate commercial disputes, certify work quality, create false completion evidence, alter dates, impersonate buyer or supplier staff, change bank details, or handle cases involving insolvency, fraud, sanctions, fake documents, missing delivery, poor performance, unresolved site defects, price disputes, penalty disputes, tax/legal questions, or customer refusal to acknowledge a payable. Suppliers remain responsible for invoice truth, work performance, delivery facts, tax treatment, and customer relationship. Buyers remain responsible for acceptance and payment decisions.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retailer promotional leakage or deductions. It is not SupplierPortal Invoice Release Desk because it excludes broad vendor-master, bank-change, tax-form, onboarding, and generic portal issues; the controlled object is specifically a buyer service-entry, goods-receipt, work-confirmation, completion, or PO-line acceptance state tied to performed service work. It is not KSeF, PEPPOL, e-invoicing, credit-memo recovery, unapplied cash cleanup, factor reserve release, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, freight audit, SAM/NCAGE, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, or a generic evidence desk.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Many suppliers already have finance, AR, project-management, customer-success, or ERP teams that should chase service-entry and goods-receipt acceptance. Large-customer AP teams may ignore third parties or insist on supplier-only portal actions. Some apparent service-entry blockers are actually poor performance, missing delivery, site defects, buyer cash delay, penalty leverage, tax/legal issues, fraud review, or unresolved commercial disputes. Buyer approvers can be slow, unavailable, political, or unwilling to certify completion. Portal and ERP variation may reduce repeatability. Small invoices cannot support the fee. Success fees may create collections or legal-boundary issues unless scoped tightly. Once suppliers see the accepted packet format, they may internalize future cases.
