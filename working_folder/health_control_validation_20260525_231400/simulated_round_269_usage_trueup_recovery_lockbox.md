## Search Frame

Round 268 showed that known vendor-funded claim paths are still capped if channel teams can internalize the workflow. Round 269 searches for current cash where the buyer controls both the source record and the invoicing/payment direction: underbilled B2B SaaS/API usage, minimum commits, overages, and contracted annual uplift clauses that are present in signed customer agreements but not invoiced because billing data, usage logs, and CRM terms are scattered.

This is not general RevOps consulting. The candidate only advances if the first proof includes a named contract, usage/uplift ledger, CFO authorization, corrected invoice, customer acknowledgment or payment, and collected fee.

## Candidate Advanced To Real Validation

Idea name: API Usage True-Up Recovery Lockbox

One-sentence thesis: recover earned but uninvoiced B2B SaaS/API revenue by taking a signed recovery mandate over named customers whose contracts already define usage overages, minimum commits, or annual true-up fees, then issuing corrected invoices only after usage logs and CFO approval support the claim.

Exact buyer: 20-200 person B2B SaaS, data API, infrastructure API, developer-tool, analytics, workflow, and vertical software vendors with enterprise contracts, usage-based pricing, minimum commits, or overage clauses, but weak billing operations.

Acute trigger: The vendor has earned usage overage, minimum-commit shortfall, storage/API-volume true-up, extra environment fee, premium feature fee, or annual usage true-up under a signed customer contract, but it was not billed because usage logs, CRM terms, invoicing, customer-success notes, and billing systems do not line up.

Control point: Signed CFO/founder recovery mandate; contract clause; named customer list; usage/export logs; entitlement or metering evidence; prior invoice history; proposed invoice schedule; customer-notice wording approved by the vendor; corrected invoice; customer acknowledgement, payment, credit note, or clean no-go.

60-day proof:

- Three signed mandates.
- Ten named customer accounts screened.
- At least 400,000 PLN in candidate underbilled revenue identified.
- Two corrected invoices issued with vendor CFO approval.
- One customer payment, acknowledged invoice, or scheduled payment.
- 30,000-90,000 PLN in fees collected or invoiced.

6-month POC:

- 20-40 vendors screened.
- 80-200 customer contracts reviewed.
- 4,000,000-12,000,000 PLN candidate underbilling reviewed.
- 1,000,000-4,000,000 PLN corrected invoices issued or scheduled.
- 400,000-1,500,000 PLN collected cash or acknowledged AR.
- 200,000-700,000 PLN startup revenue.
- Gross margin above 70% after usage-log QA, RevOps support, and contract-boundary review.

First acquisition mechanism: Target SaaS/API vendors through fractional CFOs, RevOps consultants, SaaS finance communities, usage-billing migration consultants, and founder/CFO outbound. Offer a paid "underbilling recovery screen" only for vendors with signed enterprise contracts, usage logs, and pricing clauses. Do not sell billing software or a general RevOps audit.

Economics: Charge 5,000-15,000 PLN screening/setup plus 10-20% of collected or customer-acknowledged recovered revenue. Minimum candidate recovery per customer is 25,000 PLN and preferred total mandate value is 150,000+ PLN. The buyer pays because recovered cash and corrected ARR are immediate owner earnings, and the startup is paid only when invoices are issued, acknowledged, or collected.

Copy risk: RevOps consultants, billing-platform migration firms, accountants, fractional CFOs, customer-success teams, and billing software vendors can copy. The defense is current mandate control, usage-log reconciliation playbooks by billing model, customer-notice patterns that preserve the relationship, and a record of which clauses convert to paid invoices rather than disputes.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/database/marketplace/broker: The unit is a named recovered invoice file, not an audit report or dashboard. If the contract clause, customer, usage ledger, vendor approval, corrected invoice, and payment/acknowledgement path are not present, the startup rejects the case.

Boundaries:

- No legal interpretation, collection agency work, debt collection, factoring, aggressive audit claims, or customer threats.
- Vendor counsel or CFO approves disputed clauses and customer notices.
- Reject cases with ambiguous contract language, missing usage truth, customer delivery dispute, product downtime, data-quality defects, privacy issues, or likely churn damage.
- The startup does not change billing systems, implement metering infrastructure, negotiate contract amendments, or act as customer success.

Duplicate risk:

- Not PromoLeak because this is vendor-owned SaaS/API revenue recovery, not retailer promotion leakage or deduction recovery.
- Not Amazon Vendor Central, 3PL overbilling, Retail OTIF, PartnerCenter, MDF, commercial lease deposits, or platform remittance recovery.
- Not a software app, dashboard, or RevOps consulting retainer; it is current underbilled contract revenue converted into corrected invoice/payment.

## Internal Simulation Score

Simulated score: **88.2 / 100**

Rationale:

- Stronger than ordinary recovery because the buyer owns the customer relationship, contract, usage logs, and invoice authority.
- First proof is current cash or acknowledged AR, not a memo.
- Ticket sizes can be high in usage-based SaaS/API businesses.
- No external debtor/platform permission is needed beyond the existing customer invoice path.
- Main risks are relationship damage, contract ambiguity, internal copyability, and billing-tool substitutes. It passes simulation only when accepted cases are narrow, contract-backed, CFO-approved, and cash-converting.

## Internal Kill Criteria

Kill if:

- Fewer than 20% of screened vendors have signed usage/true-up clauses and usable logs.
- Customer pushback or churn risk prevents invoicing.
- Average recovered value per customer is below 25,000 PLN.
- Vendors expect a generic billing cleanup audit instead of current invoice recovery.
- Contract interpretation requires legal work in most accepted files.
- RevOps/accounting teams internalize after the first checklist and refuse success fees.

## Decision

Advance to working-chat Zero To One validation because simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point is a vendor-owned signed contract plus current usage ledger converted into corrected invoice cash.
