## Search Implication

Even existing regulatory service books are capped if the legal/technical provider is the real scarce asset and the founder owns only billing hopes. Future rounds should prioritize control points where:

- the founder can own or assign a receivable, payment direction, route, or current customer contract without specialist provider dependency;
- ticket size and cash conversion are high enough to overcome customer reauthorization friction;
- incumbents are not naturally the first buyer of the book.
