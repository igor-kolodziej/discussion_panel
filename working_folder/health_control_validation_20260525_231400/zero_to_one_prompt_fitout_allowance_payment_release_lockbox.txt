Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

Fit-Out Allowance Payment Release Lockbox

One-sentence thesis:

A signed-mandate payment-release desk for commercial tenants whose landlord-funded fit-out allowance, opening contribution, relocation incentive, or improvement reimbursement is already earned or acknowledged but unpaid because the landlord or property manager requires a clean closeout and reimbursement packet.

Exact buyer:

Multi-site retail chains, franchisees, gyms, restaurants, showrooms, service tenants, and office tenants with named landlord incentive or fit-out allowance files above 100,000 PLN equivalent. The economic buyer is the CFO, founder, franchise owner, expansion lead, outsourced finance lead, or retail expansion operator that needs landlord cash after opening, move-in, or fit-out completion.

Acute trigger:

The tenant has opened, moved in, or completed landlord-approved works, but the landlord contribution is still unpaid. The blocker is not a new negotiation. It is missing or mismatched release evidence: invoices, payment proof, completion certificate, handover protocol, photos, as-built documents, contractor statements, VAT data, landlord forms, defect or punch-list status, insurance or permit closeout, entity details, or property-manager approval routing.

Control point:

Signed payment-release mandate for a named allowance file, plus the lease or side-letter allowance clause, landlord/property-manager correspondence, invoice and proof-of-payment folder, completion and handover evidence, site photo set, punch-list status, responsible tenant contact, landlord packet checklist, submitted reimbursement packet, payment tracker, and success-fee right. The startup controls the file assembly and release path for that named cash item through the tenant's authorized channel.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

- Three signed mandates for named allowance files above 100,000 PLN each.
- At least one landlord or property-manager written acknowledgement of required packet fields, accepted packet submission, scheduled payment, approved reimbursement, credit note, or paid amount.
- One first released payment, landlord credit, or accepted payment schedule.
- One success-fee invoice or fixed release fee collected.
- Reusable packet matrix across lease clause, invoice evidence, completion proof, landlord forms, rejection reasons, accepted packet wording, and payment timing.

6-month POC:

Handle 12-20 qualified allowance files, submit 8-12 complete landlord packets, produce 5+ landlord-accepted packets or payment schedules, release or schedule at least 750,000 PLN of tenant cash, collect 90,000-180,000 PLN in fees, and document repeat patterns by landlord, property manager, checklist, rejection reason, packet artifact, and payment path. Continue only if at least two partner channels send a second case and gross margin remains above 60% after contractor, legal-boundary, and admin costs.

First acquisition mechanism:

Outbound through tenant reps, fit-out project managers, franchise accountants, retail expansion consultants, outsourced CFOs, commercial brokers, and retail property advisers. The ask is not "do you want a lease audit"; it is "send only live, named landlord allowance files where cash is due or acknowledged and payment is stuck on closeout packet acceptance." Direct outreach can also target multi-site tenants that recently opened new stores or branches and likely have landlord incentives outstanding.

Economics:

Charge 5,000-12,000 PLN for intake/no-go and 5-12% of released cash, capped per file, or 8,000-25,000 PLN fixed for high-value non-disputed files. A single 150,000-500,000 PLN allowance can justify the fee. The first proof is cash movement, not a report. Founder work is file triage, packet assembly, checklist mapping, follow-up routing, no-go decisions, and partner coordination; lawyers, tenant reps, tax advisers, engineers, and project managers remain responsible for legal, tax, technical, lease, and construction judgments.

Copy risk:

Tenant reps, lease auditors, lawyers, construction project managers, accountants, outsourced CFOs, commercial brokers, and internal expansion teams can copy the workflow. The defense is strict live-file qualification, fast packet assembly, repeat landlord/property-manager packet memory, and partner channels that see stuck files but do not want contingency follow-up or document reconstruction.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once the startup has the signed mandate, exact lease file, landlord thread, invoice proof, closeout packet, submitted reimbursement request, and success-fee right, a competitor cannot work that exact allowance without the tenant switching mid-release. The case memory compounds by landlord, property manager, lease wording, document checklist, rejection reason, accepted packet wording, and payment path.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The output is not a lease audit memo, lease abstraction, dashboard, marketplace, or introduction. The proof is a landlord-accepted reimbursement packet, payment schedule, credit note, released cash, or no-go decision for a named allowance. It is a payment-release lockbox for current cash, not consulting.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

No lease negotiation, legal entitlement creation, rent dispute, construction claim, litigation, or collection pressure. Reject files with active defects, unresolved completion, disputed works, landlord counterclaims, insolvency, unclear authority, missing lease clause, unclear tenant entity, or a condition precedent that has not been met. Counsel, tenant reps, project managers, engineers, and tax advisers remain responsible for legal, technical, construction, tax, and lease interpretations. The startup only assembles and tracks the payment-release packet through the tenant's authorized channel.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

This is not PromoLeak, commercial lease deposit release, construction retention release, acknowledged SaaS true-up, public guarantee release, TraceFaktura, or generic lease auditing. The distinct control object is a landlord-funded tenant improvement or opening allowance with a live lease-side payment path, high average line value, and landlord packet acceptance proof.

Strongest anticipated objections:

- Tenants may already rely on tenant reps, brokers, lawyers, project managers, or accountants.
- Many apparent allowances are not actually due because conditions precedent, defects, landlord approvals, permits, rent arrears, setoffs, invoice rules, or completion requirements are unsatisfied.
- Landlords may ignore third-party coordinators and require official tenant communication.
- Success-fee attribution can be disputed if payment was already scheduled.
- Case volume depends on partner channels because public signals for stuck allowance files are weak.
- The product can drift into lease auditing, construction claims, rent disputes, or legal work unless no-go rules are enforced.
