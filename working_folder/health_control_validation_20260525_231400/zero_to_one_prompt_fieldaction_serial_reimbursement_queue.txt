Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
FieldAction Serial Reimbursement Queue

One-sentence thesis:
A recovery mandate for installers, distributors, and service contractors that already have manufacturer or distributor field-action, service-campaign, serial-batch remedy, retrofit, return, labor-reimbursement, or credit programs but have not converted eligible serial/job lines into credit memos, account offsets, or reimbursements.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE installers, distributors, service contractors, and small integrators in PV, heat pumps, refrigeration, EV charging, low-voltage/security, commercial kitchen equipment, industrial automation, UPS, and related equipment categories where manufacturers or distributors periodically issue official field actions, service campaigns, serial-batch remedies, firmware/hardware retrofit programs, replacement programs, labor allowances, freight credits, or return-credit programs.

Acute trigger:
The manufacturer or distributor has already announced a program for a named product, serial range, firmware or hardware batch, installed-base issue, service bulletin, return campaign, or retrofit remedy. The buyer has serial numbers, job evidence, return proof, or completed remedy work, but the reimbursement window, credit memo, account offset, freight credit, replacement credit, or labor allowance has not been captured before quarter close, campaign expiry, distributor account close, or installer cash strain.

Control point:
Signed mandate over one official campaign queue; official campaign bulletin, service notice, program email, portal notice, distributor letter, or credit policy; eligible serial numbers, customer/site IDs, installation dates, job dates, remedy performed, photos/screenshots, return labels, shipment proof, invoices, technician notes, and distributor/OEM account evidence; payor acknowledgement or portal evidence that the campaign exists and reimbursement or credit is available if proof is complete; submitted reimbursement packet; credit memo, account offset, or reimbursement trail; and founder fee from fixed screen plus recovered-credit success fee.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Five installers or distributors sign mandates over official service-campaign queues; at least 250,000 PLN of campaign-eligible credit or reimbursement face value is documented through campaign notices, account statements, serial/job lists, or portal evidence; two payor/distributor campaign routes are confirmed in writing; at least 50,000 PLN of credits, account offsets, reimbursements, or formally approved credits are recovered; at least three buyers pay fixed screened-queue fees before success fees; and cases without official campaign notice, serial eligibility, remedy/return proof, or open submission path are rejected.

6-month POC:
Handle 20-35 campaign queues; accept 300-800 serial/job lines; recover, offset, or formally approve 400,000-1,200,000 PLN of campaign credits and reimbursements; collect 100,000-280,000 PLN in fixed and success fees; maintain gross margin above 65% after admin reviewers and technical document checks; build a private campaign map by OEM/distributor, campaign type, serial range, proof fields, submission window, return proof, rejection reason, and credit timing; reject at least 40% of apparent queues as no official campaign, closed window, missing serial eligibility, technical dispute, unsafe installation, consumer-facing complaint, fraud risk, or legal dispute.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target installer and distributor groups where official campaigns create admin residue: PV inverter/optimizer/microinverter installers, heat-pump distributors and installers, refrigeration service firms, EV-charger installers, low-voltage/security integrators, commercial kitchen equipment service firms, UPS/electrical service firms, and industrial automation service contractors. The opening offer is: "Send only official manufacturer or distributor field-action/service-campaign queues with serials, program notice, and reimbursement path. I recover credit memos or account offsets; I do not argue warranty eligibility, inspect equipment, handle consumer disputes, or perform service work."

Economics:
Charge 2,000-6,000 PLN per screened campaign queue plus 12-25% of actual recovered or formally approved credit, with a minimum success fee per queue. For large queues, charge 8,000-20,000 PLN upfront plus a lower success fee. The fee is credible because the money is tied to a manufacturer-defined program rather than speculative warranty arguing. Costs are admin reviewers, data cleanup, and occasional technical document checks. The model avoids inventory, receivable purchase, client-fund custody, warranty adjudication, legal claims, and repair labor.

Copy risk:
OEMs, distributors, installer ERPs, warranty automation tools, outsourced admin providers, accountants, and service managers can copy pieces. The defense is narrow case ownership: signed mandates over exact campaign queues, official program terms, serial/job lists, portal histories, return proof, distributor account trails, and credit memo follow-up patterns. Longer-term advantage comes from repeated campaign-route memory across fragmented CEE distributors and installers, not from generic warranty advice.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the installer or distributor signs the mandate and provides the official campaign notice, serial/job ledger, portal exports, account statements, return proof, and distributor/OEM contact path, the startup controls those exact pending campaign-credit cases. An OEM, distributor, accountant, or admin contractor can offer a similar process later, but cannot collect or reassign the already-authorized queue without the buyer moving the mandate and rebuilding the same evidence trail.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell warranty consulting, repair services, technical diagnosis, consumer advocacy, product registration software, dashboards, or introductions. It accepts only named official field-action/service-campaign queues where the payor has already defined a reimbursement or credit route. The output is recovered cash, credit memo, account offset, formally approved credit, or documented no-go for that exact serial/job queue.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not decide warranty eligibility, certify repairs, inspect equipment, perform installation work, represent consumers, provide legal advice, hold funds, handle disputes, or guarantee manufacturer outcomes. OEMs, distributors, authorized service providers, installers, engineers, and lawyers remain responsible for campaign terms, warranty interpretation, technical assessment, repair quality, product safety, and legal claims. Reject consumer complaints, medical/clinical equipment, disputed warranty coverage, missing serial identity, unsafe installation evidence, fraud flags, chargebacks, insolvency, export-control or sanctions concerns, and any case requiring litigation or regulated claims handling.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not BatteryFit because it does not sell retrofit advice or battery economics to installers. It is not HeatQuiet because it does not handle noise complaints or heat-pump siting. It is not Solar Inverter Replacement Options, GridFund, BESSGuard, PV GridTrip, Approved RMA Labor-Credit Recovery, or other energy-procurement ideas because the founder is not advising replacements, funding release, commissioning, or ordinary warranty/RMA recovery. It is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, 3PL, freight audit, PSP reserve, KSeF, DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, ShowComms, LeaseClear, or any platform evidence desk. The payment event is a payor-defined official campaign queue over exact serial/job lines.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Official campaigns may be episodic, making deal flow lumpy. OEMs and distributors may already provide portals and account managers, so neglected campaign value may be small or caused by basic installer admin failure. Payors may refuse third-party follow-up or require installer self-submission. Many apparent campaign credits will not be collectible because the window closed, serials are outside scope, return proof is missing, installation evidence is weak, or remedy work was not performed correctly. The workflow is copyable once an installer sees the checklist. Per-line economics can be too small unless queues contain high-value hardware credits or many repeated serials. A part-time founder may struggle to chase many payors quickly. If most value is technical eligibility argument rather than official campaign credit follow-up, the model becomes warranty consulting or dispute handling and should be killed.
