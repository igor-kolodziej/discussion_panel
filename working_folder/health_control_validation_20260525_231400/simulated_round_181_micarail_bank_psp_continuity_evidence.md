## Lead Candidate: MiCARail Bank/PSP Continuity Evidence Pack

### Buyer

Polish and CEE virtual-asset service providers, crypto exchanges, brokers, OTC desks, custody-adjacent firms, fiat on/off-ramp operators, and CASP applicants with a live bank, EMI, PSP, custodian, or authorised-CASP partner request tied to MiCA transition, account continuation, onboarding, settlement, custody, wind-down, or partner approval.

### Acute Trigger

The payor rail is at risk now. A bank, EMI, PSP, custodian, or authorised CASP asks for MiCA transition evidence before continuing an account, opening a new account, approving settlement, preserving fiat rails, onboarding as a partner, accepting wind-down flow, or allowing a controlled offboarding/migration path before the 1 July 2026 transition cliff.

### Control Point

The startup controls the live rail-response file, not legal/regulatory substance:

- signed client mandate for one named bank/PSP/custody/authorised-CASP request;
- live checklist, email, request, rejection, deadline, or account-review notice from the rail provider;
- counsel-approved MiCA status note or legal boundary memo;
- AML/compliance-provider-owned policies, Travel Rule vendor evidence, KYC/KYT process evidence, custody or asset-segregation evidence where applicable, wind-down or offboarding evidence where applicable, board-approved operating scope, and no-go escalation log;
- submitted rail-response matrix;
- bank/PSP/custody/partner acknowledgement, continuation, onboarding progress, additional question, or documented refusal.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Five prepaid live rail-response mandates from Polish/CEE crypto firms with named bank/PSP/custody/authorised-CASP requests.
2. At least three cases include counsel or existing compliance-provider sign-off on the MiCA status/legal boundaries before submission.
3. At least three rail providers respond with continuation, onboarding progress, accepted evidence, narrowed conditions, additional due-diligence questions, or documented no-go.
4. One client preserves or opens a bank/PSP/custody/partner rail, or receives a written path to continuation/offboarding, after the pack is submitted.
5. At least 100,000 PLN is collected in fixed fees before total spend exceeds 100,000 PLN.

### 6-Month POC

Handle 20-35 live rail-response cases; collect 400,000-1,200,000 PLN; produce 12+ rail progress/continuation/onboarding/no-go outcomes; build a private bank/PSP/custody request taxonomy by institution, evidence type, MiCA status, wind-down scope, custody/outsourcing concern, Travel Rule proof, and AML/KYT objection; maintain gross margin above 60% after specialist reviewers and counsel coordination; reject licence-application, legal-opinion, client-asset, consumer-communication, and AML-outsourcing cases outside scope.

### Economics

Charge 20,000-35,000 PLN for a narrow bank/PSP evidence response, 35,000-70,000 PLN for urgent multi-rail or custody/partner continuity cases, and 5,000-10,000 PLN for a no-go triage where the rail cannot be credibly preserved. Buyers can justify the fee because losing fiat settlement, banking, custody, or partner acceptance can stop revenue, client withdrawals, payroll, vendor payments, or controlled wind-down.

### Copy Risk

Crypto law firms, MiCA compliance consultants, AML vendors, banks, EMIs, PSPs, custodians, authorised CASPs, and big advisory firms can copy pieces. The defense is not generic MiCA knowledge. It is fast control of specific live rail requests, counsel/compliance-provider coordination, accepted evidence formats, institution-specific objection memory, and a narrow bank/PSP continuity outcome before a broader incumbent sees the exact file.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

Once the client signs the mandate, provides the live bank/PSP/custody request, counsel-approved boundary note, compliance evidence, and rail deadline, the startup controls that response room and submission path. A lawyer or compliance consultancy can be hired later, but cannot instantly displace the already-authorized rail-response file without the client restarting the evidence room and risking the deadline.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup does not apply for CASP licences, give legal advice, sell AML outsourcing, move client assets, custody crypto, process fiat payments, introduce banks, or build a dashboard. It accepts only live bank/PSP/custody/authorised-CASP requests where the buyer already has a rail relationship or a named onboarding/review path. The output is a submitted rail-response evidence matrix and rail-provider progress, continuation, or documented no-go.

### Boundaries

Counsel, MLRO/compliance officers, AML vendors, Travel Rule vendors, custodians, banks, PSPs, and authorised CASPs remain responsible for legal opinions, licensing, AML/CFT obligations, custody, onboarding decisions, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, client communications, and asset movement. Reject licence drafting, legal strategy, regulator submissions, legal opinions, client migration execution, custody operations, consumer notices, AML outsourcing, transaction monitoring, financial promotions, reverse-solicitation advice, litigation, tax, sanctions advice, and any case involving client assets moving through the startup.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because the buyer is not an ICT vendor answering bank DORA/TPRM questionnaires. The buyer is a crypto/VASP/CASP-transition firm facing live bank/PSP/custody/authorised-CASP rail continuity under MiCA. It is not Data Act, REDBlocked, CBAM, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, 3PL, LeaseClear, ShowComms, RMA credit recovery, KSeF, or any health/Rx/clinical route. The trigger, buyer, proof artifact, and payment event are materially different: a named financial operating rail under a hard MiCA transition deadline.
