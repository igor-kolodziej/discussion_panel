Trigger: insurer asks for sprinkler/hydrant/fire-alarm inspection records, electrical thermography, fire-door records, evacuation plan, impairment logs, storage-height evidence, tenant-risk notes, hot-work controls, or contractor certificates. Control point: signed broker/owner mandate, live underwriter request, owner-approved evidence access, contractor certificates, insurer response matrix, no-go/remediation table.
