## Lead Candidate: Broker-Routed Cyber Renewal Evidence Bind Lane

### Buyer

Primary channel buyer:

- Polish/CEE commercial insurance brokers;
- specialist cyber-insurance brokers;
- MGAs or coverholder teams serving SMEs;
- broker-adjacent risk consultants who already receive cyber questionnaire panic from clients.

Economic payer:

- 20-500 employee SMEs whose cyber policy quote, renewal, premium reduction, binder, customer-required insurance certificate, or contract renewal is blocked by underwriter evidence requests.

### Acute Trigger

The insured company has a live cyber-insurance underwriting, renewal, quote, or customer-required policy binder deadline and cannot produce clean evidence for underwriter questions such as:

- MFA coverage by system;
- EDR/MDR deployment and monitoring;
- backup scope, immutability, and restore-test proof;
- privileged/admin access controls;
- patch/vulnerability cadence;
- incident-response plan and contact chain;
- phishing/security training records;
- remote access and RDP exposure;
- security exceptions and compensating controls.

The commercial pain is concrete: no policy, worse premium, worse exclusions, delayed customer contract, blocked financing/procurement requirement, or frantic broker/MSP back-and-forth.

### Transferable Control Point

The founder controls the case only if all of the following are signed or captured:

- broker/MGA routing agreement covering named blocked cyber underwriting or renewal cases;
- broker-approved payment split or referral fee;
- insured client authorization to collect evidence from the client and its MSP/security vendor;
- current underwriter questionnaire, evidence request, or renewal/binder deadline;
- prepaid fixed case fee from the insured or broker;
- documented response channel through the broker, not direct insurance advice by the startup;
- case-memory rights for anonymized question/evidence taxonomy and outcome tracking.

The founder does not sell insurance, advise on coverage, act as broker of record, certify security controls, perform penetration testing, implement technical remediation, or give legal advice.

### Sixty-Day Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

1. Build a target list of 80 Polish/CEE commercial insurance brokers, cyber brokers, MGAs, and broker-adjacent risk consultants.
2. Build a redacted sample cyber underwriting evidence pack using public/sample questionnaire fields and dummy artifacts.
3. Sign two broker routing agreements where the broker can send named current blocked cases and retain 20-30% margin.
4. Receive six named live underwriting, renewal, or binder cases with actual questionnaire/request text.
5. Close at least four prepaid cases at 5,000-15,000 PLN each.
6. Collect at least 30,000 PLN before delivery, with no free pilots.
7. For at least two cases, obtain broker or underwriter acknowledgment that the evidence response was accepted, clarified, or sufficient to continue underwriting.
8. For cases requiring technical changes, route implementation to the client's MSP/security vendor and keep the startup's role limited to evidence organization and response drafting.
9. Capture anonymized case taxonomy: question wording, evidence object, source system, gap type, MSP action needed, underwriter follow-up, final disposition.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Broker pitch:

"Your SME client is trying to renew or bind cyber insurance, but the underwriter is asking for MFA, EDR, backup, admin-access, restore-test, or IR evidence the client and MSP cannot package quickly. Send us only live blocked cases. We turn the client's existing artifacts into an underwriter-ready response pack, separate real gaps from answerable questions, and route technical remediation back to the MSP. You keep 20-30% margin and stay broker of record."

Client pitch through broker:

"This is not a cyber audit or insurance advice. We organize your existing evidence against the actual underwriter questions, identify what your MSP must confirm or fix, and produce a broker-ready response pack for this policy deadline."

### Economics

Initial pricing:

- 5,000-7,500 PLN for a single-control or narrow questionnaire rescue;
- 8,000-15,000 PLN for a full renewal/binder evidence pack;
- 2,500-5,000 PLN annual refresh for the same insured if the prior case memory exists;
- 20-30% broker margin or referral split.

Gross margin logic:

- Founder handles intake, mapping, drafting, and broker coordination.
- A cyber reviewer is paid per flagged case section, not as an open-ended consultant.
- MSP/security implementation is out of scope and paid separately by the client if needed.
- Payback is immediate because the first two prepaid cases can cover setup costs.
- Owner earnings are credible only if the average full case stays below 10-14 founder hours plus scoped reviewer cost.

### Copy-Risk Defense

Brokers, MSPs, and cyber consultants can copy the generic idea. They cannot instantly copy:

- the signed routing relationship for named live broker cases;
- prepaid current-case intake;
- broker-specific response expectations;
- accepted underwriter question-to-evidence patterns;
- anonymized outcome memory across multiple brokers and carrier/MGA workflows.

The moat is not a template. It is the compounding library of what underwriters asked, what evidence was accepted, what created follow-up, which MSP artifacts mapped to which answers, and which responses moved a live renewal forward.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup does not sell broad cyber readiness, compliance reports, an app, dashboard, or brokered insurance. It only works on live underwriting, renewal, binder, or customer-required policy cases routed by brokers and prepaid by named clients. The controlled asset is current case flow plus accepted evidence memory, not a lead list or generic template.

### Safety And Legal Boundaries

- Licensed brokers remain broker of record and handle coverage, pricing, limits, exclusions, carrier communication, and policy advice.
- Underwriters/carriers make underwriting decisions.
- MSPs/security vendors remain provider of record for implementation and technical attestations.
- The startup does not certify controls, perform audits, promise policy approval, sell insurance, interpret policy wording, or provide legal advice.
- Evidence statements must be sourced to client/MSP artifacts and marked as unknown, not implemented, or requiring advisor/MSP confirmation where appropriate.
- Any personal data, security logs, or sensitive infrastructure material is handled under NDA/DPA and minimized.

### Duplicate-Risk Check

- Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: DORA serves software vendors blocked by regulated-finance ICT supplier/procurement evidence requests. This serves insured SMEs and brokers blocked by cyber-insurance underwriting and binder evidence.
- Not REDBlocked: no Amazon Seller Central or connected-device marketplace evidence.
- Not BioSignal: no health, biosignal, investor diligence, or clinical claims.
- Not TraceFaktura: no municipal animal-care event invoice routing.
- Not HeritageDoor: no physical salvage inventory.
- Not KRS continuity: no registered office/mail custody book.

## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- This can be dismissed as a cyber-consulting service unless the first proof shows broker-owned live case routing, named underwriter requests, and prepaid current cases.
- Brokers may prefer their existing MSP/cyber partners, or they may not want to introduce a new party into underwriting.
- MSP cooperation is uncertain; poor MSP evidence can turn the work into technical remediation pressure.
- Underwriter acceptance is not controlled by the founder.
- The buyer may resist paying if the broker believes evidence help should be free support.
- Cyber/security credibility matters; a solo founder must use a named reviewer and keep the scope narrow.
- The DORA confirmed idea already proved evidence-pack demand, so duplicate risk must stay explicit: this is insurance underwriting case flow, not regulated-finance vendor procurement.
