## Useful Narrowing

Best beachhead: Polish/CEE BESS distributors, C&I storage EPCs, industrial-battery importers, and non-EU battery OEMs selling through EU channels where a named buyer has paused a PO, tender, onboarding, shipment, or draw for carbon-footprint evidence.

Accept only cases with written buyer request or rejection, named commercial event, exact battery model, exact manufacturing plant, supplier authorization, existing declaration/draft/source evidence, qualified reviewer path, explicit no-go authority, and prepaid fixed fee.

Reject no named commercial blocker, no manufacturer authorization, no plant identity, no source-data path, generic Battery Regulation readiness, formal LCA calculation unless routed to a qualified provider, safety/fire/transport/grid/warranty disputes, and unsupported environmental claims.

## Lesson

Do not continue Battery Regulation carbon-footprint order-release variants unless the control point changes from response coordination to a harder owned payment stream, paid recurring account, assigned claim, or exclusive supplier/customer case. The fresh path should move away from formal compliance domains where TIC/LCA/lab incumbents own the decisive proof.
