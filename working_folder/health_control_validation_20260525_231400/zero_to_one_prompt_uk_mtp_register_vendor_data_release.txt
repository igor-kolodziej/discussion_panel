Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
UK MTP Register Vendor Data Release Pack

One-sentence thesis:
UK financial-services firms are starting to collect cleaner data on material third-party arrangements for operational-resilience and third-party reporting, so CEE and UK software, payments, regtech, KYC, risk, workflow, data, and outsourcing vendors need a fast buyer-ready field pack when a UK regulated customer blocks onboarding, renewal, change approval, or procurement until those supplier facts are returned.

Exact buyer:
Primary buyer: 20-300 person SaaS, payments, KYC/AML, fraud/risk, regtech, data, workflow, cloud-service, business-process, and specialist outsourcing vendors that sell to UK FCA/PRA-regulated firms, payment institutions, insurers, investment firms, building societies, financial-market infrastructure participants, or regulated-finance groups.

Best first buyer: a Polish, CEE, or UK vendor with one to ten UK regulated-finance customers and a live customer request for material third-party arrangement data, operational-resilience supplier data, subcontractor/service-country fields, incident-contact fields, exit/continuity evidence, or changed-since-last-review information tied to a renewal, onboarding, contract change, procurement approval, or go-live deadline.

Channel buyer: boutique DORA, UK operational-resilience, cyber, ISO/SOC, legal, fractional-CISO, compliance, and fintech advisory firms that want to keep higher-value advisory work while routing overflow field-pack assembly and evidence collation.

Acute trigger:
A UK regulated customer sends a vendor a spreadsheet, questionnaire, portal request, contract-change checklist, or procurement condition asking for material third-party arrangement fields and operational-resilience supplier facts. The vendor has scattered answers across contracts, data-processing records, subprocessor lists, cloud regions, service descriptions, support SLAs, incident playbooks, BCP/DR notes, exit-process notes, dependency maps, security evidence, and customer-success history. The commercial event is blocked because the vendor cannot return the data quickly and confidently.

Control point:
The startup controls the exact customer request, vendor authorization, secure evidence room, response workspace, field-by-field data pack, issue split, reviewer notes, buyer response trail, and accepted/no-go memory for that named customer process. The controlled artifact is not a generic compliance report; it is a customer-specific material-third-party arrangement data pack mapped to the customer's requested fields.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid vendor mandates with written UK regulated-customer requests attached.
2. At least one partner referral source that has seen similar UK operational-resilience or third-party reporting requests.
3. Three completed field packs covering service description, entity map, service locations, subcontractors/subprocessors, data categories, cloud/hosting dependencies, incident contacts, continuity/exit facts, support SLAs, evidence index, and "needs customer/legal/vendor owner" flags.
4. Two buyer-progress outcomes: customer accepts the pack for review, narrows follow-up, unblocks onboarding, allows renewal to continue, approves a change, or gives a clean no-go reason.
5. 45,000-120,000 PLN collected from fixed fees.
6. Rejection log showing at least 35% of inbound cases rejected for no written customer request, no commercial consequence, broad legal/compliance advice, missing evidence owner, formal operational-resilience consulting, regulated-firm-side work, or remediation as the real blocker.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build source-field map, intake checklist, secure evidence room, redacted sample pack, no-go rules, and reviewer bench for UK operational-resilience, cyber, and legal issue spotting.
Month 2: close three prepaid cases and one partner source; deliver first packs and capture buyer responses.
Months 3-4: process 10-18 paid packs across SaaS, payments, regtech, KYC/risk, and data vendors; collect accepted wording, buyer follow-up patterns, field variants, and no-go reasons.
Months 5-6: complete 25-45 paid packs; collect 300,000-850,000 PLN revenue; keep gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs; prove three partner channels route second cases; convert at least eight customers into annual refresh commitments for changed-since-last-review updates.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound only to vendors likely to have UK regulated-finance customers, using public customer logos, case studies, partner pages, procurement pages, and fintech directories. Message: "If a UK bank, insurer, PSP, investment firm, or regulated-finance customer has sent you a material third-party arrangement or operational-resilience supplier-data request, I turn your existing evidence into the customer-specific field pack in 7 business days. Fixed fee. No legal opinion, no certification, no broad operational-resilience program."

Parallel channel: boutique DORA, UK operational-resilience, cyber, ISO/SOC, legal, fractional-CISO, and fintech advisers that keep advisory/remediation/legal work while referring urgent data-pack assembly.

Economics:
Charge 12,000-30,000 PLN for a 7-business-day field pack depending on urgency, number of services/entities, and subcontractor complexity. Annual refresh is 6,000-14,000 PLN when the customer asks what changed since the last review. Reviewer costs are limited to flagged sections, not full consulting. The fee is justified only when a renewal, onboarding, procurement, change approval, or strategic UK regulated customer is worth at least 100,000 PLN or materially important to the vendor.

Copy risk:
DORA advisers, UK operational-resilience consultants, cyber firms, ISO/SOC consultants, law firms, GRC tools, fractional CISOs, procurement consultants, and internal customer-success/security teams can copy pieces. The defense is speed on exact live requests, field-level UK buyer wording memory, accepted-response examples, recurring annual refreshes, and partner-routed overflow from advisers that do not want to spend senior time assembling vendor facts.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
They can copy the category, but they cannot own a specific vendor mandate, evidence room, customer spreadsheet, response workspace, issue split, buyer response trail, and accepted/no-go learning for a named customer process once it is already routed to the startup. The startup's control is over the specific renewal/onboarding/change case and the reusable field memory it creates, not over the whole UK operational-resilience market.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The output is a buyer-specific material-third-party arrangement data pack for a live customer request. It includes field responses, evidence index, dependency map, subcontractor/service-country table, continuity/exit facts, incident-contact facts, changed-since-last-review notes, and issue flags. It is not a dashboard, marketplace, generic questionnaire service, legal memo, certification, operational-resilience program, GRC implementation, or adviser introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not advise UK regulated firms, determine whether an arrangement is material, interpret FCA/PRA rules, provide legal opinions, certify operational resilience, negotiate clauses, submit regulatory reports, run incident reporting, perform formal audits, or claim compliance. The vendor and its counsel, security lead, compliance owner, and customer remain responsible for final statements and regulated decisions. Legal, cyber, and operational-resilience reviewers are used only for issue spotting and escalation flags. Reject cases needing remediation, contract negotiation, formal testing, incident crisis response, regulated-firm-side register ownership, or materiality classification.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is closest to DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, but the trigger and proof artifact are different. DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack answers EU DORA/ICT third-party-risk requests and Article 28/30-style evidence for regulated-finance renewals. This idea answers UK FCA/PRA material-third-party arrangement and operational-resilience supplier-data requests with a register-field data pack, dependency table, incident-contact facts, continuity/exit fields, and changed-since-last-review notes. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, or an Amazon/platform evidence desk.

Strongest anticipated objections:
This may be judged too close to the confirmed DORA vendor evidence idea.
UK operational-resilience advisers, DORA consultants, law firms, cyber firms, GRC tools, ISO/SOC consultants, and fractional CISOs can copy the workflow.
Many customer requests will expand into legal interpretation, materiality classification, contract clauses, remediation, formal testing, or regulated-firm-side reporting.
UK customers may prefer responses from known advisers or the vendor's existing counsel/security team.
Vendors may not have the underlying evidence, especially around subcontractors, cloud dependencies, exit processes, incident contacts, or service-country details.
The 2027 application date may make some buyers slow to pay before the reporting cycle becomes urgent.
The Warsaw founder may have weaker credibility in UK financial-services procurement without partner referrals.
Annual refresh value depends on repeated customer requests and buyer wording memory, not on a software lock-in.
Partner channels can internalize once they see demand.
Sensitive contracts, dependency maps, incident processes, subprocessor records, cloud architecture, and customer names require strong confidentiality and access controls.
