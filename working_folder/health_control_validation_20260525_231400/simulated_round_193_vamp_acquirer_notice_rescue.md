## Lead Candidate: VAMP Acquirer Notice Rescue Desk

### Why It Advances

- The trigger is live payment-account risk, not a speculative fraud audit: written acquirer/PSP notice, VAMP/risk threshold warning, fine/reserve/termination threat, or processor remediation request.
- First proof is tied to cash/account outcome: reserve reduction/release, fine avoided/credited, processing limit avoided, MID kept open, acquirer-accepted plan, or clean no-go.
- Agencies and PSP-risk consultants can route urgent cases, but many chargeback tools focus on representment rather than assembling an acquirer-ready survival file with exact network/acquirer wording.
- The founder does not move funds, give legal advice, process payments, underwrite merchants, or sell chargeback representation as the core product.

### Internal Objections

- Chargeback/fraud/PSP consultants, anti-fraud vendors, and acquirers are natural incumbents.
- Many merchants are genuinely bad risk, so the correct output is no-go, not rescue.
- Sensitive payment, fraud, customer, order, refund, and dispute data creates trust friction.
- Acquirer acceptance is outside founder control.
- Success attribution can be hard if ratios naturally improve or the merchant changes behavior.
- High-risk merchants can create reputational, legal, and fraud exposure.
