4. Liability and role confusion are non-trivial because the startup is leasing/routing medical-adjacent equipment and handling documentation.
5. Same/next-day uptime is operationally hard for a part-time solo founder.
6. Gross margin is fragile after logistics, subcontractors, parts, depreciation, insurance, and disputed device/certificate issues.
7. The desired seller book may not exist in clean transferable form.
8. Clinics may not pay until inspection or failure creates urgency.
