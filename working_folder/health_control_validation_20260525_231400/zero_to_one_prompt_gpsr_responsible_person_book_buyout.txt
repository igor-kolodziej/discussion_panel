Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
GPSR Responsible-Person Label Continuity Book Buyout

One-sentence thesis:
Option or buy a small EU Responsible Person / authorised-representative provider book serving non-EU sellers of low-risk non-food consumer products whose live EU marketplace listings and packaging already show that provider's EU contact details, then transfer only customer-approved successor mandates and annual renewal cash before paying the seller.

Exact buyer:
Existing paying clients in an acquired micro-provider book: non-EU Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, and DTC sellers of simple low-risk consumer products such as home decor, stationery, pet accessories without food or electronics, simple bags, simple textiles, and non-electrical accessories, where EU listings or labels already depend on a named EU Responsible Person / authorised representative contact.

Acute trigger:
The current EU Responsible Person provider is retiring, overloaded, terminating the service, repricing sharply, or refusing categories, while sellers need continuity because EU listings, packaging, buyer onboarding, or marketplace compliance fields already show the provider's EU name, postal address, and electronic contact. If the role is not transferred cleanly, sellers risk listing suppression, buyer rejection, relabelling work, authority-contact confusion, or losing the current compliance contact route.

Control point:
The startup signs an option over a named micro-provider book before closing, covering the customer list, current invoices, renewal calendar, RP/AR mandates where transferable, product-file index, contact inbox, address workflow, authority-request log, seller endorsement, and handoff process. The startup closes only on customers that sign a successor mandate, approve payment direction, prepay or renew for the next term, and pass the strict product-category filter.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
One provider option signed; at least 20 customer-approved successor mandates; at least 60,000 PLN in annual renewal or prepaid continuity fees collected or escrowed before seller payout; at least 200 low-risk SKUs transferred into a controlled file index; successor contact workflow and inbox live; first marketplace, buyer, or seller-account continuity acknowledgement showing the successor RP details were accepted or the listing remained live after the transition.

6-month POC:
Option or acquire one book with 40-80 retained clients and 100,000-250,000 PLN annualized renewal revenue; retain at least 70% of consenting clients after handoff; process at least 500 low-risk SKU file transfers; resolve or cleanly no-go at least 30 listing, buyer, or authority-contact continuity requests; keep average admin/reviewer time below 45 minutes per retained seller per month; collect at least 120,000 PLN cash with gross margin above 65% after reviewer, insurance, address/inbox, legal-template, and contractor costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound to small EU Responsible Person, authorised-representative, compliance-address, and GPSR service providers that appear on seller listings, public service pages, marketplace agency pages, or seller-forum recommendations and look owner-operated. The offer is a low-upfront option plus seller earnout tied only to retained prepaid renewals, so the seller gets continuity for clients without remaining the ongoing market-surveillance contact.

Economics:
Charge 1,200-4,000 PLN per seller per year for the low-risk RP continuity subscription, with SKU/file bands and separate prepaid authority-notice or buyer-request handling. Pay the seller mainly through an earnout on retained collected renewals, keeping upfront cash below 100,000 PLN. Target 65-75% gross margin because the service is annual file custody, contact continuity, inbox/address operations, and bounded response coordination, not lab testing, legal opinions, product certification, marketplace appeals, or broad compliance consulting.

Copy risk:
Large AR/RP providers, product-compliance firms, lawyers, and Amazon agencies can sell GPSR services, but they do not control the retiring provider's specific customer book, current labels/listings naming that provider, renewal calendar, inbox history, product-file index, and seller-endorsed handoff before the startup options it. The defense is not a checklist; it is book-level control plus renewal cash before close.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
An incumbent can market to the category, but cannot legally step into this provider's named client contracts, mailbox, authority-request history, renewal invoices, and seller-endorsed transition without access to the provider and customer approvals. The startup only buys retained mandates after customers prepay or approve payment direction, so control is established at the account and payment-stream level before competitors see the book.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business is an acquisition-led continuity rollup of existing RP/AR customer books. It owns signed successor mandates, current renewal invoices, contact workflow, file custody, and payment direction for named customers. It does not sell generic GPSR readiness, write reports, broker introductions, build a compliance dashboard, or promise marketplace reinstatement.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Accept only low-risk non-food, non-electrical, non-connected, non-medical, non-cosmetic, non-supplement, non-toy, non-PPE, non-battery, non-e-mobility, non-baby/safety-critical products with existing truthful product files and clear manufacturer identity. Do not certify product safety, create missing tests, issue legal opinions, advise on product classification, act as importer of record, sell products, handle recalls as a recall manager, or make marketplace appeal promises. Use legal template review, insurance review, secure file handling, customer authorization, and stop/no-go escalation for any unsafe, incomplete, suspicious, high-risk, or authority-sensitive case.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from REDBlocked because it is not an Amazon evidence-response desk for connected devices under RED/CE/GPSR triggers; it is a recurring provider-of-record continuity book for low-risk products already naming a micro-provider. It is distinct from GreenTender EPREL and other evidence-pack ideas because the control point is signed RP successor mandates and renewal payment direction, not a product database packet or buyer-response pack. It is distinct from HeritageDoor because there is no physical salvage lot; the asset is a live provider book and payment stream.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Clean seller books may be rare; many providers serve risky categories that must be rejected; legal/product-safety liability may be higher than the fees justify; customers may use the transition to move to a larger provider; marketplaces may still reject changes or ask for documents outside scope; sellers may have fake or incomplete product files; low-risk categories may be too low-ARPU; incumbent AR/RP providers can market aggressively once the opportunity is visible; the founder needs strict insurance, legal templates, secure file handling, and reviewer discipline from day one.
