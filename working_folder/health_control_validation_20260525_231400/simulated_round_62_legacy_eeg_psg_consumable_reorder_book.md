## Materially Different Candidate

`Legacy EEG/PSG Consumable + Reorder Book Buyout`

This buys or options a retiring/closing micro-distributor, service technician, sleep-lab supplier, or neurodiagnostic lab's exact-SKU consumable/accessory stock and active reorder book for EEG/PSG/EMG/sleep-study labs. The startup controls titled stock, compatibility map, phone/domain/mailbox, customer reorder history, supplier contacts, and prepaid emergency/reorder packs.

The founder does not run EEG/PSG tests, diagnose, interpret signals, handle reports, access patient recordings, provide clinical advice, or market to patients. Clinics/labs remain providers of record. The business is a B2B spare/consumable continuity bank for professional buyers.

## Buyer / Payer

- private EEG/EMG/PSG/sleep labs;
- neurology, psychiatry, sleep-medicine, ENT, pulmonology, occupational-medicine, and hospital labs with older neurodiagnostic systems;
- service technicians that need exact replacement cables/connectors/electrodes/sensors;
- small labs that lose revenue when a study is cancelled because one cap, belt, cable, airflow sensor, electrode, paste/gel, cannula, adapter, or headbox lead is missing.

## Acute Trigger

A lab has paid tests scheduled but an old-system accessory fails or goes missing. OEM lead times, discontinued connectors, small order value, procurement friction, and poor compatibility knowledge can cancel private EEG/PSG nights or delay reports. The urgent buyer pays for exact compatible stock now, not a generic catalogue.

## Control Point

The acquired/optioned asset is:

- titled exact-SKU inventory: EEG electrodes/leads/caps, PSG belts, airflow sensors/cannulas, oximeter probes, headbox cables, EMG leads, gels/pastes, adapters, chargers, dongles, and legacy connectors;
- SKU/serial/compatibility map by device model and lab;
- retiring supplier/service-technician customer list and reorder history;
- phone/domain/mailbox/WhatsApp handoff;
- supplier/distributor accounts and first-look rights to closing/upgrading lab accessory lots;
- condition, expiry, CE/instructions, lot, warranty, and storage records where relevant;
- prepaid emergency kits or monthly reorder packs from named labs;
- no patient data, no recordings, no test reports.

The hard control is: titled scarce stock + inherited reorder book + compatibility knowledge + prepaid B2B lab orders.

Required proof:

2. Titled inspected inventory lot under `45,000 PLN`, excluding expired, damaged, or legally questionable stock.
3. SKU/compatibility map covering at least 80% of the first lot by device/lab model.
4. 8-12 labs sign prepaid emergency-kit or monthly reorder-pack orders before final close.
5. `25,000+ PLN` prepaid or invoiced first-month B2B orders.
6. First 20 paid shipments delivered with zero patient data and zero clinical claims.
7. Gross margin above `35%` after stock cost, shipping, returns, damaged/obsolete reserve, seller earnout, and admin time.
8. Legal review confirms B2B professional-only medical-device supply boundaries and advertising/distributor obligations.

## Economics

Revenue:

- emergency replacement kits priced at a premium for exact compatibility;
- monthly/quarterly reorder packs for common electrodes, gels, pastes, cannulas, belts, probes, and adapters;
- tested legacy cable/connector resale;
- small retainer for reserved emergency stock for high-value labs.

Payback logic:

The founder does not buy broad catalogue inventory. The founder options an inherited reorder book, gets prepaid lab orders, then buys the exact stock lot needed to fulfill. Payback can happen in 1-3 inventory turns if the stock is scarce, clean, and matched to named labs.

## Why This Is Not A Service/Report/Dashboard/Marketplace

The business is not testing, interpretation, software, or a marketplace. It owns physical inventory and inherited order flow. A competitor can sell generic accessories, but cannot copy the specific titled stock lot, compatibility notes, old supplier phone trust, and first-month prepaid lab demand.

## Copy Risk

OEMs, distributors, and online shops can sell common accessories. The defensible wedge is older systems, exact connectors, emergency availability, inherited reorder history, first-look rights to closing lab stock, and lab-specific compatibility notes. If the first lot is commodity stock, the score falls below 82.
