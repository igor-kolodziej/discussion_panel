## Selected Candidate

**CycleGuard Genetics: IVF Carrier-Screening Consent & Sample-Completion Rail**

The idea is not to sell genetic tests, interpret results, or provide medical advice. It is a clinic-routed, neutral consent, risk-triage, and sample-completion workflow for IVF couples at the exact moment carrier screening or PGT-related genetic add-ons are discussed.

The primary control point is a signed pilot where one IVF clinic routes all eligible self-pay carrier-screening decision moments for at least 20 couples through CycleGuard before the test order, deferral, or geneticist escalation is finalized.

The reason this candidate advances is that the founder does not need a hospital procurement cycle, privileged medical status, diagnostic authority, or large software build. The 2-month proof is a paid clinic pilot, a contracted clinical genetics reviewer for escalations, lab/courier sample logistics through ordinary commercial channels, and measurable completion/doctor-time results.
