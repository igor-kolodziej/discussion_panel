# Research Notes

## Market And Timing

- Polish Ministry of Health IVF program runs from 2024 to 31 December 2028 and is funded with at least 500 million PLN annually. The ministry reports, as of 15 May 2026, 58 providers confirming 50,778 qualified couples, 28,757 clinical pregnancies, and 14,362 births under the program. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/in-vitro
- The official program page links to a map of participating IVF providers, creating a practical outbound target list for a no-status founder. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/in-vitro

## Genetic Workflow Context

- ESHRE describes expanded carrier screening for reproductive purposes as identifying couples/individuals carrying pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants for autosomal recessive or X-linked conditions, primarily to assess risks to future offspring rather than diagnose parents. Source: https://www.eshre.eu/ECS-guideline
- ESHRE's ECS good-practice work explicitly covers implementation and management pathways, genetic counselling requirements, informed consent, ethics/legal frameworks, equity, data privacy, and reporting thresholds. Source: https://www.eshre.eu/ECS-guideline
- Private Polish IVF clinics already list meaningful genetic add-on economics. Examples checked:
  - Artvimed lists PANDA Carrier for a couple at 5,800 PLN and PANDA Complete for a couple at 8,800 PLN; it also lists PGT-M setup/biopsy at 10,500 PLN and per-embryo PGT-M at 2,500 PLN. Source: https://artvimed.pl/cennik/
  - Fertilita lists embryo genetic testing including PGT-A, PGT-SR, and PGT-M from 22,000 PLN. Source: https://fertilita.pl/cennik/
  - Medisticum lists PGT-A first embryo at 5,000 PLN and PGT-M first embryo from 14,500 PLN. Source: https://medisticum.pl/cennik/

## Regulatory Boundaries

- The idea must avoid becoming an unregulated diagnostic or treatment-decision tool. EU medical device and IVD regulation applies to medical devices and in-vitro diagnostics; the POC should route consent, logistics, and human expert escalation, not independently interpret genetic results. Source: https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations_en
- Genetic and health-related data are sensitive personal data under European Commission GDPR guidance; the POC must use explicit consent, data minimization, secure storage, and processor/controller agreements with clinics. Source: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/rules-business-and-organisations/legal-grounds-processing-data/sensitive-data/what-personal-data-considered-sensitive_en

## Strategic Interpretation

The underused momentum is not "genetics is growing." It is the collision of state-funded IVF volume, private genetic add-on prices, ethical discomfort around selling add-ons to anxious couples, and a clinic manager's need to reduce physician/admin explanation time without harming trust.

The control point is the routed decision moment before carrier screening is ordered, declined, or escalated. If the founder only writes educational content or compares tests, the idea collapses into a generic report/database. If a clinic signs a paid 20-couple pilot and routes every eligible decision through the workflow, CycleGuard controls a narrow but economically valuable workflow.
