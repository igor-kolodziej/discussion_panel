# Candidate Funnel

This funnel starts from paid events and controllable resources. It does not assign validation scores.

## Why The Three Finalists Survive Candidate-Generation Screens

- **BenchHire Direct:** the company becomes the specialist recruitment intermediary, so a signed requisition and deposit route the commercial event without an external platform partner. Its modeled scale supports founder equity above 5 million PLN, and a paid mandate can be tested below 40,000 PLN within 180 days.
- **FallbackLine:** a customer-controlled call-forwarding rule can route a full after-hours window; a paid independent-contractor pilot is possible without building proprietary telephony. Its wealth path requires multi-country productization, which is explicitly a hypothesis rather than evidence.
- **GownTrace:** a site can contract an all-operator annual program, while the founder hires cleanroom expertise as a bounded paid input. A paid protocol-mapping engagement is reachable, although conversion into a trusted requalification program remains unproven.

Candidate generation only — not validated.
