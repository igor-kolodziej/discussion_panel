# Zero-to-One Evaluation: LedgerTrial v1

## Diagnosis

LedgerTrial is a legitimate structural pivot: it replaces acquisition financing with a bounded, paid known-answer test and makes human–AI interaction data intrinsic to delivery. Its strongest commercial insight is that bookkeeping owners need to test the combined automation-plus-reviewer control, while software benchmarks and training assess the two halves separately. The first paid proof is unusually reachable and the recurring economics can clear the founder-wealth gate without a heroic valuation multiple. The main uncertainty is whether independent proficiency testing earns a 45,000–60,000 PLN annual budget and renewal, or collapses into a one-off consulting benchmark that accounting-software and training incumbents can copy.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: KSeF 2.0 is live and has an official anonymized-data test environment; AI invoice/accounting automation is commercially offered; ICAS reports material accountant concern about AI errors and continued need for human judgment; recent accounting benchmarks show end-task accuracy gaps; and several AI-literacy platforms already sell role-specific assessment and evidence. The competitor evidence strengthens the need but removes any claim that assessment itself is novel. Hypotheses: a 9,500 PLN trial changes a rollout/control decision, 4,900 PLN monthly renewal exists, synthetic batches represent production risk, response rights survive contracting, direct-outbound CAC stays below 8,000 PLN, 80% logo retention is possible, and at least 1,200 qualified buyers support a 100-account conservative endpoint. No deposit, retention cohort or verified buyer census exists yet.

## Overall Score

**82/100.** Weighted points are 164 out of 200; `10 × (164 / 20) = 82`. This is an advisory score only and has no validation status.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** A paid annual contract routes a named workflow through confidential serialized batches and grants rights to de-identified outcome features. The scenario corpus, longitudinal customer history and pooled cross-firm benchmark can create switching cost and a data network effect, all provable within twelve months. The strongest copy risk is an installed accounting-software vendor or major AI-literacy/training firm adding known-answer workflow simulations and using its existing customer base before LedgerTrial accumulates a meaningful result corpus. The specific speed, independence and compounding-data path justify exceeding the ordinary copyability cap, but no Tier-1 scarcity is claimed.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, conditional on a verified denominator and recurring price.** The conservative case states 100 accounts at approximately 60,000 PLN recurring annual revenue plus baseline work, 6.285m PLN revenue, 75% gross margin, 29% operating margin, no debt, 90% founder ownership, 95% reinvestment and a 4× operating-profit value. That yields about 6.55m PLN founder equity at year eight without double-counting distributions. Expected and strong cases state customers, pricing, revenue, margins, capital, debt, dilution, reinvestment, value and timing. One hundred accounts is not intrinsically heroic across bookkeeping firms and in-house AP teams, but it becomes heroic if the promised 1,200 qualified triggered buyers or 45,000 PLN minimum ACV cannot be verified; those are hard kill conditions before a sales build-out.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The founder can offer a 9,500 PLN fixed trial and seek a 4,000 PLN non-refundable deposit within sixty days while risking at most 17,000 PLN before payment. One contracted accountant, counsel and a synthetic workspace are bounded paid inputs rather than speculative partners. No production data, ERP integration, legal authority, full platform or departure from employment is required. The decisive proof is not the deposit alone: delivery must have positive contribution and change a named rollout/control/vendor decision before the next tranche.

## Expansion Check

The first niche is narrow enough to learn and the Polish KSeF layer slows horizontal entrants. Adding adjacent accounting workflows reuses the same buyer and data model; in-house AP and one CEE pack are plausible only after repeat payment. Accounting vendors can move inward with more distribution, while horizontal training platforms can add role scenarios. LedgerTrial's outward advantage depends on accumulating cross-vendor failure traces and an independent benchmark faster than either class values the niche.

## Fatal Objections

1. The buyer may pay once before rollout but reject a recurring proficiency subscription, destroying retention and the wealth model.
2. Synthetic batch performance may not be representative enough to change a production decision, reducing the output to an interesting report.
3. Cross-client response rights may be restricted by employee/privacy terms, preventing the pooled benchmark from becoming a control point.
4. The conservative 100-account endpoint and 45,000–60,000 PLN ACV lack a verified bottom-up buyer census or comparable spend evidence.
5. An installed vendor or large training/audit firm can copy the format before LedgerTrial's data lead and switching costs exist.

None is a planning-stage veto because the exact paid offer, decision-effect criterion, data clause, first-five conversion test and buyer-census kill expose them before large capital. They must not be treated as validated facts.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Test the combined human–AI workflow with confidential known-answer inputs and sell the resulting operational decision, while retaining a lawful pooled outcome benchmark as a byproduct of paid delivery.
