## Why Only One Finalist Survives

The generator normally selects three to six finalists, but padding the set would violate its hard-gate requirement. HumanFactor Works is materially distinct because the founder acquires a finite local distribution asset and recurring paid contracts, while qualified delivery is retained through employment and seller transition rather than hoped-for partnership. Every other Wave-2 direction either fails the Founder Wealth Gate, requires uncontrolled clinical/vendor authority, or starts from generic purchasable technology.
