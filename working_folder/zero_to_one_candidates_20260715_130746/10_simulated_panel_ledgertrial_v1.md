### Ranked Objections

1. **POTENTIALLY FATAL — recurring willingness to pay.** A 9,500 PLN rollout check may sell while a 58,800 PLN annual subscription does not. Without recurrence, the wealth path, data flywheel and switching-cost thesis collapse into a boutique service.

3. Reusable de-identified reviewer rights and cross-firm comparability may fail simultaneously; heterogeneous workflows can make the pooled percentile scientifically weak.
4. The cold-outbound funnel—200 contacts to 40 substantive calls and two deposits—is a hypothesis, not distribution evidence, especially for an unknown non-accountant founder.
5. The 1,200 triggered-buyer denominator, subscription costs, 70–79% gross margins and accountant effort are unverified; early delivery-cost assumptions look optimistic.
6. Independence is commercially useful but not itself defensibility; incumbents possess stronger trust, domain staffing and installed channels.

Recommendation: **research further.** No fatal fact is presently established, but the first two objections are explicit kill conditions.

### Evidence Versus Hypotheses

The timing evidence is real: the Polish Ministry confirmed KSeF 2.0's 1 February 2026 launch. The ICAS evidence shows concern about AI errors, but it is based on UK accounting professionals and does not demonstrate Polish purchasing behavior. FinBalance demonstrates substantial model-level accounting errors, but not the validity of LedgerTrial's human-workflow product or buyer willingness to pay. Article 4 creates AI-literacy obligations, not a mandate to buy recurring proficiency testing.

Pricing, recurring cadence, conversion, gross margins, outbound funnel, retention, outcome-data rights, the 1,200-buyer denominator, synthetic representativeness and valuation remain planning-stage hypotheses. The proposed deposit, decision-effect, reuse-rights, subscription-conversion, margin and market-census tests control these hypotheses unusually well before serious capital is exposed. Long-term retention and copy resilience cannot be established inside the first 180 days.

### Founder Wealth Gate

**PASS, narrowly.** The candidate provides coherent conservative, expected and strong scenarios with ownership, dilution, debt, reinvestment, operating profit and no double-counting. The conservative case reaches approximately 6.55m PLN founder equity at year eight. At a 3× rather than 4× operating-profit multiple, 90% of the conservative business value is approximately 4.92m PLN, below the gate. The pass therefore depends on proving that LedgerTrial is recurring and transferable rather than founder-heavy, and on validating the 1,200-buyer denominator before sales hiring.

### Reachability Gate

**PASS.** A 4,000 PLN non-refundable deposit is credible paid proof. Maximum pre-deposit exposure is stated as 17,000 PLN, 180-day exposure is capped at 40,000 PLN, no full platform or production integration precedes payment, and fixed-scope specialists cover accounting and legal gaps. This is compatible with employment and the five-hour daily limit.

### Control Point And Copy Risk

**Tier 2, provisional and unearned.** Contractual outcome rights, a confidential corpus, paired human-model traces and longitudinal customer history could become a repeatably accessible compounding asset within twelve months. It is not Tier 1: nothing is exclusive, reserved, prepaid at scale or presently controlled. If early buyers reject reuse rights or recurring contracts, it falls to Tier 3.

The strongest copy risk is an installed accounting-software vendor adding native challenge batches and benchmarking with existing workflow access, telemetry, trust and distribution. It could neutralize independence by using an outside accountant or assurance partner. Before LedgerTrial has a meaningful cross-vendor dataset, this is close to a routine product addition and justifies keeping the score below 80.

### Ranked Objections And Caveats

1. **Recurring willingness to pay is the conditional fatal objection.** Concern about errors may support a one-off baseline but not a 4,900 PLN monthly subscription. Without recurrence, the pooled benchmark, switching cost, margins and founder-wealth path collapse.
2. **Representativeness creates an integration dilemma.** A separate controlled workspace may not reproduce actual master data, software rules, prompts, mappings, permissions and exception handling. Native integration could make delivery bespoke and invalidate the stated costs and speed.
3. **The control point has a severe cold start.** Peer percentiles are weak with few heterogeneous firms, while the most commercially useful data rights may be the hardest terms for risk-sensitive buyers to grant.
4. **Direct-cost assumptions may omit real delivery work.** Scenario maintenance, configuration matching, reviewer support, disputes, legal review and report interpretation could materially reduce gross contribution.
5. **Outbound economics are only specified, not evidenced.** Forty substantive calls from 200 cold firms and two deposits is plausible enough to test, but aggressive for an unknown non-accountant selling a trust-sensitive control product.
6. **The wealth denominator is not established.** One hundred recurring accounts is conservative only if 1,200 genuinely triggered, ACV-capable buyers exist and 80%+ retention is attainable.
7. **KSeF and the AI Act provide urgency, not a purchasing mandate.** The commercial case must stand entirely on changed deployment and loss-prevention decisions.

Fatal objection: **None demonstrated yet.** The leading conditional fatal objection is failure to convert paid baselines into annual recurring contracts. Failure to produce decision-grade results without bespoke integration would be a second structural fatality.

Recommendation: **research further.** Execute the paid-proof sequence; do not treat the concept as qualified for a serious platform build or scaling expenditure.

## Common Failure Mechanism And Routing Decision

Both reviewers independently identified recurring willingness to pay as the conditional fatal mechanism: a one-off synthetic rollout test does not create the renewal, data flywheel, switching cost or founder-wealth path. Both also identified synthetic-to-production validity as a structural dilemma. The next revision must leave the proficiency-test/benchmark business shape and own an actual repeated economic outcome; polishing price, scenarios or the buyer census would be cosmetic.
