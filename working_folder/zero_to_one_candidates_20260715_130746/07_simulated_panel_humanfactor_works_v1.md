### Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** This is the fatal objection against pursuing the candidate as written. The terminal-value arithmetic avoids obvious double-counting, but the conservative case is not a credible conservative financing model:

- Founder equity reaches only 5.36m PLN, leaving roughly 7% headroom above the gate.
- It simultaneously assumes 700 clients, 22% operating margin, a 4× multiple, 90% ownership, and only 400,000 PLN remaining debt.
- The stated 100,000 PLN founder capital plus roughly 900,000 PLN cumulative seller-note/retained-cash funding is not reconciled against acquiring roughly seven books.
- No annual cash-flow schedule shows taxes, note principal and interest, earnouts, working capital, operator recruitment, or later acquisition consideration.
- The only demonstrated organic channel is referrals; it is not quantified sufficiently to fill any acquisition-funding gap.

A modest miss in margin, valuation multiple, customer count, debt, or ownership takes the conservative case below 5m PLN.

### Reachability Gate

That fits the capital and employment constraints. Within 180 days, the founder must still align a qualifying seller, acceptable financing, lawful customer outreach, 70% revenue retention, 15 customer payments, seller transition, a backup specialist, and a module deposit. The sequence is ambitious, but the staged option and hard stop contain downside sufficiently for this gate.
