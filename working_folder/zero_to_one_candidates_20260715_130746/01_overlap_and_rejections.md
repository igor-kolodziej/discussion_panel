## Distilled Takeaways From `TRIED_IDEAS_AND_FAILURES_BRAIN.md`

1. The startup must own or contract the recurring economic event, not merely the sensor, parser, benchmark, report, or interface used around it.
2. A future physiological or behavioral dataset counts only when ordinary paid delivery lawfully acquires durable, usable rights; hoped-for derivative rights and revocable panels are not a moat.
3. Purchasable components, named inventory lots, ordinary test fixtures, and filed-but-unoptioned patents are not scarce control points.
4. Regulation creates relevance, not willingness to pay, vendor authority, or procurement speed.
5. Safety, clinical, QMS, certification, and OEM trust remain first-sale dependencies even when a proposal assigns formal responsibility to the customer.
6. Intelligent wearables are distribution and workflow businesses before they are sensing businesses; incumbents often own installed buyers, conformity, service logistics, and warranty trust.
7. Reachable paid proof cannot compensate for an implausible buyer denominator, near-total niche penetration, labor-scaled delivery, or unsupported margins.
8. A strong shape should route an economically mandatory or recurring event and produce a reusable lab asset as a byproduct of paid delivery.

## Additional Lessons From The Most Recent 2026-07-15 Run

- Consumer self-binding and poker-stable monitoring were killed by a small/opaque buyer denominator and buyer-owned enforcement/data.
- Generic first-person installation evidence was already recognized as vulnerable to CompanyCam, Jobber, ServiceTitan, and similar field-workflow incumbents.
- Robotics/dexterity data licensing failed when buyers required robot-specific or exclusive collection.
- Wearable returns/repair and EOL inventory failed when incumbent logistics providers already owned custody and trust.
- A future search should begin from founder-controlled access or a mandatory transaction and then prove an independent paying buyer, not from an interesting technical asset seeking a budget.

## Rejected Shape Families Unless Structurally Changed

- Reference phantoms, calibration coupons, artificial materials, QA fixtures, or ordinary hardware components.
- Wearable accessories, repair, returns, rental, or inventory without exclusive rights or unavoidable transaction control.
- Clinical-study QA, evidence archives, vigilance, or acceptance tools requiring sponsor/OEM/QMS authority.
- Revocable human panels, generic data foundries, or task corpora licensed without a profitable primary workflow.
- Safety/medical devices needing certification or incumbent adoption before paid proof.
- Generic field-service capture, dashboards, CRMs, compliance memos, consulting, or AI wrappers.
- Consumer subscriptions that require fragmented trust and large paid membership before data or distribution compounds.

## Material-Improvement Standard

A prior family can re-enter only if the paying buyer, painful event, budget owner, channel, value capture, trust mechanism, rights, transaction control, or non-heroic wealth denominator materially changes. New names, features, sensors, or presentation do not qualify.

## Rejections Added During This Run

- **Generic AI-to-human call recovery:** killed because telephony, models and dispatch labor are commodity inputs; the wealth path required a large unsupported SMB account base.
- **Instrumented cleanroom gowning requalification:** killed because site QA authority and expert trust precede the core sale and incumbents can add capture tooling.
- **Specialist assessment-led recruitment:** despite owning the placement transaction, it remained reproducible by incumbent agencies before the outcome dataset could compound.
- **Real-work overflow-to-hire microfactory:** a material pivot, but killed after re-evaluation because it substituted unknown-supplier trust for unknown-recruiter trust and depended on thin contract-manufacturing margins.

The trade-workforce, work-sample, production-audition and overflow-to-hire family is exhausted unless a future candidate has a different buyer, authority and scarce resource.
