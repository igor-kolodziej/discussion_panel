# Research And Sources — Wave 2

Research date: 2026-07-15. Evidence supports the market structure and legal obligations; seller willingness, deal price and target economics remain hypotheses.

## Polish BHP Market And Recurring Demand

- Poland's Central Institute for Labour Protection estimates approximately 5,600 entities provide BHP services. Its study also states that about one third of companies with more than 100 workers use external BHP specialists alongside internal services. This supports fragmentation, not the availability of an acquisition target. [CIOP-PIB market summary](https://www.ciop.pl/CIOPPortalWAR/appmanager/ciop/pl?_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=P55400856461607686482841)
- The State Labour Inspection says companies with up to 100 employees may assign BHP duties internally or, where competent employees are unavailable, use qualified external specialists. The specialist must meet qualification requirements. [PIP on BHP service organization](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/pytania-i-odpowiedzi/kiedy-nalezy-stworzyc-w-zakladzie-pracy-sluzbe-bhp)
- Employers must provide initial training before work and repeat preventive examinations and training. [Polish Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Policy](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/informacje-dla-pracownika-BHP)
- PIP explains which employees require periodic BHP training, who may organize it and the prescribed timing framework. [PIP training guidance](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/porady-prawne/szkolenia-w-dziedzinie-bezpieczenstwa-i-higieny-pracy)
- Published commercial pricing indicates approximately 500–800 PLN monthly for small-company BHP service and 800–1,500 PLN for 50–100 employees; periodic worker training is advertised around 100–300 PLN per person depending on cohort and role. These are vendor claims, not audited market averages. [WSBHP pricing discussion](https://www.wsbhp.pl/uslugi/), [Perfex 2026 price list](https://www.perfex.net.pl/cennik.pdf), [SEKA worker training](https://www.seka.pl/katalog-szkolen/szkolenie-okresowe-bhp-dla-pracownikow-zatrudnionych-na-stanowiskach-robotniczych-z-uwzglednieniem-prac-szczegolnie-niebezpiecznych-2825)

## Acquisition And Consolidation Evidence

- No source found establishes a pipeline of retiring Polish BHP owners or a 3× operating-profit purchase price. Those are first-test hypotheses.
- The Polish government notes that customer goodwill and enterprise assets can transfer, while licenses and administrative permissions may require separate action. Deal counsel must structure asset, company, employee and data continuity. [Biznes.gov.pl on ownership change](https://biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/00347)
- European safety services are actively consolidated: Presto reportedly reached more than 100,000 B2B customers through over 70 add-on acquisitions; Fulgard and other occupational-safety providers have been acquired; current occupational health/safety groups continue buy-and-build strategies. This demonstrates a mature strategic playbook at larger scale, not proof that the founder can source a Polish micro-deal. [Adelis/Presto case](https://adelisequity.com/cases/presto/), [Seven2/Fulgard](https://www.seven2.eu/en/2024/10/23/seven2-acquires-fulgard-a-leading-italian-workplace-safety-specialist-from-argos-wityu/), [Phenna/MDS](https://phennagroup.com/news/phenna-group-acquires-mds/)

## Human-Factors Technology Competition

- Soter sells a wearable sensor with haptic coaching and enterprise ergonomics analytics. [SoterCoach](https://www.soter.com/sotercoach)
- Ergovision and Ergzo advertise phone-camera REBA/RULA or multi-method risk assessment. The proposed acquisition cannot claim proprietary advantage from ordinary pose estimation. [Ergovision](https://visionify.ai/ergovision), [Ergzo](https://www.ergzo.com/)
- The finalist therefore treats instrumented human-factors modules as cross-sell and learning products. Its first defensible asset is the acquired local contract distribution, qualified professionals and cash flow—not an AI model.

## Directions Rejected By Evidence

- Transport-psychology is regulated but permits a non-psychologist entrepreneur to operate a registered lab only while employing an authorized psychologist and meeting facility/tool requirements. The official regulation lists three rooms and a 150 PLN transport-psychology test fee; the September 2025 Wielkopolska register contains 222 entries. [Drivers Act, current consolidated text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1210/text.html), [Ministry of Health regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/165/text.html), [Wielkopolska register](https://bip.umww.pl/artykuly/59408/pliki/20250917083052_rejestrprzedsibiorcwstanna20250916.pdf)

[Journal of Sexual Medicine comparison](https://academic.oup.com/jsm/article/23/Supplement_4/qdag118.527/8701733), [Adam Sensor technical note](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1743609522018938), [WARM system](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11413319/)

## Consequential Unknowns

- Number of local owners willing to sell, their age/motivation and time to exclusivity.
- Percentage of revenue contractually recurring and assignable/novatable.
- Normalized operating profit after replacing the owner with a market-paid qualified specialist.
- Acceptable seller-note percentage, earnout terms and client attrition through transition.
- Customer willingness to prepay/renew into the acquisition vehicle before closing.
- Customer willingness to buy an 8,500 PLN instrumented task-rehearsal module.
- Lawful transfer and later optional use of worker information; no physiological or behavioral data moat is assumed without separate permission.
