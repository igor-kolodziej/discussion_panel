# Zero-to-One Pivot: LedgerTrial

## Preserved Insight

Secure a paid, recurring route through a consequential employer workflow before building specialized technology, and make every paid delivery create a reusable human–AI interaction asset. HumanFactor Works tried to buy that route through multiple BHP acquisitions. LedgerTrial preserves the routed workflow and paid-data logic but removes business acquisition, seller financing, regulated BHP delivery and optional wearable cross-sell.

## Structural Change

LedgerTrial is an independent proficiency-testing subscription for AI-assisted bookkeeping teams. A client runs confidential, rotating batches of synthetic Polish invoices and close tasks—each with known ground truth and planted tax, duplicate, supplier, prompt-injection, classification or review traps—through the exact AI-plus-human process it intends to use on customer work. LedgerTrial measures which errors the automation creates, which errors reviewers catch, how long intervention takes and whether the workflow improves after remediation.

This is not an AI-literacy course, generic audit dashboard or consulting report. The paid object is a serialized known-answer challenge batch and a scored human–AI control result, analogous to recurring proficiency testing. The company never needs production client invoices during the initial POC: Poland's official KSeF test environment accepts anonymized data with no legal effect, and a founder-built replica can test the broader bookkeeping decision sequence.

The lab asset is intrinsic rather than optional. Every paid batch produces contractually reusable, de-identified interaction features: model/version, task type, planted failure, human detection or miss, intervention path, time to correction and final outcome. Raw client prompts or employee identity are not required for cross-client learning.

## Target Niche and Buyer

The beachhead is independent Polish bookkeeping/BPO firms with roughly 20–100 staff that are introducing AI invoice extraction, coding, reconciliation or KSeF assistance across many SME clients. The economic buyer is the owner, head of operations or quality/risk lead—not the individual accountant and not the software vendor.

The niche is narrow because all initial scenarios use Polish invoices, VAT/accounting decisions, KSeF 2.0 structures and one repeated workflow: invoice receipt through human approval. It is economically meaningful because one quiet classification or duplicate-payment failure can be multiplied across many client ledgers, and the bookkeeping firm retains professional and reputational responsibility even when software performed the first pass.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

The buyer does not fundamentally want another AI course. The feared event is deploying an apparently accurate automation, discovering months later that employees rubber-stamped systematic errors, and being unable to show clients which human controls actually worked. Vendor demos and headline field-accuracy rates test the model on favorable documents; ordinary training completion tests the employee's memory. Neither tests the combined production-shaped workflow.

The first commercial hypothesis is a **9,500 PLN Fixed Baseline Trial** for one workflow, up to fifteen reviewers and fifty synthetic documents, with a 4,000 PLN non-refundable deposit. Delivery includes the sealed batch, ground truth, response capture, adjudication and a go/no-go control report. Direct delivery cost is hypothesized below 3,500 PLN using the founder, a fixed-scope senior Polish-accountant reviewer and cloud/API spend.

If the baseline changes a software rollout, reviewer threshold or vendor-renewal decision, the recurring offer is hypothesized at **4,900 PLN per month per firm** for rotating monthly micro-batches, one quarterly deep batch, benchmark movement and controlled remediation. Pricing and renewal willingness are new hypotheses; regulation is relevance, not proof of budget.

## Value and Profit Wedge

The immediate value is avoiding false confidence before AI-assisted processing reaches many client books. The first trial can be delivered manually with a simple controlled workspace and scripted ground truth. Software is built only after payment demonstrates that an owner values an independent workflow result.

At maturity, scenario generation, batch delivery, telemetry capture and first-pass scoring become software-driven, while a senior accountant reviews disputed cases. The hypothesized mature gross margin is 70–80%, because each confidential batch can be parameterized across firms without handling their production records. One hundred firms at 4,900 PLN monthly would produce 5.88m PLN recurring revenue before paid baseline trials; that is a denominator to test, not a wealth claim or score.

## Domination Wedge

Dominate one question first: “Can this Polish bookkeeping firm's AI-plus-reviewer invoice workflow catch the failure modes it will actually face?” Do not expand into generic AI training, financial audit or ERP implementation.

Local advantage comes from continuously updating Polish VAT/KSeF decision traps, language, invoice conventions and software-specific workflows faster than horizontal AI-governance platforms. Accounting-software vendors are conflicted when independently testing their own accuracy claims. Traditional trainers test knowledge, and ordinary model benchmarks exclude the human reviewer. LedgerTrial tests the combined process and retains a cross-firm failure benchmark.

## Acquisition Channel

Build a list of 200 independent firms from public business registers, accounting-firm directories, job adverts and vendor case studies. Prioritize firms publicly hiring automation specialists, advertising KSeF automation or announcing AI/OCR deployment. Send the owner or operations lead a two-page specimen containing five synthetic traps and an exact paid offer—not a free transformation workshop.

The first-ten target is: 200 targeted firms, 40 substantive owner/operations conversations, ten fixed proposals and two independent 4,000 PLN deposits inside 180 days. The first 100 must still come from direct trigger-based outbound, proof-driven referrals and repeatable published failure reports; software-vendor or association channels receive no credit unless they later produce signed, paying accounts.

## Primary Control Point

The initial control point is a paid contract routing a named AI-plus-human workflow through serialized confidential challenge batches, with explicit rights to retain the batch, ground truth and de-identified response features. The growing asset is the versioned Polish failure corpus and cross-firm longitudinal benchmark: which combinations of document, model, user role and review rule fail in practice.

The control point is not the KSeF sandbox, an LLM API or purchasable invoices. Those are commodities. Competitors can author scenarios, but cannot instantly reproduce accumulated paired traces, longitudinal firm history and undisclosed rotating batches. The strongest copy risk is an accounting-software vendor or large training/audit firm launching its own test library with an installed customer base; independence, cross-vendor comparison, fast Polish updates and compounding outcome data must be proved strong enough to overcome that distribution disadvantage.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resources are one credible senior Polish-accountant control designer, a lawful synthetic-document generator, a controlled AI workspace, contract/data terms and direct access to firm owners through public lists.

- Contract the accountant for a fixed 8,000 PLN to design and adjudicate the first two batches; do not imply employment, endorsement or partnership.
- Use published KSeF schemas and the official anonymized-data test environment to construct synthetic invoices with no client records.
- Spend at most 6,000 PLN on counsel and data terms, 5,000 PLN on the controlled workspace and API use, and 6,000 PLN on outbound, interviews and delivery preparation.
- Do not spend more than 17,000 PLN before a 4,000 PLN customer deposit.
- Require the first two customers to grant reuse of de-identified response features and of founder-owned synthetic scenarios; otherwise deliver no cross-client benchmark claim.
- Do not integrate with an ERP, request production credentials or hire a full engineering team before two paid baseline trials.

An unknown founder can run this test because the offer asks the buyer to process synthetic records in a sealed exercise, not to grant access to live ledgers or trust a new auditor with a legal opinion. The founder's AI/ML and measurement background builds the harness; the paid accountant supplies bounded domain adjudication.

## Expansion Path

1. Prove paid baseline demand and discover whether results change a rollout, reviewer rule or vendor decision.
2. Convert at least three independent firms to rotating invoice-workflow batches and verify annual contract value, renewal and gross margin.
3. Add software-specific adapters only for systems used by paying customers; never depend on an unspecified vendor partnership.
4. Expand within the same buyer from invoice intake to reconciliations, month-end close and payroll exceptions.
5. Expand geographically by adding country-specific e-invoice/tax challenge libraries, beginning with one CEE market whose rules and buyers can be served by a contracted local control designer.
6. Only after the behavioral corpus predicts misses better than simple task difficulty, build adaptive human–AI interface interventions or sell benchmark/API access to firms and software vendors under separate contracts.

## Minimum People Stack

- Founder: sales list, measurement design, controlled workspace, AI/ML and batch operations; maximum five hours per day.
- One fixed-scope senior Polish accountant: scenario validity and disputed-case adjudication.
- Fixed-scope privacy/commercial counsel.
- Part-time engineering contractor only after the first two deposits.

No acquisition seller, BHP specialist, certification body, accounting-software partnership, production-data custodian or full-time founder is required for paid proof.

## Principal Risk

Accounting-firm owners may treat proficiency testing as optional training, prefer their software vendor's internal QA, or refuse recurring spend after a one-off rollout check. If the result does not change a deployment, control rule, customer assurance or vendor-renewal decision, the challenge corpus is interesting research rather than a business. Even with demand, broad AI-literacy and accounting-software incumbents can copy the visible format before the cross-firm dataset compounds.

## First Disconfirming Test

Before building software, show the same five-trap specimen and 9,500 PLN baseline scope to fifty qualified firms. Seek ten substantive conversations, three written proposals and one 4,000 PLN deposit within sixty days of outreach. Kill or materially change the buyer if none pays, if every buyer wants only a cheap webinar, if fewer than two will grant de-identified result rights, or if the independent result is not tied to a named rollout/control decision.

## Evidence Preserved

- Poland launched KSeF 2.0 in February 2026 and provides an official test environment in which anonymized invoices have no legal effect.
- Commercial sources report rapid adoption of invoice/KSeF automation and continued human verification; these are market signals, not audited accuracy or spend evidence.
- A 2026 ICAS study reports that 72% of surveyed accountants worry about AI producing errors or incorrect decisions and emphasizes continued human judgment.
- Public invoice benchmarks focus on model field accuracy, while recent academic accounting benchmarks show substantial task-level error. This supports the testing problem but not willingness to buy LedgerTrial.
- Existing AI-literacy platforms already offer role-based assessment and audit evidence. LedgerTrial therefore cannot rely on Article 4 compliance or generic training as its differentiator.

## New Hypotheses

- At least one of fifty qualified firms will pay the 4,000 PLN deposit for an independent combined-workflow test.
- A 9,500 PLN baseline trial changes a budgeted rollout or control decision rather than producing an ignored report.
- Firms will contractually permit reuse of de-identified interaction outcomes while keeping all production data outside LedgerTrial.
- Synthetic batches are representative enough to reveal costly human–AI failures and difficult enough that firms cannot recreate equivalent rotating tests cheaply.
- At least 40% of paid baseline customers convert to 4,900 PLN monthly recurring testing and annual retention exceeds 80%.
- Direct outbound CAC, founder time and senior-accountant adjudication permit 70%+ mature gross margin.
- The Polish buyer denominator is large enough that 100 recurring firms does not require heroic market penetration.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

LedgerTrial acquires no BHP business, client book or seller-financed asset and does not repeat a seven-acquisition wealth path. It changes the buyer from a safety-service seller/employer pair to a bookkeeping-firm operator, the paid event from contract transfer to a known-answer workflow test, the control point from purchased relationships to contracted recurring challenge flow, and the expansion engine from capital-intensive roll-up to software and proprietary human–AI outcome data. The future-lab asset is created in the first paid delivery instead of remaining an optional module.

## Return-To-Evaluator Priority

LedgerTrial is the sole proposed pivot because it preserves the strongest distribution-before-technology insight while directly removing the shared fatal financing objection. It should return to `$evaluate-zero-to-one` first. The evaluator should be especially skeptical of recurring willingness to pay, buyer denominator, incumbent accounting-software response, scenario representativeness, data-right clauses and whether a 100-account wealth path is non-heroic. No score or validation is claimed here.
