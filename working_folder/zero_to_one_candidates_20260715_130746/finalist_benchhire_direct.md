# Zero-to-One Candidate: BenchHire Direct

## One-Sentence Thesis

Build the specialist recruitment agency for low-voltage control-cabinet and cable-harness assemblers in western Poland: employers pay for hires who have passed a standardized, instrumented work sample, and a 60-day skill warranty plus linked job outcomes turns each placement into proprietary human-performance data.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build the measurement pipeline and computer-vision support with existing AI/ML and biosignal experience. Electrical assembly is close enough to the family renewable-installation business to obtain paid domain-expert help and prototype feedback, but the family company is neither the first paying customer nor a required channel. A contract recruiter and an experienced cabinet assembler can be hired per assignment; no cofounder is required before paid proof.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first customer is a 30–300 employee control-cabinet, machinery, or cable-assembly manufacturer in Greater Poland or Lower Silesia with an advertised vacancy for assemblers who must read simple schematics, crimp, route and terminate conductors accurately.

The hiring manager wants a worker who is productive quickly and does not create rework, missed delivery dates, or an early replacement. The current workaround is CV filtering, a conversation about experience, certificates that may not represent the exact task, and an employer-run trial after substantial time has already been spent. Generalist agencies optimize candidate supply and speed; specialist agencies exist, but public offers rarely make standardized, outcome-linked work evidence the product.

The consequential hypothesis is that at least one employer will pay an upfront requisition-calibration deposit because one avoided false-positive hire is worth more than the fee.

## Product Wedge

BenchHire is a direct-placement recruitment service, not assessment SaaS.

1. The employer signs a sole-agency mandate for one named requisition, pays a 2,500 PLN non-refundable calibration deposit credited against an 8,500 PLN success fee, and supplies its pass/fail priorities.
2. Candidates complete a 45-minute, de-energized bench task: interpret a one-page schematic, select parts, cut and strip wire, crimp a ferrule, route and terminate conductors, apply specified torque, and identify seeded defects.
3. Low-cost sensors log elapsed time, task order, continuity, torque events and rework. Video is restricted to hands and workpiece. A trained human applies the rubric and the employer makes the hiring decision; the first version makes no autonomous employment recommendation.
4. BenchHire presents only evidence-backed candidates and provides one replacement search if a documented task-skill failure ends the hire inside 60 days. The warranty excludes attendance, misconduct, wage disputes, unsafe employer conditions, and role changes.
5. The employer returns structured day-7, day-30 and day-60 outcomes under the service contract. Recruitment records and any separately consented R&D dataset are segregated.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Trade recruitment competitors sell access to people; assessment vendors sell tests. The overlooked wedge is to sell the employer the placement outcome while making a standardized work sample and bounded warranty the agency's underwriting system. This removes the need to persuade another agency to adopt a tool and makes post-placement outcome data part of paid delivery.

## Underused Momentum

Electrical trades remain widespread shortage occupations across Europe, SMEs report difficulty finding people with the right skills, and skills-first hiring legitimizes practical assessment. At the same time, inexpensive cameras, torque logging and local computer vision make a richer work sample possible without a robotics lab. Poland's KRAZ registration gives a concrete route to becoming the intermediary rather than depending on one.

## Beachhead Market

One role family—low-voltage cabinet and harness assembly—within a two-hour operating radius of Poznań/Ostrów Wlkp./Wrocław. The first task never uses energized equipment and does not claim to replace SEP qualifications or employer safety validation. Initial customer discovery is limited to employers with a live vacancy and at least three hires or replacements expected in 12 months.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Employer price: 2,500 PLN calibration deposit, credited toward 8,500 PLN plus VAT per completed hire.
- Hypothesized direct cost per hire at pilot scale: 600 PLN candidate sourcing and attendance incentives, 450 PLN assessor, 300 PLN facility and consumables, 250 PLN checks/admin, and 300 PLN expected warranty reserve; total 1,900 PLN.
- Hypothesized gross contribution: 6,600 PLN, or 78%, before central sales, engineering and management.
- No fee is charged to candidates. No temporary-worker payroll or housing working capital is taken during the POC.
- The deposit pays for a customer-specific role map even if no hire occurs; this prevents unpaid consulting and is the paid-proof event.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

For the first ten, build a list from live job advertisements, manufacturer directories, KRS/CEIDG records and exhibitor lists. Contact only firms with a live qualifying requisition. The offer is a paid six-candidate audition sprint, not a general HR transformation. Founder-led calls happen before work, during lunch, or after work; one contractor schedules candidates.

Customers 11–100 come from adjacent regional manufacturers and only then from a second proven role cell. Case evidence is expressed as time-to-qualified-shortlist, 60-day retention, warranty frequency and employer re-order—not an unsupported claim of predictive validity. Candidate referrals earn a fixed attendance bonus after a completed work sample.

## Primary Control Point

The first provable control point is a signed sole-agency mandate and paid deposit for one requisition. It routes the employer's candidate presentation and placement fee through BenchHire. The company also owns the task fixtures, rubric versions, warranty decisions and—only where contracts and separate candidate permission allow—de-identified task traces linked to employer outcomes.

The control point is not the camera or task bench. The strongest copy risk is that a specialist staffing incumbent adds a similar audition and warranty. Catch-up becomes harder only if BenchHire builds the densest role-specific candidate pool, cross-employer outcome calibration and credible warranty pricing before expanding.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resources and how they are secured:

- **Legal right to intermediate:** apply for KRAZ registration; budget 1,000 PLN official fee plus 5,000 PLN for Polish employment, consumer, GDPR and AI-Act advice.
- **Role expertise:** pay an experienced cabinet/harness assembler 4,000 PLN for task design, rubric calibration and ten dry runs. A family-company technician may compete for this contract but is not assumed.
- **Physical cell:** spend at most 10,000 PLN on a portable no-voltage panel, hand tools, logged torque driver, continuity tester, parts and overhead camera. Rent a training/workshop room by the day rather than depending on family premises.
- **Employer access:** public live vacancies supply the first 100-account outbound list. A mandate is sold directly; no staffing or OEM partnership is required.
- **Candidate access:** narrowly targeted job-board listings, local vocational groups, referral rewards and direct outreach. All payments and consent terms are transparent.
- **Data rights:** employer contract requires operational outcomes; optional R&D use is separately consented, de-identified and never a condition of being considered for a role. Faces are not recorded and biometric identification is prohibited.

Before serious capital, the founder can produce a KRAZ filing, legal memo, validated dry-run fixture, one paid employer mandate, six candidate sessions, one shortlist, and either a hire or a documented no-hire result.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial acquisition mechanism is trigger-based outbound to employers already advertising the exact role. This is narrower and cheaper than brand marketing. The agency later compounds a two-sided local referral loop: employers reorder after retained hires, while assessed candidates refer peers. Each new employer improves the task/outcome benchmark; each candidate increases fill speed for the next mandate.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Stage 1 dominates one regional role. Stage 2 adds adjacent de-energized cells—electromechanical assembly, HVAC control wiring, visual quality inspection—only after five independent employers reorder. Stage 3 installs supervised BenchHire cells in additional hubs and licenses internal tools to owned branches, not third-party agencies. Stage 4 offers evidence-backed cross-border placement after legal and language workflows are proven domestically.

The future-lab path is deliberate: task instrumentation develops real-time HCI and computer-vision capability; consented hand/workpiece traces plus job outcomes form a proprietary dataset; hiring assessors and ML engineers creates a technical talent network. Robotics-data licensing is optional and receives no credit in the base economics.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The wedge exploits western Poland's manufacturing base, lower-cost local task delivery, access to Polish candidates and proximity to German/Czech labor markets. KRAZ provides a low-cost lawful entry point. Local role language, wage expectations, transport radius and task conventions matter. This is not a superficial copy of a foreign assessment tool because the company earns the placement fee, holds the mandate, and backs the outcome.

## Compounding Advantage

Four assets compound together: employer mandates and reorder history, a referred candidate pool, versioned role-specific task/outcome data, and warranty-loss experience. A broad agency can buy identical tools but starts without the linked niche history or local candidate trust. If employers do not reorder or contracts do not permit usable outcome linkage, this compounding thesis fails.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Reinvested cash first funds candidate acquisition and warranty reserves, then additional assessors and cells, then hub managers and software. The company does not enter temporary staffing, payroll finance or accommodation during the employed-founder phase. A second hub opens only after three months of positive contribution margin and two independent employer reorders.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–21: 20 buyer interviews and 10 candidate interviews; sell only to live vacancies. Obtain fixed-fee legal scoping and initiate KRAZ registration.
- Days 22–45: hire the role expert; build a manual rubric and disposable prototype; run ten paid or compensated dry runs with no employer claims.
- Days 46–75: ask 30 qualifying employers for a 2,500 PLN paid requisition calibration mandate; do not build ML.
- Days 76–120: after the first deposit, build the bounded instrumented cell, recruit up to six candidates and deliver the evidence pack.
- Days 121–180: target one hire and collect day-7/day-30 outcomes; seek a second independent paid mandate.

Pre-proof spend is capped at 18,000 PLN (legal/KRAZ 6,000; expert and prototype 6,000; buyer discovery/travel 3,000; candidate discovery 2,000; contingency 1,000). The remaining cell spend is released only after a deposit. The founder keeps corporate employment and uses a contractor for daytime scheduling.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. **Discovery, maximum 8,000 PLN:** legal/KRAZ scoping, interviews and a disposable task mock-up. Stop if employers will not discuss an upfront mandate.
2. **Prepaid pilot preparation, cumulative maximum 18,000 PLN before paid proof:** expert rubric and dry runs. No production fixture or ML.
3. **After one paid deposit, cumulative maximum 40,000 PLN:** portable cell, candidate acquisition and first delivery. Stop if no qualified shortlist or no outcome access.
4. **Up to 70,000 PLN:** only after one successful delivery, plausible positive contribution and a second independent paying mandate.
5. **Up to 100,000 PLN:** only after three independent paying employers and at least one reorder or a documented outbound channel with viable economics. Family capital remains excluded until repeatable demand and positive contribution are shown.

Maximum founder loss before paid proof is 18,000 PLN; maximum first-year founder capital is 100,000 PLN.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Controlled test | Failure response |
|---|---|---|
| Employer pays a deposit, not only contingency | Ask 30 employers with live roles for the exact 2,500 PLN mandate | Kill or change buyer if none pays; do not disguise free discovery as proof |
| Candidates attend a physical test | Recruit 20, invite 10, measure show-up and cost | Add transport windows/incentive only within CAC cap; kill if qualified supply remains scarce |
| Task result predicts employer-valued behavior | Human rubric first; collect blinded employer feedback and 7/30/60-day outcomes | Narrow claims; do not automate or offer warranty if signal is absent |
| Employment/data law permits the workflow | Fixed-scope Polish counsel memo; no biometric ID or automated hiring decision | Keep tool as human evidence or stop the data feature |
| Warranty is economically bounded | Explicit exclusions and reserve; measure first 20 placements | Reprice or remove warranty if loss exceeds 8% of fees |
| Founder time is sufficient | Scheduling contractor and two fixed audition blocks monthly | Stop expansion if delivery exceeds five founder hours/day |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder leaves employment only after at least five independent paying employers, contracted recurring or repeated mandates, three consecutive months of positive contribution, a documented outbound-to-deposit conversion rate that supports payback, a trained delivery substitute, and 12 months of personal runway. A score, grant, or single large client cannot satisfy this gate.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If direct placement fails, the founder retains a portable task bench, a lawful data/consent architecture, employer interview evidence, task-instrumentation software and relationships with technical assessors. The fixture can be sold as ordinary equipment only if it has paying demand; speculative robotics-data licensing is not the fallback justification.

## Critical Evidence

- Official KRAZ entry is available at a stated 1,000 PLN fee.
- EU labor sources report widespread trade shortages and skills-matching friction.
- Live recruiters and specialist agencies prove active budgets for electrical/panel-builder placement.
- Practical skill tests exist, proving feasibility while disproving novelty of the test alone.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Employers will pay a non-refundable calibration deposit to an unknown specialist agency.
- An 8,500 PLN placement fee and 1,900 PLN direct delivery cost are attainable in this role.
- A de-energized 45-minute task separates useful skill levels and relates to 60-day outcomes.
- At least 60% of suitable candidates will separately consent to de-identified R&D use without coercion.
- Reorders and referrals can reduce acquisition cost before incumbents copy the process.

## Kill Criteria

- Zero paid deposits after 30 qualified employer pitches within 90 days.
- Fewer than six suitable candidate completions after 20,000 PLN total sourcing spend.
- No employer will contractually provide 7/30/60-day outcomes.
- The legal memo makes the proposed data capture or human-scored use impractical under the budget.
- Fewer than two independent paid mandates or no completed placement by day 180.
- After 20 placements: gross contribution below 55%, skill-related warranty incidence above 8%, or fewer than 30% of employers reorder within six months.
- Conservative scale requires temporary-worker payroll finance, exclusive external partnerships or near-total niche share.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- POC budget: 36,000 PLN target, 40,000 PLN hard cap before deeper evidence.
- Founder: sales, data architecture, software, analytics; up to five hours/day.
- Paid role expert: task/rubric and assessor training.
- Contract recruiter/coordinator: candidate scheduling after a paid mandate.
- Polish employment/privacy counsel: fixed scope.
- No ML engineer, hardware cofounder, office lease or full-time employee before two independent mandates.
