# Zero-to-One Evaluation: LedgerTrial v1 With Simulated-Panel Objections

## Diagnosis

The panel confirms the integrated-workflow insight but rejects the business shape: LedgerTrial can plausibly sell a one-off pre-rollout exercise, while its recurring 58,800 PLN contract, pooled-data network and switching cost all depend on an unproved cadence that the customer's real workflow may not support. The controlled-workspace simplification creates a second structural problem—avoiding production integration makes the result less representative, while adding integration destroys the claimed speed, margin and founder leverage. At inception the corpus is manually authorable and the control point is Tier 3, not an earned data moat. The candidate must pivot to an actual repeated economic outcome rather than another benchmark, assessment or compliance artifact.

## Evidence and Assumptions

KSeF timing, commercial accounting automation and published AI error concerns establish relevance only. They do not establish recurring Polish buyer spend, synthetic-to-production validity, reusable employee-result rights, cross-firm comparability, the 1,200-buyer denominator, retention or delivery margins. The panel also caught a capital-presentation inconsistency: individually listed resource maxima can total 25,000 PLN even though the candidate labels the pre-deposit cap 17,000 PLN; those costs must be staged rather than treated as simultaneously committed. Both fresh reviewers independently identified the same conditional fatal mechanism—no recurring WTP means no data flywheel, switching cost or wealth path.

## Overall Score

**69/100.** Weighted points are 137.5 out of 200; `10 × (137.5 / 20) = 68.75`, rounded to 69. This is a post-panel advisory diagnosis, not a gate-eligible score.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 as written at inception.** A prospective contract and manually authored corpus are accessible, but no recurring workflow, pooled dataset, exclusive right or scarce resource exists. The strongest copy risk is an installed accounting-software vendor adding native challenge batches and outside-accountant adjudication with its existing workflow telemetry and distribution. LedgerTrial could become Tier 2 after comparable longitudinal traces and enforceable reuse rights, but that future state depends on the very recurrence the panel rejected.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail as a credible conservative case, despite complete arithmetic.** At 100 recurring accounts, 58,800 PLN annual subscription price, 29% operating margin, 4× value and 90% ownership, the table reaches 6.55m PLN. A 3× value reduces founder equity to approximately 4.92m PLN, and a one-off baseline business cannot support the account economics or transferability. With neither the 1,200 qualified-buyer census nor recurring ACV/retention supported, the conservative case is too sensitive to count as an independent pass after panel objections.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The founder can ask for a 4,000 PLN deposit and deliver a bounded synthetic batch while employed and below 40,000 PLN. Resource spend must be ordered so accountant, counsel, workspace and outreach commitments do not exceed the stated pre-deposit maximum. This gate proves only that a baseline can be tested cheaply; it does not repair recurrence or defensibility.

## Expansion Check

The invoice-approval beachhead is coherent, but each software configuration and country pack adds domain labor. Horizontal training/audit firms and installed accounting vendors move inward with stronger trust and channel. LedgerTrial's outward expansion becomes asymmetric only after a benchmark corpus exists, producing a severe pre-moat vulnerability.

## Fatal Objections

1. Both independent reviewers identified recurring willingness to pay as the conditional fatal mechanism; without annual renewal, all compounding claims fail.
2. Synthetic-to-production validity creates an integration dilemma that can convert the product into training theatre or bespoke implementation.
3. The planned control point is manually reproducible before scale and has no Tier-1 resource.
4. Reusable employee-result rights and heterogeneous cross-firm comparability may fail together.
5. The conservative founder-wealth outcome is sensitive to unverified ACV, denominator, retention and a 4× value.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Measure the combined human–AI process around an economically consequential event and retain the paired failure-to-human-resolution outcome as a byproduct of paid work.
