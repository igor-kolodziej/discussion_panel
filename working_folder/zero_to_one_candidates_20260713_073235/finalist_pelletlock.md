# Zero-to-One Candidate: PelletLock Disconnect Cell

Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, panelled, chatbot-reviewed, confirmed, or validated. Research date: 2026-07-13. All prices, performance, buyer behaviour, market shares, margins, volumes, valuations, service attachment, coupling coverage, and addressable-point estimates not explicitly supported by a linked source are hypotheses to test.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell plastics plants and dry-bulk carriers a retrofit, post-purge disconnect cell that captures and weighs the pellets normally released when a pneumatic hose is uncoupled, starting with one high-frequency four-inch interface and compounding through owned tooling, verified compatibility/performance data, consumable cassettes, and installed workflow access.

## Founder Fit

The founder can own the customer-discovery system, product requirements, experimental design, data model, pricing, outbound process, supplier bidding, and evidence package while keeping his corporate job. The initial proof is bounded enough to run in 15–20 founder hours per week: observe disconnects, specify one interface, commission mechanical work, run controlled tests, and sell two defined paid pilots.

The founder should not pretend to be a pressure-systems, electrostatics, explosive-atmosphere, machinery-conformity, or polymer-process expert. Before any external live-bay test, he hires a mechanical engineer and an independent industrial-safety/ATEX adviser under capped statements of work with foreground-IP assignment and editable-file handover. Toolmaking, fabrication, materials testing, and cycle testing are ordinary paid services. No partner must grant proprietary data, distribution, approval, or free plant access for the first proof.

The founder's useful asymmetry is disciplined low-cost product development in Poland, not status or sector authority. The sales mechanism therefore relies on a weighed before/after disconnect test at the buyer's own bay rather than trust in the founder's credentials.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a Polish or nearby CEE plastics converter, compounder, recycler, pellet producer, warehouse, or dry-bulk carrier that:

- handles at least five tonnes of plastic pellets annually and receives or dispatches them through pneumatic tanker/silo transfer;
- has at least one frequently used, accessible four-inch dry-bulk hose connection;
- performs enough disconnects to produce recurring visible residual loss or cleanup work;
- owns the bay or can approve a reversible retrofit; and
- has an EHS, plant, maintenance, or operations manager with a 15,000–20,000 PLN equipment budget.

The human outcome is not “compliance software.” The shift supervisor wants the operator to finish a routine unload without pellets scattering under the trailer, without kneeling to sweep or improvise a bucket, and with defensible evidence that the chosen physical prevention measure works. The EHS manager wants fewer spill events and an auditable mass record without asking operators to complete another form. The carrier wants a repeatable disconnect process that does not create disputes about whether the vehicle or receiving site caused a spill.

Current workarounds include purging the line, tapping or blowing residual material, holding a bucket or bag under the coupling, positioning a catch pan, sweeping afterward, installing drain inserts, or using broader loading-area containment. Operation Clean Sweep guidance already recommends catch pans under unloading valves, purging lines before disconnection, and considering self-closing hose valves. These practices prove the job exists; they also mean PelletLock must beat a cheap pan on capture, operator time, cleanliness, and evidence rather than claiming the job is unserved.

## Product Wedge

PelletLock is a reversible external enclosure used only for the **post-purge hose-disconnection step**. It is not a hose coupling, valve, pressure vessel, unloading controller, legal-compliance certificate, or substitute for the site's unloading and purging procedure.

The first product envelope is one physically audited four-inch dry-bulk coupling family, likely a four-inch cam-and-groove/clamp arrangement. Published dry-bulk products also show 75 mm, 100 mm, and Storz/threaded variants, but “three- or four-inch coverage” is not assumed. The first 50 observed physical points decide the interface; a 3/4-inch plumbing fitting is not the target.

The commercial beta comprises:

1. a fixed receiver-side carrier plate and hinged antistatic external cell around the connection;
2. a cassette dock below the disconnect point;
3. a purely mechanical sequence latch that blocks access to the disconnect levers unless a cassette is seated and keeps the outer cell closed while the post-purge coupling is separated;
4. lever extensions or a guarded release mechanism matched to the approved coupling geometry;
5. a removable, sealed, serial-numbered filter/capture cassette that is weighed before and after use; and
6. an external, non-hazard-zone scale or handheld reader that records captured mass, point ID, date, and cassette ID.

The enclosure does not intentionally retain conveying pressure. The beta may be used only after the existing line is isolated and purged, under a written site procedure. Electronics remain outside any classified zone unless an adviser determines a conformity route and suitable equipment. Any site that cannot establish a safe post-purge state is outside the first envelope.

The proof product may use fabricated antistatic polymer/aluminium parts, CNC inserts, and printed sacrificial pieces. The 12-month production design moves to owned trim/drill/assembly jigs and an owned cassette-forming or mould tool only after paid evidence.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Regulation (EU) 2025/2365 creates a broad, measurable operating duty around pellet-loss prevention, containment, cleanup, risk management, and records. Article 26 says the regulation generally applies from **17 December 2027**, with specified provisions applying from **16 December 2025** and specified maritime provisions from **17 December 2028**. The Commission says the rules cover EU installations handling five tonnes or more annually, as well as carriers within scope.

The regulation does **not** prescribe PelletLock, require an automatic disconnect enclosure, or guarantee a budget. Catch pans, procedures, cleaning, sealing, and other measures can satisfy the operational need. The commercial gap is narrower: unloading disconnects are frequent, local, observable events where a physical retrofit can produce a weighed proof of effect without replacing the entire pneumatic-transfer system.

Existing offers cluster around three adjacent jobs:

- broad spill containment around loading areas, drains, railcars, or pavement;
- truck blow-off/line-clearing systems intended to reduce retained product; and
- standard dry-bulk couplings, clamps, sight glasses, and valves.

The candidate advances only if the current market lacks a comparably priced retrofit that combines post-purge enclosure, cassette capture, enforced sequence, and mass evidence for the selected coupling. This exact-equivalence claim remains unproved and requires a current supplier, patent, and distributor audit before tooling.

## Underused Momentum

The useful momentum is a timed convergence, not a trend label:

- the EU pellet-loss regulation is enacted, its general application date is known, and implementation/training/measurement infrastructure is being developed before December 2027;
- thousands of facilities already know OCS practices, so the founder does not need to invent the loss-prevention category;
- dry-bulk connections are standardized enough to support repeatable carrier plates, but fragmented enough that a compatibility library may have value;
- Polish contract fabrication, test laboratories, polymer processors, and small tooling shops can create a physical proof without a factory; and
- low-cost weighing, serialisation, and AI-assisted photo/geometry classification can make the evidence layer cheap, while the product's value remains physical capture rather than an AI wrapper.

The timing cuts both ways. Buyers may wait until 2027; standards or guidance may change; and established vendors have time to respond. Paid proof must therefore come from existing cleanup, spill-control, carrier-customer, or OCS budgets—not from an assertion that the regulation forces purchase.

## Beachhead Market

Start with one corridor: Polish plastics plants and pellet logistics operations within a practical driving radius of Warsaw–Łódź–Poznań–Wrocław that have frequent silo-truck unloads and an accessible four-inch connection matching the audited first interface.

The beachhead is one **disconnect point**, not an entire plant compliance program. A qualified bay performs at least two disconnects per week, provides 10 baseline observations, can accept a reversible carrier/guard, and has a named maintenance and EHS owner. The founder excludes low-frequency bag-only sites, marine operations, food/pharma powder transfer, unknown explosive-atmosphere classifications, incompatible hose geometries, and points where the site's purge/isolation process cannot establish a safe post-purge condition.

The beachhead is narrow enough to dominate by compatibility and proof, yet each successful plant can contain several points and can refer sister plants or its regular carriers.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

The first two customers buy a 30-day, one-bay beta pilot for **15,000 PLN** each: 6,000 PLN paid upfront and 9,000 PLN upon installation/acceptance of the agreed safe test protocol. Both payments are credited toward the commercial system if the site orders after the pilot. The beta agreement disclaims legal-compliance guarantees and measures only captured mass, event time, contamination, bypasses, and safe operation inside the declared envelope.

At repeatable production, the target list price is **€4,500** (roughly 19,500 PLN for planning) including the cell, one carrier interface, four cassettes, external weighing/ID kit, installation, and operator training. The optional **€800/year** point-care pack includes four replacement/reconditioned cassettes, wear seals/latch parts, annual mass-check/calibration, inspection checklist, and export of the point's event record. The customer can keep its raw records locally; recurring value must come from reliable physical replacements and verification, not hostage data.

### Buyer value benchmark

Before asking for a deposit, observe or reconstruct at least 10 disconnects at the prospect's exact point. Record:

- mass recovered by the site's existing pan/bag/sweep method;
- mass remaining on the ground, equipment exterior, or drain approach;
- paid operator and cleanup time;
- total post-purge-to-hose-stowed time;
- material cross-contamination or wetting;
- bypasses, forgotten equipment, and ergonomic difficulties; and
- spill/near-miss and OCS inspection evidence the site already maintains.

The live demo must then run at least 20 representative disconnect simulations with clean surrogate or the customer's approved pellet under a written procedure. The minimum advance hypotheses are at least **90% capture of the disconnect loss that escapes the existing connection**, median added disconnect time of **10 seconds or less** versus the site's correctly used catch-pan method, no visible leakage outside the cell in 19 of 20 runs, and no additional contamination or unsafe residual-pressure event.

Pellet raw-material value alone will rarely justify the system. The buyer case must combine measurable cleanup labour, avoided nuisance/spill events, more reliable operator execution, and usable physical-control evidence. Kill the site if the measured annual value cannot support a 24-month payback or an approved EHS capital rationale without invented fine avoidance.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

No distributor or privileged introduction is needed for the cold start.

**Accounts and qualification.** Build a named 200-account Poland/CEE list from public plastics-industry directories, trade-fair exhibitor lists, carrier websites, environmental permits, Google Maps, and company sites. Contact EHS, maintenance, logistics, and plant managers with a specific offer: a 45-minute “disconnect-loss weigh test” at one inactive/isolated bay, followed by a controlled prototype demonstration if the point qualifies. The founder does not sell a compliance assessment.

**First two customers.** Contact at least 60 unrelated accounts, secure 15 point-photo/measurement calls, and inspect at least eight bays. Invite qualified sites to a controlled off-line demo using their coupling geometry. Two unrelated companies—not family, employer, contractor, reseller, or one corporate group—must each sign the same scoped pilot and pay 6,000 PLN upfront. Two founder-owned beta units allow two independent 30-day bay pilots to finish by day 180. Each site pays the 9,000 PLN installation/acceptance balance before the live pilot begins.

**Customers three to ten.** Publish the exact safe-use envelope, uncut 20-cycle test, mass/time method, known compatibility list, and before/after cases from customers who consent. Approach sister plants only after securing at least three unrelated legal entities. Run monthly mobile demo days at industrial clusters and accept orders only for the verified interface. Target eight additional deposits in months 7–18; no salesperson is hired before the founder can reproduce the demo and margin.

**Customers eleven to 100.** Use a photo/measurement compatibility checker to pre-qualify bays remotely; place demo inventory with an employee salesperson in Poland and later Germany/Czechia only after 20 accepted installations. Coupling distributors, OCS trainers, EHS consultants, and tanker workshops may refer or resell after 30 points, but direct sales remain available and no channel is existential. A channel qualifies only after three independent customer orders with positive post-commission contribution.

The commercial hypotheses are: 25% of physically qualified demos convert to a paid pilot/order, at least 60% of pilots convert to retained production systems, median sales cycle is under 120 days after a live test, and at least 25% of customers add a second point within 12 months.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. **Observe before designing.** Measure 50 real physical disconnect points across at least 15 unrelated operators. Record coupling maker/family, nominal size, accessible envelope, line/purge procedure, event frequency, zone classification, existing containment, and decision owner. Photographs and dimensions require customer permission; no confidential process data is needed.
2. **Freeze one interface.** Select the single family with the highest event-weighted coverage and easiest safe external retrofit. Advance a second family only if the top two together cover at least 60% of the first 50 observed points.
3. **Buy expertise.** Obtain capped written routes from a mechanical engineer and industrial-safety/ATEX adviser before live-site development. PelletLock stays outside the pressure path, but residual pressure, static, dust, pinch, hose movement, contamination, cleaning, and explosive-atmosphere risks still require formal treatment.
4. **Own the proof rig.** Build a founder-owned surrogate connection rig and two fabricated beta cells. Run at least 100 bench cycles before any external bay and at least 500 total cycles by month 12.
5. **Sell the physical test.** Show qualified prospects an uncut capture/time trial on the selected interface. Collect two unrelated 6,000 PLN payments and then the 9,000 PLN acceptance balances for two 30-day bay pilots.
6. **Release capital only on evidence.** Complete both pilots and verify positive first-unit contribution before spending beyond the initial 40,000 PLN. Then buy the founder-owned cassette tool/jigs, independent tests, documentation, and production reservation.
7. **Reserve capacity, not speculative inventory.** Pay a modest deposit for a 500-unit capacity option with quoted price breaks; manufacture the first 20–50 units against customer prepayments. The founder's 100,000 PLN is not used to warehouse 500 systems.

By month 12, the proof is tangible: two accepted external pilots, owned design/tooling/test assets, independent reports, a verified one-interface compatibility list, positive first-unit contribution, at least one repeat or second-point order, and a reserved production route. The resource is not “relationships with industry.”

## Distribution Advantage

The initial acquisition mechanism is a point-specific weighed demonstration. It changes the trust question from “is this unknown founder a pellet-compliance authority?” to “did this device capture the mass at our coupling without slowing or endangering our operator?” A standardized photo/measurement intake rejects incompatible points before travel.

If the product works, each installation improves three sellable assets:

- the compatibility matcher and installation time for that coupling/site geometry;
- the evidence range across resin, pellet shape, humidity, purge practice, residual mass, and operator behaviour; and
- a local proof case that can be shown to a plant using the same carrier or coupling family.

Recurring cassettes and annual checks keep a legitimate relationship with the physical point and expose wear/failure data earlier than a catalogue competitor sees it. Direct evidence must remain portable and buyer-owned; the moat is faster fit and lower risk, not locking away the customer's legal records.

Distributors become leverage only after direct demand. PelletLock dies if its only plausible route to 100 customers is an unsigned exclusive distributor or a coupling OEM's permission.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

**Phase 1 — one coupling, one event.** Win the post-purge disconnect of one high-frequency four-inch interface at Polish/CEE plastics plants. Optimize safe sequence, capture, cleaning, and ten-minute installation rather than covering every hose.

**Phase 2 — interface family.** Add the second event-weighted coupling family, rapid carrier plates, resin/cleaning variants, and point-care cassettes. Use the physical-point census to avoid low-volume SKUs.

**Phase 3 — multi-point standard.** Sell plant and carrier kits covering several unload bays, with common training, spares, and records. Add a maintenance-installed configuration and an external scale/reader that works across cells.

**Phase 4 — adjacent dry-bulk disconnects.** Evaluate other non-food granules or powders only after separate dust, contamination, material-compatibility, and conformity work. Do not silently broaden into food, pharma, marine, high-pressure containment, or combustible-dust zones.

The asymmetric expansion claim is that a narrow installed base supplies the geometry and failure corpus needed to add interfaces faster and more safely than a broad containment vendor can move inward. It is false if buyers select only on shell price or if a coupling maker's universal self-closing product removes the event altogether.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers lower-cost fabrication/tooling, a dense manufacturing/logistics base, and fast travel to early CEE plants. Polish/English/German instructions, metric interfaces, local installation, and EU-focused technical documentation can make a small supplier easier to adopt than importing a generic yard-containment product.

The legal timing is EU-wide, however, and major coupling/containment vendors already operate in Europe. No formal barrier prevents them from copying the concept. The local defence must be an 18–24 month evidence and installation lead before the 17 December 2027 general application date, plus a price/installation model that an incumbent cannot easily match through a premium product line.

Foreign-market adaptation is credible only after the interface library identifies actual differences. A superficial export of the same shell is not an advantage.

## Compounding Advantage

The intended loop is:

1. more installed points produce more compatibility, residual-loss, cleaning, wear, and operator-bypass evidence;
2. that evidence reduces installation failures and makes pre-qualification faster;
3. faster, safer installs lower acquisition and warranty cost;
4. cassette and annual-check orders expose failure modes and fund continued interface development; and
5. broader verified coverage wins multi-point plants and creates more installed evidence.

The loop compounds only if customers actually reorder cassettes/checks, permit anonymized performance learning, and prefer the verified installation envelope. The founder must never claim ownership of customer compliance data or environmental outcomes. If 100 units produce no better fit, support economics, or retention than the first ten, the business is a copyable fabricated accessory.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

During corporate employment, the conservative and expected cases reinvest **95% of after-tax business cash otherwise available for distribution** into tooling, working capital, installation inventory, interface development, test evidence, service capacity, and geographic expansion. Any optional founder distribution is capped at 5%; the model assumes zero and does not count it toward wealth. The un-distributed residual is treated as operating liquidity and is not added separately to the equity calculation.

The conservative and expected tables continue using 95% reinvestment as the planning policy until the relevant scale milestone, even if the founder has left employment. The strong case reinvests **100%** through Year 9. Customer prepayments and positive contribution finance bills of material before equity; debt funds receivables/inventory only after repeat demand. Outside capital is taken only to accelerate an already proven interface/region and is explicitly reflected in ownership.

Suggested reinvestment sequence:

- first €100k equivalent: safety, two pilots, owned tools, tests, and first production reservation;
- next €250k from orders/cash: 50–100 systems, spares, product-liability cover, and installation process;
- next €750k: second interface, one application salesperson, and working capital for 250–500 units;
- later capital: additional countries and contract-manufacturing redundancy only after direct cohort contribution is positive.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

The exact paid proof is **two unrelated external companies each paying at least 6,000 PLN for a defined 30-day, one-bay PelletLock pilot by day 135, followed by a 9,000 PLN installation/acceptance balance before the live pilot; both pilots finish by day 180**. Total original founder capital at risk before those first payments is capped at 32,000 PLN and total founder commitment through day 180 is capped at no more than 40,000 PLN. Work remains 15–20 founder hours per week.

**Days 1–30 — point and buyer evidence (5,000 PLN cap).** Build 200 accounts, conduct 25 calls, inspect/measure at least 15 points across eight operators, and reach 50 observed/photo-measured points across at least 15 operators by using remote measurements where safe. Obtain two written engineering/safety scopes, FTO/novelty search quotes, material quotes, and two fabrication quotes. Select one interface only if it covers enough event-weighted volume.

**Days 31–70 — founder-owned bench proof (20,000 PLN cumulative cap).** Commission CAD and a non-live surrogate rig; fabricate the alpha; perform at least 100 post-purge-equivalent disconnect cycles with clean surrogate pellets. Measure capture, time, leakage, cleaning, cassette seal, latch bypass, grounding continuity, and pinch points. Do not test live pressure or classified-zone use.

**Days 71–105 — two beta units and witnessed demo (32,000 PLN cumulative cap).** Incorporate the safety review, build two reversible betas, and run the 20-cycle public method. Invite eight qualified sites from at least 60 outbound accounts. Provide the declared-use envelope, no-compliance-guarantee contract, and site prerequisite checklist.

**Days 106–135 — collect paid evidence.** Two unrelated customers each sign and pay 6,000 PLN. If fewer than two pay, stop; do not unlock the remaining founder capital.

**Days 136–180 — two external bay pilots within the 40,000 PLN cap.** Collect each 9,000 PLN acceptance balance before installation. Install the two existing beta units at two unrelated sites for 30 days under approved procedures. Record at least 20 real disconnect events per point where operational frequency permits; if it does not, the site was misqualified. The customers sign acceptance/rejection against capture, time, cleaning, contamination, bypass, and safety criteria. Pilot payments, not additional founder capital, cover travel, consumables, and bounded adjustments.

Required day-180 outputs are two independent paid contracts and payment records, two completed/accepted or explicitly failed pilots, event-level data, first-unit contribution calculation, exact operator objections, and a go/kill decision. One family company, one corporate group, a conditional letter of intent, a free trial, or paid consulting does not count.

The possible additional 100,000 PLN from family is excluded until paid proof, successful delivery, repeatable demand, positive contribution, and a risk-adjusted scaling case. No lease, personal guarantee, or purchase commitment beyond these ceilings is signed during POC.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder may leave corporate employment only when all are true:

- at least **10 independent paying legal entities** or **20 contracted paid points**, with no group representing more than 25% of revenue;
- at least five production systems have completed 90 days without a critical safety, contamination, or capture failure;
- positive contribution after installation, warranty reserve, travel, commission, returns, and support for two consecutive cohorts;
- at least 40% point-care attachment or an alternative demonstrated recurring/repeat-purchase mechanism;
- a repeatable acquisition signal: at least 20% of qualified demos convert and blended CAC is below €1,500 after the first ten;
- a second trained person can install/support the product without founder presence;
- written product-liability/conformity route and second-source production plan;
- contracted backlog or recurring gross contribution that supports the planned salary and working-capital need; and
- at least 12 months of personal runway outside the original 100,000 PLN business capital.

Regulatory application dates, one large customer, grants, letters of intent, or a promising distributor do not substitute for these gates.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If PelletLock fails commercially before tooling, stop at 32,000–40,000 PLN and retain the coupling census, safety scopes, CAD, rig, test protocol, buyer interviews, and manufacturing quotes. These assets can inform a different physical loss-prevention product but do not justify converting into generic compliance consulting.

If it fails after tooling because one interface is wrong, the founder can reuse the cassette, latch, measurement, rig, supplier, and evidence architecture for another verified high-volume interface, but only after new paid proof. If it fails because proper purging/catch pans solve the problem cheaply, abandon the business shape rather than add a dashboard.

Owned aluminium/fabrication jigs and a small cassette-forming tool limit stranded capital; no 500-unit speculative inventory is bought. Product/customer records and anonymized data remain subject to contractual permissions. Safety failures trigger permanent quarantine of affected units and disclosure to customers, not asset recovery through resale.

## Critical Evidence

- Regulation (EU) 2025/2365 is enacted. Article 26 provides general application from 17 December 2027, specified early provisions from 16 December 2025, and specified maritime provisions from 17 December 2028.
- The Commission states the rules apply to EU installations handling five tonnes or more of plastic pellets annually and describes prevention, containment, cleanup, plans, and records. It does not prescribe PelletLock.
- The regulation's annex includes operational prevention/containment concepts such as suitable transport means/sealing, cleaning, spill trays, and physical testing or monitoring of prevention measures. These create an evidence context, not compulsory product demand.
- OCS guidance describes catch pans, line purging, cleaning, and self-closing-valve considerations around unloading. The incumbent workaround is therefore real and cheap.
- UltraTech, Enviropod, and SolvAir sell adjacent containment, drain-capture, and line-clearing products; current equivalent-product absence has not been established.
- Official Dixon, NORRES, Alfagomma, McGill, and Kuriyama material shows multiple relevant four-inch/75–100 mm dry-bulk coupling and hose families. Published nominal sizes do not prove physical compatibility or market share.
- Plastics Europe reports more than 60,000 European plastics-value-chain companies. It does not report PelletLock's addressable disconnect-point count.

## Critical Hypotheses

1. At least two coupling families cover 60% of event-weighted disconnects across the first 50 observed CEE points.
2. A reversible external cell can be installed without entering the pressure path or creating a new unacceptable hazard.
3. The selected sites can establish and verify a safe post-purge condition consistently.
4. The product captures at least 90% of the residual mass that would otherwise escape at disconnection.
5. Median added time versus a correctly used catch pan is no more than 10 seconds.
6. Operators do not bypass the cassette/latch in more than 5% of observed pilot events.
7. Material compatibility, grounding, cleaning, dust, pinch, hose-movement, and zone risks have an affordable conformity route.
8. Two unrelated buyers pay the full 15,000 PLN pilot price without a compliance guarantee.
9. A production buyer accepts €4,500 hardware and €800/year care at a measured value case.
10. Delivered production COGS reaches €1,550 and stays below €2,100 at 100 units.
11. No present supplier has an equivalent integrated cell below roughly €5,000 with an established service channel.
12. FTO permits the latch, carrier, cassette, and sequence architecture without a licence that breaks economics.
13. At least 20,000 economically eligible EU physical points exist, with enough annual replacement/new demand to support the conservative share.
14. Point-care attachment stays above 40% and produces genuine service/cassette value.
15. Installation and support can be trained and delegated rather than becoming indefinite founder labour.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or abandon this business shape if any of the following occurs:

1. the top two coupling families cover less than 60% of the first 50 event-weighted observed points, or each viable SKU would serve too few annual events;
2. an established EU supplier already offers the same post-purge enclosure, sequence interlock, cassette capture, and mass evidence at a comparable all-in price;
3. FTO finds blocking claims and a written licence/workaround cannot fit the 100,000 PLN cycle and target margin;
4. independent safety advice says the product must contain conveying pressure, requires uncontrolled changes to the customer's pressure system, or lacks an affordable lawful route;
5. bench or external tests show static, dust, contamination, cleaning, pinch, residual-pressure, or hose-movement risk that cannot be bounded with one redesign;
6. capture is below 90%, visible escape occurs in more than one of 20 representative runs, or median added time exceeds 10 seconds after one redesign;
7. operator bypass exceeds 5% of pilot events or customers refuse the sequence latch on ergonomic/throughput grounds;
8. two unrelated customers do not each pay 6,000 PLN by day 135 and the remaining 9,000 PLN before live pilot installation;
9. fewer than two independent pilots finish by day 180, or neither customer retains/orders after the agreed test despite meeting site qualification;
10. delivered COGS exceeds €2,100 at the €4,500 price, 100-unit gross margin is below 50%, or first-year support/warranty exceeds 8% of hardware revenue;
11. a verified physical-point census below 20,000 forces the conservative case above 30% cumulative EU share, or the €10m path needs more than 25% dilution beyond the stated conservative case, more than a 6.5× operating-profit multiple, or founder-performed installations at scale;
12. point-care attachment is below 40%, cassette usage is too low to matter, and no non-artificial repeat-purchase loop exists;
13. customers consistently prefer better purging, self-closing valves, or catch pans because they achieve the outcome at a fraction of the lifecycle cost; or
14. the only path to 100 customers requires a speculative exclusive distributor, regulator endorsement, OCS approval, or coupling-OEM permission.
