# Round Five — Direct-Access Product Directions

Research date: 2026-07-13. Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, panelled, chatbot-reviewed, confirmed, or validated.

## Boundary Conditions Applied

- The family renewable-installation company may supply a workshop, technicians, repeatable tasks, a test roof/array, and internal alpha use. It is **not** accepted as paid proof, the only customer, or the scale moat.
- Every paid-proof event below must come from an unrelated external buyer within 180 days while founder cash at risk remains at or below 40,000 PLN. The founder keeps the corporate job and limits this work to five hours a day.
- Every initial foundational asset is owned outright for at most 100,000 PLN: prototype, CAD/firmware, fixture, toolhead, die, test specimen, calibration rig, or first production tooling. No external licence, exclusive partnership, DSO acceptance, insurer acceptance, or OEM permission is assumed.
- Ordinary machining, fabrication, component supply, laboratory testing, and contract engineering are cash-purchased, replaceable vendor services, not foundational partnerships. A direction dies if a supposedly replaceable supplier is actually a sole gatekeeper.
- Scale capital may exceed 100,000 PLN only after paid proof and positive first-delivery contribution economics. It is shown transparently in each founder-wealth shape.
- All prices, demand, conversion, margins, volumes, valuation multiples, and product performance below are hypotheses. Wealth illustrations count founder equity once: enterprise value minus net debt, multiplied by founder ownership. They do not also count retained earnings as distributions.
- Excluded shapes: BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, other confirmed energy-service ideas, obsolete parts/aftermarket pools, installer CRM/AI/quoting/compliance products, purchasing clubs, resource roll-ups, and products requiring a speculative wholesaler, connector maker, heat-pump OEM, DSO, insurer, or distributor agreement.

## Shared Timing Evidence

- The EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive requires suitable solar installations, where feasible, on new public and non-residential buildings over 250 m² by 31 December 2026 and adds further public, renovated non-residential, residential, and roofed-car-park deadlines through 2029–2030. This creates installation volume, but not automatic demand for any product below. [Directive (EU) 2024/1275, Article 10](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1275/oj/eng)
- The Commission's stated solar-PV ambition is at least 700 GW by 2030. [European Commission — Solar energy in buildings](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-efficiency/energy-performance-buildings/energy-performance-buildings-directive/solar-energy-buildings_fr)
- The JRC describes growing demand for PV and heat-pump installers amid broader labour shortages; CINEA cites almost 4.2 million construction vacancies through 2035. The commercial hypothesis is that tools which demonstrably remove skilled minutes can capture some of this pressure. [JRC — skills for the energy transition](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/jrc-news-and-updates/do-we-have-sufficient-skills-energy-transition-changing-labour-market-2024-01-16_en), [CINEA — construction professionals](https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/europes-construction-professionals-skilled-life-2024-10-22_en)
- Machinery placed on the EU market is subject to health, safety, documentation, and conformity duties; Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 January 2027. It is a design constraint, not a moat by itself. [EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/en)
- Electrical products in the relevant voltage bands must satisfy the Low Voltage Directive; permanently incorporated construction products may fall under the new Construction Products Regulation. [Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32014L0035), [Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3110/oj/eng)

## 1. FieldWire Cell — Mixed-Job Cable Microfactory for Small Contractors

**Customer-inside insight.** A small electrical, HVAC, PV, battery, or controls contractor does not want a miniature harness factory. It wants tomorrow morning's mixed cable set—different lengths, labels, ferrules, and job bundles—prepared without consuming a senior electrician's evening. Existing cut/strip equipment is optimized around machine operations; the proposed wedge is one compact cell that imports a job schedule and outputs many low-quantity, labelled, ferruled job packs with an automatic continuity/length record.

**Product and beachhead.** A guarded workshop machine for 0.5–16 mm² low-voltage control and switchboard wires: dereel, measure, cut, strip, ferrule, print/attach both-end IDs, and group by terminal schedule. Start with Polish renewable installers and small panel builders producing repeated but non-mass-production internal wiring. Do **not** make third-party PV DC connector assemblies in the first product.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company already has technicians, wiring tasks, cable scrap, bills of materials, and a workshop for time-and-error baselines. For 30,000–38,000 PLN the founder can own a guarded manual/semi-automatic alpha cell assembled from a dereeler, cut/strip module, ferrule unit, label printer, camera, and simple scheduler. After external deposits, up to 95,000 PLN buys the owned integrated prototype, drawings, firmware, fixtures, and safety file. No equipment-maker licence is needed.

**Independent paid proof.** Show an unedited production run against a manual control: at least 100 mixed wires, with measured labour, scrap, and error rate. Contact 40 unrelated electricians, panel shops, HVAC-control builders, and renewable installers from public directories. By day 180 require two unrelated buyers to each pay a non-refundable 2,500–5,000 PLN pilot/deposit and run their own live schedule through the cell. Founder cash risk stops at 38,000 PLN before those payments.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Toolheads, cable recipes, job-format importers, consumable label/ferrule cassettes, and installed-machine service history can extend into controls, industrial panels, prefabricated building services, and service-van cells. At 1,500 machines/year at €12,000 plus 8,000 installed machines paying €1,200/year for consumables/service, annual revenue is about €27.6 million. At 20% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €3 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 8–12 million PLN of later tooling/working-capital finance, founder equity is about €23 million in years 9–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** Komax/Schleuniger or a low-cost Asian machine maker combines existing modules with a job-pack UI and out-distributes the startup. Komax already markets flexible cut/strip equipment for low-to-mid-volume work, and compact automatic machines exist; this is evidence of competition, not white space. [Komax cut-and-strip range](https://www.komaxgroup.com/en-us/products/wire-processing/semi-automation-machines/wire-cut-and-strip), [Schleuniger cut-and-strip platform](https://www.schleuniger.com/fileadmin/schleuniger.com/products/cut-strip/Cut_Strip_EN.pdf)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if the alpha cannot cut total preparation labour by at least 35% without increasing rework; two independent users will not pay after seeing a live run; mixed-job changeovers erase the gain; the machine cannot be made safe and CE-ready within the first post-proof 100,000 PLN; or established compact machines already deliver the full job-pack workflow at an economically lower price.

## 2. JointProof — Field Verifier for PV DC Connector Terminations

**Customer-inside insight.** Installers are told to use the correct connector-specific tools and visually/tug-check field terminations, yet a connection expected to remain outdoors for decades can leave the roof without a recorded electrical and mechanical result. The proposed product is not another crimper or compliance report; it is a repeatable, de-energized end-of-termination check before mating and energization.

**Product and beachhead.** A portable fixture that identifies the selected connector/cable recipe, applies a controlled non-destructive axial proof load, performs four-wire contact-resistance measurement across a temporary test mate, checks insertion/retention geometry, and stores only a pass/fail result tied to a physical tag. Start with Polish C&I rooftop installers that make many field DC terminations. Never claim universal acceptance: each qualified recipe must be connector-family specific, and mixed-brand mating is out of scope.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company can create known-good and deliberately flawed sample harnesses and benchmark its current termination workflow without customer data. A force gauge, four-wire micro-ohm electronics, replaceable test mates, camera, enclosure, and calibration jig can produce an owned bench alpha for less than 30,000 PLN. A rugged owned prototype, firmware, fixtures, and calibration standards remain below 100,000 PLN after proof; no connector-vendor licence or approval is assumed.

**Independent paid proof.** Demonstrate repeatability on blind good/bad samples, then offer a ten-job paid field rental to unrelated installers. By day 180 require two independent installers to pay at least 4,000 PLN each and permit comparison of callback/rework findings across at least 200 field-made terminations. Founder cash exposure stops at 35,000 PLN before payment.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** The asset can compound through validated connector/cable recipes, calibration history, replaceable certified test-mate cassettes, and expansion to EV, storage, industrial DC, and preassembly QA. At 5,000 devices/year at €5,000 plus 20,000 installed devices paying €500/year for calibration/cassettes, annual revenue is €35 million. At 25% operating margin, 8× operating profit, €3 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 10–15 million PLN of later electronics/tooling finance, founder equity is about €44 million in years 9–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** Stäubli, Fluke, or a general test-equipment maker adds controlled pull and milliohm measurement to its existing PV tool family, with far greater trust and distribution. Stäubli explicitly says qualified, connector-specific tools are key to field-assembly quality, while Fluke already sells PV connector accessories. [Stäubli PV tools](https://www.staubli.com/us/en/electrical-connectors/products/renewable-energy-solutions/tools-and-accessories.html), [Fluke PV connector tool](https://www.fluke.com/en-ca/product/accessories/test-leads/tlpv-utool)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if field milliohm readings are not repeatable; a proof load changes seal/contact reliability; manufacturer instructions prohibit or make the external test meaningless; each connector needs uneconomic bespoke hardware; failure prediction is not better than a correct crimp tool plus tug; or independent installers will not pay at least €2,500-equivalent per production device. The PVEL/HelioVolta connector-safety guide documents connector mismatch and resistance risks but does not prove this device works. [PV connector safety guide](https://www.pvel.com/wp-content/uploads/PVEL-HelioVolta-Ultimate-Safety-Guide-for-Solar-PV-Connectors-Feb-2022.pdf)

## 3. StringTrace — Vendor-Neutral Physical PV Array Mapper

**Customer-inside insight.** A battery retrofit or O&M technician may inherit an array whose strings, rooftop rows, cable paths, and labels do not match reality. Existing I–V tools diagnose electrical performance after the operator builds an array model; they do not necessarily tell the technician which physical module/cable belongs to which isolated string. The valuable outcome is a trustworthy physical map before rewiring or fault isolation.

**Product and beachhead.** A de-energized transmitter/receiver kit that injects a safe coded signal into an isolated PV string or cable and lets a technician identify conductors and rooftop row/module groups, then emits a physical-to-electrical map. Start with small commercial arrays and battery/repower contractors encountering undocumented systems; do not require inverter APIs or OEM cloud access.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** Build a representative owned test roof/string at the family workshop and create controllable wiring faults. A coded-signal source, isolated probes, clamp/near-field receiver, firmware, and test array fit inside a 40,000 PLN alpha budget; the complete owned engineering prototype and safety fixtures remain below 100,000 PLN. One contract electrical engineer can be paid for bounded design work without receiving platform rights.

**Independent paid proof.** On the owned blind-wired array, first prove correct mapping across cable lengths, module types, sun conditions, and adjacent strings. Then require three unrelated PV service/retrofit firms to pay 2,000–4,000 PLN each for a field pilot on their customer-controlled sites by day 180. Founder cash risk stops at 40,000 PLN before payment.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Hardware calibration, signal-processing firmware, field topology cases, and later cable/ground-fault localization can extend to EV, building DC, telecom, and industrial wiring. At 4,000 devices/year at €4,000 plus 20,000 active subscriptions/calibration contracts at €600/year, annual revenue is €28 million. At 25% operating margin, 8× operating profit, €2 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 8–12 million PLN later product/certification capital, founder equity is about €35 million in years 9–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** Fluke/Solmetric adds physical signal-tracing to the PVA workflow and sells through its installed test-equipment channel. The PVA-1500 already measures I–V curves, stores results in an array tree, and compares strings with models. [Solmetric PVA-1500 user guide](https://www.solmetric.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SolmetricPVAnalyzer1500XXUsersGuide_en.pdf)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if PV module electronics, parallel paths, irradiance, shielding, or cable proximity make the signal ambiguous; safe isolation takes longer than manual tracing; the device cannot meet electrical safety/EMC requirements; the family test roof works but three independent sites do not; or technicians will not pay at least €2,000 per production device.

## 4. ArrayCrane — Rail-Anchored Module Change-Out Gantry

**Customer-inside insight.** Vertical lifts get new panels to a roof, but an O&M crew replacing one module in the middle of a dense pitched-roof array still has to unfasten, lift, stabilize, and move a large glass module without standing on neighbours or dismantling a long row. The wedge is change-out and retrieval inside the array, not another ladder lift.

**Product and beachhead.** A foldable, two-person micro-gantry with load-spreading adapters that clamps to qualified mounting rails, lifts one framed module vertically clear, translates it over adjacent modules, and lowers it to a roof-edge handoff. Start with service companies maintaining residential and small C&I pitched-roof arrays on two named rail families; expand compatibility only after load testing.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company can construct an owned test roof/array in its yard and supply technicians who perform timed module removals. Aluminium fabrication, a manual winch, load cell, rail adapters, ballast/load test, and drawings fit within 35,000 PLN for an alpha and below 95,000 PLN for an owned engineered prototype. No racking-vendor permission or exclusive parts are assumed.

**Independent paid proof.** Film an uncut blind module change against the current method, then invite unrelated O&M/installers to operate it on the test roof. By day 180 require two unrelated firms to pay a 5,000 PLN deposit or five-job rental and document crew count, minutes, and damage/near-miss outcomes. Founder cash risk stops at 38,000 PLN before payment.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Qualified rail adapters, training/certification, service parts, and adjacent tools for optimizers, glass, solar thermal, and façade modules can form a focused installation-equipment line. At 6,000 units/year at €4,000 plus €4 million of adapters/service, annual revenue is €28 million. At 18% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €2 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 8–15 million PLN of later tooling/inventory finance, founder equity is about €22 million in years 8–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** GEDA, Fluke/Solmetric, a racking manufacturer, or a roof-equipment fabricator adds the mid-array handoff to an established lift/tool channel. GEDA already sells solar-specific roof lifts up to 250 kg, and Solmetric sells a ladder-mounted module lift. [GEDA Solar Lift](https://www.geda.de/en/solutions/building-site/solar/), [Solmetric Module Lift](https://www.solmetric.com/modulelift.html)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if common rails or roofs cannot safely accept the temporary point loads; setup plus safety controls take as long as manual removal; compatibility resets for every rail; the gantry cannot satisfy machinery/lifting requirements within post-proof capital; or external O&M firms will not pay after operating it themselves.

## 5. RetrofitSpine — Exterior Electrification Service Route

**Customer-inside insight.** In existing light-commercial buildings, the expensive part of adding rooftop PV, EV charging, storage, or heat pumps can be routing new power/control cables through finished interiors and repeatedly reopening the building. Ordinary outdoor cable tray solves containment, not a presentable, inspectable, weather-managed route designed for successive electrification additions. The buyer wants one clean exterior route that future trades can reopen without re-damaging the building.

**Product and beachhead.** A modular façade-mounted service spine for detached workshops, retail boxes, small warehouses, and agricultural/light-industrial buildings: separated power/control channels, inspection covers, bend/entry modules, replaceable seals, drainage, fire-stop interfaces at penetrations, and reserved capacity. Start only on one- or two-storey non-residential walls where no occupied fire compartment is crossed; it is a physical kit, not design consulting.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company can install and repeatedly open a 20-metre mock wall and measure route labour using its own PV/heat-pump/EV jobs. Founder-owned CAD, full-size extrusion/bent-sheet prototypes, assembly jigs, fastener tests, and a first die/tooling package can remain below 100,000 PLN; pre-proof mock-up risk is capped at 35,000 PLN. No DSO, cable-tray vendor, or property partnership is required.

**Independent paid proof.** Build the mock-up, compare installed minutes and re-entry time with ordinary tray, then contact 30 unrelated electrical/renewable contractors and 20 light-industrial building owners. By day 180 require one external contractor and one external building owner to each pay a non-refundable 5,000 PLN design-to-supply deposit for separate live jobs; delivery is contingent on bounded product/test requirements, not on a speculative partnership.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Dies, tested connection details, penetration/fire assemblies, installer methods, and a growing accessory family can extend from light commercial into multifamily service risers and other EU climates. At 20,000 building kits/year averaging €2,500, annual revenue is €50 million. At 15% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €5 million net debt, and 60% founder ownership after 20–30 million PLN of later extrusion inventory/certification capital, founder equity is about €29 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** OBO, Niedax, or another cable-management incumbent copies the modular cover and penetration accessories through one product-line extension. OBO already sells PV cable routing and ballasted flat-roof systems. [OBO PV mounting/cable-routing instructions](https://www.obo.global/out/media/11-100_MA%20PV%20Montagesysteme%20Magic%20PV%20Flat%20Basic_EN_2025-02-06.pdf)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if each building still requires bespoke engineering; fire/UV/weather/structural testing cannot be bounded below the first post-proof 100,000 PLN; ordinary tray is less than 20% slower/cheaper enough that buyers will not switch; penetrations create unacceptable liability; or the first two unrelated buyers refuse deposits after a full-scale mock-up.

## 6. RetroPod — Manufacturer-Neutral Exterior Utility Room for Home Electrification

**Customer-inside insight.** Many retrofit homes lack a clean indoor plant room for a battery, inverter, heat-pump hydraulics, meters, protection, networking, and future service clearance. Installers improvise across garages, boiler rooms, and exposed walls. The hidden product is not another battery cabinet; it is a small factory-built exterior service room that lets multiple equipment brands live in one weatherproof, inspectable, fire-separated and replaceable envelope.

**Product and beachhead.** A flat-pack or craned insulated pod shell for detached-home and small-commercial retrofits, supplied without batteries/inverters: standardized equipment rails, two fire-separated zones, drainage/condensate route, ventilation paths, removable service panels, preformed cable/pipe entries, and documented load/clearance envelopes. Start with premium whole-home electrification projects where the alternative is building a bespoke annex or sacrificing an indoor room.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company can assemble a full shell in its yard and install ordinary dummy equipment using its own technicians. Founder-owned drawings, jigs, one full prototype shell, weather tests, and assembly manual fit within 90,000–100,000 PLN after paid proof; a 30,000–38,000 PLN non-functional full-size mock-up supports pre-sales. No equipment OEM, DSO, or building-owner partnership is required.

**Independent paid proof.** Invite unrelated whole-home installers and architects to configure equipment in the mock-up. By day 180 require two unrelated installers to each pay a non-refundable 7,500 PLN production-slot deposit tied to a named customer job, while founder risk remains below 40,000 PLN. The family company cannot be either deposit payer.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Productized shell sizes, tested wall/fire assemblies, equipment-clearance templates, installer training, and factory takt can expand to multifamily, farms, telecom, and light-commercial energy rooms. At 8,000 pods/year at €7,000, annual revenue is €56 million. At 12% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €8 million net debt, and 60% founder ownership after 30–50 million PLN of later factories/working-capital funding, founder equity is about €23 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** Outdoor cabinet, modular-building, or heat-pump manufacturers copy the shell once demand is visible and win on certification/distribution. Enwitec already offers manufacturer-independent prewired backup distributors; Greiner offers outdoor renewable-energy cabinets. These are adjacent incumbents and force the pod to prove that room-scale serviceability is a different purchase. [Enwitec PowerBackup BRAIN](https://enwitec.eu/powerbackup-brain/?lang=en), [Greiner outdoor renewable-energy cabinets](https://www.greiner.eu/en/renewable-energies/)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if local building/fire rules make the pod a bespoke permitted building rather than a repeatable product; shipping/site foundations destroy margin; equipment clearances and thermal/fire separation cannot be manufacturer-neutral; customers prefer a €2,000 ordinary cabinet; or the two external deposits do not appear after a full-scale mock-up.

## 7. FrostDock — Passive Monobloc Heat-Pump Defrost-Water Route

**Customer-inside insight.** An air-to-water heat pump can create significant defrost water exactly when outdoor trays and drains can freeze. Existing answers often add electrical tray heaters or tell the installer to route drainage case by case. The product hypothesis is a passive, retrofittable heat path that borrows a small amount of warmth from the external hydronic flow pipe and protects only the tray outlet and short drain route—without entering the refrigerant circuit, firmware, or OEM controls.

**Product and beachhead.** A universal-by-size clamp-on heat-pipe/phase-change module plus sloped drain cassette for outdoor monobloc air-to-water units in Poland, Baltics, Nordics, and other cold markets. Start with two pipe diameters and freestanding units where the hydronic line and drain are physically accessible. It is not a remote-monitoring service and makes no OEM warranty claim.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company can supply an owned used/test monobloc setup, technicians, and observed installation constraints. A freezer/cold-room rig, temperature/flow logging, machined heat path, drain cassette, and interchangeable clamps fit inside 30,000 PLN for an alpha and below 90,000 PLN for owned prototypes, tooling, and a test dossier. No heat-pump OEM licence is assumed.

**Independent paid proof.** First run repeated freeze/defrost cycles with measured heat extraction, drain clearing, and COP impact on the owned rig. Then ask unrelated heat-pump installers to pay for a winter field pilot or preorder. By day 180 require two unrelated firms to pay at least 6,000 PLN each for ten-kit lots, refundable only if the documented cold test fails—not for buyer convenience. Founder exposure stops at 35,000 PLN.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Patented heat-path geometry, cold-cycle data, clamp/tooling families, and installer distribution can extend to condensate lines, outdoor hydraulics, and light-commercial HVAC. At 150,000 kits/year at €320, annual revenue is €48 million. At 18% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €5 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 15–25 million PLN later molding/working-capital finance, founder equity is about €36 million in years 9–13. This volume is about 5% of the 2.9 million heat pumps EHPA says were sold in 21 European countries in 2025; actual applicable monobloc share is unknown. [EHPA 2026 market release](https://ehpa.org/news-and-resources/press-releases/heat-pumps-replace-middle-east-gas-imports-twice-over/)

**Strongest copy risk.** Heat-pump OEMs or drain-pan suppliers add a heat-recovery element to official accessories and remove warranty fear. Daikin already documents cold-region drain-pan heaters, Stiebel sells an electrically heated retrofit pan, and an EPO drainage-tray patent demonstrates active prior art. [Daikin installer guide](https://www.daikin.lt/content/dam/document-library/Installer-reference-guide/heat/air-to-water-heat-pump-low-temperature/EHVH04-08SU18%2C26CB6W%2C%20ERLQ-CV3_4PEN449978-1_Installer%20reference%20guide_English.pdf), [Stiebel AHP-DT.1](https://www.stiebel-eltron.com/en/home/products-solutions/renewables/heat_pump/accessories/ahp-dt-1/ahp-dt-1.html), [EPO EP3350520B1](https://data.epo.org/publication-server/rest/v1.2/publication-dates/20220615/patents/EP3350520NWB1/document.pdf)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if accessible pipe heat cannot keep the outlet open at design cold; heat extraction or condensation harms performance; freeze risk varies too much by unit/site; freedom-to-operate is blocked; OEM warranty language makes independent installers refuse the kit; or two external ten-kit payments do not follow a successful cold test.

## 8. TurfStitch — Quiet Slit-and-Close Microduct Installer

**Customer-inside insight.** A small contractor installing garden lighting, gates, irrigation controls, robot-mower boundaries, sensors, or low-voltage PV/EV communications often needs a narrow buried route, not an open trench and not a mini-excavator. Existing handheld trenchers remove spoil and still require cable placement and reinstatement. The proposed wedge is one battery machine that opens a narrow slit, feeds one qualified microduct, and rolls the turf/soil closed in the same pass.

**Product and beachhead.** A walk-behind battery tool for one shallow, code-bounded microduct size in lawns and prepared soil, initially for low-voltage/control applications—not mains power. Start with Polish landscape electricians, irrigation firms, gate/automation installers, and robot-mower installers. Renewable installations provide an internal alpha use but are not the only market.

**Directly controlled starting resource.** The family company has gardens/jobsite workflows, technicians, batteries/tools, and a yard where soil sections can be reconstructed. A slitting blade, duct feeder, closing rollers, depth limiter, guards, and owned prototype can be fabricated for 35,000–40,000 PLN; an engineered owned prototype, tooling, and test plots remain below 100,000 PLN. It needs no utility, DSO, or equipment-vendor licence.

**Independent paid proof.** Demonstrate metres/hour, reinstatement quality after 30 days, blade wear, and buried-depth variance across sand, clay, roots, and wet soil. By day 180 require three unrelated contractors in at least two trades to pay a 3,000–5,000 PLN deposit or ten-job rental. Founder cash risk stops at 40,000 PLN before those payments.

**Compounding and wealth shape — hypothesis.** Soil/blade recipes, replaceable cartridges, microduct consumables, safety/depth controls, and rental-fleet telemetry can expand into shallow fibre, perimeter security, agriculture, and selected code-compliant power routes. At 4,000 machines/year at €9,000 plus €5 million of blades/consumables, annual revenue is €41 million. At 20% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €5 million net debt, and 65% founder ownership after 15–25 million PLN later tooling/distribution capital, founder equity is about €34 million in years 9–13.

**Strongest copy risk.** MiniTrencher/GeoRipper, E-Z Trench, or a cordless outdoor-tool brand adds a feed shoe and closing rollers. GeoRipper already offers 25–60 mm trenches for cables and irrigation, and E-Z Trench claims a cable-installing trencher lineage since 1985. [GeoRipper specifications](https://georipper.ie/), [E-Z Trench cable installers](https://www.eztrench.com/cable-installers)

**Fatal dependencies / kill facts.** Kill if common roots/stones stop the single pass; turf recovery is not visibly better than a trencher; underground-service strike liability cannot be bounded; acceptable burial depths exclude too many jobs; the machine is too heavy/noisy for its niche; or three unrelated contractors will not pay after testing it on their own soil.

## Cross-Direction Proof Discipline

For every direction, the family company may document the baseline and host the alpha, but it may not sign the qualifying invoice, provide a disguised related-party deposit, or be the only source of workflow data. Before any advisory evaluation, the exact direction should have:

1. a named owned prototype/fixture/tooling bill of materials and IP assignment from every contractor;
2. a live, unedited benchmark against the current workflow;
3. a product-safety/conformity map with a quoted path rather than a vague promise;
4. at least two independent paid commitments (three where the ticket is low);
5. a bottom-up channel count and service/returns allowance; and
6. a signed stop rule that prevents more than 40,000 PLN pre-proof and more than 100,000 PLN original founder capital before repeat demand.

None of the eight directions has yet established independent willingness to pay, technical performance, freedom to operate, conformity cost, or the illustrated scale economics. Candidate generation only — not validated.
