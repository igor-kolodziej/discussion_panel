# Zero-to-One Evaluation: MouldRelay Dormant-Tool Reorder Rights

## Diagnosis

MouldRelay improves on generic spare pools by tying a physical tool to process history and an identified historical buyer, and its preorder gate limits capital risk. The weakness is structural: the customer may own the part design, can move or recreate demand, and intermittent reorders turn much of the portfolio into storage rather than a compounding asset. The proposed 1,200–1,500 active tools needed for founder wealth are not supported by a repeatable seller or utilisation channel. This shape should not consume a panel slot.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced evidence supports repair-policy momentum and long-tail spare inventory cost. Hypotheses dominate the commercial case: rights assignment, tool condition, historical buyer prepayment, annual utilisation, custody pricing, margins, and the number of acquirable active tools. No signed option, buyer order, or tool portfolio exists.

## Overall Score

**65/100.** Weighted points are `130.5 ÷ 20 = 6.525`; multiplying by 10 gives 65 after rounding. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 at best; often Tier 3.** Title to a named mould is owned, but demand and underlying design rights may remain with the customer. Strongest copy risk: the buyer supplies a sample/CAD file to another toolmaker or commissions a replacement tool. Clean exclusive production rights from both the converter and design owner would be needed for Tier 1.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail on scenario credibility.** The tables contain the required fields and avoid explicit double counting, but the conservative/expected paths require 1,200–1,500 active tools without evidence that enough tools have recurrent orders, that custody fees are accepted, or that rights transfer. High storage utilisation and thousands of clean assignments are heroic relative to the first wedge.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A ten-tool option, clean title check, and one 20,000 PLN prepaid run at 40%+ contribution fit within 180 days, 28,000 PLN, and employment. The gate proves one job, however, not portfolio repeatability.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is precise. Expansion reuses storage, moulders, and quoting, but every tool brings new rights and utilisation risk. Local toolroom economics help; broad converters can move inward easily when a reorder becomes attractive.

## Fatal Objections

1. The customer rather than MouldRelay may control the design and future production route.
2. Founder wealth requires a heroic count of active, repeat-order tools.
3. Storage and maintenance compound faster than advantage if utilisation is low.
4. The historical buyer route resets with each tool and is not an owned repeatable distribution channel.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Do not acquire production assets until a named historical buyer prepays and the complete process/qualification file transfers.
