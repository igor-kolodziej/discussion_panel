# Zero-to-One Evaluation: WooRenewal Merchant-Workflow Acquisitions

## Diagnosis

WooRenewal is materially different from a code acquisition: it buys an official update/distribution credential, installed merchant workflows, and existing renewals in one escrowed transfer. That solves cold start and produces paid proof without building or selling a new product. GPL copyability remains real, and the first qualifying sub-30,000 PLN target is not yet identified, but official transfer mechanics and transaction precedents make the resource reachable. The candidate can enter an independent panel.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: official WordPress transfer process; Freemius retirement/adoption guidance; vetted and sold plugin listings with organic installs, renewals, limited support, and high margins. Hypotheses: a qualifying small target will accept <=28,000 PLN cash plus receipts-capped note; renewal survives transfer; security/support remain bounded; related assets cross-sell; 60,000 paying sites are reachable. Marketplace claims are precedent, not due diligence.

## Overall Score

**84/100.** Weighted points are `167.5 ÷ 20 = 8.375`; multiplying by 10 gives 84 after rounding. Advisory only; not a simulated or real validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 conditional.** Official owner/commit/update authority, installed merchants, paid licences, billing, domain, code/IP, licence server, and support history are owned/contracted and transferred together. Strongest copy risk: a GPL fork or larger vendor replicates features and underprices renewal. It cannot copy the installed update path, configurations, reviews, billing cohorts, and merchant trust in one routine contract. Any missing transfer drops the asset to Tier 3.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass.** Three scenarios include merchants, ARPU, milestones, revenue, margins, acquisition capital, dilution, reinvestment, liabilities, equity value, timing and ownership. Conservative founder equity is 58.3m PLN/€13.5m after 7m PLN obligations at 85% ownership; expected is 181m PLN at 78%. No distributions are double-counted. The decisive risk is whether 60,000 sites and 35% operating margin can be reached through related acquisitions without platform/security shocks.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The candidate can acquire only after read-only receipts/cohorts, clean IP/security/support evidence, and escrow-ready official transfers. Cash at close is <=28,000 PLN and total pre-proof exposure <=40,000 PLN. The first independent renewal/new licence within 180 days is real paid proof; no product build, employment exit, or speculative marketing is required.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is one operational plugin. Expansion is restricted to the same merchant workflow until overlap and shared support are proven. Broad plugin vendors can copy features, but an acquired update/payment channel is line-specific and immediately distributive. Poland provides founder-cost advantage, while global WordPress distribution supplies the market.

## Fatal Objections

None in the written structure. The practical veto is target availability: no proceed if 100 owner contacts fail to produce five read-only data rooms or if no asset meets the cash, renewal, support, organic-acquisition and transfer criteria. A seller-personality channel or incomplete WordPress/billing transfer is fatal.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Buy the official installed update and renewal rail, never merely the GPL code.
