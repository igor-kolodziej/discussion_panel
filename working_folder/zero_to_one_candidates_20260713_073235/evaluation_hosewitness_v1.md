# Zero-to-One Evaluation: HoseWitness v1

## Diagnosis

HoseWitness has a legitimate product wedge between “the cleaning projectile exited” and “this capped assembly produced a low-debris physical screen.” Unlike JointProof, it does not need a new predictive physics claim: ISO/TS 18409 establishes hose sample collection, membrane/particle review is established, and the product explicitly limits itself to a correlated production screen. The founder can build and own the cassette, optics, adapters, specimen bank and protocol before customer cooperation, while the paid pilot tests both reference agreement and the workshop's ability to monetize a documented-clean SKU. Its main weakness is buyer economics: at €5 per cassette plus labour, usage must attach to premium/high-consequence hoses, not normal repairs.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Current sources support contamination during hose production, cleaning/sealing spend, projectile-exit verification, membrane/particle test equipment and at least 1,440 observable stationary workshop locations across three named networks. They do not prove the screen's extraction repeatability, safe residue/purge, cycle time, €15 workshop premium, 5,000 eligible-site denominator, reorder rate or incumbent response. Those assumptions are explicit and sequenced into paid tests. No panel or chatbot verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**74.25/100.** Weighted points are 148.5 across 20 weight units; `10 × 148.5 / 20 = 74.25`. Advisory only; not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** The sealed cassette/flow path, adapter/blank/purge recipes, controlled optics, firmware, specimen bank, reference corpus, lot traceability and item-level record are directly ownable. The product exists without donated customer data or exclusive partners. Strongest copy risk: Parker, Ultra Clean, HYDAC, Donaldson or MP Filtri adds residual-debris capture/imaging to an existing trusted cleaning/test workflow and bundles it through its channel.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, narrowly but credibly.** The conservative case yields about €14.0m founder equity in year 12–14 after €2.5m net obligations, with 60% ownership and no distributions double-counted. It requires 800 active sites at 4,000 screens/year and an evidenced 5,000 eligible-site denominator. Public networks show a 1,440-location observable floor before several named networks, independents and OEM cells; 5,000 is a falsifiable expansion requirement rather than an invented generic market. The gate fails if the account-level study cannot support it or if average use/premium economics weaken.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Founder exposure is <=35,000 PLN before two unrelated 6,000–9,000 PLN pilots; the owner-created cassette/rig/specimen asset does not need customer data, a distributor or formal laboratory status. Reference analysis and fluid-power review are ordinary paid services. Delivery remains part-time and total 12-month original capital is <=95,000 PLN.

## Expansion Check

The premium documented-clean hose beachhead is appropriately narrow. Expansion to additional hose/fluid/length recipes reuses the cassette/optics/blank discipline, but every new class needs reference evidence. The company becomes service-like if customer-specific validation dominates or low-use emergency counters become the target. Cross-brand neutrality and a production-speed consumable are the only credible route to moving outward faster than equipment incumbents move inward.

## Fatal Objections

1. A test-fluid rinse/purge may leave residue or create a compatibility/warranty problem worse than the contamination risk.
2. Workshop economics require a downstream specification, €15-equivalent premium or avoided loss; a “nice report” is insufficient.
3. 800 high-use sites and 4,000 annual screens are demanding and require a true 5,000-site eligible denominator.
4. Trusted cleaning/filtration incumbents can copy and bundle the workflow.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Create per-item physical evidence at the final production handoff and monetize a premium SKU, rather than merely logging that a cleaning procedure occurred.

## Recommendation

**pursue**

HoseWitness may enter an independent simulated panel after conversion to exact final-file wording. Both hard gates pass at advisory level; the panel should focus on buyer premium/utilization, fluid residue, denominator resilience and incumbent bundling.
