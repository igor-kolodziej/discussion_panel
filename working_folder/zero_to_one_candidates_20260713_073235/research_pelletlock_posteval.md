# PelletLock Decision-Critical Research After Advisory Evaluation

## Findings

- The final EU rule requires each economic operator to notify each installation and each carrier to notify its means of transport. This confirms that the regulated unit is more granular than a company, but no current public register/count yet exists before implementation.
- The Commission and regulation identify manufacturers, recyclers, converters, stockists, cleaners and carriers handling >=5 tonnes/year, but do not disclose how many installations use pneumatic bulk transfer, how many disconnect points each has, or their coupling families.
- The 2023 impact assessment and final implementation material do not provide a defensible dry-bulk disconnect-point denominator in the accessible public summary/results.
- OCS explicitly recommends catch pans, purge and possible self-closing valves. UltraTech, SolvAir and drain-capture products remain strong substitutes. No exact integrated post-purge enclosure/interlock/cassette/mass product was found in the current supplier search, but absence is not FTO or a moat.

## Decision

Desk research cannot validate the 27,000-point model. The regulation will eventually create installation/carrier registers, but waiting for them does not fix the conservative case's thin margin or easy hardware copy. The recurring/economic event and control point need material restructuring before a panel.
