# Zero-to-One Candidate: RatioSnap

Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, panelled, chatbot-reviewed, confirmed, or validated. Research date: 2026-07-13. All prices, installed-base counts, buyer behaviour, margins, volumes and valuation assumptions not explicitly supported by a source are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

RatioSnap turns the manufacturer's manual two-cup ratio check for two-component adhesive equipment into a keyed disposable last-point cassette and automated reader, giving small and mid-sized factories a fast signed proof of what actually left the dispense valve rather than asking an operator to tare, label, weigh, calculate and transcribe separate samples.

## Founder Fit

The founder fits the data model, measurement design, error analysis, calibration software, experiment plan and finite-account outbound. Benign reference fluids allow most development outside a production plant. A contract mechanical/product engineer specifies the cassette and dock; a chemical-safety specialist defines compatible materials and customer-trial handling; a small electronics contractor builds the first dual-scale/optical dock under assigned IP. These are fixed-scope vendors, not partners who must grant distribution or data.

The founder is not a dispensing-equipment engineer, adhesive formulator or plant quality authority. Customer material is handled only under the customer's safety data sheet, ventilation, PPE and waste procedure. The founder remains employed and caps work at 15–20 hours/week through proof.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is the quality/process engineer at a CEE electronics-potting, battery/power-electronics, lighting, appliance or industrial contract manufacturer operating semi-automatic 2K meter-mix equipment without trustworthy last-point inline ratio measurement. Cure quality depends on the base/catalyst ratio. Wear, blockage, trapped air, unstable pressure, check valves and material condition can cause error after an upstream control value still looks normal.

Graco's current official procedure confirms the real workaround: install a ratio-check nozzle at the dispense valve, stabilize pressure with purge shots, tare two containers, collect separated streams at the actual production flow and shot size, weigh both containers, subtract tare, apply specific-gravity conversion when volume ratio is needed, repeat shots and record the result. Other equipment manuals likewise use two cups or dishes. The ritual is valid but easy to postpone, transpose or document incompletely.

The buyer does not want a new cure prediction or a generic quality dashboard. They want the existing check completed faster, with fewer sample/identity/calculation errors and a record linked to material lot, valve, recipe and operator. The willingness-to-pay hypothesis is strongest where the adhesive is expensive, destructive cure testing is slow, off-ratio scrap is costly and the line lacks an integrated flow-ratio system.

## Product Wedge

The first product supports one valve adapter family, one shot-size band and three customer-approved chemistries:

1. a ratio-check adapter keeps A and B separated at the last point before mixing;
2. a disposable keyed cassette presents two pre-identified chemically compatible wells;
3. the dock verifies cassette identity/tare, reads two masses simultaneously, records temperature and refuses an unstable/purge shot;
4. recipe data converts mass ratio to volume ratio only when customer-supplied density/specific-gravity data permit it;
5. software returns measured ratio, tolerance result and a signed export tied to cassette/recipe/material lots; and
6. the contaminated cassette is closed and discarded through the customer's normal adhesive-waste route.

The product never claims to prove cure, bond strength, complete equipment calibration or full process compliance. It automates the existing last-point sample measurement. Plants with reliable inline flow measurement and automatic ratio alarms are excluded unless they still perform an independent physical check.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

High-end Graco/Nordson systems can monitor flow/ratio, but manufacturers still publish manual last-point sampling procedures and ratio-check adapters. That creates a middle band: installed semi-automatic and legacy equipment for which a full controls upgrade is uneconomic, yet a traceable manual check matters.

The insight is not that two scales are technologically novel. It is that the sample's identity, simultaneous capture, pre-tare, recipe conversion, acceptance logic and signed record can be packaged into one repeatable consumable event across brands. RatioSnap is commercially valid only if the full check saves at least four paid minutes, reduces expensive sample material or transcription error, and buyers pay for a signed event rather than buying another generic scale.

## Underused Momentum

- Graco's current public guidance explicitly recommends routine last-point checks and lists multiple failure modes, proving the task survives sophisticated metering equipment.
- Electrification, batteries, power electronics and thermal-management assemblies use more two-component potting, bonding and gap-filling materials. This supports a use-case pool but not demand by itself.
- Precision load cells, secure elements, temperature sensors and small-run chemically resistant molding/machining are purchasable without an OEM licence.
- Plants increasingly retain electronic batch records. RatioSnap can emit a simple signed artifact without replacing MES/QMS software.

## Beachhead Market

Start with Polish, Czech and Slovak plants running one to ten semi-automatic 2K cells for high-value potting or structural bonding, performing at least one manual ratio check per operating day, and lacking reliable last-point inline assurance. Each prospect must disclose the current check, shot size, material price, density basis, tolerance, waste route and scrap consequence before a demonstration.

Exclude spray foam/coatings, hand-mixed consumer epoxies, explosive/hazard classes outside the selected material envelope, fully automated lines already producing equivalent independent records, and plants whose proprietary valve cannot expose separated A/B streams safely.

Kill or reprice if dock gross margin is below 45%, cassette gross margin below 60%, cassette willingness to pay below €4, annual use below 200 checks per active dock, support/calibration exceeds 15% of revenue or direct CAC after the first ten exceeds €5,000.

## Primary Control Point

The founder can own within 12 months:

- cassette geometry, keys, closures, material-compatibility envelope, molds/dies and lot records;
- adapter drawings, dual-scale/optical dock, electronics, firmware and test fixtures;
- tare/temperature/density conversion and acceptance software;
- benign and customer-authorized reference-fluid tests, drift/failure signatures and cassette lot history;

- trademark, design rights and any non-obvious patentable capture/verification method.

All contractors assign foreground IP and editable files. At least two molders/machining routes are qualified or the product is killed. Customer recipes remain confidential; the shared corpus uses only separately authorized, de-identified failure/compatibility labels. This is a Tier 2 control-point hypothesis: real and directly ownable, but not yet hard to copy.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Reproduce the Graco-style two-cup reference using water/glycerol/safe viscosity-matched fluids across ratio, shot and flow ranges.
2. Build a dual-scale dock, three cassette geometries and one generic adapter for no more than 28,000 PLN.
3. Blindly compare RatioSnap with calibrated separate containers/scales; pre-register ratio error, repeatability, cross-contamination and cycle-time thresholds.
4. Obtain material-compatibility/safety, IP/FTO and machinery/electrical-product scope reviews before customer chemistry.
5. Approach 50 qualified external plants; require two unrelated 6,000–8,000 PLN prepaid 50-check pilots by day 180.
6. After payment, spend only the gated remainder up to 100,000 PLN on a guarded production-intent dock, first cassette tool, calibration fixtures, documentation and two delivered pilots.

The founder does not need proprietary equipment code, adhesive-formulator endorsement, plant data or an exclusive distribution agreement to create the base asset. Customer material trials follow the host's fixed SOP; benign-fluid agreement exists first.

## Distribution Advantage

The unknown founder leads with the buyer's own published procedure: “Bring your ratio-check nozzle and three recent records; this cassette will run beside the same calibrated-cup method.” The decision is based on method agreement, saved operator time, sample waste and record completeness, not the founder's status.

Each compatible adapter/chemistry/shot-range pack makes the account list more precise and lowers future demonstration cost. Signed cassette history and recipe configuration can embed the product into work instructions. The advantage disappears if plants accept an ordinary dual scale and reusable cups or if equipment OEMs bundle an equivalent keyed workflow.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. One valve family, three chemistries, two paid plants and 100 external checks.
2. Ten plants and three equipment families with measured calibration/support burden.
3. Add cassette sizes for small, unequal and continuous shots only after paid demand.
4. Integrate signed result export into common QMS/MES through simple files/API.
5. Expand to aerospace/rail/medical-device suppliers only after independent method evidence and their additional quality requirements.

The asymmetric thesis is a neutral cross-brand physical check that moves across legacy cells faster than each OEM chooses to retrofit its installed base. It fails if compatibility remains bespoke, if high-value plants all upgrade to inline monitoring or if the cassette is perceived as a convenience accessory with no durable workflow value.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland/CEE offers lower-cost product engineering and a dense base of contract manufacturing, electronics, automotive, appliance and industrial suppliers. Local-language demonstrations and short travel reduce first-ten CAC. EU product, chemical, waste, EMC/electrical and machinery rules may apply depending on final design and are market-access requirements rather than a moat. Global equipment vendors already serve the region; local origin alone has no protective value.

## Compounding Advantage

Every accepted check can add cassette-lot drift, shot-size limits, temperature effects, common operator mistakes, valve/adapter compatibility and failure signatures. Every installed dock creates recurring cassette/calibration demand and a configured record workflow. The intended loop is: more compatible cells -> more targeted accounts -> more paid checks -> better failure/compatibility evidence -> faster qualification -> more compatible cells.

No network effect is assumed. The loop breaks if customers will not authorize even de-identified labels, cassette use stays below 200/year, or each chemistry needs unique material engineering.

## Critical Evidence

- Graco's current official guidance recommends routine checking at the dispense valve and requires stabilized pressure, tared containers, repeat shots, weighing and specific-gravity conversion.
- Graco and Nordson sell sophisticated 2K systems and some systems include ratio alarms, proving both the value and incumbent threat.
- Multiple equipment manuals still prescribe separated cups/containers for ratio checks.
- No current source proves a 40,000-cell addressable base, a €5.50 cassette, four-minute saving, signed-record value or willingness to add a proprietary consumable.

## Critical Hypotheses

- At least 40,000 global cells perform manual last-point checks and at least 25,000 are commercially reachable without a full controls upgrade.
- RatioSnap agrees within customer tolerance across the selected shot/ratio/viscosity range.
- Plants value time, sample waste and signed identity enough to pay €5.50/check and €3,500/dock.
- Each active dock averages >=350 checks/year and adapters/recipes are reusable.
- Cassette materials/closure fit the selected chemicals without dangerous handling changes.
- Neutral cross-brand compatibility compounds before equipment OEMs bundle the function.

## Kill Criteria

- A current product already provides the same keyed simultaneous last-point capture and signed result at comparable cost.
- Reference agreement misses tolerance or cassette/tare/temperature drift causes false passes.
- Chemical exposure, fouling, closure or waste handling is worse than two cups.
- No two unrelated prepaid pilots after 50 qualified approaches and six demos by day 180.
- Accepted checks save <4 minutes, reduce no meaningful material/error risk, or buyers will pay <€4/cassette.
- Active use <200 checks/year, dock gross margin <45%, cassette gross margin <60%, or support >15% of revenue.
- Compatibility takes >5 engineering days per cell or needs proprietary OEM code/approval.
- Verified relevant-cell denominator <25,000 or conservative 6,000-dock share is heroic.
- Equipment manufacturers bundle an equivalent before the company has installed workflow switching cost.
