# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ReclaimGas v1

## Diagnosis

ReclaimGas converts tightening F-gas rules into a physical, saleable product rather than advice, and the founder has a real installer route for learning. Conditional source options, direct authorised custody and buyer prepayment create a disciplined first transaction. The weakness is scale: established gas distributors/reclaimers already control cylinders and buyers, EU HFC supply is phasing down, and the 500-tonne conservative case lacks current grade-level available-flow evidence. Paid proof is reachable, but the founder-wealth gate is not yet safe.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official sources establish service restrictions, reclaimed-gas exceptions, certification/reporting and falling HFC supply. The most specific reclaimed-share evidence is dated (3% of HFC supply in 2020); 2024 HFC placed-on-market volume fell 37% year over year. No source proves Polish homogeneous recoverable kilograms, €45–50/kg realised pricing, 80–85% yield, 25–32% gross margin or 500 tonnes/year reachable supply. Family access is evidence of a learning channel, not scale control. No external verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**70/100.** Weighted points are 139 across 20 weight units; `10 × 139 / 20 = 69.5`, rounded to 70. This is advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 after a multi-branch exclusive right; Tier 2 for the first lots.** Named kilograms, source/custody rights, buyer reservations and transaction data control real inventory. Strongest copy risk: incumbent distributors/reclaimers offer better cylinder credits and lock the same installers/sources with existing logistics.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The conservative outcome reportedly reaches about €11m founder value but depends on 500 tonnes/year, sustained €45–50/kg revenue, 15% operating margin and 63% ownership amid a regulated HFC phase-down. Current evidence does not show that 500 tonnes is below 10% of realistically reachable eligible flow; dated EU data suggest reclaimed HFC was only a small share of total supply. The outcome lacks resilience to grade mix, falling prices, lower yield, working-capital debt or incumbent response.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** An unrelated buyer's >=15,000 PLN prepayment for a lot is genuine paid proof; <=38,000 PLN is at risk, no plant/depot/inventory is bought before payment, and certified parties do physical work. The source, reclaimer, lawyer/logistics and buyer sequence is dependency-dense but explicitly gated.

## Expansion Check

The first homogeneous grade is a strong learning beachhead. Multi-branch source rights can serve many buyers, avoiding a local-site reset. Expansion across grades/countries reopens regulatory and demand questions. Polish/family access helps start; incumbents possess superior cylinder fleets, credit and customer trust.

## Fatal Objections

1. No current bottom-up evidence supports the 500-tonne conservative scale without heroic share.
2. The underlying HFC market is deliberately shrinking; transition demand may peak before the wealth horizon.
3. Incumbent distributors/reclaimers can bid for the same source rights and may have structurally lower costs.
4. Yield, contamination, cylinder and working-capital economics can erase the assumed margin.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Control homogeneous documented kilograms and prepaid offtake before touching regulated inventory; use direct authorised custody rather than founder-owned facilities.
