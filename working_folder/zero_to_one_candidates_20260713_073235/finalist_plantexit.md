# Zero-to-One Candidate: PlantExit Exclusive MRO Liquidation Mandates

## One-Sentence Thesis

Win exclusive, time-bounded sale mandates over closing Polish factories' serialised maintenance inventory and specialist fixtures, match each asset to active nearby plants before taking custody, and compound a model-to-failure-to-buyer graph that sells shutdown assets for more than scrap.

## Founder Fit

The founder can mine public restructuring data, structure inventories with computer vision, build price/match models, and run written buyer outreach. Contract industrial technicians inspect lots; a lawyer handles mandates. The model is asset-light before a sale but requires more transaction negotiation than the founder naturally prefers.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The seller is a closing/restructuring factory, insolvency practitioner, or owner facing a deadline to clear shelves of unused motors, drives, bearings, fixtures, sensors, and machine-specific spares. Current liquidation often bundles assets to brokers or scrap buyers. Buyers are maintenance teams needing an exact part faster than OEM delivery, but they distrust condition and compatibility.

## Product Wedge

One exclusive 90-day mandate for a photographed, serialised, condition-graded MRO inventory. PlantExit publishes model-specific listings, contacts likely installed-base buyers, holds buyer funds, and arranges inspection/shipping. It buys no inventory before the first sale.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Generic auctions maximise bidder count, not exact installed-base matching. The value gap lies in identifying which active machines use each part, verifying condition, and reaching maintenance buyers before the plant-clearing deadline. A data graph can improve recovery rates across repeated mandates.

## Underused Momentum

Poland's KRZ makes restructuring and insolvency information public and supports continuity/rescue. Industrial firms hold long-tail MRO stock, while EU circularity policy favours reuse. These facts support sourcing, not transaction margins.

## Beachhead Market

One closing Wielkopolska factory with 300–1,500 serialised unused or serviceable MRO items for common Siemens/SEW/ABB/Festo/SKF-era equipment plus some proprietary fixtures, excluding safety-sensitive unknown-condition parts.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Charge 18–30% of realised sale value plus inspection/shipping pass-through. Hypothesis: one mandate has 0.5–3m PLN recoverable GMV, 55% gross margin on the commission after contractor/logistics costs, and 20–28% operating margin at route density. Avoid title and working-capital risk before buyer payment.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First ten buyers come from model-number searches, direct outreach to factories with matching equipment, service contractors, and maintenance distributors. The first 100 require repeated mandates across a defined industrial corridor and an opt-in model-level buyer list.

## Primary Control Point

An executed exclusive sale mandate over the named inventory and transaction flow, including seller warranties on title, access for inspection, serial/condition data, permission to market, and fee protection. After the mandate expires, the control point disappears.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Monitor KRZ, owner announcements, industrial-property closures, and adviser networks; approach 40 cases; require a zero- or low-upfront mandate; spend <=15,000 PLN on inspection and cataloguing only after exclusivity. Obtain a paid sale within 180 days. Unknown-founder reach is plausible through public cases but trust with insolvency professionals is unproven.

## Distribution Advantage

Exact model/part SEO, a growing opt-in buyer graph, and faster inventory ingestion can reduce buyer-search cost. The strongest copy risk is an established auction house or MRO broker adding serialised listings and direct outreach.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Dominate one corridor and asset class, then add factories and model families. Use past clearing prices and compatibility evidence to underwrite minimum recovery and win better mandates. Expand to fixtures and complete line change-parts only when condition and buyer matching are provable.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish language, physical inspection, KRZ access, lower cataloguing cost, and proximity support entry. No formal barrier prevents auction houses from copying.

## Compounding Advantage

Closed transactions add buyer intent, price, condition, compatibility, and seller references. However, supply remains episodic and mandate exclusivity resets with every factory.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of distributable after-tax cash during employment and cap optional founder distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Cash funds inspection capacity, buyer acquisition, insurance, and selective minimum guarantees only after repeat sale evidence.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Approach 40 qualified closures/advisers, sign one exclusive mandate by day 75, catalogue <=300 high-value items, and close at least three buyer transactions totalling 60,000 PLN GMV by day 180. Pre-proof spend <=25,000 PLN and work remains part-time with contracted inspection.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

5,000 PLN sourcing; up to 25,000 PLN after a signed mandate; up to 60,000 PLN only after paid sales and positive contribution; full 100,000 PLN only after three mandates and repeat buyer demand. Family capital excluded until a repeatable acquisition channel exists.

## Critical Dependency Map

Seller title, exclusive mandate, inspection access, buyer trust, shipping, returns, and sufficient recoverable value are required. The inventory is rejected if condition evidence is weak or regulated/safety parts dominate.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Three independent mandates, 20 paying buyers, positive contribution after returns/logistics, a documented source channel, two repeat buyers, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

The founder retains ingestion tooling, price records, buyer opt-ins, and adviser contacts. No inventory purchase means the first failure does not strand capital.

## Critical Evidence

KRZ provides public restructuring/insolvency information; EU projects document long-tail spare inventory costs. Neither proves that sellers will grant exclusivity or buyers will trust a new intermediary.

## Critical Hypotheses

Mandates are obtainable without guarantees; 18–30% fees are accepted; serialised matching materially beats auctions; repeat supply density exists; returns/fraud do not erase margin.

## Kill Criteria

No mandate after 40 qualified approaches; fewer than 60,000 PLN paid GMV by day 180; realised take below 15%; contribution below 35%; more than 10% returns/disputes; or each mandate requires a senior adviser relationship the founder cannot repeat.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 20 hours/week; contract industrial inspector; transaction lawyer; insured warehouse/logistics only after sales. Pre-proof <=25,000 PLN; 12-month founder cap 100,000 PLN.
