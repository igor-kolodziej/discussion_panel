# JointProof Decision-Critical Research After Advisory Evaluation

## Question

Can current public/official evidence clear the exact technical and instruction-compatibility uncertainty identified by the advisory evaluation?

## Findings

- Stäubli's official material confirms that correct connector-specific assembly and qualified tooling matter, and it offers MC4/MC4-Evo 2 test plugs. This makes a temporary connection physically plausible; it does not authorize or validate JointProof's proposed proof-load/resistance protocol.
- IEA PVPS failure material confirms that connector mismatch and rising contact resistance can produce delayed faults and that connector dimensions/tolerances are not universally standardized. This strengthens the pain but makes cross-family recipe claims harder.
- IEA PVPS mobile-test guidance discusses string-level series resistance and Fluke documents insulation/protective-conductor tests. Neither establishes that a field termination's consequential latent defects can be classified immediately by a low-resistance measurement through a temporary mate.
- No official source found supplies a non-damaging axial proof-load threshold, a JointProof-style acceptance distribution, or evidence that such a test predicts long-term connector integrity without changing seals or contacts.

## Decision

Desk research cannot clear the fatal technical dependency. Only an engineer-reviewed physical experiment on the exact connector/cable recipe can do so. The pain remains real, but the candidate is not panel-ready in an idea-selection run that requires evidence-backed resource control rather than a proposed future experiment.
