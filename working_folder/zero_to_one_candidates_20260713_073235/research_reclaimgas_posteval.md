# ReclaimGas Decision-Critical Research After Advisory Review

The EEA's 2025 report provides the missing current volume fact: EU HFC reclamation was **2,086 tonnes in 2024**, up from 931 tonnes in 2023; 29 companies reported reclamation. The same report says HFC supply fell 21% in physical tonnes in 2024 and the regulation phases HFCs out by 2050. Source: https://www.eionet.europa.eu/etcs/etc-cm/products/etc-cm-report-2025-05/@@download/file/ETC%20report%202025-05-2.pdf (Table 3 and briefing).

The candidate's 500-tonne conservative scale equals about 24% of all EU HFC reclamation reported in 2024, not the required <10% of realistically reachable eligible flow. Even if reclamation grows, grade fragmentation, incumbent control and long-run HFC phase-out make the conservative bridge non-conservative. The missing fact worsens rather than resolves the Founder Wealth Gate.
