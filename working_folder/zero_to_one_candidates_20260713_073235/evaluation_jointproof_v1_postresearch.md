## Diagnosis

The missing fact is not a market-report gap that further desk research can resolve; it is the product's core experimental result. Until that result exists, the candidate asks a panel to value a hypothetical fixture and hypothetical field labels simultaneously.

## Gates

- **Reachability:** pass as an experiment plan, but not as current evidence that an external buyer should prepay.

## Strongest Objection

The device may measure a quantity that is technically real but commercially non-predictive, or may change the connector it purports to verify. That failure collapses the product, the field-data loop and the wealth model together.
