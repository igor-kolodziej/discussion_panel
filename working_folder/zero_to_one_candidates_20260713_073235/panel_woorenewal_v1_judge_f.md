## Diagnosis

WooRenewal has a strong non-obvious insight: in GPL merchant software, the scarce asset is not code but the atomic combination of the trusted update channel, installed configurations, renewal contracts, billing rail, support history, and merchant trust. That can create a genuine asset-level control point, credible switching costs, high gross margins, and immediate acquired distribution. The first-acquisition plan is unusually disciplined and can plausibly produce a paid receipt within the founder's employment, time, and 40,000 PLN risk constraints, although the conjunction of price, quality, seller-note, and transfer requirements may produce an empty target set. The decisive weakness is that the €10 million founder-wealth case is arithmetically coherent but economically unbridged: the so-called conservative scenario requires 60,000 paying sites, substantial price expansion, 35% operating margin, and 85% founder ownership without showing how ten acquisitions and 8 million PLN of acquisition capital create 54 million PLN of revenue. The idea is therefore a credible small-asset acquisition experiment, but it is not yet a credible founder-wealth vehicle as written.

- Billing agreements, customer rights/consents, update infrastructure, plugin-directory ownership, licence-server access, domain, documentation, and all contractor IP can transfer atomically and on time.
- Renewal and organic acquisition survive the ownership change.
- Related plugins create merchant overlap, cross-sell, or shared operating savings rather than resetting distribution with every acquisition.
- Blended ARR can rise from the stated initial 100–500 PLN range to 900 PLN in the conservative case while renewal remains at least 75%.
- A global 60,000-site portfolio can be assembled and grown with the stated capital, dilution, team, support, and security burden.

## Control Point

**Tier 1, conditional on completed atomic transfer.** The official WordPress owner/commit/update channel, installed base, active licences and renewals, billing rights, licence server, domain, brand, configuration compatibility, lawful customer/support data, and complete IP are owned or directly controlled after closing. The candidate correctly assigns no control value to source code alone and refuses to release escrow if any essential component is missing.

The important qualification is that no control point is owned before the first acquisition. Tier 1 applies to a successfully acquired asset, not yet to the acquisition company or its seller pipeline. Portfolio-level defensibility remains closer to Tier 2 until repeated target access, shared merchant identity, cross-sell, or shared operating infrastructure is demonstrated.

### Strongest Copy Risk

The strongest copy risk is not a literal clone taking the same WordPress owner credential. It is a larger WooCommerce or plugin vendor copying the workflow, bundling or underpricing it into an already distributed suite, and using its own installed base and brand to induce migration at renewal; a better-capitalized roll-up can also acquire competing update channels. The acquired plugin retains a real switching-cost defense, but the broader roll-up method and adjacent-product strategy are copyable, and the entire control point remains exposed to WordPress/WooCommerce platform rules and security enforcement.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The conservative-case arithmetic is internally correct: 54 million PLN revenue at 35% operating margin gives 18.9 million PLN operating profit; a 4-times multiple gives 75.6 million PLN enterprise value; subtracting 7 million PLN produces 68.6 million PLN equity; 85% founder ownership gives approximately 58.3 million PLN, or €13.5 million at 4.33 PLN/€. The model also avoids double-counting personal distributions by excluding them from the gate total.

The economic bridge is not credible enough to call that case conservative:

1. The first target floor is 100 paying sites, while the Year-14 case needs 60,000—a 600-times increase from that floor.
2. Ten assets imply an eventual average of roughly 6,000 paying sites each. The candidate supplies no per-asset acquisition sizes, organic growth rates, price-migration cohorts, churn bridge, or cross-sell build showing how those sites appear.
3. Eight million PLN of cumulative acquisition capital is not reconciled with 54 million PLN of portfolio revenue and 18.9 million PLN of operating profit. At the candidate's own two-times-earnings acquisition discipline, acquiring an already mature earnings base of that size would cost far more; therefore most value must come from post-acquisition growth, but that growth engine is not modeled.
4. The blended 900 PLN ARR is materially above the stated 100–500 PLN initial licence range. No customer mix, repricing retention, bundle adoption, or cohort evidence supports the uplift.
5. Maintaining 85% founder ownership while funding the acquisition, product, security, support, and management stack needed for 60,000 global merchants is possible in principle but unsubstantiated here.
6. The expected and strong cases amplify the same unsupported acquisition-capital efficiency, customer growth, pricing, margin, and dilution assumptions; they do not cure the missing conservative bridge.

This failure is **fatal to a `pursue` verdict under the founder's explicit €10 million gate**, although it does not prove that the first acquisition cannot become a profitable small business.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, narrowly and as a plan rather than as demonstrated evidence.**

The candidate defines a legitimate paid-proof event: an unrelated renewal or new licence settled to the founder's entity after official transfer. Spending is phased at 5,000 PLN, 12,000 PLN, and at most 40,000 PLN; cash consideration is capped at 28,000 PLN; escrow prevents release when an essential asset fails to transfer; no product build, advertising channel, privileged introduction, full-time resignation, or family capital is required. Fifteen to twenty-five founder hours per week stays within the five-hour daily ceiling, and contract security/legal help covers specialist work.

The pass is narrow because paid proof requires a chain of external successes: a qualifying low-price target must exist, the seller must provide a data room and accept contingent consideration, platform and billing rights must transfer, customer/data rights must be lawful, security/IP diligence must clear, and a receipt must arrive by Day 180. These dependencies are sequenced and loss-limited rather than wished away, which is why the gate passes; however, their conjunction makes schedule risk high. The first receipt proves transfer and cash collection only. Six to twelve months of cohorts are still required to prove renewal, support burden, contribution margin, and seller-note service, and a second acquisition is required to prove repeatability.

Employment compatibility is also conditional on the founder's actual employment agreement, outside-business/IP restrictions, and the ability to cover urgent security or checkout incidents without exceeding the time ceiling. The candidate supplies no evidence on those points.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is strong only after one specific workflow family is selected; the current list of order routing, fulfilment, returns, checkout, catalogue, and subscriptions is a search domain, not one niche. Expansion through products serving the same merchants can be asymmetric because acquired update channels and configuration history lower cross-sell and support costs. The candidate's second-asset proof rule is sensible, but 10% cross-sell or generic shared security savings alone may not establish profitable domination. Broad vendors do not need the exact credential if they can bundle a substitute and use superior distribution, while better-capitalized buyers can compete for the same neglected assets. Poland supports low-cost technical execution, but it creates no exclusive sourcing or customer-access edge; the plan is global and platform-dependent from day one.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Buy the atomic installed update-and-renewal distribution asset, not source code. That insight is commercially meaningful because it removes cold start and creates a harder-to-copy switching-cost position before feature development.

## Recommendation

**research further**

The candidate should not receive a `pursue` verdict because the Founder Wealth Gate fails. The appropriate next judgment point is primary evidence from the existing kill-gated funnel: qualifying target availability and seller terms, normalized cohorts and owner earnings, atomic transferability, and then whether the observed first- and second-asset economics can support a non-heroic bridge to the required founder wealth. This is an evidence requirement, not a rewrite or pivot of the candidate.
