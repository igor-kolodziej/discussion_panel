## Business-Transfer Momentum

- On 23 June 2026 the European Commission described SME ownership transfer as a pressing demographic challenge, with growing numbers of owners approaching retirement without a designated successor and potentially millions of firms affected in the next decade. Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/news/new-commission-guidance-will-make-it-easier-transfer-ownership-smes-2026-06-23_en
- The Commission's 22 June 2026 recommendation asks Member States to modernise legal/tax systems, support preparation and digital matchmaking, and preserve productive assets and business continuity. Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/publications/commission-recommendation-facilitate-transfer-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises_en
- The Commission says business transfers can be as important as start-ups, while current barriers include poor preparation, tax issues, weak support, and inconsistent online-market quality. Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/smes/continuity-and-exit/business-transfers_en
- More than 60% of European companies are family businesses, and the Commission lists succession preparation and access to finance as central challenges. Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/smes/sme-fundamentals/family-business_en
- The 2026 PARP SME report is based on GUS and Eurostat data and confirms the economic significance of the Polish SME base. Source: https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/publications/publication/raport-o-stanie-sektora-malych-i-srednich-przedsiebiorstw-w-polsce-2026

## Aftermarket and Repair Momentum

- Directive (EU) 2024/1799 promotes repair and, for covered product categories, access to spare parts, repair information, and software tools. Its direct legal scope is mainly consumer goods; it is timing evidence for repair policy, not proof that Afterline's industrial targets are covered. Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1799
- Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 creates an ecodesign framework covering physical goods and identifies premature obsolescence, unavailable repair information, and unavailable spare parts as relevant concerns. Product-specific duties require future delegated acts; this is directional evidence, not immediate Afterline entitlement. Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/eng
- The EU-funded DIRECTSPARE project identified the high inventory cost and complexity of long-tail spare parts and investigated on-demand supply for SMEs. This supports the problem structure but does not prove candidate demand or margins. Source: https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/213424/reporting

## Polish Industrial and Restructuring Access

- GUS reported 2024 industrial sold production up 0.3%, with new orders in machinery and equipment up about 24%; this indicates an active industrial base but does not size the exact target niche. Source: https://ssgk.stat.gov.pl/01.2025/Przemysl.html
- Poland's National Register of Debtors centralises public restructuring and insolvency information and is explicitly designed to facilitate continuity and preserve contracts where possible. It is a public sourcing channel for PlantExit, not a guaranteed lead source. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/power/krajowy-rejestr-zadluzonych-krz

## Decision-Critical Hypotheses To Test

1. At least one owner-led niche machine OEM will sign a 90–120 day exclusive aftermarket option for no more than 8,000 PLN upfront plus a performance earn-out.
2. The option can lawfully include a seller-approved installed-base communication and clean assignment/licence of the required IP, contracts, tooling, technical documentation, and service mark.
3. At least three independent installed-base customers will prepay 25,000 PLN total before closing, with at least 45% expected contribution margin.
4. One acquired line can produce at least 1.5 million PLN annual revenue at 18% or greater operating margin without founder labour scaling one-for-one.
5. Seller-financed deal sourcing can repeat without a high-status intermediary once the first reference and standard contract exist.

## MicroRouteFort Decision-Critical Evidence

- EU Bronze requires a visual perimeter deterrent, 15-lux perimeter/lanes, 25-lux entry/exit, working entry/exit CCTV, daily surveillance checks, risk/security plans, and trained staff/remote operators. Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32022R1012
- Every certified level also requires existing separate toilets/showers, warm water, daily cleaning, 24/7 snacks/drinks, dining area, free internet, personal power, emergency procedures, and from 31 December 2026 reefer power. These services therefore must already be contractually included in the first site's host facility; the POC does not build them.
- On 3 December 2025 the Commission recommended rapid upgrading of insecure existing parking to the lowest standard, further national/EU funding, and reported a 390,057-space current gap (3,250 areas) rising to 483,000 (4,025 areas) by 2040.
