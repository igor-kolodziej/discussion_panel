# Round Six — Founder-Owned Product Directions

Generated 2026-07-13. These are five new, research-backed directions only. No direction has been evaluated, scored, panelled, validated, or selected as a finalist. All market-size, pricing, adoption, margin, and valuation figures marked as hypotheses require buyer evidence.

The common design rule is that the founder can build and own the first physical resource before customer cooperation. Family-company access is not used as paid proof. Each first paid event requires two unrelated external buyers and keeps pre-proof exposure below 40,000 PLN.

## 1. HoseWitness — Per-Assembly Hydraulic Cleanliness Proof

**Commercial insight and inside view.** A hose workshop can prove that it fired a cleaning projectile, but the machine owner ultimately cares whether the *finished, capped assembly* contains cutting rubber, wire, dust, or forgotten debris. Parker says most hose contamination enters during cutting or skiving and recommends cleaning and plugging every assembly. Yet ISO/TS 18409 says its fluid-sampling methods are intended for statistical validation of cleaning methods, not systematic processing of large production batches. The gap is economical evidence for each cleanliness-critical hose, not another cleaning gun.

**Product and exact paid event.** A compact bench loop pushes a fixed, clean fluid charge through a finished hose into a sealed disposable membrane cassette. A controlled phone/reader image classifies particle count and morphology, prints a serialized pass label, and seals the hose. Offer two unrelated hydraulic workshops a prepaid 100-hose comparison pilot at 6,000-9,000 PLN each: premium assemblies are tested with HoseWitness and a blinded subset is checked by an external particle counter or conventional patch test.

**Resource bootstrap and first proof.** Spend at most 35,000 PLN on a clean-fluid reservoir, pump, reference filtration, machined adapters, commodity membranes, molded/printed cassette prototypes, lighting/optics, and reference analysis. The founder owns the cassette geometry, imaging/calibration software, adapters, protocol, labelled debris images, and per-assembly result format. The proof before serious capital is agreement versus reference counts, cycle time below four minutes, cassette gross cost below €1.20, and paid willingness to add the test to a premium hose SKU. No hose-OEM approval is assumed.

**Control-point hypothesis.** **Tier 2 initially:** the physical system and corpus are unilaterally buildable and ownable, but their commercial strength is unproved. It could compound through chemistry/size-specific cassettes, the largest cross-brand hose-contamination image corpus, OEM cleanliness profiles, and recurring per-assembly consumption.

**Conservative wealth bridge and denominator.** Hypothesis: at least 30,000 eligible hose benches worldwide serve mobile machinery, agriculture, mining, industrial maintenance, and OEM production; this must be verified from distributor/assembler directories. At steady state in year 10-12, 6,000 installed readers each consume 750 cassettes/year at €3.50 and 1,200 new/replacement readers sell at €4,000: €20.55m revenue. At 65% gross and 25% operating margin, EBIT is €5.14m. At 7× EBIT, less €3m obligations, 65% founder ownership implies about €21.5m founder equity. Capital hypothesis is €1.5m cumulative, funded mainly from deposits, gross profit, tooling finance, and only later strategic capital; 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested while employed.

**Strongest copy risk.** Parker, HYDAC, Donaldson, or Ultra Clean could combine existing cleaning, patch-test, and verification products into a per-hose workflow and use their channel advantage.

**Fatal dependencies / kill tests.** Kill if a 100-hose blind comparison cannot separate acceptably clean from contaminated assemblies; if a complete test takes over five minutes; if workshops will not pay at least €3 per premium assembly; if fluid carryover creates more risk than the test removes; or if the verified eligible-bench denominator is below 15,000.

**Sources.** [ISO/TS 18409:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/66733.html); [Parker hose technical handbook](https://www.parker.com/literature/Hose%20Products%20Division%20Europe/HPDE%20Literatures/02_C4400_UK_TechHandbook.pdf); [HYDAC CTU 1000](https://catalog.hydac.com/item/contamination-sensors/contamination-test-units/4057313); [Ultra Clean Projectile Verification System](https://ultracleantech.com/product/uc-pvs-g5-projectile-verification-system/); [EDiS European industrial-distributor network](https://edis.eu/).

## 2. DoseProof Waterline — Commissioning Proof for Poultry Medication and Vaccination

**Commercial insight and inside view.** Poultry staff are told to calibrate medicators by disconnecting the outlet, watching a measuring cup, and collecting water in a pail. Vaccine distribution is still checked with blue dye at line ends and by inspecting birds' tongues. Those rituals test fragments of the job. The grower wants to know, before a valuable flock is dosed, whether the injector ratio, transport time, and concentration uniformity across real drinker lines are all acceptable.

**Product and exact paid event.** A portable kit uses a food-compatible, non-drug tracer concentrate and two clip-in optical cells: one immediately after the medicator and one successively at distal line flush points. Software reports actual dilution, arrival time, decay, and line-to-line coefficient of variation, then guides a complete flush. Two unrelated poultry-equipment or veterinary-service firms prepay 6,000-10,000 PLN each for four between-flock house audits; the pilot never administers a medicine or vaccine.

**Resource bootstrap and first proof.** For at most 32,000 PLN, build a recirculating waterline bench, optical cells, pressure/flow sensing, food-dye or fluorescent-tracer sachets, quick-connect adapters, and a rugged case. The founder owns the cell/reader design, tracer-dose sachet specification, calibration curves, house audit protocol, and anonymized line-response library. Proof is repeatability against gravimetric medicator calibration, recovery of deliberately introduced ratio and dead-leg faults, complete tracer flushing, sub-45-minute house audit time, and two paid external audits.

**Control-point hypothesis.** **Tier 2 initially:** owner-buildable hardware, consumable and calibration data; the moat becomes credible only if the same portable protocol works across pump and house types better than cup-and-dye checks.

**Conservative wealth bridge and denominator.** Eurostat reports 8.5bn EU meat-utility chicks produced in 2024. At a hypothesis of 6.5 cycles and 40,000 birds per commercial house, that is roughly 32,700 EU broiler-house equivalents before layers, turkeys, pigs, and non-EU markets. Hypothesis: a global addressable service base of at least 5,000 integrator/veterinary/producer-group kits. In year 10-12, 2,500 active kits run 500 tests/year at €14 per test and 500 new/replacement readers sell at €5,000: €20m revenue. At 62% gross and 23% operating margin, EBIT is €4.6m; 7× EBIT less €2.5m obligations and 65% founder ownership yields about €19.3m founder equity. Cumulative capital hypothesis is €1.2m, with 95% reinvestment while employed.

**Strongest copy risk.** Dosatron or a poultry-equipment major can add first-party tracer commissioning to its pump/service channel; a simpler conductivity meter may prove adequate.

**Fatal dependencies / kill tests.** Kill if the tracer response does not predict gravimetric concentration within ±5%; if biofilm, chemistry, or pipe geometry makes cross-house calibration unstable; if a safe complete flush cannot be demonstrated; if service firms will not pay €250+ per audit; or if fewer than 3,000 independent service/integrator buying points exist globally.

**Sources.** [Chicken Farmers of Canada medicator test](https://www.chickenfarmers.ca/portal/testing-the-medicator/); [Australian National Water Biosecurity Manual — Poultry Production](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/pests-diseases-weeds/protect-animal-plant/bird-owners/water_biosecurity); [Zoetis 2025 drinking-water vaccination protocol](https://www.zoetisus.com/content/_assets/docs/Poultry/Vaccine-Administration/Protocol-for-Live-Vaccination-via-Drinking-Water.pdf); [Eurostat Poultry statistics, December 2025 data release](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/112317.pdf); [Dosatron poultry medication](https://www.dosatron.com/en-au/animal-health/poultry-farming/poultry-medication).

## 3. RatioSnap — Disposable Last-Point 2K Dispense Verification

**Commercial insight and inside view.** Two-component adhesive users can own sophisticated pumps and still discover cure failures after parts are bonded. Graco's current guidance says the ratio should be checked at the dispense valve and still requires separate tared cups, scales, repeated shots, and specific-gravity conversion. Operators therefore postpone checks, record an upstream machine value, or accept a slow manual ritual. The unmet product is a fast, cross-brand, *last-point* check that fits the actual shot and flow rate.

**Product and exact paid event.** A keyed disposable cassette replaces the two cups: separated A/B streams fill two calibrated chambers whose mass/level is read in one dock; the software applies stored density and temperature compensation, rejects unstable purge shots, and produces a signed ratio record. The initial wedge is CEE contract manufacturers using Graco/Nordson-style meter-mix systems for potting or structural bonding but lacking automated end-point QA. Two unrelated plants prepay 5,000-8,000 PLN each for 50-check pilots on one chemistry and one valve family.

**Resource bootstrap and first proof.** Spend at most 28,000 PLN on a dual microbalance/optical dock, chemically resistant molded or machined cassette iterations, valve adapters, temperature sensing, and safe trials using benign viscosity-matched fluids before customer material. The founder owns cassette geometry, dock electronics, density/temperature recipes, adapters, and cross-brand drift/failure signatures. Proof is agreement with the Graco cup-and-scale method within the customer's ratio tolerance, less than 90 seconds of operator time, no cross-contamination, and cassette gross cost below €2.50.

**Control-point hypothesis.** **Tier 2 initially:** the founder owns a real consumable/reader system, but adapters and recipes are not yet hard to copy. Repeated consumable use, chemistry-specific validation, and a cross-brand failure corpus could raise switching cost.

**Conservative wealth bridge and denominator.** FEICA says adhesives and sealants enable 250,000 products, while the exact installed meter-mix denominator is not published. The falsifiable denominator hypothesis is at least 40,000 relevant 2K work cells across Europe and North America; it must be checked from distributor installed-base counts before tooling. At year 10-12, 8,000 active docks use 300 cassettes/year at €8 and 1,500 docks sell annually at €3,000: €23.7m revenue. At 67% gross and 25% operating margin, EBIT is €5.93m; 7× EBIT less €4m obligations and 65% founder ownership gives about €24.4m founder equity. Cumulative capital hypothesis is €2m; conservative and expected cash is 95% reinvested while employed.

**Strongest copy risk.** Graco or Nordson can add a dual-scale accessory and bundle it with their ratio-check nozzle; large plants may buy fully instrumented metering instead.

**Fatal dependencies / kill tests.** Kill if customer materials attack or foul the cassette; if mass/volume conversion cannot stay inside the customer's ratio tolerance; if operators save less than five minutes per check; if buyers will not pay €7 per signed test; or if the verified relevant-cell denominator is below 20,000.

**Sources.** [Graco ratio-check procedure](https://www.graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/solutions/articles/ratio-check-guidelines-for-dispensing-2k-materials.html); [Nordson Equalizer 2K system](https://www.nordson.com/en/products/efd-products/equalizer-2k-dispensing-tool); [Nordson 2K dispense guns](https://www.nordson.com/en/products/efd-products/2k-dispense-guns); [FEICA European industry facts](https://www.feica.eu/).

## 4. AnchorDust — Final-Pass Borehole Cleanliness Cassette

**Commercial insight and inside view.** Adhesive-anchor installers must execute product-specific blow/brush/blow sequences, yet the result is normally a procedural claim rather than a measurement. Hilti states that poor cleaning can remove up to 70% of anchor performance and sells clean-as-you-drill equipment, but millions of existing conventional drills, deep holes, restricted sites, and mixed-brand jobs remain. The narrow opportunity is not another brush or vacuum: it is a disposable final pass that both removes and records residual dust before resin enters a critical hole.

**Product and exact paid event.** A diameter-specific probe seals at the bore mouth and draws a fixed filtered-air pulse from the bottom through a disposable membrane cassette. A reader grades captured mineral mass/coverage and logs hole diameter, depth, brush size, and final pass. It is sold only as contractor QA, never as an ETA-approved substitute for the manufacturer's installation instructions. Two unrelated steel, façade, or MEP contractors prepay 5,000-8,000 PLN each for a 100-hole pilot alongside their normal cleaning and scheduled proof/pull tests.

**Resource bootstrap and first proof.** At most 38,000 PLN funds controlled concrete specimens, dust dosing, probes, flow/pressure sensing, filter cassettes, reader optics, and outsourced pull testing. The founder owns probe/cassette designs, sampling protocol, image thresholds, and the paired residual-dust/pull-result corpus. Proof is repeatable recovery across orientations and depths, detection of deliberately omitted cleaning steps, no residue added to the bore, under-30-second use, and paid deployment without changing approved installation steps.

**Control-point hypothesis.** **Tier 2 initially:** directly owned product and specimen corpus, but it remains commercially weak until residual capture predicts a consequential installation error across several anchor systems.

**Conservative wealth bridge and denominator.** Eurostat counted 3.4m EU construction enterprises in 2019; specialized construction was the largest employment/value-added division. The bottom-up hypothesis is that only 0.18%, or about 6,000 anchor-heavy teams, need adopt. At 1,500 critical holes/team/year and €2.20 per cassette, recurring revenue is €19.8m; 1,200 readers/year at €2,500 add €3m, for €22.8m. At 64% gross and 22% operating margin, EBIT is €5.02m; 6.5× EBIT less €3m obligations and 70% founder ownership gives about €20.8m founder equity in year 10-12. Cumulative capital hypothesis is €1.5m, released after paid pilots and reproducible pull-test evidence; 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested while employed.

**Strongest copy risk.** Hilti or fischer can pair dust sensing with hollow drill bits or smart dispensers and make an independent cassette unnecessary.

**Fatal dependencies / kill tests.** Kill if captured dust does not distinguish omitted/failed cleaning in blinded specimens; if capture adds contamination or changes approved installation; if pull strength is uncorrelated after controlling for anchor type; if field users will not spend 30 seconds and €2 on critical holes; or if buyers insist on formal approval before any paid QA use.

**Sources.** [Hilti SafeSet and current cleaning-performance claims](https://www.hilti.com/content/hilti/W1/US/en/business/business/productivity/safeset.html); [fischer cleaning and fastening kit](https://www.fischer-international.com/en/products/chemical-fixings/accessories/cleaning-and-fastening-kit/567202-cleaning-and-fastening-kit-fis-cc); [fischer post-installed rebar guide](https://www.fischer-international.com/-/media/fixing-systems/rebrush/fiint/service/informationmaterial/documents/brochures/chemistry/rebar-connections.pdf); [Eurostat construction structure](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/htmlpub/key-figures-on-european-business-2022/construction.html).

## 5. FoamPulse — Per-Job Spray-Foam Cure Witness

**Commercial insight and inside view.** Spray-foam crews manufacture the insulation on site. Off-ratio material may be identified only by colour, feel, smell, or later core/density/adhesion tests; EPA warns that it can off-gas and lose insulation performance. Existing field kits assemble scales, corers, pull blocks, moisture meters, and operator judgment. The customer's real desire is a timestamped physical witness that the gun output began curing normally on *this job*, before thousands of euros of hidden foam are installed.

**Product and exact paid event.** At job start and after drum/nozzle interruptions, the applicator sprays one shot into a disposable insulated geometry clipped into a reusable reader. Temperature-rise curve, expansion height, set time, color, and post-cure indentation are compared with a chemistry-specific envelope; the sealed witness is retained with the job record. This is an early-warning process check, not a declaration of installed-building compliance. Two unrelated licensed contractors prepay 5,000-8,000 PLN each for 40-job pilots, with conventional density/adhesion samples retained for comparison.

**Resource bootstrap and first proof.** Keep founder exposure below 36,000 PLN by building electronics and benign expanding-material rigs internally, buying safe enclosures/PPE, and having licensed contractors perform actual two-component sprays at their sites. The founder owns the witness geometry, sensor/reader, cure-curve library, anomaly rules, and serialized consumable. Proof is blinded discrimination of manufacturer-approved versus deliberately cold, pressure-imbalanced, interrupted, or aged-material runs; under-five-minute early warning; no new exposure path; and paid use on real jobs.

**Control-point hypothesis.** **Tier 2 initially:** founder-owned reader, cartridge and cure corpus. Defensibility depends on chemistry-specific labelled curves and insurer/manufacturer acceptance earned after evidence, not assumed at launch.

**Conservative wealth bridge and denominator.** The addressable-denominator hypothesis is at least 20,000 professional high-pressure spray rigs across Europe and North America; this must be verified through chemical suppliers, rig builders, and certification directories. At year 10-12, 4,000 active rigs run 150 jobs/year with one €15 witness per job and 800 new/replacement readers sell at €3,000: €11.4m revenue. At 68% gross and 25% operating margin, EBIT is €2.85m; 7× EBIT less €2m obligations and 65% founder ownership gives about €11.7m founder equity. This is the thinnest wealth bridge of the five and fails if the rig denominator or test frequency is lower. Cumulative capital hypothesis is €1.2m with 95% reinvestment while employed.

**Strongest copy risk.** Graco, spray-foam chemical suppliers, or certification bodies can add an instrumented test cup to existing field-QA programs; contractors may regard ordinary test sprays and density kits as sufficient.

**Fatal dependencies / kill tests.** Kill if cure curves cannot detect off-ratio conditions before ordinary visual judgment; if results vary more by ambient/substrate conditions than by material fault; if the witness creates an isocyanate exposure or waste problem; if fewer than 20,000 eligible rigs exist; or if contractors will not pay €15 per retained job witness.

**Sources.** [US EPA contractor practice tool](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2015-08/documents/spf_contractor_workplace_practices_evaluation_tool.pdf); [Spray Polyurethane Foam Alliance sampling guidance](https://www.whysprayfoam.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Guidance-on-Sampling-Techniques-for-the-Inspection-of-Installed-Spray-Polyurethane-Foam.pdf); [SPFA field examinations](https://www.sprayfoam.org/fieldexams/); [CUFCA field test kit](https://cufca.org/Sys/Store/Products/317429); [Eurostat construction structure](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/htmlpub/key-figures-on-european-business-2022/construction.html).

## Cross-Direction Notes

- **Best first evidence asset:** HoseWitness can create the most legible blind benchmark without regulation, live animals, or chemistry-specific safety exposure.
- **Best recurring-consumable shape:** RatioSnap and DoseProof have strong per-check consumption if operator time savings and reference agreement are real.
- **Highest authority/liability exposure:** AnchorDust and FoamPulse must remain process-warning tools until independent evidence supports stronger claims.
- **Shared no-go rule:** discard any direction if buyers require OEM, authority, insurer, or certification-body acceptance before they will make the first paid purchase. None of those relationships is treated as a bootstrap resource.
