# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Afterline Installed-Base Succession Buyout

## Diagnosis

Afterline is a credible acquisition-led compounding business rather than a succession advisory service: it buys an installed channel and the technical rights required to monetise it, while customer prepayments gate the close. Its best feature is that distribution, product truth, and switching cost arrive in the same transaction. The founder-wealth path requires a long portfolio build and the first seller option remains unproven, but the candidate sequences those risks rather than financing them in advance. It is fit for an independent panel as written.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced evidence: the June 2026 European Commission guidance identifies a material SME succession problem; Commission material says family businesses exceed 60% of European firms; EU policy is strengthening repair and spare-part continuity; Poland has an active SME/industrial base. Hypotheses: owners will accept low-cash options/earn-outs, rights and installed-base communication are assignable, customers will prepay, and mature-line margins will reach 18–27%. Prior reviewer verdicts are absent. The exact target, option, buyer prepayments, and delivered margins are not yet market evidence.

## Overall Score

**84/100.** Weighted points are `168 ÷ 20 = 8.4`; multiplying by 10 gives 84. This is an advisory evaluator score only, not a simulated-panel or real-chat validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 if executed as specified.** The option and closing assignment/exclusive licence directly control the product-line IP/files, tooling, spares, service mark, contracts, history, and authorised installed-base route. Strongest copy risk: a better-capitalised industrial-service consolidator outbids Afterline for future sellers; it cannot copy a line already granted exclusively, but it can compete for the next one. If essential rights or customer communication remain revocable, the control point falls to Tier 3 and the candidate should be killed.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, conditional on the portfolio acquisition assumptions.** Conservative, expected, and strong cases state lines/customers, pricing logic, revenue, gross and operating margins, capital, ownership/dilution, reinvestment, value/distributions, timing, and milestones. The conservative case reaches about 44.1m PLN/€10.3m by Year 14 through 36.1m PLN of founder equity plus 8m PLN of post-employment after-tax distributions that are explicitly not retained or counted in valuation. The expected case reaches about €15.3m of founder equity by Year 11 at 82% ownership; the strong case reaches about €34.8m by Year 8 at 70%. The decisive risk is acquiring 14–16 clean lines without margin or dilution erosion, not mathematical double counting.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The exact 180-day proof is one signed option, three unrelated prepaying installed-base customers, at least 25,000 PLN prepaid, a first successful delivery, and at least 45% contribution margin, with 34,000 PLN maximum pre-proof spend. The founder can retain employment by batching calls and outsourcing legal, engineering, and field work. The critical dependencies—owner option, clean rights, installed-base activity, prepayment, safe delivery—are tested sequentially before closing rather than simultaneously after capital commitment.

## Expansion Check

The first product line is small enough to dominate. Adjacent lines reuse field technicians, supplier methods, customer sites, inventory forecasting, and the seller-financed acquisition playbook. Foreign or broad competitors can compete for future deals but cannot recreate an already assigned archive and installed-base channel. Poland/CEE supplies a meaningful sourcing and operating-cost advantage without being presented as a legal moat.

## Fatal Objections

None identified in the written plan. The strongest caveats are: no seller has accepted the proposed structure; customer-record/IP transfer may be messier than the model assumes; service liability can contaminate margins; and the conservative wealth case needs 16 successful lines over 14 years. Any one becomes fatal if the first option cannot contain the complete rights schedule or if customers will not prepay before closing.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire the installed-base distribution channel and technical truth together, and make customer prepayment a closing condition rather than paying first and hoping demand exists.
