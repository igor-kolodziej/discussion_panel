# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**QuenchPortfolio Integrator Licence** is an inventor-backed Polish spinout that options the complete relevant cold-plasma antibiotic-degradation portfolio, requires an industrial-water integrator to prepay a qualification on one live client's real effluent before exercising the licence, and scales through protected reactor/control packages, project royalties and recurring verification rather than founder-built treatment plants.

The controlled resource must combine granted Polish Pat.242412/P.431823 with the later high-throughput P.440185/PCT/PL2022/050027/EP4466234A1 family, undisclosed know-how, named inventor support and company-funded improvement rights. The first buyer is not a municipality or a speculative research partner. It is an established industrial-water integrator with a pharmaceutical or veterinary-production client, a difficult segregated antibiotic stream and a live treatment budget.

## One-Sentence Thesis

An unknown data-scientist founder can commercialise underused Polish deep technology by making a trusted integrator pay for a real-client stream decision while the spinout retains the scarce patent, reactor, control and cumulative performance layer.

## Founder Fit

The Warsaw founder can map integrators and antibiotic-production sites, design multivariate experiments, analyse energy/activity/transformation data, automate account research, model lifecycle cost and build the performance corpus. His data-science and business-analysis skills are useful at the product/evidence boundary. They do not make him a plasma engineer, environmental chemist or field-safety authority.

The POC stack is therefore mandatory: the founder at 15–20 hours/week, one named inventor or plasma engineer under equity or deliverable contract, the university owners/TTO, an accredited analytical laboratory and the integrator's process team. The inventor owns technical protocol delivery; the integrator owns plant access, balance-of-plant engineering, installation and service. If neither commits in writing, the founder does not perform technical theatre and the idea stops.

## Customer And Pain

The first economic buyer is a Polish/CEE/EU industrial-water integrator with a live pharmaceutical or veterinary-production client whose segregated API, reactor-wash, CIP or batch-cleaning stream is expensive to dispose of or difficult to treat. The integrator wants to win or retain that client project with defensible destruction of parent antibiotic compounds and residual antibacterial activity. It does not want a plasma demonstration or a generic AMR report.

Current alternatives include off-site hazardous-liquid destruction, dilution into a larger biological plant, activated carbon, ozone, UV/oxidant treatment, membranes, electrochemical oxidation and other advanced-oxidation/plasma systems. Any can be correct. QuenchPortfolio earns a place only where the licensed system produces lower lifecycle cost, less secondary waste, better activity destruction or more controllable performance in the named real matrix.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

The first product is a €5,000–€10,000 prepaid **Integrator Client-Stream Qualification**. The integrator names the live client opportunity and sample, signs confidentiality/data/sample terms and funds independent testing. The optioned inventor/lab team measures:

- parent antibiotics before/after treatment;
- residual antibacterial activity, not concentration alone;
- relevant COD/TOC, pH, salts, solvents and matrix effects;
- targeted transformation products and ecotoxicity appropriate to the compound;
- helium, electricity, electrode and consumable use;
- performance/cost against the integrator's best feasible alternative; and
- a go/no-go field-pilot specification and fixed budget range.

A passed result opens a customer-funded €50,000–€100,000 engineered field pilot. At maturity QuenchPortfolio sells/licences the protected plasma-brush/reactor/control package for roughly €70,000–€80,000 per module, earns an 8–12% technology royalty on defined eligible project value and sells €15,000–€25,000 annual electrode/control/verification support. The integrator finances and installs the balance of plant.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

The lab-to-market gap is not a shortage of antibiotic-removal papers. Clean-water removal percentages do not let an integrator bid a real plant. Each side stream changes activity, by-products, energy, helium, electrode life, pretreatment and acceptance.

The valuable company is not a wastewater consultant and not the metal skid. It is the owner of a coherent patent/know-how/improvement package plus comparable paid real-stream outcomes and a standard protected reactor/control product. That package lets an existing trusted integrator quote a difficult client project without funding open-ended research. The founder adds commercial productisation, evidence comparability, improvement IP and channel governance that a university technology-offer page does not provide.

## Underused Momentum

- The UG/PWr research laboratory lists a later high-throughput family: P.440185, PCT/PL2022/050027 and European application EP22750924.7, published as EP4466234A1 and described publicly as pending.
- University of Gdańsk technology offer 152/2025 publicly offers that Polish/international invention for licence, sale or spinout and describes it as TRL 4.
- Published laboratory work covers continuous multi-cone treatment of multiple antibiotic classes and mixtures. This is laboratory evidence, not industrial-effluent proof.
- WHO issued manufacturing-pollution guidance in 2024, and Directive (EU) 2024/3019 creates future quaternary-treatment and producer-cost pressure. Neither mandates this technology.

The technology, legal rights and urgency exist. Option terms, real-effluent superiority and customer payment remain company hypotheses.

## Beachhead Market

Start with one Polish, Czech, Slovak or German industrial-water integrator, one live antibiotic API/veterinary/formulation client, one recurring segregated aqueous stream and one antibiotic family within or close to the published portfolio. The stream must have a named treatment/disposal baseline, sampling route, engineering owner and budget.

Exclude municipal procurement, dilute hospital sewage, general wastewater, buyers seeking free demonstrations and any stream without an economic comparison. The 12-month beachhead proof is a complete IP option, named inventor commitment, one integrator prepayment, one delivered real-effluent result and a budget-backed field-pilot option—not a production module.

## Primary Control Point

The Tier-1 resource exists only if one signed field option/licence covers:

- granted Pat.242412/P.431823 and the later P.440185/PCT/EP brush family;
- co-owner authority, territory/status schedule and rights to subcontract, sublicense and sell packages;
- exclusive access to undisclosed reactor/control know-how, samples and named inventor hours;
- ownership or exclusive field rights to company-funded scale-up, control and electrode improvements;
- project-registration/channel rights; and
- customer permission to retain specified anonymised performance features.

One public patent, research collaboration, revocable inventor help or promise to negotiate is Tier 3 and rejected. A competitor can buy cameras, pumps and plasma components, but cannot use the exact claims, undisclosed operating know-how, company-funded improvements, registered projects and accumulated real-stream evidence if the package is executed as required.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

1. IP counsel verifies granted/pending status, claims, ownership, co-owner authority and freedom-to-operate red flags across both families.
2. Sign a 180-day portfolio option for <=15,000 PLN with a pre-agreed licence entry <=50,000 PLN, royalties, improvement rights, inventor hours and rights to subcontract/sell.
3. Contract one named inventor/plasma engineer for the protocol and a later technical-lead/equity path.
4. Obtain fixed accredited-lab prices and sample-safety rules before outbound.
5. Build a list of 40 industrial-water integrators and 60 likely production sites; approach technical directors only with the named-client-stream qualification.
6. By day 180 require one unrelated integrator to prepay >=20,000 PLN for a real-client stream. Generic scouting, an LOI or grant does not count.
7. Exercise the licence only after payment and only if the study fits the remaining original founder capital.
8. By month 12 deliver the result and obtain the integrator's written budget-backed field-pilot option; build no founder-funded skid.

Why an unknown founder can secure the resource: the universities publicly solicit commercialisation offers; a narrow milestone/royalty option costs them little and supplies a commercial operator. The inventor receives a defined role/equity or contracted work. The integrator buys a bounded decision for a live client rather than trusting the founder to operate a treatment plant. Proof before serious capital is the signed rights package, technical commitment and external customer payment.

### Distribution Moat

The integrator owns plant trust and engineering access; QuenchPortfolio owns the protected product and comparable evidence. A live project creates urgency, while study credit against a pilot reduces risk. Project registration prevents channel conflict. Each paid stream lets another integrator see a closer compound/matrix precedent and shortens technical diligence.

The first ten customers are integrators. Build a 40-integrator list from documented industrial-water projects and approach with the exact patent portfolio and paid live-stream protocol. Customers two and three must be unrelated. The first 100 plant installations arrive later through 15–30 performance-bound integrators. Direct account intelligence, package pricing, data rights and project registration stay central.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. One integrator, one real stream and one paid result.
2. One customer-funded field pilot and accepted performance protocol.
3. Two unrelated integrators and three modules in the same compound/matrix family.
4. Standard toll-fabricated reactor/control package and recurring verification.
5. Adjacent antibiotic families only after paid comparison studies.
6. European project-registered integrator network with minimum-performance rights.
7. Municipal/hospital applications only after industrial reference economics and procurement evidence.

The direct competitors are mature AOP technologies and other plasma designs. QuenchPortfolio wins only through measured activity destruction, lifecycle economics and protected implementation. Its strongest copy risk is a non-infringing ozone, electrochemical or plasma system that performs better. The company must file/protect meaningful improvements before disclosure and cannot rely on the word “plasma.”

## Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides the granted right, local inventors, lower-cost lab/engineering, founder proximity and the first integrator/site map. The later PCT/EP family offers a possible European protection path but is pending and must be counsel-verified. Export defence requires valid claims plus undisclosed know-how, improvement filings, reference modules and service data. Polish rights alone cannot support the wealth model.

## Why This Could Compound

Each paid study adds matrix composition, compound/activity destruction, transformation products, energy, helium, electrode wear, control settings and lifecycle-cost comparison. Each module adds validated BOM, safety, factory/field acceptance, service history and improvement inventions.

The flywheel is: integrator live projects → paid comparable qualifications → narrower guarantees and better product settings → more accepted modules → recurring service and field data → stronger improvements/references → faster integrator adoption. Customer confidentiality is preserved; only contractually retained features enter the common corpus.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Remain employed until two accepted field modules, three independent paying integrators, contracted recurring support, six months positive contribution including founder replacement, a repeatable integrator acquisition signal, signed technical leadership and at least 12 months of personal runway. One patent option, paid study or field pilot does not justify leaving employment.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

Before licence exercise, reusable assets are the IP/FTO map, integrator/site graph, protocol, lab quotes and rejection record. Customer payment is refundable only under disclosed lab-safety or company-failure conditions. If real effluent fails, do not turn the work into generic compliance consulting; let the option expire. If another documented stream exists, require a new external payment before testing it.

No founder-owned laboratory, skid or treatment plant is acquired in the POC. Later movable control/reactor prototypes are funded only by field-pilot cash and may be reused within the licensed field.

## Validation Plan

The first 180 days validate whether the complete resource can be optioned and whether one integrator pays for a live-client decision. Months 7–12 validate real-effluent destruction, activity, by-products, energy/helium/electrode economics and a budget-backed field pilot. The field pilot validates scale, uptime, integration, acceptance and contribution. A second unrelated integrator and third module validate repeat distribution.

Evidence remains separated: patent registers, TTO pages, publications, WHO and EU law establish resource/timing; signed rights, payments, laboratory reports, field acceptance, invoices and contribution statements establish this company.
