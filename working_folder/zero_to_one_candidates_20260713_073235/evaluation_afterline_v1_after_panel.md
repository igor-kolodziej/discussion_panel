# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Afterline v1 After Independent Panel

## Diagnosis

Afterline v1 still contains a strong insight—acquire technical truth and installed distribution together and condition spend on customer cash—but the panel exposes two hard-gate weaknesses in the exact text. The conservative wealth case applies ownership to business value without a terminal seller-note/debt/earn-out schedule, and the paid-proof chain asks an unknown part-time founder to clear an option, rights, customer access, prepayment, safe delivery, and contribution inside 180 days. Both judges also identify the same portfolio problem: the line-level monopoly does not make future acquisitions repeatable. The candidate cannot proceed unchanged.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence remains limited to official succession/repair context and the written capital gates. The two panel scores (76 and 74) are reviewer verdicts, not market evidence. Shared objections concern the candidate's own structure: seller acceptance, line-by-line rights, cross-line reuse, bypass risk, liability treatment, and pre-proof dependency density. No option, mandate, payment, delivery, or seller-finance quote exists.

## Overall Score

**68/100.** Weighted points are `136 ÷ 20 = 6.8`; multiplying by 10 gives 68. Advisory only; this is not a replacement for either independent panel score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 conditional for one executed line; Tier 3 for the proposed future acquisition engine.** Strongest copy risk is bypass by former technicians, suppliers, and reverse engineers who cherry-pick attractive work while Afterline bears continuity obligations. Future sellers can also be won by better-capitalised consolidators.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The exact text omits terminal net debt, seller notes, and accrued earn-outs from founder equity. Its conservative 44.1m PLN result has only a 1.1m PLN cushion over the 43m PLN gate before those liabilities. Expected scale requires 14 heterogeneous line acquisitions at 23% operating margin and 82% ownership without evidence of a repeatable seller funnel.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** Spending is capped at 34,000 PLN and employment is retained, but paid proof depends on too many uncontrolled trust, legal, customer, and delivery events within six months. The candidate lacks a paid commitment that itself grants the option, data custody, and temporary delivery rights before the rest of the chain.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is valid and local execution helps. Expansion is the failure: a sequence of bespoke micro-acquisitions does not automatically reuse distribution, technicians, liabilities, suppliers, or customer budgets. A future design must change who pays first and how the control point is secured before customer fulfilment, while showing equity value net of acquisition liabilities.

## Fatal Objections

1. Founder Wealth Gate arithmetic does not deduct acquisition liabilities.
2. Reachability depends on a dense pre-proof chain for an unknown part-time founder.
3. Each future line resets seller acquisition and diligence; the portfolio engine is not yet defensible.
4. Customer maintenance spend is bypassable even when the archive is exclusive.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Secure technical truth and installed-base distribution in the same enforceable contract, and make external cash evidence a condition before acquisition capital is released.
