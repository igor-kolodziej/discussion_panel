# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ClampWitness PV v1

## Diagnosis

ClampWitness starts from a credible bolted-joint problem and an unusually precise product claim. It also confronts the adverse evidence: generic M6/M8 metric DTIs exist, Applied Bolting already markets self-indicating DTIs for solar, TurnaSure offers M8/custom capability, and the mounting OEM/project engineer owns compatibility. That evidence collapses the supposed neglected category into a joint-specific qualification project. The founder can own one design/tool/test file, but reaching 20 million packs requires a few design-owner specifications that can copy, internalize or reject the product; the conservative wealth bridge and control point therefore fail together.

## Evidence and Assumptions

LBL supports torque/preload/documentation problems and identifies PV adaptation interest. Current supplier evidence supports generic small metric DTIs, self-indicating structural products, solar positioning and custom manufacture. SolarPower Europe supports the broad 448m-joint arithmetic only after module-power/clamp assumptions. No evidence supports 100m eligible joints, €0.62 price, 20m annual demand, a third-party approval route, 55% gross margin, or three independent specification/channel relationships. No panel or chatbot verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**59.75/100.** Weighted points are 119.5 across 20 weight units; `10 × 119.5 / 20 = 59.75`. Advisory only; not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 for one proved SKU; Tier 3 today.** The founder can own geometry, dies, load/corrosion corpus, lot fixture, instructions and manufacturing pack. The company-wide control point depends on repeated written approvals/specifications it does not own. Strongest copy risk: Applied Bolting/TurnaSure scales or customizes an existing indicator, or the mounting OEM incorporates equivalent geometry into its approved kit and starts with the warranty/channel advantage.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The conservative founder result is €11.44m—only 14% above the threshold—while assuming 20m packs/year, 22% operating margin, 65% ownership, 7× valuation, €1.5m obligations, >=100m eligible annual EU joints and at least three specification/channel relationships. Twenty million is 12.8% of the candidate's own unproved eligibility hypothesis. More importantly, the same few design owners whose approval unlocks volume can demand margin, copy or use an incumbent. Minor misses in eligibility, channel margin, dilution, obligations or volume break the gate.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** A founder-owned design/test corpus and two unrelated 5,000–10,000 PLN rig pilots can be attempted inside 39,000 PLN/180 days while employed. The pilot does not require field authorization. However, an EPC's paid study is not a product order; commercial use then requires a separate mounting-design/project authority, creating a material conversion dependency.

## Expansion Check

The first stack is narrow, but each adjacent stack changes preload, bearing, coatings, geometry, corrosion and approval. That is not asymmetric expansion until a design owner adopts a family. The incumbent can move inward using established DTI methods and the OEM can build inward from the exact joint. Poland reduces experiment cost but cannot overcome that authority structure.

## Fatal Objections

1. The conservative Founder Wealth Gate lacks resilience and depends on concentrated specification access.
2. Existing DTI suppliers already possess the core manufacturing/indicator competence and explicitly target solar or custom sizes.
3. The pilot payer and product approval owner can be different, so reachable paid engineering evidence may not convert to routed product volume.
4. Each new stack risks bespoke qualification rather than a compounding product catalogue.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A physical evidence component embedded in a repeat bill of materials can turn an unauditable installation proxy into joint-level handover evidence.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Do not return through a smaller washer, different indicator media, higher price or revised market share. A material new shape would need already controlled specification authority or a self-contained joint product whose use does not depend on an outside OEM accepting a third-party component.
