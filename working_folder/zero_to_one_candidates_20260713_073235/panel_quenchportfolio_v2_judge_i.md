## Diagnosis

This is a disciplined commercialization thesis around a potentially scarce deep-tech portfolio, with a strong narrow beachhead, explicit kill criteria, and customer-funded progression. Its best feature is that it refuses to build a plant before an integrator pays for a live-client decision. But the current company owns none of the proposed control point, has no demonstrated integrator willingness to pay, and has no real-effluent evidence that the technology beats mature alternatives on lifecycle economics. The proposition is therefore an unusually well-specified research case, not yet a credible profitable-dominance case.

## Evidence and Assumptions

The candidate itself distinguishes public resource/timing claims from company evidence. No signed portfolio option, co-owner authority, exclusive know-how, improvement rights, inventor commitment, integrator payment, real-stream result, field-pilot budget, repeat customer, or bottom-up site count is supplied. Pricing, gross margins, royalties, support revenue, sales velocity, ownership, dilution, operating margins, and valuation multiples are hypotheses. Within this judgment's restricted scope, patent, regulatory, publication, and technology-transfer claims are treated as candidate assertions rather than independently verified facts.

## Overall Score

**62/100.** Weighted total: `123 / 20 = 6.15`; multiplied by ten and rounded to the nearest integer.

The score rewards the sharp wedge, conditional control-point design, and test discipline. It is capped by missing company evidence at every decisive economic link and by failure of both hard gates.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 today.** The proposed Tier-1 package is conditional on a signed, exclusive, enforceable field option or licence covering both families, co-owner authority, know-how, inventor access, improvement rights, sublicensing, and project/data rights. None is currently controlled. A public patent, a technology-transfer offer, and an intention to negotiate are accessible inputs, not an owned control point.

**Strongest copy risk:** an established integrator or AOP vendor uses a non-infringing ozone, electrochemical, UV/oxidant, membrane, or alternative plasma process that produces better lifecycle economics, then accumulates its own client data. Customers buy safe, accepted destruction at acceptable cost—not this plasma architecture—so a superior substitute bypasses the entire patent package.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The model is arithmetically coherent and does not add distributions to retained-value equity, but it does not establish a credible conservative path to €10 million founder net worth. Its conservative case assumes 110 sites, 180 active modules, 70 package sales in year 15, €20,000 recurring revenue per module, €2 million of royalties, 32% operating margin, 55% founder ownership, valid European control, and a 7× operating-profit value. There is no bottom-up eligible-site denominator, adoption-rate evidence, capital/dilution support, or proof that integrators will accept all three monetization layers. With only about €1.3 million of cushion over the threshold, a modest miss in ownership, margin, liabilities, multiple, recurring adoption, or European enforceability breaks the gate; expected and strong cases do not cure the unsupported conservative bridge.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail, despite a well-designed budget.** The 38,000 PLN pre-proof ceiling, 15–20 hours per week, and refusal to build hardware before payment fit the founder's constraints. However, one paid qualification within 180 days depends on securing a coherent multi-owner/multi-family option at the stated caps, contracting the inventor, fixing a safe laboratory protocol, finding an integrator with a live confidential client stream, obtaining sample/client cooperation, and convincing that integrator to prepay at least 20,000 PLN. For an unknown founder with no privileged introductions, forty cold approaches does not make this chain credible enough; it is simultaneous success across several uncontrolled dependencies before paid evidence.

## Expansion Check

The first niche is well bounded and expansion by nearby antibiotic family, matrix precedent, and integrator is logically asymmetric only after exclusive European rights and retained performance data exist. Broad competitors do not need to copy the reactor if another treatment meets the same acceptance and cost threshold. Poland improves access to inventors, engineering, and the granted right, but Polish protection alone cannot support the European volume assumed by the wealth model.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest insight is that a coherent exclusive rights-and-know-how package combined with paid, comparable real-stream evidence and integrator channel governance could be more valuable than owning fabrication or operating treatment plants.
