## Diagnosis

WooRenewal contains a commercially sharp insight: in a GPL ecosystem, the official update path, installed configurations, renewal contracts, billing cohorts, support history, and merchant trust can be more valuable than the copyable code. Buying that bundle can produce immediate revenue, high gross margin, and switching costs without first winning cold customers, while escrow and staged diligence keep the initial experiment compatible with the founder's capital and employment constraints. The weakness is that the entire wedge depends on finding an unusually clean asset whose seller accepts both a low valuation and receipt-capped financing from an unknown buyer; the candidate provides transaction-shape precedents but no evidence that such a target is currently obtainable. More decisively, the stated founder-wealth scenarios do not establish a credible conservative path from one tiny acquisition to 60,000 paying sites: they assume a large organic/acquisitive scale-up, high margins, high retained ownership, and very low cumulative acquisition capital without showing the bridge.

## Evidence and Assumptions

**Evidence or mechanically verifiable structure stated in the candidate:** a formal plugin-owner/update transfer route is said to exist; plugin businesses with recurring revenue, organic acquisition, and limited support are said to have been listed or sold; escrow can condition purchase-price release on transfer; the founder caps pre-proof cash risk at 40,000 PLN. These points support the existence of the asset class and a controlled deal structure, but the cited source material was not part of the candidate file and is not treated here as proof of a qualifying target.

**Founder claims or hypotheses:** at least five credible data rooms can be obtained from 100 owner contacts; one seller will accept no more than 28,000 PLN cash plus a 24-month receipt-capped note; every necessary WordPress, IP, billing, licence, customer-data, contract, and payout right can transfer; renewal and support economics will survive ownership change; related assets will offer merchant overlap, cross-sell, or shared operating savings; directory/search distribution will remain durable; 60,000–180,000 paying sites can be reached with the stated acquisition capital and ownership.

**Consequential missing facts:** no named target, seller indication, data room, verified cohort, actual asking price, processor-transfer approval, contract/consent analysis, organic-install history, post-transfer renewal observation, cross-sell evidence, acquisition-funnel conversion, or portfolio-level cost evidence. The scoring therefore credits the explicit discipline and test design but applies a material discount to repeatability, domination, and the wealth bridge.

## Overall Score

**71/100.** Weighted points are **141.5 out of 200**, equivalent to a weighted average of **7.075/10** and rounded to **71/100**. This score recognizes an excellent acquisition thesis and disciplined proof plan, while withholding credit for an unproven acquisition supply funnel, portfolio compounding, and a founder-wealth bridge that remains heroic.

## Control Point

**Tier 1, conditional on completed and verified transfer.** Simultaneous ownership of the official WordPress owner/commit/update channel, installed base, paid licences and billing rights, licence server, brand/domain, complete IP, lawful customer rights, and compatibility/support history is an owned and directly controlled distribution/workflow asset. The escrow rule is correctly designed to reject partial transfer. Before a deal closes, however, WooRenewal controls none of this; target access and deal supply are still **Tier 3**, so the business cannot claim an operating control point merely from its acquisition criteria.

**Strongest copy risk:** a large WooCommerce/plugin-suite vendor can replicate or bundle the same functionality and push it through its own larger installed base, underpricing or eliminating the standalone renewal. It need not copy WooRenewal's exact update credential to divert new customers and gradually induce existing merchants to migrate. A GPL fork alone is less dangerous because it lacks the inherited update channel, billing cohort, configurations, and trust, but a fork combined with incumbent distribution is serious.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The model is arithmetically explicit and properly deducts notes/debt/reserves before applying founder ownership; it also excludes distributions from the gate total, so no obvious double counting occurs. The failure is credibility, not formatting.

The “conservative” case requires 60,000 paying sites at 900 PLN ARR, 54 million PLN revenue, 35% operating margin, 85% founder ownership, and only 8 million PLN of cumulative acquisition capital by Year 14. Yet the candidate starts with at least 100 sites and describes ten acquired products by Year 8 without quantifying how many sites are bought, how acquired cohorts grow, what net organic adds cost, whether site counts are unique or cross-sold licences, or how 8 million PLN can purchase and finance enough recurring gross profit to reach 18.9 million PLN of operating profit. At the candidate's own maximum of 2.5× recurring gross profit, the mature revenue base would be vastly more expensive if acquired; therefore most of the value must come from substantial post-acquisition organic growth and cross-sell, neither of which is evidenced.

The expected and strong cases amplify the same unsupported assumptions: 100,000–180,000 paying sites, 42–48% operating margin, 65–78% founder ownership, and 4–6× valuation multiples, while maintaining related-product discipline and limited capital. These are possible success outcomes, not conservative/expected evidence-backed scenarios. Until the first two assets prove repeatable acquisition, post-transfer retention, 30%+ fully loaded margin, shared distribution or cost, and a quantified capital-to-customer growth bridge, a plausible founder net worth above €10 million has not been established.

**Fatality:** fatal to the required founder-wealth gate and therefore fatal to a `pursue` verdict under the governing rules. It is not proof that the first small acquisition cannot be profitable.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, narrowly and conditionally.** The candidate offers a credible in-principle route to one real third-party receipt within 180 days while keeping employment and risking no more than 40,000 PLN: 5,000 PLN for sourcing/accounts, up to 7,000 PLN additional diligence, up to 28,000 PLN cash consideration, escrow-conditioned asset transfer, then an unrelated renewal or new licence. No new product, paid advertising channel, public persona, privileged introduction, or full-time transition must succeed first. The founder's proposed 15–25 hours per week fits the five-hours-per-day ceiling for a single acquisition, with specialist security and legal work contracted.

The pass is narrow because the plan still depends on a rare conjunction: a qualifying target, truthful data room, low price, seller acceptance of receipt-capped financing, official update transfer, complete IP, lawful customer/contract/data transfer, processor approval, and a timely renewal. These dependencies are sequenced and protected by kill rules and escrow, which contains capital loss, but the 100-contact/five-data-room conversion and seller-note acceptance are hypotheses. The first receipt proves transfer and cash collection, not annual retention, support load, acquisition payback, or portfolio repeatability; those require the stated six-to-twelve-month follow-up.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is appropriately narrow: one operationally critical WooCommerce workflow plugin with an existing paid base and evidenced retention. Remaining within one merchant workflow and requiring merchant overlap, shared infrastructure, or at least 10% cross-sell before a third acquisition is disciplined. The concern is that every later asset may reset the sourcing and negotiation problem, while broad vendors can enter via their own installed bases and ecosystem bundles. Poland offers cost-efficient technical execution and EU privacy competence, but no privileged route to global plugin supply or customer demand.

## Fatal Objections and Caveats

1. **Founder-wealth bridge is not credible as written — fatal to the Founder Wealth Gate and to `pursue`.** The scenarios label large-scale success as conservative and do not quantify the source, cost, and growth of the 60,000-site base relative to only 8 million PLN of cumulative acquisition capital.
2. **No evidence of a qualifying target at the required price and terms — potentially fatal to launch.** High renewal, low support, complete transferability, organic distribution, clean security/IP, a sub-2× earnings price, no more than 28,000 PLN cash, and a receipt-capped note form an unusually demanding conjunction. The public existence of plugin sales does not show this conjunction is available to an unknown buyer.
3. **The portfolio moat is acquired one asset at a time — serious, not fatal to the first deal.** The transferred update channel is Tier 1 after closing, but the acquisition funnel itself is not exclusive; competing buyers can bid up attractive assets, and every additional deal may require another manual sourcing cycle.
4. **A first post-transfer receipt is weak proof — serious but not fatal.** It establishes that transfer and collection worked, not that 75% renewal persists, support stays below 15% of revenue, payback remains under 30 months, or related products compound distribution.
5. **Platform, legal, and security concentration — deal-fatal when triggered.** WordPress ownership rules, payment-processor restrictions, customer-contract/consent defects, incomplete contractor IP, or a critical vulnerability can make a target non-transferable or uneconomic. The escrow kill conditions appropriately contain this risk but do not eliminate target scarcity.
6. **Part-time compatibility may break during incidents or portfolio growth — manageable for one asset, potentially fatal at scale.** Ten historical support hours per month does not bound emergency security, compatibility, seller-transition, or multi-plugin release work; the founder has no demonstrated WordPress operations team.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

In open-source commercial software, the installed update credential plus renewal and compatibility history can be a stronger distribution/control asset than the source code. The discipline of assigning zero acquisition value to code without that complete transferable bundle is the candidate's strongest insight.

## Recommendation

**research further**

The candidate is strong enough to justify the explicitly capped sourcing and data-room test, but it is not yet qualified to pursue as a founder-wealth vehicle. The first unresolved question is whether the stipulated asset and seller terms exist; the larger unresolved question is whether two related acquisitions demonstrate enough retained margin, renewal durability, shared distribution/cost, and capital-efficient customer growth to make the €10 million founder-wealth path credible.
