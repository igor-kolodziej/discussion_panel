## Research-Further Questions From The Evaluator

The advisory evaluator required seller-side evidence from three owners:

1. verified paid licences, revenue and renewal;
2. support burden, security history and code/IP quality;
3. exact Cloud plan and competitive gap;
4. complete transfer willingness, including listing, app key, IP, history, customer obligations and Atlassian approval;
5. purchase cash and receipt-capped seller-note terms inside the capital gates.

## What Public Research Closed

- Transfer mechanics are official and assignable after a genuine sale/acquisition.
- A large public pool exists: 995 Jira Data Center apps screened, 226 paid apps with no detected same-key Cloud version, 137 in the 5–60-install band.
- Public price tiers show that some small apps can support meaningful B2B ARPA.
- The March 2028 sales end and March 2029 affected Data Center EOL create a dated reason for owners to build, sell or retire.
- Marketplace billing and Cloud Migration Assistant create direct distribution mechanisms without an existential partnership.
- Broad workflow categories already have mature Cloud incumbents. Examples include Email This Issue and JEMH for email/notification workflows, and Requirements & Test Management for Jira/RMsis for traceability and testing. An acquisition must therefore be target-specific rather than justified by “no same-key Cloud version.”

## What Public Research Cannot Close

Marketplace installs do not reveal paid licences, revenue, renewal or customer concentration. Public price tiers do not reveal the tier mix. Active release histories do not reveal owner willingness to sell, support hours, code quality or Cloud intentions. Atlassian's transfer page does not pre-approve a particular transaction. No public source supplies a seller-note offer.

The three illustrative low-install leads in the canonical are not qualifying targets:

- Idea Hub: small install count and meaningful minimum price, but active ownership, uncertain paid cohort and multiple idea-management substitutes.
- SLA Timer: small and potentially affordable, but SLA timers are readily copied and crowded.
- Better Comments with Reply: a larger installed base and high minimum price, but threaded-comment substitutes exist and the feature is routine to copy.

The higher-value leads may be economically attractive products but are less likely to fit the 100,000 PLN total founder-capital envelope. A public install count multiplied by minimum annual price is not a valuation, yet it shows why “valuable enough to reach 1m PLN” and “cheap enough for the founder” can conflict.

## Authorisation Boundary And Route

No seller was contacted, no offer was made and no commitment was sought. The user explicitly excluded seller contact or commitment without separate authorisation. Therefore the decision-critical `research further` facts cannot be obtained in this run.

Under the strict routing rule, LegacyBridge cannot be changed to `pursue`, cannot enter an independent panel, and should not be structurally pivoted while the evaluator's recommendation remains `research further`. Public research is exhausted at the exact decision boundary. A future authorised seller packet to at least three owners is the only honest next test.
