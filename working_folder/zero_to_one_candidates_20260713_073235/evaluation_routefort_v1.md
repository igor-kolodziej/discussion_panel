# Zero-to-One Evaluation: RouteFort Safe Truck-Parking Site Options

## Diagnosis

RouteFort addresses an officially measured shortage with a real location control point and makes a fleet reservation precede construction. The site option and fleet block can be tested under 40,000 PLN, but delivering an operating certified site requires planning, certification, construction, security, utilities, staffing, and external finance well beyond the founder's 100,000 PLN. The wealth path is infrastructure-scale and can work with site finance, yet dilution/debt and founder capability are unusually consequential. This is not panel-ready without a named site and fleet term sheet.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official evidence supports a 390,057-space current EU shortfall and certification standards. Site rents, local competitive gap, planning, certification cost, fleet deposits, price, utilisation, site EBITDA, and project financing are hypotheses. No yard, landlord, fleet, or finance quote exists.

## Overall Score

**75/100.** Weighted points `150.5 ÷ 20 × 10 = 75.25`, rounded to 75. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 after signed long option/lease plus fleet block.** Strongest copy risk is a logistics-property owner opens nearby capacity with stronger permitting/finance. Equipment is not a moat.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass narrowly.** Scenarios deduct obligations and state sites/bays/prices/margins/capital/dilution/timing. Conservative founder equity reaches €11.7m at Year 15. The weakness is retaining 68% of the operating holdco while financing 75% of €25m site capital and carrying only €8m net obligations.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** A conditional fleet deposit within 180 days is possible under 40,000 PLN, but it is not successful delivery or positive contribution. The first operating site requires external capital, planning, certification and build before the founder's 12-month POC can prove the economics. Multiple uncontrolled dependencies remain after paid evidence.

## Expansion Check

Strong local beachhead and corridor adjacency. Competitors cannot copy a lease but can develop nearby land. Poland helps. Capital and operator credibility, not demand theory, are the blockers.

## Fatal Objections

1. First delivered proof exceeds founder POC capital and depends on external site finance.
2. Planning/access/certification/security are multiple uncontrolled dependencies.
3. Founder lacks property/logistics operating fit.
4. Conservative wealth depends on high retained ownership despite infrastructure funding.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Secure site rights and fleet bay cash before purchasing any security or construction.
