# PelletLock Advisory Re-evaluation After Research

## Diagnosis

The rule's installation/carrier notification structure supports a granular future market map, but no present source clears the physical-point denominator. The exact product remains a copyable €4,500 bay accessory whose conservative founder result is €10.40m. Better prose or a higher assumed point count cannot make that resilient.

## Gates

- **Founder Wealth:** fail. Thin clearance, unsupported point count and recurring-plan assumptions.
- **Reachability:** pass. Two external paid bay pilots remain credibly bounded.
- **Control:** Tier 2 path, but one coupling/containment product extension can copy the core.

## Strongest Objection

The business needs both a large installed physical-point base and recurring economics, yet the present hardware removes only a short, cheap-to-contain event. Catch pans, purging and self-closing equipment cap value while incumbents cap defensibility.

## Recommendation

**pivot**

Preserve the enacted pellet-loss momentum, exact recurring handoff, weighed demo and founder-owned physical asset. Materially change the buyer/economic event and control point so wealth does not depend on a thin-margin bay accessory plus an invented point denominator. Re-evaluate any pivot before paneling.
