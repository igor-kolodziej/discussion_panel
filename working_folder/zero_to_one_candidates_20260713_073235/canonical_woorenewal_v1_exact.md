# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**WooRenewal Merchant-Workflow Acquisitions** buys neglected but cash-flowing WooCommerce plugins only when the official WordPress owner/update channel, installed merchant base, paid licences and renewals, licence server, billing history, domain, brand, source/contractor IP, and support history transfer together through escrow.

This is not a generic plugin studio, code flip, SaaS build, or broad software roll-up. The first target is one operationally critical merchant workflow whose removal would break or force migration of live checkout, order, fulfilment, returns, catalogue, or subscription configuration. The acquisition closes for no more than 28,000 PLN cash plus a seller note paid only from transferred receipts. Paid proof is the first unrelated automatic renewal or new licence settled to the founder's entity after the official transfer; no new product or marketing campaign must work first.

## One-Sentence Thesis

In open-source merchant software, code is copyable but the trusted update path into installed stores and the attached renewal rail are scarce, transferable distribution assets that an unknown technical founder can buy before building anything.

## Founder Fit

The founder is a Warsaw data scientist comfortable with AI, code, cohort analysis, automation, business analysis, and finance. He can audit billing/renewal data, automate support triage, model acquisitions, and review PHP/JavaScript with AI and a contract security engineer. The business is global, asynchronous, low-camera, and compatible with corporate employment at 15–25 hours per week and no more than five hours per day.

The minimum POC stack is the founder, a contract WordPress security engineer, and a transaction/privacy lawyer. Support is contracted only after receipts exist. The founder does not need influencer distribution, privileged introductions, or a full development team.

## Customer And Pain

The end customer is a WooCommerce merchant with 1–20 staff using a plugin inside a repeated operational workflow. Removal risks broken configuration, migration work, checkout/order interruption, lost revenue, and retraining. The merchant renews because the maintained update path and compatibility are cheaper than replacing a live workflow.

The asset seller is a developer with installed users and paid licences but declining interest, a competing project, or insufficient support capacity. Current alternatives are slow retirement, abandonment, open-sourcing without continuity, selling source code without the payment rail, or continuing a tiny product indefinitely. WooRenewal offers immediate cash, a receipts-capped note, and customer continuity.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

Acquire one plugin meeting every condition:

- at least 100 independent paying merchant sites and 12 months of billing/cohort evidence;
- at least 75% annual logo renewal or an equivalent evidenced retention metric;
- verified trailing owner earnings supporting a total price not above 2.0× earnings or 2.5× recurring gross profit;
- no more than ten support hours per month and no unresolved critical vulnerability;
- mainly WordPress directory, organic search, direct, update, or merchant-referral acquisition—not the seller's personal audience or uneconomic paid ads;
- no regulated/medical/crypto/tax-compliance/generative-AI wrapper, payment custody, single external API, or one-off theme dependency;
- official WordPress owner/commit/update authority, repository and contractor IP, domain/brand, licence server, billing/payout rights, customer contracts/consents, analytics, and support history all transferable.

Escrow releases only when all listed assets transfer. Any missing update, billing, or IP asset kills the deal.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

Most small software acquisitions overvalue source code and undervalue the installed update credential. GPL lets competitors copy features, but it does not give them the official owner account, commit/update access, installed configurations, merchant trust, renewal cohorts, support history, domain/search position, or payment contracts. Those assets solve cold start and create switching cost before the founder writes a feature.

The market gap is below software private equity: neglected products too small for institutional buyers but valuable enough that existing users pay to avoid migration. WooRenewal makes security, transfer completeness, organic acquisition, renewal, and shared merchant workflow—not seller promises—the acquisition criteria.

## Underused Momentum

WordPress.org has a formal owner-transfer process: plugins under 10,000 users can transfer in the Advanced screen; larger transfers use manual review. Freemius explicitly suggests sale or adoption when a developer no longer wants to manage a product. A current vetted listing reports a 10,000-install plugin with organic WordPress/search acquisition, recurring revenue, about USD 2,445 monthly profit, and one to three weekly support hours. A sold verified WordPress plugin business reported renewal-led revenue, 78% margin, and limited weekly support. These examples prove the asset and transfer shape, not the availability of a qualifying first target at the founder's price.

## Beachhead Market

One plugin inside a narrow WooCommerce merchant-operations family: post-purchase order routing, fulfilment handoff, returns authorisation, checkout controls, catalogue operations, or subscription administration for small stores. It must store valuable configuration and require compatibility/security updates, so renewal reflects workflow continuity rather than optional content.

Stay in the chosen workflow family until two acquired products demonstrate merchant overlap, shared support/security infrastructure, or at least 10% cross-sell. Unrelated plugins are prohibited.

## Primary Control Point

The Tier-1 control point is simultaneous ownership of:

- the official WordPress.org plugin owner/commit/update channel;
- the installed base, reviews/ratings where applicable, active paid licences, renewal contracts, and billing/payout rights;
- the licence/update server and customer configuration compatibility history;
- domain, brand, documentation/search surface, source repository, releases, and complete employee/contractor IP;
- lawful customer/support/analytics data and transition rights.

Source code alone is Tier 3 and assigned zero acquisition value. An informal promise to help after closing does not substitute for a transfer.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

1. Create WordPress, billing, marketplace, escrow, legal, and security-diligence accounts.
2. Screen 150 marketplace and off-market targets from WordPress retirement/adoption signals, developer communities, Flippa/Acquire, and direct outreach.
3. Contact at least 100 qualified owners and require five read-only data rooms covering WordPress ownership, billing, analytics, helpdesk, repository, licence server, cohorts, refunds, and acquisition source.
4. Spend at most 5,000 PLN on sourcing/accounts and at most another 7,000 PLN on security, code, privacy, and legal diligence only after a credible data room exists.
5. Sign one escrowed asset purchase for no more than 28,000 PLN cash. Any additional consideration is a 24-month note serviced only from transferred receipts and capped at the agreed total value.
6. Release escrow only after official owner/update transfer, repository/IP, domain, licence server, customer contracts/consents, billing/payout, and support-history delivery.
7. Receive the first unrelated renewal or new licence within 180 days. Preserve the product and fix security; do not require feature growth for proof.

An unknown founder can secure it because public marketplaces, formal retirement/adoption channels, and developer outreach already expose sellers. The target is deliberately below institutional acquisition size. Cash, fast diligence, escrow, customer continuity, and receipt-linked consideration solve seller risk without the founder claiming status.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

First-target purchase discipline: no more than 2.0× verified trailing owner earnings or 2.5× verified annual recurring gross profit; no more than 28,000 PLN cash; acquisition-cash payback below 30 months in the no-growth case. Existing licences may begin at 100–500 PLN per site/year; later products and bundles may average 300–1,500 PLN only after retention is preserved.

Portfolio targets are 88–94% gross margin and 30–45% operating margin after hosting, billing fees, refunds, support, security, privacy/legal work, founder replacement cost, and acquisition obligations. Kill an asset if annual logo renewal falls below 75%, fully loaded support/security exceeds 15% of revenue, a critical issue remains unpatched for more than 14 days, or no-growth cash payback exceeds 30 months.

### Distribution Moat

The first ten and hundred customers are transferred merchants and renewals, not founder-acquired leads. Organic directory/search visibility, update/admin notices permitted by platform rules, documentation, merchant referrals, and cross-sell fill later growth. Acquisition is rejected when users came mainly from the seller's public persona, affiliates that do not transfer, or paid ads without short payback.

The strongest copy risk is a GPL fork or a larger plugin vendor replicating features and underpricing renewal. It cannot copy installed configurations, the official update credential, reviews, billing cohorts, support/compatibility history, and merchant trust through one vendor contract. WooRenewal must preserve that trust through security and backward compatibility.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. Preserve one acquired plugin and observe six months of post-transfer renewals, refunds, support, vulnerabilities, and organic installs.
2. Acquire a second product in the same merchant workflow only when it shares customers, keywords, support, or compatibility work.
3. Prove at least 10% cross-sell or measured shared-support/security savings before a third acquisition.
4. Consolidate billing, documentation, security, and support while keeping stable update paths and avoiding forced migrations.
5. Reach 5,000 paying merchants in one operational family before moving to an adjacent workflow.
6. Finance later assets with retained earnings and receipt-capped seller notes; venture capital is unnecessary in the base cases.

Broad vendors can develop equivalent features, but moving inward requires winning merchants one by one or acquiring the same update channel. WooRenewal expands outward from installed distribution across adjacent jobs for the same merchant.

## Local Adaptation Defense

The business is global from Poland. Warsaw/Poland provides technical talent, lower support/security cost, EU privacy discipline, and the founder's analytical fit. No Polish regulation or language barrier is claimed as a moat. The defensible asset is global installed distribution and merchant workflow history.

## Why This Could Compound

Each acquisition adds installed merchants, renewals, update permission, support cases, compatibility knowledge, keywords, documentation, and cross-sell. Shared security testing, release engineering, billing, support, and merchant identity reduce per-product cost. Renewal cash finances the next asset. The company compounds only when products share merchants or infrastructure; a bag of unrelated plugins fails the thesis.

## Reinvestment And Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax business cash available for distribution while the founder remains employed; optional distributions are capped at 5%. Strong reinvests 100%. Cash funds diligence, receipt-capped notes, security, backward compatibility, support automation, and related assets. No unrelated acquisition and no speculative feature-growth spend occurs before retention and support economics are stable.

## Reachability And 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- **Days 1–30:** acquisition scorecard; 50 listings/signals screened; 30 owner contacts; accounts and templates ready.
- **Days 31–75:** reach 100 qualified owner contacts; obtain five read-only data rooms; reject seller-audience, paid-ad, weak-renewal, and incomplete-transfer assets; sign one LOI.
- **Days 76–120:** verify 12-month receipts/cohorts, refunds, support burden, acquisition source, security, code/IP, privacy, licensing, and every platform/account transfer.
- **Days 121–160:** close through escrow with <=28,000 PLN cash and official owner/update, billing, licence-server, domain, repository/IP, and support-history transfer.
- **By day 180:** receive one unrelated automatic renewal or new licence in the founder entity; calculate post-transfer contribution and support/security time.

Maximum founder risk before paid proof is 40,000 PLN. The founder remains employed; no full product, advertising channel, or outside partnership is required.

## Capital Tranches And Maximum Loss

1. Up to 5,000 PLN for sourcing, marketplace, entity, and accounts.
2. Up to 12,000 PLN total only after a credible read-only data room, for legal/security/code/privacy diligence.
3. Up to 40,000 PLN total only after verified cohorts and escrow-ready transfers; cash consideration <=28,000 PLN.
4. Remaining original founder capital up to 100,000 PLN only after official transfer, first receipt, 90 days of stable security/support, positive contribution, and a no-growth cash-payback path below 30 months.
5. Possible family 100,000 PLN remains excluded until two acquired assets, six months of post-transfer renewal data, shared merchant/support evidence, multiple independent payers, and positive portfolio contribution.

Escrow and data-room gates cap cash loss; founder time and diligence fees are the main pre-close downside.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder remains employed until at least two acquired assets, 500 independent paying merchants, six months of post-transfer renewals, positive contribution after support/security/refunds/notes, one repeatable acquisition source, a documented acquisition payback below 30 months, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

A rejected target leaves the acquisition database, cohort and valuation model, security scripts, contract schedules, privacy/transfer checklist, and explicit rejection evidence. Escrow prevents purchase-price release when any asset fails. Existing users keep the same product/update path; WooRenewal does not force a migration merely to rebrand.

## Validation Plan

The 180-day proof validates target access, data truth, platform transfer, resource ownership, and real customer cash. Months 7–12 validate annualised renewal, refunds, vulnerabilities, support burden, organic installs, contribution, and seller-note service. A second acquisition tests whether merchant distribution or operating infrastructure actually compounds. No platform-scale claim is made before two assets and six months of post-transfer cohorts.

Evidence is separated from hypothesis: official WordPress/Freemius/processor documents prove transfer mechanics; marketplace listings prove transaction precedent; only read-only primary data, executed transfers, payouts, cohorts, and support/security logs prove the target and company.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than five credible read-only data rooms after 100 qualified owner contacts, or no qualifying LOI by day 90.
- First target requires more than 28,000 PLN cash or lacks a receipt-capped seller-note structure.
- Any missing or conditional official owner/update, billing/payout, licence-server, domain, repository/IP, customer-contract/consent, or support-history transfer.
- Customer acquisition depends mainly on seller personality, non-transferable affiliates, or paid ads without viable payback.
- No unrelated post-transfer renewal/new licence by day 180.
- Annualised logo renewal below 75%; fully loaded support/security above 15% of revenue; unresolved critical vulnerability above 14 days; refunds/chargebacks erase contribution; no-growth cash payback above 30 months.
- Second asset has no merchant overlap, 10% cross-sell, or shared support/security saving.
- Real liability, dilution, margin, or customer data removes the path to 60,000 paying sites, 30%+ operating margin, 80%+ conservative founder ownership, and €10m net founder value.
