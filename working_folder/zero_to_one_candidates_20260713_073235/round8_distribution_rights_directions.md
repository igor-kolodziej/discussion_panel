# Round Eight — Foreign Product and Distribution-Rights Directions

**Scope:** candidate generation only. This is not a score, evaluation, pivot, panel verdict, confirmation, or validation.

## Search Boundary

This pass began with one customer truth: a Polish or CEE operator will buy an unfamiliar foreign product from an unknown local company only when the product closes a costly job the customer already controls, the order can be prepaid without a bespoke site approval, and the local seller owns more than a revocable supplier relationship.

Every direction therefore had to offer:

- a current foreign manufacturer or official business-matching route that openly accepts distribution partners;
- an executed geographic or vertical right before inventory risk, not a verbal promise of future exclusivity;
- a direct payer who controls the purchase and use of the product;
- paid evidence inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN while the founder remains employed;
- a 12-month controlled asset inside the original 100,000 PLN;
- product-wide EU conformity rather than a new certification or public-site approval for every sale;
- a locally owned configuration, lifecycle, installed-base, or payment asset that a competitor cannot obtain through one supplier contract; and
- a non-heroic route to at least €10 million of founder equity in a conservative successful case.

Five directions were researched. Two retain a candidate shape subject to hard documentary gates. Three are recorded as stops because they reduce to ordinary distribution, require access the founder does not have, or reset integration at every customer.

## Shared Legal and Denominator Discipline

- The EU Vertical Block Exemption Regulation provides a legal framework for exclusive distribution, but an agreement cannot simply prohibit every cross-border passive sale. The right must be drafted by EU competition counsel around protected active-selling territories/customer groups, direct-sale and lead-routing commitments, performance milestones, cure periods, renewal, and assignability. “Exclusive Poland” is not assumed to be an absolute monopoly.
- For machinery imported from outside the EU, the Polish company would become an EU importer with conformity, documentation, traceability, instruction, and market-surveillance duties. No non-EU machine advances without the exact EU declaration of conformity, technical-document access, Polish instructions, spare-parts/warranty allocation, and a written transition plan for Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. The general CE file applies to the product type; a customer's own workplace risk assessment does not reset product certification.
- Eurostat reports **2.2 million EU manufacturing enterprises and 30.2 million persons employed in 2023**. It also reports **33.1 million EU business-economy enterprises in 2023**, with construction representing 12%, implying about 4.0 million construction enterprises. These are broad ceilings, not eligible-customer counts. Each surviving direction must construct a bottom-up named eligible account list before its wealth case is allowed to stand.
- All pricing, margins, conversion, eligible subsets, multiples, capital needs, and customer counts below are hypotheses until quotes, deposits, invoices, and signed agreements exist. Manufacturer statements such as “high margin” are not margin evidence.

## Direction 1 — FlangeClock CEE: Preconfigured Critical-Bolting Kits With a Calibration Payment Rail

### Underserved Customer Truth and Foreign Gap

The first buyer is the maintenance manager of a CEE process plant, power asset, shipyard, heavy-equipment workshop, or specialist bolting contractor. The job is not “buy a torque wrench.” The buyer needs the correct tensioning/torque kit, valid calibration, compatible accessories, a usable replacement during calibration, and spares before a planned shutdown. Broad tool catalogues leave the buyer assembling that package across vendors and carrying the cost of one missing or out-of-calibration component.

Tritorc publicly invites distributors, states that partners can secure exclusive regional rights, offers technical training and engineering support, and lists only the Netherlands under Europe on its current distributor page. That is evidence of a real contracting route and an apparent CEE white space, not proof that Poland/CEE remains unassigned. Territory availability must be confirmed in writing before any customer claim.

### Product and Exact Paid Event

Sell one serialized, job-ready **FlangeClock kit**: the foreign torque/tensioning tools, pump and accessories; an independently calibrated certificate set; Polish job cards; a configured spare/loan unit; and a prepaid 12-month calibration-and-critical-spares slot. It is a product sale with a lifecycle payment position, not on-site bolting labour, consulting, inspection, or a referral.

The first paid event is a direct 40% customer deposit against a fixed-price kit with an agreed delivery window. The plant or contractor controls its own procurement and use; no receiver, authority, insurer, or public-site owner must approve the founder's sale.

### Real Right and Foundational Control Point

Before taking deposits, require an executed agreement effective immediately for a narrow customer group—initially independent bolting contractors and owner-operated industrial maintenance teams in Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia—with:

- protected active-selling rights and manufacturer lead routing;
- no manufacturer direct selling into the protected group except named legacy accounts;
- published transfer price, warranty, spare-parts and lead-time schedules;
- objective annual targets that begin with customer-backed orders rather than speculative stock;
- a cure period, a minimum three-year term, renewal formula, and assignability on a company sale; and
- permission to sell the locally configured kit, hold a regional loan/calibration pool, and retain all local customer and lifecycle records.

At signature the right is a Tier-2 contractual asset, not a monopoly. By month 12 the defensible control must be the combination of that right plus founder-owned serialized kit configurations, calibration due dates, replacement history, compatible-accessory records, prepaid lifecycle slots, a loan pool, and two independent accredited calibration routes. A rival can sign another tool supplier; it cannot obtain this installed workflow and payment history from that contract.

### Exact Acquisition Mechanism

Build a 300-account named list from plant registers, maintenance-contractor directories, shipyard/power/process-industry exhibitor lists, and public procurement awards. Send the maintenance manager a one-page offer naming the exact kit, certificate set, spare entitlement, delivery date, and fixed deposit. A paid contract technical seller can perform clustered demonstrations one day per week; the founder owns the list, pricing, deposits, and follow-up. The first 10 customers come from 60 direct conversations and 12 demonstrations. The first 100 come from the same account list, buyer referrals that are helpful but not required, and manufacturer-routed leads guaranteed by the contract.

### 180-Day Paid Proof and 12-Month Resource

**Days 1–45, maximum 8,000 PLN:** obtain landed price, CE/document pack, warranty terms, exact territory status, and counsel's signed-right term sheet; interview 20 named buyers about current kit gaps and annual calibration spend.

**Days 46–90, cumulative 22,000 PLN:** sign the effective right; borrow or buy no more than one demonstration configuration; translate the product and safety file; contract an accredited calibration lab and one technical demonstrator.

**Days 91–180, cumulative maximum 40,000 PLN:** run 12 demonstrations and obtain deposits from at least three unrelated payers, each covering the supplier deposit and freight attributable to its order. Deliver at least one positive-contribution kit before day 180. No broad inventory is purchased.

**By month 12, cumulative maximum 100,000 PLN founder capital:** require 15 unrelated paid accounts, 30 delivered kits, at least 60% attachment of the annual lifecycle slot, the executed regional right, two calibration routes, a three-unit loan pool funded after orders, the complete serialized record, and either repeat orders or renewal prepayments. Working capital beyond the founder cap must come from customer deposits, retained gross profit, or documented supplier terms—not family money.

### Source-Backed Denominator and Conservative Founder-Wealth Case

The official ceiling is 2.2 million EU manufacturing enterprises. The commercial denominator is not known. By day 180, the founder must be able to construct at least 8,000 named CEE and adjacent-EU sites/contractors with frequent critical bolting, planned shutdowns, and purchasing authority; otherwise the wealth case is withdrawn.

**Conservative successful case, year 9–11 (hypothesis):** 600 active accounts averaging €32,000 annual product, spare, calibration-slot and replacement purchases; €19.2 million revenue; 43% gross margin; 18% operating margin (€3.46 million); €1.3 million peak growth capital, funded after proof by deposits, supplier credit and retained earnings; 72% founder ownership after a 10% option pool and 18% outside growth capital; 4.5× operating-profit value of €15.55 million; founder equity of **€11.20 million**. No personal distributions are counted. While employed, 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested and optional distributions are capped at 5%.

Intermediate milestones are 30 kits/month 12, 120 active accounts/year 4, 300/year 7, and 600/year 9–11. The 600 accounts equal 7.5% of the required 8,000-account qualified list, not 0.03% of the irrelevant broad manufacturing ceiling.

### Local Adaptation Defense, Copy Risk, and Incumbent Response

The Poland/CEE defense is a Polish-language shutdown-ready kit, local accredited calibration, next-shutdown replacement availability, and one record across tool, certificate, spares, loaner, and payment. Incumbent tool houses can discount, bundle calibration, or contract an alternative foreign OEM. The defense works only if the founder reaches a dense installed cohort whose due dates and replacement inventory produce lower downtime and acquisition cost than a catalogue competitor. The manufacturer contract alone is explicitly insufficient.

### Kill Criteria

Stop before customer deposits if the territory/customer group is already assigned; the supplier offers only revocable or non-protected resale; exclusivity requires more than 40,000 PLN of speculative inventory; any model lacks a valid product-wide EU conformity file; landed hardware gross margin is below 38%; lifecycle-slot gross margin is below 50%; ordinary Polish suppliers can deliver the complete configured/calibrated/loaner offer inside five working days at within 10% of price; fewer than three unrelated buyers deposit by day 180; delivery requires founder-performed site work; or a bottom-up 8,000-account eligible list cannot be evidenced.

## Direction 2 — MiniLiner CEE: Compact Indoor-Pipe Rehabilitation Machines With a Consumables Reorder Position

### Underserved Customer Truth and Foreign Gap

The payer is the owner of a small drain-cleaning, plumbing, or property-maintenance contractor. The customer's desired outcome is to add a higher-ticket pipe-renewal job using the same van and team, without buying a large rehabilitation rig or referring the work away. The friction is not awareness of trenchless repair; it is a compact, teachable package with dependable resin, spares, and local operating recipes for common small-diameter Polish/CEE pipes.

Croatian manufacturer NoDig currently advertises distributor applications, European manufacture, partner training/certification, spare-parts support, high-margin equipment, repeat orders, and exclusive territories in multiple European markets. Its European hub lists country channels for Croatia, Germany, Slovenia/Austria, Serbia/Balkans, Spain/Italy and France, but not Poland. This is evidence of an open contracting mechanism and an apparent Polish route gap; it is not proof that a protected territory remains available.

### Product and Exact Paid Event

Sell a fixed **MiniLiner launch pack**: one compact cleaning/lining machine selected from the manufacturer's existing CE-marked line, starter resin/consumables, Polish instructions, standard small-diameter recipe cards, training places, critical spares, and a twelve-month reorder-price schedule. The contractor, not the founder, performs and warrants its customer projects.

The first paid event is a 30% deposit from a drain contractor for a named machine and training date. There is no public procurement, municipality approval, or property-by-property product certification in the founder's sale. The contractor remains responsible for site assessment and compliant job execution.

### Real Right and Foundational Control Point

Before deposits, sign an effective Poland exclusive—or a protected compact-indoor-pipe customer-group right if national rights are unavailable—with lead routing, no direct manufacturer sales into the protected group, objective order-backed targets, warranty/spare obligations, Polish-content rights, minimum term/renewal/cure, and assignability. Broader CEE rights may be added in the same agreement only when territories are genuinely unassigned; the model does not assume serial country deals.

The durable asset is not the Croatian machine contract. It is the installed contractor cohort plus founder-owned pipe-material/diameter/job recipe history, training completion, serial and warranty records, spare demand, consumable reorder timing, and prepaid replenishment accounts. By month 12 the business must have at least 20 unrelated installed payers whose next purchases route directly through the company.

### Exact Acquisition Mechanism

Build a 250-company list from Polish plumbing/drain-cleaning registries, trenchless-conference exhibitor lists, maps and trade directories. Contact the owner with a fixed demonstration proposition: bring one real recurring job or sample pipe, watch the machine perform the standard process, and receive a quoted machine, training date, starter inputs, and payback worksheet the same day. Use two Warsaw/Poznań demonstration days rather than travelling continuously. First 10 customers require 50 owner calls and 20 demonstrations; first 100 come from the same list expanded to Czechia/Slovakia/Baltics under the single signed territory agreement.

### 180-Day Paid Proof and 12-Month Resource

**Days 1–45, maximum 7,000 PLN:** confirm the exact territory, wholesale price, product declarations, resin SDS/REACH status, warranty and training; conduct 20 owner interviews and collect actual job/referral economics.

**Days 46–90, cumulative 20,000 PLN:** sign the effective right; secure a manufacturer-loaned or deposit-backed demo unit; translate existing product/safety materials; complete manufacturer training with one contract technical trainer.

**Days 91–180, cumulative maximum 40,000 PLN:** hold two clustered demo days; obtain deposits from at least three unrelated contractors, each large enough to fund its supplier deposit; deliver and train at least one payer at positive contribution. Do not buy a machine fleet or resin inventory before deposits.

**By month 12, cumulative maximum 100,000 PLN founder capital:** require 20 installed paying contractors, 15 repeat consumable purchases, the executed right, two trained contract demonstrators, critical-spare and starter-consumable stock, complete local recipe and lifecycle records, and prepaid/scheduled reorders covering at least six months of replenishment COGS.

### Source-Backed Denominator and Conservative Founder-Wealth Case

Eurostat's 2023 business-economy figures imply about 4.0 million construction enterprises in the EU. That is only a ceiling. The founder must construct a bottom-up list of at least 12,000 drain-cleaning/plumbing/maintenance contractors across the executed territory and adjacent expansion countries that perform enough eligible small-pipe jobs and can self-finance a machine; otherwise the wealth bridge is killed.

**Conservative successful case, year 10–12 (hypothesis):** 1,000 installed contractor accounts; 350 new/replacement machine packs per year at €25,000 average recognized revenue plus €6,000 annual consumables/spares per installed account; €14.75 million revenue; 45% gross margin; 20% operating margin (€2.95 million); €1.0 million peak growth capital after proof; 72% founder ownership after an option pool and 18% outside growth capital; 5.0× operating-profit value of €14.75 million; founder equity of **€10.62 million**. No distributions are counted, and the same 95% reinvestment/5% distribution rule applies while employed.

Intermediate milestones are 20 installed accounts/month 12, 150/year 3, 450/year 6, and 1,000/year 10–12. The terminal installed base is 8.3% of the required 12,000-account qualified denominator, not a share of all EU construction firms.

### Local Adaptation Defense, Copy Risk, and Incumbent Response

CEE adaptation consists of one compact launch configuration, Polish/Czech/Slovak training rights, documented recipes for common local pipe materials and diameters, same-day critical spares, and predictable resin/consumable replenishment. Existing trenchless and drain-equipment dealers can source another machine, discount, or add training. They cannot instantly copy an installed cohort's recipes, job history, trained users, consumable timing, and prepaid reorder accounts; if those records do not improve repeat orders and contractor payback, there is no defense.

### Kill Criteria

Stop if Poland or the protected customer group is already assigned; the manufacturer refuses an effective protected right before inventory; the demo unit consumes more than 25,000 PLN before customer deposits; machine/resin conformity or Polish instructions are incomplete; the seller must approve or supervise each end job; landed pack gross margin is below 38% or consumable gross margin below 50%; fewer than three unrelated contractors deposit by day 180; fewer than half of installed customers reorder within six months; a comparable Polish package is available within 10% of price and includes equally fast spares/training; or the 12,000-account eligible list cannot be built from named businesses.

## Direction 3 — FinishCell PL: Robotic Deburring/Grinding Tool Rights — Stop After Research

### Customer Truth and Real Contracting Route

Small Polish metal fabricators want repeatable finishing without recruiting scarce manual grinders, but they do not want an automation project whose engineering cost and acceptance risk exceed the tool price. An active Enterprise Europe Network offer valid through 22 September 2026 targets Poland, permits an SME of up to 10 people to become the official country/region dealer, and says exclusivity may be granted. The Finnish company is the European hub for an open-platform Taiwanese robotic finishing tool series.

### Why This Does Not Advance

The offer explicitly expects strong metalworking links or a system integrator. The foreign tool is only one component; robot selection, guarding, extraction, programming, process recipe, safety assessment and acceptance reset at each production cell. Narrowing to one part family would turn the founder into an integrator with bespoke site acceptance, while selling the bare tool would be ordinary distribution. A recipe library would be prospective and customer-contributed, not controlled at contract signature.

No decision-ready conservative €10 million founder-wealth bridge survives without assuming hundreds of integrations the founder cannot deliver part-time or an installed-base margin not disclosed by the offer. Do not spend on exclusivity, a demo cell, or inventory.

## Direction 4 — H-Dust Poland: Exclusive Japanese Industrial Vacuum Range — Stop After Research

### Customer Truth and Real Contracting Route

Specialist contractors and small factories handling hazardous dust need a compliant vacuum, the correct filter/consumable, quick replacement and traceable maintenance rather than a generic wet/dry cleaner. An Enterprise Europe Network offer valid through 29 July 2026 explicitly seeks an **exclusive national distributor in Poland**, accepts an SME with up to 10 people, and concerns an established Japanese industrial-vacuum brand routed through its German general importer.

### Why This Does Not Advance

The active offer provides no named product, differential performance, H-class evidence, price, margin, installed-base gap or IPR; it describes a broad range “for all needs.” Exclusive access to an anonymous catalogue is a real right but still generic importing. A filter subscription, dust-task selector or loaner fleet would be easy for Kärcher, Nilfisk and specialist safety distributors to add, and the national right could disappear when volume targets are missed. The conservative wealth case would require a large share of an uncounted hazardous-dust subset at ordinary distributor margins.

Do not reopen this direction unless the disclosed manufacturer offers a protected high-hazard vertical, verified product-wide EU conformity, a proprietary high-margin consumable rail, customer-backed minimums, and a bottom-up eligible fleet large enough to carry €10 million of founder equity. A different vacuum model or higher assumed margin is not a material improvement.

## Direction 5 — GlassVolume PL: On-Site Bottle Crushers for Hotels and Bars — Stop After Research

### Customer Truth and Real Contracting Route

High-volume hotels, event venues and bars want fewer noisy bottle-bin movements, less back-of-house space and fewer staff minutes handling empty glass. Irish manufacturer PEL publicly says it is actively seeking dealers and distributors in all locations and markets for glass-bottle crushers, compactors and balers; its site also offers purchase and rental routes. Poland is not named on that page.

### Why This Does Not Advance

PEL documents a dealership route, not protected Poland/CEE rights. The machine solves a real operational pain, but the locally proposed additions—liners, collection coordination, rental and an ROI calculator—are purchasable or service-like. Turning crushed glass into a payment rail would add recycler acceptance, waste-registration, contamination, haulage and route-density dependencies, recreating the rejected two-party material-flow family. Without that flow, the company is a generic equipment reseller with modest recurring value. The manufacturer contract and a demo unit are both copyable through one supplier agreement.

No conservative founder-wealth bridge is written because doing so would require an unevidenced eligible high-volume venue count, distributor margin, replacement cycle and CEE territory protection. Do not seek deposits or buy a demo unit unless PEL grants an effective protected vertical right plus a manufacturer-owned proprietary consumable with disclosed margin and the founder can prove a large direct-payer fleet without operating collection routes.

## Round-Eight Generator Conclusion

The only directions that retain the requested structure are **FlangeClock CEE** and **MiniLiner CEE**, and both are conditional on an executed protected right before inventory, exact conformity documents, three unrelated deposits inside 180 days/40,000 PLN, positive first delivery, disclosed high-margin economics, and a bottom-up eligible denominator. Their product/payment control can compound through installed lifecycle and reorder records; the supplier agreement alone is not treated as a moat.

FinishCell PL is stopped because customer-specific integration and acceptance reset the sale. H-Dust Poland is stopped because even a real national exclusive would remain generic importing with no evidenced proprietary product rail. GlassVolume PL is stopped because the only apparent route beyond dealership is a two-party waste/logistics business already rejected in the ledger.
