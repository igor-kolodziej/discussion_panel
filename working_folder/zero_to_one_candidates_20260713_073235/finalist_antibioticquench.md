# Zero-to-One Candidate: AntibioticQuench

## One-Sentence Thesis

Option a publicly offered University of Gdańsk antibiotic-inactivation invention, sell a non-refundable qualification study on a manufacturer's real high-strength antibiotic side stream before building hardware, and convert successful studies into modular treatment systems whose exclusive field rights, scale-up know-how and growing effluent-performance library compound across Europe.

## Founder Fit

The founder can design experiments, analyse dose-response and energy data, automate prospect research, and build the performance database while remaining employed. His data-science background is relevant to multivariate process control and evidence packaging, but it does not substitute for plasma, environmental-chemistry or industrial-safety expertise. The minimum technical stack therefore uses an IP attorney, a plasma/process engineer, the licensor's inventors under contracted deliverables, and an accredited analytical laboratory as paid vendors. A contract mechanical/electrical engineer and skid fabricator enter only after paid bench evidence.

The first 180 days fit 15–20 focused hours per week: licence diligence, structured customer interviews, protocol design, account-based outbound and one controlled trial. The founder does not manufacture equipment, handle hazardous samples at home, promise regulatory compliance or leave his corporate job during this phase.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is the environmental, engineering or EHS owner at a Polish antibiotic API, veterinary-medicine or formulation plant with a segregated, measurable aqueous production stream such as reactor wash, CIP or batch-cleaning water. That person does not primarily want an antimicrobial-resistance dashboard. They want documented destruction of the parent antibiotic and its antibacterial activity at an acceptable cost, before the stream reaches a biological plant or leaves the site as expensive waste.

Current workarounds include dilution into a larger treatment train, off-site hazardous-liquid disposal, activated carbon, ozone, UV/oxidant treatment, membranes, electrochemical oxidation or incineration. Each may be the right answer. The commercial pain exists only where a concentrated recurring side stream is expensive or risky enough that a site-specific destruction step can improve total treatment cost, auditability or environmental performance. WHO's 2024 manufacturing guidance confirms that antibiotic-manufacturing pollution is a real, neglected control problem and provides targets and risk-management context; it does not prove that this plasma method is effective or economic on any customer's effluent.

## Product Wedge

The first product is a fixed-scope **Real-Effluent Destruction Qualification** priced at €4,500–€7,000 and paid before the sample is accepted. Under the optioned technology and a signed sample/data agreement, an accredited laboratory and contracted technical team test one customer's real segregated stream through a licensed bench reactor. The report includes:

- parent-compound concentration before and after treatment;
- residual antibacterial activity, not concentration alone;
- COD/TOC and relevant matrix effects;
- a targeted transformation-product and ecotoxicity screen appropriate to the compound;
- energy, electrode and consumable use per cubic metre and per gram destroyed;
- a comparison with the customer's incumbent or quoted alternative; and
- a go/no-go preliminary module specification and budget.

The study is not generic consulting: the customer buys evidence for a named stream, the company retains only contractually permitted anonymised features and operating data, and part of the study fee may be credited against a later system order. No claim is made that a laboratory result equals field acceptance.

Only after successful real-effluent work does the company offer a toll-fabricated continuous pretreatment module, initially at approximately €180,000 per module, plus an annual electrode, service, verification and process-support contract. The module treats segregated high-strength batches upstream; it is not positioned as a complete municipal wastewater replacement.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Laboratory water-removal claims do not solve the buyer's decision. Antibiotic plants have different salts, solvents, pH, suspended solids and co-contaminants, while a high removal percentage can still leave antibacterial activity or problematic transformation products. The adoption bottleneck is a trusted, comparable **stream-specific destruction and lifecycle-cost record** tied to a product the seller has the right to commercialise.

AntibioticQuench therefore begins with a paid decision product rather than an unfunded demonstration skid. Each qualified stream improves reactor recipes, electrode-life estimates, control parameters, transformation-product knowledge and proposal accuracy. The insight is commercially useful only if later studies become faster and more predictive and if the resulting module beats the best feasible alternative on total cost or risk.

## Underused Momentum

There are three pieces of external momentum:

1. **Official technical resource:** University of Gdańsk publicly lists technical offer 160/2025, Polish patent application P.431823, as available for licence, sale of rights or spin-off commercialisation. The offer describes a TRL-4 continuous dc-APGD method, tested in laboratory conditions on ampicillin, penicillin G and chloramphenicol, without plasma-forming gas.
2. **Manufacturing pressure:** WHO issued its first guidance on antibiotic-manufacturing wastewater and solid waste in September 2024, covering APIs through finished-product formulation and calling for risk management, audit and transparency.
3. **European treatment budgets:** Directive (EU) 2024/3019 introduces quaternary-treatment obligations for urban wastewater and extended producer responsibility under which relevant pharmaceutical and cosmetics producers cover at least 80% of specified treatment and monitoring costs. This creates directional spending pressure and a future pool of treatment evidence, but it does not mandate at-source industrial treatment or select this technology.

## Beachhead Market

The beachhead is not municipal wastewater and not all pharmaceuticals. It is Polish and immediately adjacent CEE production sites with:

- a recurring, segregated antibiotic-bearing aqueous stream;
- one of the antibiotic families already covered by the licensor's evidence or technically close enough for a cheap bench test;
- a named EHS/engineering owner and sampling route;
- annual disposal or treatment cost high enough to support a €4,500–€7,000 study; and
- the ability to buy a modular pretreatment asset without changing the whole treatment plant.

The founder builds the account list from official manufacturing-authorisation databases, national pharmaceutical/veterinary registers, public environmental permits and direct company documentation. The exact count of qualified sites and their stream economics are hypotheses to determine in the first 60 days. Hospital sewage and dilute municipal influent are excluded from the initial wedge because their matrices, procurement and economics are different.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First ten:** Start with 40 named Polish accounts and 20 nearby CEE accounts. Seek 15 qualified conversations, five sample/protocol reviews and one prepaid qualification by day 180. Win the first three studies in Poland through a precise, low-risk offer backed by the named university licence option and independent lab. Customers four through ten are adjacent Polish, Czech, Slovak and German manufacturing sites reached through the same compound/stream case studies. Founder-led outbound is narrow and account-specific rather than high-energy networking or content marketing.

**First hundred:** After two accepted field modules, hire technical sales and sign non-exclusive, project-registered channel agreements with industrial-water integrators in countries where the company has enforceable commercial rights. Direct target-account data remains the control channel; integrators provide local plant access, commissioning and service only under signed economics and customer-ownership terms. Each new antibiotic/stream family becomes a repeatable account list and evidence package. No unsigned integrator relationship is assumed in the first-year proof.

## Primary Control Point

The year-one control point is a signed package, not a vague university relationship:

1. a 120–180 day exclusive field-of-use option over the commercial rights the universities actually control for high-concentration pharmaceutical and veterinary-production aqueous side streams;
2. a pre-agreed conversion formula for an exclusive licence with a total entry payment no greater than 40,000 PLN, fully crediting the option fee, plus milestone royalties and objective performance obligations;
3. access to documented know-how, reactor drawings, samples and a defined block of inventor engineering hours;
4. rights to subcontract fabrication and sell systems in the licensed field;
5. clear ownership or exclusive commercial rights to company-funded scale-up improvements; and
6. customer contracts that permit retention of defined anonymised operating features, test methods and aggregated performance data.

Official evidence currently proves only that P.431823 is a Polish patent **application** publicly offered for commercialisation at TRL 4. It does not prove grant, validity, freedom to operate, territorial coverage, exclusivity, price or ownership of improvements. An IP-status and freedom-to-operate review is therefore a condition precedent. If there is no enforceable Polish protection plus contractually protected know-how and a credible new-improvement-IP route for export markets, the expansion control point is too weak.

## Distribution Advantage

The first acquisition mechanism is a finite account map and compound-specific offer, not broad awareness. Public authorisation and permit sources identify plausible sites; AI-assisted research finds EHS and engineering decision-makers; each message cites the exact stream/compound hypothesis and offers a paid protocol review. The named university option and independent laboratory reduce the credibility gap for an unknown founder without pretending that university affiliation is product approval.

The compounding distribution asset is the match between compound family, stream characteristics, achieved destruction, process recipe, energy use and buyer economics. That allows later salespeople and integrators to show a closer precedent, quote a narrower trial and shorten technical diligence. Project-registration clauses, direct ownership of customer data and recurring verification/service keep channel partners from disintermediating the company.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

The direct alternatives are ozone, activated carbon, UV/H2O2 and other advanced oxidation, electrochemical oxidation, membrane concentration, off-site destruction and other plasma configurations. AntibioticQuench has no advantage merely because it uses plasma. It wins only on one or more measured dimensions: elimination of antibacterial activity, lower total cost for concentrated side streams, fewer consumables, continuous operation, less secondary waste, or better performance in a difficult matrix.

Expansion is sequential:

1. prove one licensed antibiotic family and one segregated Polish manufacturing stream;
2. standardise a repeat module for similar Polish streams;
3. add adjacent antibiotic families only after paid comparison studies;
4. enter named CEE/EU territories only where know-how, improvement IP, data and service contracts provide defensibility; and
5. licence or sell larger multi-module systems through project-registered industrial-water channels once repeat acceptance is established.

Toll fabrication avoids a premature factory. Scale capital goes into engineering, working capital, service coverage and defensible improvements rather than commodity metalwork.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The publicly offered application is Polish, the inventors and TTO are locally reachable, and the first manufacturing accounts can be served with lower travel and engineering cost. A Polish beachhead also keeps early IP enforcement and contracted know-how close to the licensor. These are execution advantages, not a European moat.

The EU defence must be built during scale-up: exclusive access to undisclosed operating know-how, ownership or exclusive field rights to new reactor/control/electrode improvements filed before disclosure, the real-effluent performance corpus, accepted reference installations and contracted service response. If competitors can legally reproduce the process outside Poland from public material and the company cannot protect meaningful improvements, the plan must remain a smaller Polish business or be killed; a Polish filing alone cannot support the European wealth case.

## Compounding Advantage

Each paid stream adds data that a general equipment vendor does not initially possess: matrix composition, effective exposure, energy per gram destroyed, electrode wear, transformation products, antibacterial-activity results, control settings, acceptance protocol and operating cost. Repeated modules add validated BOMs, safety documentation, commissioning routines, reference customers, service history and improvement inventions.

The intended flywheel is: more paid stream qualifications → better prediction and narrower guarantees → faster module sales → more field data and service revenue → stronger compound-specific references and process improvements → lower qualification cost and more defensible entry into adjacent streams. Customer confidentiality is respected; only data rights expressly retained by contract enter the shared corpus.

## Critical Dependency Map

Dependencies are tested sequentially rather than simultaneously:

1. **Rights:** ownership, status, field exclusivity, know-how access, improvement ownership and freedom to operate are acceptable.
2. **Buyer access:** a plant can legally and safely provide a representative segregated sample and has a budget owner.
3. **Real-effluent efficacy:** the process achieves the customer's compound, antibacterial-activity and by-product criteria outside clean laboratory water.
4. **Economics:** energy, electrode life, pretreatment and analytical burden support a module with positive contribution and competitive lifecycle cost.
5. **Scale engineering:** a qualified contractor can design and toll-fabricate a safe, certifiable continuous module under customer-backed milestones.
6. **Repeatability:** an unrelated second buyer pays for the same evidence/product shape.

Failure at any step stops spend on later steps. The first 180-day proof does not require a regulator to approve a new standard, an integrator to resell the product, or a manufacturer to finance R&D beyond its prepaid study.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder stays employed until all of the following exist: at least three independent paying studies; two unrelated field-pilot or module orders with deposits; one accepted field installation; positive contribution economics with a credible >=30% module gross margin and >=50% service gross margin; a documented acquisition channel producing a qualified pipeline at least six times the next 12 months' sales target; contracted recurring service demand; and at least 12 months of personal runway. Technical contractors cover delivery until workload and revenue justify full-time hires.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If the licence, efficacy or economics fail, the founder lets the option expire and does not build a skid. Reusable assets include the IP/FTO memo, buyer map, interview record, sample-safety and analytical protocols, comparator-cost model, fabricator quotes and understanding of pharmaceutical-wastewater buying. Customer-confidential results remain restricted. The transferable asset is a rigorous paid-qualification workflow that can be applied to another **owned or licensed** treatment technology; it is not a fallback generic consultancy.
