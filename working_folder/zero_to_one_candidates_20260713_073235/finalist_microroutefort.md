# Zero-to-One Candidate: MicroRouteFort Bronze Conversion

## One-Sentence Thesis

Lease 20-bay sections of already paved, HGV-accessible and fully serviced yards, add only the EU Bronze security layer, and open the first site inside 12 months/100,000 PLN after two fleets prepay recurring corridor capacity.

## Founder Fit

The founder can map corridors and yards, model yield, automate route-specific fleet outbound, and build remote monitoring/booking. A transport-property lawyer, certified SSPA pre-auditor, security integrator and contracted site host cover specialised work. The POC is part-time; the host already runs toilets/showers/food/cleaning and the founder does not operate a truck stop.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Polish/CEE fleets on recurring international lanes need legal rest, cargo security, showers/toilets, reefer power, and a predictable arrival position. Drivers currently search ad hoc, detour, park insecurely or overflow onto ramps. The Commission reports a 390,057-space current EU shortfall. A fleet will pay when a named lane starts, a cargo-security contract renews, or reliable rest capacity reduces theft, time loss and driver frustration.

## Product Wedge

One 20-bay fenced/visually bounded section inside an existing truck service, logistics, fuel or hospitality yard within about 5 km of A1/A2/S8. The host lease must already include HGV access, separate toilets/showers, hot water/daily cleaning, 24/7 snacks/drinks, dining, free internet, personal power, reefer power, waste and emergency procedures. MicroRouteFort adds only Bronze perimeter/lighting/entry CCTV, remote checks, signage, security plan, booking/access and certification.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The Commission itself recommends rapid upgrade of insecure existing parking. The gap is not another greenfield 120-bay truck stop; it is certifying underused capacity where the expensive service infrastructure already exists. The scarce asset is the long micro-site lease on the correct corridor plus fleet blocks, not cameras.

## Underused Momentum

EU standards make Bronze requirements legible; the December 2025 Commission report quantifies 3,250 missing areas and calls for quick upgrades and funding. CEF has financed safe-parking projects. This is policy/demand context, not a substitute for a site and fleet deposit.

## Beachhead Market

One 20-bay micro-site near a congested Polish international corridor node with no certified comparable within the lane radius, hosted by a service/fuel/logistics property whose required amenities already operate 24/7. Target high-value cargo fleets running the same lane weekly.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Charge €16–20 per occupied night; reserve 8–12 bays in 90-night fleet blocks and sell residual capacity digitally. At 20 bays, 65% utilisation and €18/night, annual parking revenue is about €85,400/370,000 PLN. Target host lease/service 120,000 PLN, remote/security/cleaning allocation 55,000 PLN, utilities/insurance/maintenance 45,000 PLN, booking/payment/other 25,000 PLN, leaving about 125,000 PLN site EBITDA/34%. These are hypotheses. Reject if fully loaded EBITDA is <25%, upgrade payback >30 months or host rent/service exceeds 45% of conservative revenue.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First ten bays come from two fleet blocks before fit-out. The first 100 drivers/vehicles come from those fleets and residual booking/signage. Later sites are anchored by the same fleets' adjacent lanes; no two-sided marketplace cold start is required.

## Primary Control Point

Tier 1: 10–15-year assignable lease/option over a named 20-bay HGV section, express fit-out/parking rights and contractually included required services; then Bronze certification and prepaid/take-or-pay fleet allocations. A host service promise without lease rights is invalid.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Screen 200 host yards and 40 fleets. Spend <=8,000 PLN on a 120-day conditional option after a service/access checklist; <=8,000 PLN on independent Bronze pre-audit; <=5,000 PLN legal/insurance/lighting quote. Require a written <=60,000 PLN movable-upgrade quote and two fleet deposits totalling >=15,000 PLN for at least 12 bays/90 nights by day 180. After proof, spend up to the founder's remaining 100,000 PLN on upgrades, certification, rent deposit and working capital; open/deliver by month 12. No bathrooms, paving, grid upgrade, or building work is allowed.

## Distribution Advantage

Direct lane-specific fleet contracts anchor capacity; corridor signage and the EU parking data access point/booking channels fill the rest. Each lease adds inventory and booking data; the same fleets anchor next nodes. Copy risk is a nearby property owner adds Bronze capacity, but it cannot copy the exact lease or contracted fleet blocks.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Prove one 20-bay conversion, then option 60–120-bay existing serviced sections on adjacent routes. Standardise host lease/service schedule, pre-audit, movable security kit, remote monitoring, fleet contract and pricing. Build a CEE corridor portfolio; property/fit-out debt and CEF are used only after operating proof.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland is a major freight corridor with fragmented underused yards and lower operating costs. Local land/host sourcing and permitting matter. The defence is location rights, certification, fleet route density and a repeatable low-capex conversion model.

## Compounding Advantage

Every site adds certified bay inventory, fleet contracts, route demand, security performance, price/yield data, landlord references and host-service templates. Fleet routes create demand for adjacent nodes. Movable security/booking systems and standard agreements reduce next-site cost.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% during employment, distributions <=5%; strong reinvests 100%. Founder capital funds the first micro-conversion only. Later sites use lease/debt/CEF/infrastructure equity after contracted fleets; all liabilities/dilution are included.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–60 map 200 yards/40 fleets; days 61–100 sign one <=8,000 PLN option and pre-audit; days 101–150 secure compliant lease/service/upgrades/insurance plan; by day 180 collect two unrelated fleet deposits >=15,000 PLN for >=12 bays/90 nights. Founder risk <=30,000 PLN before deposits. Open and deliver within 12 months using total founder capital <=100,000 PLN.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

5,000 PLN mapping/outbound; 8,000 PLN option; 8,000 PLN pre-audit; 5,000 PLN legal/quotes. No upgrade spend before two deposits. Remaining founder capital to 100,000 PLN only when quote <=60,000 PLN and opening can occur without construction. Family capital excluded until operating site, three fleets, positive contribution, 65% utilisation signal and second host option.

## Critical Dependency Map

Existing required services, clean HGV access/use, long lease rights, pre-audit/upgrade quote, two fleet deposits, certification, insurance and remote operation are sequential. If the host lacks one service or requires construction/grid work, reject the site.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

One operating site, three independent fleet customers, six months positive site contribution, 65% utilisation/contracted equivalent, second site option, repeatable fleet acquisition and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Expired option leaves corridor/host/fleet maps, pre-audit checklist, quotes, lease/service schedule and buyer objections. Fleet deposits are conditionally refundable if disclosed certification/opening conditions fail. Movable hardware can be reused only after purchase gate.

## Critical Evidence

Official shortage and Bronze/service requirements; Commission quick-upgrade recommendation and CEF precedents. Site availability, cost and fleet WTP are hypotheses.

## Critical Hypotheses

At least one existing host includes every mandatory service; 20-bay Bronze upgrades <=60,000 PLN; fleets prepay before opening; €16–20 price and 65% utilisation support 25%+ EBITDA; later site finance preserves ownership.

## Kill Criteria

No compliant host option after 200; required bathroom/paving/grid construction; option >8,000 PLN; upgrade quote >60,000 PLN; no two deposits after 40 fleets; deposits <15,000 PLN; total founder opening cash >100,000 PLN; certification denied; site EBITDA <25%; utilisation <50% after six months; no second host option after operating proof.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; transport-property lawyer; independent SSPA pre-auditor; security/lighting integrator; host site-service staff; remote monitoring contractor. Pre-proof <=30,000 PLN; 12-month founder cap <=100,000 PLN.
