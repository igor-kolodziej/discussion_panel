# Round Six: Founder-Acquirable Rights, Designs, and Inventory Flows

**Status:** Generator-only research. These five directions are neither scored nor evaluated, panelled, chatbot-tested, confirmed, approved, or validated. All prices, margins, conversion rates, test outcomes, certification costs, and customer counts are hypotheses until demonstrated by quotes, tests, deposits, and repeat transactions.

## Design Constraint For This Pass

Each direction starts with an asset the founder can create, register, test, reserve, or stock without customer permission. A customer is needed to prove willingness to pay, but not to grant the underlying control point. Each asset is reusable across customers and geographies; none is a serial lease, one-title right, university licence, plugin roll-up, liquidation mandate, local route, or prospectively donated customer-data corpus.

## Direction 1 — PelletLock Disconnect Cell

**Commercial insight.** Plastic-pellet regulation is creating an equipment purchase, not merely a paperwork purchase. Regulation (EU) 2025/2365 applies from five tonnes handled per year and explicitly requires operators to prevent, contain, clean up, physically test prevention procedures, and document equipment. The awkward loss point is the last few seconds of dry-bulk unloading: residual pellets remain when a hose is uncoupled, while today's workaround is purging plus a loose catch pan or tarp. A hands-free, sealed retrofit around the coupling could remove a recurring operator failure rather than add another checklist.

**Wedge and customer.** A two-piece, antistatic disconnect hood clamps around common 3- and 4-inch dry-bulk hose couplings before separation. A flexible seal captures residual pellets into a removable, weighed filter cassette; a mechanical interlock prevents opening until the cassette is fitted. The first customer is a Polish/CEE plastics converter or pellet carrier with frequent silo-truck unloading and a 2027 equipment gap plan. This is a product sale, not an OCS audit or compliance desk.

**Exact paid event within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Spend no more than **32,000 PLN** on coupling measurement, CAD, antistatic material, three CNC/printed prototypes, and 100-cycle bench tests. Demonstrate on clean surrogate pellets at a rented workshop. Obtain two unrelated sites paying **6,000 PLN each** for a 30-day, one-bay pilot, credited against a **15,000–20,000 PLN** production system. The pilot contract specifies no compliance guarantee and compares captured mass, disconnect time, contamination, and operator bypasses against the site's present catch-pan method.

**Foundational resource bootstrap within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** Before customer delivery, the founder owns the CAD, tolerance stack, cassette geometry, test protocol, firmware only if weighing is retained, and brand. After two pilots show a useful effect, spend up to the remaining founder tranche on an EU design filing/novelty review, aluminium or polymer tooling, independent antistatic/pressure/cycle testing, and a prepaid or reserved **500-unit** Polish production window. The 12-month proof is a manufacturable design, test report, owned tool or contractually exclusive tool access, and saleable inventory—not a promised partnership. A mechanical engineer and plastics processor can be paid contractors; their cooperation is purchasable, not existential.

**Control-tier hypothesis.** **Tier 2 after tooling and the repeatable test corpus; Tier 3 today.** It can become a controlled product asset, but not a hard monopoly. The strongest copy risk is an established coupling, containment, or cleaning supplier—such as UltraTech, ENPAC, or SolvAir—adding a similar hood or automatic shutoff in one product cycle. The compounding defence would have to be a compatibility library, documented capture performance across resin/line combinations, cassette reorder history, and distributor-installed base, not the shape alone.

**Conservative founder-wealth bridge and denominator.** Plastics Europe reports more than **60,000 companies** across the European plastics value chain; the regulation covers manufacturers, recyclers, converters, stockists, cleaners, and carriers above its threshold. A Year-10 hypothesis of **3,500 systems/year at €2,000** plus **18,000 cassette/service packs at €160** gives **€9.88m revenue**. At **52% gross margin, 24% operating margin**, and a **7× operating-profit** value, the business is worth about **€16.6m**; **70% founder ownership** after contractors, working-capital debt, and limited dilution is about **€11.6m founder equity**. This uses 3,500 annual systems, below 6% of the cited 60,000-company population before non-EU expansion, but the relevant number of unloading points—not companies—is the decision-critical denominator still to verify. While employed, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested and no distribution is assumed in this equity figure.

**Fatal dependencies and kill tests.** Kill if (1) fewer than two coupling families cover 60% of observed CEE unloading events; (2) capture is below 90% of disconnect loss or adds more than ten seconds; (3) the seal causes contamination, static, blockage, or unsafe pressure retention; (4) two sites will not pay for pilots after a live bench demo; (5) freedom-to-operate identifies blocking claims; or (6) a current EU supplier already sells a functionally equivalent low-cost disconnect cell. The current official regulation names measures and equipment but does **not** prescribe this product, so mandatory demand must not be claimed.

**Key current sources.** [Regulation (EU) 2025/2365](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2365/oj), [European Commission implementation summary](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/plastics/microplastics_en), [Plastics Europe industry denominator](https://plasticseurope.org/about-us/membership/), [OCS best-practice examples](https://www.opcleansweep.eu/download_file/view/103/219/), [UltraTech competitive containment range](https://plasticpelletcontainment.com/), [SolvAir truck-cleaning competitor](https://solvair.co.uk/vehicle-blowing-systems/truck-blow-off-system-tbos/).

## Direction 2 — FlatSafe P908 LMT Battery Return Pack

**Commercial insight.** E-bike and light-mobility repair shops increasingly possess defective batteries they cannot place in ordinary parcel packaging, but premium rigid hazardous-goods cases are bulky and priced for repeated industrial use. The European market sold **5.1 million e-bikes in 2023**, and the Batteries Regulation makes producers responsible for free collection arrangements and suitable transport containers while raising LMT collection obligations. The gap may be a certified, ship-flat, one-use or limited-reuse P908 pack for a narrowly declared family of non-critically damaged sub-1-kWh LMT batteries. It must never be marketed for batteries liable to rapid disassembly or fire; those remain P911/LP906 territory.

**Wedge and customer.** A standard outer pack plus keyed mineral-fibre/intumescent inserts in three dimensional families, tamper-evident condition triage, absorbent, terminal isolation, and assembly proof photos. The first buyers are e-bike service chains, battery distributors, and producer-responsibility/recycling operators that currently dispatch rigid boxes or improvise case by case. The product is the approved packaging design and stocked kit, not dangerous-goods advice.

**Exact paid event within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Cap pre-proof spend at **38,000 PLN** for an ADR packaging adviser, pack architecture, blank samples, material fire data, drop/stack prototype tests, and a written quote and test plan from an authorised packaging test body. No defective battery is transported. Obtain two unrelated service/distribution buyers each placing a **6,000 PLN conditional deposit** for the first 50 packs at a target **€95–€125 per pack**, refundable only if the declared P908 design approval is not achieved by the agreed date. The exact paid proof is the deposit against a named approved product, not a free interview or consultancy.

**Foundational resource bootstrap within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** The founder owns the pack drawings, insert dies, bill of materials, assembly instructions, hazard-declaration boundary, test file, brand, and any design registration. Exercise post-deposit capital only if a competent testing/approval route for the exact pack design is quoted within the remaining **62,000 PLN**. Reserve corrugated converting and insert capacity through a prepaid first lot; stock only the certified configuration. The 12-month proof is a valid design-specific test/approval file, owned dies, first compliant lot, and traceable sales. A dangerous-goods safety adviser and authorised lab are ordinary paid suppliers, not speculative commercial partners.

**Control-tier hypothesis.** **Tier 2 if the test/approval file, dies, declared-use envelope, and replenishment inventory are controlled; Tier 3 before approval.** The strongest copy risk is ZARGES, DGM-style dangerous-goods packagers, or a national packaging laboratory releasing its own cheaper LMT format. Approval narrows copy but does not prevent a rival from independently testing another design.

**Conservative founder-wealth bridge and denominator.** CONEBI reports **5.1m European e-bike sales in 2023**, while JRC notes typical e-bike battery life around nine years; this creates a large future return cohort but does not prove the defect-shipment rate. A Year-11 hypothesis of **120,000 packs/year at €105** yields **€12.6m revenue**. At **42% gross margin, 18% operating margin**, and **7× operating profit**, value is about **€15.9m**; **70% founder ownership** after manufacturing finance and limited dilution is about **€11.1m**. The volume equals 2.35% of one year's cited European e-bike unit sales before power-tool, stationary-storage, and non-EU adjacencies. That ratio is a sizing check, not evidence that 2.35% will require P908 shipping. During employment, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested; the equity value does not also count retained cash as a distribution.

**Fatal dependencies and kill tests.** Kill if (1) authorised test plus approval exceeds the 100,000 PLN first-cycle cap; (2) the approval or production mark cannot legally follow the founder-controlled design/contract manufacturer; (3) fewer than 2% of service batteries require a paid P908 shipment rather than local collection; (4) landed unit cost exceeds €65 at a €105 selling price; (5) buyers will not deposit before certification despite a lab-approved test plan; (6) pack assembly creates frequent misclassification or unsafe user error; or (7) existing approved packs at comparable price and footprint already solve the problem. Do not broaden into P911 without a separate test, competent-authority route, and capital decision.

**Key current sources.** [EU Batteries Regulation 2023/1542](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj), [JRC work on portable/LMT battery collection](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC137863), [CONEBI 2023 e-bike market data](https://www.conebi.eu/pr-conebi-bimp-2024/), [UNECE 2025 P911 conditions paper, clarifying the harder critical-battery boundary](https://unece.org/sites/default/files/2025-01/ECE-TRANS-WP.15-AC1-2025-11E.pdf), [ZARGES approved battery-case range](https://www.zarges.com/uk/products/battery-case/battery-case-40582).

## Direction 3 — MethaneCheck Traceable Reference-Leak Kit

**Commercial insight.** Methane rules are moving from estimated factors to measured, verifier-reviewed evidence. EU Regulation 2024/1787 sets LDAR thresholds including **1 g/h, 5 g/h, and 17 g/h**, requires device capability/limitations and supporting data, and obliges operators to retain records. Yet an LDAR crew can arrive with a sophisticated camera or sniffer and no quick, independent demonstration of its detection performance under that day's wind, distance, and thermal background. A compact reference leak is potentially the physical equivalent of a calibration weight: it challenges the whole measurement workflow rather than trusting a self-test.

**Wedge and customer.** A rugged, non-ATEX reference station used only in a designated non-hazardous verification area. Certified methane or safe surrogate gas passes through traceable micro-orifice cartridges at regulation-relevant rates; temperature, pressure, wind, background, distance, and operator result are recorded into a signed verification receipt. The first customer is an independent LDAR contractor or gas network training centre, not a regulator. The device does not certify legal compliance or quantify live leaks.

**Exact paid event within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Spend at most **39,000 PLN** on a gas/metrology safety review, regulator/orifice prototypes, enclosure, sensors, and calibration of three cartridges by an accredited flow laboratory. Operate only at a controlled outdoor training location. Obtain two unrelated LDAR contractors each paying a **10,000 PLN purchase deposit** for a **€5,000–€7,000** kit, conditional on a witnessed blind challenge in which their normal instrument detects the declared 1/5/17 g/h points within the jointly specified operating envelope. The deposits fund the engineering lot; they do not purchase an advisory report.

**Foundational resource bootstrap within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** The founder owns enclosure/flow-path design, cartridge geometry, firmware, calibration transformations, signed-receipt schema, blind-test protocol, serial database, and brand. After deposits, buy an HSM/signing setup, commission independent calibration and safety documentation, reserve a 50-unit precision-machining/assembly lot, and stock traceable cartridges. The 12-month proof is repeatability and drift data across 100 cycles, independent certificates, two delivered kits, and a recalibration/replacement interval. Accredited calibration and machining are purchasable services; the business does not require an oil major to donate site data.

**Control-tier hypothesis.** **Tier 2 after traceable cartridges, calibration history, firmware, and an installed recalibration cycle; Tier 3 today.** The strongest copy risk is FLIR/Opgal, a calibration-gas company, or a methane test-centre bundling a reference source with existing instruments. The physical orifice alone is weak; defensibility would have to compound through audited field envelopes, cross-instrument recipes, signed serial history, and distributor access.

**Conservative founder-wealth bridge and denominator.** UNEP reports **153 OGMP 2.0 member companies** covering 42% of global oil and gas production, 80% of LNG flows, and 20% of global gas transmission/distribution pipelines. These companies contain many assets and contractor crews; company count therefore understates possible kits but does not prove crew count. A Year-10 hypothesis of **2,000 kits/year at €6,000** plus **4,000 installed kits receiving €1,200/year cartridges/calibration** gives **€16.8m revenue**. At **55% gross margin, 23% operating margin**, and **7× operating profit**, value is about **€27.0m**; **65% founder ownership** after product-liability capital, global distribution finance, and dilution is about **€17.6m**. The bridge needs an average of roughly 13 new kits per cited OGMP company per year if only those companies bought—plausible only if multi-asset contractor crews truly own separate kits, which is a primary discovery question. During employment, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested and no retained earnings are double-counted as distributions.

**Fatal dependencies and kill tests.** Kill if (1) daily/routine external field verification is not valued beyond manufacturer maintenance; (2) two contractors refuse deposits even after a traceable blind demo; (3) 1 g/h stability cannot remain within ±10% across transport and temperature; (4) gas handling makes ATEX/site permission or dangerous-goods logistics unavoidable for normal use; (5) calibration/replacement cost exceeds 20% of selling price yearly; or (6) instrument makers already supply a functionally equivalent traceable challenge. Official rules require supporting capability data but do **not** currently mandate this kit; that distinction is existential.

**Key current sources.** [EU Methane Regulation 2024/1787](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1787/oj), [European Commission methane implementation page](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/carbon-management-and-fossil-fuels/methane-emissions_en), [UNEP OGMP 2.0 denominator](https://methanedata.unep.org/frequently-asked-questions), [US EPA OGI performance protocol](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P101769L.txt), [FLIR QL320 competitive system](https://www.flir.com/en-gb/products/ql320/), [Opgal built-in calibration competitor](https://www.opgal.com/eyecgas-long-range/).

## Direction 4 — PalletCincher Reusable Mixed-Load Wrap

**Commercial insight.** Regulation (EU) 2025/40 requires at least 40% reusable transport packaging from 2030 and expressly includes pallet wrapping and straps. A February 2026 Commission decision exempted pallet wrappings/straps from certain 100% reuse cases because full conversion can impose disproportionate adaptation costs, but it left them inside the overall 40% rule. The neglected buyer is a high-mix manufacturer whose changing load heights make rigid pallet collars awkward and whose return lane cannot justify automated stretch-wrap replacement. The desired outcome is secure mixed loads with low worker effort and a simple counted-reuse record—not a sustainability dashboard.

**Wedge and customer.** A repairable textile cincher in two EPAL-compatible footprints uses sliding vertical compression webs, replaceable abrasion corners, serialized QR, and one-motion tension indication. Unlike a loose reusable blanket, it is designed for repeatable load restraint on irregular cartons and a fast flat return. The first beachhead is Polish FMCG/industrial plants moving pallets between their own factory, warehouse, and co-packer on closed routes. The family renewable installer can provide only packaging-handling observations; both paid buyers must be unrelated.

**Exact paid event within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Risk no more than **28,000 PLN** on patterns, 100 sewn prototypes, buckle/webbing tests, and a 50-cycle lane simulation. Obtain two unrelated plants each prepaying **5,000 PLN** for 50 units at a target **€80–€100**, conditional on agreed braking/tilt, damage, wrapping-time, loss-rate, and 50-return-cycle results. The paid event is a product preorder after physical demonstration, not a reuse assessment.

**Foundational resource bootstrap within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** The founder owns patterns, registered design if clear, tension indicator, repair specification, cycle-test corpus, serial schema, bill of materials, and brand. After paid proof, commission independent load-stability testing, purchase cutting dies/jigs, reserve **5,000 units** of sewing capacity through a prepaid Polish/CEE contract-manufacturing slot, and hold standard buckle/webbing safety stock. The 12-month proof is a tested production specification, owned tooling, 1,000 saleable units, and measured loss/damage on two closed lanes. No logistics operator partnership or pooled asset network is required; buyers own and return their wraps.

**Control-tier hypothesis.** **Tier 2/weak after design, tests, tools, and replenishment history; Tier 3 today.** The strongest copy risk is REUSA-WRAPS, PalletWrapz, a textile converter, or a packaging major adding the sliding compression web. The design can be copied with one supplier contract, so a viable defence must come from verified irregular-load recipes, lowest-damage performance, rapid repair parts, procurement distribution, and switching of installed operating procedures.

**Conservative founder-wealth bridge and denominator.** EPAL reports about **670m Euro pallets in circulation** and more than **100m new EPAL pallets** produced in each of 2021 and 2022. Wrap demand is not one-for-one with pallets, but the pool establishes the physical denominator. A Year-10 hypothesis of **150,000 wraps/year at €80** plus **400,000 installed units buying €8/year replacement parts/identity service** produces **€15.2m revenue**. At **44% gross margin, 18% operating margin**, and **7× operating profit**, value is about **€19.2m**; **65% founder ownership** after working-capital debt and manufacturing dilution is about **€12.5m**. Annual unit sales equal 0.15% of the cited 100m-new-pallet benchmark, but the real denominator is closed-loop pallet positions, so lane counts must replace this proxy before advancement. During employment, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested.

**Fatal dependencies and kill tests.** Kill if (1) freedom-to-operate conflicts with REUSA-WRAPS or other claims; (2) damage rate is worse than stretch film or wrap loss exceeds 3% per 50 cycles; (3) worker application takes more than 45 seconds or ergonomics are poor; (4) independent restraint tests fail the selected load classes; (5) landed cost exceeds €45 at an €80 price; (6) two plants will not preorder after a physical trial; or (7) buyers satisfy the 40% total reuse requirement more cheaply through pallets/crates and have no incentive to change wrap. The 2026 exemption weakens urgency and must be treated as adverse evidence, not hidden.

**Key current sources.** [PPWR Regulation (EU) 2025/40, Article 29](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj), [Commission Delegated Decision (EU) 2026/429 on pallet wrap/strap exemptions](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_del/2026/429/oj), [EPAL installed-pool denominator](https://www.epal-pallets.org/eu-en/the-success-system/about-epal), [EPAL production benchmark](https://www.epal-pallets.org/fileadmin/user_upload/ntg_package/images/news/EPAL-PressRelease_Statistics-2022_EN.pdf), [REUSA-WRAPS direct competitor](https://reusawraps.com/), [PalletWrapz direct competitor](https://www.palletwrapz.com/).

## Direction 5 — PatchKey Offline Secure-Update Module

**Commercial insight.** The Cyber Resilience Act obliges manufacturers of connected hardware/software to handle vulnerabilities throughout a support period, make security updates available, and meet reporting rules from September 2026 and full requirements from December 2027. Large connected-device fleets can buy cloud OTA platforms; low-volume industrial machine OEMs often update in a maintenance window using a laptop, SD card, or USB and do not want a machine reachable from the cloud. Their real need is not generic CRA advice. It is an auditable, fail-safe, offline path that proves a field machine accepted only an authorised, non-rollback update without redesigning the whole controller.

**Wedge and customer.** A small supervisor board and service key sit between the service port and supported MCU/controller families. The board verifies signed bundles in a secure element, controls reset/programming access, records anti-rollback counters, performs A/B or recovery logic where the target permits, and emits a signed service receipt. Initial compatibility targets one narrow architecture used by Polish/CEE agricultural, food-processing, or construction-equipment OEMs. It does not claim to make the full machine CRA-compliant.

**Exact paid event within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Cap founder risk at **35,000 PLN** for two evaluation boards, secure elements, bootloader/provisioning firmware, HSM tenancy, a contractor threat review, and a failure-injection demo. Obtain two unrelated low-volume machine OEMs paying **8,000 PLN each** for a fixed integration pack on an existing development board, fully credited against the first 250 modules. Delivery is a working signed-update path and recovery demonstration, not a compliance report. The founder remains employed and confines live work to supplied development hardware.

**Foundational resource bootstrap within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** The founder owns the supervisor-board design, bootloader/host code, provisioning pipeline, signing policy, failure-test suite, compatibility packs, update receipt schema, and brand. After paid proof, commission a focused penetration test, reserve a **1,000-unit** SMT lot, own the test jig, and provision the first inventory under founder-controlled keys. Commercial secure elements and MCUs are intentionally replaceable inputs; no exclusive chip-vendor partnership is assumed. The 12-month proof is two unrelated paid integrations, 1,000 fault-injection cycles without an unrecoverable brick, an independent security report, founder-controlled HSM keys, and saleable modules.

**Control-tier hypothesis.** **Tier 2 after audited code, compatibility packs, provisioning history, test jig, keys, and installed modules; Tier 3 today.** The strongest copy risk is Mender, Microchip/NXP/ST, Memfault, or an embedded distributor bundling a similar offline path. The chip is not the moat. Only fast integration across neglected low-volume boards, field-failure evidence, long-lived key custody, and recurring module placement could compound.

**Conservative founder-wealth bridge and denominator.** VDMA alone represents about **3,600 primarily medium-sized mechanical-engineering companies**; CEMA represents about 1,300 agricultural-machine manufacturers and CECE about 1,200 construction-equipment companies, with overlap, so they must not simply be added. A Year-10 hypothesis of **250 OEM platforms paying €15,000 recurring**, **50 new platform integrations/year at €25,000**, and **60,000 modules/year at €80** gives **€9.8m revenue**. At **65% gross margin, 27% operating margin**, and **7× operating profit**, value is about **€18.5m**; **65% founder ownership** after security hires and international expansion is about **€12.0m**. The 250-OEM installed base equals 6.9% of the non-additive 3,600-company VDMA denominator before other European/global equipment sectors; the module assumption averages 240 units per installed OEM per year. Both ratios require direct production-volume evidence. While employed, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested and distributions are not added to this equity value.

**Fatal dependencies and kill tests.** Kill if (1) the module cannot secure programming access without a target-board redesign; (2) fewer than three controller families cover a commercially useful set of low-volume OEMs; (3) integration exceeds five engineer-days per product; (4) two unrelated OEMs will not pay after a fault-injection demo; (5) an open Mender/Microchip stack plus ordinary distributor support performs the same job at lower total cost; (6) independent testing finds a key-extraction, rollback, or recovery flaw; or (7) product liability and ten-year key/support obligations destroy contribution margin. CRA demand is broad, but the product must win on engineering time and field reliability, not regulatory fear.

## Generator Takeaway

**PelletLock is the cleanest new asset-first direction for downstream scrutiny** because the regulation explicitly reaches equipment and physical testing, the product can be prototyped without customer data or authority, and the first resource can become owned tooling plus inventory. Its weakness is equally clear: the control point is only Tier 2 and may be copied rapidly. FlatSafe has the strongest formal design-file control but the largest approval-cost uncertainty. MethaneCheck has attractive recurring calibration economics but must prove that a non-mandated workflow is worth paying for. PalletCincher has a vast physical denominator but weak IP and adverse 2026 exemption evidence. PatchKey best fits the founder's technical profile but faces formidable incumbents and long support liabilities.
