## Evidence That Changes the Search

- Poland's mandatory KSeF has created a standardized B2B invoice source. The Ministry of Finance says KSeF 2.0 began in February 2026, became mandatory for most remaining firms in April, and permits a taxpayer to grant another entity access. That makes customer-authorized purchase-history ingestion possible; it does **not** by itself create rights to use, aggregate, or resell the data. Those rights require an explicit customer contract. [Official rollout](https://www.gov.pl/web/priorytety/p35), [official access scope](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/informacje-ogolne-ksef-20/zakres-obowiazkowego-ksef/)
- European B2C e-commerce turnover reached €842 billion in 2024; Central Europe reached €86 billion and Eastern Europe €20 billion. This establishes a large underlying transaction base, not the size or recoverability of returns. [Ecommerce Europe / EuroCommerce 2025 report release](https://ecommerce-europe.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Press-Release-2025-European-E-commerce-Report.pdf)
- Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 requires larger operators to disclose discarded unsold products and, from 19 July 2026, prohibits destruction of specified categories subject to exemptions. The 2026 implementing rules require supporting delivery and reception documentation to be retained for five years. This creates pressure for auditable recovery routes, although the initial destruction ban is category-limited and must not be generalized to all bulky goods. [Regulation text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0296), [2026 disclosure format](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0002)
- Existing purchasing groups are not hypothetical. VetFamily reports more than 10,000 veterinary clinics in fourteen countries; National Eyecare Group/CECOP reports 8,500 optical practices; care-home procurement groups and parcel aggregators already market pooled terms. These facts invalidate a generic “small buyers deserve big-buyer pricing” thesis in those sectors unless a new control point is materially different. [VetFamily](https://vetfamily.com/about/), [National Eyecare Group](https://www.nationaleyecare.co.uk/), [Collective Purchasing for care homes](https://www.collectivepurchasing.co.uk/care-homes/), [Sendcloud rates](https://sendcloud.dev/docs/shipping/shipping-rates/)

## Direction 42 — ReturnRoute: CEE Bulky-Returns Clearing Rail — retain for focused research

### Customer-inside insight

A mid-market furniture, home-and-garden, or bulky-appliance merchant does not primarily want the theoretically highest auction price for every return. It wants a predictable weekly removal cadence, no channel conflict, documented downstream disposition, and a net recovery figure after inspection and transport. Professional refurbishers and regional resellers do not want mystery pallets; they want actual condition evidence, a reliable manifest, and a pickup route whose logistics do not erase the margin.

### Evidence versus hypothesis

- **Evidence:** B-Stock already operates European liquidation marketplaces and explicitly sells customer returns, undelivered orders, and excess stock. Its presence proves transaction demand and creates a formidable copy/distribution risk. [B-Stock Europe](https://bstock.com/auctions/europe-2/), [B-Stock Supply Europe](https://bstock.com/supplystoreeurope/about/)
- **Evidence:** The EU's unsold-goods disclosure regime increases the value of documented downstream disposition for covered larger operators. It does not prove that CEE bulky-return merchants will grant exclusivity.
- **Hypothesis:** B-Stock's pallet/truckload auction model leaves an exploitable CEE gap for route-aware batches of bulky items held at merchant facilities, with same-week buyer pickup, retailer-specific channel restrictions, and lot-level inspection evidence.

### Paid proof inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN

Sign one six-month exclusive sales-agency mandate over a named merchant's recurring stream of at least 30 bulky returns per month. The mandate must give ReturnRoute the sole right to clear that stream, use anonymized condition/price/outcome data, impose destination restrictions, and collect a commission only after settlement. Recruit ten verified professional buyers and complete one paid lot sale with buyer funds collected through a licensed marketplace-payment provider and pickup performed by the buyer. Pre-proof spend is capped at 25,000 PLN: legal/agency and data-rights terms 8,000; independent inspection and photography 8,000; buyer verification, insurance advice, and minimal listing tooling 9,000. The founder does not take inventory title, lease storage, or fund transport.

### Foundational resource by month 12 and 100,000 PLN

The required resource is three rolling exclusive seller feeds, 100 verified professional buyers with deposits or transaction history, and the non-public SKU/condition/route/clearing-price dataset created by completed trades. It is secured through signed mandates and actual routed settlements, not an informal partnership. An unknown founder can ask for it because the merchant pays only from incremental recovery and receives a defined removal SLA; the buyer receives evidence and does its own pickup. Proof before serious capital is three independent seller streams, repeat purchases by at least 20 buyers, positive contribution after inspection/payment costs, and measurable improvement in days-to-clear or net recovery versus each seller's baseline.

### Founder-wealth shape

Illustrative conservative scale: €100 million annual recovered-goods transaction value at a 7% take rate produces €7 million revenue. At a 35% operating margin, €2.45 million EBITDA valued at 8× gives €19.6 million enterprise value; with no net debt and 65% founder ownership after dilution, founder equity is about €12.7 million. This requires a pan-European rather than Polish-only clearing network, but €100 million is roughly 0.012% of the documented €842 billion European e-commerce turnover; the missing fact is the eligible bulky-return recovery pool, not the top-down commerce base. During employment, 95% of after-tax distributable cash would be reinvested and optional founder distributions capped at 5%.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

Strongest copy risk: B-Stock, a large retailer, or a reverse-logistics provider can add inspection and route-aware lots. The only plausible defense is a dense CEE professional-buyer network, exclusive recurring feeds, and superior condition-to-net-recovery data. Fatal dependencies are failure to obtain a rolling seller mandate, buyer pickup defaults, misgrading liability, or evidence that transport costs make the narrow bulky category less profitable than ordinary pallet liquidation.

## Direction 43 — BatchWindow: Take-or-Pay Shelf-Stable Co-Packing Slots — retain for focused research

### Customer-inside insight

Small food brands say they need “a co-packer,” but the deeper conflict is that certified manufacturers dislike unprepared micro-clients, uncertain forecasts, packaging changes, and disruptive changeovers. The brand wants a production date it can promise to retail; the plant wants a technically complete batch and money committed before it schedules the line. A broker that merely introduces them does not control anything. A clearing company that options a homogeneous production window and fills it with deposit-backed, technically prequalified batches controls a paid capacity right and the transaction.

### Evidence versus hypothesis

- **Evidence:** European co-packers advertise both overflow production and low-MOQ runs. Viscofill advertises runs from 100 units and service across the EU; The Good Food Company advertises a 500-unit starting MOQ and four-to-eight-week lead time. [Viscofill](https://viscofill.eu/en/), [The Good Food Company](https://thegoodfood.be/)
- **Evidence:** Wonnda already claims a marketplace covering 25,000+ brands and retailers, while Agilery offers co-manufacturer search and implementation. A directory, RFQ workflow, or sourcing consultancy is therefore not a gap. [Wonnda](https://wonnda.com/), [Agilery](https://agileryfood.com/en/our-offer/manufacturing-and-operations/)
- **Hypothesis:** Neither discovery marketplace nor direct low-MOQ plant solves the specific transaction: a reserved shared-format window, funded by several brand deposits, with one packaging format, allergen regime, and documented production-readiness standard.

### Paid proof inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN

Narrow the first window to one low-risk, shelf-stable liquid format—for example, ambient-fill sauces in one jar and closure specification, excluding cold-chain, novel foods, supplements, and high-risk allergens. Obtain a written option on one named plant window for no more than 15,000 PLN, conditional on independent food-law/quality review. Collect deposits from at least three unrelated brands that together cover the option, plant deposit, and a positive transaction margin; then execute one shared-format production day. Total founder risk before deposits is capped at 35,000 PLN for food-law advice, specification templates, plant pre-audit, sampling, and the capacity option. The plant remains manufacturer of record and each brand remains responsible for an approved formula and label.

### Foundational resource by month 12 and 100,000 PLN

The resource is a portfolio of assignable or nominated take-or-pay production-window rights, standard technical-intake specifications, brand deposits, and non-public actual data on changeover time, rejection causes, yield, lead time, and reorder behavior. The first proof is one executed window with three brands; twelve-month proof is ten executed windows, at least five reordering brands, two certified plants able to accept the same format, and positive margin after quality and coordination costs. An unknown founder can acquire the first right because a plant receives one prepared counterparty and committed money for otherwise uncertain capacity, not a request for speculative cooperation.

### Founder-wealth shape

Illustrative scale: €200 million of annual routed production value at an 8% blended booking/quality/transaction fee yields €16 million revenue. At a 30% operating margin, €4.8 million EBITDA valued at 9× gives €43.2 million enterprise value; 65% founder ownership is about €28 million. The route requires expansion across repeated standardized formats and several EU plants; it does not require owning factories. During employment, 95% of after-tax distributable cash would be reinvested and distributions capped at 5%.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

Strongest copy risk: Wonnda, Agilery, or a large co-packer can add reservation and deposit mechanics. Defense must come from superior actual run data, repeat brand demand, standardized batch-readiness, and contracted capacity. Fatal dependencies are manufacturer unwillingness to grant a real window right, food-safety or recall liability leaking to the platform, insufficient formula/packaging homogeneity to share runs, or deposits failing to cover committed capacity without founder working capital.

## Direction 44 — CleanKilo: Commercial-Laundry Input Purchasing and Outcome Rail — hold for customer evidence

### Customer-inside insight

An independent industrial laundry does not actually buy detergent; it buys an acceptable cost per clean kilogram with controlled rewash, textile damage, energy, and water. Supplier-owned dosing equipment and non-comparable chemistry make invoice-price comparisons misleading. A purchasing network could route chemicals and linen purchases while building a private, normalized cost-per-clean-kilogram and rewash dataset.

### Evidence versus hypothesis

- **Evidence:** The Foundation of Polish Laundries says it represents about 130 laundries, providing a reachable public prospect universe but also a group that could copy the idea. [Foundation of Polish Laundries](https://polskiepralnie.pl/en/glowna-english/)
- **Evidence:** Ecolab's own directions state that commercial dosing varies by soil, fabric, and water conditions and is set on site by its specialist. That confirms the outcome is site-specific and makes a simple price club unsafe. [Ecolab dosing directions](https://dotcom.ecolab.com/offerings/inst/home-style-laundry-program-for-facilities/home-style-liquid-laundry-detergent)
- **Hypothesis:** Ten Polish laundries will authorize KSeF purchase-history ingestion, share production/rewash data, and commit one category of spend to a neutral tender in exchange for verified cost-per-clean-kilogram savings.

### Paid proof inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN

Obtain six-month data authorizations from five laundries, run two independent chemistry trials with a qualified laundry technologist, and collect prepaid orders or paid participation fees from at least three laundries before signing a supply framework. Cap founder risk at 35,000 PLN for data/security terms, the technologist, samples, and lab checks. Do not install or finance dosing hardware before paid proof.

### Foundational resource by month 12 and 100,000 PLN

Secure twelve-month exclusive purchasing mandates for one input category from ten laundries, a supplier framework with rebates paid only on routed orders, and rights to aggregate anonymized outcome data. The unknown founder sells measurable net savings and independence rather than software; a contracted technologist supplies domain credibility. Proof is repeat routed orders plus no deterioration in rewash or damage. No site equipment is owned.

### Founder-wealth shape

Illustrative EU scale: €500 million routed annual input spend at a 5% net take yields €25 million revenue; 30% operating margin produces €7.5 million EBITDA. At 8× and 60% founder ownership, founder equity is about €36 million. This requires expansion far beyond the 130-member Polish prospect pool and is not supported by a current estimate of eligible European spend.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

The Foundation, Ecolab/Diversey, or an existing distributor can create pooled terms; switching may require site-specific dosing changes. Fatal evidence would be that laundries refuse outcome-data sharing, supplier contracts prohibit neutral equipment, or measured savings disappear after rewash, textile damage, and technician costs.

- **Hypothesis:** Polish funeral homes have enough hidden net-price dispersion and supplier dissatisfaction to assign recurring spend to a neutral third party.

### Paid proof inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN

Obtain invoice-data authorizations and twelve-month purchasing mandates from ten homes, then collect prepaid orders for one standardized accessory category before placing a pooled order. Spend no more than 25,000 PLN on legal/data terms, samples, and buyer outreach. Avoid coffins initially because delivery reliability and customization dominate price.

### Foundational resource by month 12 and 100,000 PLN

The proposed resource is 30 exclusive category mandates, routed payments, and non-public price/reorder data, followed by one private-label accessory reserved by deposit. An unknown founder can offer secrecy and savings, but has no founder-specific trust bridge into this relationship-heavy trade.

The model is arithmetically large enough but current evidence does not show a credible path from Polish accessories to 5,000 European homes.
