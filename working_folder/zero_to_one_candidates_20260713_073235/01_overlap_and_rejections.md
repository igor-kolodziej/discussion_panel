## Why Afterline Is Materially Different

Afterline touches industrial spares and owner succession, but it is not an obsolete-part pool and not generic succession software. The company does not buy loose parts or sell advisory work. It acquires or exclusively licenses the complete aftermarket position of one active installed base: technical files, service mark, tooling, spare inventory, customer-service records, transferable service contracts, and the seller's right to communicate the continuity offer. It closes only after the signed option has produced prepaid installed-base demand. The buyer, paid-proof mechanism, and control point therefore differ materially from prior spare pools and retiring-software concepts.

## Why MouldRelay Is Only Provisionally Retained

MouldRelay acquires named physical tools and assigned reorder rights, not generic salvage. Its risk is that customers may redraw parts or move tools, so the evaluator must decide whether title plus assigned reorder history creates enough durability.

## Why PlantExit Is Only Provisionally Retained

PlantExit obtains an executed exclusive sale mandate over a named plant's MRO inventory and equipment rather than manually sourcing commodity stock. Its risk is episodic supply and incumbent auction-house trust; the evaluator must treat those as possible vetoes.

## Reconsideration Rule

A prior family may re-enter only with a materially different buyer, urgent trigger, paid acquisition mechanism, and owned, assigned, optioned, prepaid, or mandatory control point. Cosmetic product changes do not qualify.
