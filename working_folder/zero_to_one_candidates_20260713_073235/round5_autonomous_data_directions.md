## Design Constraint for This Pass

This pass deliberately excludes every shape whose initial resource appears only after a seller, authority, licence owner, partner, or customer grants access. Each direction below begins with an asset the founder can lawfully create from official/open data, code, or purchased commodity samples before the first sales call. A buyer is needed to prove willingness to pay, but not to make the initial asset exist.

The directions also exclude the current run's dead families: service/evidence desks, tender or compliance casework, prospective customer-contributed corpora, serial licences, asset roll-ups, local leases, university technology licences, generic dashboards/CRMs, and audience-led distribution.

All prices, margins, customer counts, valuation multiples, test performance, and sales cycles are explicit hypotheses. Wealth arithmetic uses business equity only and does not double-count retained cash as founder distributions. While the founder remains employed, conservative and expected cases assume 95% reinvestment of after-tax distributable cash and no more than 5% optional distributions; strong cases assume 100% reinvestment.

## Direction 1 — FeedRule CI: Executable EU Feed-Additive Release Rules

### Customer-inside insight

A feed formulator does not want another searchable register. Immediately before releasing a premix or compound-feed formula, the formulator needs a deterministic answer to: **is this exact additive, dose, species, age, use and date allowed, and which authorisation clause proves it?** The operational loss is a blocked batch, relabelling, customer rejection or recall. The Commission's register is authoritative and regularly updated, but it is organised as a searchable register linked to individual authorisation regulations, not as a version-controlled test suite that can run inside formulation software.

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Create an owned, versioned rules graph and API from the Commission's public Feed Additives Register, linked regulations, archived expired authorisations and official species/category terms. Every rule becomes an executable test with effective dates, maximum/minimum content, animal species/category, age, conditions, incompatibilities and source paragraph. Preserve every change and a regression-test corpus. The founder can compile the first 300 high-use additive authorisations and 1,500 rule tests without a mill, additive maker or software vendor. The public facts remain copyable; the owned asset is the normalized history, executable interpretations, test harness, citations and integration behavior.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Spend ceiling before paid proof: 30,000 PLN: data extraction and legal review 9,000; feed-formulation domain contractor 8,000; API/test harness 8,000; outbound and contingency 5,000.
- By day 45, publish a non-customer-dependent blind benchmark of 100 deliberately difficult formula/date cases against manual register research.
- Directly approach Polish/Czech/German independent premix makers, feed consultants and formulation-software vendors. Offer a paid 10,000–20,000 PLN **shadow release check** on a fixed batch of formulas; the customer can redact commercial quantities and ingredient names outside the checked additives.
- Paid proof is one prepaid shadow check or API integration, followed by a documented discrepancy caught before release. No partnership, certification or exclusive data right is a precondition.

### Founder-wealth shape

- **Conservative, year 12–14:** 250 mills, premix groups and software vendors at €20,000 ARR = €5.0m revenue; 82% gross and 40% operating margin = €2.0m EBITDA; €0.8m cumulative outside capital; founder 75%; 7x EBITDA = €14m business value and about €10.5m founder equity.
- **Expected, year 10–12:** 450 accounts at €25,000 = €11.25m revenue; 86% gross and 45% operating margin = €5.06m EBITDA; €2m growth capital; founder 70%; 8x = €40.5m value and €28.4m founder equity.
- **Strong, year 8–10:** 800 accounts/API contracts at €35,000 = €28m revenue; 88% gross and 48% operating margin = €13.4m EBITDA; founder 60% after €6m capital; 8x = €107m value and €64m founder equity.

The conservative case clears the wealth gate only narrowly and therefore requires evidence that at least 250 economically independent accounts exist and will pay €20,000 annually. It does not require a dominant share of all EU feed establishments, but the account and price assumptions must be verified before paneling.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

- **Strongest copy risk:** incumbent feed-formulation software can encode the same public rules; Magnifeed already claims real-time European formula/label verification.
- The advantage must be source-level auditability, historical effective-date replay, API latency and a growing regression suite—not a prettier search screen.
- Fatal if the Commission's online register already exposes the needed machine-readable rules cleanly enough that a vendor can reproduce the product through routine engineering, if experts cannot agree on deterministic interpretations, or if fewer than 30 qualified targets accept discovery and none prepays.

### Funnel disposition

**Retain for advisory evaluation.** It is the cleanest fully autonomous control point in this pass, but incumbent-copy risk and the conservative pricing/account count are decision-critical.

## Direction 2 — HaulGraph PL: Abnormal-Load Route Preflight API

### Customer-inside insight

Heavy-haul operators and wind/construction project teams do not initially need a permit application service; before quoting or committing cranes, escorts and delivery dates, they need to know which corridors are obviously impossible and where a field survey or road-manager conversation is worth buying. Today the carrier must cross-reference bridge lists, live works, statutory clearances, road geometry and multiple maps. A bad early route assumption can make a quote unprofitable or strand a load.

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Build a Poland-first constraint graph from GUGiK's freely downloadable LiDAR/NMT/NMPT, orthophotos and BDOT10k; GDDKiA's restricted-bridge list, live road disruptions and published abnormal-load requirements; public road geometry; and an articulated-vehicle swept-path solver. The initial owned asset is a timestamped 3D clearance/turn/grade/known-load constraint layer for two high-value wind or industrial corridors plus reproducible route-test artifacts. No road-manager approval is needed to create the preflight asset. The product must say **preflight, not permit** and expose source age and uncertainty.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Pre-proof spend ceiling: 34,000 PLN: geospatial compute/data work 10,000; swept-path engineer review 8,000; 200 km reference corridor and spot-check travel 6,000; product/API 6,000; outbound/legal/insurance 4,000.
- By day 60, independently reproduce 20 known obstruction/clearance cases and publish false-positive/false-negative results.
- Prospect Polish project-cargo carriers, wind-component logistics teams, crane fleets and industrial relocators with a fixed 8,000–15,000 PLN prepaid corridor preflight. Deliver three candidate routes, known blockers, source dates, uncertainty flags and exactly where a professional survey/authority decision remains mandatory.
- Proof is two unrelated paid preflights and at least one route or quote changed before permit expenditure. The company does not submit permits during the proof phase.

### Founder-wealth shape

- **Conservative, year 11–14:** 500 EU carriers/project teams at €12,000 ARR/usage = €6m revenue; 80% gross, 35% operating margin = €2.1m EBITDA; €1.5m capital; founder 70%; 7x = €14.7m value and €10.3m founder equity.

The 500-account conservative case is plausible only after multi-country expansion; Poland alone is not a €10m-founder outcome. Country expansion is data work, not a new physical lease, but each jurisdiction has different data completeness and permit rules.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

- **Strongest copy risk:** Hexagon/Octave SafeHaul, route-survey firms, TMS vendors and permit authorities can add or already offer restriction-aware routing.
- GDDKiA says actual route approval considers current works, technical condition and consent from all road managers; the engine can never imply that public data replaces those decisions.
- Fatal if LiDAR age/occlusion and missing local-road bridge data produce more than 5% serious missed blockers in the benchmark; if professional indemnity terms make preflight uneconomic; or if carriers treat preflight as inseparable from their trusted survey/permit provider.

### Funnel disposition

**Retain for advisory evaluation, narrowly.** The asset is genuinely buildable without permission and the decision value is acute, but authority/data incompleteness may reduce it to a low-priced preliminary map.

## Direction 3 — MixFail Atlas: Cross-Brand Tank-Mix Physical-Compatibility Proofbank

### Customer-inside insight

An agronomist or applicator planning a multi-product spray does not merely need the correct mixing order. They need to know whether the exact products, sequence, water hardness, pH and temperature will remain sprayable without flocculation, sediment, blocked filters or unusable tank residue. Manufacturer lists cover selected partners and commonly disclaim unlisted mixes, biological efficacy and field variation. A failed tank costs chemical, a spray window and cleanup time.

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Purchase ordinary legally marketed Polish crop-protection products, foliar nutrients and adjuvants from normal distribution. Build a standardized, recorded physical test bench: controlled water chemistries and temperatures, mixing sequence, agitation, time-series images, turbidity/settling, sieve/filter residue and redispersion. The first asset can contain 120 two-/three-product condition runs across 20 high-use products before any buyer exists. The company owns its specimens, protocols, images, measurements, failure taxonomy and derived API; it makes no biological-efficacy or crop-safety claim.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Pre-proof spend ceiling: 39,000 PLN: products/consumables 10,000; bench, imaging and water control 9,000; agronomy/test design 7,000; software/API 7,000; legal/insurance/outbound 6,000.
- Publish a blinded reproducibility study with repeated runs and disclose that physical compatibility is not legal permission, biological compatibility or crop safety.
- Sell 6,000–12,000 PLN prepaid requested-matrix packs to input retailers, independent agronomy groups, foliar-nutrition makers or smaller crop-protection manufacturers. Requested products are purchased in normal trade; no supplier partnership is required.
- Proof is three independent paid matrices, one repeat request, at least 70% contribution margin excluding founder R&D, and a contract assigning commercial rights to results while allowing the customer a private embargo period.

### Founder-wealth shape

- **Conservative, year 12–15:** 120 enterprise licences at €35,000 = €4.2m revenue; 78% gross, 32% operating margin = €1.34m EBITDA; founder 70%; 7x = €9.4m value and €6.6m founder equity. This case does **not** clear the gate.
- **Expected, year 10–12:** 180 manufacturers, distributors and platform licences at €45,000 = €8.1m revenue; 82% gross, 38% operating margin = €3.08m EBITDA; €2m expansion capital; founder 65%; 8x = €24.6m value and €16m founder equity.
- **Strong, year 8–10:** 400 global accounts at €55,000 = €22m revenue; 85% gross, 42% operating margin = €9.2m EBITDA; founder 55%; 8x = €74m value and €41m founder equity.

This survives the wealth screen only if the business becomes a multi-country data licence/API rather than a Polish test service. Before paneling, discovery must show that 180 enterprise accounts at this price is not heroic.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

- **Strongest copy risk:** BASF, Corteva, Yara and Precision Labs already maintain product-specific physical-compatibility or mixing-order tools; a coalition of manufacturers could reproduce high-volume combinations.
- The neutral cross-brand, water-condition and reproducibility history must be valuable enough to overcome free manufacturer guidance.
- Fatal if physical-only results do not change purchasing/application decisions; if product labels prohibit the relevant mixtures; if legal advice requires claims so cautious that customers will not pay; or if paid requests remain bespoke and do not add reusable matrix coverage.

### Funnel disposition

**Retain as a research candidate, not yet a finalist.** It fixes the prior prospective-corpus objection because the founder can create the first bank unilaterally, but willingness to pay and the €10m bridge remain unproven.

## Direction 4 — CleanLine Materials Graph: Disinfectant-to-Machine Material Damage API

### Customer-inside insight

A food plant sanitation manager is not choosing a chemical in the abstract. They must kill the authorised target organism at the labelled dilution/contact time **without swelling seals, crazing sight glasses, attacking conveyor plastics or corroding machine metals**. Authorised-use data and equipment-material data live separately. Chemical vendors advise on their own portfolio; OEMs often name a narrow approved list. The costly event is premature seal/part failure or downtime after a sanitation change.

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Combine ECHA's public authorised-biocide product/use facts with a founder-owned accelerated coupon bank. Purchase common authorised disinfectants and commodity, grade-certified coupons of EPDM, NBR, FKM, silicone, POM, UHMWPE, polycarbonate and 304/316 stainless. Test only label-use dilutions/contact cycles; capture mass/dimension/hardness/appearance change and exact conditions. The first 12 products × 8 materials × controls can exist without a food plant, chemical company or OEM. Do not test microbial efficacy; use only authorised target/use claims.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Pre-proof ceiling: 40,000 PLN: coupons/chemicals 10,000; controlled exposure and measurement equipment 9,000; polymer/corrosion contractor 8,000; data/API 6,000; legal/insurance/outbound 7,000.
- Produce 96 product-material cells with repeats and an honest lab-to-field limitation statement.
- Directly sell a 10,000–18,000 PLN prepaid **sanitation-change damage screen** to small food-equipment service firms, contract sanitation operators, dairies or beverage plants. The customer supplies only its candidate products and machine bill of materials after purchase; those inputs are not needed to create the core bank.
- Proof requires two unrelated paying customers, a repeat licence/request and at least one avoided candidate or accelerated follow-up test. A one-off custom coupon lab is a kill, not success.

### Founder-wealth shape

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

## Direction 5 — CoatWindow: Cross-Brand Industrial Coating Cure-Window Engine

### Customer-inside insight

Industrial coating contractors lose money when a crew mobilises into a weather window that looks acceptable on a product sheet but produces missed recoat timing, slow cure or failed adhesion on the actual substrate/preparation. Product sheets provide controlled-temperature tables; the contractor needs a cross-brand, shift-level **apply / wait / switch product** decision under temperature, humidity, dew-point margin and planned service exposure.

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Normalize public manufacturer technical/application sheets into executable cure/recoat rules, then purchase standard steel coupons and common coatings to create an owned environmental test matrix. Use a small controlled chamber, film-thickness measurement and comparative pull-off/cross-cut testing. Start with 6 common systems × 3 substrate preparations × 4 environmental profiles, with repeat images and adhesion/cure measurements. The asset exists before a contractor participates. The product remains a planning aid and never replaces the manufacturer's instructions, inspector or site acceptance.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Pre-proof ceiling: 39,000 PLN: coatings/coupons 7,000; used chamber and measurement kit 14,000; coatings inspector/contractor 7,000; data/API 6,000; legal/outbound 5,000.
- Reproduce published cure ordering and quantify where public tables are insufficient; do not claim certification-grade durability.
- Sell a fixed 12,000–20,000 PLN prepaid winter-maintenance or shutdown planning pilot to small steel fabricators, wind/energy maintenance coaters or industrial painting contractors.
- Proof requires two customers, one changed product/shift decision, one repeat paid season or annual API licence, and ≥65% contribution after tests and expert review.

### Founder-wealth shape

- **Conservative, year 12–15:** 200 accounts at €25,000 = €5m revenue; 72% gross, 30% operating margin = €1.5m EBITDA; founder 70%; 7x = €10.5m value and €7.4m founder equity. This misses the gate.
- **Expected, year 10–13:** 350 contractors, inspectors and coating suppliers at €30,000 = €10.5m revenue; 80% gross, 38% operating margin = €4m EBITDA; €2.5m capital; founder 65%; 7x = €28m value and €18.2m founder equity.
- **Strong, year 8–11:** 750 accounts/API licences at €40,000 = €30m revenue; 84% gross, 44% operating margin = €13.2m EBITDA; founder 55%; 8x = €106m value and €58m founder equity.

### Copy risk and fatal dependencies

- **Strongest copy risk:** Jotun, Hempel, Axalta and inspectors already supply product selection/application advice and own deeper product data; one major vendor can add weather-window logic for its own portfolio.
- ISO notes that adhesion methods are comparative and results can differ with substrate roughness, film thickness and practical conditions.
- Fatal if cross-brand neutrality is less valuable than warranty-backed manufacturer advice, if the first useful matrix requires far more than 100,000 PLN, or if outcome liability requires a certified lab/inspector on every job and forces revenue to scale with expert labour.

### Funnel disposition

**Reserve, behind CleanLine.** The first asset is autonomous, but incumbent technical-service and transferability risk are unusually high.

## Direction 8 — HarvestClock EU: Veterinary-Medicine Withdrawal Release API

### Asset that can be built now without permission

Use EMA's public Union Product Database website/product information to normalize product identity, country availability, route, food output and withdrawal periods, with source snapshots and effective-date changes. Create an initial cattle/pig product catalogue and deterministic date engine from public product pages. The public API currently requires registration/authentication, so the founder must verify commercial reuse and bulk-access terms before relying on it; the access-free public website is not enough evidence of an unrestricted production feed.

### Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN

- Pre-proof ceiling: 25,000 PLN: product normalization, veterinary review, API and direct outbound.
- Offer a 10,000–18,000 PLN prepaid shadow integration to livestock-management software vendors, producer groups or slaughter intake systems.

## Reinvestment and Capital Discipline Applied to All Retained Directions

- The first 180 days are capped at the stated amount, always at or below 40,000 PLN, while the founder remains employed and works no more than five hours/day.
- No physical/data scope is expanded until a paid pilot, deposit or API licence exists.
- Spending toward the original 100,000 PLN ceiling requires successful initial delivery and positive contribution margin.
- Family capital is excluded until multiple independent customers, repeat/recurring demand and a risk-adjusted scaling case exist.
- Conservative and expected scenarios reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable business cash while the founder remains employed; optional distributions are capped at 5%. Strong cases reinvest 100%.
- The founder leaves employment only after multiple independent paying customers or contracted recurring demand, positive contribution economics, a repeatable acquisition signal and at least 12 months of personal runway.
