# Zero-to-One Evaluation: LabMargin v1 — Post-Research Re-evaluation

Advisory evaluation only. This is not a simulated-panel score, working-chat score, fresh-chat score, validation result, or confirmation. The exact LabMargin v1 candidate is unchanged. This evaluation incorporates the `Decision-Critical Research Addendum — Exact LabMargin v1`, which supersedes conflicting earlier factual assumptions. It does not rewrite or pivot the candidate.

## Diagnosis

LabMargin retains a commercially coherent insight: a commercial laboratory can lose contribution margin after accepting a superficially standard quote because sample condition, preparation, repeats, capacity, subcontracting, or turnaround commitments reveal hidden cost. Its 5,000 PLN prepaid historical replay remains a bounded, founder-buildable path to paid evidence. The completed research, however, did not substantiate any of the facts required to turn that replay into the exact €24,000-ACV, 750-account, cross-lab-data business: eligible buying entities remain uncounted, €120,000 annual recoverable value per lab is unavailable, complete exports are configuration-dependent, rights authority is unproved, pooled lift is unobserved, and incumbents already own the quote/cost workflow. The exact shape therefore fails Founder Wealth, remains Tier 3, and should now be killed rather than sent into another research loop, panel, or chatbot.

### Arithmetic that remains correct

- Conservative: 750 accounts × €24,000 = €18.0 million revenue; 30% operating margin = €5.4 million operating profit; 5× = €27.0 million business value; 75% founder ownership = **€20.25 million founder stake value**.
- Expected: €54.0 million revenue × 36% operating margin × 6× × 70% = **€81.648 million founder stake value**.
- Strong: €152.0 million revenue × 40% operating margin × 7× × 62% = **€263.872 million founder stake value**.
- The candidate's price rule remains binding: €24,000 divided by a maximum 20% value share requires **€120,000 annual independently documented improvement per account** and €90 million aggregate annual customer value at 750 accounts.
- The conservative market share is `750 / eligible buying entities`. The denominator is unknown. Against 10,067 raw records, 7.45% is only a theoretical floor; 7,500 eligible accounts imply 10%, 5,000 imply 15%, 3,000 imply 25%, and 2,000 imply 37.5%.

### Claims that remain hypotheses after prescribed research

- Independent commercial labs commonly lose at least €120,000 annually from the exact quote-to-realised-margin mechanism after existing pricing, LIMS, and finance tools.
- At least 7,500 genuinely eligible six-country buying entities exist, which is the minimum required merely to keep 750 customers at 10% penetration.
- Three unrelated labs will prepay and provide a complete joined export while also signing a rights-audited perpetual derived-data rider.
- Ten labs and 10,000 rows are sufficient for a pooled model to outperform local history under leave-one-lab-out, time-split, and local-recalibration controls.
- Pooled lift is large and persistent enough to make LabMargin harder to copy than an incumbent's customer-local analytics module.
- The production offer can sustain €24,000 blended ACV, 82% gross margin, sub-nine-month CAC payback, and retention despite QBench and WiseLIMS occupying similar or lower budget ranges with broader workflow suites.
- Founder-led acquisition can move from three prepaid pilots to 30 labs in year two and 750 by years eight to nine without privileged distribution.

## Overall Score

**41/100.** Weighted points are 81.0 across 20.0 total weight, producing a weighted average of 4.05/10 and an overall score of 41 after multiplying by 10 and rounding. This is an advisory score only. The decline from v1 reflects completed adverse/absent evidence, not changed candidate wording.

## Control Point

**Current: Tier 3. No control-point upgrade is evidenced.** LabMargin has no signed lab contract, rights-audited derived-data rider, outcome corpus, completed adapter, model parameters, exclusive workflow access, reserved distribution, or routed transaction flow. A generic SaaS clause is not a laboratory-specific authority chain; a laboratory cannot grant rights withheld by upstream customer contracts, confidentiality obligations, trade-secret duties, database rights, or data-protection law.

Even ten signed contracts would create at most a potential Tier 2 position unless leave-one-lab-out and prospective tests show that the pooled corpus beats each lab's local data and delivery labour materially declines. Those results are unavailable. Non-exclusive rights would still let labs or incumbent vendors build equivalent local models.

**Strongest copy risk:** QBench, WiseLIMS, Confience, SampleManager, LabVantage, eValuate, or another installed workflow/costing vendor can add customer-local realised-cost calibration through an analytics module or ordinary contract amendment. These vendors already hold quotation, work-order, retest, resource, cost, invoice, and integration data. If local models perform adequately, they do not need the proposed pooled corpus at all; the supposed moat disappears while their trust and distribution remain superior.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The exact candidate supplies all three scenarios, correctly separates retained company value from founder distributions, uses 95% reinvestment in conservative/expected cases, allows 100% in strong success, and states customer counts, prices, revenue, margins, capital, ownership, values, and timing. The failure is not spreadsheet arithmetic. It is the lack of a credible commercial bridge after the prescribed research:

1. **The denominator is unknown.** Accreditation records cannot be treated as independent commercial buying entities. Keeping 750 customers at even 10% share requires at least 7,500 genuinely eligible accounts, which has not been established. The earlier 3,000 threshold would require 25% penetration before accounting for incumbents.
2. **The price has no compliant value proof.** Under the candidate's own rule, €24,000 ACV requires €120,000 annual improvement from the exact mechanism. Public cases concern broad automation and do not supply that baseline. Without it, the conservative €18 million revenue case has no supported price-volume foundation.
3. **The control point is not present.** Target labs' authority to grant perpetual pooled rights is unproved, complete fields are not standard, and cross-lab lift over local calibration is unavailable. The business is valued as a defensible product before its core compounding asset exists.
4. **Incumbents compress value and capture.** QBench Advanced and WiseLIMS Pro already combine much of the source workflow at approximately $25,500 and $12,720 annually. The startup cannot assume €24,000 for a narrower overlay, software margins, and strong retention without measured incremental economic lift.
5. **Distribution and capital remain aspirational.** No channel, conversion, CAC, sales-cycle, implementation, renewal, or procurement evidence supports 30 customers in year two and 750 international accounts with ≤€0.8 million cumulative capital and 75% founder ownership.
6. **Expected and strong scenarios worsen the share problem.** Their 1,800 and 4,000 accounts are 17.9% and 39.7% of the raw 10,067 records before removal of ineligible labs and group sites. True eligible shares would be higher.

Founder equity could be the correct wealth mechanism for a proven vertical software/data company, but the exact LabMargin v1 assumptions do not credibly produce that company. Further public research cannot be used to keep the gate open.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, narrowly, for paid historical-replay evidence only.** The unchanged candidate still provides an exact 5,000 PLN prepaid deliverable, an observable list-building route, three unrelated customers, an 18,000 PLN pre-proof ceiling, no family capital, and explicit kill criteria. A founder can build import/replay tooling and perform three bounded mappings while retaining employment and staying below 40,000 PLN. No distribution partner, full LIMS, certification, or full-time resignation is required before the first payment.

The pass does not extend to successful delivery, recurring conversion, or the data control point:

- Complete joined exports are only configuration-dependent possibilities; no prevalence is established.
- The first contract's derived-right requirement needs source-authority and upstream-confidentiality review, which can block or delay signature.
- The unknown founder must win trust for commercially sensitive quote/cost data in an ISO-accredited environment.
- A 20-quote or 100-order replay may not support reliable inference, and a 5,000 PLN custom replay can sell without proving €24,000 recurring product demand.
- The 1,500 PLN delivery-cost target excludes or understates founder time, data cleaning, security, legal review, and exception handling.

Thus the paid-proof mechanism is reachable enough to pass the narrow 180-day gate, but it cannot compensate for the failed wealth gate. It does not justify panel entry or additional founder capital.

## Expansion Check

The Polish materials/environmental/electrical segment is narrow enough for an outbound experiment, but its eligible buying population, event volume, data completeness, and budget remain unknown. Pooling different lab types may add domain shift before it adds signal. The six-country expansion is not asymmetric: every new country and system adds rights, language, costing, integration, validation, and procurement work, while established LIMS vendors can enter the analytical niche from an installed position.

ISO/IEC 17025 creates a shared operating vocabulary, not a local moat. Poland provides a cost base and directory access, not customer trust, exclusive data, or protected distribution. The candidate's expansion only compounds if pooled data produces verified lift and signed rights survive; neither premise is evidenced.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The exact €24,000 ACV fails its own value rule.** It requires at least €120,000 of annual improvement per lab. Exhausted public research found no baseline attributable to the exact mechanism.
2. **The 750-account wealth case has no eligible buying-entity denominator.** Raw accreditation records are not accounts. At 3,000 eligible entities, the conservative case needs 25% share; at 2,000 it needs 37.5%.
3. **The claimed control point is absent and legally conditional.** No target lab has signed a rights-audited perpetual derived-data rider, and a lab may lack authority over source-customer trade secrets, confidential data, database contents, or personal data.
4. **No evidence shows pooled data beats a local model.** Cross-lab heterogeneity may make local history more predictive. Without pooled lift, non-exclusive outcome rights do not create a moat.
5. **The standard data row is not established.** Relevant fields can exist across LIMS, finance, ERP, instrument, and manual systems, but no common joined export or target-lab prevalence is shown. Delivery may remain bespoke data engineering and costing work.
6. **Incumbents already own the workflow and adjacent analytics.** QBench, WiseLIMS, Confience, SampleManager, LabVantage, and costing products can reproduce the functional feature using installed customer data, ordinary development, and stronger trust.
7. **Scale distribution is missing.** The first three pilots have a mechanism; 30, 180, and 750 customers do not. Sensitive-data sales across six countries are unlikely to fit the stated capital and ownership path without evidence of a repeatable channel.
8. **A reachable service test is being asked to validate an unproved data platform.** Three prepaid replays can demonstrate paid analysis demand while leaving ACV, retention, pooled lift, rights, margins, and wealth economics untouched.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest insight remains the **payer-controlled pre-acceptance loss event**: quote acceptance happens before sample and capacity uncertainty fully reveal themselves, and a fixed paid replay can measure whether better conditions, pricing, turnaround, subcontracting, or no-bid decisions would have protected margin. That insight may be useful in future structural work, but it does not rescue exact LabMargin v1.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Kill the exact LabMargin v1 business shape and remove it from the current panel/chatbot funnel. Prescribed research has been exhausted without establishing the account denominator, price-supporting value, standard data access, lawful control point, or pooled model advantage. Repeating public research or polishing the wording would not change the economics. Any later reconsideration would require genuinely new non-public documentary evidence or a material structural pivot handled by the pivoter skill, followed by a fresh advisory evaluation.
