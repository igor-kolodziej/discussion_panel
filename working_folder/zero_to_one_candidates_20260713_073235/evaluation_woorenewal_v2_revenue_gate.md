# Zero-to-One Evaluation: WooRenewal v2 — Merchant-Workflow Acquisition

## Diagnosis

Lowering the satisfactory outcome to 1m PLN annual revenue removes v1's fatal 60,000-site wealth fantasy, but it does not make the expected case conservative. The acquired update and renewal rail is a real Tier-1 asset after transfer, and the 903-plugin screen plus small public sale precedents improve reachability evidence. However, no qualifying target is publicly proved, WordPress.org now warns sellers against unknown acquirers, and the model still needs roughly 100–150 paid accounts to become 1,100 in five years while adding a related asset. WooRenewal v2 is a disciplined micro-acquisition thesis, not yet an evidence-backed route to the user's revenue-quality target.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence:

- WordPress.org documents formal owner transfer and abandoned-plugin adoption, with manual review above 10,000 users.
- Freemius documents product-ownership transfer and provides licensing, subscription, payout, and tax infrastructure.
- The reproducible public screen reviewed 903 WooCommerce-related plugins and found 41 pre-data-room leads.
- Public examples show small plugin transactions and offers at roughly USD 830–6,000; one WooCommerce offer claimed 1,000+ installs, USD 317 monthly revenue, organic distribution, low support, and transferable WordPress/Freemius assets.
- A larger WPGetAPI precedent reached about 6,000 free installs and 500–600 paid users but sold for six figures, outside the founder's initial envelope.
- WordPress.org explicitly warns that unknown plugin-acquisition approaches are often untrustworthy.

Hypotheses:

- A legitimate target has at least 100 paying sites, at least 75% renewal, acceptable support/security, and clean atomic transfer while accepting no more than 28,000 PLN cash.
- The founder can overcome seller distrust without established WordPress acquisition credentials.
- One or two affordable assets can grow from roughly 100–150 to 1,100 paid accounts by Year 5 without destructive pricing, support expansion, or platform conflict.
- Shared distribution or cross-sell exists between two related assets.

Prior reviewer scores are judgments, not market evidence: v1 received 71 and 76 from independent simulated judges and 70 advisory after their objections.

## Overall Score

**71/100.** Weighted points: `141 ÷ 20 × 10 = 70.5`, rounded to 71. Advisory only; this is not a simulated-panel or chatbot validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 after atomic transfer; Tier 3 before a deal is reserved or signed.** The strongest copy risk is a larger WooCommerce suite adding the same feature and bundling it into a broader installed base. The incumbent cannot instantly copy transferred configurations, cohorts, support history, or update ownership, but can suppress new sales and pricing.

## Founder Wealth Gate / User-Defined Revenue Quality Gate

The default 10m PLN founder-wealth gate is superseded by the user's explicit run-specific target. Under the replacement gate, **fail**: the expected-success arithmetic reaches 1,001,000 PLN recognized revenue and 300,300 PLN operating profit by Year 5, but no primary evidence supports the qualifying first asset, 35%+ annual revenue growth, or second-asset cross-sell. The conservative case reaches only 540,000 PLN revenue. Indicative founder equity of roughly 761,000 PLN in the expected case is internally consistent and does not double-count retained earnings, but it is not the run's pass criterion.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass narrowly as a credible test plan, not as validated access.** A public WooCommerce plugin offer at USD 6,000 fits below the 28,000 PLN acquisition-cash cap, and the first unrelated post-transfer renewal can occur within 180 days without building a new product. Total pre-proof exposure remains at or below 40,000 PLN and the work is compatible with employment. The veto risk is seller trust and data-room/transfer completeness; no outreach or primary diligence has occurred.

## Expansion Check

The first operational workflow is a good beachhead. Requiring merchant overlap, 10% cross-sell, or shared support/security savings before a third asset is disciplined. Nevertheless, a broad plugin suite can enter the feature niche, and every acquisition may require a new trusted seller relationship. Poland lowers operating cost but does not change the global distribution contest.

## Fatal Objections

1. The Year-5 target still depends on an unproved increase from roughly 100–150 to 1,100 paid accounts.
2. None of 903 publicly screened targets proves the complete payer, renewal, price, support, security, and assignability specification.
3. WordPress.org's explicit warning against unknown plugin acquirers weakens the proposed 100-owner cold-outreach funnel.
4. A second asset can reset both seller sourcing and customer distribution instead of compounding the first.
5. The acquired channel is durable for existing users but does not prevent incumbent bundles or AI-enabled substitutes from eroding new sales.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire an official installed update channel tied to a single high-cost merchant operation, then use that exact distribution to sell a materially higher-value workflow rather than relying on a generic portfolio of annual licences.
