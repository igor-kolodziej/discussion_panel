# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CareChoice Book

## Diagnosis

CareChoice Book contains a real and useful acquisition insight: buying an intact German provider entity could replace a zero-customer startup with an existing reimbursed account book, billing identity, claims history, staff, and cash receipts. The candidate is not presently investable or panel-ready, however, because the live listing proves only a business with more than 1,000 subscription customers, €250,000–€500,000 revenue, up to five employees, and a €50,000–€250,000 asking band; it does **not** prove a share sale, a GmbH or other transferable legal entity, seller financing, earnings, provider-contract continuity, valid prequalification, or a retained qualified operator. The proposed transparent configurator is also weak as a differentiator: individual configuration, visible needs-based ordering, and human advice are contractual requirements, and several current competitors already advertise configurable boxes, portals, pauses, and direct payer billing. The stated conservative wealth case is arithmetically correct but not conservative: it requires a 40-fold account expansion, unproved 42% gross and 15% operating margins, a crowded search channel, unexplained dilution, and a terminal value that does not clearly deduct debt.

The right advisory disposition is **research further**, not pursue. Two inexpensive facts must be established before broad diligence: (1) whether the seller is offering 100% of an unchanged legal entity with its current IK, prequalification, contract participation, staff, and customer approvals intact; and (2) whether the seller will accept no more than 20,000 PLN at closing and finance more than 90% of even the bottom asking price through a non-recourse, receipts-capped note. A negative answer to either ends this exact direction.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Sourced evidence

- [Nexxt-change listing B-NA-A-4505](https://www.nexxt-change.org/DE/Verkaufsangebot/Detailseite/detailseite_jsp?adId=592029), dated 23 March 2026, is live and states only: Berlin; more than 1,000 subscription customers; up to five employees; last revenue above €250,000 and up to €500,000; asking-price band above €50,000 and up to €250,000. It does not identify the company, legal form, transaction form, EBITDA, customer activity, seller-note willingness, contracts, approvals, liabilities, or staff roles.
- The candidate's ceiling reconciliation is valid as an upper-bound check: 1,000 accounts × €42 × 12 = €504,000. [GKV-Spitzenverband](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/service/hmv/phm/p_himi.jsp) confirms the €42 monthly statutory maximum from 1 January 2025. This does not prove €38 average collections, active-account count, or margin.
- [Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/12/PD24_478_224.html) reports 4.9 million people cared for at home in December 2023: 3.1 million mainly by relatives, 1.1 million with ambulatory services, and about 680,000 at care grade 1. This is a population upper bound, not a counted pool of people with current consumables need, willingness to switch, or economic search-channel reach.
- The [current GKV contract page](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/pflegeversicherung/pflegehilfsmittelvertraege/pflegehilfsmittelvertraege.jsp) says a provider needs a relevant contract and normally prequalification for provider area 19B or 19D. It also describes multiple joinable contracts rather than a unique scarce licence.
- The [reference individual-provider contract AC/TK 19/00/P50](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/phm_vertraege/PHM_Vertrag_Einzelleistungserbringer_AC_TK_19-00-P50.pdf) ties the right to supply to continued eligibility, valid prequalification and a valid Institutionskennzeichen; it requires immediate written notice of changes affecting the contractual relationship or eligibility. It further says that when one provider takes over another provider's cases, new cost-coverage applications are required. Therefore, continuity is plausible only for a share transfer of the unchanged contracting entity, not for a loose book or ordinary asset transfer.
- The public reference contract does not disclose the target's exact contract, participation route, side agreements, or change-of-control treatment. The same legal entity would ordinarily remain the contracting party after a pure share transfer, but this is a legal inference, not evidence about B-NA-A-4505. Written confirmation from the target's German counsel, prequalification body, GKV contract counterparty/participation route, billing centre, and relevant payers remains a closing condition.
- [GKV prequalification guidance](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/krankenversicherung/hilfsmittel/praequalifizierung/hinweise_fuer_leistungserbringer/hinweise_fuer_leistungserbringer.jsp) requires prompt notice and fresh proof after material changes such as a change of legal form, company name, responsible technical manager, operating location, product scope, or insolvency status. A pure shareholder change of a legal person is not expressly listed, but any seller/operator departure that changes the technical manager or responsible person is material.
- The [GKV FAQ dated 1 April 2026](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/phm_vertraege/2026-04-01_PHM_Fragen_und_Antworten.pdf) says advice must be personal and performed by trained staff; prerecorded or AI-based video chat is insufficient. It also says individual composition must be visibly available, the €42 ceiling need not be exhausted, and all advice, packing, shipping, administration, and transport are included in the contracted reimbursement. This makes German human operations and service cost structural, not optional.
- A Poland-resident owner can in principle hold and manage a German GmbH. [IHK Berlin](https://www.ihk.de/berlin/service-und-beratung/recht-und-steuern/firma-und-rechtsformen/rechtsformen-fuer-unternehmen/gmbh-und-ug-haftungsbeschraenkt-4404470) says a German or EU residence is not generally required for appointment as managing director. That does not remove the entity's German contract, tax, employment, privacy, insolvency, customer-service, technical-management, and fulfilment duties.
- Current competitor materials demonstrate that the feature wedge is already broadly available. [desbox](https://www.desbox.de/pflegebox-beantragen) advertises an individual configurator, app/web account, flexible intervals, pauses, and payer handling; [Sanubi](https://sanubi.de/produkte/) and [proSenio Pflegebox](https://pflegebox.de/) advertise configurable recurring boxes and direct reimbursement. These are company claims, not independently audited market shares, but they falsify exclusivity of the interface concept.

### Candidate hypotheses, not evidence

- The seller will accept no more than 20,000 PLN (about €4,600 at the cited exchange rate) at closing and finance the remainder for 36–60 months without personal recourse. At the bottom of the advertised band, this asks the seller to finance more than 90% of price; no seller willingness is published.
- The target is a share-transferable legal entity rather than a sole proprietorship or asset-only sale.
- Its IK, provider-contract participation, prequalification, all active approvals, lawful recurring-order permissions, supplier arrangements, staff, and billing route will continue without interruption after control changes.
- At least 900 accounts paid in two of the last three months; average collected revenue is at least €36–€38 a month; normalized gross margin after goods, pick/pack, shipping, claim work, and service is at least 40%–42%; provider-switch churn is at most 2% monthly; denials/rework are below 4%; and cash conversion is no more than 60 days.
- Direct search can add accounts at no more than €140 collected CAC with attractive twelve-month contribution and without prohibited inducement or targeting.
- A qualified German operator and technical manager will stay while a Poland-based founder contributes at most five hours daily.

### Internal contradiction in the acquisition test

At €38 monthly collections and 42% gross margin, annual gross profit before customer support is €191.52 per account. The proposed requirement that twelve-month contribution **after** support equal at least 1.5× a €140 CAC requires at least €210, which is impossible under those same assumptions. Even with zero support cost, the largest CAC compatible with 1.5× is €127.68. The acquisition rule, margin assumption, and CAC ceiling therefore cannot all hold simultaneously.

## Overall Score

**40/100.** Weighted points are 80.5 over a total weight of 20; `10 × (80.5 ÷ 20) = 40.25`, rounded to 40. This is an advisory evaluator score only, not a simulated-panel, chatbot, confirmation, or validation score.

## Control Point

**Current tier: Tier 3. Conditional post-closing tier: Tier 1 over the existing cash-flow book only.**

Today the founder has no ownership, option, exclusivity, reserved shares, verified seller note, identified legal entity, or written continuity confirmation. The listing is evidence that something relevant is for sale, but not that the required control package can be acquired. If a 100% share purchase closes with the same legal entity, IK, valid prequalification, contract participation, active customer approvals, historical claim data, supplier continuity, and retained qualified staff, it would create Tier-1 control of present accounts and transaction flow. It would still create no exclusive control over new category demand.

The strongest copy risk is an established provider matching the transparent configurator and self-service workflow while using larger purchasing volume, an existing brand, more customer reviews, and a larger paid-search budget. This is not theoretical: configurators, flexible changes, account portals, and direct payer handling already appear in current competitor offers. The acquired book can slow displacement, but it cannot prevent it.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The table is explicit and avoids double-counting distributions, but its conservative label is not credible:

- 40,000 × €38 × 12 = €18.24 million revenue; 15% operating margin = €2.736 million operating profit; 7× = €19.152 million business value; 78% = €14.94 million founder value. The arithmetic is correct.
- The outcome requires 40-fold growth from the advertised book and 0.82% of the broad home-care population. A small population share does not make the acquisition path conservative; the relevant denominator is reachable, eligible households with product need who can be acquired profitably from competitors.
- The 42% gross and 15% operating margins are unsupported in a price-capped business where products, human advice, packing, shipping, administration, denials, and returns must all fit contracted prices. At 10% operating margin, the same 7× and 78% assumptions yield about €9.96 million—below the gate before subtracting liabilities.
- The 7× value is not labelled enterprise value or equity value. If it is enterprise value, seller-note and receivables/working-capital debt must be deducted. The model lists about €2 million of capital needs but does not show terminal net debt.
- Ownership is internally unexplained. Starting from a proposed 100% acquisition, 12% outside-equity dilution implies 88%, not 78%; every scenario contains an unexplained additional ten percentage points of founder dilution.
- The direct-search engine is unproved. At 2% monthly account loss, a 40,000-account business must replace roughly 800 accounts monthly merely to stay flat. At €140 CAC that is €1.344 million annual replacement spend, before growth acquisition. This materially pressures the 15% operating margin.
- The milestone path—900 retained accounts in year one, 3,000 in year two, 10,000 in year four, 24,000 in year six, and 40,000 in years eight to nine—has no demonstrated channel volume, CAC, management build, working-capital schedule, or seller-note amortization bridge.

Conservative, expected, and strong cases are present, but all scale the same unproved acquisition and margin assumptions. The conservative case lacks enough headroom to a modest margin, ownership, multiple, or debt haircut. The required 95% reinvestment rule is stated, yet the retained cash waterfall among taxes, seller-note payments, working capital, and acquisition is not modeled. Founder wealth cannot pass until audited target cohorts plus a paid direct-acquisition cohort support a bottom-up growth and cash-flow model with explicit debt subtraction and corrected ownership.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL on current evidence; answerable through bounded research.**

The nominal plan would cap cash outlay at 38,000 PLN before the first successor-controlled payer settlement and can be performed alongside employment during diligence. That is not yet a credible paid-proof path because all of the following must succeed before the founder controls the paid evidence:

1. The advertisement must represent a share-transferable legal person. It does not state the legal form or transaction form. If it is a sole proprietorship or asset sale, the provider changes and the reference contract requires new cost-coverage applications for taken-over cases.
2. The seller must accept no more than 20,000 PLN at closing and a non-recourse receipts-capped note for the balance. At the bottom ask, the seller would finance more than 90%; there is no evidence of willingness.
3. The target's exact provider contract, IK, prequalification, approvals, customer permissions, supplier terms, billing-centre setup, and bank flow must continue after control changes. The reference framework makes this possible for an unchanged entity but does not prove this target's position.
4. The qualified technical manager, German customer advice, fulfilment, accounting, privacy, and claims operation must remain. The listing proves only “up to five” employees, not that a competent operator exists or will stay.
5. The share company must be solvent and sufficiently capitalized. A share buyer inherits the company's liabilities, clawback exposure, employment obligations, compliance history, and cash needs. A 38,000 PLN purchase/diligence cash cap does not itself cap economic or director-liability exposure.

A Polish EU founder may legally own and potentially manage the company, but the exact operation is not realistically executable by him alone for five hours daily. It is employment-compatible only if a retained German operator and technical manager run normal business hours, the founder is not required to provide individual advice or supervise daily fulfilment, and all costs fit the current contribution margin. None is evidenced.

Before any paid option or legal diligence, the broker should answer in writing: legal form and sale type; exact low-end price; seller-note structure and recourse; active-customer and normalized EBITDA range; IK and prequalification expiry; contract participation route; identity/tenure of the responsible technical manager; and whether the seller will condition closing on written continuity confirmations. Until those answers exist, both critical-dependency count and acquisition exposure are too high for a Reachability pass.

## Expansion Check

The acquired 1,000-account book is narrow and economically meaningful if verified, but the proposed first new segment—300 caregivers already searching for a configurable §40 account—is not differentiated from the mainstream category. Germany is the appropriate operating market because the reimbursement contract, customers, employees, and fulfilment are German; Poland supplies no demonstrated regulatory, trust, sourcing, or distribution advantage. Expansion to 40,000 accounts is a national share-taking exercise against brands already offering the same user-facing features, not asymmetric movement from a protected niche.

Broad providers can enter the proposed wedge immediately because individual configuration is a contract requirement and self-service software is routinely purchasable. The only asymmetric asset is accumulated account and claims history. That history becomes meaningful only if data-room evidence shows it reduces churn, denial cost, support cost, or acquisition payback in a way competitors cannot recreate quickly. No adjacent product expansion should be credited: other reimbursed aids would add product, prequalification, expertise, contracting, and fulfilment requirements, while private-pay upsells face separate willingness-to-pay and marketing tests.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The asset may not be a transferable legal entity.** The listing does not state legal form or share-sale availability. An asset transfer can require new applications for every case, destroying the cold-start thesis.
2. **The purchase financing is unsupported.** The structure requires the seller to finance more than 90% even at the low end, accept payment only from receipts, retain transition risk, and provide no personal recourse.
3. **The conservative founder-wealth case is not conservative.** It depends on 40-fold scale, unverified margins, a contested search channel, unexplained dilution, and unclear debt treatment.
4. **The unit-economics test is internally impossible at its ceiling.** €191.52 annual gross profit before support cannot provide €210 of post-support contribution required by 1.5× a €140 CAC.
5. **The product wedge is a compliance baseline, not a moat.** Individual configuration and human advice are required; several competitors already provide configuration, portals, pauses, and payer handling.
6. **The minimum people stack is unproved.** A part-time Warsaw founder cannot personally cover German-language advice, qualified supervision, fulfilment, payer operations, privacy, tax, employment, and insolvency duties.
7. **The 40,000 PLN maximum-loss claim is incomplete.** A share acquisition imports liabilities and operating obligations; seller-note and working-capital commitments plus potential managing-director exposure are not captured by closing cash alone.
8. **No target earnings or cohort evidence exists.** Revenue and customer-count bands cannot establish active accounts, normalized contribution, churn, denial exposure, cash conversion, or seller-note service capacity.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire an **unchanged legal entity** that already controls a reimbursed recurring customer book, valid provider eligibility, payer-accepted approvals, billing history, staff, and cash receipts. This is the only insight worth preserving. It is valuable only after legal-form, continuity, seller-financing, active-account, contribution, and operator-retention evidence is secured; the configurable-box feature should not be treated as proprietary.
