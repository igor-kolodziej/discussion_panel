## Concise Diagnosis

RatioSnap addresses a real, current procedure: Graco still instructs users to separate the A/B streams at the dispense valve, tare individual containers, weigh them and calculate the ratio, while an older Nordson procedure likewise uses separate containers and recorded weights. The keyed cassette, simultaneous weighing and signed record form a coherent workflow improvement, and the candidate is unusually disciplined about scope, tests and kill criteria. The weakness is commercial: there is no evidence yet that a large segment simultaneously performs the check often, lacks inline assurance, values a signed record, and will pay at least €4–€5.50 per disposable check. The design is feasible, but the present evidence does not support either profitable domination or the claimed €10 million founder-wealth path.

Primary-source checks: [Graco's current manual ratio-check procedure](https://www.graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/solutions/articles/ratio-check-guidelines-for-dispensing-2k-materials.html), [Nordson's separate-container procedure](https://emanuals.nordson.com/automotive/zfiles/331161.pdf), [DOPAG's existing automatic ratio check with weighing scales on a large 2K system](https://www.dopag.com/products/2k-metering-systems/gear-metering-systems/compomix/), and [Graco's real-time ratio-monitoring alternative](https://www.graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/solutions/articles/real-time-ratio-monitoring-critical-for-adhesives-in-the-glass-i.html).

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The conservative-case arithmetic is internally consistent: 2.1 million cassettes at €5.50 plus 1,000 docks at €3,500 gives €15.05 million revenue; a 24% operating margin gives about €3.61 million; 7× less €3 million obligations and multiplied by 68% founder ownership gives about €15.2 million. That proves numerical possibility, not a credible conservative path.

The decisive problems are:

1. The 40,000 relevant-cell denominator is explicitly unproved. Glenmarc's claim of more than 10,000 installed machines supports the existence of a large broad equipment category, but not the subset that performs valuable manual last-point checks and would adopt this product. Even against the candidate's minimum acceptable denominator of 25,000 cells, 6,000 docks require 24% penetration, which is not conservative.
2. There is no paid evidence for €5.50 per check, 350 checks per dock per year, 6,000 active docks, or durable 24% operating margins after application engineering, calibration, chemical compatibility, product liability and international field support.
3. The model gives milestones but no evidence-backed bridge from 20 docks in year two to 300 in year four and 6,000 in year twelve, nor a repeatable channel capable of that scale.
4. Founder ownership of 68% after only about €2 million cumulative growth capital may be possible, but it is not substantiated for a tool-, inventory-, compliance-, support- and export-intensive industrial product business.
5. A 7× operating-profit value may be reasonable for a durable consumables franchise, but that durability is precisely what has not been proven. Without the consumable frequency and switching-cost assumptions, the multiple and €10 million outcome collapse together.

The wealth gate would also fail under the candidate's own stated floor if fewer than 5,000 docks, fewer than 300 checks per year, lower operating margin or lower founder ownership is required; all four remain material uncertainties.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly, for paid evidence—not for product validation.** The plan can seek two unrelated 6,000–8,000 PLN prepaid pilots by day 180 while risking no more than 28,000 PLN, keeping the job, using benign fluids before plant chemistry, and avoiding any required OEM code, licence, distributor or donated dataset. The founder's measurement and data skills fit the reference study, while fixed-scope contractors cover the missing mechanical, electronics and chemical-safety work.

The caveats are substantial: 5,000 PLN for mechanical plus electronics contracting and 3,000 PLN for safety plus FTO are probably underbudgeted; plants may not prepay before seeing evidence on their own valve and chemistry; 50 qualified approaches and six demonstrations impose meaningful part-time industrial selling; and customer-material work cannot safely outrun compatibility, SDS, waste and liability controls. Still, the gate asks whether paid proof is credibly attemptable within 180 days and 40,000 PLN—not whether delivery and scale are already proven—and this attempt is bounded and reversible.

## Control Point

**Current: Tier 3. Potential within 12 months: Tier 2.** Today the cassette geometry, tooling, adapters, firmware, recipes, signing history and installed work instructions are proposed assets, not owned/deployed control points. If the founder actually owns the designs/tooling, proves multiple reusable compatibility packs, enters plant work instructions and accumulates accepted signed history, the resulting format and switching friction could reach Tier 2. It does not reach Tier 1 because no exclusive right, supply, dataset, standard, distribution channel or transaction flow is controlled.

**Strongest copy risk:** an OEM or regional integrator combines an existing ratio-check nozzle with two load cells or ordinary pre-tared containers, barcode/RFID identity and simple record-signing software, then sells it through its trusted installed-base channel. DOPAG already advertises automatic ratio checking with weighing scales on some systems, Graco documents both valve-end physical checks and real-time monitoring, and Nordson documents separate-cup weighing. RatioSnap's integration may be better for the target legacy cell, but the components and method are visible and adjacent suppliers already possess the application knowledge. A reusable dual-cup/dual-scale fixture could also capture most of the value while removing the €5.50 consumable burden.

## Strongest Objections and Caveats

1. **Possible hollow middle.** Low-end legacy cells may tolerate cheap cups and manual records; quality-critical plants willing to pay may already demand inline monitoring, validated metrology or an OEM-backed solution. The exact segment between them may be too small for the wealth case.
2. **Value per check is unproven.** In CEE, four minutes of labor saved alone may not cover a €5.50 cassette. Material savings or avoided record errors therefore must supply most of the value, yet neither frequency nor economic magnitude is evidenced.
3. **The addressable denominator is not established.** “Installed 2K machines” is not equivalent to “semi-automatic cells with frequent, valuable, separable-stream manual checks and no equivalent record.” The wealth model depends on this distinction.
4. **Compatibility can turn the product into project work.** Valve geometry, shot size, viscosity, density, temperature, filled/abrasive chemistries, cross-contamination, closure and waste handling can force bespoke qualification. The five-engineering-day kill criterion is sensible but unproven.
5. **Gross margin may omit the expensive layer.** Cassette and dock bill-of-material targets do not establish fully loaded margin after application support, calibration traceability, failed lots, tooling amortization, compliance, returns, liability coverage and customer-specific documentation.
6. **Distribution is not yet an advantage.** A named finite list makes the first test good, but direct industrial sales are trust-heavy and founder-energy-intensive. OEMs and integrators already have the installed relationships RatioSnap lacks.
7. **The signed result can be overinterpreted.** Graco itself cautions that a ratio check covers an overall sample and may miss transient lead-lag problems; physical testing remains the stronger check of ratio/mix quality. The candidate states a properly narrow claim, but buyer usage and documentation must preserve that boundary.
8. **Existing alternatives compress differentiation.** Official product literature shows manual physical methods, automatic scale-based checks on some equipment and real-time flow/ratio monitoring. The claimed lack of an exact keyed cassette/reader does not establish freedom from close substitutes or meaningful pricing power.

## Fatal Objection

**Fatal to a pursue decision now: the Founder Wealth Gate fails because the business needs at least roughly 5,000–6,000 recurring-use installations, yet neither the relevant-cell denominator nor recurring willingness to pay has evidence.** This is not a demonstrated inherent technical fatality; it is a fatal approval objection. The bounded 180-day paid-proof test remains rational research, but the exact candidate cannot presently be treated as a credible €10 million founder-wealth opportunity.

## Recommendation

**Research further.** Do not rewrite or broaden the product; test the exact stated buyer, frequency, value, cassette price and paid-pilot assumptions before any pursuit decision.
