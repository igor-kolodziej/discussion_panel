# EU Model-Life Rights Steward

Frozen: 2026-08-21 CEST.

This is a structural pivot of Five-Day Parts Reserve. It is a new exact candidate, not a repair to the frozen original. All prices, volumes, rights, insurer terms, conversions, margins, multiples, and customer behaviour below are hypotheses until independently proved.

## Preserved Insight

Covered smartphones and slate tablets create a long operational tail: specified spare parts and repair information must remain available for prescribed periods after a model leaves the market, with five-working-day delivery during the first five years and ten working days during the final two. The named manufacturer, importer, or authorised representative remains accountable where law says so; outsourcing performance does not erase that responsibility.

The useful upstream moment is before a non-EU manufacturer launches a model in the EU, while it can still price lifecycle funding into each device and secure supplier, documentation, tooling, software, and parts rights. Commodity warehousing is not the business.

## Structural Change

- Payer: a non-EU rugged or enterprise smartphone/tablet manufacturer before EU launch, not a small Polish importer after launch.
- Role: EU authorised representative for expressly delegable, written-mandate tasks, combined with exclusive lifecycle-parts operator. No claim is made that the mandate transfers non-delegable responsibility.
- Duration: the complete prescribed model tail, with funded transition protection, not a six-month bin reservation.
- Ownership: exclusive EU aftermarket distribution and ordering rights for the named model, conditional rights to procure or reproduce named authentic parts after original production ends, and access to repair documentation and pairing workflows.
- Financing: manufacturer-funded consigned stock or a segregated lifecycle reserve. The venture does not speculate with founder-funded inventory, and the reserve is never counted as revenue or founder cash.
- Revenue: non-refundable model onboarding, earned per-device stewardship revenue, annual model minimums, and authentic-parts margin.

## Acceptance Contract

A model is accepted only if one integrated, counsel-approved contract contains every following element:

1. A written authorised-representative mandate defining included tasks, limits, authority-contact duties, information access, and the manufacturer's retained responsibilities.
2. Exclusive EU routing of all covered repairer and end-user spare-part orders through a venture-operated public model endpoint.
3. Exclusive authentic-parts distribution rights for the model and territory.
4. Rights to repair manuals, serialised-parts tools, firmware and pairing workflows, SKU specifications, supplier contacts, and the data required to fulfil orders.
5. A lawful licence to procure or reproduce named authentic parts after original production ends, subject to IP, safety, tooling, and supplier authority.
6. Manufacturer-funded inventory through consignment or a segregated per-unit lifecycle reserve; no founder-funded stock.
7. Monthly auditable EU sell-in data and reserve top-ups tied to units sold and observed failure evidence.
8. A term covering the statutory availability tail. Termination requires a funded successor, complete record transfer, and continued fulfilment for devices already sold.
9. Enforceable step-in rights, to the extent counsel confirms, over remaining stock, approved tooling, documentation, supplier records, and necessary software access if the manufacturer exits the model or becomes insolvent.
10. Manufacturer indemnity and security, such as escrow, letter of credit, or parent guarantee, covering product-origin defects and unfunded stock obligations.

Missing any of elements 2 through 9 kills the model. It may not fall back to document custody, ordinary authorised-representative administration, non-exclusive routing, a dashboard, or generic fulfilment.

## Lawful Boundary

The manufacturer remains the principal compliance actor wherever applicable law requires. The venture performs only specified tasks in its written mandate and does not advertise responsibility transfer, counterfeit parts, unauthorised software access, or an unconditional compliance guarantee.

Before live acceptance, counsel must confirm the delegable ecodesign and authorised-representative tasks; non-delegable duties; enforceability of the licence, termination, and distress protections; reserve custody and accounting; VAT; consumer and product-safety law; WEEE and battery transport; recall; privacy; insurance; and whether the venture has the appropriate means for its appointed tasks.

## Buyer and Beachhead

The beachhead is non-EU manufacturers of rugged or enterprise smartphones and slate tablets that:

- launch one to five covered models annually into the EU;
- sell a hypothesised 20,000 to 100,000 EU units per model;
- lack an integrated EU repair and lifecycle-parts network;
- have no incumbent contract already providing the complete mandate, funded reserve, supply/IP rights, ordering route, and transition protections; and
- can lawfully grant every required right.

The buyer is the manufacturer's export, legal/compliance, product, or general-management leader. Integrated brands, manufacturers unable to grant supplier/IP/software rights, and manufacturers unwilling to pre-fund the tail are excluded.

## Painful Event and Offer

The painful event is an imminent EU model launch with a seven-year operational tail but no funded operator, authentic-supply schedule, repair endpoint, pairing/documentation workflow, or successor plan. The first offer is a paid model-admission package that produces one conditional mandate, lifecycle cost model, reserve schedule, public ordering design, repairer-registration workflow, authentic-parts schedule, and controlled fulfilment test.

Hypothetical commercial terms:

- PLN 25,000 to 40,000 non-refundable onboarding per model;
- PLN 4 to 7 of earned stewardship revenue per EU unit;
- PLN 20,000 to 40,000 annual minimum per active model;
- a separate PLN 4 to 10 per EU unit paid into the segregated reserve; and
- 25% to 40% gross margin on authentic-parts transactions.

The reserve is excluded from revenue. Willingness to pay fails unless two manufacturers provide written commercial indications and at least one pays onboarding under the complete conditional mandate.

## Control Point

The primary control point is the signed, paid, model-life mandate plus exclusive aftermarket licence, public order routing, and funded reserve. The authorised-representative title and the warehouse are not scarce by themselves.

Within twelve months and total founder spend of PLN 100,000 or less, control proof requires:

- one live manufacturer mandate for one named model;
- exclusive order, distribution, and permitted data rights;
- documented authentic supply, repair-information, and pairing access;
- a funded reserve or consigned stock;
- insurer-approved liability boundaries;
- enforceable term and successor protection; and
- one eligible order fulfilled and recorded through the controlled endpoint.

If counsel says the transition and model-tail protections are unenforceable, if the manufacturer can route around the endpoint, or if required supply/software rights are revocable at will, the control point fails.

## Cold Start and Reachability Plan

Before paid proof, founder spending is capped at PLN 25,000:

- PLN 12,000 for legal scope, mandate, licence, reserve, and termination analysis;
- PLN 4,000 for insurance eligibility and liability-structure work;
- PLN 4,000 for translated research and direct outreach;
- PLN 2,000 for a secure document-room and order-flow demonstration; and
- PLN 3,000 contingency.

The founder maps 50 exact non-EU manufacturers and their next models or renewal dates from EPREL, model documentation, brand sites, marketplaces, and company registers. A weak-looking public parts route is only an outreach signal, never an accusation. The founder sends a model-specific EU lifecycle cost-and-rights checklist and offers a fixed-scope paid admission package. Testing labs, advisers, and incumbent representatives are not assumed to be channels until contracted.

Paid proof within 180 days is a PLN 25,000 to 40,000 non-refundable onboarding payment under the complete conditional mandate. Live liability begins only after the manufacturer supplies the documentation, rights, reserve, stock plan, indemnity, and insurance conditions. A further PLN 15,000 may be spent after payment to bind cover and finish onboarding. Family capital remains excluded.

The founder can perform research, modelling, sales, and vendor coordination within about five hours daily while employed. Counsel, an insurance broker, a part-time repairability/QMS engineer, and an insured 3PL perform specialist work. A named backup handles authority contact and operational exceptions before any live mandate.

## Founder-Wealth Hypothesis

The model-life cohort compounds because annual minimums and per-unit fees coexist with parts transactions throughout the tail. The following are hypotheses for independent rebuilding, not accepted forecasts.

Adverse: no manufacturer signs the complete package. Stop before accepting liability and after founder spend of no more than PLN 30,000.

Conservative year seven: 10 manufacturers and 40 active model cohorts, with 30,000 annual EU units per currently selling model. Claimed annual output is PLN 7.2m earned unit fees, PLN 1.2m model minimums, and PLN 3.0m parts revenue; claimed gross profit is PLN 7.7m and EBITDA PLN 3.7m after PLN 4.0m operating cost. At 3.5 times EBITDA and 75% founder ownership, claimed founder equity is about PLN 9.7m. The pivot claims that a 25% reduction in unit-fee and parts contribution still leaves about PLN 5.3m founder equity. The evaluator must reconstruct model counts, selling cohorts, unit fee arithmetic, reserves, insurer/claims cost, acquisition capacity, working capital, ownership dilution, tax, and any retained-liability haircut.

Expected year seven: 18 manufacturers, 72 model cohorts, 2.88m annual units, about PLN 25.4m revenue and PLN 8.9m EBITDA; at 4 times EBITDA and 65% ownership, claimed founder equity is about PLN 23m. This is not conservative proof.

While employed, 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested and optional distributions remain at or below 5%. Reserve money, working capital required for liabilities, retained cash, distributions, and terminal equity may not be double-counted.

Milestone hypotheses are one paid model by month six, one live controlled model by month twelve, three manufacturers/six models by year two, six manufacturers/20 models by year four, and 10 manufacturers/40 models by year seven.

## Defensibility and Copy Risk

For a signed model, the venture aims to control the exclusive ordering endpoint, authentic-parts distribution and conditional reproduction rights, funded reserve and stock schedule, repairer records, pairing/documentation workflows, and complete order/failure/replenishment history for the prescribed tail. Those contractual model-specific assets are the proposed switching cost.

An established authorised representative could copy the bundle for future models and begins with far more trust. The AR label, software, lifecycle model, and 3PL are not a moat. The candidate survives only if model-specific rights are enforceable for the full tail and if a small unknown entrant can repeatedly win complete packages before incumbents.

## Expansion

After three controlled manufacturer portfolios, the venture may add adjacent rugged smartphone/tablet models, negotiate end-of-production tooling and approved reproduction licences, add fulfilment nodes only when volume supports them, and offer the structure to broader private-label portfolios. Cross-model failure and reserve learning may be used only when contracts and confidentiality permit. Entering another regulated product category requires a new legal, liability, parts, and control analysis.

## Principal Risk

The existential risk is commercial and institutional: a non-EU manufacturer may accept cheap document representation but refuse a young unknown company the complete public mandate, exclusive distribution/order/data rights, IP and software access, per-unit funding, security, distress protections, and multi-year role. An established umbrella representative could add the same bundle to an existing trusted relationship.

No portal, advisory payment, non-exclusive mandate, or optimistic spreadsheet substitutes for the complete contract.

## First Disconfirming Test

Within 120 days:

1. Obtain a written legal opinion defining delegable tasks and enforceable protections.
2. Obtain two insurance indications for the exact role and liability cap.
3. Contact 30 qualified non-EU manufacturers and complete at least eight decision-maker calls.
4. Obtain three representative complete model-document samples.
5. Obtain two written commercial indications accepting the fee range and lifecycle funding.
6. Produce one executable mandate containing exclusive routing, data, authentic supply or reproduction rights, reserve funding, term protection, indemnity, and conditions precedent.

Kill immediately if no onboarding payment is signed by day 180; manufacturers accept representation but reject any required right or reserve; liability cannot be bounded and insured inside the first-year capital envelope; founder-funded inventory is required; IP/software/supply access cannot be granted; termination protection is unenforceable; an established provider offers the complete bundle at commodity pricing; or a deduplicated global payer denominator and observed price evidence cannot support the conservative portfolio.

## Evidence and Unknowns

Regulation (EU) 2023/1670 supports the parts-availability horizon and delivery deadlines. European Commission guidance supports written, specified-task authorised-representative mandates while leaving manufacturer responsibility and control in place. EPREL supports a model registry but not the claimed global buyer count, sales units, or budget. Public Polish 3PL and parts-distribution offers prove that storage and handling are commodity inputs. Existing repair networks, parts wholesalers, logistics providers, device manufacturers, and umbrella representatives are strong substitutes.

No current evidence proves the fee, the qualifying global denominator, EU unit volumes, complete rights package, manufacturer trust, insurer appetite, reserve sufficiency, parts margins, acquisition rate, retention, terminal multiple, or incumbent pricing. Every one remains a private or denominator-first proof requirement.
