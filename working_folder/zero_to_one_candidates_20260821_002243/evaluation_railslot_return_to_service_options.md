# Zero-to-One Evaluation: RailSlot Return-to-Service Options

Date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Fresh independent advisory evaluation of the exact frozen finalist. Advisory only; this is not a panel or external-validation score.

## Diagnosis

The candidate draws the ECM safety boundary mostly correctly and defines a precise capital-light commercial test. No workshop has granted the proposed firm right, and a workshop or ECM can offer the same booking through one routine contract/process change. PLN12k willingness to pay, qualifying event count, repeat acquisition, and scale margins are unsupported; only expected and strong spreadsheets cross PLN5m. Founder Wealth and Reachability fail, and current control is Tier 3.

## Evidence And Assumptions

- Freight-wagon ECM certification is mandatory. Outsourcing does not remove ECM responsibility; maintenance delivery issues release to service and ECM fleet management controls return to operation. [Regulation 2019/779](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2019/779/oj/eng), [Railway Safety Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/798/oj/eng)
- ERA reports 71,818 Polish freight wagons and 142 certified ECM organisations. The August 2026 VKM workbook has 453 distinct active Polish keeper names. These are assets/actors, not buyers, events, or purchasing authorities. [ERA factsheet](https://www.era.europa.eu/assets/factsheets/poland/ERA_factsheet_PL.html), [VKM register](https://www.era.europa.eu/domains/registers/vkm_en)
- GATX describes 6,000+ planned revisions annually, 40+ workshops, internal scheduling, its Ostróda shop, and replacement wagons. Direct maintenance/mobile alternatives also exist. [GATX](https://www.gatx.eu/pl/uslugi/utrzymanie/), [DB Cargo](https://pl.dbcargo.com/rail-pl-en/additional-services/maintenance-services), [Polski Tabor](https://polskitabor.pl/en/mobile-service/)
- A 2024 KOLTAR offer listed 55 self-unloading wagons at PLN56 net per wagon-day. PLN12k equals about 214 days at that one benchmark; it does not prove a general replacement rate but weakens the fee case absent much larger lost-hire/demurrage evidence. [KOLTAR offer](https://platformazakupowa.grupaazoty.com/zapytania_email%2C1553715%2Cc2288539d26bd67903ed44c24bba78e3.html)

Every option price, reservation cost, conversion, workshop term, bounded-scope rate, repeat frequency, CAC, loss/refund rate, margin, ownership, multiple, and annual volume remains unverified. The qualifying denominator is presently zero named buyers and zero named painful events.

At 500 options annually, the venture needs 41.7 payments monthly. At one, three, or ten options per buyer annually, it needs 500, 167, or 50 recurring qualifying buyers. The first exceeds the 453-name raw upper bound; none is supported after filtering.

## Incentive And Power Map

- Loss bearer: keeper/lessor or contractually its lessee/shipper.
- Payer: fleet owner, asset manager, or operations director; no budget is identified.
- Beneficiary: keeper and downstream user if earlier intake actually reduces outage.
- Authority: ECM controls maintenance workflow/return to operation; workshop controls release to service; RU/IM retains its safety role.
- Distributor: founder outbound from public registers; no proprietary channel.
- Information: keeper, ECM, and workshop hold due dates, queue, scope, damage, and loss facts.
- Risk bearer: venture intends only reservation risk; refund, cancellation, and liability remain unpriced.
- Transaction owner: workshop owns capacity/maintenance and ECM controls safety; venture owns an option only if all parties sign.

Workshop bargaining power is strongest because it can refuse or sell the same right directly.

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

The candidate can be lawful if it sells only commercial capacity, the ECM approves/controls the work order, the workshop retains release responsibility, and a date is never represented as technical approval. The title “Return-to-Service Options” is risky, but the frozen body disclaims the safety outcome.

Customer cash first, one named wagon/date/package, tri-party recognition, express late-arrival/hidden-damage/refund rules, and separately funded maintenance are sensible. They do not stop the workshop selling direct, the ECM coordinating itself, mobile repair, replacement wagons, ordinary advance booking, customer-by-customer reacquisition of rights, or bespoke scope divergence.

## Overall Score

**42/100.** Weighted points 83.5 across weight 20; `10 × 83.5 / 20 = 41.75`, rounded. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** One signed prepaid slot controls one transaction, not a repeatable company-level control point. Every unit is reacquired non-exclusively from a supplier that can sell direct. Even two workshops and three keepers under ordinary terms do not establish Tier 2. Strongest copier: the workshop standardising its own take-or-pay option, or an ECM booking it directly through one routine change.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** Recalculation uses zero distributions, respecting the 95% reinvestment floor and avoiding retained-cash double counting.

| Scenario | Founder result | Quality |
|---|---:|---|
| Adverse | –PLN25k | Clear stop |
| Conservative | PLN0.864m | Explicitly below PLN5m |
| Expected | PLN6.336m assuming zero net debt | Crosses only on unsupported volume/margin/multiple/ownership |
| Strong | PLN17.15m | Highly speculative; timing unspecified |

Expected assumptions require about 395 annual options to reach PLN5m; conservative assumptions require about 695. No qualifying event denominator supports either. The calculation favourably assumes zero debt; scale capital, taxes, ramp, and working capital are absent.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL as evidenced, despite a mechanically compliant test architecture.** The planned loss is PLN25k, no platform/inventory/family capital/job exit is needed, and one PLN12k prepayment would count. But payment requires a live painful event, firm workshop right, keeper prepayment, and ECM acceptance to align before any of them is evidenced. Workshop direct contracting and unsupported buyer value are existential dependencies. One delivered option would prove a sale, not 12-month/PLN100k repeatable control.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is qualitatively clear but quantitatively unknown. More workshops and work packages add heterogeneity and disintermediation. Polish language, counsel, and register familiarity help entry; an EU-wide ECM framework and cross-border incumbents prevent a durable local moat.

## Fatal Objections

1. Tier-3 control and one-routine-contract workshop/ECM disintermediation.
2. First credible PLN5m result needs 395–500 annual options with no event denominator.
3. Paid proof needs four uncontrolled parties/facts to align.
4. PLN12k buys intake priority, not completion or technical release.
5. Expected EBITDA breaks at the adverse stated unit cost and is thin at midpoint.
6. Public names do not reveal event timing; every sale is high-touch and tri-party.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A wagon-specific, dated, prepaid maintenance-capacity right with explicit late-arrival, excess-scope, and responsibility terms is sharper than informal maintenance brokerage.

## Recommendation

**Pivot.** Both hard gates fail and the exact candidate is not panel-eligible.
