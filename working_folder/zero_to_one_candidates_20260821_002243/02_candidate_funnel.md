## Mechanism-Class Coverage

The raw pool spans at least these distinct classes: mandatory-event routing, reserved capacity, title/custody, output/offtake rights, receivable purchase, bounded underwriting, site/land options, SPV share options, regulatory-right origination, data mandates, asset leasing, reuse pools, discontinuation inventory calls, and transaction-exclusive mandates.

## Finalist Freeze Boundary

Four briefs will be written. Each exact brief must state:

- the paid event and transaction owner;
- the control instrument and what survives a failed pilot;
- three founder-wealth cases with ownership and dilution assumptions;
- a <=180-day, <=PLN 40k paid proof and a <=12-month, <=PLN 100k control proof;
- contracted prerequisites rather than unspecified partners;
- kill criteria that reject the candidate before serious capital is deployed.

No finalist may inherit a score from this synthesis record.
