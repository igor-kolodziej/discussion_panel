# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Five-Day Parts Reserve

## Diagnosis

The regulation creates a real, recent fulfilment obligation, and the candidate offers a disciplined, capital-staged way to operationalise it. However, regulation proves neither an uncovered service gap nor willingness to prepay: small brands may already receive compliant factory support, while authorised representatives, wholesalers, and fulfilment providers can add the same reserved-bin workflow. The proposed contract creates temporary operational control, but not a durable scarce position. Most importantly, the founder-wealth case is extremely sensitive to an entirely unverified denominator of 60 qualifying CEE accounts and high contribution per account.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer:** manufacturer, importer, or authorised representative exposed to non-compliance, service failures, and customer dissatisfaction.
- **Payer:** the liable economic operator, if it values outsourced continuity more than factory, wholesale, or self-stock alternatives.
- **Beneficiary:** repairer and end user receiving an authentic part promptly; the operator benefits from documented fulfilment.
- **Authority holder:** market-surveillance authorities enforce the rule; the manufacturer or mandated operator controls authentic parts, repair information, and software access.
- **Distributor:** the operator's public ordering procedure and repair network; the venture controls it only after exclusive routing is signed.
- **Information holder:** manufacturer/operator holds installed base, model lifecycle, failure, and upstream-SLA data.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears fulfilment and bounded stock risk; operator retains statutory responsibility; supplier controls replenishment risk.
- **Transaction owner:** venture only during the named contract term and only for covered model/SKU orders.

## Lawful-Structure Check

The candidate does not depend on prohibited conduct. It preserves the operator's statutory responsibility, restricts activity to authorised authentic parts, conditions software access on lawful eligibility, and requires counsel to map scope. Counterfeit supply, unauthorised pairing-tool access, or unqualified battery carriage would breach the boundary, but none is indispensable. No legality-driven pivot is required; the binding uncertainty is commercial.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

The candidate circumvents early capital risk through prepayment, protected stock, a five-SKU schedule, outsourced 3PL operations, and exclusion of pairing-blocked models. It avoids consumer acquisition by targeting public economic operators. Those structures do not solve the central route-arounds: the operator can obtain a factory SLA, self-stock five bins, ask its existing representative/3PL to manage them, or replace the venture after six months. Cross-account data advantage remains hypothetical because parts may be proprietary/sparse and learning rights are absent.

## Overall Score

**50/100.** Weighted total 99.5 across 20 weight units. Good timing and proof design do not compensate for missing demand evidence, weak durability, and an unsupported wealth denominator.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** A signed prepaid schedule with exclusive named-SKU routing and protected stock creates real per-account control. It remains short, account-specific, and dependent on replaceable consent; no scarce supply or durable transaction ownership exists. The strongest copy risk is an existing representative, 3PL, wholesaler, or importer adding reserved bins and an SLA.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The adverse case is explicit. The conservative arithmetic—PLN 1.88m EBITDA x 4.5 x 75% = PLN 6.35m—is coherent but evidentially weak: contribution masks revenue/margin/variable costs; 60 accounts and PLN 68k contribution are unsupported; the multiple is optimistic for six-month contracts; CEE working capital, operations, sales, losses, and tax cash flows are not modelled; strong-success is absent; and intermediate reinvestment/capital milestones are not quantified. A 10% contribution miss already drops founder equity below the target.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly and conditionally.** Public records support prospecting; approximately PLN 13k is spent before proposed prepayment; 3PL and stock are delayed until terms/payment. The dependency chain is sequentially testable. Spending the full PLN 40k before prepayment would reverse the verdict.

## Expansion Check

The strongest beachhead is a small importer/representative with multiple low-volume models, documented upstream delay, and lawful authentic-part access. Poland is executable, not protected. CEE adds tax, nodes, language, verification, and contract costs. Incumbent entry is straightforward; pooled safety-stock savings and contractual learning remain unproved.

## Fatal Mechanism

Founder wealth depends on scaling a replaceable six-month fulfilment contract across at least 60 qualifying operators at high annual contribution before proving that this buyer population exists or lacks compliant upstream service. A lower denominator or weaker renewal economics collapses the business.

## Fatal Objections

1. No evidence supports 60 qualifying CEE accounts.
2. The duty does not prove an upstream service gap.
3. An incumbent can recreate the reserve with one stocking agreement.
4. Six-month routing does not support premium retention/valuation.
5. Model-specific, serialised parts may not pool.
6. Courier, returns, write-offs, batteries, replenishment, and support may consume margin.
7. The conservative wealth case lacks downside cushion.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A binding delivery-time obligation can support prepaid reserved capacity when inventory downside remains with the liable operator and every covered transaction is contractually routed through the reserve.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

1. De-duplicated evidence of at least 60 plausible CEE economic operators.
2. Current upstream SLAs/order evidence from five; at least three show a five-day gap.
3. Two written budget indications at proposed price.
4. One six-month prepayment plus exclusive named-SKU routing.
5. Authentic-part supply and protected stock before inventory spend.
6. One eligible order inside the period with full-cost contribution.
7. A credible renewal reason beyond temporary setup.
8. Rebuilt wealth scenarios using verified denominator, margins, capital, ownership, reinvestment, and duration-supported valuation.

## Recommendation

**Research further.** The candidate fails Founder Wealth and is ineligible for panel review in its exact wording.
