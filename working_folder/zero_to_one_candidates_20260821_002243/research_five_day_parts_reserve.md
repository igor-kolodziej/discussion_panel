# Bounded Research: Five-Day Parts Reserve

Research date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Evidence only; this memo does not score or revise the frozen candidate.

## 1. Covered-Account And Installed-Device Denominator

A live query of the [EU EPREL smartphone/tablet registry](https://eprel.ec.europa.eu/screen/product/smartphonestablets20231669), including discontinued models, returned 2,537 rows and 2,534 unique model IDs after deduplication. Counts moved slightly during pagination because EPREL is live.

For models declared available in Poland, the sample contained approximately:

- 2,454–2,457 model rows;
- 89 unique supplier organisations;
- 347 brands;
- 55 organisations acting as manufacturer, 21 as importer, and 31 as authorised representative; roles overlap.

This supports a broad registry universe of 89 Poland-declared supplier organisations, not a denominator of 60 qualifying accounts. EPREL does not publish annual units, installed base, upstream SLA status, safety-stock value, failure rates, or willingness to outsource.

The registry is highly concentrated. Seven umbrella operators represented about 1,460 Poland-available model rows—roughly 59% of the sample—including Apex CE Specialists, ERP Europe, Donghuababa, Ibertrust, ENIF Pegasus, JXGO, and CET Product Service. Consequently, hundreds of brands do not translate into hundreds of separately reachable payers.

Examples of smaller Polish-named/contact suppliers in the sampled records were mPTech (22 models), NTT System (10), Genuine Case (5), Megabajt (4), Maxcom (2), Mudita (2), Lechpol (1), and Barel Poland (1). These counts establish registry presence only, not candidate eligibility.

Using a deliberately narrow placeholder test—blank values, `/`, `-`, `N/A`, `0`, and similarly obvious non-links—1,408 of 2,454 Poland-available rows, or 57.4%, lacked a usable-looking spare-parts URL. Those rows were concentrated in 23 organisations, with 12 having placeholders on every sampled row. This is a public-data/compliance-hygiene signal, not evidence that parts are unavailable, that five-day delivery is missed, or that the organisation would buy the proposed service.

[EPREL supplier guidance](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/suppliers_en) warns that the registered supplier and consumer-facing brand can differ because one supplier may register multiple brands.

No credible model-, brand-, or importer-level installed-device denominator is public. [Eurostat Comext](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/international-trade-in-goods/database) provides aggregate CN-product trade quantities and values, while [UKE’s 2025 market report](https://www.uke.gov.pl/akt/raport-o-stanie-rynku-komunikacji-elektronicznej-w-2025-roku%2C670.html) covers communications services; neither maps devices to the obligated economic operator, SKU, or installed base.

## 2. Reserved-Parts Or Prepaid-Service Budgets

Verified analogues exist, but no public evidence was found for the candidate’s exact prepaid reserve:

- [Maxcom’s nine-month 2025 consolidated report](https://serwiskorporacyjny.maxcom.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Skonsolidowane-sprawozdanie-GK-MAXCOM-9-M-CY-2025-sig-sig-sig.pdf) reports a PLN 1.382 million warranty-repair provision at 30 September 2025, calculated from sales, warranty periods, historical defect rates, and repair costs. Nine-month revenue was PLN 69.156 million, including PLN 64.718 million from electronics. This verifies a material internal after-sales budget, but the balance covers the wider group portfolio and is neither a six-month prepaid parts reserve nor an external compliance-logistics contract.
- [NTT’s extended-warranty offer](https://ntt.pl/serwisu-komputerow-tco/) states that parts are held in NTT’s central service and authorised partners and prices optional extended cover at up to 15% of device retail price per year. [NTT Professional Services](https://serwery.ntt.pl/professional-services/) offers contractual response/repair times and original-parts support. These are prepaid/SLA analogues for computers and servers, not evidence of phone-importer reserve budgets.
- [mPTech](https://mptech.eu/en/o-nas) publicly describes its own local service, parts, distribution, and end-to-end private-label after-sales support. This verifies that at least one plausible Polish operator has an integrated alternative rather than a disclosed third-party reserve budget.

No public financial filing, tariff, procurement notice, or customer contract located in this research shows a low-volume phone/tablet importer paying PLN 4,000–7,000 monthly plus setup for five-SKU reserved capacity.

## 3. Contribution Economics And Substitute Pressure

Published Polish fulfilment prices establish a low commodity-logistics baseline:

- [Fulfillment Polska](https://fulfillment-polska.pl/en/price-en) publishes storage at PLN 39/m³/month and pick/pack from PLN 3.90 for a package up to one litre. Its [July 2026 B2B offer](https://fulfillmentpolska.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/oferta_fulfillment_polska_v2.pdf) lists monthly packages from PLN 999 for ten pallet/carton movements to PLN 3,999 for fifty, storage at PLN 80 per pallet/month, PLN 1,000 setup, and PLN 500 BaseLinker integration.
- [Padpack](https://padpack.pl/cennik) advertises no subscription or minimum and pick/pack from roughly PLN 3–4.
- [Sergo](https://sergo-trade.pl/) publishes storage from PLN 25/m²/month, pick/pack from PLN 3, dedicated areas, and 24-hour dispatch.

Storage and ordinary handling for five bins are therefore available well below the proposed PLN 4,000–7,000 monthly price. Any premium would have to arise from compliance assurance, authentic-parts sourcing, replenishment guarantees, stock-risk allocation, exception handling, or contractual liability. Public evidence supplies no quantities, part costs, failure rates, write-offs, courier costs, or claim rates from which the claimed 20–35% parts/handling ratio or PLN 68,000 annual contribution can be reproduced.

Direct wholesale substitutes already provide broad fulfilment capability:

- [Mobilepart](https://mobilepart.pl/pl/) advertises Polish B2B supply, original Service Packs and aftermarket parts, stable availability, and same-day dispatch for orders before 17:00; its GSM catalogue showed hundreds of positions.
- [FixShop](https://www.fixshop.eu/customer-service/wholesale-cooperation/) reports 500+ wholesale partners, 10,000+ spare parts, genuine/Service Pack/refurbished stock, and dispatch or delivery within 24 hours.
- [UPS Service Parts Logistics](https://www.ups.com/us/en/supplychain/logistics-solutions/post-sales-logistics) markets 850+ field stocking locations, same-/next-day service, inventory planning, and reverse logistics.
- [DHL’s service-logistics network](https://www.dhl.com/content/dam/dhl/global/csi/documents/pdf/csi-dhl-lshc-service-logistics-network.pdf.coredownload.pdf) offers field stocking, inventory visibility, time-critical parts delivery, returns, and billing. Its [Accuray case](https://www.dhl.com/sv-en/home/press/press-archive/2020/dhl-supply-chain-and-accuray-establish-global-service-logistics-platform-to-support-delivery-of-advanced-cancer-treatment-technology.html) documents an operating network of 34 warehouses across 16 countries.

These sources verify strong wholesaler/3PL substitute capability. They do not verify coverage of obscure proprietary SKUs, paired components, or supplier-restricted software.

## 4. Upstream Performance And Statutory Responsibility

[Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1670](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1670), as corrected by [Regulation (EU) 2025/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32025R2006), places the spare-parts duties on the manufacturer, importer, or authorised representative. Relevant parts must generally be delivered within five working days during the first five years and within ten working days later in the availability period.

The Commission’s official [economic-operator guidance](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/ecodesign-and-energy-label/legislative-framework/who-does-what-under-ecodesign_en) establishes that:

- a manufacturer may subcontract performance but must retain overall control and the information needed to demonstrate compliance;
- an authorised representative can perform specified tasks under a written mandate;
- an importer becomes responsible where there is no EU manufacturer or appropriately mandated authorised representative;
- a fulfilment service provider becomes the Article 4 economic operator only in limited direct-to-EU situations without an EU manufacturer, importer, or authorised representative; ordinary warehouse performance does not automatically transfer the spare-parts obligation.

Thus, the physical five-day duty can be performed through an OEM, authorised representative, wholesaler, or 3PL contract, while statutory accountability remains with the regulated role-holder. Where a manufacturer or authorised representative already provides a compliant ordering route, a second importer-side reserve is not legally required. Where the target is the responsible importer, outsourcing execution does not remove its responsibility and can still be contractually useful.

Public records do not reveal which sampled operators already have binding upstream replenishment SLAs, their actual order-to-delivery performance, or whether contract terms allocate indemnities and stock risk.

## 5. Realistically Securable Control Analogue

Public offers support the feasibility of an unknown founder buying generic, dedicated Polish warehouse capacity at low minimums. UPS and DHL establish the broader multi-client field-stocking model.

They do not establish a signed control point over the underlying transactions. No public evidence was found of a niche phone supplier granting an unknown intermediary:

- exclusive named-SKU order routing;
- consignment, replenishment, return, or buyback rights;
- guaranteed access to authentic or serialised parts and pairing software;
- sell-through, installed-base, or failure data;
- supplier-backed five-day replenishment;
- six-month importer prepayment;
- contractual control over renewal or compliance reporting.

The regulation’s professional-repairer access provisions do not automatically give a warehouse intermediary those rights.

## Proof Conditions Not Establishable From Public Evidence Alone

- Sixty accounts actually satisfy all filters: 5,000–50,000 CEE units, relevant post-June-2025 models, no five-day upstream SLA, low safety stock, and reachable decision-maker.
- Installed devices and annual sell-through by accountable entity and model.
- SKU-level failure incidence and whether five reserved SKUs cover enough incidents.
- Actual upstream fill rates and five-/ten-day order performance.
- A payer’s approved external budget and six-month prepayment authority.
- Authentic-parts pricing, minimum orders, replenishment, return, and buyback terms.
- Signed exclusive routing, data, or capacity rights.
- Fully loaded contribution after parts, obsolescence, losses, courier exceptions, claims, software access, labour, and renewal costs.

Those conditions require private sell-in/sell-through data, operational order logs, supplier quotations, customer budget interviews, and executed or executable bilateral agreements.
