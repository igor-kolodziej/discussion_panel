# Zero-to-One Re-evaluation: AwkwardSKU Surcharge Firewall

Fresh evaluator applied the complete `$evaluate-zero-to-one` skill. The prior score was not evidence. Advisory only.

## Diagnosis

The candidate addresses a real measurable loss and has an unusually testable narrow cell, prepayment, measurement protocol, staged capital, and kill criteria. It nevertheless underwrites a cost whose price, measurement, timing, claim decision, data flow, and termination rights remain controlled upstream. Research found technical access and resale analogues, but no executed instrument granting the combined rights the exact model needs. Both hard gates fail.

## Incentive And Power Map

- **Loss bearer:** merchant now; venture for conforming parcels under the proposal.
- **Payer:** merchant via prepaid labels.
- **Beneficiary:** merchant gets known COGS; upstream gets routed volume.
- **Authority:** carrier classifies/measures; carrier or broker controls access, price, claims, data, and termination.
- **Distributor:** founder outbound; referral channels are unsigned.
- **Information:** merchant holds packing facts; carrier holds scans/decisions; broker holds account invoices/feed.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears correlated charges, repricing, delayed invoices, liquidity, claims, and adverse selection.
- **Transaction owner:** without the bespoke agreement, carrier/broker owns shipment and claims events.

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

The candidate can be lawful if it is one disclosed fixed-price B2B transport service, uses truthful dimensions, has the correct postal role, and is not a separable insurance promise. It still needs the exact upstream agreement, registration/exemption, insurance opinion, data rights, and funded performance.

Narrow cells, prepayment, measurement evidence, breach exclusions, capped issuance, narrow exclusivity, reserves, and replay reduce variance. They do not create upstream permission, bind a cutoff/cost ceiling, transfer claims, cap past-label liabilities, prove demand/data learning, prevent repricing/termination, or eliminate direct quote/packaging/freight alternatives.

## Overall Score

**51/100.** Weighted points 101 across weight 20; `10 × 101 / 20 = 50.5`, rounded. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** Every claimed Tier-2 component remains prospective. The strongest copy risk is the carrier or broker, which already holds scans, invoices, claims, purchasing leverage, accounts, and distribution and can add fixed-cell pricing through a routine programme.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The stated conservative case reaches about PLN 6.76m on 672k labels, PLN 7 contribution, 4.5×, and 75% ownership, but all decisive inputs and reserve financing are unsupported; a strong-success scenario is missing.

Sensitivity at 672k labels:

- PLN 5 contribution, 4.5×, 75% → about PLN 2.23m founder equity.
- PLN 7 contribution, 3×, 75% → about PLN 4.51m.
- At 4×/75%, roughly PLN 6.50 contribution per label is required before debt.

The reserve does not cap liability, and no CAC, retention, headcount, claims workload, merchant concentration, or financing evidence supports the valuation.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL at current evidence.** The PLN 30k test fits the budget and employment, but before safe merchant prepayment the founder needs exact legal clearance, the full bespoke upstream agreement, three usable merchant histories, a positive replay cell, and merchant prepayment plus exclusivity. These are independent uncontrolled prerequisites, and available contract forms contradict the required economics. Final economics may also remain unsettled after day 180.

## Expansion Check

Adjacent cells, carriers, countries, and lanes each require new contracts and underwriting. Carriers/brokers hold superior data and relationships; merchants can redesign packaging or negotiate tariffs; specialist freight is a substitute. Market scale is sufficient, but control, economics, and distribution are not.

## Fatal Objections

1. No executed control instrument.
2. Supplier is adjudicator, data gatekeeper, repricer, and strongest copyist.
3. Reserve does not cap delayed/correlated contractual liability.
4. PLN 7–10 contribution lacks evidence and modest sensitivity breaks wealth.
5. First payment depends on multiple independent prerequisites.
6. No merchant payment, usable data, or exclusivity.
7. Data moat is speculative and incumbents start ahead.
8. Postal/insurance role is unsettled.
9. Economics may not settle by day 180.
10. Acquisition and operating leverage are unproved.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Underwrite one repeatable SKU/packaging cell from dispatch and finally settled carrier-cost data, converting post-shipment variance into a prepaid input.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

- Within 30 days/PLN 8k, a countersigned agreement or binding term sheet must grant competitive resale, own-price loss assumption, final-cost data, claim authority, finite liability, surviving data rights, and required exclusivity.
- Written postal/UKE and insurance conclusions for exact contracts.
- Rights to at least 2,000 finally charged records from three merchants in one cell within PLN 20k.
- Blind time-split replay stays positive in its worst completed month and retains at least PLN 6.50 fully loaded contribution under the 4×/75% wealth sensitivity.
- Contractual cutoff or full funding of the actual tail.
- One 100-label prepayment and 12-month cell exclusivity within PLN 30k/180 days.
- Renew only after a fully settled cohort is contribution-positive.
