# Zero-to-One Candidate: Accessibility Remedy Warranty

Frozen: 2026-08-21 CEST. Candidate generation only — not validated. Every price, conversion, claim frequency, remediation cost, customer count, margin, and multiple is a hypothesis unless explicitly sourced.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Covered non-micro Polish ecommerce merchants pay for a version-locked accessibility warranty on one merchant-controlled checkout form component, combining a manual assistive-technology baseline, venture-maintained fixes, first-remedy rights, and capped labour/service credits while excluding fines, lost sales, and external code changes.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build a reproducible evidence harness, analyse failure patterns, price capped remediation, and sell compliance-adjacent operational proof within five hours daily. A senior accessibility engineer, disabled assistive-technology testers, Polish accessibility counsel, and frontend contractor are required. The founder does not certify whole-service legal compliance.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first customer is a non-micro Polish consumer ecommerce operator covered by the Polish Accessibility Act, with one merchant-controlled web checkout form component, recurring releases, and no version-specific manual screen-reader/keyboard evidence.

Current workarounds are low-cost automated scans, one-off audits, web-agency remediation, accessibility overlays, monitoring subscriptions, internal QA, or customer support. The merchant retains legal responsibility, and a component warranty does not make the full ecommerce service compliant.

## Product Wedge

The first product is a PLN5,000, 60-day paid pilot for one named repository, component, production URL, and version range:

- baseline keyboard, focus, zoom, contrast, error, label, and two-screen-reader manual test;
- defined acceptance checklist and evidence pack;
- remediation of defects inside the merchant-controlled component;
- regression harness for the agreed states;
- first-remedy right after a covered regression;
- capped remedial labour and service credits if the venture misses its fix time.

The annual renewal hypothesis is PLN12,000 for the same bounded component, version process, scheduled manual retest, and a fixed labour/credit cap.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Audits identify failures but leave merchants to coordinate fixes and defend every subsequent release. A version-locked repair obligation may be more valuable than another report because it prices the operating follow-through. Public offers around PLN750–6k for audits and remediation from PLN999 show a cheap baseline; no source validates a PLN12k annual warranty.

## Underused Momentum

Poland's Accessibility Act has applied to covered ecommerce services since 28 June 2025 and excludes microenterprise services. It creates management attention and enforceable service duties, but responsibility remains with the merchant and the law does not require purchase from this venture.

## Beachhead Market

Initial filter:

- Poland-based consumer ecommerce service covered by the Act and not within the microenterprise service exclusion;
- merchant controls the checkout component source and release process;
- one stable web stack and repository, excluding marketplace-controlled payment UI;
- recurring releases and measurable checkout volume;
- decision-maker has an accessibility, customer-support, or enforcement concern;
- willingness to pay PLN5k for a component-only pilot.

An approximate GUS upper bound is 11.2k non-micro firms reporting some electronic orders. It includes B2B, marketplace, exempt, and non-controlled experiences and is not a qualifying buyer denominator.

## Incentive and Power Map

- Loss bearer: merchant from remediation, complaints, enforcement, support cost, and conversion loss.
- Payer: ecommerce director, digital product owner, legal/compliance lead, or agency budget owner.
- Beneficiary: disabled shoppers, merchant, customer support, and enforcement stakeholders.
- Authority holder: merchant controls releases; competent authorities interpret/enforce the Act.
- Distributor: founder direct outreach; later ecommerce agencies only under paid, conflict-disclosed terms.
- Information holder: merchant engineering/product, users, testers, and external payment providers.
- Risk bearer: venture for bounded fixes and credits; merchant for full-service compliance, fines, and business outcomes.
- Transaction owner: venture for the component warranty; merchant for its ecommerce service.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypothetical pricing and mature cost:

- PLN5k 60-day paid pilot;
- PLN12k annual component warranty;
- target annual delivery cost PLN5k–7k per customer after templates, including tester time, engineering, manual retest, support, and claim reserve;
- no cash indemnity, legal-fine coverage, revenue guarantee, or whole-site certification.

The annual fee competes with inexpensive audits and agency retainers. No observed claims data supports the target cost or margin.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First ten: target public Polish ecommerce sites whose legal entity is clearly non-micro only after verification; reproduce one checkout defect; sell the bounded paid pilot, not a free audit. Each pilot must name the repository, version, states, and exclusions.

First hundred: package one frontend-stack/component pattern only after three paid pilots produce reusable tests and similar defects. Agencies may resell only if the venture retains testing authority and first-remedy access. Whole-site bespoke remediation is excluded from the base case.

## Rule and Constraint Classification

- LAW / BINDING RULE — verified: covered ecommerce-service accessibility duties have applied since 28 June 2025.
- LAW / BINDING RULE — verified: microenterprise services are excluded from the relevant service duties.
- LAW / CONTRACT BOUNDARY — verified in principle: merchant remains responsible for the service.
- INSURANCE / CONTRACT BOUNDARY — counsel-required: exact service-credit and contingent-remediation terms.
- TECHNICAL LIMITATION — unknown: one versioned component can be isolated from external checkout dependencies.
- ECONOMIC LIMITATION — unknown: PLN12k recurring willingness to pay and 600 reachable customers.

## Lawful-Structure Check

The venture supplies testing and predefined remedial work, not legal certification or cash compensation for an uncertain loss. The contract excludes statutory fines, damages, lost revenue, payment-provider code, merchant changes outside the agreed process, content changes, unsupported browsers/assistive technology, and full-service compliance. Counsel must approve marketing claims, service credits, professional liability, data access, processor terms, and the insurance perimeter.

## Constraint-Circumvention Attempts

- One repository/component/version range avoids a whole-site guarantee.
- Manual assistive-technology testing avoids scan-only evidence.
- First-remedy access turns detected failures into an operational obligation.
- Release hooks and version locking make customer changes observable.
- Fixed labour and service-credit caps bound downside.
- External payment, identity, content, and merchant overrides are explicitly excluded.

The remaining failure is coupling: checkout behaviour may be inseparable from merchant and third-party code.

## Primary Control Point

The intended control point is the annual version-locked component licence/warranty, required release-test hook, and contractual first-remedy right supported by a proprietary regression corpus. Current control is Tier 3 until a customer pays and grants repository/release access. It could become Tier 2 if a reusable component is embedded across agencies; the statutory duty itself is not exclusive.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Build no scanning platform. Create two reusable reference components and a secure test/evidence workflow; contract one senior accessibility engineer and paid disabled testers; sell one PLN5k pilot before bespoke remediation. The merchant supplies staging access and its ordinary deployment process.

## Distribution Advantage

The founder can combine technical evidence with bounded economic risk and target covered merchants directly. There is no proprietary channel. Web agencies, accessibility consultancies, monitoring tools, and internal product teams already own the customer relationship and can add remediation retainers.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

If repeated, the venture can accumulate stack-specific accessible components, real assistive-technology regression cases, fix-time distributions, and claims-cost pricing. Expansion is from one checkout form pattern to adjacent authentication or payment-selection components only after separate versioned evidence. Generic audit reports and overlays are not the advantage.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish law, Polish-language evidence, local disabled testers, domestic enforcement context, and integration with Polish ecommerce agencies aid entry. Accessibility standards and frontend stacks are international; local adaptation is a speed advantage, not durable exclusion.

## Compounding Advantage

Potential compounding assets are verified regression cases, component fixes, tester protocols, stack-specific release hooks, false-positive history, and priced remediation frequency. They compound only if reuse outweighs manual testing and customer-specific integration.

## Founder Wealth Model

Adverse: no paid pilot after 50 qualified approaches or counsel rejects the capped structure; stop after at most PLN20k loss.

Conservative year-seven hypothesis: 100 annual customers at PLN12k = PLN1.2m revenue; 20% EBITDA margin gives PLN0.24m EBITDA. At 3x and 80% founder ownership, founder equity is about PLN0.58m.

Expected-success year-seven hypothesis: 600 annual customers at PLN12k = PLN7.2m revenue; 25% EBITDA margin gives PLN1.8m EBITDA. At 4x and 80% founder ownership, founder equity is about PLN5.76m.

Strong hypothesis: 1,500 customers at PLN15k, 30% EBITDA margin, 65% ownership, and 5x value. It requires productised components, agency distribution, low claims cost, and professional delivery capacity.

The conservative case does not reach PLN5m. Expected success requires roughly 5.4% of the overinclusive 11.2k upper bound before correcting for legal coverage, B2B, control, stack, and willingness to pay.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

While employed, reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash and distribute no more than 5%. Customer prepayments and service-credit reserves are not counted twice in terminal equity. No family capital is used before two unrelated paid pilots and one annual renewal.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–30: counsel fixes claims and exclusions; contract the accessibility engineer/testers; build two synthetic checkout reference cases; identify 50 legally screened prospects.

Days 31–90: reproduce exact issues for ten prospects without retaining personal data; conduct five buyer calls and issue three component-only proposals.

Days 91–150: one merchant pays PLN5k, grants staging/repository access, and freezes the covered version/process; the team tests and remediates the component.

By day 180: the customer accepts the evidence and signs or rejects a PLN12k annual renewal. A free scan, generic audit, letter of interest, or whole-site consultancy does not count.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-paid-proof maximum: PLN40k; planned at-risk loss: PLN20k.
- PLN5k legal/insurance review, PLN6k accessibility engineering, PLN4k disabled-tester fees, PLN3k secure test setup, PLN2k targeted acquisition.
- Contractor work above the reference build begins only after customer payment.
- Twelve-month founder-capital ceiling: PLN100k.

## Critical Dependency Map

1. A prospect is actually covered and controls the component.
2. One component/version can be contractually bounded.
3. Merchant grants source, staging, and release-hook access.
4. Disabled testers and engineer reproduce and fix defects economically.
5. Service credits remain a lawful service remedy rather than insurance.
6. Customer converts from PLN5k pilot to PLN12k annual contract.
7. Component/test reuse reduces manual cost.
8. A corrected reachable denominator supports 600 customers.

Dependencies 1–6 must align for the first meaningful proof.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Do not leave employment until at least 20 unrelated annual contracts are prepaid, five customers renew, claims/remediation cost stays below 50% of revenue for two release cycles, a senior engineer and tester bench deliver without the founder, acquisition repeats through at least one paid channel, liability remains bounded, and personal runway exceeds 12 months.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If recurring warranties fail, stop accepting contingent obligations. Reusable assets are synthetic accessible components, non-customer regression tests, tester protocols, and pricing evidence. One-off auditing or agency remediation is not counted as confirmation.

## Critical Evidence

- The Polish Act is in force for covered ecommerce services and excludes microenterprise services.
- Merchant responsibility remains; a supplier cannot transfer statutory accountability.
- The 11.2k figure is an overinclusive electronic-order upper bound, not a buyer denominator.
- Public audits/remediation are inexpensive and recurring monitoring already exists.
- No public evidence supports a PLN12k annual component warranty or its claims cost.

## Critical Hypotheses

- A covered merchant will pay PLN5k for the bounded pilot.
- It will renew at PLN12k rather than buy an audit or agency retainer.
- One component remains isolatable across releases and external dependencies.
- Manual testing plus fixes cost no more than PLN5k–7k annually at scale.
- Service credits are lawful and remain capped.
- Reusable components and agency access can reach 600 qualifying customers.

## Kill Criteria

- No PLN5k paid pilot by day 180.
- Merchant cannot prove coverage or control the checkout component.
- Counsel treats the remedy as insurance or marketing as false certification.
- External code/merchant releases make coverage unbounded.
- Annual renewal is rejected in favour of a commodity audit or ordinary agency retainer.
- Three pilots share too little reusable component/test structure.
- Corrected denominator or claims cost destroys expected founder wealth.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

POC: founder at most five hours daily; senior accessibility engineer; paid disabled screen-reader/keyboard testers; Polish accessibility and insurance counsel; frontend contractor as needed. At scale: product/engineering lead, tester panel, claims/remediation operations, agency sales, legal/quality review, and professional-liability cover.
