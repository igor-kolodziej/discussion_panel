# Zero-to-One Candidate: AwkwardSKU Surcharge Firewall

## One-Sentence Thesis

Become the transport reseller of record for four tightly measured Polish e-commerce SKU/packaging cells—starting with tubes, rolled mats, long trim bundles, and other conveyor-hostile products—by selling prepaid, immutable all-in domestic labels and absorbing the carrier's later non-standard or dimensional reclassification inside a capped portfolio reserve.

## Founder Fit

The founder's statistics, anomaly detection, finance, and data-engineering skills map directly to invoice replay, loss distributions, cell eligibility, and reserve control. Sales and analysis can be done in five daily hours while employed. No family-company dependence is required. The founder does not need to operate vehicles or warehouses.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The beachhead customer is a Polish merchant shipping 100–2,000 monthly units of one repeatable awkward product. It currently compares broker quotes, declares dimensions, and later receives non-standard/dimensional adjustments or chooses expensive manual freight. The priced loss is unpredictable delivered cost, administrative dispute time, and margin destroyed after the customer order was accepted.

## Product Wedge

The merchant prepays a block of 100 labels for one approved SKU, packaging specification, origin, weight band, and domestic service. The label price is final for that issued block. The venture buys/resells the transport service as principal, documents each packed unit at dispatch, handles permitted claims, and treats later carrier adjustments as its cost of goods unless the merchant violated the measured cell.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Carriers publish complex surcharge rules and brokers offer comparisons or bespoke quotes, but the merchant still bears post-shipment variance. The proposed product is not another audit: it converts a variable carrier invoice into a prepaid SKU-level price and owns the underwriting consequence. The gap remains a hypothesis until a resale-permitted carrier/broker contract and blind replay show positive contribution.

## Underused Momentum

Carrier machine-sort rules, dimensional scanning, and granular invoice adjustments create more observable loss data each year. Repeat e-commerce SKUs make the risk more homogeneous than generic parcel insurance or freight brokerage. Public tariffs prove material adjustment amounts, not customer demand for this product.

## Beachhead Market

Polish merchants with one repeatable cell, domestic volume of 100–2,000 parcels/month, packed length below the selected carrier's absolute maximum, no dangerous goods, and at least six months of parcel-level invoices. Start with one metro shipping origin and no returns guarantee.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer / payer:** merchant; prepays the immutable label block.
- **Beneficiary:** merchant obtains known COGS and one claims counterparty.
- **Authority holder:** carrier determines acceptance/classification under its contract.
- **Information holders:** merchant has packed-SKU facts; carrier has scans/adjustments; venture joins both histories.
- **Risk bearer / transaction owner:** venture is the disclosed B2B reseller-principal for eligible labels and bears capped reclassification cost; carrier performs carriage.
- **First paid event:** one merchant prepays 100 live labels.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Illustrative hypotheses, not facts: charge PLN 68–82 net per eligible label depending on cell; expected purchased carriage plus expected adjustments PLN 50–65; payment/support PLN 2; explicit reserve allocation PLN 5; target contribution PLN 7–10. A 100-label block therefore prepays PLN 6,800–8,200 and targets PLN 700–1,000 contribution. No label is issued outside the measured cell, and aggregate open adjustment exposure is capped at the funded reserve.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10: build a list from public stores selling rolled mats, posters, blinds, profiles, fishing rods, mouldings, and similar items; request a redacted six-month invoice/export; quote only after replay; require a paid block rather than an LOI. First 100: add outbound through packaging converters, e-commerce accountants, and 3PLs only after written referral terms; they do not control the contract or underwriting.

## Rule and Constraint Classification

- `CONTRACTUAL RULE`: carrier definition, exclusions, resale permission, invoice challenge window, and liability control each label.
- `ECONOMIC LIMITATION`: contribution must remain positive after all adjustments, loss/damage, return, support, and claim leakage.
- `OPERATIONAL FACT`: merchant packaging compliance must be observable before carrier pickup.

## Lawful-Structure Check

Polish transport/postal and insurance counsel must confirm the exact reseller/intermediary structure. The venture sells a transport service at a fixed B2B price; it does not promise a cash indemnity for a fortuitous loss. Merchant breach of the documented cell voids the fixed classification promise for that parcel. Client money is ordinary prepayment for labels, not a deposit or payment account.

## Constraint-Circumvention Attempts

A merchant can use a broker or seek a carrier custom tariff; a carrier can quote directly; a competitor can copy fixed pricing; the merchant can redesign packaging. The candidate survives only if cross-merchant cell data and routed volume lower reserve/cost enough to make switching economically unattractive. Exclusivity is limited to named eligible SKUs, so no false whole-account lock-in is assumed.

## Primary Control Point

Tier-2 contractual/transaction control: prepaid blocks plus 12-month exclusive routing of named cells, combined with carrier/broker agreements expressly permitting resale and claim handling, venture-owned adjustment history, a mandatory measurement protocol, and the funded portfolio reserve. None of these exists yet.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Obtain 2,000 historical parcel records before writing software. Secure one lawyer-reviewed resale route. Underwrite one cell with a wide safety margin, cap it at 100 labels, and fund a PLN 10k reserve. Manual spreadsheets and carrier portals suffice; automation follows only after a profitable adjustment cycle.

## Distribution Advantage

The entry list is public because merchants expose product dimensions and categories online. A concrete offer—"send the last six months for this exact SKU; receive one immutable 100-label price"—does not require status, content influence, or enterprise procurement.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Expand cell by cell, not carrier by carrier: more merchants improve the empirical loss curve, consolidated volume improves purchase rates, and claims history reveals which packaging changes reduce risk. Later expansion is to CEE domestic lanes and cross-border cells under separately replayed contracts, never by assuming Polish data transfers unchanged.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish carrier tariffs, broker contracts, claims practice, parcel geometry, merchant systems, language, and origin density require local adaptation. The same method can be re-estimated country by country; no legal monopoly is claimed.

## Compounding Advantage

Every paid label adds declared dimensions, dispatch evidence, carrier scan/classification, final cost, damage/return, and claim outcome. This can improve cell eligibility and price. Compounding fails if carriers will not provide parcel-level final data or merchants frequently violate packaging specs.

## Founder Wealth Model

All figures are hypotheses and values are before personal tax.

- **Adverse, year 4:** 80 merchants x 250 labels/month x PLN 3 contribution = PLN 0.72m annual contribution against roughly PLN 0.9m operating cost; no valuable going concern, founder wealth near zero, shut down.
- **Conservative, year 7:** 160 merchants x 350 labels/month x PLN 7 contribution = PLN 4.70m annual contribution; PLN 2.0m EBITDA after PLN 2.70m sales, operations, claims, and data costs; 4.5x EBITDA = PLN 9.0m enterprise value; 75% founder ownership after a 10% team pool and 15% outside dilution = **PLN 6.75m founder equity**.
- **Expected, year 7:** 300 merchants x 500 labels/month x PLN 9 contribution = PLN 16.2m annual contribution; PLN 9.2m EBITDA after PLN 7.0m operating cost; 5x = PLN 46m enterprise value; 70% founder ownership = **PLN 32.2m founder equity**.

The conservative threshold fails if contribution is below PLN 7, merchant retention below 80%, or the business requires working-capital equity despite prepayment.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable profit through year 5 into reserves, additional replay cells, claims operations, security, and outbound sales. Keep distributions at 5%. Label prepayment should finance purchased carriage; external capital is allowed only after two profitable cohorts and is modelled as the 15% dilution above. No personal guarantee beyond the approved tranche.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–30: counsel reviews one carrier/broker agreement and fixed-price terms; obtain at least five redacted datasets. Days 31–60: replay at least 2,000 parcels and define one cell with a 99th-percentile exposure cap. Days 61–90: sign one 12-month named-cell routing agreement and collect PLN 6.8k–8.2k for 100 labels. Days 91–180: complete the cohort, receive final adjustments, and show positive cash contribution after every cost. Maximum pre-proof cash spend: PLN 30k (PLN 8k legal, PLN 5k data/integration, PLN 10k reserve, PLN 7k contingency).

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Tranche 1: PLN 8k, legal/resale feasibility; stop without written permission.
- Tranche 2: PLN 12k, data replay and customer acquisition; stop without a prepaid block.
- Tranche 3: PLN 10k, live reserve/contingency only after prepayment.
- Twelve-month control proof may use up to PLN 100k, but open adjustment exposure may never exceed cash reserve. Founder maximum initial loss is PLN 30k.

## Critical Dependency Map

1. Signed resale/claim-handling permission—before quote.
2. Merchant final-invoice data—before underwriting.
3. Observable packaging protocol—before label issuance.
4. Carrier parcel-level final adjustments—before renewal.
5. Positive cell contribution and retention—before automation/hiring.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Do not leave employment until six consecutive months produce at least PLN 35k monthly gross contribution, two carrier routes are live, no single merchant exceeds 20% of volume, the reserve covers the modelled 99th-percentile 60-day exposure, and one operations hire can run daily exceptions.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If underwriting fails, stop issuing labels. Reusable assets are the lawful contract analysis, merchant invoice-replay dataset where retention rights permit, packaging measurement protocol, and carrier adjustment taxonomy. Prepayments for unissued labels remain refundable under the contract; no unfunded guarantee survives closure.

## Critical Evidence

- Published carrier surcharge rules and actual parcel-level adjustments.
- Written resale and claim-handling permission.
- Blind historical replay with no hindsight changes.
- One completed prepaid cohort with final carrier invoice.

## Critical Hypotheses

- One homogeneous cell has predictable positive contribution.
- Merchants pay for price certainty and grant narrow exclusivity.
- Prepayment avoids working-capital financing.
- Data improves margin faster than carriers or brokers copy the offer.
- The conservative 56,000-label monthly scale is reachable without labor scaling one-for-one.

## Kill Criteria

Kill immediately if resale is prohibited; fewer than three merchants provide usable histories; blind replay is non-positive after a PLN 5 reserve allocation; no merchant prepays 100 labels within 90 days of a valid offer; >5% of pilot parcels breach the cell; final cohort contribution is <=0; or counsel says the fixed-price structure is regulated insurance or requires capital outside the stated envelope.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Paid proof: <=PLN 30k and <=180 days. Control proof: <=PLN 100k and <=12 months. People: founder; Polish transport/postal/insurance counsel on fixed scope; part-time data protection/security review; no employee or carrier integration before proof.
