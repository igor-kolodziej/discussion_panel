# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Lab Analyzer Freedom Lease

Date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Fresh independent advisory evaluation of the exact frozen finalist. Advisory only; this is not a panel or external-validation score.

## Diagnosis

The candidate is legally plausible only in its narrow unmodified, manufacturer-intended-use form and correctly avoids inventory before a customer commitment. Its commercial thesis is unproved: incumbent bundles already combine analysers, reagents, controls, service, finance, and replacement, while “open” does not remove assay-specific conformity, QC, or serial-support constraints. The buyer denominator shrinks after ownership/lab-type filtering, and expected founder wealth falls below PLN5m after plausible fleet debt. Founder Wealth and Reachability both fail.

## Evidence And Assumptions

- IVDR Article 14 requires distributor checks; Article 16 can transfer manufacturer obligations after own-branding, intended-purpose change, or a conformity-affecting modification. Full refurbishment is separately consequential. [IVDR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/746/2025-01-10/eng)
- Lawfully placed legacy/transition devices may continue to be made available, making original placement, route, and serial evidence decisive.
- A distributor first bringing a device into Poland can have seven-day registration/information duties. [Polish Medical Devices Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/974/text/T/D20220974L.pdf)
- Polish laboratory-quality rules require documented methods, QC, corrective action, and records; the lab retains clinical responsibility. [2025 regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/961/text/O/D20250961.pdf)
- Open systems exist, but their reagent/assay designs, calibrators, controls, and support remain defined. [RIELE](https://riele.de/open-system-for-reagents/), [Diatron KleeYa](https://www.diatron.com/immunoassay-kleeya-chemiluminescent-analyzer)
- Sysmex reports seven-to-eight-year reagent/customer-care economics after placement; public procurements continue bundling analyser, consumables, QC, installation, and support. [Sysmex](https://www.sysmex.co.jp/en/ir/individual-investors/)
- Used medical-equipment finance exists, but ordinary leasing can leave title with the financier. [Pekao](https://www.pekao.com.pl/male-i-srednie-firmy/finansowanie-dzialalnosci/leasing-sprzetu-medycznego.html)

A fresh KIDL read found 2,554 sites, 1,851 exact strings, and about 1,723 simply normalised names. Removing three major chains, obvious public/hospital operators, and clear specialist labs left 833 sites/about 724 organisations. This is still an upper bound: it lacks ultimate ownership, analyser family, volume, contract expiry, supportability, authority, and deposit willingness. [KIDL](https://kidl.org.pl/laboratory)

Unsupported hypotheses include sub-PLN100k acquisition with complete provenance, PLN100k–160k annual placement revenue, 35%–40% gross margin, 15%–25% EBITDA, a four-year PLN20k commitment to an unknown entrant, finance without damaging guarantees/dilution, 100–200 supportable placements, and 3–4× cash-free/debt-free multiples.

## Incentive And Power Map

- Loss bearer: laboratory; patients/clinicians bear downstream delay/error.
- Payer: independent laboratory owner/director.
- Beneficiary: laboratory if cost/uptime improve; clinicians and patients indirectly.
- Authority: manufacturer controls intended purpose, laboratory controls clinical use, regulator enforces, financier may control title.
- Distributor: venture against OEMs and established reagent/equipment distributors.
- Information: lab, seller, OEM, reagent supplier, software licensor, and service engineer each hold indispensable fragments.
- Risk bearer: venture for asset/residual/credit/service/swap/supply; lab retains clinical responsibility.
- Transaction owner: venture would own its lease, but not conformity, software/parts, or clinical authority.

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

Leasing an unmodified lawfully placed serial under the manufacturer's identity/intended purpose can remain outside voluntary manufacturer status. Rebuilding, relabelling, changing software/purpose, or enabling undocumented assays can cross the line. The lab cannot transfer clinical responsibility.

Contract/deposit before purchase, no speculative fleet, serial-level diligence, no own branding, reagent/service/QC evidence, capped swap obligation, and matched minimum/finance are sensible. They do not make the deposit non-refundable, reserve the serial, reconcile financier title with claimed control, secure parts/software, prevent direct OEM renewal/purchase, or stop an incumbent copying the structure through one finance contract.

## Overall Score

**41/100.** Weighted points 81.5 across weight 20; `10 × 81.5 / 20 = 40.75`, rounded. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 current.** A venture-owned serial plus paid four-year minimum and exclusive routing could control one installation. None exists. Ordinary leasing may put title with the financier, while OEMs retain parts, software, conformity information, and consumable access. Strongest copy risk: an incumbent/distributor adding the same open/new analyser through one finance contract with a stronger service/replacement bundle.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The evaluator assumed zero distributions and 100% reinvestment, stricter than the required 95%, and counted retained cash only through net-debt reduction. Debt assumptions: PLN90k average asset, 80% four-year finance after the first unit, staged ramp.

| Scenario | Founder wealth | Diagnostic |
|---|---:|---|
| Adverse | –PLN30k | Clear stop |
| Conservative | About PLN0.21m | PLN0.9m EV less about PLN0.6m net debt, then 70% ownership |
| Expected | About PLN4.54m | PLN10.78m EV less about PLN4.3m net debt, then 70% ownership |
| Strong | About PLN14.4m | Requires 200 placements, large market share, finance, service network, and unproved economics |

At expected EV, terminal net debt must be below about PLN3.64m for 70% ownership to reach PLN5m. A plausible rapid fleet ramp exceeds it before reagent inventory, tax, guarantees, or swap stock. The strong 200-placement case equals 27.6% of even the loose 724-organisation upper bound. First-unit arithmetic can also exceed the founder ceiling: PLN30k diligence + nearly PLN100k asset – PLN20k deposit = as much as PLN110k before installation/working capital.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL.** The nominal PLN30k pre-proof plan fits the part-time/PLN40k envelope. But the deposit is conditional and unwound if evidence fails; no non-refundable paid-diligence element exists. An unknown entrant must secure a four-year minimum while lab, exact seller, reagent route, engineer, adviser, and service route align. KIDL has no expiry/volume/buyer fields and the serial is not reserved during diligence. This is a valid experiment design, not credible 180-day paid proof. Twelve-month/PLN100k control also fails because the asset budget can exceed the ceiling and finance may remove title.

## Expansion Check

Same-family replication can improve service and lender data, but every serial can differ in age, software, rights, and support; every assay route has separate evidence. The largest labs are excluded. Local language/service aid entry but do not block established OEMs or distributors.

## Fatal Objections

1. Expected debt-aware founder wealth is below PLN5m.
2. The 724-organisation figure is only a loose upper bound; 100–200 placements require material true-market penetration.
3. Serial parts/software/reagent/control/service access is not controlled.
4. External leasing removes title; debt finance preserves title by adding net debt/guarantees.
5. No evidence supports a paid four-year commitment inside 180 days.
6. First-unit cash can exceed PLN100k.
7. Incumbents already bundle financing, consumables, QC, service, and replacement.
8. Required regulatory, clinical, service, supply, and sales stack exceeds a solo part-time operation.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Match one exact, documented, unmodified serial and assay menu to a paid minimum contract before purchasing the asset.

## Recommendation

**Research further.** Both hard gates fail and the exact candidate is not panel-eligible.
