# Bounded Research: AwkwardSKU Surcharge Firewall

Research date: 2026-08-21 CEST. This memo resolves only the advisory evaluator's missing contractual-route questions. It does not score or revise the frozen candidate.

## Question

Can public evidence establish a carrier or authorised-broker route that permits the venture to resell transport at its own fixed price, expose the service to downstream merchants, control surcharge claims, receive parcel-level final-cost data, and retain narrow customer/SKU exclusivity?

## Verified Evidence

1. **Apaczka's API can expose service functionality to a client's counterparties.** Section 3(13) of the current API terms allows the client and its counterparties to use platform functions through the client's software. This supports technical downstream access, but it is not a grant of competitive resale economics, claims ownership, or exclusivity. [Apaczka API terms, effective 7 April 2025](https://www.apaczka.pl/app/uploads/2025/04/Regulamin-API-Apaczka-7.04.2025.pdf).

2. **Competitive use needs an individual agreement.** Section 4(1)(d) permits immediate API termination for activity competitive with Alsendo unless an individual agreement or arrangement expressly permits it. The frozen candidate's fixed-price parcel underwriting is competitive activity, so ordinary self-service API access is not its required control instrument. [Apaczka API terms](https://www.apaczka.pl/app/uploads/2025/04/Regulamin-API-Apaczka-7.04.2025.pdf).

3. **A public Furgonetka partner form permits a point to set a retail price and earn the spread.** The partner terms describe a purchase price, a merely suggested customer price, and remuneration equal to the negotiated customer price less purchase price. This is a genuine resale analogue. [Furgonetka Partner Point terms](https://c.furgonetka.pl/public.furgonetka.pl/media_help/2023_08_24_Regulamin_Punktu_Partnerskiego.pdf).

4. **That analogue passes carrier surcharges through rather than fixing them.** The same terms say additional carrier charges not listed in the service price are re-invoiced according to the carrier's charge. Claims must be submitted through the partner's platform workflow, and the partner receives no customer-group or territorial exclusivity without a separate written agreement. The public form therefore does not supply the candidate's bounded loss transfer, claim authority, or exclusive SKU route. [Furgonetka Partner Point terms](https://c.furgonetka.pl/public.furgonetka.pl/media_help/2023_08_24_Regulamin_Punktu_Partnerskiego.pdf).

5. **The broker remains dependent on carrier measurements and decisions.** Apaczka states that surcharge reports originate from carriers and that surcharge appeal decisions follow carrier decisions. It also warns that such charges usually arrive after 30–90 days and may be invoiced 6–12 months later. This confirms that the upstream supplier controls both the decision record and a long liability tail. [Apaczka surcharge and appeal explanation](https://www.apaczka.pl/centrum_pomocy/nieuczciwe-praktyki-konkurencji/); [Apaczka adjustment timing](https://www.apaczka.pl/centrum_pomocy/kiedy-otrzymam-informacje-o-doplacie-do-przesylki/).

## Unknown And Non-Public Facts

- No public carrier or broker instrument found grants all of: competitive resale, own-price loss assumption, parcel-level final-cost feed, merchant-side branding, assigned surcharge claims, a finite adjustment-closure period, and named-SKU/customer exclusivity.
- No individual term sheet, written permission, or account economics are available for this venture.
- Public partner terms do not establish that PLN 7–10 per parcel survives the full adjustment tail, damages, returns, support, bad debt, and correlated classification errors.
- No public evidence proves that merchants will prepay 100 eligible labels or accept 12-month narrow routing exclusivity.

## Decision-Critical Result

Public evidence establishes that technical API access and ordinary resale-like partner structures exist. It also establishes that the readily available forms preserve upstream control of surcharge measurement, claims, repricing, and delayed liability. The exact control instrument required by the frozen candidate remains an unexecuted bespoke agreement, not a resource an unknown founder can claim to control today.

The evaluator's bounded proof conditions therefore remain unmet. Re-evaluation must treat the required individual agreement, finite liability boundary, live contribution replay, merchant prepayment, and exclusivity as unknown rather than infer them from a generic API or partner programme.
