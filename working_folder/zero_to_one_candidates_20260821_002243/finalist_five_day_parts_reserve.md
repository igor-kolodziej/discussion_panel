# Zero-to-One Candidate: Five-Day Parts Reserve

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell covered niche smartphone and slate-tablet importers a prepaid six-month Polish fulfilment reserve for an exact model/SKU schedule, using consigned or buyback-protected parts and routed professional-repairer orders to make the EU five-working-day spare-part delivery duty operational without forcing each importer to build its own parts warehouse.

## Founder Fit

The founder can map regulated models/importers, estimate failure/stock risk, build reorder logic, and run a small outsourced fulfilment flow while employed. Finance and data skills fit reserve pricing and obsolescence control. No electronics repair, consumer support, status network, or family-company dependency is assumed.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The customer is an EU-established importer or authorised representative responsible for one or more covered low-volume private-label/niche smartphone or slate-tablet models. It must keep listed parts available and deliver in the prescribed period, yet a dedicated seven-year inventory/process is disproportionate. It currently relies on an overseas factory, keeps scattered stock, asks a generic wholesaler, or accepts compliance/service risk.

## Product Wedge

One importer prepays six monthly reservation fees. The contract names covered models, parts, availability period, minimum quantities, reorder triggers, five-working-day dispatch/delivery workflow, eligible recipients, and exclusions. The importer or manufacturer consigns stock or gives buyback/replenishment protection; the venture owns fulfilment execution and routes every in-scope order. The first live order must arrive within five working days using ordinary Polish courier service.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The rule places responsibility on each manufacturer/importer/representative, but generic GSM wholesalers optimise for popular parts rather than a seven-year, model-specific regulatory obligation. A small obligated importer may pay for reserved long-tail continuity even when unit sales no longer justify internal stock. This gap is a hypothesis; an upstream supplier that already guarantees five days makes the product unnecessary.

## Underused Momentum

Regulation (EU) 2023/1670 has applied since 20 June 2025 and combines availability, order-access, information, and delivery-time requirements. Energy labels and public product/supplier pages can help identify models. The rule proves duty, not installed base, failure rate, enforcement priority, or reserve budget.

## Beachhead Market

One Polish-established economic operator for an in-scope smartphone/tablet model placed on the EU market after the applicable date, with 5,000–50,000 CEE units, five observable high-failure listed part SKUs, no five-day upstream SLA, and stock that fits below PLN 25k. Exclude major brands, flexible-display exceptions, counterfeit/grey-market devices, hazardous battery carriage not pre-cleared, and models whose pairing software cannot be lawfully accessed.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer / payer:** obligated importer or authorised representative; prepays reserve and parts margin.
- **Beneficiary:** professional repairer/end user gets timely parts; importer reduces operational compliance risk.
- **Authority holder:** importer remains legally responsible; eligible repairer orders; market-surveillance authority enforces.
- **Information holders:** importer holds installed base/failure/availability dates; venture creates order and stock-turn history.
- **Risk bearer / transaction owner:** venture bears bounded inventory/fulfilment risk under consignment/buyback; importer retains regulatory liability; venture invoices fulfilment.
- **First paid event:** six-month reserve prepayment for one exact schedule.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypotheses: PLN 4k–7k monthly capacity reserve per importer; 20%–35% gross contribution on parts/handling; one-time PLN 5k schedule setup. A target mature account contributes PLN 48k–84k annual reserve plus PLN 15k–35k parts/handling gross profit. Stock is purchased only from prepayment or held on consignment/buyback; the reserve is revenue for kept capacity, not a refundable deposit.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10: identify supplier names from model documentation, labels, online listings, EPREL/product pages, and company registers; send the exact five-day duty and ask for model/part/installed-base data under NDA; require prepayment after a stock-gap report. First 100: extend to CEE importers using country-specific fulfilment nodes and separately verified responsible operators; acquire referrals from repair networks only under written, non-exclusive terms.

## Rule and Constraint Classification

- `LAW / BINDING RULE`: listed spare parts for covered models must be made available for the prescribed period and delivered within five working days in the first five years, subject to exact annex scope.
- `CONTRACTUAL RULE`: consignment/buyback, replenishment, order routing, and data rights determine venture control.
- `ECONOMIC LIMITATION`: reserve and parts contribution must cover long-tail obsolescence, courier, QA, returns, and software-access failures.

## Lawful-Structure Check

Product-compliance counsel maps each model/part to the annex and the venture's distributor/fulfilment duties. Only authorised authentic parts are handled; CE/product-safety, battery transport, WEEE, VAT, recall, storage, traceability, privacy, and professional-repairer eligibility are documented. Outsourcing never purports to transfer the importer's statutory responsibility.

## Constraint-Circumvention Attempts

The importer can self-stock, use its factory, contract a 3PL/wholesaler, discontinue a model, or replace the venture after six months. A wholesaler can add reserved bins. The candidate survives only if exact obligation mapping, pooled long-tail stock, order evidence, and replenishment history make the reserve cheaper and more reliable than those alternatives.

## Primary Control Point

Tier-2 contract control: exclusive Polish routing for named covered model/part orders during the paid term; consignment title or binding buyback/replenishment call rights; minimum stock schedule; serial/lot traceability; importer data access; and retained cross-account non-confidential stock-performance features. No statutory exclusivity is claimed.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Map 30 importers and ask for five exact data fields before leasing space. Use one secure 3PL shelf under a signed service order only after the importer prepays. Start with five non-counterfeit SKUs and one courier. No custom software, broad catalogue, repair shop, or speculative seven-year inventory.

## Distribution Advantage

The liable economic operator is identifiable from product documentation and public sales channels. A model-specific compliance reserve offer is narrower than generic logistics and can be sold directly without consumer acquisition or influencer credibility.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Pooling long-tail demand across importers can lower safety stock; proof of on-time orders reduces onboarding friction; stock-turn/failure data improves schedules. Expand from Poland to CEE only after local VAT, product responsibility, and delivery nodes are separately contracted. Later categories require a new product-specific law and are not assumed.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The EU duty is common, but responsible-operator identity, language, repairer verification, courier cutoffs, returns, battery carriage, tax, and market surveillance are local. Poland offers a bounded first node; defence is execution/data, not a Poland-only legal right.

## Compounding Advantage

Each paid order links model age, part, failure demand, recipient type, stockout, replenishment, delivery, return, and software pairing. Cross-importer forecasts can reduce capital and improve SLA. Compounding fails if importers prohibit any aggregate learning or each model has idiosyncratic, non-poolable parts.

## Founder Wealth Model

All figures are hypotheses, before personal tax.

- **Adverse, year 3:** 10 importers x PLN 40k annual contribution = PLN 0.4m against PLN 0.7m overhead/stock loss; close or sell inventory, founder wealth near zero.
- **Conservative, year 7:** 60 CEE importers x PLN 68k annual reserve-plus-parts contribution = PLN 4.08m; PLN 1.88m EBITDA after PLN 2.20m fulfilment, compliance, sales, and technology overhead; 4.5x = PLN 8.46m enterprise value; 75% founder ownership after team pool/financing = **PLN 6.35m founder equity**.
- **Expected, year 7:** 120 importers x PLN 95k contribution = PLN 11.4m; PLN 6.4m EBITDA after PLN 5.0m overhead; 5x = PLN 32m; 65% founder ownership after CEE fulfilment financing = **PLN 20.8m founder equity**.

The conservative case fails if 60 qualifying reachable importers do not exist, average contribution is below PLN 68k, or stock cannot remain consigned/prepaid.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Reinvest 95% of distributable profit for five years into insured 3PL nodes, compliance/QMS, replenishment calls, reserve stock, integrations, and direct sales; distribute 5%. Prepayment and consignment fund the initial node. Model 15% dilution in the conservative case and 25% in expected CEE expansion; no founder-guaranteed inventory loan.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–30: counsel maps exact annex/model/role and contract; build 30-importer list. Days 31–75: obtain model, installed-base, failure, current-stock, and supplier-SLA data from at least five. Days 76–105: sign one six-month prepaid reserve and consignment/buyback/replenishment schedule. Days 106–150: place five SKUs in a signed 3PL shelf. By day 180: receive and deliver one eligible live order within five working days and issue evidence. Maximum founder spend PLN 40k: PLN 8k legal, PLN 5k outreach/data, PLN 7k 3PL/insurance/setup, <=PLN 15k net stock after customer prepayment, PLN 5k contingency.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- PLN 8k legal scope; stop if target is not obligated or outsourcing structure fails.
- PLN 5k customer discovery; stop without five data-sharing importers.
- PLN 7k 3PL/insurance only after signed commercial terms.
- <=PLN 20k stock/contingency only after prepayment and buyback/consignment.
- Twelve-month control budget <=PLN 100k for five paid importers; no speculative inventory beyond funded schedules.

## Critical Dependency Map

1. Exact in-scope model and responsible operator.
2. Real installed-base/failure/availability data.
3. Authentic part source and lawful pairing access.
4. Prepaid reserve plus exclusive order routing.
5. Consignment/buyback/replenishment rights.
6. 3PL/courier process that meets five working days.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Stay employed until ten unrelated importers are prepaid, monthly recurring reserve exceeds PLN 45k, two quarters show >60% gross margin on reserve plus positive parts contribution after write-offs, 98% of eligible orders meet SLA, and one operations person can run daily fulfilment.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Consigned stock returns to owner; buyback stock is exercised; venture-owned stock is sold only through lawful channels. Reusable assets are model/obligation maps, importer contracts, order evidence, QMS/traceability procedures, and aggregate failure/turn data where rights permit.

## Critical Evidence

- Model-by-model annex opinion and responsible-operator identity.
- Existing upstream SLA showing the actual gap.
- Signed six-month prepayment and stock-risk allocation.
- Authentic lot/serial traceability and repairer eligibility.
- One order delivered inside five working days.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Niche importers have uncovered obligations and budget.
- Five SKUs capture enough demand to prove value.
- Importers accept routed orders and data rights.
- Stock risk can remain consigned, buyback-protected, or prepaid.
- Sixty CEE accounts at PLN 68k contribution is conservative.

## Kill Criteria

Kill if fewer than five of 30 importers share data; the first target already has a compliant five-day SLA; no six-month prepayment by day 105; no consignment/buyback/replenishment protection; stock requires >PLN 25k before payment; software/authorisation blocks lawful repairer fulfilment; the first eligible order misses five working days; or the reachable CEE denominator cannot support 60 accounts.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Paid proof: <=PLN 40k and <=180 days. Control proof: five paid importer schedules, two replenishment sources, and >=20 completed orders within <=PLN 100k and <=12 months. People: founder, product-compliance counsel, insured Polish 3PL under fixed order, part-time QMS/traceability adviser; no repair staff.
