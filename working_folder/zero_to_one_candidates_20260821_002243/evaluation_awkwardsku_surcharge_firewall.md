# Zero-to-One Evaluation: AwkwardSKU Surcharge Firewall

## Diagnosis

This addresses a real, measurable problem: merchants can discover reclassification charges only after shipment, while a fixed SKU-cell price converts variance into known COGS. The beachhead and test are concrete, and the founder fits the underwriting analysis. However, the venture underwrites a risk whose adjudicator, data gatekeeper, price setter, and strongest potential copyist is its carrier or broker. No resale permission, merchant exclusivity, final-cost feed, loss curve, or willingness-to-pay evidence exists, so the exact candidate is not ready to pursue.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer:** venture for conforming parcels; merchant for objectively proven cell breaches.
- **Payer/beneficiary:** merchant prepays for known delivered COGS; carrier gains consolidated volume.
- **Authority holder:** carrier measures/classifies; UKE/courts govern postal role.
- **Distributor:** founder outbound; proposed referrers are unsigned/non-exclusive.
- **Information holder:** merchant owns SKU/packing facts; carrier owns scans/adjustments; venture needs rights to both.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears tail adjustments, carrier repricing, liquidity, claims, and selection.
- **Transaction owner:** venture only after a lawful principal/agency structure and downstream contracts; otherwise carrier/broker owns the event.

## Lawful-Structure Check

No illegal conduct is required if the venture sells one fixed-price B2B transport service, makes no separate cash-indemnity promise, uses objective breach terms, and adopts the correct postal role. Indispensable boundaries are written resale/agency/claims authority, UKE registration or exemption, truthful carrier identity/dimensions, merchant data rights, and insurance-regulatory confirmation. If resale is unavailable or the promise is insurance, this exact candidate fails.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

Narrow cells, prepayment, packing protocol, breach carve-out, capped issuance, cell exclusivity, and joined data are better than generic brokerage. They do not cap liability for issued labels, shorten carrier-controlled adjustment tails, prevent post-term copying, remove direct tariffs/specialist carriage/packaging redesign, or prove a data learning curve.

## Overall Score

**62/100 advisory score.** Weighted points 123 / 20 = 6.15, rounded. The score stays below pursue quality because a carrier/broker can reproduce the offer unless merchant-linked data produces a demonstrated barrier.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 at current evidence.** Prepaid blocks and 12-month cell exclusivity could create contract control, but none exists and carrier permission is speculative. It can become Tier 2 after written resale, claims, final-cost data, prepayment, and exclusivity. Established brokers/carriers already possess distribution, purchasing leverage, scan outcomes, claims operations, and working capital.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass narrowly for paid evidence, not proven economics.** A PLN 6.8k–8.2k prepaid block can be attempted without software, vehicles, or leaving work, inside PLN 30k. Legal role, written permission, histories, a positive cell, then prepayment can be tested sequentially. Because adjustments may arrive after 30–90 days or later, day-180 prepayment proves demand; only a finally settled cohort proves economics.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is strong. Adjacent cells/carriers/countries each require new underwriting. Carrier/broker entry is easy; Polish adaptation is useful but not exclusionary. Merchants can redesign packaging, negotiate direct tariffs, use bespoke brokers, switch to pallet/freight, or use specialist irregular carriers.

## Sensitivity

- PLN 1 contribution change at conservative volume changes EBITDA by PLN 672k.
- Founder target at 4x/75% needs roughly PLN 6.50 contribution.
- PLN 5 contribution implies about PLN 1.98m founder equity.
- PLN 7 contribution at 3x implies about PLN 4.51m.
- PLN 5 reserve covers only ~13.5% incidence of an otherwise unpriced PLN 37 DPD charge before correlated errors/volumetric fees.

## Fatal Mechanism

The venture underwrites a cost determined and invoiced by the same supplier that controls source data, can change terms, and can copy the offer. Without enforceable data and liability boundaries, a nominal reserve does not cap already-issued-label exposure.

## Fatal Objections

1. No signed resale, claims, final-cost, prepayment, or exclusivity rights.
2. No evidence PLN 7–10 contribution survives adverse selection, drift, claims, returns, support, late adjustments, and default.
3. Incumbent data/capability may dominate the proposed moat.
4. Conservative wealth fails under modest margin/multiple compression.
5. Postal role and insurance perimeter remain counsel-required.
6. Dataset access, trust, CAC, and retention are unmeasured.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Underwrite one repeatable SKU/packaging cell from joined dispatch and final-cost data, converting post-shipment variance into a prepaid business input rather than auditing it afterward.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

1. Legal memo confirms postal role/UKE, data/claims authority, and non-insurance structure.
2. One carrier/authorised broker expressly permits the full downstream structure and final-charge access.
3. Three merchants supply >=2,000 usable homogeneous-cell records.
4. Blind replay stays positive after all costs/reserve and in its worst completed month.
5. Contractual adjustment tail is funded rather than assumed closed in 60 days.
6. One merchant prepays 100 labels and signs 12-month exact-cell exclusivity.
7. Finally settled cohort has positive cash contribution.

## Recommendation

**Research further.** Route to kill if resale is unavailable, legal structure requires disproportionate capital, replay fails, or no merchant prepays. The exact candidate fails Founder Wealth/control today and is ineligible for panel review.
