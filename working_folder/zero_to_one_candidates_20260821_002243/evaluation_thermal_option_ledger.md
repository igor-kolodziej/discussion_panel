# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Thermal Option Ledger

## Diagnosis

The valuable insight is converting feasibility work into transferable project rights rather than producing another report. The frozen candidate does not secure the economically essential right, however: its “option” provides temporary development exclusivity and only a first negotiation for a future heat agreement. It also asks the source to pay for the study while surrendering exclusivity to an unknown, undercapitalised developer, an exchange inferior to keeping its rights and using an established engineer or ESCO. The wealth model then assumes unusually high assignment fees and conversion rates without buyer evidence.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer:** source bears cooling electricity and unused-heat opportunity cost; an offtaker bears alternative fuel cost; the venture bears pre-development loss.
- **Initial payer:** source pays for the study.
- **Later payer:** offtaker, utility, infrastructure investor or equipment developer is expected to pay a reservation or assignment fee.
- **Beneficiaries:** source receives a report and possible future heat economics; offtaker may receive cheaper or lower-carbon heat; venture receives development economics.
- **Authority holder:** source controls plant cooperation and heat-contract consent; landowners and authorities control route and permits; offtaker controls demand commitment.
- **Distributor:** founder-led outbound and later regional originators; no channel is owned or contracted.
- **Information holder:** source holds operational data; engineer holds technical interpretation; future offtaker holds demand data.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears study-gap and failed-option costs; future SPV/investor is assumed to bear construction and performance risk.
- **Transaction owner:** the venture owns only temporary development exclusivity after signing. It does not own heat, a binding heat purchase agreement, route rights, or project finance.

## Lawful-Structure Check

No illegal, deceptive, coercive or harmful reliance is present. Keeping the proof stage to measurement and advice, and ring-fencing later construction and supply in a separately permitted SPV, is a coherent lawful boundary.

The unresolved legal issue is economic as much as legal: the frozen agreement promises an “option” but specifies only first negotiation over the eventual heat agreement. Even if exclusivity and non-circumvention are enforceable, this may not create an exercisable right to heat on defined volume, availability, price, liability or termination terms. Counsel can test validity and transferability, but legal validity alone cannot create source or buyer willingness.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

The candidate already attempts to:

- preserve source control of on-site use;
- give the source a standalone report;
- fund engineering from a deposit;
- screen nearby demand before expensive work;
- use exclusivity, non-circumvention and a penalty against direct bypass;
- keep construction and equipment capital outside the venture.

These reduce capital loss but do not solve the central exchange. The source can obtain a free preliminary concept or full-stack delivery from an established provider while retaining its project rights. An offtaker can decline to reserve until a definitive heat contract, route and economics exist. Different payer, ownership and risk-allocation structures remain untested, but adopting one would materially change the frozen candidate and belongs in a pivot.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 for the monetisable control point.** A signed agreement could create real temporary exclusivity, but the frozen wording does not secure a definitive heat supply, route, offtaker commitment or exercise economics. Monetisation therefore remains dependent on later negotiations with multiple uncontrolled parties.

The strongest copy risk is an established engineer or ESCO adding an exclusive-development clause to its existing free/pre-financed feasibility process. It would enter with stronger references, engineering capacity, finance access and buyer relationships.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.**

The adverse and expected cases are useful, but the required strong-success scenario is absent. More importantly, the “conservative” case is not conservative:

- Its equity value is `75% × 5 × (0.15m + 6 × 0.35m − 0.80m) = PLN 5.44m`.
- It needs at least approximately **PLN 331k net contribution per assignment** at six annual assignments merely to reach PLN 5m.
- One fewer assignment reduces founder equity to about **PLN 4.13m**.
- A 4× rather than 5× multiple reduces it to about **PLN 4.35m**.
- A modest assignment-fee haircut to PLN 300k reduces it to about **PLN 4.31m**.

Thus the path clears the target only if six high-value assignments, a 40% conversion rate, 75% ownership and a 5× multiple all hold together. Ownership of 75%/70% is also not reconciled with the stated 15% dilution. The model provides no comparable transaction evidence, buyer budgets, reachable source count or proof that site-specific development labour can be delegated without eroding margin.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.**

The financial staging itself is compatible with employment: approximately PLN 12k precedes a signed customer, the maximum stated exposure is PLN 30k, and engineering is commissioned only after a deposit. The gate fails on commercial dependency quality, not capital.

The first paid event requires one source to:

1. trust a non-engineer unknown founder with plant data;
2. pay PLN 15k–25k despite established alternatives;
3. simultaneously surrender 24 months of exclusivity;
4. accept uncertain transfer to a future SPV;
5. do so before a buyer or definitive heat agreement exists.

No evidence supports that exact bundle, and the source pays both cash and opportunity cost while the venture commits neither construction capital nor a binding offtaker. Five hours/day can support one study, but not overcome the exchange defect.

Bounded conditions required before reconsideration:

- Polish counsel confirms the exact frozen instrument is enforceable, assignable and contains sufficiently definite exercise mechanics.
- One qualifying source prepays and signs the exact bundled study-plus-option—not a study-only compromise.
- The engineer accepts a fixed scope within the stated cost cap.
- One unrelated buyer pays a non-refundable reservation against that exact controlled opportunity within the 12-month capital cap.
- Buyer evidence supports assignment economics materially above the wealth-model break-even, rather than an expression of interest.

## Sensitivity

The candidate is most sensitive to:

1. **Assignment contribution:** the conservative wealth case fails below roughly PLN 331k per closing.
2. **Annual closing count:** five rather than six conservative-case assignments fails the wealth target.
3. **Terminal multiple:** 4× rather than 5× fails the conservative case.
4. **Option quality:** if the buyer requires a definitive heat purchase agreement, route or permit before payment, the proposed option has little assignable value.
5. **Source conversion:** any preference for study-only work destroys the stated control point.
6. **Bespoke labour:** if each assignment requires founder-led negotiation across source, land, utility, engineer and investor, the claimed operating leverage disappears.

## Expansion Check

The proposed food/cold-storage beachhead is coherent, and local heat markets do reward geographic knowledge. Expansion to additional industrial clusters is possible after a successful assignment. Nevertheless, every source-offtaker pair has different temperature, seasonality, route, land, contract and financing conditions. HeatRec, Veolia and other national actors can enter this wedge with one contract change and materially greater execution credibility; the family business offers introductions but no exclusive distribution or thermal capability.

## Fatal Objections

1. **Fatal mechanism:** the source is asked to pay for the study and give away exclusivity, while the venture supplies no committed offtaker or project capital.
2. The purported option secures only development exclusivity and first negotiation, not a defined heat-supply right that a buyer can confidently acquire.
3. The conservative wealth case depends on six unusually valuable annual assignments with almost no downside tolerance.
4. Established full-stack incumbents can copy the contractual mechanism and offer superior credibility, financing and execution.
5. Regulatory momentum is overstated for the exact beachhead: the EU trigger is above 8 MW total input plus new/substantial refurbishment, while Polish UC121 was still proposed.
6. Site confidentiality and bespoke multi-party work may prevent the promised data and process compounding.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A feasibility engagement becomes strategically valuable when it creates a controlled, transferable project right instead of leaving the customer with a freely shoppable report.
