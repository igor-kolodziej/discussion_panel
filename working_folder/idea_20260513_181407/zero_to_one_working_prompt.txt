Idea: SurfaceMatch Claim Stock.

SurfaceMatch is a claims-stage exact-match material rail for Warsaw/Mazowieckie water-damage and property-restoration contractors handling cashless or insurer-documented repairs. It is not a general tile outlet, marketplace, renovation sourcing service, or dashboard.

Customer: small and mid-sized water/fire/property-restoration firms, drying firms, property managers, and insurer-repair contractors that must decide whether damaged tile/flooring/wall-surface areas can be repaired locally or require larger replacement because matching material cannot be found.

Product wedge: contractors submit every relevant live claim sample/photo/dimension set before quoting replacement. SurfaceMatch returns within 48 hours:

1. stocked/reserved exact match;
2. plausible near-match with evidence;
3. or a claims-grade no-controlled-match note.

If stock exists, SurfaceMatch reserves/sells it immediately. If no stock exists, the no-match note helps the contractor document scope honestly instead of making unsupported calls.

Primary control point: a combination of:

1. signed contractor routing pilots for live claim samples before replacement decisions;
2. owned/reserved discontinued and hard-to-find Polish/EU tile/flooring/trim stock indexed by image, dimensions, texture, manufacturer marks, batch clues, and case context;
3. first-call/consignment agreements with tile outlets, renovation contractors, property cleanout operators, and restoration firms for leftover or discontinued material.

The controlled resource is not generic inventory. It is claims-stage sample flow plus scarce indexed stock and outcome data.

Cold start under 2 months and 100,000 PLN:

- Contact 60 Warsaw/Mazowieckie restoration/drying firms that advertise cashless insurer settlement.
- Sign 3 pilot agreements where they route every relevant surface-matching claim for 60 days.
- Charge 250-500 PLN per paid search, credited against material if purchased.
- Contact 80 supply sources: tile/flooring outlets, installers, renovation crews, cleanout operators, property managers, and OLX/Allegro sellers.
- Secure 10 written first-call/consignment agreements.
- Buy or option 300-800 indexed samples/lots, prioritizing tiles/flooring used in Polish apartments from the last 10-25 years.
- Budget: 15-35k PLN initial stock/options/storage, 10k PLN contractor/supplier outreach and travel, 5-10k PLN sample photography/indexing, 5-10k PLN part-time ops help.

Proof before serious capital:

- 10 paid searches;
- 3 material orders or reservations;
- 3 contractor routing pilots;
- 10 supply agreements;
- 2 documented cases where matching/no-match evidence changes repair scope, quote speed, customer approval, or insurer documentation.

Hidden insight: restoration contractors and adjusters do not mainly need generic renovation sourcing. They need a fast decision before the replacement scope is set. A missing 15-year-old tile or panel can turn a small repair into a larger replacement, delay settlement, or create a dispute. The scarce asset is not old material alone; it is the live claim sample routed early enough, matched against controlled stock, then converted into a repair or a credible no-match record.

Underused momentum:

- Polish property-damage restoration firms already advertise insurer documentation, estimates, repairs, and cashless/direct settlement.
- Polish insurers paid 6.2B PLN in property-insurance claims in 2024, up 48.6% year over year, according to PIU.
- Polish tile outlets already sell ends of collections, small quantities, odd colors, and overstock; supply is fragmented and poorly indexed.
- US examples like itel/Nearmap and Nativo prove material matching and discontinued-stock warehouses can be real claims workflow assets, but they do not own Polish apartment-stock SKUs, local supply, Polish contractor workflows, or Polish-language claim documentation.

Why it is not merely a service or marketplace:

- It starts with signed routing of live claims before scope decisions, not open listings.
- It owns or reserves physical stock and samples, not only information.
- It creates outcome data from real claims, not a generic database.
- It monetizes both successful matches and controlled-source no-match evidence.
- The POC fails unless contractor routing, supply agreements, paid searches, and material reservations are proven.
