## Common Fatal Objections

- Physical inventory alone is insufficient unless demand flow is routed and repeated case data compounds.
- Broad spare-parts banks fail because testing/trust/working-capital requirements are larger than the founder's POC budget.
- Commodity/routed-flow ideas fail when the signed agreements are soft and incumbents can bypass the founder.
- Several shapes would become specialized brokers, not domination-oriented companies.

## Pivot Decision

The only candidate worth revising is the exact-match materials concept.

Pivot from:

> A search-and-inventory desk for discontinued tiles/flooring.

To:

> A claims-stage material matching rail that controls live sample intake from restoration contractors, owns/reserves discontinued stock, and builds a claim-resolution dataset showing match, no-match, avoided replacement, or justified replacement outcomes.

The revised version must prove:

- 3 contractor routing pilots, where every relevant tile/flooring surface-damage case is submitted before replacement.
- 10 first-call or consignment supply agreements for discontinued/overstock material.
- 300-800 indexed samples/lots within 60 days.
- 10 paid searches and 3 material orders or reservations.
- At least 2 documented cases where match evidence changes repair scope, insurer/customer approval, or contractor quote speed.
