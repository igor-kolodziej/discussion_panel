# Simulated Zero-To-One Scores: Round 5

Six local operating-rail candidates were scored by fresh simulated reviewers.

## Results

| Candidate | Score | Verdict | Main Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| TurnoverKit Apartment Continuity Rail | 76 | Proceed narrowly, but below gate | Stronger than parts banks due unit mapping and maintenance-ticket routing, but property managers/handymen can copy top-stock mapping. |
| DischargeKit Home-Readiness Rail | 74 | Proceed narrowly, but below gate | Real urgent pain, but operationally exposed and copyable by existing medical rental shops/care agencies. |
| Lithium SafeBox For E-Bike And Scooter Shops | 72 | Permission-first pilot only | Real safety pain, but legal handling/recycler dependency blocks strong control within 2 months. |
| SteriStandby Autoclave Continuity Pool | 71 | Narrow or kill | Real continuity need, but regulatory/trust burden and autoclave-service incumbents cap it. |
| Kaucja Backroom Bank | 68 | Narrow or kill | Good DRS bottleneck, but operator/legal handoff and incumbent logistics copy risk cap it. |
| Last-Vial Clinic Routing Desk | 61 | Pivot-narrow / kill | Strong failed-prescription insight, but legal/pharmacy availability incumbents and trust risk dominate. |

## Decision

No candidate in this round meets the strict simulated `>90` gate. Do not send any of these to real Zero-To-One validation.

## Pattern

The ideas are often practical and locally useful, but the simulation keeps rejecting them as domination candidates because:

- the first control point is operational but not hard;
- existing trusted incumbents can copy after demand proof;
- the market is too narrow or low-AOV;
- legal/regulated dependencies cannot be secured by an unknown founder within 2 months.
