# Research Notes

## Leading Candidate: Insurance Restoration Exact-Match Materials Desk

Working name: **SurfaceMatch Claim Stock**.

### What The Market Evidence Suggests

- Polish property-damage restoration already has a channel that can pay or route demand: damage-restoration firms advertise direct/cashless settlement with insurers, documentation, estimates, drying, repairs, and claim support.
- Discontinued and overstocked surface materials exist as fragmented supply: Polish tile outlets sell limited quantities, ends of collections, odd colors, and small lots.
- The "matching problem" is a known property-claims issue internationally: partial repair can expand into broader replacement when the original material is discontinued or visually mismatched.
- US examples prove the category can become a specialized B2B workflow, not just a hobbyist search: itel/Nearmap offers material matching for repair claims and links matching to discontinued stock reservations; Nativo stocks thousands of discontinued flooring selections.
- The Polish local wedge is not "invent matching"; it is building a local, claims-oriented controlled stock and search workflow for Polish/EU tiles, panels, trims, and small-lot repair materials where US databases and marketplaces do not cover local SKUs, language, suppliers, or restoration workflows.

### Key Sources

- Polska Grupa Osuszająca: restoration/drying network states that it can handle the process cashlessly via claim assignment/cesja. Source: https://pgopartner.pl/

### Strategic Interpretation

This idea should not be positioned as a general marketplace, tile outlet, DIY search site, or renovation-sourcing service. The high-value wedge is **claims-grade exact-match repair**:

- A restoration contractor sends photos/sample/dimensions from a live damage case.
- SurfaceMatch either finds a stocked/reserved exact match, finds a near match with evidence, or certifies "no controlled match found" so the contractor/adjuster can scope replacement honestly.
- If stocked material exists, it is reserved immediately and shipped/picked up fast.

### Control Point To Prove In 60 Days

Resource needed:

- Owned/reserved small-lot stock of discontinued/hard-to-find Polish/EU tiles, panels, plinths, trims, mosaics, and accessories.
- First-look agreements with tile outlets, small contractors, renovation crews, and property cleanout operators.
- Paid or signed demand from restoration firms that want a 48-hour match search before quoting full replacement.

Proof target:

- 500-1,000 indexed physical SKUs/small lots or at least 150 highly photographed lots if starting compactly.
- 10 written first-look/supplier agreements.
- 20 restoration/property-manager interviews.
- 5 paid searches or LOIs from restoration contractors.
- 2 cases where match availability changes repair scope, quote, or customer/insurer conversation.

### Main Risks

- Market may be too operational and low-ticket if not tied to insurance restoration economics.
- Restoration firms may prefer full replacement because it increases job value.
- Insurers may not directly care in Poland unless repair partners feel margin or approval pressure.
- Exact matching may fail too often without a much larger inventory.
- Copy risk rises if the business is only a search workflow; owned/reserved inventory and supplier first-look rights are mandatory.
