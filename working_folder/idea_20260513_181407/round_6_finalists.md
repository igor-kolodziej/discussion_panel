# Round 6 Finalists

Final synthesis round. These candidates try to avoid the repeated failure pattern of small low-AOV local stock businesses and copyable operational services.

## 1. WhiteGoods Donor Network

Exact-compatible used appliance parts sourced from returned, cosmetically damaged, unsellable, or scrapped washing machines, dishwashers, ovens, fridges, and coffee machines. The buyer sends a model plate photo; the service returns a tested donor part or repair kit.

Control point: weekly pickup rights from refurbishers/e-waste/retail return channels plus model-plate compatibility corpus.

## 2. Off-Peak Licensed Kitchen Slot Bank

Prepaid evening/weekend production slots in licensed canteen, hotel, school, and catering kitchens for micro food brands that need 50-500 compliant units without leasing a kitchen.

Control point: fixed slots, SOP templates, and batch records from real production runs.

## 3. Deposit-Native Event Serviceware Pool

Owned reusable cup/plate/crate pool plus washing capacity and deposit/loss ledger for recurring mid-size venues, food halls, outdoor markets, and corporate events.

Control point: physical inventory, washing slots, recurring venue rights, and return/loss data.

## 4. Craft Keg And Returnable Packaging Recovery Rail

Delegated recovery, identification, pickup, and resale/return routing for lost kegs, taps, crates, and returnable packaging between Polish craft breweries, pubs, and distributors.

Control point: brewery asset registry, first-call pickup rights from pubs/distributors, and recovered asset transaction flow.

## 5. Hospitality Pattern Stock Bank

Exact discontinued restaurant/hotel tableware, glassware, cutlery, buffet trays, room fixtures, and branded operational spares acquired from closures/liquidations and sold to operators who need to preserve visual/service consistency without replacing the whole set.

Control point: acquired matched lots plus pattern/brand/hotel-chain compatibility catalog.

## 6. Auto Workshop Loaner Tool Pool

Reserved access to high-cost specialty tools used rarely by independent garages: timing kits, diagnostic adapters, alignment plates, calibration fixtures, pullers, crimpers, and model-specific service tools.

Control point: owned tool pool, deposits, local garage routing, and usage/demand data.
