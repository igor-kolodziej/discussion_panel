# Physical / Routed Control Shortlist

## Selection Logic

After the compliance/document workflow round failed, the next finalist set favors ideas where the first control point is physical inventory, first-call supply, prepaid capacity, or repeated routed transactions.

## Finalists For Simulated Round 4

### 1. PVC Window Hardware Graveyard

Exact-match old Roto/Winkhaus/Siegenia/Aubi/Maco fittings, catches, stays, hinges, handles, seals, and small mechanisms for Warsaw window repair firms servicing 10-25 year old PVC windows and balcony doors.

Control point: owned/reserved deadstock and dismantled fitting inventory plus repair-shop request flow and compatibility photos.

### 2. Legacy Apartment Intercom Spares Bank

Working donor stock of discontinued and hard-to-find apartment intercom modules, panels, displays, buttons, readers, handset boards, brackets, and power/control accessories for small Warsaw intercom service firms maintaining Laskomex, Proel, Urmet, Wekta, Cyfral, Impuls, and similar systems.

Control point: first-look pickup rights from modernization crews and old service depots plus tested, photographed donor modules tied to building-system compatibility.

### 3. Linen Blackout Insurance

Emergency linen/towel backup grid for small hotels, clinics, spas, hostels, restaurants, and event venues whose laundry fails or demand spikes.

Control point: owned standardized inventory plus prepaid wash capacity from two laundries plus first-call retainers from small operators.

### 4. Six-Bag Remont Router

Micro-renovation construction-waste routing for Warsaw apartment remodel crews under six-fraction waste segregation rules: mineral, gypsum, wood, plastic, glass, metal.

Control point: reusable fraction bags, QR proof records, prepaid slots with licensed waste handlers, and first-call access to small renovation crews/building administrators.

### 5. FAKTUM Kitchen Parts Bank

Discontinued IKEA FAKTUM kitchen fronts, hinges, drawers, plinths, and rails for landlords and property managers repairing 2000s rental kitchens without full replacement.

Control point: owned/reserved stock of exact FAKTUM-compatible parts plus SEO/photo matching and removal-crew supply.

### 6. Rare Key Blank And Lock Hardware Finder

Reserved deadstock drawers of rare key blanks, mailbox/cabinet locks, cylinders, faceplates, and old lock hardware from locksmiths and closing hardware shops.

Control point: physical stock and visual key/lock profile catalog, starting with locksmith-to-locksmith matching.
