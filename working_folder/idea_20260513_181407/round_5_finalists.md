# Round 5 Finalists

This round moves away from isolated low-AOV stock niches toward controlled local operating rails.

## 1. Kaucja Backroom Bank

Locked backroom cages, bagging, overflow pickup, and reconciliation support for small Warsaw stores participating in Poland's deposit-return system.

Control point: physical cage placement and shop-level return-flow data in dense districts.

## 2. TurnoverKit Apartment Continuity Rail

Portfolio-level unit mapping and reserved exact-replacement parts for serviced apartments, PRS landlords, and property managers: FAKTUM kitchen parts, window fittings, shower rollers, fridge shelves, remotes, lockboxes, intercom handsets, toilet flush plates, and other fragile fixtures that can block a turnover.

Control point: unit-level compatibility graph plus reserved replacement stock tied to live maintenance ticket flow.

## 3. SteriStandby Autoclave Continuity Pool

Same-day loaner sterilizer / validated sterilization backup for dental, podiatry, beauty, and small medical clinics when an autoclave fails, maintenance drags, or validation tests fail.

Control point: owned tested class-B autoclaves, validation logs, delivery/setup workflow, and retainer-based first-call demand.

## 4. Last-Vial Clinic Routing Desk

Clinic-triggered legal pharmacy availability routing for one narrow medicine shortage niche. The clinic calls when a prescribed medicine cannot be found; licensed pharmacies remain sellers.

Control point: failed-prescription data plus first-call clinic routing and independent-pharmacy availability relationships.

## 5. DischargeKit Home-Readiness Rail

Same-day rental and setup of post-surgery and elderly home equipment for Warsaw flats after discharge: walkers, shower chairs, toilet risers, bed rails, pressure mattresses, cold therapy units, ramps, and fall-prevention kits.

Control point: owned fitted inventory plus referral routing from physios/private clinics and apartment-fit data.

## 6. Lithium SafeBox For E-Bike And Scooter Shops

Fire-safe temporary storage, quarantine, documentation, and licensed recycling handoff for damaged/failed lithium e-bike/scooter batteries from small repair shops.

Control point: certified/appropriate safe boxes, pickup routing from repair shops, battery condition records, and prepaid licensed recycler handoff.
