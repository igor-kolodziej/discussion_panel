# Simulated Zero-To-One Scores: Round 3

After the SurfaceMatch real-gate failure, six replacement / material-pivot candidates were scored by fresh simulated reviewers.

## Results

| Candidate | Score | Verdict | Main Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Installed-Product Warranty Evidence Gate | 76 | Kill/pivot | Useful but copyable by RMA/ERP incumbents; not a hard control point unless it owns routed claims across a product line. |
| PPWR / EPR Packaging BOM Ledger | 76 | Kill/pivot | Founder-fit and timely, but likely to become services/software that EPR/accounting incumbents can copy. |
| Passive Fire Product Substitution Lock | 74 | Kill/pivot | Strong failure mode, but public document checking is not enough; domain liability and manufacturer tools weaken defensibility. |
| KSeF Rejection Atlas | 74 | Kill/pivot | Timing and founder fit are strong, but basic validation/rejection explanation is commoditized and ERP vendors can absorb it. |
| Machinery CE Defect Index | 74 | Kill/pivot | Formal document preflight is useful but trust/certification limits value and incumbents can add AI precheck. |
| EUDR Supplier Coordinate Workflow | 72 | Kill/pivot | Real regulatory pull, but supplier chasing and dashboards risk becoming a copyable compliance service. |

## Common Fatal Pattern

The reviewers consistently capped ideas built on:

- document parsing;
- evidence reports;
- compliance dashboards;
- workflow routing without hard ownership;
- corpora that incumbents can replicate once the need is visible.

## Pivot Rule For Next Wave

The next candidate must start from at least one of:

- owned or reserved physical inventory with urgent exact-fit demand;
- signed pickup / first-refusal rights over a neglected supply stream;
- a buyer workflow that cannot proceed without the controlled resource;
- recurring transaction flow where the founder can prove actual routed cases in 2 months;
- a local compatibility / provenance dataset that compounds because it is tied to physical stock or repeated transactions.

Do not take another pure compliance/document/corpus idea to real validation unless it materially controls money, inventory, capacity, or a forced operational gate.
