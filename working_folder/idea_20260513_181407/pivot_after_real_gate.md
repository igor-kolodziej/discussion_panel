# Pivot After Real Zero-To-One Gate

## Failed Real Gate

SurfaceMatch Claim Stock passed the internal simulated gate at 91/100 but failed the working-chat real Zero-To-One gate at 79/100.

The real judge's objections:

- The exact-match stock sale is credible, but the no-match/reporting side is not trusted enough from an unknown founder.
- Signed contractor pilots are soft unless repeated live claims are actually routed before replacement scope decisions.
- Scarce inventory can become dead stock unless acquisition is shaped by observed claim frequency.
- The first version is more relationship-heavy than it appears.
- The POC metrics were too easy; proof needs repeated routed cases and at least one report/material evidence surviving insurer/customer review.

Decision: do not write a confirmed idea file for SurfaceMatch. Do not continue with a cosmetic rewrite. Only consider a material pivot that changes the control point, or replace the business shape.

## Replacement / Pivot Pool

### 1. Installed-Product Warranty Evidence Gate

Warranty claim intake for flooring, panels, countertops, shower cabins, doors, windows, and fitted furniture distributors. The workflow converts each product's warranty PDF/manual into a mobile evidence gate that collects required photos, receipt, installation conditions, measurements, batch labels, and clause-specific missing evidence before the warranty team opens the case.

Control point: routed pre-RMA intake plus SKU/warranty-clause evidence corpus for installed home products.

### 2. Passive Fire Product Substitution Lock

Quote-line and procurement interlock for passive fire protection distributors/contractors. It blocks or flags substitutions of collars, wraps, boards, sealants, doors, and firestopping systems when public DoP/ETA/technical approval evidence does not support the required EI class, substrate, pipe/cable type, thickness, or installation condition.

Control point: normalized public approval/DoP/ETA corpus plus quote/export gate in a narrow high-liability category.

### 3. KSeF Rejection Atlas

Debugging and rejection-resolution layer for Polish KSeF invoices, focused on rare XML/schema/business-rule failures for accounting offices and small ERP vendors.

Control point: anonymized corpus of rejected invoice fingerprints, fixes, and vendor quirks.

### 4. PPWR / EPR Packaging BOM Ledger

SKU-level packaging material and weight ledger for Polish D2C sellers and fulfillment houses whose accountants cannot infer reporting data from messy product photos, supplier sheets, and order exports.

Control point: reusable packaging-SKU evidence database plus recurring operational ledger used as reporting input.

### 5. EUDR Supplier Coordinate Workflow

Multilingual supplier-response workflow for Polish importers that need farm/plot coordinates, batch linkage, and declarations from coffee, cocoa, wood, rubber, leather, or furniture inputs.

Control point: supplier-response templates, validated coordinate/batch database, and repeat outreach workflows for reluctant upstream suppliers.

### 6. Machinery CE Defect Index

Formal documentation defect finder for imported machinery before Polish importers become responsible EU economic operators under the new Machinery Regulation.

Control point: corpus of public manuals, declarations, harmonized-standard references, recall/defect patterns, and importer-specific defect fingerprints.

## Early Filter

Likely weak or redundant:

- Fragile-item return claim autopack is too close to the failed parcel-claims rail pattern.
- Generic EAA accessibility packs risk becoming reports unless they control transaction remediation workflow.
- KSC/NIS2 classification risks legal ambiguity and overlaps with compliance evidence-pack territory.

Most promising for strict scoring:

1. Passive Fire Product Substitution Lock.
2. Installed-Product Warranty Evidence Gate.
3. PPWR / EPR Packaging BOM Ledger.
4. KSeF Rejection Atlas.
5. EUDR Supplier Coordinate Workflow.
6. Machinery CE Defect Index.
