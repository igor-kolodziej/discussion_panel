# Zero-to-One Candidate: UDI LaunchGraph

## One-Sentence Thesis

Archive and resolve mandatory EUDAMED device, actor and certificate changes into a low-cost commercial event feed for regional medtech distributors that cannot justify enterprise market-intelligence databases.

## Founder Fit

This is the most online-native finalist: public-data ingestion, entity resolution, change detection, search distribution and self-serve samples fit the founder's data-science/AI strengths and part-time schedule. A medtech regulatory contractor is needed to label events and prevent false commercial claims, but no privileged partnership or personal brand is required.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a CEE specialist distributor/importer in one device family—such as wound care, point-of-care diagnostics or rehabilitation—that needs to find new manufacturers, detect certificate/authorised-representative changes and monitor supplier risk. Its workaround is periodic EUDAMED search, trade shows, supplier newsletters or expensive broad intelligence. Raw records create noise; missing a useful entrant or certificate change can cost a distribution opportunity or expose a supplier problem.

## Product Wedge

A quarterly paid category feed and alert stream:

- daily archived official EUDAMED snapshots;
- resolved manufacturers, authorised representatives, devices and certificates;
- event classification with source links and confidence;
- CEE/channel relevance tags from public sources;
- weekly new/changed/inactive list and a quarterly opportunity/risk file;
- no claim that database registration equals a commercial launch.

First sale is a 12,000–20,000 PLN category-quarter, not a dashboard build or consulting project.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Mandatory public data produces an event stream whose historical state disappears unless archived. Enterprise datasets serve large strategics and investors; small regional distributors often work manually. A focused, affordable feed can win if it turns a change into a prospecting or supplier-risk decision. The business fails if customers merely want a searchable copy of EUDAMED.

## Underused Momentum

- Four EUDAMED modules became mandatory on 28 May 2026.
- Official public search/download and data-exchange documentation support systematic collection.
- Each new day of archived state increases history length; a later entrant cannot recreate missed snapshots from the live page alone.
- CEE distributors can be targeted by device category and geography without status-based introductions.

## Beachhead Market

Independent Polish, Czech, Slovak, Romanian and Baltic distributors/importers in three device families with fragmented manufacturers and meaningful channel scouting: wound care, point-of-care diagnostics and rehabilitation. Investors and consultants are excluded initially so one workflow—commercial scouting and supplier monitoring—drives the product.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid category-quarter: 15,000 PLN average.
- Annual multi-category subscription: 48,000–90,000 PLN.
- Cloud, data quality and regulatory-review cost hypothesis: 15% of revenue, giving 85% gross margin.
- Sales CAC hypothesis: 6,000 PLN per account from targeted samples/search; on a 48,000 PLN contract, first-year gross profit is 40,800 PLN and gross-profit payback is under two months after sale.
- Target operating margin is 35–45% after research, sales and product. No custom report may exceed 10% of contract value in labour.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- First 10: create three category samples and send them to 30 named CEE distributors per category after outreach is authorised; require three paid quarters from unrelated firms.
- First 100: publish source-linked change pages indexed by manufacturer/device and use paid category samples, distributor directories and referrals.
- Beyond 100: local-language self-serve subscriptions and API exports. No distributor data donation is required to seed the asset.

## Primary Control Point

Founder-owned timestamped snapshots, entity-resolution graph, event labels and historical alert outcomes. The seed control point is directly buildable without customers. It is non-exclusive Tier-2-quality: competitors can start the same archive prospectively or buy other data, but cannot recreate the founder's past snapshots and labels.

Strongest copy risk: IQVIA, GlobalData, a regulatory-data vendor or a fast scraper launches an equivalent low-cost feed. The defence requires accumulated history, CEE resolution quality, search distribution, customer workflow integration and labelled commercial outcomes—not scraping alone.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resource: 90 days of trustworthy daily EUDAMED history for three categories plus ten retrospectively verified event labels.

Secure it by ingesting official public downloads/API within documented terms, hashing snapshots, keeping immutable raw records, and using a medtech contractor to audit sampled classifications. The founder can own this asset immediately for cloud and contractor cost, without a partnership.

Proof within 12 months and 100,000 PLN: uninterrupted archive, reproducible entity graph, three paid category feeds and at least two renewals. Before serious spend, a prospect must pay for the source-linked feed rather than ask for generic research.

## Distribution Advantage

Public change pages and manufacturer/device histories can rank for exact search intent; free two-week samples demonstrate timeliness; deterministic category distributor lists seed outbound. Every new event expands the long-tail acquisition surface. Direct links and correction history build trust without founder status.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. three CEE device categories and distributor scouting;
2. supplier/certificate risk alerts for existing portfolios;
3. all CEE categories and multilingual search;
4. contract manufacturer, authorised representative and investor feeds;
5. API into distributor CRM/procurement workflows.

The expansion must preserve product economics; bespoke diligence memos are rejected.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The raw EU data are common, but CEE entity resolution, language, distributor category maps and affordable pricing create a local wedge. No formal barrier stops global incumbents. Speed and accumulated history must make the niche preferable before incumbents repackage broad datasets.

## Compounding Advantage

Daily snapshots, resolution corrections, event-to-commercial-outcome labels, saved searches and indexed pages compound. The asset exists before customer cooperation. Customer usage can improve ranking only under explicit permissions; the core moat does not depend on donated private data.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected employment years reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash in archive reliability, entity resolution, regulatory review and search distribution; founder extraction remains ≤5%. Strong success reinvests 100%. Equity value is based on operating profit; retained earnings are not added again.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: build compliant daily archive, three category graphs and source-linked sample; 8,000 PLN cloud/data engineering.
- Days 31–60: 5,000 PLN regulatory audit and correction process; enumerate 90 prospects.
- Days 61–120: after outreach is authorised, offer two-week samples and sell three 12,000–20,000 PLN paid category-quarters.
- Days 121–180: deliver weekly events and seek one paid renewal/second category.

Maximum pre-proof commitment: 22,000 PLN, compatible with employment. A signed paid feed is sufficient paid proof; retention is the stronger product signal.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. 22,000 PLN maximum before first paid feed.
2. Up to 40,000 PLN only after one sale and successful delivery.
3. Remaining founder capital only after three unrelated paid customers and one renewal or documented acquisition economics.
4. Family capital excluded until repeat demand and positive subscription contribution.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Proof | Veto |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful/reliable data access | documented terms, uninterrupted archive | prohibited reuse or unstable completeness |
| Commercial signal quality | ten audited events and customer usage | changes do not alter a prospect/risk decision |
| Willingness to pay | 3 paid quarters | annualised WTP <30,000 PLN |
| Market size | named buyer census | <1,800 reachable EU buyers at target ACV |
| Defensibility | renewals/search/usage | buyers see a commodity export; incumbent parity |
| Product leverage | labour log | custom work >10% of revenue |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Stay employed until at least ten unrelated recurring customers, positive contribution, one renewal cohort, repeatable CAC and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

The archive, entity-resolution code and change-detection pipeline can be licensed or reused for another official registry. If commercial WTP fails, do not pivot into custom medtech research or generic regulatory consulting.

## Critical Evidence

- Mandatory module timing and official public access are documented.
- The archive can be built without customer permission.
- Historical snapshots have an inherent time advantage.

## Critical Hypotheses

- EUDAMED changes predict decisions distributors value.
- 1,800 reachable EU buyers exist in the selected categories.
- At least 180 can pay 48,000 PLN annually without heroic share.
- Public data terms permit the planned reuse and retention.
- A new competitor cannot erase the history/search advantage through a routine vendor contract.

## Kill Criteria

- Reuse terms or completeness invalidate the archive.
- Fewer than three paid quarters from 90 targeted prospects.
- Annualised WTP below 30,000 PLN or no renewal within 180 days of first sale.
- Fewer than 1,800 bottom-up reachable buyers, making 180 customers >10% share.
- Customers value raw search/export rather than interpreted events.
- Gross margin below 80% or custom labour above 10% of revenue.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder up to five hours/day; part-time medtech regulatory contractor; occasional data/privacy counsel. Pre-proof 22,000 PLN; first-year cap 100,000 PLN in tranches.
