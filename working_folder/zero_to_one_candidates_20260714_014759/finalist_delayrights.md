# Zero-to-One Candidate: DelayRights

## One-Sentence Thesis

Buy the small statutory compensation and interest claims left behind after undisputed B2B/public invoices are finally paid, then turn a supplier's awkward, dormant entitlement into an owned and increasingly machine-priced receivable portfolio.

## Founder Fit

The wedge rewards quantitative screening, document automation, cohort pricing and low-social-energy outbound from public supplier lists. The founder can build the data and workflow layer while employed. A Polish commercial-litigation lawyer is required for the legal structure and exceptions; the founder must not represent clients in court or imply legal authority.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first customer is a Polish SME that completed a public contract, was paid late, and now has statutory interest plus a 40/70/100 EUR per-invoice compensation entitlement. It wants cash and closure, but will not pursue hundreds of PLN per invoice, annoy a useful buyer, or manage notices and court filings. Its workaround is to write off the ancillary claim, have accounting send an ignored reminder, or use ordinary collections only while principal remains unpaid.

DelayRights accepts only fully delivered, undisputed and already-principal-paid invoices. It excludes live performance disputes, current strategic contracts, trivial one-day delays, assignment bans and claims where proportionality or abuse concerns are material.

## Product Wedge

A fixed-price purchase offer for a batch of accrued claims:

1. supplier uploads invoice, contract/order, acceptance evidence and bank/payment export;
2. the rules engine verifies parties, due date, payment date, principal value, statutory tier, limitation and assignment language;
3. counsel-reviewed exceptions are rejected or priced;
4. DelayRights pays the seller, signs assignment, notifies the debtor and owns settlement/collection;
5. seller has no contingency invoice and no further collection administration, subject only to warranties against forged or disputed source data.

The initial product is not factoring, generic debt collection or a KSeF dashboard. Principal has already been paid; the acquired asset is the accrued ancillary entitlement.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The entitlement is individually too small and relationship-sensitive for the supplier, yet portfolios can be valuable to an owner with automated evidence checks, standardized notices and debtor-level recovery data. Banks finance principal receivables; reminder tools calculate the claim; collection firms usually work on mandates. The gap is an outright, carefully screened purchase after principal payment, when the supplier no longer needs working capital but still prefers immediate monetisation and closure.

## Underused Momentum

- Polish law provides statutory late-payment interest and recovery compensation.
- CJEU C-585/20 confirms a fixed minimum per late invoice in a case involving claims assigned to a collection company.
- Polish Supreme Court materials treat the compensation as arising without proof of incurred recovery cost and as non-accessory for limitation analysis.
- Mandatory KSeF adoption standardises invoice identifiers and dates, although it does not expose bank payment dates or override contractual restrictions.
- Public procurement award notices provide a lawful, searchable supplier universe without privileged introductions.

## Beachhead Market

Former suppliers to Polish gminas and other non-medical public entities, limited to completed contracts, principal already paid, at least 15 days late, batches of at least 20 otherwise clean invoices, and no anti-assignment clause. Initial sectors are repetitive facility supplies, maintenance, catering and routine goods rather than construction claims or healthcare.

This segment is narrow enough for one legal template and identifiable debtors, but the 2024 Polish PZP market contained roughly 153,000 awards. That is context only; the number of eligible late-paid batches is unmeasured.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypothesis for an eligible claim at maturity:

| Item | Base hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Average statutory compensation plus interest face | 420 PLN |
| Seller purchase price | 105 PLN / 25% of face |
| Expected cash collection rate | 75% of face |
| Average direct notice, payment, counsel and filing cost | 60 PLN per acquired claim |
| Expected cash collected | 315 PLN |
| Contribution after purchase and direct recovery | 150 PLN |
| Contribution margin on collected cash | 48% |

The model reports collected claim cash as revenue and claim purchase plus direct recovery as cost of revenue. It does not count the face value of uncollected claims as revenue. Portfolio pricing must be based on observed cohorts, not the table above.

First-seller CAC hypothesis is 2,500 PLN for a batch averaging 50 accepted claims, or 50 PLN per accepted claim. The expected 150 PLN contribution gives payback after roughly 17 collected claims in the first batch. Repeat uploads from the same accounting firm or supplier lower CAC.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- **First 10 sellers:** select completed awards from BZP/e-Zamówienia, rank suppliers by invoice-frequency proxy, and offer a batch purchase through written/email outreach after this research run. Require at least three unrelated sellers; no public buyer introduction is needed.
- **First 100 sellers:** add referral agreements with accounting offices only after five directly sourced cohorts recover economically. A referral is distribution, not the control point; the acquired assignment remains the resource.
- **Expansion acquisition:** publish a free KSeF/bank-export scan that shows a binding purchase range only after source documents pass rules. Search pages around 40/70/100 EUR rights can compound inbound demand.

## Primary Control Point

Tier-1-quality control exists only for each executed assignment: title to the accrued claim, source documents, seller representations, debtor notice and the right to settle or pursue. Before signature, lists and calculators are commodity inputs.

The strongest copy risk is BFF, a bank, factor or collection platform adding an ancillary-claim product. The defence is speed in a deliberately ignored small-batch cohort, accumulated debtor/document recovery curves, automated pricing and repeat accounting-firm distribution—not exclusivity over the law.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resource: legally effective ownership of ten clean claims from at least three unrelated sellers.

How secured: obtain a fixed-scope Polish legal opinion covering independent assignment, anti-assignment clauses, public-debtor notice, licensing/consumer-credit perimeter, tax/accounting and court representation; use counsel-drafted purchase documents; buy only after a complete document screen.

Why an unknown founder can secure it: the seller receives immediate cash for an asset it is already ignoring, there is no request for exclusivity over its core invoices, and public award data identifies prospects. The first purchase reserve is only 5,000 PLN.

Proof before serious capital: executed assignments, accepted debtor notices, first cash recoveries, time/cost per claim and a seller willing to submit a second batch. If counsel cannot give a clean route or contracts routinely block assignment, the candidate dies before purchases.

## Distribution Advantage

Public awards create a deterministic outbound list; KSeF/accounting export templates make qualification self-serve; exact statutory-intent search pages create inbound; and repeat batches give an accounting-office channel. The compounding advantage is a growing conversion map from contract wording and debtor identity to net recovery, enabling faster offers and lower rejection cost than generic collectors.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. dominate clean, completed Polish municipal supplier batches;
2. add non-medical public entities and private enterprise debtors with proven cohorts;
3. integrate accountant-approved KSeF and bank-export ingestion;
4. expand to one additional EU jurisdiction only after local counsel and a ring-fenced cohort demonstrate assignability and unit economics;
5. offer portfolio liquidity/API pricing to accounting and invoice platforms.

The business never needs to finance unpaid principal to reach the thesis. If it does, it has drifted into capital-intensive factoring and should be stopped.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish 40/70/100 EUR tiers, KSeF data, Polish assignment language, public-procurement records and debtor-specific procedure require local execution. EU law supplies an expansion pattern, not a one-click passport: each country needs separate counsel, forms and observed recoveries.

## Compounding Advantage

Every acquired claim produces proprietary labels: clause permissibility, document sufficiency, debtor response, settlement rate, filing cost, days to cash and seller repeat behaviour. Those labels improve bid prices and automated acceptance. Repeat seller/accountant distribution and owned receivable cohorts compound online; manual sourcing by itself does not.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

During employment, 95% of after-tax distributable cash in conservative and expected cases funds additional claim purchases, debtor-data coverage, automation and legal playbooks; founder cash extraction is optional and capped at 5%. Strong success reinvests 100%. Claim purchases are short-duration only after the first recovered cohort. No retained cash is added to the equity-value total.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 6,000 PLN fixed-scope legal opinion and contract pack; build offline rules model from public/sample documents.
- Days 31–60: 4,000 PLN secure upload/KSeF-and-bank-export prototype; prepare 100-prospect completed-award list.
- Days 61–120: after outreach is separately authorised, buy 10–20 claims from at least three sellers for no more than 5,000 PLN; send notices.
- Days 121–180: seek actual debtor cash. A seller's signed sale plus founder payment is a purchase transaction, but the decisive paid proof is at least five debtor recoveries with positive contribution after direct costs.

Maximum pre-proof commitment is 30,000 PLN, compatible with employment and well below the 40,000 PLN cap.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. **0–30,000 PLN:** legal, security, prototype, small purchases and recovery reserve.
2. **Next 30,000 PLN:** only after successful initial delivery, at least five cash recoveries and positive observed contribution.
3. **Remaining founder capital:** only after three unrelated repeat sellers or 100 recovered claims plus a documented acquisition channel.
4. **Family capital:** excluded until repeat demand, cohort recovery economics and counsel-confirmed expansion are documented.

No principal-invoice financing is permitted. Maximum founder loss before paid proof is 30,000 PLN.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Control | Proof | Veto |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent assignability | External law, founder can buy counsel | written opinion and accepted notice | adverse opinion or common assignment bans |
| Seller supply | Founder controls offer, not acceptance | 3 unrelated executed batches | no second batch or seller price >40% of face |
| Debtor payment | External | 5 cash recoveries in 180 days | <50% clean-claim recovery or >120-day median |
| Positive contribution | Founder pricing/ops | cohort cash ledger | direct cost >35% of cash or negative after purchase |
| Scalable volume | Partially controlled via distribution | 1,500 acquired claims and 3 repeat sources by Y2 | bottom-up model cannot support 60,000 at sane share |
| Reputation/proportionality | Founder policy | exclusions and complaint log | nuisance pattern or court/counsel abuse concern |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Remain employed until there are at least three unrelated repeat sellers or accounting sources, positive contribution across at least 100 cash recoveries, one repeatable acquisition channel with measured CAC, no unresolved licensing/assignment issue, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If purchasing fails, the founder retains a lawful late-payment clause library, KSeF/bank reconciliation code, public-supplier mapping and document-classification components usable in finance operations. Do not pivot back to a generic recovery desk unless a new owned transaction control point exists.

## Critical Evidence

- Statutory compensation and interest are real.
- CJEU precedent concerns assigned public-authority claims and per-invoice compensation.
- Polish Supreme Court authority supports automatic accrual and non-accessory treatment for limitation.
- Public procurement provides a large visible supplier universe.
- BFF proves receivable acquisition can be institutional, while also proving strong incumbent risk.

## Critical Hypotheses

- A clean Polish contract can assign the accrued ancillary claim independently after principal payment.
- At least 20% of approached eligible suppliers accept 20–30% of face for relationship-free closure.
- Clean public debtors pay enough assigned claims without litigation to preserve at least 40% gross margin.
- A bottom-up Polish/EU cohort can support 60,000 annual claims without heroic share.
- The offer does not require a regulated finance or collection licence beyond the planned entity/counsel structure.

## Kill Criteria

- Counsel cannot confirm the narrow assignment structure or licensing perimeter.
- More than half of sampled contracts prohibit or condition assignment.
- Fewer than three unrelated sellers sell a batch, or no seller repeats.
- Fewer than five of the first ten clean claims pay inside 180 days.
- Observed contribution is below 100 PLN per accepted claim or CAC payback exceeds the first batch.
- The conservative bottom-up market cannot support 60,000 annual claims at less than 10% of the measured eligible market.
- The business needs unpaid-principal financing, adversarial mass litigation or nuisance-sized claims to work.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- Founder: up to 5 hours/day, pricing/data/product/outbound.
- Fixed-scope Polish commercial lawyer: assignment, public-debtor procedure and template review.
- Part-time operations/paralegal contractor only after paid recovery.
- Pre-proof budget: 30,000 PLN; 180 days.
- 12-month control-point budget: no more than 100,000 PLN and funded in tranches.
