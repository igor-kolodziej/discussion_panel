# Bounded Research: Closeout Event Denominator

Research date: 2026-07-14.

The [Ministry of Justice KRS reports page](https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/SprawozdaniaKRS) publishes court-level annual text files. The 2025 archive was streamed and its 36 entrepreneur-register court reports were summed. They report **25,760 removals from the entrepreneur register in 2025**. This is an acquisition-event upper bound, not a count of solvent voluntary liquidation openings and not evidence that a removed company had public contracts or clean residual claims.

A secondary commercial registry compilation reports 4,167 capital-company liquidations in the first half of 2025. It is useful only as an order-of-magnitude cross-check, not a primary official denominator: [COIG liquidation list](https://www.coig.com.pl/lista-likwidacji-firm_2016_2015_2014_2013_wykreslenie_z_KRS.php).

Consequence: the first pivot's conservative need for 750 lots averaging 80 low-face claims is too close to the likely total eligible closeout population after applying public-supplier, clean-document and assignment filters. It should not enter a panel even though 750 is only 2.9% of all KRS removals. A stronger revision must reduce the required lot count and avoid nuisance economics by screening for higher-value, materially late invoices.
