# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Closeout Rights Lots v1

## Diagnosis

The pivot successfully fixes the live-supplier relationship conflict and reduces CAC by making a public closeout event the acquisition trigger. An authorised liquidator selling a whole batch is a stronger buyer, trust mechanism and transaction unit than ordinary supplier outreach. However, bounded KRS research shows that 750 lots averaging 80 low-face claims approaches a large share of the plausibly eligible voluntary-liquidation population after filters. The insight is strong, but the unit must be narrowed to materially late, higher-value invoices so the wealth case needs fewer lots and cannot drift into nuisance claims.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: original statutory/court sources, official KRS reporting availability and 25,760 total 2025 entrepreneur-register removals (upper bound), plus a secondary report of 4,167 capital-company liquidations in H1 2025. Hypotheses: liquidator sale process, eligible fraction, 80 claims/lot, 420 PLN face, 75% collection, 1,200 PLN lot CAC.

## Overall Score

**84/100 advisory score** (weighted total 167 / 20 × 10, rounded). This is not a simulated or real score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 per acquired lot.** Strongest copy risk is a debt buyer adding the same residual-right screen; event graph and cohort data are the catch-up defence.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail on denominator credibility.** The 13.9m PLN equity arithmetic is correct and reinvestment is compliant, but 750 eligible lots at 80 clean claims are not supported by the closeout population after filters.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Counsel, event list, one lot and cash recoveries fit the cap and employment.

## Expansion Check

The event/buyer is strong and CEE/private-debtor expansion is logical. Scaling must not require a dominant share of Polish voluntary liquidations.

## Fatal Objections

1. Eligible lots are likely too rare at the stated 80-claim low-face unit.
2. Low 40/70/100 EUR claims risk nuisance optics and litigation cost.
3. Liquidator authority/process still requires counsel proof.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Public closeout events create a low-conflict, batch acquisition channel for owned post-payment rights.
