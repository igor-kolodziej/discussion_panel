## Funnel rule

A direction advances only if its initial control point can be owned, signed, reserved or directly proved inside 12 months and 100,000 PLN; it can earn paid proof inside 180 days and 40,000 PLN while the founder stays employed; and a non-heroic founder-wealth bridge can be written. A large directory is not itself a customer denominator, and a vendor promise is not capacity control.
