# Zero-to-One Evaluation: DelayRights

## Diagnosis

This is the only finalist whose control point is an owned legal asset at the moment of sale rather than a future workflow habit. The after-principal-paid wedge is materially different from factoring and generic recovery, and paid proof is unusually direct. However, the beachhead still asks suppliers with live public-buyer exposure to trade reputation for a small payment, while the conservative wealth case needs 60,000 annual claims without a measured batch-seller population. The idea should be narrowed around an observable relationship-ending event before panel use.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced: Polish statutory rights, CJEU per-invoice treatment in an assigned-claim case, Polish Supreme Court automatic accrual/non-accessory analysis, KSeF timing, public-award volume, BFF and Terminovo competition. Hypotheses: independent Polish assignment in the exact post-payment structure, 25% seller price, 75% collection, 150 PLN contribution, 2,500 PLN seller CAC and 60,000 annual claim supply. Reviewer or skill scores are not market evidence.

## Overall Score

**80/100 advisory score.** Weighted total 160 over total weight 20, multiplied by 10. This is not a simulated or real validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 only after each executed assignment.** The company owns a legal receivable and settlement path. The strongest copy risk is BFF or an established collection/invoice platform adding a residual-right purchase product; small-batch pricing data and exact exit-event acquisition must make catch-up slower than a routine product launch.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, fragile.** The conservative scenario yields 13.9m PLN founder equity from 4.35m PLN operating profit at 4× and 80% ownership without adding retained cash. Reinvestment rules are correct. The decisive weakness is not arithmetic but the unmeasured supply and acquisition economics required to reach 60,000 annual claims.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Counsel, documents, 10–20 purchases and the first cash recoveries fit 180 days, 30,000 PLN and continued employment. Assignment/licensing counsel is one sequential veto, not several simultaneous capital commitments.

## Expansion Check

The public-supplier beachhead is coherent, with private B2B and jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction expansion. BFF validates acquisition but also proves incumbent capability. The local advantage is strongest in clause screening, KSeF/bank reconciliation and public award timing.

## Fatal Objections

1. A supplier with a live renewal relationship may reject even an attractive purchase to avoid debtor friction.
2. Common assignment restrictions could shrink the eligible cohort below the wealth requirement.
3. The 60,000-claim conservative flow is not bottom-up measured.
4. Recovery may require litigation often enough to destroy contribution and part-time compatibility.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire accrued, independently controllable ancillary rights after principal payment, but time the offer to a public, verifiable event showing the supplier relationship has ended.
