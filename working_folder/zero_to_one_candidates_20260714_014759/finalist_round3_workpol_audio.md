# Finalist: WorkPol Audio

## One-sentence thesis and founder fit

Sell staffing agencies and high-churn user-employers short, role-specific work-sample assessments that test whether a candidate can follow noisy spoken shift, safety, quality and escalation instructions—not general Polish level—then compound an owned multilingual item bank and job-outcome calibration data. The founder's data-science background fits psychometrics, scoring, experimentation and asynchronous distribution; an industrial-organizational psychologist, BHP expert and employment/privacy counsel are bounded contractors.

## Customer, pain and wedge

The first payer is a Polish temporary-work/placement agency sending warehouse or light-production candidates to user-employers. It loses recruiter time, replacement fees and client trust when a candidate who can converse socially cannot follow rapid instructions, exception codes or escalation commands on a noisy shift. The workaround is a casual interview, CEFR language test or the user-employer discovering the mismatch during induction.

The first product is a 12-minute job simulation for one of three roles: warehouse picker, packing/quality operator and basic production operator. A candidate hears short, realistically noisy Polish instructions and must sequence actions, identify a deviation, repeat a critical number, choose an escalation and recognize when to stop. It does not certify language, replace mandatory BHP/position training, or make a legal fitness determination. It returns a score plus recommended instruction-language/support band; agencies/employers retain the employment decision.

## Commercial insight, momentum and beachhead

General language fluency is a weak proxy for operational comprehension. Agencies really want fewer early failures at the client site, while user-employers must still provide workplace-specific instruction. Poland had about 1.119m foreign workers in January 2026; the 2024 official agency report counted 4,049 domestic-placement agencies, 698,081 temporary workers, 98,789 foreign placements and 18,963 user-employers. Those populations overlap, but they establish a recurring flow large enough for a focused assessment.

Beachhead: 30 agencies placing at least 200 people/year into warehouses and light production in Mazovia, Łódź and Greater Poland. First acquisition comes from the public KRAZ/agency list and named user-employer references on agency sites. A written five-item sample and fixed 100-candidate pilot are sent after outreach is authorized; no founder camera, status or influencer access is needed.

## Primary control point and cold start

The initial resource is a company-owned item bank: 180 scripts, professionally recorded/noise-mixed audio, distractors, scoring rubrics, accessibility variants and role blueprints across Polish plus the candidates' instruction/support languages. All authors/voice contractors assign copyright and derivative rights. A qualified psychologist documents job relatedness and initial validation; counsel documents GDPR, equal-treatment, retention and human-decision boundaries.

Build 60 sellable items for ≤20,000 PLN and legal/psychometric foundations for ≤8,000 PLN. By 12 months and ≤100,000 PLN, require 180 owned items, ≥3,000 completed paid assessments, item-response calibration, three employer-agreed outcome measures (such as induction completion, 30-day retention and supervisor-rated instruction errors) under explicit DPA/consent, and no customer concentration over 35%. Initial control is Tier 1 over content/software and Tier 2 over market power; calibration becomes harder to copy only when independent paid cohorts accumulate. Strongest copy risk: TestGorilla, a language-test vendor or staffing platform commissions a similar role bank and bundles it with existing distribution.

## Product economics, distribution and compounding

- 100-candidate paid pilot: 7,500 PLN, including setup and one role.
- Standard annual account minimum: 12,000 PLN plus 99 PLN per completed assessment; volume tiers never below 69 PLN.
- Direct delivery/cloud/contractor review: target 12% of revenue, or 88% gross margin.
- Agency-account CAC hypothesis: 6,000 PLN. An account running 200 tests produces 31,800 PLN annual revenue and ~27,984 PLN gross profit, paying CAC inside the first quarter after closing.
- Compounding assets: calibrated item difficulty, language/load-archetype norms, detected bad items, fraud/response patterns, integration, indexed role pages and outcome evidence. Private outcomes may improve aggregate calibration only under written permission; the business does not require customers to donate data before the first sale.
- Expansion: three Polish high-turnover roles → five CEE hiring corridors/languages → direct multi-site employers → adjacent logistics/food roles. No general personality testing, recruitment agency or BHP certification.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Business value is based on normalized operating profit. Retained cash is not added separately and no debt is assumed. While employed, conservative/expected after-tax distributable cash is 95% reinvested and optional distributions are capped at 5%; strong growth reinvests 100%.

|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 300 accounts ×12k + 60,000 tests ×99 | 9.54m PLN | 88% gross; 35% EBIT = 3.339m | ≤0.8m PLN product/sales from receipts; 85% founder; 95% reinvestment while employed, ≤5% distribution | 4× EBIT = 13.356m EV; founder equity **11.35m PLN**; no distributions added | 3 pilots M6; 20 accounts Y2; 80 Y4; 180 Y6; 300 accounts/60k tests Y8–9 |

| Strong | 1,800 accounts ×24k + 750,000 tests ×119 | 132.45m PLN | 91% gross; 45% EBIT = 59.60m | 7m PLN growth capital after renewals; 70% founder; 100% reinvestment | 5× = 298.0m EV; founder equity **208.6m PLN** | European role-comprehension standard Y8–10 |

The conservative case uses 300 of 18,963 user-employers (1.6%) and 60,000 of 698,081 annual temporary workers (8.6%). Because the pools overlap and not all roles need the product, the test-volume share is the harder assumption. It remains credible only if the qualified bottom-up pool contains ≥600,000 annual warehouse/light-production placements across Poland plus the first CEE corridors, so 60,000 is ≤10%, and the product saves more than its 99 PLN price.

## Reachability and 180-day paid-proof plan

- Days 1–21: spend 5,000 PLN on employment/privacy/psychometric structure; enumerate 60 agencies and 20 user-employers.
- Days 22–60: commission 60 items/audio for ≤15,000 PLN and a manual secure delivery/scoring flow for ≤3,000 PLN.
- Days 61–120: after outreach authorization, sell a fixed 100-candidate pilot; do not build integrations.
- Days 121–180: collect three unrelated prepaid pilots at ≥7,500 PLN each, deliver the first cohort, record completion/candidate feedback and obtain at least one agreed non-sensitive outcome measure.

Maximum pre-proof commitment is 30,000 PLN. Paid proof means cash collected from an unrelated agency/employer, not an LOI. Spend beyond 40,000 PLN only after successful delivery and positive contribution; release the rest of the original 100,000 PLN after three unrelated paying accounts and one repeat/expanded cohort. Family capital is excluded until repeat demand and acquisition economics.

## Dependencies, kill criteria and transition

| Dependency | Proof | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Job relevance/fairness | psychologist/legal memo, item analysis and human-decision boundary | Material disparate impact cannot be justified/mitigated or counsel rejects workflow |
| Buyer WTP | 3 prepaid 100-candidate pilots | Fewer than 3 pay after 60 qualified agency contacts or realized price <69 PLN/test |
| Predictive usefulness | correlation with induction/30-day/supervisor outcome | No useful lift over a five-minute structured interview after 500 assessments |
| Content control | complete assignments and independent items | Customer-specific content >20% of delivery or rights are non-exclusive |
| Distribution | 10 unrelated accounts, CAC ≤8k | Family/personal access is required or sales cycle >180 days |
| Wealth denominator | ≥600k qualified annual Poland+initial-CEE placements | Conservative 60k volume exceeds 10% or 300 accounts are not independently reachable |
| Copy defence | ≥3,000 calibrated paid records and outcome evidence by M12 | Incumbent parity before calibration creates switching/CAC disadvantage |

The founder stays employed until at least ten unrelated recurring accounts or contracted cohorts, positive contribution, a repeatable acquisition channel and 12 months of personal runway. If killed, retain the owned items, psychometric code and anonymized aggregate item statistics; do not pivot into recruiting, generic language classes, BHP certification or custom HR consulting.
