# Zero-to-One Pivot: Closeout Rights Lots

## Preserved Insight

The useful insight is that statutory late-payment interest and 40/70/100 EUR recovery compensation left after principal payment can be purchased as an owned legal asset. Assignment gives the founder control at transaction close, while standardised evidence and observed recoveries can compound into portfolio-pricing data.

## Structural Change

DelayRights targeted ordinary public suppliers, including sellers with a live or renewable buyer relationship. Closeout Rights Lots changes the customer, painful event and acquisition unit:

- buyer/seller: a solvent Polish company already in voluntary liquidation or a management-controlled business-line closeout, not an ongoing supplier protecting a renewal;
- event: a public KRS/MSiG liquidation/closeout plus completed public contracts, not generic late payment;
- acquisition unit: a whole batch of 20–200 post-payment ancillary claims, not individual invoice claims;
- value: immediate cash and removal of small residual assets from closing books, not only convenience;
- channel: public KRS/MSiG event monitoring joined to public procurement history, not broad cold outreach;
- trust: counsel-drafted asset purchase by an authorised liquidator/management, not a promise to recover money on the seller's behalf.

The first beachhead excludes bankruptcy and court/creditor-committee-controlled estates. Only a seller whose authorised liquidator or management can execute the asset sale enters the proof cohort.

## Target Niche and Buyer

Solvent Polish limited companies in voluntary liquidation that supplied non-medical public entities during the prior three years, have fully paid principal invoices, and possess at least 20 otherwise clean late-payment compensation/interest claims. The named decision-maker is the registered liquidator. A secondary later segment is a company closing an entire public-supply line with a board-approved residual-right sale.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

The liquidator must identify, monetise or dispose of residual assets and close books. Dozens of 168–430+ PLN ancillary claims are too small to collect one by one but collectively material; abandoning them sacrifices estate value, while pursuing them delays closeout. A cash purchase at 20–30% of screened face provides a documented arm's-length disposal, immediate cash and no future collection workload.

Willingness is expressed by accepting a purchase discount, not paying a service fee. The first disconfirming question is whether an authorised liquidator will sell a batch and whether counsel considers the price/process defensible.

## Value and Profit Wedge

Base hypothesis per acquired claim:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Average compensation plus interest face | 420 PLN |
| Purchase price at 25% | 105 PLN |
| Expected collection at 75% of face | 315 PLN |
| Direct notice/legal/payment cost | 60 PLN |
| Contribution after acquisition and direct recovery | 150 PLN |

At an 80-claim lot, the seller receives about 8,400 PLN immediately; expected collected cash is 25,200 PLN and expected contribution before central overhead is 12,000 PLN. Public event acquisition is hypothesised at 1,200 PLN per accepted lot, or 15 PLN per claim, materially below the original 50 PLN-per-claim seller CAC.

Collected cash is revenue; purchase price and direct recovery are cost of revenue. Uncollected face is not revenue.

Conservative wealth hypothesis: 750 acquired lots/year × 80 claims × 315 PLN collected cash = 18.9m PLN revenue; 43% gross margin and 23% operating margin = 4.35m PLN operating profit. At 4× operating profit, 80% founder ownership gives 13.9m PLN founder equity in years 7–9. Retained earnings are not added again. During employment 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested and optional distributions stay at or below 5%.

Expected: 1,500 lots × 100 claims × 351 PLN collected cash = 52.65m PLN revenue; 28% operating margin; 4.5×; 75% founder ownership = 49.7m PLN founder equity in years 7–9. Strong: 3,200 lots × 125 claims × 384 PLN = 153.6m PLN revenue; 32% operating margin; 5×; 65% ownership = 159.7m PLN in years 8–11, with 100% growth reinvestment.

The key denominator is therefore not all invoices but at least 7,500 eligible annual Polish/CEE closeout lots for the conservative share to remain below 10%. Official KRS reports make the events measurable; the fraction with public contracts and 20 clean claims is a new hypothesis.

## Domination Wedge

Start with one repeatable legal fact pattern: solvent voluntary liquidation, non-medical Polish public debtor, principal paid, at least 15 days late, no dispute, no assignment restriction, at least 20 claims and a three-year limitation screen. The narrow event creates high claims per seller, no live-renewal conflict and an authorised asset-disposal decision-maker. Dominate by quoting a lot in 48 hours and closing it with one document pack.

## Acquisition Channel

1. Monitor official KRS reports and MSiG liquidation notices.
2. Join each company to UZP/BZP award history.
3. Rank likely invoice-rich suppliers by contract type and duration.
4. Send a written lot-purchase offer to the registered liquidator after separate outreach authorisation.
5. Later add accountants/liquidation law firms only after direct-event CAC is proven; these are non-exclusive distribution channels, not required partnerships.

Search pages and a secure KSeF/bank-export scanner can compound inbound from future closeouts.

## Primary Control Point

Executed batch assignment and title to every listed accrued claim, source invoice/payment evidence, seller warranties, debtor notice and settlement rights. Each lot is Tier-1-quality owned inventory after purchase. The cross-lot control is proprietary clause, debtor, document, response, time-to-cash and cost data that improves pricing.

Strongest copy risk: BFF, a distressed-debt purchaser or collection platform notices the residual-right lot and enters. Catch-up defence is the event-linked acquisition graph, automatic batch diligence and a recovery history for small ancillary rights; no claim of exclusivity over the statute is made.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resource: clean legal title to one 20–100-claim lot from an authorised voluntary liquidator.

Bootstrap sequence:

- spend 7,000 PLN on a fixed-scope legal opinion covering independent assignment, voluntary-liquidator authority/process, anti-assignment clauses, debtor notice, tax, accounting, licensing perimeter and court representation;
- build a 250-event KRS/MSiG × BZP prospect list and secure upload for 5,000 PLN;
- reserve 15,000 PLN to buy one to three lots only after counsel and documents pass;
- reserve 6,000 PLN for notices and bounded enforcement;
- seek five debtor cash recoveries and one second lot within 180 days.

Maximum pre-proof exposure is 33,000 PLN. No unpaid principal is financed, no court-controlled estate is entered, and no seller/supplier outreach occurs during this validation run.

## Expansion Path

1. Polish voluntary-liquidation public-supplier lots.
2. Management-controlled closure of a public-supply division.
3. Private B2B debtors only after separate recovery cohorts.
4. Czech/Slovak closeouts after local counsel and ring-fenced pilots.
5. API lot pricing for liquidation accountants and legal practices.

If the business must purchase principal receivables, depend on bankruptcy auctions or litigate nuisance claims to scale, it has left the pivot and should be killed.

## Minimum People Stack

- founder: data acquisition, portfolio pricing, product and written outbound, maximum five hours/day;
- Polish commercial lawyer: fixed scope plus exception review, not a speculative partner;
- part-time paralegal/operations contractor only after positive recovery;
- later local counsel per new jurisdiction.

## Principal Risk

The structural fix may overcorrect: voluntary-liquidation suppliers with 20 clean, assignable public-invoice claims may be too rare to support 750 annual lots, and authorised liquidators may prefer to collect or auction all receivables together. The lot count must be measured before panel assumptions are treated as credible.

## First Disconfirming Test

Before 40,000 PLN of exposure and within 180 days: obtain counsel's clean opinion, identify at least 250 annualised candidate events from official data, buy at least one 20+ claim lot from an authorised liquidator, recover cash on at least five clean claims, and show at least 100 PLN observed contribution per accepted claim after lot CAC. Kill on any failed condition.

## Evidence Preserved

- Polish statutory interest and 40/70/100 EUR compensation.
- CJEU C-585/20 per-invoice treatment in an assigned-claim public-authority case.
- Polish Supreme Court automatic-accrual and non-accessory analysis.
- Official UZP/BZP supplier history.
- Official KRS reports and MSiG notices as observable acquisition events.
- BFF as proof of institutional public-receivable acquisition and as a serious incumbent threat.

## New Hypotheses

- Voluntary liquidators can and will sell independently assignable ancillary rights in a simple arm's-length process.
- At least 7,500 eligible closeout lots per year exist across Poland and reachable CEE expansion markets.
- A retained lot averages at least 80 clean claims.
- The event-linked CAC is at most 1,200 PLN per acquired lot.
- Relationship concern falls materially after voluntary liquidation.
- At least 75% of clean face is collected with at least 40% gross margin and a median under 120 days.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The original seller still cared about a live public-buyer relationship and required many fragmented supplier acquisitions. The pivot changes the buyer to an authorised closeout decision-maker, makes liquidation/line exit the urgent event, buys a whole asset lot, uses a public event-specific channel and turns balance-sheet cleanup into the value proposition. The owned claim remains the preserved control point; the broken trust and CAC mechanism do not.

## Return-to-Evaluator Priority

This single pivot should return first because it directly accepts all four evaluator objections: it removes live-renewal fear, gives one seller many claims, makes acquisition timing observable and states the exact lot denominator needed for the wealth model. It remains unvalidated until the evaluator and subsequent independent gates judge the revised wording.
