# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CRA Sector CNA + Outsourced PSIRT Rail

## Diagnosis

The offer attaches to a real urgent event and improves on generic CRA consulting by routing actual vulnerability intake. Yet CNA status is free and non-exclusive, the customer can switch the security.txt endpoint, and the founder must borrow the core credibility and delivery capability. The 200-client wealth case assumes routine expert work productises before a paid cohort demonstrates that buyers will buy annual routing rather than a tabletop. This exact business shape is too trust-heavy and service-like for panel use.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced: CRA deadlines, ENISA's free reporting platform, CNA onboarding and PSIRT operating guidance. Hypotheses: 500+ reachable sector buyers, 50–90k PLN annual value, public customer routing, 60% gross margin, expert leverage and affordable insurance.

## Overall Score

**72/100 advisory score.** Weighted total 144 over weight 20, multiplied by 10. Not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 after a signed route; Tier 3 before it.** Strongest copy risk: an established pentest/certification provider applies for CNA status and changes one customer endpoint. No exclusive rights prevent this.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass on arithmetic, weak on execution.** 200 clients at 60,000 PLN and 30% operating margin produce 3.6m PLN operating profit; 4× and 75% ownership yield 10.8m PLN. The client count, delivery leverage and retention are not evidenced, making the pass too fragile for pursue.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass for a paid tabletop; not yet for durable control.** A prepaid engagement fits 28,000 PLN and employment, but does not prove that a buyer will designate the company for real incidents.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is sensible, but adjacent sectors add protocols and safety context. International product-security firms can enter with reputation, tooling and insurance advantages.

## Fatal Objections

1. Core trust and expertise are rented, not founder-controlled.
2. CNA status and routing are reversible with one vendor change.
3. A free official portal lowers value to operational expert labour.
4. Wealth depends on 200 clients before productisation is proved.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Real vulnerability-event routing is more valuable than CRA documentation.
