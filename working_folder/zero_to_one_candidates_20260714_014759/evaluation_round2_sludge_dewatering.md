# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: Mobile Sludge Dewatering Blocks

## Diagnosis

This solves a measurable cost and controls an asset, but it is a conventional regional equipment service whose scale depends on rigs, operators, transport and site-by-site selling. The stated conservative case needs 60 accounts—15–24% of the currently hypothesized qualified Polish pool—or an unproved CEE route network. Later distributions rescue an equity value below the threshold. It conflicts with the part-time founder constraint more than the brief acknowledges.

## Evidence state

Sourced: establishment registers, existence of industrial sludge statistics, mobile rental availability and a real temporary-use procurement. Hypotheses: qualified plant count, annual recurrence, campaign price, margin, utilization, contractor autonomy and CEE replicability.

## Gates, vetoes and control point

- **Control point:** Tier 2 at proof and Tier 1 after equipment ownership. Strongest copy risk: an established wastewater contractor rents/buys the same press and bundles environmental handling.
- **Reachability Gate:** conditional pass on cash, conditional fail on part-time operability until a contractor delivers independently.
- **Founder Wealth Gate:** fail. The conservative customer share is heroic on present evidence and the operating model scales linearly.
- **Fatal vetoes:** site/liability load, local utilization resets, incumbent trust, and serial asset/operator expansion.

## Recommendation

**kill.** Do not pivot to a broader mobile-treatment roll-up; that is the same blacklisted business shape.
