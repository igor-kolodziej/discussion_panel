# Finalist: Modular Medical Animation IP Library

## Thesis and founder fit

Commission and own modular, scientifically reviewed 3D anatomy/device-procedure scene systems, then license reusable source-derived animation bundles to European medtech SMEs that cannot justify repeated bespoke agency work. The founder's data/AI skills fit asset indexing, configurable rendering, multilingual distribution and asynchronous outbound; medical review and 3D production are contracted. The business is a licensor, not a generic animation agency.

## Customer, pain, wedge and insight

The first buyer is a MEDICA-listed device SME with several product variants and thin patient/clinician education. It repeatedly needs the same anatomy, mechanism and procedure depicted across web, distributor, training and launch material. Stock media is too generic; agencies often deliver one-off videos with full assignment. The proposed gap is a reusable, scientifically reviewed scene system whose device-specific layer can change without rebuilding the anatomy and motion foundation.

The wedge is one three-scene system in a narrow procedure family—for example peripheral IV placement/complications—with a neutral anatomy base, configurable device geometry, claim-source annotations, editable language masters, stills and short web clips. One customer prepays customization but the company retains the reusable anatomy/motion/core source rights; its device layer remains confidential and customer-owned/licensed as agreed.

## Beachhead, distribution and control point

Beachhead: European SME manufacturers in one procedure family, enumerated from MEDICA's 4,821 exhibitors and CPHI's 2,900+ exhibitors. First acquisition is 100 product-qualified firms with outdated or absent educational media, reached via source-linked sample scenes and fixed deliverables after outreach authorization.

The controlled resource is the copyright-assigned 3D source system, scripts, claim annotations, modular rigs, rendering pipeline and derivative rights. Commission one production-ready system for ≤22,000 PLN plus ≤5,000 PLN medical review/legal terms. This is Tier 1 ownership of the exact assets but only Tier 2 market power because a competitor can commission alternatives. Strongest copy risk: a specialist agency or BioRender builds a similar modular library and uses existing distribution.

## Economics and compounding

- First three-scene annual licence/customized bundle: 35,000–80,000 PLN; mature blended account revenue hypothesis 120,000 PLN across licence, localizations and additional device variants.
- At reuse scale: 82% gross margin after render/medical review; CAC hypothesis 15,000 PLN; a 60,000 PLN first contract generates approximately 49,000 PLN gross profit and pays CAC on first sale.
- Each reusable anatomy system, translated master, claim map, template, buyer page and customer-approved configuration lowers delivery cost. The moat fails if each order starts from a blank scene.
- Expansion: dominate one procedure family, then adjacent consumables and distributor-localized education; no broad “medical content studio.”

## Reachability and capital tranches

- Days 1–30: select one procedure family from 100 qualified product pages; spend 3,000 PLN on claim/IP structure.
- Days 31–75: commission a sellable base/sample under assignment for ≤22,000 PLN; spend ≤5,000 PLN on medical review.
- Days 76–150: offer a fixed three-scene bundle to the 100 named firms after outreach authorization; no second system before deposit.
- By day 180: one independent buyer pays a ≥25,000 PLN non-refundable deposit or licence, delivery preserves reusable core rights, and estimated project gross margin is positive.

Maximum pre-proof spend: 30,000 PLN. Further founder capital only after successful delivery; full 100,000 PLN only after three unrelated payers and evidence that the second/third reused ≥70% of the core. Family capital excluded until repeat demand. Employment ends only after multiple recurring buyers, positive contribution, repeatable acquisition and 12 months' runway.

## Dependencies and kill criteria

| Dependency | Proof | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Reusable customer terms | Standard split between reusable core and customer layer | First three serious buyers require full assignment of all source assets |
| WTP | ≥25k PLN deposit by day 180 | 100 qualified buyers yield no deposit or annualized WTP <60k PLN |
| Reuse | ≥70% core reuse on customers 2–3 | Delivery remains bespoke or medical review resets each project |
| Wealth denominator | ≥1,000 relevant products | 50 customers exceeds 5% of qualified niche or annual value <90k PLN |
| Competitive advantage | Faster/cheaper measured delivery and retained rights | BioRender/agency substitutes erase at least 30% customer saving |

If killed, the owned base scene and modular pipeline remain licensable stock assets; do not pivot to generic commissioned videos.
