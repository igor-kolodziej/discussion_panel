- Public-data preflights and compatibility graphs that incumbents can reproduce or bundle without a proprietary feed or transaction.

- Two-party delivery or module-pool workflows requiring receiver consent at every event.

- Tool/equipment distribution exclusives where established local distributors already provide the complete lifecycle offer.

- Retail-deduction recovery and other high-trust managed services already supplied by incumbents.

- DelayRights / Closeout Rights Lots / Material-Delay Yield Lots / Severe-Delay Yield Lots: killed after simulated scores 69 and 42. Article 10(4) of the Polish Late Payment Act makes the compensation claim non-assignable, and both judges independently failed the wealth path on unproved eligible lot supply. Do not regenerate an interest-only, liquidation-trigger or renamed residual-right buyer unless it uses a materially different transferable asset and independently solves the same supply/scale/working-capital objections.

- Owned / installer-routed reactive-power savings contracts: killed after simulated scores 69 and 72. Both judges found the exact five-year customer price commercially inferior to outright purchase or ordinary finance and the installer annuity copyable through one incumbent offer. The candidate also overstated gross margin and failed to deduct asset debt from enterprise value. Do not regenerate a shortened, cheaper, warranty-labelled or dashboard-enhanced financed-cabinet/ESCO version; a new energy candidate must have a different payer, transaction, and control point.

Equivalent content is easily commissioned by staffing/assessment incumbents, and the proposed calibration data sit partly with a user-employer different from the payer. Do not regenerate with more roles, languages, AI scoring, credentials or integrations unless the new business has a different buyer-controlled control point.

## Reconsideration Rule

A previous family may re-enter only when the new candidate documents a different buyer or urgent event and a materially stronger acquisition mechanism, economics, distribution, and control point. The control point must be owned, signed, prepaid, assigned, reserved, routed, licensed, or directly buildable before multiple speculative counterparties cooperate. Cosmetic naming, higher pricing, larger market-share assumptions, or a new AI feature do not qualify.

## Required Improvement in This Run

The preferred shape has:

- one payer who directly controls the paid action;
- hundreds of high-ACV reachable buyers rather than tens of thousands of low-value users;
- a control point present at contract signature, rights issuance, or transaction capture;
- a bottom-up named buyer or event population;
- a paid proof that does not require a finished regulated product, scientific breakthrough, or several partners;
- compounding online distribution, workflow, transaction data, or rights that broad incumbents cannot reproduce through one routine contract.
