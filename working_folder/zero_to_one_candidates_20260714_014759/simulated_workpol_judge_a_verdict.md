## Diagnosis

WorkPol Audio has a sharp insight: job-specific comprehension of noisy operational instructions may predict warehouse and production success better than conversational Polish or generic language tests. The beachhead, pricing, manual pilot, contractor stack and kill criteria are concrete and compatible with the founder. However, willingness to pay, predictive lift, outcome-data access, CAC and repeatability remain hypotheses, while the owned item bank is easy for an incumbent to reproduce. It is testable but not defensible or evidenced enough.

## Copy risk and objections

Strongest copy risk: an assessment/staffing platform commissions equivalent items and bundles them through its existing base. TestGorilla already markets a Warehouse Picker test and audio/assessment capability.

1. No WTP evidence. At 200 tests, the annual minimum makes effective price 159 PLN/test, not 99 PLN.
2. Outcome data sit partly with user-employers while the agency pays.
3. Copyright protects exact items, not the blueprint or alternative recordings.
4. The wealth case is fragile: at 30% margin and 3.5×, founder equity is about 8.5m PLN.
5. Validity/fairness and possible AI Act employment-system treatment are commercial risks.
