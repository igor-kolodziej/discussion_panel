# Finalist: CEE One-Stop Sync Rights Catalogue

## Thesis and founder fit

Acquire multi-year, contractually exclusive synchronization mandates that cover both the composition/publishing and master/performer layers for a curated slate of CEE tracks, then sell fast, auditable one-stop licences to European game, advertising and audiovisual producers. The founder can operate catalogue data, search pages, buyer lists and asynchronous licensing beside employment; specialist music counsel and a commission-only curator are needed. The founder has no assumed entertainment status or introductions.

## Customer, pain, wedge and insight

The first customer is a 20–200-person European game studio or production company that wants a distinctive CEE cue without negotiating separate publisher, composer, performer and label permissions. Its workaround is production libraries, bespoke composition or slow manual clearance. ZAiKS itself explains that synchronization normally requires permissions it does not issue as a complete bundle. The gap is therefore a small catalogue whose files are genuinely ready to sign, not another music-search interface.

The first product is a fixed-scope worldwide game/trailer/web licence with a rights schedule, provenance file and 48-hour quote. It excludes broadcast or territory terms the mandate does not cover. One track is never represented as cleared until counsel has checked every relevant party.

## Beachhead, first acquisition and control point

Beachhead: European independent game studios and Polish audiovisual producers seeking dark folk, post-industrial and regionally specific instrumental cues. Buyer acquisition starts with a list segmented from Video Games Europe's approximately 6,000 studios, EGDF's 2,500 represented studios and KIPA's 170+ Polish entities. Twenty public, source-linked cue pages are used in targeted email—not influencer or founder-brand distribution.

The resource is a signed 3–5-year exclusive sync and sub-publishing mandate for defined uses/territories covering both rights layers. Secure 20–30 tracks from small labels and self-controlled artists for ≤20,000 PLN in modest option fees plus 40% of collected licence receipts. Each mandate contains a rights schedule, warranties, audit rights and a direct-signing power reviewed by Polish music counsel. This is Tier 1 for the contracted tracks; a general relationship or non-exclusive listing is not control. Strongest copy risk: a large library signs the same artists after expiry or uses substitutes that satisfy the mood equally well.

## Economics, distribution and compounding

- Average licence receipt hypothesis: 30,000 PLN; rightsholder share 40%; payment fees/counsel/fulfilment 5%; approximately 55% gross margin.
- First-customer CAC hypothesis: 7,500 PLN from 100–150 tightly targeted contacts; first-licence gross profit 16,500 PLN, giving first-sale CAC payback if conversion exists.
- Renewal is not assumed. Compounding comes from more exclusive tracks, licence templates, buyer search pages, cleared stems, usage history and repeat production accounts.
- Expansion: game cues → trailers/ads → German/Nordic supervisors → more CEE territories and commissioned stems. It must not become a generic artist agency or custom-composition studio.

Conservative annual licence volume is 4% of the 6,000-studio upper-bound pool and requires repeat buyers plus advertising/film; it is a hypothesis, not validated demand. The gate fails if a named reachable census cannot support at least 2,400 plausible licence-buying accounts or if contracts average below 20,000 PLN.

## Reachability and staged capital

- Days 1–30: spend 7,000 PLN on specialist contract/chain-of-title templates; identify 60 small rights-holders and 120 studios.
- Days 31–75: sign 20–30 fully cleared tracks for ≤13,000 PLN additional option fees; publish cue sheets and stems.
- Days 76–150: after outreach authorization, target 120 studios/producers and require licence payment before file delivery.
- By day 180: two unrelated paid licences at ≥12,000 PLN each and positive per-licence contribution.

Maximum pre-proof spend is 35,000 PLN, compatible with the five-hour/day employment constraint. Remaining founder capital is released only after two successful deliveries and a third unrelated payer; family capital is excluded until repeat demand. Leave employment only after at least ten unrelated paying accounts or contracted recurring demand, positive contribution, repeatable acquisition and 12 months of personal runway.

## Dependencies and kill criteria

| Dependency | Proof before scale | Kill condition |
|---|---|---|
| Complete rights | Counsel-checked schedules for both layers | Any material rights ambiguity or mandates are merely non-exclusive listings |
| Seller supply | 30 tracks within 20k PLN upfront | Rights owners demand advances that break first-cycle economics |
| Buyer demand | Two ≥12k PLN licences by day 180 | No two payers after 120 qualified contacts or median WTP <12k PLN |
| Repeatable economics | ≥55% gross margin and one repeat/referral | Bespoke clearance labour >15% of revenue |
| Wealth denominator | ≥2,400 named plausible accounts and repeat frequency | 240 annual licences requires >10% niche share or hit assumptions |

If killed, reusable assets are contract research, catalogue metadata and buyer interviews; do not pivot into music consulting.
