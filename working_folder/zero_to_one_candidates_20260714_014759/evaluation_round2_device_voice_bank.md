# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: Contract-Cleared EU Device Voice Bank

## Diagnosis

The candidate owns its recordings and performer permissions, but provenance is a feature that powerful voice platforms can procure through the same performer contract. No regulation requires the customer to buy this separate bank. Sixty OEMs at 100,000 PLN annual value and 3.6m PLN later distributions are not supported by current evidence, while the first deposit may buy bespoke localization rather than a reusable asset.

## Evidence state

Sourced: broad medtech company/exhibitor counts, AI transparency timing, incumbent voice libraries. Hypotheses: voice-relevant product count, procurement pain, performer cost, OEM ACV, reuse and retention.

## Gates, vetoes and control point

- **Control point:** Tier 1 for exact recordings; effective Tier 2 because one vendor contract gives an incumbent substitutes. Strongest copy risk: ElevenLabs/Acapela or a localization studio bundles a fully cleared voice and lifecycle tooling.
- **Reachability Gate:** conditional fail. The build fits 35,000 PLN, but buyer-cycle evidence does not support an independent ≥25,000 PLN deposit by day 180.
- **Founder Wealth Gate:** fail. Neither 1,200 qualified products nor 100,000 PLN recurring value is established.
- **Fatal vetoes:** generic-provider bundling, trust-heavy medical procurement, bespoke-service drift and a compliance narrative without mandated demand.

## Recommendation

**kill.** Do not cosmetically add more languages or provenance fields; that preserves the same buyer, trust mechanism and copy risk.
