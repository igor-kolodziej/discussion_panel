# Confirmed Business Idea Candidate

## Idea

**Severe-Delay Yield Lots** is a buyer of batches of statutory late-payment interest and recovery-compensation rights from solvent companies that are closing.

The first seller is the registered liquidator of a Polish company in voluntary liquidation that supplied recurring, non-medical public or B2B contracts. The company has already received every invoice principal, so this is not factoring and does not finance defaulted trade debt. What remains are small but collectively meaningful legal rights created because invoices of at least 100,000 PLN were paid at least 60 days late: statutory interest plus the applicable 100 EUR recovery compensation.

Severe-Delay Yield Lots buys only complete batches of at least 10 clean claims. It pays cash, receives schedule-level assignments, notifies debtors, and owns the settlement and recovery economics. It rejects construction/performance disputes, healthcare, live principal debt, assignment-restricted contracts, active strategic supplier relationships, delays under 60 days, invoices under 100,000 PLN, and any claim that raises proportionality or abuse concerns.

## One-Sentence Thesis

A closing supplier will sell a clean batch of already-accrued late-payment rights at a discount because immediate cash and clean books matter more than running dozens of small collections, while the buyer can compound document, debtor and recovery data into superior portfolio pricing.

## Founder Fit

The founder is a Warsaw data scientist with up to five hours per day, 100,000 PLN of initial capital and strong AI/automation leverage. Event detection, entity matching, claim calculation, document classification, cohort pricing and written acquisition fit that profile. The founder does not need a camera-based brand or constant networking.

A Polish commercial lawyer is mandatory for the initial opinion, documents and exceptions. The founder does not represent sellers, give legal advice, or appear as counsel. A part-time paralegal/operations contractor is added only after cash recoveries demonstrate positive contribution.

## Customer And Pain

The beachhead customer is the registered liquidator of a solvent Polish limited company in voluntary liquidation. The company previously supplied a recurring public or private commercial contract and has at least 10 fully paid invoices of at least 100,000 PLN that were each at least 60 days late.

The liquidator must identify, realise or dispose of residual assets and finish the company. A minimum 10-claim lot has an estimated face around 30,000 PLN: large enough to matter to owners and creditors, but repetitive and small enough per claim that internal collection can delay closeout and consume professional time. Ignoring it sacrifices value. Selling it for cash provides a documented arm's-length disposal, immediate liquidity and no future collection administration.

The current workaround is to omit the rights from closeout, send unprioritised reminders, ask a law firm to act case by case, or bundle them ambiguously with principal receivables. Severe-Delay Yield Lots makes a binding purchase offer after one evidence upload.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

The first product is a 48-hour lot quote and purchase:

1. The liquidator uploads the contract/order, invoice schedule, acceptance evidence, due dates, bank/payment export, notices and authority documents.
2. The rules engine verifies seller/debtor identity, invoice principal, contractual due date, payment date, delay, statutory rate/tier, limitation, dispute status and assignment language.
3. Counsel reviews only exceptions; rejected claims never enter the price.
4. The company offers 30% of screened face for the entire accepted lot and pays after assignment conditions are met.
5. The company owns each listed right, notifies the debtor and controls settlement or court escalation through retained counsel.

It is not a dashboard, generic debt collection, factoring, a success-fee service or a law firm. Principal must already be paid. The seller has no contingency bill and gives only defined warranties covering title, authenticity, payment dates and dispute status.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

Late-payment law creates a strange asset: it is real and automatic, yet often uneconomic for its original owner to pursue. The mismatch is strongest during company closeout. The liquidator values speed, certainty and defensible asset disposal; the buyer values a portfolio whose evidence, debtor response and recovery cost can be standardised.

Banks and factors finance principal receivables. Collection firms usually act on a mandate. Reminder software calculates 40/70/100 EUR. The wedge here is outright purchase of post-payment ancillary rights from an authorised closeout seller, with severe-delay and high-principal filters that prevent nuisance economics.

## Underused Momentum

- Polish law provides statutory commercial late-payment interest and fixed 40/70/100 EUR recovery compensation.
- The Polish Supreme Court states that the fixed compensation arises without proof that recovery costs were incurred and has treated the claim as non-accessory in limitation analysis.
- CJEU case C-585/20 concerned public-authority debts assigned by undertakings to a collection company and held that the fixed minimum is payable per late invoice.
- Mandatory KSeF adoption standardises invoice identities and dates, although bank payment dates and contractual restrictions still require source evidence.
- KRS/MSiG closeout events and UZP/BZP award histories provide an observable, lawful acquisition trigger without privileged introductions.

## Beachhead Market

The first fact pattern is deliberately narrow:

- solvent voluntary liquidation, with a registered liquidator who can execute the sale;
- Polish company and Polish non-medical public debtor;
- completed recurring supply or service contract;
- invoice principal at least 100,000 PLN;
- at least 60 days late and principal fully paid;
- at least 10 clean claims in the lot;
- no performance dispute, construction claim, healthcare debtor, anti-assignment clause or current strategic supplier relationship;
- limitation leaves enough collection time.

Bankruptcy estates, court/creditor-committee-controlled sales and unpaid principal are excluded from the POC. They would add authority and capital dependencies before proof.

## Primary Control Point

The control point is the executed schedule-level assignment: title to every listed accrued claim, complete source evidence, seller warranties, debtor notice and the right to settle or pursue. Each accepted lot is owned legal inventory at transaction close. A referral, calculator or list is not the control point.

Across lots, the company also owns a proprietary dataset joining closeout type, assignment clause, debtor, invoice value, delay, evidence quality, notice, response, recovery, time and cost. That data improves acceptance and bid prices.

The strongest copy risk is BFF, a debt purchaser, factor or collection platform adding the same residual-right product. The defence is not legal exclusivity; it is the closeout-event acquisition graph, 48-hour batch diligence, strict high-value cohort and accumulated recovery curves for a niche larger firms may initially ignore.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

The foundational resource is clean title to one 10+ claim lot from an authorised voluntary liquidator.

It is secured in sequence:

- 6,000 PLN fixed-scope Polish legal opinion and contract pack covering independent assignment after principal payment, liquidator authority and sale process, anti-assignment clauses, debtor notice, tax/accounting, licensing perimeter and court representation;
- 4,000 PLN KRS/MSiG × BZP event matching and secure document upload;
- no more than 12,000 PLN for one or two lots after the opinion and document screen;
- 6,000 PLN bounded notice and enforcement reserve.

An unknown founder can secure the resource because the liquidator is disposing of a non-core residual asset for immediate cash, not trusting the founder to manage a live supplier relationship. Public registers identify the exact decision-maker and contract history. Counsel, a transparent claim schedule and an arm's-length price supply authority rather than founder status.

Before serious capital, proof must include one executed 10+ claim assignment, accepted debtor notices, at least five debtor cash payments, at least 800 PLN observed contribution per accepted claim after lot acquisition cost, and a second qualified seller willing to transact. Maximum pre-proof exposure is 28,000 PLN.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

For a 100,000+ PLN invoice paid at least 60 days late, statutory interest plus 100 EUR compensation can plausibly approach or exceed 3,000 PLN. The model uses a 3,000 PLN average face hypothesis, but every offer uses the exact statutory rate, dates and legally prescribed EUR conversion.

| Unit item | Base hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Average accrued interest plus compensation face | 3,000 PLN |
| Seller cash price, 30% of face | 900 PLN |
| Expected cash collection, 80% of face | 2,400 PLN |
| Direct notice, payment, counsel and filing cost | 150 PLN |
| Contribution after claim purchase and direct recovery | 1,350 PLN |
| Gross margin on collected cash | 56% |

At the 10-claim minimum, the seller receives about 9,000 PLN, expected cash collection is 24,000 PLN and expected claim contribution is 13,500 PLN before lot CAC and central overhead. Event-linked CAC is hypothesised at 1,200 PLN per acquired lot. Gross-profit CAC payback therefore occurs after roughly one recovered claim in the base case.

Collected claim cash is reported as revenue. Claim purchase cost and direct recovery cost are cost of revenue. Uncollected face is never recognised as revenue. The company does not count recovered principal because it never purchases principal.

### Distribution Moat

Daily KRS/MSiG liquidation detection is joined to public procurement awards and company histories. The ranking model favours recurring contracts likely to produce 20+ large invoices. Written offers go to the registered liquidator after outreach is separately authorised.

This makes acquisition event-driven rather than generic cold sales. Later, accountants and liquidation law firms can use an API quote or refer sellers, but no partnership is required for the POC. Source-linked educational pages and a secure KSeF/bank-export scanner create compounding inbound around closeout and statutory-interest searches.

Every closed lot improves prospect ranking, clause screening and debtor pricing. A competitor can copy a calculator, but it cannot instantly copy years of event-to-recovery outcomes or indexed inbound pages.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. Dominate Polish voluntary-liquidation lots against non-medical public debtors.
2. Add solvent Polish B2B recurring-contract closeouts with high-credit-quality debtors and separate recovery cohorts.
3. Enter Czechia and Slovakia only after local legal opinions and ring-fenced paid pilots.
4. Add further EU jurisdictions only when the bottom-up closeout census keeps the required share below 10%.
5. Offer event/API pricing to liquidation accountants and lawyers.
6. Obtain institutional revolving finance only after stable recovery cohorts; never use it to buy invoice principal.

The company remains narrow: post-payment rights, material delay, material invoice, batch closeout and clean assignment. If scale requires bankruptcy auctions, unpaid principal, nuisance claims or generic legal services, the thesis has failed.

## Local Adaptation Defense

The Polish wedge combines KRS/MSiG events, BZP/UZP contracts, Polish assignment wording, statutory rates, KSeF exports and Polish debtor procedure. CJEU precedent supports a European pattern but does not create a one-click expansion passport. Every country requires a local opinion, contract, limitation map and isolated recovery cohort.

Global factors have more capital and trust, but their core product is principal receivable finance. Severe-Delay Yield Lots starts where principal has already been paid and only a closing company values immediate disposal of the residual right.

## Why This Could Compound

Each acquired claim produces labels that cannot be obtained from public law alone: clause permissibility, missing-document pattern, debtor response, voluntary payment, settlement, filing need, days to cash, direct cost and realised loss. Those labels improve portfolio bids and reduce manual review.

Closeout event pages and search content accumulate online. Repeat liquidators, accountants and legal practices can bring future lots. Cash recoveries, not founder labour, finance later inventory. Standardised diligence and notices let operations scale by exception rather than one professional per claim.

The conservative case needs 400 lots annually. This was 1.6% of all 2025 Polish KRS entrepreneur-register removals, but that denominator is only an upper bound: it includes non-voluntary removals and says nothing about eligible invoices. The operating plan therefore requires a bottom-up count of at least 4,000 eligible annual lots across Poland, Czechia and Slovakia before the conservative wealth path is accepted; the expected and strong cases require at least 8,000 and 16,000 eligible lots across broader Europe so none assumes more than 10% share.

## Reinvestment And Compounding Model

While the founder remains employed, the conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax cash legally available for distribution. Optional founder distributions cannot exceed 5%. The strong case reinvests 100% during growth. Reinvestment funds claim purchases, event coverage, automation, counsel playbooks and debtor cohorts.

Founder capital does not finance unpaid principal. Debt or strategic capital is introduced only after multiple independent sellers, stable cash recoveries, positive contribution and repeat acquisition. Retained earnings are not double-counted in the business valuation.

## Reachability And 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- **Days 1–30:** obtain the 6,000 PLN legal opinion/contract pack; build the first 250-event KRS/MSiG × BZP list; create the exact calculator and exclusion rules.
- **Days 31–60:** deploy a 4,000 PLN secure upload and batch schedule; prepare written offers but do not send them without separate outreach authorisation.
- **Days 61–120:** after outreach is authorised, obtain a signed lot from an authorised liquidator and spend no more than 12,000 PLN on one or two lots; send debtor notices.
- **Days 121–180:** require five debtor cash payments, at least 800 PLN realised contribution per accepted claim after lot CAC, and a second qualified seller willing to transact.

The signed asset purchase is a real purchase transaction with the seller. The decisive paid evidence is cash from at least five debtors. Delivery is compatible with employment because the first cohort is small, document-led and counsel-bounded.

## Capital Tranches And Maximum Loss

1. **Before paid proof: maximum 28,000 PLN** for law, secure tooling, one/two lots and bounded recovery.
2. **Next founder capital:** released only after successful notices/delivery, five cash recoveries and positive observed contribution.
3. **Remaining original 100,000 PLN:** released only after at least three unrelated acquired lots, 20+ cash recoveries, repeat or recurring seller demand, and documented CAC/payback.
4. **Possible family 100,000 PLN:** excluded until multiple independent sellers, repeatable acquisition, stable contribution, clear risk-adjusted scale and no unresolved legal perimeter.

Maximum loss before paid proof is 28,000 PLN. Every lot is capped; no portfolio is purchased because a future partner, court or financier might rescue it.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder remains employed until all conditions hold:

- at least three unrelated acquired sellers and either repeat liquidator/accountant flow or contracted recurring access;
- at least 100 cash recoveries with positive contribution economics;
- a measured acquisition channel with acceptable CAC and payback;
- no unresolved assignment, licensing, tax or representation issue;
- at least 12 months of personal runway.

No score, forecast or single successful lot authorises leaving employment.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

If claim purchase fails, reusable assets are the KRS/BZP event graph, KSeF/bank reconciliation, statutory claim calculator, clause classifier, secure document schedule and cohort-analysis code. These can be licensed to an established factor or finance-operations provider.

The founder should not pivot into a generic recovery desk, legal consultancy or unpaid-principal factor. Those shapes abandon the owned asset and controlled-loss logic.

## Validation Plan

Validation proceeds in irreversible-risk order:

1. written legal opinion on the exact fact pattern;
2. official-data census of 250 annualised Polish candidates and a bottom-up three-country eligible-lot estimate;
3. one 10+ claim purchase from an authorised voluntary liquidator;
4. accepted debtor notices;
5. five cash recoveries inside 180 days;
6. realised contribution of at least 800 PLN per accepted claim after acquisition CAC;
7. a second qualified seller and then three unrelated lots;
8. 100-claim cohort before material automation or finance;
9. local-law ring-fenced tests before every country expansion.

Research, internal review and chatbot judgment can make the proposal validated enough to pursue the 28,000 PLN test. They do not approve serious capital. Serious capital requires the real transactions above.

## Kill Criteria

- Counsel cannot confirm independent assignment, liquidator authority, licensing perimeter or a defensible sale process.
- More than half of sampled contracts prohibit or condition assignment.
- Fewer than 250 annualised Polish candidates survive public-history filters.
- The three-country bottom-up model contains fewer than 4,000 eligible annual lots.
- No authorised seller transfers a 10+ claim lot, or no second seller is willing.
- Fewer than five of the first clean claims pay inside 180 days.
- Clean-face collection is below 70%, median cash cycle exceeds 120 days, or realised contribution is below 800 PLN per accepted claim.
- Average accepted face is below 2,500 PLN or average lot is below 10 claims.
- CAC payback exceeds the first lot.
- Scaling requires unpaid-principal finance, bankruptcy auctions, nuisance claims or more than 10% of the measured eligible market.
