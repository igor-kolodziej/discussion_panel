## Work and assessment flows

- [Statistics Poland, foreigners working in Poland, January 2026](https://stat.gov.pl/statystyki-eksperymentalne/kapital-ludzki/cudzoziemcy-wykonujacy-prace-w-polsce-w-styczniu-2026-r-%2C15%2C39.html): approximately 1.119m foreign workers; establishes scale, not assessment demand.
- [Official 2024 employment-agency report](https://psz.praca.gov.pl/documents/d/urzad-pracy/raport-o-dzialalnosci-agencji-zatrudnienia-w-2024-r): 4,049 domestic placement agencies, 98,789 foreign placements, 698,081 temporary workers and 18,963 user-employers; these populations overlap and cannot be summed blindly.
- [State Labour Inspectorate on BHP duties](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/porady-prawne/obowiazki-i-prawa-pracownika-w-zakresie-bezpieczenstwa-i-higieny-pracy) and [pre-work training](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/porady-prawne/szkolenia-w-dziedzinie-bezpieczenstwa-i-higieny-pracy): workers must understand rules and employers must train them before work. WorkPol cannot replace the employer's mandatory instruction or exam.
- [PIP foreign-worker campaign](https://cudzoziemcywpracy.pip.gov.pl/) and [2025 translation requirement explanation](https://www.pip.gov.pl/aktualnosci/powierzenie-pracy-cudzoziemcowi-moze-wymagac-udzialu-tlumacza-przysieglego): employment documents must be understandable/translated. This is momentum for multilingual operations, not a legal mandate to buy an assessment.
- [PIP temporary-worker BHP responsibility](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/pytania-i-odpowiedzi/kto-przeprowadza-szkolenia-bhp-dla-pracownikow-tymczasowych): the user-employer remains responsible for workplace-specific instruction, so the product must be pre-placement work sampling, not outsourced certification.
- [Statistics Poland industrial results 2024](https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5477/9/8/1/przemysl_wyniki_dzialalnosci_2024.pdf) and [PARP food-sector skills report](https://www.parp.gov.pl/storage/publications/pdf/Raport-ywno-PARP-13-alt.pdf): context for ShiftFault/WeldSight; exact reachable subsets remain missing.
- [ULC 2026 drone briefing](https://ulc.gov.pl/_download/2026/Briefing_Dronowy_8.01.2026.pdf), [renewal guidance](https://ulc.gov.pl/drony/informacje-ogolne/przedluzanie-waznosci-kompetencji-pilota) and [designated exam-entity process](https://ulc.gov.pl/drony/prowadzenie-szkolen/informacja-o-przeprowadzaniu-egzaminow-teoretycznych-przez-wyznaczone-podmioty): prove the exam/designation mechanism and the small event count.

## Transferable rights and receivables

- [URE 2025 activity report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16226/SprawozdaniezdzialalnosciPrezesaUREw2025r.pdf) and [current Energy Efficiency Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/711/text/O/D20250711.pdf): 1,979 new applications, 1,330 certificates, 195,851 toe and an indicated market value near 434m PLN. Counsel must verify authorization, registry ownership, transfer timing and revocability for the exact contract.
- [PSE non-market redispatch information](https://www.pse.pl/pl_PL/redysponowanie-nierynkowe) and [calculation changes](https://www.pse.pl/-/zmiana-zasad-wyliczania-rekompensaty-za-redysponowanie-nierynkowe-instalacji-oze): establish curtailment compensation and correction periods; no source yet establishes assignment acceptance or seller discount.
- [Polish Public Procurement Law, current text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/793/text.pdf) and [2024 public procurement report](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/b61cce3a-f71c-4271-be11-d7eaa1a03f76): establish performance-security mechanics and procurement volume; the cash-tail pool is not measured.
- [PARP automation/robotization call](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/87946%3Aniemal-217-mln-zl-na-automatyzacje-i-robotyzacje-polskich-firm) and [GUS factoring operations 2024](https://stat.gov.pl/obszary-tematyczne/podmioty-gospodarcze-wyniki-finansowe/przedsiebiorstwa-finansowe/dzialalnosc-faktoringowa-przedsiebiorstw-finansowych-w-2024-r-%2C2%2C20.html): 31.3k clients and 523.4bn PLN purchased receivables demonstrate a mature, heavily competitive category.
- [Trade.gov.pl on assignment/factoring](https://www.trade.gov.pl/wiedza/jak-cesja-wierzytelnosci-wplywa-na-funkcjonowanie-firmy-transportowej/) and [Civil Code](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/795/ogl): assignment is generally possible subject to statute, contract and nature; exact receivables need counsel review.
- [Government DRS update](https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/16-mld-opakowan-wrocilo-do-obiegu-pol-roku-systemu-kaucyjnego) and [restaurant guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/czy-zaplace-kaucje-w-restauracji): 1.6bn returned containers and 62k points by end-May 2026, but logistics/control economics remain weak.

All prices, conversions, margins and customer behavior are hypotheses. No outreach or commitment occurred.

## Scientific benchmark assets screened after the funnel

- [European Accessibility Act](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en), [PDF/UA-2](https://pdfa.org/iso-14289-2-pdfua-2/) and [ALPSP directory](https://www.alpsp.org/membership/member-directory/) supported a STEM accessibility regression-vault direction, but its conservative wealth case reached only ~3.27m PLN founder value.
- [STM Integrity Hub](https://stm-assoc.org/what-we-do/strategic-areas/research-integrity/integrity-hub/) and [ALPSP integrity activity](https://www.alpsp.org/news-publications/industry-news/mdpi-rolls-out-ai-powered-ethics-checks-across-all-submissions/) supported hidden manuscript-integrity test cases; conservative founder value was ~4.7m PLN and a shared industry corpus is the dominant copy risk.
- [FDA electronic-record guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/electronic-systems-electronic-records-and-electronic-signatures-clinical-investigations-questions), [EMA ICH E6(R3)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-e6-good-clinical-practice-scientific-guideline) and [CDISC members](https://www.cdisc.org/members/search) supported a synthetic trial-lineage corpus, but the 77 direct technology-provider accounts could not support a conservative 10m PLN founder path.
- [SiLA 2026 AI activity](https://sila-standard.com/welcome-to-2026-the-first-sila-newsletter-of-the-year/) and [SLAS 2026 exhibitor context](https://www.slas.org/news-highlights/news-center/2026-news-releases/slas-honors-new-products-startups-and-scientific-excellence-at-its-record-breaking-2026-international-conference-and-exhibition-in-boston/) supported adversarial lab-agent protocols; only 49 directly qualified vendors and weak ownership of scientific facts killed the wealth/control case.
- [FDA AI-device performance-monitoring request](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/request-public-comment-measuring-and-evaluating-artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-device), [openFDA 510(k) API](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/510k/) and [EDPB consent materials](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/topic/consent_en) supported an EEG artifact vault, but the likely ~60-company buyer set, consent withdrawal and clinical generalisability made conservative founder wealth only ~2.53m PLN.
