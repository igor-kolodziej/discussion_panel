# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: Owned Reactive-Power Savings Contracts

## Diagnosis

This is the strongest second-pass finalist because paid proof is one measurable site, the founder has a legitimate installer-originated discovery edge, and the company owns both hardware and a five-year cash-flow contract. Its weakness is not technical feasibility but asymmetric power: Polish vendors, Orange and emerging energy-service companies already provide free bill analysis and compensation equipment, and at least one market offer claims savings without customer investment. The current candidate needs proof that installer routing plus asset underwriting can acquire a portfolio more cheaply than incumbents, not merely another financed cabinet.

## Evidence state

Sourced: regulated tariff mechanism, 1.68m A/B/C upper-bound points, numerous active equipment/service competitors and vendor-stated 6–12 month payback. Hypotheses: 2% eligibility, 36k PLN annual charges, 80% reduction, 55% savings share, 15k PLN landed cost, 13-month payback, 2–4k CAC and enforceable five-year terms.

## Gates, control point and vetoes

- **Control point:** Tier 1 at an installed site; portfolio-level Tier 2 until independent installer routing, underwriting data and asset finance compound. Strongest copy risk: an established compensation vendor or ESCO offers identical no-upfront savings share and finances it more cheaply.
- **Reachability Gate:** pass. A contract, installation and collected invoice can fit ≤180 days, ≤38,000 PLN and part-time work through licensed contractors.
- **Founder Wealth Gate:** conditional pass on arithmetic, not yet on evidence. 500 sites ×16k and 40% EBIT produces 10.88m founder equity at 4×/85% without distributions. It fails if eligibility is <2%, cohort payback exceeds 18 months, or acquisition is family-only.
- **Vetoes to resolve:** competitors already use free bill scans/no-investment claims; no exclusive installer routing; eligible-bill density unmeasured; contract enforceability and asset finance unproved.

## Recommendation

**pivot.** Preserve owned hardware, measured savings and one-site paid proof, but structurally move the control point upstream into installer-originated, exclusive savings-right acquisition plus a standardized underwriting/finance portfolio. The revision must make independent installer economics and contract routing explicit and must be re-evaluated before any panel.

## Additional competition evidence

[KOMPENSACJA.PL](https://www.kompensacja.pl/likwidacja-oplat-za-energie-bierna%2C5542.html) has operated in the category since 2000; [Voltenic](https://voltenic.pl/) advertises a bill calculator and 6–12 month payback; [Orange Polska](https://www.orange.pl/duze-firmy/kompensacja-mocy_biernej) offers free invoice analysis; [Powerberry](https://powerberry.pl/) markets compensation without customer investment. These do not disprove savings demand; they invalidate novelty based on free audit or financing alone.
