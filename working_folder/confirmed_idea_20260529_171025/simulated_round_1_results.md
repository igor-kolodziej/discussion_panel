## Repeated Objections

- The "retiring local operator book" pattern creates customer access, but not enough defensibility.
- Incumbents can copy the continuity offer once visible, often with better gross margins or technician capacity.
- The control point is too soft unless it controls payment direction, scarce inventory, admin credentials, legally transferred production rights, or recurring transaction flow.
- Part-time founder fit is weak when the product implies emergency field response.
- Gross margin targets are too low unless calculated after labor, travel, support, rework, warranty, shipping, and founder time.

## Decision

Do not send any round-1 finalist to real Zero To One. Simulated scores are all `<=85`.

Pivot away from generic local customer-book custody. The next candidate set must start with a harder control point:

- payment direction or prepaid transaction flow;
- legally transferred platform/admin asset;
- scarce named inventory already reserved by buyer deposit;
- formal assignment/mandate over a cash, order, or renewal stream;
- workflow access that creates a source-of-truth ledger before competitors see the transaction.
