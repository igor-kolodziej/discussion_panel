## Why No Candidate Passed

No Round 6 candidate cleared the required simulated `>85` gate. The best score was `67`, for SupplierPortal Payment-Blocker Release Lane. It had real cash urgency, but the reviewer capped it because the founder would control only a recovery workflow around blocked invoices, not enterprise AP priorities, payment approval, portal infrastructure, or durable payment rails.

Repeated objections across the round:

- case-level mandates and assignments protect one transaction but do not create durable distribution or market control;
- unknown-founder trust is still a bottleneck when the workflow touches ledgers, portals, contracts, payment claims, regulated goods, or clinical finance records;
- incumbents adjacent to the workflow have lower CAC and higher credibility;
- many apparent rights are small, expired, non-assignable, offset-only, or too expensive to collect;
- several candidates can produce niche cash-flow businesses, but not the domination-oriented company required by the gate.

## Notable Reviewer Findings

### ReturnPack

The reviewer accepted the hidden insight around neglected deposit ledgers and physical return rights, but scored `62` because profitability depends on a high hit rate of enforceable, accepted, recoverable deposits. Supplier acceptance, transport, sorting, cleaning, rejection, legal variation, and weak retention can consume the spread.

### ReclaimGas

The reviewer accepted the EU F-gas timing and emergency cold-chain pain, but scored `61` because scarcity may be local and intermittent, legal handling can kill the model, and existing reclaimers/wholesalers already have licenses, cylinders, logistics, certificates, and contractor trust.

### WarrantyCredit

The reviewer scored `61` because "approved" warranty credits may be offset-only, small, non-assignable, or payable only to an authorized account. The purchase model is premature without repeat OEM/AP acknowledgments and proven payment direction.

### PortalPayRelease

The reviewer scored `67`, the highest in the round. The pain is urgent and ROI-based, but payment release remains outside the founder's control. The idea risks becoming manual AP/AR admin unless repeat buyer/portal blockage patterns become a strong private playbook.

### PublicPaySidecar

The reviewer scored `48`. The economics are too thin at the proposed average claim size, and the fixed compensation component under Polish commercial-payment rules may not be freely assignable, weakening the purchase model.

### TrialSitePay

The reviewer scored `58`. The payment-query scope is sharper than broad clinical-trial admin, but still trust-heavy, episodic, relationship-driven, and easy for clinical-finance operators or SMO consultants to copy.

## Gate Decision

Because the highest simulated score was `67`, no candidate was eligible for real Zero To One validation. The browser was not used, consistent with the shared-browser constraint and the validation workflow.

No confirmed idea file may be written from this round.
