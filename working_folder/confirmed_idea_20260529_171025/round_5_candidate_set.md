# Round 5 Candidate Set: Payment And Shipment Release Mandates

Created: 2026-05-29 Europe/Warsaw

## Candidate A: DocCredit LC Discrepancy Release Mandate

### Scored Wording For Formal Simulated Review

**DocCredit LC Discrepancy Release Mandate** is a Poland/CEE payment-release service for exporters whose letter-of-credit payment is currently blocked by a live bank discrepancy/refusal notice.

The product is not generic export consulting, pre-shipment training, factoring, legal advice, bank representation, or freight forwarding. It starts only when an exporter already has a live documentary-credit payment delay: a bank discrepancy/refusal notice, LC terms, presented document set, freight/insurance/origin/source documents, and permission to coordinate a corrected presentation or applicant waiver route through the exporter and its bank/forwarder/buyer contacts.

Control point: signed payment-release mandate for a named LC case, the live bank discrepancy notice, LC terms, full presented-document set, exporter authorization, source-document issuer contacts, bank/forwarder/buyer communication thread, prepaid case fee, and anonymized discrepancy/outcome memory rights.

Cold start: target Polish/CEE exporters and export departments in machinery, food, chemicals, metals, furniture, packaging, and specialty manufacturing, plus freight forwarders, export accountants, and trade-finance consultants. The offer is: "If your LC payment is blocked by a discrepancy notice, I map the discrepancy to UCP 600/ISBP-style document logic, identify which documents can be corrected, which need applicant waiver, and which are a true no-go, then coordinate the evidence/resubmission pack with your existing bank/forwarder contacts. No payment guarantee, no legal opinion, no bank replacement."

12-month proof under 100,000 PLN: 3 signed live mandates, at least 2 payments released or applicant waivers accepted, at least 150,000 PLN equivalent moved or clarified, first 20-case discrepancy taxonomy by bank/country/document type, and one forwarder/accountant/trade-finance partner routing a second case.

Pricing: 3,000-8,000 PLN fixed fee for a live discrepancy triage/resubmission pack plus 1-3% success fee on released cash above a threshold, or 5,000-15,000 PLN fixed for high-value cases. Only accept cases with at least EUR 25,000 blocked or strategically important repeat buyer relationship.

Compounding control point: discrepancy wording, LC clause, document type, source issuer, bank practice, correction path, applicant waiver path, cycle time, and outcome by route. Once a case is signed, competitors cannot copy that exact payment-release mandate. The longer-term moat is the accepted discrepancy-resolution memory for Polish/CEE exporters and trade partners.

### Market / Rules Anchors

- ICC UCP 600 Article 14 requires banks to examine presentations on the basis of documents alone and gives banks a maximum of five banking days after presentation to determine compliance.
- ICC UCP 600 Article 16 allows refusal for non-complying presentation and allows the issuing bank to approach the applicant for waiver; refusal notice must be given by the fifth banking day.
- ICC Banking Commission Technical Advisors Briefing No. 3 says many problems arise from poor credit drafting, weak understanding of UCP workflows, document production/collation issues, excess data on documents, and restricted access to ISBP/ICC opinions.
- OCC trade-finance guidance explains that commercial documentary LCs are commonly used for shipment contracts and that prompt exporter payment depends on conforming documents; single transactions may require many documents and discrepancies can cause failure to obtain conforming documents or resolve material errors.

### Main Risk

Trade-finance expertise and trust may be too high for an unknown founder. Banks, forwarders, and export finance teams already touch the workflow. Some discrepancies are impossible because of true late shipment, expired credit, missing originals, buyer dispute, fraud/sanctions risk, or source-document issuer refusal. The business must reject legal disputes and only handle document-resolution cases where source documents or waiver paths are realistically available.

## Candidate B: DIWASS Waste Shipment Release Operator

### Scored Wording For Formal Simulated Review

**DIWASS Waste Shipment Release Operator** is a Poland/CEE release desk for recyclers, waste brokers, and manufacturers whose cross-border waste shipments are blocked or slowed by digital notification/acceptance workflows under the EU Waste Shipment Regulation and DIWASS system.

The product is not generic waste compliance, an environmental-law opinion, a dashboard, or a transport marketplace. It starts only with live or recurring shipment lanes where the waste holder already has waste code, destination facility, carrier, commercial agreement, and shipment need. The startup operates as an authorized shipper-side document/release coordinator, not as waste owner, carrier, recycler, or legal certifier.

Control point: delegated DIWASS/GIOS workflow access or formal authorization for named shipments, live shipment file, destination facility/contact, authority/consignee acceptance thread, carrier-ready document packet, prepaid per-shipment fee or monthly lane retainer, and anonymized route/waste-code/facility acceptance memory rights.

Cold start: contact 30-60 Polish recyclers/exporters, waste brokers, and industrial waste holders moving repeat EU routes. Offer fixed-fee processing of 3 live shipments or one recurring lane: if the first accepted digital flow does not move the shipment file forward, no retainer.

12-month proof under 100,000 PLN: 10 paying exporters/brokers, 150+ shipments processed, 5 recurring destination facilities/lanes, repeat accepted template library by waste code/country/facility, 70%+ gross margin on repeat lanes, and at least one customer renewing a monthly lane retainer.

Pricing: 250-700 PLN per shipment for repeat low-complexity flows; 2,000-6,000 PLN/month per recurring exporter/broker lane; higher fixed fees only for urgent or complex shipment files. Gross margin can exceed 70% only after route templates repeat.

Compounding control point: accepted route/facility/waste-code patterns, authority questions, document fields, rejection reasons, facility response habits, and carrier-ready packet templates. The founder controls live shipment files only through authorization and repeated route memory.

### Market / Rules Anchors

- European Commission announced the DIWASS platform go-live for the EU's new Waste Shipment Regulation on 2026-05-21.
- The DIWASS route is official/digital, so rejected or incomplete digital workflow can directly affect shipment timing.
- Polish GIOS also published DIWASS digitization notices, making the local route relevant for Polish exporters/brokers.

### Main Risk

This may collapse into generic waste-compliance admin. Waste brokers, large recyclers, and TMS/compliance vendors can copy once demand is visible. The ticket size may be too low unless focused on recurring lanes. Control depends on delegated workflow access and actual accepted shipment files, not advice.

## Round 5 Decision Before Scoring

Advance both A and B to fresh simulated scoring. DocCredit has stronger payment WTP and lower duplicate risk; DIWASS has stronger current regulatory timing and repeat-lane potential but may be low-ticket compliance workflow.
