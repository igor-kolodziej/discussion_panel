## Why Round 5 Failed

Both candidates had real acute pain. Both failed because their control points were not strong enough for a part-time unknown founder:

- the work is expert- and trust-heavy;
- live cases are hidden and hard to acquire cold;
- incumbents already sit next to the workflow;
- the signed mandate controls one case, not durable demand;
- regulatory/documentary mistakes carry asymmetric downside;
- compounding pattern memory helps execution but does not by itself create a hard moat.

## Pivot

Do not keep testing document-release desks unless the founder can control payment redirection, externally acknowledged receivables, an owned/titled asset, or a buyer-paid exclusive right before doing expert work.

The next search should focus on recognized payment/asset-transfer proof:

- debtor/operator/payment-provider acknowledged receivable control;
- buyer-funded acquisition of a named asset/right;
- recurring transaction flow with payment instruction or account custody;
- high-value cases where proof is released cash, not accepted advice.
