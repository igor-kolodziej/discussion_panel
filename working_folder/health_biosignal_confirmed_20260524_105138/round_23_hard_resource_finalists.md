# Round 23: Hard-Resource Finalists

## Selection Logic

Earlier candidates repeatedly failed because they were advice, evidence packets, dashboards, coordination, or routed flow without durable control. This round only carries candidates where the founder can control a physical sample, physical device, transaction release, archive custody, or case queue during the 2-month POC.

## Finalists For Simulated Scoring

### 1. SeedLot DNA Claim Rail

Genetic claim-and-payment evidence rail for Polish/CEE specialty seed distributors, greenhouse growers, nursery operators, and crop importers when a seed lot produces wrong-variety, impurity, counterfeit, or supplier-credit disputes.

Control point: signed claim mandate, retained/sealed seed samples, chain-of-custody, lab slot, and supplier-payment/credit claim protocol.

### 2. SpeciesDNA Supplier Payment-Release Gate

Food DNA verification gate tied to supplier payment release for fraud-prone meat/seafood/imported food lots.

Control point: buyer-side supplier clause routing selected lots through sealed sampling and DNA testing before payment/release.

### 3. NeuroDevice Acceptance Escrow

Accepted-use escrow for second-hand EEG/EMG/PSG/neurofeedback equipment transactions where buyers fear dead channels, missing software dongles, expired licenses, wrong accessories, or unusable exports.

Control point: seller option/ROFR, buyer deposit, physical inspection custody, technician test slot, signal-generator/phantom acceptance checklist, and escrow/release workflow.

### 4. Legacy EEG Archive Extraction Rights

Provider-side rescue of old EEG archives from proprietary workstations, CDs, dead PCs, PDFs, and vendor databases before system replacement, clinic closure, or staff retirement.

Control point: DPA/processor agreement, onsite media custody, old software/export recipes, EDF/PDF/metadata matching workflow, and archive request retainer.

### 5. OncoBlock Molecular Access Rail

Tumor block/unstained-slide retrieval and chain-of-custody handoff for oncology molecular testing, NGS, second opinions, and trial screening.

Control point: clinic-routed patient authorizations, pathology-material requests, courier chain, block/slide custody, lab handoff, and return tracking.

### 6. POC Genetic Salvage Rail

Products-of-conception specimen capture logistics for recurrent pregnancy-loss genetics when tissue is perishable, contaminated, or operationally lost before testing.

Control point: pre-positioned specimen kits, clinic-routed eligible cases, patient consent, courier pickup, lab accessioning, maternal sample coordination, and reportable-result tracking.

## Current Hypothesis

SeedLot has the strongest chance to cross the simulated `>90` gate because it ties genetics to a commercial claim/payment event rather than a health trust bottleneck. NeuroDevice Acceptance Escrow is the strongest biosignal candidate because it can control transaction release. OncoBlock and POC Salvage are more clinically meaningful but likely suffer from hospital/clinic trust and lab-copy risk.
