# Round 24: Pre-Event Routing Pivots

## Why This Round Exists

Round 23 showed that physical custody after a dispute or case starts is still too episodic. This round moves the control point **before** the incident: purchase-order clauses, all-lot sample escrow, all-event clinical SOP placement, or pre-booked test-day capacity.

## Candidate A: SpeciesDNA Supplier Payment Gate

**Scored wording draft:** SpeciesDNA Supplier Payment Gate is a genetics-enabled supplier-payment release rail for Polish/CEE seafood, meat, and high-fraud-risk food importers/private-label suppliers. It is not a generic food-authenticity test. The buyer inserts a purchase-order clause for selected high-risk suppliers/lots: final payment, supplier onboarding, or retailer shipment release is conditional on a sealed sample collected under SpeciesDNA chain-of-custody and a contracted DNA/species-authentication lab result. The founder is paid as the neutral sample custodian and release-evidence operator, with lab costs passed through.

**Control point:** buyer-signed PO/procurement clause naming the founder's workflow before supplier payment/release; physical sample custody; lab slots; recurring lot flow.

**2-month proof:** sign 2 importers/private-label suppliers to a paid 60-day new-supplier or high-risk-lot protocol; sample 20 lots; run 6-10 DNA tests; produce one supplier acceptance, payment hold, credit, or supplier correction; collect 20k-40k PLN through setup + per-lot fees.

## Candidate B: SeedLot Retained-Sample Escrow

**Scored wording draft:** SeedLot Retained-Sample Escrow is a pre-dispute genetic evidence escrow for Polish/CEE greenhouse vegetable seed distributors and crop importers. Instead of waiting for a wrong-variety dispute, the distributor routes all named high-value tomato, cucumber, pepper, or specialty seed lots above a threshold into a sealed retained-sample escrow before onward sale. Customer contracts and supplier POs name the retained sample and lab route as the dispute evidence source. If a claim appears, the founder already controls the sealed lot sample, labels, invoices, certification references, and chain-of-custody record.

**Control point:** all-lot retained sample custody + buyer/supplier contract language + lab route before dispute.

**2-month proof:** sign one distributor/importer to route all high-value lots for 60 days; escrow 20-40 lots; get two greenhouse customers to accept the escrow clause; run 2-3 test cases; collect 15k-30k PLN setup and per-lot fees.

## Candidate C: POC Genetic Salvage SOP Lock

**Scored wording draft:** POC Genetic Salvage SOP Lock is a clinic-installed specimen-capture SOP for private recurrent pregnancy-loss and IVF clinics. A clinic prepays for kits, staff micro-training, courier dispatch, and lab accession setup, and agrees that every eligible D&C/MVA/medical-miscarriage POC case during a 60-day pilot is routed through the founder's kit/courier/lab accession workflow unless the patient opts out. This is sold to the clinic as reduced lost-specimen complaints and premium recurrent-loss service quality, not as DTC grief support.

**Control point:** prepaid clinic SOP placement + all-eligible-case routing + physical kits/courier/lab accession.

**2-month proof:** 2 prepaid clinics, 20 kits placed, all eligible cases routed, 8-12 specimens processed, 80%+ reportable rate, 20k+ PLN revenue.

## Candidate D: BioSignal Applied-Use Test-Day Bank

**Scored wording draft:** BioSignal Applied-Use Test-Day Bank is a pre-booked human-factors and setup-failure test-day rail for small EU biosignal device makers. The founder controls a paid roster of representative users/operators, including older home users, caregivers, nurses, EEG techs, psychologists, and remote-monitoring users. Device makers buy a fixed test-day block before design freeze, distributor onboarding, or tender submission to record setup/placement/pairing/charging/cleaning/handoff failures on camera under a biosignal-specific critical-task protocol.

**Control point:** pre-booked representative-user/operator roster with signed video/recontact rights + paid test-day calendar + biosignal-specific setup-failure corpus.

**2-month proof:** recruit 30 participants/operators, reserve 4 test-day slots, sell one 25k-60k PLN paid formative-use batch, run 8-12 sessions, deliver task-error matrix and raw video rights.

## Current Best Bet

SpeciesDNA has the strongest pre-event economic gate because the founder can be named in supplier purchase terms before payment is released. SeedLot is next strongest because retained samples become valuable only if custody is established before the crop failure.
