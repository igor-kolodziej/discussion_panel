# Simulated Score Round 6: Family Complaint Shield

## Candidate Tested

**Family Complaint Shield** for registered private care homes in Poland. Target only registered private 20-80 bed elderly-care homes with a live or recent urgency trigger: family complaint, bad Google review, incident documentation problem, inspection stress, staff turnover, or occupancy/refund risk. The product is a 30-day paid crisis workflow, not generic care-home CRM/compliance SaaS. It installs a mandatory high-risk event rail for named residents and event classes: falls, medication handoff, pressure-sore checks, hydration/meal refusal, family contact, consent/incident notes, and owner response packets.

## Result

Simulated Zero To One score in this run: **76/100**.

## Reviewer Diagnosis

The prior working notes had this shape at 91/100, but the stricter current reviewer capped it materially lower. I treat the current score as binding for this run.

## Fatal Objections

- **Episodic demand and weak retention:** crisis urgency may not turn into ongoing monthly use.
- **Evidence-adverse buyer behavior:** some homes may resist creating written records that families or regulators can later use against them.
- **Staff compliance:** overloaded caregivers may bypass extra documentation.
- **Liability and GDPR:** resident data, family disputes, medication/fall notes, and quasi-legal positioning are sensitive.
- **Copy risk:** care-law consultants, nurse auditors, and care-home software vendors can copy the crisis-sprint/event-packet positioning.

## Control Point Tier

Tier 2 workflow-routing control point, not Tier 1.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate, so it cannot proceed to real Zero To One validation.
