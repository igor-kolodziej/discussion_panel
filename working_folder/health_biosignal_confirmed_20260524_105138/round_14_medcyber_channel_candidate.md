# Round 14 Candidate: MedCyber Channel-Locked Evidence Rail

## Candidate

**MedCyber Channel-Locked Evidence Rail** - a white-label routed cybersecurity evidence desk for EU/CEE medtech distributors, tender agencies, and medical-device sales agents handling connected biosignal/neurodiagnostic devices.

## Scored Wording Draft

MedCyber Channel-Locked Evidence Rail does not sell generic cybersecurity consulting directly to every connected-device vendor. It signs one medtech distributor, tender agency, or medical-device sales agent that already represents 5-20 connected EEG, Holter, sleep, remote-monitoring, or diagnostic-software vendors, and becomes its exclusive 6-month white-label evidence desk for hospital IT/security questionnaires, MDR cybersecurity packets, SBOM/vulnerability-response packs, data-flow diagrams, minimum IT requirement packs, and NIS2 supply-chain answers. The channel partner routes all live cybersecurity/procurement blockers for covered device categories through MedCyber; vendors pay fixed fees per blocked deal or readiness pack, and the channel earns a referral/revenue share. The control point is signed routed channel flow from a distributor/tender agency that already touches multiple vendors and hospital procurement requests, plus accepted-answer memory across device categories.

## Hidden Insight

Small connected biosignal-device vendors rarely have enough deal volume to justify a dedicated medtech cybersecurity sales desk, and a solo founder cannot efficiently find every blocked deal. But medtech distributors and tender agencies already see the same pain repeatedly: hospital IT/security asks awkward questions, vendors answer late or badly, and the channel's sale is delayed. The distributor/tender agency has the trust and deal flow; the founder supplies the specialized evidence desk. This changes the control point from one-off consulting to routed channel access.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed 6-month exclusive white-label routing agreement with one distributor/tender agency/sales agent for connected biosignal/neurodiagnostic device categories.
- Channel commits to route every covered hospital IT/security questionnaire, tender clarification, distributor onboarding security blocker, or renewal security blocker through MedCyber first.
- Pre-agreed pricing and revenue share.
- One qualified MDR/cyber reviewer under retainer.
- Shared evidence taxonomy and accepted-answer memory by device category.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Identify 40 EU/CEE medtech distributors/tender agencies selling connected diagnostic devices.
- Interview 10 about hospital IT/security blockers.
- Recruit one reviewer for 10-20 hours.
- Sign one exclusive 6-month routed-channel agreement covering at least 5 represented connected-device vendors or 10 live/expected tenders.
- Convert at least two routed opportunities into paid readiness/evidence packs of 3,000-8,000 EUR each, or one blocked deal pack of at least 8,000 EUR.
- Produce buyer-side signal: questionnaire accepted, tender clarification answered, hospital IT objections narrowed, distributor onboarding continues.

## Why This May Cross The Previous Cap

The prior MedCyber idea was capped because it depended on finding blocked deals one vendor at a time. This version starts by controlling a channel that already receives deal blockers. A distributor or tender agency can route multiple vendors and recurring tenders. The founder can secure this because the channel partner gets revenue share and faster deal continuation without hiring a specialist.

## Unit Economics

- Readiness/evidence pack: 3,000-8,000 EUR.
- Blocked-deal rescue: 8,000-25,000 EUR.
- Reviewer cost: 1,000-5,000 EUR per matter.
- Channel rev share: 15-30%.
- Target gross margin after reviewer and channel: 45-70%.

## Copy Risk

Specialist consultancies can copy the service, but they must win the channel. The defensible early asset is the exclusive routed channel agreement plus accepted-answer memory across specific connected biosignal device tenders.
