# Round 29: Live-Window Finalists

## Candidate A: EmbryoDay Recipient-Cow Slot Bank

EmbryoDay controls named, ear-tagged synchronized recipient heifer/cow slots for cattle embryo transfer. The buyer is a cattle embryo-transfer vet, genomic embryo producer, or high-index dairy breeder with embryos ready to transfer. The non-postponable trigger is an IVF/frozen embryo transfer day where recipient animals and vet time must align; if recipient slots are missing, the embryo cycle/transfer window is wasted or downgraded.

Control point: option/control of named synchronized recipient animals across 2-3 farms, plus reserved embryo-transfer vet day blocks and liquid-nitrogen/courier handoff.

2-month proof: option 30 named recipient animals; reserve 2 ET vet day blocks; get one embryo producer/breeder prepayment; execute 10-15 paid recipient+transfer slots; collect 30k-60k PLN gross cash.

## Candidate B: CloneTray Rescue Options

CloneTray controls optioned lots of named berry planting material for growers/nurseries facing missed planting or customer-delivery windows. The buyer is a Polish/CEE berry nursery or commercial grower short of named strawberry, blueberry, raspberry, or haskap plant material after supplier delay, disease rejection, weather loss, or late customer order.

Control point: optioned lots of named in-vitro/frigo/container plant material from licensed nurseries/labs, with plant passport/phytosanitary documents and quantity locked before buyer panic.

2-month proof: option 5,000-20,000 plants across 3 named varieties; get 2 urgent buyer deposits; close 3 lot transfers; collect 20k-60k PLN gross cash.

## Candidate C: TrialLock EEG/PSG Central-Read Rescue

TrialLock controls pre-reserved GCP-trained EEG readers, PSG scorers, and QA reviewer capacity for CROs/sponsors whose biosignal endpoint data are blocking database lock, interim analysis, payment milestone, or submission package.

Control point: reserved specialist calendar, audit-ready scoring SOP, locked turnaround windows, and paid rescue batch for data already collected.

2-month proof: reserve 3-5 readers/scorers for 80 hours; sign one paid rescue batch from a CRO/sponsor; score/QC 50-150 records; return lock-ready files/query log; collect 40k-100k PLN.

## Candidate D: IONM Surgery-Coverage Option Bank

IONM Surgery-Coverage Option Bank controls standby intraoperative neuromonitoring specialist/device/consumable capacity for private surgical centers or small IONM providers when an operation is scheduled within 24-96 hours and coverage failure would delay a high-value OR slot.

Control point: pre-reserved named IONM operator windows, sterile consumable kits, and backup device/rental route.

2-month proof: reserve 6-10 standby surgery windows with 2 qualified operators; buy/consign common IONM consumables under 50k PLN; sign 2 paid backup-option agreements; rescue 2-3 cases; collect 25k-70k PLN.

## Selection Hypothesis

EmbryoDay may have the strongest hard-control path because options on named synchronized animals are a physical biological resource that neither labs nor consultants can copy instantly. TrialLock may have the strongest buyer urgency and cash conversion but risks central-lab copy and CRO trust barriers.
