# Round 11 Owned Validation-Asset Finalists

The strongest new pattern from the adversarial generation agents is **owned, rights-cleared validation assets plus hard venue/channel access**. These avoid most previous failures:

- no clinic workflow trust;
- no retrospective reports;
- no simple checklist internalization;
- no dependence on hospital partnerships;
- no consumer-health advice;
- no generic dashboard.

## Finalist A: GreyShift Fatigue Safety Validation Rail

**Domain:** biosignals / EEG / 50-60 pre-elderly workers.

**Scored wording:** GreyShift Fatigue Safety Validation Rail creates a rights-cleared, commercially licensable fatigue-safety validation corpus from 50-60-year-old Polish drivers, forklift trainees, ADR trainees, machine operators, and security workers. It signs 2-3 small driver/forklift/operator training centers to an exclusive 24-month protocol arrangement: every eligible trainee can be offered a paid 12-20 minute fatigue/PVT/biosignal station during existing safety training. The station captures EEG or frontal dry-EEG where practical, EOG/blink metrics, PPG/HRV, reaction-time/PVT, simulator or task behavior, sleep debt, caffeine, chronotype, medication class, and shift-history metadata. The first buyers are fatigue-monitoring vendors, driver-monitoring/ADAS vendors, occupational safety vendors, fleet-safety insurers, and EU research groups that need older-worker safety validation data. The control point is exclusive venue access plus exclusive commercial data/recontact rights, not a fatigue model or dashboard.

**2-month proof:** sign 2 training-center exclusivity agreements, collect 80-120 usable sessions, secure one paid data option or pilot license of at least EUR 8k-15k, and prove participant/session cost below 80 PLN for short-station data or below 500 PLN for deeper sessions.

## Finalist B: EEG Artifact Benchmark Foundry

**Domain:** EEG / biosignals.

**Scored wording:** EEG Artifact Benchmark Foundry produces a rights-cleared, labeled "bad EEG" benchmark for EEG headset, BCI, neurofeedback, sleep, and cognitive-monitoring companies. It recruits 50-60-year-old Warsaw participants and deliberately records artifact-rich sessions across 2-3 accessible EEG devices: blinking, jaw clench, speech, glasses, hair/wig/hat conditions, sweat, walking, phone use, electrode drift, poor contact, cheap electrodes, and home-like electrical noise. It contracts 3-5 EEG technicians/neurophysiology students under exclusive commercial annotation terms, creates synchronized EEG/EOG/IMU/video labels and inter-rater agreement, and sells benchmark licenses or custom artifact challenge packs. The control point is exclusive commercial rights to the multi-device artifact dataset plus specialized annotation capacity.

**2-month proof:** 50 participants, 50-100 labeled hours, 20 artifact classes, commercial consent/recontact rights, and two paid pilot licenses or one paid custom benchmark from EEG/BCI/neurofeedback vendors.

## Finalist C: EarFit Neurobank

**Domain:** ear-EEG / hearables / biosignals / 50-60 adults.

**Scored wording:** EarFit Neurobank creates a rights-cleared older-adult ear morphology and fit-failure bank for in-ear EEG, sleep hearables, hearing-tech, and health-wearable companies. It signs 2 audiology/hearing-aid shops or ear-mold technicians for reserved scanning/impression capacity, then collects bilateral 3D ear scans, canal/comfort metadata, basic impedance/fit tests, wax/hair/glasses/hearing-aid interaction notes, and recontact rights from 50-65-year-old Warsaw participants. It sells dataset licenses, synthetic/physical mold sets, and paid live fit panels to device companies that need older non-student ears before tooling or clinical trials. The control point is owned scan/mold/fit data plus recontact rights and reserved audiology technician capacity.

**2-month proof:** 100 bilateral scans, 30 fit/impedance sessions, 2 audiology capacity agreements, one paid test option/license from a hearable/in-ear EEG/sleep/hearing-tech buyer.

## Finalist D: Hotel PSG Ground-Truth Nights

**Domain:** sleep biosignals / 50-60 adults.

**Scored wording:** Hotel PSG Ground-Truth Nights sells lower-friction paired PSG + wearable validation nights for sleep, recovery, menopause, wearable, and insurance/occupational-health companies. It prepays/rents weekend capacity from independent PSG technicians, portable PSG equipment, and hotel/aparthotel rooms; recruits 50-60-year-old participants; and captures rights-cleared paired gold-standard PSG plus consumer wearable data in a home-like but controlled environment. Buyers pay for validated subject-nights, custom protocol runs, or dataset licenses. The control point is prepaid PSG technician/equipment/night capacity plus exclusive rights to paired PSG/wearable recordings.

**2-month proof:** reserve 20-40 PSG nights, complete 20 validated nights, secure one paid validation batch of at least EUR 10k or two paid pilots from sleep/wearable companies.

## Finalist E: ThermoFlash Challenge Panel

**Domain:** biosignals / 50-60 women soon entering older age.

**Scored wording:** ThermoFlash Challenge Panel creates a rights-cleared objective hot-flash and sleep-disruption biosignal panel for menopause brands, sleep/wearable companies, cooling-device companies, supplement brands, pharma-adjacent researchers, and insurers. It recruits Polish women aged 50-60 with frequent vasomotor symptoms, collects 14-30 day longitudinal EDA, skin temperature, actigraphy, HRV/PPG, sleep logs, medication/supplement context, and event-button labels, and sells paid challenge runs or dataset licenses. The control point is a pre-qualified symptom-frequency panel with exclusive longitudinal biosignal rights and recontact rights.

**2-month proof:** 80 recruited women, 40 high-frequency qualified, 25 complete 14-day usable datasets, one paid challenge/data option of at least EUR 8k.

## Finalist F: Cooperative Sensor Access Rights

**Domain:** 50-60 soon elderly / aging-in-place / non-camera biosignal-adjacent home risk sensors.

**Scored wording:** Cooperative Sensor Access Rights sells aging-in-place risk kits by controlling building access rather than consumer attention. It signs Warsaw housing cooperatives for exclusive 6-12 month installation/access rights during scheduled maintenance or inspection windows for non-camera stove, water-leak, motion, door-open, temperature, and fall-risk sensor kits targeted at 50-60 adult children and older residents. The cooperative gets a revenue share or free common-area risk audit; residents or adult children prepay for installed kits and optional monthly alert routing. The control point is exclusive cooperative access and scheduled inspection-window distribution, not a generic senior app or device marketplace.

**2-month proof:** one cooperative exclusivity covering 300-500 flats, 30-50 paid resident/adult-child deposits, 20 installs, positive gross margin on kit + install, and monthly alert/maintenance conversion.
