# Round 12 Candidate: SafetySignal Article 5 Evidence Rail

## Candidate

**SafetySignal Article 5 Evidence Rail** - an EU AI Act procurement-rescue and evidence-routing service for biosignal, fatigue, driver-monitoring, worker-safety, neuroergonomics, sleepiness, stress, and attention-monitoring vendors whose workplace or education sales are blocked by Article 5 emotion-recognition concerns.

## Scored Wording Draft

SafetySignal Article 5 Evidence Rail is a vendor-paid evidence-response service for EU/CEE biosignal and fatigue-monitoring companies selling into workplaces, fleets, industrial safety, training centers, education, or occupational-health buyers. The wedge is not generic AI Act compliance. It handles active procurement, renewal, insurer, enterprise legal, or distributor objections where a buyer asks whether the product is prohibited workplace emotion recognition, biometric categorisation, or intrusive worker monitoring under the EU AI Act and GDPR. The founder takes a signed mandate for one active deal, routes the vendor's claims, sensor modalities, model outputs, UI wording, data flows, intended-use statement, human oversight, validation, and safety rationale through a fixed Article 5 Safety-Exception Evidence Pack reviewed by an AI/privacy lawyer. Pricing is 8,000-25,000 EUR per blocked-deal rescue or 3,000-8,000 EUR for a pre-procurement deal-readiness pack. The primary control point is a paid, signed active-deal evidence mandate plus access to real buyer objections and procurement/legal evidence requests, not a generic report, dashboard, or compliance template.

## Hidden Insight

The AI Act does not merely regulate "AI companies." It creates a binary sales cliff for workplace biosignal products: if the buyer's legal team reads the product as workplace emotion recognition, the sale may be dead; if the vendor can prove a narrow safety/medical intended use, avoid emotion language, show proportionality, validation, data minimisation, human oversight, and non-disciplinary boundaries, the deal can survive. Most fatigue/stress/attention vendors are technically competent but commercially naive about this boundary. Their highest-value need is not a compliance lecture; it is deal-specific evidence that lets a buyer's legal/procurement team say yes.

## Why Now

- EU AI Act Article 5 prohibits AI systems that infer emotions in workplaces and education, except for medical or safety reasons.
- Public guidance and commentary treat the safety/medical carve-out as narrow.
- Workplace AI and algorithmic management are under active scrutiny.
- Biosignal vendors increasingly sell fatigue, attention, stress, sleepiness, vigilance, and safety claims into workplaces, fleets, education, and training.
- The strongest buyers are vendors with live deals, not generic startups browsing AI Act checklists.

## Beachhead

EU/CEE vendors selling:

- driver fatigue / drowsiness monitoring;
- fleet or forklift/operator vigilance monitoring;
- wearable biosignal worker-safety systems;
- neuroergonomics / attention monitoring;
- stress or burnout products currently positioned for workplaces;
- sleepiness or fitness-for-duty systems.

Only accept first customers with an active blocked or high-risk deal: procurement question, legal objection, insurer requirement, distributor concern, public-sector tender, or enterprise renewal.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed paid mandate for one active procurement/renewal/deal objection.
- Access to actual buyer objections, procurement requests, risk questionnaires, data flows, UI screenshots, model outputs, intended-use statements, validation claims, and customer-facing wording.
- A fixed evidence format that maps the product away from prohibited emotion recognition toward strict safety/medical use, or tells the vendor the deal should not proceed.
- Lawyer-reviewed boundary memo; founder handles evidence gathering, biosignal claim analysis, product wording, validation gap map, and buyer-response packet.
- Outcome memory: what objections, wording, evidence, and safeguards unblock deals in specific EU buyer segments.

- Build a list of 80 EU/CEE vendors and distributors with workplace fatigue/stress/attention/biosignal products.
- Recruit one AI Act/privacy lawyer for 10-20 prepaid review hours.
- Create a one-page "Article 5 sales-kill audit" using public product pages to identify obvious risky claims.

- Secure one paid blocked-deal mandate of at least 8,000 EUR or two paid readiness packs of at least 3,000 EUR each.

## Why This Is Not Merely A Report

It only qualifies if tied to a live revenue event: blocked deal, renewal, tender, distributor onboarding, or enterprise legal objection. The artifact is an evidence response inside transaction flow. Static AI Act templates, generic dashboards, and speculative compliance audits are explicitly rejected.

The defense must be biosignal-specific claim knowledge, fast founder-led deal rescue, evidence memories from real procurement objections, and narrow dominance in workplace fatigue/biosignal vendors before generic firms specialize.
