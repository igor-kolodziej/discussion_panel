# Round 22 Candidate: BioSignal ImportHold Evidence Desk

## Candidate

**BioSignal ImportHold Evidence Desk** - live import/tender evidence rescue for EU/CEE distributors of connected biosignal and borderline medical devices whose shipment, hospital tender, distributor onboarding, or marketplace listing is blocked by missing MDR/IVDR/RED/EMC/RoHS/labeling/cybersecurity evidence.

## Scored Wording Draft

BioSignal ImportHold Evidence Desk is a broker-first, live-transaction rescue service for Polish/CEE customs brokers, freight forwarders, medtech distributors, and marketplace-compliance agencies handling connected biosignal products: ECG patches, pulse oximeters, smart rings/watches with health claims, sleep monitors, CPAP telemonitoring accessories, EEG/neurofeedback headsets, HRV/stress wearables, fertility/temperature wearables, and remote-monitoring kits. It only accepts live blocked or high-risk transactions: held shipment, customs/market-surveillance document request, hospital tender clarification, distributor onboarding evidence gap, Amazon/marketplace medical-device claim block, or buyer compliance rejection. The founder takes a signed mandate, routes the importer/vendor documents through a fixed evidence checklist, obtains missing declarations/instructions/labels/UDI/DoC/cyber/data-flow/claims-boundary materials from the manufacturer, translates and organizes Polish/EU evidence, and returns a broker/importer/tender-ready packet reviewed by an MDR/RED/medical-device regulatory contractor. Pricing is 1,500-4,000 EUR for a shipment/listing rescue and 4,000-12,000 EUR for a tender/distributor onboarding rescue. The control point is routed live blocked transaction flow from brokers/distributors plus category-specific accepted-evidence memory, not a generic compliance audit or dashboard.

## Hidden Insight

The connected biosignal category is commercially awkward because many products sit between consumer electronics, wellness, software, and medical-device claims. Broad importers and brokers can handle customs paperwork, and MDR consultants can write slow advice, but nobody owns the narrow, urgent evidence moment when a shipment, listing, or hospital deal is blocked by the exact claims/documents that make a biosignal product risky. The buyer pays because inventory, tender revenue, or marketplace sales are already at stake.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed routing agreement with one customs broker, freight forwarder, medtech distributor, or marketplace-compliance agency for biosignal/connected-health products.
- Signed mandate for one live blocked transaction.
- Access to real customs/tender/marketplace/buyer evidence request and manufacturer technical documents.
- Retained MDR/RED/medical-device regulatory reviewer.
- Accepted-response memory by product type, claim wording, evidence request, and route.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Build a target list of 80 brokers/forwarders/distributors/agencies touching connected health/biosignal imports.
- Recruit one MDR/RED/connected-device regulatory reviewer for 10-20 hours.
- Build 20 public-page claim-risk audits for biosignal products already sold in Poland/CEE.
- Secure one channel routing agreement and one paid live transaction rescue of at least 1,500 EUR, or two readiness/rescue packets of at least 1,000 EUR each.
- Produce one accepted or materially corrected evidence packet and get a buyer-side signal: shipment released/continued, tender clarification submitted, marketplace listing restored/continued, distributor onboarding continues, or request narrowed.

## Why This May Beat MedCyber

MedCyber depended mostly on hospital cybersecurity objections and high-trust consultant work. ImportHold starts from broader live transaction blockers where brokers/distributors already need documents fast, the ACV can be smaller but sales cycles are shorter, and the founder can win by speed, document extraction, category focus, and regulatory contractor review.

## Unit Economics

- Shipment/listing rescue: 1,500-4,000 EUR.
- Tender/onboarding rescue: 4,000-12,000 EUR.
- Reviewer cost: 300-2,000 EUR.
- Gross margin target: 55-80%.
- Cash conversion: upfront deposit before work.

## Key Risks

- Customs brokers may not have authority to make vendors/importers pay.
- Generic MDR/RED consultants can copy.
- Manufacturer documents may be unavailable or non-compliant.
- Some products are genuinely non-compliant and should be killed rather than rescued.
- Must avoid unauthorized legal claims and unsafe evidence laundering.
