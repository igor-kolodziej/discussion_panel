## Shared Objections

- Acquired books are a better control point than newly invented workflows, but in these health submarkets they still require legal consent transfer, practitioner trust, and service-heavy operations.
- Several books have weak gross profit or small ticket sizes unless scaled through many local acquisitions.
- The founder would still need relationship-heavy seller acquisition and operational management.

Return to an EEG operational-yield candidate where the control point can be created quickly without regulated diagnosis, without buying a clinic, and without transferring medical records:

**Recovered Sleep-EEG Slot Guarantee for Polish private neurodiagnostic labs.**

- mandatory routed booking flow inside one lab;
- founder-owned prepared-family standby list;
