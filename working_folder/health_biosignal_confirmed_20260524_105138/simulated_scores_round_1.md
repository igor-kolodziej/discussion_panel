## Shared Fatal Objection

The first-round candidates tried to create workflow control from scratch. Reviewers repeatedly identified the same issue: useful service, but partner-dependent, operationally heavy, and copyable by incumbents that already own customer trust, medical authority, inventory, or workflow access.

## Pivot Principle

The next round should start from a harder control point that can be secured within two months:

- bought or optioned customer books;
- recurring service/reminder rights;
- phone/domain/referral-flow custody;
- physical device or inventory fleets already tied to repeat use;
- consented records and future transaction rights;
- earnout-based acquisitions from small retiring or under-optimized operators.

Do not merely improve copy. Change the business shape from service coordination to controlled customer custody or controlled recurring resource flow.
