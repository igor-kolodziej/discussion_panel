## Pattern Learned

Pre-event routing improves proof quality but still fails if an incumbent lab, QA firm, clinic group, or regulatory consultancy can add the same workflow with more trust. The next pivot must control a resource incumbents cannot easily buy by signing one vendor contract.

## Next Pivot Constraint

Look for **distressed or fragmented physical supply with exclusive options plus buyer deposits**, where:

- supply is too annoying/small/fragmented for large incumbents to bother with initially;
- the founder can reserve or buy it under 100,000 PLN;
- buyers have urgent, high-WTP needs;
- transaction rights can be signed before competitors see demand;
- the control point is an option/inventory book, not advice or routing.
