## Updated Search Constraint

The only remaining pattern likely to clear the gate is a resource where:

- the seller is distressed enough that trust is not the primary bottleneck;
- buyers are already searchable and urgently paying;
- the founder controls inventory/rights before demand is visible;
- the first 2-month proof can show cash, not only signed intent;
- incumbents cannot neutralize the control point by adding an obvious service line.
