# Round 25 Candidate: SpecialWork Archive Rights Rail

## Candidate

**SpecialWork Archive Rights Rail** - an archive-control and claimant-intake business for Polish 50-60 workers who need certified historical employment, wage, or "work in special conditions/special character" documentation from liquidated employers to prove ZUS initial capital, bridge pension, earlier retirement, or compensation/rekompensata eligibility.

## Scored Wording Draft

SpecialWork Archive Rights Rail is not generic ZUS advice. It signs a paid exclusive intake/digitization mandate with one registered custodian of employee/payroll records from liquidated employers, covering named employer fonds where former workers are now 50-60+ and likely approaching ZUS pension/bridge-pension/rekompensata decisions. The founder pays to index the custodian's under-searchable personnel/payroll/special-work records, builds an employer-position-year map, and sells certified record search and ZUS-ready evidence packets to former workers who need proof of pre-1999 wage history or work in special conditions. The custodian remains the legal record holder and issues certified copies/extracts; the founder controls discovery, intake, indexing, document-request workflow, and claimant authorization for the signed archive scope.

## Hidden Insight

For many Polish 50-60 workers, retirement outcomes are not only about contributions already visible in ZUS. They depend on old paper evidence from employers that no longer exist. The bottleneck is not understanding the ZUS form; it is finding and extracting certified payroll/personnel/special-conditions records from a specific archive before the person files or appeals. A registered archive holding a defunct employer's records is a small monopoly over that employer's workers, but most archives are passive storage businesses, not demand-generation or claimant-intake businesses.

## Primary Control Point

- Exclusive/priority 60-90 day paid mandate with a registered employee/payroll-record custodian for named liquidated employers.
- Search/indexing rights for those records.
- Certified-copy workflow where the custodian remains the legal issuer.
- Claimant authorization and ZUS-ready packet workflow.
- Employer-position-year map that compounds across claimants from the same employer.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Use public ZUS/archive databases and custodian registers to identify 20 archive custodians with payroll/personnel records from large liquidated employers.
- Sign one paid mandate with one registered custodian covering at least 2-3 named employer fonds or one large employer fund.
- Index 500-2,000 worker-name/position/year records or at least a searchable finding aid without copying unnecessary sensitive data.
- Reach former-worker groups, local Facebook groups, unions, occupational associations, and local press around those employers.
- Sell 20 paid searches at 250-700 PLN and 5 full certified packets at 900-2,500 PLN.
- Produce at least 3 certified extracts/copies accepted into a ZUS initial-capital, pension, bridge-pension, or rekompensata workflow.

## Unit Economics

- Paid search/deposit: 250-700 PLN.
- Full certified packet: 900-2,500 PLN plus archive official fees.
- Optional appeal/document bundle: fixed price only with legal partner; no unauthorized legal representation.
- Custodian rev share: 25-45%.
- Indexing contractor: 30-60 PLN/hour or fixed batch fee.
- Target gross margin after custodian/contractor costs: 45-70%.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Secure It

The founder is not asking ZUS or a famous clinic for trust. The supply side is fragmented registered archives and storage custodians that already hold records but often monetize only by reacting to individual requests. A small archive can accept a paid indexing/intake mandate because it gets found money, fewer chaotic calls, and no need to build customer acquisition. The founder can start with one overlooked employer archive and prove paid demand before expanding.

## Key Risks

- Sensitive personal data and archive law compliance must be handled with counsel and minimum necessary indexing.
- The custodian may not have enough valuable records or may already handle demand directly.
- ZUS may reject some documentation or require additional proof.
- Legal-advice boundaries must be strict.
- CAC may be high unless employer-specific former-worker communities can be reached cheaply.
- Existing legal/ZUS advisors can copy the packet workflow, but they cannot issue certified documents from a custodian's signed archive scope.
