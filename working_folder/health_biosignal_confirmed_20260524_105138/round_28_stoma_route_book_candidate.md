# Round 28 Candidate: StomaRoute Recurring Order Book Acquisition

## Candidate

**StomaRoute Recurring Order Book Acquisition** - acquire/option retiring or under-digitized ostomy-supply customer books from small Polish NFZ-contracted medical supply shops and turn them into recurring delivery, fit-support, and accessory-margin accounts.

## Scored Wording Draft

StomaRoute Recurring Order Book Acquisition does not start as a new medical-supply ecommerce shop or hospital discharge marketplace. It buys or options one existing ostomy-supply book from a small NFZ-contracted shop, retiring medical-supply owner, or local stoma nurse/shop pair. The acquired resource is the continuity right around active recurring patients: phone/domain, seller introduction, customer re-consent, active order calendar, preferred pouch/plate/accessory profile, sizing/fitting notes where lawfully transferred with consent, NFZ/e-zlecenie workflow memory, and a small starter inventory. The first customers are 50-70 colorectal/urostomy/ileostomy patients and their family caregivers who already reorder reimbursed supplies and fear leakage, wrong fit, paperwork delays, or running out. Revenue comes from recurring supply margin/rev share, paid home delivery/support, accessory bundles, and optional nurse-fit days. The control point is the acquired recurring order book and customer authorizations, not generic advice or a shopfront.

## Hidden Insight

The first supplier after stoma surgery often owns the reorder habit. Patients do not want to comparison shop every month; they want the right bags and plates to arrive before leakage or skin trouble. Small shops and nurse-led local books have trusted recurring demand but weak digital operations, while large online suppliers compete broadly. A founder can buy continuity for one neglected book under an earnout before building software or inventory at scale.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed option/earnout acquisition of one active ostomy recurring-order book with 80-250 patients.
- Seller-led customer continuity introduction and re-consent workflow.
- Existing NFZ/e-zlecenie fulfillment route through the acquired/partner shop.
- Patient product profile and monthly reorder calendar after consent.
- Supplier terms for key ostomy brands and starter emergency inventory.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Identify 30 small medical-supply shops, stoma nurses, and retiring operators.
- Sign one option/earnout book acquisition with <30,000 PLN upfront.
- Contact 80 active customers with seller introduction.
- Secure 35 re-consents and 25 paid/reimbursed repeat orders.
- Achieve 10 second-cycle reorder commitments.
- Sell 5 paid delivery/support/accessory bundles.
- Prove contribution margin after shop rev share, delivery, nurse, and support costs.

## Unit Economics

- Gross profit/rev share per monthly patient: 40-180 PLN depending supply mix and reimbursement/margin structure.
- Paid delivery/support bundle: 30-100 PLN/month where legally and ethically appropriate.
- Nurse-fit day: 150-400 PLN per visit/session or bundled with supplier/manufacturer support.
- Book option: 10,000-30,000 PLN upfront plus earnout tied to retained customers.
- Target payback: <6 months on first book if 50+ customers are retained.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Secure It

The seller is a small local operator with an aging owner, weak ecommerce, and recurring customers too small for strategic M&A. The founder offers earnout continuity, keeps the trusted introduction, and does not ask hospitals for direct discharge access. The buyer/patient already has recurring need and reimbursement paperwork; the founder is acquiring flow rather than cold-starting trust.

## Key Risks

- NFZ/e-zlecenie, medical-device retail, and patient-data rules may require operating through a licensed/contracted shop.
- Large suppliers and manufacturers can compete on price and inventory.
- Customer re-consent may be weak if the seller relationship is not trusted.
- Margins may be lower than assumed after delivery/support costs.
- This may remain a local roll-up rather than a venture-scale company unless repeat book acquisitions are available.
