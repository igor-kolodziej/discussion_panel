# Round 8 Transaction-Recovery Finalists

The prior rounds failed because the first control point was usually soft workflow access or fragile trust. This round starts from lost transaction flow: events where money, inventory, or revenue already exists but leaks because no one owns the rescue step.

## Finalist A: GenSample Rescue Rail

**Domain:** genetics.

**Scored wording:** GenSample Rescue Rail is a B2B failed-sample recovery service for Polish/EU genetic-testing labs, IVF genetic add-on providers, pharmacogenomic testing sellers, and specialist clinics. It requires one lab to route every failed, insufficient, contaminated, unlabeled, consent-incomplete, or non-returned genetic sample/order for one product line through the founder's rescue workflow for 60 days. The founder supplies replacement kits, patient instructions, call/SMS follow-up, courier/phlebotomy slots, consent/document repair, and lab-ready chain-of-custody. Pricing is mostly success-based: 150-500 PLN per rescued usable sample or 8-15% of recovered order revenue. The control point is the routed failed-order queue plus kit/courier/phlebotomy capacity, not a dashboard or report.

**Hidden insight:** Genetic labs obsess over acquisition and analysis, but the most profitable marginal order is often the paid or near-paid order already sitting in a failed-sample queue. Patients who failed collection once are not low-intent; they are high-intent revenue at risk. Labs can send replacement kits, but they often do not run a disciplined rescue operation with behavioral instructions, reminders, consent repair, and alternative collection methods.

**2-month proof:** one signed lab pilot; 30 failed or stalled orders routed; 10+ recovered usable samples; success fee paid; documented per-order economics; DPA/processor terms; no clinical interpretation.

## Finalist B: BioSignal RMA Credit Recovery Rail

**Domain:** biosignals / medical-device distribution.

**Scored wording:** BioSignal RMA Credit Recovery Rail recovers missed manufacturer credits, replacements, and warranty reimbursements for Polish/CEE distributors and service companies selling EEG, EMG, Holter, sleep-diagnostic, ECG, pulse-oximetry, neurofeedback, and wearable medical devices. The customer routes all defective returns, rejected RMAs, dead-on-arrival devices, recurring accessory failures, serial/UDI evidence, service notes, photos/videos, and manufacturer correspondence for one brand/device line through the founder for 60 days. The founder takes physical custody where needed, reconstructs manufacturer-acceptable evidence packets, files/appeals RMAs, and is paid a percentage of recovered credit plus a small monthly handling fee. The control point is delegated claim authority plus physical defective-unit/evidence custody.

**Hidden insight:** Medical-device distributors are judged on sales and service uptime, not on systematically extracting every credit from foreign manufacturers. Defective biosignal sensors, leads, probes, Holter accessories, EEG caps, cables, batteries, and firmware/service failures become margin leakage when evidence is scattered. This is closer to revenue recovery than consulting.

**2-month proof:** one distributor signs delegated RMA routing for one brand; 20 historical defective/rejected cases imported; 10 live/appealed cases routed; at least 10,000 PLN gross recovered or written manufacturer acceptance of reopened credits; contingency invoice paid.

## Finalist C: NeuroPart Downtime Bank

**Domain:** EEG/EMG biosignal equipment.

**Scored wording:** NeuroPart Downtime Bank is a Warsaw-first inventory and compatibility service for private EEG/EMG/neurophysiology clinics running legacy or multi-brand equipment. It buys and holds high-failure, hard-to-source, or orphaned consumables and parts: electrodes, leads, caps, EMG needles, chargers, batteries, adapters, cables, gels/pastes, electrode bridges, and common accessory kits. Clinics pay for same-week rescue delivery, pre-mapped compatibility, and optional small retainer that reserves a clinic-specific emergency shelf. The control point is owned inventory plus a clinic-device compatibility map.

**Hidden insight:** A private neurophysiology clinic can lose more money from one broken lead/cable/cap than the part is worth. Distributors prioritize new systems and standard orders; small clinics need a boring local rescue layer for old and mixed fleets. The valuable asset is not generic ecommerce but knowing which part restores which device in which clinic.

**2-month proof:** acquire 30-50 SKUs under 60,000 PLN; map 20 local clinics' device/accessory needs; close three paid emergency-shelf retainers or five urgent paid orders; prove gross margin and reorder potential.

## Finalist D: Failed Home Biosignal Test Rescue Rail

**Domain:** biosignals, sleep/ECG/remote testing.

**Scored wording:** Failed Home Biosignal Test Rescue Rail is a revenue-recovery service for private providers of home sleep-apnea tests, long-Holter/patch ECG, ambulatory BP, and similar home biosignal tests. The clinic/provider routes every failed, incomplete, unreturned, artifact-heavy, electrode-detached, or non-interpretable home test through the founder for 60 days. The founder owns spare sensors/electrodes/adhesives, courier pickup/dropoff, patient instruction scripts, technician triage, and fast retest scheduling. Pricing is success-based per recovered interpretable test plus a small device-loss handling fee.

**Hidden insight:** The paid test is not lost when the sensor detaches or the device is not returned; it is lost because no one owns same-day rescue. Providers focus on ordering and interpretation, not behavioral retrieval and retest logistics.

**2-month proof:** one provider routes 30 failed/stalled home tests; 15 recovered interpretable tests or devices; success fee paid; device-loss reduction measured.

## Finalist E: EEG Raw-File Liberation For Trial Prescreening

**Domain:** EEG / clinical trial operations.

**Scored wording:** EEG Raw-File Liberation is a patient-authorized data retrieval and standardization service for epilepsy trial recruiters and specialist clinics. It uses GDPR access/data-portability requests plus patient authorization to obtain raw EEG/ambulatory EEG/video EEG files, reports, medication history, and event diaries from scattered providers, then standardizes them into trial-prescreening packets for defined epilepsy studies. The control point is patient-granted data retrieval authority plus standardized raw-file custody.

**Hidden insight:** Many epilepsy patients are invisible to trials not because they lack disease but because their evidence lives in old PDFs, closed EEG systems, and hospital archives. The first company that can repeatedly liberate raw EEG evidence for a narrow study indication can become a trial-recruitment data rail.

**2-month proof:** one trial site/CRO signs a paid prescreening pilot; 20 patient authorizations; 10 raw EEG files retrieved; 3 qualified prescreen packets accepted.

## Finalist F: Orthotic-Fall Equipment Recovery Rail

**Domain:** 50-60 soon-elderly adult children / mobility equipment.

**Scored wording:** Orthotic-Fall Equipment Recovery Rail buys, sanitizes, verifies, and rents fast-fall-risk equipment packages for 50-60 adult children after a parent's fall or hospital discharge: walkers, toilet frames, pressure mattresses, transfer boards, bed rails, shower chairs, and temporary ramps. It does not sell advice or marketplace listings. The control point is owned verified inventory plus discharge-week delivery capacity.

**Hidden insight:** Families after a fall do not know which item to buy and do not want to own a house full of unused equipment six weeks later. The first urgent purchase is really a temporary capacity problem.

**2-month proof:** acquire inventory under 50,000 PLN; sign two hospital-adjacent private physiotherapist/referral agreements; rent 20 packages with deposits; prove utilization and cleaning/logistics margins.
