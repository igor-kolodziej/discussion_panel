# Zero-to-One Candidate: ProofRail Advance

Candidate generation only — not validated or scored. Research snapshot: 2026-07-23 CEST. Assignment enforceability, payment timing, loss rates, installer willingness to pay, funding cost, acquisition cost, margins, and valuation are hypotheses until counsel review and paid recovery evidence.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Give small Polish clean-energy installers cash immediately after verified `Czyste Powietrze` work by using a real-time human–AI evidence protocol to underwrite and purchase the specific contractor receivable that banks will not finance from the installer's balance sheet alone.

## Founder Fit

The founder combines finance, data science, machine learning, experimentation, and direct access to a seven-person family renewable-installation company. That testbed can reveal where technicians miss serial numbers, before/after evidence, invoices, commissioning records, disposal proof, and program-specific limits. It cannot count as independent paid proof.

The founder can build the field-capture workflow, payment-risk ledger, and underwriting rules while employed. A Polish factoring/receivables lawyer and accountant must define the transaction; the founder must not improvise consumer credit, grant law, AML, or debt collection. Initial capital is used for one small receivable, not a loan book.

The founder lacks credit operations and collections experience. These are bounded initially through one counsel-reviewed, beneficiary-acknowledged transaction, no consumer marketing, conservative advance rates, a hard exposure cap, and no second advance until the first collection route is understood.

## Customer And Pain

The first payer is an independent Polish heat-pump, insulation, window, or ventilation installer that:

- performs three to twenty eligible jobs monthly;
- uses simplified accounting or lacks the balance sheet and full bookkeeping required by a bank product;
- has completed physical work and issued a 25,000–60,000 PLN invoice;
- must pay labour and suppliers before the beneficiary/program payment clears;
- can document a valid beneficiary contract and cooperate on an acknowledged payment route.

The installer does not mainly want help filling in a form. It wants working capital without pledging the whole company and without discovering after installation that one missing photograph, serial number, signature, price limit, or disposal record has put the invoice into a correction queue.

The current workaround is to fund jobs from retained cash, delay suppliers or payroll, use expensive general-purpose credit, refuse subsidised customers, or wait. BOŚ advertises a dedicated installer credit, but its published criteria include installation experience, full accounting, and bank creditworthiness. That leaves a plausible gap among smaller, otherwise competent installers.

The pain is priced twice:

1. trapped cash prevents the next profitable job;
2. weak contemporaneous evidence increases delay, deduction, dispute, or non-payment risk.

## Product Wedge

The founding product is one **Evidence-to-Cash Advance**:

1. Before installation, ProofRail reviews the beneficiary contract, eligible scope, invoice route, program version, operator/WFOŚiGW requirements, and proposed collection mechanism.
2. The installer pays a non-refundable 1,250 PLN underwriting and evidence-setup fee.
3. The beneficiary signs a counsel-approved acknowledgement of the contractor invoice and, where legally available, an assignment, payment instruction, or tripartite collection agreement naming the ProofRail account. ProofRail never calls an unassignable grant “collateral.”
4. During physical work, a phone-based voice/vision copilot tells the technician which evidence checkpoint is due. It binds time, location, work item, serial number, product eligibility, invoice line, responsible person, and human confirmation. AI flags omissions; a human underwriter releases the advance.
5. After completion and a sampled manual audit, ProofRail purchases or advances against 75%–82% of the accepted contractor receivable. It retains a reserve for corrections, deductions, and collection cost.
6. ProofRail receives the routed payment, deducts the disclosed evidence/finance fee, and remits the remaining reserve.

The initial contract covers one invoice and one named program version. It does not lend to the homeowner, promise grant approval, act as a WFOŚiGW/operator, certify construction quality, or fund work before the evidence and collection route exist.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

Generic factoring asks whether the debtor and seller are creditworthy. Generic grant software asks whether the documents look complete. Neither product is designed to observe the physical job while the evidence is still creatable.

ProofRail's commercial insight is that **field evidence can become an underwriting input rather than a compliance afterthought**. A small installer excluded by balance-sheet credit may still produce a financeable transaction when:

- the beneficiary and invoice are identified;
- the payment route is acknowledged;
- eligible work is witnessed contemporaneously;
- program-specific failure modes are checked before the crew leaves;
- the financier controls collection and a correction reserve.

The AI is not the product. The product is cash against a controlled receivable. The human–AI interface lowers expected loss and verification labour enough to finance a segment a bank product may exclude.

## Underused Momentum

- The official `Czyste Powietrze` counter reports more than one million applications and tens of billions of PLN requested since launch.
- For the edition beginning 31 March 2025, the official counter reported 70,259 applications and 4.401 billion PLN requested through 3 July 2026, while paid amounts lagged contracted amounts.
- NFOŚiGW has publicly instructed funds to prioritise backlogs, distinguish “approved” from “approved for payment,” request precise corrections, and pay completed corrections within defined time limits. Those interventions confirm that evidence quality and payment timing have been economically material.
- BOŚ's dedicated contractor credit confirms an institutional financing need, while its full-accounting and creditworthiness criteria define a narrower excluded beachhead.
- Current multimodal models can guide a field worker through a program-version-specific evidence sequence at marginal cost, while a human retains release authority.

Momentum is not assumed permanent. Faster public payments reduce financing duration but may also improve loss-adjusted economics. If delays become too short to support the fee, the wedge is killed rather than converted into a generic installer app.

## Beachhead Market

Begin in Mazowieckie and one adjacent voivodeship with independent installers that:

- have two to twenty field employees;
- complete at least three eligible projects monthly;
- are not using full accounting or have been declined/underserved by ordinary bank credit;
- issue invoices of 25,000–50,000 PLN;
- work with beneficiaries and operators willing to acknowledge the invoice and payment route;
- accept a capped first advance and full evidence transparency.

Exclude disputed legacy cases, blank or reconstructed documentation, already-rejected grants, cash jobs, related-party beneficiaries, contractors accused of abuse, work completed before the evidence protocol, and any transaction whose collection depends only on the beneficiary voluntarily forwarding future money.

## Business Model And Unit Economics

Founding terms:

- 1,250 PLN non-refundable underwriting/evidence setup;
- 75%–82% advance rate on an accepted contractor receivable;
- 7%–9% disclosed evidence, servicing, and finance fee, priced by expected duration and risk;
- 10%–20% correction/loss reserve released after collection;
- no additional default interest or consumer fee in the founding product.

Illustrative mature 40,000 PLN invoice at an 8% fee:

| Item | Planning amount |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | 3,200 PLN |
| Funding cost over the holding period | 1,000–1,200 PLN |
| Expected loss/fraud reserve | 400 PLN |
| Field review, servicing, and collection | 400 PLN |
| Contribution before fixed overhead | 1,200–1,400 PLN |

The transaction is rejected if the expected contribution is below 1,000 PLN, the legal route is ambiguous, or the advance would expose more than the approved single-case cap. A low loss rate is not inferred from “good documents”; it must be observed across recovered invoices.

## First 10 And 100 Customers

### First ten

1. Use the family company only to map the physical workflow and create a synthetic evidence pack.
2. Interview thirty unrelated installers and ten accountants/bookkeepers serving installation firms.
3. Approach 150 publicly listed installers with one exact offer: pay 1,250 PLN for underwriting, evidence setup, and a decision on one named invoice; receive a capped advance only if the beneficiary acknowledgement and collection route pass.
4. Fund one independent 25,000 PLN advance inside the total 40,000 PLN pre-proof risk cap.
5. Do not fund a second live receivable until the first has either collected or exposed a documented failure mode.
6. Reach ten independent paid underwriting engagements, at least five advances, three completed collections, and one repeat installer before increasing the exposure ceiling.

### First hundred

- Continue direct acquisition using program-, technology-, and voivodeship-specific pages.
- Publish anonymised timing and rejection-pattern benchmarks without exposing beneficiaries or private application data.
- Ask paid installers for one peer introduction only after collection.
- Let accountants, wholesalers, and operators refer later, but keep direct contracting, underwriting, and billing; no partnership is required for the first hundred.
- Offer a rolling invoice route only after three clean transactions from the same installer.

## Primary Control Point

The primary control point is **Tier 1 at the individual transaction and Tier 2 across the portfolio**:

- ProofRail owns or controls the funded contractor receivable through a counsel-confirmed mechanism;
- the beneficiary acknowledges the invoice and collection route;
- contemporaneous field evidence is held in a tamper-evident case ledger;
- payment, reserve release, and correction work route through ProofRail;
- repeat installers place successive transactions through the same evidence and collection workflow;
- each recovered or impaired invoice improves a program-version/technology/region failure model.

The strongest copy risk is BOŚ, a factor, wholesaler, or large installer adding the same evidence workflow through one software/vendor contract. Defence must come from faster underwriting for the excluded micro-installer segment, lawful transaction history, repeat invoice routing, correction expertise, and lower loss—not the phrase “AI verification.”

## Cold Start And Resource Bootstrap

**Resource needed:** an enforceable receivable/payment route, one independent paid installer, one correctly captured physical job, and one completed collection.

**How it is secured**

1. Pay a Polish factoring/receivables lawyer for a written memo covering assignment, beneficiary acknowledgement, direct program payment, recourse, consumer boundaries, AML/KYC, collections, and insolvency.
2. Select only the contractual route the memo permits. If no enforceable route exists, stop.
3. Build a narrow evidence checklist/capture flow for one technology and one current program version.
4. Obtain a paid 1,250 PLN underwriting engagement from an unrelated installer.
5. Require a beneficiary acknowledgement and manually verify the work/evidence.
6. Advance no more than 25,000 PLN on the first case and control payment according to the legal memo.
7. Complete collection and a reserve settlement before expanding.

The unknown founder can secure the first resource because the startup supplies the scarce thing—cash—rather than asking a bank, WFOŚiGW, operator, or wholesaler to endorse it. The family company supplies process access, not validation. Public installer lists and local search make the buyer reachable without status.

**Proof before serious capital**

- a paid independent underwriting fee;
- counsel-confirmed transaction documents;
- beneficiary acknowledgement;
- a complete, contemporaneous evidence ledger;
- one advance and one controlled collection;
- positive contribution after funding, legal allocation, review, and collection labour;
- at least one repeat or second independent paid transaction.

**Twelve-month control proof**

- at least thirty paid underwriting engagements;
- at least fifteen completed collections across ten independent installers;
- three repeat installers;
- a measured duration/loss/correction table by program version and work type;
- a signed post-proof warehouse-funding term sheet or a deliberate decision to remain small;
- no dependence on family capital or an informal payment promise.

## Distribution Advantage

Cold start is asynchronous and listable:

- public installer websites and local directories;
- high-intent pages for installer cash flow and current-program payment evidence;
- direct mail/email to owners whose offer explicitly includes `Czyste Powietrze`;
- accountants serving simplified-accounting installation firms;
- referrals after money is recovered.

Distribution compounds because a recovered first invoice reveals the installer's next jobs, preferred technologies, evidence error rate, average payment duration, and safe advance limit. A repeat installer should cost far less to underwrite than a new one. Later referral channels are useful but not existential; ProofRail continues to own the contract, data rights, underwriting decision, and collection.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. Dominate one program version, one or two installation types, and two voivodeships.
2. Reduce evidence-review time and correction rate while retaining a human release decision.
3. Convert reliable installers from single invoices to revolving, transaction-capped routes.
4. Add additional voivodeships only after local payment-duration and correction data exist.
5. Add another public retrofit program or an unsubsidised verified-installment receivable only if the same physical evidence, beneficiary acknowledgement, and collection-control mechanics transfer.
6. Offer risk-sharing or underwriting infrastructure to a funder only after ProofRail owns a demonstrated portfolio, not as a speculative partnership.

The conservative wealth case depends only on the named installer-receivable route. It does not require a generic SME factor, consumer lender, or installer CRM.

## Poland/EU Or Local Adaptation Defense

The initial advantage is specific to Polish program versions, beneficiary/operator roles, sixteen regional funds, Polish invoice and assignment law, local price/eligibility tables, installer accounting practices, and the founder's access to a live installation workflow.

This is not a regulatory monopoly. A bank or factor can enter. ProofRail must win the subscale installer by making a safe decision in days, not by requiring full accounting and general corporate collateral. It must document whether evidence actually changes payment duration or loss before claiming a durable underwriting edge.

## Why This Could Compound

Each funded job can lawfully create:

- a technician sequence of evidence prompts, confirmations, omissions, and corrections;
- work-item, product, serial, invoice, region, program-version, and payment-duration features;
- a beneficiary acknowledgement and collection outcome;
- an accepted, reduced, delayed, disputed, or defaulted label;
- a repeat installer history and safe exposure limit.

This builds advanced AI/ML capability, real-time human–AI field interfaces, technician behavioural data, proprietary underwriting/evidence IP, installer distribution, capital relationships, and transaction flow. The data asset exists because ProofRail takes economic risk and closes transactions, not because it asks installers to upload examples.

## Reinvestment And Compounding Model

Conservative and expected scenarios reinvest 95% of after-tax business cash available for distribution while the founder remains employed; optional founder distributions are capped at 5%. Strong success reinvests 100% during scale.

Reinvestment order:

1. loss reserves, legal controls, KYC/AML, and auditability;
2. additional advances to already-proven installers;
3. field-interface and underwriting automation;
4. collections and fraud operations;
5. acquisition in already-measured regions;
6. warehouse-funding covenants and reporting;
7. new work types only after the existing cohort is profitable.

Retained cash supporting the enterprise is not added again as a founder distribution.

## Reachability And 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- **Days 1–20:** map one current-program installation with permission; interview twelve independent installers; commission the legal memo; define one permitted transaction route.
- **Days 21–45:** build the narrow capture flow and manual underwriting ledger; approach fifty installers; collect the first 1,250 PLN independent underwriting fee.
- **Days 46–90:** obtain beneficiary acknowledgement, inspect one completed job, and advance no more than 25,000 PLN; keep total founder exposure at or below 40,000 PLN.
- **Days 91–135:** collect the first receivable and settle the reserve; complete two more paid underwriting files without concurrent uncontrolled exposure.
- **Days 136–180:** reach at least five independent paid underwriting engagements, three funded/recovered invoices, one repeat installer, positive contribution on recovered cases, and a documented rejection list.

The founder remains employed and processes only one live exposure initially. A family-company transaction, free evidence audit, unsigned interest, or a bank conversation does not satisfy paid proof.

## Capital Tranches And Maximum Loss

- **Tranche 1 — maximum 12,000 PLN before the first paid fee:** legal memo/contracts 7k; prototype and secure storage 2k; acquisition/interviews 1k; contingency 2k.
- **Tranche 2 — cumulative maximum 40,000 PLN after a paid fee and signed beneficiary acknowledgement but before collection:** one advance capped at 25k plus incremental review/collection cost.
- **Tranche 3 — cumulative maximum 65,000 PLN:** only after the first complete recovery, positive contribution, and a second independent paid installer; fund sequential cases, not a concurrent book.
- **Tranche 4 — cumulative maximum 100,000 PLN:** only after multiple independent recoveries, repeat demand, measured losses/duration, and a documented acquisition channel.
- Family capital is excluded until repeat demand, positive contribution, and a risk-adjusted scale case exist.
- Maximum founder cash loss before completed paid proof: **40,000 PLN**; the product is killed before breaching it.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder may leave corporate employment only after:

- at least three independent recurring installers and multiple additional payers;
- a clean cohort of completed collections with positive contribution;
- an enforceable, repeatable contract and collection route;
- a documented acquisition channel with viable economics;
- committed funding for the operating book;
- operations that do not depend on the founder manually reviewing every photograph;
- at least twelve months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

If factoring fails, ProofRail stops advances and collects outstanding cases. Reusable assets include:

- program-version evidence maps;
- a real-time technician guidance interface;
- lawful, non-personal workflow/error taxonomies;
- installer cash-flow and payment-duration knowledge;
- legal transaction templates that remain the company's property;
- relationships with installers, accountants, and funders;
- ML infrastructure for multimodal field verification.

Those assets do not justify continuing as a generic consultancy. A new business shape would require a separately paid control point.

## Validation Plan

1. Counsel validates or rejects the exact transaction route.
2. Twelve independent installer interviews test invoice size, payment duration, bank exclusion, and willingness to pay.
3. One paid underwriting fee tests budget before capital.
4. One beneficiary-acknowledged transaction tests collection control.
5. One complete recovery tests timing, deductions, labour, and contribution.
6. Five payers and one repeat test that the event recurs beyond founder/family trust.
7. Fifteen recoveries test initial loss and evidence features.
8. A warehouse-funder term sheet tests whether capital can scale without destructive dilution.

## Kill Criteria

Kill ProofRail if any of the following occurs:

- counsel cannot establish an enforceable collection route without relying on voluntary remittance;
- the model is legally consumer lending or another regulated activity the initial company cannot perform;
- no unrelated installer pays 1,250 PLN after fifty qualified conversations;
- the first advance cannot be recovered through the controlled route;
- expected losses, funding, and servicing leave less than 1,000 PLN contribution on a 40,000 PLN invoice;
- fewer than three of the first ten paid installers repeat or refer a paid peer;
- actual payment duration is too short to support the fee or too uncertain to price;
- a bank/factor serves the same simplified-accounting segment at economics ProofRail cannot match;
- scaling requires founder guarantees, family capital, or equity dilution that breaks the conservative wealth case;
- field evidence does not materially reduce corrections, loss, or underwriting labour.
