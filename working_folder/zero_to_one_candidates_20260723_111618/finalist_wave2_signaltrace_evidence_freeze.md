# Zero-to-One Candidate: SignalTrace Evidence Freeze

Candidate generation only — not validated or scored. Research snapshot: 2026-07-23 CEST. Matter frequency, willingness to pay, source-device access, tool adequacy, expert recruitment, acquisition cost, margins, and valuation are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Become the CEE lab law firms and insurers call within 48 hours to preserve and reconstruct synthetic-media, mobile, and wearable evidence before the source state disappears, then compound case appointments into validated methods, device/app expertise, and a court-credible forensic team.

## Founder Fit

The founder's AI/ML, data-science, neuroscience, EEG/biosignal, experimental-design, and technical-communication background fits manipulated-media analysis, wearable signal interpretation boundaries, reproducibility, adversarial reference generation, and client-facing technical triage.

The founder is not presently a court expert, mobile-device examiner, lawyer, or insurer. The founding product is therefore evidence preservation and technical triage, not a formal expert opinion or testimony. A court-experienced digital-forensics examiner must join through employment or vesting before the company sells full opinions as its own controlled capability. An informal subcontractor is not treated as the control point.

The work can begin alongside employment through capped matters, strict conflict checks, secure custody, and a 48-hour response window agreed only when capacity exists.

## Customer And Pain

The first payer is:

- a Polish litigation law firm holding a source phone, smartwatch-linked account, audio recording, or disputed video;
- an insurer's special-investigation or claims team facing a material authenticity or activity dispute;
- later, a corporate investigations team with lawful access to the source evidence.

The triggering event is not curiosity about whether a file “looks AI-generated.” It is a live claim, injunction, settlement, employment dispute, fraud investigation, or civil/criminal matter in which:

- the source device may be reset, updated, sold, or separated from its paired account;
- cloud retention or app-version state may change;
- a screenshot/export may strip provenance;
- the opposing party may challenge custody or analytic reproducibility;
- delay can remove settlement leverage or admissibility.

Current workarounds split the job between a general phone-forensics firm, audio/video expert, health expert, and lawyer. Each handoff risks provenance loss. Uploading a screenshot to frontier AI cannot recreate the original device, Health database, cloud export, pairing state, metadata, hash, or acquisition log.

## Product Wedge

The founding product is a prepaid **48-Hour Evidence Freeze**:

1. Counsel or the insurer identifies the lawful custodian, matter purpose, source device/account, and preservation authority.
2. SignalTrace performs a conflict, legality, and scope check; it does not advise a private party how to obtain another person's account.
3. The custodian signs an evidence-transfer and processing record.
4. SignalTrace preserves original media, metadata, account exports, relevant device/app/version state, paired-device context, hashes, acquisition steps, and every transformation.
5. It runs a reproducible initial triage across source consistency, edit history, known synthetic-media artefacts, wearable/mobile timeline coherence, and missing evidence.
6. The client receives a custody package, preservation gaps, and a decision tree: stop, request additional lawful data, commission a full reconstruction, or appoint an independent expert.

Founding price: 6,900 PLN prepaid for a tightly scoped source and matter. Full reconstruction later ranges from 18,000 to 60,000 PLN with a 50% deposit. Formal opinion/testimony is sold only after the qualified capability is internal.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

Most deepfake products sell a probability score. Most digital-forensics firms sell broad extraction or incident work. Wearable evidence is often treated as a side artefact of the phone.

The gap is **time-critical, source-first continuity across synthetic media and human telemetry**:

- preserve before detecting;
- interpret device/app/version behaviour before accepting a timeline;
- separate sensor inference from legal fact;
- reproduce the analysis rather than trust one model score;
- maintain one custody chain across media, phone, cloud, and wearable context.

The contrarian insight is that generative AI increases the value of old-fashioned custody. As synthetic content improves, the scarce asset is not another detector; it is lawful access to the original state plus a method that survives adversarial review.

## Beachhead Market

Start with Polish boutique and mid-sized firms handling:

- personal injury and insurance disputes;
- commercial and employment litigation;
- fraud and reputation matters;

The initial source set is iPhone/Apple Health/Apple Watch-linked evidence plus original audio/video files. Android and vehicle systems enter only after validated methods exist.

Exclude covert acquisition, stalkerware, password bypass, cases without lawful authority, emergency criminal-law deadlines beyond capacity, medical-causality opinions, child exploitation material, and any engagement requiring testimony before the company has the qualified internal team.

## Primary Control Point

The primary control point is **Tier 2 case custody that compounds into specialised institutional capability**:

- the client appoints SignalTrace while the source state still exists;
- SignalTrace records custody and controls the reproducible working copy;
- follow-on acquisition and reconstruction route through the lab;
- response retainers reserve capacity and reduce switching during emergencies;
- independently generated reference corpora, validated capture methods, device/app/version matrices, and court-reviewed procedures remain reusable;
- qualified examiners, engineers, and scientists become internal talent rather than rented credibility.

The strongest copy risk is an established Polish forensic laboratory adding a wearable/deepfake page and buying the same tools. SignalTrace must win on urgent preservation, cross-source specialisation, reproducibility, and faster learning across app/device versions. “AI-powered forensics” is not a moat.

## Poland/EU Or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish language, evidence procedure, privacy, court-expert practice, insurer workflows, local apps, and CEE device usage create meaningful execution work. The lab can price below large international investigations while offering a narrower response SLA.

Localisation is not a barrier. Existing firms such as EvidenceLab, Wichran, Forensyka, fonoskopia specialists, and broad digital laboratories already own trust.
