# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Canonical SealFault v1

## Diagnosis

Canonical SealFault v1 is sufficiently complete for an independent simulated panel: it owns a physical/data asset, ties the offer to expensive acceptance and regression events, gates spend on prepayment, and defines a credible path from bespoke first kits to reusable matrices. Its central vulnerability remains whether buyers value external neutrality and whether artificial defects transfer to production failure tails. The candidate avoids a profitability veto because pricing, reuse, gross-margin, and kill thresholds are explicit and the first commercial test precedes most spending.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced evidence supports package-integrity pain, active inspection budgets, strong incumbents, mono-material process challenges, and PPWR timing. Founder fit is grounded in the live profile. Independent-benchmark willingness to pay, proposed prices, transfer, second-buyer reuse, and contract retention rights are hypotheses. The earlier advisory score and recommendation are reviewer judgment, not market evidence.

## Overall Score

**74/100.** Weighted sum `118 / 16`; `10 × 7.375 = 73.75`, rounded to 74.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 with a concrete Tier 1 conversion.** All decisive inputs are purchasable and the company can directly own the bank, but no physical bank or signed customer rights exist yet. Strongest copy risk: an inspection OEM uses existing application labs to create equivalent defects and persuades buyers that neutrality adds no value.

## Expansion Check

The first material/geometry family is a credible beachhead. Expansion across recyclable films and model versions reuses the same physical-ground-truth architecture. Broad OEMs can move inward, but a neutral multi-vendor bank can expand outward through cross-vendor failure history that one OEM cannot observe.

## Fatal Objections

1. Artificial defects may not transfer to real production failure modes.
2. Buyers may not pay an unknown external benchmark provider when they can create defects internally.
3. Confidential custom work may prevent base-bank reuse and rights retention.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Numbered retained physical twins with destructive ground truth can create durable cross-vendor regression value that digital datasets alone cannot.
