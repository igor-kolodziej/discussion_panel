# Zero-to-One Evaluation: SealFault Physical Benchmark Bank

## Diagnosis

SealFault is the strongest current candidate because the product is an owned physical/data asset, the painful failure is economically clear, and the first test can expose value without claiming certification. Its vulnerability is that inspection OEMs and packaging labs can manufacture defects internally, making neutrality and cross-material retained twins the entire reason to buy. The economics are plausible for technical validation budgets, and the prepayment rule contains downside sufficiently to enter an independent panel.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sources establish active seal-inspection markets, package-integrity consequences, mono-material process challenges, and strong incumbent equipment. The existence of a neutral external benchmark budget, transfer from artificial to production defects, reusable matrices across buyers, and the proposed prices remain hypotheses. Founder FMCG context is relevant but employer data and authority are explicitly excluded.

## Overall Score

**73/100.** Weighted sum `117.5 / 16`; `10 × 7.34375 = 73.4375`, rounded to 73.

## Control Point

**Tier 2, with a credible Tier 1 conversion.** Purchased specimens, recipes, data, and rights are directly controllable; the bank becomes Tier 1 only after it exists and at least one buyer confirms external benchmark value. Strongest copy risk: an inspection OEM builds its own defect library with existing laboratory assets.

## Expansion Check

The initial flexible-package/material family is a viable beachhead. Expansion across films and inspection modalities follows the same physical-ground-truth asset, while medical packaging would require a separate authority and method bar. A broad OEM can enter, but a neutral multi-vendor bank can observe failures that one OEM cannot.

## Fatal Objections

1. Artificial defects may not predict real-line failure modes.
2. Buyers may view internal defect creation as sufficient and reject external neutrality.
3. Custom methods could prevent reuse and collapse margins into laboratory services.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Retained physical twins with destructive ground truth can be a cross-vendor asset that digital image datasets alone cannot reproduce.
