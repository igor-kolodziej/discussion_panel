# Zero-to-One Candidate: Textile-Bale Yield Underwriting

## One-Sentence Thesis

Textile recyclers will pay for a process-specific usable-yield forecast and maximum bid before buying mixed bales, because fibre composition alone does not price contamination, blends, trims, coatings, moisture, or the buyer's realized processing loss.

## Founder Fit

The founder is suited to sampling design, spectroscopy/vision data fusion, uncertainty estimates, and bid models. Field sampling and destructive testing can be contracted. The work is evidence-driven and does not require personal-brand distribution. It is less naturally aligned than FMCG or electrical work, so buyer access and operational burden must be tested early.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a small or mid-sized mechanical textile recycler or sorter in Poland/Czechia pricing a 5–20 tonne mixed post-consumer lot. A poor lot produces less saleable fibre than expected, tying up capacity and cash. Buyers currently use supplier trust, visual checks, small samples, NIR composition, and experience, but may not link pre-purchase measurements to realized output statistically.

## Product Wedge

For one narrow feedstock/process pair, Textile-Bale Yield Underwriting samples a lot, records NIR/RGB/weight/moisture/metal/contamination features, predicts usable output distribution and maximum purchase price, retains numbered samples, and later reconciles the prediction with realized output. The product is a purchase decision and outcome graph, not an NIR inspection report.

## Hidden Insight or Market Misunderstanding

Sorting technology companies sell identification accuracy. The recycler's economic variable is process-specific recovered yield. The same bale can be attractive to one line and unprofitable to another. A dataset joining origin, spectrum, contamination, process, and realized saleable output can control transaction pricing and later standardized grades or finance.

## Underused Momentum

EU textile separate collection and EPR increase feedstock volumes while the EEA warns that collected quality may fall. NIR hardware is becoming accessible, but existing systems focus on composition/sorting rather than underwriting a purchase against realized yield.

## Beachhead Market

Polish/Czech mechanical recyclers buying a repeat class of mixed cotton/polyester post-consumer bales and recording actual input/output weights. Do not start with all textiles, chemical recycling, or municipal contracts.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypotheses:

- One-lot blind decision: 1,500–4,000 PLN.
- Annual site licence after proof: 50,000–150,000 PLN.
- Transaction fee: 5–15 PLN/tonne only after routing purchases.
- Mature software/data gross margin above 70%; POC field/test margin lower.
- A buyer must avoid at least 10,000 PLN of expected loss per tested lot to support pricing.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First 10:** Map 50 recyclers/sorters; offer 10 blind historical or live trials; require outcome data rights in the paid contract; purchase several small reference lots directly; achieve three paid purchase decisions and one repeat buyer.

**First 100:** Standardize one feedstock grade, integrate the model into buyer intake, license site decision tools, and route transactions only after prediction accuracy and repeat WTP are proven. Expand by process/feedstock adjacency, not geography alone.

## Primary Control Point

Owned retained sample bank, purchased reference lots, spectra/images/tests, process metadata, realized-yield outcomes assigned through paid contracts, prediction model, and later standardized purchase-grade definitions. The recycler is a paying customer and data licensor, not an unspecified partner. The company buys processing/test work under explicit outcome-data assignment.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Spend 8,000 PLN on interviews and sampling design; 25,000–40,000 PLN on a handheld NIR unit and measurement tools; 15,000–25,000 PLN on reference lots, destructive tests, transport, and paid small processing runs; and reserve 15,000 PLN for field work and contingency. Proof under a 95,000 PLN cap: 500 retained samples, 20 complete processing outcomes, blind usable-yield error below eight percentage points, three paid decisions, and one repeat customer.

## Distribution Advantage

The trigger is each lot purchase, so value can be measured immediately. Blind backtests reduce trust demands. Once the model is embedded in intake and linked to the buyer's history, switching loses a proprietary process-specific baseline.

## Domination and Expansion Path

Dominate one feedstock/process pair, define a trusted purchase grade, route matching lots, and eventually price working capital or guarantees using the outcome history. Expand to adjacent processes only where the existing sample and supplier graph transfers.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland's sorting/export/landfill role and CEE cost base make local sampling feasible. EU EPR creates more feedstock and pressure for higher-value recycling. The moat must be private realized-yield history; public regulation and NIR hardware alone offer no defence.

## Compounding Advantage

Every purchased/tested lot improves origin, contamination, and process estimates. Retained samples allow recalibration. Repeat buyers create proprietary process baselines. Transaction routing later adds price and supplier-performance data.

## Critical Evidence

The EEA documents rising textile-waste management pressure and possible quality decline. EU policy is introducing textile EPR. NIR/RGB identification already works commercially, proving measurement feasibility and establishing incumbent hardware competition.

## Critical Hypotheses

Pre-purchase samples are representative; available measurements predict usable yield; buyers record outcomes accurately; three buyers will assign outcome rights; decision value supports at least 1,500 PLN per lot; field logistics fit a part-time founder.

## Kill Criteria

- Blind error remains above eight percentage points after 20 complete outcomes.
- Buyers will not provide/assign realized-output data even in paid trials.
- NIR composition plus public information performs as well as the full model.
- Avoided loss cannot support 1,500 PLN per lot or 50,000 PLN annual pricing.
- Representative lot purchases, sampling rights, transport, or processing exceed 100,000 PLN.
- The business remains manual inspection with no repeat model or transaction control.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Months 0–3: buyer interviews and paid blind-trial commitments, 10,000 PLN. Months 3–6: equipment/reference lots, cumulative 50,000 PLN. Months 6–9: 20 outcomes and model, cumulative 80,000 PLN. Months 9–12: paid repeat decisions, 95,000 PLN cap. Minimum stack: founder/data lead, paid textile-process advisor, field sampler, and contract laboratory/processor.
