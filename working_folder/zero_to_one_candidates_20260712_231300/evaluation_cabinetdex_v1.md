# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CabinetDex Skilled-Manipulation Task Capsules

## Diagnosis

CabinetDex has a real non-consensus insight and unusually strong founder access to a lawful skilled-trade environment, but the business currently assumes rather than demonstrates that human demonstrations plus a standard board are useful to robotics buyers before robot-specific teleoperation. Generic physical-AI data collection is already crowded, and the few plausible buyers have technical procurement power. The no-robot/prepayment rule protects downside, but public evidence does not yet establish repeatable willingness to pay or a short sales cycle.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced evidence shows robotics teams use human video and real trajectories, electrical manipulation remains difficult, and several data vendors already sell broad capture. Founder workshop access is a stated founder resource. Prices, two-buyer schema convergence, non-exclusive licensing demand, transfer from human/tool-state data to model value, and annual update retention are hypotheses. No prior reviewer or buyer verdict is market evidence.

## Overall Score

**68/100.** Weighted sum `109 / 16`; `10 × 6.8125 = 68.125`, rounded to 68.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** The fixtures and rights are purchasable and provable within the constraints, but they are not yet owned and the buyer-error feedback loop is speculative. Strongest copy risk: scaled manipulation-data vendors add electrician-operated environments and robot-specific collection once demand is visible.

## Expansion Check

The cabinet beachhead is coherent and adjacency into connectors, inverter wiring, controls, and HVAC is natural. Broad data firms can move inward if the task becomes valuable, so CabinetDex must prove that standardized physical benchmarks and retained failure history create switching value. Poland improves capture economics but is not a sales barrier.

## Fatal Objections

1. Human-video/tool-state episodes may not transfer to a buyer's robot action space.
2. The buyer universe may be too small and procurement too bespoke for repeatable catalogue economics.
3. Generic data vendors already market industrial and electrical-work capture.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The scarce unit is a reproducible, instrumented skilled-trade task with failures and recovery ground truth—not generic video hours.
