# Candidate Funnel

All prices, conversion rates, savings, margins, and sales cycles are hypotheses until a buyer quote, paid deposit, licence, or completed transaction proves them. This generator phase does not assign scores or claim validation.

## Divergent Directions

### 1. CabinetDex Skilled-Manipulation Task Capsules — retained

- **Secret:** Physical-AI teams can obtain huge volumes of household video, but scarce skilled-trade demonstrations combine deformable wiring, tools, torque, occlusion, error recovery, and a reproducible physical evaluation board.
- **Buyer/event:** European humanoid, dexterous-arm, VLA, and industrial-automation teams before a manipulation milestone or customer pilot.
- **Cold start:** Send a free 100-episode sample and shippable cabinet challenge-board specification to 60–100 robotics data/model leads; sell two schema-confirmed task packs before scaling capture.
- **Control point:** Owned mock cabinets, fixtures, instrumentation, physical benchmark boards, task ontology, and exclusive commercial data rights from paid electricians.
- **Why retained:** Tier-1-style owned physical and data assets, global software/data economics, direct founder/family-business leverage, and an explicit prepayment-before-robot-spend rule.

### 2. SealFault Physical Benchmark Bank — retained

- **Secret:** Seal-inspection vendors have product demos and good-pack images, but a neutral physical library linking controlled defect recipes to destructive leak/strength ground truth can expose model blind spots across machines and new mono-material films.
- **Buyer/event:** Machine-vision vendors, packaging OEMs, converters, and FMCG packaging labs before product validation, customer acceptance, or a new-film launch.
- **Cold start:** Interview 15 vendors/labs, pre-sell a blinded challenge kit, then manufacture only the requested defect/material matrix.
- **Control point:** Owned numbered specimens, process recipes, multi-sensor images, destructive-test results, benchmark protocol, and commercial dataset rights.
- **Why retained:** Concrete owned asset, high-value failure event, strong founder/FMCG fit, and a path from physical kits to recurring benchmark licences.

### 3. SafeCassette Legacy-Shelter Ventilation Standard — retained

- **Secret:** Poland is funding shelter modernization, but recurring legacy geometries may allow a standardized, serialised adapter interface that converts custom refurbishment into repeat hardware plus replacement cartridges.
- **Buyer/event:** Municipalities, schools, housing cooperatives, and shelter owners after a technical survey identifies obsolete or non-operational filter ventilation.
- **Cold start:** Mine public modernization notices, offer five paid geometry surveys, and require one fixed-price prototype order before tooling.
- **Control point:** Owned compatibility library, interface/design documentation, limited tooling, installed serialised adapters, and multi-year cartridge supply terms.
- **Why retained:** Immediate Polish capital and legal momentum, family installation capacity, recurring installed-base economics, and physical proof inside the budget.

### 4. AccessCore Design-In Module for Small Terminal OEMs — retained

- **Secret:** Small CEE terminal manufacturers need an economical reusable accessibility subsystem spanning tactile input, private audio, offline speech, focus state, and test evidence across low-volume product families.
- **Buyer/event:** Small kiosk, ticketing, check-in, payment, and information-terminal OEMs launching a new model after the European Accessibility Act became applicable.
- **Cold start:** Prospect manufacturers from public catalogues and procurement awards; sell three paid design-in pilots before production tooling.
- **Control point:** Owned hardware reference design, firmware, interaction library, compatibility tests, tooling, and model-specific design-in contracts.
- **Why retained:** Recurring per-unit revenue and genuine design-in switching costs, despite serious incumbent-module risk that the evaluator must test.

### 5. Textile-Bale Yield Underwriting — retained

- **Secret:** Fibre composition is not the economic decision. Recyclers buy a probability distribution of usable output after blends, moisture, trims, coatings, contamination, and their own process losses.
- **Buyer/event:** Small and mid-sized textile recyclers pricing a 5–20 tonne mixed post-consumer lot.
- **Cold start:** Offer one-bale blind trials to 30–50 recyclers and charge only where avoided overbid or yield loss clearly exceeds the fee.
- **Control point:** Owned retained sample bank, spectra/images/tests, purchased reference lots, paid processing outcomes with data assignment, and later standardized purchase grades.
- **Why retained:** A direct transaction decision, measurable value, EPR-driven supply growth, and an outcome graph rather than a generic NIR inspection service.

### 6. Warehouse Speech Reliability Corpus — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Command completion under Polish/Ukrainian accents, warehouse noise, SKU morphology, cheap headsets, and fatigue matters more than generic ASR accuracy.
- **Control:** Rights-cleared corpus and benchmark harness.
- **Reason rejected:** Voice vendors already claim multilingual/low-training operation; proving willingness to pay for an independent corpus would require the same enterprise access as the eventual sale, while global data vendors can reproduce the capture.

### 7. PJM Transaction Corpus — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Commercially rights-cleared Polish Sign Language dialogues for banking, checkout, transport disruption, and telecom support are scarcer than dictionary footage.
- **Control:** Contributor releases, raw/derived video, transactional ontology, and sublicensing rights.
- **Reason rejected:** Sign recognition/production purchasing remains immature and large buyers can commission collection; the first licence is too speculative to justify recruiting at scale.

### 8. PL-UA Call Reality Corpus — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Real code-switching calls with institutional names, numbers, addresses, accent, and noise create a local ASR failure tail.
- **Control:** Commercial speech rights and benchmark data.
- **Reason rejected:** Large language-data vendors can mobilize broader contributor pools, and no narrow transaction event creates enough buyer urgency to overcome procurement friction.

### 9. Recycled-Resin Process-Window Atlas — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** PCR certificates do not predict each mould family's stable temperature/pressure/cycle window or scrap risk.
- **Control:** Owned resin lots, standard mould trials, and cross-lot process/outcome data.
- **Reason rejected:** Transfer from a standard rig to customer moulds is the entire value claim and is unproven; the benchtop equipment and 30–50 lots strain the 100,000 PLN cap.

### 10. PCR Passport Micro-Lots — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Small converters need same-lot trial quantities and retained reference samples before committing to tonnes.
- **Control:** Owned batch inventory, retained samples, batch passports, and reserved follow-on lots.
- **Reason rejected:** Specialist distributors can split stock and add testing; without proven predictive process data this is ordinary distribution.

### 11. FuelEU Surplus Option Book — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Verified over-compliance is a time-bounded transferable balance that smaller surplus ships may under-monetize.
- **Control:** Exclusive options over named verified ship surpluses and the matching book.
- **Reason rejected:** Existing pooling platforms, bunker traders, verifiers, and classification-adjacent firms have trust and operator access; Poland provides no sufficient local barrier for an unknown entrant.

### 12. Brand-Safe Apparel Remanufacturing Cell — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** The July 2026 destruction ban creates demand for secure debranding/remanufacturing that avoids grey-market leakage.
- **Control:** Custody contracts, SKU recipes, fixtures, and vision data.
- **Reason rejected:** Brands can use derogations, discounting, donation, existing 3PLs, or recyclers; machinery and recipes are copyable before exclusive custody is proven.

### 13. Low-Micropollutant Cosmetic Formulation IP — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Future wastewater EPR charges could make validated low-hazard rinse-off formulations more valuable than reporting tools.
- **Control:** Formulation know-how/IP, field-of-use rights, and test data.
- **Reason rejected:** Contribution formulas, Polish transposition, savings, formulation performance, and manufacturer willingness to switch are all unresolved; chemistry/lab dependence exceeds founder fit for this run.

### 14. Offline-First eFTI Edge Platform — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Micro-carriers need a driver-first offline workflow rather than a full TMS when eFTI becomes operational.
- **Control:** Certification, prepaid fleet contracts, and installed history.
- **Reason rejected:** Certification cost is unresolved and incumbent TMS/telematics vendors can add the feature through routine product work.

### 15. Small-Vessel Catch-Capture Unit — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Wet hands, connectivity, weighing, and species capture remain after free electronic logbooks arrive.
- **Control:** Leased devices, vessel calibrations, and catch-recognition data.
- **Reason rejected:** Small fleet, public subsidy/free-app risk, marine-support burden, and straightforward marine-electronics copyability cap the upside.

### 16. Gluten-Guard Oat Micro-Lots — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Small gluten-free producers need title to affordable, physically segregated, sampled lots rather than only a supplier certificate.
- **Control:** Owned sealed inventory, reference samples, test history, and prepaid harvest options.
- **Reason rejected:** Established mills and buyers can contract supply, while margins, storage, testing cadence, and contamination liability make this commodity distribution unless several harvests prove otherwise.

### 17. Fresh Predator Foundry — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Local freshness and emergence rate may beat imported beneficial insects in a neglected crop/pest niche.
- **Control:** Owned mother colonies and local performance data.
- **Reason rejected:** Koppert's broad Polish catalogue, biological QA risk, seasonality, and weak founder fit overwhelm the unproven freshness advantage.

### 18. Pellet-Boiler Compatibility Atlas — rejected before finalist

- **Secret:** Legal pellet chemistry does not describe clinker, feed, ignition, and fouling behavior across burner geometries and loads.
- **Control:** Owned test rigs, retained batches, and interaction history.
- **Reason rejected:** Safe facilities consume the budget, manufacturers can test internally, and it is unclear whether avoided installer callouts fund the testing burden.

## Round Two — Directly Acquirable Rights, Consumables, and Transaction Positions

This round began only after SealFault v1 failed the simulated panel and RealLine, its one permitted structural pivot, received an advisory kill. It excludes independent testing, future data-corpus moats, exact-match salvage, obsolete-spare pools, white-certificate variants, and control points that appear only after several customers cooperate.

### 19. LyoShield Animal-Free Lyophilization Protectant — retained

- **Secret:** Small probiotic, starter-culture, and microbial-collection producers buy viable post-drying cells, not freeze-dryer hours; an animal-free protectant can remove BSA cost and animal-origin documentation without forcing a process-equipment change.
- **Paid trigger:** A producer is qualifying a new strain, reformulating an existing freeze-dried product, or trying to reduce BSA cost and animal-origin exposure.
- **Cold start:** Option an exclusive EU field licence to PL Pat.244159 / EP3670645, then sell three paid blinded strain-comparison packs before manufacturing inventory.
- **Control point:** Exclusive field licence, controlled formulation supply, purchased ingredient lots, strain-specific application dossiers, and customer-qualified specifications.
- **Why retained:** The underlying right is explicitly available for licence or sale, laboratory proof fits the capital limit, and the buyer can judge CFU survival rather than founder status.

### 20. InsulaTip EC-STM Probe Packs — retained

- **Secret:** Electrochemical STM users often tolerate manually coated tips because the global market looks too small for a specialized supplier, yet failed insulation destroys scarce instrument time and operando experiments.
- **Paid trigger:** A battery, corrosion, catalysis, or surface-science laboratory is scheduling liquid-cell EC-STM work and needs pre-qualified low-leakage probes.
- **Cold start:** Option UWr patent application P.448634, coat and cyclic-voltammetry-QA 100 purchased tips, and sell paid ten-tip evaluation packs to ten EU labs.
- **Control point:** Field licence, owned coating fixture/process window, batch QA records, probe inventory, and instrument/application-qualified SKUs.
- **Why retained:** It is a genuine recurring consumable with objective acceptance tests and a global direct-sales wedge, although market size and easy laboratory workarounds are serious unknowns.

### 21. Wantlist Reissue — retained

- **Secret:** Collector-market scarcity data can identify back-catalogue demand before rights and manufacturing are committed; a repress should be financed by buyers, not by guessing in a record shop.
- **Paid trigger:** Fans want an out-of-print Polish cult release whose used price and wantlist exceed the price of a licensed new pressing.
- **Cold start:** Secure a 12-month, 500-copy exclusive master/artwork option for one title; exercise only after 250 paid, refundable preorders.
- **Control point:** Executed exclusive repress option, verified chain of title, master/art files, paid preorder book, and the resulting catalogue/customer list.
- **Why retained:** Rights are directly contractible, current demand is observable, manufacturing is quoted, and the first production run can be fully gated by cash demand.

### 22. ShiftRoster App Buyout — retained

- **Secret:** A modest existing shift-calendar app can be worth more as a transferable distribution-and-payment asset than as software; hospital-rota import creates a sharp Polish wedge after the acquisition.
- **Paid trigger:** Existing subscriber renewal; then a nurse, resident, paramedic, or factory worker imports a rota PDF instead of rebuilding shifts by hand.
- **Cold start:** Buy only an app with verified payouts, subscriber cohorts, source/IP, store-transfer eligibility, and a price below 65,000 PLN; hold back consideration until both stores and billing transfer.
- **Control point:** Acquired code/IP, listings, ratings, users, subscriptions, billing history, and hospital-rota parsers.
- **Why retained:** Unlike the blacklisted succession pattern, the durable distribution and payment flow must transfer at closing. The unresolved risk is that a generic incumbent can add the same import workflow.

### 23. Polish Genre-Rights Options — not selected

- **Secret:** A translated sample and refundable preorder can test a proven foreign genre backlist before a five-year Polish-language licence is exercised.
- **Control point:** Exclusive Polish option and later translation/audio rights.
- **Reason not selected:** It shares the same rights/preorder engine as Wantlist Reissue but has materially higher translation, narration, discovery, and platform risk. Wantlist offers more observable scarcity and cheaper first proof.

### 24. CEE Typeface Foundry Buyout — not selected

- **Secret:** An existing royalty catalogue can finance adding complete CEE glyph coverage and enterprise tiers.
- **Control point:** Acquired copyright, source files, trademarks, distributor agreement, and royalty flow.
- **Reason not selected:** No specific transferable catalogue at the budget was identified, Monotype controls discovery and payout economics, and open-source/AI supply weakens pricing.

### 25. Tray-Fed Egg Coder and Ink Cartridge — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Smaller packing centres might need a simpler tray workflow and recurring food-grade cartridge.
- **Reason rejected:** Poland already has FarmaJet, a cartridge-only low-maintenance egg printer with local customers, while Domino, Leibinger, ANSER, and low-cost European systems cover small through industrial users. Tooling a keyed dock would not create a new control point.

### 26. Cross-Fleet Scrubber Squeegee Dock — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Mixed Hako/Nilfisk/Kärcher fleets might pay to standardize blade replacement.
- **Reason rejected:** The universal-fit and water-pickup claim is unproven, account value is low, and an aftermarket rubber converter can reproduce the holder and blades through routine tooling.

### 27. Polish Pool-Test Photometer and Reagent Disc — rejected

- **Secret tested:** A cassette shaped around Polish daily bound-chlorine documentation could simplify operator work.
- **Reason rejected:** Palintest and Lovibond already own trusted reader/reagent systems; chemistry verification and municipal switching costs exceed the proposed local usability advantage.

### 28. Segmented Paint-Booth Filter Grid — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Differential loading might let body shops replace only exhausted exhaust-filter zones.
- **Reason rejected:** The 30% saving is unproven and any filter converter can add pre-cut segments and a frame with one routine contract.

### 29. Retrofit Waterless-Urinal Cartridge — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Converting installed flush urinals could create two-to-four annual cartridge replacements.
- **Reason rejected:** The recurring event exists only after founder-created installation, established waterless specialists already own the category, and one mould/distributor contract copies the core.

### 30. Cold-Plasma Seed Sanitation Licence — rejected

- **Secret tested:** An exclusive Polish field licence might turn a public patent into a high-value seed-treatment recipe.
- **Reason rejected:** Zayndu already has installations on three continents; PETKUS Selecta sells plasma-activated-water seed equipment; Hemeris, Auxoway, and Apolarion offer direct treatment machines or trials. The patent is a crowded TRL4 route, not an asymmetric control point.

### 31. Privacy-Verifiable QRNG Module — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Continuous privacy verification might create an audit-ready entropy module for smaller EU security OEMs.
- **Reason rejected:** Mature QRNG vendors own certification, photonics supply, patents, and design-in trust. A patent option plus bench optics cannot prove certification-grade demand inside the budget.

### 32. Photocatalytic HVAC Paste Licence — rejected

- **Secret tested:** An exclusive CEE application field could embed a regenerable paste into filters or panels.
- **Reason rejected:** TiO2 photocatalysis is crowded and design-around is easy; chamber efficacy, by-products, and low-light performance would make the POC another independent testing business.

### 33. HydroProg Micro-Catchment Rights — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Acquire a working flood-warning system instead of building a dashboard.
- **Reason rejected:** The buyer is a slow public-procurement authority, value depends on local gauge access and hydrological authority, and IMGW/public systems and established consultancies control trust.

### 34. Recovered P/K Fertilizer Licence — rejected

- **Secret tested:** Pair a patented recipe with title to potassium-phosphate waste and earn both disposal and fertilizer value.
- **Reason rejected:** Waste status, batch purity, fertilizer conformity, logistics, and offtake are all unresolved; proof approaches the capital limit before a repeat margin is visible and overlaps blacklisted recovery networks.

### 35. Fiber-Optic FPV Spool — rejected

- **Secret tested:** EU-origin winding, reserved fiber stock, and design-in could create a recurrent drone consumable resistant to radio jamming.
- **Reason rejected:** Polish and EU suppliers including SESTO and Ardlat already sell systems/spools, Chinese suppliers can price aggressively, defense procurement requires reliability trust, and winding equipment plus prepaid fiber is a routine purchase rather than a durable control point.

### 36. Public Fishery-Use Rights — rejected

- **Secret tested:** A long-term public fishery-use contract could create owned permit inventory and a recurring angler membership flow.
- **Reason rejected:** Current bid windows checked were closed, established associations win on stocking and operating credibility, permit ARPU is low, and biological management exceeds founder fit.

### 37. Discontinued Roof-Tile Inventory — rejected as duplicate

- **Secret tested:** Options over demolition stock plus exact-profile matching could avoid full roof replacement.
- **Reason rejected:** This is an exact-match construction-salvage pool and materially repeats the already confirmed matched pre-war door-suite model; it is explicitly blacklisted.

### 38. Small-Project White-Certificate Aggregation — rejected as duplicate

- **Secret tested:** Installer-originated authorization before project start can aggregate otherwise stranded energy-efficiency rights.
- **Reason rejected:** White-certificate variants are explicitly blacklisted from prior runs, and established audit/energy-efficiency firms already handle applications and sales.
