# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Wantlist Reissue

## Diagnosis

Wantlist Reissue has the strongest cold start of the round: an exclusive option creates a real temporary control point and paid preorders prevent speculative inventory. The customer pain and willingness to pay can be observed title by title, and the first run fits comfortably within the budget. Its weakness is strategic scale: one title is copy-protected, but adjacent titles are contestable, and a collector's demand for an original pressing may not transfer to a repress. The exact campaign is panel-ready only after the canonical specification preserves the no-exercise-before-250-paid-orders discipline and treats genre/catalogue compounding as a hypothesis rather than an accomplished moat.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence supports transparent Discogs scarcity signals, a growing Polish vinyl category, ordinary 500-copy manufacturing, and legal licensing. The existence of a clean optionable title, rights-holder economics, preorder conversion, fully loaded contribution, refund rate, repeat-buyer overlap, and second-title rights access are hypotheses. No real title or right is claimed as secured.

## Overall Score

**70/100.** Weighted sum `111.5 / 16`; `10 × 6.96875 = 69.6875`, rounded to 70.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 for the exact title after a signed exclusive option; Tier 2 before signature.** The option, preorder authorization, master/art access, and finished inventory are contractible. Strongest copy risk: another label options adjacent desirable titles, while the original rightsholder self-represses after exclusivity.

## Expansion Check

One title is an excellent beachhead. Expansion into a micro-genre catalogue reuses collector distribution and operations, but not the exact underlying right. Foreign labels can enter, though local chain-of-title discovery and a proven Polish buyer list offer a speed advantage.

## Fatal Objections

1. Discogs wants and high used prices may measure original-object scarcity rather than demand for a new repress.
2. Clean master, artwork, mechanical, and approval rights may be fragmented.
3. The company may remain a boutique project business if repeat-buyer overlap does not reduce CAC and rights costs.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Use an exclusive option and paid preorder threshold to make the customer finance rights exercise and physical inventory.
