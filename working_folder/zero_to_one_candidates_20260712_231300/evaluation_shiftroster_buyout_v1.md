# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ShiftRoster App Buyout

## Diagnosis

ShiftRoster correctly identifies that an acquired store listing, subscribers, billing, and reviews are more valuable than source code, and the transaction can solve cold start. However, no specific asset has been identified, so the control point, price, payback, technical quality, and transferability remain a search thesis rather than a candidate resource. Even after acquisition, Polish rota import is a feature that broad shift apps can add, while platform discovery and privacy migration remain outside founder control. The candidate should not enter a panel without a named data room or signed LOI that proves the assumed asset exists.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Apple and Google document eligible transfer mechanics, and small software transactions occur. Every material commercial input—the target app, seller, verified profit, cohorts, clean IP, transfer eligibility, churn, privacy records, and Polish feature demand—is hypothetical. The proposed diligence is sound but cannot substitute for an actual acquisition target.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 today, potentially Tier 1 only at a completed clean transfer.** No target is reserved. Strongest copy risk: an established shift app adds Polish OCR import while retaining a larger installed base and review history.

## Expansion Check

The hospital-worker wedge is coherent and consumer distribution avoids procurement. Adjacent CEE markets require new layouts, labor rules, and localization but use the same product. Broad shift apps can enter through routine feature development, and the acquired asset's platform position is not locally protected.

## Fatal Objections

1. The qualifying profitable transferable asset may not exist below the price cap.
2. The local wedge is a copyable feature rather than an exclusive control point.
3. Store/platform dependence can erase distribution and profit despite ownership of code.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire verified payment flow and app-store distribution before specializing a consumer workflow.
