## Diagnosis

Two independent judges scored the exact candidate 65 and 66 and converged on the same structural objections: no proven budget for an independent provider, uncertain artificial-to-production transfer, likely customization, and fragile reuse rights. These are not cosmetic omissions; they attack willingness to pay, gross margin, retention, control-point ownership, and repeatable distribution simultaneously. The original bank shape should not be polished or sent to real validation.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced market evidence still proves inspection spending and packaging-transition pressure, not benchmark-bank demand. The two judge verdicts are independent judgment, not market evidence, but their convergence is a workflow veto. No buyer prepayment, transfer result, reuse contract, or owned bank exists.
