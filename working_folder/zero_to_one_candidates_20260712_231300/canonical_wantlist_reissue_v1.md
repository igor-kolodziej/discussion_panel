# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**Wantlist Reissue** is a rights-controlled Polish micro-label that uses observable collector scarcity to option one out-of-print cult release at a time, takes paid refundable preorders under explicit rightsholder authorization, and exercises a 500-copy pressing right only after 250 buyers have funded the run.

It is not a record shop, speculative vinyl inventory business, fan bootleg, crowdfunding agency, or generic music-marketing service. The company owns or exclusively controls the exact repress right before asking for money, verifies the master and artwork chain of title, and refuses to manufacture until the paid threshold is reached.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Collector-market scarcity can be converted into a defensible company when a founder uses transparent transaction signals to secure exclusive, title-specific repress options and lets paid buyers—not taste or inventory speculation—decide which dormant Polish catalogue rights become products.

## Founder Fit

The founder's useful edge is not celebrity taste. It is disciplined data screening, cash-gated experimentation, rights and unit-economics diligence, written customer communication, and the ability to automate repetitive catalogue research without pretending that an algorithm replaces cultural judgment.

The business can be run within five hours per day during the POC. Music/IP counsel performs chain-of-title review, a part-time production coordinator checks masters and artwork, and an ordinary pressing plant and fulfilment vendor execute bounded work. The founder retains the option, licence, preorder account, inventory, customer list, label, data, and economic risk.

## Customer And Pain

The initial customer is a Polish collector/listener who wants a specific out-of-print local cult album but will not pay the original pressing's scarcity premium. The paid trigger is a release with all of the following:

- sustained wantlist demand and completed used sales rather than only asking prices;
- a large gap between used-original price and a plausible 149–169 PLN licensed repress;
- current listening, search, community, or artist activity;
- no announced licensed repress;
- a reachable rights holder with a clean master/artwork chain of title.

The customer currently overpays for a used original, waits indefinitely, buys an unofficial copy, or accepts digital-only listening. The rights holder owns a dormant asset but often does not want to finance manufacturing, build a shop, manage 500 parcels, or risk an embarrassing unsold run.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

The first product is one numbered, licensed 500-copy vinyl edition inside one coherent Polish micro-genre. It includes:

- written master, artwork, mechanical, manufacturing, D2C, and wholesale rights;
- a defined source master and approved artwork package;
- an authorized campaign page with audio/art excerpts permitted by the option;
- a refundable preorder at 149–169 PLN with a visible 250-order exercise threshold;
- a fixed delivery window, refund rule, pressing specification, and approval calendar;
- no exercise and no manufacturing obligation if the threshold is missed.

The POC excludes deluxe box sets, new artist development, speculative multi-title pressing, international rights, and any title whose ownership cannot be verified.

## Hidden Insight Or Market Misunderstanding

Small labels usually choose by taste, pay for rights and inventory, then discover demand. The hidden insight is that obscure catalogue demand is unusually legible before production: completed collector transactions, wantlists, streaming/search persistence, community discussion, and comparable represses reveal which original objects are scarce and which music remains alive.

The non-consensus step is contractual. Public data alone is not a moat. An exclusive option with preorder authorization converts a signal into a temporary monopoly over the exact product. Paid preorders then distinguish love of an unaffordable original object from willingness to buy a legitimate new edition.

## Underused Momentum

- ZPAV reported Polish vinyl revenue of 72.1 million PLN in 2025, up 13.5%.
- Discogs exposes historical sales and wantlist behavior before capital is committed.
- Polish/EU plants quote small 500-copy runs inside the POC budget.
- Streaming, search, and community activity make dormant-catalogue attention observable.
- Many artists, heirs, dissolved labels, and boutique publishers hold rights but lack a low-risk repress operator.
- Preorders and ordinary online fulfilment allow a narrow catalogue business without retail leases, distributor advances, or influencer access.

## Beachhead Market

The beachhead is one Polish micro-genre with persistent collector behavior and reachable non-celebrity rights holders—for example one overlooked cluster within 1980s/1990s electronic, punk, jazz, metal, or regional independent music. The genre is selected only after screening 100 releases and mapping 20 rights holders.

The first title must show at least 100 distinct wantlist accounts or equivalent observable demand, at least 20 completed scarcity-priced transactions or a clearly documented low-supply history, current listener activity, no competing repress, and a rights structure that counsel can verify. These are selection hypotheses to be tested, not claims about a title already secured.

## Primary Control Point

The foundational resource is an executed, paid, exclusive 12-month option for one 500-copy repress. It must specify:

- exact recording/version, format, territory, term, and copy ceiling;
- master, artwork, mechanical, manufacturing, marketing-excerpt, D2C, and wholesale permissions;
- rightsholder warranties, indemnity allocation, source-asset delivery, and approval deadlines;
- an option fee of no more than 8,000 PLN;
- exercise converting to a five-year sell-off licence or a defined 500-copy licence;
- 10–15% net-sales royalty or a minimum guarantee credited against royalty;
- no rightsholder competing repress during the option and sell-off term;
- refund and termination mechanics if title or source assets fail diligence.

Control-point tier: **Tier 1 for the exact title after signature; Tier 2 while sourcing.** The strongest copy risk is that another label options adjacent titles or the rightsholder self-represses after exclusivity. The defence is therefore a portfolio of fulfilled options and a consented collector graph, not one contract forever.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping 

The founder secures the foundational resource through ordinary paid rights contracts, not an unspecified partnership.

1. Screen 100 releases using completed sales, wantlists, listener persistence, comparable represses, rights status, and likely source-asset condition.
2. Contact 20 rights holders with a written offer: 3,000–8,000 PLN option fee, 12-month/500-copy exclusivity, authorized preorder, defined royalty, and no manufacturing risk to the owner.
3. Retain music/IP counsel to verify master, artwork, mechanical, performer/producer, estate, and prior-label rights before public promotion.
4. Obtain a current plant quote and a test transfer of the source master before exercising.
5. Launch only after signature. Hold preorder cash separately and refund automatically if 250 paid orders are not reached within 60 days.
6. Exercise and press only after the threshold, approval, and fully loaded contribution are confirmed.

Why an unknown founder can secure it: the offer gives a dormant rightsholder immediate option income, royalty upside, contractual approval, and no inventory or fulfilment work. The founder's credibility is proven by escrowed cash, counsel-reviewed documents, a manufacturing quote, transparent economics, and paid demand—not status.

Externally checkable proof before serious capital is spent: signed option, counsel's chain-of-title checklist, source-asset receipt, plant quote, campaign authorization, payment-processor report, 250 non-founder paid orders, refund reserve, and final contribution model. Total founder cash at risk before the threshold is capped at 20,000 PLN.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture 

All numbers remain hypotheses until one option, quote, and paid campaign exist.

- Retail price: 149–169 PLN including VAT.
- Option fee: 3,000–8,000 PLN.
- Rights royalty: 10–15% of defined net sales.
- Manufacturing reference: approximately €1,779 before VAT and freight for a basic 500-copy package; title-specific mastering, test pressings, artwork, and packaging are added.
- Total first-run cash need after the preorder gate: 25,000–35,000 PLN.
- Fully loaded contribution target: 50–65 PLN per copy after VAT, payment fees, royalty, pressing, mastering, packaging, pick/pack, shipping subsidy, defects, and refunds.
- Break-even ceiling: 400 copies; target break-even: 300–350.
- Preorder CAC ceiling: 20 PLN; blended first-run CAC ceiling: 25 PLN.
- Cash conversion: customer cash arrives before option exercise/manufacturing; funds remain refundable until the threshold and title approval.
- Owner-earnings test: the first run must produce at least 20,000 PLN contribution before founder labor, and the second campaign must reuse enough buyers to lower CAC by at least 25%.

The business does not pass because 500 records sell eventually. It passes when the first run is paid before production, delivers at target margin, and creates cheaper demand for a second option.

### Distribution Moat 

The first 10 customers come from exact-title demand, not broad branding:

1. authorized email/social channels controlled by the rightsholder under the option;
2. title and catalogue search pages;
3. compliant outreach to public collector communities and specialist stores;
4. direct messages only where platform rules and consent permit;
5. five prepaid specialist-store allocations;
6. a referral link allowing a buyer to unlock a numbered insert or shipping credit without changing the pressing threshold.

The first 100 customers must come without broad paid media. The 250-order gate combines title-specific search, authorized rightsholder announcement, specialist-store deposits, collector communities, and buyer referrals. No single channel may account for more than 50% of paid orders.

The first 100 catalogue customers beyond the initial release come from consented buyer data: ranked interest in three adjacent titles, genre-specific email, bundled reservations, store allocations, and referrals. Every later option is pretested with the existing cohort before fee payment.

The moat is the intersection of clean rights access, fulfilled trust, exact-title economics, and a collector graph whose adjacent preferences are measured with money. A mailing list alone is not a moat; it must reduce CAC and improve option selection.

## Domination Path

1. Become the trusted licensed reissue operator for one neglected Polish micro-genre.
2. Fulfil one title on time with verified rights, transparent pressing quality, and no inventory overhang.
3. Use paid adjacent-title reservations to option two more releases from the same rights/community graph.
4. Standardize rights diligence, approval calendars, manufacturing, packaging, and store allocations across a catalogue.
5. Negotiate longer-duration or multi-title rights only after sell-through evidence.
6. Add CD/cassette, licensed digital, liner-note archives, or neighboring CEE rights only when the same customers and rights relationships lower entry cost.

Domination means first call on a narrow rights cluster and the densest paid buyer graph for it—not participation in the general vinyl market.

## Local Adaptation Defense

This is not a foreign-label copy justified by Polish translation. The local advantage is Polish chain-of-title work across artists, heirs, dissolved labels, producers, and collecting-society mechanics; culturally precise title selection; domestic manufacturing/fulfilment; Polish-language customer trust; and a focused collector graph.

A foreign or established Polish label can copy the preorder method. It cannot sell the same exact title during a valid exclusive, and it must separately rebuild the micro-genre rights map and buyer graph. If an attractive title can be obtained and sold equally quickly by any broad label, the option fee and campaign should be abandoned.

## Why This Could Compound

- Each signed option creates a temporary exact-title monopoly.
- Each fulfilled edition creates consented buyers with revealed genre preferences.
- Each campaign produces better demand thresholds, price elasticity, store allocations, and refund data.
- Fulfilment proof improves access to adjacent rights holders.
- Shared counsel templates, mastering, artwork, packaging, and logistics reduce marginal cost.
- A catalogue enables bundles, memberships, pre-commitments, wholesale relationships, and eventually longer-duration rights ownership.
- The best titles can shift from short options to multi-year licences or acquired rights after evidence, turning temporary control into durable cash flow.

## Validation Plan

The POC lasts no more than 12 months and costs no more than 75,000 PLN. Evidence is labeled explicitly:

- **Established evidence:** Polish vinyl revenue, public collector-market signals, ordinary rights licensing, app-free D2C payments, and available 500-copy manufacturing.
- **Unproven hypotheses:** repress conversion from original scarcity, clean option availability, 250-order acquisition, 50–65 PLN contribution, adjacent-title buyer overlap, and lower second-campaign CAC.

Validation sequence:

1. Produce a 100-title evidence table and 20-rightsholder map.
2. Obtain three written term-sheet responses and one counsel-reviewable option.
3. Verify source assets and fully loaded economics before signing.
4. Sign one option below the fee cap with explicit preorder authorization.
5. Reach 100 paid orders in 30 days and 250 in 60 days; otherwise refund and do not exercise.
6. Deliver 500 copies within the contracted window with defect/refund rate below 5%.
7. Achieve at least 50 PLN fully loaded contribution per delivered copy.
8. Obtain paid reservation interest from at least 25% of first-title buyers for one of three adjacent titles.
9. Secure a second option with no higher upfront fee and forecast at least 25% lower CAC using the existing cohort.

Passing means validated enough to pursue a second release. It does not authorize a broad catalogue acquisition, full-time hiring, or serious capital.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than three legally plausible, economically attractive option candidates remain after 100-title screening and 20 rightsholder contacts.
- No rightsholder accepts a 12-month exclusive option with preorder permission below 8,000 PLN upfront.
- Master, artwork, mechanical, performer/producer, estate, prior-label, or approval rights remain unclear after counsel review.
- Source masters fail a production test or restoration pushes break-even above 400 copies.
- Fewer than 100 paid preorders arrive in 30 days or fewer than 250 in 60 days; refund automatically and do not press.
- Founder-connected buyers exceed 10% of the 250-order threshold.
- Any one distribution channel supplies more than 50% of orders, making the launch dependent on an unowned gatekeeper.
- Fully loaded contribution falls below 50 PLN per delivered copy or the first run produces less than 20,000 PLN contribution before founder labor.
- Defects, late delivery, cancellations, and refunds exceed 5% after fulfilment.
- Fewer than 25% of delivered buyers express paid reservation interest in the next three shortlisted titles.
- The second option cannot be secured, or forecast CAC does not fall at least 25%, showing no compounding rights/distribution advantage.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

1. Choose three candidate micro-genres and define objective title filters.
2. Build the first 100-title table from completed sales, wantlists, listener persistence, competing editions, and likely rights owners.
3. Reject titles with unclear recording/version identity or announced represses.
4. Interview 15 collectors about repress versus original-pressing tradeoffs without claiming any title is secured.
5. Map 20 rights holders and send a one-page option structure to the first ten.
6. Obtain two pressing/fulfilment quotes and a music-counsel scope.
7. Build the fully loaded unit-economics model with tax, defects, refunds, and shipping.
8. Require at least three substantive rights-holder responses and one diligence-ready title before day 30; otherwise kill the shape.

## Yearly Execution Plan

**Months 1–2:** finish screening, rights outreach, collector interviews, and three term-sheet candidates. Spend no more than 6,000 PLN.

**Months 3–4:** complete chain-of-title and source-asset diligence; sign one option only if all fee, exclusivity, approval, and contribution conditions pass. Cumulative cap: 20,000 PLN.

**Months 4–6:** run the authorized preorder. Require 100 paid orders by day 30 and 250 by day 60. Refund and stop if missed.

**Months 6–9:** exercise, approve test pressing, manufacture, and fulfil. Publish delivery and defect performance to buyers. Cumulative cap: 50,000 PLN.

**Months 9–10:** reconcile actual contribution, refunds, CAC by channel, buyer consent, and adjacent-title paid interest.

**Months 10–12:** option a second title only if first-run contribution is at least 20,000 PLN, adjacent paid interest reaches 25%, and forecast CAC falls 25%. Total cap: 75,000 PLN.

## Applicable Non-Refundable Funding (optional)

No grant or subsidy is assumed. The first release must work on founder capital, an option fee, and customer preorders. Export, cultural, or catalogue-preservation funding may be investigated only after a successful first fulfilment and only where it does not delay rights control or distort title selection.
