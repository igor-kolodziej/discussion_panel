# Research and Source Ledger

Research cut-off: 2026-07-15 CEST. Material statements distinguish evidence from hypotheses. Company pages establish current product positioning or reachable channels, not independent proof of performance.

## Cross-cutting regulation and market evidence

### Platform-work algorithmic management

- **Sourced evidence:** [Directive (EU) 2024/2831](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L2831) applies algorithmic-management protections to people performing platform work, including those without an employment relationship. Article 9 covers transparency and portability of work-generated data, Article 10 requires human oversight and human decisions for suspension/termination, Article 11 provides explanation and review rights, and Article 29 requires transposition by 2 December 2026.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Eurostat experimental statistics](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20240718-1) found 3.0% of people aged 15–64 in the participating countries performed at least one hour of paid platform work during the prior 12 months in 2022; Poland was included. This is evidence of a material population, not a fleet-buyer count.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Bolt's Polish fleet page](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/fleet/) offers a fleet portal with real-time earnings and worker-performance statistics. [Bolt's Polish support page](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/support/articles/360001424593/) publicly routes workers to recommended fleet partners. These establish an observable buyer category and relevant data flow.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Bolt's Polish driver page](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/driver/) states that payouts, campaigns, fares, commissions, tips, and platform-assigned trips affect earnings. It reported a March 2026 Polish median weekly earning figure, but that marketing figure is not used in the wealth model.
- **Sourced evidence:** [FareShare research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08904) documents an academic system that helps workers recover missing wages and prepare deactivation appeals from platform data. It supports technical feasibility and pain existence; it also proves that claim preparation alone is copyable.
- **Hypothesis:** Polish/CEE fleet firms will pay for centralized discrepancy recovery and can lawfully authorize relevant worker/fleet exports. Fleet count, average workers per fleet, claim incidence, recovery yield, and 20 PLN monthly willingness to pay require paid discovery.

### Clinical and connected-wearable evidence

- **Sourced evidence:** [EU Clinical Trials Regulation, Article 58](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN-DE/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32014R0536) requires the clinical trial master file to be archived for at least 25 years after trial end unless other Union law requires longer.
- **Sourced evidence:** [FDA guidance on computerized systems in clinical trials](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/fda-bioresearch-monitoring-information/guidance-industry-computerized-systems-used-clinical-trials) addresses record retention, retrieval, and preservation when computerized systems change or are discontinued.
- **Company evidence:** [Arkivum](https://arkivum.com/pharmaceutical-life-sciences/clinical-trial-data/) markets GxP archiving that includes raw wearable data. [Veeva Study Archive](https://sites.veevavault.help/gr/sitevault/studies/archive-overview/) demonstrates incumbent control of ordinary study archives. This narrows PhysioVault to executable endpoint replay and vendor-exit proof.
- **Sourced evidence:** [EU Medical Device Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/anx_14/oj) and the [European Commission MDCG guidance index](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en) establish the vigilance and post-market framework. MDR reporting timing can be 2 days for a serious public-health threat, 10 days for death or unanticipated serious deterioration, and 15 days for other serious incidents.
- **Company evidence:** [Greenlight Guru](https://www.greenlight.guru/complaint-management-software) and [Wipro](https://www.wipro.com/business-process/solutions/post-market-surveillance/) already sell complaint/post-market workflow. IncidentFreeze must control raw evidence reconstruction and SOP routing, not become a generic QMS.
- **Hypothesis:** Small sponsors and wearable OEMs will prepay a technically scoped replay or incident rehearsal from an unknown specialist after reviewing a public demonstrator, legal scope, insurance, and security controls.

### Freight and real-time human commitments

- **Sourced evidence:** The [European Commission eFTI page](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-themes/logistics-and-multimodal-transport/efti-regulation_en) states that the eFTI Regulation becomes fully applicable in July 2027, creating momentum for structured freight information.
- **Company evidence:** [HappyRobot's Circle case](https://www.happyrobot.ai/customer-story/circle) describes phone-based freight negotiation and booking automation. [Transporeon Transport Assignment](https://www.transporeon.com/en/platform/transport-execution-visibility-hub/carrier-lsp/transport-assignment) already covers electronic offer and assignment workflows. These prove demand and the strongest copy threat.
- **Hypothesis:** A material residue of disputes begins in human calls that are not fully represented in the TMS; the tool can lawfully record/process calls with participant notice and materially reduce booking corrections or denied accessorials.

### Hearing-protector fit and recurring supply

- **Sourced evidence:** [NIOSH Publication 2025-104](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/publications/numbered/2025-104.html) recommends individual quantitative fit testing to verify hearing-protector attenuation rather than relying only on labelled ratings.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Directive 2003/10/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2003/10/2007-06-27/eng/pdf) establishes EU minimum requirements concerning worker exposure to noise and employer protective duties.
- **Company evidence:** [3M E-A-Rfit](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/hearing-protection-us/support/e-a-rfit-support/) and [Honeywell VeriPRO](https://automation.honeywell.com/us/en/products-backup/personal-protective-equipment/hearing-protection/fit-testing) demonstrate commercial quantitative fit-testing systems and manufacturer-specific control risk.
- **Hypothesis:** A used or leased multi-brand fit-test system, stock, travel, and insurance can fit inside 38,000 PLN; at least one plant will sign both a paid fit cohort and replenishment purchase order.

## Second-wave evidence and sources

### Wearable skin-contact materials and low-volume conversion

- **Company evidence:** [Solventum Medical Tape 4579](https://www.solventum.com/en-us/home/f/b5005588006/) is positioned for extended-wear medical devices and configurable conversion; [Solventum 4578](https://assets.solventum.com/is/content/mmmspinco/msd-medtech-sc-nw-extd-wear-adhesive-tape-4578-tis-en-gblpdf) supports up to 28-day wear.
- **Company evidence:** [Lohmann DuploMED long-wear adhesives](https://lohmann-tapes.com/product-technologies/longwear-skin-adhesives) and [Avery Dennison wearable material testing](https://label.averydennison.com/content/dam/medical/medicalproducts/Documents/Brochures/ADM19001_8_5x11_WP_WEARABLES-WEB.pdf) show multiple medical constructions and that material/carrier choices affect wear performance.
- **Official/standards evidence:** [PAIH's 2025 Polish life-science report](https://www.paih.gov.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/The-life-science-sector-in-Poland-2025.pdf) maps Polish medtech/R&D clusters. [ISO 10993-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/10993-1) and [ISO 10993-18](https://www.iso.org/standard/64750.html) frame biological evaluation and chemical characterization; the final-device manufacturer, not the converter, owns the device conclusion.
- **Company/competition evidence:** [BDK](https://www.bdk.uk.com/) offers ISO 13485 medical converting, prototyping, pilot production, more than 300 stocked materials, and preferred-converter status; [Gergonne](https://www.gergonne.com/en/solutions-per-market/medical/die-cut-components/) offers European medical converting with a no-tooling prototyping centre; [ADDEV Parafix](https://parafix.com/product/laird-8300-0038-44/) offers prototypes with or without tooling and already operates as a major European converter/distributor; [Stokvis](https://stokvistapes.co.uk/markets/healthcare/) describes itself as the world's largest independent self-adhesive-component converter; and [JBC](https://www.jbc-tech.com/markets/consumer-wellness/) explicitly supports wearable OEMs from prototype through scale. These substantially weaken the hypothesis that small-program converting is structurally neglected and make named-lot ownership/design-in continuity—not prototyping—the only potentially differentiated wedge.
- **Hypothesis:** Small OEMs cannot obtain named-lot continuity economically from established converters and will prepay a reservation pack. Master/slit-roll pricing, MOQ, shelf life, supplier authorization, die-cut cost, and 45-account market capacity require direct quotes and paid proof.

#### Public-only decision follow-up — 2026-07-15

- **Disclosed minimums and lead times materially weaken the low-volume gap:** [Rapid Die Cut's medical page](https://rapiddiecut.com/industries/medical) explicitly covers wearable sensors, states no minimum order quantity, and gives a standard 10-day lead time for custom parts. [Strouse](https://www.strouse.com/solutions/medical-device-attachment) is ISO 13485 certified, FDA registered, supports wearable-device attachment and rapid prototypes; its [public FAQ](https://www.strouse.com/faq) says there is no official MOQ, although complex jobs may carry a USD 500–1,000 press minimum and hard tooling can cost hundreds to thousands of dollars. These are not like-for-like quotes for SkinLot's documentation bundle and do not establish total project price, but they show that qualified medical converters can accept economically small custom work.
- **The most relevant European incumbent publishes commercial speed but keeps price private:** [ADDEV Parafix's FAQ](https://parafix.com/faqs/) confirms an undisclosed minimum order value, stock slit-roll delivery in 2–3 days, rotary converted parts in 2–3 weeks, bespoke samples, prototypes with or without tooling, ISO 13485 certification, Class 7 cleanroom capability, and customer-tool storage until 24 months of inactivity. This leaves exact price unresolved but contradicts the premise that small custom geometry, documentation, tooling continuity, and fast supply are absent.
- **Nearby non-medical converters further compress geometry and logistics advantage:** German [SMS Converting](https://www.sm-selbstklebetechnik.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/SMS-Converting-Focus-and-Products-HD.pdf) advertises one-off units, prototypes and small batches, no MOQ, use of more than ten leading material suppliers, and a one-day inquiry response. Polish [CVGS](https://www.glasstec.de/vis-content/event-glasstec2024/exh-glasstec2024.2780576/glasstec-2024-CVGS-Pro-sp.-z-o.o.-sp.-k.-Paper-glasstec2024.2780576-TP92OPkVQomwkpyCnUqcug.pdf) publishes a 1–2 week prototype lead time plus temperature/humidity-controlled raw-material and finished-goods storage. Neither source proves ISO 13485 skin-contact production, but both show that local digital cutting, controlled warehousing, and short lead time are purchasable rather than scarce.
- **Published converter inventory economics show that source-stock status, not customer geometry, drives MOQ:** [Walker Tape Converting](https://www.walkertapeconverting.com/explaining-small-orders/) says its minimum for tape held in-house is one 3-inch × 360-yard roll; a non-stock material may pass the supplier's full minimum through to the customer. This supports SkinLot's observation that material access can be the bottleneck, but also shows that established stockholding converters already use shared inventory to lower customer minimums.
- **The proposed stock-and-call-off control point is already an advertised supplier capability:** [Flexcon](https://www.flexcon.com/markets/healthcare) sells skin-contact adhesives for glucose monitors, insulin pumps, cardiac monitors and other wearable devices. Its [Customer Special Stock Agreement](https://www.flexcon.com/capabilities/customer-special-stock) sets annual volume, call-off sizes and min/max levels, then produces and holds customer inventory for release on demand. Flexcon also states that custom quotes can take as little as 24 hours, custom orders as little as eight days, and a [purchase commitment is required for special stock](https://www.flexcon.com/insights/standard-vs-custom-which-is-appropriate). A representative [DermaFlex wearable adhesive](https://www.flexcon.com/products/43160/dermaflexdfm-100-clear-h-566-80-df-pfw) includes a six-month change-notification benefit. These public terms do not promise a single named production lot, but they cover most of SkinLot's claimed continuity mechanism at the upstream material supplier.
- **Medical converters already bundle conversion with managed inventory:** [Precision Converting/Hisco](https://www.precisionconverting.com/single-source-solution) advertises ISO 13485/FDA-registered medical converting, 37 stocking locations, specialized material repackaging, and vendor-managed inventory. Hisco's [Shurmed acquisition release](https://www.hisco.com/userfiles/pdf/pr_shurmed.pdf) specifically links medical adhesive applications, 3M-preferred converting, VMI/RFID programs, specialized warehousing, chemical management and cold storage. This makes inventory management a current incumbent service rather than a new control point.
- **Continuity pain exists, but the searched public record does not establish the proposed willingness to pay:** Flexcon's change-notification term and [Screentec's wearable-biocompatibility guidance](https://screentec.com/articles/2026/06/biocompatibility-in-wearable-medical-devices-complete-guide-for-developers/) support the fact that material substitutions after biological testing can create additional testing and delay. Provider case studies such as [Strukmyer's wearable wound-therapy example](https://strukmyer.com/case-studies/custom-adhesive-converter-by-strukmyer/) show material qualification and lot-level traceability inside an incumbent converter. No independent buyer case study was found in the searched public sources showing that a small wearable OEM paid separately to reserve one exact adhesive lot, could not obtain continuity from its existing supplier/converter, or suffered a quantified loss specifically because an exact lot was unavailable.
- **Decisive facts remain non-public:** no source provides a like-for-like CEE quote covering the same manufacturer/product code, 50–5,000 customer-specific parts, exact-lot segregation, retained samples, non-substitution, documentation, replenishment reservation, landed price and lead time. No public evidence establishes willingness to pay 8,000–15,000 PLN for comparison, 18,000–35,000 PLN for reservation, or 12,000–30,000 PLN annually for continuity; nor does it establish 45 reachable recurring programs, five to seven durable annual design-ins, repeat-call-off rates, CAC, or mature margin. Public research therefore cannot resolve the commercial gate and must not be treated as validation.

### Polish atypical-speech transaction testing

- **Official evidence:** [European Accessibility Act Directive 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/0882) covers consumer banking and e-commerce accessibility, including identification, security, and payment functions. Poland's [implementing act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2024/731/ogl) has applied since 28 June 2025.
- **Research/initiative evidence:** The [Speech Accessibility Project](https://speechaccessibilityproject.beckman.illinois.edu/) and [Google's project description](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/accessibility/speech-accessibility-project/) show that atypical-speech data improve recognition and that large English-focused collection already exists. [Mozilla Common Voice Polish 26.0](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmqinmu2a00winq07gyrtri0q) is ordinary Polish speech, not the proposed transaction-outcome corpus.
- **Company/competition evidence:** [Hamming](https://hamming.ai/language/polish) already markets Polish voice-agent testing, including non-native speakers and varied speaking rates, as part of a broader QA and production-monitoring platform. It does not establish an owned Polish atypical-speech participant panel, but it is a direct route for an incumbent voice-QA vendor to add the proposed cohort.
- **Hypothesis:** Polish banks/e-commerce operators will pay 18–30k PLN for a release challenge and permit the provider to retain pooled exact-recording rights. Consent/licence withdrawal, health/disability inference, special-category data, accessibility ethics, and participant compensation require counsel and participant-centered design.

- The exact 120-day event, budget, internal-cohort and retention filters are publicly proven for **zero** names. They require buyer conversations or procurement evidence; a product page cannot establish them.
- Winning 28 annual accounts would require **103.7%** of the 27-name documented census or **90.3%** of the 31-name loose census. Even against the candidate's unsupported 60-account pool, it is **46.7%** penetration.
- For 28 wins to represent 25%, 20% or 15% penetration, the eligible annual pool must contain respectively **112, 140 or 187** independently buyable accounts. Public research found no evidence for a pool of that size under the exact filters.
- The census therefore supports a reachable prospecting test, not a non-heroic 28-account wealth model.

- [Hamming's current FAQ](https://hamming.ai/faqs) discloses no amount. Pricing is quote-driven, based mainly on test volume, with startup/SMB and enterprise plans, custom terms and SLAs; there is no conventional self-serve free trial. Its [Polish offer](https://hamming.ai/language/polish) already covers Polish voice-agent QA, and its main product supports integrations, CI/CD, accents, interruptions and production replay.

Enterprise therefore starts at $54,000/year, about **205,600 PLN** using the [NBP 14 July 2026 average rate of 3.8076 PLN/USD](https://rss.nbp.pl/kursy/TabRss.aspx?n=2026%2Fa%2F26a134). MowaForge's 180,000 PLN annual price is about $47,300: plausible for a complete regulated-enterprise QA platform, but not publicly evidenced for a narrower quarterly human-cohort service.

These UK prices are not Polish voice-testing quotes, but they make MowaForge's 9,400–10,600 PLN direct-cost budget for twelve recruited participants, research design, annotation, privacy and a retest look aggressive rather than conservative.

##### Retention-right evidence

- No reviewed public source shows a voice/ASR vendor granting an outside QA supplier five-year hosted retention of its sandbox responses for a cross-vendor failure graph.
- The observable enterprise norm pushes the other way. [Gladia's Enterprise zero-data-retention option](https://www.gladia.io/changelog) deletes audio, transcripts and metadata after processing; its public product terms emphasize contractual no-training commitments and DPAs. [Coval's plans](https://www.coval.ai/pricing) advertise only 30-day, 90-day or custom trace retention. Hamming publicly markets zero-retention defaults where required, configurable redaction and enterprise data-residency controls in its [voice-agent testing guide](https://hamming.ai/resources/voice-agent-testing-guide).
- MowaForge's output is narrower than customer production audio and could be contractually separable, so these sources do not prove every vendor will refuse. They do show that reusable hosted retention is a negotiated exception and a potential security/procurement blocker, not a default right that can support the control point before contracts exist.

##### Public-only decision

Public sources establish real voice-QA pain, real accessibility-testing labor cost, and category budgets large enough to make 180,000 PLN conceivable for a full enterprise platform. They do **not** establish a 12,000 PLN deposit, 90,000–180,000 PLN renewal willingness, five-year vendor retention permission, or a 112–187-account pool that would make 28 annual wins non-heroic. Under the public-only/no-outreach restriction, the commercial and control-point gates remain unresolved; the exact candidate should be parked rather than advanced.

### Respirator breakthrough sensor option

- **Official evidence:** CIOP-PIB documents a [working smart-filter prototype](https://newapp.ciop.pl/CIOPPortalWAR/appmanager/ciop/pl?_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=P202923601332862083041&projekty_id=4265&projekty_rok=2021) with simulated-use testing and technical documentation. Application P.442235 appears in the [official UPRP publication](https://api-ewyszukiwarka.pue.uprp.gov.pl/api/bup-or-wup/bup11_2024.pdf). CIOP publishes a [commercialization contact route](https://ciop.pl/CIOPPortalWAR/appmanager/ciop/pl?_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=P7800357221340707763185).
- **Official evidence:** [EU PPE Regulation 2016/425](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0425) governs PPE conformity. A shadow-mode integration study cannot be represented as changing an approved cartridge replacement schedule.
- **Patent/company/technical evidence:** Honeywell owns an [European end-of-service-life indicator patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP2866901B1/en); [3M](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/worker-health-safety-us/all-stories/full-story-detail/?storyid=d172297e-f78d-4b63-b109-b2ce8898c401) already sells cartridges with a visual service-life indicator in appropriate organic-vapour environments; and a [2025 technical review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adsr.202400029) catalogues multiple historical and active sensing approaches, including ammonia breakthrough. The CIOP option therefore needs unusually strong field scope, performance, economics, and freedom-to-operate to be a control point rather than merely one more sensor implementation.
- **Hypothesis:** CIOP will grant a field-exclusive, low-cost evaluation option including know-how/prototype access and no conflicting rights, and an EU PPE OEM will prepay a 20–30k PLN integration study within 180 days.

- **The public record still establishes an application, not a granted or EU-wide right:** CIOP's current [PATENTY ZŁOŻONE](https://www.ciop.pl/CIOPPortalWAR/appmanager/ciop/pl?_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=P54000838911600348061404&html_klucz=88888&html_klucz_spis=&html_tresc_id=300002227&html_tresc_root_id=3000271) page lists P.442235, while its current [PATENTY UDZIELONE](https://www.ciop.pl/CIOPPortalWAR/appmanager/ciop/pl?_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=P54000838911600348061404&html_klucz=88888&html_klucz_spis=&html_tresc_id=3001422&html_tresc_root_id=3000271) page does not. The official UPRP A1 publication records only the Polish application filed 9 September 2022. Exact-title, applicant and inventor searches found no publicly indexed EP or WO family. This is not proof that no foreign filing exists, and the current UPRP detail record was blocked by its anti-bot gate, but no public source reviewed establishes grant, useful current claim scope or protection beyond Poland.
- **Certified ammonia cartridges are an established, incumbent-served product class:** current official pages document [3M 6054 K1](https://www.3m.co.uk/3M/en_GB/p/dc/v000078859/), [Sundström SR 295 K2](https://srsafety.com/en/product/sr-295-gas-filter-k2-2), Dräger's [K/ABEK filter range](https://www.draeger.com/pl_pl/Safety/Respiratory-Protection/Selection-Guide/Types-Of-Respirators/Respirator-Filters), and Moldex [A1B1E1K1P3/A2B2E2K2 EasyLock products](https://www.moldex-europe.com/de/product-detail/easylock-kombinationsfilter/) under EN 14387. Sundström identifies Sweden as country of manufacture and supplies an EU declaration of conformity; these are real OEM product families, not a hypothetical market denominator.
- **The visible ESLI gap is ammonia-specific, not category-wide:** 3M's European [technical datasheet](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/2254353O/3m-full-face-respirator-6000-series-technical-datasheet-spanish.pdf?v=2022-11-04T13%3A58%3A49.000+0000) lists 6051i/6055i with an end-of-service-life indicator for A1/A2 organic vapours, but lists its K1/ABEK ammonia models separately without that feature. No ammonia-rated ESLI was found on the reviewed current 3M, Dräger, Sundström or Moldex pages. This supports a narrow product gap but also shows that major OEMs already possess cartridge, conformity and—in 3M's case—ESLI capability.
- **EU conformity adds an uncontrolled approval dependency to an existing cartridge:** Regulation (EU) 2016/425 [Annex V, points 7.2–7.5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0425) requires the manufacturer to disclose modifications to the approved PPE type or technical documentation that may affect conformity or certificate validity; such modifications require additional notified-body approval, and the body examines the type and performs relevant tests where necessary. Category III PPE also requires Module B plus C2 or D. The law supplies neither a fixed amendment calendar nor price, so it does not validate the proposed 180-day schedule or 4,000 PLN pre-assessment allowance.
- **Public research cannot resolve the transaction gates:** no reviewed source publishes CIOP option/licence price or availability, chain of title, confidential know-how, claim amendments, FTO opinion, ammonia-cartridge OEM annual units, smart-feature budget, Evaluation Pack budget, procurement cycle, integration willingness, notified-body test scope, amendment quote or duration. Those facts require rights-holder, OEM, counsel and notified-body counterparties. Under the public-only/no-contact constraint they remain unknown, so the candidate should be parked rather than treated as validated.
