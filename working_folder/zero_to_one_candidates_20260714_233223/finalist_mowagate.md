# Zero-to-One Candidate: MowaGate

_Candidate generation only — not validated. All prices, conversion rates, margins, market counts, and valuation multiples marked as hypotheses require paid-market evidence. This brief makes no certification, legal-compliance, clinical, diagnostic, or accessibility-conformity claim._

## One-Sentence Thesis

MowaGate sells Polish retail banks a prepaid, recurring release challenge in which directly recruited adults with hard-to-recognize speech attempt real voicebot, IVR, and voice-authentication journeys, while the paid engagement itself acquires a revocable five-year exclusive commercial licence to the exact recordings and outcome annotations that compound into a Polish atypical-speech transaction corpus.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

### Economic buyer

The beachhead buyer is the Head of Digital Channels, Head of Contact-Centre Product, or accessibility owner with release authority at a Polish retail bank that uses a voicebot, IVR, or spoken authentication step. The bank owns the value: failed authentication and abandoned calls increase assisted-service cost and lost conversion, while inaccessible identification, security, or payment functionality creates complaint and remediation exposure.

The beneficiary is also the bank's customer, but this is not a payer-beneficiary mismatch. The bank pays because it owns channel cost, release risk, conversion, and statutory service accessibility. MowaGate does not depend on an insurer, clinic, employer, or public authority paying for a benefit captured only by someone else.

### Painful event

A voice-channel release reaches acceptance or production and works on ordinary Polish test speech but repeatedly misunderstands, times out on, or blocks customers who stutter, speak slowly, have unusual articulation, use a regional pattern, or cannot follow the expected cadence. Discovery after release creates emergency remediation, additional agent calls, an accessibility complaint, or a delayed vendor acceptance decision.

### Current workaround

- Internal employees and vendor QA teams repeat ordinary happy-path phrases.
- General web-accessibility audits focus mainly on visual and keyboard interaction rather than live Polish speech recognition.
- Teams use ordinary Polish ASR corpora or synthetic audio that do not preserve the relationship among a specific transaction intent, the live system response, retries, abandonment, and successful completion.
- A one-off accessibility user test may produce a report but usually does not create a renewable, rights-cleared, cross-vendor outcome corpus.

### Evidence

- Directive (EU) 2019/882 covers consumer banking and e-commerce services and requires identification methods, electronic signatures, security, and payment functionality delivered as part of a service to be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
- Poland's implementing accessibility act entered into force on 28 June 2025.
- The Speech Accessibility Project states that mainstream speech-recognition systems do not consistently recognize diverse speech patterns and reported about 2,000 participants by September 2025.
- Google's Project Euphonia reported that additional non-standard-speech data reduced median word error rates by more than 80% in its research and says larger, more diverse datasets remain important.

### Hypotheses

- Polish bank voice-channel owners experience enough release risk or avoidable assisted-service cost to buy an external challenge before a complaint occurs.
- A budget owner will pay for transaction-level outcome evidence even though the law does not prescribe MowaGate, a specific benchmark, or a particular voice-testing method.
- Existing Polish vendors do not already own a comparable, renewable corpus of atypical Polish speech linked to live banking transaction outcomes. This absence has not been proven and must be checked during discovery.

## Product Wedge

### First paid product: one-release challenge

For a hypothesized 25,000-30,000 PLN prepaid fee, MowaGate runs one defined release challenge:

1. The buyer supplies 30-40 permitted intents, a non-production or low-risk test endpoint, expected outcomes, and prohibited actions.
2. Twenty-five directly recruited adults complete one 45-60 minute remote session using their own phone or a supplied test device. Recruitment is based on experienced interaction barriers, not a diagnosis.
3. The system records the participant's audio, timing, retries, recognition result, route, completion outcome, and device/noise metadata under explicit, granular consent.
4. MowaGate returns aggregate completion rates, failure clusters, redacted trace examples, and one retest after remediation. It does not certify legal conformity or recommend a clinical interpretation.
5. The participant agreement grants MowaGate, subject to GDPR and other non-waivable rights, a five-year exclusive commercial licence to the exact session recordings, transcripts, prompts, and derived evaluation annotations. Exclusivity applies only to those files and annotations; it never restricts the participant's ability to speak, work with another project, or license new recordings.
6. The buyer receives its report and a limited, non-transferable evaluation licence. Raw participant audio remains in MowaGate's controlled environment by default. Any buyer access to identifiable or pseudonymous raw audio requires separate participant consent, a data-processing/data-sharing arrangement, logged access, and deletion obligations.

### Why this is not a generic benchmark, dashboard, or AI wrapper

- The unit sold is a release decision using the buyer's actual permitted transaction endpoint, not a static score against a public corpus.
- Each paid run purchases new participant performance and legally structured reuse rights; the defensible asset is created by the commercial transaction itself.
- The result links speech to a live sequence of recognition, retry, routing, abandonment, and completion outcomes. Ordinary audio datasets lack this operational label.
- The same retained panel and corpus are refreshed across releases and vendors, creating cross-version and cross-system evidence unavailable to a single buyer.
- Automation reduces coordination and analysis labor, but a generic model cannot manufacture valid consent, retained participant relationships, longitudinal recordings, or lawful commercial rights.
- MowaGate makes no claim to be a harmonised standard, notified body, accredited laboratory, or accessibility certifier.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

### Evidence

Open and research corpora prove that speech diversity matters, but their purpose is usually model research. Mozilla Common Voice released a Polish scripted-speech corpus in June 2026, while the Speech Accessibility Project's public recruitment and challenge materials are centered on atypical English speech. Google's Euphonia materials point contributors to the Speech Accessibility Project and state that its own Euphonia dataset is not available to outside companies.

### Hypothesis

The overlooked product is not “better Polish ASR” or an accessibility report. It is a recurring release transaction that simultaneously gives the buyer a decision and gives MowaGate a renewable, consented, Polish transaction-outcome asset. Banks cannot recreate cross-vendor coverage by purchasing a generic dataset, while a conventional consultancy normally does not retain reusable session rights or operate a living panel.

The misunderstood tradeoff is between maximum raw-data portability and participant protection. MowaGate can preserve control and withdrawal capability by hosting raw files centrally and selling results and controlled evaluation access. That reduces immediate buyer freedom but makes a rights-cleared recurring corpus more credible and durable.

## Primary Control Point

The primary control point is a directly contracted, rights-cleared, revocable corpus of exact Polish atypical-speech transaction sessions linked to live outcome labels, plus the retained participant panel able to create fresh lots. It is neither a future hoped-for dataset nor an unspecified partnership.

### Exact rights design

- MowaGate contracts with each adult participant directly.
- The licence covers the exact session recordings, prompts, transcripts, timing, device/noise metadata, and evaluation annotations for specified evaluation and benchmarking purposes for five years.
- The licence is exclusive only for commercial reuse of those exact files and annotations. It never claims ownership of a participant's voice, condition, identity, or future speech.
- Model training, voice cloning, speaker identification, emotion inference, medical/health inference, and unrelated reuse are prohibited unless a new, specific opt-in and lawful basis are established.
- Buyer access is hosted and purpose-limited by default, preserving revocation and deletion capability.
- Every file carries a participant UUID, consent version, purpose flags, permitted buyer/access class, retention date, and deletion/tombstone status.

### Consent withdrawal and deletion

GDPR Article 7 permits consent withdrawal at any time and requires withdrawal to be as easy as giving consent. The participant keeps all compensation and experiences no penalty. On withdrawal:

1. MowaGate stops new use immediately and disables the participant UUID in future benchmark builds.
2. Identified and pseudonymous raw files are deleted within a defined target, hypothesized at 30 days, unless a narrow legal retention duty applies.
3. Hosted buyer access is revoked; contracts require buyers to delete any authorised local copy and attest deletion.
4. Aggregate statistics already irreversibly anonymised may remain only if counsel confirms they no longer constitute personal data and no participant can be singled out.
5. MowaGate does not promise an irrevocable data asset. The control proof therefore includes an attrition buffer and measures active, usable rights after withdrawals.

### GDPR and special-category treatment

Speech audio is not automatically “biometric data for uniquely identifying” a person merely because it is audio, but voice can become biometric data when processed for identification. Atypical speech and self-described access barriers may also reveal or permit inference about health or disability. MowaGate will conservatively design the first product as if Article 9 special-category risk applies:

- explicit, granular consent under Articles 6 and 9 rather than implied consent;
- a DPIA before collection because vulnerable-person, audio, and potentially special-category processing may create high risk;
- no diagnosis collection; eligibility is based on a self-described interaction barrier;
- EU-hosted encrypted storage, separate identity and trace vaults, least-privilege access, audit logs, and pseudonymisation;
- clear controller/processor roles for each buyer configuration;
- purpose-specific consent layers for collection, hosted evaluation, buyer listening, and any future training use;
- accessible plain-language and audio consent materials, a comprehension check, a cooling-off option, and withdrawal by the same channels used to enrol;
- no minors during proof and no buyer raw-data export by default.

These are design commitments, not a legal conclusion. Polish privacy counsel must confirm the lawful basis, DPIA, agreements, transfer position, retention, and deletion process before recruitment.

## Distribution Advantage

### Hypothesis

Each paid bank release funds additional domain-specific attempts, updated device/noise conditions, participant renewal, and new annotations. The next buyer therefore receives broader coverage at lower marginal collection cost, while its own paid run expands the asset again. A bank or voice vendor can build internal test speech, but it cannot cheaply reproduce cross-vendor, longitudinal Polish transaction outcomes and retained participant access from a single routine procurement.

Distribution can compound through proof rather than personal brand:

- anonymised release deltas and remediation outcomes create evidence-led outbound material;
- a standard security/privacy pack lowers procurement friction;
- retained participants shorten delivery lead time from months to weeks;
- buyers can refer MowaGate without sharing their proprietary system or customer data;
- later CEE panels are funded only by a prepaid local buyer.

The distribution advantage fails if buyers prohibit retained pooled rights or if procurement treats MowaGate as interchangeable manual usability testing.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

### Current alternatives and incumbent power

- Large platforms and research programmes have more speech-model expertise and can collect large datasets.
- Speech vendors control their own model-development and release workflows.
- Accessibility consultancies have customer trust and user-testing experience.
- Banks control whether an external challenge becomes part of release acceptance.

MowaGate does not try to replace any of those workflows. It owns a separate participant/data resource and sells a pre-release decision input. The wedge is deliberately outside the voice vendor's internal model QA so the buyer can compare systems and versions independently.

### Competitive advantage

1. Polish atypical speech linked to completed and failed real transactions, not only word error rate.
2. Direct participant contracts, hosted raw data, consent-version tracking, and deletion capability.
3. Cross-vendor and longitudinal results that no single bank sees internally.
4. A live panel capable of testing an urgent release within a defined SLA.
5. A narrow banking-intent ontology and remediation history that broad accessibility firms must build from scratch.

### Expansion path

1. Polish retail-bank IVR and voicebots.
2. Polish voice authentication and app voice input, only after separate biometric/privacy review.
3. Polish e-commerce, telecom, ticketing, and payment-service speech journeys covered by accessibility obligations or costly assisted-service fallbacks.
4. Czech and Slovak banking panels funded by first-customer prepayment.
5. A CEE cross-language release rail for voice-platform vendors and regulated enterprises.

No step requires a clinic, regulator endorsement, formal certification authority, or exclusive platform integration.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

### Evidence

Poland has a current national act implementing European accessibility requirements, and Polish ordinary-speech datasets exist. Polish transaction speech includes language-specific inflection, numbers, names, addresses, banking vocabulary, and cadence that an English atypical-speech dataset does not cover.

### Hypothesis

A Warsaw founder can recruit and support Polish participants more cheaply and sensitively than a foreign horizontal vendor, while selling to local buyers in Polish and mapping the work to the current national enforcement context. The defense is operational and data-based, not a legal monopoly. A foreign platform can enter, but it must reproduce Polish retained-panel relationships, consented transaction traces, bank-specific outcome labels, and local procurement material.

## Compounding Advantage

The compounding loop is:

1. A buyer prepays a release challenge.
2. The fee pays participants and secures new exact-session rights.
3. New attempts improve the cross-system failure taxonomy and automation.
4. Better coverage increases renewal value and shortens the next delivery.
5. Renewals finance participant retention, new conditions, and adjacent intent families.
6. The corpus and panel make each new language or channel cheaper to structure, without selling raw personal data as a commodity.

The asset compounds only while consent remains active, participants are fairly compensated, and buyers accept pooled evaluation rights. MowaGate must report usable active-rights counts net of withdrawal rather than boasting about historical recordings it can no longer process.

### Ethics and participant recruitment

- Recruit adults through transparent paid advertisements, disability/accessibility communities that permit commercial recruitment, stuttering and communication forums, university accessibility offices, and participant referrals. No one channel is required.
- Do not ask a clinic, physician, therapist, or advocacy organisation to vouch for MowaGate or supply records.
- Recruit on the basis of “voice systems often misunderstand me” rather than requesting a diagnosis.
- Pay a fixed amount for time and rights regardless of performance, do not require proof of disability, and do not make payment conditional on failure severity.
- Provide text, audio, large-print, slow-read, and support-person options. The support person may assist with comprehension but may not pressure consent.
- Separate consent to participate from consent to commercial reuse, buyer listening, and future model training. Declining an optional use does not reduce the session fee.
- Keep withdrawal easy, penalty-free, and available by email, phone, or the enrolment interface.
- Publish a participant-facing explanation of customers, use cases, prohibited uses, compensation, retention, and active-rights status.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If bank willingness to pay fails but participant rights remain active and counsel permits the intended use, reusable assets include:

- accessible consent, withdrawal, deletion, and participant-payment infrastructure;
- a recruited Polish panel that may opt into a different, specifically disclosed evaluation purpose;
- secure speech-session orchestration and annotation tooling;
- a transaction-intent and failure taxonomy;
- buyer discovery evidence showing which accessibility event is or is not budgeted;
- privacy/security procurement materials applicable to other consented human-interaction datasets.

No data are automatically repurposed after failure. A different use requires compatibility analysis and, where needed, new explicit consent. If the business closes, participants are notified, raw personal data are deleted according to the stated policy, and historical counts are not presented as an owned asset.

## Critical Evidence

1. **European Accessibility Act scope.** Directive (EU) 2019/882 covers consumer banking and e-commerce and requires accessible identification, electronic-signature, security, and payment functionality delivered as part of a covered service.
2. **Polish timing.** Poland's Act of 26 April 2024 on ensuring accessibility requirements for certain products and services entered into force on 28 June 2025.
3. **Atypical-speech technical gap.** The Speech Accessibility Project says mainstream speech-recognition systems do not always recognize diverse speech patterns and reported about 2,000 contributors by September 2025.
4. **Data can improve performance.** Google reported large median word-error-rate improvement from Project Euphonia's non-standard-speech work and explicitly says larger, more diverse datasets are needed.
5. **Ordinary Polish speech data exist.** Mozilla published Common Voice Scripted Speech 26.0 for Polish in June 2026, proving that “a Polish speech corpus” alone is not scarce.
6. **Consent withdrawal is non-waivable in the proposed model.** GDPR Article 7 gives the data subject the right to withdraw consent at any time and requires withdrawal to be as easy as giving consent. UODO repeats that withdrawal cannot create negative consequences.
7. **Special-category risk.** GDPR Article 9 restricts biometric data used for unique identification and health data, with explicit consent among the enumerated exceptions. MowaGate conservatively treats atypical-speech and potential health/biometric inference as high-risk even when it avoids diagnosis and identification.

## Critical Hypotheses

1. At least one Polish bank will pay 20,000-30,000 PLN within 180 days for a bounded external voice-release challenge.
2. At least one paid buyer will allow MowaGate to retain pooled, hosted evaluation rights rather than require exclusive ownership or deletion after delivery.
3. At least 70% of eligible adult recruits will voluntarily accept the exact-file commercial licence after an accessible comprehension check, without diagnosis-based targeting.
4. Annual participant withdrawal and unusable-right attrition can remain below 20% with fair pay, narrow purposes, hosted access, and transparent governance.
5. A counsel-reviewed Article 6/Article 9 basis, DPIA, and buyer structure can support the proposed evaluation purposes in Poland.
6. The expected buyer can measure a release, call-deflection, conversion, complaint, or vendor-acceptance value sufficient to support 90,000-150,000 PLN annual pricing.
7. The founder can reach 45 recurring CEE/EU enterprise accounts by year five without a platform partnership or status-based distribution.
8. Automation and trace reuse can support 76% gross and 27% operating margin without degrading participant safeguards.

The strongest unresolved hypothesis is number 2: whether a rational bank buyer will fund the run while allowing MowaGate to retain revocable pooled evaluation rights. Without that contractual permission, the product becomes non-compounding usability service labor.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or structurally change MowaGate if any of the following occurs:

1. Privacy counsel cannot document a defensible lawful basis, DPIA, purpose limitation, withdrawal, deletion, and buyer-access design before participant collection.
2. Fewer than three of the first ten qualified buyer conversations confirm a painful release or post-release voice-accessibility event.
3. One hundred targeted outbound messages produce fewer than five qualified calls or two scoped proposals.
4. No paid deposit, prepaid pilot, purchase order, or budget-backed commitment of at least 20,000 PLN is obtained by day 180.
5. All serious buyers require exclusive ownership of participant recordings, prohibit retained pooled rights, or demand unrestricted raw-data/model-training use.
6. Fewer than 70% of eligible seed participants accept the retained evaluation licence after comprehension checking, or an accessibility adviser finds the consent materially coercive or confusing.
7. Withdrawal/unusable-right attrition exceeds 20% annualised and replacement economics cannot preserve an active 120-person pool.
8. Valid participant-session direct cost exceeds 500 PLN or expected gross margin falls below 60% at annual pricing.
9. The first delivered buyer will not pay for a renewal or remediation retest and the second independent buyer is not secured within 12 months.
10. Delivery requires more than ten founder hours per client per quarter after the second release cycle.
11. A platform, accessibility consultancy, or Polish speech vendor demonstrates a materially equivalent transaction-outcome panel with stronger distribution and no contractual wedge MowaGate can own.
12. Buyers treat a generic internal employee test or public corpus as an adequate substitute and no measurable transaction outcome changes that behavior.
