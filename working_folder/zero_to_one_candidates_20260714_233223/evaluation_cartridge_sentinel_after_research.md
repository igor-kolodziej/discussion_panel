# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Cartridge Sentinel — After Public Research

## Diagnosis

The follow-up confirms a narrow ammonia-ESLI product gap but makes the candidate less controllable. P.442235 is still publicly listed by CIOP as filed, not granted; no public EP or WO family was found. Four incumbent product families already cover certified K/ABEK ammonia filtration, and 3M already has European ESLI capability for organic vapours. Regulation (EU) 2016/425 requires additional notified-body approval for conformity-relevant modifications to an approved PPE type, with further tests where necessary. Public sources disclose none of the option price, FTO conclusion, OEM unit economics, buyer budget, procurement cycle or conformity-amendment time and cost needed to validate the six-month proof or founder-wealth bridge. The correct disposition is **research further / park**, with no outreach or spend authorized by this review.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Decision-critical findings

- CIOP's current filed-applications page includes P.442235; its granted-patents page does not. The official A1 publication establishes a Polish filing dated 9 September 2022 and four published claims, not a grant or enforceable EU-wide control point.
- Exact-title, applicant and inventor searches found no publicly indexed EP or WO family. That absence is not proof that no foreign application exists, but no public evidence supports the candidate's assumed EU territory.
- Current official sources document ammonia-rated products from 3M (6054 K1 and ABEK variants), Dräger (K/ABEK), Sundström (SR 295 K2, made in Sweden, EN 14387 and EU 2016/425) and Moldex (EasyLock ABEK/K products). The candidate has not identified a reachable set of under-served small or mid-sized OEMs distinct from these incumbents.
- 3M's European product literature lists 6051i/6055i organic-vapour ESLI filters but conventional ammonia-rated filters. No ammonia ESLI was found in the reviewed incumbent pages. This is evidence of a specific gap, not evidence that an entrant can own or profitably reach it.
- Annex V of Regulation (EU) 2016/425 requires additional approval for modifications that may affect conformity or certificate validity, and permits the notified body to require relevant tests. Category III PPE requires Module B plus C2 or D. No authoritative source supplies a standard duration or price for this proposed modification.

### Facts that remain unknowable from public sources

- CIOP's willingness and authority to grant the hypothesised field-exclusive option, its price, territory, know-how package, sublicensing terms, improvements allocation, encumbrances and execution time.
- Current claim amendments, search opinion, grant prospects, foreign filings, assignment history and a system-level multi-jurisdiction FTO conclusion.
- OEM annual ammonia-cartridge volumes, product-refresh events, evaluation budget, willingness to pay a deposit, procurement cycle, integration preference and willingness to bear conformity work.
- Notified-body classification of the exact architecture, test matrix, amendment duration, quotation, iterations and liability allocation.

These are live-counterparty facts, not gaps that more public browsing can responsibly fill. They remain candidate hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**38/100.** Weighted points are **76.0** across **20.0** total weight: `10 × (76.0 / 20.0) = 38.0`. Public research strengthens the existence of the product gap but reduces domination and beachhead confidence because established incumbents are visible and a reachable small/mid-OEM denominator is not.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** No option is signed, P.442235 is not publicly shown as granted, and no foreign family was found. Even a field-exclusive CIOP agreement would bind CIOP within its contractual scope; it would not create FTO, stop independent ESLI architectures or substitute for EU territory. 3M already combines certified cartridge distribution with European ESLI capability, while the technical review documents multiple alternative sensor families. The proposed calibration and evidence library becomes a control point only after several launches; nothing public shows a credible route to that installed base.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** Public research produced no OEM annual-volume, smart-feature budget, recurring-fee attachment, gross-margin, renewal or acquisition-multiple evidence.

- The conservative case remains 3.79m PLN of founder wealth, below the 5m PLN target.
- The expected 6.26m PLN result still requires five OEMs, 15,000 new modules, 30,000 service modules, 55% gross margin, 18% operating margin, 88% ownership and a 4× EBIT value to hold together.
- At 3× expected-case EBIT, the same assumptions yield about 4.77m PLN, below target.
- Six OEMs × 10,000 new modules would be 60,000 modules per year—four times the written expected volume and above the written strong case's 50,000. It cannot be substituted for evidence.

The public OEM catalogue confirms that products exist, not what they sell annually or would pay an entrant. The wealth bridge remains speculative.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** The paid Evaluation Pack remains a sensible proof instrument, but the 180-day route depends on uncontrolled decisions by four kinds of counterparty: CIOP for rights and know-how, counsel for bounded FTO, an OEM for budget and deposit, and a notified body for the conformity path.

The regulatory finding is adverse to timing confidence. Annex V does not say that an approval addition takes a fixed number of weeks or costs a standard amount; it says conformity-relevant modifications require additional approval and that relevant tests may be necessary. No public quote or decision shows that the exact sensor/cartridge integration fits the candidate's 4,000 PLN pre-assessment or leaves enough of the 40,000 PLN cap for iteration. The option price, FTO scope, buyer cycle and procurement budget likewise remain unavailable publicly. With outreach prohibited, the six-month transaction path cannot be validated.

## Expansion Check

Ammonia remains a coherent narrow wedge because the reviewed 3M ESLI is organic-vapour-specific. Expansion is still not software-like: each gas, sorbent, cartridge geometry and mask system can reset sensing chemistry, residual-life mapping, environmental compensation, FTO, human factors and conformity evidence. Established OEMs enter that race with approved product families, test infrastructure, distribution and liability systems. A pending Polish application and local institute access do not reverse that asymmetry.

## Fatal Objections

1. **No present control point:** public sources show P.442235 as filed, not granted, and no public EP/WO family; the option terms remain hypothetical.
2. **No FTO:** licensing one implementation would not clear alternative patents across the sensor, placement, reader, alarm and residual-life method.
3. **Incumbent reach and bypass:** 3M, Dräger, Sundström and Moldex already serve the ammonia-cartridge category; 3M also has an ESLI platform, and alternative architectures are documented.
4. **No buyer transaction evidence:** annual units, budget, deposit, procurement cycle, integration willingness and conformity ownership are all unknown.
5. **Unpriced conformity dependency:** EU law requires additional approval for relevant modifications and permits further testing, but no scope, duration or quote exists.
6. **Fragile wealth outcome:** the conservative case fails and the expected case falls below target at 3× EBIT before any volume or margin miss.
7. **Six-month path is a chain of counterparties:** rights, FTO, technical reproduction, regulatory credibility and a cold OEM payment must all clear a no-slack cash cap.

These objections prohibit `pursue`. More public research is unlikely to resolve them.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest salvageable insight remains the **OEM-funded decision pack**: require the product owner to fund bounded integration evidence and a notified-body decision before committing capital to production or assuming final-PPE liability. That offer architecture is useful even if this particular patent asset is not.

## Recommendation

research further

Operationally, **park**. Do not contact CIOP, OEMs, counsel or notified bodies under the current public-only instruction, do not assume option economics, and do not spend against the 40,000 PLN plan. Reopen only if live counterpart evidence becomes authorized and can directly address rights terms, FTO scope, buyer budget/procurement and notified-body time/cost.
