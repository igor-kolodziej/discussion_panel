# Candidate Funnel

Candidate generation only — not validated. No numerical validation scores are assigned in this phase.

## Funnel conventions

- **Sourced evidence** means a linked official, regulatory, academic, association, or company source.
- **Founder fact** comes from the live founder profile.
- **Hypothesis** is unproved commercial, economic, or operational judgment.
- **Funnel decision** is a generator-stage screen, not a reviewer or validation verdict.

## Fifteen divergent directions

### 1. GigClaim Fleet Recovery Rail — retain as finalist

- **Customer view:** A fleet operator cannot profitably investigate hundreds of small fare, bonus, rating, and account discrepancies across driver apps; workers abandon valid claims because evidence and appeal histories are fragmented.
- **Paid wedge:** A prepaid fleet earnings-recovery audit followed by a per-covered-worker fee and a percentage of cash actually recovered. The first version is a human-reviewed evidence and appeal desk, not automated legal advice.
- **Acquisition:** A public list of Polish ride-hail and delivery fleet partners, plus direct outreach to owner-operators managing at least 100 workers. Bolt publicly maintains a Polish recommended-fleet route and a fleet portal with worker-level earnings data.
- **Control point:** A signed all-dispute routing mandate, worker/fleet-authorized exports, and contractual rights to retain pseudonymized claim→reason→appeal→decision→settlement facts. The asset is the cross-platform recovery graph, not a generic dashboard.
- **Momentum:** Directive (EU) 2024/2831 requires transparency, human review, portability of work-generated data, and national transposition by 2 December 2026.
- **Hard-gate screen:** **Provisional pass / pass.** Expected wealth case can exceed 5m PLN at 30,000 covered workers; a manual paid audit can be sold within 180 days for less than 20,000 PLN of pre-proof risk.
- **Critical hypothesis:** Fleet operators will route worker claims through an independent service and permit reusable pseudonymized outcome rights.

### 2. OrphanFleet Live — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A PV technician facing an unfamiliar or discontinued inverter loses margin on repeat truck rolls and slow OEM support.
- **Paid wedge:** Per-escalation visual copilot plus multi-brand expert routing.
- **Control point:** Cross-brand fault→action→resolution graph from paid sessions.
- **Evidence:** URE reported more than 1.6m Polish renewable microinstallations; SolarEdge, SightCall, and TeamViewer already sell remote diagnosis or visual assistance.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** Reachable through installers, but visual-support incumbents can add the multi-brand knowledge layer and the parent-company channel risks repeating a previously confirmed PV service pattern.

### 3. ScopeSeal — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A construction subcontractor performs verbally instructed extra work before scope and price are acknowledged, then loses the change-order dispute.
- **Paid wedge:** Spoken instruction→priced confirmation card before the crew proceeds.
- **Control point:** Routed instruction→acknowledgment→change order→payment trail.
- **Evidence:** Procore and Clearstory already treat change-event and signed T&M capture as core workflow.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** The event is real and reachable, but incumbents own the project workflow and Polish evidentiary usefulness is an additional unproved legal dependency.

### 4. CarrierPromise — retain as finalist

- **Customer view:** A forwarder discovers only at booking or invoice time that negotiated rate, detention, equipment, or appointment terms were remembered differently.
- **Paid wedge:** A live dispatcher-side term extractor and immediate bilingual confirmation card, initially with human quality review.
- **Control point:** Calls traverse a contracted SIP/desktop layer and create a call→commitment→booking→settlement graph.
- **Momentum:** Freight remains phone-heavy; the EU eFTI regime makes structured freight information progressively normal and becomes fully applicable in July 2027.
- **Hard-gate screen:** **Provisional pass / pass.** At 180 CEE firms paying 2,800 PLN monthly, a 27% operating margin can support the wealth gate; a 3,000 PLN desk pilot fits under 22,000 PLN pre-proof risk.
- **Critical hypothesis:** Human-call ambiguity remains costly enough after TMS adoption to justify another installed workflow layer.

### 5. YardGrade Loop — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A scrap buyer must quote a mixed load before downstream sorting or assay reveals its actual value.
- **Paid wedge:** Guided phone capture, human/AI second opinion, and quote-to-settlement reconciliation.
- **Control point:** Image→quoted grade→downstream regrade/assay→realized-margin loop.
- **Evidence:** AMCS and ScrapRight own weigh-and-pay workflows; TOMRA owns much deeper sensor/classification expertise.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** Physical heterogeneity limits phone-camera accuracy, while incumbents control both workflow and superior sensors.

### 6. PhysioVault Replay Archive — retain as finalist

- **Customer view:** A small sponsor closing a wearable-enabled clinical study must retain evidence for decades even if the device vendor, transform, or executable environment disappears.
- **Paid wedge:** Prepaid exit-and-replay pack: immutable raw export, metadata and firmware manifest, containerized derivation, expected-output tests, and custody.
- **Control point:** Signed custody/replay contracts plus founder-owned parsers, containers, normalization rules, and integrity fixtures; customer data remain customer-owned.
- **Momentum:** The EU Clinical Trials Regulation requires at least 25 years of clinical-trial master-file archiving; FDA guidance requires discontinued systems to remain retrievable and computational methods to be preserved.
- **Hard-gate screen:** **Provisional pass / pass.** Expected 5.25m PLN revenue supports founder wealth under conservative valuation; a prepaid one-study pack can be demonstrated with less than 28,000 PLN at risk.
- **Critical hypothesis:** Small sponsors will buy executable endpoint replay rather than ordinary validated storage.

### 7. IncidentFreeze for Connected Medical Wearables — retain as finalist

- **Customer view:** When a possible serious incident arrives, the quality team has days—not months—to preserve reconstructable telemetry, app logs, algorithm version, timestamps, and complaint facts.
- **Paid wedge:** Prepaid incident-freeze rehearsal, snapshot agent, and annual evidence-retention retainer mapped into the existing QMS.
- **Control point:** Complaint SOP routes covered incidents through the installed agent; founder retains generic parsers, manifests, and reconstruction logic.
- **Momentum:** EU MDR vigilance deadlines can be 2, 10, or 15 days depending on severity.
- **Hard-gate screen:** **Provisional pass / pass.** Expected 35 OEM retainers plus incident packs can support founder wealth; a paid mock or closed historical complaint fits within 25,000 PLN pre-proof risk.
- **Critical hypothesis:** An unknown specialist can obtain a paid rehearsal before it has formal QMS-vendor status.

### 8. SessionSeal Biosignal Acceptance Gate — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** If detachment, clock drift, wrong montage, or missing calibration is discovered after the participant leaves, the endpoint is already lost.
- **Paid wedge:** Protocol-compiled data sufficiency gate before a biosignal session closes.
- **Control point:** Study contracts designate the gate for every incoming file and allow non-identifying defect derivatives.
- **Evidence:** FDA and EMA treat technical glitches and missingness as data-integrity threats; Koneksa and Clario already offer vendor-agnostic pipelines and QC.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** This substantially overlaps earlier failed clinical biosignal workflow shapes; access, sponsor trust, incumbent control, and derivative-data rights remain simultaneous dependencies.

### 9. Inference Firewall for Enterprise XR Wearables — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** An XR vendor cannot prove that an app release is technically incapable of prohibited emotion inference from eye, face, voice, posture, or heart-rate features.
- **Paid wedge:** Prepaid audit plus CI/runtime policy gate and signed permitted-feature manifest.
- **Control point:** Mandatory build gate and cross-device physiological inference tests.
- **Evidence:** The EU AI Act emotion-inference prohibition in workplaces and schools has applied since February 2025; ArborXR and ManageXR already control enterprise XR deployment.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** Strong timing but a general app-security or device-management incumbent can copy the enforcement layer through one product addition.

### 10. Wearable Data Act Port — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A small connected-wearable OEM must provide user-accessible product data and metadata without exposing trade secrets or breaking privacy duties.
- **Paid wedge:** Export gateway, metadata manifest, access workflow, and conformance harness.
- **Control point:** Installed gateway plus physiological-format mappings and device-cloud adapters.
- **Evidence:** The EU Data Act creates connected-product data access duties; Steelbridge, Terra, and Thryve already provide horizontal infrastructure or wearable schemas.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** The addressable OEM universe and annual price are insufficiently grounded, and horizontal infrastructure providers can enter the niche cheaply.

### 11. PAR Rail — retain as finalist

- **Customer view:** An EHS manager buys certified earplugs but cannot prove that each worker receives the labelled attenuation; the failure appears at an audit or hearing-threshold shift.
- **Paid wedge:** Multi-brand quantitative fit day, individualized training, documented Personal Attenuation Rating, and automatic replenishment of the worker's proven SKU.
- **Control point:** Signed replenishment mandate plus pseudonymized worker→SKU→PAR history and a cross-brand fit graph.
- **Momentum:** NIOSH's 2025 guidance recommends quantitative individual fit testing; EU employer duties already require suitable hearing protection.
- **Hard-gate screen:** **Provisional pass / pass.** A leased/used fit system and test stock can produce a 5,000–8,000 PLN paid cohort below 38,000 PLN risk; expected CEE plant volume supports a 5m PLN equity route if procurement repeats.
- **Critical hypothesis:** Plants will switch replenishment to the independent fit provider instead of buying a manufacturer-specific testing service.

### 12. SmartPPE Privacy Gate — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A DPO/CISO cannot verify which physiological, location, or inferred-state data leave a smart-PPE device after firmware updates, stalling procurement.
- **Paid wedge:** Black-box device/network/API test with a machine-readable allow/block data-flow manifest.
- **Control point:** Owned device fleet, firmware fingerprints, inference-leak vectors, and replay traces.
- **Evidence:** GDPR DPIA duties and EU-OSHA material confirm privacy and adoption concerns around smart systems.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** Employer demand and 45,000 PLN recurring willingness to pay are unproved; laboratories and enterprise security teams can replicate the apparatus.

### 13. AirReserve Coach — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** Fire-academy instructors see final cylinder pressure but cannot distinguish pacing, anxiety, and scenario difficulty, shortening drills and reducing exit reserve.
- **Paid wedge:** Training-only optical pressure clip, respiration band, and post-drill coaching for non-IDLH exercises.
- **Control point:** Cross-brand hardware and consented task→breathing→air-use traces.
- **Evidence:** NIOSH investigations connect repetitive SCBA training with lower air consumption; MSA and Dräger already log operational telemetry.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** Safety trust, rugged hardware, limited training-organization count, and OEM telemetry access create too many simultaneous dependencies.

### 14. Aseptic Motion Qualifier — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** Sterile-manufacturing trainers repeatedly qualify aseptic behavior, but intervention motion and first-air obstruction remain visually judged.
- **Paid wedge:** Wrist/hand tags plus depth/UWB tracking for one real mock-up intervention, supplementing rather than replacing microbial qualification.
- **Control point:** Sensor kits, intervention compiler, and deidentified expert-annotated motion primitives.
- **Evidence:** EU GMP Annex 1 makes aseptic qualification consequential; Innerspace and Altoura already sell VR aseptic training.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** GMP-site access and expert trust are slow, while 90 high-annual-value EU sites is an ungrounded concentration assumption.

### 15. FitGraph PPE — close after funnel

- **Customer view:** A new hire discovers during issuance that certified gloves, harness, eyewear, and clothing do not fit the body or each other, causing returns, delay, and unsafe improvisation.
- **Paid wedge:** On-site scan and functional-fit session followed by supply of the selected cross-brand ensemble and managed reorders.
- **Control point:** Body→task→SKU→return graph plus routed reorders.
- **Evidence:** EU employer duties require suitable and mutually compatible PPE; major brands already operate women-specific fit programs.
- **Funnel decision:** **Close.** The expected value depends on low-margin resale scale and a 200-person outlier panel that is not independently secured in the first paid wedge.

## Second wave after first-wave exhaustion

The first wave became exhausted after GigClaim's independent panel produced an official 56 and both judges identified the same fatal payer-beneficiary mismatch. Its IdleFleet structural pivot aligned the payer but remained unpanelable because avoidable idle-day incidence was unevidenced. The other first-wave finalists required live buyer evidence or failed a hard gate. The generator was therefore invoked again inside this same run folder.

### 16. SkinLot Foundry — retain as second-wave finalist

- **Insight:** A small wearable/patch OEM often needs continuity of the exact skin-contact construction and lot it already qualified, but established converters optimize for larger runs and substitutions can reopen wear/biological evidence.
- **Paid wedge:** Prepaid geometry-and-lot reservation pack, followed only then by owned named-lot rolls, a customer-specific die, retained samples, and call-off inventory.
- **Control:** Physical named-lot inventory, customer-specific conversion tooling, and prepaid call-off rights; no supplier exclusivity is needed for the first asset.
- **Hard-gate screen:** Provisional pass/pass. A first 8–15k PLN reservation pack fits below 32k PLN risk; 45 designed-in OEM programs at 200k PLN recognized annual revenue support the expected wealth route.
- **Key unknown:** Whether small European wearable OEMs will prepay continuity and whether master/slit rolls can be purchased in workable quantities.

### 17. HeatReserve CEE — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Poland's 2027 workplace-temperature rules create a deadline for hot-work controls, and owned wearable swap inventory can be deployed faster than procurement.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Direct CORE, SlateSafety, Kenzen, BHP distributors, and occupational-health providers can localize; physiological employee data and daytime field delivery add trust and service dependencies before a durable control point exists.

### 18. WhisperStock — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Voice AI in shared offices creates a new acoustic privacy problem, and local inventory solves immediate trials and swap service.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Skyted already sells direct, through Amazon and Lyreco, while a reseller right is uncontracted; the product is conspicuous and ordinary inventory is not scarce enough.

### 19. HapticBridge Poland — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Professional haptic buyers need a local try-before-buy bench, sizes, integration help, and swap stock before approving a €6k glove.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. One demonstration kit consumes almost the entire pre-proof budget; Polish account volume is ungrounded and German/European resellers or SenseGlove can serve directly before local rights are secured.

### 20. Cartridge Sentinel — retain as second-wave finalist

- **Insight:** Time-based respirator-cartridge replacement either wastes cartridges or risks breakthrough; a specific CIOP sensor technology can be optioned before a product is built.
- **Paid wedge:** Paid OEM application/integration study using a shadow-mode sensor behind the approved sorbent bed; no change to the certified replacement schedule during proof.
- **Control:** A field-exclusive evaluation option and later licence to CIOP application P.442235 plus prototype know-how, drawings, firmware, and test reports.
- **Hard-gate screen:** Provisional pass/pass, conditional on a capped option and prototype access. A 20–30k PLN paid OEM study can fit within 34k PLN risk; six OEMs buying 10,000 modules annually supports a wealth route.
- **Key unknown:** Patent territorial coverage, option price/encumbrances, certification path, and unknown-founder OEM sales cycle.

### 21. ClearCommand — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Maintenance crews in shutdowns need speech intelligibility without lifting hearing protection; adaptive filtering can collect acknowledgement/error outcomes.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. 3M/Peltor-class incumbents own certified hearing-protection channels and can bundle communication. Eight prototype units, compatibility, and certification are too many pre-proof dependencies.

### 22. ExoCell — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Paid work-cell trials can link exoskeleton fit to task/productivity outcomes before plant-wide purchase.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. The patent is co-owned by a corporate entity, the field may already be encumbered, global vendors compete, and manufacturing/support capital exceeds what one paid trial proves.

### 23. ThermalCell — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Fixed cleanroom/inspection stations can personalize microclimate without moving the room setpoint.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Spot cooling and garments are commodity substitutes; the Polish patent is narrow and the wealth path depends on labor-intensive multi-site deployments without sufficient exclusion.

### 24. BlindTrace — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Training-only responder tracking avoids live-safety authority while creating valuable gait/building drift data.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Enforceable rights appear Polish, mature inertial/UWB vendors can outperform, and 40 high-value sites with 20 monthly wearers is an ungrounded concentrated market.

### 25. MowaGate — retain as second-wave finalist

- **Insight:** Banks and large e-commerce operators can pass ordinary Polish voicebot tests while excluding people with atypical speech; each paid release test can acquire reusable rights to exact recordings and interaction outcomes.
- **Paid wedge:** 18–30k PLN prepaid release challenge with a directly recruited, compensated panel and explicit five-year commercial licences to the exact recordings/transcripts/outcomes.
- **Control:** Contracted rights to a Polish atypical-speech transaction corpus and a retained participant panel; the buyer receives only its verdict and a limited benchmark licence.
- **Hard-gate screen:** Provisional pass/pass. The first test fits within 23k PLN; 45 clients at 120k PLN annual revenue supports expected founder wealth.
- **Key unknown:** Buyer acceptance of retained pooled rights, recruitment/consent durability, and whether banks will buy from an unknown founder within 180 days.

### 26. HumanID Edge Pool — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Accessible identity verification needs cross-device edge-case regression before certification.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Biometric video creates substantially higher privacy/security liability, while FIDO labs and large vendors already control certification trust and can recruit cohorts.

### 27. ChemGlove Rail — close after funnel

- **Insight:** A task-gated dispenser can route the approved chemical-glove transaction and replacement event, aligning EHS and procurement value.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. Ansell and vending incumbents already own selection data, supply, cabinets, and buyer trust; the expected founder wealth case requires 20m PLN low-margin throughput and 70 installed plants.

### 28. GloveXR Release Lots — close after funnel

- **Insight:** Industrial hand-tracking releases often fail only under real gloves, dirt, light, occlusion, and two-handed work; paid release lots could own exact trace rights.
- **Funnel decision:** Close. The reachable buyer universe and 130k PLN annual account value are ungrounded, and platforms can collect internal traces or route around gesture tracking. It remains too close to a reference dataset/QA benchmark shape.

### 29. HarnessCycle Smart Fleet — close after funnel

- **Funnel decision:** Close. Certified PPE rental with inspection/logistics already exists, smart custody is easy to add, and the expected case requires 4,500 capital-intensive kits and outside financing.
