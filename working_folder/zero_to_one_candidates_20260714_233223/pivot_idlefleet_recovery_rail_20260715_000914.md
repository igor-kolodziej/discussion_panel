# Zero-to-One Pivot: IdleFleet Recovery Rail

Pivot generated from the exact GigClaim v1 candidate and its post-panel evaluator diagnosis. No score or validation claim is assigned.

## Preserved Insight

Fleet intermediaries aggregate repeated, individually small human–algorithm decision failures. An outcome-priced manual workflow can route each reason→evidence→human-review→economic-outcome event and build cross-platform decision intelligence without cooperation from the algorithm owner.

## Structural Change

Remove worker wage, bonus, and fare recovery from the business. The fleet is no longer asked to fund value that belongs to workers.

IdleFleet covers only **fleet-owned economic loss** from vehicles that cannot earn rental/management revenue because a worker account is restricted, onboarding evidence is rejected, a vehicle is not correctly attached to a replacement worker, or a review/reassignment stalls. The fleet pays for verified rental days restored or avoided, plus a low per-active-vehicle workflow fee. Valid worker claims remain outside the product and cannot be suppressed or traded against fleet value.

This materially changes the painful event, measured value, fee base, data scope, and routed transaction.

## Target Niche and Buyer

Polish ride-hail fleet and vehicle-rental operators with at least 100 managed vehicles, more than one platform, weekly rental/management billing, and a recurring queue of cars or replacement drivers waiting on platform account, document, or assignment decisions.

The buyer is the owner or head of fleet operations. The fleet owns the vehicle, bears idle days, and receives the recovered rental/management revenue.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

A 150–250 PLN/day vehicle produces no rental or fleet-management revenue while the current driver account is restricted or the replacement driver/vehicle assignment is unresolved. Staff reconstruct reasons from platform messages, worker screenshots, document status, and fleet portals, then decide whether to appeal, replace evidence, or reassign the asset.

- **Evidence preserved:** Public Bolt material establishes a Polish fleet-owner channel, worker/vehicle management, earnings data, and recommended fleet partners. The Platform Work Directive establishes human review and explanations for consequential account decisions.
- **Hypothesis:** A 300-vehicle fleet experiences at least 75 avoidable idle vehicle-days per month and will pay when the service restores or avoids at least 3 PLN for each 1 PLN of fee.
- **Willingness-to-pay design:** Charge 10% only on verified fleet-owned rental/management revenue restored, plus an expected 10 PLN per actively monitored vehicle per month after a successful pilot. Never charge a percentage of worker compensation. Total fees must remain below one-third of measured fleet-owned value.

## Value and Profit Wedge

Offer a 30-day **Idle Vehicle Recovery Audit** for 5,000 PLN prepaid, covering up to 300 vehicles, the current restriction/onboarding/reassignment backlog, and a 60-day historical baseline.

For each case, the service records the fleet-owned asset, current worker/assignment, platform, stated reason, missing evidence, chosen action, human-review response, reassignment/reactivation time, idle days, and verified fleet-owned rental/management revenue restored or avoided. A human approves all evidence and actions. The fleet or worker submits through ordinary support channels under the appropriate authorization.

The 5,000 PLN deposit is credited against a 10% success fee. The pilot converts only if verified restored/avoided fleet-owned value plus conservatively measured operations labor exceeds 15,000 PLN and at least one repeated reason/action pattern exists.

## Domination Wedge

Start with the narrow queue that ordinary fleet portals expose but do not optimize across platforms: “Which vehicle is economically idle because of a platform/worker assignment state, what evidence/action is missing, and what is the fastest lawful recovery or reassignment?”

Dominate Polish multi-platform fleets with 100–500 vehicles before expanding to delivery fleets. Do not add worker wage claims, broad payroll, generic fleet management, or legal representation.

## Acquisition Channel

Build a named list from public platform-recommended fleet pages, fleet/rental websites, vehicle recruitment advertisements, and business registries. Send the owner/operations head a synthetic 90-second “idle vehicle queue” replay and a fixed 5,000 PLN offer. The pitch is fleet-owned asset utilization, not worker-rights consulting.

One paid pilot from 60 qualified approaches and three independent paid fleets by day 180 is the acquisition proof. No platform, union, insurer, or payroll partnership is required.

## Primary Control Point

A signed **idle-asset routing mandate** requires the fleet to route covered platform restrictions, onboarding failures, and driver/vehicle reassignment events through IdleFleet during the contract term. The startup records both platform review and the fleet's final economic outcome, including asset reassignment and rental billing restart.

Contracts grant rights to retain pseudonymized or truly anonymized factual derivatives where lawful: reason category, evidence completeness, action, review outcome, idle duration, reassignment pattern, and fleet-owned value restored. Raw personal data remain segregated, purpose-limited, and deleted on schedule.

The asset becomes a cross-platform reason→action→review→vehicle-utilization graph. It is Tier 2 unless repeated mandates and outcome history create switching costs.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

**Resource needed:** recurring rights to platform-decision and vehicle-utilization outcomes that the fleet owns or can lawfully authorize.

**Bootstrap:** A paying fleet supplies its current idle/backlog queue, rental/management billing rate, permitted exports/messages, and a bounded worker authorization flow for cases requiring personal evidence. The product needs no platform API. Polish counsel reviews agency, privacy, worker fairness, and the exclusion of wage claims before sale.

**12-month proof under 100,000 PLN:** three recurring fleets, at least 500 monitored vehicles, 100 closed idle/reassignment cases, two platforms, a signed routing/data-rights clause, and verified value/restoration-time baselines.

## Expansion Path

1. Restriction/onboarding backlog and rental-day recovery.
2. Cross-platform driver/vehicle reassignment recommendations.
3. Evidence-completeness prompts before onboarding or document renewal.
4. Forecasting of idle-asset risk by platform, document, and assignment pattern.
5. Delivery fleets and selected CEE markets after Polish renewal economics work.

The future-lab asset is real-time HCI and behavioral/algorithmic outcome data: how platform explanations, worker evidence, human review, and fleet action change utilization. It is not a general fleet CRM.

## Minimum People Stack

- Founder: product, analytics, sales, and final workflow quality, 20–25 hours/week.
- Fixed-scope Polish privacy/labor/commercial counsel.
- Contract data engineer after paid proof for secure ingest and two platform formats.
- Multilingual reviewer funded from pilot revenue.

No cofounder, platform relationship, employee, vehicle inventory, or full-time response desk is needed for the first paid proof.

## Principal Risk

The fleet may already solve idle vehicles by rapidly replacing workers, so platform review is not the bottleneck and avoidable idle value may be too small. Installed fleet systems can also copy a queue/reassignment feature. The control point matters only if closed cross-platform outcomes materially reduce idle time and customers route the workflow repeatedly.

## First Disconfirming Test

Before product automation, sell one non-refundable 5,000 PLN audit. Require the fleet to disclose 60 prior days of vehicle availability/rental billing and the open platform-decision/reassignment queue. Kill the pivot if:

- the buyer will not separate fleet-owned value from worker earnings;
- fewer than 25 relevant cases or 75 avoidable idle days exist per 300 vehicles per month;
- restored/avoided fleet-owned value plus labor savings is below 15,000 PLN;
- the fleet refuses recurring event routing or lawful derivative outcome rights;
- staff can solve the queue with a routine fleet-system setting or one existing vendor feature.

Pre-paid-proof spending remains below 18,000 PLN; the first 180-day cumulative cap remains 30,000 PLN.

## Evidence Preserved

- [Directive (EU) 2024/2831](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L2831) supports explanation and human review of consequential platform account decisions and work-data portability.
- [Bolt Poland fleet page](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/fleet/) supports the existence of fleet operators, managed workers/assets, and real-time fleet information.
- [Bolt recommended Polish fleets](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/support/articles/360001424593/) supports an observable initial buyer list.
- The two independent GigClaim judges agreed that fleet aggregation and a manual paid test were useful, but the original worker-recovery payer mismatch was fatal.

## New Hypotheses

1. A 300-vehicle fleet suffers at least 75 avoidable idle vehicle-days monthly from platform account, onboarding, document, or reassignment states.
2. Average verified fleet-owned rental/management value is at least 150 PLN per recovered/avoided idle day.
3. A 10 PLN/vehicle/month fee plus 10% of fleet-owned recovered value is less than one-third of measured buyer value in the expected case.
4. Fleets can lawfully authorize the required case data without turning worker consent into a coerced condition.
5. The workflow remains fair to workers and does not incentivize suppressing worker rights or unsafe rapid reassignment.
6. Eighty CEE fleets averaging 400 monitored vehicles can support a credible expected founder-wealth path without service labor scaling one-for-one.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The original fleet paid to recover money that often belonged to workers and risked exposing its own errors. IdleFleet excludes worker compensation, measures only fleet-owned asset/revenue loss, and charges the entity receiving that recovered value. It replaces the all-worker-dispute mandate with an idle-asset and reassignment route; changes the case ontology and unit economics; and makes a rational buyer directly testable. It preserves only the aggregation and closed algorithmic-decision outcome insight.

## Pivot Priority

Return IdleFleet to `$evaluate-zero-to-one` first. It directly removes the two-judge fatal objection while preserving a reachable paid audit, an observable buyer list, and the cross-platform outcome asset. Its decisive new unknown—avoidable idle days per fleet vehicle—can be falsified in the first paid transaction.
