# Zero-to-One Pivot: MowaForge Acceptance Bounty

## Preserved Insight

A paid commercial test can finance the direct recruitment and fair compensation of people whose speech automated systems routinely misunderstand, while acquiring purpose-specific rights to the exact recordings, transcripts, and system outcomes. The valuable label is not word-error rate alone; it is whether a real conversational system recovered the user's intent, completed the journey, or failed after retries.

## Structural Change

MowaGate sold first to Polish banks and required the bank to authorize a test endpoint, approve a new high-risk-data vendor, and permit cross-client reuse of bank-specific outcomes. This pivot removes that permission chain.

The first buyer becomes a European voice-agent or ASR product vendor that is about to deliver a named enterprise release and owns the sandbox, model, logs, and release budget. MowaForge tests only vendor-owned demonstration or acceptance environments using MowaForge-owned generic intent packs. It retains no bank prompt, authentication design, customer record, or enterprise-specific outcome. The vendor grants permission to record only the response of its own system to the pre-agreed test pack.

The first offer is a fixed **failure-reproduction and remediation bounty**, not a general accessibility audit. The vendor pays to learn whether a previously untested speech cohort can complete its product's core conversational behaviours before a customer acceptance event. A bounded retest is included after the vendor changes the product.

## Target Niche and Buyer

The initial niche is independent European voice-agent, conversational-AI, and ASR vendors with roughly 10–200 employees that:

- expose a documented API or controlled demo environment;
- sell into contact centres, banking, insurance, healthcare administration, travel, utilities, or public services;
- have a named enterprise acceptance, renewal, or procurement deadline within 120 days;
- own the system output and can authorize testing without requesting rights from the enterprise customer;
- lack an internal recruited cohort of Polish or CEE speakers whom ordinary ASR frequently misunderstands.

The economic buyer is the founder, VP Product, Head of Quality, or enterprise-delivery lead whose contract revenue, launch date, or renewal is at risk. This is materially different from selling to a regulated bank's multi-function procurement organization.

**Hypothesis:** at least 60 European voice vendors meet these conditions in a reachable annual cohort, and at least 20 will buy a fresh challenge or annual rail after a concrete failure trace. This denominator is not yet sourced and must be researched before panel eligibility.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

The painful event is a named enterprise acceptance or renewal in which the vendor can lose several months of revenue, services margin, or reference value because the voice flow fails on real speakers outside its ordinary QA distribution. The vendor already bears the remediation cost and can change the model, prompting, fallback, or orchestration; the beneficiary and payer are the same company.

The first paid bounty is hypothesized at 12,000–20,000 PLN for 12 recruited speakers, 20 generic intents, one controlled endpoint, a reproducible failure bundle, and one remediation retest. An annual rail is hypothesized at 90,000–180,000 PLN for quarterly fresh cohorts, two languages, version-to-version regression, and hosted access to the vendor's own traces.

These prices are hypotheses. Paid proof is collected cash or a cash-backed purchase order. A free benchmark, grant, vendor API credit, or promise to introduce an enterprise customer does not count.

## Value and Profit Wedge

The first bounty includes:

1. Twelve directly recruited adults who self-report that mainstream voice systems often misunderstand them; no diagnosis is required.
2. A generic, MowaForge-owned intent pack such as dates, numbers, names, confirmations, corrections, interruptions, and multi-turn recovery. It contains no enterprise customer's confidential flow.
3. Recorded attempts against the vendor-authorized sandbox or API, with system responses and completion outcomes.
4. A reproducible bundle of the highest-value failures and a severity-ranked remediation backlog.
5. One retest on the vendor's revised version.
6. A participant agreement granting a revocable, purpose-specific five-year commercial licence to the exact recordings, transcripts, and evaluation outcomes. It never restricts the participant from making new recordings for others.
7. A vendor agreement authorizing MowaForge to retain the response of the vendor-owned system to the generic test pack for cross-version and aggregate cross-system evaluation. It excludes customer data, customer prompts, secrets, and production traffic.

Pilot direct-cost hypothesis: 2,400–3,600 PLN participant pay, 2,000 PLN recruiting and accessibility support, 1,500 PLN secure tooling, 2,000 PLN annotation/QA, and 1,500 PLN privacy/administration, for 9,400–10,600 PLN direct cost. At a 16,000 PLN sale, gross margin would be only 34–41%; the pilot is a paid asset-acquisition event, not the mature-margin product.

Mature annual-rail direct-cost hypothesis: 55,000–75,000 PLN per 150,000 PLN contract, or 50–63% gross margin after participant sessions, recruiting, annotation, hosting, privacy, and customer support. Automation can reduce repeated scoring and orchestration, but the model does not assume participant labour disappears.

## Domination Wedge

Start with atypical Polish speech against vendor-owned conversational flows immediately before enterprise acceptance. The narrow promise is: **find reproducible completion failures that synthetic and ordinary-speech regression missed, without asking the enterprise customer to share its confidential workflow.**

MowaForge does not claim certification or general accessibility compliance. It wins only if a small voice vendor can buy it faster than recruiting a lawful cohort internally and if the failure bundle changes a release decision.

The expected-success wealth hypothesis is 28 annual vendor accounts at 180,000 PLN average revenue plus 20 one-off bounties at 16,000 PLN: 5.36m PLN revenue, 58% blended gross margin, and 23% operating margin, or approximately 1.23m PLN EBIT. At 4.5× EBIT less 200,000 PLN debt, equity value would be about 5.34m PLN; at 90% founder ownership, founder equity would be about 4.81m PLN. At least 250,000 PLN of post-transition distributions would be required to exceed 5m PLN without double counting. This route is not yet credible because the account denominator, annual price, renewal, and margin are unproved; it is included so the evaluator can reject rather than hide the hard gate.

Conservative hypothesis: 12 annual accounts at 110,000 PLN plus 12 bounties at 15,000 PLN = 1.50m PLN revenue, 50% gross margin, 10% operating margin, and less than 1m PLN founder value. Strong hypothesis: 55 annual accounts at 220,000 PLN plus 40 bounties at 18,000 PLN = 12.82m PLN revenue, 65% gross margin, 30% operating margin; 25% dilution and 100% reinvestment would still support the wealth threshold. Conservative and expected employment-period cash would be 95% reinvested and optional distributions capped at 5%; strong would reinvest 100%.

## Acquisition Channel

The first acquisition mechanism is public failure-led technical outbound:

- enumerate European voice-agent and ASR vendors from product directories, launch announcements, API documentation, conference exhibitors, job postings, and public customer stories;
- select vendors with a recent Polish/CEE launch or named regulated-enterprise deployment;
- use a public demo or ordinary developer account only where terms permit, with consented seed-panel speech, to produce a redacted failure signature that reveals no reusable raw participant audio;
- send the product/quality leader a fixed 12,000–20,000 PLN bounty offer tied to the coming release, not a generic accessibility pitch;
- require a 50% prepayment before recruiting the full cohort or doing customer-specific work.

The target is 80 named vendors, 30 event-qualified accounts, six technical calls, two proposals, and one paid bounty by day 180. No bank introduction, advocacy-group endorsement, clinic, enterprise-customer permission, or platform partnership is required.

## Primary Control Point

The 12-month control target is a directly contracted longitudinal participant panel plus a cross-vendor failure graph created only from MowaForge-owned prompts and vendor-authorized sandbox outputs:

- at least 100 compensated participants with 80 active rights net of withdrawal;
- at least 4,000 exact attempts across five independently paying vendors;
- purpose-specific licences and a live revocation/deletion ledger;
- five vendor contracts expressly allowing retention of their sandbox responses to MowaForge's generic test pack for hosted evaluation;
- longitudinal version outcomes for at least three vendors;
- reusable orchestration, annotation, recovery, and deletion tooling.

This is contractually provable without an enterprise customer's data rights. It remains Tier 3-like until paid vendors accept retention and longitudinal results improve product economics. Hamming or another QA vendor can recruit a similar cohort; MowaForge must prove speed, participant retention, and unique cross-version outcomes before claiming defensibility.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Spend up to 7,500 PLN on privacy counsel, accessible participant terms, a DPIA outline, and secure deletion tests.
2. Recruit and compensate 12 seed participants directly through paid advertisements and permitted community listings; do not request medical records or institutional referrals.
3. Build one generic intent pack and test only public demos or developer sandboxes whose terms permit the activity.
4. Publish aggregate failure categories, never reusable raw audio or an unsupported compliance claim.
5. Sell the first bounty before expanding to 25 participants or adding a language.
6. Cap founder spend before paid proof at 18,000 PLN. Release the full first-delivery budget only after 50% prepayment.
7. Cap total founder POC spend at 85,000 PLN until two independent vendors have paid and one has purchased a retest or annual rail; never use family capital in this phase.

Why an unknown founder can attempt this: the first customer is a small product vendor with a documented endpoint and a near-term product event, not a bank requiring production data access. The founder can show a concrete, consented failure signature against the vendor's public or authorized environment. This reduces but does not eliminate trust and sales-cycle risk.

## Expansion Path

1. Polish atypical-speech bounty for one vendor-owned endpoint.
2. Quarterly regression rail for that vendor's versions and orchestration changes.
3. Additional CEE languages only after a vendor prepays cohort formation.
4. Speech-plus-interaction cohorts for accent, rate, interruption, motor-speech, and low-bandwidth conditions, with consent specific to each use.
5. API and CI release gate that replays rights-active recordings inside the hosted environment.
6. Procurement evidence for voice vendors selling into covered services, without claiming legal certification.

Every new language must be customer-funded. Expansion is killed if it recreates one-off recruitment projects without cross-version renewal or if generic synthetic testing catches the same failures.

## Minimum People Stack

- Founder: product, data, orchestration, scoring, buyer discovery, and controlled delivery.
- Fractional privacy counsel: initial data-role, consent, retention, and contract design plus bounded updates.
- Part-time participant-operations coordinator after the second paid vendor.
- Lived-experience reviewer paid for protocol review, not used as a status-based endorsement or acquisition dependency.
- Contract security review before raw audio volume or enterprise procurement grows.

The founder remains employed until at least six independent paying vendors or 720,000 PLN contracted annual revenue, four renewals, positive contribution economics for six months, a repeatable non-privileged acquisition channel, and 12 months of personal runway.

## Principal Risk

The pivot may still be a specialised QA service beside Hamming. Voice vendors may regard atypical human testing as a one-off usability task, refuse retained sandbox-response rights, or copy the cohort model using an accessibility-research contractor. The addressable vendor population and annual willingness to pay may be too small for the expected wealth case, even if the first bounty is reachable.

## First Disconfirming Test

Within 180 days and 18,000 PLN pre-proof spend:

- 80 named vendors and 30 event-qualified accounts;
- at least six calls with product/quality owners;
- at least two written fixed-price bounty proposals;
- one collected 50% deposit at a price of at least 12,000 PLN;
- contract language that permits retention of vendor sandbox responses to MowaForge-owned generic prompts;
- at least ten of twelve seed participants retain active exact-file evaluation rights after an accessible comprehension check.

Kill if no deposit is collected, if all qualified vendors classify the offer as ordinary QA already covered by Hamming or internal testing, if no vendor permits longitudinal hosted retention, or if normalized pilot direct cost exceeds 70% of price.

## Evidence Preserved

- European and Polish accessibility duties create relevance for accessible voice interactions but do not mandate this product.
- Atypical speech remains materially harder for mainstream recognition systems, and diverse speech data can improve performance.
- Hamming's Polish QA offer proves an active product category and is the strongest copy/distribution threat.
- GDPR consent withdrawal and special-category/biometric risks remain non-waivable; the asset must be reported net of withdrawals.
- Poland has only about 30 commercial banks, so banks cannot be the sole expected-success account pool.

## New Hypotheses

- Small and mid-sized voice vendors buy faster than banks and can authorize their own sandbox outputs without a second customer-rights chain.
- A near-term enterprise acceptance event creates 12,000–20,000 PLN bounty willingness and 90,000–180,000 PLN renewal willingness.
- At least 60 reachable European vendors need Polish/CEE atypical-speech coverage, and at least 28 can become annual accounts without heroic share.
- Vendor-owned generic test environments contain enough interaction fidelity for the results to affect enterprise acceptance.
- Five vendors will permit hosted retention of sandbox responses to MowaForge-owned prompts.
- The cross-version failure graph reduces later delivery cost or increases renewal enough to outrun Hamming's installed automation and trust.
- Mature gross margin can exceed 55% after fair participant pay, recruitment, withdrawals, privacy, security, human QA, and customer support.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The original shape needed a Polish bank to be the buyer, authorize a system endpoint, approve a high-risk new processor, and donate cross-client value from its proprietary transaction flow. This pivot sells to the system owner before its enterprise acceptance event, uses only the vendor's authorized sandbox plus MowaForge-owned prompts, removes bank/customer data, cuts pre-proof risk from 39,500 PLN to 18,000 PLN, and makes the first paid unit a failure-reproduction bounty rather than an annual bank compliance programme.

It does not claim to solve the remaining incumbent, retention, margin, or account-count objections. Those must be judged by a fresh evaluator before any panel.

## Prioritization

This is the only pivot recommended for re-evaluation because it directly removes the bank permission and procurement chain while preserving the paid-data-acquisition mechanism. If the evaluator still finds the business service-like, easily copied by Hamming, too small for founder wealth, or unable to prove durable vendor retention, the entire atypical-speech QA shape should be closed rather than cosmetically revised again.
