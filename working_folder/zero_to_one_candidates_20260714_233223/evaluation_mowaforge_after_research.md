# Zero-to-One Evaluation: MowaForge Acceptance Bounty — After Public Research

## Diagnosis

Public evidence confirms that voice-agent QA is costly, recurring, and valuable, and that disabled-user testing carries meaningful participant and research labor. It does not confirm MowaForge's exact commercial unit: no reviewed source establishes a 12,000–20,000 PLN deposit, 90,000–180,000 PLN annual renewal, or five-year hosted retention permission from a 10–200-person European voice vendor. The bounded census found 27 documented technical-fit names and 31 under looser filters, while the wealth case needs 28 annual customers; even the candidate's unsupported 60-account denominator implies 46.7% penetration. The candidate remains a reachable paid-proof experiment but not a credible domination or founder-wealth path, so it should be parked under the present public-only restriction.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Sourced public evidence

- The bounded [named census](03_research_and_sources.md#mowaforge-public-only-decision-follow-up--2026-07-15) contains 27 companies with public evidence of approximate 10–200-person size, European operations, a voice-agent or ASR product, a controllable API/demo/platform, multilingual or CEE relevance, and enterprise use. Four near-fits create a 31-name loose pool.
- Public sources prove the candidate's decisive current-event filters for zero names: an enterprise acceptance or renewal within 120 days, an external human-QA budget, absence of an internal cohort, and willingness to permit longitudinal hosted retention are not normally disclosed.
- [Hamming](https://hamming.ai/faqs) offers startup/SMB and enterprise voice-QA pricing based mainly on test volume, but publishes no amounts. Its [Podium case](https://hamming.ai/case-studies/podium) reports 90% less manual testing time and 5,000+ monthly scenarios; its [Lilac Labs case](https://hamming.ai/blog/lilac-labs-customer-spotlight) claims 5,200 annual hours and $520,000 run-rate cost savings. These are vendor-published category evidence, not MowaForge willingness-to-pay evidence.
- [Coval](https://www.coval.ai/pricing) publishes $100/month Starter, $500/month Growth, and Enterprise from $4,500/month. At the [14 July 2026 NBP rate](https://rss.nbp.pl/kursy/TabRss.aspx?n=2026%2Fa%2F26a134), the enterprise floor is about 205,600 PLN/year. This makes 180,000 PLN plausible for a complete regulated-enterprise QA platform, not proven for MowaForge's narrower quarterly human-cohort service.
- A public [Test Partners accessibility-testing rate card](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/709130/488081854770098-pricing-document-2024-05-07-1155.pdf) prices eight participant tests at £5,000 low-cost or £9,280 standard including recruitment and incentives, roughly 25,500–47,300 PLN at the same NBP rate. The [Digital Accessibility Centre](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/93161/554089284035979-pricing-document-2025-04-17-0933.pdf) quotes £1,350/day for seven disabled testers plus technical review. The geography and scope differ, but MowaForge's 9,400–10,600 PLN direct-cost assumption for twelve participants, research, annotation, privacy and a retest is aggressive rather than conservative.
- Public accessibility procurements prove that large institutions hold material budgets, including [British Council](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/010916-2026/PDF), [ICO](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/030763-2024/PDF), [DVSA](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/006818-2026/PDF), and [Ordnance Survey](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/010165-2025). They do not establish budgets at small voice vendors.
- No public example was found of a voice/ASR vendor granting an outside QA supplier five-year retention of its sandbox responses for a cross-vendor graph. Enterprise suppliers instead advertise controls such as [Gladia zero data retention](https://www.gladia.io/changelog), Coval's 30/90-day or custom trace retention, and Hamming's security, redaction and residency controls.

### Candidate claims still treated as hypotheses

- One event-qualified vendor will pay a 50% deposit on a bounty priced at least 12,000 PLN within 180 days.
- The same buyer type will renew at 90,000–180,000 PLN for quarterly human-cohort regression rather than buy Hamming/Coval, use internal QA, or commission one-off accessibility research.
- At least 60 vendors meet every filter in a reachable annual cohort.
- Five vendors will permit hosted longitudinal retention of their responses to generic prompts.
- Mature direct costs are 55,000–75,000 PLN on a 150,000 PLN annual contract and the company can sustain 23% operating margin at 28 annual accounts.

### Evidence not supplied by public research

No deposit, purchase order, customer renewal, signed retention clause, measured Polish delivery cost, named 112–187-account eligible pool, CAC, sales-cycle distribution, or contribution-margin observation exists. Public research is therefore inconclusive on the transaction gates and cannot be treated as validation.

## Overall Score

Weights sum to exactly **20**.

Weighted points total **91.5**. Weighted average is `91.5 / 20 = 4.575`.

Overall score: `10 × 4.575 = 45.75`, rounded to **46/100**.

The score is advisory. The independent Founder Wealth Gate fails, so the candidate cannot receive a `pursue` recommendation.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 as written.** Direct participant contracts and vendor-authorized generic sandbox traces are structurally cleaner than enterprise-customer data. They are not yet an owned control point because no vendor retention clause exists, participant rights remain revocable, and the public enterprise norm emphasizes deletion, no training and limited or custom retention.

The strongest copy risk is Hamming or an incumbent voice vendor adding an accessibility-research panel while preserving its existing integrations, automation, production replay, security posture and buyer relationships. One panel-supplier contract or routine participant-recruitment process is sufficient unless MowaForge proves a speed, exclusive-access, or cross-version outcome advantage that compounds faster than the incumbent can copy.

Tier 2 would require paid contracts from several independent vendors granting the precise retention right, active participant rights net of withdrawal, and repeated evidence that longitudinal human failures improve renewal economics beyond incumbent QA.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The expected arithmetic is mechanically coherent but the denominator is now less credible.

- `28 × 180,000 PLN + 20 × 16,000 PLN = 5.36m PLN` revenue.
- At 23% operating margin, EBIT is about 1.233m PLN; 4.5× EBIT gives about 5.548m PLN enterprise value.
- Less 200,000 PLN debt gives about 5.348m PLN equity; 90% founder ownership gives about 4.813m PLN founder equity.
- The candidate needs another 250,000 PLN of net post-transition founder distributions to barely exceed 5m PLN, without modelling tax, timing or the effect of removing cash from company value.

The documented census has 27 technical-fit companies and the loose pool has 31. Twenty-eight annual accounts equal 103.7% and 90.3% of those pools respectively. Even the candidate's unproved 60-account pool requires 46.7% penetration. A less heroic 25%, 20% or 15% share requires 112, 140 or 187 eligible annual accounts; none is publicly established.

Public pricing shows that 180,000 PLN is conceivable for a full enterprise QA platform, but not that small voice vendors will spend it on this narrower service. Public participant-testing prices also suggest direct cost and labor are understated. At 28 accounts, at least 112 quarterly cycles plus 20 bounties imply at least 1,584 participant sessions before retests and new languages, while consent, withdrawals, support, endpoint setup, annotation, reproduction and customer communication remain human work.

The conservative and strong scenarios also remain incomplete on timing, capital, liabilities, valuation/distributions and operating throughput. The founder-wealth path therefore depends on heroic penetration, unproved renewal pricing, fragile value arithmetic and unmodelled service labor.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly and provisionally.** One paid bounty is still a bounded attempt rather than a multi-institution breakthrough:

- Pre-proof founder exposure is capped at 18,000 PLN, below 40,000 PLN.
- The founder can remain employed and does not need a bank, clinic, advocacy group, enterprise customer's production data, or platform partnership.
- The buyer controls its own sandbox; twelve participants and privacy counsel are purchasable inputs.
- Proof is collected cash: a 50% deposit on at least 12,000 PLN, not a free benchmark or introduction.

Public research weakens distribution confidence: only 27 documented technical fits and 31 loose fits were found, versus the candidate's target of 80 named vendors and 30 event-qualified accounts. It also cannot establish that one of them currently has the deadline, budget and retention authority required for the offer. Nevertheless, one deposit from a bounded set remains feasible to attempt within 180 days; it does not require the 28-account wealth case or full control point first.

Passing this gate means the experiment is reachable, not that public research supplied a paid signal. Under the current no-outreach restriction, the gate cannot advance beyond provisional status.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is coherent: Polish atypical-speech completion testing against vendor-controlled generic flows. Expansion to quarterly regression and additional CEE languages depends on paid renewal and customer-funded cohorts, neither of which public evidence establishes.

Competitors expand inward more easily. Hamming already covers Polish, multilingual simulation, accents, interruptions, production replay, monitoring and enterprise controls. Several census vendors have their own ASR, QA, testing or open-integration capabilities and can add a human cohort or research supplier. Poland offers recruitment and language advantages but not a protected transaction channel.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The wealth denominator fails public scrutiny.** Twenty-eight wins exceed the documented pool and consume 90.3% of the looser pool; a non-heroic 112–187-account denominator was not found.
2. **Exact willingness to pay is absent.** Category budgets and full-platform prices do not prove a 12,000 PLN bounty or 90,000–180,000 PLN renewal for this service.
3. **Retention is a negotiated exception, not a public norm.** No vendor example grants the proposed five-year cross-version right, while visible enterprise controls favor deletion and restricted use.
4. **Hamming owns the broader QA workflow.** It can add a participant cohort without surrendering integrations, monitoring, security, automation or customer trust.
5. **Service labor is likely understated.** Public disabled-user testing prices and the implied session volume challenge both pilot direct cost and mature operating margin.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Selling a fixed pre-acceptance failure bounty to the system owner removes the bank/customer permission chain and can use commercial revenue to compensate participants and acquire purpose-specific evaluation rights. That remains the strongest insight, even though public research does not support the annual business or control point.

## Recommendation

**research further**

Public research has reached its limit and remains inconclusive on the decisive transaction facts. Under the current public-only/no-outreach restriction, the operational disposition is **park**: do not panel, build the annual platform, recruit the full cohort, or spend beyond the bounded proof budget. Reconsider only if direct market evidence is later authorized and produces a qualifying deposit, renewal-budget evidence, a signed retention clause, measured delivery economics, and a materially larger eligible account denominator.
