# Baseline Reversible Economic Directions

Sixteen unscored directions derived after raw freeze. Historical fingerprints are comparison evidence only.

## DIR-001 — AI transparency release controls

- Customer / loss: AI content platforms, clinics, and agencies incur rework, delay, complaints, or exposure when generated or interactive content is released without the required mark or disclosure.
- Payer / budget: Platform product or compliance, clinic digital operations, or agency production and brand-account budgets.
- Paid trigger: A production marking integration, a live chatbot baseline scan, or clearance of an imminent synthetic-media campaign.
- Transaction owner: Product operations lead, clinic digital transformation manager, or campaign producer, with the relevant provider, deployer, agency, or brand retaining responsibility.
- Acquisition route: Direct outreach around EU releases plus referrals from asset-management integrators, medical-software implementers, and production studios.
- Compounding asset: Cross-format marking compatibility, multilingual disclosure tests, campaign precedents, and observed release failure modes.
- Raw members: `RAW-001`, `RAW-002`, `RAW-003`
- Nearest history: `HIST-018`, `HIST-025`, `HIST-027`
- Historical comparison: Shares compliance gating and media-distribution evidence primitives, while the present variants attach payment to different production or deployment events.
- Preserved disagreements: Payer and paid trigger differ among platform integration, clinic journey monitoring, and fixed-fee campaign clearance.; Transaction owners and routes remain distinct: product operations via asset workflows, clinic operations via health-software partners, and campaign producers via studios.
