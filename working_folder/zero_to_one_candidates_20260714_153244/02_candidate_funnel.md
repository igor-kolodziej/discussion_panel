## Finalist selection rationale

The four finalists are meaningfully distinct: full-stack biosignal release QA, electromechanical neurotech test hardware, real-time skilled-motion augmentation, and connection-level field-work verification. Each has a specific paid event, a public acquisition list, a sub-40,000 PLN paid-proof design, a route to a directly owned hardware/data/software asset within 12 months, and a productized scale path. None requires a clinic, research partner, manufacturer partnership, accreditation, or completed regulated product before paid proof.

Robot Skill Capture Foundry remains documented as an alternate because its strongest asset depends on customer access and derivative-data clauses; it will not advance unless the four direct-control shapes fail and the advisory evaluator finds a credible non-service path.
