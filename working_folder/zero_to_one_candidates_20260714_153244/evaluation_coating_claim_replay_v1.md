# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Coating Claim Replay

## Diagnosis

The pivot improves urgency but replaces a weak recurring product with a trust-heavy claims service. Coating suppliers already possess technical-service teams and accredited or familiar labs; an unknown founder must win authority exactly when a disputed claim makes buyers risk-averse. The control point remains prospective derivative data and case routing, and no founder-wealth scenarios are provided. This family should be closed.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence preserved: outcome-linked process capture is technically plausible. New hypotheses: suppliers have unserved paid claims, accept a non-accredited unknown vendor, pay 15,000–30,000 PLN, allow materials/data access, and buy repeat case packs. Missing: claim frequency, internal alternatives, liability, CAC, margins, customer counts, and all required wealth scenarios.

## Control Point

Tier 3. The proposed protocol and data are owned but not scarce; case flow is not contracted. Strongest copy risk: the supplier's own technical team or an incumbent lab performs the replay with existing trust.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Fail. Required scenarios, ownership, reinvestment, value/distribution, and timing are absent; the operating shape is episodic expert service.

## Reachability Gate

Fail. Hardware can fit under 40,000 PLN, but live claim access, supplier authority, materials, and non-accredited trust must all resolve before payment. This is not a controlled 180-day route for an unknown part-time founder.

## Expansion Check

Expansion adds chemical expertise and trust burden. Suppliers/labs move inward easily. Poland provides no durable barrier.

## Fatal Objections

1. Trust/authority is most difficult at the disputed event.
2. Internal technical service and labs are direct substitutes.
3. Episodic case work does not show durable recurring economics.
4. Wealth gate is unmodeled and implausible in service form.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

None beyond the general value of outcome-linked process data.
