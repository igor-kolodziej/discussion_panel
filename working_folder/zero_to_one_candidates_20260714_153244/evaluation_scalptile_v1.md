# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ScalpTile Calibration Consumables

## Diagnosis

ScalpTile materially improves the earlier phantom economics by creating a recurring, serialized consumable, and the first technical proof is cheap. However, no evidence shows dry-contact teams consume hundreds of tiles annually, accept non-accredited calibration, or cannot reproduce suitable material internally. The founder lacks materials/manufacturing fit, FTO is unknown, and the conservative wealth case requires 80 accounts each buying 450 tiles. The control point is potentially Tier 1 but unproven technically and commercially; the candidate should be closed rather than panelled.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: dry-contact mechanics matter; low-cost conductive phantoms are buildable; Fraunhofer/LabSim occupy adjacent space. Hypotheses: stable lot formulation, 180–420 PLN unit price, 450+ annual tile use, 65–78% margin, repeat ordering, non-accredited acceptance, protectable recipe, 80 global accounts. No reviewer verdict.

## Overall Score

79/100. Weighted total 157 across total weight 20: `10 × (157 / 20) = 78.5`, rounded to 79.

## Control Point

Tier 1 if a stable proprietary formulation and calibration are achieved; Tier 3 until then. Strongest copy risk: an electrode maker or materials/test vendor uses published phantom methods and commodity elastomers/printing to make its own reference.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Pass arithmetically, fail on confidence. Conservative founder equity is modeled above 12m, but only because 80 accounts buy 450 tiles annually at 250 PLN. That usage is unsubstantiated and may be heroic in a small niche. Expected/strong cases amplify the same unknown.

## Reachability Gate

Pass. A discriminating lot and 12,000–18,000 PLN preorder can fit 30,000 PLN and 180 days with a contractor; no clinic/partner is needed. FTO and formulation repeatability are controllable early checks.

## Expansion Check

Adjacencies are real but each material/interface may need new formulation and calibration. Fraunhofer, academic methods, materials firms, and internal QA can enter. Poland is not a defensible demand advantage.

## Fatal Objections

1. Consumable usage and repeat order volume are invented, not evidenced.
2. Stable formulation/calibration and FTO may require more material expertise/capital than modeled.
3. Buyers may DIY or accept ordinary phantoms rather than adopt a paid reference.
4. The founder-wealth pass rests on a small global niche with aggressive per-account volume.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Replaceable, serialized contact-interface references could turn phantom testing from a one-off apparatus into recurring QA.

## Recommendation

kill
