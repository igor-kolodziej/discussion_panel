# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Haptic Accessibility Module OEM

## Diagnosis

The pivot correctly turns the incumbent integrator into the buyer, but the economic event is too rare and the module too reproducible to support a company. The buyer can build a COTS-band interface during the project, demand work-for-hire, or avoid annual/per-cell fees; no wealth scenarios or integrator project-frequency evidence are supplied. A prepaid named project is technically reachable, but distribution depends on exactly the speculative channel acceptance the rules reject. The entire accessible-workcell shape should be closed.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: PFRON budget and public integrator lists. Hypotheses: live accessibility requests are frequent, module prepays at 12,000–20,000 PLN, per-cell licensing is acceptable, IP is retained, and two integrators reuse adapters. Missing: project frequency, install base, margins, CAC, retention, full wealth scenarios, and licensing precedent.

## Overall Score

50/100. Weighted total 99.5 across weight 20: `10 × (99.5 / 20) = 49.75`, rounded to 50.

## Control Point

Tier 3. Firmware/protocol is owned, but per-cell licensing and adapter rights are speculative; integrators can reproduce the module. Strongest copy risk is internal build/work-for-hire.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Fail. No required scenarios are supplied; rare projects and low module pricing cannot conservatively support 10m PLN founder wealth.

## Reachability Gate

Fail. Prototype cost is below 40,000 PLN, but a named accessibility project at an integrator, prepaid order, and retained IP must all align within 180 days. That is an uncontrolled channel dependency rather than direct proof.

## Expansion Check

Broader industrial haptics is crowded by wearable/alarm vendors and integrators. PFRON is local momentum but does not create high-frequency projects.

## Fatal Objections

1. Too few live projects for an OEM/licensing business.
2. Integrator can internalize or demand work-for-hire.
3. No founder-wealth path.
4. Paid proof depends on speculative channel timing.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

None with sufficient company-scale economics.

## Recommendation

kill
