# Zero-to-One Pivot: Coating Claim Replay

## Preserved Insight

Measured production outcome joined to technique and process history can identify causes that post-hoc visual inspection misses.

## Structural Change

Change the buyer from the coating shop seeking recurring coaching to an industrial-coating manufacturer or distributor facing an expensive customer complaint about adhesion, thickness, finish, or material consumption. Change value capture from monthly wearable coaching to a prepaid portable failure-replay case that separates application, preparation, material, and environment and creates a standardized claim record.

## Target Niche and Buyer

Regional industrial coating manufacturers/distributors serving small metal fabricators and processing recurring field complaints without a local applications lab. Buyer: technical service or quality manager after a customer claim above 20,000 PLN.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

A customer blames the coating while the supplier suspects preparation or application. The supplier pays 15,000–30,000 PLN to reproduce the failure and preserve a customer/account before issuing credit or replacing material. Price and frequency are hypotheses.

## Value and Profit Wedge

A portable kit captures approved gun motion, environmental conditions, preparation evidence, coating batch, settings, and ordinary thickness/adhesion outcomes during a controlled replay. The deliverable is a machine-readable root-cause comparison and reusable claim profile, not operator surveillance or certification.

## Domination Wedge

Become the standardized claims-replay workflow for one coating chemistry and regional distributor segment, with prepaid case packs and a growing material–application–failure corpus.

## Acquisition Channel

Public manufacturer/distributor lists, technical-service job posts, complaint/help pages, and trade directories. Offer one paid claim replay; no exclusive supplier partnership is assumed.

## Primary Control Point

Owned replay protocol, portable kit, batch/application/failure taxonomy, and contracted non-identifying claim derivatives. A prepaid annual case pack routes claim transaction flow through the company.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Build the portable environmental/motion/thickness capture for under 20,000 PLN. Require a paid case/deposit before travel or coating-specific work. Buyer supplies coating, substrate, and complaint context. Proof is two prepaid cases and one repeated supplier within 12 months.

## Expansion Path

Expand from one chemistry to adjacent coatings, distributor case packs, warranty-risk benchmarking, and applications-lab software. Avoid certification or legal expert claims.

## Minimum People Stack

Founder, contract coating chemist/applications engineer, and fixed-scope legal/safety review.

## Principal Risk

Suppliers already handle claims internally or through accredited labs; an unknown founder lacks credibility, and one-off disputes cannot support retention.

## First Disconfirming Test

From 40 qualified regional suppliers, obtain one prepaid case tied to a live claim. Kill if buyers require accreditation, legal-expert authority, or an incumbent lab relationship before payment.

## Evidence Preserved

Outcome-linked process data can expose failures; public industrial directories exist; the panel proved recurring shop coaching is weak.

## New Hypotheses

Live claims are frequent and costly; suppliers lack adequate internal replay; a 15,000+ PLN case is purchasable in 180 days; the founder can access claim materials; generic derivatives are permitted; case packs recur.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The buyer, urgent event, value capture, and workflow change. Payment comes from an active supplier claim, not a speculative recurring worker-coaching subscription. The pivot still fails if authority/trust or internal lab substitution blocks payment.

## Priority

This is the only coherent structural pivot because it preserves outcome-linked process data while moving to a budgeted loss event. It should be killed if independent replay cannot be purchased without accreditation or supplier trust.
