# Round 36 Candidate Set: Forced-Liquidity Title Lots

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Pivot

Recent real gates failed because:

- software/app targets with clean revenue are scarce or efficiently priced;
- client-book succession becomes labor-heavy services;
- connector succession has strong control design but target supply is thin.

Round 36 moves to forced-liquidity physical assets where the seller's alternative is storage cost, disposal, write-off, or blocked space. The founder must control title/physical custody and pre-sell or partially pre-sell the lot before meaningful cash leaves the business.

## Candidate 1: Abandoned Industrial MRO Title Receiver

### Buyer / Customer

Supply side:

- 3PL warehouses;
- industrial distributors;
- machine builders;
- plant maintenance clear-outs;
- insolvency-adjacent SMEs;
- small importers with unpaid/abandoned non-consumer industrial stock.

Demand side:

- factory maintenance teams;
- small MRO resellers;
- machine shops;
- automation/electrical/mechanical repair firms;
- used industrial-spares buyers.

### Acute Trigger

Warehouses and distributors get stuck with abandoned, unpaid, returned, or ownerless-feeling non-consumer MRO stock: bearings, belts, sensors, valves, pneumatic components, motors, contactors, cables, filters, fasteners, machine feet, safety switches, tooling, industrial consumables, and spare parts. Storage space is blocked, old customers are insolvent/unresponsive, and the lot is too small/messy for formal liquidators but too valuable to throw away.

### Primary Control Point

The founder does not broker generic surplus. The founder signs a title/settlement/release packet and takes physical custody or paid buyer-backed option over a named lot.

Control exists only if secured:

- written seller authority: owner title transfer, liquidator assignment, warehouse-authorized sale path, or explicit abandoned-stock settlement where legally clean;
- itemized lot manifest with photos, serial/SKU/brand, quantity, visible condition, and exclusions;
- physical custody or warehouse release order;
- no regulated consumer goods, food, medical/patient-use goods, chemicals, batteries, pressure equipment, safety-critical products needing certification, or counterfeit-risk branded retail goods unless specialist clearance exists;
- buyer deposits or signed purchase commitments for at least 30% of expected resale value before final cash outlay;
- first-refusal/right-to-clear future abandoned MRO lots from the same warehouse/distributor.

The control point is title plus physical release of a specific hidden lot, not a marketplace.

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- contact 120 warehouses, 3PLs, small distributors, machine builders, and industrial clear-out sellers;
- inspect 10 candidate lots and reject legally unclear or regulated lots;
- sign 2 first-look/lot-clearance agreements;
- secure one legally clean MRO lot with estimated resale value 40,000-120,000 PLN for under 25,000 PLN cash outlay or consignment/settlement terms;
- collect at least 15,000 PLN buyer deposits or paid reservations before removing the lot;
- physically custody or release the lot;
- sell enough within 30 days to recover cash outlay and show 40%+ gross margin after pickup, sorting, testing/photo labor, storage, platform fees, buyer refunds, and seller settlement;
- document why the lot was not publicly available to incumbents before the founder locked it.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Outbound script to warehouses/distributors:

"Do you have unpaid/abandoned industrial spare parts or MRO stock taking space? I buy or clear only legally transferable non-regulated industrial lots. I photograph, remove, and settle fast. No consumer returns, no chemicals, no medical, no food."

Demand mechanism:

- pre-sell manifest lines to 20-50 MRO resellers/repair firms/factories before pickup;
- focus on mixed lots with known industrial brands and universal components;
- sell through direct buyer list first, then Allegro/OLX/industry forums only after the best lines are locked.

### Gross Margin And Payback Logic

- Forced-liquidity sellers value space and certainty, not top resale price.
- One good lot can produce 20,000-60,000 PLN gross profit if bought at 10-30% of resale value and partially pre-sold.
- Labor is sorting/photo/testing, not recurring service delivery.
- Downside is capped by pre-sale deposits and narrow lot exclusions.

### Copy Risk

Liquidators and surplus dealers exist, but they cannot copy a specific signed hidden lot once the founder has title/release and deposits. Big incumbents often ignore small messy MRO lots because discovery, sorting, and legal clarity are annoying relative to ticket size.

### Why It Is Not A Generic Resale Business

Generic resale buys public inventory and lists it. This model starts with legally controlled hidden MRO lots under forced-liquidity conditions, uses buyer deposits before cash outlay, and secures first-look rights with warehouses/distributors.

### Kill Criteria

- No legally clean first-look/title path within 45 days.
- Fewer than 2 warehouses/distributors willing to show lots.
- Buyer deposits below 15,000 PLN before removal.
- Most lots are regulated, counterfeit-risk, consumer returns, or trash.
- Gross margin below 40% after labor/storage/refunds.
- Incumbent liquidators can buy the same lots before the founder locks them.

## Candidate 2: Failed Installer Warranty-Spare Lot Receiver

Acquire legally clean spare-parts lots from failed/retiring PV, heat-pump, HVAC, gate/automation, or alarm installers only if service firms prepay for named parts. Reject customer-service obligations and warranty takeover.

## Candidate 3: Machine-Builder Orphan Component Lot Buyout

Acquire unused component lots from small machine builders after canceled projects: pneumatics, sensors, rails, motors, cabinets, cables, and controls. Pre-sell to other builders/repair shops before pickup.

## Candidate 4: Distributor Deadstock First-Look Clearing Desk

Sign recurring first-look rights with small industrial distributors for aged SKUs. Requires lot-level title and buyer deposits. Reject if it is just normal discount purchasing.

## Candidate 5: Plant Closure Maintenance Cage Receiver

Acquire maintenance-cage inventory from closing plants under title/clearance agreement. Higher gross potential but more legal and access complexity.

## Candidate 6: De-Branded B2B Return Lot Receiver

Acquire non-consumer B2B return/overstock lots from 3PLs after de-branding or seller release. Reject if brand/trademark, consumer-safety, or product-liability risk is high.

## Gate Rule For Scorers

Apply the existing persistent-loop caps:

- cap below `82` if no concrete 2-month control proof;
- cap below `85` if copyable by one vendor contract, equipment purchase, or internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, owner earnings, target sourcing, legal title, or buyer deposits are not credible;
- require simulated score strictly `>87` to reach the real Zero To One browser gate.
