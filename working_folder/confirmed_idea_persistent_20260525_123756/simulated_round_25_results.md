## Fatal Pattern Extracted

Legal-use assets improved the score ceiling but still did not create enough asymmetry. The strongest ideas failed because:

- the legal-use asset can be recreated by another vendor contract or supplier relationship;
- an incumbent distributor, plant, broker, or operator can bypass the founder after discovery;
- the asset is valuable per transaction but does not obviously compound;
- economics are plausible but not specific enough: margin, deposit, sales cycle, CAC, working capital, and owner earnings remain unclear;
- regulated company acquisition creates liability and operations, not a clean startup control point.

## Next Pivot

Round 26 should target **custody of buyer-side payment/data/admin accounts** where the founder can sit inside the transaction flow, not outside as a broker:

- delegated merchant/account/admin custody;
- escrow-like payment routing or lockbox with immediate cash-control value;
- signed buyer-side mandate that prevents direct bypass;
- audit/log/data asset that compounds because the founder controls the operating account over time.

Avoid KSeF, DORA, GPSR/RED, CBAM, routebooks, public auctions, generic claims, and export rights unless payment custody changes the business shape.
