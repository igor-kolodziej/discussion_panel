## Fatal Pattern Extracted

Round 22 was still too close to brokerage. The candidates often had a plausible legal or registry asset, but the founder did not yet control a cash-realizing transaction path that was both exclusive and hard to copy.

Repeated objections:

- assignment/control was legally uncertain or only mandate-based;
- buyer payment depended on a downstream third party saying yes later;
- incumbents could copy with a small internal workflow change;
- cash conversion would be slow or litigation-adjacent;
- gross margin assumptions depended on spreads that may be thin after acquisition, legal, registry, admin, and trust costs.

## Next Pivot

Round 23 should start from titled, physical, or already-acknowledged economic assets:

- damaged cargo title plus assigned CMR/insurance claim;
- construction retention receivables with debtor acknowledgment or guarantee release path;
- hail-damaged PV hardware title plus resale/salvage cash;
- municipal/towed vehicle title;
- returnable packaging balance claims plus physical recovered inventory;
- `.pl` domain options only where the domain is generic and buyer demand is pre-validated.

The bar remains unchanged: do not advance anything to real Zero To One unless simulated score is strictly `>87`.
