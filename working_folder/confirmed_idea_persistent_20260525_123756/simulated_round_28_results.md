## Fatal Pattern Extracted

The DPR/EDPR market is real, but that is also the problem. Existing players already buy, sell, and broker the documents. A startup cannot score above the 80s unless it controls something deeper than a document stream:

- legally enforceable recipient designation in BDO before issuance;
- exclusive supply from a fragmented recycler segment incumbents cannot reach;
- buyer-side payment custody or compliance planning embedded before shortfall appears;
- verified economics per Mg after legal, admin, default, and failed-document risk.

## Next Pivot

Stop searching adjacent regulated paper markets. The next move should be a **hard adversarial reset**:

- ask only for ideas where the founder can own the choke point before any incumbent sees the demand;
- require the choke point to be unavailable through public marketplace/search/registry;
- require first cash from the buyer before supplier identity is revealed;
- reject anything where the dominant incumbent already has both sides of the transaction.
