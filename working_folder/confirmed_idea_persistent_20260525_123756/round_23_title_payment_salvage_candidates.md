# Round 23: Title, Payment-Flow, And Salvage-Control Candidates

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 22 failed because too many ideas were brokerage or mandate businesses: valuable if executed, but not structurally hard enough to copy. Round 23 therefore starts from assets where the founder can secure titled property, signed payment flow, acknowledged receivables, or physical salvage value inside 60 days.

Filter:

- reject brokerage unless the asset is already signed, titled, or physically controlled;
- reject claims unless assignment is legally clean and first cash is plausible without litigation;
- reject low-margin physical arbitrage unless one stop can produce meaningful gross profit;
- prefer assets where incumbents cannot copy because the specific lot, claim, account, route, or title is already locked.

## Source Anchors

- CMR complaints may be pursued by a person with assignment/authorization from the entitled party, but strict notice and limitation rules apply: https://gologis.pl/en/customer-site/complaints/
- CMR claim practice is documentation-heavy and deadline-sensitive; carriers often apply CMR limits and salvage-value deductions: https://gettransport.com/articles/claims-management-freight-damage-poland
- Polish construction warranty retentions are common, but `kaucja gwarancyjna` is not separately defined in the Civil Code and is usually contract-shaped: https://www.gardocki.pl/gwarancje-i-kaucje-w-kontraktach-budowlanych-kluczowe-klauzule-dla-firm/
- Construction retention receivables can be relevant even in insolvency because the contractor may receive funds from sale of the receivable, while the investor still keeps security for warranty claims: https://czasopisma.beck.pl/pl/czasopisma/mop/archiwum/2012/5/upadlosc-likwidacyjna-wykonawcy-robot-budowlanych-a-obowiazek-zwrotu-kaucji-gwarancyjnej
- PV modules are treated as electrical/electronic waste in Poland; disposal/recycling must use compliant paths, and business recyclers already offer B2B collection and processing: https://www.rawicom.pl/dla-firm/recykling/
- Used PV modules can retain meaningful performance if properly tested, but salvage value depends on condition, handling, and electrical/safety assessment: https://www.energy.gov/eere/solar/articles/salvage-values-determines-reliability-used-photovoltaics
- Warsaw ZDM explains that unclaimed towed vehicles can pass to the city after 6 months and may be sold by tender/auction: https://zdm.waw.pl/faq-sprzedaz-odholowanych-samochodow-pytania-i-odpowiedzi/
- Polish Road Traffic Law Article 50a says an unclaimed removed vehicle becomes municipal property by law after the statutory period: https://sip.lex.pl/akty-prawne/dzu-dziennik-ustaw/prawo-o-ruchu-drogowym-16798732/art-50-a
- B2B returnable packaging systems often use ownership, rental, deposits, balances, and return rules; examples show packaging can remain the operator's property and be tracked/reconciled: https://www.allgreenpallets.com/en/
- Industrial returnable packaging rules can define property, deposits, damage, and customer return obligations for pallets/containers: https://odlew.com.pl/en/company/documents-for-suppliers-and-customers/rules-for-trading-in-returnable-packaging-of-prec-odlew-sp-z-o-o/
- NASK provides an official `.pl` option agreement granting priority to register a domain name if the current domain is deleted: https://www.dns.pl/en/regulations_of_option_for_registration_a_domain_name

## Initial Read Before Scoring

The two candidates most likely to survive the stricter filter are:

1. **Returnable Packaging Balance Buyout** because the asset can be both a named receivable and physical reusable inventory, and specific balance claims become exclusive once assigned.
2. **PV Hail-Damage Salvage + Claim Split** because the founder has renewable-energy fit and can take title to a physical lot, but the idea must avoid becoming generic recycling or unsafe used-module resale.

The likely weak candidates are municipal vehicle arbitrage and `.pl` domain options because the sourcing surface is public and highly copyable.
