# Round 29: Blind Private Capacity And Hidden Inventory Control

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 28 confirmed that public or semi-public regulatory instruments are already visible to incumbents. Round 29 resets around a stricter control shape:

- the supplier identity is hidden until the buyer pays;
- the founder controls a private option on inventory/capacity that is not listed in public marketplaces, registries, or RFQ platforms;
- the buyer pays for an anonymized capability or availability spec, not for a public supplier intro;
- the 60-day proof requires paid buyer reservation/deposit before supplier reveal.

This materially changes several earlier failed industrial/capacity shapes. The asset is not "we know a supplier"; it is blind custody of an unavailable private spare, slot, cell, or machine window.

## Source Anchors

- Poland's industrial/logistics market remains active, with continued tenant demand and vacancy around the low-to-mid single digits in 2026 reporting, supporting urgent overflow/exception demand: https://ceo.com.pl/en/polands-industrial-and-logistics-market-starts-2026-with-strong-tenant-demand-97850/
- Trade.gov.pl describes Poland as a major automotive production/export base, supporting concentrated demand from automotive/Tier suppliers for industrial rescue capacity: https://www.trade.gov.pl/en/news/the-polish-automotive-industry-production-and-export/
- Cold-chain operators in Poland describe recurring collaboration and time-window challenges, supporting the premise that emergency cold-chain capacity has real operational value: https://coldchain.pl/en/challenges-of-collaboration-in-the-refrigerated-supply-chain/
- Prior rounds showed that public auction, public inventory, and generic routebook ideas fail. This round therefore requires non-public supplier identity plus paid buyer deposit before reveal.
