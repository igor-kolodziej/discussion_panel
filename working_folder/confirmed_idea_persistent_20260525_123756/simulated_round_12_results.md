## Winning Simulated Candidate

**Exact scored idea wording:**

PayeeProof Polish VoP Benchmark And Pre-Check. Non-euro PSPs face SEPA instant Verification of Payee duties in 2027, but Polish legal names, sole proprietor names, NIP/KRS variants, diacritics, and invoice/payee mismatches create false positives. PayeeProof secures rights-cleared historical payment mismatch logs from accounting offices and small PSPs plus synthetic edge cases, then sells benchmark/pre-check APIs. 2-month proof under 100k PLN: 2 paid fintech/accounting pilots, 10k named edge cases, measurable false-positive reduction. Control point: consented benchmark data and buyer-specific pre-check workflow, not a public KRS wrapper.

## Why PayeeProof Passed Simulated `>87`

- The control point is not a generic dashboard or compliance report. It is a legally usable, rights-cleared Polish payment-name mismatch corpus plus buyer-specific pre-check workflow.
- The 2-month proof path is concrete: secure two paid pilots, obtain 10k consented/anonymized edge cases from accounting/payment workflows, measure false-positive reduction, and show a buyer outcome that public registry normalization cannot reproduce.
- Gross margins are plausible because the asset can be licensed as benchmark/API data after initial corpus creation.
- The founder fit is materially better than field-service/operator ideas: data normalization, matching, evaluation, and payment workflow analysis fit a Warsaw data scientist.
- The biggest risk is clear and testable: if the founder cannot obtain real historical mismatch logs with clean rights quickly, the idea collapses into a copyable KRS/CEIDG wrapper and should be killed.

## Simulated Caveats To Carry Into Real Validation

- If PayeeProof is only public KRS/CEIDG/NIP normalization plus synthetic cases, score falls to roughly 80-83.
- Main execution risk is acquiring legally usable payment mismatch data without privileged intros.
- Initial CAC should avoid slow bank procurement; first buyers should be small PSPs, fintechs, accounting/payment platforms, ERP/payment workflow vendors, or compliance teams under the 2027 deadline.
- Incumbent VoP/RVM, PSP core-banking, KYC, or anti-fraud vendors can add a Polish-name tuning layer if they can access equivalent data.
