# Round 9 Candidate: SeptiFlow Hauler Compliance Book

Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

## Why This Pivot

The strongest current source-backed pressure is Polish gminas' mandatory recurring control of septic tanks and household wastewater treatment plants. Multiple gmina notices show the same legal pattern: at least once every two years, gminas must check whether property owners have contracts and proof of payment/emptying; haulers have quarterly reporting duties. Existing software mostly sells to gminas or sewage stations. The founder should not build another gmina platform. The wedge is to option or acquire one permitted hauler's customer/reporting book and use it as the compliance transaction rail.

## Exact Candidate Wording For Scoring

**SeptiFlow Hauler Compliance Book** acquires or options one small permitted liquid-waste/septic hauler's active customer and quarterly reporting book, then sells compliance-proof service continuity to property owners and cleaner reporting to gminas without asking the gmina to buy software.

Target asset: phone/domain/Google profile, customer list, signed owner service agreements, tank/household treatment plant notes, route history, last pickup dates, invoices/receipts, quarterly report templates, wastewater-treatment-plant discharge confirmations where available, vehicle/permit data, and 30-day owner handoff. The founder does not buy a truck in the POC; pickups remain with the seller or a permitted hauler under an earnout/subcontract.

Buyer: first, the retiring or overloaded small hauler who wants succession/earnout and less reporting burden; second, property owners with septic tanks or household treatment plants who must show an agreement and proof of regular emptying during gmina controls; third, small gminas that may accept cleaner report formats from the hauler but do not need to procure the system.

Acute trigger: gmina announces a 2025-2026 control cycle; property owners must show a contract and proof of payments/emptying; haulers must submit quarterly reports with addresses/volumes; a small hauler's records are in notebooks, receipts, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets.

Hard control point: signed 60-day option/earnout over one permitted hauler's customer book and report stream, plus customer continuity authorizations for service reminders/proof folders, and the hauler's next quarterly report routed through SeptiFlow. The controlled resource is the actual service book and report flow, not a dashboard.

2-month proof under 100,000 PLN: sign one option with a permitted hauler covering 300-1,500 customer records and at least one upcoming quarterly report; secure customer-contact permission/transition notice; digitize 300 properties with last service date, tank/POŚ type, invoice/receipt status, and required next action; get 50 property owners to accept continuity reminders/proof folders or prepaid pickup scheduling; route at least 30 paid pickups through the hauler/subcontractor; produce one quarterly report pack accepted or correction-free enough for a gmina submission; collect 15,000-30,000 PLN in setup, pickup margin, proof-folder, or reporting fees; prove at least 35% contribution margin on admin/reporting layer excluding truck operations.

Cold start: direct succession outreach to small haulers listed on gmina pages as permitted septic/liquid-waste operators, especially in communes currently announcing 2025-2026 controls. The gmina pressure creates an outbound hook to property owners through the hauler's existing records and notices.

Pricing: hauler pays 2,000-6,000 PLN for quarterly report cleanup or accepts an earnout/option discount; property owners pay 49-149 PLN/year for proof-folder/reminder continuity or book pickups through the route; pickup margin comes from scheduling density and subcontract spread. Long term, acquire hauler books, not trucks first.

Why this is not generic software/service/report/database: SeptiFlow controls an actual permitted hauler's customer book, service agreements, pickup history, and quarterly report flow. It creates compliance proof attached to real pickups and invoices before gmina controls, rather than selling generic reminders to property owners or dashboards to municipalities.

## Source Anchors

- Gov.pl/samorzad gmina notices state that gminas must run cyclical controls at least once every two years for properties with septic tanks/household wastewater treatment plants and check agreements plus proof of payment/emptying.
- The Act on maintaining cleanliness and order in municipalities includes quarterly reporting duties for entities emptying septic tanks and household wastewater treatment plant sludge.
- Existing market: Aquarius, ECOSanity, BioKontrol, and eSzambo-like tools serve gminas, stations, or sensors; the proposed wedge starts with a hauler's customer/reporting book.

## Initial Objections

- There are existing gmina/hauler software systems, so copy risk is real.
- Hauler records and customer data transfer need lawful basis and clear notices.
- Truck operations are low-margin; POC must keep truck service with permitted operators.
- Property owners may expect reminders for free unless linked to real inspection risk.
- A hauler with 300-1,500 clean records may not want to sell cheaply.
