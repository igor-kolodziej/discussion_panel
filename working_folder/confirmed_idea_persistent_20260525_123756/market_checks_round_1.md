# Market Checks Round 1

## Fire Equipment / Ppoż Devices

- Polish fire-protection rules require fire-protection devices and portable/mobile extinguishers to undergo technical inspections and maintenance according to Polish standards, technical documentation, and manufacturer instructions, not less often than annually.
- Gov.pl/PSP guidance states extinguisher maintenance should be performed by genuinely qualified people, even though there is no simple single concession for basic extinguisher servicing.
- Official source anchors:

## F-gas / CRO

- EU F-gas Regulation 2024/573 requires leak checks with frequency depending on CO2e charge, and Poland uses UDT certification and CRO equipment/operator records.
- This is attractive but certification-heavy; only viable if a certified operator/technician is retained.
- Sources:
  - https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R0573
  - https://www.udt.gov.pl/uslugi-udt/szwo-i-f-gazy/certyfikat-dla-przedsiebiorcow
  - https://www.cro.ichp.pl/o-cro

## Building / Chimney / Gas / Electrical Checks

- GUNB states annual checks cover gas installations and chimney ducts, while five-year checks include electrical and lightning installation checks.
- Route books exist through chimney masters, building managers, electricians, and inspectors, but personal qualification/trust is often central.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gov.pl/gunb/kontrole-stanu-technicznego-obiektow
  - https://www.gov.pl/web/rozwoj/terminy-okresowych-kontroli-stanu-technicznego-obiektow-budowlanych

## UDT-Related Maintenance

- UDT covers transport/lifting/pressure equipment; recurring maintenance and UDT-prep routes exist around forklifts, lifts, cranes, compressors, and car-workshop lifts.
- Private operators cannot replace official UDT inspection, and qualified conservators are needed.
- Sources:
  - https://www.udt.gov.pl/uslugi-udt/dozor-techniczny/urzadzenia-podlegajace-dozorowi-technicznemu
  - https://www.udt.gov.pl/uslugi-udt/dozor-techniczny/kwalifikacje-osob

## Waste / Grease / Medical Waste

- BDO and waste documentation create recurring customer relationships, but hazardous/medical routes carry licensing and disposal complexity.
- Grease trap routes appear more transferable if a vacuum truck operator is subcontracted and route calendar/customer relationship is controlled.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/rejestr-bdo
  - https://bdo.mos.gov.pl/
  - https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/odpady-medyczne

## Food HACCP / Pest Control

- EU food hygiene law and HACCP/GHP practice create recurring pest-monitoring requirements in food sites, although frequency is not a single national monthly statutory rule.
- This can be a real recurring route-book business if the route controls bait-station maps, monthly logs, keys/access windows, and Sanepid-ready folders.
- Sources:
  - https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32004R0852
  - https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/wymagania-higieniczne--system-haccp

## Tachograph Data

- GITD states driver card data should be downloaded at least every 28 days and tachograph/mass-memory data at least every 90 days.
- A route calendar for small fleets could be transferred if tied to archive continuity and workshop/accountant trust.
- Source:
  - https://www.gov.pl/web/gitd/jakie-sa-terminy-na-pobranie-danych-z-karty-kierowcy-i-tachografu
