# Round 38 Candidate Set: Acknowledged Payment And Registry Assets

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Pivot

Rounds 36-37 failed because physical lots and call-flow custody were still too close to liquidation or lead generation. Round 38 starts only from assets where an outside system can acknowledge the founder's control:

- court/insolvency registry claim plus assignment notice;
- operator-approved receivable plus payment redirection;
- official registry right plus buyer deposit;
- licensed data/IP rights plus paid buyer use.

This round deliberately does **not** recycle the known weak shapes unless the control point materially changes:

- no mobile app roll-up: Round 35 already failed on target-quality paradox;
- no construction retention receivables: Round 23 already failed without debtor acknowledgment;
- no issued white-certificate brokerage: Round 22 already failed as thin-spread brokerage;
- no KSeF/accounting lockbox, COD connector succession, GBP/call-routing, generic claims, generic service evidence packs, or physical surplus resale.

## Source Anchors

- The Polish Civil Code allows assignment of receivables unless blocked by statute, contract, reservation, or the nature of the obligation. Source: https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/1964/93/text.html
- KRZ is the national insolvency/debtor registry operated by the Minister of Justice and is intended to support access to information in restructuring and bankruptcy proceedings. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/power/krajowy-rejestr-zadluzonych-krz
- The official KRZ creditor instruction describes claim filing and creditor workflows in bankruptcy proceedings. Source: https://krz-info-prod.apps.ocp.prod.ms.gov.pl/krz-help/KRZ_Instrukcja_wierzyciela_v1.02_2023.07.21.pdf
- White-certificate property rights are tradable economic rights on the Polish energy exchange, and applications require an energy-efficiency audit before the project. Sources: https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/system-zobowiazujacy-do-efektywnosci-energetycznej-inaczej-zwany-bialymi-certyfikatami and https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/9359%2CEfektywnosc-energetyczna-biale-certyfikaty-PMEF-wkrotce-traca-waznosc.html
- SENT rules for apparel/footwear changed from 2026-03-17 and include exclusions for registered postal shipments under 31.5 kg handled by Polish/EU postal operators. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/ias-bialystok/przypominamy-o-zmianach-w-systemie-sent-ktore-obowiazuja-od-17-marca-2026-r

## Candidate 1: KRZ Acknowledged-Claim Cash Receiver

### Buyer / Customer

Supply side: small trade creditors, subcontractors, distributors, landlords, and service firms whose claims are already admitted, listed, or scheduled in Polish restructuring/bankruptcy proceedings visible through KRZ or case documents.

Demand side: the startup initially buys for its own account. Later buyers can be distressed-debt funds or local investors once claims are standardized and acknowledged.

### Acute Trigger

Small creditors do not want to wait through a restructuring plan, bankruptcy distribution, trustee process, or court communication cycle for a claim that is already acknowledged but slow. They prefer immediate cash, closing accounting files, and avoiding legal/admin follow-up.

### Primary Control Point

The founder does not broker vague claims. The founder buys only a named, already acknowledged or procedurally admitted claim and becomes the creditor of record or recognized assignee.

Control requires all of:

- signed receivable assignment agreement with creditor warranties;
- KRZ/court/supervisor/trustee/debtor case reference and proof the claim is admitted, on list, or covered by an approved arrangement/distribution path;
- anti-assignment, set-off, dispute, category, priority, and security review;
- written notice to trustee/supervisor/debtor and request for assignee recognition/payment instruction;
- assigned payout account and payment calendar;
- no consumer claims, mass bank insolvency noise, unclear personal-data access, litigation-only claims, or claims needing courtroom recovery.

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- screen 80-150 KRZ/restructuring cases and creditor seller leads through public notices, accountants, lawyers, and supplier communities;
- diligence 20 claims with face value 5,000-80,000 PLN;
- sign 3 assignments with total face value at least 60,000 PLN for no more than 15,000-35,000 PLN cash outlay;
- get trustee/supervisor/debtor written acknowledgment, recognized creditor update, or written payout schedule on at least 2 assigned claims;
- collect at least one payment, distribution, installment, or written scheduled payment within the 60-day window;
- document expected IRR/gross spread after legal review, notification, banking, tax, and failed-claim reserve.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

- Search KRZ and Monitor Sądowy/Gospodarczy notices for small restructurings with approved arrangements or visible creditor lists.
- Call accountants, insolvency lawyers, small suppliers, wholesalers, and contractors: "If you have an admitted claim in a restructuring/bankruptcy and want cash now, I can buy small acknowledged claims after document review."
- Avoid large bank insolvencies, mass consumer cases, and disputed claims.

### Gross Margin And Payback Logic

Target claims where the seller accepts 20-45% of expected payout because the claim is slow, administratively annoying, or too small for funds. Expected recovery can be 40-80% of face depending on arrangement category, security, debtor status, and schedule. The first proof does not need litigation or debt collection; it needs acknowledged assigned payout.

### Copy Risk

Distressed-debt investors exist, but they usually ignore tiny trade claims because acquisition/diligence/admin cost is high relative to face value. Once the startup owns a specific assigned claim and has assignee acknowledgment, incumbents cannot copy that claim. The defensible wedge is a repeatable small-claim underwriting and assignment workflow, not legal aggression.

### Kill Criteria

- No clean assignable admitted claims found within 30 days.
- Trustee/supervisor/debtor refuses to acknowledge assignment or payment instruction.
- Most claims are disputed, set-off exposed, anti-assignment blocked, consumer-sensitive, or litigation-dependent.
- Sellers demand prices that leave less than 20% expected gross spread.
- No written payout schedule or first cash within 60 days.

## Candidate 2: OEM Warranty Reimbursement Advance

Buy or advance against approved OEM warranty reimbursements owed to authorized appliance, heat-pump, e-bike, electronics, or tool-service shops.

Control exists only when the OEM portal shows an approved claim number, amount, and expected payment path, and the service shop signs assignment or irrevocable payment instruction to an account controlled through a licensed PSP/bank setup. Two-month proof: 3 shops, 20 approved claims, 40,000 PLN advanced/repaid or acknowledged. Main risk: OEM terms may ban assignment or ignore third-party payment instructions; factoring incumbents can copy if volumes are large.

## Candidate 3: Courier COD Settlement Advance

Advance against courier-acknowledged cash-on-delivery payout receivables for COD-heavy Polish e-commerce sellers.

Control exists only after courier manifest/settlement files confirm delivered COD shipments and the seller signs assignment plus payout redirection or virtual-account instruction through a licensed PSP/bank partner. Two-month proof: 5 sellers, 200 verified COD shipments, 50,000-80,000 PLN advanced, one full courier repayment cycle. Main risk: this may be ordinary micro-factoring, courier payout redirection may be impossible, and large PSP/factoring players can copy.

## Candidate 4: Pre-Work White-Certificate Rights Lockbox

This is not the failed Round 22 issued-certificate brokerage. It targets future white-certificate rights before an energy-efficiency project starts.

Buyer: SMEs, housing associations, public buildings, and installers doing LEDs, pumps, HVAC, compressed-air upgrades, heat recovery, or controls upgrades who leave certificate value unclaimed.

Control point: pre-work assignment/option over future white-certificate property rights, energy-auditor file custody, URE application packet, and buyer/offtaker deposit from an obligated party or broker. Two-month proof: 5 signed pre-work rights assignments, 2 audit/application packages filed before work starts, and 1 buyer deposit/offtake LOI. Main risk: ESCOs/auditors can copy, savings may be too small, URE rejection/lag makes 60-day cash difficult, and price volatility can crush spread.

## Candidate 5: Buyer-Funded B2B Payment-Outcome Dataset

This is not PayeeProof VoP. It targets invoice payment/default/dispute outcomes for factoring, trade-credit insurance, and B2B underwriting.

Control point: exclusive or field-of-use-restricted license to anonymized/aggregated invoice outcome rows from accounting bureaus, debt collectors, or small factors, plus a buyer deposit before enrichment. Two-month proof: 2 data suppliers, at least 100,000 invoice rows with legal memo/DPA, and one 20,000 PLN buyer deposit from a factor/credit insurer/trade-finance vendor. Main risk: same as PayeeProof if the founder cannot secure real data rights quickly; incumbents may already own better data.

## Candidate 6: SENT Apparel Postal-Operator Lane

Buyer: apparel/footwear importers and fulfillment firms moving small registered parcels through Poland after SENT apparel/footwear changes effective 2026-03-17.

Control point: postal-operator registry status, PUESC/SENT permissions, compliant parcel-lane workflow, and signed importer/fulfillment contracts for specific lanes. Two-month proof: UKE postal-operator registry entry or acquired/operator-partner account, PUESC permissions, 3 paid importer pilots, and test shipment documentation. Main risk: registry status is not proprietary, operations/compliance are service-heavy, and fulfillment/AEO/postal incumbents can copy.

## Gate Rule For Scorers

Apply the existing persistent-loop caps:

- cap below `82` if no concrete 2-month control proof;
- cap below `85` if copyable by one vendor contract, equipment purchase, or internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, owner earnings, legal transferability, debtor/operator acknowledgment, or first cash conversion are not credible;
- require simulated score strictly `>87` to reach the real Zero To One browser gate.
