No Round 40 candidate clears simulated `>87`. No real Zero To One browser validation is allowed.

## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Telco/MVNO transferability is the hard gate; a CSV of SIMs is not control.
- M2M connectivity ARPU can be too low unless books are large, quiet, and prepaid annually.
- Support leaks from SIM/APN/billing into modem, antenna, coverage, firmware, device, alarm, pump, payment, or telematics problems.
- IoT/M2M management portals are widely available, making the process copyable.
- Customer re-consent and prepaid conversion are not proven.

## Pivot For Round 41

Search outside customer-book/admin/SIM continuity. The next round should favor:

- legal title/account assets with high gross profit per event and low ongoing support;
- buyer-funded access to exact assets where the buyer already has urgent use;
- assets whose transferability is defined by registry/platform rules and whose copy risk is blocked by the specific acquired lot/account/right;
- hard proof through non-refundable deposits and first cash, not recurring low-ARPU support.
