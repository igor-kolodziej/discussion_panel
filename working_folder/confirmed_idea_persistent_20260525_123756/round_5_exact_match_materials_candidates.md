Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

## Reason For Pivot

FiskalBook failed real validation at 74/100 because it was too low-ticket, too dependent on technician capacity, and too much like local field service. Round 5 moves to high-gross-margin owned inventory where the founder can control exact lots before demand is fully visible.

## Finalists

### R5F1: TileMatch Storm-Repair Roof Tile Bank

Buy or option exact lots of discontinued and reclaimed Polish roof tiles and accessories from roof replacements, demolitions, and leftover stock: ceramic/cement tiles, ridge tiles, edge tiles, ventilation tiles, and old profiles/colors from brands such as Braas, Koramic, Roben, Tondach, Creaton, Prodach, and local prewar/PRL-era producers. Sell urgent exact-match repair lots to roofers, property managers, insurers/loss adjusters, and homeowners after wind/hail/leak repairs where replacing 20-300 matching tiles avoids full-slope or full-roof replacement.

Buyer: roofers and property managers first; insurers/loss adjusters second; homeowners only through roofers where possible.

Acute trigger: storm/hail/wind damage, leak, cracked tile line, insurance claim, or renovation where the existing roof tile is discontinued or visually mismatched.

Control point: owned or optioned exact physical lots, SKU/profile/color/photo catalog, AI-assisted visual matching workflow, paid search/reservation deposits, and first-look pickup rights from roofers/demolition crews.

2-month proof: sign 10 first-look/pickup rights with roof replacement/demolition crews; acquire or reserve 30 named lots under 35,000 PLN; catalog 15,000+ tiles/accessories; collect 10 paid exact-match search deposits from roofers/property managers/adjusters; complete 5 paid sales with >50% gross margin after pickup/storage/delivery.

First acquisition: scrape OLX/Allegro Lokalnie and roofing Facebook groups for named lots, call roofers and demolition crews with "we pay for tiles you would dump", then outbound to roofers/property managers with a photo-matching request form.

### R5F2: CarpetTile Tenant-Damage Pattern Bank

Acquire discontinued commercial carpet-tile lots from office strip-outs and sell exact pattern/color replacements to property managers and fit-out contractors avoiding whole-floor replacement after stains, leaks, tenant move-outs, or insurance repairs.

Score pressure: strong B2B but less urgent and many facilities keep spare tiles.

### R5F3: FacadeBrick Clinker Patch Bank

Acquire old facade brick/clinker lots from demolitions and sell exact-match facade repair packs to restoration contractors and property managers.

Score pressure: large avoided cost, but heavy logistics and color variability.

### R5F4: HotelBathroom Tile Deadstock Bank

Acquire discontinued bathroom tile deadstock from hotel/office/apartment renovations and sell exact replacements after leaks and tenant damage.

Score pressure: hard to identify exact models unless packaging remains.

### R5F5: Heritage Radiator Section Bank

Acquire cast-iron radiator sections/feet/valves from renovations and sell matched sections to renovators and boutique hotels.

Score pressure: overlaps heritage salvage and has transport/restoration complexity.

### R5F6: Commercial Refrigeration Glass/Door Gasket Match Bank

Acquire discontinued fridge/freezer doors, shelves, gaskets, and controllers from shop refits and sell emergency exact replacements to small grocery/cafe/restaurant technicians.

Score pressure: urgent and B2B, but SKU fragmentation and existing equipment-service incumbents are strong.

## Why R5F1 Clears The Simulated Gate

- The control point is physical and transferable: exact roof-tile lots can be bought, reserved, photographed, stored, and withheld from copycats.
- Supply is visibly fragmented: OLX and demolition-material buyers show hundreds of reclaimed roof-tile listings and lots, often sold cheaply or treated as waste.
- Demand is urgent when it exists: partial roof repair after storm/hail/wind damage can turn into a much larger replacement if matching tiles/ridge/accessory pieces cannot be found.
- Founder fit is better than field service: photo matching, profile classification, marketplace scraping, lot cataloging, request routing, and price discovery are data/AI workflows.
- Cold start does not depend on vague partnerships: the founder can buy or reserve inventory directly and collect paid search/reservation deposits from roofers and property managers.
- Incumbents are fragmented salvage yards and classifieds, not a clean exact-match availability rail for roofers/loss adjusters.

## Strongest Objections

- The market may be too local and episodic; storm demand is clustered.
- Roofers may source from their own networks or ask customers to accept mismatches.
- Storage and transport can eat margin if lots are bulky, low-value, or slow-moving.
- Insurance adjuster distribution is hard; the first beachhead should be roofers/property managers, not insurers.
- Existing OLX/salvage supply means the moat is not ownership alone; it must be exact catalog quality, paid requests, and first-look intake before lots scatter.
- If buyers only buy single tiles or refuse deposits, the idea becomes a low-margin salvage hobby.
