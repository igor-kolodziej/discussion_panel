## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Call-flow assets are not enough if the model looks like undisclosed lead resale.
- Google Business Profile ownership/review transfer may be legitimate after real business ownership transfer, but profile data must still represent the actual business and not route users deceptively.
- Partner operators may pay per verified job, but prepaid minimums before call-quality proof are not credible.
- The best local operator can copy the move by buying the retiring competitor's number/profile directly.
- The founder must either be the disclosed operator of record with subcontracted fulfillment, or avoid GBP/review-dependent demand entirely.
- Two-month proof requires actual transferred control and post-transfer call logs; a contract option alone does not prove economics.

## Pivot For Round 38

Move away from platform/search/review custody. Search for a legally recognized payment-flow asset:

- customer deposits or prepaid balances that are already held by an operator and can be assumed/serviced;
- assigned settlement-account or payout route where the founder is necessary for cash release;
- court/notarial/admin escrow or registry-controlled cash movement;
- recoverable account balances where debtor/operator acknowledgement turns control into cash within 60 days.

Reject any candidate whose control depends mainly on SEO, GBP, reviews, ads, or a vendor deciding not to bypass the founder.
