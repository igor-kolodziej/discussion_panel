## Repeated Fatal Objections

- The control points are real enough for small POCs, but not dominant enough: most can be copied by an incumbent adding one sourcing script, option template, or internal process.
- Asset/control proof is described but not yet economically proven; reviewers capped ideas below 87 when CAC, payback, owner earnings, or contribution margin were not credible.
- Unknown founder trust remains a hard cap in regulated or compliance-heavy categories: cosmetics, waste, data, device wiping, and site permissions all require credibility beyond a clever contract.
- Physical title streams are operationally messy and easy for existing operators to copy once visible.

## Decision

Do not contact real Zero To One for Round 11. No idea cleared simulated `>87`.

The next round must move from "acquire optional supply/control and find buyers" to "buyer already has urgent transaction, startup holds a mandatory release/custody/acceptance asset that can be prepaid or assigned before work starts." Search for situations where the startup can control payment release, ticket/slot ownership, legal title, escrow, or an acceptance bottleneck rather than merely owning supply.
