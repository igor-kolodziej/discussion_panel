# Round 37 Candidate Set: Admin And Call-Routing Custody Assets

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Pivot

Round 36 proved that title/custody over physical lots is not enough when the category is still ordinary liquidation. Round 37 tests a different hard control point from the original mandate: admin/call-routing custody.

The startup must acquire or option a real inbound-demand asset:

- transferable business phone number;
- Google Business Profile primary-owner transfer where the underlying business is sold or handed off legally;
- domain, email, booking mailbox, review profile, call history, and seller handoff;
- partner operator contract with prepaid minimum or non-refundable customer diagnostic deposit;
- founder-controlled call routing and payment link before dispatch.

Reject generic lead generation, SEO, directory pages, marketplaces, ads arbitrage, or route-book service operations.

## Source Anchors

- Google Business Profile supports primary ownership transfer; Google states that when transferring ownership of a business, primary ownership should be transferred to the new owner to maintain business info such as reviews: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3415281?hl=en
- Polish phone operators support reassignment/cession of numbers between people or firms; Orange describes number cession as requiring both parties and documents, while T-Mobile describes cession as transfer of a number/account from one subscriber to another: https://www.orange.pl/poradnik/przeniesienie-numeru-firmowego-na-prywatny/ and https://biznes.t-mobile.pl/pl/obsluga-klienta/cesja/czym-jest-cesja

## Candidate 1: Retiring Emergency Drain Number Custody

### Buyer / Customer

Supply side: retiring one- or two-person drain-cleaning, septic, sewer-pump, and water-damage emergency operators with real inbound phone calls, Google Business Profile reviews, domain, and local reputation.

Demand side: active plumbing/drain/septic operators who want booked emergency jobs without building a local profile from zero.

### Acute Trigger

Emergency drain/sewer calls are phone-first and high intent. A retired operator's number and Google profile can still receive urgent calls, but the owner cannot or does not want to service them. Active operators want jobs now, not SEO months later.

### Primary Control Point

The founder buys or options the retiring business's call-flow asset packet and keeps admin/payment custody:

- phone number cession/porting;
- Google Business Profile primary-owner transfer tied to an actual business asset transfer;
- domain, email, form inbox, call logs, and review/admin access;
- seller-recorded continuity notice and 30-day warm handoff;
- partner operator exclusivity for a territory/category;
- non-refundable diagnostic/emergency-call deposit collected by the founder before dispatch;
- call-routing rules and QA recording retained by the founder.

The founder does not run plumbing crews.

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- contact 150 retiring or weakly digitized emergency drain/septic operators in Poland;
- shortlist 10 with proof of inbound calls, GBP ownership, phone/domain ownership, and willingness to transfer;
- sign one conditional option or asset purchase for under 35,000 PLN upfront plus earnout;
- complete phone-number cession/porting, GBP owner transfer, domain/email transfer, and call-routing setup;
- sign two active operators willing to pay a prepaid monthly floor plus per-booked-job fee;
- collect at least 12,000 PLN of partner prepayment or customer dispatch deposits before final close;
- route 20 qualified calls and produce at least 8 booked paid jobs in 30 days;
- keep gross margin above 60% after seller earnout, call handling, payment fees, refunds, and QA.

### Economics

One local emergency number can support:

- 1,500-3,000 PLN/month partner territory minimum;
- 100-250 PLN per qualified emergency dispatch deposit;
- 8-20% booked-job referral/dispatch fee where legally structured;
- seller earnout funded from retained call-flow revenue.

Cash outlay is controlled by partner prepayment and customer deposits before close.

### Copy Risk

SEO agencies and lead generators can create pages, but they cannot copy the exact transferred phone number, call history, Google profile, reviews, domain, continuity notice, and customer recognition once legally acquired.

### Kill Criteria

- GBP transfer is rejected, suspended, or violates platform rules.
- Phone number cannot be assigned cleanly.
- Partner operators will not prepay a territory minimum.
- Customers refuse deposits before dispatch.
- Call volume is mostly spam, price-shopping, warranty calls, or non-emergency.
- Service-quality complaints threaten the profile.

## Candidate 2: Garage Door / Gate Emergency Number Custody

Acquire retiring gate, garage-door, and access-automation emergency call-flow assets. Dispatch to active installers that prepay territory minimums. Stronger repeat parts margin but more SKU-specific and lower urgency than drains.

## Candidate 3: Locksmith Lockout Number Custody

Acquire retiring locksmith call-flow assets. High urgency and phone-first demand, but scam/spam reputation, licensing/security sensitivity, platform risk, and customer-trust issues are severe.

## Candidate 4: Water-Damage Drying Response Number Custody

Acquire local emergency flood/drying call-flow assets and route to restoration firms. Ticket size is high, but insurer/customer trust, equipment capacity, and service-quality liability are heavier.

## Candidate 5: Appliance Repair Warranty-Abandoned Number Custody

Acquire retiring appliance-repair numbers/profiles and dispatch paid diagnostic visits. Better density and recurring demand, but low ticket size, warranty confusion, and many price-shopping calls cap margins.

## Candidate 6: Industrial Door / Loading Dock Emergency Number Custody

Acquire retiring B2B industrial-door, dock-leveler, and gate-service call-flow assets. Buyers are warehouses/factories; downtime is costly; fewer consumer trust issues. Supply of transferable assets may be thinner, but average job value and B2B willingness to pay are higher.

## Gate Rule For Scorers

Apply the existing persistent-loop caps:

- cap below `82` if no concrete 2-month control proof;
- cap below `85` if copyable by one vendor contract, equipment purchase, or internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, owner earnings, target sourcing, legal/admin transfer, partner prepayment, or customer cash conversion are not credible;
- require simulated score strictly `>87` to reach the real Zero To One browser gate.
