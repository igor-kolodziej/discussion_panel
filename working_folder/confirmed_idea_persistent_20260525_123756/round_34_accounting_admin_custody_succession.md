# Round 34 Candidate Set: Accounting Admin-Custody Succession

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Pivot

Rounds 31-33 showed that micro-software control can be structured well, but real validators repeatedly attacked target scarcity and repeatable owner earnings.

Round 34 moves to a more abundant micro-operator asset class:

- recurring monthly client books;
- KSeF/e-Delivery admin authorization;
- client accounting records and workflows;
- seller-endorsed handoff;
- monthly payment flow;
- regulatory timing that makes continuity valuable in 2026.

This is not a report/compliance-pack idea. The only acceptable control point is signed client-book transfer plus official/admin workflow custody and prepaid transition cash.

## Candidate 1: KSeF/e-Delivery Accounting Book Succession Lockbox

### Buyer

Polish micro and small businesses currently served by a retiring, overloaded, or under-digitized accounting office/bookkeeper.

### Acute Trigger

Mandatory KSeF and e-Delivery/e-Doręczenia requirements create a continuity problem for small accounting offices and their clients. Clients cannot afford lost invoice receipt/issuance, missed official correspondence, VAT/JPK mistakes, payroll deadlines, or a chaotic accountant handoff during the transition.

### Primary Control Point

Acquire or option an accounting micro-book only after clients re-consent and prepay transition/continuity.

Control exists only if secured:

- conditional option over one accounting office's client book, phone/domain/email where transferable, process notes, document archive, recurring billing list, calendar/deadline history, and software/accounting-system workspace where legally transferable;
- seller-mediated notice to named active clients;
- signed client novations or new service agreements;
- explicit KSeF authorization/token custody where client-approved;
- explicit e-Delivery mailbox/admin monitoring mandate where client-approved;
- transfer of monthly invoicing/payment flow;
- prepaid transition or first 2-3 months of retainer cash before closing;
- contracted certified/experienced bookkeeper delivery capacity, with tax-advisory work excluded or referred to licensed advisors.

The founder pays meaningful seller consideration only after the clients re-consent and cash clears.

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- contact 80-120 small accounting offices/bookkeepers, especially owners aged 55+, overloaded solo offices, and offices publicly warning clients about KSeF/e-Delivery transition limits;
- inspect 3 books under NDA: client count, monthly revenue, churn, document volume, software, deadlines, labor hours, liabilities, KSeF readiness, e-Delivery readiness;
- sign one conditional option over a 40-80 client micro-book with no more than 10,000 PLN upfront before customer re-consent;
- seller sends continuity notice to all active clients;
- secure at least 30 signed client consents/service agreements and at least 45,000-70,000 PLN in prepaid transition/retainer cash before closing;
- transfer KSeF/e-Delivery admin mandates for at least 20 clients;
- collect the first monthly billing run under the new operator;
- document contractor delivery cost and gross margin target above 35% after bookkeeper labor, software, legal/privacy, seller earnout, and founder operations.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Seller pitch:

"KSeF and e-Doręczenia make your client book harder to retire from. I will not ask you to sell blind. We run a client continuity campaign first. If clients re-consent and prepay transition, you get upfront/earnout and a clean handoff."

Customer pitch:

"Your accountant is transitioning out. Pay for continuity now: KSeF/e-Delivery setup custody, deadline calendar, document archive handoff, and named bookkeeper continuity. No change unless you sign."

### Gross Margin And Payback Logic

- Target book: 40-80 clients, 300-1,200 PLN/month each, weighted average 500-700 PLN/month.
- Monthly revenue retained from 30 clients: 15,000-21,000 PLN MRR.
- Prepaid transition/retainer cash: 45,000-70,000 PLN.
- Delivery cost: contractor bookkeepers and accounting software, with tax advisory excluded.
- Seller payout: small option fee plus earnout on retained gross margin, not a large upfront purchase.
- Payback is immediate if prepaid cash covers transition/legal/software and the first retained MRR supports delivery.

### Copy Risk

Any accounting office can serve clients, but competitors cannot copy:

- the exact signed client book;
- seller-endorsed trust transfer;
- document history and deadline calendar;
- KSeF/e-Delivery admin mandates;
- monthly billing flow;
- seller-supported handoff during mandatory transition.

### Why It Is Not A Generic Accounting Service

The founder is not starting an accounting firm from zero. The startup acquires a transferred recurring client book only after client re-consent, admin mandates, and prepaid transition cash are secured. The control point is the live monthly transaction/admin workflow, not advice.

### Kill Criteria

- No seller with 40+ active clients signs a conditional option within 45 days.
- Fewer than 30 clients re-consent before close.
- Prepaid transition/retainer cash below 45,000 PLN.
- KSeF/e-Delivery admin mandates cannot be transferred or newly granted by clients.
- Gross margin after bookkeeper labor, software, insurance, legal/privacy, and earnout is below 35%.
- Client files are messy enough to create hidden liability.
- Workload requires licensed tax advisory rather than bookkeeping/process custody.

## Candidate 2: KSeF Transition Client-Book Rescue For E-Commerce Accountants

Same model, but target accounting offices serving e-commerce sellers with higher KSeF/invoice volume and marketplace settlement complexity. Reject if files are messy enough to require tax advisory or dispute cleanup.

## Candidate 3: e-Doręczenia Mailbox Admin-Custody Book

Acquire client books from small virtual-office/accounting providers that already manage official correspondence for micro businesses. Control is e-Delivery admin mandate, deadline monitoring, and recurring client billing. Risk: may become a low-ticket admin service.

## Candidate 4: Payroll/PIT-ZUS Micro-Book Succession

Acquire a payroll micro-book from retiring payroll/accounting offices with recurring monthly payroll tasks. Control is client contracts, employee/payroll history, ZUS/PIT deadline calendar, and monthly payment flow. Risk: labor-heavy and trust-heavy.

## Candidate 5: One-Stack Accounting Software Migration Book

Acquire small accounting offices stuck on one legacy software stack and convert client books to one standardized KSeF/e-Delivery-ready workflow. Control is the book plus workflow migration. Risk: too much implementation service.

## Candidate 6: Kill-The-Territory Control Variant

If simulated scoring says this is merely an accounting-office roll-up with low margins and trust transfer, kill the territory. Do not lower the bar because accounting books are abundant.

## Source Anchors To Check

- Official Polish KSeF timelines and phased mandatory e-invoicing obligations in 2026.
- Official e-Doręczenia/e-Delivery timelines for entrepreneurs and businesses.
- Evidence that bookkeeping/accounting services in Poland are fragmented and can be delivered by non-licensed bookkeepers while tax advisory remains regulated.
