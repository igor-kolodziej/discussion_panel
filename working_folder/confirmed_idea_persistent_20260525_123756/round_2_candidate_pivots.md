# Round 2 Candidate Pivots

Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

## Pivot Thesis

Round 1 failed because route calendars alone are useful but copyable. Round 2 starts from custody of operational control points that customers actually lose:

- admin/installer codes;
- configuration backups;
- fob/card/remote databases;
- cloud owner/installer accounts;
- source/project files;
- spare hardware inventory tied to downtime;
- customer books with consent and handoff.

The safest legal framing is owner-authorized transfer/custody, not buying credentials and using them without the asset owner's mandate.

## Evidence Signals

- Polish intercom/domofon forums show concrete pain around installer-code refusal, factory resets that wipe settings, and communities wanting clean handover rather than hostage behavior.
- Access-control communities report admin accounts renamed to past technicians, missing Windows/server credentials, and board/committee inability to manage resident/fob records.
- CCTV/NVR complaints show buyers worry about installers holding admin access and warranty threats, although CCTV/live monitoring is a legal/GDPR trap and should be excluded from the first wedge.
- KNX/BMS users identify project files as the key asset needed to switch installers or extend a system.
- PV inverter users hit cloud installer-account walls when the installer disappears or an installation changes owner.

## Finalists For Simulated Round 2

### R2F1: GateKey Residential Access Custody Roll-Up

Acquire or option one retiring low-voltage residential installer/operator's access-custody book and use owner-authorized mandates from wspolnoty/spoldzielnie/garages to rotate and store control over intercom/RFID/gate systems.

Buyer: property managers, housing-community boards, garage associations, and small cooperatives that suffer lost fobs/remotes, tenant churn, installer-code lock-in, and no break-glass access.

Control point: signed building owner/community mandate plus transferred installer codes, admin accounts, fob/tag/remote database, receiver backups, device maps, configuration backups, phone/domain/customer book, and owner handoff. Credentials are rotated into named owner accounts and a break-glass vault.

2-month proof: acquire/option one micro-operator book under 70,000 PLN, sign 3 building mandates, rotate/administer 3 systems without reset, issue/deactivate at least 100 fobs/remotes, collect 10,000-25,000 PLN setup revenue and 1,000-3,000 PLN MRR custody/admin fees.

First acquisition: direct outreach to retiring domofon/gate/access installers, property managers with visible resident complaints, and search intent around "kod instalatora domofon", "programowanie breloka", "pilot do bramy wspolnota".

Primary caveat: avoid unauthorized access and avoid regulated alarm/CCTV/fire work in the POC; use owner authorization and licensed subcontractors where physical security-service work is needed.

### R2F2: LabelLine Zebra/ZPL Print-Station Custody Bank

Acquire a tiny e-commerce/warehouse label-printer support operator's config archive, customer book, templates, and used Zebra/scanner inventory; sell downtime-proof label-printing custody to Polish micro-warehouses that ship through Allegro, InPost, BaseLinker, DPD, DHL, and Amazon.

Buyer: e-commerce pack rooms, small warehouses, spare-parts sellers, cosmetics/supplement shops, and manufacturers where shipping stops when thermal labels, barcodes, scanners, Windows spoolers, or ZPL/PDF formats break.

Control point: owned tested printer/scanner spare bank, ZPL/PDF courier-template archive, BaseLinker/InPost/Allegro print settings, printer IP/static profiles, label/ribbon inventory, remote support agent, and assigned customer support book.

2-month proof: buy/test 20-35 used Zebra/industrial label printers and scanners under 35,000 PLN; acquire/option one micro-support book under 30,000 PLN or secure 10 custody mandates; complete 10 paid emergency fixes, 5 paid station hardening jobs, and 3 monthly retainers.

First acquisition: high-intent search/forum content for Zebra/BaseLinker/InPost label problems, Allegro seller groups, direct outbound to pack rooms with visible hiring/listing activity, and inherited customers from acquired technician.

### R2F3: PV Inverter Monitoring Custody Rescue

Acquire or option an inactive/retiring PV installer's monitoring/admin book and transfer plants into owner-held or new-installer custody so SME/prosumer PV owners are no longer locked behind vanished installer accounts.

Buyer: PV owners with offline inverter monitoring, warranty claims, new property ownership, export-limit changes, battery add-on plans, or installer bankruptcy.

Control point: signed owner mandates, cloud plant transfer process, installer/company account handoff where available, inverter/logger credentials, commissioning parameter backup, monitoring alerts, and renewable-company technician capacity.

2-month proof: secure 20 signed plant-custody mandates, transfer or document admin path for at least 10 plants, restore monitoring on 15 plants, collect 199-499 PLN setup fees and 49-99 PLN/month monitoring from at least 10 owners.

First acquisition: parents' renewable-energy company as credibility/test capacity where appropriate, direct search intent around "FusionSolar/SolarEdge/Solarman instalator zniknal", local PV owner groups, and failed-installer customer books.

### R2F4: KNX Project-File Escrow And Takeover Rail

Secure owner-authorized custody of KNX/ETS project files for high-end homes, boutique hotels, clinics, and offices so they can switch installers, extend systems, or sell property without losing automation control.

Buyer: owners of KNX/smart-building installations, architects, property buyers before closing, small hotels/offices with dead installer or planned renovation.

Control point: .knxproj file, project password, group-address export, device list, topology, contractor contacts, owner vault, and paid takeover report. Option/acquire a retiring KNX installer's project archive only with client authorization and notices.

2-month proof: secure 5 owner mandates, recover or escrow 3 project files, complete 2 paid takeover reports at 2,000-5,000 PLN, and sign one renovation/extension referral.

First acquisition: property buyer due-diligence content, KNX forum pain, architects/interior designers, and local smart-home installers exiting maintenance.

### R2F5: CCTV/NVR Break-Glass Custody For Small Commercial Sites

Owner-authorized custody handover for small shops, warehouses, schools, and clinics with NVR/admin lock-in after an installer disappears or footage is needed. The POC excludes live monitoring, alarm response, and intrusive resident CCTV.

Buyer: SMB owners and administrators who need footage after an incident, employee turnover, insurance/police request, or installer disappearance.

Control point: signed owner mandate, NVR/admin account recovery or rotation, camera activation passwords, remote-access account, config backup, footage-export procedure, and processor/GDPR packet.

2-month proof: 10 paid custody handovers at 700-2,500 PLN, 5 monthly support retainers, zero unauthorized access, and lawyer-reviewed GDPR/processor templates.

First acquisition: search intent around "Hikvision admin password installer disappeared", local SMB outbound, insurer/police incident scripts, and micro-security-installer succession.

Primary caveat: this may trigger security-service/GDPR complexity; likely weaker than GateKey unless scoped to owner-side custody only.

### R2F6: EV Charger OCPP/Admin Migration Custody

Owner-authorized migration of small landlord/fleet/private charging points from installer/operator lock-in to documented admin/OCPP custody.

Buyer: landlords, small fleets, office parks, and housing communities with charger operator changes, tariff changes, broken RFID access, or public/private character changes.

Control point: charger local admin, OCPP/backend settings, RFID/user export, load-limit configuration record, owner account, and UDT/public-private status checklist.

2-month proof: 10 paid charger custody audits, 3 successful OCPP/admin migrations, no electrical modification without qualified contractor, and 3 retainers.

Primary caveat: UDT/electrical/operator complexity probably caps this below GateKey/LabelLine unless a qualified partner is already controlled.

## Round 2 Recommendation Before Simulation

Score R2F1 and R2F2 hardest. R2F1 has the sharpest "customer lost control of their own asset" insight and repeat transaction flow from fobs/remotes. R2F2 has the cleanest legal risk and fastest B2B urgency. R2F3 has founder fit through renewable-energy context but may duplicate existing PV-adjacent ideas and relies on manufacturer portals.
