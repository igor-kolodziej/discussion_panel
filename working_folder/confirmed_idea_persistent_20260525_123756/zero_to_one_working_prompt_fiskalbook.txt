Idea: FiskalBook KSeF Service-Book Roll-Up.

Acquire or option one tiny Polish fiscal-device/POS service operator's active service book: phone number, domain/Google profile, customer list, device serials, review due dates, support inbox, spare-parts drawer, service history, manufacturer-authorized technician roster, and 30-day owner handoff. Then sell prepaid 2026 KSeF readiness and mandatory fiscal-register review/repair bundles to that customer base.

Buyer: small retailers, restaurants, salons, taxis, clinics, kiosks, and local service businesses that already depend on a cash register/fiscal printer/POS setup.

Acute trigger: mandatory KSeF 2.0 rollout, two-year technical review deadline, cash-register/printer failure that can stop legal sales, current service provider retiring/disappearing, or accountant pressure to fix invoice workflow.

Control point: signed 60-day option/earnout over the operator's routed service book and communication channels, plus contracted access to at least one KAS-listed/manufacturer-authorized technician for the top device types. The first asset is not software; it is routed recurring service demand plus authorized delivery capacity.

2-month proof under 100,000 PLN: one signed option over at least 300 active/recent devices, phone forwarding and owner transition notice, verified authorized technician coverage for the top device types, 30 prepaid review/KSeF readiness slots, 15 completed paid service events, and at least 25,000 PLN collected gross revenue before buying the whole book. Upfront spend: 10,000-25,000 PLN operator option, 10,000-20,000 PLN technician/subcontractor reserve, 5,000-10,000 PLN legal/GDPR/insurance review, small spare-parts/marketing buffer.

Cold start: call 40-80 one-person fiscal/POS service shops from Google/KAS/producer directories; target older owners overloaded by KSeF and succession; offer low upfront option plus earnout on converted customers instead of full upfront acquisition. Customers are reached through the seller's phone/domain/inbox and co-signed transition notice, not cold ads.

Pricing: 250-500 PLN mandatory technical review, 700-1,500 PLN KSeF/POS readiness slot, 1,000-4,000 PLN device/software replacement or repair event, optional 49-149 PLN/month continuity plan for due-date reminders, KSeF certificate/token/offline-flow checks, and emergency routing.

Why this is not generic KSeF consulting/SaaS: the founder does not sell public KSeF information. The POC only proceeds if the startup controls an actual service book with device serials, review dates, communication channels, owner introduction, and authorized technician capacity. Without the signed service-book option and prepaid customer slots, the idea is killed.

Domination path: roll up retiring local fiscal/POS service books city by city, standardize reminder calendars, KSeF readiness scripts, device failure data, and technician routing, then cross-sell reviews, repairs, replacements, closure/memory-readout packets, and small retailer continuity plans. The compounding asset is the installed-base ledger and service history of small fiscal devices, not a dashboard.
