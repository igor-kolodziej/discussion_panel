## Fatal Pattern Extracted

Round 23 improved the asset quality but still did not produce a Zero-To-One control point. Most ideas controlled one transaction, not a repeatable unfair source of transactions.

Repeated fatal objections:

- public or semi-public sourcing surfaces invite incumbent bidders;
- title/control over one lot does not create durable privileged flow;
- claims are deadline- and evidence-sensitive, with uncertain first cash;
- salvage spreads disappear after testing, transport, storage, legal, and failed inventory;
- physical arbitrage creates cashflow experiments, not defensible company-quality wedges;
- a stronger asset channel is required before scoring can break above the mid-70s.

## Next Pivot

Round 24 should search for embedded custody of regulated transaction capacity:

- named slots, seats, routes, meters, ports, lab capacity, repair benches, or calibration capacity;
- the startup must reserve or prepay capacity, then resell emergency continuity to a buyer whose transaction cannot proceed without the slot;
- proof must be a signed capacity option plus prepaid customer use or a written buyer commitment, not a mere referral or booking service;
- avoid public auctions, generic salvage, generic compliance, and claims without acknowledged debtor/insurer payment path.
