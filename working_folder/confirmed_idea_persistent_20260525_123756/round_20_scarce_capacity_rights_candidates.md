# Round 20 Scarce Capacity And Site-Rights Candidates

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Round

Rounds 16-19 failed because workflow control, evidence queues, and bounty rails were still copyable. This round starts from scarce physical or regulated capacity: grid connection, rooftop/site control, flexible electrical load, recycler intake, and accepted waste-shipment slots.

## Initial Preference

The strongest theoretical control points are Cable-Pooling Paid Option Book and Paid DSR Cold-Load Option Book because they control scarce MW capacity. The strongest practical 60-day execution may be Industrial Rooftop/PPA Site Option Rail because the founder has relevant renewable-energy family context and the legal option is simpler.
