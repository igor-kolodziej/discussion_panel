# Real Working-Chat Validation: FiskalBook Failed

Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

## Candidate

FiskalBook KSeF Service-Book Roll-Up.

## Simulated Gate

Simulated Zero To One-style score: **90/100**.

## Real Working-Chat Zero To One Score

**74/100**

This is below the required `>=82` working-chat threshold. The idea is **not confirmed** and no final idea file may be written for it.

## Key Objections From Zero To One

- The validator recognized a real control point: a service book, phone/domain/inbox, review dates, device IDs, owner introduction, and authorized technician capacity.
- It scored the idea below the real gate because KSeF urgency is weaker than the pitch implies as of 2026-05-25. KSeF is already mandatory for large taxpayers from 2026-02-01 and most remaining taxpayers from 2026-04-01, with the smallest invoice-volume taxpayers delayed to 2027, and 2026 is a transition year without penalties for KSeF usage errors.
- The stronger durable wedge is fiscal-device continuity: mandatory technical reviews, failures, replacement cycles, closure/memory readout, and trusted service access.
- The recurring review base is low-ticket. A 300-device book with two-year review cycles may produce only about 150 review opportunities per year; the business works only if repairs, replacements, KSeF remediation, emergency routing, and continuity plans lift ARPA materially.
- Technician capacity is a hard bottleneck; if authorized technician coverage is not locked, the service book becomes a complaint funnel.
- KSeF is not naturally attached to fiscal-device service for many small B2C-heavy customers.
- GDPR/customer-data transfer and transition notices must be handled carefully.
- The business could become manual field-service operations before it becomes a data business, which weakens fit for a part-time, low-networking founder.

## Feedback To Carry Forward

Do not advance this shape again as a KSeF-led service-book idea. A stronger version would need to be positioned as fiscal-device continuity first and prove:

- a 300+ device book with at least 70% reachable contacts, known device type, review date, and service history;
- contracted authorized technician coverage for at least 80% of the top device types;
- at least 25,000 PLN gross collected with at least 40% from non-KSeF services;
- 15 completed paid service events with measured margin after technician payout;
- 20 customers accepting future reminder/continuity contact.

Even then, the validator viewed the idea as a testable local roll-up rather than a confirmed 82+ company under the gate.
