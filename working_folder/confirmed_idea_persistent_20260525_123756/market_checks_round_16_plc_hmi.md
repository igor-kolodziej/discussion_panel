# Round 16 Market And Competition Checks: PLC/HMI Exchange Book

Created: 2026-05-25

## Checked Facts

- Siemens' 2025 SIMATIC PCS 7 Remote I/O Upgrade Guideline states that SIMATIC S7-300 / ET 200M availability ran until 1 October 2023, product cancellation takes effect on 1 October 2025, and spare-part availability is planned until 1 October 2033. It also describes modernization approaches where only some plants can upgrade in limited windows.
  - Source: https://cache.industry.siemens.com/dl/files/203/109824203/att_1320236/v1/SIMATIC_PCS_7_Remote_IO_Upgrade_Guideline.pdf

- A Polish competitor, Gal-Industry in Krakow, publicly buys automation surplus outright or on consignment, lists Siemens / Allen-Bradley / Lenze / B&R / ABB / Indramat / Fanuc / SEW stock, and explicitly advertises PLC, HMI, power-supply, and inverter parts, same-day shipping, rare/discontinued components, and a Poland/EU warehouse.
  - Source: https://gal-industry.com/

- EU Automation publicly positions itself as a supplier of new, reconditioned, obsolete, service-exchange, and repair industrial automation parts, including Siemens Simatic S5/S7 PLCs, HMIs, drives, and other automation categories.
  - Source: https://assets.euautomation.com/downloads/guides/Line-Card-us.pdf

- PLC West and similar EU sellers show visible public pricing for refurbished/used PLC, HMI, drive, and encoder parts, confirming active resale supply and direct competition.
  - Source: https://plcwest.com/

- Community pain signals from `r/PLC` show operators discussing S7-300 obsolescence, stocking spares, avoiding modernization downtime, and relying on eBay or used modules where full migration is not scheduled.
  - Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PLC/comments/1qdicao/siemens_s7300_obsolescence/
  - Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PLC/comments/15ptbhx/why_do_companies_buy_used_modules/

## Competition Implication

This cannot be pitched as a generic obsolete-parts reseller. That market already exists.

The only version worth validating is narrower:

**Retiring Automation Integrator Emergency PLC/HMI Exchange Book**: acquire/option one retiring Polish automation integrator's stock, installed-base/service-ticket archive, emergency phone/domain, plant contact context, and 30-day owner support, then combine it with owned tested exact-SKU inventory and same-day Polish dispatch/rental terms.

The control point must be:

- physical possession or signed option over named SKUs;
- bench-test videos, serial photos, and working-order classification;
- installed-base records and service tickets tied to machines/plants where legally usable;
- phone/domain/email routing from the retiring operator;
- non-compete and first-refusal rights on future decommissioned stock;
- paid reservations, emergency sales, or rentals proving current demand.

## Kill Risks

- If the founder can only buy the same parts everyone can buy on Allegro/eBay, the score should be capped below 82.
- If the retiring integrator will not transfer installed-base records or hand off the phone/domain, the idea becomes a parts shop and should fail.
- If three paid emergency events or reservations cannot be closed within 60 days from 30-50 tested SKUs, demand density is too low.
- If returns, misdiagnosis, DOA parts, and testing labor collapse gross margin below 35%, the business is not attractive.
