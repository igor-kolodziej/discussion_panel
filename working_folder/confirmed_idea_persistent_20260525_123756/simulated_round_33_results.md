## Candidate That Cleared Simulated Gate

**High-Floor COD/Settlement Continuity Buyout** scored **88/100**.

## Why It Passed Simulated `>87`

- COD settlement/reconciliation has a sharper pain than broad connector continuity: it touches cash timing, courier payouts, returns, missing settlements, and reconciliation errors.
- The 60-day proof remains hard-control based: conditional option, ticket-history review, seller-mediated customer re-consent, 15+ paying merchants, 40,000-60,000 PLN prepaid, and transfer of source/update/support assets before close.
- The high prepaid floor directly addresses the Round 32 fresh-chat failure that 25,000 PLN was insufficient to prove owner earnings.
- The model rejects spreadsheet matching, bespoke accounting cleanup, Base/BaseLinker-covered flows, official integrations, and cheap marketplace modules.

## Simulated Caveats To Carry Into Real Validation

- Target sourcing is the weakest part. There may not be enough old COD reconciliation connectors with 30-100 active merchants.
- Many workflows may collapse into BaseLinker automation, carrier exports, accounting cleanup, or spreadsheet matching.
- Support leakage can still destroy margins unless escalation is explicitly priced.
- One buyout proves cash floor, not repeatability. Owner earnings require 3-5 similar books.
