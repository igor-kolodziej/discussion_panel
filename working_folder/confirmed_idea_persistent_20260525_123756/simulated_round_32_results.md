## Candidate That Cleared Simulated Gate

**Prepaid ERP Connector Succession Escrow** scored **88/100**.

## Why It Passed Simulated `>87`

- It materially fixes the Round 31 real-gate failure by requiring customer re-consent and prepaid continuity cash before acquisition close.
- The founder does not buy dead code or assume renewal transfer. The seller earns only if named customers re-consent and prepay.
- The control point is a combined packet: conditional option over source/IP/repo/release/support assets, seller-endorsed customer notice, explicit re-consent, prepaid contracts, and narrow support scope.
- CAC/payback are more credible because the first customer acquisition happens through the seller's existing named active customers, and upfront seller payment is capped until customer cash clears.
- Copyability is lower than a normal agency/support model because competitors cannot instantly copy the seller-endorsed transition, active customer contracts, release/update path, issue history, and support inbox.

## Simulated Caveats To Carry Into Real Validation

- The proof target is aggressive: 20 of 30 named customers re-consenting and prepaying within 60 days requires seller trust and a real continuity threat.
- One 25,000 PLN prepaid batch proves risk reduction, not a scaled business. The thesis needs repeatable acquisition of similar connector books.
- Support leakage is the existential risk. If the work becomes store-by-store debugging, margins collapse.
- Seller cooperation is a hidden dependency. The seller must have named active customers, real customer trust, and willingness to endorse.
- Commodity integrations must be rejected, especially generic Fakturownia/Subiekt exports, payment gateways, broad shipping plugins, and workflows well covered by Base/BaseLinker or official/free connectors.
- The first target should be a legacy, messy, high-friction operations connector where replacement is painful and the ticket history is mostly connector-level maintenance.

## Decision

Advance Candidate 1 to real working-chat Zero To One validation.
