Idea: ImplantKey Legacy Scanbody And Driver Bank.

ImplantKey buys or options reusable, non-implantable legacy dental implant tooling from retiring dentists, prosthodontists, oral surgeons, and dental labs: drivers, torque-tool tips, scan bodies, impression copings, lab analogs, healing-cap identification samples, CAD libraries, and system catalogs. 2-month proof under 100,000 PLN: secure 5-8 signed asset options/purchases from retiring dentists/labs under 50,000 PLN; catalog 150+ exact-match professional-use items; contract one implant prosthetics technician; collect 10 dentist-paid case deposits of 300-1,000 PLN; complete 3 paid rentals/sales/searches.

No influencer dependency.

Why it is not a generic marketplace/report/dashboard/service: the startup controls the exact physical and digital compatibility assets before demand is routed, charges deposits for named cases, and can reserve/rent/sell professional-use tooling or scan-body access that cannot be solved by a public list if the exact item is unavailable.
