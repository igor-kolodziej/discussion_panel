## Repeated Fatal Objections

- A legally acknowledged asset is not enough if an incumbent can copy the sourcing/process with one internal workflow.
- First cash must be actual cash or non-refundable customer/buyer deposit, not just a schedule, LOI, or registry filing.
- Financing-style ideas are capped unless repayment routes are controlled by debtor/operator acknowledgment, not seller honesty.
- Data-rights ideas keep failing unless supplier rights and buyer deposit are both secured before enrichment.
- Regulatory-account ideas are capped if the official registration is procedural rather than scarce.

## Pivot For Round 39

The next round must start from **non-public, customer-specific operating assets where control is simultaneously mandatory and economically live**:

- assigned platform/vendor accounts with active balance/payout and account-owner consent;
- buyer-funded acquisition of a specific locked account, claim, inventory stream, or dataset before reveal;
- contracts where a third party must accept the assignee/operator for cash or work to continue;
- rights that are not merely public-registry procedural entries.

Reject financing, registry, or dataset ideas unless the first proof includes automatic payment into the founder-controlled account or a non-refundable buyer-funded acquisition deposit.
