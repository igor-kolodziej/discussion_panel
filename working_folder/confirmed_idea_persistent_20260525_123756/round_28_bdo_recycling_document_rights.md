# Round 28: BDO Electronic Recycling-Document Rights

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 27 failed because grid assets are real but bespoke and slow. Round 28 targets a government-created, electronic, material-specific rights stream with recurring buyer demand: Polish DPR/EDPR recycling documents for packaging obligations.

Unlike generic compliance reporting, DPR/EDPR documents are transaction assets. They confirm recycling/export-for-recycling of packaging waste and are used by packaging recovery organisations and producers/importers to fulfil statutory packaging obligations. From 2026, they are issued through BDO's Confirmation Module, making custody, timing, and document allocation more formal and trackable.

## Source Anchors

- BDO official notice says from 2026-01-01 applications and issuance of DPR and EDPR for packaging waste must be handled through individual BDO accounts in the Confirmation Module: https://bdo.mos.gov.pl/news/modul-potwierdzen-w-zakresie-dpr-i-edpr/
- Interzero says DPR/EDPR documents can be sold at a profit to a Packaging Recovery Organisation and that rising minimum recycling levels and legislative changes are increasing DPR prices: https://interzero.pl/en/uslugi/gospodarka-odpadami/odkup-dpr-i-edpr/
- IOOO and SKELV publicly offer purchase/sale of DPR/EDPR documents, confirming a private market exists: https://iooo.pl/en/purchase-sale-of-dpr-edpr-packaging/ and https://skelv.pl/en/purchase-sale-of-dpr-edpr/
- Ekocykl explains DPR is the document confirming recycling of packaging waste and records the mass and recycling method; producers placing packaged products on the market must meet material-specific recycling levels: https://ekocykl.pl/dokumenty-potwierdzajace-recykling-dpr-edpr-w-bdo-uporzadkowanie-systemu-od-2026-roku/
- Gov.pl and 2026 Polish packaging law changes confirm packaging/ROP reform and increasing packaging-fee pressure: https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/projekt-ustawy-o-opakowaniach-i-odpadach-opakowaniowych-uc100--droga-do-zmniejszenia-ilosci-odpadow and https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/174/ogl

## Initial Read Before Scoring

The best form is not a marketplace or compliance dashboard. It is **forward rights over exact electronic DPR/EDPR streams**, with delegated BDO workflow and buyer deposits before the documents are issued or allocated. The candidate has hard control only if the agreement gives the founder exclusive right to designate the recipient/buyer for the named document stream.
