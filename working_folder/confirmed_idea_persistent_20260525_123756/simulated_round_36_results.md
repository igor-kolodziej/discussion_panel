## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Hard title and physical custody are necessary but not sufficient if the business is still normal liquidation.
- "Hidden lot" access is weak unless the founder has exclusive recurring supply that incumbents cannot reach.
- Buyer deposits before outlay are plausible only for narrow exact-SKU needs, not broad mixed lots.
- Resale economics are easy to overstate because list prices do not equal sell-through cash.
- Sorting, testing, provenance, photos, buyer calls, refunds, storage, and liability make owner earnings fragile.
- Existing surplus dealers, liquidators, recyclers, distributors, and 3PL disposal partners can copy the shape with one relationship.

## Pivot For Round 37

Move away from generic physical title lots. The next round should target assets where the control point is:

- a registry-recognized entitlement;
- a payment account or payout route;
- a legally assigned account balance;
- a transfer/consent artifact that makes the founder the necessary operator of a cash-clearing event.

Reject any candidate that is merely resale, brokerage, advisory, marketplace listing, report generation, or "better liquidation."
