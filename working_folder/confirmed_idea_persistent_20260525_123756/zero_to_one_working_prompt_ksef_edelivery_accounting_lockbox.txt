Evaluate this business idea. Rate it from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Please cap the score below 82 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, uniquely produced, assigned, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under the constraints below. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, gross margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and owner earnings more heavily than originality.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist/software-capable operator; can start part-time with contractors; max 100,000 PLN before proof; no celebrity/influencer access; no privileged accounting-firm introductions; can cold outbound to small accounting offices/bookkeepers and hire/contract experienced bookkeeping capacity; must avoid regulated tax-advisory work unless referred to licensed advisors.

Idea: KSeF/e-Delivery Accounting Book Succession Lockbox.

This is not a generic accounting firm, KSeF consulting service, compliance report, or accounting-office roll-up bought on goodwill. The wedge is acquiring or optioning a retiring or overloaded Polish accounting micro-book only after clients re-consent, grant KSeF/e-Delivery admin mandates, and prepay transition/retainer cash before the seller is paid meaningful consideration.

Buyer/customer: Polish micro and small businesses currently served by a retiring, overloaded, or under-digitized accounting office/bookkeeper. The seller is the bookkeeper/accounting-office owner who wants a clean succession path during the 2026 KSeF/e-Doręczenia transition.

Acute trigger: mandatory KSeF and e-Doręczenia/e-Delivery requirements create a continuity problem for small accounting offices and their clients. Clients cannot afford lost invoice receipt/issuance, missed official correspondence, VAT/JPK mistakes, payroll/calendar mistakes, or chaotic accountant handoff during the transition. The continuity risk is especially acute when the old accountant does not want to modernize, hire, or carry the operational burden.

Primary control point: a transferred recurring client book plus official/admin workflow custody, not advice.

Control exists only if secured before close:
- conditional option over one accounting office's client book, phone/domain/email where transferable, process notes, document archive, recurring billing list, calendar/deadline history, and software/accounting-system workspace where legally transferable;
- seller-mediated continuity notice to named active clients;
- signed client novations or new service agreements;
- explicit KSeF authorization/token custody or equivalent client-approved KSeF workflow access;
- explicit e-Doręczenia/e-Delivery mailbox/admin monitoring mandate where client-approved and relevant;
- transfer of monthly invoicing/payment flow;
- prepaid transition cash or first 2-3 months of retainer cash before closing;
- contracted certified/experienced bookkeeper delivery capacity, with regulated tax-advisory work excluded or referred to licensed advisors;
- clean liability boundaries, file-quality diligence, OC/insurance/legal/privacy templates, and a no-cleanup-without-paid-scope rule.

60-day proof under 100,000 PLN:
- Contact 80-120 small accounting offices/bookkeepers, especially owners aged 55+, overloaded solo offices, offices with weak KSeF/e-Doręczenia readiness, and offices whose owners do not want to modernize.
- Inspect 3 books under NDA: client count, monthly revenue, churn, document volume, software, deadlines, labor hours, liabilities, KSeF readiness, e-Doręczenia readiness, file quality, tax-advisory risk, and client concentration.
- Sign one conditional option over a 40-80 client micro-book with no more than 10,000 PLN upfront before customer re-consent.
- Seller sends continuity notice to all active clients and personally endorses the handoff.
- Secure at least 30 signed client consents/service agreements and 45,000-70,000 PLN in prepaid transition/retainer cash before closing.
- Transfer KSeF/e-Doręczenia admin mandates for at least 20 clients where applicable.
- Collect the first monthly billing run under the new operator.
- Verify contribution margin above 35% after bookkeeper labor, accounting software, OC/insurance, legal/privacy work, seller earnout, and founder operations.

First acquisition mechanism:
Seller pitch:
"KSeF and e-Doręczenia make your client book harder to retire from. I will not ask you to sell blind. We run a client continuity campaign first. If clients re-consent and prepay transition, you get a small upfront/earnout and a clean handoff."

Client pitch:
"Your accountant is transitioning out. Pay for continuity now: KSeF/e-Doręczenia setup custody, deadline calendar, document archive handoff, and named bookkeeper continuity. Nothing changes unless you sign and authorize the new admin workflow."

Economics:
- Target book: 40-80 clients at 300-1,200 PLN/month each; weighted average 500-700 PLN/month.
- Retained 30-client book at 500-700 PLN/month = 15,000-21,000 PLN MRR.
- Prepaid transition/retainer cash target: 45,000-70,000 PLN.
- Seller payout: small option fee plus earnout on retained gross margin, not a large upfront acquisition.
- Delivery cost: contracted experienced bookkeepers, accounting software, insurance, privacy/legal templates, and support operations.
- The first transaction is acceptable only if prepaid cash covers transition/legal/software setup and retained MRR supports delivery with >35% contribution margin.

Defensibility:
Any accounting office can serve clients, but competitors cannot instantly copy the exact signed client book, seller-endorsed trust transfer, document history, deadline calendar, KSeF/e-Doręczenia admin mandates, monthly billing flow, and seller-supported handoff during a mandatory-transition period.

Why this is not a generic accounting service:
The founder is not starting an accounting office from zero and is not selling KSeF advice. The startup acquires a transferred recurring client book only after client re-consent, admin mandates, payment-flow transfer, and prepaid transition cash are secured. The control point is the live monthly transaction/admin workflow.

Kill criteria:
- No seller with 40+ active clients signs a conditional option within 45 days.
- Fewer than 30 clients re-consent before close.
- Prepaid transition/retainer cash below 45,000 PLN.
- KSeF/e-Doręczenia admin mandates cannot be newly granted by clients.
- Gross margin after bookkeeper labor, software, insurance, legal/privacy, and earnout is below 35%.
- Client files are messy enough to create hidden liability or unpaid cleanup work.
- Workload requires regulated tax advisory rather than bookkeeping/admin-process custody.
- Seller refuses to personally endorse the handoff.
- Clients treat the offer as normal accountant switching and refuse prepayment.
