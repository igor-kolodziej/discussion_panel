## Pivot Implication

The next candidate must avoid commodity-rental ARPU and generic service operations. It needs a control point with one of these properties:

- transaction flow that incumbents are structurally not set up to monetize;
- compliance/evidence pressure tied to actual payment or acceptance;
- a resource that can be optioned, routed, prepaid, or uniquely produced inside two months;
- repeatable cash conversion not dependent on hospital partnerships, public procurement, or generic family trust.
