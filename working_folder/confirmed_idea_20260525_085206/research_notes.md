## Demographic And Policy Momentum

- Statistics Poland reports that in 2024 Poland had about 10.0 million people aged 60+, equal to 26.6% of the population. Source: https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/en/defaultaktualnosci/3618/1/7/1/the_situation_of_older_people_in_poland_in_2024.pdf
- The same GUS projection expects people aged 60+ to reach 11.9 million in 2060, 38.3% of the population. Source: same GUS report.
- GUS notes that 23.8% of households with at least one 60+ person, and 26.6% of households composed solely of people aged 60+, lived in buildings with architectural barriers. Source: same GUS report.
- On 2026-05-05, the Polish government adopted a draft law on coordination of long-term care and older people. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/projekt-ustawy-o-koordynacji-opieki-dlugoterminowej-i-osobach-starszych
- Gov.pl senior policy notes the current long-term-care problem as fragmentation across institutions and uneven local access to support. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/senior/opieka-dlugoterminowa
- PFRON publishes an active assistive-technology rental program, with the current version effective from 2025-12-23. This is a substitute and market signal, not the startup's core route. Source: https://www.pfron.org.pl/o-funduszu/programy-i-zadania-pfron/programy-i-zadania-real/wypozyczalnia-technologii-wspomagajacych-dla-osob-z-niepelnosprawnoscia/dokumenty-programowe/aktualna-wersja-programu-wypozyczalnia-technologii-wspomagajacych-dla-osob-z-niepelnosprawnoscia-obowiazujaca-od-23-grudnia-2025-roku/

## Competitors And Substitutes

- Existing rehab-rental shops already own inventory, phones, local SEO, delivery know-how, and local trust. This is the main copy risk.
- SilverGo is a home-care management system with mobile visit lists, real-time visit time/location monitoring, documentation, payroll, invoicing, and route optimization. It is a care-operations competitor/substitute, not a rehab-inventory route-book acquisition model. Source: https://www.silvergo.com.pl/
- UDOOR offers one-day bathtub-door modifications from 3,700 PLN with installation and is a substitute for bathroom-accessibility spend. Source: https://udoor.pl/
- PFRON public rental can reduce private equipment-rental willingness to pay in some cases, but urgent hospital discharge and family convenience may still support private bridge demand.

## Commercial Inference

Commodity monthly rentals are too cheap to support a strong startup on their own. The viable wedge must monetize urgency, delivery, setup, bundled safety, family instruction, reactivation of inherited customers, and route density.

The proposed control point is not "rehab rental." It is a paid option over an existing operator's routed transaction flow before full acquisition:

- phone forwarding;
- website/domain CTA;
- customer reactivation list;
- active/recent invoices where consent or lawful transfer basis is handled;
- 20-60 physical assets;
- delivery route notes;
- owner transition support;
- non-compete.

This can be tested under 100,000 PLN by using a deposit plus earnout instead of buying a full business upfront.
