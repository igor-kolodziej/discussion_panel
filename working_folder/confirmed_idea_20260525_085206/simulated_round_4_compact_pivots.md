# Simulated Round 4 Compact Pivots

## Purpose

Round 3 showed that the remaining search space had to control payment rights, claim rights, or cash settlement flow rather than evidence, coordination, access, or delegated dispute handling.

## Gate

Required simulated score: strictly greater than 87.

## Scores

| Candidate | Score | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| R4-F1 PalletClaim Purchase Pool | 72 | Failed |
| R4-F2 DepositException Claim Purchase Pool | 72 | Failed |
| R4-F3 CreditInsurance Denied-Claim Rescue Rights | 54 | Failed |

## R4-F1: PalletClaim Purchase Pool

Strengthened version of the pallet-debt idea. Instead of a soft recovery mandate, the founder would buy or take assignment over specific documented EUR-pallet balance claims from Polish FMCG, household, food, and beauty suppliers.

### Simulated Objections

- Control improves because the startup controls named claims, but it is still asset-by-asset rather than a durable monopoly.
- Counterparties can delay, ignore, or dispute balances.
- Debt collectors, law firms, pallet brokers, and logistics auditors can copy the model once the wedge is visible.
- Profitability depends on strict claim selection, audit fees, and proof of actual collected cash or posted pallet credit.

### Stronger Proof Bar Suggested By Review

- 10,000 PLN actually collected or credited back as pallet value.
- 20,000 PLN in signed settlement acknowledgments.
- Only A-grade documented claims purchased at a steep discount.

## R4-F2: DepositException Claim Purchase Pool

Strengthened version of the deposit-system exception idea. The founder would buy or take exclusive mandates over concrete overpaid or misclassified deposit-system settlement exceptions from small beverage producers/importers.

### Simulated Objections

- Poland's deposit system creates timing pressure, but the control point is still contractual/process control rather than hard monopoly.
- Operator acceptance and settlement adjustment speed remain outside founder control.
- It may become a narrow claims-recovery service unless operator-specific precedent data compounds quickly.
- Score could improve after the first paid recovery, but not enough to justify real Zero to One validation now.

## R4-F3: CreditInsurance Denied-Claim Rescue Rights

Strengthened version of the insured-receivables idea. The founder would take assignment or exclusive mandates over rejected or at-risk trade-credit-insurance claims from Polish SMEs.

### Simulated Objections

- Real pain, but proposed file size and proof target are too small for strong owner earnings.
- Broker conflict risk is high because brokers may be implicated in the claim failure.
- The best files may be contractually incurable due to notice, buyer-limit, collection, or policy-condition failures.
- Brokers, claims lawyers, and insurer-side consultants can copy the playbook.

## Decision

No Round 4 candidate cleared the simulated `>87` gate. Do not contact real Zero to One with these variants.
