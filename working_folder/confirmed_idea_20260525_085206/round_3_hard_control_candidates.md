# Round 3 Hard-Control Candidates

Round 1 and 2 failed because most ideas were either service-like access brokerage or borrowed trust.

Round 3 reuses stronger control-point patterns found in older working folders, while checking duplicate status against confirmed ideas. These are not currently confirmed idea files.

## R3-F1: Insured Receivables Claim-Preservation Desk

Broker-white-label workflow for Polish trade-credit insurance brokers and insured SME policyholders. It monitors insured receivables weekly from AR aging exports, policy terms, buyer-limit files, invoice/payment data, broker/insurer deadlines, and prepares overdue declarations, payment-plan approvals, collection handoff packs, and claim evidence before eligibility is damaged.

Control point: signed broker/policyholder mandate over live insured AR data plus insurer-specific deadline/evidence maps and weekly claim-preservation calendar.

## R3-F2: PalletDebt Recovery Rail

90-day recovery mandate over EUR-pallet receivables for Polish FMCG/beauty/household/food manufacturers shipping recurring loads to retailer DCs, distributors, and 3PL warehouses.

Control point: signed recovery mandate over named counterparties, CMR/WZ/PZ/pallet-note evidence, authority to request statements and send balance disputes, and counterparty-specific dispute memory.

## R3-F3: Deposit System Settlement Exception Rail

Operator-ready exception recovery sprint for small and mid-sized beverage producers/importers/distributors dealing with Poland's deposit-system settlement ledger.

Control point: signed mandate over deposit settlement files, SKU/EAN master data, operator statements, domestic/export sales records, return/credit notes, exception taxonomy, and adjustment packets.

## R3-F4: Class B Autoclave Continuity Locker

Local paid retainer for small dental/aesthetic/podology clinics that reserves validated Class B autoclave emergency loaners, qualified technician SLA, biological/chemical indicator process, and continuity documentation.

Control point: owned/reserved validated emergency autoclaves plus paid priority retainers.

## R3-F5: Lift Door-Drive Donor Bank

Traceable donor inventory for older residential lift door systems and control modules, sold only through licensed lift-service firms.

Control point: first-look salvage rights from modernization crews plus owned tested donor modules and service-firm reservation deposits.

## R3-F6: CzysteCash Subsidy Receivables Purchaser

Buy or advance against legally documented Czyste Powietrze subsidy/invoice receivables from small installers after completion evidence and payment-request status are verified.

Control point: assigned receivable rights, installer origination agreements, verified completion/payment-request evidence, and underwriting data by WFOŚiGW/project type/documentation gap/payout timing.
