# Round 2 Resource-First Candidates

Round 1 failed because rehab-equipment rental economics were too commodity-like and copyable.

## R2-F1: Medycyna Pracy Fast Lane

Buy or reserve near-term occupational-medicine appointment capacity across small authorized clinics, labs, vision/audiometry providers, and occupational physicians, then sell 24-48 hour exam completion to SMEs, EORs, staffing agencies, payroll firms, and employers with urgent UoP starts.

Control point: city-level appointment liquidity for legally required employee exams, not a website.

2-month proof: reserve or prepay weekly slots across 5-10 clinics in Warsaw/Poznan/Wroclaw/Krakow, get 3-5 employer/EOR/staffing pilots, process 50-100 paid candidates, prove same/next-day completion, margin after clinic cost, and repeat employer usage.

Why it might clear the gate: employers cannot legally start workers without required occupational medical clearance; appointment supply is fragmented; large clinic networks are not optimized for urgent small-employer starts; one unknown founder can secure unused clinic capacity by prepaying or revenue-sharing without privileged introductions.

## R2-F2: EUDAMED Panic Desk For Polish Micro-Importers

Control point: SKU/certificate corpus and importer evidence workflow for a narrow ugly niche such as dental accessories, aesthetic devices, or diagnostic accessories.

2-month proof: two paid importers and two consultant referral agreements, with onboarding fees.

Risk: looks like prior compliance/evidence-pack ideas and regulatory consultants can copy.
