## Strengthened Finalist Set

### F1: Critical Controls Spare Custody Vault

Pivot from generic obsolete PLC/HMI donor bank. The founder does not try to outstock Radwell, Siemens, or Polish repair shops. Instead, the first product is a paid custody vault for Polish food, FMCG, packaging, and small manufacturing plants running discontinued Siemens S7-300/ET200M/HMI or similar control families.

The customer sends its own cold spares, program backups, panel photos, and machine-criticality map. The startup tests, labels, photographs, stores, insures, and documents a same-day swap kit, then charges setup plus monthly standby. The founder may buy only a narrow top-up donor inventory after deposits.

Control point: physical custody of customer-owned tested spare modules, program/image backups, compatibility notes, swap SOPs, and paid standby rights.

### F2: Senior Scam Handset Custody Rail

Pivot from true phone-number custody, which is legally and telecom-operationally heavy. The first product is an Android-first senior handset and home-phone setup where unknown callers are blocked/routed, trusted contacts are whitelisted, family escalation is configured, and bank/police verification scripts are physically printed and installed.

Control point: configured handset policy, family-admin custody, allowlist, recovery map, and monthly trusted-contact maintenance.

### F3: HardLock Owned-Device Doomscroll Custody

No BYOD app blocker. The startup owns or leases a managed Android phone or supervised iPhone, holds admin credentials, disables high-risk apps/sites, provides a clean phone for essential calls/maps/banking/2FA, and optionally locks the original phone physically for 8-week exam or deadline sprints.

Control point: owned/reset managed device, MDM enrollment, external admin custody, lockbox custody, prepaid 8-week commitment.

### F4: CPAP Mask Trial + OSCAR Rescue Bank

For diagnosed CPAP/APAP users failing therapy due to mask leak, discomfort, and lack of data interpretation. The startup owns sealed mask/cushion trial inventory, SD-card readers, pulse oximeters, fit accessories, and a standardized "bring this to your clinician" leak/AHI evidence workflow.

Control point: immediate sealed mask inventory, hygienic trial logistics, data intake process, and replacement conversion path.

### F5: Lithium Battery Quarantine Intake Rail

Pivot from battery recycling/repair to compliant intake custody. The startup places fire-resistant intake boxes at e-bike/scooter repair shops and routes damaged/orphan batteries to licensed recycler/collector partners with photos, labels, chain-of-custody records, and pickup SLA.

Control point: shop intake points, physical quarantine containers, chain-of-custody records, recycler handoff route.

### F6: Authorized Deadstock De-Branding Vault

No public marketplace. Brands/importers/3PLs with branded surplus sign written authorization for de-branding, relabeling, donation, export, or controlled B2B release. The startup performs secure intake, SKU cataloging, proof photos, physical de-branding, and release only to pre-contracted off-takers.

Control point: written brand authorization, physical custody of stock, de-branding station, chain-of-custody proof, off-taker commitments.

## Initial Recommendation Before Simulation

Advance F1 first. It has the clearest B2B urgency, highest ticket size, hard physical custody, low capital requirement if the first assets are customer-owned, and better founder fit than trust-heavy B2C care or regulated health workflows.
