## Common Objections

- Delegated transaction authority is valuable but usually revocable.
- Incumbents can internalize the process once the founder proves the economics.
- Several ideas are profitable services but not strong domination candidates.
- Backlog recovery often fades unless the founder becomes the live monthly exception route.
- Stronger ideas need either a harder acquired resource, a paid customer book, or a structural reason incumbents will not copy.

## Pivot Implication

The next pass should test acquisition/roll-up or rights-control ideas where the founder can buy a neglected book, route, lead source, contract, or database cheaply. The control point should be acquired or prepaid, not merely promised by a partner.
