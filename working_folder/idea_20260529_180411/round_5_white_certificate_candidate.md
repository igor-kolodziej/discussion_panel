# Round 5 Candidate - WhiteCert Pre-Start Rights Router

## Candidate

**WhiteCert Pre-Start Rights Router** captures Polish white-certificate value before it is destroyed by project start. It signs routed-flow agreements with small energy-efficiency installers and vendors, then converts eligible pre-contract quotes into customer mandates, auditor-backed applications, no-start covenants, and success-fee rights over the resulting certificates.

## Hidden Insight

Most small energy-efficiency sellers do not lose white-certificate value because the project is ineligible. They lose it because the sales process starts the work too early: contract signed, equipment ordered, deposit paid, or implementation begun before the URE application and audit path are secured.

The scarce asset is not white-certificate expertise in general. It is the **pre-start legal/evidence position**: installer routes the quote before closing, customer signs a no-start covenant and mandate, auditor capacity is reserved, baseline evidence is captured, and the certificate economics are assigned before the project becomes dead.

## Beachhead

Polish SME projects sold by small installers/vendors where the sales conversation happens before work starts and certificate value is often missed:

- commercial refrigeration and cold-room modernization;
- process heat / boiler / heat-recovery improvements;
- industrial motor, fan, pump, and VFD upgrades;
- building automation and HVAC controls for small hotels, warehouses, gyms, workshops, and food SMEs;
- non-generic OZE-adjacent energy-efficiency projects routed by the founder's family and adjacent installer network.

Avoid generic lighting and compressed-air as the first wedge because existing confirmed ideas already touch EPREL lighting and compressor data-access angles.

## Product Wedge

The first product is not consulting, a dashboard, or a generic subsidy desk. It is a transaction timing rail:

1. Installer sends a pre-contract quote before the customer signs or orders.
2. WhiteCert screens eligibility and expected certificate value.
3. Customer signs a mandate, no-start covenant, baseline-evidence checklist, and success-fee / assignment terms.
4. A qualified energy auditor prepares or approves the audit and URE application.
5. WhiteCert tracks whether URE accepts, rejects, or requests correction, and feeds outcomes back into the project-category scoring model.

## Primary Control Point

- Installer routed-flow agreement over pre-start quotes in named project categories.
- Customer mandate and no-start covenant before work begins.
- Success-fee or assignment right to part of certificate proceeds.
- Reserved auditor capacity for due-soon quotes.
- Proprietary ledger of project types, baseline evidence, URE objections/refusals, realized certificate values, and installer conversion behavior.

## 90-Day POC Under 100,000 PLN

- Sign 3 non-family installer/vendor routing agreements for pre-start quotes in two narrow categories.
- Use family/company network only as credibility and learning leverage, not as the sole channel.
- Secure an energy lawyer template and one energy auditor retainer.
- Screen 20 real pre-contract quotes.
- Get 5 signed customer mandates with no-start covenants and success-fee / assignment terms.
- File or prepare 2 URE-ready applications with auditor signoff.
- Get at least one paid retainer, application fee, or enforceable success-fee right.
- Kill if installers will not route before closing, customers refuse no-start mandates, project values are too small, or URE/auditor feedback shows the category is mostly ineligible.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Secure It

The ask is small and concrete: installers already need a way to make their quotes more attractive without discounting equipment, and customers like "found money" if it does not delay the project too much. The founder can start with scriptable outbound and family-adjacent installer credibility, while qualified auditors handle the technical/legal audit layer.

## Why Incumbents Cannot Copy With One Contract

ESCOs, utilities, auditors, and engineering firms can process white certificates, but they are not embedded at the exact moment small installers quote sub-ESCO projects. The compounding advantage is not knowing the regulation; it is owning routed quote flow before contracts are signed and learning which small project categories reliably produce positive certificate economics.
