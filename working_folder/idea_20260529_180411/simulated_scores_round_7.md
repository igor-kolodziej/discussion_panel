## Common Objections

- Inventory-led ideas still suffer from dead stock, logistics, and slow turnover unless prepayment is proven first.
- Project-based or incident-based businesses often lack repeatability and retention.
- Incumbents with existing buyer access can copy many of the wedges as an upsell.
- A directly controllable physical asset is necessary but not sufficient; the asset also needs buyer workflow access or transaction flow.
- 5h/day founder fit is strained when emergency response or warehouse-heavy operations are central.

## Pivot Implication

Next candidates should prefer:

- transaction-flow control over inventory-only control;
- paid workflow embedding rather than occasional incident response;
- recurring budgets and unavoidable deadlines;
- direct buyer pull that can be proven with deposits or paid pilots;
- a control point a small founder can secure without incumbent trust, but that incumbents are unlikely to prioritize because the niche looks too weird or small at first.
