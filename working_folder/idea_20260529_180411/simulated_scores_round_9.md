# Simulated Scores - Round 9

Round 9 tested acquired or optioned customer-book concepts. The acquired-book mechanism improved cold-start logic, but no candidate cleared the simulated `>85` gate. Do not send any Round 9 idea to real Zero To One validation.

## Common Objections

- Customer books may be personal goodwill rather than transferable contracts.
- Many concepts become profitable local service businesses but not domination-oriented startups.
- Earnout-heavy structures reduce capital risk but do not by themselves create defensibility.
- Contractors or incumbents can take the relationship once the handoff is complete.
- Most acquired-book ideas require expert accountability that does not match the founder's available time unless delivery is contractor-led from day one.

## Pivot Implication

The next pass should test scarce capacity, rights, or production bottlenecks where the founder can reserve a resource that customers cannot easily self-serve, and where the sale is capacity access rather than advisory labor.
