# Round 9 - Customer-Book Acquisition Candidates

## Selection Premise

Previous rounds failed because delegated authority was useful but revocable. This round tests acquired or optioned customer books where the founder can control recurring customer flow through a signed assignment, seller handoff, earn-out, and client consents before spending serious capital.

## Candidate A - KSeF-Native Marketplace Accounting Book Buyout

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire or option the client book of a small Polish accounting office that serves Allegro/Amazon/Shopify/D2C sellers and whose owner is overloaded by mandatory KSeF, marketplace payout reconciliation, VAT/JPK, inventory documents, and platform-fee complexity. The founder buys only a narrow client book: 20-50 recurring marketplace sellers with client consent, seller handoff, data-processing agreements, and 3-6 months of transition support from the accountant. Delivery uses one senior accounting contractor plus founder-built ingestion/reconciliation tooling for KSeF invoices, marketplace settlement exports, payment fees, WZ/PZ documents, and VAT checks. The control point is assigned recurring accounting contracts plus live invoice/payout workflow access, not generic accounting software or a KSeF dashboard.

## Candidate B - BDO Packaging Fee Client-Book Buyout

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire or option a small environmental-consulting/accounting client book of Polish micro-importers and ecommerce sellers that need recurring BDO packaging records, product-fee calculations, and annual packaging reporting but are too small for large consultants. The founder gets assigned recurring mandates and historical packaging data, then automates import-to-packaging-record capture and charges fixed monthly fees plus correction/rescue fees. The control point is assigned recurring compliance workflow and importer packaging history, not a generic BDO report desk.

## Candidate C - Food-Safety Micro-Producer HACCP Record Route Buyout

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire a retiring food-technologist/HACCP consultant's recurring micro-producer client book: bakeries, small meat shops, caterers, food trucks, and local producers that need HACCP/GHP/GMP documentation, audit preparation, allergen/label changes, and periodic sanitary-inspection support. The founder keeps a certified food-safety contractor for expert sign-off and systematizes records, reminders, and label-change workflows. The control point is assigned client relationship plus historical HACCP files and annual update cycles, not one-off food-safety consulting.

## Candidate D - Landlord Deposit & Damage Recovery Book

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire or option a small property-management administrator's tenant-exit inspection and deposit-dispute workflow for mid-market Warsaw rental apartments. The founder controls check-in/out photo evidence, inventory protocols, repair quotes, claim letters, and settlement flow for a defined landlord book; monetization is a fixed exit-inspection fee plus success share on recovered damage costs. The control point is assigned landlord workflow and evidence standard before tenant move-out, not generic legal letters.

## Candidate E - Used-Car Warranty Claim Book Buyout

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire or option the claims/admin book of a small used-car dealer warranty intermediary that processes warranty-plan, defect, and goodwill claims for independent dealers. The founder controls dealer mandates, claim history, photo/diagnostic evidence, repair invoice flows, and insurer/administrator response data; monetization is a success fee plus dealer retainer. The control point is assigned dealer claim flow, not a vehicle inspection marketplace.

## Candidate F - Local Grant-Admin Client Book Buyout

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire or option a retiring grant/admin consultant's recurring book of small municipalities, NGOs, and associations that need drawdown, procurement, indicator, and settlement documentation for already-awarded Polish/EU local grants. The founder controls active project calendars, document history, and settlement deadlines; monetization is monthly project-admin retainer plus success fees for unblocked reimbursements. The control point is active funded-project workflow access, not grant writing.

## Non-Redundancy Notes

- Candidate A is not another KSeF exception desk because it controls an acquired recurring client book and complete marketplace accounting workflow rather than only routed exceptions from an accounting office.
- Candidate B is not CBAM/EPREL/DORA/RED because buyer, regulation, and workflow differ, but it may be punished as a compliance wrapper.
- Candidate C is a recurring operating file route, not a dashboard, but may be too service-like.
- Candidate D is not parcel claims; it is pre-positioned evidence control in landlord workflows, but it may be trust-heavy.
- Candidate E may be too close to generic claims recovery.
- Candidate F may be slow, trust-heavy, and procurement-adjacent.
