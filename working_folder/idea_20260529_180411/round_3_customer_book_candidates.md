# Round 3 Customer-Book / Workflow-Control Candidates

## Reason For Pivot

The first two rounds failed because they relied on cold selling, broad inventory, or commodity resource arbitrage. Round 3 starts from a more concrete control point: acquire or option an already-routed recurring compliance workflow from a small specialist provider, especially where retirement/succession creates a seller need and the buyer has a legal recurring obligation.

## Candidate R3-A - DentX-Ray Compliance Calendar Buyout

Acquire or option the recurring dental RTG specialist-test calendar and customer book of a small Polish radiation-testing / medical-physics provider serving private dental clinics with intraoral, panoramic, cephalometric, and CBCT devices.

The product is not a generic compliance dashboard. It is a purchased or earnout-controlled book of clinics with test due dates, device inventory, last protocols, contact history, and authorization to schedule renewal tests. Execution uses the seller for transition plus PCA-accredited lab capacity for specialist tests. The founder owns scheduling, renewal reminders, device records, invoicing, and cross-sell of test-readiness packets; tests themselves are performed only by qualified/accredited parties.

Control point: signed option/asset-purchase over a named provider's calendar and customer book, plus seller transition agreement and migrated payment/scheduling flow.

## Candidate R3-B - CleanBench Validation Route Buyout

Acquire the recurring validation route of a small clean-bench / biosafety-cabinet / laminar-flow-cabinet testing technician serving IVF labs, hospital pharmacies, microbiology labs, compounding rooms, and small GMP users.

Control point: transferred customer route, device inventory, HEPA/filter history, due-date calendar, and retained technician or subcontractor capacity.

## Candidate R3-C - RetainRelease Public Contract Security Recovery Rail

Build a claim/release rail for Polish SME construction and installation contractors whose public-contract performance securities, warranty retentions, bank/insurance guarantee deposits, or post-acceptance holdbacks should be released but are missed, delayed, or poorly documented.

Control point: signed recovery mandate or assignment for named public-contract release claims plus a database of contract dates, acceptance/warranty periods, contracting authorities, and release outcomes.

## Candidate R3-D - UDTLift/Forklift Service Calendar Buyout

Acquire the recurring maintenance/documentation calendar of a retiring small forklift/hoist/warehouse-lift service firm serving SMEs whose equipment must stay ready for UDT inspection and safe operation.

Control point: acquired customer book, service records, UDT inspection calendar, technician handoff, and recurring maintenance invoices.

## Candidate R3-E - FoodMetal Detector Validation Route Buyout

Acquire the customer book and annual validation route of a technician/provider validating metal detectors, checkweighers, X-ray inspection systems, and foreign-body controls in Polish food plants under customer audits and HACCP/BRC/IFS pressure.

Control point: transferred device inventory, test-piece records, due dates, audit nonconformity history, and recurring validation workflow.

## Candidate R3-F - Local Exhaust / BHP Measurement Route Buyout

Acquire a retiring occupational-hygiene provider's recurring route for local exhaust ventilation measurements, workplace exposure measurements, and BHP documentation for small manufacturing shops.

Control point: customer book, measurement calendar, historical results, and subcontracted accredited measurement capacity.

## Initial Ranking

1. **R3-A DentX-Ray Compliance Calendar Buyout** - strongest legal recurrence, defined device calendar, high clinic density, clear acquisition control point.
2. **R3-E FoodMetal Detector Validation Route Buyout** - good FMCG/food fit and recurring audit pressure; less legally mandatory but higher B2B value.
3. **R3-C RetainRelease** - direct money recovery, but may look like another recovery desk and requires legal/collections boundaries.
4. **R3-B CleanBench Validation Route Buyout** - strong regulated labs, but likely smaller and trust-heavy.
5. **R3-D UDTLift/Forklift Service Calendar Buyout** - recurring but crowded and technician-heavy.
6. **R3-F Local Exhaust / BHP Measurement Route Buyout** - recurring but may be fragmented, low-ticket, and commodity.
