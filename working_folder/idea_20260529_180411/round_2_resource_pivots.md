# Round 2 Resource-First Pivots

## Pivot Rule From Round 1

The first panel rejected the equipment-continuity cluster because the founder would not truly control enough trust, technician capacity, or inventory uniqueness. Round 2 therefore prioritizes scarce physical resource rights, signed recovery/pickup options, prepaid demand, or regulatory supply constraints that can be proven before major capital.

## Candidate R2-A - ReclaimReserve R404A/R507 Recovery Rights

Own or reserve legal reclaimed high-GWP refrigerant flow for Polish refrigeration contractors serving small supermarkets, bakeries, cold stores, and food producers that still run R404A/R507 systems.

The founder does not operate as an informal gas trader. The first POC signs recovery-right assignments or first-call agreements with certified F-gas contractors and decommissioning jobs, routes recovered gas to an authorized reclamation facility such as PROZON or equivalent, and buys certified regenerated cylinders only after prepaid contractor demand. The beachhead is R404A/R507 because EU rules increasingly restrict high-GWP virgin gas for service while reclaimed/recycled supply remains useful for keeping legacy systems alive.

Control point: signed rights to recoverable gas from specific decommissioning/retrofit jobs, certified chain-of-custody, and physically stocked certified reclaimed cylinders against prepaid contractor reserve contracts.

## Candidate R2-B - MonumentRoof Matched Historic Roof Tile Lots

Buy or option complete matched lots of old ceramic roof tiles from demolition, roof replacement, barn clearance, and estate-renovation jobs before they are split or discarded, then sell documented lots to conservation roofers, heritage architects, churches, boutique developers, and manor-house renovators needing repair-compatible material.

Control point: physical ownership plus first-refusal pickup rights over matched lots by profile, color, age, region, frost condition, quantity, and documentation.

## Candidate R2-C - PiecKafle Complete Stove Tile Sets

Control complete antique Polish ceramic tiled stove sets from apartments, villas, schools, and institutional clear-outs before they are smashed or sold piece by piece. Sell numbered, photographed, deconstructed, restorable sets to premium interior architects, heritage renovators, boutique hospitality projects, and specialist stove builders.

Control point: owned complete tile stove sets plus first-look agreements with demolition, renovation, and estate-clearance crews.

## Candidate R2-D - BitzerCore Cold-Room Compressor Core Bank

Secure used but rebuildable Bitzer/Copeland commercial refrigeration compressor cores from decommissioned supermarket and cold-room systems, reserve rebuild capacity with one specialist, and sell emergency refurbished cores to refrigeration contractors whose food-retail clients face downtime.

Control point: owned core inventory from decommissioning jobs plus rebuild/test records and contractor prepayments.

## Candidate R2-E - HotelLinen Mono-Fiber Exit Rights

Sign pickup rights for predictable decommissioned white cotton/polycotton linens from hotels, laundries, and hospitals before they enter mixed textile waste. Sort, grade, and sell mono-fiber streams to wiper manufacturers, textile recyclers, insulation/felt producers, or upcycling workshops.

Control point: signed recurring linen exit rights and sorted inventory.

## Candidate R2-F - SteriLoan Dental Autoclave Bridge Pool

Own 2-3 validated small autoclaves and sell emergency short-term loaner coverage to dental clinics whose sterilizer failure blocks operations. Partner with a certified technician for setup, test cycles, and documentation.

Control point: owned loaner equipment and prepaid clinic coverage contracts.

## Local Ranking

1. **R2-A ReclaimReserve** - strongest regulatory momentum, resource scarcity, physically provable control, clear buyer urgency, and non-redundancy.
2. **R2-B MonumentRoof** - strong physical-control thesis, but close to the existing HeritageDoor shape.
3. **R2-C PiecKafle** - visually premium and controllable, but smaller market and still adjacent to HeritageDoor.
4. **R2-D BitzerCore** - credible pain, but similar to equipment-continuity problems and technician trust.
5. **R2-E HotelLinen** - real supply, but likely commodity/low margin.
6. **R2-F SteriLoan** - urgent pain, but service/equipment loan model is copyable and compliance-heavy.
