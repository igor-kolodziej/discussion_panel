## Filtered Finalists

### F1 - ChromatoSwap HPLC Uptime Pool

- **Hidden insight:** Small regulated QC labs do not primarily want cheaper used equipment; they want downtime optionality for old but validated instruments because batch release and customer deadlines depend on continuity.
- **Control point:** Physically owned, tested donor HPLC modules; paid standby retainers; compatibility and failure-history database by model/firmware/module.
- **Cold start:** Buy 2-3 donor Agilent 1100/1200 units or modules, contract one chromatography service technician, then sell prepaid reserve coverage to 3-5 Polish private QC labs.
- **Why it may win:** High downtime cost, recurring retainer, local dispatch advantage, defensible data from installed base and module performance.
- **Main risk:** Trust and technical competence; existing used equipment/service firms can copy if the product remains generic repair.

### F2 - PLC Ghost Stock

- **Hidden insight:** SME factories running older packaging lines are not irrationally under-modernized; they keep old PLCs because validated processes, operator habits, and production schedules make modernization riskier than stocking exact drop-in backups.
- **Control point:** Owned tested legacy PLC/servo inventory plus captured program backups from one automation integrator's installed base.
- **Cold start:** Start with one Polish food/packaging automation contractor; offer to finance and organize exact spare kits for their 20 highest-risk installed lines.
- **Why it may win:** Downtime is expensive; paid standby can work; expands by installed-base map.
- **Main risk:** Without program-backup access, it becomes a commodity used-parts reseller.

### F3 - ImplantKey Orphan Components Bank

- **Hidden insight:** Dentists do not need another implant planning app; they need a way to rescue old implants whose system, driver, or screw is unknown or discontinued.
- **Control point:** Owned discontinued component inventory, visual identification data, and paid rescue workflow with prosthodontists.
- **Cold start:** Acquire lots from retiring dentists, labs, importers, and liquidation listings; sell paid identification + component packs.
- **Why it may win:** Urgent patient cases and high willingness to pay.
- **Main risk:** Medical-device compliance, liability, and trust are heavy for an unknown founder.

### F4 - LiftBoard Emergency Pool

- **Hidden insight:** Small elevator maintenance firms lose trust not because they cannot service lifts, but because rare board failures create weeks of tenant anger while parts are hunted ad hoc.
- **Control point:** Owned tested boards and door-controller modules for a few high-frequency legacy lift models; paid reserve rights for maintenance firms.
- **Cold start:** Interview 20 maintenance firms; buy only modules repeatedly named as downtime bottlenecks.
- **Why it may win:** Recurring maintenance relationships and urgent downtime.
- **Main risk:** UDT/safety responsibility and exact compatibility make the first product slow.

### F5 - StabilityChamber Overflow Options

- **Hidden insight:** Small regulated manufacturers treat stability capacity as infrastructure, but spare validated capacity is fragmented and invisible when a chamber fails.
- **Control point:** Prepaid/optioned chamber slots with labs plus customer contracts.
- **Cold start:** Secure paid demand first, then reserve slots.
- **Why it may win:** High consequence of study interruption.
- **Main risk:** Depends on third-party capacity access; partner-dependent before resource control is proven.

### F6 - Dissolution Tester Uptime Pool

- **Hidden insight:** Generic lab-equipment service underserves old dissolution testers because emergency continuity depends on exact bath, shaft, vessel, autosampler, and method fit rather than broad repair capability.
- **Control point:** Owned tested parts and one technician bench for a narrow instrument family.
- **Cold start:** Similar to HPLC, but focused on pharma QC.
- **Why it may win:** Very high downtime pain.
- **Main risk:** Narrow market and lower founder fit than HPLC/food/cosmetics QC.
