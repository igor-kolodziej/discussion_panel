# Round 8 - Transaction-Flow Candidate Set

## Selection Premise

Asset ownership alone did not clear the simulated bar. The strongest prior unconfirmed pattern was delegated authority over a routed money-leakage stream, but parcel claims are redundant and were rejected. This round tests higher-value, more repeatable transaction flows where the founder can secure an initial mandate under 100,000 PLN and produce cash proof before software.

## Candidate A - PalletDebt Recovery Rail

**Exact idea wording:** Build an embedded EUR/EPAL pallet-balance recovery rail for one Polish food, beauty, or household-goods manufacturer or 3PL with repeated retail/DC shipments. The founder gets a signed mandate to import shipment notes, WZ/PZ, CMR/POD documents, carrier invoices, and customer pallet ledgers; reconciles who owes pallets or cash; files documented claims/invoices/offset requests; and takes a success fee plus optional monthly reconciliation fee. The control point is delegated authority over a recurring pallet-debt stream and outcome data by counterparty, not pallet-management software or pallet trading.

## Candidate B - PrivateClinic Rejection Recovery Rail

**Exact idea wording:** Build a private-medical revenue recovery rail for small Polish specialist clinics that bill private insurers, employer medical-benefit providers, or occupational-medicine customers through portals and manual back-office workflows. Start with one clinic group and only rejected/underpaid invoices or visit claims from a narrow specialty. The founder imports rejection/export files, checks contract-price tables, visit codes, eligibility, referral/document completeness, and resubmission windows; resubmits or prepares appeal packs under clinic authority; and charges a success fee on recovered revenue plus a fixed monthly exception-cleanup fee. The control point is routed rejection flow and payer-specific outcome data, not generic medical software.

## Candidate C - KSeF Exception Queue Operator

**Exact idea wording:** Build a KSeF invoice-exception continuity rail for one Polish accounting office serving 100-300 SMEs during the 2026 mandatory e-invoicing rollout. The office routes only rejected, stuck, duplicate, missing-NIP, buyer-master, and acceptance-deadline exceptions into the founder's workflow; the founder classifies, fixes, escalates, and documents cases under accountant authority. Monetization is per-client exception retainer plus urgent rescue fees. The control point is live exception-queue access and corrected-case memory from one accounting office, not a generic KSeF dashboard.

## Candidate D - PFRON Wage-Subsidy Capture Lane

**Exact idea wording:** Build a PFRON wage-subsidy capture lane for Polish payroll/accounting bureaus that serve employers of disabled workers but do not specialize in monthly subsidy documentation. The founder signs one bureau-level mandate, identifies eligible but under-claimed cases, assembles recurring document packs, tracks deadlines and rejections, and charges success/fixed fees only where subsidies are actually captured. The control point is recurring payroll-data access and bureau-level routing of eligible cases, not generic HR consulting.

## Candidate E - Reusable Glass Deposit Float Reconciliation

**Exact idea wording:** Build a reusable-glass deposit float and reconciliation rail for small Polish beverage producers entering the national deposit-return system. Start with producers using reusable glass bottles and selling through regional retailers/HoReCa: reconcile issued deposits, returned bottles, breakage, operator/retailer settlements, and customer balances; create invoice/credit packs; and take a fixed monthly fee plus recovered deposit share. The control point is live bottle/deposit ledger flow and counterparty settlement history, not an RVM or label-testing service.

## Candidate F - WarrantyCredit Recovery Rail

**Exact idea wording:** Build a warranty-credit recovery rail for small Polish B2B equipment distributors and service companies, starting outside PV/heat pumps: commercial kitchen equipment, refrigeration, cleaning machines, industrial label printers, or small lab devices. The founder gets delegated access to service tickets, serials, photos, invoices, and manufacturer portals; identifies eligible unpaid warranty credits, files claims/appeals, and monetizes as success fee plus monthly recovery retainer. The control point is manufacturer-specific claim-outcome data and routed service-ticket flow, not a repair business.

## Round 8 Non-Redundancy Notes

- PalletDebt is not CarrierProof: the transaction is reusable load-carrier debt, not parcel loss/damage, and the buyer is a manufacturer/3PL logistics-finance owner rather than ecommerce merchants.
- PrivateClinic is not biosignal/supplement safety or a health claim product; it is a revenue-cycle exception rail for clinics.
- KSeF is a current Polish infrastructure change but risks being too generic or too close to compliance workflow wrappers.
- PFRON is public-money capture via payroll workflow, not a generic grant desk, but legal/payroll trust may be high.
- Glass deposit reconciliation uses deposit transaction flow, not packaging acceptance testing.
- WarrantyCredit must avoid PV/heat-pump/PV battery installer repetition.
