# Real Working-Chat Gate 10 Failure - LatePayClaim Pool

Timestamp: 20260523_004810

## Candidate

LatePayClaim Pool.

## Scores

- Simulated Zero To One score: `84/100`
- Real working-chat Zero To One score: `74/100`

## Verdict

Fail under active threshold `>=82`. Do not send to fresh validation and do not write a confirmed idea file.

## Working-Chat Feedback

The working chat agreed the legal right and the control point are real: cession/assignment or exclusive mandate over specific ancillary claims, backed by invoice/payment evidence and debtor-response data. It explicitly said the cap-below-82 rule does not apply, but still scored the idea `74/100` because the business quality is not yet strong enough.

## Strongest Objections

- A `100k PLN` face-value claim pool is not the same as a `100k PLN` asset; voluntary collection rates may be low.
- Debtors can dispute delivery dates, payment terms, acceptance dates, offsets, waivers, limitation periods, assignment validity, or abuse-of-right arguments.
- Fixed 40/70/100 EUR compensation can look abusive when multiplied over many small invoices.
- Assignment is not frictionless and must filter anti-assignment, settlement, waiver, and full-and-final-payment language.
- Public-sector debtors may be attractive but slow.
- Legal operations can eat the margin unless claims are extremely clean.
- Seller urgency is low because the principal has already been paid.
- Law firms, debt collectors, factoring firms, and legal-tech vendors can copy the workflow.

## Tightened Gate Suggested

Pass only if a 2-month POC gets:

- 5 signed sellers;
- `100k PLN` verified assignable/mandated face value;
- 50 demand packets sent;
- at least `20k PLN` cash collected or written debtor acknowledgment.

Court-filed claims alone should not count equally because they prove legal assertiveness, not recoverability.

## Decision

Pivot away from post-payment nuisance-claim pools unless the next shape has higher willingness to pay, less adversarial collection, and paid proof before long legal recovery cycles.
