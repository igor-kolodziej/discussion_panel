# Simulated Gate: CosmeticPIF Launch Release Desk

## Exact Candidate Wording
CosmeticPIF Launch Release Desk. A reserved-safety-assessor and Responsible-Person evidence desk for Polish and CEE indie cosmetics, beauty, and dermocosmetic brands or contract manufacturers whose Amazon, Allegro, retailer, distributor, or batch launch is blocked by missing or incomplete EU Cosmetics Regulation product information file, cosmetic product safety report, CPNP notification, label and claims substantiation, Responsible Person appointment, ingredient/allergen documentation, or manufacturer/GMP evidence. It does not sell generic cosmetics consulting, branding, formula design, marketplace management, or legal advice. It activates only on a live SKU blocker: retailer or distributor portal request, marketplace compliance case, contract-manufacturer handoff, or planned batch release within 2-8 weeks.

Inputs: formula/INCI, supplier COAs/SDS, packaging and label artwork, claims, target countries and languages, manufacturer/GMP docs, stability/micro/challenge tests if any, prior CPNP/PIF status, RP status, and SKU deadline.

Output: launch-readiness gap map, safety-assessor/RP handoff pack, label/claims red flags, PIF/CPNP document checklist, retailer/marketplace evidence packet, corrected launch sequence, and for straightforward low-risk SKUs coordination through reserved qualified safety-assessor/RP capacity to produce or finalize CPSR/PIF/CPNP/RP work. Edge cases route to qualified cosmetic safety assessors or lawyers.

Control point: reserved paid safety-assessor and RP capacity for live launch-blocked SKUs, SKU evidence custody, brand/contract-manufacturer case routing, accepted/rejected retailer and marketplace evidence memory, and recurring PIF/RP update calendar.

Two-month proof: secure written capacity terms with 2 cosmetic safety assessors/RP providers; process 12 live SKU launch blockers from 4 brands, contract manufacturers, or agencies; get 6 CPNP/RP/PIF-ready submissions or retailer evidence acceptances; collect 35k PLN cash; 2 brands pay for ongoing PIF/RP update monitoring; keep subcontractor prepayment under 35k PLN.

Pricing: 3k-8k PLN per urgent SKU pack plus pass-through safety-assessor/RP costs; 1k-3k PLN/month portfolio monitoring for brands with 10+ SKUs.

Hidden insight: indie beauty brands and contract manufacturers do not pay for abstract EU cosmetics compliance until a launch slot, retailer onboarding, or marketplace SKU is blocked. The scarce first asset is not a checklist; it is reserved safety/RP capacity plus retailer-ready evidence packaging at the moment revenue is stuck.

## Simulated Score
87/100.

## Why It Clears 82
The buyer pain is tied to a live revenue gate rather than optional compliance improvement. The control point is more concrete than generic consulting because the founder must reserve paid assessor/RP capacity and build a repeated accepted-evidence memory for retailer and marketplace blockers. The initial resource is provable within two months through signed capacity terms, paid urgent SKU cases, and acceptance outcomes.

## Strongest Caveats
- Cosmetic safety assessment and Responsible Person obligations cannot be faked or automated away; qualified partners must do the regulated work.
- If assessor/RP providers already own customer acquisition, the desk can look like a lead-generation layer unless it controls live blocker routing and evidence packaging.
- High-risk formulations, borderline medicinal claims, nanomaterials, SPF, or sensitive baby/medical-adjacent claims may exceed a solo founder's liability boundary.
- Marketplaces and retailers may still reject evidence for opaque reasons, so the offer must sell a release pack and handoff outcome, not guaranteed listing.
- The founder must avoid legal/medical advice positioning and operate as a launch operations desk with qualified subcontractors.

## Decision
Pass simulated gate. Submit to real working-chat gate.
