# Simulated Scores Round 24

Round 24 tested non-door complete-set salvage categories inspired by the HeritageDoor control pattern.

## Scores

| Candidate | Score | Control Point | Result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| KafelSuite Heritage Stove Kits | 74 | Tier 2.5 / operational physical-control point | Fail |
| CastIron Heat Suites | 76 | Tier 2 local operational control point | Fail |
| StoreCold Display Case Suites | 68 | Tier 2 relationship/supply-option control point | Fail |

## Decision

No candidate clears the lowered `82` simulated threshold. The complete-set pattern only worked strongly for internal doors because demand is more visible, liability is lower, weight/logistics are manageable, and fit uncertainty is easier to inspect. Stove/radiator/refrigeration suites introduce too much functional/liability/handling drag.

## Next Pivot Rule

Avoid heavy functional objects unless the product can be sold as decorative/non-critical with high margin and low buyer responsibility. Prefer money-flow or rights-control ideas where the founder can produce evidence of value without owning operational liability.
