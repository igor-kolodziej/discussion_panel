- Fatal cap: the control point is not clearly provable as defensible within two months by an unknown solo founder.
- Profitability is plausible but not highly reliable under a five-hour/day cap.
- The post-deadline moat is uncertain; the business may collapse into a one-off seasonal hustle.
- The hardest part is distribution, not production.

## Pivot Instruction

Stop using WNI/deadline compliance as the core business. The repeated real-chat fatal objection is the same across WNI variants: deadline cliff + insufficient durable control point. Move to a business where the control point can be owned, reserved, prepaid, or routed independently of a one-time regulatory date.
