## Shared Fatal Pattern

At least four candidates were capped by the same fatal shape: useful workflows with plausible data assets, but still too dependent on incumbent trust, partner acceptance, or buyer willingness to route live work before the founder controls a harder asset.

## Decision

Do not send any Round 20 candidate to real validation. Pivot toward ideas where the initial 2-month proof can control a harder asset:

- prepaid demand;
- owned or optioned scarce supply;
- legally required scarce capacity;
- exclusive routing of transaction flow;
- data that only appears at a non-reconstructable moment;
- a buyer with urgent, measurable economic loss.
