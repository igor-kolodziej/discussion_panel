## Shared Fatal Objections

- Distribution into live blocked procurements is not controlled. Cold outbound to "AI Act help" prospects would likely become generic consulting.
- Legal trust is a copy-risk problem. Employment/privacy lawyers, AI governance boutiques, Big Four, GDPR consultants, and security-compliance providers can package similar outputs with stronger credentials.
- The evidence corpus is plausible but not yet a moat at 3 paid packs; it becomes defensible only after enough real buyer questions and accepted answers accumulate.
- The legal-advice boundary is fragile because classification rationale, transparency wording, Article 26 handoff, and worker/candidate notices are close to legal judgment.

## Pivot Lesson

Do not continue with generic AI Act/HR compliance packaging unless distribution is pre-attached through a buyer, association, lawyer, platform, marketplace, or other routed channel. The next shape should either:

- attach to a transaction or existing document bottleneck the founder can access directly; or
- use an already-routed demand source where the customer is under immediate revenue risk and the founder can prove paid demand before broad trust is needed.
