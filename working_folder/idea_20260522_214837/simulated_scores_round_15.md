## Shared Objections

- Several ideas can make money in two months, but only as high-touch narrow services.
- Physical inventory helps but creates matching, documentation, liability, and dead-stock risk.
- Workflow/control points are still mostly Tier 2 unless the founder can secure exclusivity, routed demand, or owned scarce assets.
- Existing incumbents can copy visible wrappers: packaging distributors, ESG consultants, HVAC wholesalers, accounting software vendors, and sourcing platforms.

## Decision

No candidate reached the lowered `82` simulated threshold. The strongest direction is HeatPump Orphan Parts Bank, but it needs a sharper control point: not broad "parts bank," rather a narrower emergency replacement module or model-family rescue gate where repeatability and inventory value are higher.
