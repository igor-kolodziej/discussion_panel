Idea: Subcontractor Payout Photo Gate for Polish OZE Installers. A visual QA gate used by small and mid-sized PV/heat-pump/battery/EV installers before they release subcontractor payout or customer handover. It starts only with installers who already use subcontractor crews and have callback/warranty/payout disputes. The installer updates subcontractor terms so the last 10-20% payout on each job requires a passed photo pack or a fix list. The founder reviews and later automates photo packs against a reviewer-labeled Polish defect taxonomy: PV cable routing, roof penetrations, clamps, grounding, labels, inverter clearances, battery placement, heat-pump condensate, vibration pads, outdoor clearance, pipe insulation, electrical protection, serial/label evidence. Output: pass/fail payout gate, severity-tagged fix list, exact photo crop, crew feedback card, and warranty-risk evidence folder. Control point: installer contract clause routing subcontractor payout through the QA gate, recurring live photo flow, proprietary labeled defect corpus, and pass/fail outcome data tied to callbacks/disputes. Two-month proof: 3 installers sign paid pilots and update subcontractor terms on new jobs; process 100 live jobs or 1,500 photos; catch 30 material defects before payout/handover; at least 20 subcontractor fixes completed before final payout; 20k PLN revenue. Pricing: 50-150 PLN per job gate or 1k-4k PLN/month per installer, paid because it protects final payout and reduces callbacks. Hidden insight: optional QA is ignored, but payout gates change behavior; the scarce asset is not AI vision but the defect corpus plus control over the moment subcontractors get paid. Rate 0-100 and include strongest objections/caveats.
