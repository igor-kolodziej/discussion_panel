## Exact Scored Wording

BadDebt VAT Recovery Desk. A tax-advisor-supervised recovery desk for Polish B2B companies with material overdue receivables, focused only on VAT bad-debt relief for sales invoices unpaid 90+ days but still inside the statutory correction window. Beachhead: industrial wholesalers, transport firms, recruitment agencies, B2B service firms, and suppliers that already send invoices to debt collectors but whose accountants do not systematically recover VAT from stale unpaid invoices. The desk ingests JPK_V7 exports, AR aging, invoice due dates, bank receipts, debtor/payment status, prior VAT corrections, and debt-collection status; it creates an eligible-invoice schedule, risk flags, evidence folder, and draft correction pack reviewed by a licensed tax advisor and coordinated with the existing accountant. Control point: a signed mandate plus data-processing agreement over a quantified unpaid-invoice pool and VAT correction calendar, with success fee only on VAT actually recovered/reduced or a paid fixed audit when no recovery is filed. Two-month proof: 5 companies or debt-collection-referred portfolios, 1M+ PLN gross unpaid invoice pool inside eligibility window, 100k+ PLN VAT benefit identified, 25+ invoice-level eligibility decisions, one licensed tax-advisor reviewer, and at least 5 advisor-approved/customer-approved correction items or one paid audit/success-fee mandate. Hidden insight: bad-debt VAT relief is legally known but operationally orphaned between AR, debt collection, accounting, and tax filing, so mid-market firms leave recoverable VAT in ledgers even while chasing the debtor.

## Strongest Caveats

- Regulatory dependence: credible execution needs licensed tax-advisor review and clear limits so the company is workflow/data infrastructure, not an unlicensed tax practice.
- Edge-case complexity: partial payments, offsets, restructuring, bankruptcy, related parties, foreign entities, timing mismatches, and prior corrections can explode scope.
- Accountant politics: the offer may imply that existing accountants missed recoverable VAT, so positioning must be cooperative.
- Trust barrier: access to JPK, AR aging, invoice ledgers, and debtor data requires referrals, advisor credibility, and serious data-handling process.
- One-time audit trap: durable business requires continuous monitoring, not only old recovery scans.
