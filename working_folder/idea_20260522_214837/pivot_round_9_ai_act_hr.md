# Pivot Round 9 AI Act HR Procurement

Reason for pivot: pet, WNI, OZE tracker, and recovery-kit variants repeatedly failed real gates because the first control point was too weak: generic hardware/workflow, deadline cliff, channel uncertainty, or operational support burden.

## New Candidate

**AI Act HR Procurement Evidence Pack**

AI Act HR Procurement Evidence Pack is a 5-business-day, productized evidence-response service for Polish/CEE HR-tech vendors, recruiting-software startups, and AI automation agencies whose ATS/CV-screening/interview-analysis/recruiting-chatbot product is facing a live enterprise buyer, public-sector buyer, DPO, legal, works-council, or procurement question about EU AI Act, GDPR, or HR discrimination risk.

It activates only on a concrete trigger: buyer asks for AI Act classification, Article 50 transparency wording, Annex III/high-risk explanation, Article 26 employer/deployer handoff, candidate/worker notice, human-oversight SOP, logging/record-retention evidence, bias/testing evidence, model-provider documentation, or an AI vendor-risk questionnaire blocking a sale, renewal, pilot, or procurement approval.

The founder turns product docs, prompts/model providers, data-flow notes, existing security/privacy docs, workflow screenshots, and buyer questions into a buyer-ready pack: feature-level AI inventory, provider/deployer role map, high-risk/non-high-risk classification rationale, buyer FAQ, candidate/worker transparency notice drafts, human-oversight SOP, log-retention map, bias/test evidence plan, gap list, and lawyer-review queue.

It is not legal advice, certification, generic AI governance SaaS, or broad HR consulting; legal review is a paid add-on through an employment/privacy lawyer.

Pricing: `4,000-8,000 PLN` per urgent pack or `2,500 PLN/month` retainer for recurring procurement answers.

Primary control point: live blocked procurement questions plus a proprietary HR-AI evidence-question corpus and reusable buyer-facing answer templates.

Two-month proof: `3` paid urgent packs from live buyer/procurement triggers, `1` subcontracted Polish employment/privacy lawyer reviewer, `20` reusable HR-AI questionnaire clauses, and at least one buyer accepts/forwards the pack enough to unblock or progress a deal.

## Why This Is A Material Pivot

- No hardware, no fleet, no shelter/dog-hotel logistics, no one-off WNI deadline.
- Trigger is a live sale/procurement/legal review rather than speculative regulatory education.
- Founder fit is stronger: AI/data workflows, technical documentation, procurement evidence, and low-camera outbound.
- Delivery can be productized before software and profitable from the first 1-2 customers.
- The compounding asset is not advice; it is a corpus of HR-AI buyer questions, answer templates, feature classifications, and reusable evidence packets.

## Current Market Context

- EU AI Act is in force as Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
- The European Commission states GPAI obligations apply from 2 August 2025.
- The AI Act includes high-risk AI system obligations around human oversight, logging, role responsibility, and deployment duties.
- The Commission published draft high-risk AI classification guidelines on 19 May 2026, which makes classification uncertainty commercially current even where timelines are politically shifting.
