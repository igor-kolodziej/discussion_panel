# Pivot Round 37 - Compliance Error / Acceptance Candidates

Timestamp: 20260523_010000

Active threshold: `>=82` for simulated, working-chat, and fresh-chat gates.

## Why This Batch

Repeated failures showed that:

- post-payment recovery rights stall on collectability;
- physical inventory/capacity stalls on incumbents;
- legal/regulatory rescue desks stall when the founder cannot own the final gate.

This batch focuses on acceptance/error workflows where the founder can get paid upfront for a concrete artifact, use data advantage, and prove value in weeks.

## Finalists Sent To Simulated Panel

### 1. KSeF Rejection Autopatcher

Paid rescue and correction layer for Polish accounting offices handling 20-200 SME clients now forced into KSeF/FA(3). Ingests rejected or failed KSeF invoice XML/export batches from many SME ERPs, maps each error to corrected FA(3)-compliant XML or exact client/ERP fix instruction, checks permissions/ZAW-FA/authorization and offline/QR edge cases, then returns an accountant-ready corrected export plus client-specific prevention checklist.

Control point: cross-ERP rejection/error corpus, corrected FA(3) patch library, client permission/offline-mode checklist patterns, and paid access to real failed invoice batches.

Two-month proof: 3 accounting offices pay 2,000-5,000 PLN each; process 300 rejected/failed invoices across at least 10 SME ERPs; fix or produce accepted resubmission instructions for 70%+ of cases; build top-50 error dictionary; secure recurring monthly retainer.

### 2. JPK_KR_PD Pre-Submission Anomaly Pack

Paid pre-submission diagnostic for Polish accounting offices and ERP integrators whose CIT/PIT clients must transmit JPK_KR_PD/JPK_PD structured books. Checks not only XSD validity but accounting/tax-office-readable consistency across trial balances, ledger exports, fixed-asset registers, KSeF/NIP references, chart-of-accounts mappings, and sample JPK_KR_PD files.

Control point: recurring structured-book anomaly corpus across accounting systems, correction rules, and paid data access before statutory filing.

### 3. SanctionsRoute Shipment Risk File

Paid pre-shipment risk file for Polish SME exporters of machinery parts, industrial electronics, lab equipment, bearings, sensors, CNC components, and dual-use-adjacent goods selling to Central Asia, Caucasus, Turkey, UAE, and other route-risk regions under Russia sanctions/circumvention scrutiny.

Control point: paid shipment-level document access, route/HS/buyer red-flag corpus, bank/broker accepted memo patterns, and 48h turnaround.

### 4. KIO Lockout Detector For Polish IT/Data Tenders

Fast tender-spec challenge product for Polish software, data, GIS, cybersecurity, AI, and IT-services vendors who suspect a public tender SWZ/specification is written for an incumbent. Scans live procurement documents, flags exclusionary or non-neutral requirements, maps them to PZP/KIO precedent patterns, drafts clarification questions and lawyer-reviewable KIO appeal skeleton before deadline.

Control point: paid live tender document access, category-specific lockout-pattern corpus, deadline-triggered workflow, and accepted question/appeal outcome dataset.
