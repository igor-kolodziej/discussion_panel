# Simulated Scores Round 12 - OZE Distress Lots

## Idea Scored

OZE Distress Lots is a broker-first liquidation desk for small Polish renewable-energy and thermomodernization installers that are cash-stressed by Czyste Powietrze delays, weak heat-pump demand, cancelled subsidy jobs, overbought inventory, or business wind-down. The desk does not start as a broad OLX/Allegro marketplace and does not buy inventory speculatively. It reserves or options named equipment lots and sells only verified, serial-numbered, buyer-ready lots to other installers, service companies, small developers, landlords, and technical buyers. The first narrow category is unused or nearly unused high-value OZE/thermomodernization equipment that can be verified from documents and photos: heat pumps, indoor/outdoor units, inverters, batteries, PV modules, buffer tanks, controllers, mounting kits, and premium windows/doors where the invoice, serial, warranty status, ZUM/EPREL/CE eligibility, storage condition, and transport assumptions can be checked. Every lot gets a buyer-facing lot passport: serials/photos, invoice chain, warranty/eligibility caveats, compatibility notes, missing documents, market-price comparison, transport plan, and do-not-buy warnings. The founder's family renewable-energy installation company near Ostrow Wielkopolski is used only as the first trust/sourcing node and domain QA reference, not as the whole market. The desk takes either an 8-15% broker fee, a documented spread on reserved lots, or a paid 1,500-3,000 PLN seller rescue listing fee for lots above 30,000 PLN. Cash purchases are allowed only after a buyer deposit or when resale margin is above 25% with no more than 20,000 PLN at risk. Primary control point: private distressed supply before it reaches public marketplaces, serial-numbered lot reservations/options, buyer deposits, technical verification, and a growing equipment-price/warranty/eligibility corpus. Two-month proof: reserve at least 10 lots with 250,000 PLN gross equipment value, get 3 buyer deposits, close 1-2 paid transfers with at least 10,000 PLN gross profit/fees, document 20 verified serial/price records, and keep unrecovered inventory exposure below 20,000 PLN.

## Scores

- Euclid: `72/100`
- Locke: `78/100`
- Nash: `74/100`

## Common Objections

- Buyer trust around warranty, compatibility, subsidy eligibility, storage condition, and hidden defects is the main bottleneck.
- Private supply access is plausible but relationship-heavy and not yet a hard control point.
- Broker leakage is likely unless reservations/options, exclusivity windows, and commission triggers are enforceable.
- Verification, negotiation, transport, and document work may exceed the founder's 5h/day constraint.
- The wedge is opportunistic unless it compounds into repeat seller relationships, buyer lists, and serial-level price/warranty/eligibility data.

## Decision

Failed the lowered `82` simulated threshold. Do not send to real Zero To One. Pivot away from brokered distressed physical inventory unless the next idea has a stronger routed workflow, prepaid demand, or data/control lock.
