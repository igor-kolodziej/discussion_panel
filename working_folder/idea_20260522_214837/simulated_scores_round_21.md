## Shared Fatal Pattern

These candidates improved on generic workflows but still scored below the lowered `82` gate because the initial founder-controlled asset was not hard enough. The dominant objections were:

- incumbent medical, metering, lab, energy-auditor, or installer trust;
- operational complexity under the founder's 5h/day constraint;
- buyer acceptance or external-authority acceptance as the true bottleneck;
- easy copy once the workflow is proven.

## Decision

Do not send any Round 21 candidate to real Zero To One validation. Generate a new pool where the startup controls an asset that incumbents do not casually own: legally required scarce appointments, expiring option rights, liquidation/salvage flow, prepaid recovery mandates, or compliance-triggered money already sitting in a ledger.
