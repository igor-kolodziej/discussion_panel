## Exact Scored Wording

CollectorVAT White-Label Recovery Bureau. A tax-advisor-supervised white-label back office for small and mid-sized Polish debt-collection agencies and legal collection firms that want to offer VAT bad-debt relief recovery to creditor clients without building tax operations or taking tax-advice liability. The debt collector is the first customer/channel owner, not merely a referrer. The bureau signs a white-label/revenue-share pilot with collectors, gives them a creditor consent/DPA packet and sales script, then processes creditor files only after collector-routed client consent. It focuses on domestic B2B invoices unpaid 90+ days, outsourced-accounting creditors, and 200k-500k+ PLN gross eligible receivable pools. Workflow: collector flags stale invoice portfolios, creditor authorizes limited data access, bureau ingests JPK_V7, AR aging, due dates, payments, prior corrections, assignment/factoring status, and accountant contact, then creates an invoice-level eligibility schedule, reverse-correction calendar, evidence folder, and JPK-ready correction pack reviewed by a licensed tax advisor and accepted by the creditor's accountant for filing. Two-month proof: sign 2 debt collectors into paid or revenue-share pilots, get at least 10 creditor introductions/consents, close 2 paid creditor mandates, process 1M+ PLN gross unpaid invoices, identify 100k+ PLN VAT correction potential, obtain written tax-advisor methodology approval, and get one accountant-accepted correction pack. Pricing: creditor pays fixed audit/filing-pack fee plus modest success fee; collector receives referral/revenue share; bureau owns templates, methodology, eligibility data, and reverse-correction monitoring workflow. Hidden insight: debt collectors control the exact distressed-receivable moment but rationally avoid tax-advice liability, so a specialist back office can become their ancillary VAT-liquidity product before collectors internalize it.

## Strongest Caveats

- "White-label" cannot mean that a collector appears to give tax advice; the bureau and licensed tax advisor must own tax scope, methodology, review, and client-facing tax documents.
- The creditor still pays most fees, so the collector is not fully the economic customer unless a minimum collector retainer or operating fee is secured.
- Accountants may block or slow filing if the service implies they missed relief.
- Success-fee-heavy pricing is delicate because temporary VAT liquidity is not the same as permanent recovery and tax-advisory compensation cannot be only outcome-proportional.
- Data trust requires a named tax advisor, DPA, secure data room, deletion policy, and narrow-scope access from day one.
- Larger collectors can copy the playbook if it proves profitable.
