# Pivot Round 6 Filing Desk

Reason for pivot: the physical retrofit candidate failed real working-chat at `78/100` because two-month distribution and install execution looked too heavy.

## Material Pivot

Move from **custom physical installs** to a **deadline-driven paid filing desk** with near-immediate fulfillment and low capital needs.

## Exact Candidate For Next Gate

**Wielkopolska WNI Emergency Filing Desk for animal-keeping businesses**

A paid deadline sprint for animal-keeping businesses near Wielkopolska and adjacent counties that must register/update under the 2026 animal-health/WNI rules before the June 18, 2026 window: dog/cat hotels, dog/cat breeders, shelters/foundations, stables, small beekeeping/poultry/rabbit businesses, and other non-household animal-keeping operations. The startup sells a 499-999 PLN filing pack, plus a 199 PLN urgent county call/check add-on, and a 299 PLN/month evidence/archive reminder retainer. Output: county-specific PLW/WNI checklist, filled draft application/update forms for owner signature, submission instructions, proof/evidence folder, deadline reminders, and a plain-language status tracker. No legal advice and no representation before PLW; a vet/legal reviewer checks template boundaries. The primary control point is the dated June 18 deadline, paid filings, county-specific PLW response memory, CRM of submitted entities and renewal/update dates, and recurring archive/reminder contracts. Two-month proof: 50 paid filing packs, 15 urgent check add-ons, 10 archive retainers, one written vet/legal review, documented county response memory from at least 8 counties, and positive gross margin after reviewer/admin/support costs.

## Why This Is Different From The Failed Candidate

- Delivery is administrative and repeatable; no custom construction bottleneck.
- Proof can be prepaid and fulfilled remotely.
- The founder's data/AI skill helps with scraping targets, county forms, checklist generation, CRM, reminders, and template QA.
- The immediate deadline can close customers faster than optional physical safety upgrades.
- The post-deadline control point is the operating archive and county-specific memory, not a one-time form.
