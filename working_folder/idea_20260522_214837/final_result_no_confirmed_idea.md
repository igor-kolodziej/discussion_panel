## Main Learning

The original GPS dog collar idea is structurally weak as a standalone business because hardware, virtual fences, subscriptions, and tracking alerts are already covered by strong incumbents. The best pivots moved toward pet traceability, inspection evidence, listing verification, municipal campaigns, and pre-deposit fraud checks, but all stayed below the simulated gate because their control points were still too soft or copyable.

The strongest future direction would need a harder control point than this run found, for example:

- a municipality-paid mandate with reserved vet capacity and recurring animal-compliance reporting;
- a pet-hotel inspection archive that becomes the daily operating record, not a deadline service;
- a high-value pet transaction gate that controls actual buyer demand and payments through a legally safe PSP flow;
- an insurer or official body creating real policy/procurement consequences for using the workflow.

Without one of those hard controls proven upfront, the pet-safety concepts remain useful services rather than confirmed Zero To One business ideas.
