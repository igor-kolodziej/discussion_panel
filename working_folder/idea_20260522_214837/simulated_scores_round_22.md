## Shared Fatal Pattern

The hard-asset pivot improved control points but still did not clear the lowered `82` simulation gate. The main repeated objection was that each business depends on incumbents or external gatekeepers that already own buyer trust:

- legal/factoring institutions for receivables;
- wholesalers/PSPs/ERP systems for settlement;
- industrial service firms for spares;
- labs/clinics for sample custody and occupational medicine;
- existing repair/dealer networks for forklift parts.

## Decision

No Round 22 candidate goes to real Zero To One validation. Next round needs either:

- a hard asset the founder can create rather than ask permission to access;
- a very narrow category where incumbents are structurally uninterested;
- prepaid buyer demand before supply is secured;
- a resource owner with urgent reason to grant rights because the asset is otherwise wasted.
