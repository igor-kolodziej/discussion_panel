# Pivot Round 10 Czyste Platnosci

Reason for pivot: the AI Act HR procurement pack was profitable in principle but failed simulated gates because distribution into live blocked procurements was not controlled and legal-trust copy risk was high.

## Candidate

**Czyste Platnosci Contractor Payment Rescue Pack**

Czyste Platnosci Contractor Payment Rescue Pack is a 5-business-day, productized documentation rescue service for small Polish heat-pump, window, insulation, PV/EMS, and thermomodernization contractors whose Czyste Powietrze prefinancing advance, final payment request, or contractor-paid invoice is delayed, questioned, or effectively stuck because WFOŚiGW/NFOŚiGW/operator review needs clarifications, corrected attachments, invoice cost split, protocol correction, payment proof, energy-audit/energy-certificate evidence, photos, beneficiary signatures, contractor experience proof, product eligibility evidence, or response to suspected overpricing/non-qualified costs.

It activates only on a live payment or cash-flow trigger: a contractor has unpaid receivables, beneficiary pressure, or a WFOŚiGW/operator request connected to a named application/payment request.

The founder turns the fund letter/request, WOP/WOD exports, invoices, contracts, protocols, product datasheets, photos, audit/certificate docs, bank/payment evidence, and beneficiary messages into a buyer-ready payment rescue pack: missing-document map, corrected attachment checklist, invoice/specification cost matrix, chronology, eligibility-risk flags, formal response draft, beneficiary signature packet, fund-call script, and escalation/legal-auditor queue.

It is not subsidy sales, legal representation, energy audit, installer quality passport, or generic grant consulting.

Pricing is `2,500-7,500 PLN` per stuck-payment pack or `3-6%` success fee on recovered contractor receivable above `50,000 PLN`, with a small upfront fee.

Primary control point: live stuck contractor cash, family-company access to real Czyste Powietrze documentation/payment pain, local contractor referrals, and a reusable corpus of WFOŚiGW clarification patterns and accepted response packs.

Two-month proof: `3` paid rescue packs tied to at least `150,000 PLN` of stuck or delayed contractor receivables, one local accountant/auditor or lawyer reviewer on call, `20` reusable clarification templates, one WFOŚiGW/operator response that accepts, advances, or narrows the payment issue, and positive contribution margin without building software.

## Shared Fatal Objections

- Contractor may not control the process; formal applicant, beneficiary, WFOŚiGW, and operator cooperation can block delivery.
- Many cases may be unrecoverable because of actual non-qualified costs, weak documents, overpricing, bad workmanship, or missing signatures.
- Legal/audit boundary risk is high around escalation, overpricing, eligibility disputes, and formal response drafting.
- Copy risk from local subsidy consultants, accountants, energy auditors, and lawyers is immediate.
- Control point is still operational, not structural: the founder does not control payer, rules, or beneficiary cooperation.

## Pivot Lesson

Stop trying to make contractor documentation rescue pass. The next candidate should use a truly owned or reserved asset/resource, or a pre-paid demand shape, where the founder can control the scarce thing directly rather than depending on fund/beneficiary/procurement decisions.
