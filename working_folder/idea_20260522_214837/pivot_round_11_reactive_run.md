# Pivot Round 11 ReactiveRun

Reason for pivot: service/documentation candidates kept failing because the founder did not control the payer, procurement channel, legal trust layer, or beneficiary cooperation. This pivot returns to the original dog geofence/safety seed but changes the control point from commodity collar hardware to owned/reserved safe movement space.

## Candidate

**ReactiveRun Reserved Dog Field Pass**

ReactiveRun Reserved Dog Field Pass is an operator-reserved network of private, escape-resistant dog exercise fields for Warsaw/Wielkopolska owners of reactive, fearful, high-prey-drive, adolescent, rescue, foster, and shelter dogs that cannot safely use public dog parks or off-leash forests.

It does not sell a GPS collar as the core product and does not start as a broad Sniffspot-style marketplace. It sells prepaid, guaranteed private time in certified fields: no overlapping dogs, double-gate or controlled entry, fence check, minimum fence-height standard, owner checklist, incident rules, and optional GPS collar rental for recall training or escape-prone dogs. The first supply comes from exclusive off-peak leases or option agreements with existing dog schools, equestrian yards, fenced agrotourism plots, fenced orchards, unused sports/training grounds, and rural properties within 45 minutes of Warsaw and Ostrow Wielkopolski.

Customers pay either `35-70 PLN/hour` or `149-249 PLN/month` for 4-6 private field slots. Initial demand is routed through trainers, behaviorists, vets, shelters, foster homes, and reactive-dog communities. The first wedge is not "fun dog park"; it is safe private decompression and training space for dogs that owners are afraid to release anywhere else.

Primary control point: exclusive reserved field slots, safety certification/checklist, prepaid memberships, routed trainer/vet/shelter referrals, and local field quality/reliability data. Hardware is optional: GPS trackers are rented only as an add-on, not manufactured.

Two-month proof: secure `2-3` exclusive field/time-slot agreements, pre-sell `60` monthly passes or `200` booked sessions, obtain `5` trainer/vet/shelter referral commitments, run first sessions with no safety incident, and show positive contribution margin after field lease/share, insurance, locks, cleaning/maintenance, payment fees, and support.

## Why This Is Similar To The Collar Seed

- Same user problem: a dog can move freely only inside a defined safe area.
- Stronger control point: the founder can reserve the safe area instead of competing with GPS collar incumbents.
- Subscription is still possible, but tied to real scarce access instead of commodity tracking alerts.

## Current Market Context

- Sniffspot shows the private dog park model is real internationally and claims `32,000+` private spots.
- DOG-SPOT already operates a Polish private dog-field marketplace, so this cannot be a generic marketplace copy.
- The defensible version must be operator-reserved/certified field access for difficult/reactive dogs, with exclusive inventory and routed trainer/vet/shelter demand.
