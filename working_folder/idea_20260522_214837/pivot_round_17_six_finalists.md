# Pivot Round 17 - Six Finalists

## Rationale

Prior rounds found profitable but sub-threshold ideas. The strongest pattern remains: control a scarce asset, routed commercial gate, or live revenue block. This round uses one subagent per finalist as the workflow allows.

## Finalists

### A. ShowroomKitchen Suites

Controls complete premium ex-display kitchen, wardrobe, bathroom-vanity, and built-in furniture suites from Polish showrooms before they are split, damaged, or dumped into generic classifieds. Each suite is measured, photographed, disassembled with a fitter, packed, and sold as a project-ready discounted fit-out to boutique developers, premium rental operators, flippers, project managers, and private renovators.

Control point: owned/optioned complete suites, first-refusal agreements with showrooms, buyer deposits, BOM/measurement/spec cards, and fitter network.

Two-month proof: 5 showroom first-refusal agreements, 3 controlled suites with 150,000+ PLN original retail value, 3 buyer deposits, 1 paid handoff with 15,000+ PLN gross margin, exposure below 50,000 PLN.

### B. DentalRoom Liquidation Suites

Controls complete used dental operatory suites from retiring dentists, clinics upgrading rooms, and small practices closing: dental chair/unit, compressor/suction where separable, lamp, cabinetry, autoclave where appropriate, X-ray only if legally/serviceably transferable, maintenance records, manuals, serials, and service-technician inspection notes. Sells inspected room-ready bundles to new dental practices, budget clinics, training schools, and CEE/Ukraine buyers via dental equipment service technicians.

Control point: owned/optioned complete room bundles, service-technician inspection, first-look agreements with technicians/practice brokers, buyer deposits, serial/service record corpus.

Two-month proof: option 3 rooms, get 2 buyer deposits, close 1 transfer with 20,000+ PLN gross margin/fee, no unsupported regulated equipment claim, exposure below 60,000 PLN.

### C. DepositLedger Recovery Sprint

A Polish deposit-return settlement leakage sprint for small and mid-sized beverage producers/distributors after the 1 October 2025 system launch and the 1 January 2026 marked-packaging shift. It reconciles operator invoices, product-fee exposure, deposit mark SKU lists, KSeF invoices, wholesaler/retailer sell-in/sell-out, packaging categories, returns/collection statements, and ERP/SKU master data to find overcharges, missing credits, wrong packaging classification, duplicate fees, or under-documented product-fee risk.

Control point: live operator/producer settlement files, SKU/deposit-mark mapping, accepted dispute patterns, FMCG/commercial analytics fit.

Two-month proof: 3 paid audits at 6,000-15,000 PLN or 15-25% of recovered/avoided amounts, 100 SKUs mapped, one correction/recovery/avoided product-fee exposure above 20,000 PLN, no generic compliance-only work.

### D. ToyBlocked EN71 Evidence Rescue

A white-label evidence-response desk for Amazon/Allegro account-health agencies and EU marketplace compliance agencies serving sellers/importers of toys, baby play products, and child-adjacent goods whose listing, shipment, or marketplace account is blocked by live CE/GPSR/Toy Safety/EN 71 documentation requests. It triages only live revenue-blocking cases, organizes test reports, declarations of conformity, age grading, warning labels, responsible-person details, supplier gaps, and "do not answer without lab/legal review" caveats.

Control point: agency-routed live suppression cases, accepted/rejected marketplace evidence patterns by toy category, lab/referral reviewer bench, and seller urgency.

Two-month proof: 2 agency routing agreements, 5 paid live cases at 4,000-12,000 PLN, one reinstated/progressed listing, 30 reusable evidence-request patterns, no certification/legal advice.

### E. HotelFit Liquidation Suites

Controls complete matched furniture/fixture lots from boutique hotels, aparthotels, restaurants, and serviced-apartment operators doing refits: room sets, headboards, desks, lamps, wardrobes, minibars, chairs, banquettes, outdoor furniture, and lobby/restaurant sets. Sells project-ready matched lots to small hotels, student housing, serviced apartments, glamping operators, office lounges, and developers who need cohesive furnishing fast under new-furniture cost.

Control point: owned/optioned matched lots before they are split by auction/liquidation, pickup/removal rights, buyer deposits, fit-out spec cards, logistics partners.

Two-month proof: 3 first-look/refit agreements, 2 controlled matched lots, 2 buyer deposits, one sale with 15,000+ PLN gross margin, exposure below 50,000 PLN.

### F. EU Accessibility Act Checkout Rescue

A 5-day evidence-and-fix sprint for Polish/CEE ecommerce, ticketing, booking, and subscription SaaS companies with live enterprise/public buyer, payment partner, or complaint-triggered accessibility blockers after the European Accessibility Act started applying in 2025. It focuses only on revenue-critical flows: checkout, login, subscription cancellation, ticket purchase, booking, invoices, and customer-support contact. Deliverable: WCAG issue proof, patched copy/component recommendations, buyer-facing conformance memo, and dev-ticket pack.

Control point: live revenue blocker, before/after evidence, reusable defect corpus by checkout/provider stack, agency/referral channel.

Two-month proof: 4 paid sprints at 5,000-15,000 PLN, one buyer/compliance blocker moved forward, 80 reusable defect fixes, one web agency/referrer.
