## Decision

No candidate clears the lowered `82` simulated threshold. The control points are concrete, but still too operational, too dependent on specialist trust, or too exposed to incumbent dealers/service firms.

## Lesson

High-ticket physical suites only score well when:

- the object is visibly inspectable by non-specialists;
- the buyer risk is mostly aesthetic/project fit, not hidden functionality;
- acquisition can be proven before heavy inventory risk;
- copy risk is slowed by upstream fragmentation and awkward local pickup, not expert trust.
