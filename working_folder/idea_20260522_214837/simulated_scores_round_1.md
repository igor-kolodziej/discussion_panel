## Shared Fatal Objections

The panel converged on several fatal objections:

- GPS hardware is already commoditized by Tractive, Halo, SpotOn, Invoxia, Weenect, notiOne, Kippy, Garmin, and others.
- Tracker fleet + checklist + SOP is not durable unless the startup controls a mandatory workflow.
- Deposits are weaker than non-refundable paid fees.
- Foundations, hotels, hosts, or tracker vendors can copy a PDF/playbook after a pilot proves demand.
- Rescue/foundation and pet-care operators may have weak budgets and low willingness to pay.
- Liability and GDPR create operating burden if the startup implies live emergency response.

## Pivot Direction

Do not keep polishing "collar rental plus SOP." Move the control point upstream into a transaction or compliance gate:

- verified online pet listing / reservation-deposit gate under new EU traceability rules;
- municipal repeat-stray cost recovery and owner enforcement workflow;
- pet transport custody packet only if tied to booking/payment control;
- registration/compliance workflow for commercial dog/cat holding facilities;
- adoption safety only if mandatory, paid, and embedded into foundation contracts.
