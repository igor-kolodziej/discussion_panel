# Pivot Round 5 After Real Working-Chat Failure

Reason for pivot: the real Zero To One working-chat gave the WNI/PLW readiness retrofit pack `76/100`, mainly because urgency and fast control-point proof were not strong enough for an unknown founder.

## Material Pivot

Move from **compliance-readiness audit first** to **paid physical safety retrofit first**.

The compliance/WNI/PLW context stays as a demand trigger, but the startup sells installable safety remediation with deposits and install dates as the control point.

## Exact Candidate For Next Gate

**PLW-Ready Dog Hotel Safety Retrofit Installer**

A productized B2B installation business for Polish dog hotels and dog daycares that need to look safe before summer bookings and the 2026 PLW/WNI registration/inspection cycle. The startup sells a 700 PLN escape-risk triage, credited against installation, then three standard retrofit packages at 6,000-28,000 PLN with 50% deposit to reserve an install slot. Packages include double-gate entry vestibules, self-closing latches, anti-dig fence skirts, gate/fence gap fixes, washable isolation/cleaning zones, intake/incident/rabies/hygiene binders, owner-facing before/after safety evidence, and optional 299-499 PLN/month evidence archive. No claim of government certification, PLW approval, or legal advice. A vet/behaviorist/welfare reviewer checks the checklist. The primary control point is paid retrofit deposits, reserved family-installer capacity, standard dog-hotel-specific bills of materials, before/after photo records, and a public registry of remediated sites. Two-month proof: 8 paid triage jobs, 5 retrofit deposits, 3 completed small installs or signed install dates, 2 archive retainers, 1 written expert review, and at least 20% gross margin after installer/material costs.

## Why This Is Different From The Failed Candidate

- It does not require owners to believe county PLW enforcement will be urgent enough on its own.
- The first paid proof is an installation deposit, not a compliance report.
- Profitability can happen from two or three completed jobs, not from many low-ticket retainers.
- The family installation company becomes a visible credibility and capacity asset.
- Copy risk moves from template consultants to kennel builders; the defense is dog-hotel-specific packages, evidence records, and references.
