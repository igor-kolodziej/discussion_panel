## Shared Fatal Objections

- Strong timing exists around EU dog/cat traceability and Polish KROPiK, but official registries and large platforms may commoditize basic verification.
- Most candidates have "soft" workflow access rather than a hard transaction, enforcement, inventory, data, or payment control point.
- Municipal, insurer, and legal/regulatory buyers are slow or trust-heavy for an unknown founder.
- Hardware and safety SOPs are still copyable.
- Per-case fees may be too small unless the startup owns a transaction gate or recurring system of record.

## Strongest Surviving Insight

The strongest pet-related opening is not GPS collar hardware. It is the moment when **animal identity, transaction trust, and institutional accountability become mandatory enough that someone needs a private operating layer before official systems are easy to use**.

Round 3 should focus only on stronger gates:

- vet-clinic controlled KROPiK registration/chipping workflow;
- breeder-club or marketplace verification where the startup owns waitlist/deposit flow, not only tokens;
- municipality/shelter contract audit where money recovery is clearer;
- insurer/broker term sheet with concrete policy consequences.
