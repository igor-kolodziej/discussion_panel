# Fresh-Chat Gate Failure - RetentionRelease

## Candidate

RetentionRelease Public-Contract Security Recovery.

## Scores

- Simulated Zero To One score: `86/100`
- Real working-chat Zero To One score: `84/100`
- Fresh-chat Zero To One score: `78/100`

## Verdict

Fail under the active lowered threshold of `82`. No confirmed idea file may be written for this candidate.

## Fresh-Chat Feedback

The fresh chat accepted that the legal substrate is real: Polish public procurement law creates return clocks for performance security and bid bonds, and a document-to-money workflow exists. However, it capped the score below `82` because the hardest assumption remains unproven: how often meaningful matured securities are actually forgotten, delayed, or recoverable without the contractor already knowing.

## Strongest Objections

- Forgotten-money incidence may be much lower than assumed.
- Public buyers often have procedures, and serious contractors may already track guarantees, retentions, and warranty periods.
- "Near-matured" value can inflate proof metrics; it is not equivalent to matured cash/security release.
- Trust is the bottleneck: contractors must share contracts, correspondence, invoices, defect letters, and guarantee documents.
- Success fees may face resistance when the money is already perceived as the contractor's.
- Releasing guarantees frees capacity but is perceived as less valuable than cash.
- Buyer timing is outside founder control.
- The offer is copyable by law firms unless a dataset of buyer behavior, clause templates, and objection playbooks is built.

## Fresh-Chat Suggested Stricter Gate

Pass only if:

- at least 5 exclusive mandates are signed;
- 50 completed contracts are audited;
- `1M+ PLN` of clearly matured, not merely near-matured, cash/security value is identified;
- either `100k PLN` is released or `300k+ PLN` is formally acknowledged for release.

Weak pass:

- 3 paid audits with no acknowledged release. This proves curiosity/willingness to pay, not the recovery thesis.

## Pivot Instruction

Pivot away from business shapes where the key assumption is hidden incidence of forgotten money that cannot be proven before trust-heavy archive access. Prefer recoverable-money ideas where the pool can be proven from customer-owned structured data quickly: unpaid invoice ledgers, subsidy filings, payroll/SODiR files, claim rejections, or municipal payment schedules.
