## Exact Scored Wording

CollectorVAT White-Label Recovery Bureau. A tax-advisor-supervised white-label back office for small and mid-sized Polish debt-collection agencies and legal collection firms that want to offer VAT bad-debt relief recovery to creditor clients without building tax operations or taking tax-advice liability. The debt collector is the first customer/channel owner, not merely a referrer. The bureau signs a white-label/revenue-share pilot with collectors, gives them a creditor consent/DPA packet and sales script, then processes creditor files only after collector-routed client consent. It focuses on domestic B2B invoices unpaid 90+ days, outsourced-accounting creditors, and 200k-500k+ PLN gross eligible receivable pools. Workflow: collector flags stale invoice portfolios, creditor authorizes limited data access, bureau ingests JPK_V7, AR aging, due dates, payments, prior corrections, assignment/factoring status, and accountant contact, then creates an invoice-level eligibility schedule, reverse-correction calendar, evidence folder, and JPK-ready correction pack reviewed by a licensed tax advisor and accepted by the creditor's accountant for filing. Control point: signed white-label/revenue-share operating agreement with collectors, collector-routed client consent flow, standardized creditor data room, tax-advisor QA workflow, and recurring correction/reverse-correction calendar. Two-month proof: sign 2 debt collectors into paid or revenue-share pilots, get at least 10 creditor introductions/consents, close 2 paid creditor mandates, process 1M+ PLN gross unpaid invoices, identify 100k+ PLN VAT correction potential, obtain written tax-advisor methodology approval, and get one accountant-accepted correction pack. Pricing: creditor pays fixed audit/filing-pack fee plus modest success fee; collector receives referral/revenue share; bureau owns templates, methodology, eligibility data, and reverse-correction monitoring workflow. Hidden insight: debt collectors control the exact distressed-receivable moment but rationally avoid tax-advice liability, so a specialist back office can become their ancillary VAT-liquidity product before collectors internalize it.

## Strongest Objections

- Collector lock-in is still not guaranteed. Debt collectors may pilot it, learn the economics, then internalize the workflow or route only low-quality cases.
- Accountant acceptance is the operational bottleneck. If creditor accountants reject external JPK correction packs or require heavy hand-holding, the bureau becomes a custom tax-service desk with weak margins.
- A licensed tax advisor partner is mandatory from day one because eligibility edge cases around assignments, factoring, partial payments, debtor status, reverse corrections, and prior relief can create liability.
- Many stale receivables may be already assigned, disputed, partially paid, written off, corrected, or too small.
- Pricing must distinguish temporary VAT liquidity from permanent recovery when debtor payment later triggers reverse correction.

## Control-Point Tier

Tier 2.5, with a credible path toward Tier 1.5 if 2-3 collectors become semi-exclusive white-label channels and the bureau owns the consent flow, correction calendar, accountant-acceptance workflow, and cross-collector eligibility corpus.

## Strongest Copy Risk

## Fatal Objection

No immediate fatal objection. The fatal risk is that collectors do not route enough high-quality creditor files, or that accountant/tax-advisor review shows most routed receivables are ineligible, already corrected, assigned/factored, or too low-value to support repeatable paid recovery.
