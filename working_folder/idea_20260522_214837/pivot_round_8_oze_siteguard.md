# Pivot Round 8 OZE SiteGuard

Reason for pivot: pet-sector ideas repeatedly failed real-chat gates due weak durable control points, logistics drag, or slow partner adoption.

## New Candidate

**OZE SiteGuard Jobsite Custody Kit**

A GPS/geofence and custody-evidence kit for small Polish PV, heat-pump, HVAC, and electrical installation companies that leave expensive tools, heat-pump units, inverters, copper, batteries, or PV materials on customer sites, vans, or temporary storage. The startup sells a 299-499 PLN per-job custody pack or 699-1,499 PLN/month per crew subscription with refundable tracker deposit. Each pack includes rugged GPS/LTE trackers hidden in high-value pallets/tools, geofence/tamper alerts, Bluetooth tags for toolboxes, photo/QR handoff checklists, overnight custody logs, police/insurer-ready incident packet, and supplier/customer delivery evidence. It is not generic fleet tracking; it is a small-installer jobsite custody workflow. The primary control point is the founder's family installation company as the first pilot/reference, owned tracker inventory bought only after paid preorders, installer crew onboarding, per-job custody logs, and supplier/wholesaler referral routes. Two-month proof: one family-company pilot, five non-family installers paying for at least 20 protected job-weeks, 10 tracker kits deployed, two wholesaler/supplier referral letters, one theft/loss near-miss or recovered-asset proof packet if it occurs, and positive contribution margin after hardware depreciation, SIM fees, delivery, support, and deposits.

## Why This Is Similar To The Collar Seed

- Same underlying mechanism: define a safe zone, track valuable mobile assets, and alert when they move outside the allowed area.
- Different buyer: businesses with higher willingness to pay than dog owners.
- Different control point: routed installer workflow and owned tracker kit, not consumer hardware brand.
