## Decision

No candidate passed simulated threshold. The repeated objection is that each energy idea needs a third-party trust, legal, or capacity partner before the founder can own the control point. Continue only with candidates where the founder can build or acquire the control point directly and cheaply, or where the buyer pays for access to a unique corpus generated from public/observable data rather than sensitive contributor data.
