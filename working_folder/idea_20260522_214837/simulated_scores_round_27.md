## Decision

Do not send these variants to real validation. The narrower custody framing did not improve the simulated score. It adds address/liability/RODO burden while losing some of the broader recurring-administration upside that made ExpiryVault score `82` simulated and `81` real.

## Lesson

The next candidate should not rely on customer-book goodwill or physical mail liability. Prefer assigned receivables, recoverable money rights, or existing recurring books where value transfer is visible in payment behavior rather than trust in a person.
