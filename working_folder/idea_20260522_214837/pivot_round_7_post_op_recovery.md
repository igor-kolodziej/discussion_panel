# Pivot Round 7 Post-Op Recovery

Reason for pivot: all WNI/deadline variants failed real-chat validation because the deadline cliff made the durable control point too weak.

## New Candidate

**PawRecover Home Kit for Canine Orthopedic Surgery**

A Warsaw-first rental and monitoring service for dog owners in the 6-8 week home-restriction period after TPLO/TTA, cruciate ligament, patella, fracture, or other orthopedic surgery. The startup sells through orthopedic/rehab vet referrals and direct owner search, not through consumer collar hardware. The paid product is a 899-1,499 PLN home recovery kit for 6-8 weeks plus a refundable deposit: foldable recovery pen/crate, anti-slip floor runners, ramp or stair block, support sling/harness, cone/surgical-suit options, cold-pack protocol, simple owner checklist, and a temporary activity/GPS collar configured to flag running/jumping/out-of-zone events and produce a weekly owner/vet summary. No diagnosis or medical advice; vet/rehab reviewer approves the restriction templates. Clinics get a 10-15% referral fee or a clinic-branded discharge add-on. The primary control point is owned local inventory, sanitized kit logistics, clinic discharge/referral placements, refundable deposits, activity/recovery data history, and a growing Warsaw orthopedic-clinic recovery workflow. Two-month proof: 15 paid kit rentals, 3 signed clinic/rehab referral agreements, 1 written vet/rehab review, 10 usable activity-summary reports, less than 5% equipment loss after deposits, and positive gross margin after inventory depreciation, cleaning, delivery, subscriptions, and referral fees.

## Why This Is Different

- The buyer pain occurs immediately after a high-cost surgery, not after a speculative future inspection.
- The control point is owned physical inventory plus clinic discharge routing, not a copyable checklist.
- The collar/GPS element is used as a temporary recovery-monitoring component, not as the standalone business.
- The founder's health/wearable/data interests fit the activity-summary layer.
- The business can start part-time in Warsaw with narrow inventory and can be killed quickly if clinics/owners do not pay.
