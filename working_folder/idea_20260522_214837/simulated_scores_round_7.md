## Shared Lessons

- The strongest insight is that the failure point after expensive orthopedic dog surgery is owner compliance at home, not the operation itself.
- Clinic discharge routing is the whole business. Direct owner search is useful but not sufficient.
- The tracker/collar must be positioned as an activity-summary support tool, not a medical device or reliable indoor jump detector.
- Copy risk is clinics or rehab centers bundling the kit themselves.
- Inventory/cash-profit risk is real because 6-8 week rentals lock capital for almost the whole proof window.

## Revised Exact Idea Wording For Real Gate

PawRecover Clinic-Branded TPLO/TTA Home Recovery Kit is a Warsaw-first, clinic-routed rental and monitoring service for dog owners in the 6-8 week home-restriction period after TPLO/TTA, cruciate ligament, patella, fracture, or similar orthopedic surgery. It does not sell a consumer collar. It sells a clinic-branded 6-8 week home setup for 1,199-1,499 PLN plus refundable deposit, delivered within 24-48 hours of discharge: foldable recovery pen/crate, anti-slip runners, ramp or stair block, support sling/harness, cone/surgical-suit options, cold-pack routine, owner checklist, and a temporary activity/GPS collar used only for activity/location summaries, not medical assurance. No diagnosis or medical advice; a vet/rehab reviewer approves all templates and the clinic controls medical instructions. Clinics can offer it as a discharge add-on or white-label fulfillment partner, with 10-15% referral/wholesale margin, so they avoid inventory, cleaning, deposits, support, and logistics. The primary control point is pre-sold clinic discharge slots, owned sanitized inventory, refundable deposits, clinic-branded workflows, delivery density in Warsaw, and accumulated recovery-summary examples. Two-month proof: three clinic/rehab pilot letters or signed referral pages, five premium kits purchased only after pre-orders or clinic pilot commitments, 10 paid rentals or prepaid bookings, 10 activity-summary reports, one written vet/rehab review, less than 5% equipment loss after deposits, and positive contribution margin after cleaning, delivery, subscription, depreciation, and referral fees.
