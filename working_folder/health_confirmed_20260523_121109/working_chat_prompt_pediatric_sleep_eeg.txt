Idea: Pediatric Sleep-EEG Slot Yield Rail for private Polish EEG labs.

Context: Pediatric sleep EEG and sleep-deprived EEG are operationally fragile. Private Polish EEG price lists show meaningful slot value: one clinic lists child sleep EEG at 800 PLN until 23:00 and charges 400 PLN for an unsuccessful examination caused by on-site resignation or failure to bring the child to sleep; mobile sleep EEG can be 1,100 PLN plus travel. A 261-child study of sleep-deprived EEG found 14% slept less than 15 minutes and 7% had no sleep during recording. ACNS pediatric EEG guidance says sleep recordings should be obtained whenever possible and sleep deprivation or melatonin may help, which makes failed sleep/prep operationally important.

Hidden insight: private EEG labs do not mainly need AI interpretation. They need better yield from scarce child sleep/sleep-deprived EEG slots. Parents mis-handle sleep deprivation, naps, meals, screens, hair preparation, transport, and anxiety; labs then lose long evening slots, repeat work, parent goodwill, and sometimes cancellation revenue.

Buyer and beachhead: private EEG labs and neurodiagnostic clinics in Warsaw, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, Lodz, and nearby cities that perform pediatric sleep or sleep-deprived EEG. Start with small/private labs where the owner or manager feels each failed slot.

Product wedge: a lab-routed slot-yield workflow, not a medical product. For every pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG booking in the pilot, the lab routes the parent into a founder-operated readiness flow 72h, 48h, and 12h before the exam. The workflow includes lab-approved age-specific sleep schedule instructions, readiness scoring, rescue calls for high-risk parents, hair/meal/screen/transport reminders, cancellation triage, and a prepared standby list of families willing to take recovered slots. It does not diagnose, interpret EEG, recommend treatment, change medical indications, or replace technician/neurologist instructions.

Initial acquisition: build a list of 40-80 private EEG labs from public search and clinic directories. Cold email/call owners with a "30-day sleep-EEG slot-yield sprint": route 30-50 child sleep/sleep-deprived bookings through the founder workflow; pay only per routed case or for recovered slot value; no software integration. The founder's neuroscience/EEG background gives enough domain credibility without needing celebrity or high-status introductions.

Primary control point and 2-month proof: secure one signed lab pilot where the lab makes routing mandatory for all pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG bookings during the sprint. Resource/control point: routed parent booking flow plus a founder-owned prepared standby list of at least 25 families gathered from lab referrals and high-intent search pages. Proof before serious capital: 30-50 routed bookings, at least 10 paid routed cases, parent readiness completion above 75%, identified high-risk prep cases, recovered/cancelled slots filled from standby list, measured failed-prep/no-show/late-cancel rate versus lab baseline, and lab renewal or written refusal reason. Kill if the lab only wants generic reminder templates or will not route bookings.

Pricing and economics: 80-140 PLN per routed pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG, or 20-30% of recovered slot value for rescued/cancellation-filled cases; after pilot, 2,000-6,000 PLN/month/lab depending on volume. Delivery cost is mostly templated messaging, one founder review queue, and exception calls. Expected gross margin 70-85% after the initial playbook stabilizes. A single saved or refilled 800-1,100 PLN sleep EEG slot can justify several routed cases.

Domination path: start with one lab and one measurable promise: fewer failed sleep EEG slots and faster refill of cancellations. Then become the pediatric sleep-EEG yield layer across private labs, with compounding assets in parent-prep failure patterns, age-specific readiness playbooks, cancellation/standby demand, lab-specific baseline benchmarks, and slot-yield reports. Expansion can move into mobile/home sleep EEG routing, ambulatory EEG prep, pediatric neurodiagnostic appointment yield, and other high-failure pediatric diagnostic appointments.

Why this is not merely a service/report/dashboard/database wrapper: the POC only counts if the lab routes actual bookings through the founder workflow and the founder owns standby demand that can refill slots. The control point is operational booking flow plus prepared replacement demand, not a report, dashboard, generic reminder script, or AI interpretation layer.
