# Simulated Zero To One Review: Home / Sleep-Deprived EEG Capacity Router

## Exact Idea Evaluated

Home / Sleep-Deprived EEG Capacity Router for Warsaw-first private EEG demand. The hidden insight is that many parents and adult patients do not primarily want an EEG clinic brand; they want a fast, calm, correctly performed EEG slot with physician interpretation before the next neurology decision. The scarce resource is not AI interpretation but coordinated EEG technician capacity plus interpreting physician slots. The founder reserves technician hours and physician interpretation capacity, then owns scheduling, pre-test instructions, consent/admin packets, technician dispatch, and report-delivery workflow. The founder does not diagnose, interpret EEG, make medical recommendations, or replace licensed professionals.

Buyer: parents/adult patients needing fast home/sleep-deprived EEG, private pediatric neurologists and neurology clinics that cannot offer fast EEG slots, and small clinics that need overflow capacity.

Control point proof in 2 months: signed per-case or retainer agreements with 2 EEG technicians for defined weekly availability and 1-2 licensed EEG-reading physicians/specialists for interpretation slots; pre-sell 15-25 paid appointments; complete 10 tests with report turnaround under 72 hours; own scheduling, pre-test instructions, consent/admin packet, payment, technician dispatch, and report delivery.

Initial acquisition: high-intent search pages and ads for "EEG dziecka Warszawa szybko", "EEG w domu Warszawa", "EEG sen dziecko", plus cold outreach to private pediatric neurologists and neurologists with the promise of fast external EEG capacity and clean reports.

Pricing: 650-1,200 PLN per home/sleep-deprived EEG depending on type and distance. Technician cost 200-450 PLN, physician interpretation 120-300 PLN, logistics/admin 50-150 PLN, yielding 180-400 PLN gross profit per test. A 10-test POC proves cash conversion; 100 tests/month at 250 PLN gross profit yields 25,000 PLN/month before fixed costs.

## Verdict

Score: **91/100**

Decision: **Pursue narrowly as a capacity-control and scheduling business, not a diagnostic or software business.**

## Diagnosis

This is one of the strongest EEG shapes because the first control point is a resource the founder can realistically secure: paid technician hours and interpretation slots. It avoids the weak path of AI EEG interpretation and avoids needing hospital access. Demand can be tested through prepaid appointments and high-intent search rather than personal brand. The market is not enormous at the first wedge, but the economics, payback, founder fit, and 2-month proof path are strong enough to justify real validation.

## Control Point Tier

Current tier: **B+** as a designed capacity router.

Tier after 2-month proof: **A-** if the founder has signed technician and physician capacity agreements, prepaid patient demand, completed tests, and a documented scheduling/report-turnaround workflow.

## Strongest Copy Risk

Existing EEG providers can copy ads, home appointments, or faster scheduling. The defense is not the idea of home EEG; it is controlling reliable technician capacity, interpretation slots, turnaround SLA, pediatric/sleep-prep playbooks, and neurologist referral trust before broader providers optimize the same flow.

## Strongest Objections And Caveats

- Requires careful legal/medical boundary: the founder coordinates and sells the service, but licensed professionals perform and interpret the test.
- Equipment access must be solved through technicians/providers first; buying equipment early weakens the POC.
- Paid search CAC could rise if large clinics bid aggressively.
- Existing providers may already have capacity; the wedge only works if the founder can offer faster, calmer, or better-coordinated slots.
- Liability, consent, data transfer, and result delivery need proper contracts and privacy handling from day one.

## Profitability Veto

No veto. The idea has clear willingness to pay, immediate cash conversion, measurable gross profit per test, and a concrete 2-month control-point proof. It would fail only if technician/physician capacity cannot be reserved or prepaid appointments cannot be sold.
