# Research Notes

## Official / High-Credibility Sources Checked

- Polish Ministry of Health IVF program page: the 2024-2028 national IVF program started on 1 June 2024, runs through 31 December 2028, and allocates at least 500 million PLN per year. The page reported, as of 15 May 2026, 58 confirmed providers, 50,778 qualified couples, 28,757 clinical pregnancies, and 14,362 children born under the program. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/in-vitro
- The same Ministry page states the program finances qualification, mandatory tests, and necessary IVF procedure components; it includes up to 4 cycles with own gametes or sperm donation, up to 2 cycles with donor oocytes, and up to 6 embryo-donation cycles. Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/in-vitro
- ESHRE Good Practice Recommendations on Expanded Carrier Screening in medically assisted reproduction were open for stakeholder review between 15 May and 12 June 2026. The page frames ECS as reproductive risk assessment and explicitly covers clinical implementation, genetic counselling requirements, informed consent, ethics/legal frameworks, data privacy, and technical considerations. Source: https://www.eshre.eu/ECS-guideline
- Existing project idea files checked for redundancy. The IVF workflow is not the already confirmed biosignal investor diligence pack, supplement safety passport, EEG evidence review shape, or generic health report/dashboard.

## Strategic Interpretation

The underused momentum is unusually concrete: a live Polish state-funded IVF flow with public provider count and confirmed patient volume, plus a Europe-level move toward standardizing expanded carrier screening in MAR. The commercial gap is the clinic workflow around neutral explanation, documentation, and escalation, not genetic test availability.

## Pediatric Sleep-EEG Sources

- EEG-GRAF price list: child sleep EEG 800 PLN until 23:00, plus 400 PLN per later hour; 400 PLN charged for an unsuccessful exam caused by on-site resignation or failure to bring the child to sleep. Mobile sleep EEG listed at 1,100 PLN plus travel. Source: https://eeg.med.pl/prices-of-eeg/
- Feasibility study of sleep-deprived EEG in 261 children: 14% slept less than 15 minutes and 7% had no sleep during recording. Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1090379815002238
- ACNS pediatric EEG guideline: sleep recordings should be obtained whenever possible; sleep deprivation or melatonin may help. Source: https://www.acns.org/UserFiles/file/EEGGuideline5Ped_finaltrackchangesreformat_v1.pdf
- ACNS EEG guideline 1: sleep recordings should be performed whenever possible, sleep deprivation may increase EEG yield, and patient state changes should be noted. Source: https://www.acns.org/UserFiles/file/Guideline1-MinimumTechnicalRequirementsforPerofrmingClinicalEEG_v1.pdf
