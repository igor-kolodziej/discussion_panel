# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ActionKey

## Diagnosis

ActionKey attacks a genuine structural weakness in agentic systems: software should not be trusted to faithfully display the exact high-consequence action it is asking a human to approve. The separately trusted display, issuer keys, and routed call give it a real first-sale control point, while the narrow single-action pilot keeps reachability disciplined. Its main threat is platform absorption by Microsoft, FIDO/security keys, PAM vendors, or agent platforms before independent distribution forms. It is the portfolio's strongest software/hardware economics but has the least observed budget evidence.

## Evidence and Assumptions

NIST and Microsoft establish agent-security concern; FIDO establishes exact transaction-confirmation precedent; EU human oversight is context only. The hardware-specific WTP, 160 accounts, cross-vendor routing, and 35% EBIT are founder hypotheses. The altered-argument demo is a strong disconfirming test.

## Overall Score

**79/100.** Weighted points = 157.0 of 200; `10 × (157 / 20) = 78.5`, rounded to 79.

## Control Point

**Tier 1.** Owned secure-display firmware, issuer keys, canonical schema, terminals, signed receipt registry, and contractually routed approval exist from the first pilot. Strongest copy risk: Microsoft/FIDO/Yubico/PAM adds exact-action trusted confirmation and bundles distribution. Cross-vendor neutrality, deployed keys, audit evidence, and connectors must make catch-up difficult; otherwise the score would remain capped below 80 (it rounds to 79 here).

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, expected case.** Expected 16m PLN revenue, 5.6m EBIT, 75% ownership and 4–6× yield 16.8–25.2m PLN in year 8. Conservative reaches 4.4m PLN only. One hundred sixty global enterprise accounts is demanding but not heroic if the protocol spans platforms; debt is deducted and retained cash is not double counted.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** Five prototypes, one connector, and a security demo fit under 35k PLN. One 20–40k PLN prepaid single-flow design pilot can exist without the complete platform, although enterprise security procurement may exceed 180 days. Targeting smaller agent/security vendors and regulated SaaS teams rather than banks is essential.

## Expansion Check

The one-action beachhead is coherent; schema/connectors and hardware licensing expand outward. Platform vendors are the main entry threat. EU oversight helps urgency but provides no exclusive advantage.

## Fatal Objections

1. Software/PAM confirmation may be accepted as equivalent.
2. Enterprise trust and procurement may break the 180-day gate.
3. Platform/FIDO incumbents can bundle rapidly.
4. The gateway must cryptographically bind displayed and executed arguments across two stacks.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

High-consequence AI actions need a human authorization surface whose display and keys are outside the agent/host trust boundary.
