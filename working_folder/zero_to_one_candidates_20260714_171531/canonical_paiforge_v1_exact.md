# PAIForge — Exact Candidate v1

PAIForge sells fingerprint and hand-biometric/liveness vendors rotating, serialized physical presentation-attack challenge lots so they find spoof failures before paying for formal ISO/FIDO laboratory testing. The buyer is the security or R&D team before an ISO/FIDO submission, sensor change, major algorithm release, or important procurement. Current internal sets use common silicone, gelatin, latex, printed artifacts, or public datasets and can grow stale. PAIForge is a defensive pre-certification R&D input, not an accredited test, certification claim, or anonymous artifact shop.

A starter pack costs 15,000–30,000 PLN and contains 12–20 serialized presentation-attack instruments from withheld founder-owned polymer recipe families, calibration/reference scans, a handling protocol, and a private response report against agreed readers. A rotating annual program costs 45,000–90,000 PLN initially and 90,000–140,000 PLN with mature multi-sensor coverage. Expected starter-pack direct cost is 5,000–7,000 PLN and mature gross margin is about 70%. Under a 20% price reduction, 30% higher delivery cost, and doubled acquisition cost, the starter pack still has positive contribution.

The first-sale control point is the owned material recipes, molds, process tolerances, serialized physical inventory, withheld rotating composition, and cross-reader response/calibration history. Contracts preserve generalized recipe and response improvements. The strongest copy risk is an accredited laboratory or biometric vendor manufacturing its own rotating artifacts from public materials. The product survives only if proprietary materials repeatedly expose cross-sensor failures that common internal materials miss and if continuous rotation is cheaper than maintaining equivalent internal coverage.

Cold acquisition uses 100 verified accounts from public FIDO vendor/laboratory lists, Android biometric implementers, national-ID/access-control suppliers, and biometric conference exhibitors. Five positive responders can conservatively yield one prepaid pack within 180 days. Pre-proof spend is capped at 30,000 PLN for six recipe families, molds, three commodity readers, metrology, legal review, and a verified-customer/shipment policy. No participant recruitment or accreditation is needed. By month 12 and below 100,000 PLN cumulative founder spend, the required proof is two unrelated paid vendors, two reproducible material lots, a second rotating challenge generation, and one renewal or call-off.

The founder stays employed and works no more than 25 hours weekly, owning threat selection, experiment design, cross-reader analytics, documentation, and sales. A fixed-scope polymer scientist fabricates to owned recipes; an embedded/security contractor and counsel cover readers and legitimate-customer controls. Further capital follows only successful paid delivery, positive stressed contribution, and a second buyer. Family capital is excluded. Full-time transition requires three independent recurring vendors, repeatable renewal/acquisition, positive contribution including safety/legal controls, and 12 months personal runway.

Founder-wealth scenarios exclude retained-earnings double counting and deduct debt. Conservative year 7: 70 accounts at 65,000 PLN gives 4.55 million PLN revenue, 25% EBIT, 85% ownership, and about 3.9 million PLN founder equity at 4×. Expected year 8: 180 accounts at 90,000 PLN gives 16.2 million PLN revenue, 30% EBIT, 80% ownership, and 15.6–23.3 million PLN founder equity at 4–6×. Strong year 9: 300 accounts at 120,000 PLN gives 36 million PLN revenue, 34% EBIT, 70% ownership, and about 42.8 million PLN founder equity at 5×. The expected case requires expansion beyond contact fingerprint to keep 180 accounts non-heroic. While employed, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested in conservative and expected cases and optional distributions are capped at 5%; strong success reinvests 100%.

The expansion path is from contact fingerprint/hand sensors into contactless fingerprint, palm, selected face/iris physical artifacts, and defensive licensed datasets only after recurring physical-pack demand. Future-lab assets are adversarial biometric material IP, cross-sensor physical-security datasets, reader hardware expertise, and biometric research/customer relationships.

Kill the idea if no prepaid pack appears after 100 verified accounts and 180 days; common/public materials expose the same failures as proprietary recipes; two buyers say only accredited-lab artifacts are useful; material repeatability fails across two lots; verified-customer/legal controls make CAC or delivery uneconomic; or the expected buyer denominator cannot support at least 10 million PLN of founder value without heroic modality expansion.
