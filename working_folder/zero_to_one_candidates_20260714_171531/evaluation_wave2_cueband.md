# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CueBand Duo

## Diagnosis

CueBand addresses a real accessibility outcome through a direct product rather than institutional trust, and paid preorders are reachable. Its cross-game two-wrist language is more differentiated than generic bass haptics, but Audio Radar, haptic-wearable firms and platform APIs can reproduce the feature. The expected wealth case is coherent if 20,000 global units are reachable, while stressed first-unit contribution is thin. It can enter an independent panel because both hard gates pass, but native/adjacent substitution is a serious veto.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Platform guidance and accessibility organizations support redundant audio cues; Audio Radar/Woojer prove substitutes. Direction-transfer improvement, model accuracy, price, 20k units, CAC, returns and subscription attach are hypotheses. No executed preorders are expected at this stage.

## Overall Score

**76/100.** Weighted points 151.5/200; rounded score 76.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 at product level, weak durability.** Owned hardware, models, firmware, vocabulary, inventory and customer distribution exist at first sale. Strongest copy risk: Audio Radar adds wrist haptics or platforms expose native directional-cue APIs.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, expected case.** 24.75m PLN revenue, 4.95m EBIT, 75% ownership and 4–6× give 14.8–22.3m PLN founder equity in year 7. Conservative case fails. Debt is deducted and cash not double counted.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A wired prototype and direct paid preorders fit 180 days, 38k PLN and part-time execution; no studio/platform partner is required.

## Expansion Check

The five-game accessibility beachhead is clear. Console/VR and non-game alerts expand, but platforms can enter. Poland helps build cost only.

## Fatal Objections

1. Visual/native cues may be preferred.
2. Audio Radar can add haptics.
3. False cues and latency can make information harmful.
4. Consumer CAC/returns can erase thin stressed margin.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Convert information that is normally trapped in sound into a learned, low-attention tactile language without developer integration.
