# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CommitKey Production-Database Relay

## Diagnosis

CommitKey fixes ActionKey's abstract buyer and enforcement chain, but it enters an occupied database-change workflow. Bytebase already eliminates direct access, reviews DDL/DML, applies affected-row/SQL-type approval rules, and governs execution; GitHub already gates deployments and can withhold environment secrets until approval. A separate hardware display adds protection against a compromised approval host, but smaller SaaS buyers can choose simpler Git/CI controls and larger buyers can ask their existing database/PAM vendor to add hardware-backed confirmation. The named wedge is testable, yet it cannot support a non-heroic 10m PLN founder path as written.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official Bytebase documentation shows end-to-end database change workflows, SQL review, conditional approvals, and elimination of direct database access. GitHub supports required reviewers, non-bypassable protection rules, custom protection apps, and withheld environment secrets. PostgreSQL supports certificate authentication, so the proxy is feasible but not scarce. Hardware-specific WTP and 300 accounts remain hypotheses; no executed sales are expected at this concept stage.

## Overall Score

**63/100.** Weighted points = 126 of 200; rounded score = 63.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** A deployed mTLS route is contractually/technically controlled per customer, but the underlying role/proxy/approval architecture is readily built or purchased from database-change/PAM platforms. Strongest copy risk: Bytebase, GitHub, Teleport or a PAM vendor adds hardware-backed exact-action confirmation within the existing workflow.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** Three hundred accounts at 60k PLN produces attractive arithmetic, but winning that many security-sensitive PostgreSQL customers against established end-to-end database-change and CI/CD platforms is heroic. Hardware-specific WTP may be limited to a small high-assurance subsegment.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A proxy, local database lab, attack demo and one terminal fit under 25k PLN, and a small SaaS buyer could prepay within 180 days. This gate cannot compensate for the wealth/control failure.

## Expansion Check

The niche is clear, but broad incumbents move inward easily and every adjacency has established change-management/PAM controls. Poland offers no material defense.

## Fatal Objections

1. Bytebase already controls/reviews/approves DDL and DML end to end.
2. GitHub already provides non-bypassable human/custom deployment gates and secret release.
3. Hardware protects a narrower threat than the target market usually budgets for.
4. Expected customer count is heroic against bundled incumbents.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

None within the database-change/PAM shape; the exact-action trust insight may be useful in a workflow without incumbent approval infrastructure.

## Recommendation

kill
