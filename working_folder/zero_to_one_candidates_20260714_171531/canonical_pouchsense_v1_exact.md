# PouchSense — Exact Candidate v1

PouchSense sells manufacturers of pre-gelled disposable ECG electrodes a proprietary, serialized hydrogel witness that sits inside each sealed pouch and provides an early proxy for loss of usable electrode performance. The buyer is the OEM QA or packaging team at a concrete event: shelf-life validation, a foil/seal/gel/supplier change, or a deviation investigation. Current destructive aged-product sampling is slow and sparse. The product is valuable only if its optical/electrical response predicts release-relevant electrode impedance, noise, adhesion, or other agreed limits better than a generic humidity indicator. It does not replace final-device verification and makes no clinical claim.

The first product is a 12,000–25,000 PLN paid correlation study using three founder-owned witness formulations, a reader/calibration model, serialized samples, and the buyer's existing packaged electrodes. A production design-in is 40,000–90,000 PLN, followed by witness supply at an expected 0.50 PLN per pouch (hypothesized range 0.30–0.80 PLN). Expected direct unit cost is 0.15 PLN. Under the common stress case—price down 20%, direct cost up 30%, doubled acquisition cost—the feasibility order and witness unit both retain positive contribution.

The first-sale control point is the owned formulation, serialized physical inventory, reader/calibration model, retained-lot history, and a contract preserving generalized formulation and calibration improvements. Future pooled data is not the control point. The strongest copy risk is an electrode OEM or packaging converter internalizing a humidity/ion indicator. PouchSense survives only if cross-package predictive history and approved design-ins create meaningful switching cost; a humidity-only result kills it.

Cold start is sample-led technical outbound to 100 named manufacturers and CDMOs found in public FDA and ISO 13485 records. At a 5% positive response and a conservative 20% responder-to-paid conversion, one paid feasibility is possible within 180 days. Pre-proof spend is capped at 25,000 PLN for formulations, reader, accelerated aging, limited contract chemistry, samples, and an FTO screen. The complete package integration is not built before payment. By month 12 and below 100,000 PLN cumulative original-founder spend, the required proof is two unrelated paid studies, predictive correlation across two lots, and one design-in or conditional production call-off.

The founder remains employed and spends at most 25 hours weekly. The founder owns experimental design, analysis, documentation, and sales; a fixed-scope polymer/packaging chemist, external test lab, and IP/regulatory counsel supply missing expertise. Further capital is released only after paid proof, successful delivery, positive stressed contribution, and an independent second customer. Family capital is excluded. The founder leaves employment only after at least three independent paying OEMs or recurring call-offs, positive contribution including QA/liability cost, repeatable acquisition, and 12 months personal runway.

Founder-wealth scenarios do not double-count retained earnings. Conservative year 7: 15 million witnesses at 0.45 PLN plus 12 programs at 50,000 PLN gives 7.35 million PLN revenue, 20% EBIT, 85% ownership, and about 5.0 million PLN founder equity at 4× EBIT. Expected year 8: 40 million witnesses at 0.50 PLN gives 20 million PLN revenue, 25% EBIT, 80% ownership, and 16–24 million PLN founder equity at 4–6× EBIT. Strong year 9: 80 million witnesses at 0.55 PLN gives 44 million PLN revenue, 28% EBIT, 70% ownership, and about 43.1 million PLN founder equity at 5× EBIT. Outstanding debt is deducted. While employed, 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested in conservative and expected cases and optional distributions are capped at 5%; strong success reinvests 100%.

The expansion path is from wet ECG electrodes into EEG, defibrillation, TENS/EMS, and other hydrogel skin-contact disposables only after predictive transfer is proven. The future-lab assets are physiological-interface material IP, an electrode-aging dataset, QA/manufacturing relationships, and upstream biosignal-device distribution.

Kill the idea if no paid study appears after 100 qualified accounts and 180 days; correlation is not materially better than a commodity indicator; FTO identifies blocking claims without a design-around; stressed contribution is non-positive; production proof needs more than 100,000 PLN before a second customer; or OEMs state that the witness cannot shorten, target, or reduce any release/deviation work.
