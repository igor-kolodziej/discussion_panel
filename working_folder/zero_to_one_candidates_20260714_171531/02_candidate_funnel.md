# Official 20-Direction Funnel

This is the skill-compliant official funnel. It covers all five requested first-sale control archetypes, contains more than six directions outside wearable testing or EEG, and includes contrarian shapes marked **C**. A retained direction is not validated; it only clears the generator's wealth and reachability plausibility screen for development as a finalist or backup.

## A. Proprietary consumables or reference materials

### 1. PouchSense Hydrogel Shelf-Life Witness **C** — finalist

- **Buyer/event:** wet ECG/EEG/defibrillation-electrode OEM QA at shelf-life validation, packaging change, or deviation investigation.
- **First paid offer:** 12–25k PLN feasibility, then 40–90k PLN design-in and 0.30–0.80 PLN per pouch.
- **First-sale control:** owned witness chemistry, calibration model, serialized stock, and packaging-integration specification.
- **Acquisition:** public FDA records and ISO 13485 electrode/CDMO directories; 100 named accounts at 5% response still yields five conversations.
- **M3/M6/M12 proof:** three chemistries and accelerated-aging correlation; paid feasibility; two repeat/OEM design-in decisions under 100k PLN total.
- **Wealth hypothesis:** 40m witnesses × 0.50 PLN = 20m PLN revenue; 25% EBIT, 5× EBIT, 80% founder ownership = 20m PLN founder equity before debt.
- **Copy risk:** packaging supplier or OEM internalizes the indicator; predictive validity is the decisive technical risk.
- **Future-lab asset:** electrode-aging dataset, materials IP, and upstream biosignal-OEM access.

### 2. PAIForge Biometric Challenge Artifacts **C** — finalist

- **Buyer/event:** fingerprint/hand biometric and liveness/PAD vendors before ISO/FIDO laboratory submission or a sensor/algorithm release.
- **First paid offer:** 15–30k PLN rotating challenge pack with 12–20 serialized presentation-attack instruments and response report; 45–90k PLN annual challenge subscription.
- **First-sale control:** owned polymer recipes, artifact molds, calibration files, serialized inventory, and withheld challenge composition—not a certification claim.
- **Acquisition:** FIDO vendor/accredited-lab ecosystem, public Android/FIDO implementers, and 100 named biometric vendors; sample-led technical outbound.
- **M3/M6/M12 proof:** fabricate six materials against three commodity readers; obtain a prepaid pre-cert regression pack; demonstrate a second rotating lot and one renewal under 100k PLN.
- **Wealth hypothesis:** 180 vendors/labs × 90k PLN blended annual revenue = 16.2m PLN; 30% EBIT, 5×, 80% ownership = 19.4m PLN founder equity.
- **Copy risk:** accredited labs keep their own attack recipes and vendors use public materials. Novel cross-sensor materials and continuously rotated hidden lots must create value.
- **Future-lab asset:** adversarial biometric material IP, physical-security datasets, and biometric research relationships.

### 3. SweatFlux Dynamic Challenge Cartridges — backup

- **Buyer/event:** wearable sweat-sensor OEM at chemistry, microfluidic, reagent-lot, or firmware freeze.
- **Control:** owned lyophilized formulation, sweat-rate/interferent membrane, and serialized single-use cartridge.
- **Proof/economics:** 15–25k PLN preorder under 35k founder risk; hypothesis 120 accounts × 120 cartridges × 900 PLN = 13m PLN revenue.
- **Decision:** retain as backup, not finalist; commodity artificial sweat and small buyer denominator remain decision-critical.

### 4. TactiWear Contamination/Aging Coupon Books — backup

- **Buyer/event:** tactile-sensor, gripper, prosthetic-hand, and haptic-device QA at qualification or regression.
- **Control:** owned low-force contamination/aging formulations, conditioning method, calibration curves, and lot-certified consumable stock.
- **Proof/economics:** paid beta under 35k PLN; hypothesis 250 accounts × 50k PLN annual = 12.5m PLN revenue.
- **Decision:** retain as backup; must outperform Fujifilm/Tekscan and in-house coupons on a release-relevant metric.

## B. Owned benchmark hardware or instruments

### 5. HumanEcho AMR Human-Perception Surrogate **C** — finalist

- **Buyer/event:** warehouse AMR OEM/integrator before customer acceptance, sensor change, or safety/perception software release.
- **First paid offer:** 25–40k PLN paid feasibility or preorder for an active human surrogate; production system 70–120k PLN plus 12–25k PLN randomized PPE/occlusion skin packs.
- **First-sale control:** owned thermal/optical/radar/geometry core, calibrated signature files, replaceable serialized PPE/occlusion skins, and regression software.
- **Acquisition:** ISO 3691-4 supplier lists, public AMR vendor directories, and 100 named European AMR teams; offer one mailed passive skin and a recorded repeatability comparison.
- **M3/M6/M12 proof:** passive lower-limb/PPE skins and signature measurements; paid active-core feasibility; two customers and four recurring skin SKUs under 100k PLN.
- **Wealth hypothesis:** 120 accounts × 140k PLN blended annual hardware/skin/calibration = 16.8m PLN; 27% EBIT, 5×, 78% ownership = 17.7m PLN founder equity.
- **Copy risk:** automotive target incumbents down-market or AMR OEMs build foam dummies. Warehouse-specific multimodal signatures, low price, and continuously refreshed skins are required.
- **Future-lab asset:** human-perception hardware, multi-sensor signatures, robotics/HCI capability, and AMR distribution.

### 6. LoopTruth Teleoperation/Haptic Round-Trip Calibrator — finalist

- **Buyer/event:** teleoperation, mobile-robot, haptic-device, or private-5G team at network/site acceptance or release.

- **First-sale control:** owned synchronized timebase, trigger/correlation firmware, optical/motion/force fixtures, and calibration history.
- **Acquisition:** IEEE 1918.1.1 contributors, EU teleoperation projects, and public vendor lists; 100 named accounts.
- **M3/M6/M12 proof:** bench unit under 40k PLN; paid comparison; three instruments or two design-ins and repeat calibration under 100k PLN.
- **Wealth hypothesis:** 220 accounts × 70k PLN blended annual = 15.4m PLN; 28% EBIT, 5×, 80% ownership = 17.2m PLN founder equity.
- **Copy risk:** an engineer with an oscilloscope, camera, and force sensor reproduces it. One-session device-agnostic correlation and a calibration network must save enough engineering time.
- **Future-lab asset:** tactile-internet metrology, real-time HCI expertise, and latency datasets.

## E. Owned inventory, distribution, or routed transaction flow

- **Buyer/event:** employer operating smart garments before rollout, wash damage, or shift expansion.
- **First paid offer:** 10–25k PLN setup plus 45–75 PLN per garment/month for owned spare-pod pooling, inspection, firmware, repair, and redeployment.
- **First-sale control:** serialized founder-owned spare-pod inventory and a contract routing every pod removal, wash exception, repair, and replacement through the system.
- **Acquisition:** public smart-workwear deployments, ETSA/laundry directories, and direct employer outreach; first employer can contract directly without a laundry partner.
- **M3/M6/M12 proof:** 20-pod custody process; prepaid 50-garment employer pilot; two independent fleets or 500 garments under route under 100k PLN.
- **Wealth hypothesis:** 30k garments × 55 PLN/month = 19.8m PLN recurring revenue; 23% EBIT, 5×, 75% ownership = 17.1m PLN founder equity.
- **Copy risk:** RFID laundry platform or garment OEM adds custody/repair. Cross-brand pod pooling, failure data, and replacement inventory must lower downtime enough to resist bundling.
