## New evidence and incumbent warnings

- Xbox accessibility guidance supports additional channels for audio cues; Audio Radar and Woojer prove active substitutes: <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103>, <https://audioradar.com/products/audio-radar>, <https://www.woojer.com/pages/home>.
- GlassOuse and MouthPad prove assistive input willingness and substitution; Apple head tracking and Meta EMG are platform threats: <https://glassouse.com/>, <https://www.augmental.tech/>, <https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iph9c3dc17cf/ios>, <https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/>.
- Soft Robotics published a finger discontinuation notice and preventive replacement material, establishing an operational replacement event: <https://www.softroboticsinc.com/uploads/2022/08/Traditional-Finger_DiscontinuationNotice_6_21_22-1.pdf>, <https://www.softroboticsinc.com/uploads/2023/11/Soft_Robotics_FingerPreventative_Flyer_Update_HR.pdf>.
- ATI already sells collision-protection products, making installed distribution and product liability major CrashFuse risks: <https://www.ati-ia.com/products/collision_sensor/robot_collision_sensor.aspx>.
