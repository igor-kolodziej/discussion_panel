## Verdict

**Score: 66/100**

## Strongest objections and caveats

- The founder-wealth case depends on 20,000 annual global units in a narrow segment, without bottom-up evidence that the reachable market, replacement cadence, conversion, and CAC support that volume. The conservative case explicitly fails the 10m PLN target.
- The first-100 acquisition plan is a list of plausible channels, not yet a repeatable funnel with traffic, conversion, CAC, and payback bounds. Community participation may also become high-touch.
- Accurate, low-latency event and direction inference from inconsistent game audio—especially already-rendered headphone audio—could produce false cues or cognitive burden that erase the accessibility benefit.
- The 55% gross and 20% operating margins may understate VAT, certification, warranty, returns, support, assembly, inventory, failure-rate, and accessible-onboarding costs. The annual update subscription has no demonstrated durable value.
- Twenty-five preorders are credible demand evidence but do not validate retained use, returns, repeat acquisition, or the expected-scale economics. Requiring positive delivered contribution before additional production spending also creates a potentially circular fulfillment gate.
- The prototype and preorder test appear reachable within 180 days and 38,000 PLN, but compliant small-batch manufacturing across five games within the 100,000 PLN first-year cap is materially less certain.

## Control point

**Tier 3.** The company would own its design, firmware, models, device keys, and early customer data, but these are ordinary product assets rather than scarce or exclusive market access. The proprietary corpus, learned tactile-language switching costs, installed base, and accessibility distribution are all prospective and require scale before they compound. Commodity components and unowned community/search channels leave the initial position readily reproducible.

## Strongest copy risk

A platform, game-engine, or established gaming-peripheral incumbent can combine cleaner native positional data with wrist haptics and its existing distribution. Native directional-cue APIs would be especially damaging because they bypass CueBand's hardest and least reliable component: inference from mixed audio.

## Official panel result

The official simulated score is the lower score: **65/100**. Both independent judges identified the same fatal copy/control objection. The family is closed without real-chat validation.
