# HumanEcho — Exact Candidate v1

HumanEcho sells warehouse autonomous-mobile-robot teams a compact active human surrogate with calibrated optical, thermal, radar, and geometry signatures plus replaceable randomized PPE/occlusion skins for repeatable human-detection and stopping regression. The buyer is an AMR OEM or integrator before customer acceptance, a sensor change, or a perception/safety software release. Current workarounds—foam obstacles, staff walk-throughs, open standard objects, or expensive automotive pedestrian targets—are either weakly repeatable or mismatched to narrow indoor aisles, crouched/fallen workers, reflective/black PPE, occlusion, and thermal contrast. HumanEcho supplies regression evidence and makes no statutory certification claim.

The first paid product is a 25,000–40,000 PLN feasibility/preorder based on a passive comparison and an active-core specification. A production system costs 70,000–120,000 PLN; the expected 80,000 PLN unit has a 25,000 PLN BOM. Serialized randomized PPE/occlusion skin and calibration packs cost 12,000–25,000 PLN; an expected 15,000 PLN pack has 4,000 PLN direct cost. Under a 20% price cut, 30% BOM increase, and doubled acquisition cost, both the active system and skin pack retain positive contribution.

The first-sale control point is the owned active-core design, calibrated multimodal signature files, proprietary skin formulations/geometries, serialized randomized skin inventory, and regression software/history. The strongest copy risk is an automotive pedestrian-target incumbent offering a warehouse SKU or an AMR OEM building an adequate foam dummy. HumanEcho survives only if it is materially cheaper, fits warehouse spaces, reproduces multiple sensor signatures and postures, and refreshes physical edge cases faster than internal construction. A geometry-only mannequin kills the concept.

Cold acquisition uses 100 named European/global AMR OEMs, integrators, perception suppliers, and public installation/project lists. At 5% positive response, one paid feasibility is possible if the sales artifact is a recorded cross-sensor repeatability comparison plus a mailed passive skin. Pre-proof spend is capped at 35,000 PLN for passive limb/body geometry, thermal insert, four PPE/occlusion skins, and optical/thermal measurements. The active core is not completed before payment. By month 12 and below 100,000 PLN cumulative founder spend, proof requires two unrelated paid customers, a functioning multimodal core, four replacement skin SKUs, repeat calibration, and at least one skin/calibration reorder or contracted call-off.

The founder remains employed and works at most 25 hours weekly, owning signal design, data analysis, regression software, documentation, and sales. Mechanical/embedded design, sewing/coating, thermal control, EMC/safety review, and calibration are fixed-scope contracts. Further capital is released only after paid feasibility, successful delivery, positive stressed contribution, and a second buyer. Family capital is excluded. Full-time transition requires three independent paying customers or recurring skin/calibration contracts, repeatable technical acquisition, positive support-adjusted contribution, and 12 months personal runway.

Founder-wealth scenarios deduct debt and do not add retained cash to the EBIT-based value. Conservative year 7: 45 accounts at 110,000 PLN annual value gives 4.95 million PLN revenue, 22% EBIT, 85% ownership, and about 3.7 million PLN founder equity at 4×. Expected year 8: 120 accounts at 140,000 PLN gives 16.8 million PLN revenue, 27% EBIT, 78% ownership, and 14.2–21.2 million PLN founder equity at 4–6×. Strong year 9: 250 accounts at 180,000 PLN gives 45 million PLN revenue, 30% EBIT, 68% ownership, and about 45.9 million PLN founder equity at 5×. While employed, 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested in conservative and expected cases and distributions are capped at 5%; strong success reinvests 100%.

The expansion path is from warehouse AMR human detection to cobots, industrial vehicles, security robots, and wearable-robot coexistence. Future-lab assets are multimodal human-perception hardware, calibrated physical edge-case inventory, robotics/HCI datasets, fabrication/calibration expertise, and AMR distribution.

Kill the idea if no paid feasibility appears after 100 qualified accounts and 180 days; standard foam/geometry obstacles perform equivalently; two buyers require an incumbent certified target before considering regression; recurring skins do not change release decisions; stressed contribution is non-positive; or the active core and four skin SKUs cannot be proven under 100,000 PLN.
