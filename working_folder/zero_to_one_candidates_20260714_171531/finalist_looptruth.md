# Zero-to-One Candidate: LoopTruth

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell teleoperation and haptic teams a portable device-agnostic instrument that measures complete camera-to-command-to-actuation-to-haptic latency, jitter, loss, and desynchronization in one synchronized session.

## Founder Fit

The founder can design measurements, correlate multi-rate time series, and automate reports. A contract embedded/FPGA engineer and calibration lab cover hardware/timebase work.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Teleoperation, mobile-robot, haptic, and private-5G teams debug round-trip performance with oscilloscopes, cameras, network telemetry, and ad hoc markers. Each subsystem owns a timestamp, making cross-vendor blame and site acceptance slow.

## Product Wedge

A portable calibrated unit emits synchronized optical, motion, and force triggers, observes return video/command/actuation/haptic events, and produces one trace and certificate. It is metrology, not a custom integration project.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The value is one-session attribution across the full loop rather than superior versions of tools buyers already own. The gap exists only if saved engineering time exceeds the cost of a dedicated instrument.

## Underused Momentum

IEEE haptic-codec standardization, wireless time-sensitive networking, teleoperation, and physical AI create synchronization problems across heterogeneous stacks.

## Beachhead Market

European teleoperation/haptic vendors and private-5G integrators performing customer acceptance across at least three vendor subsystems.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

15–25k PLN feasibility; 45–70k PLN instrument; 10–15k PLN annual calibration/fixtures. Expected 60k price, 20k BOM, 60–67% gross margin; 28% mature operating margin is a hypothesis.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

IEEE contributors, EU robotics projects, public teleoperation vendors, mobile-robot OEMs, haptic-device firms, and private-5G integrators provide 100 named accounts. First ten are direct technical comparisons; first 100 require instrument distributors/calibration channels.

## Primary Control Point

Owned timebase/trigger hardware, correlation firmware, fixtures, and calibration history. These are directly produced at first sale but remain reproducible with routine engineering.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

An FPGA/dev-board, camera, actuator, load cell, and fixtures cost up to 40k PLN. A paid comparison is required before production instrument tooling.

## Distribution Advantage

Published standards/project lists enable technical outbound, but no durable distribution advantage is established.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Begin with teleoperation acceptance, then haptic codec interoperability, remote surgery research, wearable telepresence, and industrial private 5G. Expansion depends on becoming a recognized calibration network.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

EU research and industrial networks help account discovery; Poland offers low-cost engineering. Neither prevents internal replication.

## Compounding Advantage

Cross-device traces and calibration histories could improve diagnostics, but buyers may not share enough data for compounding.

A defensible 100-account sensitivity produces only 4.8m PLN founder equity at 4×, causing the expected wealth gate to fail. No retained cash is double counted.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

95% reinvestment in conservative/expected cases while employed; ≤5% optional distributions; 100% reinvestment strong case. Paid comparison month 6 and three systems month 12 are required.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Build one bench unit under 40k PLN and seek a 15–25k PLN paid cross-vendor comparison from 100 accounts. This remains employment-compatible.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

Maximum pre-proof loss 40k PLN; more capital only after paid comparison and positive stressed contribution; 100k PLN ceiling until multiple customers. Family capital excluded.

## Critical Dependency Map

Buyer budget owner, dedicated-instrument WTP, calibration trust, internal-tool substitution, and a sufficiently large buyer universe.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Three independent customers, recurring calibration, positive contribution, repeatable acquisition, 12 months runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Retain timing firmware, fixtures, correlation software, and haptic/teleoperation contacts. Do not pivot to custom integration.

## Critical Evidence

IEEE haptic codecs and NIST time-sensitive networking establish technical relevance, not product demand.

## Critical Hypotheses

A one-session result saves enough engineering time; 220 annual buyers exist; a calibration brand can form before internal teams copy.

## Kill Criteria

No paid comparison after 100 accounts; buyers reproduce the result in under 90 days; 100-account wealth sensitivity remains below 10m PLN; annual calibration attach below 30%.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15 hours/week, embedded/FPGA contractor, calibration lab. Pre-proof 40k PLN.
