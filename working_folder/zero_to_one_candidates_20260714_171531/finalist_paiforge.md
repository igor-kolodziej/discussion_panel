# Zero-to-One Candidate: PAIForge

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell biometric and liveness vendors rotating, serialized physical presentation-attack challenge lots with proprietary polymer recipes and cross-sensor calibration, so they find spoof failures before paying for formal ISO/FIDO laboratory testing.

## Founder Fit

The founder brings biosignal/sensor reasoning, ML evaluation, adversarial testing, and data analysis. A contract polymer scientist fabricates to owned recipes; an embedded/security contractor maintains readers and a lawyer defines legitimate-customer and shipment controls.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Fingerprint/hand biometric component and PAD vendors face costly late failure when an unfamiliar material crosses sensors or algorithms. They cast common silicone, gelatin, latex, or printed artifacts internally, use public datasets, then submit to an accredited lab. Internal material sets grow stale and are not cross-sensor characterized.

## Product Wedge

A pre-certification challenge pack contains 12–20 serialized fingerprint/hand presentation-attack instruments from withheld recipe families, calibration/reference scans, handling protocol, and a private response report across agreed readers. It is a security R&D product, not certification or an attack service for anonymous buyers.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Formal labs own trust, but vendors want inexpensive failure discovery before the formal test. Because presentation attacks are open-ended and cross-material generalization is difficult, a rotating physical challenge subscription can be more valuable than another static dataset.

## Underused Momentum

ISO/IEC 30107, FIDO laboratory requirements, Android spoof-material guidance, and biometric deployment create continuing PAD pressure. Peer-reviewed polymer work demonstrates that novel material behavior is technically possible.

## Beachhead Market

Small and mid-sized fingerprint/hand biometric component and liveness vendors preparing an ISO/FIDO test or major sensor/algorithm release; exclude consumer hackers and anonymous purchasers.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Starter challenge pack: 15–30k PLN; expected direct cost 5–7k PLN.
- Annual rotating program: 45–90k PLN initially, 90–140k PLN at mature multi-sensor coverage.
- Expected gross margin 70%; operating margin 30% after polymer R&D, safe-customer screening, QA, sales, insurance, and reader depreciation.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

Use public FIDO vendor/accredited-lab lists, Android component vendors, national-ID/access-control suppliers, and biometric conference exhibitors. First ten are sample-led technical outbound to vendor security/R&D leads. First 100 expand across fingerprint, palm, contactless finger, and selected face/iris physical artifacts; formal labs may distribute but are not required for the first sale.

## Primary Control Point

Owned recipe families, molds, process tolerances, serialized artifact inventory, cross-reader response/calibration history, and withheld rotating composition. Contracts retain generalized recipe and response improvements. Certification authority is explicitly not claimed.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Under 22k PLN funds six material families, molds, three commodity readers, metrology, and a safe-customer policy. One 15–30k PLN prepaid pack is required before expanding readers or recipes. By month 12 and under 95k PLN cumulative spend, two unrelated buyers and one renewal/second rotation must exist.

## Distribution Advantage

The account universe is public and technically narrow. A useful first pack creates a renewal calendar tied to release/certification cycles. Withheld composition, serialized chain of custody, and safe-customer screening make ordinary ecommerce copying less attractive.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with contact fingerprint/hand sensors; expand to contactless fingerprint, palm, selected masks/eye artifacts, and licensed defensive datasets only after repeat demand. Recipe/response history and challenge rotation compound faster than a static kit.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers cost-efficient materials experimentation and EU shipping. ISO/FIDO markets are global. EU high-risk-biometric rules add context, but the product cannot depend on regulation or local trust alone.

## Compounding Advantage

Each new material, sensor physics, failure, and aging lot expands a private adversarial response map. Buyers renew for unseen lots; recipes and response data can support defensive ML tooling and research relationships.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of distributable after-tax cash while employed; founder distributions remain at or below 5%. Strong success reinvests 100%. Milestones: repeatable artifact by month 3; paid pack by month 6; second buyer/rotation by month 12; 25 accounts by year 3; 70 by year 5–7; expected scale by year 8.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Build six recipe families and a three-reader matrix in 12 weeks, then send controlled technical samples only to verified companies. Ask 100 accounts for a prepaid 15–30k PLN pre-cert regression pack. Maximum pre-proof loss is 22–30k PLN; no accreditation or participant recruitment is required.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. Up to 30k PLN for recipes, molds, readers, metrology, legal/customer policy.
2. Up to 40k PLN cumulative only after prepaid pack.
3. Up to 100k PLN after successful delivery and a second independent buyer/renewal.
4. Family capital excluded until repeat demand and safe scaling. Maximum pre-proof loss: 30k PLN.

## Critical Dependency Map

- New materials must expose incremental failures: controlled experiment, existential.
- Safe/legal distribution: verified-buyer policy and counsel, controllable.
- Accredited labs are substitutes: product must remain pre-cert R&D, not borrow authority.
- Polymer reproducibility: contract fabrication to owned tolerances; test by two lots.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

At least three independent recurring vendors, positive contribution including QA/security controls, repeatable outbound/renewal acquisition, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Retain recipes, molds, reader rigs, sensor-response data, security customer map, and polymer contractors for biometric reference materials. Do not pivot to generic biometric consulting or unvetted artifact sales.

## Critical Evidence

ISO/FIDO require PAD testing; Android recommends physical spoof materials; NIST describes an open-ended PAI set; research shows novel polymers can alter spoof performance.

## Critical Hypotheses

Vendors pay before formal testing; proprietary recipes find failures beyond public materials; verified sales are legally and reputationally manageable; 180 expected accounts exist across modalities.

## Kill Criteria

- No prepaid pack after 100 verified accounts/180 days.
- Public/common materials find the same failures as proprietary recipes.
- Two prospective buyers say only accredited-lab artifacts are useful.
- Material repeatability fails across two lots.
- Safe-customer/legal controls make CAC or delivery uneconomic.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; contract polymer scientist 40–80 hours; part-time embedded/security contractor and counsel. Pre-proof maximum 30k PLN; year-one ceiling 95k PLN.
