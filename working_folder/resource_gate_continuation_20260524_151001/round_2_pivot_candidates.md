# Round 2 Pivot Candidates

Round 1 failed because most assets were either legally uncertain, operationally copyable, or too dependent on incumbent trust. Round 2 changes the control point: the founder must be able to secure an explicit right, deposit, option, or named asset that survives first contact with regulation and buyer behavior.

## R2-1. SomnoConsent PSG Reconsent Rights Bank

- **Topic:** biosignals / sleep.
- **Buyer:** sleep-AI, CPAP/respiratory diagnostics, wearable, digital-health, and medtech companies needing EU PSG/HSAT validation data.
- **Hidden insight:** The bottleneck is not raw PSG existence; it is the missing commercial consent layer. Small labs own dormant archives and patient contact channels but will not run reconsent/data-product work themselves. AI companies need clinically real signals but cannot wait for slow hospital data-access deals.
- **Control point:** signed exclusive mandates with specific sleep labs to run a DPO-approved patient reconsent campaign over named archived PSG/HSAT nights, plus the first explicitly consented records and buyer inspection deposits. The right is not generic data brokerage; it is exclusive conversion access to named archives before competitors can touch them.
- **2-month proof:** sign 2-3 lab mandates covering named archives; lawyer/DPO memo using explicit consent for health-data processing; send lab-controlled invitations to 500-1,000 former patients; obtain 50-150 explicit commercial-validation consents; inventory EDF/scoring quality; collect 2 refundable buyer inspection deposits of 5,000-15,000 PLN.
- **Budget:** 20-30k PLN legal/DPO, 10-20k lab option fees, 15-40k patient incentives, 10-20k extraction/secure data-room work, 5-10k buyer outreach.
- **Why unknown founder can secure it:** small labs have storage burden, no data business, and no internal DPO/data-engineering product team. The founder offers cash option fees, no raw export before consent, lab-controlled patient contact, and revenue share on otherwise dormant archives.
- **Why hard to copy:** exact lab mandates and patient consents are locked once signed. Incumbents can run prospective studies, but they cannot cheaply copy the already-consented archive once the founder controls named sources.
- **Initial harsh score guess:** 91-93 if the reconsent mechanism is accepted by a DPO/lawyer and at least one buyer pays a deposit.

## R2-2. Patient-Owned PSG Data Bounty Bank

- **Topic:** biosignals / 50-60 sleep-apnea patients.
- **Buyer:** same as R2-1.
- **Control point:** explicit data-reuse assignments from individual patients who request/export their own PSG/HSAT raw files and reports.
- **2-month proof:** recruit 100 diagnosed patients through paid ads and sleep-apnea communities; pay only when EDF/report files are obtained; collect buyer deposits.
- **Kill reason:** lower lab dependence, but patient retrieval may be slow and raw files may not be included in the accessible record. Likely more copyable and messy than lab-mandate reconsent.
- **Initial guess:** 84.

## R2-3. Orphan Implant Non-Implantable Tool And Scan-Body Bank

- **Topic:** 50-60 dental continuity.
- **Control point:** sealed traceable scan bodies, torque drivers, adapters, lab analogs, CAD libraries, and only regulator-approved sealed components.
- **2-month proof:** buy/options over traceable drawers, 10 paid dentist search deposits, MDR distributor memo.
- **Kill reason:** safer than implantable parts, but may not solve the highest-pain cases where the actual abutment/screw is missing.
- **Initial guess:** 86.

## R2-4. Founder-Variant Synthetic Control Deposit Pool

- **Topic:** genetics / IVD.
- **Control point:** prepaid synthetic-control lots for CEE founder variants, independently sequence-confirmed.
- **2-month proof:** 3 lab deposits, 10-20 variant first lot, acceptance memo.
- **Kill reason:** still copyable by specialist reference-material vendors or labs via gene-synthesis contract unless the local acceptance history compounds quickly.
- **Initial guess:** 88.

## R2-5. Forensic STR Surplus-Lot Escrow

- **2-month proof:** buyer deposits before acquiring lots.

## Hard Filter Result

Only **R2-1 SomnoConsent PSG Reconsent Rights Bank** changes the underlying business shape enough to re-enter strict simulated scoring. It preserves the strongest F1 supply insight while replacing the weak legal assumption with a proof path based on explicit consent, exclusive lab mandates, and buyer deposits.
