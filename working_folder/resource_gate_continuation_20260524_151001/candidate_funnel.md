## First Hard Filter

Current finalists for stricter review:

1. **Verified Family Grave-Right Options** - strongest right/control-point candidate, direct 50-60 family buyer/trigger, taboo/distressed supply incumbents may avoid.
2. **Legacy Dental Implant Rescue Bank** - strong direct 50-60 health-transition pain and physical inventory, but traceability/liability must be narrowed.
3. **Genotype-Clear Dog Germplasm Option Pool** - genetics inventory plus proprietary genotype documentation; potentially defensible in one high-inbreeding breed.
4. **Rare-Breed Frozen Semen Estate Option Exchange** - simpler, stronger cold start than #3 but less defensible without genotype layer.
5. **Sport-Horse Frozen Semen Distress Options** - higher ticket genetics inventory; tougher incumbent landscape.
6. **Research-Grade Actigraphy Liquidation Pool** - biosignal physical inventory; included as a biosignal finalist only if scout feedback finds urgent buyers.

Likely kills before simulation:

- Human gamete, embryo, implantable medical device, prescription medication, and regulated sample-control ideas are too trust-heavy or illegal for an unknown founder within two months.
- Generic appointment slots, evidence desks, patient books, and rescue routes repeat the old failed shape.
- Commodity device inventory without exclusive options or urgent buyer deposits is not a control point.

## Scout Convergence

Two scouts independently converged on orphan dental implant compatibility as the strongest 50-60 physical-inventory candidate, but both capped it around 88 because traceability, medical-device resale, dentist willingness, and patient-supplied part use may break the POC.

The strongest biosignal scout candidate was **Retiring Sleep-Lab Raw PSG Rights Bank**, estimated around 90-92 if legal/data-protection structure is real. This is materially different from the old evidence-desk and device-service patterns because the control point is an exclusive option over named raw signal archives, not expert interpretation or routing.
