## Required Pivot

No Round 1 finalist clears the simulated gate. The next candidate must change the business shape around a stronger proof of control:

- reduce health-data legal uncertainty by using explicit re-consent, secure processing, or non-export validation;
- reduce medical-device liability by making the control point traceable and non-gray-market;
- avoid businesses where the closest incumbent can copy via one inventory allocation or one service-contract add-on;
- require paid deposits or signed exclusive mandates during the POC, not LOIs alone.

## Strongest Surviving Insight

The best raw insight remains **dormant, legally retained biosignal archives are valuable, but only if the founder can turn archive access into explicit patient-consented, buyer-deposited validation rights**. This shifts the control point from "old data may exist" to "exclusive right to convert named archived recordings into consented, commercial validation assets."
