# Round 2 Resource-First Pivots

Round 1 failed because even the best candidates still depended on legal clearance, dentist trust, clinical labor, or generic inventory. Round 2 starts from assets that can be named, optioned, assigned, verified, and sold with deposits before the founder spends deeply.

## P1. CavalierClear Frozen Semen Rights Bank

**Business shape:** A one-breed genetics rights bank for Cavalier King Charles Spaniels that secures exclusive 180-day options over named frozen semen lots from deceased, retired, or geographically inaccessible studs, pays for modern genotype/health documentation where missing, verifies semen storage and transferability, then sells reservation-backed access to serious EU breeders who need specific bloodlines without hidden EF/CCDE carrier surprises.

**Control point:** Signed exclusive option and revenue-share over named frozen semen lots held at identifiable reproduction clinics or by retiring breeders, plus test results, pedigree documentation, semen-quality proof, and buyer deposit rights.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Contract a breed-insider broker/reproduction-vet advisor; option 10-15 named lots for 1-3k PLN each plus storage-fee coverage; pay for DNA panels and documentation review; secure 3-5 breeder deposits of 500-1,500 EUR for specific lots; produce a closed data room with transferability, storage proof, genotype status, pedigree, and fee terms.

**Why this may pass:** The exact semen lots are unique. Breeders cannot buy "equivalent inventory" if they want a specific line. The founder can pay small option fees before public demand is revealed. The asset is not a dashboard, report, or service.

**Main cap risk:** Breed politics and trust. Cavaliers have high health anxiety, but some serious issues are polygenic/non-DNA-simple, so "genotype-clear" must not overclaim.

## P2. Warmblood Legacy Semen Transfer-Rights Escrow

**Business shape:** A genetics transaction gate for sport-horse breeders that secures optioned transfer rights over posthumous or retired-stallion frozen semen straws stored in EU clinics, verifies contract transferability and storage, and sells escrow-backed reservations to mare owners during breeding season.

**Control point:** Assigned transfer/right-to-sell control over named straws from named stallions, with clinic storage confirmation and seller mandate.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Reserve 20-40 straws with small option fees or broker mandates; verify transfer language; collect 5 mare-owner deposits for specific named-stallion straws; build no broad marketplace.

**Why this may pass:** Each straw is a finite, named genetic asset. Sport-horse breeding has higher transaction values than dogs and urgent seasonal timing.

**Main cap risk:** Existing stallion agents and breeding networks already own trust; many straws may be non-transferable.

## P3. Doberman Longevity Semen Option Bank

**Business shape:** A one-breed frozen semen option pool for Doberman breeders focused not on simplistic "DCM-clear" claims but on older deceased/retired studs with documented longevity, Holter/echo records, vWD status, pedigree diversity, and semen quality.

**Control point:** Exclusive options over named semen lots from older/longevity-proven lines plus documentation package and breeder deposits.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Hire breed insider; option 10 lots; verify storage, semen quality, vWD and cardiac documentation; collect deposits from 3 serious breeders.

**Why this may pass:** DCM anxiety and working-line economics create willingness to pay for specific pedigrees and older-proven males.

**Main cap risk:** DCM genetics are not clean enough for a simple genotype claim; medical records may be inconsistent.

## P4. Rare-Breed Genetic Diversity Ark For Polish/CEE Native Dogs

**Business shape:** A breed-by-breed option bank that secures frozen semen from underrepresented Polish/CEE native or rare working dog lines before older breeders exit, documents genotype/diversity, and sells reservation-backed access to breeders trying to avoid bottleneck collapse.

**Control point:** Named frozen semen options plus genotype/diversity documentation.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Select one breed, option 10 lots, secure breeder deposits, and publish no open marketplace until options are signed.

**Why this may pass:** Local language/network advantage may matter, and rare-line material is unique.

**Main cap risk:** WTP may be too low in rare/native breeds despite genetic importance.

## P5. Founder-Mutation Positive-Control Rights Bank For One Oncology Panel

**Business shape:** Secure exclusive commercial rights over rare mutation-positive residual DNA/cell-line/control material for one narrow oncology panel where EU labs need controls for validation and EQA readiness.

**Control point:** Assigned rights over named positive-control material, not generic analysis.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Legal/ethics memo, one lab/biobank material-transfer option, and paid inspection deposit from 2 molecular labs.

**Why this may pass:** Named material is scarce and buyer WTP can be high.

**Main cap risk:** Human sample legal/ethics path likely kills the 2-month proof for an unknown founder.

## P6. FoalRight Legacy Semen Reassignment Bank

**Business shape:** A sport-horse genetics transaction gate that finds EU-stored frozen semen straws/doses whose owners are retiring, exiting breeding, handling estates, or tired of storage fees, then secures assignable sale mandates or 180-day exclusive options over specific named-stallion inventory. FoalRight verifies clinic storage, transferability, IETS/EU status where relevant, nomination/registration constraints, and shipment requirements, then sells escrow-backed reservations to mare owners during the breeding season.

**Control point:** Assigned revenue/right/control over named frozen semen inventory: "Owner X, clinic Y, 6 doses / 24 straws from stallion Z, storage confirmed, transfer language checked, clinic release terms known, FoalRight has exclusive 180-day sale mandate and buyer-deposit authority." The company does not list random stallions; it controls specific straws/doses before buyer demand is exposed.

**2-month proof under 100k PLN:** Hire one equine reproduction contracts specialist or stud-season broker for 10-20k PLN; contact EU equine storage clinics and breeders with overdue/unused inventory; pay storage arrears or option fees on 15-30 named doses for 30-60k PLN; collect 5 mare-owner reservation deposits of 300-1,000 EUR for specific inventory; document one clinic-confirmed release workflow and escrow template. Kill if mandates are non-transferable or buyers will not deposit.

**Why this may pass:** The nearest incumbents are disincentivized to hold the resource. Storage clinics want paid storage and clean release paperwork, not small-lot sales; stallion agents prefer current promoted studs; mare owners cannot wait through breeding-season uncertainty. Exact doses are finite, named genetic assets, and ownership/transfer control can be secured before competitors see demand.

**Main cap risk:** Many straws may be contractually non-transferable, and equine breeding is trust-network-heavy. The POC must prove assigned sale authority, not only a list of possible sellers.

## Round 2 Selection

The strongest pivots to simulate are **P1 CavalierClear Frozen Semen Rights Bank**, **P2 Warmblood Legacy Semen Transfer-Rights Escrow**, and **P6 FoalRight Legacy Semen Reassignment Bank**. P6 is the most control-point-disciplined because it starts from assigned sale rights over named straws/doses and a storage-clinic pain point, not a general marketplace.
