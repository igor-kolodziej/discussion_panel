# Control-Point Funnel

## Early Diagnostic Shapes

1. **GridFault Attribution** — strongest hard right: a currently listed Polish university technology and patent that can be optioned/licensed, then strengthened by proprietary labelled cases.
2. **EcotypeBank Mazovia** — strongest local natural resource: exclusive donor-meadow harvest rights plus a region-bound ministerial marketing decision and finite inventory.
3. **EarLock** — strongest contracted workflow/data asset: employer term contracts plus reusable fit records and recurring replacement authorization.
4. **SpanBank** — strongest whole-asset salvage attempt: title/option over a complete bolted portal frame before demolition.

These shapes received one independent diagnostic review each. Because the full gate requires two independent reviews of an exact finalist, none of these diagnostic scores can qualify a candidate. Each scored far below a plausible pass and exposed a structural flaw, so the work used them only to force materially different pivots rather than spend a second reviewer on an ineligible version.

## Deferred Before Simulation

- **CasingReserve** — traceable truck-tyre casing first-refusal rights looked stronger than a generic exchange, but the shape remained immediately adjacent to retreaders' and tyre dealers' existing intake channels. It was not promoted to an exact finalist and received no score.

## Killed Before Simulation

- **PFAS, ROV tank, radon, roof, geomembrane, PV EL, and similar equipment services:** useful, but incumbents can copy through one equipment purchase and one staff/process change.
- **RetainOnce:** compelling human need, but clinic authority, MDR workflow, patient trust, and manufacturer dependence remain decisive.
- **Forklift Battery Exit Exchange:** real regulatory momentum, but OEM/dealer/recycler adjacency and dangerous-goods/waste handling make the first control point fragile.
- **ChargeSteward:** exclusive site rights are real, but low-utilization sites and established charging operators cap the wedge.
- **FramePassport:** a hard physical option, but engineering liability and adjacency to existing salvage reduce confidence.
- **ChipDefect Tail:** exclusive IP is possible, but the external performance and buyer base are less immediately verifiable than GridFault.

## Simulation Rule

Only an exact finalist that has a credible chance after diagnostics receives the full gate: two independent reviews. Reviewers see the complete `Personalities/ZeroToOne.txt` instructions and founder constraints, but not the required threshold, other scores, or any scoring prescription. A shared fatal objection from both reviewers kills or materially changes the shape. A single diagnostic review is never treated as a gate pass.
