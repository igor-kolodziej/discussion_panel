## CanopyTransfer

- The current consolidated Polish Nature Conservation Act is published as Dz.U. 2026 poz. 13: https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/13/ogl
- The Act lets a removal permit require transplanting and ties survival to a three-year verification period; when a charged removal is conditioned on transplanting, payment is deferred and can be remitted if the tree survives. This creates a measurable financial reason for a source owner to prefer a successful transplant over felling, but it does not prove that every commercial removal attracts a fee or transplant condition: https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/13/text.pdf
- GDOŚ confirms that commercial tree removal generally requires the competent authority's permit, lists circumference thresholds and exceptions, and explains that statutory removal fees depend on species and trunk circumference: https://www.gov.pl/web/gdos/ochrona-zadrzewien
- The official government service page states that a removal fee may be deferred and remitted where the permit provides for replacement planting; it also identifies the local permitting authorities and application path: https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/zglos-zamiar-wycinki-drzew-i-krzewow-lub-uzyskaj-na-nia-zezwolenie
- Warsaw has a formal green-protection standard for investment processes covering adapted, transplanted, and newly planted greenery: https://architektura.um.warszawa.pl/-/standardy-ochrony-i-pielegnacji-zieleni
- Warsaw's July 2026 public procurement for street-tree supply, planting, and three-year care says autumn timing and advance reservation of nursery material are operationally important. It proves an institutional market for bundled tree delivery plus multi-year care, not willingness to buy rescued mature trees: https://ezamowienia.gov.pl/mo-client-board/bzp/notice-details/2026%2FS%20124-450542
- **Unproved hypotheses:** developers will sign exclusive conditional rescue rights over named trees; a legally clean acquisition instrument can transfer the tree after severance without conflicting with the land's ownership or permit; buyers will reserve a specific rescued tree at the proposed delivered price; 18–35 cm diameter trees can be moved at the proposed cost and survival rate; mover and aftercare capacity can be reserved without a speculative fixed-cost base.

## Research Discipline

Claims above are separated into sourced facts and hypotheses. No market-size estimate, margin, licence price, permit timing, customer conversion, or resource availability is treated as fact until evidenced by a quote, contract, decision, or transaction.
