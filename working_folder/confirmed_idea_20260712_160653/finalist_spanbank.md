# Exact Candidate: SpanBank Portal-Frame Options

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist with AI/automation leverage, five hours/day, no privileged introductions or status, and no more than 100,000 PLN of founder capital in the 12-month POC. Paid structural engineers, insured dismantling contractors, and logistics providers are allowed. The founder does not perform structural certification or construction.

**SpanBank** controls complete bolted steel portal-frame buildings before demolition and resells them as engineer-screened, component-mapped reassembly kits for simple Polish agricultural storage, machinery shelters, covered yards, and low-complexity non-residential buildings. It does not sell scrap steel by weight, generic demolition, architectural design, construction management, or arbitrary reclaimed beams.

The hidden market mistake is that demolition firms and scrap buyers treat a 20–60 tonne bolted frame as commodity steel. Its higher-value unit is the entire matched structural system: columns, rafters, bracing, base plates, purlins, connection plates, bolt map, bay spacing, span, geometry, source-building record, and any original drawings/declarations. That value disappears when members are cut, mixed, or sold separately. Small buyers meanwhile face long lead times and high fabrication cost for a new simple hall but cannot safely assemble anonymous used steel.

SpanBank never speculatively buys a building. It mines public demolition permits, industrial-property redevelopment notices, insolvency auctions, and direct outreach to demolition contractors for bolted portal frames scheduled 60–180 days ahead. It signs a 60–120-day exclusive purchase/dismantling option with a fixed floor above scrap value, conditional on survey and buyer reservation. A paid structural engineer produces a reuse-screening dossier and exclusions; a LiDAR/photo/component map turns the frame into a searchable kit. SpanBank then markets the exact dimensions to agricultural-hall builders and owner-operators within a 250 km delivery radius. It exercises the option only after a buyer signs and pays at least a 30% non-refundable reservation sufficient to cover dismantling and transport commitments.

The first beachhead is complete 12–20 metre clear-span, single-storey, bolted frames for unheated agricultural machinery/storage shelters where aesthetics are secondary, buyers understand steel buildings, and a standardized builder can price foundations/cladding separately. The first ten buyers come from public building-permit data, agricultural/industrial directories, and 100 named regional hall builders; the offer is the exact kit dossier and delivery window, not a marketplace listing.

The primary control point is the signed exclusive option over a named intact building plus buyer-funded exercise, followed by physical title and the complete component/document map. The option prevents demolition/scrap channels from fragmenting that specific scarce kit during its deadline window. Over time, the control point compounds into a live option inventory by span/bay/load/region, seller/demolition relationships, reuse outcomes, and standardized acceptance data. A broad steel trader cannot recreate the already-fragmented source building later.

The 12-month proof is: 30 screened source buildings, three signed exclusive options, two engineer-cleared kits, two buyer reservations, one exercised option and paid handoff, at least 35% gross margin after seller payment, engineer, dismantling, transport coordination, and defects reserve, and zero founder inventory spend without buyer coverage. Founder capital budget is capped at 95,000 PLN: 15,000 option deposits, 20,000 engineering/surveys, 10,000 legal/insurance, 12,000 LiDAR/component capture, 8,000 buyer/seller acquisition, and 30,000 contingency/temporary storage. Transaction-specific dismantling/transport beyond this is funded from the buyer reservation.

Illustrative economics to validate, not facts: a matched kit must create at least 60,000 PLN gross profit or 35% gross margin and save the buyer at least 25% versus a comparable new fabricated frame. Kill the idea if three options cannot be signed, engineering/CE/reuse rules make the kit unmarketable, buyers will not reserve before dismantling, median logistics consume the reuse premium, or demolition/steel incumbents can systematically bid away the option spread.

EU Regulation 2024/3110 now explicitly applies to used construction products and creates a future construction digital-product-passport framework, but SpanBank does not claim that this alone creates demand. The immediate product is exclusive control of a complete matched kit at the brief moment before fragmentation; regulation is a longer-term tailwind for documented reuse.
