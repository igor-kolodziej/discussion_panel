## Evidence Required Before Any Revival

Do not rescore this shape unless real-world work first produces all of the following:

1. An executed university option or a written decision that the product can proceed independently.
2. Two paid or deposit-backed benchmark commitments from named commissioning buyers.
3. At least five usable historical cases under signed derived-data rights.
4. A preregistered result showing an incremental advantage over harmonic-power direction/manual isolation.
5. A patent attorney's written view identifying a plausibly novel improvement claim, or a decision to abandon the patent moat.
6. One commissioning firm willing to operate or resell the repeatable kit rather than buy only bespoke expertise.

Until those exist, further ideation around this same buyer, authority mechanism, and speculative IP/data resource would violate the repeated-objection rule.
