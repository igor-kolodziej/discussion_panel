Evaluate this idea.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist; part-time max 5 hours/day; under 100,000 PLN starting capital; no high-profile friends, no privileged public-sector introductions, no influencer/camera channel; strong AI/data/document-analysis leverage; Polish/EU context.

Idea: **TraceFaktura NIK-Ready Animal-Care Invoice Protocol**.

Buyer: Polish gmina treasurers, internal auditors, and secretaries responsible for defensible payment acceptance for outsourced animal-capture, shelter, and veterinary invoices.

Hidden insight: Polish gminas do not mainly need another animal database, shelter website, or welfare dashboard. Their acute pain is whether public money can be paid defensibly when each animal-care invoice line may or may not map to a provable animal event and outcome. NIK criticism of animal-homelessness spending/control, intensified Veterinary Inspection shelter controls, KROPiK identity momentum, and rising municipal animal-care costs make pre-payment evidence more valuable now.

Product: a fixed 60-day payment-control pilot, not legal advice, audit opinion, NGO activism, shelter software, or a dashboard.

The pilot delivers:

- 20 historical animal-care invoice/case lines reviewed.
- A reviewer-backed one-page invoice acceptance protocol.
- Contractor evidence checklist.
- A 60-day internal use note where new animal-care invoice lines are checked against the evidence packet before payment acceptance.
- A summary showing which lines were accepted, corrected, queried, or held.

Each evidence packet checks:

- animal ID/chip/KROPiK status where applicable;
- pickup GPS/photo/time or equivalent proof;
- contractor and location;
- shelter/vet intake;
- veterinary procedure, holding, quarantine, sterilization, adoption, return, death, or other outcome evidence;
- invoice-line match;
- missing-proof flags;
- accept/query/hold recommendation.

Control point within 2 months:

1. Secure under 15,000 PLN a paid two-person reviewer bench: one Polish public-finance/internal-audit/retired-skarbnik reviewer for payment-control language, and one shelter/veterinary operations reviewer for animal-evidence adequacy.
2. Build a public-source risk pre-screen list of 50 gminas from BIP animal-care programs, contractor names, public spend lines, and NIK/GIW/KROPiK risk factors.
3. Sell one 6,000-12,000 PLN 60-day pilot below the 2026 PZP threshold, subject to the gmina's internal purchase rules.
4. Obtain signed access to historical invoices/case evidence and a signed internal 60-day use note.
5. Route at least 10 live invoice lines through the protocol.
6. Produce proof that at least one live invoice was accepted, corrected, queried, or held because of the evidence packet before payment.

Cold start: send each target gmina a public-source pre-screen rather than a generic pitch. The ask is not "buy software"; it is "let us test a low-cost payment evidence control on a narrow animal-care invoice stream before the next inspection, controversy, contractor dispute, or audit issue."

Why an unknown solo founder can plausibly secure it: the first ask is small, below serious procurement threshold, document-led, and reviewer-backed. The gmina does not need to change contractor, adopt SaaS, expose public scandal, or sign a large partnership. The founder supplies data extraction, evidence matching, AI-assisted document review, and public-source risk targeting; reviewer credibility supplies the public-finance and animal-operations language.

Domination path: become the de facto reviewer-backed animal-care invoice evidence standard for small/medium Polish gminas and inter-municipal associations, then compound a cross-gmina evidence taxonomy: contractor evidence quality, recurring missing-proof patterns, invoice-line categories, accepted protocol language, and annual program/contract evidence clauses.
