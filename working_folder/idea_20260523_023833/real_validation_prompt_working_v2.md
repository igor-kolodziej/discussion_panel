Revised idea: **TraceFaktura Contractor-Side Animal-Care Payment Packet Rail**.

Buyer: private or NGO animal-care contractors that bill multiple Polish gminas for animal capture, transport, sheltering, veterinary procedures, quarantine, sterilization, adoption/return/death outcomes, and related homeless-animal services.

This is a pivot away from selling first to gminas. The contractor is the first buyer because it controls the raw evidence, sends invoices, feels payment delay/queries, and can route future invoice packets without requiring a gmina to first buy a new protocol.

Hidden insight: Polish gminas have a defensible-spending problem, but the party that can most easily create the missing evidence packet is the contractor. Good contractors want to be paid faster, look cleaner than weak competitors, and reduce invoice disputes. Weakly documented contractors reveal the market risk and should fail the validation gate. TraceFaktura becomes the invoice-readiness layer contractors attach before gmina payment acceptance, then later becomes the standard gminas can require in contracts.

Product: a fixed 30-60 day contractor-side payment packet pilot, not legal advice, audit opinion, NGO activism, shelter software, or a dashboard.

The pilot delivers:

- 30-80 historical invoice/case lines reviewed from one contractor across at least 2 gminas.
- Contractor evidence checklist mapped to NIK/GIW/KROPiK risk factors and common gmina payment questions.
- A reusable invoice attachment pack template the contractor sends with future invoices.
- Live 60-day use on new invoices: each invoice line gets an animal-level evidence packet before submission to the gmina.
- A payment-friction register: missing-proof causes, queried lines, accepted lines, corrected lines, held lines, days-to-acceptance, and gmina-specific evidence preferences.

Each evidence packet checks:

- animal ID/chip/KROPiK status where applicable;
- pickup location/photo/time or equivalent proof;
- contractor action and transport/shelter/vet handoff;
- shelter/vet intake;
- veterinary procedure, quarantine, holding, sterilization, adoption, return, death, or other outcome evidence;
- invoice-line match;
- missing-proof flags;
- gmina-ready attachment summary.

Control point within 2 months:

1. Secure one animal-care contractor with at least 3 active gmina relationships and recurring invoices.
2. Obtain signed access to historical invoices/case records and permission to package future invoice evidence.
3. Process 30-80 historical invoice/case lines and identify recurring evidence gaps or prove unusually clean documentation.

5. Produce proof that at least one gmina accepted, queried less, corrected, or paid/approved a line with the packet attached.
6. Collect 6,000-15,000 PLN from the contractor or a setup fee plus per-invoice-line fee.
