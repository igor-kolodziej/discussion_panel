# Real Zero To One Working-Chat Validation 4

## Idea

TraceFaktura Live Event Capture Rail for Polish municipal animal-care contractors.

## Score

83/100.

## Result

Passes the revised user threshold of 82.

## Control-Point Tier

Tier 2+.

The chatbot judged that the idea does not own the gmina payment rail, so it is not Tier 1, but it can own or routinely create the live event ledger: timestamped pickup/intake/vet/outcome evidence captured during the contractor's normal work before invoices exist. This is materially stronger than the earlier after-the-fact packet version.

## Main Caveats And Objections

- The control point is real only if the contractor mandates usage for new events; selective use collapses the control point.
- The strongest copy risk is incumbent shelter or animal-management software adding a mobile evidence-capture module.
- The defense must be outcome memory: which fields gminas accept, which proof gaps trigger queries, which invoice-line types are disputed, and which packet formats work.
- Field adoption is the largest execution risk; fewer than 70% of eligible events captured without founder chasing would be fatal.
- Contractors with the worst pain may have weak or intentionally selective evidence culture and may be bad customers.
- Gminas may ignore packets or payment delays may come from unrelated reasons.
- Hashing/timestamping helps but is not official legal proof.
- GDPR/data minimization must be handled carefully where owner/adopter/vet documents contain personal data.
- Monthly retainer needs to attach to recurring invoice preparation, not abstract compliance.
- Market may be narrow unless it expands into broader municipal service evidence rails.

## First 30-Day Proof Required

- One contractor with 3+ active gmina clients signs and pays at least 3,000-6,000 PLN upfront.
- Contractor agrees that all eligible new events during the pilot must go through TraceFaktura.
- At least 15 live events captured by day 20.
- Median capture time under 90 seconds for simple events.
- At least 70% of required fields completed without founder correction.
- At least one staff user other than the owner uses it repeatedly.
- At least 5 invoice lines generated from event objects by day 30.
- Contractor is willing to send packet attachments to at least one gmina.

## 60-Day Pass Gate

- 40+ live events captured.
- 10+ invoice lines generated from TraceFaktura objects.
- Packets sent to 2+ gminas.
- At least one gmina accepts, pays, asks fewer questions, or gives written feedback.
- Staff capture median remains under 90 seconds for simple events.
- Contractor pays 6,000-15,000 PLN.
- Missing-field rate or invoice-prep time improves versus prior process.
- Contractor wants continuation for the next invoice cycle.
