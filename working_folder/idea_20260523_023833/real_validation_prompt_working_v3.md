Hidden insight: gminas have a defensible-spending problem, contractors have a payment-timing problem, and both problems meet at the same object: the invoice line. If an animal-care invoice line has strong animal-level evidence, it is a better candidate for faster acceptance, fewer disputes, and eventually low-risk receivable financing. If the evidence is weak, that is precisely the risk signal.

Product: a 60-day fixed-scope pilot, not legal advice, audit opinion, NGO activism, shelter software, a dashboard, or general factoring.

The pilot has two layers:

1. **Evidence-readiness rail:** 30-80 historical invoice/case lines reviewed; contractor evidence checklist; gmina-ready invoice attachment packet; live packet use on at least 10 new invoice lines.
2. **Receivable-readiness test:** after evidence scoring, the cleanest invoice batch is either assigned to TraceFaktura/partner under a narrow written receivable-assignment or prepayment arrangement, or explicitly marked as finance-ready with a signed contractor agreement and payment route prepared.
