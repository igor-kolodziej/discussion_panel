# Research Notes

Research date: 2026-05-23

## Elderly Care / Long-Term Care

- Statistics Poland's 2024 older-people publication reports 903 social assistance houses and 680 establishments ensuring 24-hour care for disabled, chronically ill, or older people in 2024.
  Source: https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/older-people/older-people/the-situation-of-older-people-in-poland-in-2024%2C1%2C7.html
- BGK reported in February 2026 that Poland lacks about 33,000 long-term-care places and that the sector includes 1,583 care homes, including 903 DPS and 680 PDOC facilities.
  Source: https://www.bgk.pl/aktualnosc/raport-bgk-w-polsce-brakuje-33-tys-miejsc-w-placowkach-opieki-dlugoterminowej-1/
- Voivodes publish public registers of facilities providing 24-hour care to disabled, chronically ill, or older people; this creates a concrete target list for private-care-home outreach.
  Source example: https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-mazowiecki/rejestr-placowek-zapewniajacych-calodobowa-opieke-osobom-niepelnosprawnym-przewlekle-chorym-lub-osobom-w-podeszlym-wieku-w-tym-prowadzonych-na-podstawie-przepisow-o-dzialalnosci-gospodarczej
- Government guidance states that 24-hour care facilities require voivode permission; voivodes can revoke permission and stop operation if resident life or health is endangered.
  Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/bezpieczniejsze-placowki-calodobowej-opieki
- RPO/KMP historical visit documentation shows that missing registers of injuries, complaints, extraordinary events such as falls/self-harm, deaths, exits, and visits have been called out as facility-documentation problems.
  Source: https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/content/kmp/1526298300-wizytacja-w-plac%C3%B3wce-ca%C5%82odobowej-opieki-pensjonat-dla-os%C3%B3b-starszych-w-alwerni-ul-koryci%C5%84skiego-8
- Competitors exist at the care-home management layer, e.g. SIDDO and DPSP; the wedge must avoid being generic care-home management software.
  Sources: https://siddo.pl/ and https://dpsp.pl/

## Animal Shelters / Municipal Animal Welfare

- The Polish government reported that by the end of February 2026 the Veterinary Inspection carried out 753 shelter inspections and found irregularities in 60 shelters; 17 administrative decisions were issued, including 2 deletions from the register.
  Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/rolnictwo/753-kontrole-schronisk--inspekcja-weterynaryjna-wzmacnia-nadzor-zima
- GIW publishes shelter inspection materials, including annual shelter visit reports, the 2024 Chief Veterinary Officer instruction for shelter controls, and SPIWET shelter checklists published/updated in May 2026.
  Sources: https://www.wetgiw.gov.pl/nadzor-weterynaryjny/schroniska-dla-bezdomnych-zwierzat and https://www.wetgiw.gov.pl/publikacje/listy-kontrolne-spiwet
- KROPiK: in April 2026 the Sejm accepted a bill for mandatory dog marking and a registry covering dogs and cats, targeting around 8 million dogs and 6 million cats over staged implementation.
  Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/rolnictwo/czipowanie-psow-obowiazkowe-w-calej-polsce-sejm-przyjal-ustawe-o-kropik
- The government positioned KROPiK as a way to link an animal to its owner or shelter and reduce homelessness.
  Source: https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/walczymy-z-bezdomnoscia-zwierzat--obowiazkowe-czipowanie-oraz-rejestr-psow-i-kotow
- Existing shelter software/open-data competitors exist, including DomDlaZwierzaka.pl and OtwarteSchroniska.org.pl, so a shelter idea must own inspection/municipal evidence flow, not generic shelter records or adoption pages.
  Sources: https://www.domdlazwierzaka.pl/ and https://otwarteschroniska.org.pl/

## Veterinary Clinics / Antimicrobial Use

- EMA states that from January 2024 all EU/EEA Member States must submit annual antimicrobial sales/use data via the ASU platform under Regulation (EU) 2019/6.
  Source: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/new-platform-collection-sales-and-use-data-antimicrobials-animals
- EMA's ASU Platform page says Member States must report data on sales and use of antimicrobial medicinal products in animals; protocols/templates are provided for competent authorities.
  Source: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/veterinary-regulatory-overview/antimicrobial-resistance-veterinary-medicine/antimicrobial-sales-use-platform
- EMA notes Regulation (EU) 2019/6 applies from 28 January 2022 and strengthened EU action against antimicrobial resistance through prudent/responsible-use measures.
  Source: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/veterinary-regulatory-overview/veterinary-medicinal-products-regulation
- Polish GIW has public content on antibacterial veterinary medicinal products and links to EMA antimicrobial-sales data context.
  Source: https://wetgiw.gov.pl/nadzor-weterynaryjny/przeciwbakteryjne-produkty-lecznicze-weterynaryjne
- Vet-clinic software competitors with recall/appointment/documentation features exist, including VetFlow, Flow.vet, VetFile, Vetina, Med-Pro, and others; a vet-recall idea must control delegated outreach and booked slots, not just reminders.
  Sources: https://vet-flow.pl/, https://flow.vet/, https://www.vetfile.pl/en, https://vetina.eu/, https://med-pro.vet/en

## Senior Pet Continuity / Pet Emergency Care

- Polish pet-sitter/boarding marketplaces exist, e.g. Petsy, so a pet-continuity idea must control emergency standby capacity and pre-authorized handoff, not ordinary pet sitting.
  Source: https://petsy.pl/
- Pet emergency ID apps such as PetValid already provide QR/medical/contact cards, so a continuity idea must avoid being only a QR profile.
  Source: https://www.petvalid.com/
- International examples show pet-care authorization forms and emergency planning patterns exist, but the local wedge would be reserved capacity plus elderly/family crisis workflow.
  Source example: https://www.hospets.org/uploads/1/4/0/1/140120955/hospets_pet_care_power_of_attorney.pdf
