# Real Zero To One Fresh-Chat Validation 2

## Idea

TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot for per-event Polish municipal animal-care contractors.

## Score

83/100.

## Result

Passes the revised user threshold of 82.

## Control-Point Tier

Tier 2: operational source-of-truth rail.

The chatbot judged that the idea is not Tier 1 because it does not own payment, procurement, legal obligation, animal intake, shelter access, municipal acceptance, or a regulated registry. It is stronger than a Tier 3 report/dashboard because the POC requires prepaid pilot, all-event routing for specific gminas, capture before invoice preparation, at least one gmina format acknowledgement/correction, and a live event ledger as the source evidence object.

## Strongest Copy Risk

Template absorption. Once a contractor learns which fields a gmina accepts, the contractor can copy the packet format into Google Forms, Airtable, MS Forms, WhatsApp folders, Drive, or existing shelter/admin software. Local competitors can clone the field schema quickly.

Defense must become staff habit, live ledger history, gmina-specific acceptance rules, missing-field risk detection before month-end, and proof that packets reduce questions or improve acceptance.

## Fatal Objections

- Contractors may not actually want more auditability, especially if ambiguity currently benefits them.
- The buyer pain may be episodic rather than recurring if invoice disputes are rare and gminas already pay reliably.
- Event volume may be too low unless the contractor serves several gminas.
- Gmina acknowledgement can be too soft unless it is field-level correction or usable verification feedback.
- Staff compliance is the product risk; if capture happens after the event or depends on one manager, the ledger is not real.

## Nonfatal Caveats

- The POC can be built under 100,000 PLN as mobile forms, structured storage, audit timestamps, packet generator, OCR/LLM extraction, and manual QA, not polished SaaS.
- Public procurement risk is lower because the gmina is not the buyer, but gmina behavior still matters for packet value.
- The first contractor may be more like a design partner, which is acceptable only if prepaid and all events are routed.
- The initial moat is weak until repeated usage across contractors/gminas creates field rules, accepted packet examples, and workflow lock-in.

## Exact 30-Day Kill Gates From Chatbot

- By day 14, kill or narrow unless one contractor has paid 3,000-5,000 PLN upfront and signed a written pilot scope naming at least 2 gminas where all new eligible billable events must be captured through TraceFaktura first.
- By day 21, kill unless the contractor has sent the one-page packet standard to at least 2 relevant gmina contacts and at least 1 gmina accountant/contract owner has provided written acknowledgement, field correction, or usable verification feedback.
- By day 30, kill unless there are 20 live eligible events routed through TraceFaktura, with at least 80% captured at the operational moment rather than reconstructed later.
- By day 30, kill unless at least 3 non-founder users capture or review events.
- By day 30, kill unless median normal-event capture time is under 90 seconds, with p90 under 180 seconds excluding unusually complex veterinary cases.
- By day 30, kill unless at least 5 invoice-line evidence objects are generated from live captured events and are ready to send or already sent to a gmina.
- By day 30, kill unless missing-field checks catch real issues before month-end: at least 5 missing/ambiguous fields detected and fixed, or documented reduction in manual reconstruction versus prior process.
- By day 30, kill unless the contractor gives one written economic signal: expected invoice-prep time down 30-50%, fewer gmina questions expected because of named fields, improved renewal/audit evidence, or reduced risk of rejected/disputed lines.

## Final Judgment From Chatbot

Pursue, but only with brutal POC gating. The idea is a legitimately testable niche-control idea and deserves a paid POC attempt, not more desk research. The score only holds if the first pilot proves mandatory routing and gmina-format feedback.
