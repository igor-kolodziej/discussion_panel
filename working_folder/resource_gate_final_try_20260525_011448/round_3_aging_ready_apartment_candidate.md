# Round 3 Candidate: Aging-Ready Apartment Option Pool

## Scored Wording

Aging-Ready Apartment Option Pool is a Warsaw-focused buyer-prepaid transaction-control business for 50-60-year-old professionals and couples making their "last independent apartment" move before mobility decline, spouse illness, forced downsizing, or parent-care obligations.

It does not sell a report, marketplace, senior-housing portal, advisory service, or ordinary brokerage. It controls actual named flats before buyers can access them: seller-side assignable purchase options or preliminary agreements on specific Warsaw apartments that meet an aging-ready spec:

- no stairs from street to door;
- elevator or ground floor;
- walkable pharmacy/grocery/transit;
- bathroom conversion feasibility;
- low-maintenance building;
- acceptable resale liquidity;
- clinic/hospital access without car dependence;
- no obvious barrier-removal traps.

## Buyer-Prepaid Demand

Target buyer: 50-60 Warsaw apartment owners or buyers with roughly 500,000-1,200,000 PLN purchasing power who know they should move before a crisis but do not want to identify as elderly, and adult children organizing a move for parents.

Payment structure:

- 3,000-5,000 PLN non-refundable search/qualification mandate from the buyer.
- 20,000-40,000 PLN refundable option escrow when shown a controlled flat.
- 1.5-3.0% success fee on completed transaction, partly credited from the mandate.

## Primary Control Point

The control point is signed seller-side control over exact flats:

- specific apartment address and unit;
- seller identity and asking price;
- aging-ready checklist and photo proof;
- signed time-limited exclusivity, option, or assignable preliminary agreement;
- right to release/assign the opportunity to a qualified buyer;
- legal/notarial template and escrow rules;
- 30-60 day window backed by option fee.

Once a flat is controlled, competitors cannot copy that exact inventory with a vendor contract, process change, or equipment purchase.

## 2-Month POC Under 100,000 PLN

Pass conditions:

1. Get at least 8 prepaid buyer mandates at 3,000+ PLN each from 50-60 buyers or adult children of 50-70 parents.
2. Sign at least 3 seller-side controlled options or assignable preliminary agreements on qualifying flats, with legal review.
3. Get at least 1 buyer to place 20,000+ PLN into escrow against one controlled flat.
4. Show CAC per paid mandate below 1,500 PLN from cold channels: Meta/Google/search, Otodom/OLX seller outreach, local seminars, physiotherapy/orthopedic clinic flyers without exclusive partnerships.

Budget:

- 20,000-45,000 PLN seller option fees.
- 10,000-15,000 PLN legal/notary templates and escrow setup.
- 10,000-20,000 PLN ads/outbound/landing page.
- 5,000-10,000 PLN accessibility inspections and photography.
- Reserve buffer under 100,000 PLN.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Secure It

The supply side does not require medical trust. Sellers care about certainty, speed, paperwork, and money. An unknown founder can offer a clean buyer profile, lawyer-reviewed template, immediate option fee, and 30-45 day decision window.

Most real estate agents prefer listings and commissions, not principal-like option risk or a narrow aging-readiness underwriting process. Developers and sellers also dislike openly branding inventory as "senior" stock. The founder can win awkward but valuable flats before they are packaged as a category.

## Unit Economics

Example 800,000 PLN flat:

- Buyer mandate: 3,000-5,000 PLN.
- Success fee: 12,000-24,000 PLN at 1.5-3%.
- Possible seller-side commission: 1-2% if legally and transparently structured.
- Legal/inspection/CAC per matched case: roughly 2,000-5,000 PLN.
- Option fee: 5,000-15,000 PLN, ideally credited/refundable on close.
- Expected gross profit per closed transaction: 10,000-25,000 PLN, potentially higher with seller commission.

## Strongest Objections

- Sellers may refuse assignable options without large deposits in a liquid market.
- Buyers may not pay non-refundable mandates for a category they do not yet name.
- The model may collapse into real-estate brokerage if the founder does not actually control inventory.
- Real estate agents can copy the positioning if margins are proven, although they cannot copy already-controlled flats.
- Legal structure, consumer rights, double-side fees, and escrow must be reviewed carefully.
