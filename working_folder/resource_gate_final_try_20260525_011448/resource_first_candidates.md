## Leading Candidate Wording For Scoring

**ImplantKey Orphan Implant Tooling Escrow** buys, options, and consigns named lots of discontinued dental implant prosthetic tooling and compatibility assets from retiring dentists, closed clinics, dental labs, and small distributors, then sells urgent exact-match searches, rentals, and compliant components through dentists treating 50-60-year-old patients with older implants.

The first business is not a consumer marketplace and not a broad obsolete implant reseller. It starts with reusable and lower-regulatory-risk compatibility assets: torque drivers, adapters, scan bodies, impression copings, lab analogs, compatible screws where permitted, CAD libraries, old catalogs, platform crosswalks, and sealed traceable manufacturer components only after Polish/EU medical-device distributor review. It excludes unsealed, expired, relabelled, unknown-provenance, patient-sourced, or clinically unsafe stock.

The primary control point is signed 90-day exclusive purchase/consignment/option control over named physical part/tool lots plus a dentist-paid case-deposit workflow. Once ImplantKey owns or options a specific drawer of Zimmer/Ankylos/Astra/Nobel/old Straumann/etc. drivers, scan bodies, analogs, and catalogs, a competitor cannot copy that exact asset with a vendor contract.

Two-month proof under 100,000 PLN: secure 4-6 named deadstock lots; photograph/index 250-600 usable assets; contract one prosthodontic technician/implant coordinator for compatibility triage; get legal/distributor review on what can be rented/sold and under what terms; collect 10+ dentist case deposits of 300-1,000 PLN for exact legacy matches; complete 3+ successful paid matches/rentals or written no-match refunds.
