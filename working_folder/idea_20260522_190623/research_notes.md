# Research Notes

## OZE / PV Installed-Base Context

- URE reported on 2026-03-31 that Poland had **1,636,673 RES micro-installations** connected at the end of 2025, with PV accounting for 99.9% of the smallest RES installations. Prosumers owned more than 98.5% of these micro-installations. Source: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/518%2CURE-report-The-number-of-RES-micro-installations-in-Poland-has-already-exceeded-.html
- IEO's "Photovoltaic Market in Poland 2025" summary says Poland had **21.8 GW installed PV capacity at the end of Q1 2025**, 59% in microgeneration units. Source: https://ieo.pl/en/pv-report
- UOKiK continues active enforcement in the OZE market. A 2026-03-27 release reports more than 7M PLN in fines related to renewable-energy companies and notes consumer risk around contracts, hidden terms, and specific PV/heat-pump sales practices. Source: https://uokik.gov.pl/public/index.php/en/clean-energy-and-dirty-practices-the-president-of-uokik-has-imposed-fines-totalling-over-7-million-pln-on-companies-in-the-renewable-energy-sector
- UOKiK's 2025-10-22 release says rapid renewable-market expansion attracted companies using unfair contracts, pressure, and misleading communications; it also states the contractor is responsible for a proper technical audit before concluding a sale/installation contract. Source: https://uokik.gov.pl/en/clean-energy-on-fair-terms-actions-taken-by-the-president-of-uokik-in-the-renewable-energy-sector
- A non-official but directly relevant market article claims dozens of Polish PV installers have closed or stopped operating after the 2020-2022 boom and estimates 150k-250k affected owners. Treat this as a directional claim to verify in outreach, not as authoritative market sizing. Source: https://systemy-fotowoltaika.pl/co-zrobic-gdy-firma-instalacyjna-zbankrutuje-prawa-wlasciciela-instalacji/

## Implication

The market has a large installed base and visible trust/aftercare friction. The key idea should not be "another PV service" or "another customer education site." It needs a **routed installed-base control point**: retiring/closing/overloaded installers initiate a customer handoff and transfer technical files, while the founder/family service partner converts that base into paid aftercare and retrofit demand.

## Non-Redundancy Notes

- Different from BatteryFit: BatteryFit sells analysis packets to installers for retrofit sales. OZE Orphan Portfolio Rescue controls the customer relationship and technical file after the original installer can no longer service it.
- Different from Heat-Pump Commissioning Passport: that idea tries to create an economic gate through distributors before/after installs. This idea acquires aftercare rights over existing installed bases.
- Different from generic PV servicing: no routed handoff rights means no idea. Cold-call servicing must be capped below 85.
