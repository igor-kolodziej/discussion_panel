## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Four candidates were capped by **incumbent copy risk**: ColdRoom, Passive Fire, Lift Parts, PV Inverters.
- Three candidates were capped by **liability/regulatory trust**: Passive Fire, Lift Parts, ColdRoom.
- Two candidates were capped by **soft/routed-control uncertainty**: OZE Orphan and EUDR.
- Two candidates were capped by **inventory/dead-stock risk**: PV Inverters and Lift Parts.

## Decision

No candidate achieved the required simulated score `>90`; therefore none can go to the real Zero To One chatbot under the current gate.

Pivot direction:

- Preserve the strongest pattern from OZE Orphan Portfolio Rescue: control of a routed installed base, not cold outbound.
- Strengthen the control point from "installer handoff rights" to a harder economic gate where the resource owner has an immediate reason to transfer or route the installed base.
- Avoid generic repair/service and avoid taking warranty liability.

## Pivot Hypothesis

The stronger shape may be a **lender/insurer/administrator-routed installed-base preservation layer** where the founder controls technical-file recovery and paid inspection conversion because a third party already has an economic reason to prevent asset deterioration, dispute, or claim failure.

But if the control point remains a soft referral, it should be capped below 85.
