## Candidate Notes

### Remote-Read Heat Meter Slot Bank

Strongest insight: the scarce asset before the 2027 remote-read deadline is not the meter, but signed building approval, resident access, inventory, and installer calendar.

Fatal cap: incumbents already own devices, billing software, service teams, and property-manager relationships. It becomes strong only with exclusive building mandates, resident-access calendar control, customer deposits, supplier allocation, installer priority, and recurring readout/billing rights.

Needs to exceed 90:

- 3 exclusive signed mandates;
- deposits large enough to fund device procurement;
- supplier allocation for named device types;
- installer calendar reservation with penalties or priority terms;
- at least one recurring readout/billing contract after installation.

### HPLC / GC Module Loaner Bank

Strongest insight: accredited labs fear audit-safe throughput continuity when a module fails, not merely repair cost.

Fatal cap: existing OEMs, service firms, rental firms, and used-equipment dealers can copy parts of the model; founder lacks lab-equipment credibility; 100k PLN may only buy a narrow credible inventory; emergency uptime conflicts with 5h/day unless a service engineer owns SLA delivery.

Needs to exceed 90:

- 3 paid emergency memberships from real accredited labs;
- owned tested inventory for one exact brand/generation;
- signed SLA with credible service engineer;
- one real swap accepted into a lab quality system;
- complete serial, verification, maintenance, firmware/software, and return-condition documentation;
- evidence that customers pay before failure, not only during panic.

### Driver Sleep-Apnea Fitness-To-Drive Fast Lane

Strongest insight: this is not wellness sleep tracking. Suspected moderate/severe OSA is a driver fitness/licensing workflow, so the scarce asset is fast defensible evidence.

Fatal cap: licensed physicians and occupational-medicine clinics own the medical/legal authority; positive cases may be bottlenecked by treatment/CPAP/compliance proof rather than testing; fleets and drivers may resist a workflow that removes drivers from service.

Needs to exceed 90:

- signed occupational clinic or fleet contract routing suspected drivers;
- 5-10 validated HSAT devices under SLA;
- contracted sleep physician/pulmonologist 48-72h turnaround;
- 10 paid tests from routed channel;
- several reports accepted in driver medical decision workflows;
- clear legal/data structure showing the startup is logistics/evidence infrastructure, not practicing medicine.

### White Certificate Pre-Start Rights Router

Strongest insight: Polish white-certificate value can be destroyed before the customer knows it exists if the project starts before application/evidence is secured.

Fatal cap: ESCOs, auditors, utilities, and engineering firms already sell white-certificate services; installers may resist anything that slows closing; value per small project may be too low; defensibility depends on routed quote flow before contract signing.

Needs to exceed 90:

- 3 non-family installers route real quotes before signing;
- 5 customers sign mandates with no-start covenants;
- 2 applications filed or ready-to-file with auditor signoff;
- at least 1 customer/installer pays upfront or assigns a success-fee right;
- one narrow high-value project category proves repeatable economics.

### PFAS-Free Food-Packaging Slot Bank

Strongest insight: the PPWR/PFAS pressure point is not mainly legal advice; it is substitute sourcing, PFAS evidence, converter MOQ/production capacity, and inventory transition.

Fatal cap: food brands already have packaging distributors, and distributors/converters/compliance firms can bundle PFAS-free migration quickly. Lab slots and SKU files are useful but not defensible.

Needs to exceed 90:

- signed priority production capacity or short-term exclusivity for 3-5 replacement formats;
- 10 paid SKU deposits from food SMEs;
- evidence customers route procurement through the startup because capacity is actually reserved.

### Lead Exposure Surveillance Rail

Strongest insight: lowered EU lead limits make informal worker monitoring weaker, and the hard asset is the legally sensitive blood-test / doctor / evidence chain.

Fatal cap: occupational-medicine clinics legally own much of the employer medical exam relationship; doctors and labs own interpretation/testing; sensitive health data and RODO handling are heavy; buyer urgency may be uneven.

Needs to exceed 90:

- 3 signed employer rosters;
- named occupational physician partner with reserved interpretation capacity;
- lab SLA for blood lead tests;
- lawyer-approved RODO/medical workflow;
- recurring paid monitoring contracts;
- proof that clinics/BHP providers route cases through the startup instead of copying it.

## Round 3 Common Fatal Objections

The same cap keeps recurring:

- incumbents already own the trusted workflow;
- the founder can coordinate, but does not yet control authority;
- capacity reservations are useful only if contractual, exclusive, or backed by paid demand;
- one-shot deadline projects need recurring data or workflow rights after the deadline;
- medical/lab ideas require credible licensed operators from day one;
- high scores require routed flow or accepted evidence custody, not just a clever service package.

## Pivot Instruction

Round 4 should stop trying to turn professional-service choke points into startups unless the first asset is genuinely ownable. Prefer shapes where:

- the founder can own physical inventory cheaply and customers reserve it before use;
- there is a legally required or economically necessary evidence artifact that customers cannot reconstruct later;
- the buyer has immediate financial upside/downside, not abstract compliance;
- the incumbent's normal workflow is too slow, too expensive, or not interested in the small-account segment;
- the startup can start as an emergency/rescue lane and compound into the system of record.

Highest raw material to transform:

1. Remote-read meter mandates, if upgraded into recurring billing/readout rights for neglected buildings.
2. HPLC/GC loaner modules, if narrowed to one exact installed base with a credible service engineer.
3. Driver OSA fast lane, if a clinic/fleet actually routes cases and accepted reports become the evidence asset.
