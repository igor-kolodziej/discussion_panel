# Round 4 Finalists

Round 4 pivoted away from incumbent-owned workflow categories after real HeatRead validation failed at `74/100`. These finalists emphasize owned physical inventory, first-mile custody, prepaid emergency slots, or asset reservations.

## 1. Damaged Lithium Battery Quarantine Evidence Rail

Target buyer: e-bike/scooter shops, power-tool rental chains, PV/battery installers, small battery/electronics importers, and producer-responsibility operators that need evidence from collection points.

Hidden insight: the hard problem is not generic battery compliance. It is proving what happened to a swollen, damaged, returned, or fire-risk lithium pack before it gets mixed, moved, or quietly dumped. Once it leaves the shop, serial, condition, source, weight, and custody cannot be reconstructed.

Primary control point:

- signed collection-point mandate;
- fire-safe quarantine boxes;
- tamper seals;
- serial/photo capture;
- weigh-in and condition record;
- licensed collector handoff;
- BDO/KPO evidence pack.

Two-month proof:

- 5-10 shops or installers signed;
- 30-50 packs processed;
- one licensed collector agreement;
- one importer/PRO paying for monthly custody evidence;
- documented handoff packs.

Main copy risk: recyclers and producer-responsibility operators can copy, but they usually see waste only after first-mile evidence is already lost.

## 2. MV Cable Fault Repair Slot Bank

Target buyer: factories, logistics parks, cold-storage sites, private campuses, waterworks, and large farms with customer-owned 15/20 kV infrastructure.

Hidden insight: the emergency bottleneck is often not "electrician." It is the correct MV joint/termination kit, fault-location crew, and certified cable jointer. A small cable fault can stop an entire private installation.

Primary control point:

- stocked common 12/20 kV joint/termination kits;
- transition kits for older cable types;
- signed emergency hours from 2 cable jointers/fault-location providers;
- pre-surveyed customer cable specs and one-line diagrams;
- named kit reservation and response path for paid customers.

Two-month proof:

- spend 30-60k PLN on narrow kit inventory;
- sign two subcontractor SLAs;
- pre-survey 10 sites;
- sell 3-5 paid retainers with named kit reservation and response path.

Main copy risk: DSOs and big electrical contractors. Defense is pre-surveyed private networks plus reserved kits and subcontractor priority before failure.

## 3. Small-Waterworks Microbiological Recovery Lane

Target buyer: small gmina waterworks, private water supplies, resorts/campsites, schools, and food plants with own wells.

Hidden insight: after Sanepid says water is unfit or conditionally fit, the painful lane is same-day chlorination/flushing, replacement-water logistics, lab resampling, and evidence back to the inspector.

Primary control point:

- mobile chlorination/dosing skid;
- chlorine meters and sampling kits;
- lab priority slot;
- plumber/operator partner;
- bottled-water/tanker fallback reservation;
- site-specific PSSE response binder.

Two-month proof:

- lease/buy dosing equipment;
- sign lab SLA and tanker/bottled-water fallback;
- get 3 small operators to pay emergency retainers;
- run one mock chlorination/resampling drill.

Main copy risk: water-treatment firms and municipal utilities. Defense is pre-mapped sites, reserved response assets, and inspector-ready incident documentation.

## 4. Obsolete Bakery Oven Donor Bank

Target buyer: small bakery chains, supermarket/convenience bake-off counters, independent bakeries, and bakery-equipment service technicians.

Hidden insight: the scarce asset is not oven service. It is matched, tested donor inventory for older WIESHEU / MIWE / DEBAG / Wachtel / Hein ovens: controller boards, touch displays, steam modules, fan motors, sensors, ignition parts, doors, and seals.

Primary control point:

- owned donor ovens;
- tested parts register by model/serial;
- exclusive first-pickup agreements with 2-3 used bakery-equipment dealers/liquidators;
- paid emergency access for service techs or bakeries.

Two-month proof:

- buy 5-8 donor ovens or incomplete machines;
- strip/test 30-50 critical parts;
- sign 3 service techs or bakeries on paid emergency access;
- execute 1-2 real part swaps.

Main copy risk: used-equipment dealers or service techs can copy after seeing demand. Defense is exclusive pickup rights plus exact tested inventory by model/serial.

## 5. Legal-For-Trade Weighbridge Donor Stack

Target buyer: grain elevators, scrap yards, quarries, concrete plants, feed mills, and recycling yards using legal-for-trade truck scales.

Hidden insight: a broken weighbridge is not just downtime. If it is used for trade, repair and re-legalization evidence matter. Damaged/incomplete measuring instruments can be refused legalization.

Primary control point:

- donor inventory of exact load cells, junction boxes, indicators, printer modules, cable kits, and seals;
- reserved scale-technician and legalization-prep slots;
- customer-specific prefit file with exact scale components, serials, calibration history, and emergency swap documentation.

Two-month proof:

- inspect 20 weighbridges;
- sign 5 paid emergency retainers;
- stock top 10 failure parts for 2-3 common legacy families;
- reserve one technician;
- execute one real or simulated swap with a legal-metrology-ready document pack.

Main copy risk: incumbent scale-service companies. Defense must be narrow donor inventory plus customer-specific prefit files, not general repair brokerage.

## 6. PV Hail-Damage Module-To-Recycling Custody Rail

Target buyer: PV installers, farm/SME PV owners, insurers/loss adjusters, and WEEE producer-compliance organizations.

Hidden insight: damaged PV modules need serial-level proof before removal. Insurance, warranty, second-life, and WEEE disposal all depend on evidence captured on the roof and during palletization. After modules are removed, sold, or mixed, proof is gone.

Primary control point:

- pre-removal damage/serial manifest;
- timestamped photos;
- sealed pallets;
- installer handoff;
- recycler receipt;
- BDO/KPO trail;
- insurer-ready custody pack.

Two-month proof:

- parents' company or adjacent installers route 2-3 real damaged/replaced PV jobs;
- process 100+ modules;
- sign one recycler;
- get one loss-adjuster/installer evidence template accepted.

Main copy risk: installers/recyclers can copy, but neither naturally owns neutral roof-to-recycler evidence. Risk: this may duplicate general PV/OZE territory unless narrowed to insurer/warranty custody, not recycling.
