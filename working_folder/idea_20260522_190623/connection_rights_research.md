# Connection-Rights Research Notes

## Official Signals

- URE's cable-pooling summary for 2024 says provisions enabling cable pooling have been in force since **2023-10-01**. In 2024, **12 shared grid connections** were completed, **49 cable-pooling agreements** were signed, **130 applications** were submitted, conditions were issued for **62** installations, and **47** applications were refused. URE notes shared connections are useful because they optimize existing infrastructure and fuller RES generation use. Source: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/494%2CThe-President-of-the-URE-summarises-the-first-year-of-cable-pooling-in-Poland.html
- URE's 2024 position on OZE connections and cable pooling says the publication was prepared because of the growing number of connection refusals and disputes between project developers and grid operators. It states cable pooling allows two or more RES installations to connect at one point, and notes lack of unified practical interpretation as a barrier. Source: https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/11817%2CPrzylaczanie-OZE-do-sieci-i-cable-pooling-stanowisko-Prezesa-Urzedu-Regulacji-En.html
- URE's 2025 small RES report says the number of small RES generators rose from **3,761 to 4,333**, the number of their installations from **6,977 to 8,077**, and total installed capacity of small RES exceeded **5.9 GW** by the end of 2025. Source: https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/13289%2CRaport-URE-w-2025-r-w-malych-instalacjach-OZE-wytworzono-blisko-55-TWh-energii.html
- URE's 2026 micro-installation report says Poland had **1,636,673** RES micro-installations connected at the end of 2025, PV made up 99.9% of the smallest RES installations, and prosumers owned more than 98.5%. Source: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/518%2CURE-report-The-number-of-RES-micro-installations-in-Poland-has-already-exceeded-.html

## Interpretation

Connection capacity is becoming the scarce asset. Land and panels are not enough; developers need grid access, and URE's cable-pooling data shows that shared-connection practice exists but remains early and operationally messy.

The hidden opportunity is not to advise developers that cable pooling exists. It is to **control optioned access to already-connected or connection-capable owners** before developers find them:

- old small PV/biogas/wind sites;
- farms and agro-businesses with connection rights or expansion potential;
- SMEs with underused MV connections;
- existing RES producers whose connection point could support storage or complementary RES under an owner/developer arrangement.

## Key Control-Point Test

Do not count:

- a list of sites;
- a map;
- an owner's verbal interest;
- a consulting memo.

Count only:

- signed 6-12 month exclusive mandate/option;
- owner consent to share a developer data room;
- connection agreement/conditions;
- export/meter history;
- site map and land/path notes;
- a success-fee or paid-access term.

## Strong POC Definition

Within 60 days:

- 3 signed exclusive mandates/options;
- 1 complete connection-right data room;
- 1 developer LOI, paid review, or success-fee mandate;
- no more than 15,000 PLN spent before signed owner mandates exist.
