# Real Validation Failed: HeatRead Takeover Contracts

## Gate

Real Zero To One working-chat validation.

## Idea Sent

HeatRead Takeover Contracts for orphan small buildings: a 4-5 year remote-readout and heat-cost allocation operator for neglected 30-180 flat Polish housing communities facing the 2027 remote-read heat/hot-water meter requirement. The proposed control point was a signed exclusive building mandate, customer deposit, resident/device register, supplier/readout arrangement, installer calendar, recurring readout/billing data custody, and complaint/evidence archive.

## Score

`74/100`

## Verdict

Failed the real `>=85` gate. Do not create a confirmed idea file.

## Key Chatbot Diagnosis

The regulatory pain is real, and the proposed control-point stack is concrete enough to validate in two months. However, the core offering is not novel enough: Ista, Techem, Brunata, and similar players already sell remote readout, heat-cost allocation, portals, and settlement services in Poland.

The actual wedge is not a new category; it is serving neglected small buildings faster and more locally than incumbents bother to. That can be a local service business, but not a high-defensibility startup.

## Strongest Objections

- Incumbents already offer much of the remote-readout and heat-cost allocation workflow.
- Housing-community decisions may not move within two months because resolutions and owner votes are slow.
- The control point is fake unless contracts explicitly include exclusivity, readout/API/export rights, resident/device register control, settlement-file authority, and installation subcontracting authority.
- Resident complaints can consume founder time and create liability unless scripts, audit trails, response SLAs, and liability caps are strict.
- The founder's 5h/day constraint is a major operational problem once resident access, suppliers, complaints, and settlement questions begin.
- AI/data skills are not the wedge at the start; the real wedge is local operational trust.

## Useful Feedback To Preserve

The chatbot said this deserves a score only if the two-month POC proves:

- signed 4-5 year exclusive readout/billing mandate;
- deposit or prepayment covering device procurement;
- named supplier allocation and written readout/API/export terms;
- building-level resident/device register under the startup's process;
- installer calendar reservation;
- pilot staircase or building section completed;
- liability-limited contract with complaint/evidence procedure.

Without those, score drops to roughly `58-62`.

## Pivot Instruction

Pivot away from remote-read metering and other incumbent-owned service categories. Future candidates need a harder first asset:

- owned physical inventory;
- exclusive supply/pickup rights;
- routed transaction flow where incumbents do not already sit;
- legally necessary evidence custody that cannot be reconstructed later;
- customer prepayment or reservation before use;
- a niche too small/annoying for incumbents but with a control point stronger than local service.
