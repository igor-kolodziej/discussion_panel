# Round 3 Health / Medical-Adjacent Scout

Context: founder is a Warsaw data scientist with neuroscience/data science background, interest in health/longevity/wearables, girlfriend is a medical student, max 5h/day, 100k PLN, introverted/no camera. Existing BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack is excluded.

Round 1 and 2 failed mainly because control points were conditional: reports, maps, audits, weak referrals, non-binding reservations, or easy incumbent copy.

Round 3 should only include health ideas where the two-month POC can secure one of:

- routed clinician / employer / clinic workflow;
- legally required evidence custody;
- reserved specialized clinician or lab capacity;
- physical medical device inventory;
- exclusive patient/data cohort.

## 1. Lead Exposure Surveillance Rail

Target buyer: Polish SME employers with lead exposure risk and their occupational-medicine / BHP providers. Start with battery service/recycling, metal foundries, renovation/paint removal, shooting ranges, stained-glass workshops, metalwork, and scrap/recycling yards.

Hidden insight: the scarce asset is not another BHP checklist. The trigger is a legally sensitive worker-health evidence chain: blood lead testing, occupational physician interpretation, repeat surveillance schedule, employer evidence custody, and follow-up when thresholds are exceeded. EU 2024 changes lowered the occupational lead exposure limits and biological limit value, making old informal monitoring insufficient.

Specific control point:

- employer-signed worker monitoring roster;
- lab agreement for blood lead sample processing;
- occupational-medicine physician interpretation capacity;
- chain-of-custody and legally indexed evidence archive;
- recurring test schedule controlled by the startup.

Why the founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:

- no software build is needed first;
- buy/prepay 50-100 lab tests and physician review blocks;
- recruit 2-3 local employers by written technical outreach, BHP offices, and occupational-medicine clinics;
- build intake/evidence templates and reminder ledger personally;
- data-science fit is strong for exposure registry, abnormal-result routing, and audit-ready evidence files.

Copy risk: occupational-medicine clinics, BHP consultants, or labs can copy the package. Defense is the employer roster, recurring surveillance calendar, chain-of-custody process, and integrated doctor/lab workflow. Do not sell a dashboard.

Likely simulated Zero-To-One range: 86-91. It can cross 90 only if the first POC gets signed employer rosters plus physician/lab capacity, not just interest.

Sources checked: EU Council lead/diisocyanate limit update, Directive 98/24/EC, Directive 2024/869/EU.

## 2. Driver Sleep-Apnea Fitness-to-Drive Fast Lane

Target buyer: occupational-medicine clinics, fleet/logistics employers, bus/coach operators, and private driver-medical exam providers.

Hidden insight: sleep apnea is not just a wellness/longevity niche. EU driving-licence rules require suspected moderate/severe obstructive sleep apnea cases to be referred for authorized medical advice before licence issue or renewal. The hard bottleneck is fast evidence: validated home sleep-apnea testing inventory, sleep physician interpretation, and employer/occupational-medicine routing.

Specific control point:

- 5-10 rented or purchased validated home sleep-apnea test devices;
- reserved pulmonologist/sleep-physician report slots;
- one occupational-medicine clinic or fleet routing suspected drivers;
- legally archived test/report/compliance evidence;
- repeat compliance checks for treated drivers where required.

Why the founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:

- lease or buy a small HSAT fleet instead of building hardware;
- sign one sleep physician for paid interpretation blocks;
- sell to one occupational-medicine clinic as a 48-72h overflow path;
- founder's quantified-self/sleep interest maps to device QC, signal quality, and report workflow;
- girlfriend/medical-student network helps find clinical advisors without the founder becoming the clinician.

Copy risk: sleep labs, occupational-medicine networks, and telemedicine clinics. Defense is routed clinic/fleet flow plus device inventory and reserved interpretation capacity. A consumer sleep score app is not defensible; a licence-related workflow can be.

Likely simulated Zero-To-One range: 87-92. It can cross 90 if the POC secures routed cases from an occupational clinic or fleet, not just direct-to-consumer tests.

Sources checked: Commission Directive 2014/85/EU and EU sleep-apnea driving-licence commentary.

## 3. Sharps Exposure Emergency Rail

Target buyer: small dental chains, aesthetic-medicine clinics, private outpatient clinics, and possibly tattoo/piercing chains as a second beachhead.

Hidden insight: small clinics often have paper post-exposure procedures but weak real execution when a needle-stick or blood exposure happens. The scarce asset is a live route inside the time window: infectious-disease clinician, baseline/source labs, PEP decision, prescription/access path, follow-up test schedule, and incident evidence custody.

Specific control point:

- prepaid infectious-disease clinician triage capacity;
- lab draw / testing workflow for exposed worker and source patient where lawful;
- signed clinic incident-routing mandate;
- PEP access pathway through partner clinic/pharmacy/doctor;
- incident file and follow-up evidence custody.

Why the founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:

- start with retainers from 10-20 dental/aesthetic clinics;
- prepay clinician on-call blocks and lab agreements;
- run two mock exposure drills as proof;
- build a simple incident ledger and follow-up scheduler;
- girlfriend's medical network can help source protocol review and clinician intros.

Copy risk: hospitals, occupational-medicine providers, insurers, and infectious-disease clinics. Biggest operational caveat is on-call urgency and liability; this probably needs a clinician-owned protocol and contracted triage from day one.

Likely simulated Zero-To-One range: 84-90. It is hard-control and urgent, but may be capped by emergency operations under the founder's 5h/day constraint unless the clinician partner owns first-line triage.

Sources checked: Polish AIDS Centre / Ministry of Health post-exposure materials, Polish post-exposure recommendations, Wroclaw Medical University occupational exposure procedure.

## Recommendation

Best Round 3 finalists:

1. Driver Sleep-Apnea Fitness-to-Drive Fast Lane.
2. Lead Exposure Surveillance Rail.

Sharps Exposure Emergency Rail is worth keeping as a backup, but only if a clinical partner can own urgent triage; otherwise it violates the founder's time constraint.
