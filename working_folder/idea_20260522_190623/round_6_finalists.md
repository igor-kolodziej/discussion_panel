# Round 6 Finalists

Round 6 uses the scout outputs but tightens each idea around a concrete control point that can be secured in two months. Ideas that remain generic consulting, generic evidence packs, dashboards, or soft partner capacity should fail the simulated gate.

## 1. Insured Receivables Claim-Preservation Desk

Target buyer: Polish trade-credit insurance brokers and their SME policyholders: wholesalers, importers, B2B distributors, and manufacturers insured by Coface, Atradius, Allianz Trade, KUKE, or similar carriers.

Hidden insight: companies do not only lose money because debtors default. They lose insured recovery because operational events before the claim are mishandled: overdue declarations, credit-limit checks, stop-shipment rules, payment-plan approvals, collection handoff deadlines, and document packs. Brokers sell policies but usually do not sit inside the client's AR ledger every week; insurers receive incomplete or late claim evidence after eligibility has already been damaged.

Product wedge: broker-white-label claim-preservation desk. The startup monitors insured receivables weekly from AR aging exports, policy schedules, buyer-limit files, invoice/payment data, and broker/insurer rules. It prepares overdue notices, deadline alerts, payment-plan approval requests, collection handoff packs, and claim-ready evidence binders. The client or broker remains the official policyholder/contact; the startup owns the event calendar, evidence workflow, insurer-specific checklists, and recovery-risk register.

Primary control point:

- signed policyholder mandate to receive weekly AR aging and policy-limit data;
- broker-routed access to insured receivable portfolios;
- insurer-specific deadline and evidence maps;
- prepared-notification and claim-pack workflow;
- live log of protected receivables at risk of becoming ineligible.

Two-month proof:

- one broker signs a pilot covering 3-5 policyholders or one insured company signs directly;
- ingest 50-200 open receivables and policy terms;
- identify at least 250k PLN of receivables whose coverage is at risk due to missing notice, limit, payment-plan, or document steps;
- submit or prepare at least 5 overdue/claim-preservation action packs;
- get one broker or policyholder to pay a fixed pilot or monthly retainer.

Why not generic consulting/dashboard: the buyer is paying to preserve claim eligibility and insured recovery, not to view AR. The controlled workflow is the live deadline/evidence operation tied to policy terms and broker routing.

Main copy risk: brokers or insurers can internalize the workflow. Defense is broker-white-label positioning, focus on ERP-light SME policyholders, weekly operational data access, and insurer-specific evidence playbooks across many small accounts.

## 2. IVF Cryo-Storage Consent & Renewal Rail

Target buyer: private IVF clinics and fertility networks in Poland with growing embryo, oocyte, sperm, and gamete storage populations.

Hidden insight: cryo-storage is not passive freezer rental. Clinics accumulate a high-friction backlog of expiring consents, missing relationship-status confirmations, arrears, transfer requests, disposition decisions, communication failures, and legally sensitive patient records. Public IVF funding increases patient volume, and incoming EU SoHO rules increase the value of clean, current consent and traceability workflows.

Product wedge: clinic-branded renewal and disposition workflow for frozen material storage accounts. The startup does not make medical or legal decisions. It runs the operational rail: account list cleanup, patient contact schedule, clinic-approved scripts, payment-renewal links, consent packet tracking, transfer-out routing, escalation queues, and evidence archive. Clinic staff approve final scripts and decisions; the startup owns workflow throughput and unresolved-account intelligence.

Primary control point:

- signed clinic workflow mandate and DPA;
- routed access to 200-500 storage accounts;
- clinic-approved contact scripts and consent/payment packet templates;
- status ledger for renewal, arrears, transfer, disposition, no-contact, and escalation cases;
- monthly recovered-storage-revenue and risk-backlog report.

Two-month proof:

- one clinic routes 200-500 accounts;
- 50 completed renewals or status confirmations;
- 20 arrears/forward-storage payments recovered;
- 5 transfer-out or disposition cases cleanly closed;
- signed DPA, clinic-approved scripts, and measurable backlog reduction.

Why not generic consulting/dashboard: the control point is live routed patient-account workflow and signed storage decisions, not advice or reporting.

Main copy risk: IVF clinic software vendors and clinics can internalize. Defense is becoming the operational recovery specialist for neglected accounts and building a clinic-trusted renewal/disposition playbook before software vendors prioritize this niche.

## 3. Class B Autoclave Continuity Locker

Target buyer: dental clinics, oral-surgery clinics, orthodontic groups, podology clinics, and aesthetic-medicine clinics performing invasive procedures.

Hidden insight: small clinics are fragile because one failed Class B autoclave can stop revenue, cancel procedures, or create unsafe workarounds. The immediate scarce asset is not compliance advice or monitoring software; it is a validated sterilization continuity packet: emergency loaner, technician response, biological/chemical indicator process, installation/qualification evidence, and documentation acceptable to clinic management.

Product wedge: local paid retainer that reserves validated Class B autoclave continuity. The startup owns or reserves 2-3 validated loaner autoclaves, standardized transport/placement workflow, consumables/test indicators, technician SLA, and incident documentation pack. It does not service medical devices itself; qualified technicians handle installation/service.

Primary control point:

- owned or reserved validated Class B autoclaves;
- technician SLA for emergency placement;
- biological/chemical indicator and documentation packet;
- paid clinic retainers with named loaner priority;
- local density in Warsaw/Lodz/Poznan where same-day placement is realistic.

Two-month proof:

- buy/reserve 2-3 used or demo Class B autoclaves under 100k PLN total;
- sign technician SLA;
- sign 10 clinic retainers or deposits;
- run 2 paid mock deployments or one real deployment;
- produce one clinic-accepted validation/continuity packet.

Why not generic consulting/dashboard: the control point is physical validated emergency equipment plus documented continuity, not advice.

Main copy risk: dental equipment service firms and distributors can copy. Defense is local retainer density, same-day loaner stock, and documentation discipline for small clinics ignored by larger service contracts.

## 4. Lift Door-Drive Donor Bank

Target buyer: independent Polish lift-service companies maintaining older residential lifts for housing cooperatives, property managers, and small commercial buildings.

Hidden insight: for many older lifts, the pain is not a full modernization decision. It is keeping the lift running for 2-8 weeks or months when door operators, controller boards, inverters, light curtains, displays, or interface modules fail and OEM replacement is slow, expensive, or bundled into modernization pressure. Independent lift-service firms need traceable, tested bridge stock but cannot individually build enough donor supply.

Product wedge: traceable donor inventory for a narrow installed base of older lift door systems and control modules. The startup signs first-look salvage rights from modernization crews, buys removed modules, tests/photographs/catalogs them, records serial/provenance, and sells or reserves bridge modules only through licensed lift-service firms. It is not a lift service company and does not install components.

Primary control point:

- signed first-look/right-of-purchase agreements with 2-3 modernization contractors or building clear-out channels;
- physically owned tested donor modules for 1-2 common door/operator families;
- serial/provenance/test record;
- service-firm reservation/deposit agreements;
- UDT-aware documentation boundaries.

Two-month proof:

- acquire/test 10-20 high-frequency donor modules;
- secure 2 salvage-rights MOUs;
- get 3 paid reservations or standby agreements from independent service firms;
- execute one real or simulated module handoff through a licensed service firm.

Why not generic resale/service: the control point is narrow traceable emergency bridge stock from controlled salvage rights, not used-parts listing or lift repair brokerage.

Main copy risk: existing lift-parts suppliers or lift-service firms can copy. Defense is early salvage pipeline control plus tested stock for neglected older installed-base niches that big OEMs prefer to modernize.

## 5. PalletDebt Recovery Rail

Target buyer: Polish food, beverage, beauty, household, and FMCG manufacturers shipping recurring EUR-pallet loads to retailer DCs, distributors, and 3PL warehouses.

Hidden insight: EUR-pallet imbalances are treated as logistics noise, but they behave like cash receivables. Missing pallet returns, wrong pallet notes, unbalanced carrier accounts, and retailer/DC discrepancies hide across CMRs, WZ/PZ, delivery notes, pallet promissory notes, carrier statements, and emails. Many companies accept the loss because no one owns the end-to-end evidence chain.

Product wedge: 90-day recovery mandate for pallet receivables. The startup gets authority to request counterparty pallet statements, reconcile shipment-level evidence, prepare balance-dispute files, and recover cash or physical pallet credits. It starts with one supplier and 3-5 counterparties, priced as fixed audit plus success fee on recovered value.

Primary control point:

- signed recovery mandate over named counterparties;
- access to CMR/WZ/PZ/delivery/pallet-note evidence;
- authority to request statements and send balance disputes;
- counterparty-specific recovery playbook;
- ledger of pallet receivables by carrier, retailer DC, and distributor.

Two-month proof:

- one supplier signs a 90-day mandate;
- reconcile 500-2,000 pallet movements;
- identify 30k-150k PLN equivalent in missing pallet value;
- submit 3-5 counterparty dispute packs;
- recover at least one cash/credit/pallet settlement or secure a success-fee agreement.

Why not generic consulting/dashboard: the output is recovered pallet value and enforceable dispute packs, not visibility.

Main copy risk: 3PLs, TMS vendors, pallet-pool operators, or logistics consultants can copy. Defense is signed recovery mandates, counterparty dispute memory, and category-specific evidence templates.

## 6. Deposit System Settlement Exception Rail

Target buyer: small and mid-sized beverage producers, importers, beer distributors, and regional retail chains dealing with Poland's deposit system.

Hidden insight: deposit systems create a new money ledger, not just environmental compliance. The painful edge cases are not normal returns; they are SKU/EAN errors, exports treated as domestic, damaged labels, promo packs, private-label imports, operator statement mismatches, cross-border flows, and unclaimed adjustments. Smaller producers/importers lack a settlement-exception team.

Product wedge: operator-ready exception recovery sprint. The startup reconciles SKU/EAN master data, operator statements, domestic/export sales files, returns, credit/debit notes, and packaging-flow evidence. It prepares settlement exception packets and dispute trackers for one producer or distributor.

Primary control point:

- signed mandate over one producer/distributor deposit-settlement file;
- access to SKU/EAN master, operator statements, sales/export records, and return/credit notes;
- exception taxonomy by operator, SKU, and flow type;
- prepared dispute packets and adjustment ledger.

Two-month proof:

- one producer/importer grants data and dispute mandate;
- process 50-200 SKUs or 2-3 months of flows;
- identify 20k-100k PLN in mis-settlements, wrong EAN treatment, export leakage, or unclaimed adjustments;
- submit at least 3 operator-ready exception packets;
- secure fixed audit payment or success-fee recovery.

Why not generic consulting/dashboard: the product is recovered or avoided settlement money via operator-ready exception packets, not deposit compliance advice.

Main copy risk: deposit-system operators, ERP vendors, or consultants can add reports. Defense is specializing in messy importer/exporter exceptions and operator-specific evidence patterns.
