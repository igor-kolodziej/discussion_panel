## Shared Failure Pattern

The simulated panel rejected all six because the proposed control points were either:

- temporary client mandates without durable workflow ownership;
- sensitive access that an unknown founder probably cannot secure inside two months;
- buyable physical inventory already controlled or easily copied by incumbent service firms;
- generic reconciliation/recovery workflows adjacent to existing software, brokers, ERP vendors, operators, or consultants.

## Candidate Notes

### Insured Receivables Claim-Preservation Desk

Real pain, but distribution and trust are weak. Brokers and insurers can copy the workflow, and the founder lacks a trade-credit network. It becomes viable only if one broker routes 3-5 policyholders or one insured SME grants paid AR/policy access quickly.

### IVF Cryo-Storage Consent & Renewal Rail

Strong operational pain but fatal clinic-access and legal sensitivity risk. The clinic owns patient trust, records, consent authority, payment relationship, and final decisions.

### Class B Autoclave Continuity Locker

Incumbent autoclave service firms already advertise loaner/replacement equipment. The equipment is buyable and the workflow can be copied by service incumbents with one packaging step.

### Lift Door-Drive Donor Bank

The pain is real, but UDT/liability ambiguity and weak prepayment make this look like risky used-parts resale. It needs exclusive salvage and paid service-firm reservations before it is worth revisiting.

### PalletDebt Recovery Rail

Not a direct duplicate of PromoLeak, but structurally adjacent to document-backed FMCG recovery. Existing pallet-management tools and 3PL/TMS incumbents can absorb the recovery workflow.

### Deposit System Settlement Exception Rail

Timing is real, but operator acceptance, producer/importer data access, and value discovery are unproven. The workflow is too close to deposit compliance reconciliation unless one messy importer/exporter file shows material recoverable exceptions.

## Pivot Instruction

Stop pursuing generic evidence desks, soft workflow mandates, and buyable emergency stock. The next pass should prioritize upstream physical assets or rights where the valuable unit is destroyed, split, scrapped, or made inaccessible before premium/high-downtime buyers see it.
