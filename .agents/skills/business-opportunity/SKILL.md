# Business Opportunity

Operate the repository workflow; do not invent a parallel process. The main agent owns canonical state and delegates bounded, role-separated work. The CLI owns validation, score computation, transitions, campaign patience, imports, and publication.

## Begin Or Resume

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `config/opportunity-workflow.json`, and current CLI help.
2. Read `PERSONALITY_SITUATION.md` as the founder filter. Load `Personalities/ZeroToOne.txt` only for evaluation, never to shape discovery.
3. Create or resume the requested run or campaign through the CLI. Follow recorded state; never reconstruct it from chat or hand-edit managed files.
4. Read [workflow.md](references/workflow.md) before assigning work or advancing a stage. Read [artifact-contracts.md](references/artifact-contracts.md) before producing a canonical artifact.
5. Before using delayed history, identify `opportunity_outcome_quarantine_v1` rows and exclude their named outcomes and learning digests from business-decision use. A quarantine record may establish only that an artifact is workflow-test evidence; do not reuse its candidate content, scores, or conclusions.

## Execute A Cohort

- Keep scouts blind to history, the rubric, target, scores, rankings, verdicts, and one another. Run every configured lane to its configured seed budget. Mechanism-first starts from a control point the founder could lawfully own or contract and an existing paid event or measurable loss. Weak-signal starts from observed purchasing, workaround, pricing, labor-allocation, or transaction change. Future-backcast starts from a future operating state that requires a missing physical, contractual, financial, or coordination mechanism. Do not merge these search contexts. Form the fresh pool before delayed history review.
- Every candidate records its configured `discovery_lane`, complete critical-control-point contract, commercial mechanics, and controlled structural signature. Record unknowns rather than inventing ownership or contract proof. Fill the configured mechanism-first founder-access reserve only with legally usable founder access or operating relationships, never confidential employer resources. `founder_fit` is always a nonempty JSON array of distinct strings, never a scalar. Candidate claims declare `claim_type`; do not invent evidence or repair semantic content to satisfy the schema. A rejected candidate artifact is a visible mechanical failure and may use only the configured retry budget.
- Include the generated candidate constraints explicitly in each scout's initial assignment. Mechanism-first discovery rejects an `externally_controlled` critical mechanism, an `unknown` paid event, or a `present` confidential-employer dependency. Distinguish unverified acquisition from established external control before authoring the first response; unsigned rights remain unproven, and adverse control judgments must never be relabeled to pass validation. The two `customer_relationship_owner` fields must describe the same owner, including any uncertainty. Before assigning gap recovery, compare the canonical shortfall with the remaining configured capacity; preserve an impossible continuation as incomplete rather than launching work that cannot satisfy the gate.
- Record exact fingerprint uniqueness and fill any configured lane shortfall before screening. When discovery advances, use the CLI-generated lane-balanced batches. Give each batch to one fresh evaluator through `status <run-id> --batch <batch-id>`; that evaluator must read the founder and evaluator snapshot paths returned by that scoped view, verify each file against its returned SHA-256, and then score every candidate in its batch. Those two bound snapshots are required context rather than forbidden repository reads. The evaluator sees no other batch, score, ranking, history, or selection rationale. Do not treat the internal `calibration` stage name as an invitation to make an editorial selection.
- After complete primary screening, call `advance`. If it remains in calibration with a secondary plan, assign each `status <run-id> --secondary-batch <id>` view to one fresh evaluator and use the `secondary-evaluation` kind. Require the same read-and-hash verification of the two returned founder/evaluator snapshots. Give that evaluator only those bound snapshots, its returned candidates, and the evaluation contract—never primary scores, ranks, cutoff information, history, or rationale. Complete every planned set before advancing again; the CLI alone aggregates the recorded inputs.
- Let the CLI advance the canonical score prefix from every batch, deduplicate the combined winners by structural signature, and backfill a collision from the next-highest score in that same batch. Exact fingerprint uniqueness remains a separate metric. The generated shortlist is immutable and score-selected; do not author a portfolio selection, create a core/wildcard eligibility gate, or substitute a lower-scoring candidate by judgment. The three discovery lanes preserve creative variance before scoring, including future-facing and weak-signal ideas.
- Keep each discovery candidate's commercial core immutable. Store research separately against its exact v1 hash, distinguish evidence, inference, and unknowns, and test the claimed control-point status and contractibility. Cover direct buyer or paid-event evidence, distribution and acquisition, fully loaded unit economics, and rights/control/access/contractibility with claim and bounded source-class references or an explicit `unknown`. Do not add generic sources merely to fill coverage. Apply the narrow fatal-evidence rules; missing private validation remains uncertainty.
- Give every researched candidate its required fresh working evaluation. Research cannot advance or terminate until coverage is exact and complete.
- Give an evaluator one strict JSON response contract. Let the CLI attach immutable identity and deterministically derive weights, exclusions, adjustment, rounding, final score, and qualification. Do not create a separate authored wrapper or total.
- Let the CLI rank the researched pool by its fresh score, using stable candidate ID only for an exact tie, and select the configured development set. Give each constructor only `status <run-id> --constructor <candidate-id>` and the development-response contract; never give it prior scores, ranks, the evaluator, or the qualification rule. Preserve one result per selected candidate: either an admitted v2 that identifies and changes the binding dependency, control and distribution instruments, customer-relationship ownership, fully loaded economics, incumbent-response defense, and supporting research claims, or `no_valid_redesign` with the original v1 unchanged. Preflight v2 before starting its canonical job. If research finds the critical mechanism externally controlled, any redesign must change that control structure and remove, replace, or make it contractible. An honest `unknown` resulting status remains admissible when v2 specifies the concrete acquisition instrument, launch controller, and refusal fallback; an externally controlled v2 is not admissible.
- Research must address every structured falsification category, including control-point obtainability, substitutes and incumbent response, unsigned external dependencies, willingness/urgency/repeat economics, cold start and data-moat formation, and ownership of the critical mechanism. Each category references explicit research claims; an `unknown` remains uncertainty. Candidate-only researchers use `status <run-id> --candidate <candidate-id>` and may read only the returned candidate and founder snapshot context. They must not call broad status or inspect portfolio, evaluation, campaign, or unrelated-candidate state.
- After construction, use `status <run-id> --development-lineage <candidate-id>` for each configured independent development evaluator. Every evaluator sees both immutable versions under the same evidence and evaluator conditions; the research-phase result does not satisfy this gate. The CLI computes the configured conservative score from every recorded judgment. A redesign survives only when that conservative score is strictly higher; equal or lower preserves the original. Keep every version, judgment, identity, and aggregation input for audit.
- Rank one winning version per developed lineage by conservative score, using stable candidate ID only for an exact tie, and freeze the configured top prefix as hash-bound references. Never allow both versions of one idea to occupy finalist slots. Only an all-direct-fatal scored research decision may close `no_finalist`; once development starts, continue its configured top versions to holdout. Follow [history-and-holdout.md](references/history-and-holdout.md). Every valid external import is binding.

## Continue A Campaign

Publish and validate each terminal cohort, then let the CLI determine whether the campaign qualifies, continues, plateaus, or reaches its configured cap. Campaign progress is score-based; calibration labels and reworded or industry-swapped archetypes never reset patience. For continuation, give scouts only the generated sanitized gap brief. Do not reveal candidate identities, scores, rankings, qualification rules, selection rationale, holdout feedback, or learning rows, and do not add unconfigured search loops.

## Keep State Visible

If a fresh role cannot start because the delegation tool has no free slot, continue already-running work and wait for capacity; never reuse a completed actor for a different role. A standalone CLI worker is a separate transport: launch it from a fresh physical temporary directory outside every repository, with a fresh runtime home, ephemeral execution, user configuration ignored, and automatic project-document/memory loading disabled. Starting inside a repository can expose its workflow skill even when the role prompt forbids that read. Before using a fallback for business work, run a harmless probe of the exact setup and inspect startup context, reads, and file writes. Supply only the role's bounded inputs and returned snapshot bindings, preserve first responses and tool logs, and keep the main agent as canonical writer. If forbidden context is read, preserve the failed attempt and exclude its output; a valid adverse judgment must never be retried. Native holdouts still require the separate filesystem boundary in [history-and-holdout.md](references/history-and-holdout.md).

The main agent uses broad `status` before acting; every delegated role uses only its scoped context. A scoped status view returns snapshot bindings as paths and hashes, not inline source text. Returned artifact and snapshot paths are relative to the run directory; include that directory in each assignment and resolve the paths against it before reading. Explicitly require a scoped evaluator to read and hash-verify both returned founder and evaluator snapshots before judging; require a researcher or constructor to do the same for each snapshot its scoped view returns. Those bound files are the only permitted repository reads beyond the scoped artifacts, and a prompt must never prohibit them. Before assigning any structured-output role except a packet-only native holdout, call `preflight <run-id> --kind <kind>` and give that role only its returned template, enums, constraints, and bounded context. After the first substantive response, call preflight again with `--input` before starting the canonical job. For a native holdout, the main agent performs preflight itself: first export the finalist, create a fresh temporary response path outside managed run state, and call `holdout-assignment`. Launch the judge with no inherited workflow or repository context and give it exactly the returned immutable packet, strict response contract, and destination. The judge must not load repository instructions, skills, CLI help/status/preflight, history, prior evaluations, rankings, competing candidates, or any other judge output. The main agent reads the completed temporary response, preflights it, and performs canonical job completion/import. This packet boundary is mechanically shaped by the CLI; filesystem isolation of the launched judge remains an orchestration guarantee.

Preserve the first substantive response outside managed state before any correction. Every input preflight must include `--original-input <preserved-first-response>` alongside `--input`; retain its comparison result in the audit. The CLI blocks authored-content changes, including evidence statuses, control assessments, source classifications, claims, scores and rationales. Research may repair only category claim/source references to unchanged existing evidence; the same author must justify those references. This exception never permits changing an evidence status, even to an existing unknown claim. Candidate redesigns may repair only the two derived changed-field declaration lists; canonical validation must recompute them against the immutable parent, with all underlying candidate content and economic rationale fixed. The same constructor must justify this mechanical repair. All roles may trim surrounding whitespace from string values as a same-author mechanical repair; internal whitespace, words, numbers, judgments, and structure remain fixed. Other differences require identical parsed content outside the explicit reference/declaration exceptions. A `semantic_correction_forbidden` or `correction_unverifiable` result is not a schema retry: preserve the incomplete work and diagnose the contract before further admission. Do not omit the comparison or replace the preserved original to obtain a receipt.

Preflight invokes the same canonical validator used at completion; it is not a second schema implementation. Return its first pointer-level error to the same role for schema-only correction and preserve every semantic claim, score, rationale, and judgment. Once preflight is valid, preserve both `validation.input_sha256` and `validation.preflight_receipt.receipt_sha256`, start the job, and complete it with the unchanged input plus `--preflight-sha256` and `--preflight-receipt-sha256`; do not shop among drafts. Preflight is read-only and consumes no attempt. The completion event preserves the full contextual receipt, and `check` rejects a missing or inconsistent receipt. Run `check` after recovery, import, finalization, or publication. Record every started job, completion, failure, and constructor outcome through the CLI. The CLI records and checks screening, working-evaluator, constructor, and holdout identities; keep scout and researcher contexts distinct in orchestration. Retry only transport, parser, schema, or incomplete-output failures within the configured mechanical-attempt budget. Never retry a valid adverse evaluation, `no_valid_redesign`, or holdout to improve the outcome; exhausted failures stay visible for an honest resume.

## Finish

Finalize, inspect the structured report and rendered report, and publish. Confirm that publication contains one compact learning-digest row for every researched candidate and that the history entry references the digest. Terminal semantics are strict:

- `qualified`: required holdouts and any binding external results pass the configured rule;
- `no_qualifier`: at least one finalist completed required holdouts, but none passed;
- `no_finalist`: required working evaluations exist, but no candidate reached holdout, so official scores are null; or
- `contested`: a binding result prevents confirmation.

Preserve strongest-candidate diagnostics and reopen evidence without implying market validation. Never manufacture a score or use `no_qualifier` for a scoreless run.

If direct evidence later proves that a published run violated a required orchestration or evaluation boundary, use `quarantine-outcome` with a precise reason instead of editing or deleting the publication. Confirm that `check` validates its immutable sidecar and append-only history binding. Never use a quarantined outcome or digest for discovery, overlap analysis, scoring, ranking, qualification, or campaign progression; it remains workflow-test evidence only.

Do not enter `prompts/PromoLeak/` unless the user explicitly requests that separate playbook.
