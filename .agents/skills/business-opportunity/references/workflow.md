# Workflow

Read this reference when starting a cohort or campaign, assigning subagents, advancing a stage, or deciding whether evidence is fatal, a candidate may develop, or a campaign should continue.

## Ownership And Role Isolation

The main agent creates or resumes state with `scripts/opportunity.py` and remains the only canonical writer. Subagents receive bounded assignments with a lane, evidence standard, output contract, and stop condition. Keep scout and researcher contexts distinct by orchestration. The structured records mechanically preserve working-evaluator, constructor, and holdout identities.

Keep generation, research, working evaluation, and construction assignments context-distinct. The CLI enforces that a constructor identity cannot also be a working evaluator and that recorded working-evaluator and constructor identities cannot serve as holdout judges; holdout identities are distinct from one another. Scout and researcher freshness remains an explicit main-agent assignment duty because those records do not claim a mechanically verified actor identity. Do not infer freshness from a renamed output file.

Initial scouts receive relevant founder facts but not historical ideas, failure memory, the evaluator, qualification rule, prior scores, rankings, verdicts, or another scout's work. Campaign continuation does not relax this boundary.

## Discovery, Screening, And Portfolio Binding

Run every configured discovery lane to its configured seed budget before delayed history calibration. Mechanism-first begins with a control point the founder could realistically and lawfully acquire or contract plus an existing paid event or measurable loss. Weak-signal begins with observed purchasing, workaround, pricing, labor-allocation, or transaction change. Future-backcast begins with a future operating state that requires a missing physical, contractual, financial, or coordination mechanism. Keep their inputs and assignments separate. Fill the configured mechanism-first access reserve only with lawful founder-controlled assets, access, or operating relationships; never infer access to confidential employer resources. Require source support for time-sensitive claims, tag each candidate with its configured `discovery_lane`, and classify preliminary claims through the strict `claim_type` contract. `founder_fit` is a nonempty JSON array of distinct strings; a scalar is a mechanical schema failure, not content to coerce.

Each discovery candidate v1 contains the canonical six-field fingerprint and a `structure` object containing exactly:

- `commercial_archetype`: the primary way value is sold and captured;
- `control_point`: the scarce right, workflow position, asset, or relationship the business must control; and
- `critical_dependency`: the external condition whose failure most directly breaks delivery or economics.

It also records a complete critical-control-point contract: controlled subject, present and launch controller, acquisition instrument, exclusivity, duration, revocability, transferability, renewal, refusal fallback, replaceability, customer-relationship control, mechanism ownership/dependency, controlled status, lawful founder-access basis, and employer-resource dependency. Its commercial mechanics identify the paid event or loss, payer, budget owner, purchase and renewal events, distribution origin, customer-relationship owner, fully loaded economics, incumbent response, and bundling resistance. The canonical structural signature combines controlled commercial-model, control-point, and dependency categories; `other` uses a normalized descriptor.

Run exact fingerprint deduplication first and report only `exact_fingerprint_unique_count` as exact uniqueness. Report structural-signature variety separately; do not describe it as exact uniqueness. Lexical similarity remains advisory. The CLI may require its bounded gap scout for an exact-uniqueness, lane, or founder-access-reserve shortfall; it does not use prose labels as evidence of semantic progress. Do not leave discovery until the configured unique, lane-balanced screening pool and access reserve are complete.

When discovery advances, the CLI writes `portfolio/screening-batches.json`. The split is deterministic and each batch contains the same configured number from every discovery lane. Assign one fresh evaluator to each batch. That evaluator obtains context only through `python3 scripts/opportunity.py status <run-id> --batch <batch-id>`, then reads the founder and evaluator snapshot paths in that scoped response and verifies their bytes against the returned SHA-256 values. The bindings are references, not inline source text, so both reads are mandatory and must be explicitly allowed in the assignment. The evaluator scores every candidate in that batch under the bound canonical rubric and receives no other batch, prior score, ranking, history, selection rationale, or qualification rule. Use one evaluator identity for all candidates in a batch and never reuse that identity across batches. The active CLI retains the internal stage name `calibration`; it now means this bounded screening step, not an editorial calibration agent.

After primary screening coverage is complete, call `advance`. When the configured boundary is close, it writes `portfolio/secondary-screening.json` and remains in calibration. Assign each deterministic set through `status <run-id> --secondary-batch <secondary-batch-id>` to one evaluator fresh from all primary batch roles and use kind `secondary-evaluation`. That evaluator reads and hash-verifies both snapshot bindings before scoring and receives only those bound sources, the candidates, and strict response contract—never primary scores, ranks, cutoff data, history, or selection rationale. Finish every planned set; the CLI then writes the immutable aggregation inputs and canonical score in `portfolio/screening-aggregation.json`.

The CLI takes the configured top canonical-score prefix from each batch, using stable candidate ID only for an exact tie. It deduplicates the combined winners by structural signature; when a winner is removed, the next-highest distinct candidate from that same batch fills the slot. The CLI generates immutable `portfolio/selection.json`. Do not author a selection, apply core/wildcard eligibility, substitute by judgment, or create a portfolio amendment. Creative variance comes from the independent lanes before every candidate is scored.

Only after this fresh, blinded selection may a candidate researcher receive relevant delayed failure memory for adversarial falsification. Never give history to scouts or screening evaluators, and never let historical overlap change the deterministic score-selected shortlist.

## Attempt-Neutral Output Preflight

For every structured role except a packet-only native holdout, obtain its exact contract with `python3 scripts/opportunity.py preflight <run-id> --kind <kind>`. The result is role-specific and exposes the exact JSON skeleton, enums, canonical evaluator factor names when applicable, and relevant constraints without mutating state or consuming a job attempt. The main agent performs native-holdout preflight after the isolated judge writes its temporary response.

After the role returns its first substantive response, save it outside CLI-managed run state and call preflight again with `--input` and the intended `--job-id`. Return any JSON-pointer diagnostics to the same role for schema-only correction. Do not alter or invite reconsideration of claims, scores, rationales, evidence judgments, constructor outcomes, or other semantic content. When validation succeeds, preserve the reported input hash and receipt hash, start the job, and complete that job with the unchanged file, `--preflight-sha256`, and `--preflight-receipt-sha256`. A hash or contextual-receipt mismatch is an integrity conflict. The completion event stores the full receipt and `check` validates it against the immutable manifest, stage, job, artifact kind, input hash, and canonical artifact hash. Do not preflight multiple semantic variants and select the most favorable one.

Preflight calls the same canonical artifact validator used at completion; do not maintain or reason from a second validator. It reports the first canonical pointer-level failure so the same role can correct only the invalid JSON shape or enum without changing semantic content. Any transport, parser, schema, or incomplete-output failure after the job starts remains a visible attempt. A valid adverse evaluation or `no_valid_redesign` proceeds unchanged.

## Research And Working Evaluation

Research every active shortlist entry. A candidate-only researcher must obtain context with `python3 scripts/opportunity.py status <run-id> --candidate <candidate-id>` and may read only the returned candidate artifact and founder snapshot. Do not give that role broad status output, selection/calibration files, rankings, scores, campaign state, competing candidates, or unrelated jobs. Research is a separate artifact that hash-binds the exact discovery v1; it cannot revise the candidate's commercial core. Test the claimed critical-control-point ownership and contractibility, buyer or paid-event evidence, distribution and acquisition, fully loaded unit economics, incumbent response, constraints, founder feasibility, and the recurring proxy errors defined by the artifact contract. Each commercial-evidence category binds claims and bounded source classes or explicitly records `unknown`. Keep sourced contrary evidence separate from unknowns and include at least one contradicting source; do not inflate generic source volume to fill categories.

The structured `falsification` map must cover every exact category in the artifact contract by referencing one or more research claim IDs. Actively test whether the control point is obtainable and contractible; direct substitutes, incumbent bundling, and likely responses; unsigned partner, rights-holder, data-provider, or distribution dependencies; willingness to pay, urgency, renewal, CAC, payback, gross margin, and repeat economics; cold start and whether a claimed data moat can form; and whether the business owns the critical mechanism or merely names one. Claims may resolve to evidence, inference, or unknown. Missing proof remains uncertainty under the fatal-evidence rule.

Missing customer validation, conversion evidence, signed partners, or private operating data is uncertainty, not a fatal finding. Mark a research result fatal only when direct evidence establishes one of these conditions:

- the offer or required conduct is illegal;
- an essential right cannot be obtained;
- conservative unit economics are mathematically impossible; or
- execution requires a non-delegable founder commitment incompatible with the canonical founder profile.

Every researched candidate receives exactly the configured working-evaluation coverage before the research stage may advance or terminate. Working evaluators read and hash-verify the immutable founder and evaluator snapshots referenced by their scoped context, then receive the immutable candidate, its research, and no other source context—not the target, ranking, generator rationale, or another evaluation. They return one strict JSON response with candidate identity, judge identity, factor judgments, interaction adjustment, assumptions, and diagnostics. The response contains no trusted total. The CLI validates it, attaches immutable candidate and rubric hashes plus the phase-specific evaluation type, and deterministically computes exclusions, effective weights, constrained and rounded scores, and qualification.

Create `portfolio/development-decision.json` through kind `portfolio-decision`. Rank nonfatal candidates by recomputed working score and use stable candidate ID only for an exact tie. Select no more than the configured development maximum. The CLI verifies evaluation coverage and the deterministic score prefix before accepting the record; each candidate decision preserves its disposition, rationale, and any direct fatal evidence.

## Construction, Re-evaluation, And Freeze

Give each selected candidate one structural-constructor pass using only `status <run-id> --constructor <candidate-id>` and the `development-result` contract. Never give it prior scores, rankings, the evaluator, or the qualification rule. The constructor authors the strict hash-free response and references research claims in every falsification category. If it emits a redesign candidate, validate and store that candidate first; the CLI then derives the exact base and final versions and hashes from canonical state. Do not author or perform hash-only search-and-replace rebinding. Preserve either:

- an admitted development v2 that descends directly from the researched v1, declares every changed fingerprint and commercial-structure field, identifies the dependency removed/replaced/made contractible, records the resulting control status and control/distribution instruments, owns or explicitly characterizes the customer relationship, changes fully loaded economics, addresses incumbent response, and cites supporting research claims; or
- `no_valid_redesign`, with the concentrated limiter, attempted structural change, and evidence explaining why no lawful improvement survived.

Do not treat prose polishing as redesign. Preflight a proposed v2 before starting its canonical candidate job. If research found the critical mechanism externally controlled, v2 must change the critical-control structure and remove, replace, or make it contractible; it cannot remain externally controlled. Missing launch proof may remain honestly `unknown` when v2 specifies a concrete acquisition instrument, launch controller, and counterparty-refusal fallback. There is no research copy, promotion copy, or frozen copy of a candidate. Preserve v1 and create v2 only for the admitted redesign. After construction, give each configured independent evaluator the same `status <run-id> --development-lineage <candidate-id>` context containing both immutable versions and the same evidence. The research-phase evaluation cannot satisfy development coverage. The CLI calculates the configured conservative score from all judgments; v2 wins only when its conservative score is strictly higher than v1. Keep every version and individual evaluation for audit, and never rerun an adverse comparison.

The CLI writes `portfolio/version-selection.json`, preserving every aggregation input and yielding exactly one winning version per developed lineage. It ranks those lineage winners by conservative score, using stable candidate ID only for an exact tie, and writes the configured top prefix to `portfolio/finalists.json`. Both versions of one idea can never occupy finalist slots. Never copy or mutate a finalist. In the active schema, `no_finalist` is available only at scored research when every shortlisted candidate has a validated direct-evidence fatal disposition, so no development candidate exists. Once development begins, the configured top lineage winners must continue to holdout; a low working score is not an eligibility escape hatch.

## Holdout, Publication, And Campaign Progression

For every finalist reference, the main agent calls `export-external`, creates a fresh temporary JSON destination outside managed run state, and obtains `holdout-assignment`. Launch each native judge without inherited repository or workflow context and give it exactly the assignment's immutable packet, strict response contract, and temporary destination. The judge must not load repository instructions, workflow skills, CLI help/status/preflight, history, prior evaluations, rankings, competing candidates, or another judge's output. The main agent reads the response, performs preflight, and completes the canonical holdout job. The CLI mechanically restricts the assignment envelope and destination; preventing an independently launched judge from reading repository files remains an orchestration-only guarantee. Valid external evaluations are optional but binding.

After required holdouts and any external import, run `check`, `finalize`, inspect the structured and rendered reports, and `publish`. `no_qualifier` is valid only when at least one finalist completed required holdouts and failed the configured rule. A scoreless completed cohort is `no_finalist`, never `no_qualifier`.

The CLI uses exit code `4` for a validated terminal result without a qualifier. Treat that as an expected business outcome: inspect the emitted report, publish it, and continue or finalize the campaign as directed. Do not confuse it with an input or state-integrity failure.

For an attached campaign, publish and validate the cohort before asking the CLI for the next action. The CLI alone applies the configured cohort minimum, maximum, score-improvement signals, plateau patience, and immediate qualification stop. Free-text archetypes, new wording, and industry swaps do not count as progress or reset patience. A single regression consumes at most one no-progress step and cannot bypass the configured minimum or patience.

When continuation is allowed, pass new scouts only the CLI-generated sanitized gap brief: deficient factor names and sanitized missing-archetype labels. These labels are search guidance, not a progress signal. Never add candidate names, scores, score deltas, rankings, thresholds, selection rationale, holdout feedback, or learning-digest content. Stop only on qualification, validated plateau, configured cap, or a visible unrecoverable integrity failure.

Publication creates a compact `learning-digest.jsonl` row for every researched candidate from existing canonical evidence and hash-binds that digest from the history index. Inspect its coverage before treating publication as complete. Later candidate research may load only relevant non-quarantined rows after its cohort's fresh score-selected pool exists; scouts and screening evaluators never receive them. If direct evidence proves a published run violated a required boundary, use `quarantine-outcome` and retain it only as workflow-test evidence; never hand-edit or delete its original report or history row.
