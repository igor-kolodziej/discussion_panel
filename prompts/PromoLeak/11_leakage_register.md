# Leakage Register

## Objective
Produce the audit deliverable: a leakage register with evidence, financial impact, and practical recovery or prevention actions.

## Prerequisites
- Reconciliation model has produced candidate findings.
- Source evidence is linked.
- Findings are separated into hard findings and hypotheses.

## Inputs
- Reconciliation model.
- Evidence links.
- Audit scope.
- Brand/channel context.

## Instructions
1. Convert candidate findings into a clean leakage register.
2. Classify each finding as recoverable, preventable, informational, or needs more evidence.
3. Estimate PLN impact conservatively.
4. Link each finding to source evidence.
5. Draft dispute language only where evidence is strong.
6. Add renegotiation or stop-doing notes for preventable leakage.
7. Avoid claims and accounting-certification language.

## Subagents
- Use one subagent to challenge weak findings.
- Use one subagent to review dispute drafts for tone and evidence sufficiency.

## Output
Return:
- leakage register table
- executive summary
- evidence index
- dispute draft snippets
- renegotiation notes
- open questions
