# Landing Page
context: ideas/CONFIRMED_IDEA_20260511_113716.md
## Objective
Draft concise one-page site copy that explains PromoLeak as a document-backed revenue-recovery sprint for Polish challenger brands.

## Prerequisites
- Audit scope is defined.
- Pilot offer and pricing range are known.
- Confidentiality positioning is drafted or at least directionally agreed.

## Inputs
- Audit scope memo.
- Target customer definition.
- Pilot offer.
- Document request list if available (be realistic and do the proper research what documents will really be required)

## Instructions
1. Write for founders, CFOs, heads of sales, and finance leads.
2. Make the promise concrete: find recoverable or preventable leakage in one channel.
3. Mention document types without sounding like a generic accounting service.
4. Include confidentiality/NDA reassurance.
5. Include a simple call to action for a narrow pilot.
6. Avoid buzzwords, dashboards, AI hype, and broad consulting claims.

## Subagents
- Use one subagent to critique credibility and clarity.
- Use one subagent to flag phrases that sound like legal/accounting advice.
- Use subagents wherever needed to get better results.

## Output
Return landing-page copy with:
- headline
- subheadline
- who it is for
- pain section
- what is reviewed
- what is delivered
- confidentiality note
- pilot CTA
