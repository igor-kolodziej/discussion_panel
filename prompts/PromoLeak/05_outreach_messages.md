# Outreach Messages

## Objective
Write short outreach messages that ask qualified brands for a paid or NDA-backed document-access pilot.

## Prerequisites
- Audit scope is defined.
- Target buyer roles are selected.
- Landing-page copy or one-sentence positioning exists.

## Inputs
- Audit scope memo.
- Target customer profile.
- Pilot offer and pricing.
- Any sample leakage hypotheses by channel.

## Instructions
1. Write separate variants for founder, CFO/finance lead, and head of sales/commercial lead.
2. Keep each message short and specific.
3. Lead with recoverable or preventable leakage, not software.
4. Ask for one narrow next step: 20-minute call or NDA-backed sample review.
5. Include a respectful follow-up version.
6. Avoid exaggerated claims, fear tactics, and spam-like personalization.

## Subagents
- Use one subagent to critique spam risk and credibility.
- Use one subagent to sharpen the commercial pain and CTA.

## Output
Return:
- founder message
- CFO/finance message
- head-of-sales message
- first follow-up
- final follow-up
- subject-line options
