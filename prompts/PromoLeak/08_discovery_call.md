# Discovery Call

## Objective
Prepare and run a focused discovery call that qualifies pain, document access, and willingness to pay.

## Prerequisites
- Target brand is qualified.
- Outreach has generated a response.
- Audit scope and pilot offer are known.

## Inputs
- Target-list entry.
- Outreach thread.
- Audit scope.
- Any public information about the brand's channels.

## Instructions
1. Confirm channel exposure and current deduction/reconciliation process.
2. Ask about credit notes, remittances, promo settlements, retail-media charges, and marketplace/distributor deductions.
3. Identify who owns recovery decisions internally.
4. Ask whether real documents can be shared under NDA.
5. Ask directly whether a fixed audit fee or success fee is plausible.
6. End with a concrete next step.

## Subagents
- Use one subagent before important calls to research the brand and likely channels.
- Use one subagent after the call to critique whether the opportunity is real or just polite interest.

## Output
Return:
- call script
- qualification questions
- red flags
- next-step options
- post-call summary template
