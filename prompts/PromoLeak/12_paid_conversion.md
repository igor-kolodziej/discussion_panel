# Paid Conversion

## Objective
Convert an initial pilot or discovery process into paid work: fixed audit fee, success-fee recovery, or second-channel expansion.

## Prerequisites
- A discovery call, document pilot, or first findings review has happened.
- There is either evidence of leakage or a clear reason to continue.
- Buyer and decision owner are identified.

## Inputs
- Call notes.
- Initial findings or leakage register.
- Pricing assumptions: 4,000-8,000 PLN fixed audit or 15-25% success fee.
- Any buyer objections.

## Instructions
1. Choose the right ask: fixed fee, success fee, or expanded audit.
2. Tie the ask to evidence and expected value.
3. Keep the offer narrow: one retailer/channel or one second channel.
4. State what the buyer gets and what is out of scope.
5. Include a fallback if the buyer is interested but not ready.

## Subagents
- Use one subagent to critique pricing and value framing.
- Use one subagent to draft a sharper follow-up email if the buyer stalls.

## Output
Return:
- recommended paid offer
- buyer-facing follow-up email
- objection responses
- next-step script
- fallback offer
