# Target List

## Objective
Build a qualified list of 30 Polish challenger brands likely to suffer retailer, distributor, marketplace, promotion, or retail-media leakage.

## Prerequisites
- Audit scope is defined.
- Target categories are selected: beauty, supplements, household, and FMCG.
- Outreach will target decision makers, not generic inboxes where possible.

## Inputs
- Audit scope memo.
- Public sources: brand websites, retailer listings, Allegro/marketplace presence, LinkedIn, trade articles, company registries, and category rankings.
- Any founder network constraints or conflict-of-interest exclusions.

## Instructions
1. Find brands with visible drugstore, marketplace, distributor, or modern-trade exposure.
2. Prioritize brands likely in the 5-50M PLN revenue range or similar operational complexity.
3. Identify founder, CFO, head of sales, finance lead, or marketplace/commercial lead.
4. Add evidence that each brand fits the target profile.
5. Exclude brands that are too tiny, too enterprise, or conflict-sensitive.

## Subagents
- Use subagents in parallel by category: beauty, supplements, household/FMCG.
- Ask one subagent to check the final list for weak fits and duplicates.

## Output
Return a table with 30 targets:
- brand
- category
- likely channels
- fit evidence
- decision-maker name/title if found
- contact path
- priority score 1-5
- notes/risk
