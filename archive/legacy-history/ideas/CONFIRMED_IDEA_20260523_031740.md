# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot for per-event Polish municipal animal-care contractors.**

TraceFaktura is a mobile-first live event evidence rail for private contractors that bill Polish gminas for stray-animal pickup, transport, shelter intake/day, veterinary treatment, sterilization, quarantine, adoption, return, death documentation, or similar animal-level events. It creates invoice evidence at the operational moment, before month-end reconstruction, then turns those events into gmina-specific invoice attachment packets.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Polish animal-care contractors serving multiple gminas will pay for a mandatory live event-routing rail when it makes every future invoice line easier to defend, verify, and prepare than scattered WhatsApp photos, paper protocols, vet notes, chip numbers, and staff memory.

## Founder Fit

This fits a technical, part-time, low-status-sales founder because the first version can be built with mobile forms, structured storage, OCR/LLM extraction, audit timestamps, packet generation, and manual QA under 100,000 PLN. The founder does not need gmina procurement, a public-sector partnership, a shelter acquisition, a regulated registry, or factoring capital. The work is mostly data extraction, workflow design, field validation, and precise outbound from public BIP/procurement data.

The hard non-code task is securing one prepaid, mandatory all-event-routing pilot from a contractor with visible evidence/payment pain. That is realistic if the founder filters tightly for per-event multi-gmina contracts and rejects low-pain or lump-sum-only buyers.

## Customer And Pain

The first customer is a private animal-care contractor with active work for at least 2-3 gminas and case/event/line-item economics. These contractors may handle pickup, intake, transport, veterinary handoff, shelter stay, treatment, sterilization, quarantine, adoption/return, or death documentation.

The pain is not generic shelter management. It is that invoice evidence is often created too late, from fragmented operational artifacts:

- WhatsApp photos and messages.
- Paper pickup or handoff protocols.
- Vet notes and invoices.
- Chip/tag numbers or provisional animal IDs.
- Staff memory.
- Shelter or contractor spreadsheets.
- Gmina-specific attachment expectations.

The buyer pays only if prior gmina questions, delayed acceptance, audit/renewal anxiety, or month-end invoice-prep labor are concrete. If a contractor says documentation is merely annoying, they are not the first buyer.

## Product Wedge

TraceFaktura starts as a strict paid pilot, not broad SaaS.

Contractor staff, dispatchers, shelter workers, drivers, or cooperating vets capture every eligible billable animal event through a lightweight QR/mobile/WhatsApp-entry flow:

- animal ID or provisional ID;
- service type;
- date/time;
- location;
- photo/video;
- chip/tag when available;
- counterparty;
- handoff/protocol fields;
- vet or shelter document;
- responsible person.

TraceFaktura turns those events into invoice-line evidence objects, flags missing fields before month-end, and generates gmina-specific packet attachments. AI handles OCR, extraction, duplicate detection, missing-field checks, photo/document parsing, and Polish packet wording. The defensible asset is not AI text; it is the live event ledger plus outcome memory about what each gmina accepts.

## Hidden Insight Or Market Misunderstanding

The obvious view is that shelters need better software or that gminas need compliance dashboards. The hidden insight is narrower: the invoice becomes defensible or weak at the moment the animal event happens.

By month-end, the contractor can often reconstruct a plausible packet, but not a clean source-of-truth ledger. TraceFaktura moves upstream from report generation to event capture. That is the difference between selling paperwork and owning a source evidence rail.

The uncomfortable truth is that not all contractors will want this. It selects for honest, process-heavy contractors who want auditability, renewal defense, and less administrative drag. Contractors that benefit from ambiguity are bad customers.

## Underused Momentum

Polish gminas have statutory responsibilities for homeless-animal care and catching, and gmina programs cover shelter placement, sterilization/castration, owner search, blind-litter euthanasia, and 24/7 veterinary care for road incidents. Costs are borne by the gmina.

NIK's 2024 homeless-animal-care findings support the pressure backdrop: controlled gminas had serious organization and verification problems, and spending was concentrated around shelters and animal capture. GIW/Veterinary Inspection activity and shelter inspection materials show animal-care operations are an active public-interest monitoring area. KROPiK, the planned dog/cat identification registry, adds traceability momentum, but the idea should not depend on KROPiK rollout.

Underused capture layer: public BIP notices, Platforma Zakupowa tenders, gmina animal-care program documents, contract awards, and price schedules expose enough of the contractor landscape to build a targeted outbound list without privileged access.

## Beachhead Market

Narrow beachhead: private Polish municipal animal-care contractors that:

- serve at least 2-3 gminas;
- have per-event or animal-level billing exposure;
- perform recurring capture/intake/vet/shelter/sterilization documentation;
- have visible invoice-line complexity or evidence/payment friction;
- can mandate staff workflow for at least two named gminas.

The first wedge explicitly excludes mostly lump-sum readiness contracts, optional usage pilots, historical cleanup-only projects, and demo-only buyers.

## Primary Control Point

The primary control point is a **paid, signed, all-event routing mandate** inside one contractor's workflow for specific gminas.

The pilot contract requires all new eligible billable animal events for at least two named gminas to be captured through TraceFaktura first. That uniquely creates the source ledger before invoice preparation. Selective capture collapses the control point and fails the POC.

The second control-point proof is early gmina-format feedback. The contractor must send a one-page invoice attachment standard to existing gmina contacts asking whether the packet scope is sufficient for current invoice verification or what fields should be changed. This is not new procurement or a partnership; it is operational format acknowledgement inside an existing contract.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

Resource needed: one contractor's mandatory live billable-event workflow for at least two gminas, plus written gmina packet-format acknowledgement, correction, or usable feedback.

How to secure it:

1. Build a list of at least 30 qualified contractors from BIP notices, Platforma Zakupowa tenders, gmina animal-care programs, contract awards, price schedules, and contractor/shelter/vet pages.
2. Filter for per-event or animal-level contracts and contractors serving multiple gminas.
3. Send direct outbound around a precise pain: "I found your gmina contracts appear to bill animal-level events. I will build your next invoice packets from live pickup/intake/vet events instead of month-end reconstruction. Pilot only starts if you prepay and agree to all-event routing."
4. Require 3,000-5,000 PLN prepaid by day 10-14 and 6,000-15,000 PLN total pilot fee if all-event routing starts.
5. Reject pilots without all-event routing, gmina-format feedback, or real per-event economics.

Why an unknown founder can secure it:

The founder is not asking a gmina to buy software or integrate systems. The contractor already controls staff workflow and has existing gmina communication channels. Public procurement data identifies the first target list. A minimal product can be built cheaply, and manual QA is acceptable during the POC.

Proof before serious capital:

- prepaid pilot;
- signed all-event routing agreement for at least two named gminas;
- at least one gmina written acknowledgement, field correction, or usable packet-format feedback by day 14-21;
- 20 live events by day 30 and 40+ by day 60;
- 5 invoice-line evidence objects by day 30 and 10+ by day 60;
- packets sent to 2+ gminas by day 60.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

Pilot pricing:

- 3,000-5,000 PLN prepaid deposit by day 10-14.
- 6,000-15,000 PLN total for the 60-day pilot if all-event routing starts.

Post-POC pricing:

- 1,000-3,000 PLN/month per contractor.
- Optional per-event or per-invoice packet fees for higher-volume workflows.

Cost profile:

- MVP: mobile form/QR/WhatsApp-entry surface, storage, OCR/LLM extraction, packet generator, audit timestamps, basic access control, manual QA.
- Under 100,000 PLN for two months, especially if built with low-code tools and one contractor's workflow.

Value capture depends on measurable buyer value: invoice-prep time reduced 30-50%, fewer missing fields, fewer internal follow-ups, clearer renewal/audit evidence, fewer gmina clarification questions, or less risk of rejected/disputed lines.

### Distribution Moat

Initial distribution is precise outbound from public contract data, not content or status. The first wedge is strong because the outbound can reference the target's actual gmina contracts and likely documentation burden.

The moat is not the mobile form. The moat must become:

- accepted gmina packet rules;
- event histories;
- missing-field logic;
- staff habit;
- contractor renewal/audit evidence;
- proof that packets reduce questions or survive municipal invoice review;
- a proprietary map of what each gmina accepts for each animal-event type.

## Domination Path

Phase 1: Win one prepaid contractor pilot with mandatory all-event routing for at least two gminas.

Phase 2: Convert accepted/corrected packet formats into reusable gmina-specific evidence rules.

Phase 3: Use the first contractor's outcome memory to sell adjacent contractors: "we know which fields reduce questions in this gmina/service type."

Phase 4: Become the default evidence rail for per-event municipal animal-care contractors in one region.

Phase 5: Expand into adjacent municipal service evidence rails only after the animal-care workflow shows recurring value.

## Local Adaptation Defense

This is locally defended by Polish gmina duties, Polish BIP/procurement transparency, local invoice-attachment expectations, Polish-language administrative wording, and gmina-specific evidence habits. A foreign shelter-software vendor can copy forms, but it will not start with local gmina packet acceptance history, contractor-specific staff habits, or Polish public procurement targeting.

Local incumbents can still copy the surface. The defense must come from getting into the workflow first and accumulating accepted packet rules.

## Why This Could Compound

Each event teaches which fields are missing, which event types create disputes, which packet formats get accepted, which gminas ask follow-up questions, and which contractor workflows produce clean evidence.

The compounding niche is narrow but real:

- operational event data;
- invoice-line conversion rules;
- gmina-specific packet memory;
- contractor staff workflow habits;
- renewal/audit evidence examples.

Broad competitors can build mobile forms. They cannot instantly recreate repeated accepted evidence outcomes across contractors and gminas if TraceFaktura captures the first live ledgers.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or radically narrow if:

- fewer than 30 qualified contractors can be found from public sources by day 7;
- by day 14, fewer than 3 contractors confirm concrete evidence/payment/admin pain;
- no contractor prepays 3,000-5,000 PLN by day 14;
- the pilot does not name at least two gminas and require all-event routing;
- usage is optional, partial, demo-only, historical cleanup, or founder-entered;
- no gmina provides written acknowledgement, field correction, or usable packet-format feedback by day 21;
- by day 30, fewer than 20 live eligible events are captured;
- fewer than 80% of events are captured at the operational moment rather than reconstructed later;
- fewer than 3 non-founder users use the system;
- capture time exceeds 90 seconds median for normal events;
- fewer than 5 invoice-line evidence objects can be generated by day 30;
- no missing-field or invoice-prep improvement is measurable;
- the only perceived benefit is "nice documentation."

Hard exclusions: lump-sum-only contracts, optional staff usage, generic reporting, demo data, public-sector procurement dependency, and contractors unwilling to route all events.
