# Zero-to-One Evaluation: GymSignal Media Rail v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

The paid proof remains reachable, but the business is an outbiddable vertical DOOH operator whose scarce input is advertiser demand rather than venue access. The panel adds credible incumbent evidence and shows that the proposed conservative site yield/fill/margin combination is a success case.

## Evidence and Assumptions

DOOH demand and gym supply exist; actual independent-gym inventory gaps, advertiser renewal, fill, attribution, and site economics remain hypotheses.

## 16-Area Scorecard

The prior 78/100 score overvalued defensibility, distribution, durability, and wealth. Revised area scores: core insight 7; pain/WTP 6; economics 5; domination 6; beachhead 7; expansion 7; defensibility 4; local advantage 6; advantage 5; timing 7; distribution 4; durability 4; founder fit 5; test quality 9; wealth 4; reachability 9.

## Overall Score

**61/100 advisory after panel.**

## Control Point

**Tier 3 company-level; conditional site-level contract control.** Strongest copy risk is incumbent outbidding with an existing sales force.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The 300-site, 3,000 PLN yield, 22% margin, 75% ownership, 3× case has insufficient downside cushion and an overstated denominator.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** One campaign/three sites fit the 180-day/28,000 PLN constraint.

## Expansion Check

Expansion magnifies the two-sided sales and field-operations burden before it creates durable network effects.

## Fatal Objections

Outbiddable rights, incumbent demand distribution, and a failed conservative wealth case.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Own context-specific inventory and sell economic conversion, not sensing.

## Recommendation

kill

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ScopeBand v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

The behavioural timing insight and paid proof survive, but both judges agree the business remains a feature until crew habit, routing, and data history are proven. The broad denominator and high-margin 1,200-customer case understate fragmented-SMB acquisition/support and client/crew friction.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Change-order workflows exist; incremental verbal leakage, one-button lift, client payment, retention, and switching cost remain hypotheses.

## 16-Area Scorecard

The prior 78/100 score overvalued defensibility, durability, distribution, and wealth. Revised area scores: insight 8; pain 7; economics 7; domination 6; beachhead 8; expansion 7; defensibility 4; local 7; advantage 6; timing 8; distribution 6; durability 4; founder fit 9; test 9; wealth 5; reachability 10.

## Overall Score

**68/100 advisory after panel.**

## Control Point

**Tier 3 with a possible Tier-2 path.** Strongest copy risk is bundling by an installed Polish CRM/accounting/payment provider.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail after panel stress.** The conservative case only narrowly clears 5m and does not price qualified-market shrinkage, SMB CAC/churn, onboarding/support, or transaction-fee compression.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Three pilots and routed deposits remain highly credible within 20,000 PLN.

## Expansion Check

The niche is good, but CEE expansion does not overcome the bundled-feature problem.

## Fatal Objections

No hard routing authority, easily bundled components, and brittle founder-wealth economics.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Capture a verbal scope change at the moment of request and tie price to recovered cash.

## Recommendation

kill

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CharacterContinuity Rail v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

Casting creates real continuity value, but the intermediary is least valuable before casting—when it must win—and can be bypassed afterward through direct performer/studio rights. The reachable indie buyer may not produce enough recurring lines to support the annual price, while larger buyers bring stronger procurement and existing audio partners.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Rights contracts and market infrastructure exist; willingness to pay, exclusivity, renewals, second updates, vendor portability, and margins remain hypotheses.

## 16-Area Scorecard

The prior 75/100 score overvalued WTP, control, durability, and wealth. Revised area scores: insight 8; pain 5; economics 5; domination 5; beachhead 6; expansion 7; defensibility 4; local 7; advantage 5; timing 8; distribution 4; durability 4; founder fit 7; test 8; wealth 4; reachability 8.

## Overall Score

**58/100 advisory after panel.**

## Control Point

**Tier 3 before a cast contract; conditional Tier 2 during a term.** Strongest copy risk is direct studio–actor contracting or a localisation/voice platform bundling the workflow.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** Two hundred active characters at the stated price/margin is not conservative once update frequency, legal/QA labour, renewal and direct bypass are stressed.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** One setup remains achievable within 180 days/32,000 PLN.

## Expansion Check

More languages and studios increase legal/audio operations faster than rights scarcity.

## Fatal Objections

Buyer price mismatch, temporary/non-scarce options, direct bypass, and brittle wealth economics.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Character-specific continuity becomes valuable after casting.

## Recommendation

kill
