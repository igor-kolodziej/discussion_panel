# Zero-to-One Candidate: ScopeBand

**Canonical business specification: v1, frozen 2026-07-15 Europe/Warsaw for independent simulation.**

Wave-2 candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

ScopeBand gives small renovation and specialist contractors a one-press wearable capture at the exact moment a client requests extra work, then turns the spoken request into a priced, signed, deposited change order before labour or materials leak outside the contract.

## Founder Fit

The founder has AI/ML, analytics, product, and direct family exposure to installation/contractor workflows. Family activity supplies discovery and testing only; independent contractors must provide paid proof. Off-the-shelf Bluetooth buttons/earbuds/phones avoid custom hardware before demand.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is an owner-operated bathroom, kitchen, electrical, HVAC, premium furniture, or fit-out contractor with 2–20 field staff. Clients request “small” changes verbally while work is underway. The crew agrees, details blur, materials/labour are spent, and the contractor later absorbs the cost or argues during invoicing. Current apps require stopping work, opening a project, typing, pricing, and chasing a signature; crews instead use chat, notes, or memory.

## Product Wedge

- Contractor signs an all-out-of-scope routing rule for the pilot and equips one crew with a press-to-record button plus phone/earbud.
- On request, the worker presses once and records a short confirmation with client/site context; ScopeBand drafts scope, exclusions, price from the contractor's catalogue, and schedule impact.
- Contractor approves the draft; client receives a Polish mobile page to accept, e-sign, and pay a material deposit through a licensed PSP before work proceeds.
- ScopeBand stores the evidence/approval/payment reference and later links it to final invoice/dispute outcome.
- Pilot price: 2,500 PLN for four weeks plus 2% of routed deposits; no complete CRM replacement.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Change-order software starts after somebody decides to document the change. The actual leak is behavioural: a verbal request occurs while hands are occupied and social pressure favours “yes.” A one-button capture at the moment of agreement can recover revenue that later admin cannot reconstruct. Pricing as a share of deposited changes aligns the product with recovered cash.

## Underused Momentum

Poland has 346,200 construction microenterprises, while mobile payments, speech models, e-signature, and inexpensive wearable controls make field capture cheap. US construction platforms validate change-order approvals/payments but are broader, more expensive, and not designed around Polish verbal microchanges.

## Beachhead Market

Warsaw/Mazowieckie owner-operated renovation/electrical/bathroom/kitchen/fit-out contractors with at least 20 active jobs/year, average project value above 30,000 PLN, and recurring client-requested changes. Exclude large general contractors with enterprise project systems and informal firms unwilling to invoice changes.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Pilot: 2,500 PLN/four weeks/one crew plus 2% of routed deposits.
- Recurring: 400–800 PLN per contractor/month plus 3% of routed change deposits, capped/volume-priced for larger customers.
- Licensed PSP handles funds; ScopeBand invoices its fee and never holds client deposits.
- Scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 80%; operating margin 30%–35% after speech processing, support, payment reconciliation, sales, and hardware replacement.
- Contractor value proof: documented additional gross profit and faster deposit collection at least 5× the subscription/transaction fee, net of changes that would have been documented anyway.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build 120 named Warsaw prospects from CEIDG/public websites, Google, Oferteo, trade groups, and supplier directories; qualify project type and crew count by phone.
- Send a fixed “recover your next verbal change” offer with a 90-second demo and 2,500 PLN pilot, not a free custom build.
- Conversion event: paid pilot, all-out-of-scope routing clause, one crew onboarded, client message template, price catalogue, and first signed/deposited change.
- First ten: direct founder outreach and contractor referrals; family company is not counted as paid proof.
- First 100: publish anonymised recovered-value case studies, integrate Polish invoicing/accounting exports, and offer material wholesalers a referral fee only after direct channel proof; no supplier partnership is assumed.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed compounding Tier 2:** contractually routed all-out-of-scope events, signed scope evidence, deposit references, price catalogue, and change-to-dispute/outcome history. The button and AI draft are copyable. Switching cost must come from crew habit, client evidence, templates, payment history, and benchmarking that increase captured value.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Needed resource: one crew's real change requests and deposit flow.

1. Use family context for 20 unpaid workflow observations and a safe prototype.
2. Buy ten commodity buttons/earbuds for under 3,000 PLN; use phones and manual drafting.
3. Sell three independent 2,500 PLN pilots from 120 named prospects.
4. Route 20 signed changes and at least five deposits; compare capture to prior month/baseline.
5. Within 12 months/100,000 PLN prove 20 paying contractors, 500 routed changes, 80%+ eligible-event adherence, repeat renewals, and positive contribution.

An unknown founder can reach fragmented contractors directly, and the buyer can test one crew without replacing its CRM/accounting or making a speculative partnership.

## Distribution Advantage

Recovered cash creates concrete case studies and referrals among trades. One price/template library serves similar jobs. Later wholesaler/accountant referrals can lower CAC, but the company retains direct billing, routing contract, and event history.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Dominate verbal microchange capture for Polish renovation/specialist crews, then expand to CEE languages and adjacent field services. Add pricing suggestions, schedule impact, dispute prevention, and supplier-material ordering only after event density. Broad CRMs can add voice capture; ScopeBand must build faster crew habit, Polish payment/invoice workflow, and outcome benchmarks.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish microcontractors are numerous and underserved by US enterprise tools; language, invoicing, consumer-contract, and payment localisation matter. The founder has genuine workflow access. The defence is local event density and routing, not the Polish market alone.

## Compounding Advantage

Each event links noisy field speech, client intent, scope language, contractor edit, price, signature, deposit, project, invoice, and dispute outcome. This improves speech-to-contract models, price suggestions, missing-scope prompts, and crew HCI. Ordinary delivery builds real-time voice HCI, behavioural/transaction data, wearable interaction expertise, proprietary models, contractor distribution, and cash flow.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment and cap distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds local speech accuracy, buttons/support, payments/invoicing, trade acquisition, and event/outcome models.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–21: 20 workflow observations/interviews and legal/payment review.
- Days 22–45: one-button manual prototype, scope templates, and 120-account list.
- Days 46–100: five deposits/proposals and three paid 2,500 PLN pilots.
- Days 101–180: 20 signed changes, five deposits, positive contribution, and one renewal/second independent order with evidence of previously unbilled value.

Founder remains employed/five hours daily. Paid proof is contractor cash and routed deposit, not app usage.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 20,000 PLN: legal/payment 4k; devices 3k; prototype/tools 6k; outreach/travel 3k; contingency 4k.
- Up to 55,000 PLN after three paid pilots and successful contribution-positive delivery.
- Up to 100,000 PLN after 20 payers or repeatable acquisition/renewal.
- Family capital excluded until repeat demand and economics.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Test | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Contractors pay/document changes | Three paid pilots | Kill after 120 qualified prospects/no three payers |
| One-button changes behaviour | Eligible-event adherence and baseline | Kill wearable wedge if phone/text is equally used |
| Clients sign/pay | 20 signatures/five deposits | Narrow terms or kill if social friction blocks use |
| Routed value supports fee | Fully loaded recovered value | Kill if changes are too small/infrequent |
| Copy/durability | Renewal and 80% routing | Must compound workflow/history; do not claim button moat |
| Legal/payment | Counsel/PSP | No fund custody; compliant consumer change terms |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after 50+ independent paying contractors, renewals, positive contribution after support/refunds, a repeatable channel, at least 500 monthly routed events, operations not founder-bound, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Devices are reusable/resalable. Retain lawful speech-to-scope tooling, templates, contractor research, and derived annotations; delete customer/site data per contract. A generic contractor CRM is not the fallback.

## Critical Evidence

The contractor denominator is large and incumbent products validate the change-order/payment workflow. No source proves verbal leakage magnitude or one-button lift in Poland.

## Critical Hypotheses

Verbal leakage is economically meaningful; one-button capture improves adherence; clients accept mobile signature/deposit; average routed value supports fees; 1,200 contractors can be acquired while preserving 30% operating margin.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than three paid pilots from 120 qualified contractors within 180 days.
- Fewer than 20 signed changes/five deposits or no evidence of incremental recovered value.
- Eligible-event capture below 70% or crews revert to chat/phone.
- Fully loaded gross margin below 60%, support above 25% of revenue, or CAC above first-year gross profit.
- No renewal/all-event routing, showing it is a disposable feature.
- A Polish CRM copies the workflow and contractors switch without losing material value.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

20,000 PLN pre-proof; 100,000 PLN first year. Founder product/ML/sales; part-time engineer after payment; fixed-fee legal/payment review; no custom hardware manufacturing.
