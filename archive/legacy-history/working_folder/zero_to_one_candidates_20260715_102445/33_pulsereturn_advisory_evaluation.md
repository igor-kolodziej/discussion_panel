# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PulseReturn Rail v1

Applied with `$evaluate-zero-to-one` after pivot research. The company is at idea/planning/discovery stage; no batch, mandate, inventory, data, or operating proof is assumed. Advisory only.

## Diagnosis

PulseReturn moves the technical insight into an already-budgeted warranty/return event and proposes a genuinely contractable control point: paid routed devices, custody, tools/spares, disposition authority, and derivative rights. Its small-wearable niche may be neglected by mature lifecycle operators, and a paid batch can test value before a depot or inventory commitment. The hard uncertainty is the intersection of independent-brand EU volume, inadequate current service, willingness to trust an unknown depot, and sufficient routed units for 400,000 PLN ACV. Unlike the standalone fixture, that uncertainty can be falsified directly through 100-account qualification, paid batches, and routing conversion.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Evidence: consumer repair/replacement is an existing economic event; EU circularity pressure is increasing; B2X, MTTC, and Metarvo prove lifecycle/recovery budgets and strong competition.
- Hypotheses: at least 30 qualified independent brands each support about 1,500 annual routed returns/equivalent ACV; one prepays 15,000 PLN; a batch finds >15,000 PLN value; two brands grant routing; gross/operating margins reach 55%/25%.
- No current customer, batch, mandate, brand tool access, failure corpus, or margin proof exists.

## Overall Score

Weighted points: 154 / 20; overall **77/100**.

## Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1 after a signed mandate; Tier 3/unproved today.** A paid defined return stream, physical custody, disposition authority, prepaid minimum, consigned tools/spares, and derivative rights directly control workflow/inventory. Strongest copy risk: B2X/MTTC/another depot or the brand itself adds model-specific PPG diagnostics and uses existing logistics trust. Pool density and cross-brand history must make the narrow cell better before incumbents enter.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Provisional pass.** Conservative arithmetic is coherent: 30 × 400,000 PLN = 12m revenue; 25% operating = 3m; 3× = 9m; 75% founder = 6.75m, with 95% reinvestment and distributions ≤5% during employment. Thirty brands each shipping roughly 30,000 units at a hypothesised 5% return rate is not facially heroic globally, but the qualifying intersection and 400k ACV have no source. The explicit 100-account/40-qualification test must fail the gate if fewer than 30 are plausible.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A brand can pay for a bounded existing batch before routing, inventory, consumer refunds, permanent facilities, or a full diagnostic platform. The 35,000 PLN cap, contract technician, five-hour day, and second-stage routing test fit the constraints. Brand trust is the main risk, not an unspecified partnership.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is narrow enough; adjacent wearables share custody/disposition/recommerce. Large operators already possess infrastructure and can enter through ordinary process changes, so domination depends on being uneconomic for them to specialise early and on faster evidence/recovery.

## Fatal Objections

None identified at discovery-stage pre-screen. Decisive caveats are the qualified-brand denominator, current-depot comparison, batch-to-routing conversion, sealed-device recoverability, fully loaded margin, and incumbent response.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Make the brand's recurring return/replacement event pay for rights-clean intelligent-wearable failure intelligence and physical workflow control.

## Recommendation

**pursue**

The exact specification is eligible for independent simulation because both hard gates pass provisionally and the missing evidence can be tested before serious capital.
