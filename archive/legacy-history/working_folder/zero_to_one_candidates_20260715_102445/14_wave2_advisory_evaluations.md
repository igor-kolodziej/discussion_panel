# Wave 2 Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluations

Discovery-stage disclosure for every evaluation: no pilot, contract, proprietary dataset, or control proof is assumed. The evaluator judges whether the proposed path can produce missing proof quickly and affordably, while penalising unsupported demand, control, economics, and dependencies. Advisory scores are not validation scores.

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: GymSignal Media Rail

## Diagnosis

GymSignal has the strongest hard-resource structure in wave two: low-cost exclusive venue inventory is directly contractable, advertiser cash can precede hardware scale, and the attention/offer dataset is created by the paid campaign. The two-sided cold start is real, but the first three venues and one advertiser are small enough to assemble sequentially. Its main weakness is not technology but sales yield—3,000 PLN per site/month and advertiser renewal must be proven before a 300-site wealth case is credible.

## Evidence and Assumptions

IAB establishes DOOH momentum, EuropeActive the venue denominator, and Quividi/Vistar technical and competitive feasibility. Venue exclusivity price, advertiser WTP, conversion lift, renewal, site yield, and margins are hypotheses. The plan correctly refuses to scale installations before prepaid demand.

## Overall Score

## Control Point

**Tier 1 once venues sign prepaid/guaranteed exclusivity and options.** Strongest copy risk: a DOOH network outbids GymSignal for venues after proof. Catch-up requires many site contracts, installations, and advertiser history rather than one equipment purchase, but renewal economics are decisive.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass.** All scenarios state sites, price, revenue, margins, capital, ownership, reinvestment, value, and timing. The conservative 300-site case reaches 5.346m PLN founder equity at 3× operating profit/75% ownership without double-counting. The thin cushion makes real site yield and sales cost kill variables.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Conditional venue contracts plus one 6,000–10,000 PLN prepaid campaign are achievable within 180 days and 28,000 PLN while employed; installations are staged after cash.

## Expansion Check

The Warsaw independent-gym beachhead is small enough to control and has national/adjacent-venue expansion. Quividi/Vistar provide technology or demand competition but do not automatically own the fragmented inventory. Poland helps supply acquisition, not legal protection.

## Fatal Objections

None identified at planning stage. The non-fatal but decisive caveats are two-sided sales, low local advertiser budgets, venue churn, and attention becoming a vanity metric without offer conversion.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Own exclusive context-specific attention inventory and advertiser transactions; treat sensing as replaceable measurement.

## Recommendation

pursue

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CharacterContinuity Rail

## Diagnosis

CharacterContinuity converts generic voice supply into a more defensible moment: once a studio casts a performer-model as a recurring character, continuity, usage history, and two contracts create switching cost. The proof path is capital-light and locally reachable, but legal durability and direct contracting are serious: a studio may prefer to own the model/rights, and actors may refuse useful exclusivity or retain termination rights incompatible with continuity. The company must prove a second paid update, not merely a first voice pack.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Studio denominators, disclosure requirements, contract feasibility, and marketplace competition are sourced. Actor option acceptance, studio price, recurring updates, rights durability, vendor portability, gross margin, and 200-character scale are hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**75/100.** Weighted sum 150; `10 × (150 / 20) = 75`.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 per cast character if both contracts are enforceable; Tier 3 for uncased actor options.** Strongest copy risk: ElevenLabs or the studio signs the actor directly and owns the same model/workflow. The defence is already-cast exclusivity plus direction/approval history, not actor supply.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, sensitive.** The tables are complete and the conservative 200-character case reaches 5.4m PLN founder equity. It remains credible only if second updates make revenue recurring and legal/QA delivery does not scale one-for-one.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Ten bounded options and one 15,000–25,000 PLN paid setup can be pursued inside 180 days/32,000 PLN without building a model or securing AAA access.

## Expansion Check

The local studio/talent cluster is useful; EU/global studios provide denominator. Generic marketplaces and direct studio contracting are strong entrants. Expansion must follow active character continuity, not a broad voice marketplace.

## Fatal Objections

None identified at planning stage, provided contracts permit a reliable studio term and the first three characters are killed without second paid updates.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Create rights/control at the moment a synthetic performer is cast into a continuing commercial character.

## Recommendation

pursue

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: WorkScore Passport

The portability right creates unusual timing and a clear future-lab dataset, while a manual export-based membership is directly testable. The business remains a price-sensitive B2C subscription with non-exclusive legal access, incident-driven value, high trust/security needs, and no proven economical channel to 20,000 retained members. The conservative wealth case clears 5m by only about 54,000 PLN, so small CAC/support/retention deviations break the independent gate.

The Directive and worker denominator are sourced. Worker pre-crisis WTP, national/API implementation, retention, incident value, legal boundaries, referral CAC, and margin are hypotheses.

**72/100.** Weighted sum 143.5; `10 × (143.5 / 20) = 71.75`, rounded to 72.

## Control Point

# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ScopeBand

## Diagnosis

ScopeBand has the clearest direct economic event in wave two: a verbal request becomes signed/deposited recovered revenue, and the founder can test the behaviour with commodity hardware before building software. The Polish denominator and founder trade access make acquisition credible, while one-crew pilots minimise trust and capital. Its vulnerability is copy: the button/voice draft is a feature that Buildertrend, BuildBook, or a Polish contractor app can add; durability must come from all-event routing, habit, payment evidence, and outcome/pricing history.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Statistics Poland supports the broad denominator; incumbent products validate change-order/payment workflow. Verbal leakage, one-button lift, client acceptance, routed value, annual retention, and 30% margin are hypotheses. The proof path measures each within real paid jobs.

Crew habit, signed evidence, templates, and pricing/outcome history must make switching materially costly.

## Founder Wealth Gate

## Reachability Gate

## Fatal Objections

None identified at planning stage. The strongest caveat is that the wearable may not improve capture over a simple phone/WhatsApp workflow, eliminating differentiation and compounding data.

Capture and price the verbal scope change before extra work begins, then tie value to the deposited transaction.
