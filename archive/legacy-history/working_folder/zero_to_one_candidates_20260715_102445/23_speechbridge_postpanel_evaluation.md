# Zero-to-One Evaluation: SpeechBridge Workline v1 After Independent Panel

Applied with `$evaluate-zero-to-one` to the exact frozen canonical candidate and the complete panel objections. This is an advisory diagnosis, not validation.

## Diagnosis

SpeechBridge identifies a severe, specific moment of failure and proposes one of the run's cheapest credible paid experiments. The panel does not invalidate that user need; it exposes a company-shape failure: the direct subscription must assemble fragmented trust and distribution while the likely copiers already control call identity, audio routing, accessibility interfaces, and installed users. The proposed longitudinal data could improve one member's experience, but portability, deletion, within-person specificity, and the need for human support weaken cross-user compounding. The 4,000-member conservative case is therefore not yet a credible Founder Wealth Gate path.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Evidence: Voiceitt, Google Project Relate, and Apple Personal Voice demonstrate non-standard-speech demand, technical difficulty, and platform substitution.
- Candidate hypothesis: at least three of 30 offers prepay, three renew, and repeated personal history improves call outcomes.
- Candidate hypothesis: 4,000 members sustain 249 PLN/month, 70% gross margin, 25% operating margin, and acceptable global acquisition economics.
- External verdicts: independent simulated judges scored 62 and 74. Both independently identified incumbent audio/distribution control, unproved acquisition to 4,000 members, and support intensity. These verdicts are judgment, not market evidence, but the shared objections are structurally credible.
- Missing: bottom-up paying denominator, channel conversion, real telephony performance, renewal, fully loaded support, and longitudinal lift.

## Overall Score

Weighted points: 126.5 across 20 total weight; `10 × (126.5 / 20) = 63.25`, rounded to **63/100**.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 today; proposed Tier 2 only after proof.** The strongest copy risk is an OS, meeting platform, carrier, or specialist accessibility vendor combining personal recognition, captions, restatement, and trusted call identity. The individual history can create useful switching friction but is consent-dependent, portable/deletable, and may not create a strong aggregate model advantage.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The scenario arithmetic and reinvestment treatment are internally consistent, but 4,000 global paying members at 249 PLN/month with 25% operating margin is not a conservative path until a bottom-up denominator, repeatable channel, retention, and bounded support are credible. Modest margin or ownership deterioration consumes the buffer above 5m PLN. The strong pain does not compensate for this independent gate.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A five-customer prepaid programme, browser-based manual delivery, 25,000 PLN pre-proof risk, direct acquisition, and renewal/referral tests fit 180 days, five hours daily, and continued employment. No clinic, insurer, carrier integration, or complete product is required before payment.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is clear, but expansion deepens exposure to incumbent call surfaces and employer procurement. Polish/EU privacy/localisation is useful execution, not exclusion. A direct subscription can discover the need but does not yet show a path to retain profits when platforms bundle components.

## Fatal Objections

1. The same platforms that can copy the feature already own distribution, identity, audio routing, and trust.
2. The 4,000-member wealth path requires an unproved global acquisition/retention engine and is mislabeled conservative.
3. Highest-value calls may retain enough human support to prevent software margins.
4. The proposed data asset may improve one user but has limited cross-user exclusivity and must remain portable/deletable.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Non-standard speech creates measurable failure during economically important voice interactions; user-controlled intervention and task-completion outcomes can be tested cheaply, and repeated release/call outcomes can generate valuable speech-to-intent intelligence.

## Recommendation

**pivot**

The pivot must change buyer, budget, acquisition channel, and control event. It must not repeat the direct-to-individual global subscription or depend on a separate Workline number outrunning integrated platforms.
