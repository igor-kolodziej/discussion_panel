# Zero-to-One Evaluation: TiltLock v1 After Panel Objections

This is an advisory evaluation of the exact frozen candidate and complete Round-1 panel objections. It is not a simulated or real validation score and does not change the canonical specification.

## Diagnosis

The panel evidence confirms that TiltLock is an excellent paid-discovery experiment but a weak company shape. Its core behavioural insight survives, yet the buyer most affected by the painful event does not control—or reliably want—hard enforcement, while trackers and operators own stronger telemetry and distribution. Physiology is a hypothesis layered on top of this misalignment; if it adds little, the product becomes a bypassable blocker with ordinary community acquisition.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced evidence establishes hand-history access, platform prohibitions, existing alerts, and adjacent products. The two independent panel verdicts are judgments, not market evidence, but their agreement identifies a structural dependency the original advisory review underweighted. WTP, adverse-session persistence, incremental physiological lift, retention, platform permission, support cost, and CAC all remain hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**58/100 advisory.** Weighted sum 115.25; `10 × (115.25 / 20) = 57.625`, rounded to 58.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** Strongest copy risk: a tracker or operator adds the same user rules with better telemetry, existing trust, installed distribution, and—in the operator case—non-bypassable account controls.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail after panel stress.** The tables are complete, but the conservative case depends on 6,000 globally acquired and retained users at consumer-subscription margins before the design shows why the target user will keep restrictions enabled. The arithmetic cannot compensate for distribution and retention assumptions.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Ten cohort sales under 24,000 PLN and 180 days remain plausible alongside employment. Reachability does not offset the independent wealth-gate failure.

## Expansion Check

Poker is a coherent experiment, but operators and trackers can enter from stronger positions. Trading and esports do not inherit the same customer relationship or policy environment. The local advantage is insufficient.

## Fatal Objections

1. Buyer/enforcer misalignment: the user can reject the product precisely during the costly event.
2. Incumbents control stronger enforcement and installed distribution.
3. Physiology may not add meaningful predictive or economic value.
4. The conservative wealth scenario depends on substantial global consumer-subscription success.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Precommitted interventions become valuable when state impairs judgment; the economically aligned buyer should control or benefit from the enforced workflow.
