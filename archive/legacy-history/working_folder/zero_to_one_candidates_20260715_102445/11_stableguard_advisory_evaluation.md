# Zero-to-One Evaluation: StableGuard Bankroll Control Rail v1

Advisory evaluation only. The company is at idea/planning/discovery stage; absent pilots and contracts are judged through the proposed proof path, while unsupported denominator, price, control, and retention assumptions remain penalised.

## Diagnosis

StableGuard materially fixes TiltLock's buyer/enforcer mismatch: the stable has capital at risk and can condition future backing on a review. The pivot also removes physiology as an existential dependency and keeps proof below the time/capital cap. It still lacks a credible wealth denominator and asymmetric control: the public record shows several stables and one large claimed player base, but not 50–150 qualified buyers at the proposed per-seat price, while the stable can preserve final authority and recreate the workflow internally.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Company sources establish bankroll provision, coaching, monitoring, applications, hand-history review, and off-platform backing. They do not establish audited stable counts, average active seats, breach losses, software budgets, renewal, data rights, or the 2,000 PLN annual seat fee. The paid pilot is a valid discovery test; it cannot make the unsupported conservative denominator or switching cost credible before that evidence exists.

## Overall Score

**63/100.** Weighted sum 125.5; `10 × (125.5 / 20) = 62.75`, rounded to 63.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 before contract; conditional Tier 2 after a paid MSA routes a real funding decision.** Strongest copy risk: a stable adds the rule checklist and review to its existing coach/ledger workflow after seeing the pilot, retaining all players, funds, and data. The provider does not own a Tier-1 transaction because the stable keeps custody and final decision authority.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The scenario table is complete and mathematically clears 5m PLN, but the supposed conservative case requires 4,000 active seats across 50 stables at 2,000 PLN annually, 30% operating margin, and 80% founder ownership. Current research neither establishes that buyer/seat denominator nor that small opaque stables support this software spend; the case is an expected-success hypothesis, not a conservative wealth path.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** One 15,000–25,000 PLN pilot, ten users, and a routed review decision can be attempted within 180 days and 35,000 PLN alongside employment without an operator partnership or full product.

## Expansion Check

The niche is coherent, but platform operators, trackers, and each stable can enter from stronger relationships. The cross-stable benchmark is useful only after many buyers grant rights. Prop trading and esports do not inherit privileged distribution.

## Fatal Objections

1. The reachable, budgeted buyer denominator is not established and may be too small for the conservative wealth case.
2. The stable owns bankroll, players, contract authority, and source data and can internalise the workflow.
3. The same poker trust/access problem remains, now concentrated in opaque business buyers rather than consumers.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Sell state-dependent intervention to an economically aligned party that controls a real consequence, not to the impaired individual.

## Recommendation

kill

This pivot is discarded. Revisiting poker would require independently evidenced distribution or a different hard control point, not another player/stable workflow variant.
