# Zero-to-One Candidate: NeuroPort Uptime Library

Wave-4 candidate generation only — not validated. Research-use only. All commercial numbers are hypotheses unless cited.

## One-Sentence Thesis

NeuroPort Uptime Library gives small European EEG/EMG/human-factors labs a paid annual pool of tested original spares, passive adapters, trigger cables, loaner interconnects, and configuration records so a failed or changed signal chain does not cancel participant sessions.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build compatibility records, test automation, inventory prediction, secure configuration, and publication-based acquisition. A contract electronics/test engineer and neuroscience technician verify research-only interconnects. The company excludes clinical use, active-electrode conversion, medical repair, calibration claims requiring accreditation, and manufacturer-authorised claims.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is a university, corporate human-factors, BCI, or wearable-research lab with expensive amplifiers and scheduled participants. A failed trigger cable, battery, optical link, passive adapter, or discontinued connector can cancel sessions and delay a grant. Labs hold random spares, borrow internally, wait for distributors/manufacturers, or improvise cables.

## Product Wedge

- 7,500 PLN paid Signal-Chain Continuity Audit: serial/configuration map, passive-interconnect/trigger test, critical-spare plan, and one reserved original or validated passive loaner.
- Annual membership 24,000–60,000 PLN/lab: serialised reserve allocation, quarterly remote integrity check, 48–72h EU shipment of covered loaners/spares, configuration record, and two pre-study signal-chain checks.
- Parts are manufacturer-original or explicitly passive research adapters tested against published electrical requirements. No unauthorised active-electrode conversion or subject-safety claim.
- Paid reservation precedes scarce inventory purchase. Customer data stay isolated; NeuroPort owns its compatibility/test protocols, inventory condition history, and permitted de-identified failure taxonomy.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Neurophysiology vendors sell high-quality equipment and accessories, but a small lab's failure cost is the missed participant/session, not the part. Manufacturers optimise their own ecosystem; a shared multi-vendor reserve and configuration layer can serve labs too small to stock every fragile interconnect. The membership wins only if avoided cancellations and faster study changes exceed the fee.

## Underused Momentum

BioSemi's connector transition creates a visible planning event, and major amplifier families support thousands of publications and cross-system adapters. More multimodal studies combine EEG, EMG, eye tracking, motion, and triggers, increasing interconnect complexity. Official upgrade routes and existing adapters are strong substitutes, not proof of independent demand.

## Beachhead Market

Polish/CEE research labs using non-medical Brain Products/BioSemi-class systems plus at least one peripheral modality, running scheduled human sessions, and lacking a documented spare/loaner plan. Begin with passive trigger/interconnect/battery/optical accessories; exclude clinical and MR-room adaptations.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Audit 7,500 PLN; annual membership 24,000–60,000 PLN; replacement parts at disclosed margin; urgent uncovered work quoted separately.
- Shared inventory target 55%–65% gross margin after depreciation, losses, engineering, shipping, insurance, and support; 25%–30% operating margin at scale.
- Pool parts across compatible labs and purchase against reservations. If every lab requires unique inventory or continuous engineering, economics fail.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Mine recent public methods sections and lab equipment pages for exact amplifier/accessory configurations; build 100 named PIs/lab managers/technicians.
- Send a model-specific continuity checklist and fixed 7,500 PLN audit, referencing an official connector/support change only where applicable.
- Conversion is payment, current-device proof, signed research-only scope, reserve allocation, and one integrity test.
- First ten direct; no manufacturer/distributor partnership. First 100 through publication-specific outreach, lab-manager referrals, compatible-pool density, and paid distributor referrals only after direct proof.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1 physical/contract control plus compounding Tier 2 workflow:** owned, tested, serialised original/compatible inventory; paid priority reservations; test fixtures/protocols; and lab-specific configuration/failure history. Inventory is purchasable and can depreciate; the control becomes durable only when pool density, compatibility history, and recurring contracts improve utilisation and response beyond a lab's own spares.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Qualify 30 labs and identify the same two or three costly failure points.
2. Buy one low-cost test fixture and at most one original spare after interviews.
3. Sell a 7,500 PLN audit and 5,000+ PLN reservation before purchasing the relevant pool.
4. Conduct an integrity/loaner drill without human-subject data.
5. Within 12 months/100,000 PLN, own/test 30 covered accessories across three configurations, sign five annual labs, perform 20 checks/drills, and document one avoided cancellation or faster configuration change.

## Distribution Advantage

Research publications expose the exact installed equipment and responsible lab, enabling unusually specific outreach. Each nearby compatible lab improves inventory utilisation and response. The advantage disappears if labs buy once, manufacturers respond faster, or procurement blocks small vendors.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with CEE signal-chain continuity, then expand to EU multi-vendor trigger/synchronisation, mobile biosignal wearables, eye-tracking/mocap interconnects, and managed research-device pools. Long-term software predicts failures and validates configurations.

Manufacturers, distributors, university core facilities, and labs can stock the same parts. NeuroPort must be cheaper than isolated spares because a shared pool and compatibility history cover more configurations per PLN.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides lower test/logistics cost and fast CEE/EU shipping; local labs may be below the attention threshold of Western distributors. Research-only scope and transparent manufacturer independence reduce regulatory exposure. Geography is not a moat.

## Compounding Advantage

Delivery accumulates device/interconnect failure histories, compatibility maps, configuration records, inventory-utilisation data, research relationships, wearable/neurotech hardware expertise, and skilled technician access. These assets support the future lab without selling sensitive biosignals.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment and cap optional distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Reinvestment follows reservations into compatible inventory, test fixtures, insurance/security, and software.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: qualify 30 labs/configurations and verify missed-session economics.
- Days 31–75: 100-account list, checklist, 20 audit proposals.
- Days 76–120: one paid 7,500 PLN audit/reservation.
- Days 121–180: relevant inventory purchase, integrity/loaner drill, and annual membership or second paid audit.

Founder remains employed/five hours daily; inventory follows payment.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 30,000 PLN: original test/spare parts 10k; test fixture/tools 7k; engineering 5k; insurance/legal 4k; outreach/shipping 2k; contingency 2k.
- Up to 60,000 PLN after paid reservation/drill.
- Up to 100,000 PLN after three independent paying labs and positive pool contribution.
- Family capital excluded until repeat membership and utilisation economics.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after ten independent annual labs, positive contribution for six months, one renewal, 50%+ covered-inventory utilisation, repeatable publication outreach, recurring gross profit above 1.5× burn, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Original accessories can be resold at uncertain value. Reusable assets include test fixtures, compatibility records, device inventory, research relationships, and diagnostics software. A generic electronics repair shop is not the fallback.

## Critical Evidence

Official sources establish costly accessories, connector transitions, cross-system adapters, and a large publication footprint. They do not prove missed-session frequency, independent-vendor acceptance, annual budgets, or the 250-lab denominator.

## Critical Hypotheses

One lab prepays; a shared pool covers repeated configurations; five labs renew; 60% gross margin works; 250 labs pay 48k/year; official manufacturer service does not dominate.

## Kill Criteria

- No paid audit/reservation after 20 qualified proposals.
- Labs will buy only parts once or require manufacturer-authorised service.
- Less than three configurations repeat across 30 qualified labs.
- Inventory/support drives gross margin below 45%.
- No annual membership/renewal in 12 months.
- Conservative founder wealth falls below 5m.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

30,000 PLN pre-proof; 100,000 PLN year one. Founder software/sales/inventory; contract electronics/test engineer; neuroscience technician; fixed-fee legal/insurance review; part-time logistics after contracts.
