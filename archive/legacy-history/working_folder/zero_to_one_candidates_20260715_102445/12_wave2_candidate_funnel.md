## Wave 2 Finalists

1. **GymSignal Media Rail** — exclusive independent-gym media inventory with edge-measured attentive exposure and routed offer conversion.
2. **CharacterContinuity Rail** — consent-cleared, category-exclusive character voice models whose continuing lines and royalties route through one rights/generation system.
3. **WorkScore Passport** — portable algorithmic-work evidence and human-review membership using the EU platform-work data-transfer right.
4. **ScopeBand** — a press-to-record field wearable that converts verbal scope changes into signed, deposited change orders before extra work begins.

## Main-Agent Improvement Record

### AttentionGym → GymSignal Media Rail

- Changed the advertiser promise from vague “attention” to a prepaid vertical campaign with attentive-view evidence plus a trackable trial, class, consultation, or product-offer conversion.
- Required six-month exclusive venue media rights, extension options, a minimum venue guarantee, and advertiser payment before installing more than three sites.
- Kept all vision anonymous and edge-computed; no identity, face template, or emotion inference is retained.
- Made the control point the reserved venue inventory and advertiser order flow, not the camera model.

### Replica Residuals CEE → CharacterContinuity Rail

- Narrowed the buyer to live-service, episodic, and update-heavy game studios where an already cast character creates switching cost.
- Added two contracts: defined category/time performer-model rights and a studio character-continuity licence routing all new synthetic lines and usage reports through the system.
- Made model vendors replaceable and introduced human direction/pronunciation memory as the compounding workflow.
- Required paid studio demand before large actor guarantees; first ten voice options are capped and time-limited.

### WorkScore Vault → WorkScore Passport

- Added a concrete conversion event: a 299 PLN founding annual membership plus fixed-fee evidence pack when earnings, allocation, rating, or access is challenged.
- Used the Directive's third-party transfer provision as access momentum, not as automatic budget or exclusivity.
- Narrowed the first member to Polish couriers/drivers using two or more platforms and made manual exports sufficient before national APIs exist.
- Made subscription economics independent of legal damages or data resale; the strategic asset is the longitudinal authorised record and appeal outcome taxonomy.

### Generic change-order app → ScopeBand

- Restricted capture to a single press-to-record step at the moment a client requests extra work, before labour/material is committed.
- Priced on recovered/routed change value: a modest monthly fee plus a percentage of deposited changes.
- Added an all-out-of-scope routing clause and payment through a licensed PSP; the startup never holds funds.
- Chose renovation, electrical, bathroom, kitchen, and fit-out contractors whose owners are reachable through public lists and founder-adjacent trade context.
