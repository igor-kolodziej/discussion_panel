# VoiceAccess Release Gate Research

Sources retrieved 2026-07-15. This research tests the pivot; it is not validation.

## Regulatory Timing

- The European Accessibility Act requires covered products and services to satisfy accessibility requirements; the Directive covers areas including electronic communications, banking, transport, and e-commerce. It includes specific real-time text/total-conversation requirements for electronic communications. It does **not** prescribe the proposed dysarthric-speech voicebot regression test or make the proposed report a compliance certificate. [Directive (EU) 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng)
- Poland's implementing Act is in force from 28 June 2025. [Polish legal act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2024/731/ogl)
- Poland's Office of Electronic Communications lists telephony, electronic banking, e-commerce, payments, and transport among covered categories. This establishes accessibility pressure, not a budget for VoiceAccess. [UKE accessibility overview](https://uke.gov.pl/en/dostepnosc-produktow-i-uslug/?contrast=black-yellow)

## Buyer and Installed-Use Evidence

- PKO Bank Polski reported 12 voicebots and 11.5 million completed conversations by 2022, establishing substantial Polish production use and repeated model/flow changes. It does not identify independent accessibility-test purchasing. [PKO Bank Polski](https://media.pkobp.pl/194055-voiceboty-pko-banku-polskiego-najlepsza-technologia-wspierajaca-dzialalnosc-contact-center)
- PwC Poland publicly sells conversational-AI implementation across banking, telecom, commerce, insurance, transport, and other workflows, illustrating an incumbent-integrator route. [PwC Poland](https://www.pwc.pl/pl/case-studies/chatbot-i-voicebot-podnoszenie-wydajnosci-obslugi-klientow.html)
- Public vendor/integrator case material can seed a named prospect list, but no source found establishes 40 independent European buyers at the proposed price.

## Direct Alternatives and Copy Risk

- Evalgent already markets release testing for voice agents across scenarios, profiles, tasks, and metrics. [Evalgent](https://www.evalgent.com/)
- Coval markets automated testing of voice agents across noisy callers, releases, and realistic workflows. [Coval](https://www.coval.ai/)
- VoiceEval offers voice-agent QA integrations, while accessibility-user-testing providers such as Alana recruit lived-experience testers. [VoiceEval](https://voiceeval.com/), [Alana](https://alanaaccessibility.com/)
- Voiceitt sells an API for making voice products accessible using its proprietary non-standard-speech database and personalised training. It can be a substitute for remediation or an exceptionally strong entrant into testing. [Voiceitt](https://voiceitt.com/)

## Decision-Critical Findings

1. The regulatory date and production voicebot use are real.
2. The regulation does not create a mandatory dysarthric-speech release test or recognised certification route.
3. Voice-agent regression testing is already a commercial category, with well-funded or specialised platforms able to add speech profiles.
4. Real-user accessibility testing is already purchasable as a service, and Voiceitt owns a relevant proprietary corpus/API.
5. The pivot's annual price, 40-customer denominator, reusable-rights acceptance, and ability to productise participant work remain unsupported.

The evidence weakens the claimed asymmetry: the narrow Polish benchmark may be differentiated, but the control point can be approached through one panel contract plus an existing voice-evaluation platform unless cross-customer data rights and repeated release routing become unusually strong.
