# Zero-to-One Candidate: HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail

Wave-3 candidate generation only — not validated. All commercial numbers are hypotheses.

## One-Sentence Thesis

HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail buys, tests, reserves, and configures scarce end-of-life HoloLens 2 devices so European industrial teams can keep deployed training/remote-assistance workflows alive through Microsoft's support horizon while migrating to a successor platform on a paid schedule.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build inventory diagnostics, device telemetry, configuration records, migration analysis, and AR usage analytics. A contract Unity/enterprise-device engineer covers app compatibility after customer payment. The first offer is audit, reservation, and loaner continuity—not unauthorised component repair or a new headset.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The buyer is a European manufacturer, utility, training provider, or AR integrator with a live HoloLens 2 workflow and fewer than 50 devices. Microsoft no longer manufactures the hardware and it is out of stock; final security servicing has a defined horizon. One failed device, lost configuration, or delayed migration can interrupt training, remote assistance, or a customer contract.

Current workarounds are holding untested spare units, buying ad hoc used devices, relying on an integrator, or rushing a migration. The buyer wants a tested reserved spare, recoverable configuration, and an exit plan without immediately rewriting the application.

## Product Wedge

- 15,000 PLN paid continuity audit: fleet serials/condition, app/dependency map, configuration backup, failure exposure, successor shortlist, and 90-day exit milestones.
- 5,000–15,000 PLN non-refundable priority reservation per site, credited to an annual contract.
- Annual 60,000–180,000 PLN contract depending on reserved devices/response: tested loaner pool, 48–72h EU shipment, configuration restore, quarterly device health check, and migration lab hours.
- Used devices are purchased only after paid audit/reservation; all units are serialised, securely reset, tested, insured/shipped, and never represented as Microsoft-authorised repair.
- Customer applications/data remain segregated; compatibility results can be retained only in de-identified form by contract.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

End-of-life enterprise hardware creates a short window when exact compatibility is more valuable than superior new hardware. Large integrators prefer migration projects; small deployed fleets need a bridge and cannot economically build a spare pool, diagnostics process, and cross-platform lab. Owning tested units plus customer-specific configuration turns secondary-market devices into time-critical uptime inventory.

## Underused Momentum

Microsoft officially ended manufacturing and reports global stock exhaustion, while security servicing continues through 2027. Enterprises therefore face a bounded bridge period, and Microsoft has documented real industrial deployments. The timing creates urgency but does not prove the number of active small fleets.

## Beachhead Market

CEE/DACH industrial and training organisations or smaller MR integrators with 3–30 active HoloLens 2 units, a named production/training workflow, and no sufficient tested spare pool. Exclude defence, clinical use, unsupported safety claims, and fleets requiring authorised component repair.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid audit 15,000 PLN; reservation 5,000–15,000 PLN; annual uptime/migration contract 60,000–180,000 PLN/site.
- Used device purchase hypothesis: 4,000–6,000 PLN; reserve two to four devices across compatible customers, not one per site.
- Scaled gross-margin hypothesis: 60% after device depreciation, engineer time, shipping, insurance, losses, and storage; operating margin 25%–30% after enterprise sales/security.
- Value proof: avoided downtime/procurement delay and paid migration progress must exceed annual contract cost; no generic “innovation” ROI.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build 100 named prospects from Microsoft's public MRPP list, public HoloLens case studies, integrator websites, job posts, and conference presentations; qualify active deployments by direct question.
- Send a fixed 15,000 PLN “HoloLens End-of-Life Exposure Audit” with the official Microsoft timeline and a sample serial/configuration register.
- Conversion event: paid audit, current-device proof, signed reservation, approved security handling, and one configuration/loaner test.
- First ten: direct technical outreach to CEE/DACH integrators/end users, with paid referrals only after direct proof.
- First 100: publish anonymised compatibility/migration matrices and sell through integrators while retaining inventory reservations and end-customer uptime contracts.

## Primary Control Point

**Proposed Tier 1:** physically owned, tested, serialised HoloLens 2 inventory; paid priority reservations; customer configuration/app compatibility records; and limited secondary-market sourcing relationships. Scarcity is time-bounded and inventory can depreciate rapidly, so signed reservations—not speculative stock—must control purchasing.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Buy one used unit for diagnostics only after ten customer interviews.
2. Produce a standard health/configuration/secure-reset protocol and audit template.
3. Sell one 15,000 PLN audit and 5,000+ PLN reservation before purchasing a three-unit pool.
4. Demonstrate customer configuration restore and loaner shipment.
5. Within 12 months/100,000 PLN, own/test 10–15 units backed by at least three independent annual contracts/reservations and complete one paid migration proof.

An unknown founder can source units openly and sell a bounded continuity audit using Microsoft's official deadline. Enterprise trust and sales cycle remain the decisive reachability risks.

## Distribution Advantage

The public installed ecosystem produces a finite named prospect list. Each customer compatibility result, device source, and successful swap improves future sales and utilisation. Integrators can refer customers whose small fleets they do not want to stock, but no partnership is assumed for first proof.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with HoloLens 2 uptime. Expand only through paid customers into successor-platform migration, cross-vendor industrial AR test fleets, managed device pools, and application compatibility. The shrinking wedge must generate cash and distribution; it cannot rely on terminal HoloLens revenue indefinitely. Large integrators and AR vendors can enter, but a dense shared inventory pool and CEE compatibility history can serve smaller fleets more economically.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers lower technical/warehouse cost and fast EU shipping, with CEE customers potentially underserved by Western integrators. GDPR/security and EU logistics can be standardised. Geography is not a barrier; owned scarce inventory and reserved response are the defence.

## Compounding Advantage

Each device/customer adds failure/condition history, configuration variants, app dependencies, migration outcomes, and supplier reliability. This improves inventory purchasing, device health models, compatibility and migration estimates. Ordinary delivery builds intelligent-wearable hardware expertise, AR HCI/ML, enterprise distribution, skilled engineer access, proprietary operational data, and capital.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment and cap distributions at 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Inventory is bought only against paid reservations; reinvestment shifts toward successor test fleets, security, diagnostics, and migration tooling.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 20 technical interviews, one diagnostic unit, protocol, security/legal terms.
- Days 31–75: 100-account list and 20 targeted audit proposals.
- Days 76–135: one paid 15,000 PLN audit and reservation.
- Days 136–180: buy/test three units, run one configuration/loaner test, secure annual contract or second independent paid audit.

Founder remains employed/five hours daily. Paid evidence precedes the pooled inventory.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- Pre-proof maximum 35,000 PLN: legal/security/insurance 6k; one diagnostic unit 6k; contractor 8k; outreach/travel 5k; three-unit purchase only after payment 10k net of received cash.
- Up to 65,000 PLN after paid audit/reservation and successful test.
- Up to 100,000 PLN after three independent paying customers and positive contribution.
- Family capital excluded until repeat contracts and migration economics.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Leave employment only after five independent contracted sites, positive fully loaded contribution, repeat audit-to-contract conversion, at least one successor migration, recurring gross profit covering 1.5× burn, inventory utilisation proof, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Units can be resold but not assumed at cost. Reusable assets include diagnostics, secure configuration workflows, compatibility matrices, AR code, engineer/supplier relationships, and customer distribution. A generic device-rental business is not the fallback.

## Critical Evidence

Microsoft officially confirms production ended/global stock exhaustion and a defined support horizon. Active fleet count, budget, used supply economics, and migration demand are not established.

## Critical Hypotheses

At least 30 qualified fleets exist in the first 100 accounts; one pays within 180 days; used devices remain testable/available below 6k; reserved pool economics work; 80 multi-vendor sites are reachable without service-heavy margins.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than 30 active qualified fleets among 100 named prospects.
- No paid audit/reservation within 180 days.
- Used-device failure/loss and engineer support reduce gross margin below 45%.
- Security/procurement prevents a loaner/configuration test inside six months.
- No paid successor migration or multi-vendor contract within 12 months.
- Real site denominator/price/labour causes conservative founder wealth below 5m.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

35,000 PLN pre-proof; 100,000 PLN first year. Founder diagnostics/data/sales; contract Unity/device engineer after payment; fixed-fee enterprise security/legal/insurance review; part-time logistics after contracts.
