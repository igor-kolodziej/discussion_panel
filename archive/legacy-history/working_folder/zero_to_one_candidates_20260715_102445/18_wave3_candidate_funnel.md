## Finalists

1. **HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail** — scarce EOL device reservation plus continuity and migration for deployed enterprise AR workflows.
2. **SpeechBridge Workline** — a persistent, personalised work-call number for professionals with non-standard or progressively changing speech.

## Improvement Record

### Generic EOL repair → HoloLens Uptime & Exit Rail

- Used Microsoft's official “no longer manufactured/out of stock” status and security-support horizon as a concrete timing event.
- Avoided unauthorised repair claims: the wedge is tested loaner reservation, configuration continuity, and paid exit planning.
- Required customer deposits before secondary-market inventory purchases and recorded serialised custody/condition.
- Made expansion a multi-vendor industrial-AR continuity operation, but required the shrinking HoloLens wedge to be profitable on its own.

### Generic accessibility app → SpeechBridge Workline

- Narrowed to economically important work calls and a persistent business number rather than generic speech transcription.
- Made the member's personalised correction history and caller trust the switching asset; all calls offer transparent synthetic-restatement and human-caption fallback.
- Required prepaid 90-day programmes before building telephony infrastructure.
- Made subscription economics independent of selling sensitive speech data; research use is separate and optional.
