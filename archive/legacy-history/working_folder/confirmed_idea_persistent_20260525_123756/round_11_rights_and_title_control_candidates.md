## Finalists For Simulated Review

The six finalists selected for independent simulated scoring are:

1. **PIFVault Cosmetic SKU Rights Exchange** - strongest asset-control candidate: legal/IP/regulatory file bundles can be optioned before spending heavily, and the buyer pain is commercial launch speed rather than vague compliance.
2. **DIWASS Annex VII Shipment Custody** - strongest timing candidate: official EU digital shipment switch occurred on 2026-05-21, but it risks being only compliance workflow.
3. **Polish VoiceRights Data Vault** - strongest founder-fit data asset candidate: rights-cleared Polish speech/text eval data may compound if legally secured, but buyer sales and GDPR are hard.
4. **ReturnStream Title Receiver** - strongest title-transfer cash-cycle candidate: actual inventory title and fast resale, but reverse logistics is crowded.
5. **EV Garage Charging Site Option Bank** - strongest location-rights/family-fit candidate: local site options are specific assets, but co-op/admin sales may be slow.
6. **Used IT Refresh Forward Rights** - strongest simple physical-rights candidate: forward contracts on refresh devices are signable, but refurb incumbents exist.

## Current Source Anchors

- EU CPNP is the official free notification system under Regulation 1223/2009; responsible persons submit required product information before placing cosmetics on the EU market.
- Polish sanitary pages confirm cosmetic products are registered in CPNP, while CPNP notification alone is not proof that the product meets all requirements.
- DIWASS and the revised Waste Shipment Regulation went live on 2026-05-21, and the Commission says PIC procedures must be processed digitally through DIWASS.
- Allegro/InPost and Allegro policy pages confirm standardized Polish e-commerce returns and seller refund obligations, supporting return-flow pressure but not exclusivity.
- Subagent pain mining found current Reddit/community friction around rentals, KSeF, apartment EV charging, customs parcels, medical access, and English-speaking admin, but most are service-heavy without hard control.
