## Main Feedback

The chatbot judged the idea as unusually concrete and better than generic SMB IT support because the asset is custody of live renewal flows. It refused to score it above 82 because the hard part is not the operational concept; it is whether an unknown part-time solo founder can source and close one retiring operator fast enough, then legally and trustfully convert client custody before churn, messy access transfer, or support overload appears.

## Strongest Objections

1. **The best sellers may not sell to an unknown founder.** Retiring webmasters care about reputation and legacy client relationships; the founder needs credibility, insurance, a security checklist, sample handoff agreements, and a client-protection earnout.
2. **The client book may be economically poor.** Many books can contain low-ARPU, high-touch, outdated WordPress/Joomla/shared-hosting clients paying only a few hundred PLN yearly.
3. **The founder can accidentally buy a helpdesk trap.** The offer must stay focused on custody, renewals, admin recovery, DNS/SSL/email continuity, backups, and incident response windows, not unlimited support.
4. **Transferability is not guaranteed.** Registrar panels, domain authinfo, hosting terms, client consent, registrant emails, and processor/controller roles can block or slow migration.
5. **Big players can copy the care-plan surface.** Home.pl, cyber_Folks, LH.pl, nazwa.pl, and agencies can offer managed services; the only defensible wedge is buying neglected micro-books before larger players bother.

## POC Standard From Real Feedback

The chatbot would only consider the POC successful if it achieves:

- signed option/earnout on one real seller book with at least 30 active paying clients;
- at least 20 client assets moved into founder-controlled or delegated workflow;
- at least 10 paid invoices collected from inherited clients;
- at least 3 real continuity fixes completed;
- seller phone/email/domain routing demonstrably forwarding urgent requests to the founder;
- no legal ambiguity around client notices, data transfer, and processor/controller role.

## Implication For Next Round

Do not continue with SMB web custody unless the seller book is already signed. The next idea should have a hard resource that can be bought, reserved, assigned, or measured without needing fragile personal trust transfer from an operator to many small clients.
