# Round 33 Candidate Set: High-Floor Connector Continuity

Created: 2026-05-25

## Round 32 Fresh-Gate Failure To Fix

Fresh-chat Zero To One scored Prepaid ERP Connector Succession Escrow at `77/100`.

The control point was accepted as real, but the economics failed because:

- `25,000 PLN` prepaid may prove urgency without proving owner earnings;
- target quality may be scarce;
- support leakage can turn the asset into low-margin agency work;
- one acquisition does not prove repeatability;
- broad ERP/accounting connector wording still included too many commodity substitutes.

Round 33 keeps the control point but raises the proof threshold and narrows the first beachhead to one stack.

## Candidate 1: High-Floor One-Stack Connector Continuity Buyout

### Buyer

Polish/CEE merchants on one specific e-commerce + ERP/accounting operations stack, selected after target screening. Example stack categories:

- PrestaShop + one local ERP/accounting/warehouse workflow;
- WooCommerce + one local ERP/accounting/warehouse workflow;
- one agency-built order/invoice/warehouse/COD reconciliation connector used by 30-100 merchants.

The first target may not be a generic Fakturownia invoice export, generic Subiekt bridge, broad payment gateway, broad shipping plugin, parcel-locker connector, or any workflow already cheaply solved by Base/BaseLinker, official/free integrations, a strong marketplace module, or a commodity Sfera/API integrator.

### Acute Trigger

The original connector developer is tired, inactive, overloaded, or exiting support. Merchants face a continuity risk for a workflow that touches orders, stock, invoice corrections, warehouse status, B2B account exports, COD reconciliation, or ERP-specific mapping. The workflow must be operationally embedded enough that migration costs exceed the proposed annual continuity payment.

### Primary Control Point

The founder signs a conditional option but pays no meaningful acquisition consideration until a high prepaid customer floor clears.

Control exists only if all are secured:

- source/IP/repo/release/update credentials;
- support inbox and issue history;
- anonymized support-ticket review before option fee;
- seller-mediated customer continuity campaign;
- explicit customer re-consent;
- at least 40,000-60,000 PLN prepaid 12-month maintenance/transition-support cash before close;
- at least 15 merchants paying 2,500+ PLN/year or equivalent prepaid package;
- proof that 70%+ of historical support issues are reusable connector-level maintenance;
- paid escalation menu for PHP/server/theme/store debugging, ERP mapping cleanup, accounting reconciliation, custom checkout conflicts, emergency fixes, and KSeF-related work;
- seller earnout funded only from retained customer cash.

This changes the model from "risk-reduced acquisition" to "merchant-funded acquisition with owner-earnings floor."

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- screen 60 target sellers in one stack family;
- hold at least 15 seller calls;
- inspect anonymized support history from at least 3 candidate assets;
- sign one conditional option with no more than 5,000 PLN refundable/creditable option fee before customer cash;
- seller sends continuity notice to 40-60 named active customers;
- secure at least 15 signed re-consents and 40,000-60,000 PLN prepaid before close;
- reject the deal if prepaid average is under 2,000 PLN/customer or if ticket history shows less than 70% reusable connector maintenance;
- close transfer only after cash/re-consent threshold clears;
- ship one scoped patch within 14 days of close.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Outbound to agencies/freelancers with old support-tail connectors:

"I buy only if your customers prepay continuity first. You get a small option fee and earnout, but no awkward blind sale. I handle the support tail if the customers prove they value it."

Screen one stack at a time so diligence becomes reusable.

### Gross Margin And Payback Logic

- Customer cash before close: 40,000-60,000 PLN.
- Upfront option fee before customer proof: max 5,000 PLN.
- First patch: 8,000-20,000 PLN, only if scoped and one patch helps many customers.
- Legal/privacy/templates: 5,000-12,000 PLN.
- Seller earnout: 20-30% of retained maintenance revenue, paid from collected revenue.
- Expected immediate contribution after first patch/legal/earnout must still be positive.
- Year-1 owner earnings become plausible only if one operator can hold 3-5 similar connector books in the same stack with shared developer context and strict support scope.

### Copy Risk

A small agency can compete for future support, but cannot easily copy the exact seller-endorsed handoff, re-consented prepaid customer contracts, update channel, issue history, and one-stack continuity brand once customers have paid.

### Why It Is Not A Service/Report/Dashboard/Marketplace Wrapper

The startup controls code/update/support assets and signed prepaid customer contracts before closing. It does not merely advise, list providers, sell reports, or broker support.

## Candidate 2: High-Floor COD/Settlement Continuity Buyout

Same model but only for COD settlement/reconciliation connectors where merchants prepay 40,000+ PLN before close and ticket history proves reusable connector work. Reject if it is bespoke accounting cleanup or spreadsheet work.

## Candidate 3: High-Floor Warehouse/Stock Sync Continuity Buyout

Same model but only for warehouse/order-status connectors where stock/order sync failure directly affects overselling, fulfillment, or marketplace penalties. Reject if Base/BaseLinker or an official integration covers the workflow.

## Candidate 4: High-Floor B2B Order Export Continuity Buyout

Same model but only for B2B wholesale order/account export connectors with customer-specific formats that are painful to migrate but reused across many merchants. Reject one-off custom export scripts.

## Candidate 5: High-Floor Agency Tool Spinout

Same model but for a repeated agency-built internal connector with 30-100 clients. Requires contract assignment, deployment/update access, support inbox, code/IP, 50,000+ PLN prepaid, and no bespoke support majority.

## Candidate 6: Kill-The-Category Control Variant

If no one-stack connector target can meet the 40,000-60,000 PLN prepaid floor inside 60 days, kill the software connector territory. The repeated fresh objection would then be decisive: good assets are too scarce and bad assets become agency work.

## Gate Rule For Scorers

Apply the existing persistent-loop caps:

- cap below `82` if no concrete 2-month control proof;
- cap below `85` if copyable by one vendor contract, equipment purchase, or internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, or owner earnings are not credible;
- require simulated score strictly `>87` to reach the real Zero To One browser gate.
