# Round 27: Grid-Connection And Power-Capacity Project Assets

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 26 found real legal-use assets, but quota/authorisation brokerage still suffered from direct-bypass and official matchmaking. Grid-connection assets are more location-specific: once a founder controls an SPV, land lease, connection condition/agreement, or cable-pooling right, an incumbent cannot copy that exact grid position by signing another vendor contract.

This round uses the founder's renewable-energy family context and targets hard local energy assets under an option-first model, not capital-intensive development.

## Source Anchors

- URE reported that in 2024 there were 6,259 refusals to issue grid connection conditions for RES, with refused capacity just under 42.4 GW, confirming acute connection scarcity: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/436%2Cdok.html
- URE says Poland is working to address connection refusals and improve transparency through digitisation, commercial grid-connection agreements, and cable pooling: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/425%2CActivities-of-the-President-of-the-URE-for-the-development-and-modernisation-of-.html
- URE published a first-year cable-pooling monitoring summary for 2024, confirming that shared grid connections are an active regulatory framework with issued/refused conditions: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/494%2CThe-President-of-the-URE-summarises-the-first-year-of-cable-pooling-in-Poland.html
- PSE's grid-capacity public information and industry analyses show strong BESS queue pressure and limited available transmission capacity; the specific project asset must therefore be verified against PSE/DSO documents before any buyer pitch: https://www.pse.pl/obszary-dzialalnosci/krajowy-system-elektroenergetyczny/informacja-o-dostepnej-mocy-przylaczeniowej
- URE's 2024 redispatch report shows growing non-market RES curtailment, supporting demand for storage/cable-pooling and flexibility assets, though prior curtailment-claim ideas failed as claims brokerage: https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/477%2CElectricity-market-The-2024-Report-on-Redispatching-Mechanisms-from-the-Presiden.html

## Initial Read Before Scoring

The strongest candidate is **Grid-Connection SPV Option Desk** because it controls an already scarce legal/project asset, not a process. **Cable-Pooling Rights Packager** is second if the agreement is assignable and includes production/profile data, host exclusivity, and buyer deposit. Other candidates are included as comparators but are likely to be capped as real-estate brokerage, advisory, or high-liability project acquisition.
