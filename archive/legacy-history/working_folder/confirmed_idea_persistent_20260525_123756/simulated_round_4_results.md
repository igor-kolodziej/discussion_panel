## Key Learning

Buying a route book solves cold start, but it does not itself satisfy domination-grade control. Local route businesses need either:

- a legally scarce entitlement;
- exclusive assigned cash rights;
- transaction/payment release authority;
- very high gross margin with repeat demand;
- or a route that incumbents cannot easily copy because the physical asset is regulated, identity-bound, or customer-switching friction is extreme.

Round 5 should move away from commodity routes and toward assignable money rights, official payment-release rights, or high-value asset custody where the founder controls a cash event rather than a local service calendar.
