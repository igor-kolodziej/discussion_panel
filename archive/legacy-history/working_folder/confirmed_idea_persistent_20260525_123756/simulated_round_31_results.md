## Candidate That Cleared Simulated Gate

**Orphaned Polish E-Commerce Connector Payment-Flow Acquisition** scored **88/100**.

Exact scored wording:

Acquire abandoned or under-maintained Polish/CEE e-commerce connector plugins/modules where merchants already depend on the installed update/payment/support channel for checkout, shipping labels, COD, payment status, ERP export, invoices, parcel lockers, Allegro/Baselinker, or local carrier/payment integrations.

The control point is not writing software from zero. It is acquiring the source code, IP, update credentials, plugin listing or seller account where transferable, license server, domain/docs, support inbox, customer list, renewal schedule, and seller handoff/non-compete. Competitors can build a new connector, but they cannot instantly copy the installed base, update channel, reviews/history, support inbox, license renewals, and customer bug history.

60-day proof under 100k PLN: build a list of 30-50 stale Polish/localized e-commerce connectors, sign one option/acquisition LOI for under 40k PLN upfront plus earnout, transfer the repo/domain/support/license/payment flow or collect explicit customer re-consent, verify at least 50 active paid installs or 20 recurring support customers, ship one compatibility/security/API update within 14 days of control, and collect 15k-30k PLN in renewals, transition support, prepaid maintenance, or retained license cash.

Payback logic: buy for 15k-40k PLN upfront plus 20-35% earnout. If 50 customers pay 500-1,500 PLN/year, or 20-30 merchants buy 1,000-2,000 PLN transition support, upfront payback can happen in roughly 3-6 months. Gross margins should be software-like if support is tightly scoped and one freelance PHP/WooCommerce/PrestaShop developer handles patches.

## Why It Passed Simulated `>87`

- The control point is hard and transferable if diligence passes: source, IP, update channel, license server, support inbox, customer list, and renewal/payment flow.
- Copyability is lower than greenfield software because incumbents cannot instantly copy the already-installed update channel, reviews/history, bug history, support relationship, and license renewals.
- The 60-day proof is concrete and cash-based: one signed acquisition option, transferred control, one shipped update, and 15k-30k PLN collected.
- Founder fit is strong relative to field-service ideas: technical diligence, data/customer analysis, support process design, and contractor management.
- Budget is plausible if the first deal is structured as low upfront plus seller earnout.

## Simulated Caveats To Carry Into Real Validation

- Marketplace/listing, payment, and customer-data transferability must be verified asset by asset.
- Many abandoned plugins have no paid renewal flow. Free installs alone do not count.
- Support scope must be sharply limited to compatibility/security/API maintenance; otherwise this becomes a low-ticket agency.
- The first acquisition target must be revenue-critical enough that merchants will pay maintenance, not a commodity widget.
- A platform/carrier/payment provider releasing an official free connector is a kill risk for some categories.

## Decision

Advance Candidate 1 to real working-chat Zero To One validation.
