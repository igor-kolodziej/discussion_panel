Why not: the idea has a concrete 2-month proof path and plausible gross margins, so it does not fail the profitability veto outright. But it hits the **copy cap below 85** and likely the **economics credibility cap below 87** until paid demand is proven. Siemens confirms S7-300/ET200M phase-out/spare-only dynamics, which supports urgency, but Siemens still plans spare availability until 2033, weakening desperation for some buyers. Radwell, EU Automation, UNIS/S7-style suppliers already compete on obsolete automation parts, testing, warranty, and delivery.

Strongest objections/caveats:
- Demand is exact-SKU and lumpy. Thirty to fifty SKUs may not match enough real failures.
- The unknown founder has a trust problem: factories may not risk downtime on an unproven seller unless testing, warranty, and provenance are excellent.
- Inventory can trap cash. A 100,000 PLN budget is enough to start, but also enough to buy the wrong shelf stock.
- The installed-base archive may be stale, legally constrained, or commercially weaker than assumed.
- SEO and Allegro/OLX are not enough for 2-month emergency demand proof; inherited call routing or prepaid plant demand must carry the proof.

Strongest copy risk:
**A local automation integrator, Radwell-like supplier, or EU refurbisher can copy the visible model by buying retiring integrator stock/books and layering it onto existing trust, testing, and warranty infrastructure.** Radwell advertises broad automation spares, repair, 24/7 support, large stock, and 2-year warranty; EU Automation pages show in-stock Siemens parts with immediate dispatch and warranty; S7 Automation advertises testing and warranties for Siemens PLC/HMI parts. That means the defense cannot be “used PLC stock”; it must be proprietary local routing plus cabinet-level installed-base intelligence.

Concrete improvement:
Before buying broad inventory, use the retiring integrator archive to identify **20 named plants with specific installed critical modules**, then close **5 paid SKU reservation deposits** or insurance-stock purchases tied to exact cabinet photos/part numbers. If the founder can pair those deposits with exclusive 12-month emergency call routing from the retiring integrator, this could move toward **88+** because CAC, payback, and demand proof become much more credible.
