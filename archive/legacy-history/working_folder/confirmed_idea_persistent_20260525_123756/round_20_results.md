## Lesson

Signed exclusivity over a site or capacity helps, but scoring agents still see most versions as origination/brokerage. To clear the gate, the business must control an existing recurring cash-flow asset, a legally transferred operating book, or a specific operator succession event where revenue and customer rights move during the POC.

## Next Pivot

Return to micro-operator succession, but with stricter constraints:

- only businesses with current recurring invoices and customer renewal rights;
- transfer must include phone/domain, records, contracts, and first cash collection;
- founder must be able to prove the book is not just goodwill;
- first acquisition must be feasible under 100,000 PLN through seller financing/earnout;
- avoid routes already scored weak unless the shape includes actual billing/payment transfer.
