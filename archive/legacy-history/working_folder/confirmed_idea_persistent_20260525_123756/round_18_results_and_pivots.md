## Repeated Fatal Objections

1. Buyer-side LOIs are too weak; paid reservations or bounties are needed before investing in supply acquisition.
2. Transaction-control ideas need the buyer to pay before supply is assembled, otherwise they look like brokerage.
3. Regulatory or legal leverage is helpful but not enough if the implementation can be copied by law firms, consultants, or incumbents.
4. Operator/book acquisition remains attractive only if actual cash flow or transaction rights transfer quickly.

## Next Pivot

Search for ideas where the initial buyer pays for a reserved, assigned, or exclusive asset before the founder spends much capital:

- paid bounties for verified DSR kW before site acquisition;
- paid buyer mandate for rooftop/grid options before owner outreach;
- paid shortage-bounty for exact scarce industrial assets;
- paid procurement mandate where the founder controls the acquired supply/option;
- paid reserve for retiring operator books where the buyer or financier already values the book.

The next pass should emphasize buyer-funded control, not founder-funded speculation.
