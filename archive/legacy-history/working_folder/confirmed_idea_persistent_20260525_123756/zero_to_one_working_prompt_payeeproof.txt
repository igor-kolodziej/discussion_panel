Idea: PayeeProof Polish VoP Benchmark And Pre-Check.

Non-euro payment service providers face SEPA instant Verification of Payee duties in 2027. Polish payment names are unusually messy: legal company names, sole-proprietor names, trade names, NIP/KRS variants, bank-account owner names, diacritics, abbreviations, invoice payee names, and payroll/vendor aliases often differ enough to create false positives or poor user warnings. Generic public-registry matching can normalize some fields, but it does not capture the messy historical payment mismatch cases that actually cause false alarms, support tickets, failed payments, or risky overrides.

Product wedge: PayeeProof builds a rights-cleared Polish beneficiary-name mismatch benchmark and pre-check API for small PSPs, fintechs, accounting/payment platforms, ERP vendors, and payment-workflow tools preparing for VoP. It starts by securing consented/anonymized historical mismatch logs and edge cases from accounting offices, small PSPs, payment platforms, and finance teams: intended payee name from invoice/ERP, bank-account holder string where available, NIP/KRS/CEIDG fields, user-entered beneficiary text, outcome label, and false-positive/false-negative notes. It combines those with synthetic edge cases and public registry normalization, but the defensible asset is the legally usable historical mismatch corpus and outcome labels.

Primary control point: exclusive or at least field-of-use-restricted rights to a Polish payment-name mismatch corpus plus buyer-specific pre-check/evaluation workflow. This is not a public KRS/CEIDG wrapper, generic compliance report, dashboard, or consulting memo. The resource is controlled only if signed data-contribution agreements, anonymization/processing terms, and commercial licensing rights are secured.

2-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- Sign 3-5 data-contribution agreements with accounting offices, small PSPs, ERP/payment workflow vendors, or finance teams. Target 10,000 named edge cases or historical mismatch/payment-name examples with lawful rights, anonymization, and field-of-use licensing.
- Pay a Polish privacy/IT lawyer for consent/DPA/anonymization/license templates and a limited DPIA-style memo.
- Build a benchmark and pre-check prototype that evaluates Polish name matching: legal names, trade names, NIP/KRS/CEIDG records, diacritics, common abbreviations, sole-proprietor formats, bank-owner/invoice-recipient mismatch, and payroll/vendor aliases.
- Run two paid pilots with small PSPs, fintech/accounting platforms, ERP payment modules, or payment-workflow vendors. Proof target: at least 10,000 tested cases, measured false-positive reduction versus public-registry/string-matching baseline, and at least 15,000-30,000 PLN collected in pilot fees or committed paid evaluation.

Cold start:
- Do not sell to large banks first. Start with small PSPs, fintech tools, accounting platforms, payment automation vendors, ERP add-ons, and accounting offices with payment-workflow pain.
- Acquire data contributors by offering a paid data-cleanup/benchmark report and discounted access to the resulting benchmark; acquire first buyers by offering a fixed-price Polish VoP readiness benchmark test against their historical payment-name failures.

Economics:
- Pilot price: 7,500-20,000 PLN per benchmark/evaluation sprint.
- Later pricing: 2,000-8,000 PLN/month per API/evaluation seat for smaller vendors, plus enterprise benchmark licenses for larger PSPs.
- Gross margin should be high after legal/data acquisition and annotation; main costs are legal setup, data cleaning, matching engineering, and sales.

Why now:
- EU Instant Payments Regulation has made Verification of Payee part of the instant-payment rollout. Euro-area PSPs already entered the VoP phase, while PSPs in non-euro member states such as Poland face the later 2027 deadlines. This creates a preparation window.
- The Polish specificity is local: KRS/CEIDG/NIP data, Polish diacritics, sole-proprietor naming, invoice/payee conventions, and local accounting workflows.

Main kill criteria:
- No clean data-contribution agreement within 30 days.
- No paid pilot or committed paid evaluation within 60 days.
- Buyers say public registry matching and existing VoP vendors are good enough.
- Legal review says the historical examples cannot be licensed or anonymized safely enough for commercial benchmark use.
- The corpus does not outperform a public-registry/string-matching baseline.
