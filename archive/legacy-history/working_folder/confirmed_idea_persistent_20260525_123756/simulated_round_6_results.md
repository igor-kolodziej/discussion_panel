## Next Pivot

Search for a stronger control point where:

- the customer cannot simply take the recovered file/spec and rebid;
- the asset is tied to a legal, financial, contractual, or physical custody gate;
- the first POC can secure signed rights, prepaid deposits, assigned cashflow, or installed asset control;
- copy risk is not solved by "existing vendor adds the same process."
