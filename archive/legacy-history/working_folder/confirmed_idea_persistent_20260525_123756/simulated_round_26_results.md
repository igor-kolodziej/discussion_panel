## Fatal Pattern Extracted

The F-gas legal-use layer is the strongest control territory so far, but still falls short under strict simulation because:

- the EU F-gas Portal already provides a matchmaking route;
- buyer and quota holder can bypass once introduced;
- most value may be captured by incumbent chemical traders, refrigerant distributors, customs brokers, or quota holders;
- reference-value acquisition may be real control but is diligence-heavy and may exceed the 100k PLN / 60-day constraints;
- economics need actual spread and deposit evidence before owner earnings are credible.

## Possible Material Reframe

The only way this territory may break `>87` is if the business stops being a broker and becomes a **buyer-side quota/procurement lockbox**:

- buyer signs recurring mandate for all 2026 HFC quota/authorisation needs;
- founder controls buyer-side F-gas Portal admin/account workflow, not just introduction;
- quota holder options are signed before the buyer sees counterparties;
- margin is from procurement savings/risk avoidance plus guaranteed legal availability, not one-off spread.

That reframe must still survive direct-bypass and incumbent-trader objections.
