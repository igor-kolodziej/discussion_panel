# Round 34 Market And Competition Checks: Accounting Admin-Custody Succession

Created: 2026-05-25

## Checked Facts

- The official KSeF site states the mandatory KSeF timeline includes 1 April 2026 for remaining taxpayers, with an exception through end-2026 for very small monthly gross invoice sales below 10,000 PLN. KSeF materials also explain invoice receipt through KSeF for small entrepreneurs during the phased rollout.

Government material also states that from 1 January 2026 e-Doręczenia became the default communication channel with public administration where special provisions do not apply.

## Market Implication

This cannot be pitched as "start an accounting office" or "KSeF compliance help." Those are generic and copyable.

The only potentially defensible wedge is a **succession/control transaction**:

- a retiring/overloaded accounting office has a live recurring client book;
- clients sign new service agreements or novations before close;
- KSeF/e-Delivery admin mandates are newly granted by clients;
- monthly payment flow transfers;
- seller handoff carries trust and deadline/file context;
- tax advisory remains excluded or referred.

## Remaining Kill Risks

- If clients do not re-consent and prepay, the founder only bought goodwill.
- If delivery labor consumes margin, this is a low-margin accounting service.
- If hidden liability from messy files or bad historical treatment is high, the acquisition is dangerous.
- If the seller refuses to do real client handoff and admin authorization transfer, the control point collapses.
- If the founder cannot secure competent bookkeeper capacity and insurance/legal boundaries, the idea should fail.
