# Round 35 Market And Competition Checks: Mobile App Store Assets

Created: 2026-05-25

## Checked Facts

- Apple documents app transfer in App Store Connect and says apps can be transferred to another developer when criteria are met. Apple also states transferred apps retain reviews and ratings during/after transfer, and users continue receiving updates.
  - Source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer
  - Source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer

- Google Play documents app transfer to another developer account and states app transfer can include users, download statistics, ratings, reviews, subscriptions, store listing, target audience, content rating, and other assets subject to conditions.
  - Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247

- App-marketplace listings show small apps are bought and sold as revenue assets, but seller asking prices often imply multiples that can exceed a 100,000 PLN proof budget. This means the first proof needs distressed/off-market sourcing, small upfront plus earnout, or very small live-revenue targets.
  - Source: https://appbusinessbrokers.com/
  - Source: https://appbroker.io/

## Market Implication

The app-store asset control point is real and cleaner than customer-list transfer:

- platform listing;
- reviews/ratings;
- installed base;
- update channel;
- subscription/IAP/ad revenue;
- store analytics;
- source/content rights.

The business still fails if the founder can only buy tiny/no-revenue apps or if app prices are too high for 100,000 PLN.

## Remaining Kill Risks

- Target apps with meaningful recurring revenue may sell at multiples too high for proof-stage capital.
- Exam/certification content rights may not transfer or may require frequent updates.
- App stores can reject updates, terminate accounts, or require transfer criteria.
- B2C churn and low ARPU can make owner earnings weak.
- Official/free exam apps can compress willingness to pay.
- One acquired app proves control, not repeatable acquisition pipeline.
