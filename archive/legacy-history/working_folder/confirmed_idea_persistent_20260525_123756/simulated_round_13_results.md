## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Owned equipment or spare inventory is not enough if incumbents can replicate it with one purchasing decision.
- Emergency services score below the bar unless they control an existing demand route, installed-base record, customer book, assigned rights, or exclusive supply stream.
- Per-incident urgency is attractive, but retention and owner earnings are weak without a recurring control layer.
- Compliance-adjacent work becomes liability-heavy when the founder does not control certification authority or buyer workflow.

## Consequence

No Round 13 idea clears the simulated `>87` gate. Do not contact real Zero To One for these shapes. The next round should start from transferable control first: assigned installed-base archives, title/consignment to obsolete inventory, phone/domain/customer-book options, prepaid inspection slots, or custody of a recurring transaction route.
