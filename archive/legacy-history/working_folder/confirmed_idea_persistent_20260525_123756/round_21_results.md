## Lesson

Micro-operator succession can produce owner income but keeps being capped below 85 because it is a local service tuck-in. Even contracts, installed records, novations, phone/domain, and first cash are not enough for the Zero-To-One standard unless the book creates structural control or a repeatable roll-up with demonstrated acquisition pipeline.

## Next Pivot

Stop trying to force routebooks. Search for high-value rights where:

- the buyer has a transaction blocked by missing unique information, rights, or evidence;
- the founder can secure assigned rights or paid mandate before doing work;
- incumbents cannot copy by adding one service line;
- the proof is cash or signed transaction flow inside 60 days.
