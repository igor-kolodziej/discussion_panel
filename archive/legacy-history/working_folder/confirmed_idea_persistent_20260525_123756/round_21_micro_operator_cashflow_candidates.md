# Round 21 Micro-Operator Cashflow Candidates

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Round

Earlier "routebook" attempts failed because they controlled calendars or goodwill, not cashflow and site-embedded assets. This round only advances micro-acquisition ideas where the option packet includes contracts, installed assets, site maps/logs, phone/domain, customer re-consent or novation, seller handoff, and first invoice collection.

## Initial Candidate To Score Hardest

HACCP Pest Monitoring Book Acquisition. It has the strongest mixture of recurring invoices, installed physical assets, documented monitoring logs, audit continuity, low capex, seller-financed acquisition, and immediate first-cash proof. It is not merely DDD service if the acquisition packet controls installed stations, maps, logs, contracts, customer renewals, and the seller handoff.

## Source Anchors

- GIS confirms food businesses must implement procedures based on HACCP under EU food-hygiene rules.
- Polish DDD companies publicly sell HACCP/IFS/BRC-compatible monitoring and documentation, confirming the market exists but is fragmented.
- The control point must be contracts + installed stations + audit logs; without those, the candidate becomes a weak generic DDD route.
