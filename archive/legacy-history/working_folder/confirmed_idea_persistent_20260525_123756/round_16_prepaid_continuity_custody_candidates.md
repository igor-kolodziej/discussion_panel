# Round 16 Prepaid Continuity Custody Candidates

Created: 2026-05-25

This round pivots from emergency stock resale to prepaid custody of live customer configurations, source files, backups, restore kits, and continuity rights. The control point is no longer generic inventory. It is customer-specific operational memory that can be lost when an integrator, technician, old laptop, or legacy controller disappears.

## Why This Pivot

Round 15 failed because even tested emergency stock is copyable by parts dealers, OEM service networks, wholesalers, or prepared customers. The stronger hidden asset is the restore package: PLC/HMI/drive/CNC/robot/lab-method files, symbols/comments, passwords, source projects, license dongles, engineering PC images, and exact hardware map. These are not recreated by buying one module.

## Candidate Preference Before Simulation

The strongest candidate is #1, Retiring Integrator PLC Source-Code & Restore Escrow Rail. It directly uses the best part of the PLC/HMI donor-bank thesis (urgent obsolete controls) but replaces copyable stock with unique source/project custody and customer-authorized continuity files. It also creates a better cold start because the retiring integrator's customer handoff is the demand channel.

The second candidate is #2, Customer-Funded PLC Spare Twin Locker, but it may be capped if evaluators see it as an ordinary integrator service. Candidate #1 is stronger because it controls the upstream retiring-integrator archive and can later attach #2 as a paid module.

## Source Anchors To Carry Forward

- Siemens documentation confirms S7-300/ET200M product phase-out from October 2023 and type discontinuation from October 2025.
- Siemens TIA Portal documentation states S7-300/400 projects can be migrated into TIA Portal and then migrated to S7-1500, which makes original project files valuable for transition.
- PLC community discussions repeatedly note that S7-300 uploads often lack comments, symbols, or HMI source; source/project files matter for maintainability and migration.
- NIST/CISA industrial-control guidance emphasizes backed-up and separately stored PLC logic, configurations, engineering drawings, and tools for recovery.
