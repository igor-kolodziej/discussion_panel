# Round 4 Control-Point Scout

Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

Scope: 20 Poland/EU business ideas where an unknown solo founder with under 100,000 PLN can control a transferable asset, route, file archive, installed device base, assigned right, or recurring transaction path inside 2 months. Avoided prior weak shapes: generic elder care, generic rehab rental, generic compliance desks, speculative spare-parts pools, generic KSeF rescue, generic credential custody, and regulated medical/dental inventory without routed case flow.

## 1. DieLock Gasket Archive Roll-Up

Buyer: maintenance managers, plant engineers, food/packaging factories, machine shops, farms, and local MRO suppliers needing exact replacement gaskets/seals.

Acute trigger: a pump, mixer, tank, conveyor, compressor, packaging line, or old machine leaks or stops; the customer needs the same gasket profile/material now, not a redesign.

Hard control point: signed option or acquisition of a retiring gasket/rubber workshop's phone number, domain, last 24 months invoices, customer reorder book, CAD/scan files, physical cutting dies/templates, material notes, supplier relationships, and 30-day owner handoff.

2-month proof under 100k PLN: option one tiny shop/archive for 20k-60k PLN plus earnout; catalog 200-500 dies/templates; call 40 active/recent customers; collect 10 paid reorder deposits; complete 5 paid reorders through a subcontract cutter or leased press.

First acquisition mechanism: owner-succession outreach to small gasket/rubber/cutting shops; customer reactivation from acquired invoice history; exact-query landing pages for "uszczelka do [machine/model]" and "dorobienie uszczelki [city]".

Unit economics: dead archive bought cheaply; emergency reorder 150-2,000 PLN; gross margin 45-70% if subcontracted well; payback from 20-40 reorders or one mid-sized recurring plant account.

Copy risk: competitors can make a gasket from a sample, but cannot copy closed-loop customer history, original dies, material notes, phone/domain, and owner trust in one vendor purchase.

Why not service/marketplace/dashboard: the startup owns production memory and reorder rights, not just advice or listings.
