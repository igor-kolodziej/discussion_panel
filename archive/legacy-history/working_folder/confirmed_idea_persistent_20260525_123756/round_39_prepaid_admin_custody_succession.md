# Round 39 Candidate Set: Prepaid Admin-Custody Succession

Created: 2026-05-25

## Why This Pivot

The real Zero To One gate liked the DomainDeadman control point but scored it `78` because the first version assumed:

- an unknown founder could close a retiring webmaster book quickly;
- clients would trust the handoff;
- the book would not be low-ARPU helpdesk debt;
- registrar/hosting/DNS transfer would be clean.

Round 39 keeps the strongest part and changes the proof structure. The founder does **not** buy the book first. The founder signs a conditional seller option, runs a seller-endorsed continuity campaign, and closes only after named clients re-consent, move/delegate admin custody, and prepay continuity invoices.

This is not generic SMB IT support, web care plans, MSP resale, or a dashboard. The control point is paid delegated custody over domain/DNS/email/hosting renewal-critical assets before acquisition close.

## Source Anchors

- NASK's `.pl` registry says the registrant has rights and obligations and may request authinfo; the address in registry records is used for confirmations of significant changes such as registrant-data changes and transfer confirmation: https://www.dns.pl/en/registrant_rights_domain_pl
- NASK describes `.pl` domain transfer through authinfo and registrar procedures, confirming that domain-service transfer is an operationally controllable process if the registrant and registrar cooperate: https://www.dns.pl/en/transfer_domain_pl
- NASK technical conditions define Authinfo as the code authorizing a change of Partner/registrar in NASK systems: https://www.dns.pl/en/registration_domain_pl/technical_conditions

## Candidate 1: Prepaid DNS/Email Continuity Succession Escrow

### Buyer / Customer

Polish B2B SMBs whose domains, DNS, email, SSL, Microsoft 365/Google Workspace, hosting, transactional mail, backups, or ecommerce DNS records are managed by a retiring webmaster, tiny hosting reseller, local WordPress agency, or one-person IT freelancer.

### Acute Trigger

The business loses email, website, ecommerce checkout, invoice delivery, or domain control when the old webmaster retires, gets sick, becomes unreachable, or keeps assets in a shared reseller panel. The owner often discovers the risk only when a renewal, DNS change, SSL certificate, mailbox, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, or registrar transfer is urgent.

### Primary Control Point

The founder does not buy a client book speculatively. The founder signs a conditional option with the seller and closes only after buyer-side control is proven.

Control exists only when each client signs and pays for:

- delegated admin access or transfer plan for domains, DNS, registrar, SSL, hosting, email, Microsoft 365/Google Workspace, transactional email, and backup records;
- confirmed registrant/business-owner authority for the domain;
- renewal calendar and invoice authority;
- 90-day emergency continuity authorization;
- data-processing and security scope;
- explicit exclusion of unlimited website development/helpdesk support;
- first prepaid continuity invoice or setup/renewal payment.

The acquisition closes only on re-consented, prepaid, scoped custody accounts. Low-ARPU clients and open-ended support cases are rejected before purchase.

### Two-Month Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- contact 100 retiring or weakly digitized webmasters, micro-hosting resellers, small WordPress agencies, and one-person IT freelancers;
- inspect 10 books and reject books without live invoices, renewal calendars, or admin access;
- sign one conditional option with a seller for no more than 5,000-10,000 PLN option fee plus earnout;
- seller sends continuity notice to at least 40 active clients;
- secure 20 written client re-consents;
- move/delegate at least 20 domains/DNS/email/hosting/SSL assets into founder-controlled or jointly controlled workflow;
- collect at least 20,000 PLN prepaid continuity/setup/renewal cash before closing the acquisition;
- complete 5 paid continuity fixes: authinfo recovery, DNS backup, MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC repair, SSL renewal, registrar access handoff, mailbox admin recovery, backup verification, or renewal-risk map;
- close only on accounts where support scope, access custody, and payment are already real.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Seller pitch:

"You have clients who depend on you for domains and email, but you do not want to be their emergency person forever. I will not buy blind. You introduce only active clients to a continuity handoff. Clients who re-consent and prepay become part of the transfer; you receive an earnout from retained accounts."

Client pitch:

"Your domain, DNS, email and renewal records are currently dependent on one person. We will not redesign your website. We will secure admin custody, document the renewal path, fix the immediate access gaps, and become the 90-day continuity desk."

### Economics

- 500-2,500 PLN setup/admin-recovery fee per client.
- 200-1,000 PLN renewal/risk-cleanup payment depending on asset count.
- 99-499 PLN/month continuity retainer for businesses with ecommerce, B2B email dependence, or multiple domains/mailboxes.
- Seller earnout funded only from retained prepaid client revenue.
- Contractor work is scoped to access custody, DNS/email/security basics, and renewal operations.

The first proof is cash-positive only if at least 20,000 PLN clears before acquisition close. The model is killed if the founder must finance seller goodwill or broad support obligations.

### Copy Risk

Large registrars, agencies, and MSPs can provide managed DNS/email services, but they cannot copy the seller-endorsed handoff, specific renewal calendar, old registrar/reseller access path, and prepaid delegated custody for named clients once customers re-consent. A competitor can copy the service layer; it cannot copy the already routed, prepaid account set.

### Why It Is Not A Service/Report/Dashboard/Marketplace

The startup controls admin access, renewal calendars, registrar/DNS/email/SSL records, client consent, seller handoff, and prepaid payment flow. It does not sell advice or a website-support marketplace. Any client not moved into delegated custody is not counted.

### Kill Criteria

- No seller option within 30 days.
- Fewer than 20 client re-consents or less than 20,000 PLN prepaid before close.
- Clients ask for open-ended WordPress/web/email helpdesk instead of custody.
- Registrar/hosting/M365/Google Workspace access cannot be delegated or transferred cleanly.
- Most accounts pay under 500 PLN/year or require high-touch support.
- Security/privacy review says the data-processing and admin-custody scope is unsafe.

## Candidate 2: Microsoft 365 Tenant Break-Glass Succession Custody

Acquire or option retiring small-business IT/MSP tenant-admin books only after 15 clients re-consent and prepay for break-glass admin custody, MFA recovery, backup verification, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, license renewal calendar, and emergency contact transfer. Strong pain, but tenant-security liability and MSP competition are heavier than Candidate 1.

## Candidate 3: Transactional Email / Deliverability Custody Succession

Acquire small ecommerce/email-sending admin books around SMTP, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, sender domains, invoice emails, Woo/PrestaShop transactional mail, and renewal calendars. Prepaid custody before close. Risk: can collapse into email consulting unless actual sender-domain/admin access is delegated.

## Candidate 4: B2B Domain Portfolio Renewal Rescue

Acquire neglected B2B domain portfolios from closing SMEs, holding companies, and webmasters only when buyer companies prepay for exact domain rescue or license. Risk: domainers and registrars copy; buyer demand is episodic.

## Candidate 5: Industrial Remote-Access Credential Custody

Acquire retiring automation/IT integrator remote-access/VPN credential books only after factories re-consent and prepay for credential inventory, owner-controlled password escrow, break-glass process, and revocation plan. Strong downtime/security pain, but cybersecurity liability and plant trust are high.

## Candidate 6: Accounting Software Admin Continuity Custody

Acquire tiny bookkeepers' admin/password/renewal custody for Fakturownia, wfirma, Comarch, Subiekt, BaseLinker, payroll and bank-file workflows only after clients re-consent and prepay. Risk: too close to accounting support/KSeF failed territory unless strictly admin custody and no accounting work.

## Gate Rule For Scorers

Apply the existing persistent-loop caps:

- cap below `82` if no concrete 2-month control proof;
- cap below `85` if copyable by one vendor contract, equipment purchase, or internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, owner earnings, legal/admin transfer, seller sourcing, client re-consent, prepaid cash, or support scope are not credible;
- require simulated score strictly `>87` to reach the real Zero To One browser gate.
