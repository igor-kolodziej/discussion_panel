## Candidate That Cleared Simulated Gate

**Orphan Tooling Reorder Rights Roll-Up** scored **88/100**.

Control tier: **Tier 1.5: physical tooling + usage/title rights + dormant customer demand**.

Scoring caveats:

- Tool ownership/title may be messy; many molds are customer-owned even if stored at the toolshop.
- Reorder demand may be stale; a tool archive can be dead inventory.
- Production routing can create quality/liability problems if replacement parts fail.
- Toolshops and liquidators can copy locally, but usually do not want messy long-tail reactivation.

Required strengthening before real Zero To One:

- Require written title or usage authorization per tool.
- Require signed toolshop option covering physical custody/inspection, CAD/CAM/drawings, reorder history, phone/domain or customer handoff, and owner support.
- Require at least one reactivated buyer PO/deposit before treating the POC as validated.
- Frame competition correctly: Xometry/Protolabs/Polish toolshops are substitutes for new production, not holders of the orphaned tool/right archive.
