## Fatal Pattern Extracted

The hard-reset shape improved novelty but still did not produce a confirmed venture-grade idea. Blind supplier identity helps protect supply, but it does not solve:

- buyer trust during emergencies;
- liability and quality/certification concerns;
- stochastic demand matching;
- founder-heavy supply audits;
- incumbent imitation after the first few transactions;
- unproven owner earnings after failed matches, support, insurance, and custody.

## Next Pivot

Do not keep widening blind-capacity marketplaces. Round 30 must be narrower:

- one exact scarce asset class;
- buyer payment/deposit before broad supply buildout;
- physically owned or contractually controlled stock/capacity, not just options;
- immediate cash conversion from a single proof transaction;
- no broad network needed to prove the idea.
