## Fatal Pattern Extracted

Grid-position ideas are closer to the hard-control target than most prior rounds. They failed because:

- clean connection/project assets may already be priced by informed sellers;
- the work is bespoke legal/technical origination;
- buyers need diligence and investment committees, creating slow cash conversion;
- developers, energy lawyers, grid brokers, and infrastructure funds can copy the workflow;
- the founder's advantage must be proprietary stalled-project sourcing, not merely knowing that grid positions are scarce.

## Next Pivot

Do not keep iterating within generic energy origination. Round 28 should target government-created or platform-created cash rights with:

- automatic payment or refund path;
- assignable or controllable claim/credit/certificate;
- low litigation;
- under-100k acquisition;
- buyer-side demand that is immediate and recurring.
