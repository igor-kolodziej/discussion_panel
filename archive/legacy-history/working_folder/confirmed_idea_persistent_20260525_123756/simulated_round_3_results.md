## Repeated Fatal Objections

- Standalone emergency inventory gets capped when the supply stream is not exclusive and demand is not already routed.
- Route or option control must include active transaction flow, deployed assets, phone/domain/customer habit, invoices, and transition support, not just a list or sourcing plan.
- Healthcare/dental ideas remain trust-heavy unless the founder controls actual clinic/lab case flow or signed cohort access.
- Compliance/evidence layers are not enough unless the startup controls a release/payment/acceptance event.

## Material Pivot For Round 4

Round 4 should start from micro-operator succession where the founder can option an already-running route book and its physical assets within two months:

- active customer book;
- phone/domain/call routing;
- deployed containers/cylinders/devices at customer sites;
- active/recent invoices and repeat collections/deliveries;
- owner transition support;
- non-compete and reactivation rights;
- preferably a regulated or compliance-shaped transaction flow.

The next strongest pattern is not "buy inventory and hope." It is "option a tiny recurring operator whose deployed assets already route demand."
