# Round 22: Assigned Rights And Expiring Transaction Assets

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 21 proved that local micro-operator route books can produce owner income but do not create a Zero-To-One quality position. The next search therefore moved to rights that can be assigned, optioned, or physically controlled before a broad market notices:

- statutory receivables and add-on claims;
- renewable-energy registry assets and compensation rights;
- exclusive local feedstock/offtake rights;
- distressed IP/domain/content bundles;
- official invoice/settlement control;
- expiring customs or regulatory transaction paths.

The hard filter remains:

- cap below `82` if a 60-day control proof is not concrete;
- cap below `85` if one incumbent can copy it with one new service line, one vendor contract, or one internal process;
- cap below `87` if CAC, payback, margins, and owner earnings are not credible.

## Immediate Cuts From Source/Agent Feedback

- Deposit-System Cash Rights Route: live and testable but scored 64 by a current-facts subagent because the economics collapse to 0.10-0.25 PLN per PET/can before labor unless there is direct operator-side economics. Do not advance without bulk redemption and dense high-volume accounts.
- 90-Day Customs Cargo Rescue: scored 72 by a current-law subagent. It has real urgency but too much downside from customs debt, liens, product compliance, and broker-channel copy risk.
- RES Curtailment Claim Buyer: scored 76 by a current-law subagent. The real market exists, but PSE/WOZE self-service and O&M/law-firm copy risk cap it unless a proprietary underpayment benchmark appears.
