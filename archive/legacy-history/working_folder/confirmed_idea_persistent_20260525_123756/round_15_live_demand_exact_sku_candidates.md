## Candidate Preference Before Scoring

The first candidate to advance hardest is PLC/HMI Line-Down Installed-Base Donor Bank. It directly fixes the Orphan Tooling weakness: title is clear because inventory is purchased or optioned, physical usability is demonstrable by bench-test video, and demand is live because exact-SKU failure can stop production. The hard question is whether the installed-base archive and same-day Polish custody are enough to beat existing obsolete-parts dealers.

Second preference is Lab Instrument Release-Block Donor Bank. It has strong buyer pain and high ticket size but may be capped by OEM/exchange competition and qualification requirements.

The contract-book candidates are economically attractive but likely face consent/licensing friction and slower transfer decisions. They remain useful fallback shapes if donor-bank competition caps the score.
