## Current Best Candidate

**CrateSlot ISPM-15 Export Rescue** is the strongest control-first candidate in this set because:

- The control point is certified capacity, not a report: emergency production slots and compliant marking through a registered ISPM-15 producer.
- Official Polish lists expose many small registered producers; an unknown founder can realistically offer one micro-packer demand capture and after-hours sales rather than asking a large incumbent for partnership.
- Customer pain is urgent and measurable: an export shipment can be delayed, reworked, or rejected if wood packaging lacks compliant ISPM-15 treatment/marking or if custom load-securing is wrong.
- POC does not require buying machinery. The founder only needs a signed slot/phone-routing option, a crate design/quote workflow, and 3 paid rush jobs.

## Source Anchors

- PIORiN/Gov.pl explains that wood packaging should be heat treated to at least 56 C for at least 30 minutes and recommends ISPM-15-compliant packaging for exports to third countries.
- PIORiN publishes a national list of codes of wood packaging material producers under FAO/IPPC/ISPM 15.
- European Commission states EU wood-packaging requirements are based on ISPM 15 and that compliant wood packaging is treated, officially marked, and debarked for relevant trade.
- Commercial Polish producers visibly offer ISPM-15 certified custom pallets/crates and treatment services, supporting supply feasibility while also showing the copy risk.
