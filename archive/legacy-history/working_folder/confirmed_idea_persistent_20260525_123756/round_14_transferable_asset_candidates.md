# Round 14 Transferable Asset And Route-Rights Candidates

Created: 2026-05-25

This round starts from the fatal objections in Round 13. The bar is no longer "urgent problem plus owned equipment." Each finalist must control a transferable asset, contract book, archive, installed-base record, assigned recovery right, route right, or title/custody flow that can be proven in 2 months under 100,000 PLN.

## Why These Were Chosen

- They control transferable assets or recurring transaction routes, not just advice or emergency labor.
- They can produce proof before serious capital: signed options, assigned contracts, recovered assets, first paid jobs, or inventory custody.
- They are narrow enough for an unknown Polish founder to source by cold outreach, classifieds, local business succession, and direct buyer pain.
- They avoid the failed pattern of generic compliance evidence packs, dashboards, health trust claims, and one-off equipment rentals.
