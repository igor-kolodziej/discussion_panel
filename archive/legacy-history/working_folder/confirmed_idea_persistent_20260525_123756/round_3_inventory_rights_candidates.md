# Round 3 Inventory/Rights Candidates

Date: 2026-05-25 Europe/Warsaw.

## Pivot Thesis

Rounds 1-2 showed that calendars and credential custody are credible cash-flow wedges but get capped as copyable local services. Round 3 uses harder control points:

- owned or optioned exact-match physical inventory;
- buyer-paid deposits before deep inventory spend;
- signed first-look streams from retiring/modernizing operators;
- professional-only use where the startup controls the availability bottleneck but qualified users control clinical/technical execution.

## Finalists For Simulated Round 3

### R3F1: ImplantKey Legacy Scanbody And Driver Bank

ImplantKey buys or options reusable, non-implantable legacy dental implant tooling from retiring dentists, prosthodontists, oral surgeons, and dental labs: drivers, torque-tool tips, scan bodies, impression copings, lab analogs, healing-cap identification samples, CAD libraries, and system catalogs. It sells dentist-paid case search/reservation deposits and professional-only rentals/sales to dentists and labs handling inherited, unknown, discontinued, or orphaned implant cases.

Buyer: dentists, prosthodontists, implant coordinators, and dental labs with a patient who has an older implant and needs a screw, crown, scan, abutment, or restoration path without explant surgery.

Acute trigger: a 50-60-year-old patient presents with a loose/broken crown, unknown implant, missing screw/driver, no original dentist records, discontinued system, or failed restoration. The dentist cannot proceed until the system is identified and the exact driver/scan body/lab analog is available.

Hard control point: owned or optioned exact-match non-implantable tools and digital compatibility assets for 3-5 named legacy systems, plus dentist-paid case deposits. The POC explicitly excludes sale of unsealed, expired, repackaged, or unknown-provenance implantable components.

2-month proof: secure 5-8 signed asset options or purchases from retiring dentists/labs under 50,000 PLN; build a catalog of 150+ exact-match professional-use items; contract one implant prosthetics technician for triage; collect 10 dentist-paid case deposits of 300-1,000 PLN; complete 3 paid rentals/sales/searches; kill if dentists refuse to deposit or if useful inventory is not found.

First acquisition: direct outreach to retiring dentists/labs, dental Facebook/forum groups, exact-match SEO around "unknown implant", "jaki implant", "scanbody [brand]", and dentist/lab outbound with "do not throw away old implant kits".

Why it may clear the gate: exact physical assets cannot be copied once optioned, the avoided-cost wedge is large, cash can convert through deposits before inventory spend, and the first market is narrow enough to dominate in Poland/CEE.

### R3F2: BoilerBoard Emergency Gas-Boiler PCB Donor Bank

BoilerBoard buys, tests, and options obsolete/refurbished PCB/controller boards for common Polish gas boilers and sells emergency professional-only exchange/rental inventory to licensed heating technicians.

Buyer: gas/heating technicians and small service firms whose customer has no heat/hot water because an older Vaillant/Junkers/Viessmann/De Dietrich/Termet boiler board failed.

Acute trigger: no heat/hot water, obsolete or slow OEM board, customer facing replacement boiler quote, technician needs exact board fast.

Hard control point: owned tested board inventory, donor boilers/boards, SKU compatibility matrix, test videos, and technician-paid reservations.

2-month proof: acquire 30-50 boards/donor units under 45,000 PLN, contract electronics repair/test specialist, collect 10 technician reservations/deposits, complete 5 paid emergency sales/exchanges, and prove margin >45%.

Primary caveat: gas-appliance liability; sell only to qualified technicians, not DIY end users.

### R3F3: Intercom Donor Module Bank

Buy/option decommissioned Polish intercom/access modules from modernization contractors and retiring installers; sell exact Laskomex/ACO/Cyfral/Roger modules, RFID readers, panels, receivers, and programming accessories to property managers and local installers.

Buyer: property managers and low-voltage installers with a dead panel/central module in a building where full replacement is expensive and disruptive.

Hard control point: first-look strip-out rights from 5 modernization installers plus owned tested inventory and compatibility notes.

2-month proof: sign 5 first-look rights, acquire 80+ modules under 30,000 PLN, complete 5 paid emergency sales/rentals, and collect 3 building reservation deposits.

### R3F4: Reclaimed R404A/R410A Reservation Pool

Secure first-look rights over recovered refrigerant from small HVACR contractors and route it to certified reclamation/sale partners, then sell reservation deposits to service firms maintaining older supermarket/refrigeration/AC systems.

Buyer: HVACR service companies and equipment owners maintaining high-GWP systems where reclaimed gas availability/pricing matters.

Hard control point: signed recovered-gas first-look rights and certified reclaimer intake/availability, not generic F-gas consulting.

2-month proof: sign 10 contractor first-look agreements, route 50+ kg for certified reclaim intake, collect 5 buyer reservation deposits.

Primary caveat: regulatory/certification and incumbent reclaimers may own the real control point.

### R3F5: Forklift Charger/Controller Donor Bank

Acquire obsolete forklift battery chargers, controller boards, contactors, DC/DC modules, and test fixtures from closing warehouses and forklift service shops; sell exact-match emergency replacements to forklift technicians and small warehouses.

Buyer: forklift service technicians and warehouse operators whose charger/controller failure stops loading operations.

Hard control point: owned tested inventory plus technician request book.

2-month proof: acquire 30-40 SKUs under 50,000 PLN, get 10 paid request deposits, complete 5 emergency sales/rentals.

### R3F6: Dental Autoclave Loaner And Parts Bank

Buy validated used class-B autoclaves and repair parts, then sell same-day loaner continuity to dental/beauty/vet clinics whose sterilizer failure stops procedures.

Buyer: small clinics with one sterilizer, urgent patient schedule, and service delay.

Hard control point: owned validated loaner inventory and service-shop route book.

2-month proof: buy 4-6 used validated autoclaves under 70,000 PLN, sign 3 repair-shop referral routes, complete 5 paid loaner rentals.

Primary caveat: visible incumbents already offer loaner equipment, so likely weaker than ImplantKey.

### R3F7: ISPM-15 Export Pallet Rescue Operator

Buy or reserve heat-treated, stamped ISPM-15 pallets/crates from small certified packaging shops and export packers, then sell same-day export-shipment rescue inventory to SMEs whose non-compliant pallets would hold a shipment at dispatch, customs, port, or customer receiving.

Buyer: Polish exporters, freight forwarders, small manufacturers, and industrial packaging coordinators with urgent export shipments outside the EU where untreated or incorrectly documented wood packaging can stop the load.

Acute trigger: freight is ready, buyer/exporter discovers pallets or crates are not ISPM-15 stamped/documented, or forwarder/customer rejects the packaging before loading.

Hard control point: owned or prepaid reserved stamped pallets/crates plus signed same-day availability from 3-5 small certified heat-treatment/packaging shops; export-lane compliance template and proof photos attach to each lot.

2-month proof: reserve or buy 500-1,500 compliant pallets/crates under 40,000 PLN; sign 3 first-look/reservation agreements; collect 10 paid emergency reservations or rush deliveries; prove >35% gross margin after transport.

Primary caveat: if certified packaging shops already solve urgent demand directly, the startup becomes a courier/broker unless it controls prepaid inventory in locations where same-day availability is scarce.

### R3F8: Assignable BESS/Grid-Connection Option Packets

Originate tiny 1-5 MW battery-storage/cable-pooling project packets by securing landowner options, power-of-attorney, survey data, and submitted or issued grid-connection documentation; sell only legally assignable packets to storage developers on paid diligence deposits.

Buyer: Polish BESS developers and renewable-energy project shops looking for small, low-friction sites with land control and grid paperwork already started.

Acute trigger: developers need a pipeline of sites but DSO paperwork, landowner discovery, and cable-pooling negotiation are slow and fragmented.

Hard control point: signed land option plus document custody and assignment language, not a lead list. The asset is the packet: land rights, connection-file status, energy-production context, and owner authorization.

2-month proof: sign 3 landowner options near existing PV/wind/grid infrastructure under 30,000 PLN in option fees and surveys; receive one written DSO filing receipt or written technical pre-check; collect one developer-paid diligence deposit or LOI with refundable diligence fee.

Primary caveat: grid processes may be too slow for a true 60-day proof, and if the packet is not legally assignable it is only consulting.

### R3F9: Deposit-System rPET/Aluminium Inventory Bank

Secure first-look or prepaid collection-node rights over clean deposit-return PET bottle and aluminium can flows, then sell tagged, weighed, photographed, contamination-checked lots to recyclers, packaging buyers, or processors who need predictable local feedstock.

Buyer: recyclers, can/PET processors, packaging buyers, and operators that value clean, traceable post-consumer material as Poland's deposit-return system scales.

Acute trigger: buyers want predictable clean feedstock, while small collection nodes and local operators lack a commercial capture layer.

Hard control point: signed first-look/consignment rights from collection nodes plus physical lot custody and lot-level QA documentation.

2-month proof: sign 5 collection-node first-look agreements; control 3-10 tons of material; collect one buyer deposit or paid offtake test; prove positive unit margin after hauling, sorting, and rejects.

Primary caveat: economics may be crushed by incumbent recyclers/logistics firms unless the startup controls unusually clean local nodes or a buyer pays for traceability.

## Recommendation Before Simulation

Advance R3F1 hardest. It has the strongest combination of:

- exact inventory control;
- high avoided-cost willingness to pay;
- 50-60-year-old patient relevance;
- professional buyer gate;
- buyer deposits before deep inventory;
- non-obvious supply from retiring dentists/labs.

R3F2 and R3F3 are practical but face stronger existing parts-market competition. R3F4 may be regulatorily attractive but likely controlled by certified reclaimers. R3F6 has visible incumbents.
