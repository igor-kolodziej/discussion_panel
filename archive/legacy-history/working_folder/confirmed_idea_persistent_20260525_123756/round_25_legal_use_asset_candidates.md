# Round 25: Legal-Use Assets With Attached Export Or Operating Rights

Created: 2026-05-25

## Pivot Rationale

Round 24 failed because reservable capacity can be copied by the incumbent who already owns the technician, workshop, vehicle, or customer trust. Round 25 therefore shifts to **legal-use assets** that already exist and cannot be recreated quickly by a buyer:

- active import/export product registrations;
- quota/history/permit accounts;
- approved facilities or establishment numbers;
- licensed company shells where the operating right is the scarce asset.

The target is not generic compliance. The startup must option or control the legal-use asset and then resell/route transaction flow through it.

## Source Anchors

- CIRS summarizes China MARA rules: imported feed/feed additives and pet food must be registered with MARA and obtain a registered licence; foreign manufacturers use a Chinese agent, and pet compound feed/feed additive premix are covered: https://www.cirs-group.com/en/food/mara-imported-feed-and-pet-food-registration
- FDA confirms China maintains product registration and licensing requirements for feed additives, premix, and compound feed separate from GACC facility registration, and that completion is often a prerequisite for import into China: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/cvm-updates/fda-explains-revised-submission-process-us-animal-feed-additive-premix-and-compound-feed-facilities
- ChinaFeed/MARA announcement PDF for 2026 registrations lists Polish UNIPRO Sp. z o.o. products including Vetfood FLORActiv, ACID Balance, CARDIO Force, and URO FORCE as pet nutritional supplements / pet feed additive premix for dogs/cats, certificate numbers `(2026) 外饲准字265-268`, valid `2026.02-2031.02`: https://www.chinafeed.org.cn/xwdt_10092/hyxw/202603/P020260316763807268463.pdf
- USDA FAS says China's 2025 pet food market is expected to grow 6-8% in high-end and functional products, with high-demand categories including nutritional supplements: https://www.fas.usda.gov/data/china-pet-food-market-update-2025
- Trade.gov.pl says Poland was the 5th largest dog/cat food exporter by share in 2022 and Polish dog/cat food exports reached PLN 9.6B in 2024, up 9% year over year: https://www.trade.gov.pl/en/news/poland-position-is-growing-in-strength-on-the-pet-food-market/
- GITD manages road-transport licences and permits in Poland, including Community licences and foreign permits: https://www.gov.pl/web/gitd/licencje-zezwolenia-zaswiadczenia
- ChinaFeed and feed-registration sources also show the registration process is product/facility-specific and time-consuming, which creates potential value in already registered SKUs.

## Initial Read Before Scoring

The strongest candidate is **China-Registered Polish Pet Supplement SKU Rights Desk**. It differs from prior supplement/compliance ideas because the product registration is already issued, named, valid through 2031, and tied to exact SKUs. The founder's control point is not a report, dashboard, or marketplace: it is an exclusive export/distribution option over a legally importable product asset plus stock allocation and Chinese importer handoff.

The other legal-use ideas are kept as comparators but likely suffer from licence-transfer friction, operating liability, or incumbent direct sourcing.
