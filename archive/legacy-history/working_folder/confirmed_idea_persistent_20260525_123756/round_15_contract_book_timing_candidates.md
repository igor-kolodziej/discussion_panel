## Simulated Selection Logic

HeatLedger is selected first because it changes the failed pattern most materially:

- It starts from an already-paid recurring workflow, not speculative future demand.
- The control point is assignable contracts and installed-device data, not generic service labor.
- The EU remote-reading deadline creates time pressure before 1 January 2027.
- The unknown founder can secure one small book through succession/earnout rather than convincing institutions from zero.
- The POC can prove control with written transfer consent and collected cash, not only customer interest.

The other candidates remain fallback candidates if HeatLedger fails real validation.
