## Selection Logic

The PLC/HMI exchange book is the first candidate that directly answers the previous real-chat objections:

- rights are cleaner than orphan tooling because stock is physically purchased or optioned by SKU;
- physical usability is testable before sale through bench videos and serial records;
- demand is live and urgent because exact-part failures stop production;
- the installed-base archive improves targeting but is not the only value source;
- ticket size can support testing, returns, courier, and contractor time.

The HPLC/GC donor bank is economically attractive but more trust-heavy and regulated-lab-sensitive. Cold-room controllers have urgent pain but weaker defensibility because refrigeration installers and wholesalers can copy the stocking motion. Alarm/elevator books remain capped by consent, licensing, trust, and liability.
