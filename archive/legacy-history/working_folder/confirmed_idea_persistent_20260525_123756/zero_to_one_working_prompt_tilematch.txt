Idea: TileMatch Storm-Repair Roof Tile Bank.

TileMatch buys or options exact lots of discontinued and reclaimed Polish roof tiles and accessories from roof replacements, demolitions, and leftover stock: ceramic/cement tiles, ridge tiles, edge tiles, ventilation tiles, and old profiles/colors from brands such as Braas, Koramic, Roben, Tondach, Creaton, Prodach, and local prewar/PRL-era producers. It sells urgent exact-match repair lots to roofers, property managers, loss adjusters, and homeowners after wind/hail/leak repairs where replacing 20-300 matching tiles avoids full-slope or full-roof replacement.

Buyer: roofers and property managers first; insurers/loss adjusters second; homeowners only through roofers where possible.

Acute trigger: storm/hail/wind damage, leak, cracked tile line, insurance claim, or renovation where the existing roof tile is discontinued, visually mismatched, or only available as scattered reclaimed lots.

Control point: owned or optioned exact physical lots, SKU/profile/color/photo catalog, AI-assisted visual matching workflow, paid search/reservation deposits, and first-look pickup rights from roofers/demolition crews. The startup does not broker generic roofing work; it controls the exact material.

2-month proof under 100,000 PLN: sign 10 first-look/pickup rights with roof replacement or demolition crews; acquire or reserve 30 named lots under 35,000 PLN; catalog 15,000+ tiles/accessories with photos, measurements, profile/color notes, location and pickup/delivery cost; collect 10 paid exact-match search deposits of 150-500 PLN from roofers/property managers/loss adjusters; complete 5 paid sales with >50% gross margin after pickup/storage/delivery. Kill if buyers refuse deposits or if logistics consume margin.

Cold start: scrape OLX/Allegro Lokalnie and roofing Facebook groups for named lots; call roofers/demolition crews with "we pay for reusable tiles you would dump"; run exact-query landing pages for common discontinued profiles; outbound to roofers/property managers with a photo-matching request form and "send us three photos and measurements; we either find a match or refund the search deposit."

Pricing: search/reservation deposit 150-500 PLN; urgent lot sale 500-5,000 PLN depending on rarity and quantity; delivery/pallet handling charged separately; premium for ridge/edge/ventilation accessories where full replacement or visible mismatch is expensive. Target buy price 0-3 PLN/tile from demolition/leftover stock and sell price 5-25 PLN/tile or accessory-equivalent premium when exact match avoids a much larger repair.

Why this is not a generic marketplace: no value is claimed without owned/reserved inventory, item-level photos/measurements, paid search deposits, and first-look intake from crews before lots scatter into classifieds or landfill. The company is the exact-match material bank and routing layer, not a list of links.

Domination path: start in Mazowieckie/Lodz/Wielkopolska, dominate exact-match roof tile requests for 20-30 common profiles/colors, become the call roofers make before telling a customer/insurer that no match exists, then expand into facade brick/clinker and commercial flooring exact-match repair lots. The compounding asset is the physical lot base plus a profile/color/request/outcome database.
