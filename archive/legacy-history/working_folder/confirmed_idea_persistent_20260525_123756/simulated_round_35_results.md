## Fatal Pattern Extracted

Mobile app-store assets have cleaner transfer mechanics than customer-list or service-book acquisitions, but they still fail the gate because:

- good live-revenue apps are unlikely to sell cheaply;
- cheap apps are likely cheap for a reason: low revenue, bad content rights, stale content, official/free competition, support/update debt, or platform risk;
- one app transfer proves control, not a repeatable acquisition edge;
- owner earnings require a portfolio, and repeatable sourcing is unproven under 100,000 PLN.

## Decision

Do not contact real Zero To One for Round 35. No idea cleared simulated `>87`.

Next pivot must avoid:

- labor-heavy professional services;
- scarce micro-SaaS/app acquisition targets;
- generic customer-book roll-ups;
- broad asset acquisitions where good targets are priced efficiently.

Search for assets with a forced-liquidity/salvage/timing event where acquisition price is structurally low because the seller's alternative is immediate loss, disposal, penalty, or stranded value.
