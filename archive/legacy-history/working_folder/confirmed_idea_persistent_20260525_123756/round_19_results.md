## Lesson

Buyer-paid bounties are useful for POC design, but subagents still view them as workflow services unless the startup controls a scarce supply asset, statutory right, facility slot, settlement rail, or exclusive physical/transaction flow.

## Next Pivot

Move away from compliance-bounty rails. Score ideas where:

- a transaction physically cannot happen without reserved capacity or assigned flow;
- the startup has an exclusive slot, option, title, or settlement right;
- the first buyer pays for access to that controlled asset, not for chasing paperwork.
