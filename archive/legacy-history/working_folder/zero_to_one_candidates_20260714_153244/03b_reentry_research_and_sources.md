# Research and Sources — Generator Re-entry

## Evidence

- [PFRON workplace adaptation](https://www.pfron.org.pl/pracodawcy/adaptacja-stanowisk-pracy/) permits reimbursement for workplace adaptations, devices, assistive technologies, and software; the maximum is twenty times average pay per adapted workstation. Spending before the employer's agreement is not assumed reimbursable.
- [PFRON workplace equipment](https://www.pfron.org.pl/pracodawcy/wyposazenie-stanowisk-pracy/) permits up to fifteen times average pay for qualifying newly employed disabled workers, with a 36-month employment obligation and a positive PIP opinion.
- The government's [2026 employer support summary](https://niepelnosprawni.gov.pl/dofinansowania-i-ulgi-dla-pracodawcy/) states up to 141,300 PLN for workplace equipment and confirms support for assistive technologies and adaptation.
- Poland had [152 Zakłady Aktywności Zawodowej and 8,178 disabled employees in them at end-2025](https://niepelnosprawni.gov.pl/baza-wiedzy/niepelnosprawnosc-w-liczbach/rynek-pracy/dane-dotyczace-zakladow-aktywnosci-zawodowej/), providing a finite, public first-market map. This does not reveal hearing-loss counts or budgets.
- Polish workplace-sign rules and PIP machine guidance require signals appropriate to worker perception; the exact safety use and conformity route must be assessed per deployment. [Official regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2003/1650/text.html), [PIP machine guidance](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/porady-prawne/maszyny-i-inne-urzadzenia-techniczne?tmpl=pdf%3Ftmpl%3Dpdf).
- Adjacent haptic alert competition exists: [Reactec](https://www.reactec.com/products/r-link/havs/) supports multi-haptic alerts and messages for workers; [MDH](https://www.mdh-uk.co.uk/) and [ALRM](https://www.getalrm.com/) provide vibrating/deaf emergency alarms; [AlarmAlly](https://european-social-fund-plus.ec.europa.eu/en/social-innovation-match/case-study/alarmally) is a Polish fire-alarm accessibility solution. AndonBridge must remain a configurable machine/production-event workcell interface, not a general fire or proximity alarm.
- Dry-electrode geometry, pressure, hair, and motion affect contact performance, while a [2025 low-cost printed EEG phantom](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40871838/) demonstrates material feasibility. [Geometry study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13265750/), [flower electrode study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10547758/).
- [Fraunhofer PhantomHead](https://www.idmt.fraunhofer.de/en/institute/projects-products/projects/phantomhead.html) and [Brain Products LabSim](https://www.brainproducts.com/solutions/labsim/) are adjacent competition; ScalpTile must be a reproducible consumable contact-QA substrate, not a generic phantom or signal generator.
- EMG research repeatedly identifies cross-session/electrode-shift failures. [Electrode-shift work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02773), [GRABMyo public dataset](https://www.physionet.org/content/grabmyo/1.1.0/), and [EMaGer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38083603/) show both the problem and strong free-data competition.
- Human biosignal data needs a valid GDPR Article 9 basis when it is health data; pseudonymized data remains personal. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/), [EDPB anonymization explanation](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme-data-protection-guide/faq-frequently-asked-questions/answer/what-difference-between_en).

## Hypotheses

- An employer will pay 18,000–35,000 PLN for an accessible workcell pilot before or alongside reimbursement and will value production independence rather than only legal compliance.
- A dry-contact device team will reorder calibrated disposable contact tiles at 150–400 PLN each and accept serialized calibration traceability from an unknown supplier.
- An EMG-interface buyer will prepay a 12,000–25,000 PLN protocol reservation for a condition absent from free datasets before participant recruitment.
- Every price, conversion, margin, retention, and founder-wealth assumption in the new briefs remains unvalidated.
