# Zero-to-One Evaluation: SignalChain Sentinel exact v1 after panel

## Diagnosis

The independent panel objections confirm that the canonical's core control point arrives too late: the buyer can reproduce the release decision internally before adapter history or cross-vendor data compounds. Both judges also identify the same bespoke-integration, unproven-WTP, and supplier-qualification risks. The exact candidate still passes the formal wealth model and reachability arithmetic, but the acquisition and defensibility assumptions are too weak for `pursue`. A revision must change the buyer's urgent event and obtain contractual control of an operational release decision or physical fleet, rather than sell optional QA back to the product maker.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence remains the published update/data-quality problem and affordable replay equipment. New reviewer verdicts: scores 70 and 78, both Tier 2 conditional/provisional, both naming OEM/internal-lab reproduction as strongest copy risk. Hypotheses still unproved: external annual willingness to pay, reusable adapters, anonymized rights, short procurement, and 70–80% recurring gross margin. The reviewers' agreement is judgment, not market evidence, but it exposes the same structural dependency from two independent readings.

## Overall Score

64/100. Weighted total 128 across total weight 20: `10 × (128 / 20) = 64`.

## Control Point

Tier 3 in practical launch conditions. The asset is directly buildable, but the buyer can substitute internal QA through an equipment purchase and routine process change before switching costs exist. Strongest copy risk: OEM or test-equipment incumbent internalizes the full chain.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Pass on spreadsheet structure, fail on confidence. Expected founder net worth is modeled at 23.8m PLN with correct ownership, reinvestment, debt, and no double count, but 150 recurring lines and 37% operating margin depend on the disproven assumption that external monitoring resists internalization. The gate cannot support `pursue` when operating scale is the same weak point named by both judges.

## Reachability Gate

Pass with reduced confidence. A deposit can theoretically be won under 35,000 PLN and within 180 days, but buyer qualification/calibration requirements may lengthen the cycle. It remains compatible with employment and has no clinic/partnership dependency.

## Expansion Check

The product can add modalities, but each adds fixtures and validation labor. Cross-vendor breadth does not become exclusive. EU positioning is insufficient against equipment vendors or internal QA.

## Fatal Objections

1. Same buyer controls an adequate substitute and has confidentiality incentives to internalize it.
2. Device-specific integration undermines recurring margins and founder time.
3. External WTP and qualification path are unproved.
4. Expected wealth requires precisely the scale that these weaknesses obstruct.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A known-input physical-to-cloud baseline can reveal release changes that component tests miss; value increases when it controls an operational decision rather than offering optional external QA.
