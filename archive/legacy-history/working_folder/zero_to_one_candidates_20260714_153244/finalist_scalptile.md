# Zero-to-One Candidate: ScalpTile Calibration Consumables

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell serialized, calibrated artificial scalp-and-hair contact tiles as a recurring engineering and production-QA consumable for dry EEG, ear-EEG, EMG, and head-worn electrode teams that cannot use human heads as a stable reference.

## Founder Fit

The founder understands biosignals, EEG, noisy interfaces, data calibration, and ML; a materials/mechanical contractor closes the main gap. The business creates neurohardware/material IP, calibration datasets, test-manufacturer distribution, and future-lab expertise without patients or a clinic.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a dry-contact electrode or headset hardware lead freezing an electrode geometry, supplier lot, or assembly process. Human contact varies; simple conductive foam lacks hair/elasticity/impedance behavior; a full phantom is expensive and not a disposable batch reference. The buyer risks late redesign or inconsistent incoming/production QA.

## Product Wedge

A pack of serialized 10×10 cm interchangeable tiles with specified geometry, synthetic hair density, elastic modulus range, surface/contact impedance curve, and calibration certificate. The customer repeatedly presses its electrode/mount against the tile under a defined load/motion script and compares batches/designs. V1 is research/pre-compliance equipment with no skin-safety, comfort, or clinical claim.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Existing phantoms reproduce a head or electrical waveform; the missing product is a replaceable, lot-traceable contact-interface reference that an engineer can consume during design and production QA. Recurring revenue comes from physical lots and calibration, not a one-off phantom report.

## Underused Momentum

Low-cost conductive printing and elastomers, dry-electrode growth, and mobile neurotechnology increase contact-interface iteration. Research documents geometry, hair, pressure, and motion effects. The opportunity exists only if a formulation produces stable, discriminating lots.

## Beachhead Market

European dry-EEG/ear-EEG product teams and research-equipment manufacturers with physical electrode production or multiple design lots. Exclude purely academic users needing DIY-cheap materials and regulated biocompatibility testing.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid design/batch comparison with prototype tiles: 12,000–22,000 PLN, 55–70% contribution margin.
- Starter calibration kit: 18,000–35,000 PLN, 45–60% margin.
- Consumables: 180–420 PLN per serialized tile, target 65–78% gross margin at volume.
- Annual calibration/data subscription: 12,000–30,000 PLN per site, 80%+ margin.
- All WTP/volume figures are hypotheses.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10 from CORDIS/EUDAMED/product pages, conference exhibitors, and recent dry-electrode papers; publish repeatability/discrimination data and require 40% preorder before a customer-specific hair/impedance lot. First 100 through direct technical content, sample packs, repeat orders, and later test-equipment distributors after direct economics.

## Primary Control Point

Owned material formulation, production process, calibration rig/curve, serialization database, retained reference lots, and order history. Trade-secret recipes and possibly patents/design rights are secured before scale. Twelve-month proof: three paid customers, three repeat lots, coefficient-of-variation target met, and at least one repeat order.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Spend 18,000–30,000 PLN on conductive/elastomer materials, printing/molds, impedance/load measurement, and fixed-scope material science. Demonstrate stable lots and discriminate two electrodes. Require preorder before custom formulation. No partner, clinic, participant, or finished medical product.

## Distribution Advantage

Teams are enumerable through papers/products/grants. Calibration data is the credibility engine. Repeat consumables and stored reference lots create reordering/switching costs.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Differentiate from full phantoms and LabSim through disposable contact mechanics and batch traceability. Expand scalp to ear, forearm EMG/ECG patches, sweat/temperature variants, and OEM QA packs. Copy risk is a materials/test vendor reproducing formulation; lot history and calibration must compound faster.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Polish materials/prototyping cost and EU neurotech proximity help. Research-use labeling and EU calibration support are entry advantages only.

## Compounding Advantage

Every lot adds stability, failure, and electrode-contact data; retained samples support longitudinal comparison; consumables finance better manufacturing and broader interface libraries.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Ninety-five percent employment-period reinvestment funds process control, molds, calibration, inventory only against orders, IP/FTO, and sales; optional distributions max 5%. Strong success reinvests 100%.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–60 formulation/calibration; days 61–90 publish discrimination data and list 60 teams; days 91–150 seek a 12,000–18,000 PLN preorder/comparison; days 151–180 deliver lot and seek repeat order. Max pre-proof 30,000 PLN, five hours/day with contractor.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

8,000 PLN feasibility; 30,000 PLN hard pre-proof; 60,000 PLN after preorder/delivery; 100,000 PLN after three customers/repeat order. Family capital excluded.

## Critical Dependency Map

Formulation stability/discrimination; FTO; buyer acceptance of non-accredited reference; repeat consumption; global TAM; material contractor capability. All except paid demand can be bench-tested or professionally reviewed.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Five independent payers, three repeat orders, positive lot contribution, reproducible manufacturing, repeatable acquisition, and 12 months runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Formulations, calibration rig/data, molds, and contact-interface expertise can serve patches, EMG, wearables, or be licensed as research materials.

## Critical Evidence

Peer-reviewed low-cost phantom feasibility and dry-contact literature; Fraunhofer/LabSim validate adjacent demand and competition.

## Critical Hypotheses

Stable lots are technically possible under 30k; device teams reorder; 80 accounts are reachable; price/margin hold; formulation is protectable; non-accredited calibration changes decisions.

## Kill Criteria

Coefficient variation cannot meet buyer-useful threshold; no preorder by day 180; three teams prefer DIY; FTO blocks design; consumable demand below 100 tiles/account/year; repeat-order rate below 40%; gross margin below 60%.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

30,000 PLN pre-proof; founder, contract materials/mechanical engineer, neuroengineering calibration reviewer, IP/FTO adviser.
