# Zero-to-One Evaluation: EMG Shift Atlas

## Diagnosis

EMG Shift Atlas directly owns a potentially scarce dataset and cleverly requires buyer sponsorship before a cohort, making reachability strong. But it is still a dataset vendor facing free public corpora, buyer-specific geometry/tasks, uncertain re-licensing rights, and a small visible buyer universe. The expected wealth case requires 60 sponsored modules, 140 licenses, and 90 API clients; conservative barely passes and depends on two licenses per module. Without evidence that a buyer prepays a missing condition, the shape remains speculative and should not be panelled.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: electrode shift/cross-session failure is documented; public datasets exist; modern commercial sEMG validates technical demand; GDPR/EDPB set data constraints. Hypotheses: 12,000–25,000 PLN reservations, 60,000–150,000 PLN modules, multiple licenses, commercial consent durability, recruitability, 25 modules/year. No reviewer verdict.

## Overall Score

79/100. Weighted total 157.5 across total weight 20: `10 × (157.5 / 20) = 78.75`, rounded to 79.

## Control Point

Tier 1 for directly consented data once collected; Tier 3 before a sponsored contract. Strongest copy risk: a well-funded interface company recruits its own exact cohort and keeps task/electrode data exclusive, while free datasets satisfy everyone else.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Pass with low confidence. Conservative reaches about 10.5m only with 25 sponsored modules, 55 licenses, and 35 API clients; expected reaches 37.7m with much larger counts. Reinvestment/ownership/no-double-counting are correct, but the customer universe and multi-license rate are not evidenced and may be heroic.

## Reachability Gate

Pass. A 12,000–25,000 PLN protocol reservation can be sought within 180 days after a 25,000 PLN demonstrator/legal setup; the full cohort is not built first. Direct recruitment avoids a clinic or partner and fits employment with a coordinator.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is clear and data adjacencies broad, but each buyer may need an exact private geometry/task. Large firms internalize; public data moves inward. Poland offers cost, not distribution dominance.

## Fatal Objections

1. No evidence a buyer prepays an external dataset rather than recruits internally.
2. Sponsor exclusivity can prevent profitable re-licensing; non-exclusive rights may reduce first-buyer WTP.
3. Free datasets eliminate generic value and fragment demand into bespoke modules.
4. Founder-wealth scale depends on a large number of scarce technical buyers.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Only buyer-sponsored, deliberately shifted/repeated-use EMG conditions justify proprietary data creation.
