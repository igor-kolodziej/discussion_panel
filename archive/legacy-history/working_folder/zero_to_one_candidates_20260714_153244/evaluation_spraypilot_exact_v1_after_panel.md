# Zero-to-One Evaluation: SprayPilot exact v1 after panel

## Diagnosis

Both independent judges converged at 65 and identified the same structural failure: commodity sensing is sold into a domain where coating incumbents already own applications expertise, customer trust, process variables, and distribution. They also agree that 20–30 pieces may not disentangle confounders and that deployment can remain bespoke, eliminating recurring margins. The exact candidate's formal wealth and paid-proof models remain arithmetically possible, but the control point is absent at first sale and may never become scarce. A pivot must move value to a buyer with an urgent warranty/customer-failure event and directly controlled transaction/workflow, not simply improve the sensor.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence is limited to existing training spend, affordable sensing, and public shop discovery. New reviewer verdicts: two scores of 65; both classify launch control as Tier 3 and name a coating incumbent's distribution/expertise as the strongest copy risk. Hypotheses still unproved: recurring WTP, adequate sample size, hazard admission, cross-shop reuse, rights, worker adoption, and owner-led conversion. Reviewer agreement is judgment, not market evidence, but it confirms the same structural exposure.

## Overall Score

58/100. Weighted total 115 across total weight 20: `10 × (115 / 20) = 57.5`, rounded to 58.

## Control Point

Tier 3. The owned software and proposed derivative data are not yet scarce, exclusive, or proven reusable. Strongest copy risk: coating-equipment/training incumbent bundles sensing and feedback through its existing channel and richer process access.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Fail on confidence. The arithmetic reaches 10.3m conservative and 37.1m expected, but both rely on recurring subscriptions, reusable calibration, and low-customization deployment that both judges directly reject as unproven. A spreadsheet pass cannot compensate for a service-heavy operating model.

## Reachability Gate

Pass conditionally. A paid shadow pilot can fit 15,000 PLN and 180 days, but hazardous-area approval and unknown-founder credibility remain dependencies. It does not rescue the weak control point.

## Expansion Check

Adjacent skilled processes repeat calibration and channel disadvantages. Existing equipment/training providers move inward more easily than the founder expands outward. Poland is an access wedge only.

## Fatal Objections

1. Incumbents own trust, domain variables, service, and distribution.
2. Technical confounders can prevent a decision-relevant model.
3. Recurring value may vanish after training.
4. Bespoke on-site delivery blocks wealth-scale margins.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Outcome-linked skilled-motion data is more commercially valuable than generic training telemetry.
