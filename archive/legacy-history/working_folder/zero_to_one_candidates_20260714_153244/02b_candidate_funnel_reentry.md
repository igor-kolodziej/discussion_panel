# Candidate Funnel — Generator Re-entry

Candidate generation only — not evaluated or validated. This second pass remains inside the original run folder and explicitly rejects control points that appear only after many deployments.

## Accumulated rejection boundary

- Do not sell optional QA to a buyer that can internalize it with routine equipment/process.
- Do not rely on a commodity sensor plus future pooled data against an incumbent that already owns domain expertise and distribution.
- Do not substitute a trust-heavy claims/clinical service for a weak product.
- Require a control point present at or contractually secured by the first paid deployment.
