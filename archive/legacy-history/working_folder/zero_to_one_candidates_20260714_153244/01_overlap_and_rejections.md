## Cross-Run Vetoes

- Generic AI wrappers, dashboards, CRMs, coaching or reports with no routed workflow or owned asset.
- Commodity sensors or equipment-led measurement services copied by one purchase and one process change.
- Unspecified partnerships, generic coordination, manual marketplaces or future exclusivity.
- Health-data resale or secondary use as the primary control point.
- Clinical diagnosis, treatment, interpretation or authority claims by the founder.
- Service businesses whose founder labour scales approximately one-for-one with revenue.
- Founder-wealth cases supported only by a heroic market share, margin, valuation multiple or dilution assumption.

## Material-Difference Rule

A prior family may re-enter only with a different buyer or urgent event and a materially different acquisition mechanism, economics, distribution and control point. The control point must be directly owned, signed, prepaid, routed, reserved, licensed or buildable under the founder's twelve-month and 100,000 PLN limits. A new sensor, AI feature, report name, pricing claim or target geography is not a material improvement.
