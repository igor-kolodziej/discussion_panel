# Zero-to-One Evaluation: AndonBridge exact v1 after panel

## Diagnosis

The two independent judges agree on the structural failure: the factory's existing PLC integrator owns machine access, trust, and maintenance distribution and can reproduce the workcell using ordinary components before AndonBridge compounds. Both also reject the recurring support assumption and question whether the finite niche supports 350 cells. The exact candidate remains technically reachable, but the founder-wealth path is no longer credible enough and the control point is Tier 3/provisional at launch. A valid pivot must change buyer and channel so the company does not compete head-on with the incumbent integrator for each cell.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence remains PFRON funding, public employer/ZAZ lists, and adjacent haptic products. New verdicts: 61/Tier 3 and 67/provisional Tier 2, both naming incumbent PLC integrator bundling as strongest copy risk. Hypotheses still unproved: recurring support, employer WTP, 180-day procurement, cross-site adapter reuse, 350-cell scale, and ordinary-employer expansion.

## Overall Score

58/100. Weighted total 116 across weight 20: `10 × (116 / 20) = 58`.

## Control Point

Tier 3. Owned firmware/protocol is commodity-adjacent; event maps are customer-specific and the incumbent already controls access. Strongest copy risk is bundled PLC integration and maintenance.

## Founder Wealth Gate

Fail on credibility. The conservative arithmetic reaches 10.1m, but both judges reject the support attachment, 350-cell scale, 32% operating margin, and reuse assumptions. Expected success repeats these weaknesses. Correct reinvestment and no double-counting cannot rescue implausible operating scale.

## Reachability Gate

Pass conditionally. A direct paid pilot can fit 20,000 PLN/180 days, but public/protected-employer procurement and incumbent trust may delay it. Technical reach does not cure distribution/control failure.

## Expansion Check

Broader markets are easier for existing automation/wearable vendors. Poland's funding advantage is shared. The startup does not expand outward more easily than incumbents move inward.

## Fatal Objections

1. Incumbent integrator owns trust, access, and maintenance channel.
2. Recurring support value is unproved and essential to wealth.
3. Initial niche is too small for the conservative scale without unproved expansion.
4. Site-specific labor can prevent compounding.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Accessible haptic event semantics and worker validation can be a product component even if direct workcell integration is not an independent company.

## Recommendation

pivot
