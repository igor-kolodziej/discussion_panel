## Finalist Set

The four finalists are intentionally different:

1. **WardTalk** — direct-pay, mandatory-adjacent clinical-language practice.
2. **VocalReserve** — employer-paid, privacy-preserving closed-loop voice-load intervention.
3. **ProcedurePosture** — leased niche wearable with procedure-aware haptics.
4. **LinePool (Custobot Poland)** — pooled, delegated human–AI negotiation tied to verified savings.

Each has a preliminary credible path to more than 5 million PLN of founder-owned value and a paid proof inside 180 days with no more than 40,000 PLN at risk. Those are planning-stage screens, not gate validation.

## Why Only Four

Adding technically attractive wearable testing, security, repair, simulation, or training concepts would pad the set with business shapes whose historical objections are already known. The four retained candidates expose different decisive unknowns that can be tested cheaply:

- mandatory-event conversion and CEE localisation;
- worker adoption plus employer economic value;
- specialist willingness to pay versus a generic sensor;
- provider acceptance plus case-labour automation.

## Wave-Two Finalist Set

1. **ProofRail Advance** — real-time human–AI field evidence used to underwrite and purchase a contractor receivable.
2. **SignalTrace Evidence Freeze** — urgent forensic custody for synthetic media and mobile/wearable evidence.
3. **LisaGuard Legacy SLS Continuity** — a conditional exclusive legacy-hardware/IP carve-out with real-time first-person service support.

These are candidate-stage briefs, not scores or validation. Each has a preliminary path to the Founder Wealth and Reachability thresholds, but each also has one decisive fact that can kill it cheaply:

- ProofRail: whether the receivable/payment route is enforceable and recoverable;
- SignalTrace: whether an unknown founder can win paid custody work before possessing court-expert status;
- LisaGuard: whether the exact exclusive legacy asset package can be optioned below the 100,000 PLN ceiling.

The set is limited to three because every other second-wave direction failed the prompt-proof, control-point, or conservative wealth test. LinePool remains only a reserve comparison and is not a wave-two finalist.
