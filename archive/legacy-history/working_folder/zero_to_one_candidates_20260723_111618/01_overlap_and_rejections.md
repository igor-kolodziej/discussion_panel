## Distilled Takeaways From 99 Tried Ideas

1. **Own the economic event, not merely a product near it.** Sensors, parsers, reports, fixtures, datasets, and interfaces were repeatedly route-around-able because an OEM, platform, employer, laboratory, clinic, or incumbent vendor owned the decision and workflow.
2. **Reachability and wealth are independent.** Many concepts could obtain a small paid pilot yet still required heroic account counts, unit volumes, margins, market share, or founder labor to reach 5 million PLN.
3. **Data is only a control point when paid delivery secures durable usable rights.** Revocable panels, hoped-for derivative rights, customer-specific recordings, and buyer-demanded exclusivity do not create a reusable moat.
4. **Legal or physical ownership is not automatically economic control.** Purchasable equipment, named lots, ordinary inventory, files, and filed-but-unoptioned patents remain easy to route around.
5. **Trust-heavy workflows defeat unknown founders.** Clinical authority, QMS access, certification, insurer acceptance, OEM design-in, safety liability, and procurement trust cannot all precede the first sale.
6. **Regulation creates relevance, not a budget or mandate for this vendor.**
7. **Wearable hardware is usually a channel business first.** Incumbents already own installed users, distribution, conformity, service logistics, and warranty credibility.
8. **A one-off test does not become recurring because the underlying risk recurs.** Renewal must be linked to a repeated paid event with observable economic value.
9. **Synthetic or standardized tests face a relevance dilemma.** Cheap comparability can make them unlike production; integration can make them bespoke and labor-heavy.
10. **Future-lab asset creation must be intrinsic to the profitable core.** The paid transaction itself must repeatedly create physiological/behavioral data, HCI capability, hardware know-how, research access, IP, skilled-team capability, capital, or distribution.

## Exhausted Or Heavily Penalized Shapes

- Reference phantoms, calibration coupons, artificial materials, ordinary QA rigs, and internal test fixtures.
- Wearable accessories, chargers, connectors, batteries, repair parts, rentals, returns, or end-of-life inventory without exclusive rights or unavoidable routing.
- Clinical-study QA, evidence archives, vigilance, or acceptance tools requiring sponsor/OEM/QMS authority.
- Revocable human panels, generic data foundries, and task corpora whose customers demand exclusivity or robot/platform-specific collection.
- Safety or medical devices needing certification and incumbent adoption before paid proof.
- Consumer subscriptions requiring fragmented trust and large membership before data or distribution compounds.
- Generic approval/consent/privacy hardware that cannot enforce action inside the underlying platform.
- Generic AI wrappers, dashboards, CRMs, compliance memos, consulting, and custom research.
- Test or proficiency products whose recurring willingness to pay is not attached to a mandatory release, transaction, or renewal event.
- Two-sided models needing simultaneous venue/participant and advertiser/buyer liquidity.

## Material Re-Entry Standard

A prior family may re-enter only if the buyer, painful event, budget owner, acquisition channel, value capture, trust mechanism, scarce resource, rights position, transaction control, economics, or timing materially changes. A new name, geography, sensor, feature, or presentation does not qualify.

## Design Boundary For This Run

Prioritize candidates where:

- the first paid contract itself routes a repeated economic decision or transaction;
- consent/data rights or workflow access are bought or contractually secured during ordinary delivery;
- the founder can reach buyers through a listable, asynchronous channel rather than high-status networking;
- the wedge can initially be delivered with commodity hardware or human operations, but each delivery improves proprietary models, data, protocols, distribution, or switching cost;
- the expected wealth case works from a defensible denominator and does not rely on a future unrelated business.

## Exact Historical-Family Checks Added During Research

### Outsourced CRA PSIRT — closed, including a wearable-only relabel

The July 14 run already evaluated **CRA Sector CNA + Outsourced PSIRT Rail** and killed it. Its buyer was a small connected-product manufacturer; its trust mechanism was a hired security expert; its acquisition offer was a paid tabletop; its hoped-for control was a revocable `security.txt`/PSIRT route; and its wealth case depended on productising senior incident-response work. The current “wearable PSIRT cooperative” changes only the device vertical and adds a prepaid bounty reserve. It does not fix rented credibility, easy endpoint switching, incumbent security-firm entry, or service-heavy delivery. It is therefore rejected rather than presented as a new finalist.

### Test bench or release-custody families — not re-entered as finalists

The earlier SignalChain/TrialFleet family established that a routed release decision is better than optional QA, but failed because incumbents could internalise ordinary test equipment and because the proposed buyer did not reliably own release authority. A private-label wearable importer does own its factory-payment decision, which is a material improvement, but TÜV/Eurofins/SGS and existing supplier-QA firms still own trust, methods, and distribution. The proposed 72-hour import-acceptance rail remains a useful research direction, but it is not advanced until a paid importer quality agreement proves that a new specialist—not an incumbent lab—can become the named recurring release gate.

### Consumer N-of-1 trials — closed without a new scarce input

BlindProof already showed that paid personal experiments are technically reachable but that repeat purchase, high consumer willingness to pay for null results, and a conservative acquisition denominator were unproved. The new software-only “N-of-One Trial Club” removes ingestible risk but also removes the earlier physical differentiation. Without exclusive protocols, distribution, or a scarce intervention supply, it is weaker rather than materially improved.

### Poker physiology — closed

TiltLock and StableGuard were already panelled/evaluated. The family failed on buyer/enforcer mismatch, small or unproved buyer denominators, incumbent access to stronger telemetry, and physiology adding uncertain lift over behavioral data. A post-session diagnostic removes enforcement risk but not the distribution, copy, denominator, or incremental-signal objections. It is not re-entered.

### Surgical/OSCE motor scoring versus WardTalk

The prior OSCE idea sold video-based motor-skill assessment through medical-school authority and was rejected because faculty acceptance and incumbent assessment vendors controlled the workflow. **WardTalk is materially different:** it sells directly to foreign clinicians and English-track students facing a statutory/practical Polish-language task; the first transaction requires no university recognition, faculty integration, clinical claim, or school procurement. The control risk moves from institutional authority to direct user retention, local-language content rights, and longitudinal dialogue data. That difference is material enough for candidate-stage consideration.

## Prompt-Proof Test For Wave Two

Reject a direction before finalist status when any of these is true:

- a competent customer can obtain most of the outcome by uploading its documents/data to frontier AI and iterating prompts;
- the only proprietary element is a prompt, workflow template, rubric, report, dashboard, conversation, or ordinary retrieval layer;
- a generic sensor plus an app reproduces the paid benefit;
- the customer can hire one ordinary contractor or incumbent vendor to implement the core control point;
- the proposed dataset appears only after large adoption and does not improve the first customer's economics;
- the pain is aspiration, wellness, convenience, learning, curiosity, or “better decisions” without a priced loss, deadline, denied transaction, or resource constraint.

Passing the prompt-proof test is necessary but not sufficient. The direction must still clear wealth, reachability, founder fit, distribution, and control-point requirements.

## Wave-Two Structural Decisions

### Why cash plus evidence is materially different from another installer app

ProofRail does not charge for a checklist, report, computer-vision score, or subsidy application. It advances cash only after the installer, beneficiary, physical work, invoice, and program-specific evidence are bound into one underwritten transaction. The assigned receivable/payment route and capital exposure are the product. If legal counsel cannot create an enforceable collection route, the idea is killed; it does not fall back to software or consulting.

### Why urgent custody is materially different from a deepfake detector

SignalTrace starts while the source device and accounts still exist. Its deliverable is a defensible preservation package: original files, hashes, acquisition log, app/device/version state, cloud export, and custody record. A classifier output or AI opinion cannot recreate a destroyed source state. Confidential client evidence is not counted as reusable model data; only independently generated reference corpora, validated methods, and non-case-specific device/app matrices may compound.

### Why the SLS idea is not another repair or orphan-device roll-up

LisaGuard advances only if it can purchase an exclusive, named Lisa/Lisa Pro legacy package below the hard capital ceiling. Ordinary spare-parts sourcing, informal cooperation, and a non-exclusive reseller agreement do not qualify. The conservative wealth model must work from the current named installed base through support, parts, materials profiles, refurbishment, and fallback production; it cannot assume acquisition of a second orphaned brand.

### Why HoloLens Guides migration was not advanced

The December 2026 shutdown is a genuine forced event, but Microsoft provides an export route and incumbent AR platforms already advertise migration. Content migration, a replacement runtime, and used headset inventory can be added through one vendor contract. Without exclusive rights or a provable switching-cost loop, urgency alone does not clear the control-point bar.
