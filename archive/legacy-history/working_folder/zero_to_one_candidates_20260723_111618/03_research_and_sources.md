# Research And Sources

Research snapshot: 2026-07-23 CEST. Sources establish context, rules, populations, competitors, or technical feasibility. They do not prove willingness to pay, margins, retention, contract acceptance, or valuation.

## Research Method

- The live founder files and failure brain were read completely.
- Confirmed-idea history was indexed as overlap evidence only.
- Relevant manifests, decisions, advisory evaluations, and panel objections were searched before allowing a failed family to re-enter.
- Public research prioritised official Polish/EU sources, primary research, industry associations, and direct competitor documentation.
- Three independent generation-stage scans covered human–AI transaction control, biosignal economics, and wearable/hardware wedges. They did not score or validate candidates.

## Evidence Discipline For The Next Phase

The four finalist briefs use arithmetic scenarios to test whether a wealth path can exist. They do not treat those assumptions as market evidence. Advisory evaluation should focus first on:

1. whether the conservative denominator is reachable through the stated acquisition mechanism;
2. whether the first paid contract creates the claimed control point rather than only a service engagement;
3. whether the retained data/IP rights are lawful, accepted, and actually reusable;
4. whether labour, support, liability, and hardware replacement preserve the proposed margins;
5. whether an incumbent can copy the core benefit before paid delivery compounds an advantage.

## Wave-Two Evidence Discipline

Before advisory evaluation, the founder should choose which briefs deserve scarce validation effort. For every selected idea, the evaluator should treat the following as unresolved:

1. the exact control contract or asset option;
2. the conservative paid denominator;
3. whether a first paid engagement creates the claimed control rather than a service invoice;
4. loss, liability, labour, and capital costs;
5. whether a bank, laboratory, OEM, or platform can copy the economics through one contract or equipment purchase.
