# Zero-to-One Candidate: LedgerTrial v1

## Idea

LedgerTrial is an independent proficiency-testing subscription for Polish AI-assisted bookkeeping workflows. It sends confidential, rotating batches of synthetic invoices and close tasks with known ground truth through the same AI-plus-human review process a firm intends to use on client work, then measures which errors the model creates, which errors reviewers catch, time to intervention and whether controls improve. Every paying subscriber contributes contractually reusable de-identified interaction outcomes to a pooled human–AI reliability benchmark.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Accounting-firm owners will pay for recurring independent evidence that their AI-plus-reviewer workflow catches costly Polish invoice and bookkeeping failures—not another AI course—and the rotating challenge corpus plus pooled response benchmark can become a defensible human–AI reliability asset.

## Founder Fit

The founder can design controlled experiments, build the AI harness, analyze error patterns and sell a precise go/no-go test. His data-science, AI/ML, neuroscience/behavioral-measurement and finance knowledge fit the product; he does not present himself as an accountant, auditor or tax authority. A fixed-scope senior Polish accountant owns scenario validity and adjudicates disputed cases. Privacy/commercial counsel defines data terms.

The POC is compatible with corporate employment: the founder works at most five hours per day, batch sessions are scheduled, and the accountant can handle daytime adjudication. The idea is killed if buyers require the founder to perform open-ended implementation, tax advice or production bookkeeping.

## Customer And Pain

The initial buyer is the owner, operations head or quality lead of an independent Polish bookkeeping/BPO firm with roughly 20–100 employees that is introducing AI invoice extraction, coding, reconciliation or KSeF assistance across many SME client books.

From the buyer's point of view, the dangerous failure is not that staff cannot define “hallucination.” It is that a convincing automation demo is rolled out widely, reviewers begin rubber-stamping outputs, and a systematic VAT classification, duplicate, supplier or reconciliation error remains quiet across many ledgers. The bookkeeping firm—not the model vendor—faces client remediation, professional liability, lost trust and rework. Vendor accuracy claims test favorable documents; generic training completion tests memory. Neither tests the actual combined AI-plus-human control.

The current workaround is a small internal sample, senior-accountant spot checks, vendor QA or delayed discovery during month-end/client review. These alternatives lack secret rotating cases, cross-vendor comparison and an external peer benchmark. Whether that gap has a recurring budget is the first commercial hypothesis.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

The first paid product is a **9,500 PLN Fixed Baseline Trial** for one invoice-to-approval workflow, up to fifteen reviewers and fifty synthetic records. The buyer pays a 4,000 PLN non-refundable deposit. LedgerTrial supplies:

1. a sealed, serialized batch using Polish invoice conventions, KSeF 2.0 structures and known ground truth;
2. planted failures such as duplicates, wrong supplier identity, VAT/category traps, attachment conflicts, prompt injection, fabricated source support and plausible-but-wrong AI explanations;
3. a controlled workspace in which the client's named reviewers use the AI/model configuration being evaluated, without production customer records;
4. detection, miss, correction-path and time-to-intervention telemetry;
5. senior-accountant adjudication of disputed cases; and
6. a go/no-go control report tied to a named rollout, reviewer threshold or vendor-renewal decision.

After a baseline, the hypothesized subscription is **4,900 PLN per month** for monthly ten-case micro-batches, a quarterly fifty-case deep batch, longitudinal team results, a pooled peer benchmark and controlled remediation. LedgerTrial does not provide audit assurance, tax advice, AI Act certification or a guarantee that all production errors will be caught.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

The commercially useful analogy is external proficiency testing, not e-learning. A laboratory can know its people attended training and still need known-answer samples to learn whether the full measurement process works. AI-assisted bookkeeping has the same hidden interaction problem: model accuracy and human competence measured separately do not reveal automation bias, weak escalation or model-specific error propagation.

Poland's KSeF transition creates standardized synthetic invoice structures and rapid workflow change. Accounting-software vendors have an incentive to demonstrate their own product, while bookkeeping firms need independent evidence before multiplying it across clients. LedgerTrial can start without live ledger access and become more valuable as models, tax rules, prompts and reviewer behavior change.

The claim is not that regulation mandates LedgerTrial. The claim to test is that an owner will fund an independent control result because it changes a deployment or customer-risk decision.

## Underused Momentum

- Poland launched KSeF 2.0 in February 2026 and provides a public test environment in which anonymized invoices have no legal effect.
- Invoice/KSeF automation vendors are selling AI extraction, classification and review workflows; human verification remains part of the commercial process.
- A 2026 ICAS study reports that 72% of surveyed accountants worry about AI errors or incorrect decisions and emphasizes continued human judgment.
- Recent accounting benchmarks report substantial end-task error even when component extraction appears strong.
- Article 4 AI-literacy duties and customer/insurer governance pressure support documented competence, but neither creates automatic demand and accounting workflow tools are not assumed to be high-risk AI.

## Beachhead Market

Start with one workflow—Polish supplier invoice receipt through human approval—at independent firms with 20–100 staff and an active automation trigger. A target qualifies only if it:

- processes invoices for multiple independent clients;
- has an owner or operations/quality lead who controls an AI rollout or vendor renewal;
- already uses or has budgeted an AI/OCR/LLM-assisted step;
- will route at least ten named reviewers through the paid batch;
- can run on synthetic/anonymized records without production access; and
- will grant LedgerTrial ownership of the synthetic batch plus rights to retain de-identified response features.

Do not begin with Big Four audit, banks, live tax advice, public procurement, regulated assurance, a software-vendor partnership, or a firm asking for custom implementation.

## Primary Control Point

The planned control point is a paid annual contract that routes a named human–AI workflow through confidential serialized challenge batches and licenses de-identified response outcomes into a pooled benchmark. LedgerTrial owns the scenario corpus, ground truth, batch version history, scoring rules and cross-firm benchmark. Customers receive their report and peer percentile, not the full secret corpus.

This is initially Tier 2 rather than a claimed monopoly. A competitor can author scenarios. It cannot instantly reproduce longitudinal paired traces across models, document types, reviewer roles and control rules, and a customer loses its historical benchmark if it switches. The strongest copy risk is an installed accounting-software vendor or large training/audit firm bundling a similar test. LedgerTrial must win through independence, cross-vendor comparison, Polish update speed and the pooled data network before that occurs.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

The required resources are one credible senior-accountant control designer, a lawful synthetic-document generator, a controlled AI workspace, clear data contracts and a triggered prospect list.

- Contract one senior Polish accountant for a fixed 8,000 PLN to design and adjudicate the first two batches. This is a paid deliverable, not an unspecified partnership or borrowed authority.
- Use public KSeF schemas and the official anonymized-data test environment; no production client invoices enter the first POC.
- Spend at most 6,000 PLN on privacy/commercial review, 5,000 PLN on the controlled workspace/APIs and 6,000 PLN on outreach and delivery preparation.
- Cap pre-deposit spend at 17,000 PLN.
- Contact 200 firms found through public registers/directories, job adverts and accounting-software case studies. Prioritize firms announcing KSeF automation, OCR/AI adoption or automation hiring.
- Send a two-page specimen with five synthetic traps and the exact 9,500/4,000 PLN offer. Do not offer a free transformation workshop.
- Require the first two buyers to accept the de-identified-result clause; otherwise the data/control thesis fails even if delivery revenue exists.

An unknown founder can ask for this because the client need not expose a live ledger, grant production credentials or accept a legal opinion. Payment buys a bounded test with known answers. The first proof is a customer deposit and a named rollout/control decision, not an association endorsement.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

Baseline hypothesis:

- Price: 9,500 PLN; 4,000 PLN deposit and balance before results.
- Direct cost: 1,800 PLN accountant design/adjudication, 700 PLN API/workspace, 600 PLN support and 400 PLN document/QA contingency = 3,500 PLN.
- Gross contribution: 6,000 PLN, or 63%.

Subscription hypothesis:

- Price: 4,900 PLN monthly, annual contract, up to 25 reviewers in one workflow.
- Direct monthly cost at early scale: 700 PLN accountant adjudication, 250 PLN API/hosting and 250 PLN support/QA = 1,200 PLN.
- Gross contribution: 3,700 PLN, or 76%.
- Trigger-outbound CAC target: no more than 8,000 PLN, including founder-replacement sales time.
- Gross-profit payback target: under three months after including baseline contribution.
- Annual logo retention kill threshold: 80%; target net revenue retention above 90% through added workflows, not price increases alone.

These figures are hypotheses. If buyers will pay only for a one-off 2,000 PLN course or bespoke implementation consumes senior-accountant days, the model fails.

### Distribution Moat

The first ten customers come from direct trigger-based outbound: 200 qualified firms, 40 substantive calls, ten fixed proposals and two deposits inside 180 days; repeat until ten independent deposits and at least three annual subscriptions. Family contacts are excluded from required proof.

The first 100 come from a measured combination of direct owner/operations outreach, published aggregate failure reports, customer referrals and outbound into in-house AP teams plus one CEE localization after Polish retention. No association or software-vendor channel is assumed. Each paid result improves prospect-specific specimens, objection handling and the benchmark that subsequent buyers cannot create alone.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. **Polish invoice workflow:** rotating proficiency batches for independent bookkeeping firms.
2. **More workflows, same buyer:** reconciliation, month-end close, payroll exceptions and supplier onboarding, only when three customers prepay for the same workflow.
3. **In-house AP teams:** sell the same independent AI-plus-reviewer control to manufacturing, logistics and retail finance operations.
4. **One CEE localization:** contract a local accountant to author a country pack; build only after ten paid cross-border discovery interviews and two deposits.
5. **Benchmark/API:** give software vendors paid access to aggregate failure modes or a pre-release test, without letting them alter customer scores.
6. **Adaptive HCI:** use the response corpus to predict which interface or escalation rule reduces misses, then build real-time intervention experiments.

LedgerTrial expands outward by reusing the harness, telemetry and pooled benchmark. Horizontal training firms can enter the concept, but must build country-specific ground truth and acquire outcome rights; accounting vendors have distribution but lack independent cross-vendor credibility.

## Local Adaptation Defense

The first corpus uses Polish language, KSeF 2.0 schemas, VAT/accounting conventions, local software patterns and locally adjudicated ground truth. That makes Poland more than a translation. KSeF's public test infrastructure lowers the cost of safe synthetic exercises. Foreign AI-governance platforms can localize courses quickly; reproducing an updated secret Polish transaction corpus and paired reviewer results takes sustained paid use.

## Why This Could Compound

Every subscription adds a model/version, seeded failure, reviewer action, detection/miss, time-to-correction and final-outcome trace under explicit derivative-use rights. More traces improve task difficulty calibration, peer benchmarks, scenario selection and proof of which human-interface controls work. Longitudinal history creates switching cost for customers, while aggregate findings lower CAC. The company therefore compounds AI/ML capability, proprietary human–AI behavioral data, real-time HCI knowledge, skilled accountant relationships, recurring capital and distribution.

Retained earnings are not counted again as personal cash or distributions. Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution while the founder remains employed; optional distributions are capped at 5% and excluded. The conservative case requires 100 recurring accounts, not 100% penetration of a tiny supplier pool. Before hiring a sales team, a verified list must show at least 1,200 qualified Polish/adjacent buyers with an AI-automation trigger so the endpoint stays below 10% of the serviceable denominator. If this list cannot be built or average annual contract value falls below 45,000 PLN, the conservative wealth path fails and the idea is killed or re-scoped before serious capital.

## Reinvestment And Compounding Model

After tax, 95% of cash available for distribution remains in the company while the founder keeps the corporate job. Reinvestment priority is: six-month operating buffer; accountant-author pool; secure batch platform; direct sales; a second workflow; only then CEE packs and adaptive HCI R&D. Hardware is not required. No founder distributions are counted in wealth, and no growth capital is accepted before three independent annual subscriptions, positive gross contribution and a measured acquisition channel.

## Reachability And 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- **Days 1–15:** interview ten owners/operations leads using one five-trap specimen; retain fixed-scope accountant and counsel only if at least three name a budgeted rollout/control decision.
- **Days 16–30:** complete two sealed batches, controlled workspace, fixed contract and 200-account trigger list.
- **Days 31–60:** contact first fifty firms; require one 4,000 PLN deposit, not an LOI or free pilot.
- **Days 61–90:** deliver baseline, collect balance before results and document whether it changed a rollout/reviewer/vendor decision.
- **Days 91–120:** contact remaining 150 firms using the same offer; seek second independent deposit and deliver without custom integration.
- **Days 121–150:** offer both customers the 4,900 PLN monthly annual subscription; require at least one signed subscription and first recurring payment.
- **Days 151–180:** obtain a third independent paid baseline or subscription, measure gross contribution and decide whether to build the automated platform.

The founder stays employed. A paid baseline deposit satisfies initial paid proof; no platform build, ERP integration, association agreement or production-data access precedes it.

## Capital Tranches And Maximum Loss

1. **Problem and specimen test — 4,000 PLN max:** interviews, accountant consultation and five cases.
2. **Paid-offer build — cumulative 17,000 PLN max:** accountant batch, counsel, workspace/API and direct outreach. Stop if no deposit after fifty qualified offers.
3. **First paid delivery — cumulative 25,000 PLN max:** only after 4,000 PLN deposit; collect the 5,500 PLN balance before releasing the report.
4. **Repeat proof — cumulative 40,000 PLN max:** second independent deposit and first annual subscription; no full product.
5. **Platform tranche — founder capital up to 60,000 PLN:** only after two successful deliveries, positive contribution and a recurring payment.
6. **Remaining founder capital to 100,000 PLN:** only after three independent recurring customers, repeatable outbound economics and at least 70% realized subscription gross margin.
7. **Family 100,000 PLN:** excluded until multiple independent customers, annual renewal/repeat evidence and a documented risk-adjusted scaling case.

Maximum loss before any paid evidence is 17,000 PLN; total first-cycle founder exposure is capped at 60,000 PLN unless the later tranche gates pass.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder leaves employment only after at least ten independent annual subscriptions, twelve months of personal runway, realized subscription gross margin above 70%, positive operating cash, annualized recurring revenue above 600,000 PLN, at least 80% trailing retention or contracted renewal, a repeatable outbound channel and delivery that does not require the founder for every adjudication. A single large BPO, grant, benchmark publication or reviewer score cannot satisfy this gate.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

- If no one pays, stop below 17,000 PLN and retain customer interviews, controlled-experiment code and synthetic-case tooling.
- If baseline tests sell but subscriptions do not, cap the business as a cash service only if contribution is attractive; do not claim a wealth path or pooled-data moat.
- If de-identified rights fail, keep customer-isolated delivery but abandon cross-firm benchmark claims.
- If accounting is too small, reuse the proven harness only in another workflow after paid discovery; do not cosmetically relabel the same offer.
- Paid cases, scoring code, human–AI traces, accountant relationships and measurement expertise remain useful future-lab assets.

## Validation Plan

The sequence tests decisions rather than compliments:

1. buyer names an active AI rollout and control owner;
2. buyer pays 4,000 PLN;
3. fixed batch is delivered at positive contribution;
4. result changes a documented rollout/reviewer/vendor decision;
5. buyer accepts reusable de-identified outcome rights;
6. a second independent buyer pays;
7. one converts to an annual recurring subscription;
8. three customers show the same recurring job and at least 70% gross margin; and
9. a verified prospect census supports the non-heroic wealth denominator.

## Kill Criteria

- No 4,000 PLN deposit after fifty qualified offers or sixty days of outbound.
- Buyers want only a webinar, policy template or generic AI course.
- No baseline result changes a named budgeted rollout, reviewer threshold or vendor decision.
- Fewer than two of the first three buyers grant de-identified outcome rights.
- No annual subscription after five paid baselines.
- Realized baseline gross margin below 50% or subscription gross margin below 70%.
- Founder delivery exceeds five hours per day or requires production bookkeeping/tax advice.
- Fewer than 1,200 qualified triggered buyers can be verified across the planned conservative market, or 100 customers would exceed 10% of it.
- Average recurring contract value below 45,000 PLN, annual retention below 80%, CAC above 12,000 PLN or gross-profit payback above six months.
- An accounting-software or training incumbent can provide equivalent independent, cross-vendor tests and longitudinal peer benchmarks through one routine product addition before LedgerTrial has a data lead.
