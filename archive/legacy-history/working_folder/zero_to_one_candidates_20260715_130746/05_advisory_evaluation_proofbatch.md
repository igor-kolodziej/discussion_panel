# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ProofBatch Talent Cell

## Diagnosis

ProofBatch materially fixes BenchHire's simulated-score trust problem by selling accepted production output under a real purchase order. It also creates a cleaner two-sided cold start: customer work funds paid worker trials, while a hire is optional. The change introduces a harder dependency, however—an unknown founder must qualify as a reliable contract manufacturer—and the production order, fixtures, IPC-trained supervision and hire option are all reproducible by existing low-volume manufacturers. The wealth case is labor- and quality-operation-heavy and clears 5m PLN only narrowly under unsupported margins, so the pivot should be killed rather than polished into another supplier-trust shape.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: prototype and low-volume harness manufacturing is an established purchase category; IPC/WHMA-A-620 defines accepted industry practice and training is available in Poland; active firms such as Eltechnik and Vernex establish capable competition. Hypotheses: willingness to issue a paid qualification order to an unknown cell, customer-supplied parts, a 43% pilot and 38% mature gross margin, conversion fees, process-recording rights, lawful short engagements, repeat POs and 10m PLN revenue without proportional founder/senior-supervisor labor. The production category evidence simultaneously weakens novelty and distribution advantage.

## Overall Score

**73/100.** Weighted points are 146 out of 200; `10 × (146 / 20) = 73`.

## Control Point

**Tier 2.** A signed purchase order, component custody and paid worker roster directly control one batch and are provable within 12 months. They are non-exclusive and routine in contract manufacturing. The strongest copy risk is a trusted low-volume harness producer adding a recruiting/conversion offer, or a staffing firm contracting an existing workshop.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The conservative endpoint produces 5.28m PLN founder equity only at 10m PLN revenue, 15% operating margin, a 4× multiple and 88% ownership. Reaching that revenue requires many supervised batches, supplier-quality systems and line managers; the model does not establish that founder labor and senior quality labor decouple or that customer-supplied components remain standard at scale. Expected/strong cases magnify the same unsupported operational leverage. Reinvestment is stated correctly and there is no double-counting, but the path is too thin and assumption-sensitive.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, weak.** The exact 5,000 PLN paid supplier-qualification deposit can be asked for inside 60 days, pre-proof loss is capped at 15,000 PLN, and equipment follows payment. One customer, one hired supervisor and customer-supplied components are a bounded dependency set. The chance that an unknown, non-certified cell receives the order is uncertain, but the test is honest and compatible with employment.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is precise, but each new customer performs supplier qualification and can choose established manufacturers. A second cell increases throughput, not necessarily defensibility. Local cost helps, while Vernex-like networks and established Polish producers can move inward with more trust and capacity.

## Fatal Objections

1. The core paid proof requires supplier-quality trust the founder does not possess, merely replacing recruiter trust with manufacturing trust.
2. A contract manufacturer can add the hire option through one recruiting contract; a staffing agency can add production through one workshop contract.
3. The 5m PLN path is a narrow multiple-based outcome from unsupported production margins and managerial leverage.
4. Customer IP, recording restrictions and worker consent may prevent the claimed data asset even if production succeeds.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Use paid real work—not simulated tasks—to finance and validate human skill, and make any data asset a byproduct of accepted customer output.

## Recommendation

**kill**

The same technical-workforce family is exhausted for this run unless a future direction changes the buyer, trust mechanism, authority claim and resource control—not merely the task or production format.
