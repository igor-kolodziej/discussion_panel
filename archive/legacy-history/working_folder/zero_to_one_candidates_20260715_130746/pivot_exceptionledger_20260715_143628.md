# Zero-to-One Pivot: ExceptionLedger

## Preserved Insight

The valuable insight is not synthetic testing. It is measuring the combined human–AI process around a consequential economic event and retaining the paired failure-to-human-resolution outcome during paid delivery. ExceptionLedger preserves that loop but accepts the panel's objections: it abandons proficiency tests, benchmark subscriptions and claims that a controlled exercise represents production.

## Structural Change

ExceptionLedger is a managed resolution rail for the long tail of invoice exceptions left behind by AI/OCR/KSeF automation. A bookkeeping firm routes defined unresolved cases—low-confidence supplier matches, conflicting invoice fields, duplicate ambiguity, KSeF/schema rejection, attachment inconsistency or an AI explanation that the reviewer cannot verify—into a secure queue. ExceptionLedger returns an evidence-linked resolution through a paid Polish-accountant reviewer and its own AI workspace. The customer pays only for a resolution it accepts into its ordinary workflow; rejected or reversed resolutions receive no fee.

The paid event is now a real exception outcome, not a course, report or simulation. Ground truth is the customer's accepted posting or corrected KSeF outcome plus a thirty-day non-reversal window. Each completed event can create a contractually reusable de-identified trace: source exception type, AI proposal, human evidence requested, resolution, acceptance/reversal and time.

## Target Niche and Buyer

The beachhead remains independent Polish bookkeeping/BPO firms with roughly 20–100 staff, but only those already operating an AI/OCR-assisted invoice workflow with a visible unresolved-exception queue. The economic buyer is the operations owner who allocates scarce senior-accountant time, not an AI-training or compliance buyer.

The initial scope excludes tax opinions, client representation, audit assurance, ambiguous legal interpretation, payment authorization and any case without a defined customer acceptance state. ExceptionLedger begins with one software-agnostic package format rather than an ERP integration.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

Automation removes easy data entry but concentrates difficult, irregular cases on senior staff. Those cases interrupt planned work, create queue spikes and prevent a firm from increasing clients per accountant. Vendor support can explain a technical feature; it does not take responsibility for reaching a customer-accepted bookkeeping outcome across vendors and source documents.

The first commercial hypothesis is a **6,000 PLN prepaid Resolution Lot** covering up to fifty eligible exceptions during thirty days, with a 3,000 PLN non-refundable activation payment and 120 PLN charged for each accepted outcome. Unused volume expires only after the delivery window, and any resolution rejected by the customer or reversed within thirty days is credited. The buyer must compare this with fully loaded senior-accountant time and backlog cost, not a training budget.

## Value and Profit Wedge

The first lot is delivered manually through a secure upload/queue, one contracted senior accountant and founder-built evidence retrieval. Direct-cost hypothesis is 60 PLN per eligible case including reviewer time, QA and infrastructure, leaving 60 PLN contribution per accepted resolution before acquisition costs. Cases outside the objective scope are rejected without charge.

At scale, repeated exception types receive structured evidence templates and automation, while humans retain final review. The business charges per accepted outcome or annual committed volume; it does not sell seats. Better routing and automation must lower cost per accepted exception without reducing quality. Actual volume, loaded expert cost, reversal rate, CAC, gross margin and annual commitment are new hypotheses.

## Domination Wedge

Dominate one residual queue first: Polish supplier-invoice exceptions after automated extraction and before posting. Do not become a general bookkeeping outsourcer.

The advantage to test is a cross-vendor resolution memory: which evidence and reviewer action converts a specific AI/technical exception into an accepted, non-reversed result. Accounting vendors see only their own product tickets; individual firms see only their own queue. ExceptionLedger can use the pooled outcome history to route cases to the right evidence template and reviewer faster.

## Acquisition Channel

Build a public list of 150 firms with current OCR/AI/KSeF automation evidence from job adverts, software case studies and service pages. Send the owner or operations lead a fixed eligibility card and the exact 6,000 PLN lot. Ask for the last thirty days' count of cases that consumed more than fifteen senior minutes; do not offer a free process audit.

The paid-proof target is fifty owner conversations, five written lots and one 3,000 PLN activation payment within ninety days. The first ten customers must come from the same triggered outbound and customer referrals. No software-vendor, association or accounting-network partnership receives validation credit.

## Primary Control Point

The planned control point is a twelve-month primary-routing clause for defined exception categories, prepaid volume and explicit rights to retain de-identified exception→evidence→human-resolution→acceptance/reversal facts. This routes a repeated transaction instead of hoping that a buyer renews a test.

The strongest copy risk is the installed accounting-software vendor adding expert escalation or the bookkeeping firm hiring another senior accountant. ExceptionLedger must prove that cross-vendor pooled resolution history improves time, acceptance and cost enough that those substitutes cannot match economics through one support-team change. Until paid routing and outcome rights exist, the control point remains prospective.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

- Contract two senior Polish accountants on fixed per-accepted-case terms; neither is described as a partner, certifier or co-founder.
- Spend up to 7,000 PLN on privacy, professional-scope and liability terms; 5,000 PLN on a secure queue and access controls; 4,000 PLN on the first evidence templates; and 4,000 PLN on triggered outbound.
- Stage commitments so no more than 15,000 PLN is at risk before the first 3,000 PLN activation payment and no more than 30,000 PLN before a successfully accepted and paid lot.
- Require customer-provided redaction or data minimization for the first lot; no direct ERP credential, payment authority or unrestricted client archive.
- Require the activation contract to define eligible categories, customer acceptance, reversal/credit, data retention, professional exclusions and de-identified derivative rights.
- Stop if safe redaction prevents resolution or if real production access creates vendor-qualification/security costs beyond the first-cycle budget.

An unknown founder can offer a small queue because the customer keeps final approval and pays only for accepted outcomes. The paid accountants supply domain work under contract. No authority claim, acquisition, certifier or software partnership is required for the first lot.

## Expansion Path

1. Prove eligible exception volume, accepted-outcome WTP, loaded per-case cost and derivative rights with one lot.
2. Win three annual primary-routing commitments in the same invoice workflow and show at least 60% gross contribution after reversals and expert QA.
3. Automate evidence gathering only for a pattern observed in at least fifty accepted paid cases.
4. Add a second accounting-software input format only against a customer's prepaid volume.
5. Expand within the same buyer to reconciliation and month-end exceptions only when three customers prepay.
6. Add one CEE country after an employed/contracted local accountant validates scope and two customers deposit.
7. Use the outcome corpus to build adaptive human–AI exception interfaces and model-routing IP.

## Minimum People Stack

- Founder: sales, queue design, AI/ML, outcome analysis and automation; maximum five hours/day.
- Two contracted senior accountants paid per accepted case and available in bounded service windows.
- Fixed-scope privacy/commercial/professional-liability counsel.
- Security contractor only after one paid lot; engineer only after three annual commitments.

## Principal Risk

The same facts that make exceptions valuable can make them non-standard, confidential and dependent on professional judgment. ExceptionLedger may become ordinary outsourced bookkeeping with weak margins, daytime SLA pressure and liability, while accounting vendors can bundle support and firms can hire internally. If objective acceptance cannot be defined without tax/legal authority or production access, the pivot is not viable.

## First Disconfirming Test

Before software, ask fifty qualified operations owners for a thirty-day eligible-exception count and offer the same 6,000/3,000 PLN lot. Kill if no one activates, median eligible volume is below fifty cases per month, accepted WTP is below twice loaded expert cost, customers will not grant primary routing and derivative outcome rights, or safe delivery requires a direct ERP integration/security qualification before payment.

## Evidence Preserved

- KSeF 2.0 and accounting automation create real invoice-processing workflows.
- Published sources show continued need for human judgment and material AI error concern.
- LedgerTrial's panel agreed that the combined human–AI process is a meaningful measurement unit and that bounded paid proof is reachable.
- No source currently proves exception incidence, willingness to outsource, per-case price, reversal rate, expert capacity or reusable rights.

## New Hypotheses

- A qualifying firm has at least fifty objective, outsourceable exceptions per month.
- The operations owner will prepay 3,000 PLN and route cases outside the firm.
- 120 PLN per accepted case is below loaded internal backlog cost and above twice fully loaded external delivery cost.
- Customer acceptance and thirty-day non-reversal are valid outcome labels.
- Data minimization permits resolution without open production-system access.
- Two contractors can meet the SLA while the founder remains employed.
- Three customers will sign annual primary-routing commitments with derivative outcome rights.
- Fifty accepted examples per pattern are sufficient to automate evidence collection and decouple margin from senior labor.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

ExceptionLedger does not sell a proficiency test, synthetic benchmark, compliance artifact or annual assessment. It owns a live repeated economic unit—an accepted resolution—and earns revenue only when that unit is completed. Production acceptance fixes the synthetic-validity objection, per-outcome pricing fixes the arbitrary testing cadence, and primary routing creates transaction flow before a benchmark. The revision keeps the accounting buyer but changes the pain, value capture, delivery model, trust mechanism and control point. If the evaluator finds that these changes merely recreate outsourced bookkeeping or vendor support, the entire accounting pattern should be killed rather than revised again.

## Return-To-Evaluator Priority

ExceptionLedger is the only pivot because it directly answers both panel objections with an actual recurring outcome. It should return to `$evaluate-zero-to-one` first. The evaluator should focus on professional authority, data/security access, exception incidence, labor leverage, SLA compatibility, incumbent support and whether a non-heroic founder-wealth path can exist. No score or validation is claimed.
