# Research And Sources

Research date: 2026-07-15. Current market, regulatory, and competitor claims are separated from business hypotheses.

## Evidence Supporting The Skilled-Placement Search

- The European Labour Authority's 2025 report records 2,617 shortage occupations across the countries studied and says cross-border matching is obstructed by qualification recognition and language barriers. Trades and related occupations are repeatedly represented. [ELA 2025 report](https://www.ela.europa.eu/en/publications/labour-shortages-and-surpluses-europe-2025)
- ELA's 2024 report states that welders and building electricians were among the most widespread shortage occupations, while 98% of shortage occupations had a corresponding surplus somewhere else in the reporting countries. [ELA 2024 report](https://www.ela.europa.eu/en/publications/labour-shortages-and-surpluses-europe-2024)
- The European Commission reported in June 2026 that 46% of EU SMEs face difficulty finding workers with the right skills. [European Commission Eurobarometer release](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/eurobarometer-highlights-obstacles-and-opportunities-european-smes-recruiting-outside-eu-2026-06-01_en)
- World Employment Confederation reports 61 million people placed by the private employment-services industry in its 2026 impact report, including 2.5 million direct-recruitment placements. This is broad denominator evidence, not proof of demand for the proposed niche. [WEC Industry Impact Report 2026](https://www.weceurope.org/publication-post/industry-impact-report-2026/)
- Poland requires employment agencies to be entered in KRAZ; the current official page states a 1,000 PLN registration fee. This makes direct transaction ownership administratively testable, although legal advice is still required. [Polish Ministry KRAZ page](https://psz.praca.gov.pl/rynek-pracy/bazy-danych/rejestr-agencji-zatrudnienia)
- Current vacancies confirm that Polish employers and agencies recruit for control-cabinet production, electrical testing, schematic reading, wiring and assembly roles. [Manpower control-cabinet quality role](https://www.manpower.pl/pl/job/21479/specjalista-ka-ds-testow-kontroli-jakosci), [Randstad electrician role](https://www.randstad.pl/en/jobs/elektryk-km-6-14-14-22_koszalin_103558/)
- Specialist agencies already recruit electricians and panel builders across Poland, Germany and the Netherlands. This proves a budget and also establishes the main competitive threat. [TecLine](https://tecline.com/), [Riverstate](https://www.riverstate.de/en/industries/skilled-workers-from-poland/), [Personal Polen](https://personal-polen.de/en/recruitment-agency-electrician-electrical-engineering/)
- Practical assessment is not novel by itself. Foundry Sparks, Scientific Management and TradeTasks advertise practical or AI-assisted trade assessment. The proposed differentiation therefore has to be direct niche placement, a paid mandate, an outcome warranty, and linked post-placement outcomes—not the test fixture alone. [Foundry Sparks](https://sparksatfoundry.com/), [Scientific Management electrical assessment](https://scientific-management.com/skills-assessment-programs/electrical-skills-assessments/), [TradeTasks](https://tradetasks.ai/)
- OECD's 2026 skills-first report discusses job-specific practical assessments, reinforcing that the method is accepted but not proprietary. [OECD, A Skills-First Labour Market](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/06/a-skills-first-labour-market_bd036db7/2e1b85f0-en.pdf)

## Employment-AI Boundary

- Recruitment and selection AI can fall under the EU AI Act's high-risk employment category. The candidate therefore keeps the first product human-judged, does not identify people biometrically, and treats computer vision as measurement support until a legal memo and applicable conformity path are secured. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1689)
- The Commission's current implementation timeline says Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027 after the AI Omnibus changes. This timing may change again and is not a reason to defer legal review. [European Commission AI Act framework](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

## Evidence And Competition For Human Fallback

- IBM describes representative real-world scenarios, human-in-the-loop assessment and simulation as parts of agent evaluation. [IBM agent evaluation](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-agent-evaluation/jcr%3Acontent)
- Scale's 2026 HiL-Bench tests whether agents know when to ask for help, showing that handoff behavior is an active technical problem. [Scale HiL-Bench](https://scale.com/blog/hil)
- Frontiermind explicitly markets operational simulations, human readiness and agent-readiness tests; RakFort markets human readiness and escalation paths; Talkdesk is previewing scenario-based agent evaluation. These results killed a generic enterprise flight-test finalist. [Frontiermind](https://www.frontiermind.ai/ai-readiness), [RakFort](https://rakfort.com/solutions/copilotready), [Talkdesk](https://support.talkdesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/50071730170779--Preview-Agent-Evaluation-Overview)
- Humanloop, HITL.sh and multiple emerging vendors provide evaluation or human-oversight infrastructure. [Humanloop](https://humanloop.com/home), [HITL.sh](https://docs.hitl.sh/)
- **Hypothesis:** Polish home-service companies will pay 1,250 PLN monthly plus a booked-job fee for a bounded after-hours overflow service, and will permit de-identified handoff traces under a data-processing agreement. No source proves this price or retention.

## Evidence And Competition For Cleanroom Qualification

- EU GMP Annex 1 entered into operation in August 2023 (with one delayed point) and sets the live regulatory context for sterile manufacturing. [European Commission Annex 1 publication](https://health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/revision-manufacture-sterile-medicinal-products-2022-08-25_en)
- The Polish Clean Technologies Association offers cleanroom training, practical demonstrations, exams and on-site delivery. [PSCT cleanroom course](https://psct.pl/en/courses/farmacja-w-cleanroom/)
- CWS already combines cleanroom garments, cleaning and training; Polish consultancies offer training, qualification and audit services. These are strong distribution and trust incumbents. [CWS Cleanrooms](https://www.cws.com/en/cleanroom/full-service-provider), [CRK Cleanroom](https://crk-cleanroom.pl/en/a-few-words-about-crk/)
- SGS lists a two-day cleanroom qualification course at 1,600 PLN plus VAT; this is evidence that sites buy specialist training, but not evidence for the proposed per-operator price. [SGS Academy Poland](https://learning.sgs.com/lmt/clmsCatalogDetails.prMain?in_from_module=CLMSSHARE.PRMAIN&in_language_identifier=pl&in_offeringId=185800262&in_region=pl&site=sgsssc)
- **Hypothesis:** a site QA lead will pay for protocol mapping within 180 days and subsequently route annual gowning practice through an instrumented service while allowing aggregate, non-identifying motion features to be retained.

[IFAB body-camera trial](https://www.theifab.com/trials/body-cameras-grassroots-level/), [FA Law 5](https://www.thefa.com/football-rules-governance/lawsandrules/laws/football-11-11/law-5---the-referee), [Reveal referee safety camera](https://www.revealmedia.co.uk/referee-safety-cam)
- HabitAware has an established BFRB wearable and patent position. [HabitAware](https://habitaware.com/)
- Clutch already advertises phone-camera measurement of mirror and head-check behavior for driving practice. [Clutch](https://www.learnwithclutch.com/)
- AAS/PED harm-reduction need is documented, but established memberships and clinics already serve the category and GDPR Article 9 makes health-data governance consequential. [Clinical guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10962511/), [Team Madsen](https://team-madsen.com/en/), [GDPR Article 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/art_9/oj)

## Evidence Limits

No desk source establishes willingness to pay, acquisition cost, placement failure attributable to task skill, candidate show-up rate, customer retention, permission to reuse behavioral traces, or the proposed valuation multiples. These remain explicit test hypotheses. No reviewer score is treated as market evidence.
