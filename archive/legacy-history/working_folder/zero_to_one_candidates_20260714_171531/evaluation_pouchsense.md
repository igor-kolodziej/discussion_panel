# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PouchSense

## Diagnosis

PouchSense has a real manufacturing event, a per-unit revenue path, and an owned physical input on the first invoice. The strongest commercial insight is that a release-relevant electrochemical proxy can be worth much more than a generic humidity indicator. Its weaknesses are unproved predictive validity, old indicator patent art, and the ease with which a large OEM or pouch converter can internalize the concept after learning it works. It is worth independent scrutiny only as a tightly bounded paid-correlation wedge, not as a production rollout assumption.

## Evidence and Assumptions

FDA guidance and patent art support shelf-life relevance and prior indicator concepts. Prices, conversion, per-unit adoption, 40m expected volume, and the ability to reduce destructive sampling are founder hypotheses. The stress model shows positive contribution under price/cost pressure. No customer verdict exists.

## Overall Score

**73/100.** Weighted points = 146.0 of 200; `10 × (146 / 20) = 73`.

## Control Point

**Tier 1.** Owned formulation, serialized physical inventory, reader/calibration model, and retained lots exist at first sale. Strongest copy risk: a packaging converter or electrode OEM reproduces an indicator and builds its own correlation model. Predictive cross-package history and approved design-ins are the only credible catch-up friction.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, expected case; conservative case fails.** Expected 20m PLN revenue, 5m EBIT, 80% ownership and 4–6× EBIT yield 16–24m PLN founder equity in year 8. Forty million witnesses is plausible only across several global design-ins and must not be inferred from one pilot. Retained cash is not added to the EBIT value; debt is deducted.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** One paid correlation study can be sold within 180 days before packaging integration, with 25k PLN maximum pre-proof loss and bounded chemistry/lab contractors. The critical dependencies are FTO and predictive correlation, both testable before serious capital.

## Expansion Check

The wet-ECG packaging-change beachhead is coherent; EEG/defibrillation/TENS pads are plausible adjacencies. An incumbent can enter, but an approved formulation plus retained aging history creates some switching cost. Poland is an engineering base, not the sales moat.

## Fatal Objections

1. A humidity/ion-response witness may not predict release-relevant electrode failure.
2. Patent/FTO and packaging validation may block integration.
3. Large OEMs can internalize after feasibility.
4. Willingness to pay for the feasibility study is inferred, not observed.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Embed a cheap witness into a high-volume physiological consumable when it predicts a costly release decision better than environmental monitoring.
