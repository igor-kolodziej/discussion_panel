# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PAIForge

## Diagnosis

PAIForge has an unusually clear first-sale asset: proprietary physical attack instruments, not a report or future dataset. The pre-certification event is credible because formal laboratory failure is expensive, and rotating unknown materials address a documented generalization problem. The main risks are a smaller buyer universe than modeled, accredited labs' existing recipe capability, safe-customer/legal burden, and uncertainty that vendors will buy physical packs from an unknown supplier. It deserves independent paneling as a defensive R&D consumable, never as a certification proxy.

## Evidence and Assumptions

ISO/FIDO/Android/NIST sources establish physical PAD testing and open-ended attack materials; peer-reviewed work establishes novel polymer behavior. The 15–30k starter price, 180 expected accounts, renewal frequency, and incremental failure discovery are hypotheses. No accreditation or participant access is assumed.

## Overall Score

**74/100.** Weighted points = 147.5 of 200; `10 × (147.5 / 20) = 73.75`, rounded to 74.

## Control Point

**Tier 1.** Owned recipes, molds, serialized stock, withheld compositions, and cross-reader calibration are tangible at first order. Strongest copy risk: an accredited lab or biometric vendor manufactures its own rotating artifacts from public recipes. Continuous material innovation and cross-sensor response history must be better than internal casting.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, expected case with buyer-count caveat.** Expected 16.2m PLN revenue and 4.86m EBIT at 80% ownership yield 15.6–23.3m PLN at 4–6× in year 8. The conservative case yields only 3.9m PLN. Expansion beyond contact fingerprint is required to make 180 accounts non-heroic; debt is deducted and retained earnings are not double counted.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Six recipe families, three readers, legal customer controls, and one prepaid 15–30k pack fit within 30k PLN pre-proof exposure and 25 hours/week. Certification authority and external participants are not prerequisites.

## Expansion Check

Fingerprint vendors preparing formal tests form a narrow beachhead; palm/contactless and other physical modalities expand the denominator. Accredited labs are both substitute and possible channel. EU location adds logistics/research access, not a moat.

## Fatal Objections

1. Internal/common attack materials may expose the same failures.
2. Vendor trust and safe distribution may make CAC too high.
3. Expected recurring buyer count may require a broader modality set than the founder can support.
4. Labs may refuse to buy or recognize external artifacts.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Sell rotating physical adversarial inputs before a costly formal validation event, while owning the material recipe and challenge inventory.
