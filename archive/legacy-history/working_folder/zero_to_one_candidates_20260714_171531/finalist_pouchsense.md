# Zero-to-One Candidate: PouchSense

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell electrode OEMs a proprietary, serialized hydrogel witness integrated into each wet-electrode pouch that predicts loss of usable electrochemical performance earlier and more cheaply than destructive aged-product sampling alone.

## Founder Fit

The founder understands biosignal quality, experimental design, data modeling, and regulated evidence. A contract polymer/packaging chemist and external test laboratory cover formulation and metrology; no employer resources are used.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Wet ECG/EEG/defibrillation-electrode OEM QA and packaging teams must support shelf life, packaging changes, supplier changes, and deviation investigations. They currently age finished electrodes, open pouches, and perform destructive electrical/adhesive tests. Results are slow and sparse, while an unnoticed pouch or hydrogel drift can create scrap, complaints, or a delayed release.

## Product Wedge

A non-patient-contact witness sits inside the sealed pouch and changes a measurable optical/electrical signature with moisture/ion-mobility exposure. PouchSense supplies the formulation, serialized witness stock, reader/calibration model, retained lots, and correlation protocol. It does not replace final-device verification or make a clinical claim.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The buyer does not want another humidity sticker; it wants an early, package-level proxy for whether the electrode will still meet its release-relevant impedance/noise/adhesion limits. If one low-cost witness can reduce destructive sampling or shorten a deviation decision, per-pouch economics become possible.

## Underused Momentum

Disposable biosignal electrodes are mature, high-volume products with regulated shelf-life evidence and continuing packaging/material changes. Existing indicator patent art proves the concept is imaginable but also makes predictive performance and FTO the decisive gap.

## Beachhead Market

EU and US-facing manufacturers/CDMOs of pre-gelled single-use ECG monitoring electrodes, narrowed first to teams changing foil, seal, gel, or sterilization/aging conditions.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid feasibility: 12–25k PLN; stressed first-order contribution remains positive.
- Design-in/NRE: 40–90k PLN.
- Pilot/production witness: 0.30–0.80 PLN per pouch; expected 0.50 PLN price, 0.15 PLN direct cost, 70% gross margin before account support.
- Mature model: about 75% gross margin including NRE; expected 25% operating margin after chemistry, QA, sales, retained lots, and liability insurance.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

Build 100 named accounts from public FDA electrode records, ISO 13485 manufacturer/CDMO directories, and electrode-converter exhibitor lists. Send a technical one-page correlation protocol and a serialized sample only after qualification. First ten come from direct sample-led outbound; first 100 require packaging/electrode-converter design-in referrals and distributor coverage, but the first paid proof does not depend on a partner.

## Primary Control Point

Owned formulation, reader/calibration model, serialized witness inventory, retained-lot history, and buyer contract preserving generalized formulation/calibration improvements. The first paid study uses the owned witness; future pooled data alone is not the control point.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Under 18k PLN buys three formulations, a simple reader, accelerated-aging materials, commercial electrode samples, and limited contract chemistry. A 12–25k PLN paid feasibility order is required before buyer-specific packaging integration. By month 12 and under 90k PLN cumulative spend, two paid studies and one conditional call-off must exist.

## Distribution Advantage

Public regulatory/manufacturer records create a finite technical account list. Retained lots and cross-package aging histories make each later sales conversation more concrete; approved design-ins create high switching cost and converter referrals.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with wet ECG electrodes; expand to EEG, defibrillation pads, TENS/EMS pads, and other hydrogel skin-contact disposables only after predictive transfer is shown. The defensibility is correlation across real aged products plus lot history, not the visible indicator.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland is a cost-efficient formulation/analysis base near EU medtech converters. EU/US regulatory requirements are global, so revenue must be international; local geography alone is not a moat.

## Compounding Advantage

Every qualified formulation, pouch structure, hydrogel chemistry, and retained lot improves failure-mode coverage. Design-ins, QA documentation, and production call-offs compound into upstream distribution and a proprietary electrode-aging library.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

While employed, 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested in conservative and expected cases; optional founder distributions are capped at 5%. Strong success reinvests 100%. Milestones: paid correlation by month 6; two customers and conditional call-off by month 12; qualified pilot production by year 2; 5m units/year by years 3–4; expected scale by year 8.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Weeks 1–6: FTO screen, three witness prototypes, benchtop reader, account list. Weeks 7–12: accelerated-aging correlation on commercial electrodes and concise sample pack. Weeks 13–26: 100-account outbound; require one 12–25k PLN paid buyer-specific correlation. Maximum pre-proof spend is 18–25k PLN; the complete production product is not built.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. Up to 25k PLN pre-proof for chemistry, reader, FTO screen, and samples.
2. Up to 40k PLN cumulative only against the paid feasibility order.
3. Up to 100k PLN only after successful delivery, positive contribution, and a second independent paid customer or production call-off.
4. Family capital excluded until repeat demand and scaling economics exist. Maximum planned original-founder loss before paid proof: 25k PLN.

## Critical Dependency Map

- Witness must correlate with release-relevant electrode performance: controlled by experiment, existential.
- FTO around package indicators: bounded professional search, existential before integration.
- OEM validation and liability: one paid study can test it; full production approval is not required for paid proof.
- Manufacturing: small lots can be outsourced; no exclusive supplier assumed.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

No resignation until at least three independent paying OEMs or contracted recurring call-offs, positive contribution after QA/liability costs, a repeatable technical acquisition channel, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If integration fails, retain formulation experiments, reader electronics, electrode-aging data, manufacturer map, and materials contractors for other biosignal consumables. Do not pivot to generic electrode testing or evidence consulting.

## Critical Evidence

FDA guidance includes shelf-life/performance considerations; patent art shows package-level impedance indicators have been contemplated.

## Critical Hypotheses

The witness predicts usable electrode behavior rather than humidity alone; buyers will pay for a correlation study; FTO and package compatibility are tractable; expected volume can be reached without one dominant OEM.

## Kill Criteria

- No paid feasibility/deposit after 100 qualified accounts and 180 days.
- Correlation below a predeclared useful threshold or no advantage over a commodity humidity indicator.
- FTO counsel identifies blocking claims without a credible design-around.
- Stressed contribution is non-positive or integration requires more than 100k PLN before a second customer.
- OEMs state destructive aged-product testing cannot be reduced or accelerated by the witness.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; contract polymer/packaging chemist 30–60 hours; external electrical/adhesion lab; part-time IP/regulatory counsel. Pre-proof 25k PLN maximum; 12-month original-capital ceiling 100k PLN.
