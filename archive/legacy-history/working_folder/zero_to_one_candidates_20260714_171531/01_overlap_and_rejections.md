# Overlap and Rejection Boundary

## Prior confirmed families to avoid

- Battery/EMS retrofit qualification, promotion recovery, CBAM continuity, Data Act machine-data access, EPREL tender evidence, supplement/lab passports, heat-pump acoustic evidence, wearable investor claim diligence, marketplace evidence rescue, DORA renewal evidence, municipal animal-care transaction routing, and owned heritage-door inventory.

## Closed future-lab shapes

- SignalChain Sentinel and TrialFleet Release Custody: buyer/internal lab can reproduce the QA workflow; CRO authority/QMS blocks reachability.
- SprayPilot and Coating Claim Replay: incumbent owns applications expertise, trust, and distribution; service/confounder risk.
- AndonBridge and Haptic Accessibility Module OEM: trusted PLC integrator owns factory access and can bundle the hardware; recurring support and market scale fail.
- DryEEG FitGrid and ScalpTile: small/uncertain TAM, DIY/Fraunhofer/FTO exposure, speculative repeat consumption.
- EMG Shift Atlas: free/internal datasets, buyer-specific tasks, sponsor exclusivity, speculative re-licensing.
- Generic wearable/biosignal testing, evidence, dashboards, coaching, synchronization boxes, ordinary phantoms, and commodity sensors.
- Clinic/EEG capacity, patient books, reserved diagnostic resources, health-data resale/archive rights, consumer N-of-1 tests, and participant/data access dependent on an external authority.
- Robot-skill capture services dependent on factory access and derivative rights; open task-board kits without owned instrumentation or consumables.

## Re-entry standard

A prior family may re-enter only with a different buyer, urgent event, acquisition channel, profit model, and a first-sale control point. Cosmetic feature, geography, or naming changes are not material.

## New search bias

- Scarce or serialized consumables and reference materials.
- Owned benchmark hardware with proprietary instrumentation, randomization, consumables, or transaction flow.
- Directly owned specialized inventory or component supply.
- Real-time HCI where the startup owns the device/protocol/distribution rather than integrating for an incumbent.
- Prepaid human-data modules only when collection and reusable commercial rights are secured before recruitment.
- Contrarian non-health HCI and augmentation businesses with direct recurring economic value.

## Rejections established in this run

- Per-package electrode-aging witnesses: OEM owns the decisive validation data and can internalize the proxy; per-unit use is not required.
- Rotating biometric attack/reference lots: accredited laboratories and reader vendors control trust and can bundle the materials.
- Warehouse multimodal human surrogates: established automotive target vendors can adapt calibration, manufacturing and channels.
- Generic cross-vendor hardware approval terminals: buyer and enforcement point remain abstract; platform/PAM/FIDO vendors can bundle.
- PostgreSQL destructive-write approval relay: Bytebase and deployment-control incumbents already own the workflow and can add stronger confirmation.
- Cross-game directional wrist haptics: Audio Radar/platforms can combine cleaner signals with haptic endpoints and existing distribution.
- Jaw dry-EMG assistive switch: Emego and GlassOuse already own adjacent compliant products/channels; multi-gesture jaw input is an incremental feature with FTO and reliability exposure.
- Long-tail soft-gripper replacements and sacrificial overload cartridges: reachable paid orders do not produce a non-heroic 10m PLN founder-wealth path before incumbents respond.
