## Primary-agent synthesis during deduplication

- **PAIForge** combines the neuro-materials lane with ISO/FIDO presentation-attack testing: serialized physical biometric attack instruments with owned polymer recipes, sensor-response calibration histories, and rotating challenge lots for pre-certification R&D. It is materially different from an ordinary skin phantom because its paid event is adversarial security regression and its controlled asset is the attack-material formulation plus challenge inventory.
- **HumanEcho** combines the robotics perception and anthropomorphic-reference lanes: a warehouse-specific active human surrogate with replaceable PPE/occlusion skins for AMR detection and stopping regression. It is materially different from an open NIST board because it produces calibrated multimodal human signatures and recurring randomized skins.

## Immediate deduplication rules

- TonePulse, Interface Matrix, SweatSkin, and Haptic Reference Tiles overlap prior phantom/reference shapes and require evidence that physical lots change a release decision.
- DryPin, PFAS-interface assemblies, washable contact rails, and force-sensing liners are component-supply variants; only the strongest owned-design/production shape can advance.
- BlindBench, FlexFault, PolyPick, OptiGhost, AgentDesk, and ExoFit are benchmark hardware; only those with hidden state, proprietary telemetry, or human-safety relevance survive.
- Smart-glasses inserts, hygiene cartridges, hearable fit, battery rights, pod circulation, and harness routing are inventory/transaction businesses; a signed route or directly owned rights are mandatory.
- Human-data modules advance only when the buyer prepays collection and the contract preserves reusable commercial rights before recruitment.
