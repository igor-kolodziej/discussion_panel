# Zero-to-One Evaluation: JawKey v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

The exact canonical failed independently at 57 and 57. Existing Emego and GlassOuse muscle switches, active clench/earpiece patent claims, and purchasable EMG components reduce JawKey to an incremental multi-gesture form-factor extension. Daily separation of four to six commands from speech, chewing, sweat, movement and placement drift remains existential; regulatory, Bluetooth and small-batch costs also undermine the proposed 38,000 PLN proof path. The judges did not require executed paid evidence. This family should be killed, not cosmetically pivoted to a different user.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Emego already offers wireless dry-EMG switching around the jaw; GlassOuse sells a muscle switch in an established assistive catalogue; Apple supports multiple built-in Switch Control inputs. Primary research supports jaw/EMG feasibility but not broad all-day multi-command reliability. The Bluetooth qualification fee and EU product obligations are real cost/dependency issues. Prices, daily reliability, pad attach, support load, CAC, returns and 25,000-unit demand remain hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**56/100.** Weighted points = 111.5 of 200; rounded score = 56. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** Electrode geometry, AFE, IMU, firmware, pads and inventory are ordinary owned product assets. The dataset and installed base are prospective. Strongest copy risk is Emego/GSPK adding an IMU, multi-gesture classification and direct HID to its compliant wireless dry-EMG jaw-capable switch.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The 25,000-device expected case clears 10m PLN arithmetically but lacks bottom-up market, channel throughput, CAC, returns, support and working-capital evidence. The conservative case is only 2.65m PLN founder equity.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail as written.** A functional prototype may fit the cap, but the candidate does not reconcile branded Bluetooth qualification, EU radio/product compliance and a supported deliverable batch with a 38,000 PLN pre-proof ceiling. A refundable preorder can test interest but cannot establish the promised sale path without resolving this dependency.

## Fatal Objections

1. Existing Emego/GlassOuse offerings make the wedge incremental.
2. Four-to-six-command daily reliability is unproved and existential.
3. Bluetooth, compliance, support and production costs undermine reachability/margin.
4. Active patent adjacency requires freedom-to-operate review.
5. No bottom-up route to 25,000 annual devices.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Jaw and facial neuromotor signals can be a private input channel, but an unknown founder does not control this channel through the proposed assistive switch.

## Recommendation

kill
