# Uniform Pre-Panel Stress Tests

These are founder simulations, not advisory or validation scores. Each finalist is tested on the same six scenarios. A family is closed before advisory evaluation when it fails two or more simulations.

## Common modeling rules

- Cold acquisition begins with 100 named, individually relevant accounts. Positive response scenarios are 5%, 10%, and 15%. The conservative path assumes only 20% of positive responders buy, rounded down only after the portfolio is aggregated; a credible single paid deposit/feasibility order must remain possible within 180 days.
- Price falls 20%; acquisition cost doubles; BOM/delivery cost rises 30%. Contribution margin must remain positive without counting future data value.
- Founder capacity is capped at 25 hours/week while employed. Specialist work is bounded and purchased from contractors; no employer facilities or relationships are assumed.
- The closest incumbent notices after customer three and responds within six months.
- Control proof states what is owned, signed, produced, or routed by months 3, 6, and 12; original-founder spend remains below 100k PLN and no more than 40k precedes paid proof.
- Wealth uses 4–6× normalized EBIT, net of modeled debt, with no retained-earnings/distribution double count. Expected success must clear 10m PLN founder net worth without requiring the strong case.

## 1. PouchSense Hydrogel Shelf-Life Witness

### Cold acquisition — pass, hypothesis-heavy

The 100-account list can be built from FDA-cleared ECG/electrode records and public ISO 13485 electrode/CDMO directories. At 5%, 10%, and 15% response, the funnel yields 5, 10, and 15 technical conversations. At a conservative 20% responder-to-paid-feasibility conversion, that is 1, 2, or 3 paid 12–25k PLN studies. The sale does not require production integration: it buys a correlation experiment against the buyer's existing packaged electrode.

### Price pressure — pass

Stress a 20k PLN feasibility order to 16k PLN. Base direct delivery is modeled at 5k PLN laboratory/material cost and 2k PLN acquisition; stressed costs are 6.5k and 4k, leaving 5.5k PLN contribution. At scale, a 0.50 PLN witness falls to 0.40 PLN while a 0.15 PLN direct unit cost rises to 0.195 PLN; allowing 0.03 PLN fulfilment leaves 0.175 PLN positive unit contribution.

### Founder capacity — pass with bounded specialists

Founder work is customer selection, experimental design, analysis, documentation, and sales (15–20 hours/week). A polymer/packaging chemist and accredited measurement laboratory are contracted for 30–60 hours and discrete tests. Production integration is prohibited before paid feasibility.

### Incumbent response — conditional pass

An electrode OEM or pouch converter can create a humidity indicator. PouchSense survives only if the response predicts electrode impedance/noise/safety after aging across packaging conditions, supported by retained lots and an owned calibration dataset. A humidity-only response is an immediate kill.

### Control-point proof — pass

- **M3, ≤18k PLN:** three owned witness formulations, serialized prototypes, experiment protocol, and baseline aging data.
- **M6, cumulative ≤40k PLN and only after a paid order for the final 10–20k:** paid buyer-specific correlation; formulation and calibration improvements retained by contract.
- **M12, cumulative ≤90k PLN after successful delivery:** two independently paid studies, one design-in/conditional call-off, owned retained-lot library, and 5,000–20,000 pilot witnesses.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — pass in expected case

- **Conservative year 7:** 15m witnesses × 0.45 PLN + 12 programs × 50k = 7.35m revenue; 20% EBIT = 1.47m; 4× and 85% ownership = 5.0m PLN equity. Does not clear 10m.
- **Expected year 8:** 40m × 0.50 PLN = 20m revenue; 25% EBIT = 5m; 4×/5×/6× and 80% ownership = 16m/20m/24m PLN founder equity. Clears without distributions.
- **Strong year 9:** 80m × 0.55 PLN = 44m revenue; 28% EBIT = 12.32m; 5× and 70% ownership = 43.1m PLN.

**Result:** 0 failures. Advance to advisory evaluation.

## 2. PAIForge Biometric Challenge Artifacts

### Cold acquisition — pass, hypothesis-heavy

FIDO's public vendor/lab ecosystem, Android biometric implementers, national-ID suppliers, access-control OEMs, and biometric conferences can produce 100 named accounts. Five positive responses conservatively yield one prepaid 15–30k PLN pre-certification regression pack. The offer makes no certification claim and does not require laboratory accreditation.

### Price pressure — pass, narrow buffer on the first pack

A 20k PLN starter pack falls to 16k. Modeled materials/metrology and contractor delivery of 5k rises to 6.5k; 2.5k CAC doubles to 5k; shipment/handling of 1k leaves 3.5k contribution. A 70k PLN renewal falling to 56k retains a wider margin because mold, recipe, and reader fixtures already exist.

### Founder capacity — pass with a contract polymer scientist

The founder owns threat selection, experimental design, cross-reader analytics, and vendor sales. A polymer scientist/fabrication contractor works to fixed recipes; a security lawyer reviews safe-customer policy once. Capacity is capped at two pilots concurrently while employed.

### Incumbent response — conditional pass

Accredited labs already fabricate attack instruments and could sell kits; vendors can cast common silicone/gelatin artifacts. The defense is not the public material name: it is an owned recipe family, withheld rotating composition, cross-sensor response characterization, and rapid challenge-lot refresh. If common public materials find the same failures, the family dies.

### Control-point proof — pass

- **M3, ≤22k PLN:** six owned material recipes, molds, serialized artifacts, three-reader response matrix, and documented safe-customer policy.
- **M6, cumulative ≤40k PLN:** a prepaid challenge pack, contract retaining generalized material/response IP, and repeatability across two lots.
- **M12, cumulative ≤95k PLN after paid proof:** two unrelated buyers, a second rotating challenge generation, owned recipe/response library, and at least one renewal or call-off.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — pass in expected case

- **Conservative year 7:** 70 accounts × 65k PLN = 4.55m revenue; 25% EBIT = 1.14m; 4× and 85% = 3.9m PLN equity. Does not clear.
- **Expected year 8:** 180 accounts × 90k PLN = 16.2m; 30% EBIT = 4.86m; 4×/5×/6× and 80% = 15.6m/19.4m/23.3m PLN. Clears.
- **Strong year 9:** 300 accounts × 120k PLN = 36m; 34% EBIT = 12.24m; 5× and 70% = 42.8m PLN.

**Result:** 0 failures. Advance to advisory evaluation.

## 3. HumanEcho AMR Human-Perception Surrogate

### Cold acquisition — pass, hypothesis-heavy

European AMR OEMs/integrators and safety/perception suppliers provide more than 100 public engineering accounts. Five positive responses allow one 25–40k PLN paid feasibility order if the first sales artifact is a recorded cross-sensor repeatability comparison and a mailed passive PPE skin—not a promise of certification.

### Price pressure — pass

An 80k PLN production unit falls to 64k. A 25k BOM rises to 32.5k; 7k CAC doubles to 14k; 5k calibration/freight leaves 12.5k positive contribution. A 15k skin pack falls to 12k while a 4k direct cost rises to 5.2k and 2k sales/fulfilment leaves 4.8k.

### Founder capacity — pass with manufacturing subcontracted

Founder effort is signal design, analysis, sales, and acceptance documentation. Mechanical fabrication, sewing/coating, thermal control, EMC/safety review, and calibration are fixed-scope contracts. Before leaving work, the founder supports at most two active systems and ships standardized skins only.

### Incumbent response — conditional pass

Automotive pedestrian-target suppliers can down-market and AMR OEMs can build foam obstacles. HumanEcho must be materially cheaper, fit warehouse aisles, cover crouched/fallen/occluded/PPE cases, and supply calibrated optical/thermal/radar signatures plus continuously rotated skins. A geometry-only mannequin fails.

### Control-point proof — pass

- **M3, ≤25k PLN:** owned passive limb/body geometry, four PPE/occlusion skin formulations, optical/thermal measurements, and serialized physical stock.
- **M6, cumulative ≤40k PLN:** paid active-core feasibility and contract retaining core/skin/signature IP.
- **M12, cumulative ≤100k PLN after paid proof:** active multimodal core, two unrelated paid customers, four replacement skin SKUs, and regression software/calibration history.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — pass in expected case

- **Conservative year 7:** 45 accounts × 110k PLN = 4.95m revenue; 22% EBIT = 1.09m; 4× and 85% = 3.7m PLN. Does not clear.
- **Expected year 8:** 120 accounts × 140k PLN = 16.8m; 27% EBIT = 4.54m; 4×/5×/6× and 78% = 14.2m/17.7m/21.2m PLN. Clears.
- **Strong year 9:** 250 accounts × 180k PLN = 45m; 30% EBIT = 13.5m; 5× and 68% = 45.9m PLN.

**Result:** 0 failures. Advance to advisory evaluation.

## 4. LoopTruth Teleoperation/Haptic Calibrator

### Cold acquisition — pass, hypothesis-heavy

IEEE contributors, EU project participants, teleoperation vendors, mobile-robot OEMs, haptic vendors, and private-5G integrators can form 100 named accounts. Five positive responses could yield one 15–25k PLN paid cross-vendor comparison, although the budget owner is unclear.

### Price pressure — marginal pass

A 60k PLN unit falls to 48k. A 20k BOM rises to 26k; 6k CAC doubles to 12k; 5k calibration/freight leaves 5k positive contribution. This is too thin unless annual calibration or fixtures attach.

### Founder capacity — pass for the pilot, conditional at scale

A firmware/test contractor and calibration lab build the first unit while the founder designs correlations and sells. Six instruments/year are feasible while employed; a technician is required before scale.

### Incumbent response — fail

The buyer's existing oscilloscope, camera, load cell, and network tools plus routine engineering can reconstruct the measurement. IEEE/NIST standards make the setup easier to reproduce. No scarce component, protected reference material, or routed workflow prevents an incumbent response within six months.

### Control-point proof — pass, but only Tier-2-like

- **M3:** owned timebase/trigger firmware and fixtures.
- **M6:** paid comparison and one instrument.
- **M12:** calibration histories and several devices.

This is produced IP, but it does not create a hard control point by itself.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — fail

The expected 220-account model lacks a bottom-up proof that 220 teams will buy a dedicated instrument and annual calibration. A more defensible 100-account case at 60k PLN annual yields 6m revenue, 25% EBIT = 1.5m, 4× and 80% = 4.8m PLN equity. Even 150 accounts at 70k, 28% EBIT, 5×, 80% yields 11.76m but requires capturing a very large share of a diffuse, internally equipped niche. Expected success therefore fails the non-heroic wealth gate.

**Result:** 2 failures. Close before advisory evaluation. Preserve the synchronized round-trip measurement insight, but do not panel this business shape.

## 5. ActionKey Agent-Approval Terminal

### Cold acquisition — pass, trust-sensitive

Public enterprise-agent builders, NIST respondents, security vendors, regulated SaaS firms, and enterprise AI platform teams provide 100 named accounts. At five positive responses, a single 20–40k PLN paid design pilot is credible only when tied to one irreversible tool call and a working secure-display attack demo.

### Price pressure — pass

A 30k PLN pilot falls to 24k. Prototype/BOM and contractor cost of 6k rises to 7.8k; 4k CAC doubles to 8k; security review/fulfilment of 3k leaves 5.2k contribution. At scale, gateway gross margin is software-like and terminal BOM is a minority of blended revenue.

### Founder capacity — pass with security/hardware specialists

Founder owns threat modeling, policy schema, AI-tool integration, analytics, and sales. A secure-hardware engineer and penetration tester work fixed scopes. The first pilot permits only one action type and one agent stack; no broad governance suite is built.

### Incumbent response — fail unless cross-vendor routing is proven

Microsoft, cloud agent platforms, FIDO, Yubico, and privileged-access vendors can add software confirmation or secure-display devices. ActionKey has six months to prove that a separately trusted, cross-vendor display plus signed exact-argument receipts blocks attacks platform-local approval cannot. Without two routed stacks, the incumbent response is fatal.

### Control-point proof — pass

- **M3, ≤30k PLN:** five secure-element/e-ink terminals, owned firmware, issuer keys, exact-argument schema, and red-team demo.
- **M6, cumulative ≤40k PLN:** prepaid single-workflow pilot and contract routing every covered approval through the terminal.
- **M12, cumulative ≤100k PLN after paid proof:** two unrelated customers, two agent stacks, signed receipt registry, and independent penetration report.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — pass in expected case

- **Conservative year 7:** 60 accounts × 80k PLN = 4.8m revenue; 28% EBIT = 1.34m; 4× and 82% = 4.4m PLN. Does not clear.
- **Expected year 8:** 160 accounts × 100k PLN = 16m; 35% EBIT = 5.6m; 4×/5×/6× and 75% = 16.8m/21m/25.2m PLN. Clears.
- **Strong year 9:** 350 accounts × 140k PLN = 49m; 38% EBIT = 18.62m; 6× and 62% = 69.3m PLN.

**Result:** 1 failure. Advance to advisory evaluation, with cross-vendor need and platform response as explicit veto risks.

## 6. SmartPod Workwear Sensor-Pod Circulation

### Cold acquisition — fail

The public installed base of detachable-pod smart garments is not demonstrated. ETSA lists textile-service companies, not employers with already deployed compatible pods. At 5% positive response, the model cannot show that one of five responders has 50 compatible garments and authority to route pod custody without an OEM or laundry partner.

### Price pressure — marginal pass

Monthly price falls from 55 to 44 PLN/garment. Modeled pod depreciation/repair of 18 rises to 23.4; doubled CAC amortization is 10; support/logistics is 6, leaving 4.6 PLN positive contribution. A small loss or shipping increase destroys the margin.

### Founder capacity — pass at pilot only

Fifty garments can be supported within 25 hours/week using scheduled batches. Above 500 garments, warehouse/technician capacity is mandatory and must be paid from contribution margin.

### Incumbent response — fail

An RFID laundry platform, garment OEM, or textile-service provider can add pod custody and replacement using one ordinary process change. Cross-brand pooling is unlikely without OEM access to compatible spare pods and firmware.

### Control-point proof — fail

At M3 the founder can own 20 spare pods and a ledger, but this stock is model-specific and ordinarily purchasable. At M6 a single employer route is contractually controlled; at M12 the route remains copyable and depends on access to several OEM pod models. No durable Tier 1 or credible compounding Tier 2 control point is proven under 100k PLN.

### Founder wealth sensitivity — fail

The 30,000-garment expected model is not grounded in a named installed-base denominator. A more defensible 5,000 garments × 55 PLN/month yields 3.3m revenue; 18% EBIT = 0.59m; 4× and 80% = 1.9m PLN equity. The wealth path requires a heroic share of an unproved category.

**Result:** 4 failures. Close before advisory evaluation. Do not re-enter through a laundry partnership or generic fleet dashboard.
