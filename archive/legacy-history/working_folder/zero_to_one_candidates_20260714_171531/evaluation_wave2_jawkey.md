# Zero-to-One Evaluation: JawKey

## Diagnosis

JawKey is the strongest candidate so far because it combines a specific underserved user, direct self-serve proof, an owned biosignal device, repeat pads, and a credible path from assistive input to silent human–AI control. Unlike lab-reference ideas, it does not need an institution to confer authority before the first order. The existential questions are everyday false triggers, comfort, whether jaw control is genuinely preferred to existing switches, and whether Apple/Meta/GlassOuse can substitute. Its economics and capital path justify independent paneling.

## Evidence and Assumptions

GlassOuse, MouthPad, OS switch control and platform EMG/gesture products establish category demand and competition. Cross-user jaw separability, comfort, 25k units, CAC, returns, pad attach and 18% EBIT are hypotheses. Absence of executed sales is appropriate to the pre-implementation stage; the paid beta is the proposed proof.

## Overall Score

**83/100.** Weighted points 165.5/200; rounded score 83. This is advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1.** Owned electrode geometry, AFE, enclosure, firmware/model, calibration, inventory, opt-in jaw traces and pad supply are present at first order. Strongest copy risk: GlassOuse adds EMG or Apple/Meta offers adequate camera/wrist gesture control through its installed base.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, expected case.** 28.7m PLN revenue, 5.17m EBIT, 70% ownership and 4–6× produce 14.5–21.7m PLN founder equity in year 7. Twenty-five thousand global units across assistive and later hands-busy use is demanding but not inherently heroic. Debt is deducted and retained cash not double counted.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Ten prototypes and paid 899 PLN beta preorders fit 38k PLN and 180 days. Standard Bluetooth HID avoids clinic/platform partnerships; 25 preorders are the continuation threshold.

## Expansion Check

Assistive jaw switch is narrow and coherent. Silent AI and industrial/XR are asymmetric only if the neuromotor dataset and form factor transfer. Platform EMG is a major threat but may target a different wrist/glasses ecosystem.

## Fatal Objections

1. Speech/eating/teeth-clenching false triggers.
2. Dry behind-ear EMG may not be robust across users/hair/skin.
3. Existing head/bite/camera switches may be preferred.
4. Returns/support and platform imitation can erode margin.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Turn a private, low-motion neuromotor signal into standard cross-platform control without an intraoral device or visible speech.
