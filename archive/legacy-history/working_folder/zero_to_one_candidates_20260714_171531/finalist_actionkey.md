# Zero-to-One Candidate: ActionKey

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell enterprises a separately trusted e-ink approval terminal that displays exact consequential AI-agent action arguments, signs the human decision, and can physically block or kill routed actions across agent platforms.

## Founder Fit

The founder can model agent/tool flows, build policy/data systems, and run attack experiments. Secure-element firmware, hardware layout, penetration testing, and cryptographic review use bounded specialists.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

CISOs and agent-platform owners want agents to pay, delete, send, change access, or release data but cannot trust a prompt displayed by the same possibly compromised host/agent. Current controls are software approvals, logs, PAM, and security keys that often approve authentication rather than the exact human-readable action arguments.

## Product Wedge

A five-device pilot covers one irreversible tool call. A terminal receives a canonical signed action payload over an isolated channel, shows exact recipient/amount/resource/permission, requires explicit input, signs a receipt, and exposes a hardware kill switch. A small gateway refuses the covered call without that receipt.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Agent security products focus on policy and observability. The contrarian gap is a minimal physical trust edge for the small set of actions where a human wants proof that the displayed approval is not controlled by the agent requesting it.

## Underused Momentum

Enterprise agent deployment, NIST AI-agent security work, secure-payment confirmation precedent, and EU human-oversight expectations create urgency. None mandates this device.

## Beachhead Market

Security-conscious enterprise agent builders allowing one high-consequence cross-system action—initially payment release, privileged access, bulk deletion, or sensitive export—where the host compromise threat is explicit.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid single-flow design pilot: 20–40k PLN.
- Terminal: 2.5–4k PLN, expected BOM 0.8–1.2k PLN.
- Policy/receipt gateway: 40–100k PLN annual per account.
- Expected blended gross margin 72%; mature operating margin 35% after security engineering, support, hardware, audits, and sales.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

Build 100 accounts from NIST respondents, public agent platform builders, regulated SaaS/security teams, and enterprise AI engineering groups. First ten use one red-team demo and one action schema; first 100 require security integrators/PAM/agent-platform connectors, but the first pilot is direct.

## Primary Control Point

Owned trusted-display firmware, secure-element issuer keys, canonical action schema, device inventory, signed receipt registry, and a contract routing every covered approval through the gateway. Data value is secondary.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Up to 30k PLN builds five prototypes, issuer service, one tool connector, and an altered-argument red-team demo. A 20–40k PLN prepaid design pilot is required before production hardware or additional connectors. Month 12 proof is two unrelated customers/two stacks under 100k PLN.

## Distribution Advantage

The attack demo communicates value without founder status. Each connector and audited receipt schema reduces later integration cost; cross-vendor support can become more neutral than a platform-local control.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start with exact-argument approval for one agent action. Expand to standardized schemas, dual control, policy-bound hardware receipts, physical emergency stop, and licensed integration into terminals/wearables. Cross-vendor neutrality and security evidence must outpace platform replication.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

EU regulated buyers and human-oversight expectations can create early demand; Poland offers efficient security engineering. The market must be global, and regulation is not the moat.

## Compounding Advantage

Issuer trust, connector coverage, canonical action schemas, security audits, deployed terminals, and receipt verification create switching costs and a human–AI authorization protocol asset.

Milestones: attack demo month 3; paid pilot month 6; two stacks/two customers month 12; ten accounts year 2; 50 years 4–5; expected scale year 8.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Build one exact-argument workflow and five terminals within 12 weeks. Demonstrate host-message substitution and physical blocking. Approach 100 named security/agent teams for a prepaid 20–40k PLN design pilot. Maximum pre-proof loss 30–35k PLN; no broad platform is built.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. Up to 35k PLN for prototypes, one connector, threat model, counsel, and attack demo.
2. Up to 40k PLN cumulative before paid proof; production run only against the pilot.
3. Up to 100k PLN after delivery, positive stressed contribution, and a second customer/contracted recurring demand.
4. Family capital excluded. Maximum pre-proof loss: 35k PLN.

## Critical Dependency Map

- Buyer accepts separately trusted display threat model: paid pilot tests it.
- Cross-vendor exact-action schema: one connector initially, two by month 12.
- Platform/FIDO incumbent response: existential unless neutrality/receipts are superior.
- Hardware security: independent penetration review before claims.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Three independent paying accounts or contracted ARR, positive contribution including security/audit cost, repeatable acquisition, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Retain secure-display prototypes, action schemas, gateway code, attack harness, cryptographic receipts, and enterprise-security account map. Do not pivot to generic agent governance/dashboard software.

## Critical Evidence

NIST and Microsoft document agent-security concerns; FIDO shows transaction-confirmation precedent; EU AI Act Article 14 provides oversight context.

## Critical Hypotheses

Host-compromise/exact-argument risk has a budget; hardware is preferred to software confirmation; two platforms can be routed; 160 global accounts are attainable.

## Kill Criteria

- No prepaid pilot after 100 qualified accounts and 180 days.
- Buyers accept platform-local approval as equivalent.
- Two agent stacks cannot be routed under 100k PLN.
- Independent red team finds the terminal/gateway fails to bind displayed and executed arguments.
- Microsoft/FIDO/PAM incumbent feature removes WTP before customer three.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; contract secure-hardware engineer; penetration tester; part-time cryptography/legal review. Pre-proof 35k PLN; year-one ceiling 100k PLN.
