# Second-Wave Uniform Stress Tests

Same six simulations and kill rule as first wave. These are not scores.

## CueBand Duo

- **Cold acquisition — pass:** 100 qualified accessibility/gaming prospects at 5/10/15% response yield 5/10/15 paid-beta candidates. Even one 999 PLN preorder meets paid commitment; 20 preorders are the continuation threshold.
- **Price/cost — pass, thin:** 999 PLN falls to 799; a 400 PLN BOM rises to 520; 100 PLN CAC doubles to 200, leaving 79 PLN before overhead. Retail economics must improve before tooling.
- **Founder capacity — pass:** founder handles models/product/sales; one embedded contractor and contract assembler. Maximum two prototype cohorts while employed.
- **Incumbent response — fail:** Audio Radar can add wrist haptics and platform owners can expose native cue APIs. CueBand needs demonstrably lower visual load, cross-game classification, and learned tactile language before customer three.
- **Control proof — pass:** M3 owned dual-wrist prototype/models/corpus; M6 paid preorders; M12 serialized inventory, device keys, six cue packs and opt-in traces under 90k PLN.
- **Wealth sensitivity — pass expected:** 20k devices plus subscriptions gives 24.75m revenue and 4.95m EBIT; 4× and 75% = 14.8m PLN. Five-thousand-device conservative case fails, but 20k global units is plausible across deaf and silent-play segments if CAC/returns hold.
- **Result:** 1 failure; advance.

## JawKey

- **Cold acquisition — pass:** assistive-tech forums, marketplaces and loan/deposit applications make one paid 899 PLN preorder within 180 days credible at 5% response; 25 preorders continue the project.
- **Price/cost — pass:** 899 falls to 719; 350 PLN BOM rises to 455; 70 PLN CAC doubles to 140, leaving 124 PLN before overhead.
- **Founder capacity — pass with specialists:** ten prototypes and one standardized HID accessory fit 25 hours/week using analog/embedded and accessibility contractors.
- **Incumbent response — conditional pass:** GlassOuse/Apple/Meta can add gestures/EMG, but JawKey is behind-ear, private, cross-platform, and accessible without a platform/head-motion/intraoral dependency. It fails if those differences do not change user preference.
- **Control proof — pass:** M3 owned electrode/AFE/enclosure/model prototypes; M6 paid preorders; M12 inventory, pad SKU and opt-in multi-user traces under 95k PLN.
- **Wealth sensitivity — pass expected:** 25k devices plus pads gives 28.7m revenue and 5.17m EBIT; 4× and 70% = 14.5m PLN. The global assistive plus hands-busy denominator is large enough if false triggers and returns are controlled.
- **Result:** 0 failures; advance.

## OrphanFinger

- **Cold acquisition — pass:** exact discontinued part-number search, public notices and integrator listings support one 20–30% tooling deposit among five positive responders without a partner.
- **Price/cost — pass:** expected 1,800 PLN falls to 1,440; 500 PLN direct cost rises to 650; 150 PLN CAC doubles to 300; 200 PLN QA/liability leaves 290 PLN positive contribution.
- **Founder capacity — pass at three SKUs:** data/search/inventory work stays bounded; molding/testing contracted. Ten-plus SKUs need operations support funded by sales.
- **Incumbent response — conditional pass:** original OEM exited the SKU and a generic molder can copy one finger. Paid molds, exact-intent pages, batch documentation and stocked long tail provide speed, not technological exclusivity.
- **Control proof — pass:** M3 owned test geometry; M6 deposit-funded mold/lot; M12 three molds, specs and stock under 100k PLN.
- **Wealth sensitivity — fail:** the expected 30 SKUs ×500 sets/year is unsupported. At 30 ×200 ×1,800 = 10.8m revenue, 18% EBIT, 4× and 80% = 6.2m PLN. The family needs a broader wear-parts catalogue before the expected gate is non-heroic.
- **Result:** 1 failure; advance to advisory with wealth veto risk.

## CrashFuse

- **Cold acquisition — pass:** 100 public integrators at 5% response can yield one 8–18k PLN paid dummy-load configuration.
- **Price/cost — marginal pass:** 10k falls to 8k; 3k BOM rises to 3.9k; 1k CAC doubles to 2k; 1k test/liability leaves 1.1k contribution.
- **Founder capacity — pass for prototype:** mechanical/test contractors make one standardized interface feasible; scale is separate.
- **Incumbent response — fail:** ATI/DESTACO already sell collision protection and can add sacrificial cartridges through installed channels.
- **Control proof — pass but Tier-2-like:** owned geometry/load maps/stock exist, yet ordinary engineering reproduces them.
- **Wealth sensitivity — fail:** the expected 6,000 assemblies/year is heroic against established EOAT incumbents. At 1,500 assemblies and 4,500 cartridges, revenue is 19.1m PLN; 12% EBIT, 3×, 75% gives 5.2m PLN.
- **Result:** 2 failures; close before advisory evaluation.

## Result

CueBand Duo, JawKey and OrphanFinger advance to separate advisory evaluation. CrashFuse closes.
