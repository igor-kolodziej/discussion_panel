# Zero-to-One Candidate: JawKey

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell a behind-ear dry-EMG/IMU module that turns four to six deliberate jaw gestures into standard Bluetooth switch/keyboard commands for people who cannot reliably use hands and do not want voice, eye tracking, head motion, or an intraoral device.

## Founder Fit

Neuroscience, biosignal and ML experience fit electrode placement, false-trigger modeling, calibration and accessibility analytics. A contract analog/embedded engineer and occupational-therapy/accessibility researcher fill hardware and user-needs gaps.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Users with limited hand movement, tremor, fatigue, or episodic weakness need a reliable low-fatigue switch. Bite switches are intraoral; head mice require movement; voice is public/noisy; cameras need line of sight; eye tracking is expensive. JawKey aims for discreet cross-platform input.

## Product Wedge

An ear/glasses-hook module combines dry EMG and inertial sensing, local calibration, four to six intentional gestures, Bluetooth HID, and a replaceable skin-pad SKU. It is a computer input accessory, not a diagnostic or therapeutic device.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Assistive users often assemble compromises around their remaining reliable movement. Jaw movement is private, fast, and available when speech or head motion is not; the commercial gap is a self-contained non-intraoral switch rather than a clinic-custom system.

## Underused Momentum

Operating systems already support switch control, low-power wearable inference is accessible, and GlassOuse/MouthPad validate willingness to buy alternative inputs. Platform gesture/EMG launches validate demand and create copy risk.

## Beachhead Market

Adults with limited/variable hand control who can deliberately contract jaw muscles and already use iOS/Android/Windows switch control; developer edition initially avoids medical claims.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

899 PLN paid developer preorder, 1,299 PLN retail, replacement pad packs 60–120 PLN. Expected blended hardware revenue 1,100 PLN, BOM/fulfilment 350–450 PLN, 55% gross margin and 18% operating margin. Stressed 20% lower price, 30% higher BOM, and doubled 70 PLN CAC retain positive contribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First ten via assistive-tech forums, accessibility device communities, and paid beta applications; first 100 through accessibility marketplaces/direct search, device-loan deposits, clinician/AT directories, and standard Bluetooth-HID demos. No clinic partnership is required for first sale.

## Primary Control Point

Owned electrode geometry, analog front end, enclosure, firmware/model, calibration protocol, serialized inventory, opt-in jaw-gesture dataset, and replaceable pad supply.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

M3: ten prototypes and false-trigger bench/day-use tests under 24k PLN. M6: at least 25 paid preorders before cumulative spend reaches 38k. M12: 100 delivered devices, replaceable pad SKU, measured daily reliability, and ≤95k founder capital.

## Distribution Advantage

Standard HID makes the product self-contained and demonstrable without an app/platform deal. A device-loan/deposit path reduces trust barriers, while user calibration data improves model/generalization and direct search coverage.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Start as an assistive switch; expand to silent AI commands, hands-busy industrial input, AR/VR control and licensable jaw-gesture modules. Lower fatigue/privacy/cross-platform operation and a neuromotor dataset must make it better than head/camera/bite inputs.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland supports low-cost engineering; accessibility distributors and standards are global. EU accessibility momentum helps but does not protect the product.

## Compounding Advantage

Opt-in multi-user jaw traces, electrode/pad iterations, false-trigger data, local models, installed devices, and accessibility distribution improve both current reliability and future silent interfaces.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

95% reinvestment and ≤5% optional distributions while employed in conservative/expected; strong 100%. Prototype M3, 25 preorders M6, 100 units M12, 1,000 year 2, 6,000 year 3–4, expected scale year 7.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Build ten prototypes, publish false-trigger/latency/comfort protocol, and use paid beta applications with loan/deposit. One preorder is paid proof; 25 are needed to continue. Maximum pre-proof loss 38k PLN and no production tooling before payment.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

24k prototypes; ≤38k before paid proof; ≤95k after 25 preorders and positive contribution; remaining founder capital only after delivered reliability/returns; family capital excluded.

## Critical Dependency Map

Jaw EMG separability across users, electrode comfort, false triggers during speech/eating, accessibility safety, consumer returns, Bluetooth reliability, and platform/competitor response.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

At least 500 paid devices or contracted recurring demand, positive delivered contribution, repeatable direct channel, three independent cohorts, and 12 months runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Retain dry-electrode/AFE hardware, neuromotor data, low-power inference, pad consumables and accessibility relationships for silent HCI. Avoid medical-diagnosis pivot.

## Critical Evidence

GlassOuse, MouthPad and Apple head tracking show alternative-input demand; Meta's EMG interface shows large-platform interest and copy risk.

## Critical Hypotheses

Four to six gestures are reliable during daily life; 25k global units are reachable; user comfort/returns support margin; platform gestures do not substitute.

## Kill Criteria

Fewer than 25 paid preorders in 180 days; false triggers during speech/eating exceed threshold; median users prefer existing switches; stressed contribution non-positive; product requires individualized clinical fitting; expected CAC/returns block 25k-unit wealth case.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week, analog/embedded contractor, accessibility researcher/OT contractor, contract assembler after preorders. Pre-proof 38k PLN.
