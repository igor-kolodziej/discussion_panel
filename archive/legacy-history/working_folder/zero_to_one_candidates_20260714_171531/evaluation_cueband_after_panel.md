# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CueBand Duo v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

The exact canonical failed independently at 65 and 66 despite both judges being instructed that executed sales were not expected. The failure is structural: the startup would infer direction from mixed audio while Audio Radar or a platform can use cleaner signals, add haptic output, and distribute through an installed channel. The proposed tactile corpus and switching costs do not exist at first sale, and the path from 100 discoverable buyers to 20,000 annual devices is unsupported. This business family should be killed rather than reframed.

## Evidence and Assumptions

The panel recognized a credible part-time prototype and preorder test. It did not demand prior paid evidence. Both reviewers classified the control point Tier 3 and independently identified incumbent/platform copying plus weak bottom-up scale. Prices, returns, CAC, subscription attachment, classification accuracy, manufacturing burden and 20,000-unit demand remain hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**61/100.** Weighted points = 122.5 of 200; rounded score = 61. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** Owned product assets are reproducible; the proposed corpus, learned vocabulary and installed base are future possibilities. Strongest copy risk is Audio Radar or a platform pairing haptic endpoints with a cleaner directional signal and incumbent distribution.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The expected calculation clears 10m PLN arithmetically, but the candidate provides no bottom-up, repeatable route from the first 100 customers to 20,000 annual devices at the assumed margins and returns. The conservative case is 2.9m PLN.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass for paid interest, not delivered economics.** A prototype and preorder are plausible within 180 days and 38k PLN. This does not cure control or wealth failure.

## Fatal Objections

1. Adjacent incumbent and platform use cleaner data and existing distribution.
2. Cross-game classification/false-cue performance is existential.
3. Control assets compound only after scale.
4. No bottom-up acquisition bridge to the expected wealth case.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A low-visual-load tactile language can provide redundant access to spatial information, but this business shape does not control the clean signal or channel.

## Recommendation

kill
