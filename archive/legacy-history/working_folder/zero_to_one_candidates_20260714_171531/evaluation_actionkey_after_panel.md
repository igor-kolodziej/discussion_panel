# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ActionKey v1 After Panel

## Diagnosis

The panel confirms that the trusted-display insight is strong but the proposed business is not yet a business wedge. “One irreversible action” leaves buyer, budget, loss event, integration and enforcement abstract, while cross-vendor scope multiplies custom work. Conditional transaction routing is a Tier 1 design, but the platform/PAM/FIDO response can arrive before distribution or switching costs. A structural pivot is required to a specific smaller buyer and a downstream enforcement point that rejects all unapproved writes.

## Evidence and Assumptions

NIST/Microsoft/FIDO evidence supports the security problem. Panel scores were 55 and 73; both granted conditional Tier 1 and identified generic workflow plus incumbent bundling as fatal. No paid evidence is expected at this pre-implementation stage. Prices, procurement, margin and 160-account scale remain hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**66/100.** Weighted points = 131.5 of 200; rounded score = 66.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 conditional, Tier 3 before route.** A signed contract and downstream-only proxy credential can control a transaction. In v1 the enforcement point and bypass closure are unspecified. Strongest copy risk is bundled exact-action confirmation from a hyperscaler/PAM/FIDO vendor.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass mathematically, fail on business specificity.** Expected equity clears 10m PLN, but 160 accounts and 35% EBIT are not credible until a standardized workflow replaces bespoke connectors.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail as written.** Generic CISO procurement, five trustworthy prototypes and one arbitrary connector do not form a credible 180-day route. A smaller named buyer and one self-contained enforcement path are required.

## Expansion Check

Expansion is broad, but there is no beachhead. Platform incumbents can enter immediately. EU context does not solve acquisition.

## Fatal Objections

1. No named buyer/workflow/budget or loss event.
2. Incomplete display-to-execution enforcement and bypass closure.
3. Incumbent bundling before switching costs.
4. Integration and assurance labor undermine margin/reachability.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A human must approve the exact destructive action through a trust surface outside the AI agent/host, and the downstream resource must accept the action only with that signed receipt.
