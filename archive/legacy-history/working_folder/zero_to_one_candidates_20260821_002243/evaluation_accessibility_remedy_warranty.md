# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Accessibility Remedy Warranty

Date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Fresh independent advisory evaluation of the exact frozen finalist. Advisory only; this is not a panel or external-validation score.

## Diagnosis

The sharply bounded paid pilot is credible: one merchant-controlled versioned checkout component, manual assistive-technology evidence, remediation, and a first-remedy obligation. The company thesis is not. An existing web agency can add substantially the same testing and capped remediation SLA to an ordinary retainer. The venture cannot transfer the merchant's statutory responsibility and controls no scarce asset, channel, or workflow. Founder Wealth fails, Reachability passes with moderate confidence, and 12-month control proof fails.

## Evidence And Assumptions

Directly supported:

- Polish accessibility rules have applied since 28 June 2025 to covered consumer ecommerce; services provided by microenterprises are excluded; the service provider remains responsible. [Polish Accessibility Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/731/text.html)
- Enforcement can include compliance orders, service prohibition, and monetary penalties.
- The frozen candidate excludes fines, lost sales, and third-party code.
- Public audit/remediation offers span roughly PLN750–6k, remediation from PLN999, and recurring monitoring.
- The proposed first proof is a PLN5k payment before day 150 with PLN20k planned founder risk.

Unsupported hypotheses include treating the 11.2k electronic-order upper bound as a SAM, PLN12k annual willingness to pay, PLN5k–7k annual delivery cost, renewal, claim frequency, remediation hours, CAC, churn, 600 reachable customers, a 4× multiple, 80% founder ownership, cross-merchant corpus reuse, and agency resale without agency internalisation.

Other primary perimeter sources: [Polish Insurance Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2015/1844/text/U/D20151844Lj.pdf), [Polish Civil Code](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/795/text/O/D20260795.pdf), [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

The service is conditionally lawful. It leaves responsibility with the merchant and avoids fine/lost-revenue indemnity. Re-performing the vendor's own work, bounded remediation, and credits for its own SLA can be ordinary service remedies. Exact triggers still need insurance/liability counsel, and repository/staging access needs privacy terms.

Merchants can route around through periodic specialist audit plus incumbent-agency repair, an agency retainer with manual tests/SLA, internal automated checks plus occasional manual testing, or a component vendor's fleet-wide fix. Version locking, release hooks, first-remedy rights, and a corpus improve discipline but do not make catch-up hard. The one-vendor agency route-around caps the score below 80.

## Unit Economics And Denominator

At PLN12k annual price and PLN5k–7k claimed delivery cost, gross profit is PLN5k–7k and gross margin 41.7%–58.3%. The PLN5k pilot cannot cover all PLN10k budgeted engineering/tester work if that work is customer-specific. CAC must remain below roughly PLN5k–7k for one-year gross-profit payback, before overhead; neither CAC nor renewal is evidenced.

A corrected denominator is:

`11,200 × B2C share × direct merchant-controlled checkout share × supported component/stack share × recurring-release need × reachable budget/channel share`.

None of those fractions is measured. Six hundred customers are 5.36% of the unfiltered upper bound; if the filtered pool is 20%, they require 26.8% penetration, and if 10%, 53.6%. The 1,500-customer strong case is already 13.4% of the unfiltered bound.

**46/100.** Weighted points 92 across weight 20; `10 × 92 / 20 = 46`. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3. Twelve-month/PLN100k control proof: FAIL.** A repository hook and first-remedy right are account-level contracted access, not a scarce market route; they are revocable and replaceable. Tier 2 would require repeatable access plus switching cost or demonstrably compounding performance that the frozen plan does not prove.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

| Scenario | Founder result | Diagnostic |
|---|---:|---|
| Adverse | –PLN20k | Clear stop |
| Conservative | PLN0.576m | Explicitly below PLN5m |
| Expected | PLN5.76m before debt/reserves | Only PLN0.76m above gate; unsupported 600-customer case |
| Strong | PLN21.94m | No credible denominator, horizon, workforce, capital, or channel |

At 500 customers, otherwise expected assumptions yield PLN4.8m. At 20% EBITDA with 600 customers, result is PLN4.608m; at 3×, PLN4.32m. Service-credit reserves, net debt, or dilution reduce equity.

The candidate states 95% reinvestment and no separate retained-cash addition. Using EBITDA as an overgenerous distributable-cash ceiling, annual founder distributions while employed would be capped around PLN9.6k conservative and PLN72k expected before personal tax; actual cash is lower after tax, working capital, reserves, and capex. None is added to founder wealth, and prepayments/reserves are obligations, not wealth.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS — moderate confidence.** One named version-locked component, a PLN5k paid pilot before day 150, PLN20k planned risk, specialist contractors/testers, continued employment, and explicit non-qualifying free evidence form a credible experiment. The 50→5→3→1 funnel is unvalidated, but a single low-ticket regulation-triggered pilot is plausible. It proves buying interest/delivery access, not renewal economics or control.

## Expansion Check

More components, stacks, and agency resale are possible but weaken the boundary: more external causation disputes, less corpus reuse, greater agency visibility/internalisation, and higher liability. International expansion adds law/contract work. None validates 600 customers.

## Fatal Objections

1. Incumbent agency can bundle the same manual testing/remediation SLA.
2. Source/release access is nonexclusive, revocable, and not durable control.
3. The 11.2k figure is an upper bound; 600 may require implausible penetration.
4. PLN12k renewal competes with much cheaper audit/remediation.
5. Manual testing and remedies scale with customers unless reuse is proven.
6. Modest changes to count, margin, multiple, ownership, or debt break wealth.
7. Exact insurance/remedy trigger remains counsel-required.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Report-only accessibility work leaves the merchant coordinating the fix. Version-specific evidence coupled to a named party's bounded first-remedy obligation aligns incentives better than an audit alone.

## Recommendation

**Pivot.** The paid pilot is reachable, but Founder Wealth and durable control fail. The exact candidate is not panel-eligible.
