# Zero-to-One Evaluation: HeatCall Demand Mandate

Fresh evaluator used the complete `$evaluate-zero-to-one` skill and did not generate or pivot this candidate. Advisory score only.

## Diagnosis

HeatCall fixes the original incentive error: a committed buyer pays first and the source receives compensation for a short buyer-specific call. The proposed instruments could create Tier-1 control on one transaction, but acquiring both instruments is the hardest unproven part. The conservative wealth case reaches only about PLN 1.07m, and the expected case crosses PLN 5m only with aggressive closing volume, margins, retention, and valuation. Buyer prepayment, distribution, qualified-buyer denominator, and rapid two-instrument control are not credible enough for either hard gate.

## Evidence And Assumptions

### Sourced evidence

- [Directive (EU) 2023/1791](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/1791/oj/) creates defined assessment duties but compels neither a buyer nor source to hire HeatCall.
- [Commission Recommendation (EU) 2026/917](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026H0917) discusses barriers, intermediaries, aggregation, and long-term heating/cooling agreements but is non-binding and only indirect support.
- [URE market data](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/cieplo/charakterystyka-rynku/12966%2C2024.html) reports 398 licensed heat enterprises in 2024, including 347 with transmission/distribution licences, while noting exemptions below 5 MW. This does not establish 40 qualified private buyers in the chosen corridor.
- [HeatRec](https://heatrec.eu/en/) offers a free preliminary concept and full feasibility, funding, permitting, design, construction, and service; its published two-month simplified-feasibility and four-to-six-month development timelines are adverse evidence against the candidate's compressed schedule.
- [Veolia ESCO Polska](https://escopolska.pl/) already offers industrial efficiency and savings-based arrangements.
- [ORLEN Accelerator](https://www.orlen.pl/en/about-the-company/innovations/accelerator) validates industrial interest, not this intermediary's price or channel.
- [Polish Civil Code](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/795/text/T/D20260795L.pdf) supplies general contract tools but does not establish that the bespoke call has the intended remedy.

### Unverified hypotheses

- Forty buyers satisfy every authority, load, geography, timing, and budget filter.
- An unknown founder obtains PLN 20k–40k prepayment, sensitive demand data, and exclusivity before showing a source.
- A source accepts PLN 5k–15k for definite exclusivity, and the buyer pays a second PLN 25k milestone before route, permits, or finance.
- Closing, recurring-mandate, and administration prices are supportable.
- Sixteen mandates, twelve annual closings, and thirty administered projects can support 68% gross and 32% operating margin.
- Buyers permit reusable data and renew instead of internalising sourcing.

No interviews, mandates, calls, comparable fees, CAC, cycle, retention, or specialist-hour data exist.

## Lawful-Structure Check

No deception, coercion, hidden dual representation, or illegal dependence is required. The buyer is disclosed, the source is compensated, the engineer signs technical work, and construction remains outside the venture. Exact price, volume, delivery, availability, remedy, assignment, agency, energy-market, land, and permit terms remain counsel-dependent. The main failures are commercial rather than unlawful.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

The pivot legitimately moves payment to the buyer, compensates the source, names the buyer, limits assignment, attaches complete terms, uses buyer capital, excludes public procurement, discloses agency, and requires data permission. Contractual freedom cannot manufacture consent; named specialists do not transfer references; short exclusivity remains temporary; buyer funding does not shorten the multi-party sale; data licences may be refused.

## Overall Score

**50/100.** Weighted sum 99 across weight 20; `10 × 99 / 20 = 49.5`, rounded. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Current Tier 3; conditional Tier 1 per executed deal.** A prepaid mandate plus definite tripartite call would control one transaction, but both are speculative, correlated, and manually coordinated. The strongest copy risk is an engineering, ESCO, or development incumbent adding buyer-side origination and compensated-call documents to its existing technical and finance stack.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

- Conservative, year 7: `PLN 1.8m × 22% × 3 × 90% ≈ PLN 1.07m` founder equity. At the same economics, approximately PLN 8.42m revenue is needed for PLN 5m.
- Expected, year 8: `PLN 6.6m × 32% × 4 × 80% ≈ PLN 6.76m`; at 3× it falls to PLN 5.07m, and at 25% margin plus 3× to PLN 3.96m.
- Strong, year 9: approximately PLN 20.48m, but 30 mandates, 24 closings, 60 projects, 35% margin, and 5× are unsupported upside assumptions.

The model omits credible denominator, closing/staffing/CAC/cycle/retention evidence and later insurance, commission, legal, and technical overhead.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL.** Capital is disciplined, but before first payment the founder must identify qualified buyers, reach budget owners cold, overcome missing references, obtain sensitive data, prepayment, and exclusivity before showing a source, despite an incumbent offering a free concept and full stack.

Only about 65 days then remain to screen 30 sources, find technical/route fit, negotiate the compensated call and attached agreement, complete reviews, and obtain a second milestone. These correlated buyer, source, engineering, legal, and budget dependencies are inconsistent with the evidence and may exceed five hours daily.

## Expansion Check

The niche is focused, but the 40 local and 16 recurring buyer denominators are not substantiated. Annual mandates and administration are logical adjacencies; CEE expansion resets law, sources, and distribution. Incumbents enter from stronger engineering and financing positions.

## Fatal Objections

1. Conservative founder equity is only about PLN 1.07m.
2. Unknown-founder prepayment, sensitive data, and exclusivity before a source are unsupported.
3. The two-part control point requires correlated acceptances not credible inside 180 days.
4. Both buyer denominators are unverified.
5. Twelve bespoke closings and thirty administered projects are not reconciled with labor, CAC, cycle, or margin.
6. A full-stack incumbent can copy the structure before data compounds.
7. The call creates temporary deal control, not durable company control.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

- Document 40 prospects meeting every filter and their budget owners.
- Counsel confirms the mandate/call form within PLN 7k; secure the engineer within cap.
- By day 100, one unrelated non-family buyer prepays at least PLN 20k and supplies the exact mandate, data, and price envelope.
- By day 165, one source signs the exact buyer-funded compensated call.
- By day 180, buyer pays a second non-refundable PLN 25k milestone with founder cash exposure <=PLN 15k.
- Within 12 months, obtain a second unrelated mandate and exercise or funded extension.
- Actual hours, costs, source conversion, contribution, and founder time must support delegated delivery and >=25% operating margin.
- Direct price evidence must support closing and administration fees.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A buyer-funded procurement mandate paired with a compensated, objectively exercisable source call can convert analysis into controlled transaction rights without founder infrastructure capital.
