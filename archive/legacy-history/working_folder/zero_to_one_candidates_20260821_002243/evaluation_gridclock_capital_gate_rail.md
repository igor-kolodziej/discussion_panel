# Zero-to-One Evaluation: GridClock Capital-Gate Rail

Fresh evaluator used the complete `$evaluate-zero-to-one` skill and did not generate or pivot this exact candidate. The score is advisory only.

## Diagnosis

GridClock converts a real new statutory clock into a much more reachable, single-sided workflow. A fixed-fee reconciliation is credible as a bounded first sale, but the candidate is not yet more than specialised data reconciliation and compliance administration. Its recurring mandate, internal capital-gate status, derived-data rights, pricing, retention, and mature gross margin are hypotheses controlled by customers. The conservative founder-wealth case clears PLN 5 million by only about 12% and requires an unverified 36-account denominator, high annual pricing, 82% gross margin, and a 4× valuation simultaneously.

## Evidence And Assumptions

### Supported

- Poland's March 2026 Energy Law amendment establishes a one-year ordinary connection-condition validity period, subject to exceptions and transition rules.
- Statutory DSO publication obligations plausibly support a public time-indexed project dataset.
- GridLink advertises adjacent Polish grid-risk, diligence, M&A, and bankability work, supporting demand but disproving that diligence alone is differentiated.
- The founder fits data ingestion, entity resolution, anomaly detection, workflow automation, and quantitative delivery.
- The staged test fits the stated founder time and capital limits.

### Unproven

- A qualifying list of 60 Polish targets, much less 36 conservative customers, exists.
- Buyers will pay PLN 12k–20k for reconciliation before access and approximately PLN 48k plus PLN 1.8k per SPV annually.
- Customers will route all in-scope SPVs through the mandate, make its packet a required capital-release artifact, and license derived outcome data.
- Annual gross retention reaches 85%, mature gross margin reaches 78%–87%, CAC stays under PLN 15k, and CEE expansion does not recreate the product country by country.

No interviews, proposals, deposits, annual orders, renewals, measured delivery costs, data clauses, customer actions, or source-quality measurements exist.

## Lawful-Structure Check

The candidate does not require deception, coercion, transaction intermediation, or handling client funds. Written counsel clearance is still required for source reuse, private records, derived-data clauses, liability, and factual-monitoring language. These are launch conditions, not a moat. If the archive or data-right design cannot be cleared within budget, the intended control point fails.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

The pivot legitimately removes project ownership, brokerage, closings, and two-sided trust; selects one loss-bearing payer; uses client-controlled storage; targets independent developers; requires payment before private access; uses public records for acquisition; excludes acute rescue; and attempts annual routing plus historical/data rights.

The remaining power problem is not solved: the customer controls mandate scope, internal adoption, evidence, labels, reuse permission, and renewal. Public capture is non-exclusive, and an advisor or internal team can reproduce the method.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS narrowly, only for paid reconciliation proof.** The plan risks roughly PLN 29k before the intended deposit and caps 180-day exposure at PLN 39k. It requires neither a full product nor employment exit, an exclusive partner, a project closing, or family capital. A one-off payment does not validate annual demand, control, scalability, or wealth.

## Expansion Check

The Polish post-2026 niche and same-customer connection/security clocks are coherent. Lender procurement and CEE legal schemas are materially harder. A project-management vendor, advisor, or PMO can enter this niche more easily than GridClock can enter its broader relationships. Poland gives timing and execution advantage, not legal exclusivity.

## Fatal Objections

1. Thirty-six conservative accounts are unsupported and brittle; even a proposed 60-account list would imply 60% penetration.
2. The primary control point is customer-granted and nonexistent.
3. One paid reconciliation does not prove roughly PLN 111k annual willingness to pay.
4. Client-controlled storage, bespoke documents, verification, and security may prevent the assumed service-to-software margin.
5. Data defensibility needs both customer permission and a measurable learning effect.
6. Project-stage churn threatens 85% account retention.
7. Incumbent copying is a routine process change.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

- A named qualified universe of roughly 180 accounts, so 36 does not exceed 20% conservative penetration, with country-specific rule/budget relevance.
- Three unrelated paid reconciliations at stated price, prepaid before private access.
- Two 12-month portfolio-wide mandates using the stated formula and automatic in-scope additions.
- One counsel-cleared derived-data clause plus measurable evidence that the labels improve an outcome.
- Fully loaded delivery measurements above 70% current contribution margin and a credible 78%+ path.
- Reuse of the same product/schema on the second and third implementations without bespoke rebuilding.
- Written clearance for source reuse and private-record handling.
- Recurring budget or renewal evidence; one cleanup engagement is insufficient.

Failure to secure annual mandates, capital-gate use, or data rights within 12 months is control-point failure.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A continuously timestamped DSO archive joined to verified SPV identities and customer-confirmed discrepancy outcomes could become proprietary if recurring portfolio mandates supply the private verification layer.
