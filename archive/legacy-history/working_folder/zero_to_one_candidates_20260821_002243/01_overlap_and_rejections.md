## Repeated Failure Shapes Applied After Raw Generation

The live failure brain records 99 prior ideas. The following recurring mechanisms were treated as warnings, not automatic bans:

1. A report, audit, passport, or compliance memo with no contracted control over the subsequent transaction.
2. A generic marketplace that needs both sides before one party will pay.
3. Purchased hardware or inventory presented as a moat when incumbents can buy the same asset.
4. A regulation used as evidence of relevance but not of a private buyer, budget, or supplier mandate.
5. A data-access right that does not convey the tools, authority, or workflow required to act on the data.
6. A one-off diagnostic relabelled as recurring revenue without a mandatory renewal event.
7. Safety, clinical, QMS, or licensed authority described as a future partnership rather than an executed prerequisite.
8. A founder-wealth case requiring near-total penetration of a small buyer denominator or labor that scales one-for-one with revenue.
9. Options, LOIs, or reservations that are revocable, non-exclusive, unassignable, or cheap for the counterparty to route around.
10. Physical custody and operational data that improve the operator internally but do not create customer switching cost or transaction control.
