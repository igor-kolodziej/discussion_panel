## Cross-Portfolio Evidence

### Parcel surcharge economics

- **Verified tariff fact:** DPD's published domestic Pickup price lists apply a non-standard-parcel surcharge and a volumetric-weight fee. The public version effective 1 September 2025 shows PLN 29.90 for non-standard and PLN 3.00 per started volumetric kilogram over the threshold; DPD's documents page lists newer 1 March 2026 commercial documents. Sources: [DPD domestic price list](https://www.dpd.com/pl/en/dpd-pickup-domestic-price-list/), [DPD Poland document library](https://www.dpd.com/pl/pl/moje-dpd/dokumenty-dpd-polska/).
- **Verified classification fact:** DPD describes non-standard/oversize classification as depending on dimensions, shape, packaging, and sortability; InPost also reserves dimensional/non-standard assessments and extra fees in its published price documents. Sources: [DPD bulky-shipment explanation](https://www.dpd.com/pl/pl/2025/03/13/przesylki-gabarytowe-cennik-i-zasady-naliczania-oplat/), [InPost price lists](https://inpost.pl/cenniki).
- **Adverse market fact:** Apaczka already offers individual quotes for unusual, irregular, and bulky consignments. This proves demand and a route-around alternative; it does not show that Apaczka prepays/underwrites a SKU cohort at one immutable delivered price. Source: [Apaczka individual valuation](https://www.apaczka.pl/blog/jak-nadac-nietypowa-przesylke-indywidualna-wycena-dla-twojej-firmy/).
- **Unverified hypotheses:** a carrier or authorised broker will permit the venture to resell transport under its own all-in label; 2,000 historical shipments can be obtained with final adjustments; four narrow SKU families have stable enough loss distributions; merchants will grant 12-month exclusive routing for the covered cells.

### Grid-condition/SPV option timing

- **Verified law:** Poland's Act of 13 March 2026 changes Energy Law Article 7(8i): electricity-grid connection conditions are valid for one year from delivery, with stated exceptions; during validity, the conditions plus draft agreement are a conditional DSO commitment. The statute also adds deposits/security and transition rules. Source: [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 516, especially page 13 lines corresponding to Article 7(8i)–(8i1)](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/516/text/O/D20260516.pdf).
- **Verified market signal:** a public June 2026 listing offered 100% of a Polish PV project SPV with permit, signed connection agreement, and a stated June 2027 connection-agreement validity for EUR 20,000 plus specified reimbursements. This proves small-project SPVs can be publicly marketed and priced; it does not prove a seller will grant a cheap call before RTB. Source: [Gramwzielone listing](https://www.gramwzielone.pl/gielda-oze/sprzedam/20364531/sprzedaz-spv-projekt-pv-rtb-2-mw-pozwolenie-na-budowe-woj-opolskie).
- **Verified scarcity signal:** public market research in 2026 describes a very large Polish BESS/renewables connection queue and far fewer signed agreements than projects with conditions. This is directional commercial research, not a legal source. Source: [Modo Energy Poland BESS pipeline](https://modoenergy.com/research/poland-battery-pipeline-buildout-queue-capacity-market-auctions-subsidy-grid-connection-queue).
- **Verified incumbent capability:** GridLink advertises Polish grid-condition technical due diligence, bankability, M&A, and UC84 risk assessment. The finalist cannot claim diligence itself is unique. Source: [GridLink](https://gridlink.pl/en/index.html).
- **Unverified hypotheses:** owners of near-expiry, non-RTB SPVs prefer a low-cost salvage option to lapse; a call/irrevocable offer over shares can be made enforceable at the proposed budget; a buyer will pay for co-option/assignment before connection agreement; the relevant DSO/land/change-of-control terms survive the share transfer.

### Five-working-day phone/tablet spare parts

- **Verified law:** Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1670 requires manufacturers, importers, or authorised representatives to make listed smartphone/tablet spare parts available for the prescribed period and, for the first five years, deliver them within five working days after an order. Some parts may be limited to registered professional repairers. Source: [Regulation (EU) 2023/1670](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1670).
- **Verified official explanation:** Commission supplier guidance reiterates the five-working-day period, registration/access rules, and product/part scope. Source: [EU supplier guidance, version 19 August 2025](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/document/download/ad9a3460-9012-4e7e-90e2-0dcb545f5022_en?filename=CS_EN-MOBILES-Guidelines_Suppliers_2025_07_23_0.pdf).
- **Verified alternative supply:** Polish wholesalers already carry broad phone parts and consumer electronics; generic stocking/dispatch is not unique. Sources: [Mobilepart](https://mobilepart.pl/pl/), [Pines](https://www.pines.com.pl/), [Partner Tele](https://partnertele.com/).
- **Unverified hypotheses:** at least one small responsible importer lacks EU upstream fulfilment and owns enough covered installed units; it will pay a monthly reserve rather than self-stock; five high-failure SKUs can be held on consignment/buyback; professional-repairer orders and software pairing can be lawfully completed within the SLA.

### Medical-device discontinuation calls

- **Verified law:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 added MDR/IVDR Article 10a. From 10 January 2025, a manufacturer anticipating a qualifying interruption/discontinuation that could foreseeably cause serious harm must normally notify the competent authority and direct supply-chain recipients at least six months ahead; recipient economic operators propagate the information to direct customers. Source: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1860](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1860/oj).
- **Verified official interpretation:** Commission Q&A limits the duty to the qualifying serious-harm perimeter and explains timing/recipients; not every discontinuation triggers a public notice. Source: [Commission Article 10a Q&A](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/b431b10f-8512-4f47-9191-e1b84b2f9a27_en?filename=mdr_qna-article10a_mdr-ivdr_en.pdf).
- **Verified existing practice:** distributor channels already publish discontinuation notices, allocation, usage reports, residual-stock management, and substitutions. This is strong route-around evidence. Source: [Concordance supplier updates](https://www.concordancehealthcare.com/supplier-updates).
- **Unverified hypotheses:** a compliant Polish distributor will forward covered notices and grant exclusive calls on named lots; a hospital/private group can buy a commercial reservation without an open tender; one narrow UDI category has recurring shortage value but simple storage/QMS; option premiums support the founder-wealth case.

### Industrial waste-heat project rights

- **Verified rule context:** Directive (EU) 2023/1791 requires Member-State cost-benefit analysis and consideration of industrial waste heat; Annex XI specifies project comparisons, distance, demand points, technical feasibility, and financial cash flows. It does not compel a source to sell heat or buy a private study. Source: [Directive (EU) 2023/1791](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/1791/oj/).
- **Verified incumbent capability:** HeatRec advertises industrial waste-heat identification, feasibility studies, cost calculations, audit, design, permitting, construction, and service across Poland/Western Europe. Veolia ESCO also offers industrial efficiency and shared-savings models. Ordinary feasibility/ESCO work is therefore not new. Sources: [HeatRec](https://heatrec.eu/en/), [Veolia ESCO Polska](https://escopolska.pl/).
- **Verified buyer interest signal:** ORLEN's 2026 accelerator themes expressly include thermal integration and industrial waste-heat utilisation. This shows interest, not an accessible paid buyer or option budget. Source: [ORLEN Accelerator](https://www.orlen.pl/en/about-the-company/innovations/accelerator).
- **Unverified hypotheses:** an industrial source will pay PLN 15k–25k and grant a 24-month assignable option/non-circumvention right; a nearby offtaker/investor exists; the option can be transferred to an SPV; later infrastructure capital can be raised without founder guarantee; retained 10%–15% project participation is commercially plausible.

## Regeneration Wave One: Neutral Evidence Atoms

### Non-market renewable curtailment compensation

- **Binding right and payment form:** Article 13(7) of Regulation (EU) 2019/943 requires financial compensation for qualifying non-market redispatch, subject to exclusions such as accepted non-firm connection terms. Poland's 2023 Energy Law amendment provides that compensation is calculated and paid under an agreement between the issuing system operator and the generator/storage owner. Sources: [Regulation (EU) 2019/943](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PL/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R0943), [Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1681](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/1681/text/T/D20231681L.pdf).
- **Large and rising event volume:** URE reports that 2024 OSP non-market reductions reached 597.26 GWh for PV and about 125.1 GWh for wind; OSD reductions added 24.12 GWh PV and about 2.8 GWh wind. These figures establish event scale, not the eligible compensation amount or serviceable fee pool. Source: [URE 2024 redispatch report summary, 27 October 2025](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/12923%2CRynek-energii-elektrycznej-sprawozdanie-Prezesa-URE-dotyczace-mechanizmow-redysp.html).
- **Accessible operational route:** PSE's WOZE process expressly allows authorised representatives, and until bulk portal functionality exists it allows service providers handling many claimants to submit multiple applications by email. PSE also publishes event notices, formulas, and current documents. Source: [PSE non-market redispatch process](https://www.pse.pl/pl_PL/redysponowanie-nierynkowe), [PSE wind-installation application page](https://www.pse.pl/instalacje-fw).
- **Adverse competition:** Nowa Energia publicly describes a PV compensation-application tool; legal advisers market compensation support; HAL Group describes automation work for calculations and documentation. Sources: [Nowa Energia tool](https://nowa-energia.com.pl/2024/10/11/uruchomilismy-narzedzie-do-obslugi-wnioskow-o-rekompensaty-za-redysponowanie-nierynkowe-zrodel-pv/), [RSP Legal](https://rsplegal.pl/czwartek-z-energia-rekompensaty-za-nierynkowe-ograniczenie-pracy-oze/), [HAL Group](https://www.hal-group.net/work).
- **Unknowns:** eligibility/account denominator, actual claim and payment values, decision time, assignability or payment-direction rights, willingness to prepay or pay a success fee, access to source/settlement data, fully loaded processing labor, and whether recurring portfolio mandates create defensibility.

### PFAS firefighting-foam transition

- **Binding transition/management conditions:** Regulation (EU) 2025/1988 restricts PFAS in firefighting foams on phased dates and, from 23 October 2026 for covered continuing use, imposes conditions including separate collection, adequate destruction/irreversible transformation, and a site-specific management plan. The many use-specific derogations must not be collapsed into one deadline. Source: [Regulation (EU) 2025/1988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/1988/oj/eng).
- **Direct demand evidence:** a Polish cement plant issued a request for professional draining, removal, and disposal of old PFAS-containing foam and contaminated components. This is one procurement event, not a market denominator. Source: [Holcim Małogoszcz request](https://oneplace.marketplanet.pl/zapytania-ofertowe-przetargi/-/rfp/view/3057878/zapytanie-2-spuszczenie-i-utylizacja-starego-srodka-pianotworczego-w-cementowni-malogoszcz).
- **Existing capacity/adverse capability:** international specialists already offer complete AFFF transition and cleanout; Polish waste/fire-extinguisher operators advertise compliant treatment/logistics, although exact PFAS destruction capacity for concentrated foam remains unverified. Sources: [ECT2](https://www.ect2.com/afff-cleanout-transition/), [NEW TECH LAB](https://www.newtechlab.pl/).
- **Unknowns:** Polish assay/destruction capacity, lawful export route, site denominator and stock volumes, insurance, customer deposit size, whether treatment slots are scarce/reservable, and whether an unknown founder can contract the required capacity without becoming a replaceable coordinator.
## Regeneration Wave One — Asset Batch

All sources below were accessed 2026-08-21. Broad registries are upper bounds, not buyer denominators.

### Cloud Exit Bond

- Data Act Articles 23–30 require switching support, usually after up to two months' notice and a 30-day transition, with a technically justified alternative up to seven months. Provider switching charges become zero on 12 January 2027. Ordinary services, customer engineering, conversion, destination cost, and dual-running remain; functional-equivalence assistance is mainly an IaaS duty and provider IP/trade secrets are excluded. [Data Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj/eng).
- AWS, Google, and Azure already publish formal exit-data programs; destination providers and integrators offer migration tooling. This removes egress-fee scarcity but not executable workload recovery. [AWS exit transfer](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/free-data-transfer-out-to-internet-when-moving-out-of-aws/), [Google Cloud Exit](https://cloud.google.com/exit-cloud), [Azure cancellation/egress](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription).
- A manual source set verified ten Polish public-cloud customer references but does not reveal portable workloads, spend, renewal timing, internal engineering, or willingness to buy. No source supports 60 customers at PLN160k annually.
- First disconfirming proof: one paid PLN20k drill on one named workload with customer-owned export, destination acceptance, full restore, measured RTO/RPO, dependency inventory, and only capped remedial labour/service credits. Cash indemnity remains insurance-perimeter counsel work.

### Battery Residual Floor

- Present BMS state-of-health access does not cover ordinary forklift/off-road industrial traction batteries. Battery passports start 18 February 2027 for newly placed LMT, EV, and industrial batteries above 2 kWh; legacy packs do not become passported retroactively, restricted-field access rules were still incomplete. [Batteries Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02023R1542-20250731), [Commission DPP timetable](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en).
- Repurposing can make the operator a manufacturer and producer with conformity, EPR, transport, testing, warranty, and possible waste obligations. Title wording does not remove them.
- Toyota reports about 250k European rental trucks but retains traceability and internal reuse; it is not a Polish/reachable pack denominator. Jungheinrich already bundles battery rental, service, recycling, refurbishment, stationary second life, and return control. [Toyota circularity](https://toyota-forklifts.eu/about-toyota/sustainability/environment/circular-economy/), [Jungheinrich rental](https://www.jungheinrich.pl/produkty/wynajem-wozkow-widlowych/wynajem-akumulator%C3%B3w-i-prostownik%C3%B3w-439910), [Jungheinrich lifecycle](https://www.jungheinrich.co.uk/your-solutions/lithium-ion-technology/lithium-ion-products/li-ion-battery-life-cycle-1291296).
- New Polish lead-acid traction packs are publicly listed around PLN3.5k–27.8k, making a PLN2.5k floor fee economically unsupported. Exact shape rejected.

### RailSlot

- Freight-wagon ECM certification is mandatory. Delivery can be outsourced, but the maintenance-delivery party issues release to service and the ECM retains return-to-operation responsibility. [Regulation 2019/779](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2019/779/oj/eng).
- ERA reports 71,818 Polish freight wagons and 142 ECM-certified organisations. A mechanical VKM read found 453 exact Polish keeper names and 305 unique websites, but includes non-buyers and gives no intervention rate or merchant capacity. [ERA Poland factsheet](https://www.era.europa.eu/assets/factsheets/poland/ERA_factsheet_PL.html), [VKM register](https://www.era.europa.eu/domains/registers/vkm_en).
- GATX directly schedules 6,000+ planned revisions annually across 40+ workshops, runs an Ostróda shop with 2,000 revisions and 8,000 wheelset repairs, and can provide replacement wagons. Direct alternatives include Tabor Dębica, Polski Tabor, and DB Cargo Polska. [GATX](https://www.gatx.eu/pl/uslugi/utrzymanie/).
- No public priority-slot price, forfeiture term, or assignability evidence exists. Cheapest proof is one tri-party keeper–ECM–maintenance-delivery agreement for one wagon, exact work/window, a PLN12k customer payment before the full shop deposit, and explicit late-arrival/excess-scope/transfer terms.

### Lab Analyzer Freedom Lease

- An unmodified compliant used analyser can be distributed after CE/DoC/information/UDI checks. Own-brand marketing, intended-purpose change, material modification, or full refurbishment can make the venture the manufacturer. [IVDR Arts. 2, 14, 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/746/2025-01-10/eng).
- Open systems exist, but each reagent/calibrator/control still has an intended-use and performance path. [RIELE](https://riele.de/open-system-for-reagents/), [Diatron KleeYa](https://www.diatron.com/immunoassay-kleeya-chemiluminescent-analyzer).
- KIDL lists 2,554 laboratory sites/1,851 exact entity strings. A deliberately loose mechanical private/general-lab filter leaves at most 613 sites/518 normalised organisations; analyzer type, volume, contract expiry, ownership, and buying authority are unknown. At least 225 rows match Diagnostyka/ALAB/Synevo brand families and cannot be treated as independent buyers. [KIDL](https://kidl.org.pl/laboratory).
- Sysmex and Roche explicitly validate incumbent analyzer-placement plus reagent/service economics. Polish public procurements show 490k and 976k PLN bundled packages but do not support a sub-100k used asset or PLN140k annual unit revenue. [Sysmex](https://www.sysmex.co.jp/en/ir/individual-investors/), [Roche 2025](https://assets.roche.com/f/176343/x/fca190f63e/fb25e.pdf).
- One hundred placements imply roughly PLN10m gross analyzer capital before swaps/reagents/maintenance. Cheapest proof is one PLN20k conditional order for one serial, full regulatory/service/reagent/QC evidence, minimum tests, swap SLA, and a purchase below PLN100k.

### Fit-Out Future Title

- Polish Civil Code Arts. 47–48 prevent attached building components from being separate owned objects before detachment; Art. 155 §2 requires delivery/possession for ownership transfer of future/type-identified goods. A pre-strip deed can create a conditional sale claim, not present separate title. [Civil Code](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1061/text.html).
- Waste status depends on discard intent, not contract labels. Used construction products must be non-waste/end-of-waste, and first supply after deinstallation can be a new placing on the market. [Waste Framework Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02008L0098), [CPR 2024/3110](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3110/oj).
- JLL/Tétris and major landlords already inventory, coordinate, reuse, donate, and execute circular fit-outs. Public portfolio/lease areas do not measure annual reusable strip-out. No evidence supports PLN65/m² or 120k m²/year. [JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-uk/guides/why-circular-fit-outs-make-environmental-and-commercial-sense), [Tétris Poland](https://www.tetris-db.com/en/where-to-find-us/poland/).
- Exact present-title mechanism rejected.

### Battery repurpose

- A repurposer that first markets the resulting battery becomes producer/EPR actor. Waste status needs documented state-of-health/testing and intended further use; ADR classification varies with condition. No defensible Polish reusable-return-stream denominator or public yield/margin exists. greenSANE already builds second-life systems; POSCO Brzeg Dolny directly sources scrap/used batteries. [Batteries Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1542), [ADR](https://unece.org/info/Transport/Dangerous-Goods/pub/395786), [greenSANE](https://greensane.pl/en/), [POSCO](https://newsroom.posco.com/en/posco-builds-ev-battery-recycling-plant-in-poland/).
- Exact title-taking producer rejected without one named importer stream, licensed chain, testing protocol, and buyer-backed batch.

### Accessibility component warranty

- Poland's Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025 to covered consumer ecommerce, excluding microenterprise services. Merchant responsibility remains. An approximate GUS upper bound is 11.2k non-micro firms with some electronic orders, but includes B2B, marketplaces, and firms without controlled checkout components. [Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/731/text.html), [official guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/dostepnosc-cyfrowa/polski-akt-o-dostepnosci--uslugi-handlu-elektronicznego).
- Public audit/remediation offers range from roughly PLN750–6k, with remediation from PLN999 and recurring monitoring available. A PLN12k annual warranty needs value beyond commodity audit. Retain only one named, versioned component pilot with baseline assistive-technology test, capped labour/credits, external-change exclusions, and measured claims cost.

### Waste-shipment guarantee

- Guarantees are approved per notification number, mass, transports, beneficiary, and sum. An umbrella bank facility may support issuance, but an allocation slot is not a transferable regulator-approved asset. Official GIOŚ reports show only 282 decisions in 2024 and 322 in 2025; approximate normalisation found about 72 and 92 unique Polish notifiers. The proposed 500 annual notifications exceeds the total national decision flow. [WSR Art. 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1157/oj/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1157), [GIOŚ register](https://www.gov.pl/web/gios/rejestr-zgloszen).
- Stena and Moravia already bundle classification, notification, regulator contact, guarantee calculation/arrangement, and closure. Exact umbrella-slot mechanism rejected.

### Reusable heat-equipment racks

- PPWR 2030 transport-packaging targets contain exemptions and await methodology. CHEP, bekuplast, PFP, and DAJANO already rent/pool/design transport assets. Public steel-container comparators imply 150 units can cost well above PLN100k.
- 80k rotations with 150 racks requires 533 cycles/rack/year—one full cycle every 16.4 hours. Exact economics rejected. [PPWR Art. 29](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj/eng), [CHEP](https://www.chep.com/pl/en/why-chep).

### No-Burn apparel

- ESPR prohibits large enterprises from destroying unsold apparel from 19 July 2026, but recycling is itself destruction absent a derogation; genuine reuse/resale differs. Stock Polska, PALLEXA, Ceneco, and Unfrosen already buy/title/route excess stock, including pre-collection payment and thousands of buyers. Exact candidate has a wrong default recycling branch and mature route-around. [ESPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/eng), [Delegated Regulation 2026/296](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0296), [Stock Polska](https://www.stock-polska.com/for-sellers), [Unfrosen](https://www.unfrosen.pl/pages/sell-excess-fashion-inventory).

### Sewage-ash phosphorus

- Directive 2024/3019 encourages nutrient recovery but leaves any numerical phosphorus rate to a delegated act due by 2028. Polish strategy says only mono-incineration ash is plausible. Historical evidence suggested about 11 mono-incinerators; current thermal sludge tonnage is not ash tonnage. REMONDIS and EasyMining operate/fund plants at 20k–30k t/year and can contract utilities directly. Public disposal tenders do not establish a PLN500/t contribution. Exact aggregator held/rejected. [Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L3019), [REMONDIS](https://www.remondis-sustainability.com/sustainability-from-a-to-z/phosphorus-recovery/), [EasyMining](https://www.easymining.com/projects/plant-projects/Ash2Phos-plant-schkopau/).

### TraceSwap

- Pharmaceutical GDP supports risk-based calibration and at least annual transport-monitor calibration, not a universal rental mandate. The official active cold-chain wholesale subset is 217 sites/168 unique owner NIPs, not 850 facilities. Ellab already rents calibrated systems with hot-swap support; simple Testo loggers and calibration are inexpensive. Exact shape rejected. [GDP](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52013XC1123%2801%29), [official registry](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/dataset/2134%2Crejestr-hurtowni-farmaceutycznych), [Ellab](https://www.ellab.com/monitoring/continuous-monitoring-systems/monitoring-equipment-rentals/).

### GrainFloor

- Food/feed contaminant rules are defect and end-use specific; feed traders can be regulated even without custody. Broad production/purchase tonnage is not the subset with lawful alternative use and unmatched demand. ELEWARR/ADM/Cargill already test, discount, clean, dry, reject, and buy. Proposed PLN100/t premium/spread is about 12% of current wheat price and unobserved; 2,000 tonnes has ~PLN1.6m face. Exact broad put rejected. [Regulation 2023/915](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ENG/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R0915), [ELEWARR](https://elewarr.pl/oferta/).

### CancelStock

- Polish Civil Code Art. 644 already places much WIP cancellation cost on the customer. Protolabs/Xometry terms, progress billing, retention of title, and trade-credit insurance route around. Broad GUS survey count cannot support 800 eligible custom-WIP contracts; title, IP, waste, and adverse selection remain. Exact broad floor rejected. ### ChemCase

- PPWR Article 29 expressly excludes dangerous-goods transport packaging from the claimed target. SCHÜTZ, Greif, and Mauser already collect/recondition IBCs and drums. 250–500 packages cannot produce 120k rotations; implied cycles are physically shorter than incumbent collection lead times. Exact shape rejected. [PPWR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj/eng), [SCHÜTZ](https://www.schuetz-packaging.net/schuetz-poland/en/ticket-service/), [Mauser](https://mauserpackaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/SD-115-Collection-Terms-and-Conditions-Rev.pdf).

### RainRight

- No alienable statutory roof-runoff licence exists; the control is an ordinary property contract. At Warsaw rainfall, 500k m³ requires ~1.22m m² roof before losses. Direct tank/retention suppliers let owners internalise. Proposed PLN20/m³ exceeds Warsaw's ~PLN6.02/m³ water tariff and even the combined water/sewer tariff. Exact external-sale shape rejected. [Water Law](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/download.xsp/WDU20250000960/T/D20250960L.pdf), [MPWiK tariff](https://mpwik.com.pl/view/taryfa-za-zbiorowe-zaopatrzenie-w-wode-i-zbiorowe-odprowadzanie-sciekow-na-terenie-miasta-stolecznego-warszawy-gmin-michalowice-nieporet-raszyn-serock-wieliszew-oraz-miast-piastow-i-pruszkow-obowizujca-od-11-lipca-2026-roku).

### Orphanware

- CRA creates support obligations but no source/build/signing transfer. Software-law error-correction/interoperability exceptions do not grant signed-update distribution. Escrow providers publish low-thousands-euro plans, and Evernex/Rimini Street already serve EOL estates. No denominator or <PLN80k exclusive licence evidence exists. Generic shape rejected until one named SKU and signed rights-owner option exists. [CRA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), [EscrowDirect](https://pricing.escrowdirect.eu/pricing?lang=en), [Evernex](https://evernex.com/pl/baza-danych-eosl/).
