# Zero-to-One Evaluation: GridClock SPV Call Book

## Diagnosis

The new Polish regime creates a real sourceable clock: ordinary electricity conditions issued from 30 April 2026 generally last one year, and DSOs must publish applicant/location/capacity/date data. An executed call over a named SPV could create genuine deal control without construction finance. The candidate nevertheless depends on a fragile overlap: a distressed owner granting cheap control, a clean project, and a professional buyer prepaying before RTB. Its wealth model assumes high close rates, contribution, and valuation without transaction evidence, while incumbents can copy the structure and possess buyer trust.

**Fatal mechanism:** maturity-value adverse selection. Projects cheap enough for an unknown intermediary to option for PLN 5k–12k are likely least able to support a PLN 150k–350k assignment fee; projects valuable enough for that fee attract direct buyers and developers before the clock becomes acute.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer:** owner loses sunk work/optionality at lapse.
- **Payer:** downstream developer funds reservation, assignment/exercise, owner strike, and later obligations.
- **Beneficiaries:** owner salvages value; buyer gets screened path; venture captures fee.
- **Authority holders:** DSO, shareholders, landowners, public permitting bodies.
- **Distributor:** founder cold outreach; no exclusive sourcing/buyer channel.
- **Information holder:** DSO/SPV owner; public record is partial.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears option/legal/technical/circumvention/expiry; buyer later bears project capital.
- **Transaction owner:** owner until option; venture controls only that deal until expiry.

## Lawful-Structure Check

No prohibited conduct is indispensable. The candidate correctly targets corporate rights, named buyers, diligence, and buyer-funded closing. The option must use required form, fit SPV articles, and survive land, permit, DSO, beneficial-owner, and change-of-control terms. Whether assignment/co-exercise preserves the stronger remedy is counsel-required. Commercial adverse selection and copyability remain after legality, so structural pivot is warranted.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

Corporate-share control avoids pretending the condition is transferable; buyer reservation avoids balance-sheet capital; simultaneous closing avoids strike finance; named outreach avoids a public share marketplace. But sellers can list directly, buyers can mine the same records, and advisors can introduce the same call with greater trust. No exclusive sourcing, buyer mandate, fee evidence, or switching cost exists beyond each expiring option. Changing payer, maturity band, transaction role, or long-term control is a pivot, not a wording repair.

## Overall Score

**46/100.** Weighted points 91.5 / 20 = 45.75. Economics, two-sided distribution, durability, and both hard gates—not originality or legality—hold the result down.

## Control Point

**Tier 1, conditional and deal-specific.** A properly executed call over 100% of a named SPV is exclusive. None exists, and the business has no Tier-1 owner-funnel or buyer-distribution control. Established brokers/advisors/developers can combine public clocks with the same template and stronger relationships.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The scenario set lacks a separately labelled strong-success case, and the threshold case is not conservative: 24 options/10 closes means ~42% close-through; PLN 250k is the top stated contribution; a lumpy expiring key-person business gets 5x; dilution is unsupported.

| Assumption | Annual closes needed for PLN 5m founder equity |
|---|---:|
| PLN 250k contribution, PLN 1m overhead, 5x, 75% | 10 |
| PLN 150k, same other inputs | 16 |
| PLN 100k, same other inputs | 24 |
| PLN 250k, 3x, 65% | 15 |

At 20% close-through, five closes x PLN 250k leave PLN 250k EBITDA after overhead, worth ~PLN 0.94m to founder at 5x/75%. The stated 95% reinvestment is not translated to accumulated cash.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** Budget/calendar are explicit, but paid proof requires one asset simultaneously to have in-scope remaining life, correct owner matching, full disclosure, clean land/corporate/tax/DSO treatment, enforceable assignable call, seller price <=PLN 15k, and buyer prepayment before RTB. The budget tolerates no failed asset. The first acute expiry cohort arrives near the end of the window. Counsel/engineer are purchasable; seller control, quality, and institutional payment are independent existential dependencies.

## Expansion Check

The statutory cohort is a real beachhead, but frozen wording mixes technologies and no power/value band. Adjacent DSOs/technologies add denominator and variation. Polish law/data give local advantage accessible to every developer, broker, lawyer, and advisor; no evidence the option book compounds faster.

## Sensitivity

- Seller option >PLN 15k breaks the proof budget.
- If buyers require RTB/investment committee before payment, the paid event disappears.
- Low maturity raises rejection; high maturity raises strike/incumbent competition.
- 42% to 20% close-through destroys the wealth case.
- PLN 250k to PLN 150k contribution raises required closes from 10 to 16.
- 5x/75% to 3x/65% requires 15 closes even at PLN 250k.
- Legal/technical delay directly erodes the expiring asset.

## Fatal Mechanism

Maturity-value adverse selection: cheap seller control and high buyer-paid fees are least likely to coexist in the same project.

## Fatal Objections

1. Maturity-value adverse selection.
2. Wealth needs top-range contribution, ~42% close-through, and 5x.
3. Sensitive seller documents and institutional buyer payment both precede revenue.
4. Call protects one deal while the business method remains copyable.
5. Budget/time tolerate no failed first option.
6. Every asset expires, forcing manual replenishment.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A public, time-indexed map of Polish SPVs approaching grid-condition expiry, combined with a standard legal/technical red-flag taxonomy.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

- Counsel confirms form, assignment/co-exercise, Article 182¹ outreach, and typical DSO/land control terms within budget.
- 100 correctly named post-30-April owners from current DSO records.
- Five full document sets; two owners accept <=PLN 15k options.
- Three unrelated buyers provide written, budget-backed pre-RTB reservation criteria.
- Ten comparable actual near-expiry transactions support fees/attrition.
- One option and one non-refundable reservation within PLN 40k.

## Recommendation

**Pivot.** The exact candidate fails both hard gates and is ineligible for panel review.
