## Immediate Historical Cuts

- FlexMW site options, FuelEU calls, F-gas delegations, curtailment claims, construction retention, Data Act machine procurement, lift-service routes, PFAS destruction capacity, generic clinical archives, and distressed software tails all have prior near-exact tests. Wording changes do not reset their evidence.
- CBAM/EUDR titles create statutory responsibility and working-capital exposure; they do not create upstream truth, cheap finance, or supplier exclusivity.
- Local container, deposit, textile, garage, parts, feedstock, and waste routes remain reproducible by operators that already own vehicles, facilities, licences, or customer relationships.

## Bounded Research Questions

The retained directions must answer, before finalist status:

1. What is the deduplicated reachable buyer count after legal/technical/volume filters?
2. What observed contract, price, bid, option, or asset term supports the first payment and control instrument?
3. Who funds inventory, collateral, warranty, and downside before paid proof and at scale?
4. Can the founder secure the exact right within 180 days/40k PLN and prove durable control within 12 months/100k PLN?
5. Can an incumbent reproduce the control with one ordinary customer, vendor, workshop, bank, or equipment contract?
6. Does conservative founder wealth still exceed 5m PLN after lower margin/multiple/ownership, operating staff, claims, asset debt, and non-selling cohorts?
