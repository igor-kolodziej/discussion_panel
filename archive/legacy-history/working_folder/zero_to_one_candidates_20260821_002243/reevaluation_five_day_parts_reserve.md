# Zero-to-One Re-evaluation: Five-Day Parts Reserve

Fresh evaluator applied the complete `$evaluate-zero-to-one` skill. The prior score only defined proof questions. Advisory only.

## Diagnosis

The five-working-day obligation is real, but it binds the manufacturer, importer, or authorised representative; outsourcing physical performance does not transfer accountability. Research weakens the market case: 89 Poland-declared supplier organisations are a broad universe, importer and authorised-representative roles total at most 52 before overlap, and seven umbrella operators represent about 59% of Poland-available model rows. Cheap 3PLs, same-day wholesalers, and integrated service make the proposed PLN 4k–7k monthly premium and exclusive routing difficult to assume. Founder Wealth fails; a paid test remains narrowly reachable because inventory is staged after prepayment.

## Incentive And Power Map

- **Loss bearer / payer:** regulated operator bears compliance/service/warranty loss and pays only if external reserve beats factory, self-stock, AR, wholesaler, or 3PL.
- **Beneficiary:** repairer/user gets faster parts; operator gets records.
- **Authority:** market-surveillance authority enforces; operator retains accountability.
- **Distributor:** operator page/repair network; venture controls orders only after exclusivity.
- **Information:** operator/manufacturer holds installed base, failures, SLA, stock, lifecycle, and software access.
- **Supply controller:** manufacturer/authorised channel controls authentic parts, replenishment, serialisation, and pairing.
- **Risk bearer:** venture bears service/inventory risk; operator retains statutory liability.
- **Transaction owner:** venture only for the exact paid named-SKU term.

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

No deception, counterfeit supply, unlawful liability transfer, unauthorised pairing, or unsafe transport is required. The venture can perform and document fulfilment but cannot claim that outsourcing transfers statutory accountability.

Prepayment, consignment/buyback, outsourced shelf, five SKUs, pairing exclusions, EPREL outreach, routing exclusivity, and one-model scope reduce risk. They do not prevent self-stock, upstream SLAs, existing 3PL reserved bins, wholesalers, or six-month replacement. Different payer/ownership/duration/risk structures require a true pivot.

## Overall Score

**42/100.** Weighted points 84.5 across weight 20; `10 × 84.5 / 20 = 42.25`, rounded. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 current.** A paid bundle could control one account, but all rights are speculative. Strongest copy risk: importer, AR, wholesaler, or 3PL adds reserved bins, SLA, and logging through a routine change; six-month control is not durable.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** Stated conservative arithmetic gives PLN 6.35m founder equity from 60 accounts × PLN 68k contribution, PLN 1.88m EBITDA, 4.5×, and 75% ownership, but every decisive input is unsupported and a strong-success case is missing.

Sensitivity:

- 10% contribution decline → PLN 4.97m.
- 3.5× rather than 4.5× → PLN 4.94m.
- PLN 0.4m extra overhead → PLN 5.00m.
- 50 accounts → PLN 4.05m; 40 accounts → PLN 1.76m.

At least 55 accounts are required under stated assumptions. Poland has at most 52 importer-plus-AR role entries before overlap, forcing unproved CEE expansion and high penetration.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS narrowly and conditionally.** Roughly PLN 13k precedes payment; 3PL setup and up to PLN 15k inventory follow a signed prepaid protected contract. The single contract must nevertheless include data, gap acknowledgement, PLN 24k–42k prepayment plus setup, exclusive routing, and downside protection.

The verdict becomes fail if another upstream party must first grant protection, stock precedes payment, spend exceeds PLN 40k, exclusivity is omitted, or the reserve is refundable rather than revenue.

## Expansion Check

CEE expansion adds legal-entity mapping, acquisition, fulfilment nodes, tax/transport, and authentic-parts rights. Umbrella representatives reduce payer count and increase bargaining power. Pooling compounds only with transferable data and interchangeable demand, neither proven.

## Fatal Objections

1. Market denominator does not support 60 as conservative.
2. Legal duty does not prove an external gap or budget.
3. Premium is unsupported against cheap substitutes.
4. Full control bundle is unproved.
5. Small deviations break wealth.
6. Six-month contracts do not support durable value.
7. Proprietary/serialised parts may prevent pooling.
8. Integrated operators/umbrella representatives are substitutes with bargaining power.

## Bounded Proof Conditions

- Deduplicated, filtered CEE denominator comfortably above 60.
- Five targets' SLA/order logs; at least three with real gap.
- Two written budget indications at stated price/prepayment.
- One executable agreement with prepayment, exclusive routing, non-refundable treatment, data rights, and stock-risk allocation.
- Authentic-part quotes covering traceability, MOQ, software, return, buyback, and replenishment.
- Twenty orders with fully loaded contribution.
- Renewal reason/duration supporting better than commodity valuation.
- Rebuilt four-scenario wealth model using verified inputs.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A binding delivery deadline can support prepaid capacity only when an upstream gap is documented, every transaction is routed, and inventory downside stays with the regulated operator.
