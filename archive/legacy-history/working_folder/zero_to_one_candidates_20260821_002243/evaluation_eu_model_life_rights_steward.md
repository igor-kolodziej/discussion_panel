# Zero-to-One Evaluation: EU Model-Life Rights Steward

Date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Independent advisory evaluation of the exact frozen pivot in pivot_eu_model_life_rights_steward.md.

## Diagnosis

The pivot genuinely improves on commodity warehousing by moving the transaction before launch and making model-life rights, routing, and manufacturer funding the product. It nevertheless requires one manufacturer to grant every valuable right despite existing representative, ODM, component-supplier, software-licensor, and insurer control. The economics also treat too many accumulated seven-year cohorts as simultaneously selling new devices. Future EU product-liability rules can expose the authorised representative directly; contractual indemnity may provide recourse but cannot eliminate injured-party claims.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1670, corrected by Regulation (EU) 2025/2006, verifies parts-availability, public-ordering, repair-information, pairing-access, and five-/ten-working-day obligations.
- Commission guidance verifies a specified written authorised-representative mandate and appropriate means while leaving un-delegated manufacturer responsibilities in place.
- The 2022 Blue Guide describes authorised-representative tasks as generally administrative; manufacturing conformity and technical-document preparation normally stay with the manufacturer.
- Directive (EU) 2024/2853 applies to products placed after 9 December 2026. Article 8 includes an authorised representative among potentially liable EU operators where the manufacturer is outside the EU; Article 15 prevents contractual exclusion of liability to injured persons. Final Polish implementation remains counsel-required.
- A live 21 August 2026 EPREL query returned 2,537 smartphone/tablet rows. Authorised representatives covered 1,813 rows, 31 representative organisations, and 282 unique declared supplier/trademark strings.
- A crude rugged proxy—authorised-representative rows with at least IP5x/IPx4—contained 767 rows, 98 supplier/trademark strings, and 24 representatives. Six representatives controlled about 73% of those rows. Seventy strings had only one to five rows.
- The 98 strings are an upper-bound screening pool, not 98 qualifying legal manufacturers. Duplicates, mainstream brands, integrated networks, missing volume, missing rights, and absent budgets must be removed.
- Public basic representation offers in the hundreds to low thousands of euros annually establish a cheap baseline, though not a like-for-like lifecycle bundle.

Every price, model volume, conversion, cohort duration, margin, insurer term, reserve sufficiency, licence, software right, step-in right, ownership, and multiple remains unverified. No evidence shows one manufacturer will replace/bypass its representative, prepay, grant routing/distribution/data/supply/software/reproduction/distress rights, fund the reserve, and provide adequate liability security.

## Incentive and Power Map

- Loss bearer: manufacturer, importer, current representative, and potentially the venture as authorised representative.
- Payer: non-EU manufacturer before launch.
- Beneficiaries: manufacturer, distributors, repairers, owners.
- Authority holder: EU/national surveillance authorities and courts.
- Distributor: incumbent importer/representative and public parts-order route until the full contract transfers control.
- Information holder: manufacturer, ODM, component suppliers, software licensors, incumbent representative, repair network.
- Risk bearer: manufacturer for funded stock/reserve; venture for execution, authority response, contracts, and potentially non-waivable product-liability exposure.
- Transaction owner: venture only if exclusive routing survives the complete tail.

## Lawful-Structure Check

The model can be lawful if all licences, mandates, reserves, safety duties, and roles are validly documented. The problem is risk allocation, not a categorical ban. Future direct AR exposure may remain despite indemnity. Reproduction requires real IP, supplier, tooling, and safety authority; wording alone cannot make a reproduced component authentic.

## Constraint-Circumvention Audit

Valid improvements include selling pre-launch, refusing founder-funded inventory, conditioning activation on all rights, requiring exclusive routing, segregating reserve funding, and refusing a generic representation fallback.

They do not solve:

1. manufacturer non-ownership of tooling, firmware, component IP, or supplier rights;
2. multiple uncontrolled approvals inside the first contract; and
3. incumbents' existing relationships and ability to add an operating partner.

## Control Point

Tier 3 currently; conditional Tier 1 only after the complete contract is executed and enforceable.

The mandate, exclusive route, licence, reserve, and step-in rights would be strong. Today they are speculative and multi-party-dependent. The strongest copier is an incumbent representative adding the same lifecycle bundle for future models.

The 17-selling-model proxy uses roughly three years of declared placement duration within a seven-year ramp. It exposes the hidden assumption that nearly all accumulated cohorts continue generating new-device fees.

Sensitivity:

- At 3.5x and 75% ownership, at least PLN1.905m EBITDA is needed for PLN5m equity.
- Under expected margins and PLN4m operating cost, at least 29 of 40 cohorts must still be selling at 30k units and PLN6.
- At 2.5x, about 34 selling cohorts are required.
- Even all 40 selling falls from about PLN9.5m to PLN5.1m after a 25% contribution reduction.
- At 3x the stressed case is about PLN4.4m; at 65% ownership about PLN4.45m.
- A further PLN0.2m annual claims/insurance cost drops it below PLN5m.

The 95% reinvestment rule is preserved; reserve cash and distributions are not double-counted. Reinvestment cannot cure negative or small profit.

## Reachability Gate

FAIL.

The spend schedule fits the formal limits, but the exact candidate refuses to count a readiness audit. Before the first 25–40k PLN payment an unknown entrant must win incumbent displacement, exclusive routing, supply/reproduction/software/data rights, reserve funding, security, tail protection, and insurability. No target indication, redline, insurer quote, or rights schedule supports that within 180 days.

Twelve-month control also fails: the manufacturer's ability to grant supplier/tooling/software/distress rights and the venture's ability to insure direct liability inside 100k PLN remain unresolved.

## Expansion Check

The 70 small rugged-proxy strings must still be filtered for duplicates, integrated brands, volume, existing full-service contracts, grantable rights, and funding willingness. Ten wins could require a heroic fraction of the true denominator. Expansion repeats the same institutional sale, while incumbents already aggregate the manufacturers.

## Fatal Objections

1. Potentially direct, non-waivable post-2026 AR product-liability exposure.
2. Manufacturer may not own the ODM/tooling/firmware/supplier/IP rights.
3. No evidence for a complete-rights sale within 180 days.
4. Accumulated tail cohorts were treated as selling cohorts.
5. True qualifying denominator is far smaller and incumbent-concentrated.
6. No premium willingness-to-pay evidence.
7. Step-in/indemnity may fail during insolvency.
8. Live authority, order, recall, and claim duties conflict with a five-hour side business.

## Research-Further Needs

- Written legal opinion on delegation, liability, reproduction, reserve custody, and distress rights.
- Two binding insurer/broker indications for exact scope.
- Deduplicated legal-manufacturer denominator with volumes, representatives, launches, and rights ownership.
- Two written manufacturer indications accepting every right/funding term.
- One redlined mandate plus evidence the manufacturer can bind its ODM/licensors/suppliers.
- Cohort economics separating selling models from service tails and liability capital.
- Like-for-like incumbent quotations.
- Paid onboarding before live obligations.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Secure model-life exclusivity, transaction routing, and manufacturer funding before launch, and accept no live liability before those controls exist.

## Recommendation

Research further. Both hard gates fail. This exact candidate is not eligible for simulated-panel review.
