# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Cloud Exit Bond

Date: 2026-08-21 CEST. Fresh independent advisory evaluation of the exact frozen finalist. Advisory only; this is not a panel or external-validation score.

## Diagnosis

The insight is real: downloadable cloud data is not an executable, independently tested workload. A PLN20k restore drill is a credible paid-evidence product. The PLN120k–180k annual option, 60-customer denominator, retention, 55% gross margin, and reusable learning are unsupported. More decisively, the customer owns the data, destination, source, and restore artefacts, so an MSP or internal SRE team can reproduce the offer through an ordinary engagement. Founder Wealth fails, Reachability passes with low confidence, and the Tier-3 control point vetoes pursuit.

## Evidence And Assumptions

- The Data Act requires switching assistance and contractual switching provisions; zero switching charges start 12 January 2027. Its strongest functional-equivalence duty concerns infrastructure-only services and it does not guarantee application conversion or successful restoration. [Data Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj/eng)
- The Polish enforcement framework was still presented as a draft versioned 6 July 2026. Direct EU duties still apply. [Polish draft](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/projekt-ustawy-o-sprawiedliwym-dostepie-do-danych-i-ich-wykorzystywaniu)
- AWS, Google, and Azure already provide formal exit or free-egress routes. [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/free-data-transfer-out-to-internet-when-moving-out-of-aws/), [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/exit-cloud), [Azure](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription)
- DORA provides a stronger demand signal for covered financial entities' critical ICT exit plans, but also increases supplier diligence and control requirements. [DORA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj?uri=CELEX%3A32022R2554)
- Broad Polish cloud adoption and ten public references do not establish the filtered PostgreSQL/container buyer denominator.
- OChK, Hostersi, other MSPs, cloud partners, disaster-recovery tools, and internal SRE teams have direct route-around capability. [OChK](https://ochk.cloud/pl/produkty/platforma-ochk), [Hostersi](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-ejmeyvv5slgne), [AWS DRS pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/disaster-recovery/pricing/)

Unsupported hypotheses include one payment from 40 cold targets, annual conversion at PLN120k–180k, 60 reachable recurring buyers, 55% gross margin, low churn, adequate service-credit reserves, 65% expected founder ownership, and permitted reuse of confidential failure patterns.

## Incentive And Power Map

- Loss bearer: customer and its users.
- Payer: CTO/CIO, risk, or revenue owner; no named budget is verified.
- Beneficiary: customer, enterprise buyers, lenders, regulators, and users.
- Authority: customer plus its security/privacy/legal approvers; source provider controls protected managed services.
- Distributor: founder outbound; later advisers and MSPs are unsigned hypotheses.
- Information: customer engineering and source provider.
- Risk bearer: venture for labour and credits; customer for business interruption and excluded dependencies.
- Transaction owner: venture for its contract, but the customer owns every useful production and restore asset.

## Lawful-Structure And Circumvention Audit

Customer-controlled accounts, express authority, protected-IP exclusions, and defined engineering work are plausibly lawful. Scheduled remediation of the venture's own work can be an ordinary service; contingent protection and credits still need a written Polish insurance/liability opinion.

One bounded workload, scheduled drills, explicit exclusions, capped labour/credits, customer-funded destination resources, prepayment, and rejection of bespoke estates are valid constraints. They do not remove GDPR/security procurement, hidden dependencies, the insurance perimeter, customer ownership of the artefacts, confidentiality limits on reuse, unsigned channels, or incumbent copyability.

## Overall Score

**50/100.** Weighted points 99 across weight 20; `10 × 99 / 20 = 49.5`, rounded. Advisory only. The one-contract incumbent route-around independently caps the result below 80.

## Control Point

**Tier 3. Control proof: FAIL.** The annual contract is revenue, not scarce control over workflow, data, distribution, or transaction flow. The customer owns the useful assets and can replace the supplier at renewal. Within 12 months/PLN100k the founder could prove one restore and perhaps one annual contract, but not Tier-2 compounding control. Strongest copy risk: an established MSP or internal SRE team adding restore acceptance tests to existing DR/migration work.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

| Scenario | Founder result | Quality |
|---|---:|---|
| Adverse | –PLN25k | Clear stop |
| Conservative | PLN0.5625m | Explicitly below PLN5m |
| Expected | PLN7.982m assuming zero net debt | Arithmetic passes; all decisive commercial inputs unsupported |
| Strong | At least PLN17.325m on stated outline | Incomplete timing, churn, capital, margin, and organisation model |

The 95% reinvestment rule is stated but not modelled through tax, working capital, reserves, capital, or the end of employment. No cash is explicitly double-counted, but applying an EBITDA multiple and multiplying by ownership assumes zero net debt and ignores prepayment/service-credit liabilities. The expected case cannot overcome the conservative failure.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS — low confidence.** The PLN20k drill, not the annual option, can plausibly be sold and delivered inside 180 days while the founder stays employed. Planned pre-proof spending totals exactly PLN40k; the customer funds destination resources and specialist delivery is delegated. Confidence is low because the 40→8→3→1 funnel is unproved, security review may consume the window, and there is no budget slack. These risks do not make one contracted drill structurally impossible.

## Expansion Check

Additional services add delivery and liability faster than proven reuse. Polish language, contracts, proximity, and cost help entry but do not block domestic or global cloud firms. Sixty active annual customers cannot be inferred from cloud adoption, and churn would require materially more cumulative acquisition.

## Fatal Objections

1. Tier-3 control and ordinary MSP/internal-team route-around.
2. Conservative founder equity is only about PLN0.56m.
3. No evidence for recurring PLN120k–180k willingness to pay.
4. CAC, churn, specialist hours, credits, security cost, cash tax, and working capital are absent.
5. First-drill economics may be contribution-negative.
6. Unknown supplier trust conflicts with revenue-critical access.
7. Insurance/liability boundary remains unresolved.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Cloud-export rights and free egress do not make an application restorable; the meaningful proof is an independently executed restore against explicit acceptance criteria.

## Recommendation

**Pivot.** The exact candidate fails Founder Wealth and control proof. It is not panel-eligible even though the first paid drill narrowly passes Reachability.
