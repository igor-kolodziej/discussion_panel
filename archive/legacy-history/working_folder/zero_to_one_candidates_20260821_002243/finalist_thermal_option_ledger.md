# Zero-to-One Candidate: Thermal Option Ledger

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell Polish food-processing and cold-storage plants a fixed-price waste-heat feasibility study only when the engagement simultaneously grants the venture a 24-month exclusive, assignable option to develop the plant's off-site heat use, then monetise a controlled project through an offtaker reservation, assignment fee, or retained minority SPV participation without funding pipes or plant equipment.

## Founder Fit

The founder can map heat sources/demand, structure project economics, analyse time series, and manage option/data rooms. The family renewables company provides only local industrial introductions if available; an independent fixed-price thermal engineer signs the technical work. Five hours/day is feasible for one study/option at a time before full development.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Source customer: a food plant or cold store rejects condenser/process heat while paying electricity for cooling and facing energy/carbon scrutiny. It can order an audit, ask an equipment vendor, or do nothing; ordinary studies often end without an owner of the external development work. Later payer: a district-heating company, greenhouse, neighbouring process, infrastructure investor, or equipment developer that values a quantified, exclusively controlled source.

## Product Wedge

For PLN 15k–25k, the source receives a bounded study of measured heat profile, on-site uses, external demand points, concept economics, and kill conditions. In the same signed agreement, it grants the venture a 24-month exclusive option over off-site development: data/site access, non-circumvention, first negotiation for a heat agreement, and assignment to a named project SPV. On-site self-use remains with the source. The venture commissions a named engineer only after deposit.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Engineering firms already sell feasibility and build recovery systems. The proposed distinction is transaction ownership: the paid study must acquire the exclusive development right rather than leave a report the source can shop. The source gets a lower-risk, decision-ready analysis; the venture accepts that most options expire. The mechanism fails if sources will pay for a study but refuse assignable rights.

## Underused Momentum

EU energy-efficiency rules require heat/cooling planning and specified cost-benefit consideration of industrial waste heat; industrial heat pumps and decarbonisation improve potential. ORLEN and incumbents publicly seek or offer waste-heat solutions. These signals prove technical/commercial attention, not a private option budget or viable source-offtaker distance.

## Beachhead Market

One Wielkopolska/central-Poland food processor or cold store with >=0.5 MW average reject heat for >=4,000 hours/year, a measurable water/refrigeration stream, an accessible meter point, and at least one plausible heat demand within 3 km. Exclude hazardous/process-confidential streams, sources with planned shutdown, and cases where the first measurement/study exceeds PLN 18k.

## Incentive and Power Map

- **Loss bearer / initial payer:** source pays cooling/energy cost and prepays the study.
- **Later payer / beneficiary:** offtaker or infrastructure investor pays reservation/assignment/development economics; source gains study and possible heat revenue/cost reduction.
- **Authority holders:** source controls plant/data/heat contract; landowners and public bodies control routes/permits; offtaker controls demand.
- **Information holders:** source has operations; engineer measures; venture owns the contracted diligence compilation and option book.
- **Risk bearer / transaction owner:** venture bears capped study/option development cost; later SPV/investor bears construction; venture owns only the option until exercise/assignment.
- **First paid event:** source prepays the fixed study and executes the option agreement.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypotheses: source study price PLN 18k–25k; engineer/meter/counsel variable cost PLN 10k–18k; study contribution PLN 5k–12k. A buyer later pays PLN 20k–50k non-refundable diligence reservation credited to a PLN 250k–600k option assignment/development fee. Retained 5%–15% project participation is upside only and excluded from the base founder-wealth math. Failed options are allocated across assignment contribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10 sources: build a map from public permits, industrial registries, refrigeration-heavy facility lists, and direct calls in one region; ask for twelve months of operating/cooling data; quote only after desktop source/demand fit. First ten buyers are named local heat utilities, greenhouse/process owners, and energy-infrastructure developers contacted after source permission. One hundred sources require regional originators and a standard option/data-room process, not a public marketplace.

## Rule and Constraint Classification

- `LAW / BINDING RULE`: EU/Polish planning and installation-specific cost-benefit duties may require waste-heat consideration in defined cases, but do not compel a deal with the venture.
- `PHYSICAL CONSTRAINT`: temperature, flow, simultaneity, distance, route, return temperature, and source continuity govern value.
- `CONTRACTUAL RULE`: option exclusivity, assignability, site/data access, and heat-agreement path govern control.
- `ECONOMIC LIMITATION`: avoided/source value and offtaker value must exceed heat pump, pipe, easement, backup, and capital costs.

## Lawful-Structure Check

Polish energy/real-estate counsel reviews the option, heat sale, concession/licensing perimeter, land/easement, permits, metering, competition, public procurement/state aid, environmental and data terms. The proof stage performs measurement/advice only. No construction, heat supply, public claim, or investor solicitation occurs before a separately financed SPV and full permits/contracts.

## Constraint-Circumvention Attempts

The source can hire HeatRec/Veolia/another ESCO, develop internally, disclose the study to a utility, or stop the heat stream. An offtaker can approach the source directly. Only a valid non-circumvention/exclusive option and source cooperation create temporary control. The venture has no monopoly after expiry and cannot prevent source operational changes.

## Primary Control Point

Tier-2 legal/project control: a paid-study agreement containing a 24-month exclusive, assignable off-site development option; source data/site access; non-circumvention and penalty; first negotiation over a long-form heat agreement; and transfer to a named SPV. The option is valuable only while the physical/economic source remains valid.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Contract one fixed-price engineer and one counsel before selling. Desktop-map 30 sources and every demand point within 3 km. Ask five plants for minimal data. Sell one study/option, fund engineer from the deposit, and do not reserve land, order a heat pump, or promise project finance.

## Distribution Advantage

Facilities and likely energy-intensive processes can be identified from public records and local industrial maps. The offer gives the source a useful bounded study even if no external project works, allowing direct founder-led sales without an investor brand.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

The option ledger makes comparable sources legible to repeat buyers. Measured failures improve screening; completed options build buyer/source trust and standard heat agreements. Expand from one process/temperature/geography to adjacent clusters, then other Polish waste-heat sources. Do not expand before one assignment proves that rights, not reports, are purchased.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Heat is intensely local: temperature, Polish permits, municipal utilities, land routes, tariffs, contracts, and seasonality resist generic cross-border platforms. Defence is a controlled local option book and closing history, while national ESCOs remain capable competitors.

## Compounding Advantage

Each source-paid study adds measured profile, demand-distance map, route/capex assumptions, option terms, buyer objections, and closing outcome. Those data can reduce wasted diligence and price options. Compounding fails if source confidentiality prevents reusable features or each project remains bespoke.

## Founder Wealth Model

All figures are hypotheses, before personal tax, and exclude retained project equity.

- **Adverse, year 3:** eight studies x PLN 7k contribution and zero assignments = PLN 56k against PLN 350k overhead; stop, founder wealth near zero.
- **Conservative, year 7:** 15 paid studies x PLN 10k contribution = PLN 0.15m plus six assignments x PLN 350k net contribution = PLN 2.10m; PLN 1.45m EBITDA after PLN 0.80m originator/data/legal overhead; 5x = PLN 7.25m; 75% founder ownership = **PLN 5.44m founder equity**.
- **Expected, year 7:** 24 studies x PLN 15k contribution = PLN 0.36m plus ten assignments x PLN 600k = PLN 6.0m; PLN 4.06m EBITDA after PLN 2.30m overhead; 6x = PLN 24.36m; 70% founder ownership = **PLN 17.05m founder equity**.

The model fails if assignment contribution is below PLN 350k, fewer than six annual options close by year 7, or construction/strike capital moves onto the venture balance sheet.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Reinvest 95% of distributable profit for five years in source measurement, counsel, option deposits only where needed, secure data rooms, originators, and buyer coverage; distribute 5%. Study deposits fund technical work. Model 15% dilution only after two buyer reservations and one assignment; every construction SPV must be separately financed without founder guarantee.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–30: counsel/engineer freeze scope, contract, and budget; map 30 sources/demand rings. Days 31–75: five sources share minimal profiles; perform desktop screens. Days 76–105: one source signs the option and prepays PLN 15k–25k. Days 106–160: engineer measures and delivers the study. By day 180: source accepts delivery and at least five named offtakers/investors receive the authorised teaser. Maximum founder cash spend PLN 30k: PLN 8k legal, PLN 4k sourcing/data, <=PLN 15k engineer/meter bridge, PLN 3k contingency; source deposit offsets the technical cost.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

- PLN 8k contract/perimeter; stop if option cannot be assigned/enforced.
- PLN 4k source map/outreach; stop without five data-sharing sources.
- <=PLN 18k engineering only after source prepayment and signed option.
- Twelve-month cap PLN 100k for three paid-study options and one buyer reservation; zero construction/equipment/land capital.

## Critical Dependency Map

1. Source data and continuous usable heat.
2. Source willingness to pay and grant option together.
3. Fixed-price independent engineer.
4. Physically/economically plausible nearby demand.
5. Enforceable route from option to heat agreement/SPV.
6. Buyer reservation before further development spend.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Stay employed until three sources have prepaid and granted options, one unrelated buyer has paid a reservation, one assignment has closed for >=PLN 250k net contribution, six months of founder-equivalent cash runway exists, and all later construction capital is ring-fenced without personal guarantee.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

An invalid project ends at study/option expiry. The source retains its contracted report; venture retains only authorised aggregate screening features, forms, source/demand map, buyer graph, and diligence outcomes. No equipment, lease, or construction liability remains.

## Critical Evidence

- Twelve-month measured source profile and process continuity.
- Signed source-paid option in counsel-approved form.
- Independent engineer's temperature/flow/distance economics.
- At least one offtaker's data-backed demand confirmation.
- Paid buyer reservation and, ultimately, one assignment.

## Critical Hypotheses

- A source accepts study payment and off-site option as one transaction.
- Nearby demand can be identified before expensive engineering.
- Buyers value controlled pre-feasibility options.
- Six PLN 350k net assignments/year is conservative by year 7.
- Standardisation and data reduce bespoke labor enough for valuation.

## Kill Criteria

Kill if fewer than five of 30 sources share minimal data; no source signs and prepays by day 105; every source will pay only without an option; engineer cost exceeds the source payment plus PLN 15k; no plausible demand lies within the screened ring; source/opportunity NPV is non-positive under adverse energy/capex assumptions; no buyer reserves within 12 months; or any project requires founder-guaranteed construction capital.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Paid proof: <=PLN 30k and <=180 days. Control proof: three source-paid options and one paid buyer reservation within <=PLN 100k and <=12 months. People: founder, fixed-price thermal engineer, Polish energy/real-estate counsel, optional route surveyor only after buyer reservation; no construction team.
