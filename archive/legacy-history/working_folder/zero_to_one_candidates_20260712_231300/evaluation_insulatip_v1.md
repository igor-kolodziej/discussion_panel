# Zero-to-One Evaluation: InsulaTip EC-STM Probe Packs

## Diagnosis

InsulaTip turns a laboratory technique into a real consumable with an objective leakage test, and the public paper makes the pain unusually concrete. However, the same paper shows modest yield rather than a decisive process advantage, and decades of published wax, varnish, epoxy, and electropaint workarounds limit the patent's power. The beachhead may contain only dozens of active laboratories with low and irregular consumption. Even if the product is profitable, the candidate lacks a credible path from a tiny component catalogue to a domination-scale company under the stated founder constraints.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence establishes EC-STM's insulation requirement, public licence availability, manual variability, a 60-tip test, and existing ordinary probe suppliers. The active-lab count, annual tip consumption, evaluation-pack willingness to pay, licensed field scope, batch yield above 70%, compatibility, and reorder economics are hypotheses. The POC is affordable but market-size and design-around objections are structural rather than simple evidence gaps.

## Overall Score

**55/100.** Weighted sum `87.5 / 16`; `10 × 5.46875 = 54.6875`, rounded to 55.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 at best after licensing; Tier 3 today.** The right is identifiable and a fixture can be owned, but alternative published coating processes remain accessible. Strongest copy risk: a probe vendor adds a coated SKU or a lab builds a simple automated dip/rotation jig.

## Expansion Check

One EC-STM geometry is a clean niche, but expansion into SECM, AFM, and microelectrodes changes product and qualification requirements. A broad probe supplier can enter through routine process development and existing distribution. Polish origin provides no persistent demand-side barrier.

## Fatal Objections

1. The active customer pool and annual consumable volume may be too small for venture-scale owner earnings.
2. The licensed method does not appear categorically superior to cheap, published alternatives.
3. Existing probe vendors can add insulation and QA without acquiring the candidate's distribution.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Objective leakage grading can convert manually prepared electrochemical probes into a trusted catalogue consumable.
