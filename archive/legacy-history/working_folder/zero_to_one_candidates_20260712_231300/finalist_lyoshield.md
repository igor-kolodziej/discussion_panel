# Zero-to-One Candidate: LyoShield Animal-Free Lyophilization Protectant

## One-Sentence Thesis

Option an exclusive EU field licence to a patented wheat-peptone lyophilization protectant, prove strain-by-strain survival with blinded outsourced tests, and sell a qualified animal-free dry reagent to small probiotic, starter-culture, and microbial-production companies that cannot justify developing their own formulation.

## Founder Fit

The founder's experimental-design, data-science, health, and FMCG background fits a quantified ingredient-qualification business better than a relationship-heavy biotech consultancy. The founder can manage study design, supplier qualification, statistics, batch records, and written technical sales in five hours per day. A contract microbiologist and accredited or customer-accepted laboratory handle culturing and CFU work; the company owns the licence, purchased inputs, formulation batches, test data it commissions, specifications, and customer contracts.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is a small or mid-sized EU producer of probiotic supplements, microbial biocontrols, fermentation starters, or reference cultures that freeze-dries live organisms. Its paid trigger is qualification of a new strain, a disappointing viable-cell yield, rising BSA cost, or a requirement to remove animal-origin inputs.

The buyer wants stable post-drying viable count, predictable shelf-life work, and fewer animal-origin declarations without changing its freeze dryer. It currently uses an internal sugar/protein recipe, ATCC-style BSA-containing Reagent 18, a contract manufacturer's formula, or repeated trial-and-error. Large producers can formulate internally; LyoShield deliberately starts with smaller manufacturers whose scientific staff and batch volume make an external qualified reagent cheaper than a development project.

## Product Wedge

The first product is not a new freeze dryer and not a broad formulation service. It is a controlled dry protectant concentrate covered by an exclusive field licence, sold with a paid qualification pack:

- three blinded formulations: current customer control, LyoShield, and a defined benchmark;
- pre- and post-lyophilization CFU recovery on one to three strains;
- ingredient origin, allergen, microbiological, and batch documentation;
- a recommended concentration/process window limited to the tested strain and cycle;
- a pilot production lot and repeat supply specification.

The product makes no shelf-life, therapeutic, GMP, or regulatory claim beyond the evidence actually generated. Pharmaceutical uses are excluded from the POC.

## Hidden Insight or Market Misunderstanding

The common view is that microbial freeze-drying performance belongs inside a proprietary process recipe and therefore only equipment vendors or major culture houses can win. The narrower secret is that a small producer does not want another machine or a scientific project: it wants an externally supplied, animal-free protectant that preserves viable cells on its specific strains and arrives with enough documentation to qualify. A simple molecule mix can become defensible only when an exclusive claim set is paired with purchased batch control and a growing library of customer-qualified strain/process specifications.

## Underused Momentum

- The University of Gdańsk publicly offers a licence or sale of PL Pat.244159 / EP3670645 at TRL4.
- The published formulation replaces BSA with wheat peptone and reports similar or sometimes better survival across tested organisms.
- Probiotic, fermentation, biocontrol, and microbial-production categories create repeated freeze-drying qualification events.
- Manufacturers face continuing pressure to document animal-origin inputs, allergens, and supply consistency.
- Outsourced microbiology and small freeze-drying runs make a bounded proof possible without building a laboratory.

## Beachhead Market

EU small and mid-sized producers of non-pharmaceutical freeze-dried bacterial probiotic or starter-culture products, beginning with firms that already use contract freeze drying or operate one small production dryer. Exclude vaccines, biologic drugs, clinical cell products, and very large culture companies during the first year.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

All numbers are hypotheses until supplier quotes and paid trials exist.

- Paid one-strain qualification pack: 8,000–18,000 PLN.
- Three-strain pack: 18,000–35,000 PLN.
- Pilot reagent lot: 2,000–6,000 PLN.
- Recurring dry concentrate: 400–900 PLN/kg or a minimum 1,500–3,000 PLN order, priced below the customer's BSA-equivalent input and internal development cost.
- Target product gross margin: 60–80% before university royalty and technical support.
- Hypothesized university economics: no more than 20,000 PLN option/upfront payment, then 3–7% net-sales royalty plus milestones.

The POC passes only if three paid qualification packs produce at least one repeat reagent order and one written annual-volume forecast. It does not pass on laboratory superiority alone.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First 10:**

1. Build a named list of 60 EU probiotic, starter-culture, microbial-biostimulant, and collection producers from product catalogues, trade associations, and contract-manufacturing directories.
2. Interview 12 process/R&D leads about current protectants, BSA use, failed yield, qualification burden, batch size, and decision authority.
3. Ask UG for patent status, prosecution file, current licences, field exclusivity, inventor know-how, sample data, and option economics before paying more than 5,000 PLN.
4. Obtain three non-binding letters stating the exact strain, paid-test price, and acceptance criterion.
5. Sign the field option and sell three blinded, fixed-scope paid qualification packs.
6. Convert at least one passing trial into a repeat supply order before manufacturing more than 25 kg.

**First 100:**

- Standardize qualified SKUs by organism family and use case rather than custom formulation.
- Sell through direct technical outbound and specialist ingredient distributors only after the company retains the customer, qualification, and field-right economics.
- Add private-label supply for contract freeze dryers while preserving the licensed formulation and batch specification.
- Expand from probiotic bacteria to yeasts, biocontrol organisms, and culture collections only after separate paid qualification.
- Use customer-approved aggregate evidence to shorten later qualification, without claiming transfer across untested strains.

## Primary Control Point

The initial Tier-1 candidate control point is an exclusive EU licence for specified non-pharmaceutical probiotic and starter-culture fields, including the right to manufacture and sell the patented composition. It is supported by owned ingredient inventory, batch records, test samples, commissioned data rights, strain/process qualification dossiers, trademarks, and repeat supply contracts.

The right is not assumed. Before serious spending, the founder must produce an executed option or licence naming territory, field, exclusivity, claims, sublicensing/manufacturing rights, royalty, milestones, patent-maintenance obligations, access to inventor know-how, and termination conditions.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Resource needed: enforceable exclusive field rights plus objective evidence that at least one commercial strain performs and reorders.

1. Patent/legal and freedom-to-operate review: 8,000–15,000 PLN.
2. Licence option/upfront cap: 5,000–20,000 PLN; use milestones and royalties instead of a large purchase price.
3. Ingredients, controlled mixing/packing, stability samples, and documentation: 8,000–15,000 PLN.
4. Outsourced blinded CFU and freeze-drying trials: 25,000–40,000 PLN, released only against paid customer scopes.
5. Contract microbiologist/quality support: 8,000–12,000 PLN.
6. Insurance, shipping, sales, and contingency: 10,000–15,000 PLN.

Total POC cap: 90,000 PLN. Why an unknown founder can secure it: UG invites ordinary commercial offers; ingredients, labs, and contractors are purchasable; buyers judge paid blinded survival results and documents rather than personal status. Externally checkable proof within 12 months is the executed exclusive option/licence, laboratory reports, retained samples, customer acceptance criteria, paid invoices, one repeat purchase, and an annual-volume forecast.

## Distribution Advantage

Each outbound message begins with a calculable substitution question: current protectant, BSA or animal-origin constraint, starting CFU, recovered CFU, batch size, and qualification event. The founder offers a bounded paid comparison, not a broad innovation pitch. A producer that qualifies the reagent incurs switching and revalidation cost, while its repeat supply orders produce references and organism-family know-how for adjacent buyers.

## Domination and Expansion Path

1. Win the narrow EU animal-free protectant niche for small probiotic and starter-culture producers.
2. Become the default qualified reagent across several Lactobacillus/Bacillus/yeast families, with reproducible batch documentation.
3. Add contract freeze-dryer private-label supply and distributor inventory without giving away field rights.
4. Negotiate additional protectant/formulation IP and build a portfolio for adjacent microbial products.
5. Expand geographically or into higher-regulation applications only after the original field has repeat purchases, quality systems, and financing.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The local advantage is not a Polish label. It is access to licensable Polish university IP, lower-cost commissioned development, and proximity to CEE food/supplement/fermentation manufacturers. The licence must cover a commercially meaningful EU field; otherwise a foreign ingredients company can reproduce the playbook. EU documentation, allergen, animal-origin, and batch expectations make a qualified local supplier more valuable than a raw recipe.

## Compounding Advantage

- Exclusive field rights prevent exact commercial use of the licensed claims in the territory.
- Each paid qualification creates a strain/process dossier and acceptance window.
- Repeat ingredient supply produces batch-performance history and switching cost.
- Shared organism-family evidence reduces later CAC and trial cost.
- Additional protectant IP can be acquired into the same qualified distribution channel.

## Critical Evidence

- UG explicitly lists the protected invention, TRL4, and licence or sale routes.
- The European publication states composition ranges and reports survival testing across several microbial strains.
- ATCC documents the BSA-containing comparison recipe.
- Every decisive POC input can be bought or contracted without a strategic partner.

## Critical Hypotheses

- The European right is granted/in force in useful countries and can be licensed exclusively in the chosen field.
- Small producers use enough BSA or suffer enough qualification pain to pay for an external protectant.
- The formulation delivers equal or better viable recovery on customer strains and cycles, not only the university set.
- Wheat allergen/documentation does not create a larger burden than BSA removal solves.
- The patent claims are difficult enough to design around and repeat supply gross profit is large enough to fund technical support.

## Kill Criteria

- UG will not grant a commercially useful exclusive field option within 20,000 PLN upfront and milestones.
- Patent review finds the European protection absent, weak, near-expiry in the practical field, or easily designed around without proprietary know-how.
- Fewer than three of 40 qualified prospects accept a paid comparison or provide a written acceptance criterion.
- No customer strain meets its pre-agreed recovery criterion in the first three paid trials.
- A passing trial produces no repeat order or annual-volume forecast within 90 days.
- Product gross margin after lab release, packing, royalty, quality, and support is below 50%, or annual gross profit per qualified customer is below 10,000 PLN.
- Qualification becomes pharmaceutical-grade validation or requires more than 100,000 PLN before commercial proof.
