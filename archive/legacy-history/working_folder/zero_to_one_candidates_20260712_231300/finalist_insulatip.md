# Zero-to-One Candidate: InsulaTip EC-STM Probe Packs

## One-Sentence Thesis

Licence an automated probe-coating method and sell cyclic-voltammetry-qualified, partially insulated EC-STM tip packs to battery, corrosion, catalysis, and surface-science laboratories that currently lose instrument time to inconsistent manual coating.

## Founder Fit

This is a measurement-driven consumable business: the founder can manage protocol design, QA data, batch statistics, global written outbound, and supplier operations. A part-time electrochemist and a precision-fabrication contractor cover domain and fixture work. The founder does not need to operate a public laboratory; purchased tips, a licensed fixture, small controlled coating setup, and outsourced SEM/application checks are sufficient for the POC.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The initial buyer is an EU laboratory with an EC-STM liquid-cell instrument studying electrocatalysis, corrosion, batteries, or electrode interfaces. Before an in-situ or operando campaign, the laboratory must insulate most of the conductive tip so faradaic current does not overwhelm tunnelling current.

Today researchers often dip-coat tips in wax, varnish, epoxy, or electrophoretic paint. Performance depends on operator skill; a pinhole, excessive exposed area, or covered apex can waste sample preparation and scarce microscope time. Ordinary STM probes are purchasable, but qualified low-leakage EC-STM tips are not a simple commodity catalogue item.

## Product Wedge

- Ten-tip evaluation pack using one common Bruker/Keysight/Unisoku-compatible geometry.
- Each tip has a batch ID, optical image, coating recipe, cyclic-voltammetry leakage class, packaging date, and recommended electrolyte constraints.
- Two grades: research grade below a pre-agreed leakage threshold and screening grade for method development.
- Paid replacement guarantee for tips failing the documented incoming test.

The POC sells probes, not a coating machine. A machine or OEM process licence is a later product only after repeat consumable demand.

## Hidden Insight or Market Misunderstanding

The market appears too small because laboratories hide the pain inside graduate-student craft. The real purchased outcome is not a sharpened wire but one less failed day on a scarce instrument. If objective leakage QA makes a €100–250 tip cheaper than repeated operator preparation, a tiny global niche can support high-margin direct sales and later own the coating standard.

## Underused Momentum

- UWr offers licence or sale of P.448634 and states TRL6.
- The inventors published a controlled translation/rotation coater and measured batch leakage variation.
- EC-STM is relevant to growing operando research in batteries, corrosion, and electrocatalysis.
- Ordinary STM tip suppliers prove catalogue purchasing, while EC users still document manual coating workarounds.
- Small-batch precision fabrication, vision inspection, and CV logging are affordable.

## Beachhead Market

EU academic and industrial EC-STM laboratories using tungsten or Pt/Ir wire tips in liquid cells, beginning with one tip geometry and aqueous electrolyte class. Exclude broad AFM, biomedical microelectrodes, UHV STM, and custom instrument integration during POC.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypotheses:

- Ten-tip evaluation pack: €900–1,800.
- Standard qualified tip: €120–250.
- Custom electrolyte/geometry pack: €2,000–5,000 NRE plus tips.
- Target gross margin: 55–70% after purchased blanks, coating, QA, rejects, and shipping.
- Reorder hypothesis: 20–60 tips per active laboratory per year.

The business passes only if at least five laboratories pay for evaluation packs, three reorder, and batch yield exceeds 70% at the promised leakage class.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First 10:** map 80 EC-STM papers from the last five years, identify corresponding laboratories and instrument models, interview 15 users, then send a measured two-tip sample only after securing five written evaluation criteria. Sell ten paid ten-tip packs with a replacement guarantee and instrument-fit sheet.

**First 100:** publish non-customer-specific QA distributions, add qualified geometries only after five requests, sell through specialist probe distributors while keeping batch data and direct technical support, and add standing quarterly replenishment. Expand to SECM/SVET microelectrodes only where the same coating/QA process transfers.

## Primary Control Point

An exclusive field licence to P.448634 for commercial EC-STM probe coating, combined with the owned fixture, process windows, purchased probe inventory, coating/QC records, customer-qualified compatibility table, brand, and reorder accounts. The licence must explicitly cover manufacture and sale of coated consumables rather than only the machine.

## Distribution Advantage

Public papers enumerate the exact labs, instruments, electrolytes, and corresponding authors. Outreach can cite a user's published coating method and offer a pre-defined leakage class, fit drawing, and replacement guarantee. This is more precise than generic scientific-instrument sales and does not require institutional status.

## Domination and Expansion Path

1. Own qualified coated tips for one EC-STM geometry/electrolyte class.
2. Expand the compatibility and leakage-grade catalogue from paid requests.
3. Sign distributor/OEM supply while retaining batch process and QA data.
4. Sell coating fixtures or licences to probe makers after consumable proof.
5. Extend to adjacent insulated electrochemical probes where the same process creates a better yield than manual work.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

UWr IP and low-cost precision fabrication give a Polish entry point, while the market must be global from day one. The defence is not localization; it is the exclusive field right, tested batch yield, compatibility catalogue, and fast EU shipping. Without the exclusive consumable field and objective QA advantage, the idea is a weak foreign-facing component shop.

## Compounding Advantage

- Each shipment adds process-yield and electrolyte-compatibility evidence.
- Published instrument/lab mapping makes direct distribution repeatable.
- Reorders create switching cost around a known leakage grade.
- A broader probe catalogue amortizes the same fixture and QA setup.
- OEM supply can turn a niche consumable into a manufacturing standard.

## Critical Evidence

- UWr lists the right as available and TRL6.
- Bruker describes the required partially insulated EC-STM tip.

- Commercial suppliers already sell ordinary tips, proving catalogue purchasing.

## Critical Hypotheses

- At least 50–100 active addressable EC-STM laboratories exist and consume enough tips.
- The licensed method produces materially better yield/cost than manual coating or existing vendors.
- The consumable field can be licensed exclusively within the budget.
- Customers accept CV leakage and geometry evidence without a prestigious probe brand.
- Annual gross profit per active lab exceeds the direct-support burden.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than 30 active addressable labs can be verified or fewer than five of 40 contacted labs will pay for an evaluation pack.
- Batch yield below the promised leakage class remains under 70% after two process iterations.
- Three paid evaluations do not produce at least one reorder within 90 days.
- The right cannot cover coated-consumable sales exclusively for no more than 15,000 PLN upfront.
- Customers already buy a satisfactory qualified alternative or value a tip below €80.
- A lab can reproduce equivalent yield with a published jig costing under €5,000 and less than two hours of monthly labor.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack
