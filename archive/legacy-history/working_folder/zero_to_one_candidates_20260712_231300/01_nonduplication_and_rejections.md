## Repeatedly Failed or Saturated Shapes

- Generic evidence, compliance, release, claim, discrepancy, refund, recovery, tender, or due-diligence desks.
- Agency-routed case books, referral lanes, and white-label partner desks without an owned asset.
- Operator-heavy route-book acquisitions, generic collection routes, and commodity recovery networks.
- Retiring-software succession without transferable durable rights.
- Used/obsolete spare-part pools for industrial, laboratory, medical, energy, HVAC, elevator, refrigeration, or dental equipment.
- Exact-match construction salvage and adjacent heritage-material pools.
- Grid connection, EV charging, rooftop, meter, curtailment, white-certificate, and F-gas quota variants.
- Reserved clinic, care, diagnostic, lab, biosample, genetic, wearable, or specialist capacity dependent on an outside authority accepting an unknown intermediary.
- DORA/NIS2/AI Act/CRA/KSeF/EUDR/CBAM/PFAS/EcoVadis/marketplace/procurement evidence variants.
- Invoice/payment unblock, refund, chargeback, customs recovery, core-credit, receivable, and statutory-compensation variants.
- Equipment-led inspection services that an incumbent can reproduce with one purchase and one routine process change.

## Material-Difference Rule

A new candidate touching an adjacent sector survives only when all three differ materially:

1. Buyer and urgent trigger.
2. First paid acquisition mechanism.
3. Hard resource or control point secured by ownership, option, prepayment, exclusive contract, assigned right, custody, mandatory workflow routing, or transaction control rather than cooperation alone.

The business must become more than a manual service and must not rely on an incumbent platform, regulator, buyer, lab, or generic partner to create the decisive value.
