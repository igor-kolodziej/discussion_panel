# Zero-to-One Candidate: SealFault Physical Benchmark Bank

## One-Sentence Thesis

Packaging-inspection vendors and FMCG packaging teams will pay for blind, rights-cleared physical seal-defect challenge kits that connect controlled material/process faults to destructive ground truth and reveal failure modes their own curated demos miss.

## Founder Fit

The founder brings data science, experimental design, AI/vision literacy, and FMCG context without using confidential employer data. The work is measurable, largely asynchronous, and can begin with purchased materials and tabletop equipment. Packaging engineers and technicians can be hired for bounded method review; the company—not an outside lab—owns the specimens, protocol, images, and outcomes.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Initial buyers are European machine-vision vendors and packaging-inspection OEMs validating seal-detection systems for flexible food and household-product packs. Secondary buyers are converters and FMCG packaging-development labs moving to new recyclable mono-material films.

Their risk is a false sense of coverage. A system can perform well on curated factory images but miss low-frequency combinations of contamination, wrinkles, channel leaks, temperature/pressure drift, print, film structure, or partial seal failure. Buyers currently create ad hoc defects, borrow customer samples, rely on proprietary internal libraries, or validate only what their equipment can conveniently observe. That makes cross-machine comparison and repeat regression difficult.

## Product Wedge

SealFault sells a numbered physical challenge kit plus a licensed digital benchmark:

- 100–300 sealed specimens spanning a buyer-agreed material, geometry, and defect matrix.
- Matched controls and blind held-out specimens.
- Controlled process recipes and environmental history.
- RGB/thermal/OCT-compatible reference imagery where available.
- Seal-strength, burst, dye, vacuum, or other fit-for-purpose destructive/non-destructive outcome data.
- Chain of custody, retained twins, benchmark scoring, and regression updates.

The POC covers one narrow format—heat-sealed flexible pouches or trays—and never claims regulatory accreditation or universal package-integrity certification.

## Hidden Insight or Market Misunderstanding

The inspection market sells sensors and algorithms, but the neutral scarce asset may be a persistent physical ground-truth bank. Artificial defects are easy to make badly and hard to make reproducibly. A retained twin and destructive outcome tie the digital image to physical reality. Cross-vendor blind testing creates information that no single equipment vendor observes.

## Underused Momentum

- PPWR pressure is accelerating material and design changes, including recyclable mono-material formats with different process windows.
- Seal integrity remains critical to contamination, leakage, shelf life, and recall risk.
- Vision, thermal, OCT, force-decay, and other inspection vendors create demand for evaluation but also fragment ground truth across methods.
- AI inspection systems need regression sets as films, printing, sealants, and model versions change.

## Beachhead Market

European vendors and integrators selling seal-inspection systems for flexible food and household packaging, starting with firms that need to demonstrate performance on new recyclable film structures to converters or FMCG customers.

This is a narrow, enumerable market with high contract values. The first kit targets one geometry and material family rather than “all packaging.”

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Hypotheses pending quotes and paid orders:

- Discovery/coverage workshop: 3,000–8,000 PLN, creditable against a kit.
- Standard physical challenge kit: 12,000–30,000 PLN.
- Digital benchmark/evaluation licence: 40,000–120,000 PLN per company.
- Custom blind validation campaign: 30,000–90,000 PLN.
- Annual material/defect update: 20,000–60,000 PLN.
- Target gross margin: 55–70% after the first standardized matrix; lower on one-off custom work.

The initial bank costs 55,000–85,000 PLN. It requires two 20,000–30,000 PLN prepaid kits or one larger licence to validate commercial viability before expanding equipment.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First 10:**

1. Map 50 vision vendors, seal-inspection OEMs, packaging-machine OEMs, converters, and FMCG packaging labs.
2. Ask 15 technical leads for the five defect/material combinations they cannot test confidently.
3. Publish a non-sensitive ten-specimen demo and benchmark protocol.
4. Offer five fixed-scope blind kits; require 50% prepayment before manufacturing the buyer-specific matrix.
5. Use completed blind results to sell repeat regression packs, without publishing a customer's performance without permission.

**First 100:**

- Sell standard matrices through direct technical outbound and packaging/vision conferences.
- Add material families only where at least three buyers share the need.
- License digital twins and scoring APIs to inspection vendors.
- Offer cross-version regression subscriptions and confidential vendor benchmarking.
- Expand from food pouches/trays into household, medical, and industrial flexible packaging only after separate safety and method requirements are understood.

## Primary Control Point

The company owns and physically custodies the numbered specimen bank, retained twins, fixtures, process recipes, raw sensor data, destructive-test outcomes, labels, benchmark software, and commercial training/evaluation rights. Customer kits are sold or leased under terms that preserve the company's right to reuse the non-confidential base defect recipe and anonymized aggregate outcomes.

This control point is secured through purchases and contracts: materials and equipment are bought; technicians and laboratories, if used, are paid vendors assigning all generated data. No outside partner must grant the core asset.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Resource needed: a reproducible defect matrix tied to credible physical ground truth.

Bootstrap:

1. Spend up to 5,000 PLN on 15 buyer interviews and protocol design.
2. Require two preorders or one larger paid validation before exceeding 15,000 PLN.
3. Spend 20,000–35,000 PLN on tabletop sealing, fixtures, cameras, thermal sensing where justified, and measurement tools.
4. Spend 12,000–20,000 PLN on films, consumables, retained twins, and controlled experiments.
5. Spend 8,000–15,000 PLN on destructive tests, calibration, method review, and contractor work.
6. Reserve 10,000 PLN for shipping, legal terms, insurance, and contingency.

Why an unknown founder can secure it: every decisive input can be purchased; FMCG/vision prospects are publicly identifiable; the first offer is a concrete blind challenge, not an authority claim. Proof before serious capital is a 300–500-specimen bank, repeatability results, retained twins, one external blind failure discovery, and two paid kits or one annual licence.

## Distribution Advantage

SealFault targets vendors at the moment they must prove a new sensor/model/material combination. A physical kit travels across organizations and produces a comparable result. Each customer false negative identifies a new sellable specimen family. Confidentiality can be preserved while aggregate failure coverage makes the bank more complete.

## Domination and Expansion Path

1. Become the preferred independent challenge bank for one flexible-package geometry/material family.
2. Add PPWR-driven mono-material transitions and recurring model-version regression.
3. Expand across seal-inspection modalities while retaining physical ground truth as the neutral layer.
4. Build a cross-vendor failure ontology and scoring standard.
5. Add adjacent package-integrity domains only where the existing physical/data asset shortens entry.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers a meaningful FMCG, packaging, plastics, and machine-building base with lower experimental costs. EU material transitions create recurring local demand. The company can ship standardized specimens across the EU. The defence is the owned cross-material physical/outcome bank and neutral benchmark history, not Polish regulation alone.

## Compounding Advantage

- Retained physical twins make old results reproducible.
- New films and model versions create recurring demand.
- Customer failure discoveries expand the most valuable tails.
- Standardized fixtures reduce marginal cost per new matrix.
- Cross-vendor history becomes harder for a single OEM to reproduce.
- Physical kits lead to high-margin digital licences and update subscriptions.

## Critical Evidence

- Commercial vendors already sell seal inspection and leak testing, proving the pain and budget category.
- PPWR and recyclable material transitions create new process conditions.
- Fraunhofer work documents narrow mono-material process windows and continuous inspection needs.
- The founder can purchase all initial assets and keep the rights chain independent of an employer or partner.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Vendors will pay an unknown neutral party for blind physical benchmarks.
- Deliberately created defects transfer to real production failure modes.
- Two buyers share enough of one matrix to support reuse rather than custom consulting.
- Basic ground-truth methods are credible without accreditation during the POC.
- Customer confidentiality terms still allow the base bank and aggregate ontology to compound.

## Kill Criteria

- No two prepaid kits or one meaningful licence after 50 qualified outreaches and 15 technical discovery conversations.
- Buyers say internal defect creation is adequate and refuse even a low-cost blind test.
- Artificial-defect results fail to correlate with production samples supplied under a paid pilot.
- Every buyer requires a unique non-reusable method, turning the business into custom laboratory work.
- Credible method, insurance, and safety requirements push the POC above 100,000 PLN.
- An inspection OEM can reproduce the full cross-material bank through one routine internal project with no value from neutrality or retained twins.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

- Month 0–2: interviews, protocol, demo specimens; 10,000 PLN cap.
- Month 2–5: first buyer-backed matrix and equipment; cumulative 45,000 PLN cap.
- Month 5–8: 300–500 specimens, ground truth, and blind pilot; cumulative 75,000 PLN cap.
- Month 8–12: repeat kit/licence and update pack; 95,000 PLN total cap.

Minimum stack: founder/data/experiment lead, part-time packaging engineer or technician, and paid external test/calibration support. No accredited-lab buildout and no full-time staff during POC.
