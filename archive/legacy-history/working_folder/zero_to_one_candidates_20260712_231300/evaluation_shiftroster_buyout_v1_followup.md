# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ShiftRoster After Decision-Critical Research

## Diagnosis

Follow-up research found no qualifying acquisition target and found a direct product competitor already offering the proposed core wedge. Shift2Cal scans paper schedules, screenshots, PDFs, and chat images, targets the same shift-worker professions, supports Polish, syncs calendars, and sells subscriptions. Buying a small generic shift app would therefore acquire a weaker listing and then fund catch-up to an existing product. The shape is an ordinary micro-app acquisition plus a copyable feature, not a new control point.

## Evidence and Assumptions

App marketplaces and transfer processes exist. No specific sub-65,000 PLN target with verified profit was identified. The exact product wedge is publicly live in Polish and many other languages. A hypothetical target could still be profitable, but that would be an investment opportunity rather than evidence for this candidate's zero-to-one thesis.

## Control Point

**Tier 3.** No acquisition target is contracted, and even a completed transfer would not control the proposed feature category. Strongest copy risk is already realized: Shift2Cal offers the same import/calendar workflow in Polish.

## Expansion Check

The local wedge is occupied and the adjacent professions/languages are explicitly targeted by the incumbent. Any acquired small app must compete on store discovery and product execution without an asymmetric local resource.

## Fatal Objections

1. No qualifying transferable asset was identified.
2. A direct competitor already offers the exact PDF/image-to-calendar wedge in Polish.
3. Ownership of a smaller listing does not prevent routine feature and distribution competition.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Verified payment flow and distribution can be more valuable than code in a small software acquisition.
