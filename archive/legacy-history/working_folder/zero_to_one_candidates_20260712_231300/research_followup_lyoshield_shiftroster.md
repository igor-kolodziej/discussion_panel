# Decision-Critical Follow-Up: LyoShield and ShiftRoster

Research date: 2026-07-13.

## LyoShield

The missing question was whether BSA is a sufficiently common, costly commercial pain and whether the licensed wheat-peptone formula has a narrow competitive field.

- Published probiotic/starter-culture work repeatedly uses inexpensive skim milk, sucrose, trehalose, lactose, maltodextrin, inulin, glycerol, and combinations rather than BSA as the default commercial route. [PubMed long-term storage comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25380798/), [2025 skim-milk study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41304174/), [open probiotic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4519473/)
- Results are highly strain- and storage-specific. Several inexpensive protectants reach high immediate survival, while different matrices dominate long-term or room-temperature performance.
- The UG patent demonstrates an alternative to ATCC Reagent 18 but does not establish that small commercial producers currently buy meaningful BSA volumes or want a universal third-party reagent.

Decision: the gap resolved negatively. The original paid trigger is not evidenced, the alternative formulation field is broad, and every customer would still require strain/storage qualification. Do not panel.

## ShiftRoster

The missing questions were whether the acquisition target exists and whether Polish PDF/image rota import is still an uncrowded post-acquisition wedge.

- Public app-sale marketplaces exist, but no specific shift app with verified profit, clean IP, and a sub-65,000 PLN transfer was identified. [Flippa app marketplace](https://flippa.com/apps), [Acquire mobile apps](https://acquire.com/mobile-apps-for-sale/)
- More importantly, Shift2Cal already scans paper schedules, screenshots, PDFs, and chat images into calendars, explicitly targets nurses, doctors, factory workers, police, and firefighters, supports Polish among 35 languages, and monetizes with subscriptions. [Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shift-to-calendar-ai-planner/id6755889602), [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.loudsrl.shift2calendar)

Decision: the local product wedge is already present in a directly competing app. An acquisition would buy entry into an existing feature race, not create a new control point. Do not panel.
