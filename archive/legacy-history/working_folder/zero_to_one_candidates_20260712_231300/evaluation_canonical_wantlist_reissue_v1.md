# Zero-to-One Evaluation: Canonical Wantlist Reissue v1

## Diagnosis

The canonical version preserves the strongest feature of the candidate: an exact-title exclusive option is secured before promotion and customer cash gates manufacturing before inventory risk. It also makes the compounding test falsifiable by requiring paid adjacent-title interest and lower second-campaign CAC. The main strategic concern remains that temporary title monopolies do not automatically become a durable catalogue moat, and collector scarcity may attach to original objects rather than the music. Those risks are exposed cheaply enough to justify independent review rather than further rewriting.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence supports public scarcity signals, category revenue, ordinary rights licensing, and affordable manufacturing. The exact title, option, source assets, 250 paid buyers, contribution, channel mix, adjacent interest, and second-right economics remain explicitly labelled hypotheses. No reviewer score is treated as evidence, and the SHA-256 identifies the exact version under review.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 for the exact title after option signature; Tier 2 during sourcing.** The signed option, source assets, preorder authorization, and sell-off licence are enforceable and provable within the POC. Strongest copy risk: another label obtains adjacent catalogue rights or a rightsholder waits out the option and self-represses.

## Expansion Check

One title is a crisp beachhead. A micro-genre catalogue is adjacent through shared buyers, rights-holder networks, manufacturing, and stores, but the canonical kill criteria correctly require the second option and lower CAC before claiming compounding. Broad labels can enter, while they cannot sell the same title during exclusivity.

## Fatal Objections

1. Original-pressing scarcity may not translate into 250 repress buyers.
2. Rights and source assets can be fragmented or poor even when an artist is cooperative.
3. Temporary title options may remain serial projects unless first-release buyers materially improve second-title economics.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

An exclusive option plus a paid preorder threshold turns collector-market data into a controllable, customer-financed inventory decision.
