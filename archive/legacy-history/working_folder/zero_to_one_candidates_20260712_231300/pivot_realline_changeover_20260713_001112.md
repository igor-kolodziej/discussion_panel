# Zero-to-One Pivot: RealLine Packaging Changeover Rail

## Preserved Insight

Physical retained samples linked to measured outcomes are more durable and decision-useful than digital-only images or generic inspection reports. Preserve the idea of a longitudinal physical/data record, but accept the panel's conclusion that an unknown independent benchmark seller cannot rely on artificial defects, neutrality, or cross-vendor licensing demand.

## Structural Change

Change the buyer from inspection vendors to small and mid-sized flexible-packaging converters and co-packers. Change the painful event from model validation to an actual film, sealant, recipe, or machine changeover on a live production line. Replace artificial specimen creation with mandatory capture of real first-run packs, line settings, in-process failures, accepted settings, and retained physical samples.

The product becomes a recurring line workflow: every qualifying packaging-material changeover on one contracted line is routed through the same acceptance sequence before normal production release. It is not an independent laboratory, defect dataset, or one-off consultancy.

## Target Niche and Buyer

Polish flexible-pouch and tray converters or co-packers with one to five heat-sealing lines, repeated short production runs, and active migration toward recyclable mono-material structures. The budget owner is the plant manager, process/quality manager, or operations director responsible for startup scrap, downtime, complaints, and missed delivery windows.

Exclude pharmaceutical/medical packaging, large plants with mature in-house process-development laboratories, and customers unwilling to route every qualifying changeover through the workflow.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

The painful event is a new film, sealant layer, supplier lot, product recipe, or major line-setting change that creates several hours of unstable output, excess scrap, repeated destructive checks, or customer-acceptance risk.

**Hypotheses requiring research:** a target plant loses at least 15,000–50,000 PLN across labour, film, downtime, delayed output, and repeat trials during a difficult changeover; it will pay 5,000–15,000 PLN per event or 2,000–5,000 PLN monthly when the rail shortens time to a stable accepted process window by at least 30%.

## Value and Profit Wedge

RealLine supplies a portable sensor/measurement kit, a fixed first-run sequence, structured capture of material and machine variables, retained good/failure samples, and a line-specific process-window model. It returns an accepted operating window and a repeatable startup recipe for the next comparable run.

Revenue hypotheses:

- 15,000–30,000 PLN onboarding per line, including baseline and kit configuration.
- 5,000–15,000 PLN per material/changeover event.
- 2,000–5,000 PLN monthly after three events for workflow access, retained-sample custody, and model/history updates.
- Target gross margin above 55% after the first three changeovers on a line.

The first event is partly manual. Margin must improve because the same line, sensors, retained samples, and accepted settings are reused—not because the founder sells more engineering hours.

## Domination Wedge

Dominate changeover acceptance for one narrow combination: Polish heat-sealed mono-material pouch converters with frequent short runs. Each contracted line accumulates its own material × setting × failure × accepted-output history. The product becomes harder to remove when the plant relies on it to reproduce prior operating windows and compare supplier lots.

The company expands outward through the customer's adjacent lines and repeated film families, then across converters using the same machine/film combinations. It does not begin as a generic manufacturing analytics platform.

## Acquisition Channel

- Build a list of 40–60 Polish converters and co-packers from packaging fairs, industry directories, PPWR/mono-material product announcements, and public catalogues.
- Target plants publicly launching recyclable structures or advertising short-run flexible packaging.
- Offer a paid retrospective loss reconstruction followed by one deposit-backed live changeover.
- Require the buyer to identify the next scheduled changeover, baseline its expected scrap/time, and pay 50% before equipment configuration.
- Use a measured before/after result from the first plant to approach converters with the same line/film family.

The acquisition mechanism is a live scheduled production event, not a general claim about regulation or independent authority.

## Primary Control Point

The intended control point is a signed **all-changeover routing mandate** for a named line and defined material/changeover class, combined with:

- Company-owned portable capture and measurement kit.
- Custody of contracted retained samples.
- Rights to line-specific raw and derived process/outcome data.
- Rights to reuse anonymized parameter relationships across customers where contracts allow.
- A recurring acceptance history the customer loses if it stops routing events.

The control point is valid only when the first contract requires all eligible changeovers on the named line for at least six months. Selective one-off projects do not create control and must be rejected.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Spend no more than 7,500 PLN on 15 plant interviews, loss-model design, legal terms, and the first portable-kit specification.
2. Obtain two deposit-backed live changeovers before spending more than 15,000 PLN.
3. Spend 20,000–35,000 PLN on portable sealing/measurement tools, sensors, fixtures, storage, and calibration appropriate to the exact line event.
4. Spend 10,000–20,000 PLN on a packaging-process contractor and on-site capture support under assignment.
5. Reserve 15,000 PLN for travel, insurance, shipping, replacements, and contingency.

Proof within 12 months and 90,000 PLN: two paid changeovers, one six-month all-event routing contract, at least 30% reduction in time or scrap versus the plant's accepted baseline, three repeated material/line events, retained-sample/data rights, and gross margin improving on the third event.

## Expansion Path

1. One line and one material family.
2. All relevant material changes on that line.
3. Adjacent lines inside the same converter.
4. Other converters using the same machine/film family.
5. Supplier-lot comparison, startup recipe licensing, and material-procurement recommendations based on real outcome history.

Expansion fails if every line requires unrelated custom engineering or if customers refuse cross-event data retention.

## Minimum People Stack

- Founder: data model, product, experiment design, sales, and workflow automation.
- Contract packaging/process engineer: protocol and first-event supervision.
- Part-time field technician: kit setup, sample custody, and measurements.
- Legal/insurance review for production access, liability boundaries, and data/sample rights.

No full-time hires or permanent laboratory are required during the POC.

## Principal Risk

Film suppliers, packaging-machine OEMs, and experienced plant engineers may already provide changeover support at no incremental charge and possess deeper process authority. Plants may also refuse production access, mandatory workflow routing, retained-sample custody, or derived-data rights. If the only sellable product is one-off troubleshooting, this pivot reproduces the original service/reuse failure and must be killed.

## First Disconfirming Test

Contact 40 qualified plants and complete 15 process-manager conversations. Kill the pivot unless two plants identify a scheduled changeover with estimated loss above 15,000 PLN, accept a quantified before/after baseline, and pay a deposit for the same narrow workflow. After the first live event, kill unless the buyer signs a six-month all-eligible-changeover routing term and the method reduces measured scrap or stabilization time by at least 30%.

## Evidence Preserved

- Seal integrity and process stability are established economic/quality concerns.
- Mono-material packaging transitions create new process windows.
- Existing inspection and test equipment proves measurements are technically purchasable.
- The founder can combine data/experiment skills with bounded packaging expertise.

Panel scores and evaluator opinions are reviewer judgments, not market evidence. Their convergent objections define what this pivot must no longer assume.

## New Hypotheses

- Small converters have measurable changeover losses large enough to fund the stated fees.
- The same line/material family repeats enough to improve margin and retention.
- Plants will sign all-event routing and grant usable derived-data/sample rights.
- Portable measurements can reduce stabilization time without controlling the line or assuming product-release authority.
- Film suppliers and machine OEMs do not already solve the problem adequately for free.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

- Different buyer: converter/co-packer operations, not inspection vendors.
- Different painful event: live material/line changeover, not external model validation.
- Different evidence: actual production failures and accepted settings, not manufactured artificial defects.
- Different trust mechanism: measured before/after operational savings on the buyer's line, not neutral authority.
- Different control point: mandatory routing of all eligible changeovers on a contracted line, not hoped-for cross-vendor benchmark adoption.
- Different value capture: recurring operational workflow and per-event savings, not speculative dataset licences.

## Prioritization

Return this single pivot to `$evaluate-zero-to-one` first. It directly addresses artificial-to-production transfer and independent-benchmark WTP while preserving physical outcome custody. If the evaluator finds that production access, all-event routing, OEM/film-supplier competition, or service-heavy economics remain vetoes, kill the full SealFault/packaging-test family rather than producing another variant.
