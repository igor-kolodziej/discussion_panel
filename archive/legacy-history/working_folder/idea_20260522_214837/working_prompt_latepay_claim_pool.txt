Evaluate this idea. Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist, max 5h/day, 100k PLN capital ceiling, low camera/social preference, AI/data leverage, Poland/EU context. Idea: LatePayClaim Pool. A claim-acquisition and recovery desk for Polish SMEs that have already been paid late by public-sector buyers, hospitals, universities, utilities, municipalities, construction general contractors, or large corporate buyers, but never claimed the residual statutory late-payment interest and fixed recovery-cost compensation. It does not chase unpaid principal. It targets suppliers after principal has been paid and the commercial relationship is ended, low-dependence, or public-procurement-based. The seller uploads invoice list, due dates, payment dates, contract/payment terms, buyer identity, prior demands, and proof of performance/payment. The system computes invoice-level statutory interest and 40/70/100 EUR compensation under Polish commercial-transaction late-payment rules, legal reviewer filters prohibited assignments, disputed contracts, relationship-sensitive accounts, and weak evidence, then the seller signs either a cession/assignment of specific ancillary claims or an exclusive collection mandate. The desk sends batch demand letters and routes EPU/lawyer escalation only for clean claims. Control point: owned or exclusively mandated ancillary receivable rights over specific already-paid-late invoices, plus evidence folders and debtor response data. Two-month proof: sign 5 sellers, review 1,000 invoices or 2M+ PLN paid-late principal, acquire or mandate 100k PLN face value of ancillary claims, send 50 demand packets, get 20k PLN collected, settled, acknowledged, or court-filed, with legal-opinion approval of the assignment/mandate template. Pricing: claim purchase at 15-30% of verified face value, or no-upfront mandate with 30-45% recovery fee. Hidden insight: SMEs often will not confront buyers over post-payment late-interest rights because the main invoice is already paid and the residual claim feels socially awkward; an assignee can turn this ignored right into cash and build a repeatable debtor-response dataset. Rate 0-100 and include strongest objections/caveats. Cap below 82 if no concrete control point can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, uniquely produced, or credibly proven in a 2-month POC by an unknown solo founder.
