# Pivot Round 22 - Hard Asset Candidate Pool

Round 20 and 21 showed that useful compliance/evidence workflows keep scoring in the 70s unless the startup controls a harder resource. This round selects candidates where the first two months can produce a tangible or legal asset: receivable rights, settlement money flow, scarce inventory, routed medical/logistics flow, or donor vehicles.

## Finalists To Score

### 1. CzysteCash Subsidy Receivables Purchaser

Buy or advance against legally documented Czyste Powietrze subsidy/invoice receivables from small installers after completion evidence and payment-request status are verified. The startup pays the installer faster at a discount and collects from the expected WFOŚiGW/NFOŚiGW payment path or documented repayment flow.

Control point: assigned receivable rights, installer origination agreements, verified completion/payment-request evidence, and an underwriting dataset by installer, WFOŚiGW, project type, documentation gap, and payout timing.

Two-month proof: legal memo on assignment/factoring structure, two installer origination agreements, 3-5 funded receivables under 75,000 PLN exposure, 150,000-250,000 PLN signed pipeline, no funding without signed rights and field/document verification.

### 2. DepositRouter Returnable Packaging Settlement Rail

Operate a settlement layer for reusable crates, kegs, glass bottles, insulated boxes, or similar reusable packaging between one regional wholesaler/producer and small retailers. Start with one asset class and route deposit adjustments, disputes, and net settlement through the startup or a PSP/escrow-safe structure.

Control point: signed mandate over deposit settlement files, counterparty acceptance of the ledger as source of truth, disputed deposit recovery, and recurring transaction history by asset/counterparty.

Two-month proof: one producer/wholesaler with 30-100 counterparties, one asset class, 4-6 weeks of live settlement, 30,000 PLN disputed deposits resolved or prevented, and 10,000 PLN/month equivalent fees or retained recovery share.

### 3. Obsolete Automation Spares Bank For Municipal Plants

Acquire, test, catalog, and hold scarce obsolete automation parts for Polish water, sewage, heating, and food-processing plants: Siemens S5/S7-300 modules, old HMIs, drives, inverters, encoders, power supplies, and discontinued I/O cards. Sell guaranteed replacements and paid emergency access.

Control point: owned tested inventory, first-refusal agreements with modernization contractors removing legacy cabinets, serial/provenance/test records, and emergency reserve contracts with plants or maintenance firms.

Two-month proof: buy 50-150 high-demand modules for 30,000-50,000 PLN, sign 3 contractor first-refusal agreements, secure 5 prepaid reserve contracts, and complete 2 paid transactions above generic used-market price because the unit is tested/matched/immediate.

### 4. UN3373 Sample Custody Route

Controlled first-mile biological sample pickup for small private clinics and labs that need compliant same-day movement of blood, swabs, and other diagnostic samples to partner laboratories. The wedge is serialized packaging, tamper labels, pickup scans, temperature tags, courier identity, and timestamped handoff evidence.

Control point: fixed clinic pickup routes, accepted destination-lab handoff protocol, serialized sample-origin ledger, trained couriers, and non-reconstructable first-mile evidence.

Two-month proof: 10 paid clinic pickup plans, 1-2 destination labs accepting the chain, 1,000 paid sample movements in month two, under 40,000 PLN packaging/logging/courier setup, and no claim to provide diagnostics.

### 5. Forklift Donor Parts Harvest

Buy or option end-of-life electric forklifts, pallet trucks, reach trucks, and warehouse vehicles, then harvest and document high-value spare parts for one narrow model family used heavily in Poland.

Control point: owned donor vehicles, first-refusal deals with repair shops/leasing fleets/fleet operators disposing of non-running units, traceable part records, and pre-sold repair-shop demand.

Two-month proof: interview 20 repair shops, select one model family, buy or option 3-6 donor units for 40,000-70,000 PLN, pre-sell 10-20 parts before teardown, and prove gross margin with scrap/parts downside recovery.

### 6. Occupational Medicine Slot Bank

Option same-day/next-day medycyna pracy appointment capacity from small occupational-medicine clinics and resell prepaid hiring throughput to staffing agencies, warehouses, food-production sites, security firms, and logistics operators.

Control point: exclusive appointment blocks or minimum-guarantee capacity from clinics, prepaid staffing-agency demand, clean referral packet workflow, no-show/start-date data, and repeat worker flow.

Two-month proof: 200-500 optioned slots/month across 3-6 clinics, 3 staffing agencies buying prepaid blocks of 50-150 exams/month, 30,000-60,000 PLN clinic option/minimum guarantees, and measurable faster legal starts.
