# Pivot Round 12 OZE Distress Lots

Reason for pivot: dog/geofence and documentation/service ideas failed because control points were operational, copyable, or dependent on third-party decisions. This pivot uses a controlled physical asset: reserved distressed equipment lots.

## Candidate

**OZE Distress Lots Verified Equipment Desk**

OZE Distress Lots is a broker-first liquidation desk for small Polish renewable-energy and thermomodernization installers that are cash-stressed by Czyste Powietrze delays, weak heat-pump demand, cancelled subsidy jobs, overbought inventory, or business wind-down. The desk does not start as a broad OLX/Allegro marketplace and does not buy inventory speculatively. It reserves or options named equipment lots and sells only verified, serial-numbered, buyer-ready lots to other installers, service companies, small developers, landlords, and technical buyers.

The first narrow category is unused or nearly unused high-value OZE/thermomodernization equipment that can be verified from documents and photos: heat pumps, indoor/outdoor units, inverters, batteries, PV modules, buffer tanks, controllers, mounting kits, and premium windows/doors where the invoice, serial, warranty status, ZUM/EPREL/CE eligibility, storage condition, and transport assumptions can be checked. Every lot gets a buyer-facing lot passport: serials/photos, invoice chain, warranty/eligibility caveats, compatibility notes, missing documents, market-price comparison, transport plan, and "do not buy" warnings.

The founder's family renewable-energy installation company near Ostrow Wielkopolski is used only as the first trust/sourcing node and domain QA reference, not as the whole market. The desk takes either an 8-15% broker fee, a documented spread on reserved lots, or a paid 1,500-3,000 PLN seller rescue listing fee for lots above 30,000 PLN. Cash purchases are allowed only after a buyer deposit or when resale margin is above 25% with no more than 20,000 PLN at risk.

Primary control point: private distressed supply before it reaches public marketplaces, serial-numbered lot reservations/options, buyer deposits, technical verification, and a growing equipment-price/warranty/eligibility corpus. Two-month proof: reserve at least 10 lots with 250,000 PLN gross equipment value, get 3 buyer deposits, close 1-2 paid transfers with at least 10,000 PLN gross profit/fees, document 20 verified serial/price records, and keep unrecovered inventory exposure below 20,000 PLN.

## Why This Is A Material Pivot

- The scarce thing is not advice, forms, dashboards, or generic workflow. It is named discounted equipment supply.
- Sellers have an immediate cash-flow reason to cooperate.
- Buyers get a tangible discount plus reduced risk versus random OLX listings.
- The founder can validate before major spend by using reservations, deposits, and family-company technical inspection.

## Non-Duplication Notes

- Not BatteryFit: no customer ROI/subsidy retrofit sales packet.
- Not the heat-pump commissioning passport: no installation warranty/quality gate.
- Not HeatQuiet: no noise/siting dispute workflow.
- Not Data Act/CBAM/GreenTender/PromoLeak: no compliance document desk or tender/import evidence pack.
- Not the confirmed reclaimed interior suite idea: different supply base, buyer, category, technical verification, and regulatory/warranty data.
