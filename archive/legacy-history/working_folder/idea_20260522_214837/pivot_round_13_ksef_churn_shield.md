# Pivot Round 13 - KSeF Churn Shield

## Why This Pivot

The last profitable physical-inventory ideas kept failing below `82` because the founder could not hard-control enough trust, supply quality, or transaction flow within two months. This pivot moves to a live Polish regulatory/software failure trigger where the founder's data/AI skill is stronger and the foundational resource can be secured as workflow access to real customer support logs, KSeF XML/status failures, and repeatable regression scripts.

## Market Trigger Checked

- KSeF 2.0 is mandatory for large Polish taxpayers from 1 February 2026 and for most other entrepreneurs from 1 April 2026, with the smallest <=10,000 PLN monthly invoice-sales group deferred to 1 January 2027.
- Receiving invoices through KSeF has been mandatory from 1 February 2026.
- FA(3) is the binding structured-invoice schema from 1 February 2026.
- API KSeF 2.0 was made available for commercial-system verification on production before the rollout, with official integrator documentation and OpenAPI support.

## Candidate

**KSeF Churn Shield** is a 7-business-day failure-drill and customer-evidence pack for Polish vertical SaaS vendors whose customers now issue or receive mandatory KSeF invoices through their product and are opening support tickets, threatening churn, delaying renewal, or refusing onboarding because invoice flows fail or feel unsafe.

The first beachhead is not generic ERP, not big accounting systems, and not tax consulting. It is 10-80 person Polish vertical SaaS vendors in categories such as clinic/veterinary systems, field-service software, property-management tools, gym/membership systems, e-commerce/warehouse tools, B2B service CRMs, and appointment/POS systems. These vendors have enough customers to suffer KSeF support pain but are often too small to have mature compliance QA, incident-response evidence, and accountant-facing runbooks.

The product activates only on a live KSeF integration trigger:

- schema or FA(3) validation failures,
- token/certificate/authorization expiry,
- failed sends or UPO/status confusion,
- offline/outage fallback uncertainty,
- correction/advance/final invoice edge cases,
- buyer NIP or customer-master-data mismatches,
- accounting-office export/import confusion,
- customer asks for proof/runbook before renewal or onboarding.

The founder ingests anonymized KSeF XMLs, API status/error logs, support tickets, sandbox scripts, and customer/accountant objections. The deliverable is:

- vendor-specific failure-mode map,
- reproducible KSeF regression test suite,
- support triage macros,
- customer-facing "how invoicing works / what to do if KSeF fails" runbook,
- accountant handoff FAQ,
- incident-drill report usable in renewal/onboarding,
- monitoring checklist,
- explicit "not supported / tax adviser required" edge-case list.

It is not KSeF implementation, tax advice, legal advice, generic compliance consulting, or a dashboard. It sells reduced churn/support load and renewal evidence for software vendors with live KSeF pain.

## Economics

- Urgent 7-day drill: 8,000-18,000 PLN.
- Follow-on aftercare/regression check: 2,500-6,000 PLN/month.
- Two-month profit target: 3 paid drills, at least 30,000 PLN gross revenue, at least 20,000 PLN gross profit after minor subcontracted accountant/developer review.

## Primary Control Point

The controlled resource is not public KSeF knowledge. It is privileged access to anonymized live vendor support logs, customer objections, KSeF XML/API failures, and accepted proof artifacts from multiple vertical SaaS workflows. Each paid drill adds reusable scripts, error mappings, and language that one broad KSeF consultant does not have per vertical.

## Two-Month Proof

- Sell 3 paid urgent drills to Polish vertical SaaS vendors with live KSeF support/renewal pain.
- Collect at least 200 anonymized error/status/support cases.
- Build one reusable FA(3)/KSeF failure corpus and regression harness per vertical.
- Convert at least one vendor to aftercare.
- Document one customer renewal, onboarding, or accounting-office handoff unblocked by the evidence pack.

## Non-Duplication

This is not the existing PromoLeak Recovery Desk: no FMCG trade deductions, retail remittances, commercial promotion leakage, or recovery audit.

This is not the existing DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: no regulated-finance procurement, DORA/ICT third-party risk questionnaire, bank-renewal evidence, or security/legal evidence pack.

It uses KSeF timing, but the buyer and pain are different: Polish vertical SaaS vendors protecting their customer workflows from invoice-flow failures and churn.

## Kill Risks

- If vertical SaaS vendors see KSeF as solved by their ERP/accounting integration provider and will not pay urgently.
- If all valuable KSeF failures are handled by existing integrators with no remaining support/renewal pain.
- If access to logs/XMLs cannot be secured because of privacy, liability, or vendor defensiveness.
- If the work becomes generic QA testing without a reusable vertical failure corpus or customer-renewal evidence.
