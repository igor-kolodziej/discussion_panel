# Simulated Gate: BatteryEPR Listing Release Desk

## Exact Candidate Wording
BatteryEPR Listing Release Desk. A white-label urgent compliance release desk for Polish/CEE marketplace agencies, prep centers, and sellers whose battery or battery-containing SKUs are blocked, warned, or country-restricted on Allegro, Amazon EU, TikTok Shop, or other EU marketplaces because they lack valid Battery Regulation/EPR registration numbers, producer-responsibility evidence, eco-fee fields, or country-specific battery reporting setup after the August 2025 EU battery EPR enforcement wave. It does not sell generic EPR consulting, broad marketplace management, legal advice, or a compliance dashboard. It activates only on a live marketplace notice, Account Health warning, blocked offer, country-level sales restriction, or agency-routed seller case involving batteries, powerbanks, toys, pet electronics, LED gadgets, small consumer electronics, e-bike/scooter accessories, tools, or products shipped with embedded/removable batteries. The seller or agency submits the marketplace notice, SKU/ASIN/offer IDs, countries sold, battery type/category/weight/count where available, product listing screenshots, supplier invoices, existing packaging/WEEE/battery registrations, producer/importer status, and company documents. The desk returns a country-by-country release pack: producer-status decision tree, register-or-deactivate-country recommendation, PRO/authorized-representative application checklist, battery category/weight table, marketplace evidence upload packet, eco-fee disclosure values where required, seller-facing status tracker, and recurring reporting calendar. For simple cases, the desk helps submit or coordinate EPR/producers-organization applications and marketplace evidence uploads under seller authorization; for legal edge cases it routes to an EPR lawyer/provider. Control point: live marketplace listing/account-health blockers routed through agencies and sellers, seller authorization over specific registration/evidence submissions, accepted/rejected evidence memory by marketplace/country/product category, and recurring country reporting calendars once numbers are obtained. Two-month proof: 3 marketplace agencies or EU prep centers sign paid/routed case agreements; 8 sellers pay; 25 blocked/high-risk battery SKUs processed; 15 marketplace evidence submissions accepted, country restrictions cleared, or registrations formally submitted with marketplace case movement; 25k PLN collected; 3 sellers convert to monthly/annual reporting retainers. Pricing: 2k-6k PLN per urgent seller pack, plus 500-1,500 PLN/month or annual reporting fee per seller/country set. Hidden insight: sellers do not buy EPR compliance while revenue is flowing; they pay when a specific SKU, country, or account-health workflow is blocked, and marketplace agencies own the panic channel but usually do not want country-by-country battery EPR operations.

## Simulated Score
86/100.

## Why It Clears 82
This has a visible external control event: a blocked/warned marketplace listing or country sales restriction. The first product is not education; it is release of revenue already at risk. The foundational resource can be proven in two months through agency-routed live notices, seller authorization, submitted registrations/evidence, accepted marketplace uploads, and recurring reporting calendars.

## Strongest Caveats
- It risks overlap with existing marketplace-compliance rescue ideas; it must stay strictly focused on battery EPR/listing release rather than connected-device safety/cyber evidence.
- EPR providers already exist, so the wedge must be urgency, agency routing, and marketplace-specific evidence handling.
- Registration timelines can exceed two months in some countries, so proof must include accepted evidence or clear marketplace movement, not only final registration numbers.
- Legal edge cases require routing to an EPR lawyer/provider.
- The long-term moat depends on recurring reporting calendars and accepted/rejected marketplace evidence memory, not on one-time filings.
