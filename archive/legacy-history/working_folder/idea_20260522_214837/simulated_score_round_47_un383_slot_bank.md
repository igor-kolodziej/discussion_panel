# Simulated Gate: UN38 Slot Bank

## Exact Candidate Wording
UN38 Slot Bank. A reserved-capacity battery compliance rescue desk for Polish/CEE Amazon, Temu, Allegro, EU prep-center, 3PL, and freight-forwarder cases where lithium-battery products are blocked from listing, FBA inbound, dangerous-goods approval, or international transport because the seller's UN38.3 test summary, SDS/MSDS, battery exemption sheet, state-of-charge declaration, or model-level battery paperwork is missing, mismatched, outdated, unverifiable, or rejected. It does not sell broad CE consulting, EPR registration, or generic marketplace appeals. It starts with powerbanks, pet electronics, LED gadgets, small consumer electronics, toys, tools, e-bike/scooter accessories, and devices with embedded/removable lithium cells where the revenue blocker is a concrete Amazon/Temu/3PL/freight request. The seller/agency/3PL uploads the platform or carrier notice, ASIN/SKU/shipment ID, battery model/spec, supplier UN38.3 files, SDS/MSDS, product labels/photos, invoices, packing/transport details, and battery cell/pack relationship. The desk first verifies whether existing documents match the actual battery model and request; if they do, it returns a corrected evidence packet and upload/escalation wording. If they do not, the desk uses prepaid or optioned expedited review/testing slots with accredited battery labs and dangerous-goods documentation partners to produce or repair the missing test-summary/document path. Control point: prepaid/optioned lab-review or test slots, seller sample/document custody, live blocked ASIN/shipment cases routed through agencies/3PLs, accepted/rejected evidence memory by platform/carrier/battery type, and a growing supplier-report verification corpus. Two-month proof: reserve or option 6 expedited lab-review/testing slots under 35k PLN; sign 3 routed-case agreements with marketplace agencies, prep centers, or freight forwarders; process 20 live blocked lithium-battery SKU/shipment cases; clear 10 cases through accepted corrected evidence or carrier/platform movement; put at least 4 missing-document cases into reserved lab/test-summary workflow; collect 35k PLN cash; keep prepaid slot exposure below 45k PLN. Pricing: 1.5k-4k PLN for document verification/release packet; 6k-14k PLN for expedited lab-slot coordination plus pass-through test costs; agency/referral fee 10-20%. Hidden insight: sellers believe supplier battery paperwork is a commodity until Amazon, Temu, a carrier, or a forwarder rejects it for exact model mismatch; the scarce asset is fast model-level diagnosis plus reserved accredited capacity, not another compliance checklist.

## Simulated Score
87/100.

## Why It Clears 82
This is stronger than the BatteryEPR filing version because the founder can secure a concrete foundational resource: reserved or prepaid lab-review/testing capacity. The live blocker is visible, the buyer pain is urgent, and the service can prove value through accepted corrected evidence, carrier/platform movement, or use of pre-reserved testing slots.

## Strongest Caveats
- Marketplace/carrier acceptance remains external, so the promise must be "evidence path and movement," not guaranteed approval.
- Lab timelines and destructive sample requirements can still delay cases.
- The founder needs credible lab/DG documentation partners from day one.
- Some supplier reports may be fake or impossible to repair without retesting.
- The idea may remain a profitable specialist desk unless the accepted/rejected evidence corpus and agency/3PL routing compound.
