# Simulated Scores Round 16 - OrphanPump Service-Book Rescue

## Idea Scored

OrphanPump Service-Book Rescue is a B2B2C after-sales continuity rail for small Polish heat-pump/PV installers, importers, and subsidy-era operators that have 50-400 installed heat-pump customers but are exiting heat pumps, overloaded with callbacks, losing manufacturer support, or unable to keep servicing private-label/importer brands. It signs a narrow service-book transfer/mandate with one installer/importer/service company at a time, uses a co-branded service-continuity transfer notice, and sells paid annual service/triage plans, diagnostics, certified-installer routing, and parts sourcing only after paid cases/deposits.

## Scores

- Poincare: `77/100`
- Gibbs: `74/100`
- Mencius: `76/100`

## Common Objections

- GDPR/contact legality and co-branded customer communication rights are the first kill gate.
- Field capacity and certified HVAC/F-gas partner reliability are as important as demand.
- Bad cohorts may contain angry customers, missing documentation, private-label parts, warranty disputes, and unresolved installation errors.
- The service-book mandate is stronger than lead gen but still not hard monopoly-grade unless exclusive with ROFR, contractor SLAs, and legal data advantage.
- Two-month gross profit is plausible, but net profit is tight after legal, insurance, setup, and operational handling.

## Decision

Failed the lowered `82` simulated threshold. Do not send to real Zero To One. The next pivot must include a hard outside economic gate or controlled institution, not just acquired service books.
