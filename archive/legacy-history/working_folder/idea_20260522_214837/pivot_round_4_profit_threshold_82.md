## Round 4 Direction

The previous pet ideas failed because they were mostly software/service wrappers around soft workflows. Round 4 introduces a harder profit control point: physical retrofit scope, installer calendar, site audit evidence, registration/inspection deadline, and recurring operating archive.

### 1. PLW Pet Hotel Escape-Proof Retrofit & Inspection Archive

Polish dog/cat hotels and temporary care facilities now face registration/update pressure under the 2026 animal-health regime. Many need not only a form but a defensible facility state: entry vestibules, anti-escape gates, safe partitions, washable/isolation areas, records, vaccination/rabies checks, hygiene logs, incident logs, owner contracts, and PLW-ready evidence.

The startup sells a paid sprint:

- PLW registration/update packet and county-specific request tracker;
- site escape-risk and hygiene/readiness audit;
- fixed-scope retrofit quote for double-gate vestibule, anti-dig fence base, kennel partitions, washable surfaces, camera/GPS intake, signage, and record binder;
- reserved local installer slots through the founder's family installation-company credibility and subcontractors;
- recurring inspection archive retainer for hygiene logs, animal records, vaccination reminders, incident logs, and PLW correspondence.

Control point:

- paid site audits;
- customer retrofit deposits;
- reserved installer calendar/material quotes;
- facility evidence archive;
- vet/legal-reviewed template pack.

Two-month proof:

- 10 paid audits at 800-1,500 PLN;
- 3 retrofit deposits of 5,000-15,000 PLN;
- 2 completed small retrofits or signed install dates;
- 5 paid archive retainers;
- one written vet/legal review.

Why it may pass lower threshold:

- direct profit path through audits, installations, and retainers;
- family installation-company leverage;
- deadline-driven demand;
- control point includes physical work and operating records, not only a PDF.

### 2. Pet Hotel Quarantine & Hygiene Retrofit Pack

Narrower physical version: dog/cat hotels need isolation/quarantine and cleanable surfaces to be inspection-ready. The product sells modular isolation pens, washable wall/floor upgrades, disinfection workflow, animal record archive, and PLW evidence packet.

Control point:

- installed physical quarantine modules;
- disinfection/animal health logs;
- facility evidence archive.

Two-month proof:

- 8 paid audits;
- 3 quarantine module deposits;
- 1 completed installation;
- 5 archive retainers.

Risk:

- may be too operational/local and not scalable enough.

### 6. Pet Facility Buyer/Operator Due Diligence For Acquisitions/Leases

For people buying/leasing pet hotels, dog daycares, or breeding facilities: inspection/registration/retrofit diligence before transaction close. Includes PLW status, animal-health registration, facility defects, escape risk, retrofit budget, and contract risk.

Control point:

- transaction diligence packet;
- retrofit vendor quotes;
- buyer-side advisory at deal moment.

Risk:

- smaller deal flow and consulting-like.
