# Pivot Round 2

## Design Constraint From Round 1

The dog collar seed cannot be the business. The business must control a moment where pet safety/traceability is already mandatory, money is changing hands, or an institution is exposed to cost/liability.

## New Finalist Candidates

### 1. PetListing Traceability Deposit Gate

Legal breeders, shelters, and serious private rehomers need online listings that can survive new EU/Polish traceability expectations. The product verifies microchip/KROPiK or pre-registration status, seller identity, breeder/shelter credentials, animal age/vaccination/rabies/passport evidence where relevant, and creates a listing token plus optional buyer reservation-deposit flow.

Control point:

- signed admin/moderation agreements with niche breed marketplaces, kennel association pages, breeder clubs, and high-signal Facebook groups;
- every new listing in the partner channel must pass through the verification form/token before approval;
- paid verification/reservation deposits from sellers or buyers;
- evidence archive and failed-verification memory.

Two-month proof:

- 5 signed channel-admin agreements covering one narrow category, preferably pedigree/expensive/high-fraud puppy listings;
- 100 listings routed through the gate;
- 40 paid verifications or buyer reservation deposits;
- 10 failed/flagged listings showing the gate does work;
- documented willingness from admins to keep the token requirement.

Why stronger:

- EU Council approval on 22 May 2026 says online advertisements for cats/dogs will need verifiable identification information.
- Poland's KROPiK creates national identification momentum.
- The control point is listing approval and transaction deposit, not tracker hardware.

Primary copy risk:

- OLX/large marketplaces, kennel clubs, KROPiK/mObywatel, or payment providers add verification.

### 2. Municipal Repeat-Stray Cost Recovery Desk

For Polish municipalities paying shelters/animal-capture providers, the desk turns repeated owned-dog escapes into owner-linked cost recovery and compliance follow-up. It reconciles shelter intake logs, chip/KROPiK/SAFE-ANIMAL data, pickup photos, municipal invoices, owner contact evidence, and local fee/fine rules to prepare recovery packets and owner notices.

Control point:

- signed municipal mandate and shelter/operator data access;
- routed repeat-stray intake records;
- success fee or fixed pilot fee tied to recovered municipal costs or avoided shelter days.

Two-month proof:

- 3 municipalities sign pilot mandates;
- 200 historical cases screened;
- 30 owner-linked repeat/chargeable cases identified;
- 10 notices sent or municipal claims prepared;
- first recovered costs or approved process continuation.

Why stronger:

- Municipal animal homelessness cost is real and politically salient.
- KROPiK makes owner identification more actionable.
- Control point is municipal data/mandate, not consumer hardware.

Primary copy risk:

- municipal software vendors, shelter operators, legal/admin offices.

### 3. Verified Pet Transport Booking Gate

Shift from "tracker for transporters" to booking/payment evidence gate for legal cross-border dog/cat movement. Shelters/breeders/importers book transport only through a form that captures microchip, passport/health certificate, handoff signatures, GPS route, timestamps, exception log, and buyer-facing custody packet.

Control point:

- buyer-side booking/check-in flow;
- paid per-trip verification fee;
- signed preferred-provider terms with transporters;
- transport evidence archive.

Two-month proof:

- 2 shelters/breeders/importers require the packet for transport;
- 3 transporters accept the workflow;
- 50 paid trips routed through the booking gate;
- no free pilots counted.

Primary copy risk:

- transport platforms, fleet proof-of-delivery software, TRACES-adjacent tools.

### 4. Commercial Dog/Cat Facility Registration Pack

Polish dog hotels, breeding facilities, shelters, and other commercial dog/cat holding facilities face new registration expectations under animal-health/welfare rules. The pack helps facilities compile registration documents, animal records, hygiene/welfare SOPs, vet contact records, chip/KROPiK workflow, and inspection-ready evidence.

Control point:

- facility registration workflow access and recurring evidence archive;
- paid registration-prep retainer;
- local veterinary-law template library.

Two-month proof:

- 10 paid facilities;
- 3 submitted registration packets;
- one written vet/legal review;
- recurring monthly evidence-retainer intent.

Primary copy risk:

- vets, accountants, local consultants, facility software.

### 5. Sighthound Mandatory Adoption Safety Standard

Harden the Round 1 breed-rescue candidate: start with sighthounds only; require mandatory paid handoff pack, non-refundable 300-400 PLN fee plus refundable tracker deposit, no 24/7 rescue promise, only protocol/fleet/incident records.

Control point:

- mandatory routing addenda with 2+ breed foundations;
- owned tracker fleet;
- breed-specific safety data.

Two-month proof:

- 30 paid handoffs;
- <10% device loss;
- <60 min founder time per handoff after setup.

Primary copy risk:

- Tractive/Weenect rescue program or foundations internalizing.

### 6. Insurer-Backed Pet-Care Escape Evidence Standard

Pet-care operators get lower claims friction or better terms if they follow a GPS-backed incident evidence standard during high-risk stays/walks.

Control point:

- insurer/broker written standard;
- operator SOP routing;
- incident evidence archive.

Two-month proof:

- one broker/insurer written recognition;
- 5 paid operators;
- 30 tracked high-risk stays/walks.

Primary copy risk:

- insurer/platform owns channel; startup becomes implementation vendor.

## Best Round 2 Thesis

**PetListing Traceability Deposit Gate** is the strongest Round 2 candidate. It uses the same pet-safety/traceability market but moves from collar hardware to a regulatory transaction gate: online listings and buyer deposits. It also fits the founder better because it is data/workflow/evidence-heavy rather than field operations-heavy.
