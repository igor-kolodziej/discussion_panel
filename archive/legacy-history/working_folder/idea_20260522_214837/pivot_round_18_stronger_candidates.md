# Pivot Round 18 - Stronger Candidates From Generation Pass

## Shortlist

### 1. TrialLedger Clinical Site Payment Recovery Desk

A recovery desk for independent Polish clinical trial sites, SMOs, and private clinics running commercial trials. It reconciles clinical trial agreements, budgets, visit/event logs, EDC/source records, screen failures, unscheduled procedures, monitor visits, SAE/admin work, archiving, close-out activities, pass-through invoices, and sponsor/CRO payment reports to find underbilled or unpaid items. The startup obtains delegated authority to prepare and submit sponsor/CRO payment claim packs. Monetization: 6,000-15,000 PLN fixed audit or 15-25% of recovered payments.

Control point: site-routed CTA/budget/payment records, delegated recovery authority, sponsor/CRO response patterns, and a growing event-to-budget recovery corpus.

Two-month proof: 2 sites under NDA, 4 trial audits, 100+ billable events checked, 2 claim packs submitted, one recovered/approved payment or one paid fixed audit, positive margin.

### 2. Retailer SKU Launch Acceptance Desk

A launch-unblock desk for Polish challenger FMCG, beauty, supplement, and household brands whose accepted buyer deal is stalled by retailer/distributor/marketplace item-master rejections: GS1 fields, pack hierarchy, logistics dimensions, allergen/claims fields, images, packshots, price/promo setup, EDI fields, regulatory attachments, or product-document mismatch. It works only on live SKU launches or relaunches after a buyer/sales channel says yes. Monetization: 3,000-10,000 PLN per launch pack or 800-2,500 PLN per rejected-SKU repair.

Control point: live rejected/accepted portal patterns by retailer and category, delegated launch-data repair authority, and distributor/packaging-agency routing.

Two-month proof: 2 distributors/brands route live launch rejects, 10 SKUs processed, 5 accepted submissions or fewer approval rounds documented, 20,000 PLN gross revenue, 100 reusable field/rejection mappings.

### 3. PV Roof Insurer Evidence Pack

An insurance-renewal evidence packet for Polish SMEs with 30-500 kW rooftop PV facing property-insurance renewal, broker questions, bank/leasing diligence, premium exclusions, or fire-risk evidence requests. It reconstructs roof/PV documentation, maintenance records, layout, shutdown, inverter placement, fire access, thermography where available, installer docs, and "missing evidence" caveats. Monetization: 1,500-5,000 PLN per packet plus paid electrical/fire expert review when needed.

Control point: broker-routed renewal cases, underwriter-accepted checklist, family installer documentation knowledge, and repeated insurer question memory.

Two-month proof: 2 insurance brokers route cases, 5 paid packets, one written underwriter/broker acceptance, 30 historical PV docs reconstructed.

### 4. Occupational-Medicine Fast-Lane Slot Bank

A reserved occupational-medicine exam slot and referral-packet cleanup rail for staffing agencies and SME employers hiring warehouse, logistics, food-production, cleaning, and manufacturing workers who cannot start without valid Polish occupational-medicine clearance. It options blocks of clinic slots, prepares employer referral packets correctly, routes workers, reduces no-shows, and reports start-date reliability. Monetization: employer setup fee plus margin per completed exam.

Control point: optioned local clinic slots, staffing-agency routing, worker appointment flow, referral template accuracy, and no-show/start-date data.

Two-month proof: reserve 50-100 exam slots with 2 clinics, onboard 2 agencies, route 40 workers, generate 8,000+ PLN gross margin or equivalent setup fees, document faster starts.

### 5. UDI TenderLock Medical-Device Bid Pack

A live-tender rescue service for small Polish medical-device distributors whose hospital bid is blocked by MDR/UDI/EUDAMED evidence gaps: certificates, declarations, UDI/device registration, Polish IFU, responsible-operator data, model mismatch, or expired notified-body documents. It works only on live tenders or hospital clarification windows. Monetization: 4,000-12,000 PLN urgent bid pack.

Control point: live tender/SKU evidence files, accepted/rejected hospital-answer library, reviewer bench, and SKU evidence vault.

Two-month proof: 3 paid live bid packs, 50 SKUs mapped, one tender question accepted or bid moved forward.

### 6. Allegro Grey-Channel Enforcement Rail

A delegated takedown and evidence-response rail for Polish beauty, dermocosmetic, household, and niche FMCG brands losing margin to unauthorized Allegro listings, fake/grey imports, expired stock, misleading photos, IP misuse, or distribution-policy violations. It uses brand mandate/power of attorney, Allegro WOP/reporting tools where applicable, evidence packs, seller mapping, and accepted/rejected complaint memory. Monetization: 3,000-8,000 PLN setup plus monthly retainer or success fee.

Control point: recurring brand mandate, reporting account/process, accepted complaint patterns, seller network map, and authorization to act.

Two-month proof: 3 brand mandates, 100 listings mapped, 30 complaints filed, 10-20 listings removed/altered, 20,000 PLN gross revenue.
