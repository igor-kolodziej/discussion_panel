# Pivot Round 3

## Reason For Pivot

Round 2 confirmed that pet traceability has real timing, but the control points remain too soft:

- verification tokens are vulnerable to official platform/KROPiK verification;
- municipal recovery depends on legal collectability and political willingness;
- transport custody needs a high-volume buyer to mandate the workflow;
- facility registration is likely a services sprint unless recurring archive retention is proven;
- adoption safety is too narrow or copyable unless legally/contractually mandatory.

## Round 3 Finalists

### 1. Verified Litter Waitlist Gate

For one high-value Polish pedigree breed category with scam/moderation pain, the startup runs a mandatory breeder-admin approval and buyer waitlist gate. Sellers cannot post in partner breed channels without a verified litter page; buyers join a verified waitlist and can place reservation deposits through a licensed PSP/payment link controlled by the breeder, with the startup controlling the evidence workflow, listing token, buyer/seller identity checks, and failed-verification memory.

Control point:

- signed mandatory posting/admin agreements with 5 niche breed channels;
- buyer waitlist and verification flow;
- litter evidence archive;
- paid verification fees;
- optional reservation deposits processed by PSP, not held by founder.

Two-month proof:

- 5 mandatory admin/channel gates in one breed;
- 100 seller/buyer verification events;
- 40 paid verification fees;
- 20 buyer waitlist entries;
- 5 reservation deposits through PSP links;
- 10 meaningful failed/flagged listings.

Why stronger than Round 2:

- controls both listing approval and buyer demand signal, not only a badge;
- avoids founder custody of escrow money;
- starts where scams and high transaction value create willingness to pay.

### 2. KROPiK Vet Clinic Pre-Registration Rail

Small vet clinics need to register dogs/cats into KROPiK while collecting clean owner consent/data, scheduling chipping/rabies visits, and avoiding data-entry overload. The founder provides a clinic-branded pre-registration intake, data-cleaning queue, owner reminder system, and batch clinic submission support.

Control point:

- signed clinic intake workflow;
- owner pre-registration forms routed before visit;
- clinic appointment queue and owner data archive.

Two-month proof:

- 10 clinics sign intake pilots;
- 1,000 owner pre-registration submissions;
- 500 paid or scheduled chip/rabies visits;
- clinics pay setup/retainer.

Risk:

- clinic-management software or official KROPiK forms internalize the workflow.

### 3. Restricted/Sighthound Permit Compliance Gate

Poland has special permit contexts for sighthounds and dangerous/restricted breeds. The startup handles a permit-readiness packet: municipal permit application evidence, chip/KROPiK readiness, breeder/rescue contract appendix, liability-insurance quote, GPS safety setup, and post-transfer incident log.

Control point:

- signed breeder/rescue mandatory routing;
- permit packet templates by municipality;
- linked insurance/GPS partners;
- paid owner handoff.

Two-month proof:

- 3 breed organizations require the packet;
- 30 paid permit/handoff packets;
- 10 submitted permit packets;
- one insurer/broker quote integration.

Risk:

- niche size and copyability by breeders/rescues/lawyers.

### 4. Pet Facility Inspection Archive Retainer

Narrow the facility registration idea to ongoing inspection evidence for registered dog/cat hotels and commercial breeders. The first paid product is a registration sprint only when bundled with a paid archive retainer for animal records, vaccination/rabies reminders, hygiene logs, incident logs, PLW correspondence, and recurring inspection packet generation.

Control point:

- recurring evidence archive;
- facility operating records;
- vet/legal-reviewed templates;
- county-specific PLW request memory.

Two-month proof:

- 10 paid registration sprint customers;
- 5 paid archive retainers;
- 3 submitted packets;
- one written vet/legal review.

Risk:

- still services-heavy and copyable by animal lawyers/vets unless archive use persists.

### 5. Buyer-Side Puppy Fraud Evidence Desk

For high-value puppy/cat buyers, a prepayment verification packet checks seller identity, kennel/breeder credentials, animal age/chip/vaccination evidence, image reuse, phone/account reuse, contract/payment red flags, and transport evidence before any deposit is paid.

Control point:

- paid buyer-side decision flow;
- scam/failure memory;
- partnerships with breed communities.

Two-month proof:

- 100 paid checks through 5 breed communities;
- 20 avoided/scam-flagged payments;
- repeat referral by communities.

Risk:

- direct-to-consumer support burden and weak channel control.

### 6. Microchip Campaign Operator For Municipalities

Municipalities sponsor chipping/rabies catch-up days under KROPiK pressure. The startup reserves vet capacity, chip inventory, owner booking slots, reminder workflow, and post-event registration evidence.

Control point:

- municipality-paid campaign mandate;
- reserved vet calendars;
- chip inventory;
- owner booking queue.

Two-month proof:

- 3 municipal campaigns signed;
- 3 vet teams reserved;
- 1,000 owner bookings;
- event economics proven.

Risk:

- low-margin local event operations and vets/municipalities can internalize.

## Leading Round 3 Hypothesis

**Verified Litter Waitlist Gate** is the strongest remaining pet idea because it can control a transaction-adjacent private marketplace workflow before official systems catch up. It must be killed if admin gates do not own meaningful buyer attention or if sellers/buyers refuse to pay for verification.
