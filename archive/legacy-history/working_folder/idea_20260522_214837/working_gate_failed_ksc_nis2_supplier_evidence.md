## Exact Score Rationale Summary
The working validator said this is a real timing wedge because Poland's amended KSC regime is now in force and supplier-risk pressure can flow down to smaller vendors. It liked the hidden insight that vendors experience KSC/NIS2 as a blocked procurement or renewal answer rather than as a desire to buy broad compliance.

It capped the score below 82 because the control point is real but not owned: the desk can route a live buyer request through the vendor and get paid for urgent evidence assembly, but it does not control the buyer, procurement process, legal interpretation, or the vendor's actual security posture.

## Strongest Caveats
- Buyer requests may require actual controls, not just evidence formatting.
- Some target vendors may not themselves be KSC-regulated; the pitch must be customer-driven supplier-risk response, not self-compliance.
- 10k-20k PLN only works when the blocked renewal/tender value is large.
- Legal and cyber boundaries are serious around contract clauses, incident reporting, audit rights, and declarations.
- An unknown solo founder needs a named KSC/NIS2 cyber reviewer from day one.
- Consultants can copy the service; accepted/rejected answer memory is not enough in the first two months.

## Decision
Pivot. Do not run fresh validation.
