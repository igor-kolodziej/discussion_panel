## Exact Scored Wording

CollectorRouted VAT Bad-Debt Recovery Sprint. A tax-advisor-supervised recovery desk for debt-collection-referred Polish B2B creditors with 90+ day unpaid domestic VAT invoices, outsourced accounting, and at least 200k-500k PLN gross eligible receivables. It does not sell broad tax optimization. It starts where a creditor has already admitted the receivable is stale enough to send to a debt collector, but neither the collector nor accountant operationally owns VAT bad-debt relief. The desk signs a paid fixed audit or success-fee mandate and DPA over a specific unpaid-invoice pool, then ingests JPK_V7 exports, AR aging, invoice due dates, bank receipts, debtor/payment status, prior VAT corrections, assignment/factoring/collection status, and accountant contact. It creates an invoice-level eligibility schedule, reverse-correction calendar, evidence folder, and JPK-ready correction pack reviewed by a licensed tax advisor and accepted by the client accountant for filing. Control point: routed access from debt collectors/accountants plus signed mandate over a quantified invoice pool and recurring VAT correction/reverse-correction calendar; not a one-off dashboard. Two-month proof: at least 2 paid mandates, process 1M+ PLN gross unpaid invoices, identify 100k+ PLN VAT correction potential, obtain tax-advisor approval, and get at least one correction pack accepted by the client accountant for filing. Pricing: fixed audit fee plus modest success fee defined differently for permanent write-off vs temporary liquidity/reverse-correction cases. Hidden insight: debt collection monetizes gross receivable pursuit while accountants file compliance, leaving VAT liquidity recovery orphaned at the exact moment the stale-invoice pool is already identified.

- Dirty exports and edge cases could make operations labor-heavy if not standardized.
