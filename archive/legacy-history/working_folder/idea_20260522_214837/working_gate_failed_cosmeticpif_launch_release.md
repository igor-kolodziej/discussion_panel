# Working-Chat Gate Failed: CosmeticPIF Launch Release Desk

## Idea
CosmeticPIF Launch Release Desk.

## Simulated Score
87/100.

## Working-Chat Score
74/100.

## Result
Failed the active threshold of 82.

## Exact Score Rationale Summary
The working validator said the pain is real and the legal substrate is real: EU cosmetics require a Responsible Person, product information file, safety assessment/CPSR, CPNP notification, GMP/manufacturing evidence, and claims support where relevant before being placed on the EU market.

It still scored 74 because the true scarce asset is controlled by qualified safety assessors and Responsible Person providers, not by the desk. An unknown solo founder can route launch blockers and assemble evidence, but cannot reliably own the professional judgment, RP liability, lab/test timing, or marketplace/retailer acceptance.

## Strongest Caveats
- The first product should be triage and assessor/RP handoff, not a promised launch release.
- Many blocked launches are not document-packaging problems: missing CPSR, missing challenge/micro/stability tests, unsupported claims, restricted ingredients, or weak manufacturer evidence can create long remediation projects.
- CPNP itself is free, so the value cannot be framed as registration.
- Qualified safety assessor/RP providers are the trust center; without written priority terms, the desk becomes a referral layer.
- Claims substantiation and dermocosmetic positioning create legal/scientific ambiguity.
- Beauty distribution is trust-heavy and weaker for a low-camera unknown founder without a cosmetics network.
- Monitoring retainers only work after the desk has solved real launch blockers and is close to RP/update obligations.

## Decision
Pivot away from cosmetics compliance broker shapes. Prioritize candidates where the founder can own or reserve a physical/resource bottleneck, routed workflow, or transaction flow directly within two months.
