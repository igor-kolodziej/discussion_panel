# Pivot Round 19 - Hard Rights Candidates

## Candidates To Score

### 1. RoofRights Grid-Capacity Options

Options small commercial roofs or land parcels with usable grid feasibility, owner consent, and high onsite load for PV, storage, EV charging, or self-consumption energy projects, then sells qualified project rights/data rooms to EPCs, ESCOs, or developers.

Control point: 90-180 day exclusive development mandate from site owners, bills, connection documents, roof/land photos, and right to route EPC proposals.

Two-month proof: 5 signed site mandates, 2 developer/EPC fee agreements, 1 paid site package or success-fee contract.

### 2. SubstrateRights Biogas First-Look Desk

Controls local organic substrate supply for biogas/biomethane developers by signing first-look rights with farms, dairies, food processors, distilleries, mills, cold-chain waste generators, and crop-residue owners.

Control point: exclusive first-look rights on named tonnage streams, quality/seasonality/logistics data, and owner-approved developer introductions.

Two-month proof: 4-6 substrate first-look letters, basic composition/sample data, one developer paid access/diligence/success-fee mandate.

### 3. Czyste Payout Rescue Desk

A correction-to-payout service for Polish installers and beneficiaries stuck after Czyste Powietrze payment requests, missing annexes, bad invoice descriptions, wrong cost-category splits, proof-of-payment gaps, or WFOŚiGW correction letters.

Control point: installer-routed payment files, beneficiary authorization, accepted-correction library by WFOŚiGW, and released-cash trigger.

Two-month proof: one installer routes 15-30 stuck cases; 5 corrections submitted; 2 payouts or written complete statuses; fees 700-2,000 PLN per rescued file or 3-7% of released subsidy.

### 4. Fleet Return Charge Defense Desk

A pre-return and post-charge evidence desk for Polish SMEs returning leased/long-term-rental vehicles facing excess wear charges for scratches, missing equipment, tires, service records, mileage, or return-protocol disputes.

Control point: delegated access to vehicle-return calendar, photo protocols, service records, PZWLP-style damage taxonomy, dispute outcomes, and broker/fleet routing.

Two-month proof: 2 fleets, 20 cars audited, 5 dispute packs, one avoided/reduced charge, fees 400-900 PLN per vehicle plus 15-25% of reduced charges.

### 5. Supplier QA Gate Evidence Pack

A live supplier-approval rescue for Polish food, supplement, cosmetic, and pet-care co-packers whose retailer, private-label buyer, or brand QA onboarding is blocked by HACCP/BRC/IFS/quality-system/document-evidence questionnaires.

Control point: delegated response authority, buyer-specific accepted-answer data, QA practitioner bench, and co-packer live order urgency.

Two-month proof: 30 co-packers contacted, 5 live blocked questionnaires found, 2 paid rescue packs at 5,000-15,000 PLN, one buyer approval or next-round progression.
