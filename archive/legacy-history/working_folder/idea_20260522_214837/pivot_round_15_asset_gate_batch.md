# Pivot Round 15 - Asset/Gate Batch

## Why This Batch

Rounds 9-14 repeatedly failed because service artifacts were too copyable, brokered inventory was too trust-heavy, and claim economics were legally brittle. This batch tests ideas where the founder can control a harder resource within two months:

- physically owned or optioned inventory,
- live regulatory/import gate,
- live retailer commercial gate,
- routed client workflow.

## Finalists

### A. BeautyPack Deadstock Suites

A broker-first inventory desk for Polish/CEE indie cosmetics, supplement, home-care, and beauty brands that need small-to-mid production packaging lots faster and below MOQ. It buys, options, or takes consignment on complete unbranded packaging deadstock suites from contract manufacturers, packaging distributors, printers, brand closures, and cancelled product launches: bottles, jars, pumps, caps, cartons, tubes, scoops, sachets, and matching closure components with usable compliance/spec documents.

The startup does not list random leftovers. It controls complete production-ready packaging lots with spec passports: material, capacity, neck/closure fit, food-contact/cosmetic suitability where documented, color, count, pallet count, minimum usable batch, defect notes, storage condition, supplier paperwork, and "do not buy" caveats. Buyers reserve whole lots with deposits.

Control point: owned/optioned physical lots, first-look agreements with manufacturers/distributors, buyer deposits, and a growing packaging-spec/availability/clearing-price corpus.

Two-month proof: control 5 lots worth 80,000+ PLN original purchase value, obtain 3 buyer deposits, close 1-2 transfers with 10,000+ PLN gross margin/fees, keep inventory exposure below 30,000 PLN.

### B. EUDR Broker-Branded DDS Rescue Desk

A broker-first evidence service for Polish customs brokers and freight forwarders serving SME importers of EUDR-relevant products, starting with coffee/cocoa/chocolate/wood-product importers that need due-diligence statement readiness, supplier geolocation/document requests, product-scope classification, and order-continuity evidence.

It activates only on live imports, retailer requests, or supplier-document gaps. Broker sends invoices, packing lists, CN codes, supplier declarations, product descriptions, country of origin, existing geolocation/plot/cooperative data, and buyer deadlines. The desk returns a broker-branded continuity packet: EUDR scope risk, missing evidence list, supplier request pack, DDS readiness checklist, country/supplier risk notes, and "cannot safely claim compliant" warnings.

Control point: broker-routed import files, repeated supplier evidence patterns, importer urgency, and broker-branded distribution.

Two-month proof: 2 broker pilots, 5 paid importer packets at 4,000-12,000 PLN each, 50 reusable supplier/evidence clauses, one broker continues routing live EUDR cases.

### C. RetailerScope3 Supplier Evidence Unblock

A 7-business-day evidence-response service for Polish challenger FMCG, beauty, supplement, household, and pet-care brands whose listing renewal, annual supplier review, new-store onboarding, or retailer portal submission is blocked by live sustainability/ESG/CSRD/Scope 3 supplier-data requests from retailers, marketplaces, or distributors.

It ingests the exact retailer questionnaire plus packaging specs, BDO/EPR records, ingredient/supplier certificates, transport/energy invoices, product BOMs, audit docs, EcoVadis-style answers, and prior retailer communication. It returns a buyer-ready answer pack: defensible answers now, evidence attachments, supplier requests, red/yellow/green gaps, "do not answer without advisor" caveats, and a renewal/onboarding memo.

Control point: live retailer questionnaires, accepted/rejected answer memory by retailer/category, and broker/sales-agent referral channels.

Two-month proof: 3 paid live requests at 8,000-20,000 PLN, one sales broker/referrer, 50 reusable question-answer mappings, one buyer submission accepted or moved forward.

### D. HeatPump Orphan Parts Bank

A parts-and-repair routing desk for Polish homeowners, small property managers, and installers facing out-of-warranty or installer-orphaned heat pumps from private-label/importer brands installed during the subsidy boom. The original installer is gone or refuses service, and local HVAC contractors cannot quickly identify or source control boards, sensors, fans, pumps, flow switches, communication modules, or error-code-compatible replacements.

The founder starts with a narrow list of 5-8 model families visible through family-company and installer networks. The bank buys or consigns high-failure parts, creates model/error-code/part cross-reference sheets, and routes repair jobs to local HVAC installers who handle field work. It sells paid compatibility diagnosis plus urgent parts at margin.

Control point: owned scarce parts, model/part compatibility corpus, installer routing, and orphan-case intake.

Two-month proof: 30 orphan cases screened, 10 paid diagnostics, 5 part sales or repair referrals, 15,000 PLN gross margin/fees, inventory exposure below 40,000 PLN.

### E. e-Doreczenia Deadline Inbox Rail

A delegated official-inbox setup and monitoring rail for Polish accounting offices serving SMEs that now must use official electronic delivery addresses. The accounting office adds a client authorization/addendum for mailbox setup, routing, weekly evidence logs, deadline extraction, and "must forward to lawyer/tax adviser" flags.

This is not legal advice or tax representation. It is inbox activation, access hygiene, routing, evidence archiving, and deadline visibility for clients who are likely to miss legally effective electronic correspondence.

Control point: accounting-office client addenda, delegated mailbox/routing workflow, delivery/deadline archive, and recurring monitoring fees.

Two-month proof: one accounting office pilot, 20 clients onboarded at 300-700 PLN setup plus 99-199 PLN/month, zero missed weekly checks, and one documented deadline caught.

### F. Contract Manufacturer Slot Rescue

A reservation desk for Polish/CEE indie cosmetics, supplement, and pet-care brands needing fast small-batch production after a launch delay, stockout, or retailer deadline. It options cancelled or underused GMP/ISO production slots from small contract manufacturers and packages each slot with batch size, formulation limits, packaging compatibility, documentation needs, QC timeline, and handoff terms.

It is not manufacturing consulting or a marketplace. It only sells reserved near-term production capacity with clear technical constraints and deposit terms.

Control point: written slot reservations, brand deposits, and utilization memory for manufacturer capacity.

Two-month proof: 3 manufacturer slot agreements, 5 reserved slot listings, 2 buyer deposits, one paid production handoff, and 10,000 PLN gross broker fees with no capex.
