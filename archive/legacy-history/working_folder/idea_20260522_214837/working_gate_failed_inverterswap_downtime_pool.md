## Strongest Caveats
- Do not buy 12-18 inverters before proving regional model/failure clustering.
- The first proof should validate case flow and failure density, not inventory size.
- Many apparent inverter failures are actually string faults, insulation faults, surge damage, grid-voltage issues, AC protection issues, connector problems, or monitoring errors.
- Compatibility is more complex than power class: MPPT count, string voltage/current, phase, grid settings, commissioning access, warranty status, and protection requirements matter.
- Refurbished/exchange liability requires serial tracking, test reports, written warranty terms, and acceptance protocols.
- Priority retainers will likely sell only after a first successful rescue.
- 20 live downtime cases in two months may be difficult without existing service queues from routed installers.

## Decision
Pivot away from speculative spare-parts and inventory-pool shapes unless demand is routed and clustered before capital is committed.
