# Pivot Round 29: Assigned / Recoverable Money Rights

## Threshold Override

Per latest user instruction, active final thresholds are:

- simulated Zero To One score `>=82`;
- real working-chat Zero To One score `>=82`;
- real fresh-chat Zero To One score `>=82`.

The current pivot moves away from pet GPS/collars and soft compliance workflows into recoverable money where the buyer already has a legal, contractual, tax, subsidy, or administrative claim. The hypothesis is that a stronger control point may exist when the founder secures a mandate over an already-existing money right, ingests the records, produces a claim packet, and charges on recovered cash or measurable benefit.

## Candidate Pool

### 1. BadDebt Tax Recovery Engine

A success-fee recovery desk for Polish B2B companies with 5-100M PLN revenue and many unpaid invoices where VAT and income tax have already been paid on invoices that remain unpaid past statutory bad-debt-relief thresholds. Beachhead: wholesalers, industrial suppliers, agencies, IT services, recruitment firms with weak AR/tax coordination.

Control point: signed mandate over the company unpaid-invoice tax recovery workflow, data access, correction calendar, and debtor/invoice recovery corpus.

2-month proof: 3-5 companies, 500k-2M PLN gross unpaid invoice pool, 10-30 correction candidates, 50k-200k PLN recoverable tax benefit identified, at least 5 filed/advisor-approved corrections or one paid success-fee/fixed audit.

### 2. Non-Public Education Subsidy Recovery

A success-fee municipal subsidy recovery desk for Polish non-public kindergartens, schools, and special-needs/therapy-heavy education providers. It audits municipal education subsidy payments against student counts, disability/SEN certificates, attendance/monthly reports, municipal rate calculations, public budget inputs, delayed adjustments, and category weights.

Control point: signed recovery mandate over a school historical subsidy files, municipal-methodology database, and claim workflow.

2-month proof: 5 schools, 500k PLN plausible underpayment identified, 2 signed recovery mandates, 1-2 pre-claim/claim packets filed or paid audits.

### 3. Public Procurement Retention / Guarantee Release Recovery

A success-fee recovery desk for Polish contractors/subcontractors doing public-sector contracts with municipalities, hospitals, universities, utilities, and state entities. It finds bid bonds, performance securities, warranty retentions, guarantee deposits, and bank/insurance guarantees that should be returned or reduced after acceptance, partial acceptance, warranty milestone, defect cure, or contract closeout.

Control point: signed mandate over matured/near-matured returnable security rights and public-buyer response dataset.

2-month proof: audit 50 finished contracts for 20 contractors; identify 1M+ PLN matured or near-matured securities; get 100k+ PLN released or formally acknowledged; charge fixed audit + success fee.

### 4. PropertyTaxBack

A success-fee commercial real-estate tax recovery desk for Polish owners/operators of warehouses, retail parks, hotels, offices, industrial plants, and mixed-use assets. It audits local real estate tax declarations against floor plans, EGiB/land-building register, occupancy permits, technical areas, mezzanines, unusable space, renovations, land/building category, and municipal rates to find overpaid property tax and prepare correction/refund/offset claims with a property-tax lawyer.

Control point: signed refund mandate, property/tax document dataset, municipality response/refund database.

2-month proof: 5-10 owners, 20 properties, 300k PLN recoverable value identified, 2-3 correction claims/pre-claim opinions, one signed success-fee mandate.

### 5. NFZ Underpaid Claims Recovery

A success-fee revenue-recovery desk for private clinics/outpatient specialist centers/rehab/diagnostics providers with NFZ contracts. It audits rejected/downgraded/undercoded claims where services were performed but coding, attachments, referral rules, settlement limits, annexes, or appeal timing failed.

Control point: signed recovery mandate over historical NFZ claims and denial-pattern corpus.

2-month proof: 3 clinics, 12-24 months history, 100k-300k PLN resubmittable/recoverable claims identified/resubmitted, one paid fixed audit or success-fee agreement.

### 6. PFRON Wage Subsidy Recovery Audit

A success-fee/subscription recovery desk for Polish employers of disabled workers: cleaning/security/BPO, manufacturing SMEs, care providers, protected-work enterprises. It audits SODiR/PFRON wage subsidies for missed/reduced/denied months due employee status errors, disability-degree changes, working-time errors, late filings, de minimis constraints, ZUS/tax arrears flags, or documentation gaps.

Control point: signed mandate over employer subsidy history and recurring monthly subsidy workflow.

2-month proof: 3 employers with 20+ disabled employees, 150k PLN historical recoverable/missed subsidy or monthly leakage identified, 5 correction packs, one paid audit/success-fee deal.

## Finalist Rationale

These are non-redundant with prior confirmed ideas because the control point is not connected-device evidence, retailer deductions, parcel claims, energy commissioning, product claims, or building retrofit. They all test a harder pattern: controlled access to historical money-right records plus a recovery mandate.

## Known Global Risks

- Several may be capped as tax/legal/accounting services if the control point is only expertise.
- Success fees may be constrained by professional rules or customer trust; fallback must include fixed audit fees.
- Recovery time may exceed 2 months, so proof must include signed mandates, filed correction packets, acknowledged claims, or paid audits, not only cash received.
- The founder may need a licensed tax advisor, lawyer, NFZ billing specialist, or payroll/PFRON specialist as reviewer.
