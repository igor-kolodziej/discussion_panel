## Exact Scored Idea Wording For Real Gate

PLW-Ready Dog Hotel Safety Retrofit Installer is a productized B2B installation business for Polish dog hotels and dog daycares that need to look safe before summer bookings and the 2026 PLW/WNI registration/inspection cycle. The startup sells a 700 PLN escape-risk triage, credited against installation, then three standard retrofit packages at 6,000-28,000 PLN with 50% deposit to reserve an install slot. Packages include double-gate entry vestibules, self-closing latches, anti-dig fence skirts, gate/fence gap fixes, washable isolation/cleaning zones, intake/incident/rabies/hygiene binders, owner-facing before/after safety evidence, and optional 299-499 PLN/month evidence archive. No claim of government certification, PLW approval, or legal advice. A vet/behaviorist/welfare reviewer checks the checklist. The primary control point is paid retrofit deposits, reserved family-installer capacity, standard dog-hotel-specific bills of materials, before/after photo records, and a public registry of remediated sites. Two-month proof: 8 paid triage jobs, 5 retrofit deposits, 3 completed small installs or signed install dates, 2 archive retainers, 1 written expert review, and at least 20% gross margin after installer/material costs.
