## Selected Real-Gate Candidate

**RetentionRelease Public-Contract Security Recovery**, sharpened for real validation:

An exclusive-mandate recovery desk for Polish public-sector contractors that finds and releases matured performance securities, warranty retentions, bid bonds, guarantee deposits, and bank/insurance guarantees tied to completed public contracts. It starts with prime contractors in construction, installation, maintenance, and technical services who have 10-100 completed public contracts and weak post-acceptance finance tracking. The desk ingests contracts, SWZ/PZP clauses, acceptance protocols, warranty dates, guarantee instruments, annexes, defect correspondence, invoices, and public-buyer correspondence; produces a release packet with amount/date/legal basis/demand letter and, through a procurement-law partner, sends or supports release requests. The control point is an exclusive signed recovery mandate plus limited authorization/power of attorney over identifiable matured or near-matured returnable security rights, with data access to the contractor contract archive and a growing database of public-buyer release behavior. Two-month proof: audit 50 finished contracts for 10-20 contractors; identify 1M+ PLN matured or near-matured securities; secure at least 100k PLN released or formally acknowledged for release, or at least 3 paid audits/success-fee mandates if buyer payment timing runs longer. Revenue: fixed audit fee plus success fee on released cash/canceled guarantees.
