## Exact Scored Idea Wording For Real Gate

ExpiryVault Legal Entity Renewal Books. Acquire or option customer books from retiring one-person accountants, legal admin providers, and virtual-office operators who manage recurring annual filings, KRS/CRBR/company-record updates, board changes, beneficial-owner updates, registered-office mail, and compliance reminders for micro-spolki, foundations, associations, foreign-owned Polish SPVs, and dormant companies. The startup does not provide legal advice; it buys the renewal calendar, document templates, customer contacts, mail/archive custody where applicable, and recurring invoice rights, then routes regulated work to accountants/lawyers/notaries. Two-month proof: 2 LOIs with retiring providers over 50+ entities, 20 customer opt-ins after seller introduction, 10 paid renewal/compliance events, one legal/accounting subcontractor, and earnout-based acquisition under 30,000 PLN. Hidden insight: the asset is not generic company compliance knowledge; it is a sticky renewal calendar and admin custody for small entities whose owners forget obligations and value continuity. Control point is acquired entity calendar, contact channel, document archive/mail custody, and recurring billing rights.

## Simulated Caveats

- Legal/regulatory boundary is thin: "not legal advice" may not protect the model if customers perceive the company as the accountable compliance operator.
- Acquisition quality risk: retired-provider books may contain stale entities, bad contact data, underpriced retainers, undocumented obligations, or liability residue.
- Seller supply is the real bottleneck.
- Customer opt-in may collapse if continuity depends on personal trust in the retiring accountant/admin.
- Operational custody of mail/archive/documents creates serious process, privacy, and liability burden before software leverage compounds.
- Small-entity willingness to pay may be low unless the service bundles "peace of mind" and missed-deadline prevention into recurring retainers.

## Fatal Objection To Test In Real Gate

Fatal only if customer books cannot be legally/economically transferred with consent and recurring billing rights. Without that, the idea becomes a generic compliance reminder agency.
