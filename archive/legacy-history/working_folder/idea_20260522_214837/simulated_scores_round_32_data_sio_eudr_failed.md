# Simulated Scores Round 32: SIO, Data Act, EUDR

## Scores

| Candidate | Score | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| SIO Special-Needs Subsidy Leak Detector | 78 | Fail |
| Data Act Telemetry Release Desk | 74 | Fail |
| EUDR PlotLock | 74 | Fail |

## Key Objections

### SIO Special-Needs Subsidy Leak Detector

- Trust is the main sale because owners must share sensitive child/SIO/certificate data.
- Legal/accounting boundary risk requires reviewer/partner structure.
- Workflow may be labor-heavy for a 5h/day founder.
- Recovery may be slow or contested by municipalities.
- Distribution is not solved.

### Data Act Telemetry Release Desk

- Data Act rights may be narrower than pitch implies.
- OEM refusal may turn the business into slow legal escalation.
- Initial segment is too broad across many equipment types.
- Mandates alone are weak defensibility.
- Buyer urgency is uncertain.

### EUDR PlotLock

- Regulatory simplification/timeline delay reduces urgency.
- EU system already handles some reuse.
- Supplier trust/geolocation permission is hard for an unknown founder.
- Liability ambiguity weakens willingness to pay.
- Too broad across commodities and supply chains.

## Decision

No candidate goes to real validation. Pivot to a more immediate, shipment-blocking, paid trigger where the buyer has urgent economic pain and the founder can produce a concrete pack inside days.
