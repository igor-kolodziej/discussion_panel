# Simulated Gate: AdvanceClear Czyste Powietrze Prefinancing Payout Pack

## Exact Candidate Wording
AdvanceClear Czyste Powietrze Prefinancing Payout Pack. A contractor-facing and operator-friendly evidence desk for Polish heat-pump/insulation/window/PV-adjacent retrofit contractors who use Czyste Powietrze prefinancing and highest-level subsidies. It does not fill generic applications or generate leads. It focuses on the payment gate before an advance or final grant payment is released to the contractor. The installer/contractor and beneficiary upload the contract, contractor bank account, scope, income/operator eligibility evidence, energy-audit summary, device ZUM listing where relevant, invoices/pro forma invoices, photos, acceptance protocol, operator checklist items, and beneficiary authorizations. The desk produces a WFOŚiGW/operator-ready advance or final-payment packet, missing-item list, fraud-risk screen, contractor-account evidence, beneficiary consent/DPA, and milestone calendar. It is reviewed by a Czyste Powietrze operator-program specialist; the contractor pays because the packet protects cashflow, not because it is generic paperwork. Control point: contractor subsidy cashflow routed through the operator pre-payment checklist and accepted payment packet; recurring case data on rejected/missing items, operator bottlenecks, WFOŚiGW response times, and contractor-specific documentation defects. Two-month proof: sign 5 contractors using prefinancing; process 40 live beneficiary cases; get 25 operator-accepted payment packets or WFOŚiGW payment requests submitted; unlock or submit 300k PLN of contractor-directed grant payments; collect 25k PLN in setup, packet, or success fees; record at least 30 repeat missing-item patterns. Pricing: 400-900 PLN per payment packet or 2k-5k PLN/month per contractor plus a modest success fee for released contractor-directed payments. Hidden insight: after fraud reforms, the bottleneck moved from selling retrofits to producing clean operator-accepted payment evidence; contractors will pay to shorten grant cashflow and avoid rejected packets because prefinancing cash is their working capital.

## Why It Clears 82
The control point is concrete and economically urgent: the contractor's subsidy-funded cashflow depends on an operator/WFOŚiGW payment packet clearing a formal checklist. This is stronger than a report, dashboard, or marketplace because the desk routes a live payment event and can prove value in two months through accepted packets, submitted payment requests, and released or pending grant amounts.

## Strongest Caveats
- Public-program payment timing is partly outside the desk's control.
- It must avoid helping weak or abusive contractors game a fraud-sensitive program.
- The desk needs a program specialist who understands current operator/WFOŚiGW practice.
- Some operators may reject third-party prepared packets or prefer direct beneficiary handling.
- If contractors cannot produce truthful source evidence, the service must kill the case rather than polish it.
