## Fresh-Chat Feedback

The fresh chat judged the core insight as strong and validated the legal substrate. It agreed that Polish VAT bad-debt relief can support a real wedge because unpaid invoices over 90 days can trigger VAT correction, with the recovery process requiring invoice-level handling and later reverse corrections when payment occurs.

The score was capped below `82` because the commercial proof was too soft:

- "5 approved correction items or one paid audit/success-fee mandate" proves procedural feasibility, not repeatable customer acquisition or paid willingness to pay.
- The proposed beachhead was too broad.
- Accountant cooperation, debt-collector referrals, and data access remain unproven.
- VAT recovered may be temporary liquidity benefit if the debtor later pays, so pricing must handle reverse corrections.
- KSeF and ERP/accounting vendors may automate the easier parts over time.

## Stronger Version Suggested By Fresh Chat

Narrow beachhead:

**Debt-collection-referred Polish B2B creditors with 90+ day unpaid domestic VAT invoices, outsourced accounting, and at least 200k-500k PLN gross eligible receivables.**

Stronger 2-month gate:

- at least 2 paid mandates with fixed audit fee or signed success-fee agreement;
- process `1M+ PLN` gross unpaid invoices;
- identify `100k+ PLN` VAT correction potential;
- obtain licensed tax-advisor approval;
- get at least one correction pack accepted by the client's accountant for filing.

## Pivot Instruction

Do not abandon the tax-recovery shape yet. Materially narrow it to a routed debt-collector/referral wedge and make paid mandates plus accountant-accepted filing the required proof, not just identified benefit.
