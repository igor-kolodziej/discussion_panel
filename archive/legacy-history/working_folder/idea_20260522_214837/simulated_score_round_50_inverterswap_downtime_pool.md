# Simulated Gate: InverterSwap Downtime Pool

## Exact Candidate Wording
InverterSwap Downtime Pool. A stocked exchange and diagnostic rail for Polish SMEs, farms, shops, workshops, cold stores, and local public facilities with 10-80 kWp rooftop PV systems whose production is stopped or materially reduced by a failed string inverter, long warranty/RMA delay, missing installer support, or discontinued/importer-private-label model. It does not sell broad PV maintenance, new installations, monitoring dashboards, generic audits, or speculative spare parts. It activates only on a live downtime trigger: inverter offline, recurring fault code, grid-protection fault, no manufacturer response, failed RMA path, or electricity-bill/export loss visible in monitoring.

Beachhead: Wielkopolskie and neighboring regions, starting with inverter families that appear repeatedly in small commercial PV portfolios and can be safely swapped by certified electricians under documented compatibility rules. Exclude large utility-scale plants, high-voltage work, roof reconstruction, battery retrofits, and warranty disputes that require litigation.

Inputs: inverter model/serial/photos, fault logs, monitoring screenshots, AC/DC string design where available, installation photos, meter/export data, original installer/warranty status, grid settings, and site-access constraints.

Output: 24h triage decision, production-loss estimate, RMA/warranty evidence pack, compatible swap/loaner option where safe, electrician job pack, commissioning checklist, and post-swap verification report. The desk can either coordinate a paid temporary loaner, refurbished exchange, or fast replacement from an owned tested pool; field work is done by signed electricians/PV service partners, including the family OZE company where appropriate.

Control point: owned and tested narrow inverter inventory, model/fault compatibility matrix, signed swap capacity with 3 local electrician/PV service crews, first-refusal service routing from the family OZE company plus 2 non-family installers, and downtime/failure corpus by inverter family. The scarce asset is not a repair checklist; it is same-week access to compatible tested units and a job-ready swap path when ordinary warranty channels are slow.

Two-month proof: buy or option 12-18 tested inverter units from 4-6 narrow model families for under 55k PLN; sign 3 electrician/PV service crews with fixed swap-day pricing; secure routed failure access from the family OZE company plus 2 non-family installers; process 20 live downtime cases; complete 8 paid swaps, loaners, or replacements; collect 45k PLN cash; recover at least 20k PLN of inventory value through deposits, resale, or refurbished exchange; log 40 model/fault/compatibility cases; keep unrecovered inventory exposure below 45k PLN.

Pricing: 500-900 PLN 24h triage, 2k-5k PLN urgent swap coordination margin plus electrician pass-through, 4k-12k PLN exchange/refurbished unit sale or refundable loaner deposit depending on model and power class, and 400-900 PLN/month for small portfolios that reserve priority swap access after the first incident.

Hidden insight: small commercial PV owners do not need another monitoring dashboard; when production stops, they need a compatible inverter this week. Installers and distributors optimize for new installs, warranties, and standard SKUs, leaving an ugly but profitable orphan-downtime market where a narrow inventory pool plus failure corpus can restore production faster than the formal RMA chain.

## Simulated Score
84/100.

## Why It Clears 82
The idea has a directly ownable resource: tested inverter inventory and signed swap-day capacity. It uses the founder's family OZE context without depending solely on it. The buyer pain is measurable as lost production, electricity cost, and business disruption. The two-month proof is concrete and can fail fast if inventory demand is too fragmented.

## Strongest Caveats
- SKU dispersion can destroy the inventory thesis unless the first pool is extremely narrow.
- Inverter replacement has electrical safety, warranty, grid-code, and liability constraints; qualified field partners and clear compatibility rules are mandatory.
- Distributors and larger service companies can copy the pool if demand is obvious.
- Cash can get trapped in slow-moving stock.
- PV downtime may not be urgent enough for small owners in winter or low-production periods.
- Manufacturer warranty/RMA paths may undercut paid urgent replacements for owners willing to wait.

Pass simulated gate. Submit to real working-chat gate.
