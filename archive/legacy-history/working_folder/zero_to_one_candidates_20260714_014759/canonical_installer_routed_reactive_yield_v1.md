# Canonical Candidate: Installer-Routed Reactive Yield Portfolio


### One-sentence thesis

Embed a 24-hour reactive-charge underwriting option in independent electricians' ordinary SME quote workflow, then own the compensation cabinet and five-year measured-savings receivable while paying the originator from collected cash, building a contracted, telemetry-verified yield portfolio that equipment vendors cannot copy retroactively.

### Founder fit

The founder's parents' installation business supplies technical learning and the first quoting-workflow test, while the founder's data/AI background fits bill parsing, underwriting, telemetry and portfolio operations. The first paid site and the first scalable channel must be unrelated independent businesses. Licensed contractors perform every installation; the founder remains employed and works up to five hours/day.

### Customer and job to be done

The end customer is a B/C-tariff SME paying at least 24,000 PLN/year of technically fixable reactive charges. It wants the charge removed without trusting a savings forecast, funding equipment, selecting a topology or monitoring failure. The channel customer is an independent electrician already inside the panel/bill workflow but unwilling to finance, engineer and monitor this niche. The electrician wants recurring revenue without liability or working capital.

### Product wedge

1. Electrician uploads 12 months of customer bills plus a short load checklist under customer consent.
2. The company returns an approval, conservative savings floor and standardized contract within 24 hours.
3. End customer signs a five-year contract before equipment is ordered. Company owns the serial-numbered cabinet, telemetry and removal rights.
4. Licensed electrician installs to the company's design/protection specification and is paid an installation fee.
5. Monthly supplier bill plus telemetry determine avoided charge. Customer keeps at least 40% of verified savings; the company invoices at most 60%; the originator receives 10% of the company's collected receipts.

No generic audit fee, electricity supply, insurance promise or regulated investment return is sold. Counsel must confirm contract, tax, accounting, consumer exclusion and energy-law treatment before use.

### Commercial insight and underused momentum

Reactive charges are visible and technically removable, but the market sells boxes and audits after the customer searches for the category. Ordinary electricians see switchboards, invoices, PV/LED upgrades and customer complaints first. Giving them a zero-capital recurring claim turns a fragmented trusted workforce into a discovery layer. Rising electronic billing, remotely read meters and cheap telemetry make approval and verification less manual; standardized cabinets make assets removable and financeable.

### Beachhead and cold start

Start with four load archetypes—cold rooms, small food plants, fabrication/workshops and LED/PV-heavy retail—within two regional service radii. The resource sequence is:

- founder/family technical sandbox, with no related-party sale counted as proof;
- one unrelated end customer contract and cabinet;
- three unrelated collected sites and two independent originators;
- ≥20 sites with forecast/payback data, then an asset-finance proposal;
- ten signed independent originators and ≥50 active sites within 12–24 months.

The first originator offer is specific: no fee, no capital, no system design, paid installation work plus 10% of collected five-year receipts, with customer ownership/consent protected and no blanket exclusivity. The agreement reserves to the company only submitted qualified sites for 90 days and active contracted sites for the contract term. This is realistic for an unknown founder because the electrician gives up no existing revenue and gains a recurring share; it is proven only by signed agreements and submitted bills.

### Primary control point and copy risk

Control comprises company title to installed/removable cabinets; five-year measured-savings receivables; customer access/telemetry/removal terms; site-specific originator non-circumvention; realized forecast/payback data; and contracted originator economics. It is Tier 1 for every live site and becomes a Tier 1/2 portfolio control point after multi-originator density. The strongest copy risk is an established compensation vendor or ESCO offering the same originator share with cheaper capital. Defence is speed of approval, load-archetype accuracy, paid originator annuities, installed contracts and a lower-cost asset facility—not a patent or generic software.

### Unit economics and value capture

Target site underwriting case:

- baseline reactive charge: 36,000 PLN/year;
- approved measured reduction: 80%, or 28,800 PLN/year;
- customer keeps 40%: 11,520 PLN/year;
- company bills 60%: 17,280 PLN/year;
- originator gets 10% of collected company receipts: 1,728 PLN/year;
- monitoring/service reserve: 2,000 PLN/year;
- net pre-finance site cash: 13,552 PLN/year;
- landed company asset: 15,000 PLN; unlevered cash payback approximately 13.3 months;
- initial activation CAC, excluding originator revenue share: ≤2,000 PLN; paid back by first two invoices.

Portfolio target: ≥65% gross margin after originator share, service and depreciation; ≥38% EBIT margin after underwriting, sales, bad debt and central operations. Kill if actual asset payback exceeds 18 months or forecast savings miss by more than 20%.

### Founder Wealth Gate

No retained cash is added to terminal business value. Conservative/expected after-tax cash available for distribution is 95% reinvested while the founder remains employed and optional distributions are capped at 5%; strong success reinvests 100% during high growth.

The conservative case needs 500 sites. The most relevant official denominator is URE's 55,422 B-tariff points, whose medium-voltage/business load profiles are materially more likely to face reactive-energy charging than the broader 1.68m A/B/C upper bound. Five hundred is 0.9% of B-tariff points before selectively adding charge-bearing C-tariff businesses. This does not prove that 500 sites have the target 24,000–36,000 PLN annual charge, so a 200-bill sample remains the first decisive operating test; it does make the required share non-heroic if only a modest minority qualifies. The model also requires contract-backed asset finance after the first 20–50 sites; no founder claim assumes 7.5m PLN of personal capital.

### Reachability and 180-day paid-proof plan

- Days 1–21: spend 4,000 PLN on energy/electrical/contract review; build 3,000 PLN bill parser/underwriting prototype; use consented historical bills to measure eligibility.
- Days 22–45: obtain two replaceable cabinet/installation quotations and one insurer view; cap work at 4,000 PLN.
- Days 46–75: after outreach authorization, sign two independent originator agreements and review up to 50 submitted/named business bills.
- Days 76–110: one unrelated customer signs before order; ≤27,000 PLN reserved for cabinet, installation, telemetry and contingency.
- Days 111–180: licensed installation; collect two post-install bills and the first savings-share invoice. The independent payer and positive site contribution—not a related family project—are paid proof.

Maximum pre-proof risk: 38,000 PLN. Further spending requires successful delivery, forecast error ≤20% and positive contribution. The remainder of the original 100,000 PLN requires three unrelated paying sites, ≥12 combined site-months and a documented originator/direct CAC signal. Family capital is excluded until repeat demand and a risk-adjusted scaling case.

### Full-time transition, dependencies and kill criteria

Stay employed until at least ten unrelated paying sites or contracted recurring demand, positive cohort economics, a repeatable independent-originator channel and 12 months of personal runway.

Kill the shape if any of the following occurs:

- fewer than 2% of 200 consented real bills show ≥24,000 PLN/year of technically fixable charges;
- two post-install bills show <80% of forecast savings or measurement cannot be contractually audited;
- landed asset payback is >18 months or annual net site cash is <10,000 PLN;
- customer can terminate while retaining the benefit without make-whole/removal rights;
- no two independent originators sign or no unrelated customer contracts inside 180 days;
- installers demand upfront acquisition fees instead of performance-linked share;
- after 20 sites, no credible asset-finance provider will lend against the contracts without unlimited founder guarantees;
- a scaled incumbent matches the originator offer and raises CAC above one year of site gross contribution.

Failure leaves removable equipment, telemetry, bill parser, underwriting/error data and contract templates. Do not fall back to generic bill audits, consulting or equipment resale.
