# Candidate Funnel

Generated on 2026-07-14 from the live founder files, current market/regulatory research, the confirmed-idea index, and the latest rejection record. This is candidate generation only, not evaluation or validation.

## Funnel Standard

A direction is retained only if an unknown founder can name the first acquisition motion, obtain or directly prove a control point within 12 months and 100,000 PLN, seek paid proof within 180 days and 40,000 PLN while employed, and state a non-heroic route to at least 10 million PLN of founder net worth. Unsupported quantities remain hypotheses and become kill criteria.

## 1. DelayRights — Purchased Rights From Already-Paid Late B2B Invoices

- **Insight:** suppliers routinely abandon the statutory interest and 40/70/100 EUR recovery compensation after principal is finally paid because pursuing it risks the buyer relationship and each claim is too small. Buying the accrued ancillary claim outright turns a neglected entitlement into a controlled receivable.
- **Customer view:** the supplier wants a small immediate payment and administrative closure without financing the principal invoice or running a collection process.
- **First acquisition:** use public procurement award notices to identify former suppliers, then offer a fixed cash purchase only for completed, undisputed, already-paid invoices with a material delay and no contractual assignment ban. No outreach is authorised in this research run; this is the later test design.
- **Control point:** executed assignment of an accrued claim, source invoice, payment-date evidence and notice to the debtor. CJEU C-585/20 is direct evidence of assigned public-authority claims being pursued by a collection company; Polish counsel must confirm the deliberately narrow structure.
- **Online compounding:** debtor/payment-behaviour cohorts, document sufficiency, settlement response and recovery-cost curves improve pricing and automated screening.
- **Generator decision:** **retain as a finalist.** The control point exists at purchase rather than after hoped-for data sharing. Competition from BFF and ordinary collections is real, but the exact wedge is small ancillary claims after principal payment, not invoice finance.

## 2. CRA Sector CNA + Outsourced PSIRT Rail

- **Insight:** from 11 September 2026 connected-product manufacturers face rapid CRA reporting, while many CEE industrial-device makers lack a public vulnerability intake, CVE issuance route and product incident team.
- **Customer view:** a manufacturer wants a real vulnerability handled correctly, quickly and quietly; it does not primarily want a compliance memo.
- **First acquisition:** enumerate connected HVAC, energy-controller, measurement and workshop-machinery makers with no security.txt, disclosure policy, CVE history or visible PSIRT; sell a paid tabletop and intake setup.
- **Control point:** sector-scoped CNA authorisation plus a product-level PSIRT mandate, customer security.txt routing, owned intake endpoint and advisory history. CNA status alone is non-exclusive and insufficient.
- **Online compounding:** product/dependency context, disclosure history, reusable triage and advisory workflows.
- **Generator decision:** **retain as a finalist.** Paid proof and a 10m-PLN route are possible, but public designation by buyers, senior cyber capability and delivery leverage are explicit hypotheses.

## 3. ColdStore Flex Mandate

- **Insight:** refrigerated warehouses already contain thermal storage; a constrained behind-the-meter controller can shift compressor load against day-ahead prices before the business depends on balancing-market participation.
- **Customer view:** the owner wants lower electricity cost without product-temperature, equipment-warranty or food-safety risk.
- **First acquisition:** offer a paid historical replay to named Polish cold stores using interval energy and temperature histories, then seek a site-level exclusive dispatch mandate and performance share.
- **Control point:** 24–36 month exclusive dispatch contract for a named site, installed gateway, meter access and verified-savings formula, with a physical customer kill switch.
- **Online compounding:** controller adapters, thermal-response models, baselines and safety envelopes improve deployment economics across sites.
- **Generator decision:** **retain as a finalist.** The initial replay is reachable; annual site value, interface repeatability and liability are the decisive tests.

## 4. UDI LaunchGraph

- **Insight:** mandatory EUDAMED modules create a public stream of device, actor and certificate changes, but regional distributors and investors need interpreted commercial-change signals and history rather than raw searches.
- **Customer view:** a specialist distributor wants early evidence of a new category entrant, certificate change, inactive manufacturer or channel-opening event before rivals notice it.
- **First acquisition:** build a daily CEE/category history from the official public data, provide a two-week sample to named distributors and sell a quarterly category feed.
- **Control point:** founder-owned timestamped snapshots, entity resolution and change history created without customer cooperation. The history cannot be recreated retroactively, although a competitor can start archiving prospectively.
- **Online compounding:** longitudinal entity/device/certificate graph, labelled commercial outcomes and search-led distribution.
- **Generator decision:** **retain as a finalist.** The seed asset is directly ownable and the pilot is cheap; the link between database change and commercial willingness to pay is unproved.

## 5. Firmware Custody Rail for Private-Label Energy Hardware

- **Insight:** an importer can remain responsible for a connected product even when an overseas OEM controls source, build and signing.
- **Potential control:** SKU-level exclusive vulnerability mandate, source-modification licence, reproducible build and dual-control signing access.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** The importer often cannot grant the required source rights without a second speculative OEM negotiation. If buyers purchase only an audit or the work remains one senior engineer per family, neither control nor margins survive.

## 6. Small CMR Cargo-Claim Title Buyer

- **Insight:** sub-deductible cross-border cargo claims can be bought rather than handled on contingency.
- **Potential control:** executed assignment of a complete CMR claim.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** Each claim resets evidence, jurisdiction, debtor and litigation risk; 4,000 annual claims and 8,000 PLN gross spread are unproved, and existing handlers already serve the workflow. Reconsider only after a narrow-corridor legal opinion and a cohort of cash recoveries.

## 7. DIWASS Movement Rail

- **Insight:** notified cross-border waste movements became digital in May 2026.
- **Potential control:** contractual all-movement routing for one lane through commercial API software.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** The free DIWASS interface is a substitute, the API is non-exclusive, and every shipment still requires several actors. This recreates the previously rejected repeated-consent pattern.

## 8. PlatformReview Human Adverse-Decision Rail

- **Insight:** labour-platform rules require explanations and human review of automated decisions.
- **Potential control:** all covered adverse decisions contractually enter a case ledger before effect.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** The buyer population is uncounted, national labour law fragments delivery, and human review scales with case volume. It resembles a trust-heavy managed service an incumbent platform can internalise.

## 9. PFAS Foam Destruction Capacity Bank

- **Insight:** affected foam stocks and wastewater need separate collection and adequate destruction from October 2026.
- **Potential control:** prepaid PFAS-specific destruction capacity matched to a customer deposit and accepted waste batch.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** No affordable facility reservation is evidenced, incumbent hazardous-waste groups control the physical route, and the 400-site recurring-revenue thesis is uncounted and likely one-cycle.

## 10. Certified PCR Feedstock Option Book

- **Insight:** smaller film converters may need specification-matched recycled polymer allocations under PPWR.
- **Potential control:** back-to-back converter take-or-pay and recycler output allocation.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** No exclusive allocation or 400 PLN/tonne durable spread is evidenced; existing distributors can sign the same supply, and 30,000 tonnes creates working-capital and concentration risk.

## 11. White-Certificate Rights Advance

- **Insight:** eligible Polish efficiency projects can create tradable certificate rights, but applications must precede the investment.
- **Potential control:** assignment of future rights plus the pre-project application position.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** Assignability before issuance and time to monetisation are unresolved, established ESCOs exist, and a 10-toe minimum project is only about 23,000 PLN at the researched Q1 2026 price. Wealth would require a large share of national issuance.

## 12. Battery-Passport Import Mandate

- **Insight:** industrial and EV battery passports become mandatory in February 2027.
- **Potential control:** importer appointment, identifier and hosted record.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** EU interoperability and transfer duties intentionally weaken vendor lock-in; the economic operator can change providers, and the offer collapses into generic compliance hosting without warranty, BMS or take-back transaction rights.

## 13. eFTI Certified Inspection Gateway

- **Insight:** authorities must accept certified electronic freight information from July 2027.
- **Potential control:** certification and embedded all-shipment integration.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** Certification cost/path cannot yet be secured within 12 months and 100,000 PLN, while TMS incumbents can add the standards once final specifications arrive. A mapping pilot would not prove the foundational resource.

## 14. KIO Appeal-Fee Underwriter

- **Insight:** 7,500–20,000 PLN filing fees deter some SME procurement appeals despite a rapid adjudication event.
- **Potential control:** case-funding economics around a lawyer-screened appeal.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** The bidder retains the appeal right and can settle; adverse selection and legal trust are high, and a wealth case would need an implausible share of Poland's roughly 6,000 annual appeals.

## 15. Battery-Passport Continuity Trustee

- **Insight:** a printed QR and long lifecycle initially appear to create durable identity control.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** This is the same battery-passport family with different language; open transfer and limits on data reuse remove the proposed non-portable asset. Cosmetic distinction is not a structural improvement.

## 16. Refrigerant Reclamation Exchange

- **Insight:** F-gas phase-down could make recovered refrigerant valuable.
- **Decision:** **reject at funnel.** PROZON already buys refrigerant, supplies cylinders, operates a partner network, regenerates material and sells reclaimed products. The core collection and transaction rail is incumbent-owned.
