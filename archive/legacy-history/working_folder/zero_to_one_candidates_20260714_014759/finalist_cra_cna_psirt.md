# Zero-to-One Candidate: CRA Sector CNA + Outsourced PSIRT Rail

## One-Sentence Thesis

Become the narrow product-security incident desk and CVE authority for CEE connected HVAC and energy-control manufacturers that must handle real vulnerability events under the Cyber Resilience Act but cannot justify a full internal PSIRT.

## Founder Fit

The founder can build manufacturer/product mapping, secure case workflow, structured triage and automation, but does not have sufficient product-security credibility alone. A named senior product-security contractor with coordinated-disclosure experience is mandatory from the first paid pilot; specialist legal advice defines CRA scope and liability. The founder owns data/product/operations and avoids public influencer distribution.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The beachhead buyer is a 20–250-person Polish/Czech connected HVAC controller, heat-pump gateway, PV/energy controller or measurement-device manufacturer with multiple firmware products but no visible PSIRT, security.txt or CNA relationship. When a researcher reports a vulnerability, it must reproduce, classify, coordinate, communicate and possibly report it on short deadlines. Today the founder/CTO improvises by email, hires a pentest company, or hopes the OEM handles it.

## Product Wedge

A paid 30-day tabletop and intake activation:

- product and support-period inventory;
- security.txt and disclosure endpoint;
- simulated researcher report and 24/72-hour decision path;
- CRA Single Reporting Platform runbook;
- draft advisory and CVE flow;
- named on-call roles and evidence ledger;
- optional 12-month product-level PSIRT mandate after the tabletop.

It is not a compliance gap assessment. The recurring product is actual all-event vulnerability intake, coordination, CVE publication within approved scope and incident evidence.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The free ENISA reporting portal solves form transmission, not the operational work of reproducing a vulnerability, coordinating firmware/OEM actors, deciding disclosure, publishing a coherent advisory and keeping an evidence trail. General cybersecurity providers sell audits and pentests; large manufacturers maintain PSIRTs. A narrow shared PSIRT can spread senior capability over smaller product makers, but only if customers publicly route real reports rather than buying a one-off memo.

## Underused Momentum

- CRA reporting starts 11 September 2026, with a 24-hour early warning and subsequent notifications.
- CNA onboarding has no monetary fee and provides a defined CVE-publication role, although it is not exclusive.
- Many smaller manufacturers have no public security.txt, disclosure policy or CVE history, creating a prospecting signal.
- CEE hardware makers face the same EU obligations but may be under-served by global enterprise platforms.

## Beachhead Market

Connected HVAC and energy-control manufacturers headquartered in Poland and Czechia, with 3–30 supported product families, no public PSIRT and an English-capable technical lead. The sector is narrow enough to reuse protocol, firmware and safety context, yet expands to measurement, access-control and workshop equipment.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- 18,000 PLN tabletop/intake activation, prepaid.
- 60,000 PLN annual base mandate per manufacturer, covering product registry, intake, tabletop refresh and defined incident hours.
- Overage at 450 PLN/hour or an event package; severe response requires a separately approved scope.
- Direct senior-contractor, secure-platform and insurance allocation hypothesis: 24,000 PLN/year per client, giving 60% recurring gross margin.
- Sales CAC hypothesis: 9,000 PLN per client; first-year gross contribution including setup is about 46,800 PLN, producing under-three-month gross-profit payback after activation.

At scale, automation must keep routine intake/coordination below 30 senior hours per client/year. If expert time scales one-for-one with revenue, the model is not viable.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Build 150 named manufacturers from trade-fair lists, product catalogues and CE/technical pages; rank absence of security.txt/policy/CVE evidence.
- First 10 come from direct written technical outreach and a paid, fixed-scope tabletop—not a free audit and not the family company's endorsement.
- First 100 expand through firmware labs, certification bodies and product-security contractors only after direct CAC and renewal are demonstrated. These are non-exclusive channels, not the control point.

## Primary Control Point

The control point is the combination of approved sector CNA scope and each signed product-level PSIRT mandate: the customer points security.txt/contact routes to the company, names it as coordinator, and places every covered report in the owned timestamped ledger. CNA status alone is Tier 3 because any qualified organisation can apply; a signed all-product route plus accumulated case history can become Tier 2.

Strongest copy risk: a pentest, certification or vulnerability-management incumbent adds CRA intake and underprices the service. Catch-up defence depends on sector product context, public CVE/advisory record, renewal workflow and fast standardized incident execution.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resource: credible response capability and a governed route for real product reports.

Secure it by contracting a senior PSIRT practitioner for tabletop delivery and escalation; publish a disclosure policy; complete CNA training/onboarding for a deliberately narrow scope; deploy isolated intake and case logging; then obtain three unrelated prepaid customer mandates. Unknown-founder credibility comes from bounded senior delivery, transparent scope and a paid simulation before production—not a personal claim of expertise.

Proof within 12 months and 100,000 PLN: public CNA listing/scope, two named responders, three customer security.txt routes, three paid table-tops and at least one coordinated test/advisory. If CNA scope is refused, customer product CVEs cannot enter scope, or no buyer publicly routes reports, stop.

## Distribution Advantage

Absence of disclosure infrastructure is publicly observable and makes outbound deterministic. Every published advisory/CVE is a durable competence artifact and search result. Sector device knowledge and incident templates lower activation cost; customer security.txt links create inbound researcher routing rather than founder-led content marketing.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. Polish/Czech HVAC and energy controls;
2. adjacent measurement and building controls using the same channels/protocols;
3. multilingual EU shared-PSIRT coverage;
4. product-lifecycle vulnerability intelligence and recurring component alerts;
5. insurer/certification evidence exports after actual incident cohorts exist.

The company should not become a generic vCISO, audit or compliance consultancy.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Local-language manufacturer response, CEE price point, EU CRA deadlines and sector concentration support entry. Global vulnerability platforms can enter; the local defence is product-level routing plus a public handling record, not regulatory exclusivity.

## Compounding Advantage

Advisory templates, vulnerability taxonomies, researcher relationships, component/product mappings and response-time data compound. Customer routes and public CVE records increase switching cost and inbound flow. Confidential vulnerability data stays siloed; no claim depends on pooling it without contract permission.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Ninety-five percent of after-tax distributable cash in conservative/expected employment years funds senior responder capacity, security, insurance, CNA coverage and workflow automation; founder distributions remain at or below 5%. Strong growth reinvests 100%. Valuation uses operating profit only.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- 0–30 days: 8,000 PLN legal/insurance scope and 8,000 PLN senior contractor reservation; build 150-account list.
- 31–75 days: disclosure site, security.txt, secure intake and tabletop assets for 7,000 PLN.
- 76–150 days: after outreach is authorised, sell three 18,000 PLN prepaid table-tops; deliver with the named contractor.
- By day 180: require at least one signed annual mandate and public customer routing. Paid proof is the prepaid tabletop; recurring proof is the mandate.

Maximum pre-proof commitment: 28,000 PLN. The founder stays employed and does not promise 24/7 production response before paid capacity exists.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. 28,000 PLN maximum before a paid tabletop.
2. Up to 60,000 PLN only after successful tabletop delivery and positive contribution.
3. Remaining original 100,000 PLN only after three unrelated paying customers and one annual mandate.
4. Family capital excluded until renewals, repeatable CAC and insurable positive economics exist.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Proof | Veto |
|---|---|---|
| CNA scope | onboarding acceptance and public listing | refused/inapplicable client scope |
| Expert credibility | named contractor and successful tabletop | no qualified responder inside budget |
| Customer routing | security.txt/contact points and contract | buyers buy memos but refuse real routing |
| Annual WTP | prepaid mandate ≥50,000 PLN | one-off only or low ACV |
| Delivery leverage | hours/case ledger | <60% GM or >30 senior hours/client/year routine load |
| Insurability | broker/counsel terms | excluded liability or unaffordable cover |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Stay employed until at least five unrelated annual mandates, positive contribution on three table-tops, a repeatable acquisition source, CNA scope, insured escalation capacity and 12 months of personal runway exist.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Reusable assets include the manufacturer/product map, secure intake, tabletop library and structured product-security workflows. If real routing fails, do not retreat to generic CRA consulting; the founder can license the workflow to an established PSIRT or stop.

## Critical Evidence

- CRA reporting deadlines and obligations are official.
- ENISA provides the reporting portal but not outsourced product response.
- CNA onboarding is defined and has no monetary fee.
- Public security.txt/policy gaps can seed a named prospect list.

## Critical Hypotheses

- At least 500 reachable CEE/EU manufacturers fit the sector and size filter.
- Buyers will pay at least 50,000 PLN annually and publicly route real reports.
- CNA scope covers customer products and materially improves trust/workflow.
- Routine delivery remains at least 60% gross margin.
- A senior contractor and insurance can be secured within the initial budget.

## Kill Criteria

- CNA or coordinated-disclosure scope cannot cover customer products.
- Fewer than three paid table-tops from 150 accounts or no annual mandate by day 180.
- Buyers refuse public routing or security.txt changes.
- Annual WTP below 50,000 PLN or routine gross margin below 60%.
- One senior expert cannot support at least 20 routine client mandates with tooling.
- Conservative named buyer count cannot support 200 clients below 10% share.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder up to five hours/day; one senior PSIRT contractor; fixed-scope CRA/product counsel; part-time secure-platform engineer after paid proof. Pre-proof 28,000 PLN; first-year maximum 100,000 PLN in gated tranches.
