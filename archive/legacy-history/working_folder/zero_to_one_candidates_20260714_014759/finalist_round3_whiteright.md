# Finalist: WhiteRight Originator

## Thesis, customer and control

Before an industrial energy-efficiency project starts, contract to become the authorized applicant/initial registry holder for the resulting Polish energy-efficiency certificates, take a fixed 20–25% of transferable property rights plus an 8,000 PLN origination fee, and use standardized evidence/auditor workflows to build a rights portfolio. The payer/seller is the project owner; a power trader is only a later replaceable buyer of issued rights, not needed for first paid proof.

The customer wants to avoid losing a certificate by starting work before eligibility is documented and does not want to manage audit/URE procedure. The resource is a counsel-approved pre-start authorization and economic-right agreement, audit evidence, application position and later registry account/title. Tier 1 if the exact rights/authorization are enforceable and non-revocable after application; otherwise reject. Strongest copy risk: established energy auditors/ESCOs offer the same revenue share with stronger industrial trust.

## Beachhead and first customer

Target energy-efficiency projects estimated at 50–200 toe among named industrial grant recipients and larger food/logistics facilities, not household heat pumps. First acquisition uses official grant/project lists and energy-manager directories after outreach authorization; the family installation company is technical learning only and related-party work does not count.

Paid proof inside 180 days: one unrelated owner pays the 8,000 PLN origination fee and signs a ≥10-toe rights share before project commencement. Spend ≤18,000 PLN on counsel, auditor pre-check and application work; no rights purchase or construction capital.

## Economics and wealth

URE reports 1,979 new applications in 2025 and 1,330 issued certificates totaling 195,851 toe, with an indicated value near 434m PLN (about 2,216 PLN/toe). Hypothesis: an average controlled project yields 100 toe and 25% share, or 55,400 PLN rights revenue plus 8,000 PLN fee. Contractor/audit/filing cost is 35%; target 46% EBIT margin.

| Scenario | Projects and economics | Revenue / EBIT | Capital, ownership | Founder value / timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 120 projects ×100 toe ×2,216 ×25% + 120 ×8k fees | 7.61m / 46% = 3.50m PLN EBIT | 0.8m working capital from fees; 85% founder; 95% reinvestment/≤5% distributions while employed | 4× =14.0m EV; founder equity **11.9m PLN**; M6 fee/right, 10 projects Y2, 40 Y4, 120 annual Y8–10 |
| Expected | 250 ×120 toe ×2,400 ×25% + fees | 20.0m / 50% =10.0m EBIT | 2m PLN; 80% founder | 4.5×=45m; founder equity **36m**, Y7–9 |
| Strong | 500 ×140 toe ×2,600 ×27% + fees | 53.9m / 52% =28.0m EBIT | 5m PLN after issued cohorts; 70% founder, 100% growth reinvestment | 5×=140m; founder equity **98m**, Y8–10 |

The conservative 120 applications are 6.1% of 2025 new applications and their 12,000 toe equal 6.1% of issued 2025 volume. This is borderline and requires a materially narrower origination channel that general auditors cannot copy. Revenue timing may lag years; cash fees must fund operations without treating unissued rights as cash.

## Dependencies and gates

Reachability passes only if counsel confirms control and an independent owner prepays 8,000 PLN within 180 days and 40,000 PLN risk. Founder Wealth passes arithmetically but fails if 120 projects require heroically broad industrial sales or issue cycles prevent operating cash.

Kill if authorization is revocable or cannot secure economics; projects average <50 toe; no independent prepaid fee after 40 qualified prospects; URE issue/rejection timing makes fee-funded operations unprofitable; delivery is >25 consultant hours/project; or established auditors force the rights share below 15%.

Stay employed until multiple independent paid originations, issued/monetized evidence, repeatable acquisition and 12 months' runway. No family capital before repeat demand.
