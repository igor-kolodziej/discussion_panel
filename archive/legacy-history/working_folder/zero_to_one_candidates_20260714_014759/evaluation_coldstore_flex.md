# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ColdStore Flex Mandate

## Diagnosis

The paid replay is an intelligent low-risk wedge, and a signed site-dispatch mandate would control a real transaction. But site value, controller compatibility and liability are all simultaneous unknowns, while international aggregators and refrigeration OEMs already possess the technical shape. One vendor contract can reproduce the control at each site, and every deployment risks becoming bespoke engineering. The candidate should be killed rather than polished without actual site data, which outreach is outside this research run.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced: named Polish facilities, dynamic-price uptake and EU technical work. Hypotheses: 200,000 PLN safe annual savings, 100,000 PLN company revenue per site, 1,500 compatible EU sites, customer dispatch authority, 55% gross margin and affordable insurance.

## Overall Score

**72/100 advisory score.** Weighted total 144 over weight 20, multiplied by 10. Not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 per signed site; Tier 3 before dispatch.** Strongest copy risk is an OEM, electricity supplier or flexibility aggregator signing the same type of site contract and using standard controls hardware. Cross-site learning has not yet created catch-up cost.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass on arithmetic, fail on evidence quality.** 150 sites at 100,000 PLN with 25% operating margin give 3.75m PLN operating profit; 4× and 80% ownership yield 12m PLN. The compatible-site denominator and customer-value floor are absent, so the gate is not credible enough for pursue.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass for paid discovery, conditional for delivery.** A 12,000 PLN replay fits 25,000 PLN pre-proof spend and employment. Converting it to safe dispatch adds controller, liability and customer-authority dependencies.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is clear, but every adjacent controller/site risks new engineering. Strong international and OEM entrants can move inward with existing credibility.

## Fatal Objections

1. Three simultaneous unproved dependencies: savings, controller access and liability.
2. Site control resets and can be copied through a routine contract.
3. The conservative wealth case rests on 150 high-value compatible sites not yet counted.
4. Part-time founder cannot safely own industrial dispatch without a mature operating team.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Use a customer-paid historical replay before any control hardware or market participation.

## Recommendation

kill
