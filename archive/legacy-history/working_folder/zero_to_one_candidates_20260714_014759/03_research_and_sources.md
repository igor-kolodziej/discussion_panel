# Research and Sources

Research date: 2026-07-14. Direct links are preserved here; unsupported quantities in the candidate briefs are hypotheses, not market facts. No customer, supplier, partner or seller was contacted.

## Late-Payment Rights

- [Polish consolidated Late Payment Act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2023/1790/ogl) — statutory interest and recovery-compensation basis.
- [Polish Supreme Court, III CZP 94/15](https://www.sn.pl/sprawy/SitePages/Zagadnienia_prawne_SN.aspx?ItemSID=698-16544171-be1b-4089-b74b-413997467af2&ListName=Zagadnienia_prawne&Rok=2015) — compensation arises without proof that recovery cost was incurred.
- [Polish Supreme Court, III CZP 37/20](https://www.sn.pl/sites/orzecznictwo/OrzeczeniaHTML/iii%20czp%2037-20.docx.html) and [II CSKP 895/22](https://www.sn.pl/sites/orzecznictwo/OrzeczeniaHTML/ii%20cskp%20895-22.docx.html) — limitation analysis and treatment of the compensation claim as non-accessory.
- [CJEU C-585/20 / BFF Finance Iberia](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/redirect/juris/liste.jsf?num=C-585%2F20) — assigned public-authority receivables and fixed compensation per late invoice.
- [EU Late Payment Directive 2011/7/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/7/oj/eng) and [Your Europe explanation](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/finance-funding/making-receiving-payments/late-payment/index_en.htm).
- [EU Payment Observatory 2025](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/smes/challenges-and-resilience/late-payment/eu-payment-observatory/observatory-analysis_en) — late-payment prevalence, time spent chasing and public-sector performance.
- [Polish Public Procurement Office 2024 system report](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/b61cce3a-f71c-4271-be11-d7eaa1a03f76) — 330.4bn PLN PZP market and about 153,000 awards; it does not state eligible delayed-invoice volume.
- [KSeF official portal](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/) and [April 2026 second-stage guidance](https://www.podatki.gov.pl/aktualnosci/co-warto-wiedziec-przed-startem-ii-etapu-wdrozenia-krajowego-systemu-faktur) — structured invoice adoption is timing momentum, not a legal shortcut to bank/payment data.
- [BFF Banking Group services](https://www.bff.com/en/Services) — strong incumbent evidence in public-sector receivable finance; the proposed wedge must remain ancillary claims after principal payment, not ordinary factoring.
- [Terminovo compensation calculator](https://www.terminovo.pl/en/tools/compensation-calculator/) — a Polish workflow competitor automates calculation and reminders, but does not publicly present an outright-purchase wedge.
- [Example public contract containing an assignment restriction](https://samorzad.gov.pl/attachment/dede32b7-2cf9-4dc1-aa0a-f5988f377ec6) — proof that assignment screening is a hard requirement.

## CRA, CNA and Product Security

- [European Commission CRA reporting](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) — 24-hour early warning and subsequent reporting from 11 September 2026.
- [Cyber Resilience Act text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/2024-11-20/eng) — manufacturer vulnerability-handling obligations.
- [ENISA Single Reporting Platform](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security-and-certification/single-reporting-platform-srp) — the government reporting endpoint is free, so a vendor must control operational response rather than form submission.
- [ENISA SME readiness note](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/news/where-do-smes-stand-in-preparing-for-the-cyber-resilience-act).
- [CVE Program partner/CNA requirements](https://www.cve.org/partnerinformation/partner) — no monetary onboarding fee, public disclosure capability, scope and training requirements.
- [FIRST PSIRT Services Framework](https://www.first.org/standards/frameworks/psirts/psirt_services_framework_v1-1) — operating scope for a real response capability.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

1. DelayRights: Polish independent assignment of accrued compensation/interest, effect of assignment prohibitions, economically recoverable cohort size, debtor response and seller relationship tolerance.
2. CRA rail: obtainable CNA scope, buyer willingness to publicly route incidents, annual retainer value, insurability and expert-hours per product.
3. ColdStore Flex: site savings after safety constraints, controller interface repeatability, installation/liability cost and conversion from replay to dispatch mandate.
4. UDI LaunchGraph: legal/data terms, event completeness, number of reachable regional buyers and whether an observed change predicts a decision worth at least 30,000 PLN annually.
