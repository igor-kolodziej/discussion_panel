# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: WorkPol Audio

## Diagnosis

The candidate has a specific inside-customer insight: informal Polish conversation and CEFR do not test whether a temporary worker can parse noisy, role-specific instructions. The payer, first acquisition list, paid pilot, owned seed asset and legal boundary are clear. The conservative wealth bridge clears without debt or distributions at 1.6% of user-employers and 8.6% of temporary-worker flow, although exact qualified placements and 99 PLN willingness to pay remain unproved.

Its weak point is defensibility. Content ownership is Tier 1 but market power is Tier 2; a staffing platform or assessment vendor can commission competing items. The only credible catch-up cost is paid cross-agency calibration against job outcomes. The business must therefore prove lift over a structured interview and accumulate independent cohorts quickly; role libraries alone are not a moat.

## Evidence state

Sourced: agency/user-employer/worker counts, foreign-worker scale, employer training duties and language-document momentum. Hypotheses: three-pilot conversion, 99 PLN price, 60k test volume, 35% EBIT, predictive lift, consented outcome labels and incumbent response.

## Gates and control

- **Founder Wealth Gate: pass, conditional but credible.** 300 accounts/60k tests generate 9.54m PLN revenue, 3.339m EBIT and 11.35m founder equity at 4×/85%. No debt, retained cash or distributions are added. Kill if qualified flow <600k or target volume exceeds 10%.
- **Reachability Gate: pass.** Three unrelated 7.5k PLN pilots and the 60-item asset fit ≤180 days, ≤30k PLN and employment.
- **Control point:** Tier 1 owned item/audio/software; Tier 2 business power until ≥3,000 independent calibrated paid records. Strongest copy risk: a staffing/assessment incumbent commissions the same role bank and bundles it at near-zero marginal price.

## Fatal objections to carry forward

1. Employers may treat a five-minute structured interview as sufficient.
2. Outcome-label access and employment fairness/GDPR may block calibration.
3. 60k annual tests is a meaningful share of qualified temporary flow.
4. Incumbents can copy seed content; only outcome lift and distribution speed matter.
5. Agencies may push payment to user-employers, slowing a single-payer sale.

## Recommendation

**pursue.** Both hard gates pass and the paid proof is exceptionally cheap. Freeze the exact candidate for independent simulated review. The advisory score is not validation; judges should decide whether calibration/distribution are enough to overcome copy risk.
