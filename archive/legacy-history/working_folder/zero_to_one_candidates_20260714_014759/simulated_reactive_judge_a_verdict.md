## Full reasoning

The candidate addresses a real, measurable cost with plausible technical remediation, strong founder fit, explicit kill criteria, and a capital-efficient first-site plan. The official market denominator is correctly cited: URE reports 55,422 B-tariff points, while PGE's tariff confirms reactive-energy charging can also apply below medium voltage. However, the denominator does not establish how many sites have the required charge level, and the exact five-year commercial terms appear vulnerable to both customer rejection and aggressive incumbent undercutting. This is a credible proof-stage business hypothesis, not yet a demonstrated path to profitable domination.

The strongest economic objection is willingness to pay: at the target site, the customer pays 86,400 PLN over five years for a 15,000 PLN asset while retaining only 40% of savings. Risk transfer, monitoring, and zero upfront capital have value, but this is an unusually expensive effective financing/service package for equipment with roughly five months' gross technical payback. Customer self-purchase, ordinary leasing, or a cheaper incumbent offer could collapse pricing.

The stated gross-margin target is also internally inconsistent. With five-year straight-line depreciation, annual revenue of 17,280 PLN less the originator share, service reserve, and 3,000 PLN depreciation leaves 10,552 PLN, or about 61%, not the claimed minimum 65%.

Market evidence strengthens the pain but weakens defensibility. Orange already advertises free invoice analysis, guaranteed savings, remote monitoring, and payment from savings, as a service, in installments, or outright. Polish EPC/ESCO structures are also standardized rather than novel.

## Control point and strongest copy risk

An executed contract, company-owned removable cabinet, telemetry rights, and measured-savings receivable provide genuine contractual and asset control over each installed site. They do not create a portfolio-level acquisition moat: originators are non-exclusive, unsubmitted sites remain open, and the forecast dataset is reproducible by established installers.

The strongest copy risk is an established compensation vendor, Orange-like provider, or ESCO adding one electrician referral agreement, paying a higher originator share, and offering the customer a materially smaller savings share using cheaper capital and existing trust. Existing contracts cannot be copied retroactively, but virtually every future site can be contested.

## Founder Wealth objection

The wealth table converts enterprise value directly into founder equity without subtracting asset-facility or lease debt. In the conservative case, 12.92m PLN enterprise value at 85% ownership clears 10m PLN only if net debt is below roughly 1.15m PLN, yet the model requires a 7.5m PLN installed asset base and explicitly anticipates asset-backed leases. No cohort-level debt amortization, financing cost, replacement capital, or terminal net-debt schedule establishes that condition.

The expected and strong cases might still exceed the target after correcting for debt, but the stated founder values are overstated and the conservative case does not currently support its claimed threshold. Reinvestment and distribution treatment is disciplined and avoids retained-cash double counting, but that does not repair the enterprise-value-to-equity error.

## Reachability reasoning

The plan can plausibly obtain unrelated paid proof inside 180 days while risking 38,000 PLN, retaining employment, and outsourcing licensed installation. It orders equipment only after contract signature and does not depend on family capital or a completed platform. The tightest risk is fitting installation plus two post-install billing periods into the remaining schedule, but it is feasible with monthly billing and fast contractor availability.

## Strongest caveats

- Five-year 60% savings share may be commercially unacceptable once customers compare outright equipment cost.
- Asset-finance path is asserted, not evidenced.
- Independent electricians' bill access and origination willingness are unproved.
- 55,422 points do not establish charge incidence above the target threshold.
- Tariff/load changes, removal enforceability, bad debt and cabinet reuse remain unresolved.
- Incumbents can copy the referral economics with one routine channel agreement.
