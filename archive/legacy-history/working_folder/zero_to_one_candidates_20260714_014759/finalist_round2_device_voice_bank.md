# Finalist: Contract-Cleared EU Device Voice Bank

## Thesis and founder fit

Create a small bank of Polish/German/CEE synthetic device voices whose performer consent, training inputs, derivative uses, term, territory and revocation rules are explicit, then license fixed voice packs to medical and industrial OEMs across product generations. The founder can build demos, provenance records and an outbound engine asynchronously; a voice director, performers and IP/privacy counsel are contractors. No celebrity, influencer or privileged OEM access is assumed.

## Customer, pain, wedge and insight

The first buyer is a European SME making a patient-facing device, laboratory instrument or industrial safety terminal. Its pain is a stable multilingual voice with a procurement file that survives model updates—not access to generic text-to-speech. The workaround is cloud voices, recording studios or a voice vendor. The proposed gap exists only if the OEM will pay materially more for long-term, contract-cleared continuity and controlled retraining.

The wedge is one Polish/German safety-prompt pack: a contracted performer pair, approved pronunciation lexicon, owned raw sessions, model/data sheet, 50 standard prompts and a defined update licence. It makes no claim that AI Act transparency creates a mandatory purchase category.

## Beachhead, acquisition and control point

Beachhead: MEDICA-listed European device SMEs with multilingual patient/operator prompts and long product lives. First acquisition uses a product-category-filtered list from MEDICA's 4,821 exhibitors and the broader MedTech Europe company base. A fixed demo/provenance packet is sent to product, localization and regulatory leaders after outreach is authorized.

The controlled resource is six performer agreements and company-owned recordings covering synthetic/model use, derivatives, named device categories, territories, term, compensation and withdrawal consequences. Secure the first two language pairs for ≤24,000 PLN and legal review for ≤8,000 PLN. The company may license only the scope it owns. This is Tier 1 for the recordings/licensed likeness scope; it is not exclusivity over a language or voice style. Strongest copy risk: ElevenLabs, Acapela or a studio offers an adequate fully cleared voice bundled with superior tooling.

## Economics and compounding

- Two-language setup/first-year licence: 75,000–150,000 PLN hypothesis; annual update/continuity licence 40,000–90,000 PLN.
- Performer royalties, inference, QA and localization produce an estimated 72% gross margin; CAC hypothesis 18,000 PLN; gross-profit payback on the first 100,000 PLN contract is under four months after closing.
- Accumulated pronunciation dictionaries, device-validated prompt sets, performer continuity, update histories and OEM integrations can compound. Generic model quality does not.
- Expansion is from safety/patient prompts to more language pairs and industrial devices; custom voiceover projects are rejected unless the company keeps reusable rights.

## Reachability and capital tranches

- Days 1–30: 8,000 PLN counsel work and 50-product buyer census.
- Days 31–75: contract/record two performers and build a two-language demo for ≤24,000 PLN.
- Days 76–150: after outreach authorization, sell a defined pack; no additional languages before a deposit.
- By day 180: one independent OEM pays a ≥25,000 PLN non-refundable deposit; deliver the agreed prompt pack with positive contribution.

Pre-proof maximum: 35,000 PLN. Spend beyond 40,000 PLN only after successful delivery and contribution proof; remaining founder capital after three independent OEMs and an update/renewal; family capital only after repeatable demand. Employment ends only after multiple recurring payers, positive contribution, repeatable acquisition and 12 months' runway.

## Dependencies and kill criteria

| Dependency | Proof | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Durable performer rights | Counsel-approved contracts within budget | Required rights cost >12k PLN per language or withdrawal makes product continuity unfinanceable |
| OEM need/WTP | ≥25k PLN deposit by day 180 | 80 qualified contacts yield no deposit or buyers accept bundled generic voices |
| Product leverage | Second customer reuses ≥70% of assets | Work becomes bespoke recording/localization |
| Wealth denominator | 1,200 named voice-relevant products | 60 customers exceeds 5% or ACV <75k PLN |
| Regulatory claim | Written counsel memo | Proposition depends on a false statement that AI law mandates this vendor |

If killed, retain performer templates, pronunciation tooling and demo software; do not become a generic AI voice agency.
