## Diagnosis incorporating both judges

The simulated scores are 65 and 72. Both judges independently identify the same fatal structure: equivalent role-specific audio tests can be commissioned by an incumbent with distribution, while the proposed calibration advantage requires outcome data held by a second party that the paying agency does not control.
