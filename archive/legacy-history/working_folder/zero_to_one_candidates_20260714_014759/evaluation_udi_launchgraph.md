# Zero-to-One Evaluation: UDI LaunchGraph

## Diagnosis

This has excellent founder fit, proof speed and an immediately buildable seed asset. Its weakness is economic power: official public data and prospective archiving are easy to copy, and the proposed customer must pay 48,000 PLN annually for an unproved link between a registry change and a commercial decision. The 180-customer conservative case implies a material share of a hypothesised buyer universe. Historical snapshots alone are not enough to justify panel use.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Sourced: mandatory EUDAMED timing, public access and official documentation. Hypotheses: lawful commercial reuse details, 1,800 reachable buyers, 30–90k PLN WTP, event-to-opportunity value, 35–45% operating margin and search-led CAC.

## Overall Score

**70/100 advisory score.** Weighted total 140 over weight 20, multiplied by 10. Not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 at best.** The founder directly owns snapshots and labels, but the strongest copy risk is an incumbent or fast entrant beginning the same archive and bundling it with existing medtech data. Catch-up cost is measured in history length, not exclusive rights.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail on credibility despite correct arithmetic.** At 180 × 48,000 PLN, 35% operating margin, 4× and 85% ownership, founder equity is 10.28m PLN. But 180 customers and 48,000 PLN ACV are both unproved and together require a borderline share of a hypothesised niche.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** A source-compliant archive and paid category feed fit 180 days, 22,000 PLN and employment. This does not compensate for the wealth/control weakness.

## Expansion Check

Category and country expansion is easy technically, but broad incumbents can move inward with more data and brand trust. Local language alone does not preserve profit.

## Fatal Objections

1. One routine data-engineering project can copy the control point prospectively.
2. Commercial outcome value and 48,000 PLN WTP are unproved.
3. The wealth case combines high ACV with material niche share.
4. Customers may buy only custom research, collapsing margins.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Mandatory registry changes create a directly ownable historical event stream with excellent test speed.

## Recommendation

kill
