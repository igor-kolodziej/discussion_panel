## Full reasoning

This is a well-structured, reachable shared-savings ESCO model with unusually clear kill criteria, strong founder fit, and genuine site-level contractual control. It falls short because the proposal has not demonstrated that SMEs will accept an economically expensive five-year contract for equipment that vendors market as having a 6–12 month purchase payback, and because the electrician channel can be copied with a routine commercial offer.

## Strongest objections and caveats

1. At the underwriting case, the customer gives up 86,400 PLN over five years for a 15,000 PLN asset. Incumbent marketing claims direct purchase payback of roughly 6–12 months and a useful life exceeding ten years. That makes a 60% five-year savings share hard to sell to financially healthy SMEs and creates buyer-remorse, renegotiation and enforcement risk.
2. URE supports 55,422 B-tariff points but not the count with at least 24,000 PLN of technically fixable annual charges. At a 2% qualification rate, 500 sites require screening roughly 25,000 bills and 1,200 sites roughly 60,000 bills before conversion loss.
3. With five-year straight-line depreciation, `(17,280 − 1,728 − 2,000 − 3,000) / 17,280 = 61.1%`, not 65%. Finance cost, bad debt, VAT/working-capital timing, failures, truck rolls, enforcement and redeployment remain unproved.
4. Asset finance is existential. Financing unfamiliar five-year SME performance receivables after 20–50 sites may require guarantees, reserves or dilution.
5. Twenty-four-hour underwriting may outrun technical certainty: topology and defensible savings floors can require site measurement, harmonic analysis and load-change assumptions.

## Control point and strongest copy risk

Cabinet title, signed receivable, telemetry, removal rights and non-circumvention are direct control inside 12 months/100,000 PLN. The broad acquisition control is not Tier 1 because originators are non-exclusive and portfolio data are hypothetical.

The single strongest copy risk is an established vendor or ESCO offering electricians the same installation fee plus 10% receipt share while using cheaper capital and existing credibility. Orange already offers free bill analysis, guaranteed savings and payment from savings; adding the annuity is routine.

## Founder Wealth reasoning

The expected case could clear even after subtracting a full 18m PLN facility: `(40.824m EV − 18m net debt) × 80% = 18.26m PLN`. However, the conservative table incorrectly converts enterprise value directly to founder equity. With 7.5m PLN debt outstanding, founder equity would be `(12.92m − 7.5m) × 85% = 4.61m PLN`, not 10.98m. A debt-amortization schedule is essential.

## Reachability reasoning

A signed unrelated customer precedes equipment ordering, exposure is 38,000 PLN, contractors install, and work can coexist with employment. Schedule slack is thin: 50 bills gives only one expected qualifier at the 2% floor, and two post-install bills plus collection must fit before day 180.
