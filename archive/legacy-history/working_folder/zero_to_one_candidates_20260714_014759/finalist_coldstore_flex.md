# Zero-to-One Candidate: ColdStore Flex Mandate

## One-Sentence Thesis

Turn the thermal inertia already inside independent cold stores into contracted behind-the-meter electricity savings, beginning with paid historical replays and graduating only proven sites to exclusive constrained dispatch.

## Founder Fit

This uses the founder's data-science background for time-series baselines, optimisation and monitoring while avoiding influencer distribution. A refrigeration-controls engineer and food-safety/liability review are mandatory. The founder must not personally improvise PLC changes or operate sites beyond a customer-approved safety envelope.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is an independent Polish frozen-food warehouse or plant with material refrigeration load and indexed/dynamic electricity prices. It wants savings without temperature excursions, compressor damage, warranty problems or operational distraction. Today it uses static setpoints, a general energy audit or an electricity supplier; international flexibility aggregators often prioritise larger portfolios and regulated market participation.

## Product Wedge

A 10,000–15,000 PLN paid replay of 60–90 days of interval power, price, ambient temperature, setpoint, compressor and product-temperature data. The deliverable is a counterfactual savings range, risk envelope, compatible control path and a firm go/no-go. The fee is credited only if the customer signs a constrained dispatch pilot.

The second product is a site gateway operating behind the meter, within hard temperature/equipment constraints, under a 24–36 month exclusive dispatch mandate and verified-savings share. Balancing-market revenue is excluded from the proof stage.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Cold stores already own the storage medium; the adoption barrier is trusted control and evidence, not hardware cost. A paid replay lets the buyer prove ordinary tariff-shifting economics before granting dispatch. The narrow local wedge can move faster than broad demand-response platforms if it standardises common controllers and gives the site a physical kill switch.

## Underused Momentum

- Dynamic and indexed power pricing makes intra-day load timing commercially visible.
- EU-funded field work supports technical feasibility of flexible refrigeration, though it does not prove Polish site economics.
- Public cold-industry membership lists create a named acquisition universe.
- Electricity-market rules increasingly recognise demand response, offering later upside that is not required for the first savings product.

## Beachhead Market

Independent Polish cold stores/frozen-food plants with at least 500 kW controllable refrigeration load, 15-minute meter data, stable product-temperature instrumentation, compatible controller access and annual electricity spend high enough to support at least 200,000 PLN of conservative savings. Sites needing OEM permission from several vendors are rejected.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

- Paid replay: 12,000 PLN average, estimated 65% gross margin.
- Dispatch setup: 30,000 PLN customer-funded hardware/integration deposit.
- Recurring performance revenue: 30% of verified savings, minimum 100,000 PLN annual platform/operations value per site after proof.
- Direct site operations, controls support, telemetry and hardware amortisation: 40–45% of revenue at early scale; target recurring gross margin 60%.
- CAC hypothesis: 25,000 PLN including unsuccessful replays; first-year recurring gross contribution 60,000 PLN gives payback under five months after dispatch activation. The replay itself must be positive contribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

- Rank named association facilities by public capacity proxies, controller evidence and tariff exposure.
- First 10: direct operational/finance outreach with a paid replay, not a free energy audit. Require three unrelated sites and at least two controller families.
- First 100: repeat controller integrations, refrigeration-service firms and energy advisers can refer sites only after direct conversion is proven. The customer-controlled dispatch contract remains the control point.

## Primary Control Point

A 24–36 month exclusive behind-the-meter dispatch mandate for a named site, an installed owned/leased gateway, direct meter/temperature feeds and a contractually defined verified-savings share. Customer safety override is mandatory and does not eliminate economic control while the agreement is active.

Strongest copy risk: refrigeration OEMs, electricity suppliers, GridBeyond, Fusebox or a controls integrator adds tariff optimisation. Defence requires fast replay-to-control conversion, cross-site controller adapters, credible baselines and safe operating history. One routine vendor contract can copy a single site, so cross-site learning and switching cost must be demonstrated.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Required resource: one paid site dataset followed by a constrained dispatch mandate on a compatible controller.

Secure it through a paid replay that quantifies value before control, a named controls contractor, a site-funded setup deposit and a contract limiting operation to a jointly approved safety envelope. An unknown founder can sell an evidence replay without claiming grid-market status; the customer itself controls meter and controller access.

Proof within 12 months and 100,000 PLN: three paid replays, one customer-funded gateway, one six-week controlled pilot with no safety breach and positive verified savings, and a signed 24-month mandate. Reject incompatible sites rather than paying for bespoke OEM integration.

## Distribution Advantage

A named cold-store list and electricity-cost event support targeted outbound. Paid replay outputs create finance-grade proof. Reusable adapters and measured savings by controller/site type improve close rates and make referrals from refrigeration service firms useful without depending on exclusive partners.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. Polish independent frozen warehouses on compatible controllers;
2. food plants and supermarket distribution cold rooms;
3. Czech/Slovak sites with the same control stack;
4. portfolio bidding into flexibility/balancing markets only after direct savings is profitable;
5. adjacent thermal loads such as ice plants and chilled-water storage.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Local language, tariff reconciliation, on-site controls capacity and fragmented mid-market facilities create an entry wedge. This is not a regulatory moat. Broad aggregators can move inward; the company must be faster and more economical on compatible mid-sized sites.

## Compounding Advantage

Controller adapters, thermal models, safe setpoint envelopes, baseline evidence and failure data reduce deployment cost across sites. Contracted dispatch creates recurring transaction flow. Site data cannot be pooled beyond contractual permissions; reusable model parameters and integrations are the durable asset.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected scenarios reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable cash during employment in adapters, monitoring, controls capacity and site acquisition; founder distributions remain ≤5%. Strong success reinvests 100%. Customer deposits or leases fund hardware; founder cash does not subsidise a fleet before repeat paid evidence.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

- Days 1–30: 5,000 PLN legal/safety framing; reserve a controls engineer for 8,000 PLN; create compatibility screen and named list.
- Days 31–90: after outreach authorisation, sell three 12,000 PLN paid replays; spend up to 7,000 PLN on secure analysis tooling.
- Days 91–150: deliver replays and seek one 30,000 PLN customer-funded integration deposit.
- By day 180: the paid replay satisfies the paid-proof event. Do not install unless replay value, compatibility and customer deposit support positive contribution.

Maximum founder pre-proof commitment: 25,000 PLN; compatible with employment.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

1. Up to 25,000 PLN before first paid replay.
2. Up to 40,000 PLN total only after paid replay and positive delivery contribution.
3. Remaining founder 100,000 PLN only after one successful controlled pilot and three unrelated paid replays.
4. Family capital excluded until multiple dispatch mandates, positive site contribution and a repeatable acquisition signal.

## Critical Dependency Map

| Dependency | Proof | Veto |
|---|---|---|
| Sufficient site value | paid replay | <200,000 PLN safe annual savings or <100,000 PLN revenue potential |
| Controller access | documented interface + test | bespoke OEM permission for most sites |
| Safety/liability | approved envelope, insurance terms | uninsurable food/equipment risk |
| Dispatch authority | signed 24–36 month mandate | customer buys analysis but refuses control |
| Repeat deployment | second controller/site | one-off engineering >40% of revenue |
| Market size | named compatible-site model | fewer than 1,500 reachable EU sites at target economics |

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Stay employed until at least three unrelated contracted dispatch sites, positive contribution, no safety incident, a repeatable replay-to-mandate acquisition signal and 12 months of personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Historical replay tooling, tariff reconciliation, thermal modelling and controller maps remain useful for energy analytics. If dispatch authority fails, do not pivot into generic energy audits; license tools to a qualified integrator or stop.

## Critical Evidence

- Flexible refrigeration is technically demonstrated in EU work.
- Named Polish cold-industry facilities are publicly discoverable.
- Dynamic/indexed pricing creates potential tariff-shifting value.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Safe, ordinary tariff shifting creates at least 200,000 PLN annual savings at a meaningful subset of sites.
- At least 1,500 EU sites fit size, pricing and controller filters.
- A customer will pay for a replay, then grant exclusive dispatch.
- Reusable interfaces keep gross margin above 55%.
- Insurance and food-safety constraints are affordable.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than three paid replays from 60 qualified sites.
- No dispatch deposit/mandate within 180 days after a positive replay.
- Safe annual savings below 200,000 PLN at the first two sites.
- Integration exceeds 40,000 PLN or needs bespoke OEM approval at most sites.
- Recurring gross margin below 55%, payback over 12 months or any material safety incident.
- Conservative market requires more than 10% of bottom-up compatible sites.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder up to five hours/day; contracted refrigeration-controls engineer; fixed-scope food-safety/liability counsel; site electrician only after customer deposit. Pre-proof budget 25,000 PLN; 12-month maximum 100,000 PLN in tranches.
