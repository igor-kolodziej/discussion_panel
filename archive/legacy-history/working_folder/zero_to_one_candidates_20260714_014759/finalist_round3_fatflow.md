# Finalist: FATFlow

## Thesis, customer and control

Purchase only B2B automation/robotics supplier invoices after factory/site acceptance milestones, serial-number evidence and debtor no-offset acknowledgment, using technical verification to fund receivables ordinary factors reject. The installer/integrator is the seller; the industrial debtor is the payer. The owned asset is the assigned receivable. Family renewable-installation experience supplies an underwriting sandbox but no related-party invoice counts as proof.

First acquisition: target independent automation integrators through public award/exhibitor lists, buy one 40,000 PLN debtor-confirmed invoice for ≤35,000 PLN, notify the debtor and collect inside 180 days while maximum outstanding exposure stays ≤40,000 PLN. Legal/AML/tax review precedes any purchase. Control is Tier 1 for an accepted assignment; strongest copy risk is a general factor adding one technical checklist or engineer.

## Economics and wealth

Pricing hypothesis: 3.5% for a 60-day turn. Funding 1.2%, expected loss 0.5%, technical/CAC 0.7%, leaving 1.1% contribution before fixed cost. GUS counted 31.3k factoring clients and 523.4bn PLN purchased invoices in 2024, demonstrating category scale and competition.

| Scenario | Annual purchased volume | Revenue / profit after funding and losses | Equity/funding/ownership | Founder wealth / timing |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Conservative | 200m PLN | 7.0m fee revenue; 2.2m contribution; 1.0m pre-tax profit | average book 33m, 25m debt, 8m true equity; 70% founder | A cautious 5× after-tax profit plus 8m required book equity yields ~12.05m company equity; founder **8.44m**, so gate fails without ≥1.6m non-double-counted later distributions; Y9–10 |
| Expected | 500m PLN | 17.5m revenue; 5.5m contribution; 3.5m pre-tax | 20m book equity; 65% founder | 5× after-tax profit + book equity ≈34.2m; founder **22.2m**, Y7–9 |
| Strong | 1.2bn PLN | 42m revenue; 13.2m contribution; 9m pre-tax | 45m book equity; 55% founder | 6× after-tax profit + book equity ≈88.7m; founder **48.8m**, Y8–10 |

The valuation convention treats required tangible equity plus a franchise value on after-tax profit; any lender/valuator may use a lower price-to-book/earnings framework. Conservative volume is 0.038% of national factoring but must come from a narrow automation niche. Warehouse funding after 10–25 clean collections is a critical dependency, not assumed capital.

## Gates and kill criteria

Reachability passes if one unrelated assigned invoice is collected within 180 days and ≤40,000 PLN. The Founder Wealth Gate fails conservatively as written and cannot be rescued by expected scale alone without a credible capital/ownership path.

Kill if debtor confirmation is absent in >30% of sampled invoices, anti-assignment clauses exceed 25%, annualized loss exceeds 0.7%, contribution is <1% per turn, technical checks do not approve invoices general factors decline, or no warehouse-funding proposal follows ten clean independent deals. Do not pivot into factoring brokerage or generic invoice finance.
