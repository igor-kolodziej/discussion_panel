# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: WhiteRight Originator

## Diagnosis

The regulated rights pool is real and the 6.1% volume share is mathematically possible, but the business is an energy-audit/application service paid partly in a delayed asset. The unknown founder must win 120 industrial projects per year, coordinate auditors, survive multi-year issuance and compete with established ESCOs that already have trust. The exact enforceability and registry position are not yet proven, so the control point is conditional rather than present.

**Advisory score: 66/100.** Not a validation score.

- Control point: Tier 1 only after counsel-approved non-revocable authorization/rights economics; Tier 3 before that. Strongest copy risk is an established auditor adding the same share clause.
- Founder Wealth Gate: fail on credibility despite arithmetic. Six percent of all applications/issued toe is not conservative for an unknown part-time founder and requires linear expert work.
- Reachability Gate: conditional pass for an 8k fee/right, but industrial acquisition and legal control inside 180 days are unproved.
- Fatal objections: service-like scale, issue delay, industrial trust, auditor dependence, revocability and a narrow national market.

## Recommendation

**kill.** Do not pivot into a white-certificate desk, smaller rights share or trader partnership; those preserve the same service/trust/issuance structure.
