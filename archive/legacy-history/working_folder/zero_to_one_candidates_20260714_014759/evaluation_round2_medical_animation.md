# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: Modular Medical Animation IP Library

## Diagnosis

Modularity can improve agency economics, but the candidate has not shown that customers will accept retained reusable rights or pay 120,000 PLN annually. The owned source files are directly commissionable by competitors, and a first paid customization can validate agency demand while saying nothing about catalogue reuse. This remains a service-to-library hypothesis rather than a controlled market wedge.

## Evidence state

Sourced: broad exhibitor/company pools, copyright mechanics and scaled visual incumbents. Hypotheses: relevant procedure-family denominator, annual ACV, 70% reuse, licensing terms, sales cycle and customer saving.

## Gates, vetoes and control point

- **Control point:** Tier 1 for the exact commissioned scenes, Tier 2 commercially. Strongest copy risk: a specialist animation agency uses the same modular-production process and a stronger portfolio.
- **Reachability Gate:** pass for a project deposit, but not necessarily for product demand.
- **Founder Wealth Gate:** fail. The qualified denominator, recurring ACV and margin after medical customization are not evidenced.
- **Fatal vetoes:** full-assignment buyer norms; service drift; one-vendor-contract copy; trust-heavy acquisition.

## Recommendation

**kill.** A structural pivot would need a buyer-mandated workflow or exclusive source material, not additional scene families.
