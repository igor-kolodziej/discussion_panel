# Advisory Zero-to-One Evaluation: FATFlow

## Diagnosis

Technical verification is a useful underwriting feature, but debtor-confirmed accepted invoices are precisely the assets general factors already finance. A factor can hire an engineer or adopt the checklist through one process change. At 1.1% contribution per turn, the business needs large debt facilities and 200m–500m PLN annual volume; the conservative founder value still fails. One collected invoice validates assignment, not underwriting advantage or funding access.

**Advisory score: 58/100.** Not a validation score.

- Control point: Tier 1 per purchased invoice, Tier 2/3 business power. Strongest copy risk: a general factor adds technical review and uses cheaper capital.
- Founder Wealth Gate: fail. Conservative founder equity is 8.44m PLN and relies on later distributions; volume, debt and ownership are aggressive.
- Reachability Gate: pass for one purchased/collected invoice inside 35k PLN, but this does not compensate.
- Fatal objections: mature market, thin spread, capital scale, warehouse dependency, anti-assignment and no demonstrated loss advantage.

## Recommendation

**kill.** Do not pivot into factoring brokerage, another installation vertical or a larger fee assumption.
