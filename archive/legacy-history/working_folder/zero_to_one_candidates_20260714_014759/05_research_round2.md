# Research Ledger — Generator Pass 2

Research was performed on 2026-07-14 using three independent generation/research workstreams plus primary-agent synthesis. Sources establish legal mechanisms, named prospect pools, infrastructure availability or competitive context; prices and conversion rates remain hypotheses until paid proof. No outreach occurred.

## Rights and reusable online assets

- [ZAiKS explanation of synchronization clearance](https://akademia.zaiks.org.pl/wiedza/muzyka-w-swiecie-influencerow-i-postow-sponsorowanych-a-prawo-autorskie): synchronization normally needs separate permission from the composer/publisher and performer/label; ZAiKS does not issue the complete permission. This supports the clearance-friction insight, not demand or price.
- [Polish Copyright Act, official consolidated text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2021/1062/text.html) and [EUIPO Copyright Knowledge Centre](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/copyright-knowledge-centre): legal foundation for assignments/licences. Specialist counsel must still verify every mandate and chain of title.
- [Video Games Europe 2024 key facts](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/VGE-2024-KF-2024.pdf): approximately 6,000 European game studios; a broad upper-bound prospect universe, not 6,000 proven sync buyers.
- [EGDF membership/data](https://www.egdf.eu/category/data-studies/egdf-members/): approximately 2,500 represented studios. [KIPA](https://kipa.pl/kipa/o-nas/) states 170+ Polish audiovisual entities. [ZPAV](https://zpav.pl/onas/index.php) provides a Polish rights-holder starting universe.
- [Epidemic Sound business offer](https://www.epidemicsound.com/business/): evidence of a scaled incumbent and bundled licensing competition.
- [MEDICA directory](https://www.medica-tradefair.com/vis/v1/en/directory/w?lang_code=en): 4,821 exhibitors in the cited 2025 directory. [MedTech Europe company data](https://www.medtecheurope.org/datahub/employment-companies/) reports 38,000+ European medtech companies, about 90% SMEs. Neither source proves demand for synthetic voices or animation.
- [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en) and [European Commission Article 50 guidance process](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-opens-consultation-draft-guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations): transparency timing may increase provenance diligence but does not create exclusivity or a mandated procurement category.
- [ElevenLabs Voice Library](https://elevenlabs.io/voice-library) and [BioRender](https://www.biorender.com/): incumbent/copy-risk anchors for voice and medical visual content.
- [CPHI Europe exhibitor facts](https://www.cphi.com/europe/exhibit/why-exhibit/) and [MedTech Europe industry facts](https://www.medtecheurope.org/about-the-industry/facts-figures/): broad named-prospect and sector context for reusable medical animation. They do not validate 100,000 PLN annual contract value.
- [EUIPO designs](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/designs), [design licensing/management](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/designs/after-applying/manage-my-application) and [modernised EU design framework](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/cs/news/the-modernised-eu-design-legal-framework-is-now-fully-applicable): registrations can be licensed, but individual designs can be designed around.

## Controlled industrial assets and capacity

- Poland's Chief Veterinary Inspectorate lists [approved dairy establishments](https://zywnosc.wetgiw.gov.pl/spi/zatw/index.php?lng=0&sekcja=10) and [approved meat establishments](https://zywnosc.wetgiw.gov.pl/spi/zatw/index.php?lng=0&sekcja=1000). The cited registers contain 691 dairy and 1,684 meat establishments; only an estimated 250–400 larger plants with suitable sludge are plausible first prospects, and that qualification remains to be proven.
- [GUS Local Data Bank metadata](https://bdl.stat.gov.pl/bdl/metadane/cechy/1704) confirms official statistics for industrial wastewater plants and industrial-treatment sludge. It supports the existence of the problem, not the serviceable count.
- [KUGLER mobile screw-press rental](https://www.kugler-gmbh.de/en/miete/schneckenpressen-und-eindicker/) confirms rental/hire-purchase/pilot availability. A [current used-unit listing](https://www.werktuigen.com/ecs%2Beich%2Bmaschinenbau%2Bgmbh-vs20%2Fs4c/wt-209-73632) showed an €8,500 compact unit; it is not a binding quote and may exclude transport, commissioning and fit-for-feedstock requirements.
- An [official Polish procurement notice](https://ezamowienia.gov.pl/mo-client-board/bzp/notice-details/id/08de478a-ae85-8691-d6d3-f5000162976e) explicitly required a mobile press/station during a fixed-press replacement, demonstrating a real temporary-use case.
- [Data Center Map Poland](https://www.datacentermap.com/poland/) names 110 facilities operated by 67 organizations. [UKANA Rental](https://www.ukanarental.com/) advertises 0–8 MVA rental equipment and test engineers. These support technical availability, not unbundled customer access.
- [URE's 2024 activity report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/15446/Sprawozdanie2024.pdf) counted 1,118 A-tariff, 55,422 B-tariff and 1,621,171 C-tariff points. [PGE Dystrybucja's 2026 tariff](https://pgedystrybucja.pl/content/download/15f0a79cad0e85c168cd6b8430b4a2e9/file/tekst-jednolity-od-01.02.2026_taryfa-pge-dystrybucja-na-rok-2026.pdf?contentId=27274&inLanguage=pol-PL&version=3) documents reactive-energy charging. These are an upper-bound meter universe; the count with large, technically fixable charges is unknown.
- [PWEA member directory](https://www.psew.pl/czlonkowie/) and [URE renewable-installation data](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/oze/potencjal-krajowy-oze/8108%2CInstalacje-odnawialnych-zrodel-energii-stan-na-30-wrzesnia-2025-r.html) provide renewable operator/developer starting lists.
- [POLMED member directory](https://polmed.org.pl/czlonkowie/), [ISO 11135](https://www.iso.org/standard/70849.html), [STERIS Venlo](https://www.steris-ast.com/our-locations/venlo-the-netherlands) and a [live EO batch reference](https://measurlabs.com/products/ethylene-oxide-sterilization-and-sterilization-residues/) establish supply/prospect context but no evidence that a new intermediary can reserve and resell hard capacity.
- [Polish Chamber of Chemical Industry members](https://pipc.org.pl/en/members/) and [CBRE ADR warehouse overview](https://www.cbre.pl/insights/articles/magazyny-adr) support the ADR prospect and operating constraints. The wholesale-rate assumption is unverified.

## Explicit competitive exclusions

- Used perpetual licences: [usedSoft](https://www.usedsoft.com/de/), [Forscope](https://www.forscope.ro/autocad-lt) and [PREO](https://www.preo-ag.com/en) demonstrate mature European competition and trust/history advantages.
- Medical exams/OSCE: [EgzaminLEK](https://imsc.cm-uj.krakow.pl/patronages/egzaminlek/), [LEPoLEK](https://lepolek.pl/platforma/o-nas), [OpenLEK](https://www.openlek.pl/), [OSCE.ai](https://osce.ai/pricing/), [OSCEmed](https://www.oscemed.com/) and [OSCELab](https://oscelab.com/en/oscelab) make a generic question-bank or OSCE simulator non-zero-to-one.
- Repair marketplace: the [Council of the EU right-to-repair overview](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/right-to-repair-products/) and [Directive (EU) 2024/1799](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1799) describe an EU online repair platform expected in 2027, weakening a generic directory/marketplace wedge.

## Research discipline carried forward

All quoted unit economics are underwriting hypotheses. A finalist must distinguish a named upper-bound directory from qualified reachable buyers; require a deposit or paid engagement before scaling; obtain specialist legal review before acquiring rights; and kill the shape if its control point is revocable, its first contribution margin is negative, or its conservative wealth case needs heroic penetration.
