# Finalist: Owned Reactive-Power Savings Contracts

## Thesis and founder fit

Install and retain ownership of remotely metered reactive-power compensation cabinets at Polish SME sites, charging a fixed share of verified avoided reactive-energy fees for five years. An online bill screener and installer-originated contract clause identify sites before competitors; company-owned hardware, telemetry and cash-flow contracts turn a copied electrical fix into a financed portfolio. The founder's parents' installation business provides technical learning and a real first distribution edge, but paid proof must come from an unrelated customer. The founder can underwrite bills, build software and manage contracts beside employment while licensed electricians install and service equipment.

## Customer, pain, wedge and insight

The first payer is a B/C-tariff SME—such as a cold store, workshop, food plant or retail site—with at least 3,000 PLN/month of recurring, technically fixable reactive-energy charges. Its workaround is paying the bill, buying a cabinet, or requesting an electrician's one-off quote. Many SMEs do not want to diagnose, fund or monitor a technical asset whose savings they distrust.

The wedge is not a report. The customer uploads 12 bills, receives a conservative savings band, and signs a five-year no-upfront contract. The company installs its cabinet, meters performance and invoices an agreed share of verified avoided fees. If savings do not occur, the fee falls and the removable asset can be redeployed.

The commercial insight is that hardware vendors sell equipment while electricity suppliers monetize inattention. A savings principal can acquire long-lived cash flows by combining bill discovery, financing, installation and continuous verification. The offer must avoid regulated energy-supply claims and must be reviewed by Polish energy/electrical counsel.

## Beachhead, first acquisition and control point

Beachhead: independently owned Warsaw/Mazovia and Greater Poland SMEs reachable by licensed electrical installers, starting with cold storage, fabrication, food processing and retail sites. URE counted 1,118 A-, 55,422 B- and 1,621,171 C-tariff points at end-2024. This is an upper bound; the decisive first research asset is a consented sample of 200 bills to measure how many have ≥24,000 PLN/year of fixable charges.

First-customer mechanism: add a bill-scan and savings-share option to the family installer's quoting workflow, use that channel only to learn and source bill owners, and require the first paid contract to be an unrelated business. Thereafter, pay independent electricians a fixed activation bounty only after two confirming bills; publish a self-serve bill screener for direct acquisition.

The control point is Tier 1: company title to the serial-numbered cabinet; a five-year site agreement granting access, telemetry and a share of measured savings; remote measurement history; and the right to remove/redeploy on default. Before installation, a customer contract and supplier quote reserve the exact asset. Strongest copy risk: electrical contractors, cabinet manufacturers or ESCOs adopt the same financing contract with lower capital cost and established trust.

## Unit economics, distribution moat and compounding

- Target site: 36,000 PLN historic annual reactive charges; ≥80% measured reduction = 28,800 PLN saved; company receives 55% = 15,840 PLN/year; customer keeps 12,960 PLN with no capex.
- Cabinet, installation, telemetry and commissioning hypothesis: 15,000 PLN; annual monitoring/service 2,000 PLN; first-year gross contribution 13,840 PLN and hardware cash payback approximately 13 months before CAC/finance.
- CAC hypothesis: 2,000 PLN for installer-originated activation; payback within two customer invoices. Direct/search CAC cap is 4,000 PLN.
- Portfolio gross margin after service and hardware depreciation target: 67%; EBITDA margin at scale 40% after sales, underwriting, bad debt and central operations.
- Compounding: each retained cabinet produces contracted cash flow; bill-to-savings models improve qualification; installer bounties create distribution; remote evidence lowers sales friction and supports equipment finance. Hardware alone is copyable, but an installed contracted portfolio cannot be copied by one vendor purchase.
- Expansion: four SME verticals in two regions → national installer channel → adjacent power-quality assets only after the same bill/contract economics are proven.

Founder net worth is equity value plus cash actually distributed, with no retained-cash double count. Conservative and expected employment-period after-tax distributable cash is 95% reinvested and no more than 5% optionally distributed; strong growth reinvests 100%.

Five hundred sites are 0.03% of the 1.68m A/B/C upper-bound points but the eligible subset is unknown. The conservative gate passes only if the 200-bill audit finds at least 2% with ≥24,000 PLN annual fixable charges (implying a broad pool on the order of 33,000 points before further filters), achieved site payback is ≤18 months, and installer/direct acquisition can access 500 without heroic share. Until those facts are observed, the arithmetic passes but the evidence is conditional.

## Reinvestment and capital tranches

1. Before paid proof, commit at most 38,000 PLN: 4,000 PLN legal/electrical review, 3,000 PLN bill screener/telemetry prototype, ≤4,000 PLN acquisition and ≤27,000 PLN only against a signed independent customer contract for cabinet, installation and contingency. No speculative fleet.
2. Spend beyond 40,000 PLN only after the first site delivers two confirming bills, forecast error ≤20%, positive contribution and enforceable payment/access.
3. Deploy the remainder of the original 100,000 PLN only after three unrelated paying sites, at least 12 combined site-months, no safety failure and a documented installer/direct CAC channel.
4. Family capital is excluded until repeat demand, cohort payback and an asset-finance scaling case are documented. Asset finance is sought only against installed contracts, never to speculate on equipment.

## Reachability and 180-day paid-proof plan

- Days 1–21: specialist counsel/electrical engineer validates tariff logic, contract, measurement and insurance; build the bill parser and screen consented historical family-business bills plus public volunteers, without contacting new prospects.
- Days 22–45: identify at least four eligible unrelated SMEs from a 200-bill sample; obtain two cabinet/installation quotes and fix a serial-number/telemetry spec.
- Days 46–90: after outreach authorization, offer the no-upfront savings share through the family channel and a 50-company named list; customer signs before equipment order.
- Days 91–140: licensed contractor installs one cabinet at an unrelated site; baseline and telemetry locked.
- Days 141–180: obtain two supplier bills showing ≥80% of forecast saving; invoice the contractual share. Paid proof is the first collected savings-share invoice, not a letter of interest.

This phase remains within five founder hours/day because the licensed contractor performs site work. Reachability passes if the first independent contract is signed and one invoice is collected within 180 days at ≤38,000 PLN exposure.

## Critical dependency map and kill criteria

| Dependency | Proof before scale | Kill condition |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible pain density | 200 real bills with consent | <2% show ≥24k PLN/year technically fixable charges |
| Savings accuracy | Two post-install bills and telemetry | Savings <80% of forecast or baseline can be gamed |
| Asset payback | Full landed cost and collected fee | Unlevered hardware payback >18 months or first-year gross contribution <10k PLN |
| Contract control | Counsel-approved title/access/measurement/default terms | Customer can keep benefit while terminating fee/access without make-whole |
| Safety/operability | Licensed install, protection design, insurer acceptance | Founder must perform site work; material fire/grid/liability exclusion |
| Distribution | Three unrelated payers; installer/direct CAC ≤4k PLN | Family channel cannot generalize and independent electricians will not refer |
| Finance | Indicative asset-backed facility after ≥20 proven sites | Scale requires venture equity or personal guarantees before cohort proof |
| Copy defence | Contracted installed base and bill-model advantage | OEM/ESCO matches economics before 20 sites and CAC rises above one-year gross contribution |

## Full-time transition and failure recovery

The founder remains employed until at least ten unrelated paying sites or equivalent contracted recurring demand, positive cohort contribution, forecast accuracy, a repeatable acquisition channel and 12 months of personal runway. If the model fails, removable cabinets, telemetry, tariff parser, bill classifier and electrical-contract templates are reusable or saleable. Do not pivot into generic electrical consulting, bill audits or equipment resale.
