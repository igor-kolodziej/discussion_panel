## Strongest Copy Risk

A large clinical-data broker, CRO, sleep-device company, or medtech vendor can copy the pattern by contracting with other sleep labs after the market is proven. They cannot copy the exact signed mandates, but they can pursue parallel supply if SleepVault does not move quickly to become the default private-lab monetization route across Poland/CEE.

## Strongest Objections / Caveats

- **Legal path may still be narrower than the commercial promise.** Pseudonymised PSG/HST data remains personal data if re-identification is possible; secure processing, data minimisation, HDAB-style controls, and controller/processor structure matter.
- **Buyer full revenue may lag.** Reservation deposits are plausible inside 2 months; full six-figure access may require legal review, procurement, and data-quality checks.
- **Lab quality is variable.** Archives may have bad exports, missing scorer labels, proprietary formats, weak metadata, or inconsistent device configurations.
- **EHDS may commoditize access later.** The defense is not "health data exists"; it is exclusive preparation mandates, provenance QA, local supply relationships, and buyer-specific validation packaging.
- **Trust still matters.** The founder must use legal/DPO and sleep-clinical contractors to avoid looking like a data scavenger.

## Profitability / CAC / Payback View

The economics are credible. A POC budget under 100,000 PLN can secure several mandates, legal templates, sample inventory, and buyer deposits. CAC is manageable because the buyer universe is narrow, public, and high-value: sleep-AI, CPAP, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, wearable, and clinical-validation teams. Payback can be fast if one buyer pays a 10,000-25,000 PLN reservation deposit during the same period that labs sign mandates, and a single 40,000-120,000 PLN feasibility package can repay much of the POC cost. Retention and repeatability are plausible because buyers need additional cohorts, device diversity, failure cases, labels, demographics, and regional validation.
