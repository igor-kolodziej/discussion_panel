Evaluate this idea.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist with neuroscience, data science, and business-analysis background; unknown solo founder; max 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; part-time start; no celebrity, privileged introductions, or influencer distribution.

Idea: SleepVault PSG Archive Rights Bank.

SleepVault is a Poland/CEE biosignal data-rights company that secures exclusive commercial-readiness options over named archived raw polysomnography and home sleep test recordings from small, retiring, or under-monetized sleep labs, then turns legally cleared archives into a secure validation benchmark for sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, wearable, and digital-health companies.

Buyer: EU/US sleep-AI, oximetry/PPG, wearable, respiratory diagnostics, and medtech companies that need independent PSG comparison data before certification, investor diligence, clinical validation design, or enterprise sale.

Pain: public sleep datasets are useful but overused, demographically imperfect, and not always commercially or regionally suitable. Companies claiming sleep staging, apnea detection, arousal detection, or respiratory-event estimation need raw reference signals and scored labels from real clinical workflows, but negotiating one-off hospital data access is slow.

Control point: signed exclusive commercial-readiness options over named archives at specific sleep labs, for example: Lab A, 2016-2024, 1,400 EDF nights, consent/legal status to be reviewed, metadata inventory, no competing commercial data option for 12 months. The founder does not buy anonymous generic data; he controls a queue of named archives and their cleanup/provenance rights before competitors.

2-month POC under 100,000 PLN: spend 15-30k PLN on lawyer/DPO route memo and option templates; pay 3-8k PLN option fees to 4-8 small labs for 90-180 day exclusivity over named archives; inventory 300-800 redacted nights locally/securely without uncontrolled raw export; obtain 2-3 buyer LOIs or refundable inspection deposits of 5-15k PLN for a secure sample data room after legal clearance. Kill if no lab signs exclusivity or no buyer deposits.

Important caveat: pseudonymized PSG/HSAT data should be treated as personal health data unless legal/DPO review supports a narrower route. The POC is not claiming unrestricted raw data resale. It proves signed archive options, redacted inventory, legal route, and buyer deposits for controlled inspection.

Economics: option cost 3-8k PLN per lab plus legal/cleanup; first benchmark access priced 40-120k PLN per buyer for secure evaluation, or 150-400k PLN for broader licensed data rights once legal route is proven. Repeat revenue comes from additional buyers, new archives, rescoring, and EU validation packages.

Why not easy to copy: the asset is not equipment or a vendor relationship; it is fragmented named archives with local provenance, device heterogeneity, scorer labels, and legal-chain documentation. Once optioned, the same archive is unavailable. Large medtech vendors can negotiate hospital studies, but they are slow and conflicted; broad data brokers lack sleep-lab relationships and signal cleanup depth.
