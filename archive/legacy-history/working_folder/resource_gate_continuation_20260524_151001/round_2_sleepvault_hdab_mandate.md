# Round 2 Pivot: SleepVault HDAB-Ready Sleep Archive Mandates

## Exact Scored Idea Wording

**SleepVault** secures exclusive 180-day data-holder mandates over named Polish/CEE private sleep-lab PSG/HST archives, then turns those archives into HDAB/GDPR-ready, secure-enclave validation cohorts for sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, CPAP, and wearable companies. The company does not begin by selling raw patient data. It first buys control over the commercial-readiness workflow: signed exclusive mandates from data holders, metadata inventory, provenance/label QA, DPO/lawyer route memo, and buyer-paid reservation deposits for secure feasibility review.

## Why This Is A Material Pivot From F1

F1 scored 88 because the archive option was real, but the legal right to commercial secondary use was under-specified. This version makes the first control point narrower and more provable:

- **Supply-side control:** exclusive mandate over named archives from the data holder, including archive inventory, commercial-readiness preparation, buyer introduction, and revenue-share terms.
- **Legal route control:** no raw export before legal clearance; the POC produces a DPO/lawyer memo and secure-processing/HDAB-style route instead of assuming anonymisation solves everything.
- **Demand-side proof:** refundable buyer reservation or inspection deposits tied to a named cohort feasibility room, not generic LOIs.
- **Kill path:** if labs will not sign mandates or buyers will not deposit against metadata-only feasibility, the idea is dead before expensive extraction.

## Buyer

EU/US sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, CPAP, digital-health, clinical-trial tooling, and wearable companies that need independent, EU-relevant reference data before certification planning, investor diligence, clinical-validation design, enterprise procurement, or strategic partnership.

## Beachhead

Adult obstructive sleep apnea and sleep staging validation cohorts from private Polish/CEE PSG and home sleep test labs using mainstream devices and human scorer labels.

This is narrow enough to dominate because the first buyer question is not "all sleep data"; it is: "Can my algorithm survive comparison against real clinical PSG/HST records from multiple local devices and scorers?"

## Hidden Insight

Most observers think the scarce asset is a big hospital dataset. The more actionable scarce asset is the **exclusive preparation mandate** over messy, legally retained private-lab archives before they enter any broad EHDS catalog or get cleaned by a better-funded data intermediary. EHDS increases demand for prepared health data, but private sleep labs do not automatically become data vendors. The missing commercial layer is a founder who can cheaply reserve named archives, clean the metadata, document provenance, and route buyer demand into lawful secure access.

## Primary Control Point

Signed exclusive commercial-readiness mandates over named sleep-lab archives:

> Lab A, 2017-2025, approximately 1,200 PSG/HST nights, named devices, scorer labels, metadata fields, no competing commercial-readiness or data-buyer mandate for 180 days, small option fee plus revenue share, no raw export before DPO/legal approval.

The mandate is not a generic partnership. It is an assigned right/control over a named asset workflow: inventory, legal-readiness preparation, buyer reservation, and revenue share around that archive.

## 2-Month POC Under 100,000 PLN

Budget:

- 20,000-30,000 PLN: Polish/EU health-data lawyer + DPO memo and mandate templates.
- 15,000-35,000 PLN: option/mandate fees to 3-5 private labs.
- 10,000-20,000 PLN: technical inventory, EDF/HST metadata extraction, secure storage setup, redaction pipeline.
- 5,000-15,000 PLN: sleep physician/scorer QA sample review.
- 5,000-10,000 PLN: buyer outreach, secure data-room tooling, admin.

Proof to produce:

1. Two signed exclusive mandates naming archive scope, devices, approximate record count, and commercial-readiness rights.
2. Metadata-only inventory of at least 500 records without uncontrolled raw export.
3. DPO/lawyer memo naming lawful pathways, no-go paths, and required secure-processing/HDAB-style controls.
4. At least one refundable buyer reservation/inspection deposit of 10,000-25,000 PLN, or two 5,000-15,000 PLN deposits, for feasibility review of the named cohort after legal gates.
5. Written kill/no-kill decision based on whether the asset is legally and commercially usable.

## Why An Unknown Founder Can Secure It

Small private sleep labs are not data businesses. They face storage, migration, device-export, and legal uncertainty, but old archives may have dormant value. The founder offers:

- small upfront cash;
- no patient-care disruption;
- no raw export before legal review;
- a clear revenue share;
- technical extraction work the lab does not want to do;
- a way to prepare for EHDS-era secondary-use requests without hiring a data team.

The founder's neuroscience/data/business background is credible enough for archive inventory and buyer-facing technical diligence, while legal and sleep-scoring gaps are covered by fixed-budget contractors.

## Why This Is Not A Service / Report / Dashboard / Database Wrapper

The first asset is not a report template or a dashboard. It is a contractually controlled queue of named archives plus paid buyer reservations. The service work exists only to make the asset legally usable. If another company wants the same cohort, it cannot buy one vendor subscription or equipment kit; it must win different labs or wait out the exclusivity.

## Economics

- Lab option/mandate cost: 3,000-8,000 PLN per lab plus revenue share.
- Initial buyer reservation: 10,000-25,000 PLN refundable/creditable toward access.
- First paid secure benchmark access: 40,000-120,000 PLN per buyer for feasibility/validation package.
- Broader licensed or secure-enclave cohort access after legal clearance: 150,000-400,000 PLN per buyer/cohort.
- Gross margin can be high after legal/extraction setup because the same prepared cohort can serve several non-exclusive validation buyers if contract terms allow.

## Domination Path

1. Own the first named private sleep-lab archive mandates in Poland/CEE.
2. Standardize metadata, scorer-label provenance, device mapping, and legal readiness.
3. Become the default monetization route for small/retiring sleep labs.
4. Expand from OSA/sleep staging to arousal, PLM, REM behavior disorder, signal-failure cases, home sleep tests, CPAP response, and adjacent respiratory/cardiometabolic signals.
5. Use accumulated provenance and buyer requirements to build the strongest CEE sleep-validation supply map.

## Main Remaining Risks

- Legal route may still be slower or narrower than buyer expectations.
- Some labs may not have usable exports, labels, consent history, or metadata.
- Buyers may want full regulatory-grade validation, not feasibility data.
- EHDS/HDAB implementation may be too slow for immediate transaction revenue.
- A better-capitalized data intermediary can copy the category with other labs if SleepVault moves slowly.
