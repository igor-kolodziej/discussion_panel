# Strict Simulated Zero To One Review: F1 SleepVault PSG Archive Rights Bank

## Score

**88 / 100**

## Diagnosis

This is a real resource-control idea, not a disguised consulting desk. The strongest part is that it targets named, fragmented PSG archives before they become clean commercial data products, and those exact archives cannot be copied once optioned. The economics can work if even one archive becomes legally usable: low five-figure option costs can become six-figure validation/data-room revenue. The score is capped below 90 because the first 2-month proof secures archive options and metadata access, but may not yet prove a lawful commercial reuse route for EU health data; without that, the asset may be a locked vault rather than sellable inventory.

## Control Point Tier

**Tier B+ / conditional hard asset control.**

The control point is stronger than a vendor relationship or workflow because it can be expressed as signed exclusive options over named archives. It is weaker than Tier A because the commercially valuable right depends on legal basis, anonymization/pseudonymization feasibility, controller/processor structure, and lab documentation quality. An option over data that cannot lawfully be commercialized is not yet a full control point.

## Strongest Copy Risk

A better-capitalized sleep diagnostics vendor, CRO, or data broker could copy the pattern by signing archive-access deals with other labs once SleepVault proves demand. They cannot copy the exact optioned archives, but they can pursue parallel supply unless SleepVault rapidly becomes the default buyer/clearinghouse for retiring and under-monetized sleep labs across Poland/CEE.

## Strongest Objections / Caveats

- **Legal basis risk:** PSG files are health data. Commercial secondary use may require more than de-identification and a DPO memo; true anonymization of rich biosignal + metadata may be difficult.
- **Buyer qualification risk:** Medtech buyers may not accept the dataset for regulatory-grade validation unless scoring provenance, consent/legal basis, device calibration, and population metadata are very strong.
- **Sales-cycle risk:** A 5-15k PLN inspection deposit is plausible, but full 40-400k PLN data revenue may take longer than the POC cycle.
- **Supply quality risk:** Small labs may have inconsistent scoring, missing metadata, proprietary export constraints, poor EDF hygiene, or mixed device formats.
- **Repeatability risk:** Initial archive arbitrage is attractive; long-term retention depends on becoming a trusted legal/technical clearinghouse, not merely selling one dataset.

## Profitability / CAC / Payback View

The gross-margin profile is attractive if legal clearance works: option fees and extraction/QA costs are modest relative to possible validation-license pricing. CAC is tolerable because the buyer list is narrow and discoverable: sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, wearable, CPAP, oximetry, and clinical-validation teams can be reached directly. Payback can be proven fast only if buyer inspection deposits are collected during the same window as lab options. Retention is medium, not automatic: buyers may return for additional cohorts, failure cases, country-specific validation, rescoring, or benchmark updates, but this has to be deliberately productized.

## Best Move

**Narrow and test, not yet send as a >90 finalist.**

The best move is to reframe the POC around one legally easier, commercially specific sub-asset: a signed exclusive option over de-identified, scorer-labeled adult obstructive sleep apnea PSG/HST archives from private labs, plus a buyer-paid secure inspection deposit for non-export sample evaluation. The required proof is not “we found old PSG data”; it is: (1) a DPO/lawyer memo naming a lawful route, (2) at least two signed lab exclusivity options naming archive counts and systems, (3) a metadata-only inventory proving scale/quality, and (4) at least one refundable buyer deposit tied to the legal route.

## Threshold Decision

F1 is strong enough to keep in the finalist set, but **does not clear the simulated `>90` gate in its current form**. It should be pivoted into a narrower control-point proof where the legal right and buyer deposit are demonstrated together.
