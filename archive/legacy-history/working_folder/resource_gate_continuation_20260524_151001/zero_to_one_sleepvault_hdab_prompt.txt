Evaluate this idea.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist with neuroscience, data science, and business-analysis background; unknown solo founder; max 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; part-time start; no celebrity, privileged introductions, or influencer distribution.

Idea: SleepVault HDAB-Ready Sleep Archive Mandates.

SleepVault secures exclusive 180-day data-holder mandates over named Polish/CEE private sleep-lab PSG/HST archives, then turns those archives into HDAB/GDPR-ready, secure-enclave validation cohorts for sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, CPAP, and wearable companies. The company does not begin by selling raw patient data. It first buys control over the commercial-readiness workflow: signed exclusive mandates from data holders, metadata inventory, provenance/label QA, DPO/lawyer route memo, and buyer-paid reservation deposits for secure feasibility review.

Buyer: EU/US sleep-AI, respiratory diagnostics, oximetry/PPG, CPAP, digital-health, clinical-trial tooling, and wearable companies that need independent, EU-relevant reference data before certification planning, investor diligence, clinical-validation design, enterprise procurement, or strategic partnership.

Beachhead: adult obstructive sleep apnea and sleep-staging validation cohorts from private Polish/CEE PSG and home sleep test labs using mainstream devices and human scorer labels.

Primary control point: signed exclusive commercial-readiness mandates over named sleep-lab archives. Example: Lab A, 2017-2025, approximately 1,200 PSG/HST nights, named devices, scorer labels, metadata fields, no competing commercial-readiness or data-buyer mandate for 180 days, small option fee plus revenue share, no raw export before DPO/legal approval. The mandate controls inventory, legal-readiness preparation, buyer reservation, and revenue share around that archive.

2-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 20,000-30,000 PLN: Polish/EU health-data lawyer plus DPO memo and mandate templates.
- 15,000-35,000 PLN: option/mandate fees to 3-5 private labs.
- 10,000-20,000 PLN: technical inventory, EDF/HST metadata extraction, secure storage setup, redaction pipeline.
- 5,000-15,000 PLN: sleep physician/scorer QA sample review.
- 5,000-10,000 PLN: buyer outreach and secure data-room tooling.

Proof to produce within 2 months:
1. Two signed exclusive mandates naming archive scope, devices, approximate record count, and commercial-readiness rights.
2. Metadata-only inventory of at least 500 records without uncontrolled raw export.
3. DPO/lawyer memo naming lawful pathways, no-go paths, and required secure-processing/HDAB-style controls.
4. At least one refundable buyer reservation/inspection deposit of 10,000-25,000 PLN, or two 5,000-15,000 PLN deposits, for feasibility review of the named cohort after legal gates.
5. Written kill/no-kill decision based on whether the asset is legally and commercially usable.

Why an unknown founder can secure it: small private sleep labs are not data businesses. They face storage, migration, device-export, and legal uncertainty, but old archives may have dormant value. The founder offers small upfront cash, no patient-care disruption, no raw export before legal review, a revenue share, and technical extraction work the lab does not want to do.

Why not easy to copy: the asset is not equipment or a vendor relationship. It is a contractually controlled queue of named archives plus paid buyer reservations. If another company wants the same cohort, it cannot buy one vendor subscription or equipment kit; it must win different labs or wait out the exclusivity.

Economics: lab option/mandate cost 3,000-8,000 PLN per lab plus revenue share. Initial buyer reservation 10,000-25,000 PLN refundable/creditable toward access. First paid secure benchmark access 40,000-120,000 PLN per buyer. Broader licensed or secure-enclave cohort access after legal clearance 150,000-400,000 PLN per buyer/cohort. Gross margin can be high after legal/extraction setup because the same prepared cohort can serve several non-exclusive validation buyers if contract terms allow.

Rate this idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score. Cap the score below 85 if it lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, uniquely produced, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality.
