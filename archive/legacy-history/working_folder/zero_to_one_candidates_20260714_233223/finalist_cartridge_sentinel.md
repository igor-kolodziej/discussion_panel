# Zero-to-One Candidate: Cartridge Sentinel

## One-Sentence Thesis

Cartridge Sentinel licenses CIOP-PIB's protected chemical-breakthrough sensor and turns it into an OEM-ready, certifiable module that gives manufacturers of reusable respirators an objective end-of-service indicator, starting with ammonia cartridges used in industrial refrigeration and fertiliser handling.

## Founder Fit

**Evidence**

- The founder is a Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience and biosignal experience, 100,000 PLN of available capital, a maximum of five hours per day for business building, and a preference for quantitative products that can be proved before large spending.
- The CIOP-PIB project record says that the institute already built the sensor, an electronic device and housing, tested the complete arrangement for field of view, breathing resistance and leak-tightness, verified alarm behaviour in simulated working conditions, prepared technical documentation, and filed patent application P.442235.
- The first commercial proof can be an OEM-paid engineering evaluation using bench and shadow-mode tests. It does not require the founder to recruit patients, enter a clinic, manufacture a certified final respirator, or ask a worker to rely on an unapproved alarm.

**Hypotheses**

- The founder's signal-processing, experimental-design and data-product skills should transfer to calibration, compensation, test automation, failure analysis and technical OEM sales.
- A fractional chemical-sensor engineer and a PPE regulatory specialist can cover the founder's material-science and conformity-assessment gaps without becoming full-time co-founders before paid proof.
- CIOP-PIB will allow an unknown new company to contract a narrow exclusive evaluation option and, after paid OEM evidence, a field licence on terms below the founder's 100,000 PLN resource-control ceiling.

The founder should not personally certify the product, provide occupational-hygiene advice, or sell a finished protective device before the appropriate conformity path is complete. The initial company is the IP licensee, module developer and OEM integration partner; the final-PPE manufacturer remains responsible for the certified product placed on the market.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The initial paying customer is the product director, R&D lead or owner of a small or mid-sized European manufacturer of reusable half masks, full-face masks, filters or private-label respiratory PPE.

From that buyer's point of view:

- It sells cartridges whose safe service life changes with contaminant, concentration, humidity, temperature, breathing rate and prior use.
- A conservative fixed replacement schedule can discard usable cartridges; an aggressive schedule or reliance on smell can expose workers after breakthrough.
- Adding a credible end-of-service indicator could differentiate a cartridge family in distributor tenders and high-consequence applications, but the OEM does not want to fund a multi-year sensor-material research programme from scratch.
- The buyer cannot accept a clever prototype alone. It needs repeatable manufacturing, environmental compensation, connector compatibility, safe failure behaviour, documentation and a credible EU conformity route.
- The painful commercial event is a live product-line refresh, tender, customer escalation or planned certification cycle in which the OEM must decide whether a smart-cartridge feature is technically and economically viable.

**Sourced evidence**

- CIOP-PIB states that the project addressed harmful gases and vapours with low odour-detection thresholds and that safe cartridge use depends on knowledge of protective service time.
- CIOP-PIB reports that its prototype alarmed optically and acoustically at approximately 1 ppm ammonia in the reported tests, and that it created technical documentation intended to support industrial-scale transfer.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/425 requires respiratory PPE to maintain contaminant penetration at a level that is not prejudicial under foreseeable conditions of use. The regulation establishes the product-safety context; it does not create a specific legal mandate to include Cartridge Sentinel.

**Current workaround hypothesis**

OEMs and employers use change schedules, cartridge service-life tables or calculators, conservative discard intervals, smell or irritation reports, and product-specific administrative rules. The frequency, cost and failure rate of these workarounds require direct OEM discovery; the existence of a safety problem does not itself prove a separate smart-module budget.

## Product Wedge

The first product is a fixed-scope **Cartridge Sentinel OEM Evaluation Pack**, not a production respirator and not generic consulting.

For one cartridge and mask interface, the pack provides:

1. A licensed reference sensor and electronics assembly based on the CIOP-PIB technical package.
2. A mechanical interface concept that preserves the customer's existing facepiece and cartridge architecture as far as technically possible.
3. Bench response traces across a defined ammonia concentration, humidity and temperature matrix, performed by a competent contracted laboratory where required.
4. A shadow-mode demonstrator that records and alarms but is not used to extend the approved cartridge-change schedule.
5. A preliminary unit-cost model, failure-mode register and manufacturing plan.
6. A written regulatory-path workshop with a fractional PPE conformity specialist.
7. A go/no-go specification for a later certified development programme.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

**Evidence**

- CIOP-PIB has already carried out material, electronics, ergonomic, leak-tightness, breathing-resistance and simulated-use work and has filed a defined industrial-property application.
- CIOP-PIB says it created technical documentation and materials for domestic manufacturers, end users and labour inspectors, showing an explicit intention to move the result toward industry.
- The official UPRP publication identifies CIOP-PIB as the applicant for P.442235 and describes a concrete sensor construction using interdigitated gold-coated electrodes and an active polymer layer containing carbon nanotubes or reduced graphene oxide and polyaniline.

**Hypothesis**

The market gap is not another gas detector or occupational-safety report. It is the missing commercialisation layer between a Polish institute's protected prototype and a respirator OEM's certifiable product family:

- field-limited IP rights;
- reproducible sensor production;
- mask and cartridge adapter engineering;
- compensation and calibration data;
- conformity documentation;
- low-volume contract manufacturing;
- an OEM channel that pays for development before scale.

An OEM may prefer a licensed, pre-tested route if it shortens the product decision and creates a defensible differentiator. This is unproved. The first paid Evaluation Pack must establish willingness to pay before the founder exercises the full licence.

## Underused Momentum

- The research cost, initial prototype, technical documentation and Polish patent application already exist; the founder is not starting from a blank material-science programme.
- CIOP-PIB reports that the work was presented to manufacturers, end users and inspectors, but the public sources reviewed do not identify a commercial product based on P.442235.
- EU PPE rules make certification and documented protective performance unavoidable. This raises the burden, but it can also favour an OEM-ready evidence package over an unstructured prototype.
- Industrial refrigeration, fertiliser, chemical processing and maintenance already buy respiratory protection. Cartridge Sentinel attempts to capture an existing PPE product budget rather than create a consumer wellness budget.
- Contract electronics manufacturing and accredited gas-exposure testing allow the company to defer its own production and laboratory investment.

The strongest timing hypothesis is that a smaller OEM wants a smart-cartridge differentiator now and cannot obtain one cheaply from an incumbent. Public research does not establish the number of such OEMs or their development budgets.

## Beachhead Market

The beachhead is **European reusable-respirator OEMs and private-label PPE suppliers serving ammonia refrigeration, fertiliser and industrial chemical maintenance**, beginning with Polish and nearby CEE manufacturers that can make an engineering decision without a global headquarters cycle.

The first target account should have:

- an existing reusable mask and replaceable cartridge product family;
- internal product engineering but no proprietary chemical-sensor platform;
- access to a current conformity-assessment partner;
- a customer segment in which ammonia or another specifically validated gas creates a credible end-of-service problem;
- authority to buy a 24,000-36,000 PLN evaluation without committing to full certification;
- a product refresh, tender or key-account request within 12 months.

The initial use claim remains limited to the substances and conditions supported by the licensed technical file and new bench evidence. The company must not extrapolate the reported ammonia result to all organic vapours.

The primary control point is a **signed, field-limited exclusive evaluation option that converts into a licence to P.442235 and the non-public technical package**, combined with OEM-specific adapter, calibration and conformity assets created through paid work.

Within the first 30 days, the founder approaches the CIOP-PIB Centre for Promotion and Implementation, whose public remit includes supporting implementation and commercialisation of institute research. The requested sequence is:

5. **Pre-agreed licence economics:** maximum 45,000 PLN additional upfront fee, 4-6% royalty on licensed net module revenue or an agreed per-unit minimum, access to the technical package and inventor support, and a right to sublicense only through named certified OEM product programmes.
6. **Improvement rights:** the startup owns or exclusively controls its independently funded adapter designs, compensation algorithms, manufacturing-test software and customer-specific integrations; CIOP receives no automatic ownership beyond a narrowly negotiated research licence.

The option, licence, legal review, technical access and initial inventor support must fit within **92,000 PLN in total first-year rights/control cost** and within the founder's 100,000 PLN resource-control ceiling. The first 40,000 PLN proof tranche does not exercise the full licence.

**Territory discipline**

Public evidence reviewed establishes Polish application P.442235 and its A1 publication. It does not establish a granted Polish patent, an EP application, national validations or an enforceable EU patent family. Patent exclusivity must therefore be described as Polish and pending unless official register diligence proves more. Contractual know-how exclusivity can cover a wider field only to the extent CIOP owns and can control that know-how; it cannot stop independent foreign development.

The candidate is killed if CIOP will not document the territory, status, chain of title and prior rights, or if the necessary field is already licensed.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial distribution advantage is a narrow technical-sales package built around a real protected asset:

- a CIOP-sourced evidence file rather than a founder-made safety claim;
- one substance, one cartridge and one interface per evaluation;
- fixed price and scope;
- a bench-first conformity boundary;
- a low-capital way for a smaller OEM to decide whether to build a smart product line;
- local Polish engineering cost and direct access to the institute's commercialisation route.

Distribution can compound through:

- conversion of one product family into additional cartridges within the same OEM;
- private-label variants that reuse the sensor and test system;
- notified-body and laboratory familiarity with the evidence package;
- specialist PPE distributors bringing named end-user requests to their OEMs;
- an adapter and calibration library that makes later evaluations faster and more predictable.

No referral, notified-body, distributor or manufacturer relationship counts as controlled until it is signed and paid or contains a binding purchase commitment.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

### Initial advantage

- A specific protected Polish sensor construction and access to the institute's technical package.
- Reported complete-system and simulated-use work, reducing the first technical unknowns.
- A bench-first OEM offer that separates a paid feasibility decision from expensive certification.
- Reusable compensation, manufacturing-test and adapter software that the university patent alone does not provide.

### Expansion path

1. One ammonia cartridge and one reusable facepiece.
2. Additional facepieces from the same OEM using the same validated chemistry.
3. Other ammonia-focused OEMs and private-label families.
4. Additional gases or organic vapours only after customer-funded validation and claim review.
5. EU/CEE product families where patent, know-how, certification and freedom-to-operate permit.
6. An OEM calibration and manufacturing-evidence platform for several licensed sensor chemistries.

### Why expansion could be asymmetric

Cartridge Sentinel can move outward from one validated sensor into adjacent adapters and evidence packages while reusing its manufacturing and certification work. A broad gas-sensor vendor or respirator incumbent can move inward, but must still solve mask integration, environmental compensation, failure behaviour, documentation and conformity for the same use case.

The asymmetry remains modest until the startup owns several paid OEM integrations and manufacturing/calibration data. The patent application alone is not enough.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

- CIOP-PIB, the applicant and technical source, is in Warsaw and operates in the Polish occupational-safety system; this lowers diligence and inventor-access cost for the founder.
- The Polish application can create a domestic legal barrier if granted and maintained, but it does not establish EU exclusion rights.
- Polish engineering, laboratory and contract-manufacturing cost can make a narrow OEM evaluation economical for smaller CEE suppliers.
- Local-language documentation and access to Polish industrial refrigeration, fertiliser and maintenance users can create application knowledge before expanding westward.
- EU PPE rules create a common regulatory framework for later expansion, while each product and certification programme still requires exact conformity work.

The local defense is the combination of contract rights, institute know-how, low-cost productisation, calibration evidence and OEM workflow. It is not a claim that a Polish patent blocks independent EU competitors.

## Compounding Advantage

Each paid OEM programme can add:

- another validated cartridge and facepiece adapter;
- environmental response and drift curves;
- sensor manufacturing-yield and end-of-line-test data;
- a failure-mode and conformity evidence module;
- an installed calibration and firmware relationship;
- annual module fees and repeat purchase orders;
- permissioned, non-customer-identifying substance and hardware performance statistics.

Over time, the compounding asset becomes a **substance × environment × sorbent × cartridge geometry × sensor response × failure-mode graph** plus the software and manufacturing controls required to reproduce it. Worker health data are unnecessary and should not be collected in the initial model.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If OEM willingness to pay fails before the full licence:

- the option expires or is returned, capping IP loss;
- no certified product inventory exists;
- the founder retains a cleaned OEM prospect map, regulatory pathway memo, manufacturing quotations, test automation and knowledge of PPE procurement;
- generic information and founder-owned code can support later sensor-product diligence, but the founder must not use CIOP confidential know-how after rights end.

If the sensor works but certification economics fail:

- explore only customer-funded research-use or manufacturing-monitoring applications permitted by the licence;
- do not rebrand an uncertified safety component as wellness or sell it directly to workers;
- release the exclusive field if no compliant commercial route remains.

If the rights are unavailable, the founder does not substitute a generic gas-sensor consultancy. The control-point thesis has failed and the candidate is closed.
