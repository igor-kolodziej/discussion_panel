# Confirmed Business Idea Candidate

## Idea

**GigClaim Fleet Recovery Rail** is a worker-side system of record for algorithmically managed earnings. It begins as a prepaid, human-reviewed recovery service for Polish ride-hail fleets: detect disputed fares, bonuses, ratings, and account decisions; assemble authorized evidence; route internal platform appeals; and measure what is corrected, rejected, recovered, and how long review takes.

The business earns a per-covered-worker fee plus a percentage of cash actually recovered. Its strategic asset is not appeal text. It is a permissioned, cross-platform claim→reason→evidence→human-review→settlement dataset and the real-time human–AI interface that helps a worker or fleet understand a decision and supply the missing evidence.

## One-Sentence Thesis

One fleet aggregates hundreds of small algorithmic-work discrepancies that are uneconomic for workers to pursue individually, allowing an outcome-priced recovery service to acquire cross-platform decision and settlement data before it builds software or obtains platform cooperation.

## Founder Fit

- **Founder fact:** The founder is a Warsaw data scientist with AI/ML, analytics, behavioral-data, neuroscience, and business-analysis experience. The first work is data normalization, anomaly detection, evidence assembly, and human-facing explanation—not hardware, diagnosis, or litigation.
- **Founder fact:** The 180-day phase fits approximately 20–25 hours per week alongside corporate employment: founder-led sales, one capped weekly statement batch, asynchronous claim review, and bounded contractor support.
- **Founder fact:** The founder has no privileged introductions. Acquisition therefore starts with public fleet-partner pages and an offer whose value is measured in recovered cash and saved handling time.
- **Capability gap:** Polish privacy/labor counsel must approve the data, authorization, retention, and legal-service boundary. Multilingual review is contracted only after paid proof.

## Customer And Pain

The initial buyer is a Polish ride-hail fleet operator managing about 100–1,000 drivers across one or more platforms. The fleet wants correct weekly settlements, fewer worker escalations, less dispatcher time spent on screenshots and support tickets, and less vehicle downtime from unexplained account restrictions.

Today, workers send screenshots through messaging apps; a dispatcher compares statements, campaign terms, trip histories, and support responses manually; small discrepancies are abandoned; and the same failure pattern is rediscovered across workers. A fleet can aggregate this low-value work, but does not have a cross-platform claim history or evidence-quality model.

- **Sourced evidence:** [Bolt's Polish fleet page](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/fleet/) gives fleet owners worker-level earnings and performance statistics, establishing the buyer and a recurring data flow.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Directive (EU) 2024/2831](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L2831) covers algorithmic decisions affecting earnings, work assignment, ratings, and account access, and provides transparency, portability, human-oversight, explanation, and review rights.
- **Hypothesis:** A 300-worker fleet loses or spends at least 5,000 PLN monthly across recoverable discrepancies, idle vehicles, and staff handling. The paid pilot must measure this rather than assume it.
- **Buyer-conflict risk:** A fleet may reject a service that exposes its own settlement mistakes or empowers worker claims. The first sale must prove that at least one fleet sees correct recovery as retention and operational value.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

Sell a 30-day **Fleet Earnings Recovery Audit**:

1. The fleet signs a narrow routing mandate and data-processing terms; participating workers authorize specified data access and internal-platform appeal support under the counsel-approved legal basis.
2. The service ingests weekly statements, trip/campaign evidence, worker screenshots, and support history through permitted exports or forwarded files—never credential sharing or access-control bypass.
3. Software flags inconsistencies and assembles an evidence pack. A human reviewer approves every proposed claim.
4. The fleet or authorized worker submits through the platform's ordinary support or review channel. GigClaim tracks reason, response, correction, recovery, and elapsed time.
5. The buyer receives a recovery and handling-time report, plus unresolved categories and missing-evidence patterns.

The initial audit covers at most 50 workers and costs 5,000 PLN prepaid. It is credited against a 20% cash-recovery fee. GigClaim does not litigate, determine employment status, or promise legal representation. The customer controls every submission; matters requiring licensed practice are outside scope.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

Platforms optimize platform-side transactions and possess platform-side data. Fleets optimize vehicle utilization, worker onboarding, and settlements. Individual workers lack the time, data structure, and repeated experience to pursue small discrepancies. The unowned layer is a cross-platform portfolio of worker-side economic failures and the evidence/review outcome for each one.

The fleet intermediary is the overlooked buyer: it aggregates workers, weekly files, payment, and repeated support events. A manual service can be paid from recovered value before software exists. It can secure outcome rights directly from customers and workers without a platform partnership.

## Underused Momentum

- **Sourced evidence:** Directive (EU) 2024/2831 must be transposed by 2 December 2026. Article 9 covers transparency and portability of work-generated data, including ratings and reviews; Articles 10–11 require human oversight, reasons, and review for consequential decisions.
- **Sourced evidence:** [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20240718-1) found 3.0% of people aged 15–64 in participating countries performed paid platform work during the prior 12 months; Poland was included.
- **Sourced evidence:** Bolt publicly recruits Polish fleet owners and routes drivers toward [recommended fleet partners](https://bolt.eu/en-pl/support/articles/360001424593/), making the first buyer list observable.
- **Discipline:** The directive is momentum, not a first-sale dependency. The audit must create value through existing statements, GDPR access/export routes, and ordinary platform support before Polish implementation is complete.

## Beachhead Market

Polish ride-hail fleet partners with at least 100 active workers, two or more platform relationships, weekly settlements, and an owner or operations lead still handling disputes manually. Prioritize Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, Poznań, Łódź, and the Tri-City.

The first market is not individual workers. One fleet consolidates acquisition, authorization administration, weekly files, payment, and repeated outcomes. Delivery fleets are an adjacency only after the ride-hail claim ontology and economics work.

## Primary Control Point

The primary control point is a **contracted all-dispute routing rail**. For the contract term, the fleet routes covered earnings and account disputes through GigClaim. Participating workers provide a narrow, revocable authorization. The contract grants GigClaim rights to retain pseudonymized factual derivatives—claim type, evidence completeness, stated reason, decision, correction, recovery, and timing—while raw personal data remain purpose-limited and are deleted on schedule.

This is **Tier 2 at inception**: contractually routed workflow with compounding data and switching costs, but no statutory exclusivity and no control over platform decisions. It becomes defensible only if cross-platform closed outcomes and fleet history accumulate faster than an incumbent can recreate them.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

**Foundational resource needed:** authorization and rights to a recurring flow of claim, decision, and settlement outcomes across at least two platforms.

**Mechanism:** A paying fleet signs the routing mandate and data-processing agreement. Each participating worker completes the counsel-approved authorization flow. GigClaim uses customer-held statements, formal exports, emails, and user-provided evidence; it requires no platform API or partnership.

**Why an unknown founder can secure it:** The offer is narrow, outcome-linked, begins with a small paid audit, and reduces a process the buyer already performs. It does not require the buyer to accept a medical claim, replace a safety system, or migrate its whole platform.

**Proof within 12 months and 100,000 PLN:** three independent paying fleets; at least 500 opted-in workers; at least 100 closed reconciliation/claim outcomes; two platforms represented; signed derivative-data rights; and documented recovery and handling-time baselines.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

- **Pilot:** 5,000 PLN prepaid for up to 50 workers and 30 days, credited against a 20% fee on cash actually recovered.
- **Recurring hypothesis:** 20 PLN per covered worker per month plus 20% of recovered cash.
- **Expected mature 600-worker fleet:** 144,000 PLN annual base fee plus 60,000 PLN success fee if 300,000 PLN is recovered, for 204,000 PLN revenue.
- **Variable-cost hypothesis:** 45,000 PLN per 600-worker account for review labor, support, processing, storage, and language handling, producing 159,000 PLN contribution and a 78% contribution margin before central product, sales, security, and legal expense.
- **Value proof:** A paid audit must produce at least twice its fee in recovered cash plus conservatively valued handling-time savings. No fee is charged on unsupported or unresolved amounts.

### Distribution Moat

Public platform fleet pages, fleet websites, and recruitment activity expose the initial buyer universe. One fleet aggregates hundreds of users, so acquisition occurs per organization rather than per worker. Recovery reports can become referral artifacts for fleet accountants and operations managers. The business does not require a union, influencer, celebrity, or platform referral.

Distribution becomes defensible only when fleet mandates renew, worker authorization is high, and historical settlement outcomes create switching costs. A public lead list alone is not a moat.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. Polish ride-hail settlement and campaign discrepancy recovery.
2. Account-decision evidence: reason, evidence, human review, and outcome tracking.
3. Cross-platform worker ledger under explicit authorization.
4. Models predicting recoverability, missing evidence, and vehicle/worker downtime.
5. Real-time multilingual explanations and evidence prompts before submission.
6. Delivery-fleet and CEE expansion using the same decision/outcome schema with local legal adaptation.

The strongest copy risk is a fleet/payroll system or platform adding reconciliation and dispute tracking using its installed source data. GigClaim's defense is independent cross-platform outcomes, a fleet's historical claim record, and faster learning about which evidence changes human-review decisions.

## Local Adaptation Defense

Poland has a visible fleet-intermediary structure, multilingual worker populations, weekly settlement practices, and imminent implementation of an EU directive. GigClaim begins with Polish payout terminology, file formats, support routes, language needs, accounting reality, and counsel-reviewed authorization.

These create execution speed, not legal exclusivity. The control must compound before platforms or horizontal fleet systems adapt.

## Why This Could Compound

Every paid case improves statement/campaign parsers, the claim taxonomy, evidence-completeness checks, expected recovery and response-time estimates, multilingual explanations, and the benchmark of stated algorithmic reason versus human-review outcome. A fleet's historical ledger becomes costly to abandon.

The future lab gains advanced AI/ML capability, high-frequency human behavioral/economic data, real-time human–computer interface expertise, proprietary evaluation datasets, and distribution to algorithmically managed worker cohorts. It gains no biometric or medical data in this wedge.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder leaves corporate employment only after all are true:

- at least five independent paying fleets or contracted recurring demand covering 2,000 workers;
- positive contribution economics across three paid deliveries;
- at least one renewal or six months of contracted recurring fees;
- a documented outbound or referral channel with credible acquisition economics;

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

If recovery fees are weak but reconciliation saves time, the workflow may narrow to fleet payout reconciliation. If fleets reject reusable derivatives, the parsers and evidence workflow remain usable but the compounding thesis fails and GigClaim should not scale as proposed.

Reusable future-lab assets include multilingual human–AI explanation patterns, an algorithmic-decision evidence ontology, authorized data-portability tooling, anomaly-detection models, secure behavioral-data pipelines, and an evidence-completeness versus human-review benchmark.

## Validation Plan

Before any serious capital:

1. Confirm with counsel that the non-legal recovery workflow, data basis, worker authorization, and derivative rights are viable.
2. Obtain one non-refundable 5,000 PLN audit from a fleet that knowingly permits measurement of fleet-caused as well as platform-caused errors.
3. Measure invited-worker authorization, claim incidence, evidence completeness, handling hours, platform response, corrected amounts, time saved, and gross contribution.
4. Require one recurring routing mandate and signed derivative rights before automating a second platform.
5. Validate account size and acquisition by obtaining three independent paid customers within 180 days.
6. Freeze any proposed business revision and restart advisory, simulated, and real-chat validation if customer, price, data rights, buyer, or control point changes.

## Kill Criteria

- No 5,000 PLN paid audit by day 120 after 80 qualified approaches.
- Fewer than two independent paying fleets by day 180.
- Counsel finds the wedge requires licensed representation or an unlawful data basis that cannot be removed without destroying value.
- Fewer than 50% of invited workers authorize the necessary flow.
- Recovered cash plus conservative handling-time savings is less than twice the pilot fee.
- Fewer than 20 closed outcomes or no repeated claim category after 500 worker-months.
- Buyers refuse both routing and reusable pseudonymized outcome rights.
- Fleets consistently reject visibility into fleet-caused errors or use the product to suppress valid worker claims.
- A platform/fleet incumbent offers equivalent cross-platform recovery and historical outcomes before three recurring contracts.
