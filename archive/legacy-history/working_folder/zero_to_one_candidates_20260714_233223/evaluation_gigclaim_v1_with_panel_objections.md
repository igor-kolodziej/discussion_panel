# Zero-to-One Evaluation: GigClaim Fleet Recovery Rail v1 After Panel

Advisory evaluation of the exact frozen candidate and independent-panel objections. This is not a simulated or real validation score.

## Diagnosis

The independent panel exposed a structural economic-buyer failure that the first advisory pass underweighted. The service charges a fleet while a large share of recovered cash belongs to workers; in the mature example the fleet pays 204,000 PLN against 300,000 PLN recovered, so the proposal needs very large additional fleet-owned savings to be rational. Because the same conflict also weakens authorization, all-dispute routing, renewal, data rights, margins, and the expected wealth case, it is not a pricing-detail objection. The aggregation and closed-outcome insight remains valuable, but the buyer, value capture, or controlled transaction must change materially.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- **Exact candidate:** frozen SHA-256 `19d41629bcb396891457dae1bea4e7d0e24c843fe00ac34f287e9ff0c06d80b9`.
- **Independent verdict:** Judge A scored 56 and Judge B scored 60; official lower score 56.
- **Shared fatal objection:** both judges independently concluded that the fleet is not yet a rational payer because workers receive much of the recovery and fleet-caused errors may be exposed.
- **Sourced evidence:** Directive rights and fleet data availability support technical/legal momentum but do not prove fleet-owned economic value or willingness to pay.
- **Candidate hypothesis rejected as expected-success evidence:** 500 PLN recovered per worker, 20 PLN monthly base, 20% recovery fee, 78% gross margin, and 50 fleets averaging 600 workers cannot simultaneously be treated as credible without buyer-owned value evidence.

## Overall Score

**60/100.** Weighted component total is 120 across 20 weight points: `10 × (120 / 20) = 60`. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 in design, Tier 3 until contracted.** A recurring routing mandate and outcome rights could create access and switching costs, but the economic-buyer conflict makes the mandate unlikely to be durable. The strongest copy risk is an installed fleet/payroll system adding reconciliation and dispute tracking using source data and existing trust.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The arithmetic is complete, debt is deducted, ownership is stated, and reinvestment is correct. The expected-success operating case is not credible because it stacks an unproved rational payer, 30,000 opted-in workers, 15m PLN recovered, high service margins, and low capital needs. The conservative case produces only 331,000 PLN founder equity, so the wealth result is not robust to normal underperformance.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** A 5,000 PLN audit can be sold and delivered manually inside 180 days, 18,000 PLN pre-proof risk, 28,000 PLN total early exposure, and 25 hours per week. That proves only reachability of an experiment, not recurring demand or the control point.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead remains compact and the algorithmic-management data model can expand to delivery and CEE. Yet no adjacency fixes a first buyer who may not retain recovered value. Platforms and fleet/payroll incumbents control better source access and can improve native review. The local advantage is operational and temporary.

## Fatal Objections

1. The selected fleet payer may not own enough of the recovery or savings to justify both the recurring worker fee and recovery fee.
2. Fleets may resist exposing fleet-caused errors, creating a conflict with valid worker claims and the proposed all-dispute mandate.
3. Human review, multilingual evidence, authorization, and platform follow-up make the modeled service margins doubtful.
4. Revocable worker authorization and data-protection limits weaken reusable outcome rights.
5. The expected wealth case requires all of the above uncertainties to resolve favorably at once.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Aggregate a repeated portfolio of individually uneconomic human–algorithm decision disputes, route them through an outcome-priced workflow, and retain closed-loop reason→evidence→human-review→economic-outcome data without depending on the algorithm owner.
