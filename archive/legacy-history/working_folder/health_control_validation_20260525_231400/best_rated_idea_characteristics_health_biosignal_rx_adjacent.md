# Best-Rated Idea Characteristics: Health / Genetics / Biosignal / Rx-Adjacent Focus

Date: 2026-05-26

## Pattern Across The Best Scores

The best-rated ideas are not apps, reports, coaching services, dashboards, or drug marketplaces. They are small succession/acquisition plays where the founder can control a specific live asset within 60 days:

- titled inventory with serial/SKU specificity;
- signed customer/reorder book;
- phone/domain/mailbox/call-routing handoff;
- regulated transaction entity or payment flow;
- prepaid orders, retainers, deposits, or settlement evidence;
- capacity that is booked and paid before closing;
- old-owner/operator handoff that reduces CAC;
- exact workflow evidence: pulled order, fulfilled order, accepted sample, completed slot, paid invoice.

Ideas score lower when they depend on:

- patient trust without a transferable book;
- partner-owned regulated rails;
- generic coordination;
- clinical advice, diagnosis, prescribing, or interpretation;
- health-data resale/reuse;
- routes with high liability and low density;
- payers that might agree in theory but will not prepay.

## Genetics / Molecular Diagnostics

Best shapes:

1. Oncology FFPE block/slide retrieval or archive custody only if the founder secures a specific archive/custody right, not generic courier work.
2. Court-grade DNA kinship collection-point roll-up, where control is the chain-of-custody kit flow plus local lawyer/referral/payment book.
3. Molecular lab qPCR/NGS line-down donor bank, where control is titled tested hardware/spares plus prepaid emergency loan/resale demand.

Gate lesson:

Specimen logistics alone already failed at `73` because route density, liability, protocol restrictions, and generic courier substitutes were too strong. Genetics ideas need archive rights, collection-point succession, or titled instrument/spare ownership.

## EEG / Biosignals

Best shapes:

1. Legacy EEG/PSG consumable and reorder-book buyout: titled exact-SKU stock plus retiring reseller/lab reorder book, phone/domain/mailbox, compatibility map, and prepaid lab orders.
2. TMS/neurostimulation coil spare bank: titled tested spare coil/module plus emergency retainers from private clinics.
3. EEG/EMG line-down loaner book only if there is an acquired active service-ticket book and prepaid retainers.

Gate lesson:

Generic device rental or archive rescue scores too low. The strong version looks like an industrial spare-parts business for biosignal clinics: exact connector/cable/electrode/cap scarcity, inherited reorder flow, and prepaid emergency kits.

## 50-60 / Soon-Elderly Chronic Care

Best shapes:

1. Adult diabetes Group R NFZ realizator continuity buyout: control the NFZ-contracted realizator entity and live eZWM/payment flow, not just patient mandates.
2. Obesity/GLP-1 sleep-apnea clearance slot bank: only if diagnostic capacity, physician-read slots, and obesity-clinic deposits are reserved/prepaid.
3. CPAP mask replacement mandate book: weaker unless the founder controls a retiring shop book, inventory, and realizator/payment flow.

Gate lesson:

Patient-book ideas pass only when the business controls an existing transaction node. Partner-only arrangements get capped because the regulated partner can capture the value.

## Legal GLP-1 / Addictive-Drug-Adjacent

Do not pursue direct sale, resale, brokerage, public advertising, or delivery of prescription drugs. The compliant business surface is around capacity, compliance, records, and provider-of-record workflows.

Better shapes:

1. GLP-1/obesity sleep-apnea diagnostic slot bank: biosignal capacity around comorbidity workup, not medication sale.
2. Controlled-substance in-person compliance conversion desk: call-book plus compliant room/doctor-session capacity for clinics affected by in-person examination rules; high legal/reputation risk.
3. GLP-1 continuation lab/safety workflow escrow: likely too service-like unless attached to an acquired clinic book and prepaid lab capacity.

Gate lesson:

Rx-adjacent ideas must explicitly keep licensed clinicians/pharmacies as provider/seller of record and avoid prescription guarantees. The asset should be appointment/capacity/payment flow, not drug demand.

## Highest-Signal Score Pattern

To deserve real-gate testing, the next idea should be phrased as:

`Buy/option a specific live book/entity/stock lot; prove prepaid current-month demand; fulfill real transactions; show margin and settlement; only then close.`

Any idea that starts with "build a platform for..." or "coordinate..." should be rejected unless it owns title, payment flow, capacity, call routing, or legal transaction custody.
