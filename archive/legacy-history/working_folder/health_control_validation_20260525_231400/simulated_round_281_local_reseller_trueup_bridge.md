## Selected Candidate

**Idea name:** Local Reseller-of-Record True-Up Bridge

### Thesis

When a B2B SaaS, API, data, or developer-tool vendor has an already-accepted true-up, renewal, or usage overage blocked only by enterprise procurement/vendor-master/payment routing, the startup becomes a temporary local reseller-of-record for that named transaction. The controlled asset is the customer-issued PO/payment direction to the startup plus the vendor's back-to-back authorization, allowing the founder to route current cash and retain a spread without taking product, clinical, financial, or legal-provider responsibility.

### Control Table

| Field | Forced answer |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Small or foreign B2B SaaS/API/data/developer-tool vendors with enterprise customers in the EU/CEE that have accepted a renewal, usage true-up, or overage but cannot pay the vendor directly on time. |
| Acute trigger | Finance, procurement, or vendor-master blockage after the business owner/customer has accepted the amount: no local supplier record, PO must go to an approved EU entity, VAT/currency/entity routing problem, or the customer's procurement calendar will miss the vendor's quarter/year-end. |
| Transferable control point | Signed back-to-back reseller/order authorization from the vendor and a customer-issued PO or payment-direction confirmation naming the startup as reseller/payee for the specific accepted transaction. |
| Signed/titled/assigned/prepaid within 60 days | Vendor authorization, customer PO/payment direction, customer invoice from startup, vendor invoice to startup, first customer payment received or scheduled under no-advance terms, first vendor remittance, and retained fee/spread. |
| First acquisition mechanism | Mine partner ecosystems and RevOps/CFO communities for vendors with accepted true-ups/renewals blocked by vendor-master or local invoice requirements; use a short "only accepted amounts with customer PO intent" intake. |
| Gross margin and payback logic | Setup fee plus 2-8% transaction spread/success fee on 150k-1m PLN transactions. No inventory and minimal delivery work. Contractors handle VAT/accounting review and contract templates. Payback comes from one or two routed transactions. |
| Copy risk | Large VARs, distributors, cloud marketplaces, procurement BPOs, and fractional CFO/RevOps firms can copy the shape. |
| Why incumbents cannot copy before control | The startup only accepts cases where the customer has already agreed to issue a named PO/payment direction and the vendor signs transaction-specific authorization. Once that PO/payment stream is controlled, a later VAR cannot recover the same transaction without restarting procurement. |
| Not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker | The startup is not writing advice or introducing parties. It becomes the named reseller/payee on a specific transaction and earns from routed cash, with explicit boundaries and back-to-back terms. |

### Internal Simulation

**Simulated score: 88**

Reasons this clears the simulation gate:

- Stronger than Round 280 because it controls a customer-issued PO/payment stream, not an AP follow-up file.
- Cash can move inside the proof window if the case is already accepted and blocked only by vendor-master/local-invoicing/procurement routing.
- The first proof is not interest, meetings, or a report; it is a PO, paid invoice, vendor remittance, and retained margin.
- The startup can reject any case requiring lending, factoring, regulated payment services, product certification, clinical data, legal representation, sanctions exposure, or unresolved tax/legal authority.
- It is ugly, transactional, finance/admin-heavy, and narrow enough for a solo founder to test part-time with contractors.

Reasons the score does not exceed 88:

- Procurement may prefer established VARs or approved supplier catalogs.
- Vendor approval of an unknown reseller is not guaranteed.
- Tax, VAT, product liability, data-processing, and reseller-of-record obligations need tight boundaries.
- The company could become a one-off PO workaround rather than a repeatable recovery machine.
- Existing distributors and resellers are a real copy threat once the pattern is visible.

### Internal Gate Decision

Advance to real working-chat validation because simulated score is strictly >87 and the candidate materially improves the failed Round 280 control point.
