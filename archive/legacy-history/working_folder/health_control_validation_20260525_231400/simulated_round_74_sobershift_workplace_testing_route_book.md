# Simulated Round 74: SoberShift Workplace Alcohol/Drug Testing Route-Book Buyout

Date: 2026-05-26

## Why This Pivot

The biosignal procurement evidence desk failed because a live questionnaire is urgent but not durable control. The next shape must own contracts, payment flow, device IDs, calibration calendars, consumable stock, or an acquired route book.

This pivot stays within the user's "addictive drug" interest but avoids direct sale, brokerage, advertising, or delivery of addictive, prescription, or controlled substances. The business sells workplace safety/compliance infrastructure around legally permitted alcohol and "substances acting similarly to alcohol" checks in Poland.

## Candidate

`SoberShift Workplace Alcohol/Drug Testing Route-Book Buyout`

Acquire or option a small Polish BHP/HR-compliance/operator book that already supports workplace sobriety and drug-testing programs for safety-sensitive employers. Control the employer account book, installed breathalyser/device IDs, calibration and verification due dates, policy/protocol templates, test-log workflow, drug-test consumable inventory, service phone/domain/mailbox, and seller handoff.

The startup does not sell drugs, treat addiction, give employment-law advice, decide disciplinary outcomes, perform laboratory toxicology, or guarantee legal compliance. Employers remain responsible for work-regulation adoption, employee communication, work admission decisions, and personnel actions. Accredited laboratories, device services, and legal counsel remain provider of record where required.

## Buyer

- Logistics, warehousing, construction, manufacturing, chemicals, energy, utilities, transport, security, and industrial employers in Poland.
- 50-500 employee sites with safety-sensitive jobs, shift work, forklifts, vehicles, machinery, contractors, or high-value property risk.
- HR, BHP, operations, plant managers, and compliance owners who need a repeatable testing process rather than one-off legal memos.

## Acute Trigger

The Polish Labour Code framework now allows employer sobriety checks and checks for substances acting similarly to alcohol when implemented under internal regulations and conditions. Employers that operate safety-sensitive sites need:

- calibrated/verifiable breathalyser devices;
- clear testing policy and employee categories;
- drug-test consumables where appropriate;
- device calibration reminders and certificate archive;
- authorized tester training records;
- incident and refusal workflow;
- audit-ready test logs and retention rules;
- fast replacement/calibration when a device is unavailable.

The pain is acute after an accident, near-miss, insurer request, contractor-site requirement, PIP/HR review, or management decision to implement preventive checks.

## Control Point

The hard asset is an acquired live route/account book:

- signed employer monthly accounts or paid retainers;
- installed device IDs, serial numbers, locations, calibration/verification certificates, and next-due dates;
- assigned customer contracts or customer-approved new contracts before close;
- service phone/domain/mailbox and ticket history;
- drug-test consumable stock and reorder records;
- device loaner/replacement pool;
- testing calendar, site map, authorized tester roster, training records, and protocol templates;
- accredited calibration/service routing agreements;
- seller-endorsed handoff to HR/BHP/plant contacts.

The founder controls renewal/payment flow and device continuity. It is not a legal newsletter, dashboard, or generic BHP training offer.

## 2-Month Proof

Required proof before close:

1. Signed option or asset-purchase LOI over one small operator/BHP consultant book with at least 5 active employer accounts.
2. Customer-approved transfer or new contract for at least 3 employers.
3. At least 10 identifiable installed or leased breathalyser/testing devices with certificate/due-date records.
4. 25,000+ PLN collected or prepaid across monthly retainers, calibration renewals, device rentals, consumable packs, and one implementation/test-day.
5. One completed calibration/verification batch routed through an accredited/service provider.
6. One completed employer test-day or internal tester training/logging handoff, with no legal advice or disciplinary decision by the startup.
7. 50%+ gross margin after device service, consumables, training contractor, legal template review, seller earnout, support, and admin.
8. Written boundary memo: employer remains controller/responsible party; startup is device/admin/service operator, not employer, police, lab, doctor, or lawyer.

## First Acquisition Mechanism

- Search for small BHP consultancies, HR compliance consultants, breathalyser service resellers, industrial safety trainers, and local operators that sold sobriety-testing setup after the 2023 Labour Code change but do not want recurring admin.
- Offer a revenue-share/earnout: seller keeps 15-25% of transferred first-year gross profit, buyer takes device-calendar/admin/payment flow.
- Call employers with existing policies and devices approaching calibration/verification dates, not employers with abstract interest.
- Use device service providers and drug-test distributors as referral sources only after the startup controls employer accounts.

## Economics

Revenue:

- 800-2,500 PLN/month per employer for device-calendar custody, certificate archive, loaner access, support phone, protocol/log admin, and calibration routing;
- 150-600 PLN per calibration/service event, depending on device and routing;
- 400-1,500 PLN per drug-test consumable pack or replacement supply order, where legally appropriate;
- 2,000-8,000 PLN implementation or re-implementation sprint for one site;
- 3,000-12,000 PLN annual refresh per multi-site employer.

Gross margin:

- 50-65% if calibration/service and legal review are routed externally and the founder owns admin/payment flow.
- Payback can be under 60 days if 3-5 employers prepay annual/quarterly retainers or calibration batches before close.

## Copy Risk

Incumbents exist:

- breathalyser sellers and authorized service/calibration firms;
- workplace sobriety-system vendors;
- BHP/legal consultancies;
- drug-test distributors.

The wedge is not "sell breathalysers." It is acquiring small live employer books and becoming the operator of record for calibration calendars, device continuity, testing workflow admin, consumables, and payment renewal.

Copy risk is capped if the first book includes signed employer accounts, device IDs, calendars, certificate archive, support phone, seller handoff, and prepaid renewals. If the founder only sells new setup from zero, the score should collapse.

## Why This Is Not Selling Addictive Drugs

The startup does not sell alcohol, controlled substances, prescription drugs, narcotics, opioids, stimulants, GLP-1 drugs, or any addictive product. It sells legally bounded workplace testing infrastructure and recurring device/service administration.
