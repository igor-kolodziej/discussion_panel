## Simulated Pass Candidate

Idea name: BrokerLock Cyber Subjectivity Case-Book Option

Simulated score: 88.5 / 100

One-sentence thesis: Option or sign exclusive overflow terms with small cyber/commercial brokers and MSPs that already receive repeated cyber-insurance bind and renewal subjectivities, then monetize the authorized live case book through prepaid evidence-room and underwriter-response packets.

Exact buyer: Polish/CEE cyber-insurance brokers, commercial brokers with cyber lines, MGAs/coverholder partners, and MSPs supporting 50-500 employee SMEs. The end payor is an SME whose cyber quote, renewal, binder, ransomware coverage, funds-transfer coverage, surcharge, exclusion, or customer-contract insurance certificate is blocked by a live underwriter request.

Acute trigger: A policy bind or renewal deadline is blocked because the insurer, MGA, or underwriter asks for concrete proof around MFA, EDR/MDR, backups, restore testing, privileged access, patching, security awareness, incident response, funds-transfer controls, external scan findings, or control exceptions.

Control point: Signed exclusive or priority overflow routing terms with brokers/MSPs; direct client authorization for each policy event; underwriter subjectivity wording; policy deadline; payment direction or broker guarantee; evidence-room custody; MSP/admin-console exports; reviewer queue; and a case-memory taxonomy of accepted evidence and exception/no-go wording.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof: Two brokers/MSPs sign exclusive or priority overflow terms for cyber subjectivity cases; 8-12 live bind/renewal cases are prepaid or broker-guaranteed; every case includes a real underwriter request, policy deadline, client authorization, existing technical evidence or MSP source, and broker approval path; at least five cases produce bind, renewal, cleaner quote, exclusion/sublimit reduction, underwriter progress, accepted exception wording, or clean remediation/no-go; one broker routes a second case.

6-month POC: Handle 40-80 routed policy-event cases; collect 400,000-1,200,000 PLN; document 25+ bind/renewal/progress outcomes; build a private library of underwriter wording and evidence formats; keep gross margin above 60%; keep turnaround under five business days for evidence-ready cases; decline implementation-heavy cases.

First acquisition mechanism: Start with 80 Polish/CEE cyber/commercial brokers and MSPs; pitch only their next live underwriter subjectivity, not general readiness. Offer a paid overflow product that brokers can resell or pass through while keeping their insurance relationship and advice role.

Economics: 5,000-8,000 PLN wholesale for narrow evidence-ready control packets; 10,000-18,000 PLN for full renewal/bind subjectivity packs; 18,000-35,000 PLN for urgent multi-entity or contract-critical cases. Reviewer cost should stay below 35-40% of fee.

Copy risk: Brokers, MSPs, MSSPs, cyber consultants, insurers, MGAs, and underwriting platforms can copy pieces. The defense must be the specific routed book: signed overflow terms, client authorization, current policy deadlines, payment direction, evidence-room control, accepted exception language, and repeat broker trust before competitors see the case.

Why it is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker: The startup does not sell insurance, implement controls, provide generic readiness, broker policies, or run a dashboard. It controls authorized broker/MSP-routed policy-event evidence rooms and produces underwriter subjectivity artifacts for named bind/renewal deadlines.
