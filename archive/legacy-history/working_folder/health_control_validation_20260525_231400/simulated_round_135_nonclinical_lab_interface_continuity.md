## Selected Candidate

Non-Clinical Lab Interface Continuity Buyout.

## Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- It keeps the strongest old control point: seller-mediated acquisition of live production connectors, code, mappings, runbooks, credentials, support history, support inbox, customer book, and prepaid customer continuity before close.
- It removes the old fatal objections: no clinical/patient/genetic data, no Article 9 health data, no MDR/IVDR boundary, no diagnostic result interpretation.
- The first proof is not "interest": signed seller option, customer-approved admin transfer, 25,000+ PLN prepaid transition cash, credential rotation, and one live ticket fixed.
- Acquisition-led CAC is credible because the seller introduces inherited lab customers at the moment continuity risk is visible.
- The business is not a generic integration agency because it buys embedded production continuity rather than selling net-new projects.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Not capped below 82: concrete 6-month control proof exists through seller option, IP custody, customer-approved transfer, prepaid continuity, and live ticket resolution.
- Not capped below 85: LIMS vendors/integrators can copy new work, but they cannot instantly copy a seller-mediated inherited book with code, mappings, credentials, support history, and customer prepayments.
- Not capped below 87: CAC, payback, margin, and cash conversion are credible if the first option requires 25,000+ PLN prepaid customer cash before close and seller earnout instead of heavy upfront price.
- Main residual risk: exact seller profile may be rare, production support still needs fast response, and old connectors may be bespoke chaos.
