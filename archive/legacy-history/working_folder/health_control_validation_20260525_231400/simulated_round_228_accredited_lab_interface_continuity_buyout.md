## Selected Candidate

**AccreditedLab Interface Continuity Buyout**

One-sentence thesis: Buy or option the installed support books of retiring micro-vendors who maintain production instrument-export parsers, certificate-template generators, and LIMS upload scripts for non-clinical ISO/IEC 17025 calibration, metrology, materials, NDT, and manufacturing QC labs, then convert the installed base into prepaid continuity contracts before seller payout.

## Why It Passes Internal Simulation

- The control object is concrete: source code, parser mappings, historical test files, customer-specific templates, support history, runbooks, credentials rotated after transfer, and customer-approved support/payment transfer.
- The trigger is not generic IT consulting. A lab has certificates, customer test reports, audit evidence, or production release paperwork depending on old scripts whose author is retiring or whose Windows/instrument/LIMS environment is changing.
- It avoids the fatal health-data burden of clinical lab interface variants. No patient data, diagnostic interpretation, HL7/LIS result delivery, or medical-device workflow is accepted.
- It is stronger than calibration-renewal admin because the startup controls a production dependency that customers cannot rebuild with a spreadsheet.
- Cash conversion is believable if customers prepay 6,000-18,000 PLN/year continuity before close and seller payout is earnout-based.
- Copy risk exists, but a competitor cannot copy the exact installed scripts, mappings, historical files, and transfer approvals after the book is optioned.

## Internal Cap Notes

- Not capped below 82: 60-day proof is specific and cash-based: seller option, customer approvals, prepaid continuity, code transfer, and a live ticket resolved.
- Barely not capped below 85 for copy risk: LIMS vendors and freelancers can build replacements, but cannot instantly inherit the exact installed dependency book and support history.
- Barely not capped below 87 for economics: annual continuity fees and paid upgrade tickets can support good margin if support creep is capped and only reusable production dependencies are acquired.
- Capped at 88.7 because seller supply, code quality, customer consent, and technical support creep are serious. It fails if the first seller book is messy, custom chaos, or too small.
