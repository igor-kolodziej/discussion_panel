## Selected Candidate

**Bank AI Model-Risk Renewal Evidence Pack**

### Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- The trigger is a live bank/insurer renewal, procurement, rollout, or production-use blocker, not generic AI governance curiosity.
- The evidence object is AI-specific: model/system inventory, use-case boundary, data provenance, evals, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, logging, monitoring, change process, incident runbook, fallback, and customer-specific response wording.
- This is materially distinct from DORA: DORA supplier evidence is ICT operational resilience and third-party risk; this is model-risk and AI-system behavior evidence.
- The founder can reject regulated credit/underwriting/HR/clinical decisions and only accept AI workflow vendors where evidence can be organized without validating model performance or giving legal opinions.
- A named AI governance/model-risk reviewer can be retained per case, while the founder handles evidence extraction, response structure, gap split, and reusable case memory.

### Internal Objections

- AI governance consultants, Big Four teams, cyber/security firms, lawyers, GRC tools, and internal bank model-risk teams can copy much of the surface.
- Unknown-founder credibility is weak without a named senior reviewer.
- Some bank requests will require real model validation, legal classification, DPIA/FRIA, security remediation, contract negotiation, or product changes.
- AI/RAG evidence variants recently failed, including one that passed working but failed fresh.
- The candidate should be killed if Zero To One treats it as DORA line-extension, generic AI questionnaire support, or copyable consulting.
