## Search Frame

Recent failures show that recovery mandates, public-form workflows, software access desks, and generic evidence packets fail when incumbents already own the decisive workflow. This round tests a narrower current order-release gate: rechargeable industrial battery and BESS sellers whose live EU customer purchase order, distributor onboarding, project shipment, or financier acceptance is blocked by Battery Regulation carbon-footprint declaration evidence.

Regulatory context checked before simulation:

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Article 7 creates carbon-footprint declaration obligations for electric-vehicle batteries, rechargeable industrial batteries above 2 kWh, and LMT batteries by model and manufacturing plant.
- EUR-Lex consolidated text lists staged dates for carbon-footprint declarations and related later performance-class/threshold requirements.
- The European Commission JRC maintains battery carbon-footprint methodology work under the Batteries Regulation.

This candidate does not sell battery passports, generic Battery Regulation readiness, LCA software, certification, or engineering advice. It accepts only live commercial blockers where a named buyer or distributor asks for a carbon-footprint declaration path, model/manufacturing-plant evidence, or a qualified no-go before money or shipment moves.

## Lead Candidate

### Idea Name

BESS Carbon-Footprint PO Release Desk.

### Internal Simulated Score

88.2 / 100.

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The trigger is live money, not general readiness: a named customer, distributor, EPC, project buyer, or financier is blocking a BESS/industrial-battery purchase order, shipment, onboarding, or draw because the carbon-footprint declaration path is unclear.
- Battery model and manufacturing-plant evidence is specific; a competitor cannot take over the exact buyer thread once the founder has the mandate, data room, qualified-reviewer notes, response log, and customer-ready answer.
- The POC can produce cash inside 60 days: prepaid urgent case fees and at least one buyer acceptance, narrowed rejection, lab/LCA-needed no-go, or order-release progress record.
- Capital need is low if the founder never pays for testing/certification and uses qualified reviewers on scoped per-case terms.
- The boundary is clearer than generic Battery Regulation consulting: no declaration invention, no certification, no LCA claim unless a qualified party owns it, no battery engineering, no shipment dangerous-goods work.

### Internal Objections

- TIC firms, LCA consultants, product-compliance advisers, battery OEMs, distributors, and EPC advisers are natural incumbents and can copy the category.
- Many sellers will not have real manufacturing-plant data, energy mix data, bill of materials, model scope, or methodology alignment.
- If a true carbon-footprint calculation or verification is missing, the honest answer may be no-go or qualified-provider referral, not release.
- Buyer urgency may concentrate in larger distributors and projects that already use compliance advisers.
- Deadlines and delegated/implementing acts may create confusion that only legal/compliance specialists can safely interpret.
- Battery safety, fire, transport, waste, grid-code, and warranty issues can contaminate intake unless rejected.
- The business could be treated as another evidence desk unless the first proof ties directly to order release or paid no-go outcomes.

## 60-Day POC

- Three prepaid live BESS or rechargeable-industrial-battery order blockers at 18,000-45,000 PLN per case.
- At least 500,000 PLN of named order, shipment, onboarding, tender, or draw value per case.
- One qualified LCA/TIC/product-compliance reviewer engaged on scoped per-case terms.
- Customer request text, product/model/manufacturing-plant evidence, supplier authorization, and buyer deadline for every case.
- One accepted customer response, narrowed rejection, formal lab/LCA-needed no-go, or order-release/procurement-progress outcome.
- 60,000-135,000 PLN collected and at least 50% gross margin after reviewer cost.

## Kill Criteria

Kill the branch if:

- Most leads lack written buyer/customer blockers.
- More than half of qualified cases require fresh LCA calculation, certification, testing, or legal advice before any response can be honest.
- Buyer order value is usually below 250,000 PLN.
- Qualified reviewer cost exceeds 40% of case fee.
- TIC/LCA partners refuse white-label or scoped review because they want the full client relationship.
- Customers treat the response as low-value readiness work rather than PO/shipment/draw release.
- The evaluator says the idea is mostly Battery Regulation consulting with a better label.

Advance to working-chat Zero To One validation because the internal simulated score is strictly above `87`, the prompt can be kept clean, and the proof artifact is a live order-release outcome or paid no-go tied to a named BESS/industrial-battery commercial blocker.
