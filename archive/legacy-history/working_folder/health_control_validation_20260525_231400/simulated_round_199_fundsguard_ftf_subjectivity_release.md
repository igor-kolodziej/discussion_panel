## Search Frame

Recent failures show that "evidence desk" is too weak when the decisive facts are legal, payroll, waste-classification, authority, platform, or technical-remediation decisions. This round tests a narrower insurance-bind subjectivity where the evidence is mostly finance/admin control proof already inside the buyer: AP approval rules, bank dual authorization, vendor-bank-change procedure, callback records, payment limits, ERP role evidence, and CFO attestations.

The candidate is deliberately not a broad cyber-insurance evidence pack. It targets a current bind/renewal blocker for funds-transfer fraud, social-engineering fraud, invoice-manipulation, or computer-crime coverage, where the broker/underwriter has already written a specific subjectivity or exclusion.

## Why It Clears The Simulated Gate

- The buyer has a dated bind/renewal blocker, exclusion, sublimit, premium consequence, lender/customer requirement, or board deadline.
- The evidence is usually CFO/AP/bank workflow proof rather than deep cyber remediation: vendor-master change rules, bank portal dual control, payment limits, callback logs, approval matrix, and training acknowledgements.
- The hard proof is external: underwriter/broker response, revised subjectivity status, restored sublimit, bind progress, or documented no-go.
- The founder can run the first POC without being an insurance broker, cyber auditor, law firm, or claims advocate if every answer is insured-approved and broker-routed.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Cap below 82 if the case requires the startup to state coverage, advise on insurance placement, answer for the insured, or beautify weak controls.
- Cap below 85 if the case is broad cyber underwriting evidence rather than a funds-transfer/social-engineering subjectivity with CFO-owned proof.
- Cap below 87 if most cases require control implementation rather than packaging existing truthful evidence.
- Cap below 87 if broker channels cannot deliver repeat paid cases or if underwriter responses remain too subjective to count.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

Zero To One may treat this as another insurance evidence desk with broker/MSP/accountant copy risk and underwriter decisions outside founder control. The score only deserves to stay above 87 internally if the narrower FTF/social-engineering subjectivity and finance-admin evidence are materially stronger than prior cyber-insurance evidence branches.
