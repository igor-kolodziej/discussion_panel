Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: KSC Customer Renewal Evidence Pack.

One-sentence thesis: sell prepaid 7-business-day evidence-response packs to Polish/CEE MSPs, SaaS vendors, hosting providers, OT maintenance firms, software integrators, and IT service suppliers whose newly KSC/NIS2-registered customers are blocking renewal, system access, procurement, or contract continuation until supplier cybersecurity, incident-contact, continuity, subcontractor, and service-evidence questions are answered.

Exact buyer: 10-250 person suppliers that sell IT, hosting, SaaS, MSP, OT maintenance, system integration, helpdesk, data-processing, or software-support services to Polish essential or important entities under the amended National Cybersecurity System Act. The first wedge is suppliers to telecom, postal, public-sector, trust-service, digital infrastructure, managed IT, industrial, utility, food, waste, and transport entities that are newly entered into or self-registering in the KSC list.

Acute trigger: a customer that has received a KSC/Wykaz notice, is self-registering between May and October 2026, or is preparing KSC/NIS2 obligations sends a supplier request before renewal, access continuation, procurement approval, or contract signature. Typical requests:

- identify security and incident contacts;
- describe incident notification path and escalation times;
- list subcontractors/subprocessors and service dependencies;
- provide continuity, backup, logging, MFA, access-control, patching, vulnerability, and availability evidence;
- explain how customer incidents are detected, escalated, and documented;
- provide evidence attachments or management-approved answers for the customer's supplier-risk file;
- update contract appendices, service contacts, or operational evidence before access or renewal continues.

Control point: the founder controls value only when all of these are present:

- a live customer email, questionnaire, procurement request, access-retention request, renewal checklist, or contract blocker tied to KSC/NIS2, Wykaz KSC registration, S46 incident reporting, or the customer's essential/important entity obligations;
- a prepaid supplier ticket before delivery starts;
- customer deadline or business consequence, such as renewal, access, procurement, audit, or contract signature;
- supplier evidence folder or facts: service scope, security contacts, incident process, subcontractors, hosting/subprocessor notes, backup/continuity/logging/access-control proof, existing policies, screenshots, and prior customer answers;
- fixed output scope: Polish/English answer matrix, evidence attachment index, missing-evidence list, safe "do not answer yet" items, management-approval checklist, and items for legal/cyber review;
- reusable accepted/rejected response memory by sector and buyer question;
- written boundary that the founder does not certify cybersecurity, perform an audit, implement controls, provide legal advice, or claim KSC/NIS2 compliance.

6-month proof:

1. Sign referral/payment-split terms with 3 cyber boutiques, MSPs, IT accountants, DPO/privacy firms, or sector consultants that already see KSC/NIS2 customer requests.
2. Collect 20 anonymized live supplier questions from KSC/NIS2 customer requests.
3. Close 3 prepaid tickets at 8,000-25,000 PLN each.
4. Deliver within 7 business days per ticket: answer matrix, evidence index, missing-evidence list, management-approval checklist, review handoff, and customer-ready response pack.
5. Get proof that at least 2 customer requests advanced: renewal/access/procurement moved forward, customer accepted the packet, or the customer returned a narrower follow-up rather than rejecting the supplier.
6. Build a sector case-memory file covering telecom/postal/public/digital/industrial/customer-question variants, accepted evidence types, rejected overclaims, and recurring missing items.
7. Prove 60%+ gross margin after contractor review, cyber reviewer, project management, secure handling, translation, and referral share.

First acquisition mechanism:

Agency / MSP / cyber boutique pitch:
"When a supplier client gets a KSC/NIS2 customer evidence request, send the live questionnaire. I turn their actual service facts and existing evidence into a customer-ready Polish/English answer pack in 7 business days. You keep the client relationship and receive a referral or delivery share."

Supplier pitch:
"Send the live KSC/NIS2 customer request and your current service/security evidence. In 7 business days you get a structured answer matrix, attachment index, missing-evidence list, items that need management/legal/cyber approval, and wording that avoids overclaiming compliance. This is for active customer blockers, not generic readiness."

Economics:

- Price: 8,000-25,000 PLN per live customer blocker, depending on number of services, evidence volume, deadline, and language needs.
- Contractor/cyber reviewer/translation cost: 2,000-7,000 PLN per ticket.
- Referral share: 10-25%.
- Target gross margin: 60%+.
- Cash conversion: payment upfront or 70% upfront / 30% on delivery.
- Payback: one ticket funds the manual workflow; three tickets prove whether accepted-answer memory and channel routing reduce delivery time.

Copy risk:

Cyber consultants, MSPs, lawyers, GRC platforms, DPO firms, and security-questionnaire vendors can copy pieces of this. The wedge is not "NIS2 consulting." It is a fast, prepaid response to live KSC/NIS2 customer blockers, with Polish sector context, customer-ready evidence organization, and accepted/rejected response memory from real supplier renewals and access-retention cases. Once a channel partner routes a current case and the supplier prepays, competitors cannot copy that case before delivery.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup owns a ticket book of prepaid live customer blockers and produces a buyer/customer-ready answer pack from existing supplier facts and evidence. It does not sell generic KSC readiness, an audit, certification, legal advice, GRC software, an MSP service, or a marketplace. The compounding asset is accepted-case memory from real KSC/NIS2 customer reviews.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- No legal advice, KSC/NIS2 compliance certification, cybersecurity audit, penetration test, managed security implementation, or control remediation.
- Cyber reviewers validate whether evidence is internally coherent, not whether the supplier is legally compliant.
- Lawyers, customer counsel, DPOs, CISOs, or management approve final legal positions and commitments.
- The supplier remains responsible for actual controls, incidents, contracts, subcontractors, access, logging, backup, and continuity.
- The product refuses unsupported answers and marks gaps instead of inventing evidence.
- Sensitive customer and security data are handled under NDA/DPA where needed, with least-data intake, redaction, secure storage, and deletion schedule.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed and unconfirmed ideas:

- Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: this is Polish KSC/NIS2 supplier evidence for essential and important entities outside the regulated-finance DORA context.
- Not Broker-Routed Cyber Renewal Evidence Bind Lane: no insurance underwriting or broker policy renewal; the customer is an actual regulated/registered KSC entity blocking access, procurement, or contract continuation.
- Not AI Act Transparency Ticket Book: no AI chatbot/content transparency.
- Not REDBlocked, CBAM, EUDR, GreenTender, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, or PromoLeak: no Amazon listing evidence, import emissions, deforestation, EPREL lighting tenders, health diligence, municipal event rail, or retailer deduction recovery.
