Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
Customer Concentration Price-Retention Pack

One-sentence thesis:
Lower-middle-market sellers with an active LOI, buyer diligence process, financing review, or exclusivity period will pay for a fast customer-concentration price-retention pack when a buyer, lender, IC, or QoE adviser uses top-customer risk to demand a price cut, escrow, earnout, delayed close, or financing condition.

Exact buyer:
Founder-led B2B companies, independent sponsors, lower-middle-market sellers, sell-side CEOs, CFOs, boutique M&A advisers, fractional CFOs, and search-fund or micro-PE sellers in Poland/CEE and EU-adjacent markets. The first buyer has a live transaction or financing process where customer concentration has become a written objection, not a generic plan to prepare for sale. Best first categories are B2B services, niche manufacturing, distribution, MSPs, vertical software, industrial services, maintenance firms, and project-based businesses with one to five large customers.

Acute trigger:
A buyer, lender, IC, QoE provider, debt provider, investment committee, or legal/commercial diligence team has written that customer concentration threatens valuation, leverage, escrow, earnout, holdback, closing certainty, or investment committee approval. The seller believes the concentration risk is overstated because the top customer has long tenure, recurring purchase behavior, embedded switching cost, multi-site adoption, strong renewal history, low dispute history, expansion path, multiple buyer contacts, technical integration, or near-term replacement pipeline.

Control point:
A prepaid mandate over one named transaction or financing process; the buyer or lender objection text; LOI/exclusivity or process evidence; customer revenue and gross-margin bridge; 24-36 month purchase history; contract, PO, MSA, SLA, renewal, framework, or account terms where available; churn/non-renewal history; customer contact and sponsor map; usage or delivery proof; support/dispute history; expansion and pipeline evidence; customer reference-call preparation pack where allowed; replacement pipeline and account-diversification plan; seller-approved answer chronology; adviser routing path; and submitted concentration response packet. The startup controls the current evidence room, issue map, response chronology, and packet used by the seller's existing deal owner.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three prepaid live deal cases at 30,000-90,000 PLN each, each with written customer-concentration objection text and at least 2,000,000 PLN equivalent of purchase-price, escrow, earnout, holdback, debt-capacity, or closing consequence at stake. One submitted concentration packet produces buyer/lender acknowledgement, narrowed follow-up, reduced holdback ask, restored timeline, approved financing progress, written clean no-go, or a documented decision not to contest the retrade. One paid sell-side QoE, M&A, or fractional-CFO reviewer is attached for finance/accounting boundary checks. No success-fee reliance until fixed-fee willingness is proven.

6-month POC:
Complete 8-15 paid concentration price-retention packs, covering 40,000,000-250,000,000 PLN of enterprise value or financing exposure. Collect 350,000-1,200,000 PLN in fixed fees and limited success-tied bonuses where measurement is explicit. Produce 5+ buyer/lender/deal-progress outcomes: price cut reduced, escrow/holdback narrowed, earnout converted, financing condition clarified, IC question closed, timeline restored, or clean no-go accepted. Build a private concentration-risk evidence taxonomy by industry, customer tenure, renewal type, account sponsor depth, contract strength, switching cost, margin dependence, replacement pipeline, and accepted buyer objection wording. Keep gross margin above 60% after reviewer, secure data-room, analysis, and contractor costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target boutique M&A advisers, sell-side QoE providers, fractional CFOs, transaction lawyers, independent sponsors, search-fund brokers, business brokers, and PE operating partners with a narrow message: "If a live buyer or lender is using customer concentration to retrade price, escrow, earnout, or financing, I build the evidence pack for the seller's existing deal owner in five business days. I do not value the company, negotiate, give legal advice, or replace QoE. I only turn existing customer facts into a buyer-ready response or clean no-go." Direct seller outreach is secondary and only to companies publicly in sale/financing preparation or referred by advisers.

Economics:
Charge 30,000-45,000 PLN for a single-customer concentration response tied to a live written objection, 45,000-90,000 PLN for multi-customer or lender/QoE-heavy files, and 5-8% of a specifically documented reduction in escrow, holdback, or price cut only when the seller and adviser define the measurement upfront. Fixed fees dominate because attribution is messy. Buyers pay because a small packet fee is cheap relative to a 500,000-10,000,000 PLN retrade, holdback, earnout, or financing impact.

Copy risk:
Investment bankers, sell-side QoE firms, fractional CFOs, transaction lawyers, commercial-diligence consultants, operating partners, and internal CFO teams can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is current-case control plus repeated pattern memory: once the seller mandates the file, shares the live objection, assigns the internal owner, and routes the packet through the existing deal adviser, the startup controls the evidence chronology and response structure for that live retrade before another provider can restart.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the seller signs the prepaid mandate, provides the buyer/lender objection, opens the customer evidence room, and routes the submitted concentration packet through the banker, CFO, or adviser, a competitor cannot instantly displace the active response without recreating the evidence bridge, customer chronology, margin map, reference preparation, no-go logic, and adviser-approved response under a live deadline. Incumbents can compete for future deals, but they do not control the already-paid current deal file.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It accepts only active transactions or financing processes with written customer-concentration objections tied to price, escrow, earnout, holdback, debt capacity, IC approval, or closing timeline. The output is a deal-response artifact used in an active money decision: concentration issue map, customer evidence chronology, margin and tenure bridge, renewal/switching-cost support, reference-call pack where allowed, buyer-ready response packet, and clean no-go where concentration risk is real. It does not sell valuation, generic exit readiness, software, dashboards, market research, lead generation, introductions, or broad consulting.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, securities advice, valuation opinions, fairness opinions, audit opinions, tax advice, accounting opinions, lender advice, negotiation strategy, customer pressure, or investment recommendations. The seller's banker, counsel, QoE provider, lender, board, and management remain responsible for transaction terms, legal positions, valuation, disclosure, accounting, financing, negotiation, and final decisions. Cases are rejected if the seller asks for misleading customer claims, concealed churn, undisclosed disputes, fabricated reference support, manipulated revenue recognition, customer pressure, securities-law advice, tax/accounting opinions, or legal negotiation.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, GreenTender, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, KSeF release, AI/RAG evidence, cyber-insurance evidence, product-compliance release, physical salvage, or a route/book buyout. The buyer trigger, control point, and payment event are materially different: live sell-side M&A or financing retrade over customer concentration, not regulatory supplier evidence, marketplace listing release, investor healthtech claim diligence, public tender product evidence, import continuity, retailer deductions, or physical asset control.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Bankers, QoE firms, fractional CFOs, and transaction lawyers already advise on customer concentration. Access to live deals is referral-heavy and hard for an unknown solo founder. Buyers may use concentration as negotiation leverage even after a strong packet. Some concentration risk is real because contracts are weak, the customer is leaving, usage is down, the sponsor changed, margins are low, or replacement pipeline is fake. Success-fee attribution can be disputed. Sellers may want the startup to overstate customer certainty or hide churn. Customer reference calls and customer outreach can be sensitive during a confidential process. Data access is trust-heavy because the founder sees revenue, margin, customer contracts, pipeline, churn, and deal communications. One or two saved retrades may prove a useful expert service, not a scalable company.
