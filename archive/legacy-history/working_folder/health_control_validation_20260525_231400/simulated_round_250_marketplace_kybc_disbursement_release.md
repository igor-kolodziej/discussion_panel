## Selected Candidate

**Marketplace KYBC Disbursement Release Mandate**

One-sentence thesis: EU/CEE marketplace sellers will pay for a narrow disbursement-release mandate when Amazon, Kaufland, bol, Zalando, Booking, TikTok Shop, or similar EU marketplace platforms hold payouts, suppress selling privileges, or block EU offers because trader identity, bank-account, VAT, trade-register, address, or beneficial-owner evidence does not match the platform's KYBC / DSA trader-traceability requirements.

### Why It Clears Simulation

This is not a generic marketplace account-health desk:

- The accepted case must have held cash, suspended selling privileges, blocked listing activation, or an explicit platform KYBC/trader-verification request.
- The startup controls a seller-authorized live account case, exact mismatch matrix, evidence room, platform ticket chronology, and fee trigger.
- The proof is released disbursement, selling privilege restoration, platform acceptance, or a paid no-go where platform/source facts are inconsistent.
- The first cases are routed through Amazon/account-health agencies, seller accountants, marketplace aggregators, and e-commerce operators that do not want to chase identity mismatch loops themselves.
- It avoids product compliance, tax advice, listing optimization, ad/PPC, product appeals, counterfeit, safety, VAT-fiscal representation, and account sales.

### Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof: passes only for live accounts with held cash and seller mandate; listing hygiene alone fails.
- Copy risk: platform agencies can copy the workflow, but cannot copy a specific seller mandate, live ticket, document room, platform chronology, and success-fee trigger once controlled.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: passes if cases require at least 50,000 PLN held cash or 100,000 PLN monthly GMV at risk and prepaid triage is mandatory.
- Vague partnerships: passes only if first acquisition uses paid live cases or signed agency routing; soft referrals do not count.
- Legal/tax boundary: passes only if entity, tax, UBO, and account evidence are supplied or reviewed by seller/accountant/lawyer; the startup does not determine tax status, beneficial ownership law, or platform legal entitlement.

Simulated score: **88.2 / 100**

## Kill Conditions

- Fewer than 10 prepaid current KYBC/disbursement cases can be sourced in 60 days.
- Less than 40% of accepted cases produce platform acceptance, released cash, restored selling privileges, or a clean paid no-go.
- Most cases turn into VAT, tax, legal, UBO, address-office, or product-compliance remediation outside the mandate.
- Marketplace agencies say they already solve these cases cheaply in-house.
- Held cash is usually below 50,000 PLN or sellers refuse prepaid triage.
- Platform response times make the release outcome too slow to attribute.
- Gross margin falls below 60% after agency split, accountant/legal reviewer costs, secure intake, and founder time.
- Zero To One treats the idea as ordinary marketplace account-health consulting rather than a hard cash-release mandate.
