## Lead Candidate

**HeritageLight Matched Fixture Suites**

### Internal Simulated Score

89 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The controlled unit is a complete matched lighting suite from one property, not individual sconces or chandeliers.
- It preserves visible historical continuity across rooms, corridors, staircases, lobbies, restaurants, or hotel suites.
- It avoids the main physical failures: it is compact, shippable, easy to store, and does not require chimneys, plumbing, masonry, or large-format fit.
- Electrical safety is bounded by selling unrestored/de-energized fixture suites with buyer-selected electrician/restorer handoff.
- Proof is concrete: titled suites, site photos, counts, condition notes, buyer deposits, and a paid handoff.

### Internal Objections

- Antique lighting dealers and restorers already trade individual fixtures and some sets.
- Reproduction lighting is a strong substitute.
- Electrical rewiring and code compliance must remain outside scope.
- Complete matched suites may be rare because sellers split valuable pieces.
- Demand may be lumpy by period, finish, shade completeness, dimensions, and project taste.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the asset profile is materially different from the failed bulky heritage variants.
