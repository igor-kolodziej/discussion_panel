# Simulated Round 82: Crisis Event-Routing Mandates

Created: 2026-05-27 Europe/Warsaw

## Search Frame

Round 81 proved that a live event ledger is not enough if the buyer merely has better evidence. Round 82 searches for mandatory event-routing mandates where the buyer is already in a crisis or revenue-protection situation: complaint, refund threat, inspection, occupancy loss, public-review damage, contract renewal risk, or payment acceptance.

The control point must be a signed prepaid mandate over live current events, cases, or accounts inside 60 days. The startup cannot be a generic app, dashboard, consultant, or legal/clinical authority.

## Lead Candidate

**Family Complaint Shield** clears simulated `>87` as a crisis workflow, not an app. The control point is a prepaid written mandate requiring all high-risk events for named residents/classes to be routed through the workflow for 30 days. The care home remains the care provider and data controller. The startup acts as processor/workflow operator, with a contracted nurse/care-quality reviewer for templates and edge cases and optional care-law review for response wording.

## 60-Day Proof Required

- one or two registered private care homes with a live/recent complaint, bad review, inspection stress, refund threat, or occupancy risk;
- signed paid sprint, DPA, named event classes, and owner authority to mandate staff capture;
- all high-risk events for named residents/classes routed for 30 days;
- at least 30 high-risk event entries or a smaller number with documented live complaint relevance;
- at least two owner-ready family/inspection response packets;
- one owner-confirmed retained-bed, reduced-refund, reduced-complaint, avoided escalation, or retained-occupancy result;
- reviewers used for QA and edge cases, not every routine entry;
- no diagnosis, prescribing, care-plan decisions, certification, legal representation, or patient-data resale.

## Strongest Simulated Objections

- Trust and resident data are the largest risks. The POC needs DPA, narrow resident/event scope, secure handling, reviewer names, and clear controller/processor boundaries.
- Staff compliance can break the product if the workflow adds burden during already understaffed shifts.
- Homes without visible crisis triggers will postpone.
- The product can collapse into bespoke consulting unless event classes, response packets, and reviewer use standardize quickly.
- Retention after the crisis is uncertain; monthly continuation must be tied to family response, handover quality, and inspection readiness.

## Duplicate Risk

This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, HeatQuiet, PromoLeak, Data Act, CBAM, GreenTender, BatteryFit, parcel claims, or heritage-door inventory. It is closest in structure to TraceFaktura because it uses mandatory live event routing, but the buyer, event class, safety boundary, and acute economics are different: private care-home owner risk, family complaints, refunds, reviews, inspection anxiety, and occupancy retention rather than municipal animal-care invoice evidence.

## Decision

Write the clean prompt and submit Family Complaint Shield to real working-chat Zero To One validation. Treat simulated score `90` as a pass.
