Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
OEM Warranty Chargeback Rebuttal Pack

One-sentence thesis:
Tier 2/Tier 3 automotive and industrial suppliers will pay for an OEM-ready rebuttal pack when a customer assigns warranty costs, field-failure reserves, debit memos, or supplier-responsibility claims that can be reduced with lot trace, failure facts, test history, contract terms, and accepted precedent structure.

Exact buyer:
CEE Tier 2/Tier 3 automotive and industrial suppliers: CFOs, quality directors, warranty managers, commercial leads, and plant managers at component, harness, casting, plastics, metalwork, electronics, HVAC, machinery, sensor, and assembly suppliers that have received a written OEM/customer warranty debit memo, field-failure allocation, reserve booking, chargeback, recovery request, or supplier-responsibility notice.

Acute trigger:
An OEM, Tier 1, machinery customer, or industrial customer assigns six-figure warranty cost, books a supplier-responsibility reserve, issues a debit memo, withholds payment, demands contribution to a field action, or requests evidence by a deadline. The supplier believes the assignment is overstated, wrongly allocated, unsupported by lot/serial evidence, outside the suspect population, contradicted by test records, or not fully tied to its contractual responsibility.

Control point:
A prepaid rebuttal mandate over one named OEM/customer assignment, plus the customer notice or debit memo, claim spreadsheet, serial/lot population, shipment history, test records, quality records, failure descriptions, return/teardown facts where available, service-bulletin or field-action context, contract and warranty clause excerpts supplied by the buyer, prior correspondence, responsible internal owner, and an OEM-ready exhibit chronology. The startup controls the rebuttal file, evidence index, reviewer flags, and response structure for that active money decision.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three prepaid rebuttal cases at 20,000-60,000 PLN each, each tied to a named OEM/customer assignment above 150,000 PLN; one submitted rebuttal pack; one OEM/customer reopening, reduction, reversal, delayed reserve, scheduled credit, narrowed claim population, or written clean no-go; and one paid quality/warranty reviewer attached for flagged technical sections.

6-month POC:
Complete 5-10 paid rebuttal packs for CEE suppliers, covering 2,000,000-8,000,000 PLN of assigned warranty/debit/reserve exposure. Show 500,000-2,000,000 PLN of measurable reduction, reversal, delayed reserve, reopened review, scheduled credit, or clean no-go impact. Collect 150,000-500,000 PLN revenue, keep gross margin above 55% after quality/warranty reviewers, contract-boundary reviewers, translations, and evidence-cleanup contractors, and build a private case library of OEM-specific rebuttal patterns, exhibit ordering, failure taxonomies, and common no-go reasons.

First acquisition mechanism:
Targeted outbound to CEE automotive and industrial supplier CFOs, quality directors, and commercial leads, plus fractional CFOs, automotive quality consultants, IATF/quality advisers, warranty consultants, and restructuring advisers who see supplier debit memos. The first message asks for one live written OEM/customer warranty debit, reserve, chargeback, or field-cost assignment above 150,000 PLN, not general quality consulting. Offer a fixed-scope seven-business-day diagnostic and rebuttal pack with strict escalation for engineering, legal, or product-redesign issues.

Economics:
Charge 20,000-35,000 PLN for a diagnostic/rebuttal pack on assignments up to 500,000 PLN, 35,000-60,000 PLN for urgent or multi-lot cases, plus 5-12% of measurable reversed, reduced, credited, or delayed liability where legally and contractually appropriate. Reviewers are paid per flagged technical or contract-boundary section. Buyers pay because one OEM debit memo, reserve, or field-action allocation can erase margin far beyond the pack fee.

Copy risk:
Quality consultants, warranty consultants, automotive lawyers, OEM account managers, plant quality teams, IATF consultants, and internal supplier teams can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is narrow current-case control and compounding case memory: OEM-specific claim language, accepted exhibit order, serial/lot population logic, failure-mode rebuttal patterns, no-go taxonomy, and response structures tied to real debit/reversal outcomes.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the supplier signs the mandate, shares the OEM/customer notice, assigns the internal owner, and authorizes the rebuttal file, the startup controls that current evidence chronology, exhibit index, reviewer notes, response structure, and OEM communication trail before the response deadline. Incumbents can compete for future cases, but they cannot instantly replace an active signed rebuttal file without restarting evidence collection and reviewer work.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It accepts only live OEM/customer warranty debit, reserve, chargeback, field-action, or supplier-responsibility assignments with money at stake. The output is an evidence-controlled rebuttal packet used in the active customer decision: chronology, lot/serial map, claim-population screen, exhibit index, unanswered questions, reviewer flags, and response structure. It is not generic quality consulting, warranty administration, legal representation, product certification, root-cause engineering, software, a dashboard, a marketplace, or introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No legal opinions, litigation strategy, insurance adjustment, product certification, root-cause certification, IATF certification, safety certification, product redesign, field-repair management, recall management, or false technical claims. Licensed counsel handles legal interpretation if needed. Qualified engineers or quality/warranty reviewers handle flagged technical judgments. The startup organizes evidence and response structure for the supplier's decision owner; it does not certify that the supplier is or is not responsible.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack Safety, or HeatQuiet. It is also different from prior RMA and warranty-credit recovery failures: those tried to recover small installer/OEM credits from payor programs, while this defends against or reduces a customer-assigned warranty liability, debit memo, reserve, or field-action cost allocation for industrial suppliers.

Strongest anticipated objections:
OEMs may ignore a rebuttal from an unknown third-party packager. Serious cases may require recognized quality engineers, labs, lawyers, or root-cause investigation. Suppliers may not have clean lot/serial trace, test records, return facts, contract terms, or failure data. Internal quality and commercial teams may already handle rebuttals. Case cycles can be long, confidential, and relationship-heavy. Success-fee attribution may be disputed. The work can drift into legal argument, warranty interpretation, product-quality certification, or engineering consulting. One or two reductions may not prove repeatable acquisition. Data sensitivity is high because the founder handles customer claims, quality records, pricing exposure, and supplier commercial correspondence.
