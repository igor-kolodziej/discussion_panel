## Search Implication

The only version worth real-gating is not a consultant or dashboard:

- a PPC/feed agency signs exclusive or priority routing for current suspended Merchant Center cases;
- affected merchants authorize account access and pay upfront;
- founder builds a case memory around accepted fixes, review timing, policy evidence, and feed/site mismatch patterns;
- outcome is reinstatement or a documented no-go, not legal advice or guaranteed platform approval.
