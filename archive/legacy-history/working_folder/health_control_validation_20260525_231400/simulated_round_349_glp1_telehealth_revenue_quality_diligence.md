## Search Frame

Round 348 failed because a marketplace product-compliance desk without a cash control point looked too close to REDBlocked and depended on manufacturer/lab evidence. This round changes surface area: investor/acquirer-paid diligence for a live capital-allocation decision where the buyer already has budget, timeline, and permission to request evidence.

The candidate is deliberately not a healthcare delivery, pharmacy, prescribing, advertising, or patient-support business. It evaluates business-model and revenue-quality risk for investors, acquirers, lenders, and strategic buyers before they fund or buy GLP-1 telehealth, weight-loss clinic, and pharmacy-dependent growth businesses.

Current factual backdrop used for internal simulation:

- FDA stated on 2026-04-01 that GLP-1 compounders must meet the normal FD&C Act conditions after shortage-driven enforcement discretion wound down, and that semaglutide and tirzepatide are not currently on FDA's drug shortage list.
- FDA announced in 2026-04/05 that it proposes to exclude semaglutide, tirzepatide, and liraglutide from the 503B bulks list and invited comments through 2026-06-29.
- This creates a live diligence problem: many DTC telehealth and obesity-care growth stories still rest on revenue, retention, CAC, pharmacy supply, compounding, branded-drug conversion, refund/chargeback, and clinical-operations claims that can change materially as compounding and supply conditions tighten.

## Lead Candidate

**GLP-1 Telehealth Revenue-Quality Diligence Pack**

### Internal Simulated Score

88.4 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The buyer is a capital allocator or acquirer with a live decision and budget, not a consumer patient or operating clinic.
- The trigger is unusually current: GLP-1 compounding and supply assumptions have changed, while many telehealth growth stories still depend on them.
- The output can change investment terms, LOI conditions, earnout/holdback design, legal diligence scope, lender underwriting, or a no-go decision.
- The founder does not need to prescribe, dispense, advertise, interpret clinical tests, handle patient care, or sell GLP-1 products.
- The pack is fixed-scope and evidence-based: revenue cohort quality, CAC/payback, refund/chargeback, pharmacy/provider continuity, branded/compounded conversion exposure, claims/ad funnel, and no-go legal/clinical flags routed to reviewers.
- The fee is credible because a single bad obesity-telehealth acquisition, financing, or rollout decision can misprice revenue quality by millions.

### Internal Cap Audit

- Not capped below 82: the 60-day proof is three prepaid live-deal mandates and one documented IC/LOI/procurement/lender impact, not interviews or content.
- Not capped below 85: healthcare diligence firms and lawyers can copy broad diligence, but this wedge is a fast GLP-1 revenue-quality artifact tied to a current regulatory/commercial discontinuity and capital event.
- Not capped below 87: CAC is plausible through narrow investor/acquirer outreach; payback is credible if the first three packs are prepaid at 15k-45k PLN; gross margins can stay above 60% with reviewer triage.
- Health/Rx cap is avoided only because the startup does not serve patients, prescribe, dispense, recommend medications, interpret clinical outcomes, manage PHI-heavy workflows, or provide legal/clinical opinions. It rejects cases needing patient-level clinical judgment or routes them to counsel/clinicians.

### Internal Objections

- Healthcare transaction diligence, FDA/regulatory counsel, pharmacy consultants, QoE firms, and PE operating partners are natural incumbents.
- Founder credibility is a serious risk without a named healthcare regulatory reviewer, pharmacy-operations reviewer, and consumer-subscription/CAC reviewer.
- Targets may refuse to share enough cohort, pharmacy, provider, refund, chargeback, and ad funnel data.
- The work can drift into legal advice, medical judgment, patient-data review, compounding legality opinions, pharmacy compliance, or clinical outcome validation.
- Some buyers may only invest in operator-side legal diligence from known healthcare counsel.
- The GLP-1 market and enforcement posture may shift quickly; the product must stay decision-linked rather than become generic market commentary.
- It is adjacent in shape to BioSignal but differentiated by buyer claim, evidence type, payment event, and Rx-specific business-model diligence.

## Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the candidate is materially different from the exhausted recovery, marketplace, physical-lot, and generic evidence-pack branches.
