## Lead Candidate: CATCH Fish Import Release Desk

### Buyer

EU/CEE seafood importers, customs brokers, cold-chain forwarders, and seafood wholesalers importing fishery products into the EU where a current consignment, retailer order, customs/border step, or cold-chain slot is blocked by CATCH certificate data, catch-certificate chain, processing statement, non-manipulation document, importer declaration, or duplicate-reference errors.

### Acute Trigger

Since 10 January 2026, EU importers or their representatives must use CATCH for fishery-product import documentation. A live shipment can be slowed when paper catch certificates, simplified certificates, processing statements, linked certificates, product codes, species, vessel/trip data, exporter/operator records, validating authority data, or non-manipulation documents do not copy cleanly into CATCH or do not match broker/import documents.

### Control Point

Signed importer or broker mandate for a named consignment; supplied paper certificates and linked documents; broker-provided CN/HS/import reference data; CATCH draft/status screenshots or case access under the importer/broker account; supplier/exporter contact trail; error log; field-by-field source-vs-CATCH reconciliation; authority/broker response chronology; and release/progress/no-go ledger. The startup controls the current evidence file and filing chronology, not the official authority decision.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Two customs brokers or seafood importers sign prepaid CATCH release case agreements.
2. Five live or recently blocked consignments are accepted only with importer/broker authorization, shipment value, cold-chain/demurrage/order consequence, and complete source-document set.
3. At least three cases have CATCH draft/status evidence or broker written issue text.
4. At least two cases produce broker acceptance, authority progress, importer declaration submission, corrected duplicate/reference path, release to next import step, retailer/customer hold lifted, or clean no-go.
5. At least 60,000 PLN is collected in fixed and urgent case fees.
6. A reusable issue taxonomy exists across certificate type, country/authority, species/product-code pattern, processing-statement link, duplicate-reference issue, operator-creation issue, missing supplier field, and no-go cause.
