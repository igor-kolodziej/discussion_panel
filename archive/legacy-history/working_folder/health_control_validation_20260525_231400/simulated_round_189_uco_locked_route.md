## Search Frame

Round 185 failed because payor-approved residual assignment sounded clean on paper, but the transfer permission and seller supply were the real bottlenecks. The target shape is not a brokered introduction, marketplace, dashboard, compliance report, or generic waste admin service. The acceptable shape is a signed route/payment stream: named food sites sign exclusive used-cooking-oil collection direction, the legal collector/handler path is documented, the downstream recycler/offtaker gives a purchase commitment or prepay, and the first paid pickup clears inside 60 days.

## Lead Candidate

**UCO Locked Route With Recycler Prepay**

### Internal Score Rationale

This clears the simulation gate only under a narrow version. The startup does not "help restaurants recycle oil" and does not sell compliance paperwork. It locks a named route before the first sale: food sites sign exclusive collection direction, licensed collection/transport/provider responsibilities are assigned to the proper party, BDO/KPO documentation follows each pickup, a downstream recycler/feedstock buyer commits to buy or prepay on a quality formula, and the first paid pickup proves cash movement.

The strongest wedge is not single restaurants. It is dense clusters where the site operator is irritated by unpaid or unreliable collectors: ghost kitchens, food courts, hotel kitchens, franchisees, catering commissaries, mall restaurants, and independent groups with 5-30 kitchens. The founder can underwrite only routes with short pickup radius, minimum liters per month, sealed containers, contamination rules, site photo proof, and a recycler offtake price before signing.

Main internal doubts:

- incumbent collectors can copy or outbid;
- UCO is a commodity and margins may be thin;
- fraud, water/food contamination, theft, and grey-market leakage are real;
- legal collection, storage, transport, and waste-record duties must be handled by registered/licensed parties;
- restaurants may not honor exclusivity if a collector pays more in cash;
- a part-time founder could get pulled into field operations.

The simulated pass depends on strict proof: route density, signed site exclusivity, recycler purchase order or prepay, legal provider-of-record boundaries, and first cash before scale. If Zero To One treats it as a commodity waste broker or low-margin logistics job, kill the branch.
