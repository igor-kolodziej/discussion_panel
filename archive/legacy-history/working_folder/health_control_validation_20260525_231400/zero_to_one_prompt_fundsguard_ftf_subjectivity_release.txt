Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
FundsGuard FTF Subjectivity Release Pack

One-sentence thesis:
A broker-routed, CFO/AP evidence release desk for SMEs whose crime, cyber, or management-liability policy bind or renewal is blocked by a funds-transfer fraud, social-engineering fraud, invoice-manipulation, or vendor-bank-change subjectivity, exclusion, low sublimit, or premium penalty that can be answered with truthful payment-control proof.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE SMEs with 50-500 employees, export exposure, vendor-heavy AP, outsourced accounting, distributed approval teams, bank-transfer risk, customer/lender insurance requirements, or recent payment-fraud scare. The first buyer has a named broker and underwriter request tied to a live policy bind, renewal, sublimit, exclusion, ransomware/funds-transfer/social-engineering endorsement, lender covenant, customer insurance condition, or board deadline. The economic buyer is the CFO, owner, controller, finance director, or broker account executive who needs truthful proof of existing payment controls fast. Exclude claim disputes, coverage opinions, post-loss advocacy, companies currently under fraud investigation, and cases where the controls do not exist.

Acute trigger:
A broker or underwriter writes that coverage for funds-transfer fraud, social-engineering fraud, invoice manipulation, business email compromise, computer crime, or fraudulent vendor-bank-change exposure will be declined, sublimited, excluded, priced worse, or held before bind/renewal unless the insured provides concrete evidence of payment controls. Typical requested proof includes dual authorization, payment approval matrix, bank portal permissions, payment limits, vendor-bank-change callback procedure, sample change logs, segregation of duties, ERP/AP role exports, executive approval rules, out-of-band verification, employee training acknowledgement, incident escalation path, and CFO-approved statements. The buyer is not buying broad cyber readiness; it is paying because a specific policy or commercial requirement is blocked now.

Control point:
Signed insured and broker authorization over the named subjectivity; exact underwriter wording; current policy/bind/renewal deadline; requested coverage, sublimit, exclusion, or premium consequence; CFO/AP owner; payment-control evidence list; bank portal role screenshots or exports supplied by the insured; ERP/AP approval matrix; vendor-master change procedure; callback and second-channel verification examples; sample approved payment or vendor-change logs with sensitive data minimized; training and escalation evidence; broker-approved submission path; insured-approved answer set; and underwriter response tracker. The startup controls the active evidence file, missing-proof log, broker submission packet, no-go record, and fee right for the named subjectivity. It does not answer insurance applications on behalf of the insured, place insurance, advise on coverage, certify controls, perform an audit, or remediate absent controls.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign three broker referral routes or case-by-case routing agreements for FTF/social-engineering subjectivity files; take six prepaid live cases at 7,000-24,000 PLN each; every case has exact underwriter wording, named policy/bind/renewal deadline, CFO/AP owner, broker authorization, and a coverage or commercial consequence above 100,000 PLN. Produce at least four documented outcomes: underwriter accepts the evidence packet, subjectivity is removed, sublimit/exclusion improves, bind/renewal progresses, broker confirms the packet was submitted and answered the question, or paid clean no-go because the controls are absent or unsafe to state. Collect at least 70,000 PLN within 60 days.

6-month POC:
Process 35-70 live FTF/social-engineering subjectivity cases through 6-12 brokers, MSP/accounting referral partners, and finance-director networks; collect 450,000-1,200,000 PLN in prepaid fixed fees and broker-wholesale fees; document 25+ accepted submissions, improved subjectivity/sublimit/exclusion outcomes, bind/renewal progress events, or clean no-go decisions; build a private taxonomy of underwriter wording, requested proof, bank/ERP/AP evidence formats, acceptable answer language, no-go patterns, and broker submission outcomes; keep gross margin above 65% after insurance-boundary reviewer and finance-control reviewer costs; reject any case where the insured wants the startup to overstate controls or answer the insurance application for them.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with cyber/crime insurance brokers, regional broker networks, accounting firms, outsourced CFOs, MSPs that support finance systems, and SMEs that recently saw BEC or invoice-fraud scares. Partner pitch: "When an underwriter asks your client for payment-control proof before giving or restoring funds-transfer/social-engineering coverage, route the subjectivity file to me. You keep insurance advice and placement. I assemble CFO-approved AP, bank, ERP, vendor-change, callback, training, and escalation evidence into a broker-ready answer or a clean no-go." Direct outreach targets CFOs and owners using narrow language around blocked funds-transfer/social-engineering coverage, not general cyber consulting.

Economics:
Charge 7,000-10,000 PLN for a paid no-go/triage packet, 12,000-24,000 PLN for a complete broker-ready FTF subjectivity release pack, and 25,000-45,000 PLN for urgent multi-entity, lender/customer-required, or board-deadline cases. Broker wholesale pricing can preserve broker margin. The fee is rational when a 12,000-24,000 PLN packet helps preserve policy bind, avoid a severe sublimit/exclusion, satisfy a customer or lender insurance condition, or reduce premium/friction on a material policy. Capital stays below 100,000 PLN because the startup does not place insurance, buy claims, finance premiums, or implement enterprise systems.

Copy risk:
Insurance brokers, MGAs, underwriters, accountants, outsourced CFOs, MSPs, cyber consultants, internal finance teams, and GRC tools can copy pieces. The defense is narrow subjectivity focus, live broker-routed deadlines, underwriter wording memory, CFO/AP evidence templates, broker submission format memory, no-go discipline, and a private outcome library for FTF/social-engineering payment-control proof rather than broad cyber controls.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the insured and broker sign the mandate, provide the exact subjectivity, policy deadline, requested coverage, AP/bank/ERP owner, and evidence access, the startup controls the evidence room, missing-proof log, broker-ready packet, insured approval workflow, underwriter response tracker, and fee right for that named bind or renewal blocker. A broker, accountant, MSP, or consultant can compete generally, but it must displace the already-authorized case while the underwriter deadline is running.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business refuses broad cyber readiness, generic insurance questionnaires, policy shopping, claims advocacy, coverage advice, dashboards, marketplaces, and payment-process consulting without a live underwriter subjectivity. It accepts only named FTF/social-engineering bind or renewal blockers and produces a broker-ready evidence packet, insured-approved answer set, missing-proof/no-go log, and outcome record. The buyer pays to move a specific policy or commercial requirement, not to receive a generic report.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not act as insurance broker, MGA, underwriter, claims advocate, lawyer, auditor, accountant, cyber assessor, payment-service provider, or control certifier. It does not state coverage, compare policies, recommend insurance placement, submit application answers without insured approval, certify that controls are effective, or help misrepresent weak controls. Brokers, insurers, counsel, auditors, accountants, MSPs, banks, ERP administrators, and the insured remain responsible for insurance placement, coverage interpretation, final application answers, control operation, legal/accounting treatment, bank permissions, and underwriter decisions. Cases involving active fraud, claim disputes, suspected misstatement, nonexistent controls, executive pressure to overstate controls, or legal/coverage interpretation are declined or routed to qualified advisers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it is not regulated-finance ICT supplier evidence, Article 28/30, bank procurement, or DORA questionnaire response. It is not CyberBind's broad cyber-insurance evidence branch because the control artifact is finance/AP payment-control proof for funds-transfer/social-engineering subjectivities, not MFA/EDR/backup/patching cyber-control evidence. It is not PromoLeak because it is not retail deduction recovery. It is not CBAM, REDBlocked, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, GreenTender EPREL, BatteryFit, HeritageDoor, BioSignal, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, TraceFaktura, Amazon recovery, 3PL recovery, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Insurance brokers may already handle underwriter subjectivities. Underwriters can still decline, sublimit, exclude, surcharge, or ask more questions even after a good packet. Many cases will reveal absent or weak controls, forcing no-go or implementation outside scope. Misstatement risk is severe if the insured wants to make controls sound stronger than they are. Accountants, outsourced CFOs, MSPs, and cyber consultants can assemble AP/bank evidence. SMEs may resist 12,000-24,000 PLN fees unless the coverage or commercial consequence is clearly large. Broker channels may compress margins or keep the work. Access to bank portal roles, AP approvals, vendor master files, and payment logs is sensitive. The business may be episodic around renewals rather than recurring. It can drift into generic payment-controls consulting unless the live underwriter subjectivity filter is enforced.
