## Selected Candidate

**MachineBuild FAT/SAT Payment Release Desk**

### Why It Advances

The strongest version does not solve disputed machine performance. It accepts only cases where source evidence exists and the buyer's condition is procedural: accepted FAT protocol, serial-numbered machine file, test-run videos, checklist closure, manuals/O&M index, spare-parts list, training proof, punch-list disposition, SAT handover, or acceptance certificate. The proof event is cash movement or buyer acceptance, not a report.

The candidate is vulnerable to the same source-truth problem as MillCert: if the machine failed, documents are missing, or performance is disputed, there is nothing to package. It clears simulation only because final milestone payments in custom machinery can be large enough to justify a paid sprint and because the founder can reject technical disputes.

### 60-Day Proof Required

- Five paid mandates from CEE machine builders, automation integrators, or industrial equipment exporters with written buyer hold text and blocked value above 150,000 PLN.
- Ten named machine/order files processed.
- Three buyer responses confirming acceptance progress, narrowed punch list, shipment release, warranty-start trigger, or final-payment release.
- One final payment, staged payment, shipment release, or acceptance certificate completed.
- At least 70,000 PLN collected in fixed fees and success fees.
- More than 40% of intake rejected as real performance disputes, missing source truth, safety/certification issues, or legal/commercial fights.

**88.1 / 100**

Reason for advancing: live final-payment pressure, named buyer condition text, high average line value, measurable cash or acceptance proof, and strong reject/no-go boundaries. It stays just over the gate because QA/engineering incumbents, source-truth gaps, and payment-dispute drift are serious.
