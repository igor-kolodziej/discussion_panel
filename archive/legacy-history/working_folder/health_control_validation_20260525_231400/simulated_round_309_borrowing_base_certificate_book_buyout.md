## Internal Cap Check

- Concrete 6-month control proof exists: seller option, customer reauthorizations, prior accepted lender reports, lender templates, secure vault transfer, current invoices, payment-direction transfer, first month submitted reports, and customer/lender non-objection.
- First proof is current cash movement: customers pay the new operator for the current reporting period before seller payout; reports are submitted and accepted or no-go'd.
- This is materially stronger than Round 308 because it controls recurring demand and inherited trust before urgent deficiency rescue is sold.
- Copy risk remains high: accountants, controllers, fractional CFOs, and lenders can copy the work, but they cannot copy a specific transferred book with customer authorizations and payment direction.
- Seller sourcing is the biggest risk. A clean book must be small enough for a 100,000 PLN option/earnout structure but organized enough to transfer safely.
- The product must reject broad accounting cleanup, CFO advisory, loan brokerage, lender negotiation, insolvency, fraud, and records so broken that seller history cannot be trusted.
- Internal score clears the gate because the candidate uses the highest-signal pattern from prior near-passes: buy/option a live book, transfer payment direction, prove current-month transactions, then close.
