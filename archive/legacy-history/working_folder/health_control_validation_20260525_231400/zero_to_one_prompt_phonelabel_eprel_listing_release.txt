Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
PhoneLabel EPREL Listing Release

One-sentence thesis:
A 5-business-day listing-release pack for EU/CEE electronics importers, device distributors, marketplace agencies, and private-label phone/tablet sellers whose retailer, marketplace, public buyer, or B2B customer onboarding is blocked by the new EU smartphone/tablet energy-label, EPREL, repairability, battery-endurance, reliability, IP-rating, spare-parts, or software-update evidence requirements.

Exact buyer:
CEE electronics importers, rugged-tablet distributors, private-label smartphone/tablet sellers, refurbished-device brands selling new models/accessory bundles, marketplace agencies, B2B device resellers, school/public-sector tablet suppliers, and ecommerce compliance agencies handling models placed on the EU market after the June 2025 smartphone/tablet ecodesign and energy-labelling rules began applying.

Acute trigger:
A retailer, marketplace, distributor, school/public buyer, ecommerce platform, or B2B customer blocks listing, onboarding, tender submission, marketplace expansion, or paid order release because the seller cannot provide model-matched EU energy label, EPREL entry, product information sheet, repairability class, battery endurance/cycle evidence, reliability/drop class, IP rating, spare-parts/software-update support evidence, supplier declaration, or correct online-label display materials.

Control point:
Signed live listing/onboarding evidence mandate with the retailer/marketplace/customer request, SKU/GTIN/model list, supplier identity, model launch/placing-on-market date, EPREL model links or absence, energy labels, product information sheets, repairability/reliability/battery/IP fields, supplier declarations, spare-parts/software-update evidence, screenshot pack for online listing, mismatch table, supplier RFI, customer response wording, and no-go log. The startup controls the active listing-release evidence file, not official manufacturer/importer obligations.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three marketplace agencies, electronics distributors, or device importers route at least one live case each; ten prepaid live cases at 4,000-15,000 PLN each; every case has a blocked listing, onboarding, tender, marketplace expansion, or customer order tied to smartphone/tablet energy-label or EPREL evidence; at least five cases involve repairability, battery endurance, reliability/drop, IP rating, spare-parts, software-update, or model-mismatch issues rather than simply downloading an obvious label; at least five cases produce accepted evidence, listing/onboarding progress, supplier correction, or clean no-go; gross margin above 60%.

6-month POC:
Handle 30-70 paid cases through 5-10 agencies/distributors/importers; collect 180,000-500,000 PLN in gross fees; keep turnaround under 5 business days for evidence-ready cases; get at least three channels to send 5+ cases; document 20+ outcomes as listing/onboarding progress, accepted label/PIS/EPREL evidence, supplier correction, retailer answer, tender progress, or clean no-go; build a private taxonomy of retailer/marketplace wording, EPREL/model mismatch, repairability/battery/IP evidence gaps, supplier response patterns, and accepted answer formats.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 80 electronics importers, rugged tablet distributors, Amazon/ecommerce agencies, school/public-sector tablet suppliers, and private-label device sellers. Offer: "If your EU phone/tablet listing or customer onboarding is blocked by EPREL, energy-label, repairability, battery-endurance, IP, spare-parts, or software-update evidence, send the request, model list, supplier docs, and EPREL links. I return a 5-day listing-release pack: evidence matrix, missing fields, supplier RFI, customer response wording, screenshots, and clean no-go."

Economics:
Charge 4,000-6,000 PLN for evidence-ready single-model cases, 7,000-12,000 PLN for multi-model or mismatch cases, and 12,000-15,000 PLN for urgent tender/customer-order cases with repairability, battery, IP, spare-parts, or software-update evidence gaps. Reviewer cost is low for EPREL/label checks and 1,000-4,000 PLN where technical documentation or legal-boundary review is needed. The buyer pays because listing, marketplace expansion, tender submission, or customer onboarding is blocked.

Copy risk:
Manufacturers, importers, electronics compliance consultants, marketplace agencies, retailers, and EPREL tooling providers can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-release focus, CEE importer and agency channels, request wording memory, model-mismatch taxonomy, supplier RFI templates, accepted retailer/marketplace answer formats, and fast case handling for non-EU supplier documentation gaps.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the importer/agency signs the mandate, prepays, and shares the customer request, SKU/model list, supplier files, EPREL links or missing records, and listing deadline, the startup controls the active evidence-response file for that listing/onboarding event. A generic consultant would need to displace the customer-specific evidence matrix, supplier RFI, screenshot pack, mismatch log, and customer response workflow under deadline pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live listing, marketplace, tender, onboarding, or order blockers tied to smartphone/tablet energy-label and EPREL evidence. The output is a release artifact used in an active customer workflow: evidence matrix, model/GTIN/EPREL match table, label/PIS screenshot pack, repairability/battery/IP/spare-parts/software-update evidence map, supplier RFI, response wording, and no-go log. It is not generic EPREL readiness, software, dashboard, broad compliance consulting, public database scraping, or brokered introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not create official EPREL registrations unless the responsible supplier/importer uses its own authorized account; does not provide legal advice, conformity assessment, lab testing, product certification, manufacturer/importer role determination, or guarantees of retailer/marketplace acceptance. Manufacturers, importers, suppliers, authorized representatives, retailers, marketplaces, and legal/compliance advisers remain responsible for EPREL registration, technical documentation, testing, product claims, labels, and legal compliance. Cases with missing official data, false supplier claims, mismatched model identifiers, forged labels, or unclear importer authority are declined or routed to qualified advisers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is adjacent to GreenTender EPREL only in using EPREL as a proof source. It is materially different in buyer, product, trigger, and artifact: smartphone/tablet retailer/marketplace/tender listing release after the 2025 phone/tablet ecodesign and energy-label rules, not public lighting tender bid packs for LED distributors. It also includes repairability, battery endurance, reliability, IP rating, spare-parts, software-update, and online-label display evidence, not lighting luminaire bid evidence. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, ButtonCellBlocked, EPRBlocked, or any health/Rx route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Large manufacturers and serious importers should already handle EPREL and labels. Simple cases may be solved by downloading the label and product information sheet. Retailers and marketplaces may standardize the workflow quickly. Many blockers may require the actual responsible supplier to register or correct EPREL data, which is outside the founder's control. Electronics compliance consultants and EPREL tooling providers can copy the service. The idea risks being perceived as a GreenTender EPREL variant unless phone/tablet repairability, battery, IP, software-update, and model-mismatch cases show distinct repeated pain.
