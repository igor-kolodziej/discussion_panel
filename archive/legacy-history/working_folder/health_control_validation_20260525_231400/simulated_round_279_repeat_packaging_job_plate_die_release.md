# Simulated Round 279: RepeatPack Current-Job Plate/Die Release Lockbox

Date: 2026-05-31 Europe/Warsaw

## Search Frame

Recent failures show that ordinary recovery mandates, admin books, platform cases, and physical lots are capped when the buyer can use incumbents or when the founder only coordinates evidence. This round tests a narrower current-cash variant of the best recent book-buyout signal: control of exact production-memory assets for a live repeat packaging run.

The target is not a generic prepress book. It is a current packaging print job where shipment, retailer delivery, launch, or customer payment is blocked because a retiring broker, failed prepress operator, old printer, plate house, or production freelancer controls the exact repeat-run assets: source files, approved proofs, color targets, cutter/rotary dies, flexo plates, sleeves, barcode/version history, job notes, and approval emails.

## Lead Candidate

**RepeatPack Current-Job Plate/Die Release Lockbox**

### One-Sentence Thesis

Become the paid release operator for current repeat packaging jobs where brands or suppliers cannot print on time because an old broker, retiring prepress operator, failed printer, plate house, or freelancer controls the exact approved production assets.

### Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- The buyer has a current payment/shipment event: retailer delivery, private-label launch, seasonal stock, export shipment, or customer invoice depends on the repeat packaging run.
- The control point is not a customer list. It is exact production memory: files, fonts/links, proofs, color standards, barcode/version history, print notes, plates, dies, sleeves, approval emails, and job tickets.
- The founder can require customer authorization and current job deposit before paying the seller or spending on cleanup.
- The first proof is a released print job, accepted printer handoff, paid no-go, or current PO protected, not a report.
- Compared with prior RepeatPack/PackPlate attempts, the scope rejects generic print brokerage and continuity subscriptions unless a named current job is at risk.

### Internal Cap Pressure

- Printers, plate houses, prepress shops, and packaging agencies are natural incumbents.
- File ownership can be messy across brand, printer, agency, designer, plate house, and broker.
- Regulated label content can create liability; the founder must avoid legal, claims, ingredients, allergen, pharma, supplement, food-contact, recycling, and safety-mark review.
- Customers can recreate files if time and budget allow.
- Seller supply is episodic unless retiring brokers, failed printers, and plate houses become a repeat channel.

The candidate advances because the narrowest form controls a current job and exact assets before the incumbent can recreate them, and because payment conversion is visible inside 60 days.

## 60-Day Control Proof

- 3 signed seller/source agreements from retiring brokers, prepress operators, old printers, plate houses, or packaging freelancers covering current-job asset release terms.
- 6 prepaid customer-authorized live repeat-job release cases, each with a current PO, shipment/retailer/customer deadline, or printer-booking date.
- Custody or authorized transfer of at least 80 SKUs/files/proofs/plates/dies across those cases.
- One packaging prepress/color contractor on paid per-case terms.
- 3 printer-accepted release packets or physical/digital asset handoffs.
- 2 print jobs released, rescheduled successfully, or converted into paid no-go/recreation decisions.
- 60,000-180,000 PLN collected in release fees, urgent asset-transfer fees, and paid no-go decisions.
- Gross margin above 55% after seller payouts, contractor review, secure file storage, courier/asset handling, and rejection reserve.

## Decision

Advance to Zero To One working-chat validation.
