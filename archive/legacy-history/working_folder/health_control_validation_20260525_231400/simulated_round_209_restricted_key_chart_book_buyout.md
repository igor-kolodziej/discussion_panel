## Selected Candidate

**Restricted-Key Chart Continuity Book Buyout**

### Why This Advanced

The candidate improves on ordinary micro-acquisition failures because the book contains a switching-cost asset:

- installed restricted key systems;
- master-key and cylinder charts;
- customer authorization records;
- blank/keyway access;
- current reorder need;
- seller endorsement;
- customer-approved payment transfer.

If a building loses continuity, the alternative can be full rekeying, security review, or switching locksmiths with incomplete chart reconstruction. That makes the continuity book materially stronger than generic consumables, fiscal-device service, or software support books.

### Control Point

- Seller option over key charts, bitting records, master-key hierarchy, restricted blank stock, cylinder/core records, authorization lists, customer contracts/invoices, phone/domain/mailbox, supplier/manufacturer route, and seller endorsement.
- Customer reauthorization for key/cylinder orders and payment direction.
- Secure chain-of-custody, access logs, encrypted storage, and named locksmith contractor for cutting/pinning.
- No emergency lockouts, no open-ended security consulting, and no unauthorized duplication.

### 60-Day Proof

- Sign one retiring commercial locksmith seller option.
- Verify at least 30 active commercial/building sites with restricted key charts.
- Obtain 10 customer reauthorizations for controlled key/cylinder ordering and payment transfer.
- Complete 50 authorized key, cylinder, core, or restricted-blank orders through a qualified locksmith route.
- Collect at least 25,000 PLN before seller payout.
- Establish secure chart custody, dual-control access, customer authorization checks, and documented no-go rules.

### Economics

- 80-250 PLN gross margin per restricted key/order line where authorization exists.
- 300-1,500 PLN gross margin per cylinder/core/rekey subset.
- 1,500-6,000 PLN annual key-control continuity retainers for larger property managers or campuses.
- Seller payout mostly earnout on collected orders and retained customers, keeping upfront cash below 100,000 PLN.
- Margins work only if the book is geographically/customer dense and support is limited to authorized key/cylinder continuity.

### Internal Caps Applied

- Capped below 92 because commercial locksmiths and security integrators are natural acquirers and can copy future books.
- Capped below 90 because security trust, secure custody, and locksmith contractor reliability are real risks.
- Not capped below 87 because the founder controls a specific installed-system chart book that customers cannot easily recreate without cost, not just a service list or commodity route.
