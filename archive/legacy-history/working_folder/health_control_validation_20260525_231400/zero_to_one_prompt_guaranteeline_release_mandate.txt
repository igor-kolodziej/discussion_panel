Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
GuaranteeLine Release Mandate

One-sentence thesis:
Recover and cancel already-releasable bank guarantees, performance bonds, warranty bonds, bid bonds, and related cash-margin items for contractors and B2B suppliers whose completed projects still consume bank limit or collateral.

Exact buyer:
CEE construction subcontractors, machine builders, industrial service firms, equipment exporters, and public-procurement suppliers with active guarantee schedules where old guarantees are still consuming bank limit, cash margin, or annual fees after delivery, acceptance, tender close, or warranty expiry.

Acute trigger:
The buyer cannot issue a new guarantee, bid on a tender, free a cash margin deposit, or renew a bank facility cleanly because old guarantees remain active even though the contractual release condition appears to have happened. The buyer can show a bank guarantee ledger with guarantee numbers, beneficiaries, expiry or release conditions, bank fees, and collateral or limit usage.

Control point:
A signed release mandate over named guarantee/collateral items, plus the active bank guarantee ledger, contract/tender acceptance evidence, warranty-expiry evidence, beneficiary contacts, bank cancellation requirements, and authority to coordinate release packets through buyer-approved channels.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days the founder must have signed mandates covering at least 10 named guarantee items or 500,000 PLN equivalent of guarantee/collateral capacity, complete release packets for each accepted case, at least two beneficiary release letters or written release approvals, and at least one bank cancellation, collateral release, fee stop, or credit-limit release. Cases with performance disputes, open defects, penalties, insolvency, litigation, or unclear release conditions are rejected.

6-month POC:
Run 30-50 guarantee reviews from 8-15 suppliers, accept only undisputed releasable items, obtain beneficiary release approvals, submit bank cancellation packets, and prove at least 1.5-3.0 million PLN of guarantee limit or collateral released or annual guarantee fees stopped. Track no-go reasons by beneficiary type, bank form, release clause, and missing evidence so the process becomes a repeatable cancellation playbook.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start through bank-guarantee brokers, surety brokers, tender writers, fractional CFOs, accountants, and construction/industrial controllers who see active guarantee schedules but do not want to chase old beneficiary releases one by one. The first outreach asks for an active guarantee ledger review, not a generic finance consultation.

Economics:
Charge a 1,500-4,000 PLN prepaid dossier fee for each accepted guarantee item or bundle, plus 3-8% of released cash collateral or a success fee tied to the first-year bank fee/capacity value released. Gross margin is high because the work is document assembly, beneficiary routing, and bank cancellation coordination; contractors or admin help can be added per case. Payback is immediate if a blocked new tender, bank line, cash-margin deposit, or recurring guarantee fee is released.

Copy risk:
CFOs, accountants, project managers, banks, tender consultants, quantity surveyors, and guarantee brokers can all chase releases. The defensible wedge is the signed queue of named guarantee items, beneficiary-specific cancellation memory, release-packet templates, and refusal discipline around disputed construction or performance issues.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Banks issue or cancel guarantees but usually do not chase beneficiaries for old release letters. Project teams close work but often do not own bank-limit pressure. Accountants see guarantee fees but not the acceptance trail. Brokers focus on issuing new guarantees. Once the founder has a signed mandate over a named active guarantee, the guarantee ledger, beneficiary route, and accepted release evidence, a competitor cannot release that exact item without the buyer reassigning the case.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The deliverable is not advice, a list, a dashboard, or an introduction. Each accepted unit is a named guarantee/collateral item with a mandate, release packet, beneficiary release action, and bank cancellation or collateral/limit release. If the guarantee cannot be released, the case is closed as no-go instead of becoming ongoing consulting.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not issue guarantees, broker new bank products, provide legal opinions, litigate, negotiate disputed settlement amounts, act as a bank, or replace the buyer's lawyer, quantity surveyor, or project manager. It only handles undisputed administrative cancellation of guarantees where the buyer can document that the release condition has already occurred. Any disputed defect, penalty, insolvency, litigation, fraud, or unclear contractual interpretation is referred out or rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from PromoLeak, which recovers retail promotion and deduction leakage, and from CBAM Import Continuity Desk, which preserves import compliance continuity. It is also distinct from TraceFaktura and invoice-release ideas because the controlled object is not an invoice or municipal payment packet; it is a bank guarantee/collateral item consuming credit capacity after the underlying obligation should be releasable.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Many buyers should already have finance teams, project managers, accountants, or guarantee brokers who can chase releases. Beneficiaries may delay or refuse because of undocumented defects or commercial leverage. Construction and public-procurement files often hide disputes that make the release condition less clear than the buyer claims. Banks and brokers could add this as a service if enough demand appears. A part-time founder may struggle with beneficiary follow-up across many small cases. The idea only works if case values are large enough, release conditions are objective, and the founder refuses messy retention, defect, litigation, or settlement files.
