## Simulated Pass Candidate

### AzbestKPO All-Property Settlement Rail - 90

One-sentence thesis: sell a prepaid all-property evidence routing pilot to asbestos-removal contractors so every gmina-funded property removal produces the BDO/KPO, photo, protocol, weight, owner, landfill, and invoice-line packet needed for contractor payment and fund/gmina settlement.

Buyer: licensed asbestos-removal/waste contractors serving multiple Polish gminas under 2026 seasonal removal, collection, transport, and disposal contracts, especially contractors that must process lists of private properties and settle actual removed quantities.

Acute trigger: missing or inconsistent property-level documentation can delay contractor invoice acceptance, gmina reimbursement/fund settlement, or final contract acceptance. The contractor cannot reconstruct reliable evidence after crews leave each property.

Transferable control point: a signed paid pilot requiring all current jobs for two named gminas to be routed through the evidence rail first, plus written gmina packet-format acknowledgement/correction, plus live property-event ledger and invoice packet generation for current-month work.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 5,000-15,000 PLN prepaid pilot from one contractor.
- All-property routing mandate for at least two named gminas or one gmina with 20+ property removals.
- Property list/work-order import and field capture flow for scheduled removals.
- Gmina packet-format acknowledgement, field correction, or usable feedback on the proposed property settlement packet.
- 10-30 live property events captured with photos, property IDs, dates, crew/vehicle, owner confirmation where available, KPO number/status or exported KPO PDF supplied by the responsible party, actual weight/quantity, landfill/recipient evidence, protocol, and invoice-line linkage.
- At least one invoice/gmina/fund settlement packet generated from live captured events.

First acquisition mechanism: build a target list from public BIP/procurement notices, gmina asbestos-program pages, WFOŚiGW program references, and contractor award documents. Contact contractors with named gmina/property-list evidence, not generic software messaging. Offer a 60-day paid pilot that starts only if the contractor mandates all-property routing for the selected current jobs.

Gross margin and payback logic: first pilot fee of 5,000-15,000 PLN funds setup and manual QA. Post-pilot pricing can be 1,500-4,000 PLN/month during active season plus 50-150 PLN per property packet or a small percentage of documented invoice value, depending on volume. Manual QA is acceptable at first because packet errors can delay meaningful contract/fund payments. The founder should not build a broad SaaS before one contractor proves that cleaner packets reduce month-end reconstruction or payment friction.

Copy risk: BDO tools, waste consultants, environmental software, spreadsheets, generic field-service apps, and the contractor's internal admin can copy pieces. The defense is entering the contractor's live all-property workflow before invoice settlement, learning the exact gmina/fund packet rules, and owning the source ledger for current jobs.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured: once a contractor routes all selected gmina properties through the rail and the gmina has corrected or acknowledged the packet format, a later competitor cannot recreate the original timestamped property events, missing-field catches, photos, KPO references, weight/protocol links, or gmina-specific settlement memory for that work package.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker: the business is not writing environmental advice or selling a dashboard. It controls a paid live event routing mandate and produces settlement packets from source events. The contractor and licensed waste chain remain responsible for asbestos work, BDO obligations, KPO issuance/status, worker safety, transport, landfill transfer, and legal/environmental compliance.

Reasons this could still fail:
- Contractors may already have sufficient spreadsheets/BDO exports and not value an extra rail.
- Gmina payment friction may be weaker than assumed.
- Seasonal timing may make 60-day proof hard if no active property list is available.
- Field crews may resist extra capture unless the contractor mandates it.
- The founder must avoid acting as environmental consultant, BDO responsible party, asbestos safety adviser, or waste broker.
- Unit economics can be too small if property volumes are low or contractors have thin margins.

Decision: advance this candidate to real Zero To One validation.
