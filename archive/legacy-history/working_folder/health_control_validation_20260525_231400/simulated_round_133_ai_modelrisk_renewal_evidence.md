## Selected Candidate

## Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- The control point is a live bank/insurer renewal or procurement case, not broad AI compliance readiness.
- The buyer has current contract value at risk; fees can be paid out of a renewal, expansion, or blocked procurement motion.
- The artifact is concrete: bank request matrix, use-case boundary, model/system inventory, data-flow map, eval evidence, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, logs, incident/change process, and customer-specific response package.
- The founder can refuse regulated credit/insurance underwriting, clinical, biometric, employment scoring, and legal conclusions while still helping AI workflow vendors answer bank model-risk requests.
- The duplicate risk with DORA is real but not fatal if the proof artifact stays materially different: AI system behavior/model-risk evidence instead of ICT operational-resilience third-party-risk evidence.
- Copy risk exists, but a live renewal request plus customer-specific evidence room is harder to displace quickly than a generic AI governance consulting offer.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Not capped below 87: CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion are credible if cases are accepted only when renewal/PO value is at least 200,000 PLN or EUR-equivalent and the fee is prepaid.
