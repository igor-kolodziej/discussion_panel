## Search Posture

Recent failures show that exact-site control is not enough when the business still has to sell campaigns, manage installation, or borrow buyer trust. This round keeps the local asset logic but changes the proof object to an already-paying recurring rent stream.

The selected candidate buys or takes a success-fee lockbox over small property-host receivables where a creditworthy operator already pays monthly or quarterly rent/revenue share for a parcel locker, ATM, vending bank, telecom cabinet, ad cabinet, microcell, EV charger, or similar installed machine. The founder advances cash or administers a payment-direction assignment only after the operator/payer acknowledges the assignment or redirect.

## Selected Candidate

**Micro-Infrastructure Host Rent Stream Assignment**

One-sentence thesis: Small landlords and site owners hosting parcel lockers, ATMs, vending banks, telecom cabinets, microcells, EV chargers, or similar installed machines will sell or lockbox already-paying host-rent streams when the founder secures payer acknowledgement and first redirected payment before capital is committed.

### Why It Clears Simulation

The control point is harder than a service mandate:

- there is an existing machine/site contract;
- the operator has already been paying rent, commission, or revenue share;
- the founder accepts only non-disputed rent streams with clear owner authority;
- the founder commits capital only after written payer acknowledgement or accepted payment-direction change;
- the first proof is redirected rent or operator-confirmed scheduled payment.

This avoids the repeated AP/recovery failure where the decisive payer action remains vague. Here the payer acknowledgement itself is the purchase condition.

### Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof: passes if payer acknowledgement and first redirected payment are required.
- One vendor/consultant copy risk: partially passes. Factors, operators, and landlords can copy future streams, but not an already-assigned and acknowledged rent stream.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: passes only with 15-35% discount to remaining stream value, minimum 1,000 PLN monthly rent/commission, and no capital before acknowledgement.
- Vague partnerships/goodwill: passes only if each stream has contract, authority, payer statement, and assignment notice.
- Legal/provider limits: passes only with counsel-reviewed assignment form, no consumer lending, no deposit-taking, no factoring of disputed invoices, and no operator contract breach.

Simulated score: **88.1 / 100**

## 6-Month POC

1. Source 50-100 host contracts through property managers, small landlords, retail strips, fuel stations, gyms, schools, car washes, storage sites, and industrial properties.
2. Accept only existing paid host streams from creditworthy operators, with no site dispute, no maintenance dispute, no arrears, and clear host authority.
3. Sign 10 host mandates/options.
4. Obtain 3-5 operator acknowledgements or accepted payment-direction changes.
5. Buy or lockbox 3 streams only after payer acceptance.
6. Collect first redirected payments.
7. Keep exposure below 100,000 PLN and target a 20-35% gross spread over expected collected cash, or a 10-20% success fee where no upfront purchase is used.

## Kill Conditions

- Operators routinely prohibit assignment, ignore notices, or require full contract novation.
- Host rents are too small to support diligence and legal work.
- Property owners do not want upfront cash at a discount.
- Streams depend on site performance, footfall, revenue share, maintenance, or operator discretion rather than fixed rent.
- Payer acknowledgement takes longer than 30 days.
- Legal review indicates the model is regulated lending, factoring, debt collection, or financial intermediation beyond the POC structure.
- First 100 host outreach attempts produce fewer than 3 payer-acknowledged streams.
