## Fresh Chat Summary

Fresh Zero To One confirmed that the core-deposit mechanism is real and that OEM/reman programs tie credits to return condition, documentation, timing, matching part numbers, packaging, and acceptance criteria. It still scored the idea only 70 because the recoverable pool may collapse after supplier policy screens, old claims, missing proof, inspection results, and trust/distribution friction.

## Strongest Objections

1. "Open core value" is not the same as recoverable value; after age buckets, missing shipment proof, damaged cores, wrong part numbers, supplier-specific rules, and write-offs, recoverability may drop sharply.
2. Suppliers have the leverage; the startup can reconstruct evidence but cannot force credit approval.
3. The first sale requires trust before proof because the founder needs supplier statements, AP lines, portal access, branch records, shipment proofs, and credit memo history.
4. Prepaid fees may be hard initially because buyers may expect AP, parts managers, or supplier reps to do the work.
5. The real workflow may be labor-heavy: chasing branch staff, missing labels, inconsistent part numbers, old courier proofs, portal screenshots, invoice corrections, and supplier-specific exceptions.
6. No obvious regulatory, budget, or market shock forces buyers to act now.
7. The unknown Warsaw part-time founder has no stated parts/OEM background, creating a major execution and trust risk.
8. Case-file control is temporary; taxonomy and supplier memory help but do not create a hard moat.
9. Customers may use the desk once and then improve internal controls.
10. The 6-month POC targets were judged too aggressive without existing access to dealer groups, parts directors, fleet workshops, or reman distributors.

## Lesson

Working-chat cash-recovery optimism is not enough. Fresh validation is heavily penalizing ideas without a current forcing function, channel advantage, or founder credibility. Next rounds should preserve hard cash/control but add an external deadline, mandatory buyer gate, or channel that makes "why now" obvious and reduces trust friction.
