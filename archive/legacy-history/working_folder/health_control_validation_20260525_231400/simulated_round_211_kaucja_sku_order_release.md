## Selected Candidate

**Kaucja SKU Order Release Desk**

One-sentence thesis: unlock Polish beverage retail/distributor orders for named SKUs stuck on deposit-return-system readiness by controlling the operator onboarding file, deposit-mark label proof, SKU/EAN mapping, retailer/distributor acceptance, and released purchase order.

## Why It Passes Internal Simulation

- The Polish deposit-return system is already live, and 2026 is the first full year in which producers who joined the system can only market covered beverages with the required deposit mark.
- The payment event is concrete: a retailer, wholesaler, or distributor blocks listing, replenishment, or purchase orders until deposit-system readiness is proven.
- The proof is not a report: operator onboarding, label/deposit mark proof, SKU/EAN mapping, retailer/distributor acceptance, and a released PO or shipment authorization.
- The Warsaw location is a real advantage because this is Polish-language, operator-specific, retailer-specific, and deadline-driven.
- Incumbents exist, but most are compliance advisers, operators, printers, packaging agencies, or EPR consultants; they do not necessarily operate a 10-day order-release queue around named blocked SKUs.
- The POC is testable inside 60 days with a small number of importers/producers and current SKU blocks.

## Internal Cap Check

- Concrete 6-month control proof: yes. Operator onboarding plus retailer/distributor acceptance plus released POs.
- Copyable by one vendor contract/equipment purchase/consultant/hire: partially, but the case-specific control over exact SKUs, retailer requests, label proofs, and operator files is stronger than generic consulting.
- CAC, payback, margins, cash conversion, owner earnings: credible if focused only on blocked purchase orders above 75,000 PLN and priced as urgent release, not hourly compliance.
- Vague partnerships/goodwill/interviews: avoid. Require named buyer hold and signed authorization before work.
- Regulated-data/legal authority: manageable if boundaries exclude legal certification and keep producer/operator responsibilities intact.
