## Simulated Pass Candidate

### IMDS PPAP Material-Data Release Desk - 90

One-sentence thesis: a prepaid live-case desk for Polish/CEE automotive suppliers whose PPAP/ISIR/customer part approval is blocked by missing or rejected IMDS material data, producing source-evidence-backed IMDS submissions and accepted MDS IDs for named part numbers.

Buyer: Polish/CEE Tier 2/3 automotive component suppliers with live OEM/Tier 1 customer requests or rejections around IMDS MDS submissions. Best first buyers are too small to have a full material-compliance team but large enough that a delayed PPAP, SOP, shipment, or customer approval costs real money.

Acute trigger: a customer launch, PPAP/ISIR submission, engineering change, requalification, or part approval is blocked because an IMDS material data sheet is missing, rejected, incomplete, incorrectly structured, or unsupported by supplier material data.

Transferable control point: prepaid current case with customer request/rejection text, supplier authorization, part numbers, BOM/material/SDS/cert evidence, supplier declaration chain, IMDS account/submission path, expert review, live rejection/acceptance tracking, and resulting MDS ID/customer progress.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:

- 3 prepaid live IMDS/PPAP blocker cases from at least 2 suppliers or one quality/IATF consultant channel.
- One IMDS/material-compliance reviewer on paid per-case terms.
- Supplier authorization and data-handling terms.
- Source evidence folders for at least 10 part numbers or 3 part families.
- At least 5 MDS submissions, revisions, or customer-response packets created from real source evidence.
- At least 2 accepted MDS IDs, customer-narrowed rejection reasons, or PPAP/ISIR progress outcomes.
- First 20,000-50,000 PLN collected in setup, urgent case, or per-part-family fees.

First acquisition mechanism: target Polish/CEE automotive quality consultants, IATF 16949 consultants, PPAP/APQP freelancers, and Tier 2/3 suppliers in plastics, rubber, stamped metal, castings, fasteners, cable harnesses, electronics housings, surface coatings, and assemblies. Ask only for live IMDS rejection/request cases tied to customer approval, not general compliance curiosity.

Gross margin and payback logic: price 2,000-4,000 PLN for triage, 4,000-12,000 PLN per part family or accepted submission package, and 15,000-30,000 PLN for urgent launch sprints with multiple related parts. Reviewer cost is scoped per case. AI/data leverage helps parse BOMs, SDSs, supplier declarations, GADSL/IMDS structure, rejection reasons, and reusable evidence checklists, while the expert validates boundaries.

Copy risk: IMDS consultants, quality consultants, compliance firms, and internal quality teams can do the work. The defense is narrow Polish/CEE mid-market focus, accepted/rejected customer-specific case memory, fast launch-block response, source-evidence workflow, and relationships with quality consultants that do not want to staff IMDS specialist work.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured: once the startup controls the paid live case, customer rejection trail, part numbers, source evidence folders, supplier authorization, reviewer notes, and submission status, competitors cannot act on that exact case without the supplier switching. The startup's case memory compounds by customer/OEM, material type, rejection pattern, part family, and supplier-data gap.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker: the output is an accepted or customer-actionable IMDS MDS submission for named part numbers, not a compliance report or dashboard. The proof is portal/customer progress: MDS ID accepted, rejection reason resolved or narrowed, PPAP/ISIR package progressed, or no-go due missing source truth.

Reasons this could still fail:

- Good IMDS consultants and automotive quality firms already exist.
- Missing supplier material truth cannot be invented.
- Automotive suppliers may expect their quality team or Tier 1 customer to guide them.
- Some cases require lab/material analysis, supplier renegotiation, or legal/compliance decisions outside scope.
- Data quality is often poor; BOM/SDS/material declarations can be incomplete or inconsistent.
- Part-time founder needs a credible reviewer and strict delivery boundaries.

Decision: advance this candidate to real Zero To One validation.
