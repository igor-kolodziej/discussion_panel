Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
RAGRedTeam Reviewer Slot Bank

One-sentence thesis:
A prepaid specialist-capacity lane that reserves named AI-security reviewer slots for appsec boutiques, SOC2 consultants, and fractional-CISO firms whose SaaS clients need bounded RAG or agent red-team test annexes for live enterprise buyer reviews.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized application-security boutiques, penetration-testing firms, SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, virtual security teams, and security-questionnaire firms serving 20-150 person B2B SaaS vendors with non-regulated RAG, AI assistant, knowledge-base, document-processing, customer-support automation, workflow automation, or tool-using agent features. The partner already owns the broader security engagement and client trust, but lacks reserved AI-security reviewer capacity for prompt injection, RAG source isolation, cross-tenant leakage, unsafe tool calls, excessive permissions, logging, and human-review evidence.

Acute trigger:
The partner's SaaS client has a live enterprise buyer, renewal, procurement, security review, or customer expansion blocked by a request for independent AI-security test evidence, RAG or agent red-team traces, prompt-injection checks, source-isolation proof, tool-permission testing, logging evidence, human-review evidence, or an AI-security annex inside the partner's security report. The buyer deadline is too urgent for the partner to recruit and train an AI-security specialist, and too narrow for a large AI red-team vendor's broad project cycle.

Control point:
Signed 90-day capacity contracts with two or more named AI-security reviewers covering bounded RAG/agent test slots, availability windows, rate cards, reviewer-signoff rules, confidentiality, conflict limits, and scope/no-go boundaries. Separately, signed partner slot agreements with appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO firms cover current client tickets, prepaid or partner-guaranteed slot fees, client authorization, report-annex integration, evidence handling inside partner workspaces, payment split, non-circumvention, and outcome reporting. For each slot, the startup controls the reserved reviewer calendar, scoped test plan, test traces, rework/no-go table, reviewer notes, buyer-safe annex, and deadline-specific delivery queue.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Two named AI-security reviewers sign paid capacity agreements for at least eight bounded RAG/agent review slots over 90 days; four appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO partners sign slot agreements; eight live client slots are prepaid or partner-guaranteed at 25,000-75,000 PLN each; every slot has a named SaaS client, buyer or security-review blocker, non-high-risk RAG/agent scope, authorized sandbox/test workspace, and partner report path; at least three slots produce reviewer-signed annexes accepted into partner reports or buyer responses; two partners buy a second slot; gross margin stays above 45% after reviewer costs.

6-month POC:
Maintain a bench of 4-6 named AI-security reviewers; sell 30-50 paid RAG/agent review slots through 8-12 appsec/SOC2/fractional-CISO partners; collect 1,000,000-2,500,000 PLN in slot fees; document 20+ buyer-progress outcomes, 15 partner-accepted annexes, 10 remediation/no-go decisions, and 5 accepted lower-risk deployment scopes; get at least five partners to buy 4+ slots; keep bounded delivery under 7 business days for evidence-ready products; maintain a private taxonomy of buyer requests, prompt-injection patterns, source-isolation failures, excessive-agency defects, logging gaps, human-review defects, reviewer caveats, accepted annex formats, and no-go triggers.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 150 Polish/CEE/EU appsec boutiques, small penetration-testing firms, SOC2 consultants, fractional CISOs, and security-questionnaire firms. Pitch only prepaid scarce-capacity economics: "Keep the client and the report. When your SaaS client has a RAG or agent feature and a buyer asks for independent AI-security test evidence, reserve a named reviewer slot through me. I bring the reviewer bench, bounded test plan, traces, caveats, and buyer-safe annex in seven business days; you remain the client-facing security provider."

Economics:
Charge partners 25,000-40,000 PLN for narrow RAG prompt-injection/source-isolation slots, 40,000-60,000 PLN for multi-feature RAG or tool-using agent slots, and 60,000-90,000 PLN for urgent high-value renewal or multi-tenant deployment slots. Direct-bill SaaS clients 50,000-120,000 PLN where the partner prefers referral economics. Named reviewer costs are 10,000-30,000 PLN per slot depending on scope and seniority, with a small upfront retainer to reserve availability. The partner pays or guarantees payment because the slot protects the partner's client relationship, adds a credible AI-security annex, and avoids losing urgent AI work to a larger red-team vendor.

Copy risk:
AI security vendors, application-security firms, penetration testers, red-team providers, GRC platforms, and internal security teams can copy pieces. The defense is reserved named reviewer capacity, signed partner slot lanes, partner-trusted delivery inside existing engagements, buyer-safe trace formatting, reviewer caveat discipline, accepted/rejected annex memory, and fast bounded slots before partners have enough volume to hire their own AI-security staff.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the reviewer capacity is reserved, the partner signs the slot agreement, the SaaS client authorizes the test workspace, and payment is prepaid or guaranteed for a named buyer deadline, the startup controls the live reviewer calendar, test slot, traces, reviewer notes, and report annex for that procurement event. A larger AI-security vendor or internal appsec team would need to displace the partner-approved reviewer slot while the buyer review is already waiting and the named reviewer capacity is already committed.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only prepaid or partner-guaranteed RAG/agent AI-security review slots tied to a current buyer, renewal, procurement, security-review, or partner-report deadline. The controlled unit is a reserved named-reviewer slot plus deadline-specific test annex, not a general consulting engagement. The output includes scoped test plan, prompt-injection traces, source-isolation checks, tool-permission tests, log/human-review evidence, reviewer-signed caveats, rework/no-go table, and buyer-safe annex wording. It is not generic AI-security consulting, legal AI governance, full red-team certification, product remediation, SaaS, dashboard, database, marketplace, template pack, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, AI Act classification opinions, DPIAs, high-risk AI conformity assessment, SOC2 certification, ISO 42001 implementation, formal penetration-test certification, model validation, safety certification, product remediation, production incident response, or guarantees of buyer approval. Named reviewers sign only bounded technical observations within the agreed test scope. The partner remains the client-facing security provider and owns final report framing, legal escalation, certification claims, and remediation scope. Cases involving regulated high-risk AI, autonomous adverse decisions, clinical data, credit/insurance/HR decisioning, biometrics, law enforcement, live production sensitive data without proper authorization, destructive agent actions, or buyer demands for formal certification are declined or routed to qualified firms.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA because it does not handle ICT third-party-risk, Article 28/30 DORA evidence, exit plans, subcontracting, audit rights, or regulated-finance operational resilience. It is not RAGProbe Buyer Security Evidence Sprint because the hard control point is reserved named reviewer capacity and prepaid partner slots, not a standalone sprint controlled by the founder. It is not RAGModule AppSec Overflow Bench because the buyer is purchasing scarce reviewer slots with formal reviewer sign-off, not only a white-label annex module. It is not AIQ Partner Ticket Book because the artifact is bounded red-team traces and reviewer caveats, not questionnaire answer packaging. It is not AI Model-Risk Renewal Evidence Pack, VoiceAI Article 50, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
This may collapse into a brokered expert-services lane if the startup cannot standardize intake, trace capture, caveat language, annex format, and partner workflow. AI-security vendors and appsec firms are natural incumbents. Named reviewers may be hard to reserve, expensive, inconsistent, or unwilling to sign tight turnaround commitments. Partner wholesale margins can compress after reviewer costs. Buyers may demand formal penetration-test reports, certification, legal AI classification, ISO 42001, or model validation outside scope. Tool-using agent tests can create side effects unless sandboxed. Short bounded tests can create false assurance if caveats are weak. Partners may internalize the playbook after repeated slots. Sensitive prompts, logs, RAG corpora, tool maps, and source documents require strict evidence handling inside partner-controlled workspaces.
