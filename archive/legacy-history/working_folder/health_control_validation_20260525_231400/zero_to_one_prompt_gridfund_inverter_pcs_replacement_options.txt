Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
GridFund Inverter/PCS Replacement Options

One-sentence thesis:
A CEE project-finance and EPC-routed release desk for EU-backed solar, wind, and BESS projects whose funding, procurement approval, grant drawdown, or lender technical approval is blocked because the selected inverter or BESS power-conversion system comes from a high-risk country, with the startup controlling a signed live project case plus a non-high-risk replacement equipment quote or reservation.

Exact buyer:
CEE renewable-energy developers, BESS developers, EPCs, project-finance advisers, grant beneficiaries, lender technical-adviser support teams, and equipment distributors working on solar, wind, or battery-storage projects that use or planned to use inverters or BESS PCS from China, Russia, Iran, North Korea, or supplier structures flagged as high-risk for EU-backed funding.

Acute trigger:
A live project has a written funder, EIB/EIF/EBRD/EU-funding, grant, bank, lender technical adviser, public-procurement, or investor condition saying the project cannot draw funds, close financing, pass procurement, continue grant approval, or proceed with the current equipment choice unless high-risk-country inverter or PCS exposure is removed, explained, replaced, or cleanly no-go'd.

Control point:
Signed project-owner/EPC mandate for one live funding or procurement blocker, affected project BoM, inverter/PCS supplier and model list, current quote or purchase order, funder or procurement condition text, project timeline, grid/design owner, warranty and bankability owner, and at least one dated non-high-risk replacement quote, distributor reservation, OEM allocation note, or conditional equipment option. The startup controls the replacement-options packet and status workflow, not the engineering or funding decision.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, secure two non-high-risk inverter or PCS distributors/OEM representatives willing to issue dated quotes or conditional reservation notes, sign three paid live project mandates at 20,000-90,000 PLN each, collect the funder/procurement condition and BoM for every case, produce two replacement-options packets, and obtain at least one funder/procurement/lender-technical-adviser progress outcome or clean no-go. At least one case must include a paid reservation, option fee, or success fee tied to replacement equipment availability rather than only a memo.

6-month POC:
Handle 20-40 live project cases, collect 800,000-2,500,000 PLN in case fees, urgent fees, option/reservation fees, and partner-wholesale fees, document 12+ funder/procurement progress or clean no-go outcomes, secure 5+ repeat EPC/developer/adviser/distributor channels, and build a private taxonomy of high-risk supplier triggers, inverter/PCS model exposure, accepted non-high-risk supplier evidence, replacement lead-time patterns, lender technical-adviser questions, delta-cost treatment, warranty/bankability escalations, and no-go reasons.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a target list from public renewable/BESS project announcements, EU/EIB/EBRD/grant-backed project references, CEE EPC portfolios, developer procurement announcements, and inverter/PCS supplier press releases. Outbound only asks for live affected projects: "If EU-backed funding, grant approval, lender review, or procurement is blocked because the inverter or BESS PCS supplier is treated as high-risk, send the condition text and BoM. I return a replacement-options packet with dated non-high-risk quotes/reservations and escalation/no-go notes. Engineers, EPCs, lawyers, funders, and technical advisers remain responsible for final decisions."

Economics:
Charge 20,000-35,000 PLN for a narrow supplier-origin and replacement quote packet, 40,000-70,000 PLN for a full funder/procurement response with two or more replacement options, and 70,000-120,000 PLN for urgent multi-project or BESS PCS cases. Add distributor-paid referral or option fees only where transparent and approved by the project owner. The buyer pays because a single financing drawdown, procurement approval, grant condition, or project delay can be worth millions of PLN.

Copy risk:
EPC procurement teams, inverter distributors, renewable consultants, lender technical advisers, project-finance advisers, grant consultants, law firms, cybersecurity consultants, and developers can copy pieces. The defense is live blocked-project intake, dated replacement quote/reservation control, CEE supplier availability memory, funder-condition response patterns, fast no-go discipline, and relationships with non-high-risk inverter and PCS channels.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the project owner or EPC signs the mandate, sends the funder/procurement condition, BoM, current inverter/PCS exposure, timeline, and payment, and the startup secures dated non-high-risk replacement quotes or reservations for that exact project, competitors cannot act on the same replacement option without the project team switching and potentially losing the reserved slot or quote clock.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It accepts only live funding or procurement blockers tied to an actual project and actual inverter/PCS exposure. The output is an operational release packet: exposure matrix, supplier-origin evidence index, dated replacement quote or reservation, delta-cost and lead-time comparison, funder/procurement response wording, engineering/warranty/bankability escalation list, and clean no-go where replacement cannot be responsibly supported. It is not a generic market report, renewable consulting, dashboard, database, tender alert, equipment marketplace, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No legal advice, no procurement-law opinion, no financing advice, no cybersecurity certification, no engineering design, no grid-code analysis, no warranty or bankability opinion, no acting as EPC, lender technical adviser, funder, distributor of record, or manufacturer representative unless separately contracted by an authorized distributor. Engineers, EPCs, funders, lenders, lawyers, technical advisers, OEMs, distributors, insurers, grant authorities, and project owners remain responsible for eligibility, engineering, procurement, warranty, cybersecurity, grid compliance, and final equipment decisions.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not BatteryFit because it does not evaluate prosumer retrofit economics for PV installers. It is not BESSGuard because it does not handle commissioning or warranty/RMA evidence after installation. It is not GreenTender because it is not public-lighting tender EPREL evidence. It is not Data Act, CBAM, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeatQuiet, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, Supplement Stack, RAGProbe, CyberBind, CleanRIPE, KSeF, or any health/Rx/clinical route. The buyer, trigger, control point, payment event, and proof artifact are materially different: EU-backed renewable/BESS project funding or procurement release through non-high-risk inverter/PCS replacement options.

Strongest anticipated objections:
EPC procurement teams, renewable developers, distributors, project-finance advisers, and lender technical advisers may solve this internally. Replacement equipment may require engineering redesign, grid-code review, warranty and bankability approval, procurement-law review, or lender technical-adviser signoff outside founder control. The founder may not secure meaningful non-high-risk distributor reservations without industry relationships. Some projects may not be affected, may be grandfathered, or may wait for funder guidance. Supplier-origin and control evidence can become legal/procurement interpretation. The opportunity may be a short transition window rather than a durable business. Distributor referral fees could create conflicts unless transparent. A Warsaw solo founder needs credible renewable-procurement and technical-adviser support immediately.
