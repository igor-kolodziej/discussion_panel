Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: KRS Address + e-Doreczenia Continuity Book Buyout.

One-sentence thesis: acquire or option a small closing Polish registered-office/mail-custody provider only after active companies re-consent, the physical address/right is secured, payment is redirected, and current-month continuity cash is collected.

Exact buyer: existing customers of small Polish registered-office, virtual-office, coworking-mailbox, secretarial, or accounting-side address providers. The first customers are micro sp. z o.o. companies, foreign-owned Polish companies, foundations or associations with business activity, dormant-but-active KRS entities, small agencies, importers, consultants, and ecommerce sellers using a rented registered office and mail handling address.

Acute trigger: the current provider is retiring, losing its lease, closing the coworking/mailbox location, or reducing mail handling while official correspondence has become more digital and more deadline-sensitive. KRS companies already need e-Doreczenia addresses, and CEIDG businesses face the 2026-10-01 deadline unless earlier changes triggered the obligation. For the customer, missed tax, court, ZUS, KRS, city-office, bailiff, or contract mail can create real deadlines, default assumptions, fines, blocked filings, or panic work with accountants/lawyers.

Control point: the founder only buys or closes on retained accounts where control is real. Control requires:

- conditional option or acquisition agreement over the provider's active address/mail-custody book;
- lease assignment, sublease, landlord consent, or new equivalent physical address capacity;
- seller-endorsed customer transfer notice;
- signed customer re-consent to the founder/new entity as address/mail-custody provider;
- direct payment transfer to the founder/new entity;
- handoff of mailbox IDs, mail logs, forwarding instructions, scan rules, phone/domain/email, service templates, and unresolved mail cases;
- e-Doreczenia onboarding/notification workflow where the company remains account/controller and the founder acts only under lawful authorisation.

The startup does not become an accountant, lawyer, e-Doreczenia qualified provider, or government-delivery operator.

6-month proof:

1. Contact 80-120 small virtual-office, coworking, secretarial, and accounting-side address providers.
2. Inspect 5 books and reject any without clean customer contracts, mail logs, address rights, and current payments.
3. Sign one option with a provider for a small option fee and earnout only on retained customers.
4. Secure the address lease/sublease/landlord or equivalent address continuity before customer notices.
5. Seller sends transfer notice to 60-150 active customers.
6. At least 40 customers sign new service terms and payment transfer.
7. Collect at least 25,000 PLN prepaid setup/continuity/first-period cash before meaningful seller close.
8. Complete 20 live mail/e-Doreczenia onboarding or continuity actions.
9. By month 6, retain at least 60 paying companies, prove gross margin above 55%, and keep ordinary account handling below 15 minutes per company per month.

First acquisition mechanism:

Seller pitch:
"You have registered-address clients and mail duties you no longer want. I will not buy the book blind. You introduce only active clients. You get an earnout on customers who re-consent and prepay, and no value is paid for stale names."

Customer pitch:
"Your registered-office/mail provider is changing. We keep the address/mail handoff orderly, move payment and forwarding instructions cleanly, and help you check e-Doreczenia readiness without taking over legal/accounting decisions."

Economics:

- 99-299 PLN/month per active company depending on mail volume, scan frequency, forwarding, and address city;
- 199-799 PLN one-time transfer/onboarding fee;
- 100-500 PLN pass-through or marked-up KRS/CEIDG/e-Doreczenia readiness admin support only where lawful and scoped;
- optional 499-899 PLN/month tier for foreign-owned or high-mail customers needing bilingual forwarding and accountant/lawyer coordination.

The proof must show at least 25,000 PLN cash collected before close, gross margin above 55% after rent/address cost, mail scanning, forwarding, payment fees, seller earnout, and admin time, and no open-ended legal/accounting support inside the base service.

Copy risk:

Virtual-office incumbents can market to the same general customer type. They cannot copy a seller-endorsed transfer of a specific address book after customers re-consent, payment is redirected, mail logs are handed over, and the physical address/right is controlled. The book is sticky because changing a registered office can trigger KRS/CEIDG updates, bank/vendor/accountant updates, and missed-correspondence risk.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup buys or options an operating book only after customer payment transfer. It controls the physical address/mail custody workflow and current customer cash. It does not sell a guide, SaaS dashboard, lead list, e-Doreczenia setup checklist, or legal advice.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- Customers remain responsible for KRS/CEIDG, tax, ZUS, court, contractual, and e-Doreczenia decisions.
- Accountants/lawyers handle filings or legal advice.
- The founder handles address/mail custody and administrative continuity under written authorization and DPA where needed.
- The founder does not read legal/tax mail beyond contracted scan/index/forwarding instructions.
- No e-Doreczenia login, provider change, or mailbox administration occurs without lawful company authorization and provider-compliant process.
- KYC/AML-style screening rejects suspicious shell/fraud customers and customers seeking concealment.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

- Not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: no ICT supplier evidence pack.
- Not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: no Amazon account-health evidence.
- Not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack: no health or investor diligence.
- Not TraceFaktura municipal animal-care invoice evidence: no municipal event invoice ledger.
- Not HeritageDoor Suites: no physical salvage inventory.
- Not KSeF/e-Delivery Accounting Book Succession Lockbox: this does not acquire accounting work, bookkeeping liability, invoice workflows, or tax filings. It is a registered-address/mail-custody book with e-Doreczenia readiness as a bounded admin layer.
