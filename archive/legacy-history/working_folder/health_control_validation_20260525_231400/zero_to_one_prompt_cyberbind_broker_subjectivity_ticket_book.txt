Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CyberBind Broker Subjectivity Ticket Book

One-sentence thesis:
A broker-paid, prepaid ticket-book for cyber/commercial insurance brokers and MSP-broker pairs that already control SME cyber-insurance clients but need a fast evidence bench for live underwriter subjectivities blocking bind, renewal, exclusions, surcharges, limits, or customer-contract coverage proof.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized Polish/CEE cyber-insurance brokers, commercial insurance brokers with cyber books, broker networks, and MSP-broker pairs serving 100-500 employee SMEs. The paying buyer is the broker or broker-MSP pair that already owns the insured relationship and underwriter channel. End users are SME insureds with a current cyber policy bind, renewal, conditional quote, ransomware/funds-transfer exclusion, surcharge, sublimit, decline, or follow-up held by underwriter/MGA questions. The first broker has repeated SME cyber renewal/bind traffic and at least six live or recent subjectivities per quarter.

Acute trigger:
The broker has a queue of live underwriter or MGA subjectivities asking for evidence before binding, renewing, removing exclusions, improving sublimits, avoiding surcharge, or issuing customer-required coverage proof. Typical conditions include MFA scope, EDR/MDR coverage, backup restore evidence, privileged-account controls, patch cadence, security-awareness training, funds-transfer controls, incident-response contacts, external scan findings, remote-access controls, cyber questionnaire inconsistencies, or written remediation/no-go proof. The broker is at risk of losing renewal timing, commission, client trust, coverage terms, or customer-contract deadlines if the queue is slow or poorly answered.

Control point:
Signed broker ticket-block agreement; prepaid ticket minimum; broker authorization; ticket intake rules; end-client authorization and management signoff boundary; exact underwriter/MGA subjectivity wording; policy bind/renewal deadline; premium/coverage consequence where available; MSP/IT evidence owner; evidence-room checklist; approved response path; screenshot/export/log handling rules; no-remediation/no-false-statement rules; broker approval before submission; underwriter response tracker; no-go and remediation referral ledger; and right to retain anonymized wording/outcome taxonomy. The startup controls the broker's prepaid live subjectivity queue and delivery chronology, not insurance advice, underwriting decisions, cyber implementation, legal opinions, or broker licensing.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three brokers or broker-MSP pairs sign prepaid ticket-block agreements covering at least 18 live cyber-insurance subjectivity tickets. At least 120,000 PLN is collected before delivery begins. Every accepted ticket has written underwriter/MGA condition text, a bind or renewal deadline, client authorization, broker approval path, MSP/IT evidence owner, and insured management signoff boundary. At least 10 client-authorized evidence packets are delivered. At least 6 tickets produce underwriter-accepted evidence, bind or renewal progress, narrowed exclusion or surcharge, coverage issued, paid remediation/no-go, or broker-approved no-submit. At least two brokers buy a second block or sign a second-block commitment before day 60. More than 35% of intake is rejected because controls are missing, evidence is unsafe, or the broker/MSP must remediate first.

6-month POC:
Operate 8-15 broker or broker-MSP ticket blocks; process 80-160 live subjectivity tickets; collect 600,000-1,800,000 PLN in prepaid ticket-block and renewal-block revenue; document 50+ underwriter/broker outcomes or paid no-go decisions; prove at least five brokers buy three or more blocks; keep gross margin above 60% after cyber reviewer, secure evidence handling, translation, and broker support costs; keep turnaround below five business days for evidence-ready tickets; build an anonymized taxonomy of underwriter wording, evidence object, MSP export type, accepted response pattern, remediation/no-go trigger, carrier/MGA follow-up, and broker channel quality.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with cyber-insurance brokers, commercial brokers that write cyber policies, broker associations, MSPs that already support cyber renewals, and boutique cyber-risk advisers. Broker pitch: "Prepay a block of 6-10 subjectivity tickets. You keep the client and underwriter relationship. I turn existing MSP/client evidence into broker-approved evidence packets, no-go memos, or remediation referrals within five business days, and I reject tickets where controls are missing." MSP-broker pitch: "Keep remediation revenue. Send only the evidence-ready underwriter queue that blocks bind or renewal." Initial outreach asks for a live queue count, not a general partnership call.

Economics:
Charge brokers 5,000-9,000 PLN wholesale per prepaid ticket in blocks of 6-10, with unused-ticket expiry or rollover only if the broker submits eligible live tickets. Brokers resell or bundle at 8,000-18,000 PLN per client ticket, or use the block to protect commission and client retention. Larger urgent files cost 12,000-25,000 PLN wholesale if multiple evidence domains or carrier follow-ups are involved. Buyer ROI is credible because one saved 30,000-150,000 PLN annual-premium renewal, protected broker commission, avoided ransomware/funds-transfer exclusion, or customer-contract coverage proof can justify a block. Founder capital stays under 100,000 PLN because work is evidence reconstruction, secure handling, reviewer coordination, and broker workflow, not cyber implementation or insurance brokerage.

Copy risk:
Brokers, MSPs, MSSPs, cyber boutiques, vCISOs, MGAs, insurer platforms, GRC platforms, and security-questionnaire vendors can copy parts. The defense is not generic cybersecurity knowledge. It is prepaid broker-queue control, repeat broker blocks, underwriter wording memory, accepted evidence-object memory, no-go discipline, MSP export patterns, broker-approved response language, and fast delivery before a carrier/broker deadline. The broker's second-block purchase is the main proof that this is a controlled ticket book rather than one-off consulting.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a broker prepays a block, assigns live tickets, obtains client authorizations, and routes MSP/IT evidence through the startup's intake, the startup controls the active ticket queue, evidence-room status, broker approval chronology, underwriter response tracker, and ticket-block fee right. An MSP or cyber consultant can compete generally, but cannot instantly take the already-prepaid broker queue without the broker reassigning client authorizations, evidence flow, submission chronology, and renewal deadlines.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell generic cyber readiness, implementation, vCISO work, insurance brokerage, underwriter access, a dashboard, a questionnaire database, or introductions. It accepts only broker-assigned live subjectivity tickets with current bind/renewal/coverage consequences and produces broker-approved evidence packets, remediation/no-go memos, or no-submit decisions tied to those tickets. No broker-paid block and no live underwriter wording means no case.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not act as insurance broker, insurance adviser, claims handler, lawyer, MSP, MSSP, vCISO, underwriter, auditor, penetration tester, incident responder, or cyber-control implementer. It does not make coverage representations, legal conclusions, or false security claims. Brokers remain responsible for insurance advice, carrier communication, client relationship, and submissions. Insured management approves every factual statement. MSPs/MSSPs/internal IT remain responsible for actual controls, exports, screenshots, logs, remediation, and security operations. Lawyers handle legal wording where needed. Reject breach/incident cases, claims disputes, litigation, deceptive clients, missing core controls, healthcare/clinical records, classified/sanctions issues, and any request to make absent controls sound present.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is distinct from DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because the external gatekeeper is an insurer/MGA underwriter and the paid control point is a broker-prepaid subjectivity ticket book, not a regulated-finance customer DORA/ICT supplier renewal request. It is distinct from REDBlocked because there is no Amazon Seller Central product-compliance ticket or connected-device evidence. It is distinct from BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, RAGProbe, AIQ, KSC/NIS2, and property underwriting evidence. The buyer, trigger, control point, payment event, and proof artifact are materially different: broker-owned cyber-insurance subjectivity tickets with bind/renewal/exclusion/surcharge outcomes.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Brokers and MSPs may already handle cyber subjectivities with screenshots, exports, backup evidence, EDR reports, MFA scope notes, and client calls. Many tickets require implementation, not evidence packaging. Misstatements in insurance applications or underwriter responses can create severe coverage, legal, and trust risk. Brokers may resist prepaid blocks before proof. MSPs may prefer to sell remediation and internalize evidence work. Underwriters may still decline, surcharge, exclude, or ask more questions. Evidence collection exposes sensitive security gaps, admin screenshots, endpoint coverage, backup architecture, privileged access, scan findings, and incident contacts. Insurer platforms may standardize intake. Broker economics can compress if brokers expect margin and clients resist ticket fees. The business can drift into generic cyber consulting unless it accepts only broker-paid live subjectivity queues.
