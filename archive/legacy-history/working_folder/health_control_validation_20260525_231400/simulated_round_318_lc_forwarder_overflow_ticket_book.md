## Selected Candidate

**LC Forwarder Overflow Ticket Book**

## Internal Score: 88.6

Why it clears simulation:

- It directly addresses the Round 317 miss: client trust and acquisition come from freight forwarders, export accountants, chambers, or trade-finance advisers already inside the exporter's workflow.
- The first proof is not a soft referral. It requires partner-sold or partner-routed paid tickets, exporter consent, LC files, discrepancy notices, and an assigned reviewer before the founder claims traction.
- The partner has a reason not to internalize immediately: occasional LC discrepancies are high-stress, specialist, and reputation-sensitive, but may not justify a full-time LC expert.
- The startup can compound discrepancy-type memory across partners while partners keep the client relationship.
- Cash is current and formal: bank payment, accepted resubmission, applicant waiver, discrepancy narrowing, discounting release, or paid no-go.

## Internal Concerns

- White-label partner queues can become hidden subcontract labor with limited moat.
- Forwarders/export accountants/chambers may internalize the checklist if volume is real.
- A named LC reviewer is required from day one; without that, trust is weak.
- The six-month case volume is dependent on partner behavior and could be lumpy.
- The business can drift into LC advice, legal interpretation, or bank/applicant pressure.

## 60-Day Proof Standard

- 2-3 signed partner overflow agreements with freight forwarders, export accountants, chambers/export desks, or trade-finance advisers.
- 5 prepaid live tickets, each with exporter consent, named LC, written bank discrepancy/refusal/reserve notice, full document set, and partner-billed or partner-approved payment.
- 2-3 objective outcomes: payment release, bank acceptance, applicant waiver, accepted resubmission, discrepancy narrowed, discounting release, or paid clean no-go.
- 80,000-160,000 PLN collected across partner/client payments.
- One named LC/documentary-credit reviewer retained and visible in partner materials.
- One partner sends a second case.
- More than 50% rejection/no-go for fraud, sanctions, cargo/title dispute, legal interpretation, buyer refusal unrelated to documents, or cases without written discrepancy notice.

## Duplicate And Branch Check

- This is not Round 317 reworded. The control point is partner-routed paid ticket flow and trust-channel ownership, not a cold direct exporter mandate.
- Not DORA/REDBlocked/GreenTender/Data Act/CBAM/PromoLeak: no regulated-finance evidence, marketplace product evidence, public tender mapping, machine data access, customs/import continuity, or retailer deduction recovery.
- It remains close to trade-finance consulting; real validation must determine whether partner-routed paid flow is enough to clear the higher bar.
