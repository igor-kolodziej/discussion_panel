## Selected Candidate

**Idea:** PostedWorker A1 Invoice Release Desk

**Simulated score:** 88/100

## Why It Clears The Simulated Gate

- The trigger is concrete: named workers, named site/customer, written blocker, invoice batch, site access, retention release, or framework renewal.
- The 60-day proof is official: issued A1 certificates, host-country posted-worker declarations, customer/site acceptance, released invoice, or clean no-go.
- The buyer pays because teams cannot enter the site or current invoices/retentions are held.
- Polish/CEE language, ZUS/A1 workflow, host-country portal documentation, and subcontractor admin reality create a local wedge.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Cap below 82 if the startup sells general posted-worker compliance, employment advice, immigration support, or payroll outsourcing.
- Cap below 85 if payroll offices, HR firms, and law firms can solve the specific blocked invoice/site access without operational friction.
- Cap below 87 if no buyer/site/invoice blocker exists or if A1 issuance timing is outside founder control.
- Cap below 82 if the work requires legal determinations about employment status, permanent establishment, tax residence, wage law, social-security affiliation, or illegal posting structures.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

Zero To One may view this as HR/payroll/legal coordination owned by accountants, payroll bureaus, law firms, and larger subcontractors. It also may reject the founder fit because worker data and cross-border employment records are sensitive.
