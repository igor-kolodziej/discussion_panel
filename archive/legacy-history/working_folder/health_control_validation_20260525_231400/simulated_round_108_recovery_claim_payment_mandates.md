## Simulated Pass Candidate

### Amazon Vendor Central Shortage/Chargeback Recovery Mandate - 90

One-sentence thesis: sign recovery mandates with EU/CEE brands selling 1P to Amazon, extract their live Vendor Central deduction queue, file evidence-backed shortage/chargeback disputes, and collect success fees only from recovered credits.

Buyer: EU/CEE brands and distributors with Amazon Vendor Central 1P accounts, recurring PO/invoice/remittance flows, and unexplained or under-disputed shortage claims, chargebacks, price claims, returns, coop/accrual deductions, or compliance fees.

Acute trigger: Amazon has already short-paid invoices or deducted cash. Dispute windows and evidence freshness decay. The brand's finance/ops team lacks time to reconcile POs, ASNs, invoices, remittances, POD/BOL, delivery confirmations, chargeback codes, and Vendor Central dispute paths.

Transferable control point: signed recovery mandate plus delegated Vendor Central/export access, deduction register, evidence folder, dispute authority under client account rules, success-fee right, and live claim-status tracking.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- One signed recovery mandate with success-fee terms and data access authorization.
- Vendor Central/remittance/PO/invoice/chargeback exports for a defined lookback window.
- Claim register with at least 50,000-150,000 PLN in candidate deductions.
- 10-30 evidence-backed disputes filed or reopened.
- Amazon acknowledgement, case status, credit memo, reversal, or payment evidence for at least one claim.
- First success-fee invoice or paid recovery fee from recovered/credited cash.

First acquisition mechanism: target Polish/CEE brands that are large enough to be Amazon 1P vendors but not large enough for enterprise deduction software. Use Amazon ops agencies, fractional CFOs, e-commerce finance consultants, and public brand/vendor lists. Offer a no-recovery/no-success-fee pilot with a small setup fee only if data cleanup is heavy.

Gross margin and payback logic: charge 20-30% of recovered cash plus optional 2,000-8,000 PLN setup for messy exports. A single recovered 20,000-50,000 PLN shortage/chargeback queue can justify the first engagement. Founder work is data reconciliation and dispute packet assembly, not inventory, logistics, or legal representation.

Copy risk: SupplyPike/SPS, Threecolts, Recofoxx, Amazon vendor consultants, AP audit firms, and brand finance teams can do this. The defense is narrow EU/CEE mid-market focus, current mandate/access, case-memory by Amazon deduction type, and immediate first-cash proof. Incumbents cannot copy a specific client's active claim queue after mandate/access is signed.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured: once the brand signs the mandate, grants exports/access, and the desk files current disputes, competitors cannot access or act on those same claims unless the client switches. The desk controls the live queue, evidence trail, claim status, and success-fee right for that mandate period.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/marketplace/broker: the business is not selling an audit report or dashboard. It acts on a signed recovery mandate, files disputes, tracks claim status, and earns from recovered credits/cash. The proof is first recovered cash or credited invoices, not a prettier leakage register.

Reasons this could still fail:
- Amazon Vendor Central recovery is already served by strong incumbents.
- Some deductions are valid and unrecoverable.
- Vendor Central dispute windows, evidence standards, and opaque denials can reduce win rates.
- Brands may be too small for meaningful claim value or too large and already served.
- Access/security/legal approval can slow onboarding.
- The founder must avoid legal claims, false evidence, and overpromising recovery.

Decision: advance this candidate to real Zero To One validation.
