## Validator Summary

## Strongest Objections

1. Many ADMs are valid because the agency really made a fare, refund, reissue, waiver, tax, no-show, card, booking, or interline error.
2. BSPlink and airline-policy time windows can kill recovery, especially for old post-billing cases.
3. Fare construction, GDS history, ticketing, refund, waiver, interline, tax/YQ, no-show, schedule-change, and NDC expertise cannot be faked.
4. Airlines control final acceptance, and some may reject disputes or prefer relationship handling over strict evidence.
5. Evidence access includes passenger, ticketing, payment, agency-account, correspondence, and financial data, creating trust and DPA friction.
6. Success-fee attribution can become disputed unless claim IDs, filing timestamps, baseline status, dispute text, airline responses, and fee triggers are exact.
7. Distribution is relationship-heavy; 20 agencies in six months is optimistic without trusted introducers.
8. Small ADM chasing can become low-margin admin labor.
9. Large agencies may already do this well, while weak agencies may have messy evidence and low trust.
10. The moat is operational rather than structural; a mandate protects one batch but not future batches.

## Useful Narrowing

The evaluator said the best first version is narrower:

- three agencies rather than five if they have real ADM density;
- 50+ ADMs screened;
- 25,000-50,000 PLN equivalent filed or escalated;
- one documented cancellation, ACM, reversal, or written airline acceptance;
- one senior ticketing/GDS reviewer attached before selling;
- no case filed without evidence and airline-policy basis.

Even then, the evaluator framed it as a good profitable specialist wedge, not a confirmed high-score company.

Fail. Continue to Round 225.

## Search Lesson

Official monetary claim queues still fail if the real scarce input is specialist trust and the mandate only controls one batch. The next recovery/control candidate needs either a stronger transferred payment stream, a source of exclusive case flow, or a controlled asset that does not depend on expert relationship credibility as much as BSP/GDS ticketing does.
