## Why This Is Not A Duplicate

This is not Atlassian Data Center App Continuity Buyout because the controlled asset is not a Marketplace app listing, source code, app key, or app support stream. The controlled asset is a seller-optioned customer transition/payment stream from a VMware cloud/service provider or MSP affected by partner-route disruption.

It is not OneStack Critical Connector because it is not a custom ERP connector support book. It is a channel-transition lockbox around existing VMware customer support/renewal/migration continuity, with authorized provider-of-record routing.

It is not a platform evidence desk because the first proof is customer-approved payment direction and transition orders, not a packet submitted to a platform reviewer.

## Internal Objections To Watch In Real Validation

- The business may be scored as brokerage or MSP migration consulting.
- Authorized Broadcom/VMware partners can buy or capture the best books directly.
- Seller supply may be rare or poor quality.
- Customer trust may not transfer from the old provider to an unknown startup.
- Production migration and support can become heavy, urgent, and risky.
- Licensing/resale boundaries are strict; the startup must not become unauthorized reseller or legal/licensing adviser.
- Customers may use the disruption to leave VMware or renegotiate with large incumbents.
- Gross margin can disappear if every case needs bespoke architecture and migration support.
