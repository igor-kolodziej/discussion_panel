3. Hard cases may require changing suppliers or not shipping, not better packaging.
4. The importer/operator remains responsible for due diligence, legal conclusions, Information System submission, supplier choice, and commercial decisions.
5. EUDR platforms, certification bodies, commodity traders, customs brokers, law firms, GIS providers, and ERP vendors can sell broader EUDR programs.
6. The POC may be too early in May 2026 because many importers may delay paid action until late 2026.
7. GIS review can create false confidence unless restricted to issue-spotting.
8. Customs brokers may refer clients but avoid liability or wording that implies broker endorsement.
9. Case pricing only works for high-value shipments, contracts, or buyer relationships.
10. Direct importer outreach may be slow unless broker, accountant, or buyer channels already have live written requests.
