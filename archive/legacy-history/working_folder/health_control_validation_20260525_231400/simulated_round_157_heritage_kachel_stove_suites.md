### Why It Clears Simulation

- It follows the HeritageDoor pattern but is not a door or generic salvage: the valuable unit is a complete numbered stove suite from one property, not loose tiles or random antique pieces.
- The asset can be physically controlled within 60 days through title or exclusive pickup rights before demolition, renovation, estate clearance, or stove dismantling.
- Buyer deposits are the proof: architects, stove fitters, boutique developers, hotels, or HNW owners reserve a full suite before restoration spend.
- Safety and heating liability can be bounded by selling the documented suite and handing installation/functional rebuild to a qualified stove fitter/restorer; decorative-only use is allowed when chimney/safety is unresolved.
- Logistics are more manageable than FF&E/radiators/roof tiles if the first lots are complete but compact enough to palletize and store, while value per cubic meter can be high.

### Internal Objections

- Demand may be narrow, and the first buyer has to care about authentic tiled-stove visual continuity.
- Reproduction tiles and newly built tiled stoves are substitutes.
- Missing/cracked tiles, soot, glaze damage, and poor dismantling can destroy value.
- Functional rebuild requires a qualified stove fitter, chimney/safety decisions, and building rules outside the startup's role.
- Existing stove restorers and antique dealers may already control some supply.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the idea has a materially different controlled asset from existing confirmed HeritageDoor Suites.
