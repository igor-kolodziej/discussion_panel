## Strongest Objections Captured

1. VAT advisers and Amazon agencies may already solve the exact cases.
2. Many cases are outside startup control if the VAT number, fiscal representative, tax authority status, or entity details are actually wrong.
3. Amazon support can stay slow, opaque, or wrong even when the evidence is correct.
4. The Italy/VIES/fiscal-representation wave may be temporary rather than durable.
5. Direct seller trust is weak; agency routing is mandatory.
6. Seller Central access creates security, blame, and operational risk.
7. The line between operational cure and tax advice is thin.
8. Fees only work for high-value blocked inventory; smaller sellers will not pay enough.
9. Agencies can internalize the playbook after a handful of cases.
10. The workflow can drift into generic Amazon account-health consulting.

## Evaluator's Suggested Narrowing

Only pursue the agency-routed version:

> Seller Central VAT/VIES inventory-status case desk for tax advisers and Amazon agencies -- only where the tax facts are already established by a qualified adviser.

Even under that narrower framing, the evaluator viewed the realistic 6-month POC as 150,000-450,000 PLN from 15-35 cases, not the proposed 400,000-1,200,000 PLN.

## Lesson

Do not continue platform/tax evidence-desk variants unless the founder controls more than a live case packet. Real-time regulatory confusion and inventory pressure are not enough when tax advisers, Amazon agencies, and Seller Central operators are natural owners of the workflow and the startup cannot force the outcome.
