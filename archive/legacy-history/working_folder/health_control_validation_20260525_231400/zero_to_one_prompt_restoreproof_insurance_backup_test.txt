Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
RestoreProof Cyber-Insurance Backup Test Evidence Bench

One-sentence thesis:
A broker- and MSP-routed evidence-generation bench for SMEs whose cyber-insurance bind, renewal, ransomware coverage, premium, or exclusion decision is blocked because the underwriter asks for proof that backups are isolated/immutable and have actually restored, with the startup delivering a dated tested-restore artifact rather than a generic cyber evidence pack.

Exact buyer:
Cyber-insurance brokers, managing general agents, and MSPs serving 50-500 employee SMEs where a live underwriter, carrier, or MGA request asks for tested backup restore evidence, backup isolation/immutability evidence, recovery-test documentation, or backup-system proof before quote, bind, renewal, ransomware coverage, funds-transfer coverage, or premium/exclusion decision. The end beneficiary is the insured SME, but the first paid channel is the broker or MSP that already controls the renewal conversation.

Acute trigger:
A named cyber-insurance application, renewal, bind, subjectivity, conditional quote, ransomware sublimit, funds-transfer exclusion, premium increase, customer-contract requirement, or underwriter follow-up is stuck because the insured cannot show recent, dated, credible evidence that critical backups can be restored and are protected from ordinary ransomware compromise.

Control point:
Broker/MSP-routed paid case with the exact underwriter request, renewal deadline, insured approval, current backup platform or MSP backup service details, agreed sample restore scope, MSP/IT owner technical contact, no-go criteria, and written permission to produce a buyer-safe restore evidence artifact. The startup controls the case workflow, restore-test evidence pack, timestamps, screenshots/log extracts, exception register, and underwriter response draft. MSPs, backup vendors, insured IT owners, brokers, and underwriters remain responsible for technical systems, insurance advice, and final coverage decisions.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign two broker or MSP routing agreements, sell at least ten prepaid live subjectivity cases or two prepaid case blocks, complete six dated restore-test evidence artifacts, obtain at least three underwriter/broker progress outcomes such as subjectivity cleared, follow-up reduced, quote/bind/renewal advanced, ransomware exclusion reconsidered, or clean no-go accepted, and keep gross margin above 55% after backup/technical contractor costs. Reject cases where no recent backup exists, the MSP cannot authorize a test, the insured wants false statements, or the request requires full remediation before any truthful evidence can be produced.

6-month POC:
Handle 60-120 paid live cases through 6-12 brokers/MSPs, generate 600,000-1,800,000 PLN revenue from fixed fees and prepaid broker/MSP case blocks, document 35+ underwriter progress or clean no-go outcomes, maintain sub-5-business-day turnaround for evidence-ready cases, build a private library of underwriter restore-test wording, accepted artifact formats, backup-platform evidence patterns, common no-go reasons, broker response language, and contractor runbooks for M365, Google Workspace, Veeam, Datto/Kaseya, Acronis, Cove/N-able, Rubrik, Cohesity, and common MSP backup stacks.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target cyber-insurance brokers and MSPs that already complain about last-minute renewal evidence work. The outreach is not broad cyber consulting: "If a cyber underwriter is asking for proof that backups restore, send the exact subjectivity and your backup platform. We run or witness a bounded test restore with your MSP/IT owner, produce a buyer-safe dated evidence artifact, and flag no-go/remediation cases. You keep the client and insurance advice."

Economics:
Charge 6,000-10,000 PLN for a narrow restore-test evidence case, 12,000-20,000 PLN for multi-system or urgent renewal cases, and 40,000-90,000 PLN for prepaid broker/MSP blocks of 5-10 cases. Technical contractors are paid per test/witness/review. Brokers and MSPs pay because one stuck renewal, ransomware exclusion, premium load, or customer-contract requirement can cost far more than the fee, and because collecting restore proof under deadline is tedious, risky, and interrupts higher-value brokerage or MSP work.

Copy risk:
Brokers, MSPs, backup vendors, cyber consultants, vCISOs, insurers, and GRC platforms can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-case qualification, broker/MSP white-label routing, repeat underwriter wording memory, accepted artifact formats, restore-test runbooks by backup platform, fast no-go discipline, and prepaid case blocks that assign the current renewal queue before competitors react.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a broker or MSP signs a paid case block, routes a live underwriter subjectivity, obtains insured approval, and the startup schedules the restore test with the MSP/IT owner, the startup controls the immediate case workflow, evidence artifact, timestamps, exception register, and broker response package for that renewal. A competitor can build a similar service later, but cannot instantly take the already-routed paid cases or the dated restore-test artifacts in progress.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It does not sell cyber readiness, insurance advice, a dashboard, a database, a marketplace, or a generic questionnaire pack. It accepts only live cyber-insurance blockers with an exact request for tested backup/restore evidence. The output is an operational artifact created from a bounded restore test: sample scope, backup-platform evidence, restore log or screenshot extract, timestamped result, exception/no-go register, insured approval line, broker-ready response, and remediation handoff where the test fails.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
No insurance advice, no legal advice, no warranty of coverage, no false attestation, no claim handling, no incident response, no security certification, no penetration test, no full backup architecture design, no admin access without MSP/IT-owner authorization, and no handling of patient or highly sensitive regulated personal data in test samples. Brokers remain responsible for insurance advice and carrier communication. MSPs, backup vendors, and insured IT owners remain responsible for systems, backups, restoration, security controls, and remediation. The startup only coordinates or witnesses bounded tests and prepares truthful evidence artifacts approved by the insured and broker/MSP.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it is not regulated-finance vendor evidence and not DORA/ICT third-party-risk procurement. It is not CyberBind as previously tested because it is not broad cyber-insurance subjectivity packaging across MFA, EDR, patching, IR, and backups. It is narrower and harder-control: a live underwriter backup-restore evidence blocker, broker/MSP-routed paid case block, and an actual dated restore-test artifact. It is not RAGProbe, AI security, NIS2/KSC, EAA, CBAM, REDBlocked, PromoLeak, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Brokers and MSPs may already be able to produce backup evidence themselves. Many clients will lack real tested backups, so cases become remediation/no-go rather than evidence generation. Underwriters may still decline, surcharge, exclude ransomware, or request more evidence. Backup-platform access is sensitive and MSPs may refuse an outside reviewer. The founder may need credible backup/BCDR contractors immediately. Evidence quality can vary by platform and restore scope. Misstatement risk is severe if the insured wants to exaggerate recovery capability. The business could be a narrow white-label service unless broker case blocks repeat and accepted underwriter artifact formats compound.
