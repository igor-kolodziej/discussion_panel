Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
MachineBuild FAT/SAT Payment Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A fixed-fee final-payment release desk for CEE custom machine builders and automation integrators whose buyer is holding a milestone payment, shipment release, warranty start, or acceptance certificate until factory/site acceptance evidence, punch-list disposition, and handover documents are packaged against the buyer's exact condition text.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE custom machinery builders, packaging-line builders, automation integrators, industrial equipment exporters, robot-cell integrators, electrical-panel builders, and specialist OEMs selling machines or lines to industrial customers where 10-30% of contract value, shipment permission, warranty start, or final acceptance is tied to FAT, SAT, handover, or punch-list evidence. The first buyer has a named machine/order and at least 150,000 PLN of blocked payment or shipment value.

Acute trigger:
The industrial buyer, procurement team, project manager, QA department, or AP team sends written condition text before paying the final milestone, authorizing shipment, starting warranty, signing acceptance, or releasing a retention. The condition asks for accepted FAT/SAT protocol, test-run videos, serial-numbered machine file, manual/O&M index, electrical/pneumatic drawings, I/O list, spare-parts list, training proof, punch-list closure, defect disposition, commissioning protocol, or buyer-ready handover packet. The machine builder believes the source evidence exists but is scattered across PM, engineering, automation, QA, manuals, and service teams.

Control point:
Signed payment-release mandate over a named machine/order; buyer condition text; contract milestone and blocked value; machine serial/order file; FAT/SAT protocol; test evidence, videos, logs, and photos; punch-list register with owner/status/no-go flags; manual/O&M/drawing index; spare-parts and training evidence; buyer correspondence trail; internal evidence owners; response wording; payment/release tracker; and clean rejection rules for real performance disputes, missing source truth, safety certification, CE/legal opinions, unresolved machine defects, or buyer commercial leverage.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Five machine builders or integrators sign paid mandates covering at least ten named machine/order files with written buyer condition text and more than 150,000 PLN blocked value per account. At least three buyer responses confirm acceptance progress, narrowed punch list, shipment release, warranty-start trigger, final-payment release, or clean no-go escalation. At least one final payment, staged payment, shipment release, signed acceptance certificate, or warranty-start certificate completes. At least 70,000 PLN is collected in fixed fees or success fees. More than 40% of intake is rejected because the real issue is performance failure, missing documents, safety certification, CE/legal opinion, or a commercial dispute.

6-month POC:
Process 35-70 machine/order files across 15-30 CEE machine builders and automation integrators; release or progress 8,000,000-25,000,000 PLN of final payment, shipment, warranty-start, or acceptance value; collect 450,000-1,300,000 PLN in fixed release fees, urgent fees, and narrow success fees; build buyer-condition memory by industry, country, machine type, FAT/SAT clause, punch-list pattern, document gap, evidence owner, and accepted response format; keep gross margin above 65% after QA/automation/documentation reviewer cost; reject at least 30% of cases as true defects, legal disputes, or source-truth failures.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound to owners, CFOs, project managers, export managers, and service managers at Polish/CEE machine builders, packaging-equipment builders, robot-cell integrators, and automation firms: "If a buyer is holding final payment or shipment because FAT/SAT, punch-list, manuals, drawings, training proof, or handover evidence is scattered, send the exact buyer condition and blocked value. I package only existing source truth into a buyer-ready release file and reject performance disputes." Partner routes include export-document agents, factoring brokers, industrial accountants, machine-safety consultants, CE consultants, and technical-writing shops that see held final invoices but do not want project closeout work.

Economics:
Charge 12,000-25,000 PLN for a bounded final-payment release file, 25,000-55,000 PLN for urgent multi-discipline FAT/SAT handover files, and an optional 1-3% success fee only where final-payment or shipment release is contractually attributable. Use QA, automation, documentation, or machine-safety reviewers only for source-evidence review and no-go flags, not certification. A 500,000 PLN final milestone can justify a 20,000-40,000 PLN fee if payment moves or the buyer issue narrows within days.

Copy risk:
Machine builders, project managers, QA teams, automation engineers, CE consultants, technical writers, machine-safety firms, lawyers, and industrial consultants can copy pieces. The defense is narrow current-payment urgency, buyer-condition memory, fast cross-function evidence assembly, payment-release discipline, and refusal to handle true performance disputes or certification work. Once a builder signs the mandate and provides the buyer hold, contract milestone, machine file, FAT/SAT evidence, and payment tracker, the startup controls that active release packet and fee right.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup controls the named buyer condition, source-evidence room, punch-list ledger, response chronology, and payment-release mandate for a specific machine/order, another consultant can compete generally but cannot instantly replace the active release file without the builder re-opening the evidence room, reassigning internal owners, and risking more buyer delay under the same payment deadline.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only named machine/order files with written buyer condition text and blocked final-payment, shipment, warranty-start, or acceptance value. The output is a buyer-ready payment-release artifact: FAT/SAT evidence packet, punch-list disposition, handover index, source-truth map, response wording, release tracker, and no-go escalation. It does not sell generic QA consulting, CE compliance, machine safety certification, project management, software, dashboards, databases, equipment brokerage, or expert introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not certify machine safety, CE compliance, performance, conformity, installation, commissioning, legal interpretation, warranty obligations, or buyer acceptance. It does not alter documents, backdate records, create missing source truth, approve machine defects, perform engineering design, operate machines, or negotiate legal claims. Machine builders remain responsible for truthfulness, performance, engineering, safety, CE documentation, manuals, warranty, customer communication, and contract obligations. Cases involving unresolved machine defects, safety or CE certification, legal disputes, penalties, liquidated damages, insolvency, sanctions, product-safety claims, clinical/medical equipment, or missing source evidence are declined or routed to qualified firms.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, Amazon Vendor Central, RAGProbe, AIQ, ToolTitle, MillCert, KSeF, or cyber-insurance. The distinct control object is a named custom-machine buyer acceptance condition tied to final payment or shipment release, not regulatory evidence, marketplace evidence, investor diligence, physical salvage, deduction recovery, import continuity, machine-data access, AI security, supplier-held tooling title, material certificates, invoice formatting, or insurance underwriting evidence.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Many cases will be real machine-performance disputes, not evidence gaps. Buyer acceptance remains outside founder control. Internal QA and project managers should already own FAT/SAT closeout. Evidence may be missing, contradictory, or unusable. The buyer may be using documentation as leverage for defects, delays, penalties, or commercial renegotiation. Engineering reviewers can consume margin. A wrong packet can damage a buyer relationship or worsen a dispute. Machine-safety, CE, warranty, commissioning, and legal issues can leak in quickly. The business may be episodic unless machine builders repeatedly have scattered closeout evidence across many orders.
