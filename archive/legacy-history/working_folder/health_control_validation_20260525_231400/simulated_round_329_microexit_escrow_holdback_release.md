## Selected Candidate

**Idea name:** MicroExit Escrow Holdback Release File

**Internal simulated score:** 88.3 / 100

## Thesis

Small-business sellers often have 50,000-500,000 PLN equivalent trapped in post-close escrow, holdback, transition-payment, or seller-note release conditions. A Warsaw founder can sell a narrow release file that turns contractually due administrative conditions into a buyer/escrow-agent-ready packet, while counsel/accountants remain responsible for legal and tax issues.

## Why It Clears Simulation

- Current cash is already contractually set aside or scheduled; the buyer is not speculating.
- The control point is a signed seller mandate over a named release event and the evidence room, not a generic M&A advisory service.
- The first proof is objective: buyer release acknowledgement, escrow-agent instruction, payment receipt, narrowed no-go, or seller invoice paid.
- Ticket size can support 15,000-50,000 PLN fixed fees for clean high-value files.
- The founder can reject legal/commercial disputes and work only where the condition is administrative: transfer receipts, access handoff, transition checklist, notice proofs, inventory report, customer notification, source/domain/account receipt, training log, or time-based maturity notice.

## Hard Boundaries

- No earnout interpretation, working-capital adjustment, indemnity claim, fraud, breach allegation, valuation dispute, tax clearance, litigation, non-compete enforcement, legal opinion, escrow-agent representation, or negotiation.
- Seller's lawyer/accountant remains owner of legal/tax/commercial advice.
- Startup does not hold escrow funds, act as escrow agent, buy receivables, collect debt, or provide M&A brokerage.
- Accept only files with purchase-agreement release condition text, seller authority, release value above 50,000 PLN equivalent, named buyer/escrow-agent contact path, and counsel/accountant boundary.

## 60-Day Proof

- Sign 3 paid mandates from small business sellers with escrow/holdback/seller-note release events due within 90 days.
- Each mandate includes release-condition excerpts, release value, named counterparty/escrow contact path, seller counsel/accountant contact if any, and a no-dispute warranty.
- Deliver 3 release files and obtain at least 2 buyer/escrow/counsel acknowledgements, document-request replies, release instructions, payment dates, or clean no-go decisions.
- Produce at least 1 actual released payment or escrow instruction.
- Collect 45,000-120,000 PLN in fixed/release-linked fees.

## 6-Month POC

- 15-25 paid mandates.
- 3M-12M PLN equivalent release value under mandate.
- 10-18 release files submitted.
- 6-12 objective outcomes: release instruction, payment, partial release, narrowed document request, or clean no-go.
- 300,000-900,000 PLN fees invoiced/collected.
- 5 repeat referral channels among micro-M&A brokers, search-fund operators, outsourced CFOs, small-business accountants, post-close integration advisers, and boutique deal lawyers.
- Gross margin above 65% after legal-template review and accountant/counsel boundary checks.

## Economics

Charge 12,000-20,000 PLN for clean time-based release or single-condition file, 20,000-40,000 PLN for multi-asset transition/escrow files, and 40,000-65,000 PLN for urgent multi-counterparty releases above 500,000 PLN. Add 1-3% release-linked bonus only where buyer/escrow instruction or payment timing can be attributed to the packet. No speculative contingency-only work.

## Copy Risk

Lawyers, accountants, M&A brokers, outsourced CFOs, and post-close integration consultants can copy the general workflow. The edge must be narrow, fast, and seller-side: repeated micro-exit condition language, evidence checklists, release packet formats, no-go triage, and referrals from brokers whose clients are too small to justify expensive legal project management.

## Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

For a signed release event, once the seller authorizes the startup, pays the fixed fee, shares the release-condition text, and routes evidence assembly through the startup's release room, the founder controls that specific packet, submission log, counterparty response trail, and fee right. Incumbents can compete for future files, but not easily displace a paid release file already moving through buyer/escrow review.

## Why It Is Not a Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, or Generic Broker

It accepts only named post-close payment release files with a release amount, condition text, deadline, seller authority, counterparty path, and objective outcome. The deliverable is a submitted release packet tied to payment or no-go. It does not source deals, broker businesses, negotiate terms, litigate, value companies, provide legal opinions, manage generic post-close integration, or sell software.

## Duplicate-Risk Distinction

This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, KSeF, ABL borrowing-base, true-up receivable, guarantee-line, construction retention, LC discrepancy, or platform evidence rescue. The buyer is a post-close small-business seller, the trigger is contractually held post-close consideration, the control point is a release-condition evidence file, and the payment event is escrow/holdback/seller-note release.

## Strongest Objections

1. Lawyers and accountants are the natural owners of post-close escrow/holdback releases.
2. Clean administrative files may be too easy for the seller or broker to handle, while painful files are usually legal/commercial disputes.
3. Purchase agreements are bespoke; release conditions may require interpretation that the startup cannot provide.
4. Buyer cooperation is outside control, and buyers may use holdbacks as leverage.
5. Escrow agents may only accept instructions from counsel or named parties.
6. Direct seller outreach can look opportunistic after an exit.
7. Micro-M&A deal flow may be too small or hidden unless brokers route cases.
8. Fixed fees may feel high if the seller expects the money anyway.
9. Success-fee attribution is weak if release happens on maturity date regardless of the packet.
10. Data rooms include sensitive company, employee, customer, contract, and financial records.

## Kill Criteria

- Fewer than 3 prepaid files after 100 targeted broker/accountant/seller outreaches.
- More than 50% of qualified files contain legal interpretation, indemnity, earnout, working-capital, tax, fraud, or litigation issues.
- Escrow agents/buyers refuse to acknowledge seller-prepared release files without counsel.
- No buyer/escrow/counsel acknowledgement or payment instruction from first 3 submitted files.
- Average fee below 15,000 PLN or legal/accountant review consumes more than 35% of revenue.
