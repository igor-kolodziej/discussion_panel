## Strongest Objections Captured

1. AVILOO, Moba, DEKRA, Autel, MAHLE, vsNEW, and others already provide battery-health tests or certificates.
2. Large lessors and fleets are already normalizing battery-health certificates and can internalize or outsource directly to those providers.
3. State-of-health methods are not standardized enough for the startup to claim definitive battery value.
4. BMS/OEM/app data alone may be weak evidence; buyers may still require independent testing.
5. Data Act requests may be too slow, incomplete, or inconsistent for auction/trade-in deadlines.
6. Dealers will pay only if the file changes purchase price, discount, reserve, lease-end dispute, or buyer acceptance.
7. Named EV workshop or battery-test partners are mandatory for credibility.
8. Connected-vehicle data creates privacy risk around trip, location, account, charging, and user-behavior data.
9. Dealers selling cars may dislike paying for files that reveal bad battery condition and reduce price.
10. The six-month VIN/batch target is too ambitious for an unknown part-time founder without strong partners.

## Useful Narrowing From Evaluator

Best version:

> VIN battery evidence packs for fleet remarketing, lease-end disposal, and auction reserve decisions where an independent EV workshop performs the technical test and the desk assembles warranty, recall, owner-authorized data, buyer objections, and decision wording.

More realistic POC:

- 40-100 VINs.
- 4-8 paid batches.
- 2-3 repeat channels.
- 15-25 documented buyer/auction/lease responses.
- 50,000-180,000 PLN collected.

Avoid:

- one-off consumer certificates;
- generic EV inspection;
- warranty claims;
- legal disputes;
- unauthorized OEM/app access;
- claims of battery safety certification or definitive SOH.
