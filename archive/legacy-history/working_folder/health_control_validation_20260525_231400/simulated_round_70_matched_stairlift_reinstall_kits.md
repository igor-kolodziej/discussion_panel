## Materially Different Candidate

`Matched Stairlift Reinstall Kit Bank`

This acquires complete used stairlift systems from estate clear-outs, renovations, downsizing families, care-home refits, and demolition contractors before they are scrapped or split. Each kit is kept as a matched package: rail sections, chair, motor/gearbox, controls/remotes, batteries, charger, brackets, photos, measurements, removal notes, service history, and condition record. It sells project-ready low-cost stairlift reinstall kits through qualified installer partners to families preparing homes for 50-60+ spouses/parents or soon-elderly owners who need fast mobility adaptation.

The startup does not give medical advice, disability assessment, or perform installation unless using qualified installers. It controls complete titled kits, removal/pickup rights, measurement records, installer handoff, and paid reservations.

## Buyer / Payer

- adult children and 50-60+ homeowners adapting homes after injury, surgery, falls, or parent move-in;
- stairlift installers who need cheaper/fast used kit options for price-sensitive buyers;
- occupational therapists/social workers/care coordinators who refer families needing immediate home adaptation;
- estate clear-out firms, renovation crews, and families removing old stairlifts who want quick paid pickup.

## Acute Trigger

A spouse/parent cannot safely use stairs after surgery, fall, stroke, arthritis flare, or frailty shift. New stairlift quotes may be expensive or slow. Used systems often get discarded during renovation because buyers cannot verify completeness, fit, or installer readiness. A complete measured kit with installer review can turn waste into a fast paid install.

## Control Point

The acquired/optioned asset is:

- complete titled stairlift kits, not random chairs or rails;
- rail geometry, staircase measurements, photos, handedness, landing constraints, rail length, curve/straight type, bracket count, and removal notes;
- battery/charger/control/remotes condition;
- old service/install paperwork where available;
- first-refusal pickup rights with estate clearers, renovation crews, demolition contractors, care-home refitters, and stairlift installers;
- installer partner review and "fit candidate" notes;
- paid family/installer reservations before refurbishment or delivery.

The hard control is: complete matched kit + measurement/condition record + first-refusal removal rights + installer partner + paid reservation.

## Why This Is Not A Service/Report/Dashboard/Marketplace

This owns complete physical kits with measurements and installer-reviewed fit notes. It is not a directory, care-coordination service, or generic used-equipment marketplace.
