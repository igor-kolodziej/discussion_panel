Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
Pediatric Sleep-EEG Slot Yield Rail

One-sentence thesis:
Private EEG labs lose scarce pediatric sleep and sleep-deprived EEG capacity when parents mishandle preparation, children fail to sleep, or families cancel late; the wedge is a lab-routed, paid slot-yield workflow that controls every booked pediatric sleep-EEG readiness path and a standby family list, while the lab and licensed clinicians remain responsible for all medical work.

Exact buyer:
Private EEG labs, neurodiagnostic clinics, and small neurology clinics in Poland that perform pediatric sleep EEG, sleep-deprived EEG, or home/mobile pediatric sleep EEG and feel the operational cost of failed sleep, late cancellations, parent anxiety, bad hair/prep, missed arrival windows, or long evening slots that cannot be refilled. Start with owner-managed private labs in Warsaw, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, Lodz, and nearby cities where each failed child sleep slot is financially visible. The lab is the buyer and medical provider; parents are routed only through the lab-approved workflow.

Acute trigger:
A booked pediatric sleep or sleep-deprived EEG slot has meaningful failure risk because the child may not sleep, the parent may misunderstand sleep-deprivation timing, meals, screens, medication questions, hair preparation, transport timing, or arrival expectations, or the family may cancel late. The lab loses slot revenue, technician time, evening capacity, parent goodwill, and sometimes a repeat booking. The stronger commercial trigger is a lab with recent failed pediatric sleep EEGs, late cancels, or a visible gap between booked long slots and usable recordings.

Control point:
A signed lab pilot makes the workflow mandatory for a defined set of booked pediatric sleep or sleep-deprived EEG appointments for 30 days. The startup controls the non-clinical readiness queue, lab-approved instruction delivery, parent completion checks, risk scoring, exception routing back to lab staff, cancellation triage, standby-family list, refill attempts for released slots, timestamped readiness logs, and slot-yield report. The lab controls medical indications, clinical instructions, technician work, recording, report interpretation, patient relationship, consent, and medical records.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
One signed paid lab pilot covering all pediatric sleep or sleep-deprived EEG bookings for 30 days, with lab-approved parent instructions and DPA/processor terms. Route 30-50 booked pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG appointments through the workflow, collect payment for at least 10 routed cases or a fixed pilot fee, build a standby list of at least 25 families willing to take recovered slots, complete readiness checks for at least 75% of routed bookings, flag high-risk prep cases before appointment day, fill at least one late-cancelled or released slot from standby demand, and produce a before/after or baseline-vs-pilot report on failed sleep, no-show, late-cancel, and refill outcomes. Target 12,000-35,000 PLN collected in the first 60 days across one or two labs.

6-month POC:
Sign 6-12 private EEG labs or neurodiagnostic clinics into paid pilots or monthly slot-yield retainers. Route 500-1,500 pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG bookings. Maintain city-specific standby lists with 250+ opted-in families. Show lab-level improvements in readiness completion, fewer avoidable no-sleep failures where baseline exists, fewer late-cancel empty slots, or more refilled long slots. Collect 180,000-600,000 PLN revenue through per-routed-case fees, recovered-slot fees, and monthly retainers. Keep gross margin above 70% after support calls, workflow tooling, data-processing costs, and a contracted nurse/EEG-lab reviewer for instruction QA. Expand only after the pediatric sleep EEG workflow proves measurable lift.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 80-120 private EEG labs and neurodiagnostic clinics from public price lists, clinic directories, and search results. Contact owner/managers with a narrow offer: "Route your next 30 pediatric sleep or sleep-deprived EEG bookings through a readiness and standby-fill workflow; pay only a fixed pilot fee or per routed case; no software integration and no clinical claims." Use public price-list evidence, failed-slot fees, and parent-prep friction as conversation anchors. In parallel, create search pages for parents looking for fast child sleep EEG slots, but use them mainly to build standby demand routed back through participating labs.

Economics:
Charge 80-160 PLN per routed pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG booking, or a 3,000-8,000 PLN 30-day lab pilot, plus 20-30% of recovered slot value for late-cancel slots filled from the standby list. A single saved or refilled 800-1,100 PLN pediatric sleep EEG slot can justify several routed cases. Delivery costs are mostly templated messaging, completion checks, exception calls, standby-list management, and simple reporting. No EEG equipment is bought in the first POC. No physician interpretation or technician labor is resold by the startup.

Copy risk:
Labs can copy basic reminder messages, clinic software can add SMS sequences, and larger neurodiagnostic groups can internalize parent prep. The defense is that most labs do not treat pediatric sleep-EEG yield as a focused revenue operation, while the startup accumulates cross-lab age-specific readiness patterns, refusal/cancel reasons, parent confusion points, standby-family demand, refill scripts, timing benchmarks, and measurable slot-yield reports. The workflow becomes harder to copy if labs rely on the external standby list and if the founder owns the fastest parent-prep and recovered-slot process across multiple city labs.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
For a specific pilot, the lab has already authorized the startup to route every included pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG booking through the readiness queue and standby-fill workflow. The startup controls the live parent completion state, risk flags, cancellation/refill attempts, standby family matching, and pilot report for those exact booked slots. A clinic system or internal receptionist can copy generic reminders later, but cannot retroactively control the in-flight readiness queue, standby demand, and measured outcomes for the already-routed pilot period.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business does not sell generic patient reminders, clinic software, a directory of EEG labs, medical advice, a dashboard, or a report. It operates a controlled slot-yield workflow for named booked pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG appointments: lab-approved readiness path, completion checks, risk escalation, cancellation triage, standby list, recovered-slot fill attempts, and measured revenue/yield outcomes. The POC counts only if a lab makes routing mandatory for real bookings and pays for routed cases, recovered slots, or a fixed pilot.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not diagnose, interpret EEG, recommend treatment, decide whether an EEG is indicated, change sleep-deprivation or medication instructions, provide medical advice, handle raw EEG recordings, produce medical records, employ itself as a medical provider, or promise diagnostic accuracy. The lab remains medical provider and patient relationship owner. Licensed EEG technicians perform recordings. Licensed/qualified physicians or EEG specialists interpret reports. Lab staff approve all parent-facing instructions and receive every clinical or safety question. The startup signs DPA/processor terms, processes only the minimum scheduling/readiness data needed for the workflow, deletes or returns data under the lab agreement, and routes any medical, medication, seizure, safety, consent, or clinical question back to the lab.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack because it does not review startup claims for investors and does not produce a memo. It is not Supplement Stack Safety because it does not involve supplements or consumer safety triage. It is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk, HeritageDoor Suites, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM Import Continuity, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs, HeatQuiet, or any Amazon/platform/recovery/evidence-desk idea. The buyer trigger, control point, proof artifact, and economics are materially different: lab-routed pediatric sleep-EEG booking yield and standby-slot recovery, not regulatory procurement evidence, physical heritage assets, claim recovery, import continuity, or marketplace listing rescue.

Strongest anticipated objections:
The buyer pool may be smaller than it looks after filtering for private labs with meaningful pediatric sleep EEG volume. Labs may believe simple SMS reminders are enough. Staff may resist an outside workflow touching parents. Parents may ignore completion checks despite routing. Measured improvement may be noisy without clean baseline failure and cancellation data. The standby list may be hard to build or may not match the exact time/location constraints of released slots. Medical-data and parent trust boundaries require disciplined contracts and lab approval. The business could remain a useful niche service rather than a large company unless it expands into other high-failure neurodiagnostic and pediatric appointment-yield workflows. Labs can internalize the playbook if the startup does not create cross-lab benchmarks, standby demand, and measurable outcomes quickly.
