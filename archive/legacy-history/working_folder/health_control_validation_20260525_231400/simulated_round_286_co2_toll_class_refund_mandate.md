## Internal Strict Simulation

### Why It Clears The Simulation Gate

- **Concrete control proof:** signed fleet mandate, vehicle list, Toll Collect statements, registration/CoC/CIF evidence, portal application screenshots, first class-change confirmation, refund filing, approved refund, offset, future-rate correction, or clean no-go.
- **Cash measurable:** per-vehicle toll statements allow before/after toll-rate delta and refund/first-year savings calculation.
- **Current official trigger:** CO2 emission class is already a toll tariff feature; evidence checks and customer-portal corrections are active.
- **Buyer pain:** high-mileage cross-border fleets can overpay materially if eligible trucks remain in default or wrong classes.
- **No regulated advice if bounded:** the startup does not decide legal entitlement; it reconstructs vehicle evidence and portal claim files for fleet-owned submission or authorized submission.
- **Founder fit:** data extraction from toll statements, VIN/registration parsing, document collection, portal workflow tracking, and Polish/English/German operations fit an analytical Warsaw founder with contractors.

### Internal Objections

1. Toll-card providers, EETS providers, truck dealers, leasing companies, fleet accountants, and Toll Collect self-service can handle many cases.
2. Many vehicles will correctly remain class 1, especially older vehicles or vehicles without required evidence.
3. Retroactive refunds may be limited by rules, deadlines, or customer error.
4. Class corrections may produce future savings more often than cash refunds, weakening success-fee logic.
5. Data access includes vehicle registrations, Toll Collect portal data, invoices, OBU/account status, and fleet spend.
6. If the founder accepts appeals, fines, legal arguments, or tax/VAT issues, the scope becomes unsafe.
7. Cold outbound to fleets may be noisy; channels through fleet accountants, leasing companies, truck dealers, or associations are required.
