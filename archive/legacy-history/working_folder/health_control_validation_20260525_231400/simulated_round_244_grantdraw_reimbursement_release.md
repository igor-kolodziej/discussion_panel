## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- Grant consultants, accountants, auditors, and beneficiary project managers are natural incumbents.
- Many delayed reimbursement cases are delayed because of genuine eligibility, procurement, VAT, deliverable, conflict-of-interest, state-aid, audit, or public-finance problems.
- Authority acceptance and payment timing remain outside founder control.
- Sensitive financial, payroll, supplier, and public-project data create trust friction.
- Success fees may be awkward for public money; fixed fees and authority-accepted task milestones are safer.
- The market may be lumpy across programs; one generic desk can become program-by-program procedural archaeology.
- It is only strong if it starts after a signed grant agreement, incurred eligible costs, and a written payment-request deficiency or deadline.
