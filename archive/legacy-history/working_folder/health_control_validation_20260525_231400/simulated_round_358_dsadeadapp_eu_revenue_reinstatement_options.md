## Selected Candidate

Idea name: DSAApp EU Revenue Reinstatement Option Bank

One-sentence thesis: Option, acquire, or revenue-share with small app publishers whose iOS or Android apps have non-EU revenue and historical EU demand but whose EU app-store availability, updates, or monetization is blocked by DSA trader-verification status, restoring EU availability before any seller payout.

## Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof exists: app option/transfer, platform status, restored availability, payout.
- Copy risk is meaningful, but transaction-specific app control prevents immediate copy of a signed file.
- CAC/payback is credible only if the founder filters for apps with historical EU revenue or visible non-EU MRR; weak apps must be rejected.
- Not legal/tax advice if the seller supplies true identity/contact documents and platform terms are followed.
- Main cap risk is platform/account transfer, seller scarcity, and technical debt. The idea clears simulated gate only because it refuses upfront purchases and treats no-go outcomes as acceptable proof.
