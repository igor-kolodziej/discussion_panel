Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: F-Gas Import Licence Continuity Desk.

One-sentence thesis:
Provide a broker-first import-continuity desk for SME HVAC, refrigeration, heat-pump, refrigerant, and pre-charged-equipment importers whose shipments or planned import flows are blocked or at risk because F-gas Portal registration, import licence status, HFC quota/authorization coverage, CO2e threshold, declaration-of-conformity, labelling, or customs document evidence is incomplete.

Exact buyer:
Primary channel buyers are Polish/CEE customs brokers, freight forwarders, and customs agencies serving importers of refrigeration equipment, air-conditioning equipment, heat pumps, HVAC units, pre-charged products/equipment, refrigerant cylinders, and components containing or relying on fluorinated greenhouse gases.

Economic buyers are SME importers, HVAC distributors, refrigeration wholesalers, heat-pump distributors, service-equipment importers, and equipment traders whose non-EU imports may require F-gas Portal registration/licence evidence, quota authorization coverage, declaration of conformity, labelling evidence, supplier refrigerant quantities, or customs/SW documentation before release or continued import planning.

Acute trigger:
Regulation (EU) 2024/573 applies to fluorinated greenhouse gases and products/equipment containing or relying on them. The Commission F-gas Portal is used for HFC quota management and import/export licensing. Importers of pre-charged equipment/products may need valid F-gas Portal registration as an import licence, quota or authorization coverage for relevant HFC quantities, declaration-of-conformity evidence, labelling, and shipment/product data. If the importer or broker cannot prove the required portal/licence/quota/declaration status, a shipment can be delayed, import planning can stop, or the broker/importer can face urgent compliance escalation.

Transferable control point:
- Broker-routed current shipment or import-planning case, not generic F-gas readiness.
- Importer authorization to review customs documents, supplier/product documents, F-gas Portal status screenshots/exports, quota or authorization evidence, and product refrigerant data.
- Shipment/product file: CN/HS code if already supplied by broker, invoice, packing list, product model, supplier data sheet, refrigerant type, charge quantity, number of units, CO2e calculation inputs, declaration-of-conformity status, labels/photos where available, F-gas Portal registration ID/status, quota/authorization coverage notes, and customs questions.
- Prepaid fixed-scope continuity packet before work begins.
- Expert escalation lane for F-gas/customs/tax/legal questions; founder does not decide legal status or file customs declarations.
- Case memory by product type, refrigerant, source country, supplier document gaps, portal-status issue, quota-authorization pattern, customs question, and broker/importer outcome.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 2-3 broker/freight-forwarder/customs-agency routing agreements.
- 5-10 prepaid current shipment or import-planning cases with importer authorization.
- Data-processing/NDA terms for supplier/import documents and portal-status evidence.
- Optional reviewer/escalation terms with F-gas/customs specialist for flagged cases.
- First 3-5 completed broker-branded continuity packets with documented outcome: release-readiness evidence, missing supplier documents, quota/authorization gap, portal-registration gap, customs-specialist escalation, paused import decision, or no-go.

6-month proof:
1. Build a target list of 40 Polish/CEE customs brokers/freight forwarders and 40 SME HVAC/refrigeration/heat-pump importers/distributors with non-EU equipment flows.
2. Sign 2-3 broker/forwarder routing agreements with partners that already see F-gas import-document questions.
3. Close 10-25 prepaid current cases at 6,000-25,000 PLN each.
4. Collect 80,000-300,000 PLN revenue with >60% gross margin after reviewer/partner/tooling costs.
5. Complete 10+ broker-branded continuity packets within 3-7 business days.
6. Show at least 5 cases where the packet reduces customs/broker back-and-forth, identifies a missing portal/licence/quota/declaration item before shipment, supports import planning, triggers paid specialist escalation, or prevents a bad shipment from proceeding.
7. Produce a repeatable F-gas import evidence checklist for one narrow segment first: pre-charged HVAC/heat-pump/refrigeration equipment.
8. Reject or escalate cases requiring legal opinions, customs declarations, quota trading, portal filings, chemical safety advice, product conformity certification, or false supplier statements.

First acquisition mechanism:
Broker pitch: "Your HVAC/refrigeration importer clients now face F-gas portal, licence, quota, declaration, and labelling questions that are not ordinary customs admin. Send one current import case. I turn the documents you already hold into a broker-branded continuity packet: what is present, missing, inconsistent, needs importer action, needs supplier evidence, or needs F-gas/customs specialist escalation. You keep the customs relationship and filing work."

Importer pitch through broker: "Before the shipment is stuck, send the broker-authorized file for one product family or current shipment. We organize F-gas Portal/licence/quota/declaration/label evidence and tell you what is ready, missing, or unsafe to proceed without specialist review. We do not file customs declarations or replace your legal/customs adviser."

Economics:
- Fixed-scope case fee: 6,000-12,000 PLN for one shipment/product-family continuity packet.
- Urgent or multi-product case: 12,000-25,000 PLN where shipment value or import flow justifies it.
- Partner share: 15-30% for broker/forwarder routing.
- Reviewer/escalation cost: 1,000-5,000 PLN only for flagged F-gas/customs questions.
- Buyer value: a delayed HVAC/heat-pump/refrigeration shipment or paused import flow can be worth far more than the fee; brokers also protect client relationships and avoid being blamed for missing F-gas evidence.
- No free generic readiness reports. No quota brokerage. No legal advice. No customs filing. No work without a current import/shipment case and prepaid fee.

Copy risk:
Customs brokers, F-gas consultants, HVAC compliance consultants, and Big Four can copy generic F-gas explanations. They cannot instantly copy signed broker lanes, current shipment/product files, importer authorizations, supplier-document-gap memory, portal-status issue patterns, and broker-branded packet formats once those cases are in process. The first defensible asset is a local broker workflow around current import continuity, not a regulation summary.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder starts with brokers and importers that have current document pain. Once the broker routes a shipment file and the importer authorizes review, another consultant would need to displace an active import-continuity process during a shipment or ordering deadline. Each case improves a practical map of which equipment files, supplier documents, portal statuses, quota/authorization evidence, and labels cause friction.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls prepaid current import/shipment files routed by brokers and returns broker-branded continuity packets tied to actual F-gas import evidence gaps. It does not sell a dashboard, generic climate compliance report, quota marketplace, legal advisory, customs filing, or broad HVAC consulting.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not act as importer, customs declarant, indirect representative, quota broker, F-gas Portal filer, legal adviser, tax adviser, chemical-safety adviser, product certifier, or customs broker. The importer and broker remain responsible for legal compliance, portal submissions, customs declarations, quota/authorization validity, declarations of conformity, product labelling, and final shipment decisions. F-gas/customs/legal specialists handle flagged interpretation or filing tasks. Cases with suspected false documents, illegal refrigerants, quota trading requests, sanctions/customs fraud, disputed importer identity, or missing supplier truth are rejected or escalated.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not CBAM Import Continuity Desk. CBAM covers carbon-emissions exposure, authorised CBAM declarant status, default values, certificate-price planning, and CBAM import thresholds. F-Gas Import Licence Continuity Desk covers Regulation (EU) 2024/573, F-gas Portal registration/licence status, HFC quota/authorization coverage, pre-charged equipment declarations, refrigerant quantity/CO2e evidence, and customs import evidence for HVAC/refrigeration/heat-pump equipment.

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk, BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack, TraceFaktura, EUDR, ICS2, RED Cyber, KasaSerwis, MailDomain, or HeritageDoor Suites.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether brokers already handle F-gas import evidence internally or with existing F-gas consultants.
- Whether enough SME HVAC/refrigeration/heat-pump importers have current shipment pain rather than general curiosity.
- Whether F-gas portal/quota/licence questions require specialist legal/customs action too often for a fixed pack to be valuable.
- Whether the founder can access portal-status evidence and supplier refrigerant data quickly enough.
- Whether pricing is credible before a broker relationship exists.
- Whether the idea is too close to the confirmed CBAM broker-first import continuity pattern despite a different regulatory domain and control file.
