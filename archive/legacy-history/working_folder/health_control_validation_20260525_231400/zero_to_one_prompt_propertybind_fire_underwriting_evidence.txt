Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
PropertyBind Fire Underwriting Evidence Release Pack

One-sentence thesis:
A broker-routed underwriting response desk for commercial property owners whose policy bind, renewal, lender insurance condition, or property closing is blocked by a live insurer request for fire-protection, electrical, thermography, sprinkler, alarm, storage, protective-safeguard, or loss-control evidence.

Exact buyer:
Commercial-property brokers, wholesale brokers, property owners, property managers, warehouse operators, light-industrial owners, retail-park owners, hotel groups, multifamily owners, and CRE buyers with a live insurer, MGA, underwriter, risk-engineer, lender, or broker request blocking policy bind, renewal, financing, lease covenant compliance, or property closing.

Acute trigger:
An insurer will not bind, renew, remove a condition, quote competitively, or satisfy a lender until fire-protection and property-risk evidence is complete. The request may cover sprinkler inspections, fire-alarm tests, hydrant/water supply, electrical thermography, electrical repairs, fire doors, impairment logs, hot-work controls, battery/lithium storage, tenant hazards, storage height, roof/electrical upgrades, protective safeguards, loss-control recommendation closure, or contractor certificates.

Control point:
Signed broker or owner mandate for one named policy event or closing; live underwriter/MGA/risk-engineer/lender request and deadline; existing inspection certificates, contractor reports, photos, invoices, impairment logs, fire-alarm/sprinkler records, thermography reports, tenant/hazard notes, and protective-safeguard evidence; qualified reviewer or contractor sign-off where technical interpretation is needed; underwriter response matrix; missing-evidence/no-go table; submitted broker-ready packet; and insurer, broker, or lender acknowledgement, progress, bind/renewal, narrowed subjectivity, or documented no-go. The startup controls a live broker/owner-authorized underwriting response room.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three brokers or property managers sign routing agreements for live fire/property underwriting subjectivities; eight prepaid live cases are accepted, each with a written underwriter/broker/lender evidence request and policy or closing deadline; five cases produce bind, renewal, quote progress, narrowed subjectivity, lender acceptance, or documented no-go; one broker routes a second case; at least 80,000 PLN is collected before total spend exceeds 100,000 PLN.

6-month POC:
Handle 30-60 live subjectivity cases; collect 300,000-900,000 PLN; produce 20+ bind, renewal, progress, or no-go outcomes; build a private taxonomy of underwriter/fire-protection requests, accepted evidence forms, contractor certificate gaps, buyer property classes, and no-go patterns; keep gross margin above 60% after fire/risk reviewers and admin; reject cases needing engineering certification, remediation execution, insurance advice, claims handling, disputed coverage, or unsafe unknown conditions.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with commercial-property brokers, wholesale brokers, fire-protection contractors, thermographers, and property managers. The message is: "If a carrier, MGA, lender, or risk engineer has a live fire/electrical/protective-safeguard evidence request blocking bind or renewal, route the file. I coordinate the evidence room and response matrix with qualified contractors where needed; I do not sell insurance, certify systems, or perform remediation."

Economics:
Charge 6,000-12,000 PLN for narrow evidence-response cases, 12,000-25,000 PLN for multi-system fire/electrical packets, and 25,000-45,000 PLN for urgent warehouse/industrial/multisite bind or financing blockers. Brokers can pass the fee through to owners, owners can justify it against premium increases, lender deadlines, vacancy risk, failed policy bind, or delayed closing, and the founder avoids capital risk.

Copy risk:
Brokers, risk engineers, loss-control consultants, fire-protection contractors, thermographers, property managers, insurers, and specialist inspection firms can copy pieces. The defense is the specific live policy-event evidence room, broker/owner authorization, accepted underwriter wording, qualified reviewer bench, and repeated case memory by underwriter request type and property class.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the broker or owner signs the mandate, provides the live underwriter request, evidence access, deadline, and property file, the startup controls that exact policy-event response room. A broker, fire contractor, or risk engineer can do similar work later, but cannot instantly displace the submitted response file and case-memory path without the broker/owner restarting the evidence process during a bind or renewal deadline.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell insurance, broker policies, perform inspections, certify fire systems, engineer sprinkler designs, remediate buildings, appraise property, adjust claims, or provide generic risk reports. It accepts only live underwriting/bind/renewal/closing blockers with a written evidence request and deadline. The output is a submitted underwriter-response packet and bind/renewal/progress/no-go outcome for a named policy event.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Licensed brokers remain responsible for insurance advice, policy placement, coverage interpretation, and carrier communication. Fire contractors, thermographers, engineers, electricians, alarm contractors, sprinkler inspectors, and property managers remain responsible for inspections, repairs, technical certification, code compliance, and site safety. The startup rejects engineering design, legal advice, claims handling, public adjusting, coverage disputes, remediation execution, unsafe unknown conditions, missing authority, fake certificates, and any case requiring the founder to certify property safety.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not CyberBind or BrokerLock because the buyer trigger is commercial property/fire protection underwriting, not cyber insurance controls. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, Data Act, CBAM, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, KSeF, LeaseClear, ShowComms, RMA credit recovery, MiCARail, or any health/Rx/clinical route. The payment event is insurance bind/renewal/financing progress on a named property policy event through physical fire-risk evidence.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Brokers, risk engineers, fire contractors, thermographers, and loss-control consultants may already handle these requests. Unknown-founder trust is weak when property coverage and lender deadlines are at stake. Many cases will require real inspections, repairs, sprinkler design, code compliance, or engineering certification rather than evidence organization. Brokers may not want a third party between them and the carrier. Underwriters may ignore a packet if it is not signed by a recognised broker, engineer, or contractor. The founder may become a low-margin document coordinator unless broker routing and repeated accepted evidence patterns compound quickly.
