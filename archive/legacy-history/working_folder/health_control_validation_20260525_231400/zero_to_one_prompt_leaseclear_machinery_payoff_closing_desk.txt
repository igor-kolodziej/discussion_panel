Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
LeaseClear Machinery Payoff Closing Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A closing desk for already-matched used industrial-equipment sales where the buyer is ready but payment is blocked because the machine is still leased, financed, pledged, or title-controlled, and the transaction needs a clean tri-party payoff, release, invoice, and handover file.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE SME equipment sellers, lessees, used-equipment buyers, search funds, machine dealers, insolvency asset managers, and brokers trying to close a named sale of a serial-numbered industrial machine or equipment lot where buyer and seller already exist but clean title cannot be proven yet.

Acute trigger:
A used-equipment sale worth 150,000-1,000,000 PLN is ready to close, but cash cannot move because the seller does not hold clean title or cannot prove lien-free transfer. The buyer will not wire until the lessor, lender, or pledge holder confirms payoff, release, VAT/invoice chain, pickup consent, asset identity, and no duplicate sale. The seller needs proceeds or wants to retire, close, or upgrade, and the buyer has a production, resale, or installation deadline.

Control point:
Signed seller or lessee mandate for one named machine or lot; signed buyer reservation or deposit subject to clean payoff/release; lessor or lender payoff letter or balance statement; law-firm escrow or direct buyer-to-lessor payoff instruction; release or consent letter from lessor/lender; serial-number and asset-identification packet; invoice/VAT and handover checklist; pickup/release authorization; closing statement; and founder fee paid from closing or upfront. The startup controls the live closing file, not the buyer introduction.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three live equipment-sale closing mandates where buyer and seller already exist and blocked transaction value is above 150,000 PLN; one lawyer/accountant escrow or direct-payoff structure memo confirming the founder is not holding client funds, providing legal advice, or acting as unlicensed lender/broker; two lessor/lender payoff or release paths obtained in writing; one transaction closes with clean payoff, release, buyer handover, and founder fee paid; at least 25,000 PLN in collected fees or escrow-approved closing fees before total spend exceeds 100,000 PLN.

6-month POC:
Handle 15-25 screened live closing files; accept 8-12 clean cases; close 5-8 transactions; move 2,000,000-6,000,000 PLN of equipment sale value through lawyer escrow or direct payoff structures; collect 120,000-300,000 PLN in fixed, success, or closing fees; keep rejected cases above 40% if ownership, dispute, fraud, insolvency, export-control, tax, title, or safety risk is unclear; build a private payoff/release pattern library by lessor type, asset class, invoice-chain problem, and buyer objection.

First acquisition mechanism:
Contact used-equipment brokers, machine dealers, leasing brokers, insolvency asset managers, accountants serving machine shops, and SME owners with a narrow offer: "If buyer and seller already exist but title/payoff/release is blocking payment, I coordinate the closing file with the lessor, buyer, seller, and escrow lawyer. I do not list the machine or hold funds. I get paid only on clean closing or with a fixed screened-file fee."

Economics:
Charge 8,000-18,000 PLN fixed closing/admin fees for clean single-machine files, plus 1-3% of transaction value for larger or urgent lots, with a cap or split between buyer and seller. The fee is small relative to a blocked 150,000-1,000,000 PLN transaction, lessor payoff, buyer production deadline, seller closure need, or broker commission at risk. The model avoids inventory purchase, receivable purchase, and founder custody of client money.

Copy risk:
Equipment dealers, leasing companies, machine brokers, insolvency firms, accountants, and lawyers can solve parts of this. The defense is narrow specialization in already-matched buyer/seller/lessor equipment-sale closings, repeated payoff/release patterns, buyer-safe closing checklists, fast tri-party coordination, and signed control of the active closing file.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the seller or lessee signs the closing mandate, the buyer signs a conditional reservation or deposit, and the lessor/lender provides payoff or release instructions for the exact serial-numbered asset, the startup controls that active closing workflow. A dealer, lawyer, or lessor can offer a similar process later, but cannot instantly displace the already-authorized closing file without the parties restarting or reassigning the transaction.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only current equipment sales with named buyer, named seller, identified serial-numbered asset, blocked payoff/title issue, and imminent cash movement. It does not list equipment, introduce buyers, appraise machinery, provide legal advice, finance purchases, hold escrow funds, build a marketplace, sell a title report, or broker generic introductions. The output is a completed payoff/release/handover closing for one specific transaction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Use law-firm escrow or direct buyer-to-lessor payoff, not founder custody of client funds. The startup does not provide legal advice, tax advice, financing, regulated escrow, debt collection, insolvency administration, equipment inspection, safety certification, export-control advice, sanctions advice, or machinery appraisal. Lawyers, accountants, lessors, lenders, equipment inspectors, technical experts, and the parties remain responsible for legal, tax, financing, technical, safety, and accounting decisions. Reject disputed ownership, litigation, insolvency without appointed authority, stolen-risk flags, sanctions or export-control risk, unsafe equipment, missing serial identity, buyer/seller mismatch, tax fraud risk, anti-assignment restrictions that cannot be cleared, or unclear authority to sell.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not the failed forklift lease-end option buyout because the founder is not speculating on residual purchase options or buying used forklifts. The buyer and seller already exist; the control point is the cash/title/payoff closing file. It is not ShowComms, hotel FF&E, HeritageDoor, or other physical-lot ideas because the startup does not own inventory. It is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, 3PL recovery, LC discrepancy, retention release, PFRON, DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, BioSignal, or TraceFaktura because the payment event is not a deduction, platform claim, regulatory evidence packet, public offset, or municipal invoice rail.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Equipment dealers, brokers, leasing companies, accountants, and lawyers may already handle clean payoff/title closings. Many apparent cases will be messy: hidden liens, anti-assignment clauses, insolvency, tax/VAT chain issues, missing authority, unsafe equipment, export controls, disputed condition, buyer financing, or fraud flags. Unknown-founder trust is weak around high-value machinery and title documents. If the case is clean enough, parties may handle it themselves; if painful enough, it may require lawyers or litigation. Deal flow may be lumpy. The founder can become a transaction coordinator with weak defensibility unless repeated broker referrals and payoff-pattern memory compound.
