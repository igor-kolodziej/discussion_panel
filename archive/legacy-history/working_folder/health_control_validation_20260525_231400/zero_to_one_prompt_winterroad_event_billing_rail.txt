Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: WinterRoad Event Billing Rail.

One-sentence thesis: become the mandatory current-month event-evidence rail for small Polish winter road, parking-lot, school, and facility snow/ice-maintenance contractors whose gmina or institutional invoices are disputed unless callout time, weather trigger, route, vehicle/equipment hours, salt/sand usage, before/after photos, and acceptance notes are captured at the operational moment.

Exact buyer: small and mid-sized contractors that perform winter road maintenance, parking-lot snow clearing, school/facility access clearing, sanding/salting, emergency icy-road response, sidewalk clearing, or public-infrastructure winter callouts for gminas, counties, schools, housing associations, property managers, logistics parks, hospitals, and industrial sites. First wedge is contractors with 2-20 vehicles/equipment units and 2-15 institutional contracts that currently reconstruct monthly invoices from driver notes, phone calls, photos, GPS snippets, and salt invoices.

Acute trigger: the contractor's customer disputes or delays a winter invoice because event proof is weak, scattered, late, or inconsistent. Common blockers:

- Was there a customer callout or weather trigger?
- Which route/segment/site was serviced?
- What time did the crew arrive and leave?
- Which vehicle, plow, spreader, loader, or tractor was used?
- How much salt, sand, or mixture was applied?
- Were before/after conditions documented?
- Did the customer representative, dispatcher, or site contact accept the event?
- Why do weather records, driver notes, and invoice lines not match?

Control point: the founder controls value only when the contractor agrees that all winter billable events for a named contract/month must route through the founder's intake before invoice submission:

- signed contractor agreement for one customer contract, one route cluster, or one winter month;
- prepaid setup/monthly fee or prepaid event bundle before the first billable event;
- named customer contract, invoice rules, unit rates, sites/routes, vehicles, material types, acceptance requirements, and billing calendar;
- event intake via phone/WhatsApp/form/SMS/photo upload/GPS pin and dispatcher confirmation, without requiring custom software first;
- daily/weekly evidence packet: event log, weather note/source, route/site photos, vehicle/equipment time, material usage, exceptions, missing evidence, and customer signoff/acceptance notes;
- month-end invoice attachment packet matched to customer contract terms;
- written rule that the founder does not certify road safety, weather conditions, public procurement compliance, or legal entitlement to payment.

6-month proof:

1. Recruit 20 winter contractors before the season or before framework-contract renewal.
2. Close 3 prepaid pilots for named customer contracts or route clusters.
3. Capture at least 50 live winter events/callouts or preseason paid dry-run events using the contractor's actual contract rules.
4. Produce invoice attachment packets for at least 2 customer billing periods.
5. Prove that at least one customer accepts, pays faster, asks fewer follow-up questions, or narrows a dispute because the packet exists.
6. Prove at least 6,000 PLN monthly recurring revenue or 20,000 PLN seasonal prepaid revenue from pilots.
7. Show 70%+ gross margin using simple intake, templates, contractor/admin labor, and no custom app build.
8. Build a case-memory file of accepted event proofs, rejected proofs, customer-specific invoice rules, material-usage evidence, and weather-trigger dispute patterns.

First acquisition mechanism:

Contractor pitch:
"Your winter invoice gets challenged because evidence is reconstructed after the fact. For one route/customer this season, every callout routes through my evidence intake before invoice submission. You get weekly and month-end packets matched to the contract: weather trigger, route/site, time, vehicle, material, before/after photos, exceptions, and acceptance notes."

Customer/property-manager pitch used by contractor:
"For this route, every snow/ice event will have a standard attachment packet. You can check route, time, material usage, photos, and acceptance notes during the month instead of debating the invoice after month-end."

Economics:

- Setup fee: 2,000-6,000 PLN per customer contract/route cluster.
- Monthly/event-season fee: 1,500-5,000 PLN per contract depending on event volume and sites.
- Optional per-event fee: 50-150 PLN for high-volume callouts.
- Target first pilot: 8,000-20,000 PLN seasonal prepayment or 3,000-8,000 PLN for a one-month proof contract.
- Costs: intake/admin contractors, template preparation, secure storage, phone/SMS/form tools, customer-specific invoice packet preparation.
- Target gross margin: 70%+ before custom software.
- Cash conversion: prepaid month/season or prepaid event bundle; no payment contingent on disputed invoice recovery.

Copy risk:

Fleet GPS, work-order tools, forms, WhatsApp, photos, and municipal contract software already exist. The wedge is not software. It is forcing the contractor's current billable events through one contract-specific evidence rail before month-end, with customer invoice terms encoded into the packet and accepted/rejected proof memory by gmina/customer. Once the founder is the named evidence intake for a route/month and event data starts flowing through that rail, a competitor cannot reconstruct the same current-month evidence after the fact.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup controls the live event stream for a named winter contract/month before invoices are issued. It does not sell a dashboard, fleet tracker, claims report, marketplace, route app, or after-the-fact dispute memo. The first version can be manual and phone/form-based, but the control point is mandatory event routing and invoice-attachment production.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- The founder does not certify road safety, snow/ice removal quality, public procurement compliance, weather truth, legal entitlement to payment, or contract interpretation.
- Contractors remain responsible for actual service delivery, vehicles, crews, salt/sand use, safety, permits, employment, and invoice correctness.
- Customer signoff and contractor management approval remain required where the contract requires them.
- The rail records evidence and flags missing items; it does not invent route completion or material usage.
- No child/student data, health data, or personal surveillance beyond operational vehicle/crew/site evidence needed for contract billing.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed and unconfirmed ideas:

- Not TraceFaktura animal-care event rail: no animal pickup, shelter intake, vet treatment, adoption, quarantine, death documentation, or animal-level municipal events. This is seasonal winter infrastructure maintenance with vehicles, routes, material usage, weather triggers, and road/facility invoice packets.
- Not DORA, KSC, AI Act, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, EUDR, GreenTender, PromoLeak, HeatQuiet, or HeritageDoor: no compliance questionnaire, platform listing rescue, investor diligence, import evidence, product tender, retailer deduction recovery, noise dispute, or salvage asset.
- Not generic fleet GPS: customers already may have GPS traces; the product turns all contract-required evidence into a customer-ready invoice packet during the billing month.
