## Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- It uses a real and common operational pain: Polish prosumer PV inverters shutting down or curtailing due grid overvoltage, phase imbalance, settings, or DSO-side voltage conditions.
- The buyer is not a homeowner alone; it is the installer/service company/distributor trying to avoid unpaid warranty visits, refunds, hostile reviews, and blame for grid problems.
- The control point is a live complaint ticket with inverter logs, voltage evidence, settings record, phase/load observations, customer handoff, DSO complaint package, and remediation/no-fault decision.
- The first proof is commercial: prepaid cases, one installer ticket closed, one DSO/customer response used, or one remediation sale converted.
- The duplicate risk with BatteryFit and HeatQuiet is manageable because this is not battery retrofit economics or heat-pump noise; it is grid-overvoltage complaint responsibility and evidence.

- Not capped below 87: margins/payback are credible only if sold through installers/distributors with multiple complaints, not one-off homeowner sales.
