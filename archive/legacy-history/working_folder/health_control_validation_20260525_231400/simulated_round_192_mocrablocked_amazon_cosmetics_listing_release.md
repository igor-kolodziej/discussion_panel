## Lead Candidate: MoCRABlocked Amazon Cosmetics Listing Release Desk

### Why It Advances

- It is triggered by a named commercial blocker, not broad MoCRA readiness.
- Agencies aggregate urgent seller cases and can resell the desk without hiring FDA/MoCRA expertise.
- The paid artifact is a live ASIN/SKU release packet with request interpretation, SKU-to-source evidence map, inconsistency log, supplier questions, draft response, and submit/cannot-submit/escalate decision.
- It stays outside drug/SPF/clinical categories and rejects cases where product truth, facility identity, or label/ingredient source truth is missing.
- First proof can be fast: agency agreements, paid cases, and listing/FBA/retailer/importer progress or clean no-go outcomes.

### Internal Objections

- It is still evidence-response work, and previous Amazon evidence desks have mostly failed.
- FDA/MoCRA consultants, US responsible-person providers, label advisers, and Amazon agencies are natural incumbents.
- Hard cases require actual facility registration, product listing, label correction, safety substantiation, adverse-event process, responsible-person support, or legal/regulatory judgment outside founder control.
- Amazon and retailers may remain opaque even with a good packet.
- Cosmetics claims can drift into drug/SPF/medical/label/safety issues.
- The transition may standardize quickly as agencies and cosmetics consultants learn the process.
