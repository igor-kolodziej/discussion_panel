Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

Factor-Confirmed KSeF Funding Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:

Polish suppliers using factoring or invoice finance now have receivables that factors are otherwise willing to fund or release, but the advance, reserve release, debtor confirmation, or eligibility decision is blocked by a specific KSeF/AP evidence condition; the startup accepts only factor-confirmed held funding files and converts the blocker into factor funding, reserve release, receivable eligibility restored, accepted debtor confirmation, or paid clean no-go.

Exact buyer:

Polish SME and mid-market B2B suppliers with invoices or receivables above 100,000 PLN that are already inside a factoring, invoice-finance, receivables-purchase, reverse-factoring, or brokered funding workflow. The strongest channel buyers are factoring brokers, receivables-finance advisers, outsourced CFOs, accounting offices, and finance teams whose clients have a written hold notice from a factor or finance provider saying that funding, eligibility, debtor confirmation, reserve release, or purchase of a named receivable is blocked by a KSeF/AP evidence defect.

Acute trigger:

A factor, invoice-finance provider, receivables purchaser, or factoring broker has written that a named receivable cannot be funded, purchased, confirmed, moved from pending to eligible, released from reserve, or treated as clean until KSeF number, UPO/receipt evidence, FA(3) XML or visualization, debtor access, buyer NIP/legal-entity mapping, PO/GRN/WZ tie-out, correction route, invoice reference, debtor acknowledgement, or AP confirmation matches the finance provider's eligibility rules.

Control point:

Signed funding-release mandate over a named receivable or reserve file; factor or broker written hold condition; invoice amount and debtor; KSeF number, UPO, FA(3) XML, visualization, QR/access evidence, rejection/error log, correction history, PO/order/GRN/WZ references, delivery/acceptance evidence where relevant, debtor AP/procurement contact path, seller accountant or invoicing owner, factor portal or case thread, response matrix, and release ledger showing factor funding, reserve release, eligibility restored, debtor confirmation accepted, narrowed follow-up, or clean no-go.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Six screened factor-confirmed files and three prepaid mandates from at least two suppliers or two factoring/accounting channels. Every accepted file must have receivable value above 100,000 PLN, written factor or finance-provider hold text, and a named KSeF/AP evidence condition blocking funding, eligibility, confirmation, or reserve release. At least two cases produce factor funding, reserve release, eligibility restored, debtor confirmation accepted, narrowed follow-up, or written clean no-go. First 45,000-110,000 PLN collected in fixed and release fees. No case is accepted without seller accountant or finance-owner involvement when correction legality, VAT treatment, cancellation/reissue, or tax judgment is required.

6-month POC:

Screen 60-100 factor-confirmed KSeF/AP funding blockers, accept 20-35 paid files, cover 4,000,000-14,000,000 PLN of receivable or reserve value, produce 15+ factor funding, reserve release, eligibility, debtor-confirmation, narrowed-follow-up, or clean no-go outcomes, collect 250,000-650,000 PLN in fees, secure at least two repeat referral channels from factoring brokers, invoice-finance providers, outsourced CFOs, accounting offices, or receivables advisers, reject at least 45% of weak cases, and keep gross margin above 65% after finance-ops, accounting, secure-data, and reviewer costs.

First acquisition mechanism:

Start with factoring brokers, receivables-finance advisers, invoice-finance providers, outsourced CFOs, accounting offices, and finance leads serving Polish suppliers. The outreach asks for one current receivable above 100,000 PLN where the factor already wants to fund, purchase, confirm, or release value but has written a KSeF/AP evidence condition that the supplier cannot clear quickly. The first channel pitch is: send only factor-confirmed held receivables; no general KSeF help, no disputed debt, no old collections, no tax-only correction files.

Economics:

Charge 8,000-16,000 PLN prepaid for a single factor-confirmed release file, 18,000-32,000 PLN for urgent multi-document or reserve-release files, and 0.5-1.25% of newly funded, reserve-released, or eligibility-restored value only when attribution is explicit in the before-state hold text and after-state release evidence. Fixed fees dominate because payment timing attribution can be disputed. No financing, factoring, invoice purchase, tax advisory, ERP implementation, debt collection, litigation, or payment handling is required.

Copy risk:

Factors, factoring brokers, accountants, KSeF software vendors, ERP integrators, outsourced CFOs, AP automation vendors, and receivables-finance advisers can copy pieces. The narrow defense is control over the live factor-confirmed file: written factor hold condition, seller mandate, KSeF/AP evidence, accountant route, debtor contact path, factor case thread, release ledger, and accepted/no-go outcome memory. The longer-term defense is a private map of factor-specific eligibility conditions, debtor-confirmation wording, KSeF evidence patterns, correction no-go causes, and channel trust.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

The startup does not own KSeF expertise or factoring relationships generally. It controls a specific factor-confirmed funding-release file after mandate: named receivable, factor hold condition, KSeF/AP blocker, seller finance authority, accountant-approved path, debtor AP route, factor case thread, fee right, and release ledger. Factors, accountants, and ERP vendors can solve broad KSeF or finance workflows, but they do not automatically control this urgent file once the mandate, evidence room, and response path are active.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The product is a controlled funding-release case for a named receivable. It starts only when a factor, invoice-finance provider, or broker has already written that funding, reserve release, debtor confirmation, or eligibility is held by a specific KSeF/AP evidence condition. The output is factor funding, reserve release, eligibility restored, debtor confirmation accepted, narrowed follow-up, or paid clean no-go. It is not KSeF readiness consulting, tax advisory, ERP support, AP chasing, debt collection, factoring brokerage, a dashboard, or a marketplace for accountants.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

The startup does not provide tax advice, legal advice, VAT opinions, accounting services, debt collection, financing, factoring, invoice purchase, payment services, escrow, ERP implementation, or certification of KSeF compliance. The seller remains invoice issuer and remains responsible for invoice truth, tax treatment, corrections, customer communication, and payment terms. The factor, broker, seller accountant, tax adviser, ERP vendor, legal counsel, or debtor AP team approves funding terms, correction legality, VAT treatment, receivable eligibility, and any disputed contractual issue. Cases involving fraud, fake delivery, disputed quantity, disputed price, insolvency, sanctions, litigation, tax avoidance, or buyer nonpayment unrelated to a factor-confirmed KSeF/AP evidence condition are rejected or routed out.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

This is not TraceFaktura because it is not municipal animal-care event routing. It is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retailer promotion deductions or trade-spend leakage. It is materially narrower than prior KSeF payment-release ideas because every accepted case must begin with a written factor or finance-provider hold condition over a named receivable, and the proof artifact is funding, reserve release, eligibility, debtor-confirmation acceptance, narrowed follow-up, or clean no-go. It is not DORA, CBAM, Data Act, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, GreenTender, Supplement Stack Safety, or an evidence-only compliance desk.

Strongest anticipated objections:

Clean factor-confirmed KSeF-only blockers may be rarer than expected because factors, brokers, accountants, and ERP vendors should already resolve many cases. Factor hold wording may hide deeper issues: debtor credit risk, delivery dispute, PO mismatch, GRN/WZ mismatch, buyer cash delay, reserve policy, covenant breach, fraud concern, setoff, tax uncertainty, or debtor non-confirmation. Factors may refuse to work with an outside coordinator and require supplier-submitted documents. The pain may be concentrated in the 2026 KSeF transition and normalize later. Success-fee attribution can be disputed. Sensitive finance and invoice data creates trust friction. The business can decay into generic KSeF support or receivables-finance admin unless intake stays strict.
