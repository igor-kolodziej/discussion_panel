## Selected Candidate

**Qualified-Signature Renewal Book Buyout**

### Why This Advanced

The strongest version is a micro-acquisition of an existing mobile registration agent/e-signature reseller book, not a new QES service. The startup closes only after customers approve renewal/payment transfer and the qualified trust service provider confirms the reseller/agent route. The provider remains certificate issuer and identity verifier.

### Control Point

- Seller option over customer list, renewal calendar, phone/domain/mailbox, service history, and provider/reseller route.
- Customer approvals and payment direction before seller payout.
- QSTP/provider remains provider, identity verifier, and certificate issuer.
- Current-year renewal cash before close.

### 60-Day Proof

- One seller option.
- 100 expiring certificates in the next 120 days.
- 40 customer approvals.
- 50,000 PLN collected in renewal fees.
- Provider route confirmed in writing.

### Economics

- 250-800 PLN gross margin per certificate renewal plus corporate admin packages.
- 40 approvals can generate 20,000-50,000 PLN near-term gross margin depending on client mix.
- Seller paid via earnout on retained renewals.
- Upsell annual corporate signature calendar/admin packages without taking provider-of-record role.

### Internal Caps Applied

- Capped below 93 because QSTPs can sell direct and provider margin may be limited.
- Capped below 90 because good seller books may be scarce and customers may switch.
- Not capped below 87 because the startup controls a current expiring-certificate calendar, warm seller handoff, payment direction, and QSTP route while avoiding regulated provider-of-record duties.
