## Strongest Objections

1. Seller sourcing is the bottleneck. The founder needs a provider that is closing, retiring, losing a lease, or tired enough to accept an earnout, while still having clean contracts, active customers, usable mail logs, transferable address rights, and no hidden liability.

2. If the physical address cannot continue, the book value collapses. Address continuity is the actual asset. Uncertain landlord consent, hostile building management, short lease terms, unclear mailbox capacity, seller-dependent mail access, or forced immediate address changes undermine the thesis.

3. The KRS e-Doreczenia urgency is weaker than framed. Existing KRS entities registered before 2025 already had the 2025-04-01 obligation, while the 2026-10-01 CEIDG deadline is less relevant to a KRS registered-office book. e-Doreczenia is useful as a checklist, not the main KRS wave.

4. e-Doreczenia may reduce physical-mail value over time even though it adds administrative complexity. The premium tier must depend on foreign-owner/admin pain and reliable mail custody, not only deadline anxiety.

5. Virtual-office books can contain bad customers: dormant, unreachable, fraudulent, debt/VAT-risk, shell-like, or concealment-seeking companies. The POC would need KYC refresh, contact/payment updates, termination rights, and willingness to reject suspicious accounts.

6. Mail-handling liability is asymmetric. A 99-299 PLN/month customer can create large downside if a court, tax, ZUS, bailiff, or KRS letter is missed. The model needs timestamping, scan/index logs, notification logs, clear service limits, no legal/tax interpretation, and insurance.

7. Customer re-consent may reveal weak book quality. Churn could be high once customers are asked to sign new terms and redirect payments, especially if the old provider had cheap commodity pricing or weak relationships.

## Interpretation For Next Rounds

This failure is useful because it rejects ordinary micro-operator book transfer as too small even when payment transfer and current-month cash are concrete. The next candidates need a more acute, higher-value control point: owned current cases, broker/channel-routed prepaid demand, materially higher avoided loss, or scarce asset ownership. Simple continuity books with low ARPU and commodity alternatives should be capped below the real-gate threshold.
