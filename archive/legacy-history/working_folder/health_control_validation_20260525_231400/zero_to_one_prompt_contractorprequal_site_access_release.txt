Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
ContractorPrequal Site-Access Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A prepaid release desk for CEE industrial contractors whose won job, site access, worker roster, PO activation, onboarding, or invoice path is blocked by a named customer or third-party contractor-prequalification portal until HSE, insurance, training, licence, equipment, subcontractor, or policy evidence is accepted.

Exact buyer:
CEE maintenance, installation, industrial cleaning, electrical, mechanical, scaffolding, crane/lifting, HVAC, telecom field, facility, industrial services, and specialty contractors that have already won or are close to customer work but cannot start, dispatch workers, activate a PO, pass onboarding, renew a contract, or invoice because an industrial customer or third-party prequalification portal has not accepted contractor evidence. Best first buyers are 20-300 person contractors serving factories, utilities, logistics sites, data centers, chemical plants, food plants, telecom networks, retail chains, or infrastructure owners.

Acute trigger:
A customer, plant, project owner, general contractor, or third-party prequalification portal blocks site access, onboarding, PO activation, job start, renewal, worker roster approval, or invoice approval until the contractor clears specific HSE, insurance, training, licensing, policy, incident-rate, subcontractor, equipment, quality, or worker-document requirements. Delay burns mobilized crew time, equipment reservations, shutdown/turnaround slots, customer trust, and current cash.

Control point:
Signed release mandate for a named customer/job/portal; exact portal item list or customer condition text; account access under contractor authorization or contractor-provided screenshots/exports; insurance certificates; HSE policies; training matrix; worker certificates; equipment inspection certificates; licence/registration documents; subcontractor list; incident-rate or accident-history documents where available; nonconformity/correction tracker; submitted response chronology; reviewer no-go list; customer/portal progress ledger; and rejection rules where real missing insurance, training, licences, safety program, equipment inspection, or customer-mandated remediation must be solved by qualified providers first.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Eight prepaid live site-access or onboarding release cases at 6,000-25,000 PLN each. Every case has written customer/portal condition text, a named job/PO/site/roster, deadline, blocked value, and contractor authorization. Five cases produce portal progress, accepted document set, site-access restoration, PO activation, worker roster clearance, invoice path restored, or clean no-go. Two referral sources among industrial accountants, insurance brokers, HSE consultants, staffing coordinators, equipment rental firms, or service networks send repeat cases. At least 80,000 PLN is collected and gross margin stays above 60% after HSE reviewer and document-operations costs.

6-month POC:
Handle 35-80 live contractor-prequalification release cases across 20-50 contractors and 5-10 referral partners. Collect 350,000-1,200,000 PLN in fixed case fees, urgent fees, and partner-wholesale fees. Document 25+ outcomes as portal acceptance, site access, PO activation, worker clearance, renewed vendor status, invoice path restored, or clean no-go. Build a private issue taxonomy by customer, portal, industry, document type, insurance clause, HSE policy gap, training/certificate pattern, worker-roster issue, equipment-inspection issue, subcontractor gap, and no-go cause. Keep average turnaround under five business days for evidence-ready cases. Reject at least 30% of intake where actual insurance, training, licences, safety program, equipment inspection, or incident remediation is missing.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound to owners, operations managers, shutdown coordinators, and finance leads at industrial contractors: "If a won job or active site is stuck in Avetta, ISNetworld, Achilles, SAP Ariba, a customer supplier portal, or a plant HSE onboarding process, send the exact item list and blocked job value. I package existing evidence, submit or prepare the portal response under your authorization, and give a clean no-go where real HSE, insurance, licence, training, or equipment gaps must be fixed." Partner routes are insurance brokers, HSE consultants, payroll/staffing coordinators, equipment rental firms, and industrial accountants who see contractors blocked by customer portals but do not want to run evidence assembly.

Economics:
Charge 6,000-10,000 PLN for a bounded single-customer evidence-ready release case, 12,000-25,000 PLN for urgent multi-worker or multi-document site-access holds, and 25,000-45,000 PLN for shutdown/turnaround or multi-site blocked job files. Partner wholesale can be 5,000-15,000 PLN per case. Contractor ROI is credible when a 100,000-1,000,000+ PLN job, mobilized crew, equipment slot, or invoice path is held by portal acceptance. Costs are document operations, secure handling, translation, and HSE reviewer checks, not safety training delivery, insurance brokerage, legal advice, or remediation.

Copy risk:
HSE consultants, insurance brokers, industrial admins, customer-success teams at portals, safety training firms, staffing coordinators, and contractor back offices can copy pieces. The defense is narrow live-job focus, fast evidence assembly, customer/portal wording memory, repeat referral partners, no-go discipline, and active control over the submitted response chronology for a named blocked job before competitors see the case.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the contractor signs the prepaid mandate, supplies the portal item list, authorizes account access or screenshots, provides the source evidence, assigns internal evidence owners, and uses the startup's submission chronology under a job-start or invoice deadline, another provider cannot take over that exact blocked site-access file without the contractor switching mid-deadline. Competitors can sell general HSE support, but they do not control the already-paid release file, response chronology, and customer/portal outcome tracker for that named job.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only named contractor-prequalification blockers tied to a won job, active site, worker roster, PO activation, renewal, onboarding, or invoice path. The output is a release artifact used in an active workflow: portal-ready evidence file, missing-item map, submitted response chronology, customer/portal response wording, accepted document set, site-access progress, or clean no-go. It does not sell generic HSE consulting, safety training, insurance brokerage, legal review, software, dashboards, databases, marketplaces, or introductions.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, safety certification, safety training, insurance brokerage, licence issuance, occupational-health services, engineering certification, equipment inspection, incident investigation, or guarantees of site access. Qualified HSE advisers, insurers, brokers, trainers, licensed contractors, inspectors, lawyers, customers, and portal operators remain responsible for safety programs, training, insurance, licences, equipment condition, legal compliance, and final acceptance. Cases are rejected if source evidence is missing, insurance is inadequate, training is false or expired, licences are absent, worker documents are unsafe to handle, equipment inspections are missing, incident remediation is unresolved, the contractor wants documents overstated, or the customer requires actual remediation before access.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA because it does not handle regulated-finance ICT supplier evidence. It is not NIS2/KSC supplier evidence, AIQ, RAGProbe, CyberBind, GreenTender, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, or any health/Rx/clinical route. The buyer trigger, control point, payment event, and proof artifact are materially different: industrial contractor site-access and customer-portal acceptance tied to a current job, not bank supplier risk, AI/security questionnaires, public tender SKU evidence, product listing release, diligence, municipal invoice routing, retailer deductions, import continuity, machine-data access, salvage, energy retrofit analysis, or medical data.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Customer portals, HSE consultants, insurance brokers, and contractor back offices may already solve many cases. Hard cases require real missing insurance, training, licences, safety programs, equipment inspections, incident remediation, or customer-specific safety changes outside founder control. Portal acceptance remains outside the startup's control. An unknown part-time founder may not be trusted with worker records, incident history, insurance documents, licences, training certificates, or customer portal access. Some workflows may be low-fee administrative upload work. The business can drift into generic HSE consulting, safety training, legal advice, insurance brokerage, or remediation coordination. If the first cases are not tied to blocked current jobs and measurable site-access outcomes, buyers may refuse premium pricing.
