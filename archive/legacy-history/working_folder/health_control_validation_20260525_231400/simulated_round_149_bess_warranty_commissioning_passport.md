This round explicitly avoids duplicating the confirmed heat-pump commissioning passport. The searched control point must be a different buyer, trigger, payment event, and proof artifact.

## Lead Candidate

**BESSGuard Warranty Commissioning Passport**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The economic gate is external and concrete: distributor warranty handling, preferred installer status, final installer margin/rebate, reserve release, replacement eligibility, or priority support.
- BESS installation errors can be expensive enough to motivate a distributor or warranty channel: battery damage, inverter mismatch, BMS settings, grid-code settings, thermal placement, ventilation, fire-clearance evidence, telemetry setup, firmware, SoH baseline, and customer handover.
- The founder does not certify electrical/fire safety. The startup produces a distributor-scoped evidence packet using installer-supplied data and named contractor review where needed.
- The 60-day proof is a signed distributor recognition and paid live commissioning packets, not a generic service launch.
- It is materially different from the confirmed heat-pump commissioning passport: different equipment, buyer trigger, risk object, proof fields, payment event, and channel economics.

### Internal Objections

- OEMs and distributors may already require commissioning forms or portal workflows.
- Electrical/fire safety boundaries are serious; qualified professionals must own installation, certification, fire design, and grid compliance.
- A Warsaw unknown founder must borrow credibility from a named BESS/electrical reviewer.
- Residential battery jobs may be too small; the wedge must start with commercial/industrial BESS and higher-value multi-inverter systems.
- The idea becomes a duplicate of prior energy passports if it is framed as generic installer QA rather than BESS warranty-release evidence.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the proof object is a distributor-controlled economic gate tied to current paid jobs.
