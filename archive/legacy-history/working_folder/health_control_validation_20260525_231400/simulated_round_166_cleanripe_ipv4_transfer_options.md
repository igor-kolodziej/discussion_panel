### Why It Clears Simulation

- The controlled asset is exact and numbered: a specific RIPE-region `/24`, PI block, or adjacent clean IPv4 resource.

### Internal Objections

- Established IPv4 brokers and leasing platforms are strong incumbents.
- Sellers may already know the value of IPv4 space or prefer known brokers.
- Cleanliness diligence is serious: spam reputation, hijack history, RPKI, route objects, geolocation, abuse contacts, prior leases, sanctions/KYC, and title must be checked.
- Buyer demand exists but pricing can be volatile and IPv6 adoption caps long-term growth.
- A part-time founder must avoid becoming abuse desk, network operator, ISP, or LIR unless properly structured.
- The first proof must be signed exclusivity plus buyer deposit, not "I found possible sellers."
