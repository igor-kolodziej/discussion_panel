## Source Checks Affecting The Lead Candidate

- Polish occupational medicine is performed by doctors, nurses, psychologists, and other qualified professionals, independently of employers and employees: https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/medycyna-pracy
- Employers must refer workers for preventive medical examinations based on position and work-condition hazards: https://www.gov.pl/web/your-europe/preventive-medical-examinations
- Polish law allows alcohol and "substances similar to alcohol" checks in defined workplace safety conditions, but this supports narrow sobriety/drug-testing operations, not broad medication-risk screening: https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/kontrola-trzezwosci and https://www.gov.pl/web/family/control-of-employees-sobriety

## Fatal Objections

- Employer/worker medication screening is a bad control point unless the startup becomes a licensed occupational-medicine provider or controls a compliance/device route book.
- Claim/report ideas need legal or expert authority that the founder does not own.
- Patient/family claims are fragmented; CAC and proof burden are likely worse than the headline.
- The GLP-1/medication angle is commercially hot, but the lawful transaction owner is usually a doctor, pharmacy, clinic, employer, PSP, or law firm.

## Round 43 Pivot

Use the only durable insight from Round 42: workplace drug/sobriety testing is a lawful, acute employer safety workflow, but the business must control **installed testing devices, consumable supply, calibration schedule, policy documentation, and recurring employer accounts**. Search for an acquire/option route-book shape rather than a health-advice shape.
