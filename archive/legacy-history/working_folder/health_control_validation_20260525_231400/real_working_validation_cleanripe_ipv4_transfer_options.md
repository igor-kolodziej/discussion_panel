Do not build a platform and do not start with leasing. Run a 45-60 day supply test focused only on Polish/CEE companies holding unused or weakly routed `/24` to `/22` RIPE resources with no current broker relationship.

Proof should be:

- 10 verified candidate holders contacted;
- 3 authority-confirmed seller calls;
- 1 signed exclusive mandate or option over a named prefix;
- 1 buyer willing to place escrow or a binding refundable deposit for that named prefix;
- RIPE/LIR transfer path confirmed before marketing the block.
