# Simulated Round 125: Embedded Production Continuity Books

Date: 2026-05-29
Gate setting: simulated score must be strictly `>87`; real working and fresh Zero To One gates are `>=85`.

## Search Premise

Recent recovery mandates, evidence desks, platform cases, and generic micro-acquisitions failed because the founder controlled only a claim, packet, or support process. The strongest subthreshold wins were continuity buyouts where the founder acquired a live production dependency, customer-approved transfer, prepayment, source artifacts, runbooks, and one resolved live ticket before close.

This round searches for non-health, non-regulatory, production-critical continuity books where the controlled asset is already embedded inside shipment, acceptance, or production workflows.

Internal scoring caps applied here only:

- cap below 82 without concrete 6-month control proof;
- cap below 85 if one vendor contract, equipment purchase, consultant, or hire can copy it;
- cap below 87 if CAC, payback, margins, cash conversion, or owner earnings are weak;
- cap below 87 if dependent on vague partnerships/goodwill/interviews;
- cap below 82 if clinical/legal/patient/regulated-data authority is unresolved.

## Lead Candidate: CMM Program Continuity Buyout

### Buyer

Polish/CEE precision-machining, plastics, sheet-metal, electronics, and component suppliers whose shipments, first-article inspections, PPAP/FAI packages, customer scorecards, or serial-production releases depend on accepted CMM measurement programs and repeatable inspection-report exports.

### Acute Trigger

A retiring, overloaded, or one-person CMM programmer/metrology microshop supports customer-specific inspection programs. If that maintainer disappears, a supplier can lose the ability to regenerate accepted reports, update programs after drawing revisions, recover after CMM/software/probe changes, support audits, or keep shipments moving for customers that require dimensional reports.

### Control Point

Option or acquire the microshop's live CMM program continuity book:

- CMM program files and version history;
- customer/machine/controller/software map;
- drawing/characteristic mapping where transferable;
- fixture/probe/setup notes;
- accepted report templates and export formats;
- prior measurement reports and change tickets;
- calibration/traceability document references where relevant;
- support inbox, phone/domain, renewal calendar, and seller handoff;
- customer-approved admin and payment transfer;
- named CMM programmer/metrology contractor on per-ticket terms.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Signed option over one named microshop's CMM program/support book covering IP, transferable files, runbooks, customer list, support history, seller handoff, and earnout.
2. IP, confidentiality, customer-data, and assignment review completed before close.
3. At least six supplier customers approve transfer of support owner, support inbox, access control, and payment direction.
4. At least 40,000 PLN clears before close through 3-month prepaid continuity contracts or annual support deposits.
5. Exact program custody is established in a versioned repository or secure customer-approved evidence room.
6. At least one live drawing revision, software/probe change, report-export issue, or audit-support ticket is resolved by the new owner and accepted by the customer.
7. At least one accepted inspection report, FAI/PPAP measurement attachment, or shipment-support report is regenerated from the acquired program workflow.
8. Support history shows repeatable maintenance rather than bespoke open-ended metrology consulting; target contribution margin above 55% after contractor support, security, legal, and seller earnout.

### Economics

Charge 2,000-6,000 PLN per customer per month for continuity coverage, 5,000-20,000 PLN for drawing-revision/program-update tickets, and 10,000-30,000 PLN for a transition/repository hardening sprint on inherited customers. Contractor cost is per ticket or fixed retainer for a named CMM programmer. Six customers prepaying 40,000 PLN funds diligence and transition; a 15-30 customer book can create meaningful owner earnings if scope stays on inherited programs.

### Copy Risk

Metrology labs, Zeiss/Hexagon/Mitutoyo resellers, CMM programmers, quality consultants, and large suppliers can create or replace CMM programs. The defense is not generic metrology skill; it is seller-endorsed custody of exact accepted programs, report formats, setup notes, support history, customer trust, and prepaid continuity contracts before competitors see the handoff risk.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not Lab Interface Continuity Buyout because it does not handle clinical/lab-result data, LIMS interfaces, diagnostic workflows, genetic data, or health-data security. It is not Retail EDI Continuity because the controlled artifact is an accepted dimensional-inspection program/report workflow rather than order-to-cash messaging. It is not Factory Control Spares because there is no speculative inventory or emergency spare-parts bank.

### Internal Simulated Score

**89 / 100**

Reason for advancing: the lead has current commercial urgency, a concrete acquisition-led control point, pre-close cash proof, non-health/non-legal boundaries, and a narrower operational surface than EDI or industrial spares.
