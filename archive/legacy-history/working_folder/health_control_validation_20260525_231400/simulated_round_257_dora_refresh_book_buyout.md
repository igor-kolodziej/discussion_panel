## Selected Candidate - 88.2

### Idea Name

DORARenew Evidence-Refresh Book Buyout.

### Exact Buyer / Seller Pair

Seller/acquisition target: a small Polish/CEE DORA, cyber, ISO/SOC, fractional-CISO, vendor-risk, or fintech-compliance boutique with 8-40 software/vendor clients that already paid for DORA/ICT supplier evidence responses in 2025-2026 and now need annual refresh, buyer pushback, changed-since-last-review updates, subcontractor updates, and renewal-calendar maintenance.

Economic customer: 20-150 person software-product vendors, fintech infrastructure vendors, risk/KYC/fraud vendors, payments vendors, leasing/claims workflow vendors, and analytics vendors with 2-10 regulated-finance customers and existing evidence folders.

### Acute Trigger

The seller/consultant is overloaded, retiring, moving upmarket, leaving freelancing, or wants to stop low-margin evidence refresh work. Its vendor clients still face bank/insurer/payment-institution renewal refreshes, annual customer questionnaires, changed-since-last-review requests, follow-up questions, subcontractor updates, incident/BCP/DR refreshes, and evidence-room maintenance.

The buyer is not paying for generic DORA readiness. It is paying to keep already-accepted regulated-finance customer evidence current without losing the seller's original workflow memory.

### Transferable Control Point

The startup does not cold-sell DORA packs. It acquires or options a current evidence-refresh book:

- seller option/asset-purchase/earnout agreement;
- customer-approved direct billing/payment direction before seller payout;
- renewal calendar and next review dates;
- prior accepted evidence packs, buyer question wording, pushback history, and changed-since-last-review notes;
- secure client evidence rooms and source-document indexes;
- customer reauthorization and seller warm handoff;
- contractor/reviewer bench for legal/cyber flags;
- first current-month refresh invoice paid to the new entity.

### 60-Day Proof

- One signed seller option or earnout agreement over an evidence-refresh book.
- At least 8 customer reauthorizations and payment-direction approvals.
- At least 120,000 PLN of contracted or paid refresh revenue transferred or newly invoiced.
- At least 4 refresh packs delivered or scheduled against named bank/insurer/PSP renewal calendars.
- At least 2 buyer-progress outcomes: accepted refresh, answered pushback, renewal continued, or paid no-go/remediation decision.
- One cyber/security reviewer and one legal-boundary reviewer on paid per-case terms.
- Seller payout contingent on customer cash, not just list transfer.

### Internal Cap Application

This clears the simulation gate only if the acquired asset is current cash plus customer-approved payment direction. It would fall below gate if it becomes a cold-start DORA pack, generic consulting, a lead list, or a seller's informal goodwill without direct customer reauthorization. It also fails if the first book is mostly remediation, legal negotiation, ISO implementation, or broad vCISO work rather than recurring refresh packets.

### 6-Month POC

- 2 seller books optioned or one book plus 15 direct customer approvals.
- 20-35 paying vendors under direct payment direction.
- 600,000-1,500,000 PLN annualized refresh revenue under contract.
- 300,000-700,000 PLN collected cash.
- 20+ refresh/pushback/change-update outcomes.
- 5+ customers complete a second review/update cycle.
- Gross margin above 60% after reviewers and seller earnout.
- Less than 30% of work hours spent on remediation/legal/security implementation outside refresh scope.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Outbound is to consultants, not vendors:

- small DORA/cyber/ISO/SOC/fractional-CISO boutiques;
- solo consultants who handled vendor-side DORA requests in 2025;
- fintech compliance advisers who do not want recurring evidence refresh ops;
- ISO/SOC firms whose clients now ask for DORA refreshes but where the firm wants advisory/remediation, not packet maintenance;
- retiring or job-taking freelancers with an annual questionnaire calendar.

Offer:

"If you have vendor clients whose bank/insurer DORA evidence responses now need annual refresh and buyer pushback handling, I buy or revenue-share the refresh book. You keep advisory/legal/security remediation if you want it; I take current evidence-room maintenance, changed-since-last-review packs, customer questionnaire refreshes, and direct billing after each client reauthorizes."

### Economics

Revenue:

- 8,000-18,000 PLN per annual refresh pack;
- 5,000-12,000 PLN for buyer pushback or changed-since-last-review mini-pack;
- 2,000-6,000 PLN/month for evidence-room maintenance where repeated bank customers exist;
- seller earnout of 20-35% of collected revenue for 6-12 months, or acquisition payment only after customer payment direction is proven.

Cost:

- founder evidence-room operations and account management;
- cyber/security reviewer for control-evidence flags;
- legal-boundary reviewer for clause/topic flags only;
- secure storage and audit trail;
- no software build before repeat refresh calendars prove demand.

Cash conversion:

The first proof is collected customer cash under new payment direction, not a consultant referral or pipeline promise.

### Copy Risk

High at the category level: DORA boutiques, cyber consultants, law firms, ISO/SOC firms, fractional CISOs, and GRC tools can all sell evidence support. The narrower asset is the exact transferred client book, prior accepted packs, renewal calendars, buyer pushback memory, and customer-approved payment direction.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

They can copy future DORA refresh services, but not the transferred book after customers reauthorize:

- the seller's warm handoff carries trust for the exact clients;
- prior accepted packs and buyer pushback notes reduce delivery risk;
- direct payment direction gives the startup current cash before seller payout;
- client evidence-room snapshots and renewal calendars are already organized;
- if clients reauthorize, a competitor must displace an active refresh owner, not win a cold lead.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The product is an acquired current payment book with client reauthorization, renewal dates, prior accepted evidence artifacts, and direct billing. The startup does not sell generic DORA advice, dashboards, marketplace introductions, readiness reports, or certification. The commercial unit is transferred renewal-refresh ownership over specific vendor customers.

### Boundaries And Legal/Provider Limits

- The startup does not provide legal opinions, DORA certification, formal security audit, ISO/SOC implementation, penetration testing, vCISO retainer, bank-side TPRM tooling, or contract negotiation.
- Vendor management, legal, security, and executive owners approve every response.
- Legal and security reviewers only flag issues and route work; final decisions remain with the vendor and its advisers.
- Remediation, new controls, contract negotiation, technical implementation, and unsafe answer requests are excluded or separately referred.

### Duplicate-Risk Distinction

This is adjacent to the confirmed DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, but the control point, acquisition mechanism, and first proof are different.

The confirmed idea is a founder-originated vendor-side DORA evidence response product sold into live bank-renewal blockers. DORARenew starts by acquiring or optioning an already-paid refresh book from a consultant, with customer-approved payment direction, prior accepted packs, renewal calendars, and seller handoff. The payment event is current renewal-refresh cash after transfer, not the first sale of a DORA pack. The proof artifact is transferred account ownership plus paid refresh outcomes, not only a completed evidence packet.

Kill if the evaluator treats this as the same company or only an add-on to the confirmed DORA idea.

### Strongest Anticipated Objections

1. It may be too close to the already confirmed DORA evidence-pack idea.
2. Clean seller books may be rare; consultants may keep good clients and sell only low-quality leftovers.
3. Customer trust may remain with the seller, not transfer to an unknown founder.
4. Annual refresh revenue may be smaller than first-response urgent revenue.
5. Many refreshes will reveal remediation, legal, security, or contract gaps outside scope.
6. GRC tools and consultants may automate or internalize refresh workflows.
7. Seller records may be messy, incomplete, or legally sensitive.
8. Direct payment direction may take longer than 60 days because customers need procurement and NDA updates.
9. The founder still needs reviewer credibility for sensitive bank evidence.
10. If the book depends on the seller's judgment rather than reusable evidence-room process, the asset is not transferable.

## Decision

Advance DORARenew Evidence-Refresh Book Buyout to real Zero To One validation. It passes simulation at 88.2 only as a payment-book acquisition with customer reauthorization, not as another cold-start DORA evidence service.
