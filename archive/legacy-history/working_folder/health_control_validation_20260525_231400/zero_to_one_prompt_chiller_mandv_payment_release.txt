Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
ChillerM&V Payment-Release Data Room

One-sentence thesis:
ESCOs, energy auditors, refrigeration/chiller service firms, and facility-service partners often have savings invoices, success fees, rebates, or stage payments blocked because the customer CFO or facility owner will not accept claimed chiller/HVAC/refrigeration savings without clean measured evidence; the startup builds owner-authorized payment-release data rooms from BMS/controller/meter exports and temporary logger fallback.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE ESCOs, energy auditors, refrigeration service firms, chiller service firms, industrial HVAC service partners, facility-service companies, and energy-efficiency contractors that already delivered or are delivering chiller, refrigeration, pump/VFD, BMS scheduling, setpoint, demand-charge, or HVAC optimization work for factories, cold stores, warehouses, retail sites, logistics facilities, offices, hospitals, hotels, and food plants.

The economic beneficiary is the service firm or ESCO whose invoice, bonus, rebate, success fee, or stage payment is blocked or delayed, and the facility owner/CFO who needs a bounded evidence room before accepting payment.

Acute trigger:
A named customer, CFO, facility owner, procurement team, asset manager, finance controller, lender, rebate administrator, or project board asks for measured proof before approving an ESCO invoice, success-fee calculation, bonus payment, rebate file, stage payment, renewal, or next-site rollout. The project is already sold or delivered; the issue is that measured savings, operating-period comparability, baseline/post-period data, BMS trend logs, meter exports, chiller controller logs, weather/load adjustment evidence, or no-go gaps are scattered or not buyer-ready.

Control point:
The startup accepts only prepaid live payment-release files with customer or facility-owner authorization, the service firm's payment condition text, scoped equipment/site list, baseline period, post-change period, available meter/BMS/controller exports, temporary logger fallback path, agreed boundaries, data-room index, calculation workpaper, issue/no-go log, and customer-safe response wording.

The startup does not certify savings, provide engineering signoff, operate equipment, change controls, diagnose faults, guarantee payment release, perform legal contract interpretation, or replace IPMVP/M&V experts. It organizes source data, exposes data gaps, prepares bounded measurement evidence, attaches an energy/M&V reviewer for flagged cases, and returns a pay/rework/no-go evidence room.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, close four prepaid payment-release data-room files from at least two ESCOs, energy auditors, or chiller/refrigeration service partners; collect at least 60,000 PLN; obtain customer or facility-owner authorization for every accepted site; ingest at least two BMS/controller/meter exports or temporary logger datasets; deliver at least three payment-release, rework, or no-go rooms; document at least one payment-progress outcome such as accepted stage evidence, invoice approval, narrowed dispute, paid no-go/rework decision, rebate administrator response, or next-site approval; attach one qualified M&V/energy reviewer for flagged calculations; and reject every case that is really unresolved engineering, comfort, food-temperature, safety, warranty, legal, or contract interpretation work.

6-month POC:
Deliver 20-35 payment-release data rooms; collect 350,000-900,000 PLN revenue; document 12+ payment-progress, invoice-approval, narrowed-dispute, rebate-response, next-site, paid rework, or clean no-go outcomes; get four service partners to route at least three files each; maintain gross margin above 60%; keep average delivery under ten business days for evidence-ready files; build a reusable accepted/rejected chiller/HVAC/refrigeration evidence taxonomy; and keep fewer than 25% of accepted cases drifting into controls remediation, engineering redesign, legal dispute, food-safety, warranty, or comfort-complaint work.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with ESCOs, energy auditors, refrigeration/chiller service firms, BMS integrators that do optimization projects, and facility-service partners that already sell savings-backed projects but struggle to turn raw site data into customer-acceptable payment evidence. The partner offer is: "Send only live payment-release files. We organize owner-authorized chiller/BMS/meter data, produce a bounded evidence room in 7-10 business days, and return pay/rework/no-go wording you can use with the customer. We do not certify savings, change controls, or argue legal contract terms."

Direct outreach is limited to cases where the service firm publicly sells energy-savings projects or where a facility owner is visibly rolling out chiller/HVAC optimization across multiple sites and needs independent data-room discipline before approving repeat payments.

Economics:
Charge 12,000-35,000 PLN per fixed-scope payment-release data room depending on site count, data source count, deadline, baseline/post-period complexity, and reviewer load. Add a capped 3-8% payment-release bonus only where the payment condition, amount, baseline, delivery timestamp, and attribution are clean. Costs are founder data-room assembly, export normalization, temporary logger coordination where needed, secure evidence storage, M&V/energy reviewer time on flagged files, and contractor help for repeatable parsing. The product dies if every case needs custom engineering, control changes, legal argument, or senior reviewer hours above 40% of fee.

Copy risk:
ESCOs, energy auditors, BMS integrators, chiller service firms, IPMVP/M&V consultants, facility managers, OEM service teams, utility rebate administrators, and energy-management software vendors can copy pieces. The narrow advantage is fast payment-release workflow memory: accepted/rejected data-room patterns, baseline/post-period issue taxonomy, controller/BMS export playbooks, logger fallback recipes, buyer-safe pay/rework/no-go wording, and partner trust from repeated live payment files.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
The startup controls only the prepaid live payment-release file after the service firm and customer supply the exact payment condition, owner authorization, data exports, logger access, scope, and fee terms. An ESCO or M&V consultant can copy future work, but it cannot instantly own the current evidence room, data index, issue/no-go log, reviewer notes, partner approval path, and payment-deadline response once the prepaid file is active.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell generic energy audits, dashboards, M&V software, energy consulting, chiller optimization, BMS tuning, legal claims, or brokered introductions. It accepts only a named payment-release blocker and produces a bounded source-data room, calculation workpaper, data-gap/no-go table, payment-release wording, and rework boundary.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not certify savings, provide engineering signoff, operate or reconfigure equipment, approve food-safety temperatures, approve comfort conditions, make warranty claims, give legal interpretations, represent parties in disputes, or guarantee invoice payment. ESCOs, service firms, facility owners, M&V reviewers, equipment providers, legal advisers, and rebate administrators remain responsible for final commercial claims, equipment operation, safety, comfort, legal positions, and payment acceptance.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act Machine Data Liberation Desk, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, CO2TollClass, or payment-release/AR mandates. It is closest to the confirmed Data Act machine-data pattern, but materially different: the buyer is an ESCO/service partner with a live blocked payment, the domain is chiller/HVAC/refrigeration/BMS data rather than compressed air, and the proof artifact is a payment-release M&V data room rather than a compressed-air savings prospecting memo.

Strongest anticipated objections:
ESCOs, energy auditors, BMS integrators, and M&V consultants can internalize the work. Many disputes are not data-room problems; they involve weak savings, changed loads, bad baselines, comfort complaints, equipment faults, control issues, warranty disputes, or contract ambiguity. BMS/controller access is sensitive. Temporary logger setup can delay delivery. Facility owners may demand formal IPMVP certification, engineering signoff, legal review, or remediation instead of a bounded evidence room. The idea risks duplicate perception with the confirmed Data Act machine-data desk if it does not stay tied to blocked payments, chiller/HVAC/refrigeration data, and pay/rework/no-go outcomes.
