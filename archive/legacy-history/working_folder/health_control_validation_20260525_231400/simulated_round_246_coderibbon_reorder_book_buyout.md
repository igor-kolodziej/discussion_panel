## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- OEMs, distributors, coding-printer service firms, label converters, and plant procurement teams are natural competitors.
- Compatibility and quality mistakes can stop a production line or create unreadable date/lot codes.
- The first seller profile may be scarce: retiring, trusted, clean customer/order records, transferable terms, and no OEM conflict.
- Some plants may use the handoff to switch to OEM direct or larger distributors.
- Margins may compress if the startup competes only on price or must hold stock.
- Support creep is dangerous: the startup must reject printer troubleshooting unless separately contracted to qualified service partners.
- The idea is only strong if the founder buys current customer-approved reorder/payment direction, not a customer list.
