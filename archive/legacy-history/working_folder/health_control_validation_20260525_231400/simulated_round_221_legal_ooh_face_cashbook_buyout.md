## Lead Candidate: Legal OOH Face Cashbook Buyout

### Buyer / Payor

The economic seller is a retiring or distracted micro outdoor-advertising operator, sign shop, local media seller, or property manager with 15-80 legal small-format advertising faces. The payors are current advertisers, agencies, local service businesses, real-estate developers, clinics, schools, franchisees, and regional brands already paying for those exact faces or booked upcoming campaigns.

### Acute Trigger

The seller wants to retire, stop collecting rents and ad invoices, or dispose of small OOH faces before leases, municipal paperwork, advertiser renewals, and maintenance tasks fragment. Advertisers need continuity for current campaigns and booked seasonal placements. Site owners want rent paid and a single accountable operator rather than abandoned signs.

### Transferable Control Point

Signed seller option or acquisition agreement; list of every ad face with address, dimensions, photos, site-owner contract or written consent, permit/legality evidence where applicable, rent schedule, advertiser contract, booking calendar, artwork handoff, invoice history, arrears list, maintenance notes, sign-structure ownership, electricity/lighting responsibilities, and site-owner/advertiser-approved payment direction transfer.

### 60-Day Proof

1. One seller option over at least 15 legal ad faces.
2. Site-owner written consent or assignment path for at least 8 faces.
3. Advertiser payment-direction approvals on at least 8 current or booked placements.
4. First-month advertiser cash collected on at least 30,000 PLN of booked placements before acquisition close.
5. No-payment or removal decision on illegal, non-transferable, unpaid, unsafe, or permit-unclear faces.
6. Seller consideration paid mostly as earnout from retained faces.

### Internal Simulated Score

**88.0 / 100**

This clears simulation only because the control proof is current cash plus exclusive site-right assets, not a route list or prospecting brokerage. It would fall below 87 if the faces are illegal, leases are non-transferable, advertiser renewals are vague, or seller payment happens before current cash transfers.
