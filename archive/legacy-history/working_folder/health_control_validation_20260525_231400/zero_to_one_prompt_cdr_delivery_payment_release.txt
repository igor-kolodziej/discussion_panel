Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CDR Delivery Payment Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
Corporate carbon-removal buyers and CDR resellers increasingly have signed purchases, prepayments, invoice holdbacks, retirement requests, or reporting deadlines where cash cannot move until a specific registry, retirement, chain-of-custody, and delivery-evidence packet is accepted; the startup turns only named, paid CDR delivery exceptions into buyer/supplier-approved release or no-go files.

Exact buyer:
EU/CEE corporate sustainability and finance teams that have already bought or contracted carbon-removal credits, CDR resellers and marketplaces with buyer delivery exceptions, and climate funds or offtake buyers with milestone payments tied to named biochar, enhanced weathering, DAC, bio-oil, mineralization, or other carbon-removal deliveries.

Acute trigger:
A named CDR delivery, invoice, retirement, prepayment, reseller payout, or offtake milestone is blocked because the buyer, reseller, auditor, finance team, or end customer does not yet accept the delivery evidence. The blocked object must be concrete: contract, invoice, registry batch, retirement request, certificate serials, delivery statement, holdback, escrow, or reporting deadline.

Control point:
The startup controls a signed release mandate over a specific blocked delivery packet. Control includes buyer/supplier or reseller authorization, exact tonnes/serials/project/batch, invoice or payment amount, current status, evidence owner list, permitted communications, no-go authority, and success-fee or fixed-fee terms. The startup does not buy or sell credits; it controls the release file and payment/retirement acceptance trail for one named delivery queue.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, prove one signed buyer/supplier or reseller release mandate over at least 150,000 PLN equivalent of blocked CDR delivery/payment value; one prepaid fixed triage fee; exact registry serials or project/batch identifiers; an evidence checklist covering certificate, registry, retirement, invoice, delivery, chain-of-custody, lab/MRV/audit, and buyer-acceptance objects; and one accepted release, payment scheduled, retirement completed, or written paid no-go/rework decision.

6-month POC:
Complete 6-12 paid release/no-go cases across 2-4 repeat buyers, resellers, marketplaces, or climate-fund operators. Screen at least 2,000,000 PLN equivalent of blocked or pending CDR delivery value. Produce 4+ release/payment/retirement outcomes, 4+ paid no-go/rework outcomes, 150,000-400,000 PLN revenue, 60%+ gross margin after reviewer costs, and a strict evidence taxonomy by CDR pathway, registry, buyer wording, and common failure mode.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with carbon-credit buyers, CDR marketplaces, CDR resellers, climate procurement advisers, and boutique carbon-removal project financiers who already see delivery exceptions. The outreach is not "buy credits through us." It is: "When a named CDR delivery, retirement, invoice, or holdback is stuck because buyer evidence is incomplete, I will build the release/no-go file, chase the exact source documents, and preserve a clean acceptance trail. I do not broker credits, rate projects, certify removals, or write ESG claims."

Economics:
Minimum 12,000-35,000 PLN prepaid triage/release fee per blocked delivery queue, plus either 1-3% of payment/holdback value actually released or a 20,000-60,000 PLN reseller batch retainer for repeated delivery exceptions. Reject cases below 150,000 PLN blocked value unless a reseller supplies repeated queues. Variable costs are reviewer checks, secure evidence handling, document normalization, and source chasing. No software build until repeated case types appear.

Copy risk:
Carbon marketplaces, brokers, MRV consultants, ratings firms, ESG advisers, auditors, and lawyers can copy generic delivery checklists. The narrower control point is the signed mandate over exact serials/tonnes, the buyer/supplier acceptance trail, and the payment/retirement release sequence. Case memory compounds around which evidence objects buyers accept, which registry fields fail, which delivery statements create no-go, and which CDR pathways repeatedly block payment.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup holds the signed release mandate, current evidence room, buyer/supplier communication authority, no-go authority, fee terms, and exact payment/retirement queue, an incumbent can compete for future cases but cannot take over that named queue without the parties revoking the mandate. The startup is paid for controlling that specific blocked delivery and turning it into accepted release, scheduled payment, completed retirement, or paid no-go/rework.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
It is not a carbon-credit marketplace, offset broker, ESG report, project rating, dashboard, or MRV consultancy. The commercial unit is a named blocked delivery/payment queue with signed authority, exact evidence owners, exact serials/tonnes, and a release/no-go outcome. The output packet exists only to move or reject a specific payment, retirement, invoice, or milestone.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not verify carbon removals, certify credits, rate projects, sell credits, advise on net-zero claims, provide legal/accounting/tax/assurance opinions, guarantee registry acceptance, or decide whether a credit is suitable for a corporate claim. Registry rules, legal/accounting treatment, ESG assurance, MRV verification, and contractual disputes remain with qualified providers. Reject fraud suspicion, falsified certificates, unclear credit ownership, double-counting indicators, sanctions concerns, reversal disputes, litigation threats, or requests to make weak credits look acceptable.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
It is not PromoLeak because there are no FMCG retail deductions or promotion claims. It is not CBAM because there is no customs/import continuity or carbon-border reporting. It is not BioSignal or PFAS diligence because the buyer is not paying for an investor memo; the buyer/reseller has a named CDR delivery, serial, retirement, invoice, or payment holdback already in progress. It is not a marketplace or broker because no credits are sourced or sold.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Carbon-credit buyers may already rely on brokers, marketplaces, registries, ratings firms, MRV consultants, carbon lawyers, auditors, and ESG advisers. Missing evidence may be impossible to create, making many cases paid no-go rather than release. Buyer acceptance remains outside founder control. Carbon-removal claims are reputationally sensitive, and a delivery packet can be mistaken for assurance or project quality rating. Case flow may be sparse for an unknown Warsaw founder. Suppliers may resist sharing lab data, feedstock records, chain-of-custody proof, registry evidence, or audit details. Payment-release attribution can be disputed. The business can drift into carbon brokerage, project rating, ESG reporting, legal disputes, or MRV consulting if boundaries are not enforced.
