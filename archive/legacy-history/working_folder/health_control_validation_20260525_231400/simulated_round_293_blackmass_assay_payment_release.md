## Candidate

**BlackMass Assay Payment-Release Lockbox**

One-sentence thesis:

Help Polish and CEE battery recyclers, pre-processors, battery-scrap traders, and industrial scrap holders release payment on named black-mass or battery-material lots where an offtaker/refiner is holding cash or applying a haircut because assay, moisture, impurity, mass-balance, chain-of-custody, or lot-document evidence has not been accepted.

## Control Table Summary

Buyer:

Small and mid-sized Polish/CEE battery recyclers, battery pre-processors, dismantlers, production-scrap holders, battery-scrap traders, and industrial sellers that already sold or tendered a named black-mass, cathode/anode scrap, battery-production scrap, or battery-material lot.

Acute trigger:

Payment, holdback release, settlement price, or lot acceptance is blocked by buyer/offtaker questions around assay mismatch, moisture/impurity adjustment, sample trail, weigh-ticket or mass-balance tie-out, chain-of-custody, lot identity, or inconsistent lot documents.

Transferable control point:

Signed lot-payment release mandate for a named lot; lot/batch ID; buyer/offtaker issue text; invoice, PO, settlement statement, or offtake reference; assay/weigh/moisture/mass-balance evidence; responsible-party transport and waste records; seller-approved response packet; and fee right.

60-day proof:

Four signed mandates over named lots with at least 2,000,000 PLN total held, haircut, or disputed payment value; two lots with independent or offtaker assay documents plus mass-balance evidence; one paid reviewer; one acceptance, corrected settlement, released holdback, partial payment, or explicit written no-go; at least 40,000 PLN collected.

Economics:

5,000-12,000 PLN paid lot screen for material values above 250,000 PLN, 12,000-30,000 PLN urgent release packet, and 1-3% of released holdback, corrected haircut, or payment value. No inventory purchase, financing, material handling, transport, sampling, or brokerage.

Copy risk:

Recyclers, scrap traders, refiners, labs, waste brokers, environmental consultants, accountants, and in-house commercial teams can copy generic checklists. They cannot copy a signed mandate over a specific lot, seller evidence room, buyer/offtaker issue thread, release response, and fee right once controlled.

Boundaries:

The startup does not own, buy, sell, broker, transport, store, process, sample, classify, export, import, or certify waste batteries, black mass, hazardous waste, or battery materials. It does not issue assays, recycling-efficiency verification, recycled-content verification, legal opinions, waste-shipment advice, transport documents, sanctions/origin opinions, or environmental compliance certifications.
