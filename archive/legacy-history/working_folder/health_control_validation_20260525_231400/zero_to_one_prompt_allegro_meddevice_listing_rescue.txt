Idea: Allegro Medical-Device Listing Rescue Desk.

A Polish 5-business-day paid rescue desk for small Allegro sellers/importers whose live medical-device or health-device listings are blocked, suspended, suppressed, or conversion-killed by missing CE/MDR/GPSR/advertising/documentation evidence. Focus products: CPAP accessories, pulse oximeters, blood-pressure monitors, glucose meters, TENS/EMS, hearing-care accessories, mobility aids, thermometers, nebulizers, orthopedic supports, and regulated wellness/biosignal devices bought by 50-60+ and soon-elderly consumers.

The startup does not sell devices, give medical advice, certify products, act as notified body, or guarantee reinstatement. It controls a live blocked listing case, seller admin/document workflow, buyer-ready evidence pack, platform appeal text, missing-document escalation, and paid rescue/relisting economics.

Buyer: Polish Allegro sellers/importers/distributors with sales frozen by listing suppression or document request; EU/CEE health-device brands blocked in Polish marketplace; 3PL/import agents and agencies serving Allegro sellers.

Acute trigger: a specific listing/SKU has revenue blocked: offer removed, ad traffic stopped, account warning, inventory stranded, or compliance-document request. Buyer pays because a live transaction flow is blocked.

Control point: blocked Allegro listing/SKU evidence, seller admin screenshots/case history, product label/IFU/manual/DoC/CE/MDR/GPSR/advertising evidence, Polish-language requirements, importer/manufacturer details, supplier evidence, platform-specific response pack and appeal text, and fixed-fee paid rescue contract.

2-month proof:
1. 30 seller interviews with live/recent health-device listing-document problems;
2. 3 paid live blocked-listing rescue cases;
3. 15k+ PLN collected;
4. at least one listing reinstated/accepted or conclusively diagnosed as unfixable due missing upstream document;
5. case library of recurring defects by product class;
6. counsel-reviewed boundaries: no certification, no notified-body claims, no medical advice, no device sale, no compliance guarantee;
7. 60%+ gross margin after legal spot checks/contractor help.

Not generic compliance: it activates only after a live Allegro listing/SKU sales flow is blocked. No live block, no project.

Risks: Allegro may not expose enough cases; sellers may be price-sensitive; many cases may be unfixable; platform policy can change; agencies/consultants can copy; must avoid legal/certification claims.
