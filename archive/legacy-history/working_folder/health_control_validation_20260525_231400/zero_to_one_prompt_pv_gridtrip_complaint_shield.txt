Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
PV GridTrip Complaint Shield

One-sentence thesis:
A channel-sold complaint shield for Polish PV installers, service companies, distributors, and warranty teams handling customers whose PV inverters repeatedly shut down, curtail, or show "over voltage", "grid fault", or high AC voltage errors, where the installer needs a fast evidence pack showing whether the cause is grid-side, installation-side, settings-side, phase/load-side, or a paid remediation opportunity.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized Polish PV installers, PV service companies, inverter/battery distributors, warranty teams, and energy retrofit firms with existing prosumer and small commercial PV customers. The best first buyer has 50-1,000 installed PV systems, recurring customer complaints about inverter shutdowns or lost production, and no disciplined process for turning inverter logs and voltage evidence into customer, DSO, warranty, or remediation decisions.

Acute trigger:
A customer reports repeated inverter trips, "grid fault", "over voltage", high AC voltage, lost production, or failed self-consumption and blames the installer. The installer faces warranty visits, angry calls, refund threats, bad reviews, manufacturer warranty arguments, or DSO/customer finger-pointing. The installer needs to prove quickly whether the problem is likely grid overvoltage, phase imbalance, undersized AC cabling, wrong inverter settings, poor connection, local load pattern, export behavior, inverter fault, or a solvable retrofit.

Control point:
Signed complaint-shield mandate for one live installer/customer case with:
- customer complaint, service ticket, warranty ticket, or bad-review threat;
- inverter brand/model, commissioning date, AC/DC configuration, settings snapshot, firmware where available;
- inverter event logs, production gaps, AC voltage logs, and grid-fault timestamps;
- temporary power-quality logger or electrician measurement where needed;
- phase/load observations, connection/cabling notes, and DSO service territory;
- customer explanation pack;
- DSO complaint or measurement-request package when grid-side evidence supports it;
- installer-fix / DSO-fix / manufacturer-warranty / export-limit / phase-balance / battery-load-shifting / no-go recommendation.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid complaint-shield cases at 1,500-5,000 PLN each.
2. At least two cases come through installers, distributors, or warranty teams rather than direct homeowners.
3. At least one case uses inverter logs plus measured voltage evidence to close an installer service ticket, support a DSO complaint, avoid an unpaid warranty visit, trigger a paid remediation quote, or document manufacturer escalation.
4. One qualified electrician or PV service technician is available per case on paid contractor terms for measurement and safety-boundary issue spotting.
5. A repeat taxonomy of cases: grid overvoltage, phase imbalance, wrong inverter settings, poor AC connection, undersized cable, local load/export pattern, inverter defect, DSO response pattern, and remediation/no-fault outcome.
6. One installer or distributor sends a second case or agrees to a prepaid monthly complaint bundle.

6-month POC:
Month 1: build a PV overvoltage complaint intake form, inverter-log request checklist, DSO complaint template, customer explanation template, and safety boundary. Recruit one qualified electrician/PV technician for measurements and field issue spotting. Build a target list of 80 PV installers, inverter distributors, service teams, and retrofit firms.

Month 2: close three prepaid live cases. Accept only cases with a real customer complaint, inverter logs or app access screenshots, customer permission, installation details, and installer participation. Deliver a GridTrip pack: timeline of trips, voltage/log evidence, likely responsibility branch, DSO/customer response, warranty/remediation path, and no-go/safety notes.

Month 3: document whether at least one case moves commercially: customer accepts explanation, DSO complaint is submitted, DSO measurement is requested, installer avoids an unpaid visit, manufacturer warranty is escalated, remediation quote is accepted, or the case is killed as unsafe/installation-side.

Months 4-6: complete 20-60 paid cases, collect 60,000-240,000 PLN, keep gross margin above 60% after electrician/PV-tech costs, secure 3-5 installer/distributor channels, and build a private inverter/DSO/complaint pattern library by brand, voltage event, DSO region, fix type, and customer outcome.

First acquisition mechanism:
Installer pitch:
"When a customer blames you for overvoltage/grid-fault shutdowns, send the inverter logs, installation details, settings screenshots, complaint text, and customer permission. I return a GridTrip pack: what the logs show, what to measure, whether this looks grid-side or installation-side, what to send the DSO/customer/manufacturer, and what paid remediation is honest to quote."

Distributor pitch:
"Your installers keep the customer relationship. I take the ugly inverter-shutdown complaint files where no one knows whether to blame the grid, settings, wiring, inverter, phase imbalance, or customer load pattern."

Economics:
Charge 1,500-2,500 PLN for a log-only complaint pack, 2,500-5,000 PLN when measurement coordination, DSO package, or manufacturer/warranty escalation is included, and 6,000-15,000 PLN/month for installer/distributor complaint bundles after proof. Contractor measurement/issue-spotting cost is 300-1,500 PLN per case. The installer pays because one angry PV customer can consume multiple unpaid visits, create bad reviews, block referrals, and hide a legitimate battery/export-limit/phase-balancing remediation sale.

Copy risk:
PV installers, electricians, inverter distributors, monitoring platforms, energy auditors, and DSOs can copy pieces. The defense is a narrow channel-sold workflow, fast inverter-log triage, DSO-specific response templates, repeated brand/DSO/event-pattern memory, and a clear "installer no-fault vs fix vs remediation" decision artifact.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once an installer assigns the live complaint, prepays the case, shares customer permission, inverter logs, settings, installation details, and DSO/customer correspondence, the startup controls that active complaint file and response artifact. Another provider must displace the GridTrip timeline, measurement plan, DSO/customer response, remediation branch, and customer explanation under complaint pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live inverter-shutdown or overvoltage complaint cases from installers, service firms, distributors, or warranty teams. The output is a complaint-resolution artifact used in an active case: event timeline, voltage/log evidence, responsibility branch, DSO/customer/manufacturer response package, remediation/no-fault recommendation, and safety/no-go notes. It is not PV installation, electrical contracting, generic energy auditing, battery sales, a monitoring dashboard, marketplace, database, or brokered technician introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not perform electrical work, alter inverter settings, certify installations, guarantee DSO action, provide legal claims, promise production recovery, or override manufacturer/DSO requirements. Qualified electricians/PV technicians perform any required measurements or field checks. The installer, electrician, DSO, manufacturer, and customer remain responsible for installation safety, settings changes, grid work, warranty decisions, and remediation. Cases involving unsafe wiring, fire risk, unauthorized settings, missing protection devices, suspected fraud, or customer/installer conflict beyond technical evidence are declined or escalated.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not BatteryFit Retrofit Pack because it does not decide whether a battery/EMS/heat-storage retrofit is worth selling to an existing PV customer. This is a live complaint shield for inverter shutdown and grid-overvoltage responsibility. It is not HeatQuiet because it is not heat-pump noise or neighbor complaints. It is not the heat-pump commissioning passport because it handles post-install PV grid-trip complaints, not commissioning proof. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, BioSignal, GreenTender, HeritageDoor, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
- Competent installers and electricians may already know how to diagnose inverter overvoltage and DSO issues.
- Ticket sizes may be too low unless sold through installers with repeated complaints.
- DSOs may be slow or unresponsive, so the pack cannot promise resolution.
- Some cases will reveal installation mistakes, unsafe wiring, wrong settings, or poor customer expectations, making channel relationships delicate.
- Inverter monitoring platforms already show parts of the evidence.
- Customers may not pay and installers may resist paying for complaints they feel the grid caused.
- The workflow may become field service or electrical contracting if boundaries are not strict.
- The idea is adjacent to prior Polish energy/installer concepts and must prove this complaint category is independently frequent and paid.
