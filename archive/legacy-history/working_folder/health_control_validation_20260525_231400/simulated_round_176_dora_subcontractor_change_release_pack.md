## Simulated Pass Candidate

### DORA Subcontractor Change Release Pack - 88.5

One-sentence thesis: a vendor-side, event-scoped release pack for SaaS and software-product vendors whose bank, insurer, payment-institution, leasing, or fintech customer has blocked a cloud migration, hosting change, subprocessor addition, support-provider change, or material ICT subcontractor change until DORA-style subcontractor, exit, security, incident, location, and continuity evidence is acceptable.

Buyer: 20-200 person Polish/CEE SaaS, fintech-infrastructure, payments, KYC/AML, risk, workflow, analytics, claims, leasing, document, and B2B software vendors with existing regulated-finance customers and a current customer blocking or threatening a go-live, production change, renewal milestone, invoice, or expansion because a material ICT subcontractor or subprocessor change has not been accepted.

Acute trigger: the vendor needs to change AWS/Azure/GCP region/account, add a model/API provider, move hosting, replace an MSP/support vendor, add a subcontracted development/support team, move support outside the EU, add an incident/monitoring provider, or change a critical SaaS dependency. A regulated financial customer asks for a change notification, subcontractor evidence, data-location summary, access-control summary, exit/data-return story, BCP/DR impact, incident-support summary, subprocessor/subcontractor terms, or management approval before allowing production use or paying the next milestone.

Hard control point: the startup controls one named customer change case after the vendor prepays and grants evidence-room access: live bank/customer request, change description, current and proposed subcontractor/subprocessor details, contracts/DPA/SLA snippets, data-flow and location evidence, security/BCP/DR artifacts, exit/data-return/deletion notes, reviewer issue log, final customer-ready change packet, and customer response/outcome record.

60-day proof: three prepaid live vendor change cases at 15,000+ PLN each; one customer-accepted change notification/consent/no-objection, one clean no-go/rework decision, one partner referral from a DORA/cyber/legal/fractional-CISO boutique, and at least 45,000 PLN collected before broad template building.

6-month POC: 8-12 paid change-release cases; 5+ accepted/no-objection/rework/no-go outcomes documented; 2+ partners routing repeat cases; 200,000-350,000 PLN revenue; gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs; fewer than 30% of accepted cases become broad contract negotiation, cloud engineering, remediation, or legal-advice projects.

Why it can exceed the internal gate: it reuses the only high-scoring DORA pattern but changes the unit from general renewal evidence to a narrower current-change release artifact. The buyer pays because a specific bank customer is blocking go-live, invoice, renewal milestone, or production change. The first artifact is not a readiness report; it is accepted customer progress or clean rejection on a named change.

Internal objections:

- Duplicate risk with the confirmed DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack is serious. The distinction must remain event-scoped: material subcontractor/subprocessor/cloud change acceptance, not broad renewal questionnaire response.
- Some cases need legal advice, contract negotiation, security remediation, cloud architecture, or formal audit.
- Banks and insurers may reject even good packets or require their own forms.
- DORA/cyber/legal boutiques can copy the format.
- If the vendor has no real evidence or a risky subcontractor setup, the only truthful output is no-go.
- Sensitive contracts and outsourcing data require strict handling.
- Demand may be episodic unless partner channels repeatedly surface change blockers.
