Official Polish sources show:

- The KSC amendment entered into force on 2026-04-03.
- Ministerial ex officio entries into the Wykaz KSC occurred before self-registration opened.

- Self-registration runs from 2026-05-07 to 2026-10-03.
- Entities entered ex officio receive notifications and requests to supplement required data; the deadline for submitting missing information is 6 months from delivery of the request.
- The law implements NIS2 and expands obligations for essential and important entities.
