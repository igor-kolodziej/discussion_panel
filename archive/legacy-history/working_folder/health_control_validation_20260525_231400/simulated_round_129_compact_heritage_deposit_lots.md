## Lead Candidate: HeritageWindow Cremone Hardware Suites

### Buyer

Historic-window restoration firms, premium joinery shops, interior architects, heritage renovation contractors, boutique developers, and HNW owners restoring pre-war apartments or townhouse windows in Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, and similar cities.

### Acute Trigger

During pre-war apartment or townhouse renovation, original windows may survive or be rebuilt, but the visible hardware is missing, mismatched, overpainted, replaced with plastic/modern handles, or split across singles. A premium restoration wants a coherent run of original brass/bronze/nickel window hardware across multiple rooms, especially cremone/espagnolette handles and rods, window stays, latches, pulls, hinges, escutcheons, and matched screws/backplates where recoverable.

### Control Point

Title or first-refusal/pickup rights over a complete matched window-hardware suite from one property before it is fragmented, scrapped, sold as singles, or lost during window replacement:

- cremone/espagnolette handles and rods;
- latches, stays, pulls, hinges, backplates, escutcheons, and visible fasteners where recoverable;
- source address/provenance note;
- window count, dimensions, photos, condition notes, and numbered bagging;
- restorer/joiner fit notes;
- buyer deposit before restoration or resale.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three controlled complete window-hardware suites from real properties, each documented before removal with photos/video, window counts, and seller handover.
2. At least 80 individual pieces numbered, bagged, and condition-cataloged.
3. Three buyer deposits from architects, window restorers, joiners, or premium owners for full suites or named projects, not decorative singles.
4. One restoration/metalwork partner willing to quote cleaning, de-painting, repair, replating, rod adjustment, and missing-screw matching.
5. One suite sold or reserved at 50%+ expected gross margin after pickup, cataloging, partner work, and seller payout.

### Economics

Pay 1,000-5,000 PLN per property/suite depending on count and quality, or per-piece minimum for crews. Sell raw cataloged suites for 6,000-18,000 PLN and restored/refit-ready suites for 15,000-45,000 PLN through restorers/joiners/architects. Logistics are compact: numbered bags, parts bins, photo catalog, and partner restoration rather than bulky inventory.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not HeritageDoor Suites. HeritageDoor controls complete interior door sets with frames/casings/hinges/handles/locks and sells the door rhythm itself. HeritageWindow controls compact original window operating hardware for surviving or rebuilt windows, excluding door leaves and interior-door suites. The buyer trigger is window hardware mismatch during historic-window restoration, not missing matched internal doors.
