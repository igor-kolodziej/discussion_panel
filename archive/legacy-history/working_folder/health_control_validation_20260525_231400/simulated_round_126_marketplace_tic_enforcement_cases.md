## Lead Candidate: SupplementTIC Amazon 90-Day Case Desk

### Buyer

Amazon account-health, reinstatement, and marketplace-compliance agencies serving supplement brands and EU/CEE supplement brands selling on Amazon.com, plus Amazon supplement sellers that have received a live dietary-supplement TIC/cGMP notice, ASIN warning, compliance request, or deactivation risk.

### Acute Trigger

Amazon's 2026 dietary-supplement enforcement requires affected sellers to work through approved testing, inspection, and certification providers. Sellers with live notices have a short clock to initiate or validate the required documentation. High-revenue ASINs can be deactivated if the seller misunderstands the request, chooses the wrong path, has manufacturer documents that do not match, has Supplement Facts / detail-page claim mismatches, or fails to get the TIC process initiated through the right route.

### Control Point

Agency-routed live case with:

- seller authorization;
- Amazon notice / Account Health request text;
- ASIN and variation list;
- Supplement Facts panel and product images;
- manufacturer/facility GMP certificate status;
- COAs, lab reports, lot/batch details, and product specs where available;
- detail-page title, bullet, image, and claim inventory;
- selected Amazon-approved TIC provider or accepted certification program route;
- TIC request form / intake confirmation / quote / lab acknowledgement where obtained;
- direct TIC/lab payment by seller, not by the startup;
- fixed output: case map, missing-evidence list, claim-panel mismatch list, TIC initiation checklist, no-go/escalation memo, and agency/Seller Central response language.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid live Amazon supplement TIC/compliance cases at 6,000-20,000 PLN each.
2. At least two cases routed through Amazon account-health, reinstatement, marketplace, or supplement-compliance agencies.
3. At least two TIC provider initiations, intake confirmations, quote acknowledgements, or accepted-document-review starts.
4. At least one case produces a concrete platform/commercial outcome: ASIN remains live during the 90-day process, deactivated ASIN moves to document-review/reinstatement path, TIC confirms existing documentation can be reviewed, seller receives a clean no-go before wasting lab spend, or claim-panel mismatch is corrected before submission.
5. One named supplement regulatory/TIC-process reviewer on paid per-case terms.
6. Private taxonomy across 20+ rejected/missing evidence patterns: certificate issuer, facility scope, COA age, product name mismatch, batch/lot mismatch, label claim mismatch, Supplement Facts mismatch, certification-program eligibility, high-risk category testing, and seller/TIC submission routing.
