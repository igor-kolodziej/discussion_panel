## Selected Candidate

**CyberBind Broker Subjectivity Ticket Book**

### Why It Advances

The previous single-case CyberBind candidate scored 82 because the pain was real but the channel could internalize, many cases needed implementation, and direct SME trust was fragile. This version changes the proof object:

- the paying buyer is a broker or broker-MSP pair, not a cold SME;
- the controlled asset is a prepaid queue of live underwriter subjectivities, not one response packet;
- the first cash is a broker block payment before individual delivery;
- client authorization and MSP evidence ownership are prerequisites;
- reorder from the same broker is a required proof point.

This does not remove all copy risk, but it tests whether broker workflow ownership and prepaid ticket blocks create enough control to beat the single-case ceiling.

### 60-Day Proof Required

- Three brokers or broker-MSP pairs sign prepaid ticket-block agreements covering at least 18 live cyber-insurance subjectivity tickets.
- At least 120,000 PLN is collected before delivery begins.
- Every ticket has a written underwriter/MGA condition, bind/renewal deadline, client authorization, broker approval path, MSP/IT evidence owner, and insured management signoff boundary.
- At least 10 client-authorized evidence packets are delivered.
- At least 6 tickets produce underwriter-accepted evidence, bind/renewal progress, narrowed exclusion/surcharge, coverage issued, paid remediation/no-go, or broker-approved no-submit.
- At least two brokers buy a second block or commit to a second block before day 60.
- More than 35% of submitted opportunities are rejected because the control is missing and the broker/MSP must remediate first.

### Internal Simulated Score

**88.4 / 100**

Reason for advancing: the broker-paid ticket-book structure improves CAC, trust, cash conversion, and defensibility versus the single-case CyberBind near-miss. It stays below 90 because brokers, MSPs, cyber boutiques, MGAs, and GRC tools can still copy the workflow, and missing-control cases can destroy delivery margins.
