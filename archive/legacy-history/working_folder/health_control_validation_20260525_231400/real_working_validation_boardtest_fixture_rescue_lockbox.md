## Lesson

Exact production asset custody is still not enough when the buyer's real fear is technical/legal trust and production validation. Future production-continuity ideas need either an incumbent-trusted channel already attached to the buyer's workflow, or a payment stream where the founder controls cash movement rather than becoming a disputed bridge between asset possession and technical acceptance.
