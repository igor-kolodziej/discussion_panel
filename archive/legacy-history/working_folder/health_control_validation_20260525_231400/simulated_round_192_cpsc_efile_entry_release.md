## Advanced Candidate

**CPSC eFile Entry Release Desk**

### Thesis

Starting 8 July 2026, many imported consumer products subject to CPSC certificate requirements need certificate data electronically filed for import entry. Small EU/CEE brands, Amazon sellers, and importers will be forced to assemble broker-ready certificate data across product IDs, HTS supplied by the broker, manufacturer/importer details, applicable rules, lab reports, CPC/GCC fields, and Product Registry/eFiling artifacts. The startup earns from named shipments and SKUs where a broker, 3PL, importer, or retailer needs the packet before entry, FBA delivery, or PO fulfillment can move.

### Buyer

US importers of record, customs brokers, 3PLs, Amazon/marketplace agencies, and EU/CEE consumer-product brands importing CPSC-regulated consumer products into the US. First cases should focus on non-food, non-medical, non-cosmetic, non-firearm consumer products where existing test reports and source truth are available.

### Control Point

Signed importer/broker/seller authorization over named SKUs and shipments; broker-supplied HTS and entry context; exact SKU/model/product IDs; product photos and labels; manufacturer/importer details; applicable CPSC rule set supplied or confirmed by qualified provider; CPC/GCC fields; test reports and lab accreditation evidence where required; Product Registry or eFiling data trail; broker acknowledgement, eFiling acceptance, entry progress, or clean no-go.

### Why It Clears Simulation

This is not a speculative compliance readiness desk. The 60-day proof is broker/eFiling progress on named entries before or around a hard official date. The data-assembly gap is real because brokers can transmit data but may not want to reconstruct product certificate evidence from EU/CEE sellers, factories, labs, Amazon agencies, and product files.

### Strongest Anticipated Objections

- Customs brokers and product-safety labs are natural incumbents.
- HTS/classification, rule applicability, certificate sufficiency, and product-safety judgment can become legal/compliance advice.
- Many products lack valid test reports or truthful manufacturer data.
- Broker/eFiling acceptance does not guarantee CPSC or CBP will never question the entry.
- Demand may spike around the deadline but later standardize.
- Case volume can become SKU-by-SKU labor unless pricing is strict.
- Some sellers may misunderstand eFiling as product approval.
