## Selected Candidate

**OEM Warranty Chargeback Rebuttal Pack**

One-sentence thesis: Tier 2/Tier 3 automotive and industrial suppliers will pay for an OEM-ready rebuttal pack when a customer assigns warranty costs, field-failure reserves, debit memos, or supplier-responsibility claims that can be reduced with lot trace, failure facts, test history, contract terms, and accepted precedent structure.

### Why It Clears Simulation

The candidate is materially different from prior RMA and warranty-credit failures:

- prior failures tried to recover small installer/OEM credits from payor programs;
- this candidate defends against or reduces a customer-assigned liability, reserve, chargeback, or debit memo;
- the buyer is a supplier CFO/quality/commercial owner with six-figure margin impact;
- the external decision-maker is the OEM/customer warranty, quality, commercial, or supplier-recovery team;
- the proof is a reopened case, reduced assignment, reversed debit memo, delayed reserve, scheduled credit, or written no-go.

The case memory could compound around OEM-specific exhibit ordering, failure taxonomies, serial/lot tracing, responsible-cause boundaries, warranty language, and rebuttal narratives. The founder does not need to certify product quality or litigate; the work is assembling a decision file from records the supplier already owns and routing hard technical/legal questions to qualified reviewers.

### Internal Cap Review

- Concrete 6-month control proof: passes if at least one OEM case is reopened, reduced, reversed, or cleanly no-go'd.
- One consultant copy risk: partially passes. Quality consultants and lawyers can copy future cases, but cannot copy a signed current rebuttal mandate and OEM response trail once controlled.
- CAC/payback/margins/cash conversion: passes if cases are six figures and pricing includes prepaid diagnostic plus success fee on reduced liability.
- Vague partnerships/goodwill: passes only if cases come with actual OEM notices/debit memos, not general warranty worries.
- Legal/provider limits: passes only if no legal opinion, no product certification, no root-cause certification, no litigation, and reviewer escalation is explicit.

Simulated score: **88.8 / 100**

## 6-Month POC

1. Build a target list of CEE Tier 2/Tier 3 suppliers in automotive, machinery, HVAC, industrial electronics, metal/plastic components, harnesses, sensors, castings, and assemblies.
2. Recruit one paid quality/warranty reviewer and one contract/legal-boundary reviewer for flagged sections.
3. Accept only cases with a written OEM/customer debit memo, field-failure cost allocation, warranty reserve, chargeback, or supplier-responsibility request above 150,000 PLN.
4. Produce a fixed-scope rebuttal pack: issue chronology, OEM claim text, serial/lot population, shipment/test history, failure-mode map, known-good vs suspect batches, contract/warranty clause index, evidence exhibits, unanswered questions, reviewer flags, and proposed response structure.
5. Target 5-10 prepaid cases, 250,000-750,000 PLN assigned liability under review, 100,000-300,000 PLN reduced/reversed/delayed/no-go impact, and 100,000-300,000 PLN revenue.

## Kill Conditions

- Suppliers cannot share enough quality, lot, serial, test, shipment, contract, warranty, or OEM correspondence evidence.
- Most cases require root-cause engineering, litigation, warranty legal opinions, or product redesign before any useful rebuttal can be made.
- OEMs refuse to reopen or reconsider based on supplier evidence unless it comes from a known lawyer or recognized quality consultancy.
- Case cycles routinely exceed 90 days before any measurable reopening/reduction/no-go.
- Average assigned amount is below 150,000 PLN.
- Gross margin falls below 55% after reviewers, translations, evidence cleanup, and technical contractor costs.
- Buyers treat the pack as ordinary quality-team work and will not prepay.
