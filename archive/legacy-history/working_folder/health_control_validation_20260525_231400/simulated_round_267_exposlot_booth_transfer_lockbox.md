## Candidate Advanced To Real Validation

Idea name: ExpoSlot Booth Transfer Lockbox

One-sentence thesis: option organizer-approved cancellations of sold-out B2B trade-fair booths and sponsorship packages, then transfer the exact slot to a late buyer that pays a deposit before the founder owes the seller.

Exact buyer: Late B2B exhibitors, distributors, equipment vendors, and regional manufacturers that need presence at a named industry fair after the fair has sold out or category sponsorship has closed.

Acute trigger: A key industry fair is sold out, the buyer missed the application deadline, a distributor principal demands presence, a competitor secured the visible category position, or a sales launch requires a booth/sponsor slot before the next annual cycle.

Control point: Organizer-approved option or novation right over one exact booth/sponsorship package from a canceling exhibitor, plus a buyer deposit or escrow instruction for that exact slot before seller payout.

60-day proof:

- Two signed seller options over named booths or sponsorship packages.
- Written organizer confirmation that each transfer/novation is allowed and the required documents/fees are known.
- Two buyer deposits or escrow instructions over the exact booth/package.
- One completed organizer-approved transfer or a clean no-go where organizer refusal is documented before capital loss.

6-month POC:

- 8-15 controlled transfer files.
- 5-8 completed transfers.
- 150,000-450,000 PLN gross transaction value.
- 50,000-150,000 PLN net fee/spread revenue.
- Zero speculative booth purchases without organizer transfer approval and buyer deposit.

First acquisition mechanism: Build lists from exhibitor catalogs and sponsor pages for sold-out or high-demand CEE/EU industry fairs. Contact exhibitors that downsized, changed distributor, lost budget, changed product launch timing, or may cancel. Separately contact distributors/manufacturers that missed the deadline, are on waitlists, or visibly sponsor competing fairs but not the target fair.

Gross margin and payback logic: Charge 8-15% of booth/sponsor package value or a fixed 5,000-25,000 PLN transfer fee depending on ticket size. Founder costs are outreach, organizer coordination, contract review, and escrow/payment handling. Cash conversion is fast if the buyer deposits before seller payout and the organizer confirms the transfer path.

Copy risk: Organizers, exhibition sales agencies, stand builders, PR agencies, and industry associations can copy. The defense is exact slot control before the buyer sees it, a private map of cancel-risk exhibitors, organizer transfer rules by fair, and repeated late-buyer demand patterns by category.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/database/marketplace/broker: The startup does not sell a list of fairs, a dashboard, or generic introductions. Each accepted case starts with a specific transferable booth/sponsorship right, written organizer path, and buyer deposit over that exact slot. Without control of the exact slot and deposit, there is no case.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

- No consumer ticket scalping, pass resale, or unauthorized transfer.
- No speculative purchases before organizer transfer permission and buyer deposit.
- Organizer rules, exhibitor contract terms, VAT/invoicing, insurance, stand build, shipping, booth staffing, marketing claims, and product compliance remain with the buyer/seller and their advisors.
- Reject non-transferable, lottery/allocation, personal-access, regulated medical/pharma, weapons/defense-sensitive, political, adult, gambling, sanctions-sensitive, or misrepresented exhibitor categories.
- Use lawyer-reviewed option/novation templates only after early validation, not as legal advice to parties.

Duplicate risk:

- Not HeritageDoor or another physical salvage lot; no bulky asset is moved.
- Not PromoLeak; it does not audit retailer trade spend or promo deductions.
- Not evidence desk, platform case rescue, DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, or TraceFaktura.
- The core proof artifact is organizer-approved transfer of a scarce booth/sponsor slot plus buyer deposit.

## Internal Simulation Score

Simulated score: **88.4 / 100**

Rationale:

- Strong control object for a solo founder: exact organizer-approved slot option plus buyer deposit.
- Cash conversion can occur within weeks, not months.
- No storage, shipping, field-service, clinical, legal representation, platform account-health, or regulated-data burden if categories are filtered.
- Incumbent risk is real because organizers can resell cancellations and agencies can broker access, but many organizers are not optimized for private late-buyer matching across specific categories and cancel-risk exhibitors.
- Defensibility is transaction-specific rather than structural; six-month proof must show repeated control of real cancellation paths and buyer deposits. Still passes simulation because the asset is scarce, compact, time-sensitive, and directly deposit-backed.

## Internal Kill Criteria

Kill if any of the following occurs:

- Organizer transfer permission cannot be obtained before buyer/seller money moves.
- Buyers refuse deposits before organizer approval.
- Organizers route all cancellations through their own waitlist and block third-party economics.
- Average net fee is below 5,000 PLN or requires too many hours per file.
- More than 30% of leads involve non-transferable categories, fraud risk, sanctions-sensitive products, or disguised sponsorship rule evasion.
- Seller options are not exclusive enough to prevent the seller or organizer from bypassing the founder after demand is surfaced.

Advance to working-chat Zero To One validation because simulated score is strictly above 87 and the candidate controls a specific scarce capacity asset with buyer deposits, not a generic evidence file or recovery mandate.
