Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

BESS Carbon-Footprint PO Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:

BESS and rechargeable-industrial-battery importers, distributors, and integrators will pay for a tightly scoped order-release desk when a named EU customer, distributor, EPC, project buyer, or financier blocks a purchase order, shipment, onboarding, tender response, or draw because Battery Regulation carbon-footprint declaration evidence for a specific battery model and manufacturing plant is missing, unclear, or rejected.

Exact buyer:

Polish and CEE BESS importers, energy-storage distributors, commercial battery integrators, UPS/industrial battery suppliers, C&I storage EPCs, and non-EU battery manufacturers selling through EU distributors. The best first buyers have 20 kWh to multi-MWh rechargeable industrial battery or stationary energy-storage products, live EU customer demand, and no internal specialist team for Battery Regulation carbon-footprint response work.

Acute trigger:

A named buyer, distributor, public or private tender, project owner, EPC, lender, or procurement team asks for carbon-footprint declaration evidence, model/manufacturing-plant details, methodology status, supporting supplier data, EU economic-operator responsibility, or a qualified no-go before releasing a purchase order, shipment, onboarding approval, tender submission, project draw, or distributor acceptance. The seller has commercial value blocked now and cannot answer honestly with generic battery-passport readiness language.

Control point:

Signed seller mandate; named customer or procurement blocker; written buyer request or rejection text; exact battery model and manufacturing plant; supplier authorization to gather source documents; existing carbon-footprint declaration or draft if available; bill-of-materials and energy/source-data inventory only where the supplier can lawfully provide it; qualified LCA/TIC/product-compliance reviewer route; evidence-gap log; customer-ready response packet; lab/LCA-needed no-go wording where the declaration cannot honestly be supported; buyer response and outcome record.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Three prepaid live cases with named order, shipment, tender, onboarding, or draw blockers. Each case has written buyer request text, seller authorization, model/manufacturing-plant details, product documentation, a scoped qualified-reviewer path, and a prepaid fixed fee. At least one case produces buyer acceptance, narrowed rejection, procurement progress, order-release progress, or a paid clean no-go that sends the seller to a qualified LCA/TIC provider before false claims are made. Target 60,000-135,000 PLN collected.

6-month POC:

Handle 12-25 paid BESS or rechargeable-industrial-battery order-release cases. Collect 250,000-700,000 PLN in fixed fees. Produce 8-15 objective outcomes: customer-accepted response, narrowed missing-evidence list, procurement/tender/onboarding progress, order-release progress, LCA/TIC handoff accepted, or clean no-go. Build a case library by battery category, model/manufacturing-plant evidence, buyer wording, documentation gaps, reviewer questions, accepted response patterns, and rejected unsafe claims. Maintain gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs and reject at least 35% of weak or unsafe leads.

First acquisition mechanism:

Start through BESS distributors, C&I energy-storage installers, battery-product compliance boutiques, LCA/TIC consultants willing to route overflow, energy-storage EPCs, and importers with non-EU battery suppliers. The outreach is not "Battery Regulation consulting." It asks for one live commercial blocker: "Send only a named BESS or industrial-battery order where the buyer has asked for carbon-footprint declaration evidence before PO, shipment, tender, onboarding, or draw release."

Economics:

Charge 18,000-30,000 PLN for a standard order-release response and 30,000-45,000 PLN for urgent multi-model or tender/draw cases. Qualified reviewer work is scoped and passed through or included with margin discipline. No success fee is needed for early proof; the fixed fee is justified because one BESS order, project shipment, tender, distributor onboarding, or project draw can be worth hundreds of thousands to millions of PLN. Testing, certification, formal LCA calculation, and legal opinions are separate provider work, not bundled promises.

Copy risk:

TIC firms, LCA consultants, battery OEMs, product-compliance advisers, ESG consultants, energy-storage EPC advisers, distributors, and larger importers can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is early control over live buyer request wording, exact model/manufacturing-plant evidence folders, reviewer issue logs, unsafe-claim no-go patterns, accepted customer response wording, and partner-routed current cases before broad compliance providers package this as a fast order-release product.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once the seller signs the mandate, pays the fixed fee, opens the model/manufacturing-plant evidence room, authorizes supplier document collection, shares the customer request or rejection, and routes the response through the desk, a later incumbent cannot access or influence that exact PO, tender, shipment, onboarding, or draw blocker without the seller switching mid-process and rebuilding the evidence chronology. Incumbents can compete for future cases, but not the controlled live case already in motion.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The unit is a live order-release case with a written buyer blocker and an outcome record. The output is not a generic Battery Regulation readiness report, software dashboard, marketplace, lead introduction, or battery-passport database. Each case ends with customer-accepted response, narrowed rejection, procurement progress, LCA/TIC handoff, order-release progress, or paid clean no-go when the seller lacks honest source evidence.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

The startup does not calculate or certify carbon footprints unless a qualified provider separately performs that work. It does not issue legal opinions, conformity assessments, Battery Regulation compliance certifications, product-safety certificates, battery passports, environmental claims, recycled-content claims, dangerous-goods shipment documents, waste-battery registrations, fire-safety opinions, grid-code opinions, warranty decisions, or engineering signoff. Battery manufacturers, importers, distributors, qualified LCA/TIC providers, product-compliance advisers, legal counsel, EPCs, and buyers remain responsible for regulated decisions and technical claims.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

Not BatteryFit, which qualifies PV battery retrofit economics for installers. Not REDBlocked, which handles Amazon connected-device evidence. Not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs, which are public-tender product evidence. Not CBAM Import Continuity, Data Act Machine Data Liberation, DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack, BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, HeritageDoor, Supplement Stack Safety, or HeatQuiet. The distinct control point is a live BESS or rechargeable-industrial-battery commercial blocker tied to Battery Regulation carbon-footprint evidence for a named model and manufacturing plant.

Strongest anticipated objections:

TIC and LCA incumbents are closer to formal carbon-footprint work. Many sellers will lack real manufacturing-plant data, energy data, bill-of-materials detail, or methodology alignment. Customers may require formal calculation or certification rather than a response packet. Delegated and implementing act timing can create legal/compliance ambiguity. The buyer pool may be narrower than all BESS sellers. Qualified reviewer cost can compress margin. Large distributors and EPCs may internalize the checklist. Battery safety, fire, transport, waste, grid, and warranty questions can contaminate cases. The business can look like generic Battery Regulation consulting unless every intake starts from a named blocked PO, tender, shipment, onboarding, or draw.
