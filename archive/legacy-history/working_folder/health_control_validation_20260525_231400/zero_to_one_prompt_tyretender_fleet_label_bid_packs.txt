Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

TyreTender Fleet Label Bid Packs

One-sentence thesis:

Fleet tyre distributors and importers lose or avoid public-sector fleet tenders when product labels, EPREL product-information sheets, load/speed fit, wet grip, rolling resistance, external-noise, and severe-snow evidence do not line up before the bid deadline; a narrow bid-release pack turns one live tender and one distributor catalog into a submit/no-bid decision.

Exact buyer:

Polish and CEE tyre distributors, importers, fleet-supply resellers, and regional tyre chains bidding into municipal bus, waste-collection, road-maintenance, utility, police/municipal-service, airport, and public-works fleet tyre tenders or framework agreements.

Acute trigger:

A live public or quasi-public fleet tyre tender has a bid deadline inside 2-4 weeks and asks for tyre-label, EPREL, product-information-sheet, wet-grip, rolling-resistance, external-noise, severe-snow/3PMSF, load index, speed rating, axle/vehicle fit, brand/model, retread/new tyre, or equivalent-evidence fields that the distributor cannot cleanly reconcile across its proposed SKUs.

Control point:

The founder controls the exact tender file, buyer question log, distributor SKU export, EPREL links/PDFs, tyre-label/PIS evidence room, vehicle/axle/load/speed fit matrix, exception/no-go table, and final bid evidence annex for that deadline. The startup does not control product truth, public award decisions, tyre safety, or supplier registration.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Three prepaid live bid packs from at least two tyre distributors or fleet-supply resellers; each includes the tender notice, deadline, proposed SKU list, EPREL/product-label evidence, model/size/load/speed matrix, severe-snow/3PMSF flags where relevant, distributor approval owner, and submit/no-bid record. At least two packs produce a submitted bid, formal clarification answer, buyer no-objection on evidence, or documented no-bid avoiding a non-compliant submission. Collect 18,000-54,000 PLN with gross margin above 70%.

6-month POC:

Deliver 20-35 paid bid packs across 8-15 distributors/importers, cover 120-300 tyre SKUs and 40-80 vehicle/spec matrices, collect 180,000-450,000 PLN, keep average turnaround below five business days, and document at least 12 measurable outcomes: submitted bids, clarification responses accepted, SKU substitutions, no-bid decisions, or avoided disqualifications. At least four buyers should return for a second tender or framework lot.

First acquisition mechanism:

Scrape Polish and CEE procurement portals for live fleet tyre, bus tyre, winter tyre, waste-truck tyre, road-maintenance, utility-fleet, and airport tyre tenders. Build a target list of tyre distributors and regional chains that publicly bid, appear in award notices, or sell commercial/fleet tyres. Outreach with a concrete tender-specific message: "This tender asks for label, EPREL/PIS, load/speed, wet grip, noise, and snow evidence across multiple tyre sizes. I can turn your proposed SKU list into a bid-ready annex or no-bid matrix in five business days. Fixed fee, no award guarantee, no product certification."

Economics:

Charge 6,000-12,000 PLN for a single-lot evidence pack, 12,000-18,000 PLN for multi-lot fleet tenders, and 2,000-4,000 PLN for paid no-bid/substitution matrices. Founder time should fall from 6-10 hours per first pack to 3-5 hours after templates. Cost is mostly data extraction, EPREL/PDF verification, tender parsing, and optional tyre/fleet reviewer spot-check. The fee is small relative to a municipal fleet framework, lost bid, clarification failure, emergency SKU substitution, or reputational cost of a non-compliant submission.

Copy risk:

Tyre manufacturers, distributor bid teams, procurement consultants, and large tyre chains can copy the checklist. The defense is narrow tender timing, repeated tender-language memory, fast EPREL/PIS retrieval, size/load/speed/snow-fit matrix templates, buyer-clarification history, and a private library of accepted/rejected fleet-tyre evidence annexes by procurer, fleet type, tyre class, and specification wording.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once a distributor prepays for a named tender and gives the proposed SKU list, bid deadline, vehicle/spec table, buyer questions, and evidence-room route, the founder controls that exact bid annex workflow for the live deadline. A manufacturer or competing consultant can copy the category later, but cannot instantly own the distributor-approved pack, exception log, tender-specific fit matrix, and bid-response chronology already in progress.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

It accepts only named live tenders or framework lots and returns a bid-use artifact: EPREL/PIS evidence annex, tyre-label archive, fit/compliance matrix, SKU substitutions, clarification wording, and submit/no-bid record. No live tender means no engagement. It does not introduce buyers, run a marketplace, certify products, sell tyres, negotiate prices, or build software before repeated paid bid packs.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

Do not certify tyre safety, create EPREL registrations, alter supplier labels, claim regulatory compliance, give legal procurement advice, decide equivalent-product acceptance, or guarantee bid award. Suppliers remain responsible for EPREL registration and product truth. Distributors remain responsible for bid submission, pricing, delivery, warranties, and tender representations. The startup flags mismatches and routes uncertain cases to supplier/manufacturer clarification or a no-bid recommendation.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

The obvious duplicate risk is GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs. This candidate is materially different only if it stays in fleet tyres: buyer is tyre distributor/importer, trigger is fleet tyre tender or framework, artifact is tyre label/PIS plus vehicle/axle/load/speed/snow fit matrix, and the payment event is fleet tyre bid submission. It must not handle lighting, lamps, luminaires, or generic EPREL product tenders.

Strongest anticipated objections:

It may still be too close to GreenTender because it reuses live tenders plus EPREL evidence. Tyre manufacturers and large distributors may already provide labels, PIS files, and tender support. Many tenders may ask simple documents that can be downloaded from EPREL, making fees hard to justify. The hard work may be commercial pricing, stock, delivery, warranty, or equivalent-product acceptance, not label evidence. Fleet tyre tenders may be too sporadic for a repeatable small company unless the founder covers multiple CEE markets. Local public procurement rules and clarification wording can become legal advice. Distributor SKU data can be messy or late, compressing turnaround. A no-bid decision is useful but harder to sell than a submission pack. Manufacturers, wholesalers, and procurement consultants can internalize once the matrix is visible. The idea only works if early cases prove that tender evidence mismatch, not price alone, is a repeated urgent blocker.
