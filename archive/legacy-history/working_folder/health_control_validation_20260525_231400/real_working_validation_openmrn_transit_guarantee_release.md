## Reviewer Diagnosis

The evaluator accepted the process-level premise: open NCTS transit movements can consume guarantee capacity, and official write-off/guarantee release is a real artifact rather than a report.

The idea failed because the business looks like a specialist cash-flow service with difficult casework, not a defensible company. The reviewer emphasized authority trust, proof quality, slow cross-border customs timelines, adverse selection, and founder fit as the key blockers.

## Strongest Objections

- Customs agents and forwarders may not want to admit they have this backlog.
- Outsourced cases are likely to be the worst, least recoverable MRNs.
- Official proof standards are strict; ordinary commercial evidence is not enough.
- 60-day proof is aggressive because enquiry procedures can involve multi-week response windows.
- A signed mandate may not overcome customs authority preference for the holder of procedure or an authorized customs representative.
- Success-fee attribution to released guarantee capacity can be disputed.
- Assigned cases protect only the current batch, not the business category.
- 300-500 MRNs across 8-15 buyers is too operationally heavy for a part-time founder.
- Some MRNs may involve real irregularities or customs debt, creating liability exposure.

## Reviewer Suggested Narrow Version

The reviewer said the only testable version would be much narrower:

NCTS Guarantee Capacity Recovery for arrived-but-not-discharged MRNs, one corridor, fixed proof checklist, no-success/no-success-fee.

Required constraints:

- Minimum buyer queue: 20 open MRNs.
- Visible guarantee pain: available balance below operating need.
- Accept only cases with official proof categories likely available.
- Price mostly per MRN closed, not as a percentage of freed guarantee.
- Start with one authority pair/corridor.
- Require buyer-side named customs contact.
- Use a licensed/authorized customs-agent contractor from day one.

The reviewer said even the narrowed version would rise only to 72/100 if backed by a credible customs-agent partner and real open-MRN ledgers.

## Lesson

Hard official release artifacts are still insufficient when the buyer's own incumbent profession controls the workflow and authority trust. Avoid more customs-release variants unless the founder controls a paid queue or asset that customs agents cannot internalize.
