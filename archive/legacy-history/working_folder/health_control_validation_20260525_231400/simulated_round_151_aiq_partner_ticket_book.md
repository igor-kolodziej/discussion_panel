## Lead Candidate

**AIQ Partner Ticket Book**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It directly addresses the two biggest Round 150 objections: unknown-founder trust and direct CAC.
- Partners already have NDA/DPA/client trust and questionnaire access, but AI sections are new enough that they may prefer overflow delivery rather than building a bench immediately.
- The control point is a signed routing agreement plus current tickets and billing authority, not just a response mandate from one SaaS vendor.
- The output remains bounded to live deal-release AI questionnaire answers, evidence room, accepted/rework/no-go status, and partner-branded delivery.
- It has concrete 60-day proof: partner routing agreements, prepaid tickets, buyer-progress outcomes, repeat partner cases, and gross margin.

### Internal Objections

- Strong SOC2/fractional-CISO partners can learn the workflow and internalize after one or two cases.
- Partner exclusivity may be weak unless the startup performs quickly and invisibly.
- The startup still depends on vendor evidence quality and legal/high-risk exclusions.
- It could become low-margin subcontracted questionnaire labor.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and this is the strongest available upgrade to the highest recent working score.
