## Control Thesis

Specialist subcontractors often have completed, accepted work where 5-10% cash is retained as warranty/security. In some contracts that retained cash can be replaced by an accepted bank guarantee, insurance guarantee, retention bond, maintenance bond, or similar beneficiary-accepted security.

The startup does not chase disputed debt, buy claims, provide guarantees, act as broker, or hold escrow. It controls only clean signed mandates over specific retention files, the contract clause and retention ledger, the guarantee-option packet through authorized providers, the beneficiary acceptance request, and the release-status workflow until retained cash is actually paid out.

## Internal Cap Check

- Concrete control proof exists only if the 60-day POC gets signed contractor mandates, guarantee quotes/term sheets from authorized channels, beneficiary acceptance requests, at least one accepted replacement guarantee, and at least one released-cash event.
- Copy risk is high because guarantee brokers, banks, insurers, surety brokers, accountants, and construction lawyers can copy the workflow. It stays above the simulated gate only because the selected wedge controls specific neglected smaller-subcontractor release files before incumbents are engaged.
- Cash conversion is plausible only if the retained amounts are large enough, the substitution clause is clear, the files are clean, and fees are paid from released cash.
- Legal and provider boundaries are strict: no unlicensed brokerage, legal advice, debt collection, guarantee issuance, escrow, or disputed construction claims.

## Kill Criteria

- Clean contracts rarely allow retention-cash substitution.
- Beneficiaries refuse replacement guarantees or demand legal negotiation.
- Guarantee providers reject the target subcontractor profile or require collateral that eliminates cash-release value.
- More than 40% of qualified cases contain defects, setoff, penalties, insolvency, disputed handover, or unclear acceptance.
- No accepted guarantee or released-cash event can be completed inside 60 days.
- Founder fees fall below 10,000 PLN per case or gross margin below 60% after broker/legal review.
