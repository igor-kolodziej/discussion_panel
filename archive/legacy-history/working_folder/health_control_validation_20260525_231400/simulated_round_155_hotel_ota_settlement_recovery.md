## Lead Candidate

**StayLedger OTA Settlement Recovery Mandate**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It is current-cash recovery rather than evidence-only release.
- Hotels repeatedly reconcile OTA extranet, PMS, acquirer, bank, and commission invoice files, creating a real document surface for AI-assisted matching and human judgment.
- The control point is concrete: signed mandate, read-only extranet/PMS/acquirer exports, OTA claim IDs, credit-note requests, VCC recovery queue, and success-fee/payment terms.
- First proof can be cash movement: OTA credit note, VCC balance recovered, commission adjustment, or prevented current-month overpayment.
- It is not generic hotel revenue management, rate shopping, channel management, or BI. The only valid first product is recovered or prevented money.
- It differs from PromoLeak: hotel OTAs, PMS/folio/card-acquirer reconciliation, VCC breakage, no-show/cancellation commissions, and OTA claim IDs are materially different from FMCG retailer deductions and retail-media charges.

### Internal Objections

- Hotels may already have revenue managers, controllers, accountants, PMS/channel-manager tools, or OTA reconciliation vendors.
- OTA support can be slow and opaque, and hotels may fear damaging platform relationships.
- Some differences are valid contract terms, tax treatment, cancellation policy, or front-desk process failures rather than recoverable errors.
- Independent hotels may be too small; the first buyer must be multi-property independents or groups with enough OTA volume.
- Gross margin dies if the founder chases low-value line items or messy PMS cleanup.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the idea returns to the preferred pattern: signed recovery mandate, current cash, repeated files, and first proof as actual credit/payment movement.
