Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
StayLedger OTA Settlement Recovery Mandate

One-sentence thesis:
A 14-day recovery sprint for independent hotels and small hotel groups that finds and recovers OTA settlement leakage across Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, PMS folios, virtual credit cards, acquirer settlements, no-shows, cancellations, taxes, fees, commissions, and chargebacks.

Exact buyer:
Independent hotels, aparthotels, boutique hotel groups, regional hotel operators, and small management companies with 50-800 rooms across one to ten properties, meaningful Booking.com/Expedia/Agoda/OTA volume, and no dedicated OTA reconciliation team. The first buyer is the owner, controller, finance lead, revenue manager, or hotel accountant responsible for OTA commission invoices, VCC settlement, PMS folios, and monthly bank/card reconciliation.

Acute trigger:
The hotel has current or recent OTA commission invoices, virtual-card settlements, no-show/cancellation adjustments, refund reversals, rate/tax/fee mismatches, chargebacks, or partner-support disputes that the finance team suspects are wrong but has not reconciled across the OTA extranet, PMS, acquirer, bank statement, and folio records. The trigger is cash already deducted, uncollected, overpaid, undercharged, or about to be paid in the current settlement cycle.

Control point:
Signed recovery mandate for named OTA channels and date ranges; read-only or exported Booking.com/Expedia/Agoda reservation and commission files; PMS folio exports; acquirer settlement files for OTA virtual cards; bank/card settlement files; no-show/cancellation/refund logs; tax/fee configuration screenshots where relevant; agreed claim-submission authority or hotel-approved claim drafts; success-fee or fixed-audit payment terms; and a hotel-approved recovery queue with OTA claim IDs, credit-note requests, VCC charge/retry items, disputed commission lines, and prevented current-month overpayments.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Five hotels or one small hotel group signs recovery mandates covering at least 1,500 OTA reservations or 500,000 PLN equivalent in recent OTA gross booking value; at least three mandates are prepaid fixed audits or success-fee mandates with minimum fees; the startup receives PMS, OTA, acquirer, and commission exports for scoped periods; at least 40,000 PLN in plausible recoverable or preventable leakage is identified; at least one OTA credit note, VCC recovery, commission adjustment, or current-month invoice reduction is accepted or paid; at least 15,000 PLN in audit or success fees is collected; no case requires legal action, tax advice, or guest-facing dispute handling.

6-month POC:
Process 30-60 hotel mandates across 10,000+ OTA reservations and 4,000,000+ PLN equivalent in OTA gross booking value; collect 250,000-700,000 PLN in fixed audit and success fees; document 500,000+ PLN in recoverable or prevented leakage; recover or prevent at least 150,000 PLN in confirmed credit notes, VCC collections, commission corrections, invoice reductions, or chargeback reversals; build a private taxonomy of OTA leakage patterns by channel, property type, PMS, acquirer, cancellation/no-show process, tax/fee configuration, VCC window, and claim wording; secure repeat mandates from at least five hotels or two groups.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target Polish/CEE independent hotels, aparthotels, boutique groups, revenue managers, hotel accountants, PMS/channel-manager consultants, and outsourced finance firms. Pitch only the cash-recovery sprint: "Give me one OTA channel and one recent settlement period. I reconcile OTA bookings, PMS folios, VCC/acquirer settlements, commission invoices, cancellations, no-shows, and refunds. You get a recovery queue, claim drafts, evidence links, and only pay ongoing fees if money is recovered or current overpayment is prevented."

Economics:
Charge a 5,000-12,000 PLN fixed audit fee for one property/channel/quarter or 15-25% of confirmed recovered or prevented cash, with a minimum fee for data-heavy cases. Charge 15,000-30,000 PLN for multi-property or multi-channel sprints. Use contractors only for hotel accounting/PMS export cleanup and selected OTA-specific review. Gross margin should exceed 60% once extraction and matching templates stabilize. The buyer pays because a single multi-property OTA commission or VCC recovery can exceed the audit fee, and the sprint protects cash already leaking out of monthly settlement.

Copy risk:
Hotel revenue managers, controllers, accountants, PMS/channel-manager vendors, OTA reconciliation tools, revenue-management consultants, and OTA account managers can copy pieces. The defense is signed current mandates, fast cash-first audit scope, OTA/PMS/acquirer matching recipes, accepted claim wording, VCC recovery patterns, no-show/cancellation commission evidence memory, and repeat hotel/accountant channels before generalist tools focus on small independent groups.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the hotel signs the recovery mandate, exports the scoped OTA/PMS/acquirer/bank files, approves the recovery queue, and authorizes claim submissions or claim drafts for named OTA lines, the startup controls the current claim file, evidence links, OTA case IDs, credit-note requests, and success-fee entitlement for that settlement period. A revenue manager, accountant, PMS vendor, or OTA tool would need to displace an already-approved recovery queue while the claim window and current invoice cycle are open.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only signed OTA settlement recovery mandates tied to current or recent cash leakage. The output is a hotel-approved recovery queue with evidence-backed OTA claim IDs, VCC charge/retry items, commission disputes, credit-note requests, chargeback/refund reversals, and prevented current-month invoice overpayments. It is not hotel revenue management, rate shopping, channel management, generic bookkeeping, tax advice, BI, SaaS, dashboard, database, marketplace, OTA consulting, or brokered introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, tax advice, debt collection, guest-facing dispute handling, card-scheme advice, chargeback legal representation, hotel rate strategy, channel-management strategy, or guarantees of OTA acceptance. The hotel, accountant, revenue manager, PMS vendor, acquirer, OTA, and legal/tax advisors remain responsible for contracts, tax treatment, guest disputes, cancellation policies, folio correctness, card processing, and final claim submission where required. Cases involving guest fraud, consumer complaints, litigation threats, tax classification, unclear cancellation policy, missing source records, or hotel process failures without recoverable OTA error are rejected or marked no-go.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not PromoLeak because it does not handle FMCG, beauty, supplements, household brands, drugstores, modern trade, marketplace retail deductions, promotion agreements, retail-media charges, sell-out files, or trade-promotion leakage. The buyer is a hotel operator, the payment event is OTA settlement leakage, the document surface is OTA/PMS/acquirer/bank/folio data, and the proof artifact is OTA credit notes, VCC recovery, commission adjustments, invoice reductions, or chargeback/refund reversals. It is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, 3PL overbilling, freight audit, KSeF payment release, or construction retention recovery.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Hotels may already have revenue managers, controllers, accountants, PMS reports, channel-manager tools, or OTA reconciliation vendors. Many suspected errors may be valid contract terms, cancellation-policy outcomes, no-show process failures, tax/fee configuration problems, front-desk undercharges, or PMS cleanup rather than recoverable OTA mistakes. OTA support can be slow, opaque, and relationship-sensitive. Some hotels may be too small for fees unless scoped to groups or high-OTA-volume properties. Success fees may be hard to define for prevented overpayments. Data exports can be messy across PMS, channel manager, acquirer, and bank files. OTA claim windows or VCC charge windows may expire. The business can become generic hotel accounting cleanup unless it rejects low-value line items and non-recoverable process defects.
