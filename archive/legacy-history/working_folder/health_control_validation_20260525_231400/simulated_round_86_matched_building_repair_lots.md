## Lead Candidate: StormTile Matched Historic Roof-Repair Lot Bank

### Buyer

Primary buyers and channels:

- roofing contractors repairing old clay/concrete/slate roofs;
- heritage-roof restoration firms;
- property managers and HOAs managing older apartment houses, villas, churches, schools, manor houses, and boutique hotels;
- boutique developers restoring pre-war or historic buildings;
- insurers/loss adjusters indirectly, when a storm or leak claim requires repair pricing;
- demolition, roof-replacement, and estate-clearance crews as supply sources.

### Acute Trigger

A roof repair is live and the roofer cannot finish a patch, flashing, ridge, valley, chimney, storm, or leak repair because the existing roof uses an old clay, concrete, slate, ridge-cap, hip-cap, monk-and-nun, karpiówka, or other special tile profile that is discontinued, weathered, or visually/physically mismatched by new stock.

The owner's alternative is costly:

- leave a leak unresolved;
- install a visible mismatch on a premium or heritage roof;
- delay the project while hunting single pieces;
- custom-order expensive replacement pieces;
- remove tiles from hidden roof areas;
- expand a small repair into a large reroof;
- fight an insurer, conservator, HOA, or owner over repair scope.

### Transferable Control Point

Control exists only when the founder has:

- signed first-refusal/pickup agreements with roofers, demolition firms, roof-replacement crews, clear-out firms, and reclaimed-material sources;
- titled counted lots of roof tiles or special fittings before they are split, crushed, mixed, sold as singles, or discarded;
- lot records: source property, profile, approximate age, dimensions, color/weathering, condition photos, quantity, broken-rate, pallet IDs, special fittings, ridge/hip/eave/valley pieces;
- roofer/restorer sample-match notes for each buyer reservation;
- buyer deposit or reservation for a specific project and lot/profile before major acquisition whenever possible;
- clear title/provenance and rejection of suspect material.

The founder does not perform roofing, structural assessment, waterproofing design, conservation approval, insurance adjustment, or engineering. Licensed/qualified roofers and conservators remain provider of record.

### Sixty-Day Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

1. Build a supply list of 120 roofers, demolition crews, roof-replacement firms, estate-clearance teams, and reclaimed-material yards in Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, and heritage-building regions.
2. Sign at least 5 first-refusal or pickup-option agreements where the source calls before discarding, mixing, or selling old roof lots.
3. Inspect 6 candidate lots and reject any with unclear title, high breakage, uncertain profile, poor condition, contamination concerns, or no buyer match.
4. Acquire or option 2 counted lots under 45,000 PLN total after roofer/buyer interest is proven.
5. Produce lot cards: photos, dimensions, quantities, sample tile, profile notes, weathering/color, source area, special fittings, and breakage allowance.
6. Secure 3 paid reservations from roofers/property managers/owners for active repair projects, with at least 20,000 PLN collected before or immediately after acquisition.
7. Complete one paid handoff of a matched patch lot to a roofer or restorer.
8. Prove at least 45% gross margin after acquisition, sorting, breakage reserve, palletizing, storage, transport, seller payment, and sales cost.
9. Keep all installation, roof-safety, conservation, and insurance-scope decisions outside the startup.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Supply pitch:

"Before you throw away, crush, or mix old roof tiles from a replacement or demolition job, send me photos and a count. I buy complete usable lots fast, especially matching ridge caps, hip caps, special fittings, and old clay profiles. You do not need to list, store, sort, or answer buyer calls."

Buyer pitch:

"If your repair is blocked because the roof needs matching old tiles or fittings, send one sample/photo set and the needed quantity. We reserve a counted lot matched by profile, dimensions, weathering, and roofer confirmation. Your roofer installs; we only sell the matched material lot."

### Economics

Revenue:

- 8,000-35,000 PLN for ordinary matched patch lots depending on quantity, profile, color, and urgency;
- 25,000-70,000+ PLN for larger heritage repair lots with ridge/hip/eave/fitting pieces;
- premium per-piece pricing for scarce special shapes where a roofer needs small exact quantities;
- reservation deposits from roofers/property managers before a lot is moved or split.

Cost logic:

- acquisition and source payment are lot-based, not retail-piece priced;
- sorting, breakage allowance, palletizing, transport, storage, and sample delivery are explicit costs;
- the founder avoids full-roof replacement inventory and only buys lots tied to active repair demand or highly repeatable profiles;
- payback can happen from one or two project handoffs if buyer deposits precede acquisition.

### Copy-Risk Defense

Reclaimed-material yards and roofers already exist. The defensible wedge is narrower:

- first-call source access before roof lots are mixed or destroyed;
- titled counted lot control;
- active repair-project deposits;
- lot cards that roofers can trust quickly;
- special-shape and profile memory;
- buyer/supply memory by roof type, region, and project trigger.

Incumbents can sell old roof tiles generally. They cannot copy a specific counted lot, source agreement, buyer reservation, and sample match once those are controlled.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup owns or options physical roof-repair lots and sells counted matched material to active repair projects. It does not sell advice, search reports, listings, design, roofing services, conservation consulting, insurance help, or a marketplace.

### Safety And Legal Boundaries

- Roofers/restorers remain responsible for installation, roof access, waterproofing, fastening, load, ventilation, safety, and workmanship.
- Conservators/architects/owners handle protected-building approvals and aesthetic decisions.
- Insurers/loss adjusters handle claim scope and coverage.
- The startup does not guarantee roof performance or certify material fitness beyond honest condition/source/quantity records and clear sale terms.
- Any suspect, stolen, contaminated, heavily broken, or unsafe material is rejected.
- Buyer gets sample/inspection and return terms only for lot misdescription, not installation failure.

### Duplicate-Risk Check

- Not HeritageDoor Suites: this is exterior roof-repair material for active leaks/storm/conservation repairs, not complete interior door suites for premium apartment aesthetics.
- Not HeritageRadiator: no heating system, pressure testing, or plumbing.
- Not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, KRS continuity, or cyber-insurance evidence: no evidence desk, account rescue, health diligence, municipal invoice routing, registered-office book, or insurance underwriting.

## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- Existing reclaimed-material yards and roofers already trade old tiles.
- Logistics are heavy: pallets, breakage, storage, transport, and weather protection.
- Buyer urgency must be proven; decorative or speculative demand is not enough.
- Exact matching can fail on subtle size, curvature, weathering, profile, or fastening differences.
- Some roofers may prefer their own sources or new replacement products.
- Full-roof quantities can exceed the founder's capital and storage; the wedge must stay repair lots and special fittings.
- Conservation/building approvals and insurance disputes can pull the startup toward advice unless boundaries are strict.
