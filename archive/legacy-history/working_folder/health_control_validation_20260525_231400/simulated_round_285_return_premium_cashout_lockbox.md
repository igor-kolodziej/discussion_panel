## Selected Round 285 Candidate

**ReturnPremium Fleet/Site Cancellation Cash-Out Lockbox**

The selected candidate recovers or offsets insurer/broker-acknowledged unearned-premium credits after midterm fleet, property, liability, cyber, or package-policy cancellations caused by fleet reduction, site closure, broker change, legal-entity cleanup, or M&A carve-out.

## Internal Strict Simulation

### Why It Clears The Simulation Gate

- **Current cash/control:** only accepted after a broker/insurer return-premium statement, cancellation endorsement, credit note, account statement, or premium-finance payoff/refund statement exists.
- **No claim/legal role:** no loss adjustment, coverage decision, policy advice, insurance claim handling, litigation, or financing.
- **Buyer pain:** PE-backed and multi-site firms often have fragmented policy schedules after closures, fleet changes, acquisitions, divestitures, or broker changes; month-end/M&A reporting values cash cleanup.
- **Control artifact:** policyholder mandate, policy schedule, endorsement list, broker/insurer acknowledgement, before/after statement, and fee right over named lines.
- **Cash timing:** refunds/offsets can arrive inside 60 days if the credit is already acknowledged and the issue is payment routing, stale account, broker-change handoff, premium-finance netting, or internal neglect.
- **Unit economics:** fixed fee plus modest success fee over six-figure batches can support high gross margin without capital exposure.

### Internal Objections

1. Brokers and accountants are natural owners; the founder must find files they have neglected because incentives are weak after cancellation, old broker replacement, or post-close cleanup.
2. Clean credits may be auto-refunded or offset without help.
3. Messy credits may involve minimum retained premiums, taxes/fees, premium finance, installments, unpaid premiums, policyholder authority, cancellation wording, or entity changes.
4. Success fees can be disputed if refunds would have arrived anyway.
5. Direct cold outreach to CFOs about insurance refunds may look low-trust unless routed through PE ops, accountants, fleet disposal, or new brokers.
6. It may become generic AP credit recovery unless intake stays limited to named insurer/broker return-premium artifacts.

### Internal Score

**88.0 / 100**

This clears the simulated gate because it targets current insurer/broker-acknowledged cash with signed authority and first proof is refund/offset movement, not a report. It is weaker than an owned recurring book, but stronger than broad insurance claims, AP credits, or carrier-credit recovery because it avoids loss adjustment and begins only from official return-premium artifacts.
