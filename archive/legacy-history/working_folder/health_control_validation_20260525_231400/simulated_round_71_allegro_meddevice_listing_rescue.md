## Materially Different Candidate

`Allegro Medical-Device Listing Rescue Desk`

This is a Polish, 5-business-day paid rescue desk for small Allegro sellers/importers whose live medical-device or health-device listings are blocked, suspended, suppressed, or conversion-killed by missing CE/MDR/GPSR/advertising/documentation evidence. It focuses on products bought by 50-60+ and soon-elderly consumers: CPAP accessories, pulse oximeters, blood-pressure monitors, glucose meters, TENS/EMS, hearing-care accessories, mobility aids, thermometers, nebulizers, orthopedic supports, and regulated wellness/biosignal devices.

The startup does not sell devices, provide medical advice, certify products, act as notified body, or guarantee reinstatement. It controls a live blocked listing case, seller admin/document workflow, buyer-ready evidence pack, platform appeal text, missing-document escalation, and paid rescue/relisting economics.

## Buyer / Payer

- small Polish Allegro sellers/importers/distributors with sales frozen by listing suppression or document request;
- EU/CEE health-device brands whose Polish marketplace channel is blocked;
- 3PL/import agents with sellers stuck on compliance evidence;
- agencies serving Allegro sellers but lacking MDR/GPSR/advertising fluency.

## Acute Trigger

The seller has live revenue blocked: listing suppressed, offer removed, ad traffic stopped, inventory stranded, account warning, or compliance-document request. Generic compliance reports do not matter. The buyer pays only because a specific live listing and SKU revenue are blocked.

## Control Point

The controlled asset is the live transaction case:

- blocked Allegro listing/offer/SKU evidence;
- seller's admin access/screenshots/case history;
- product label, IFU/manual, DoC, CE/MDR/GPSR/advertising evidence, Polish-language requirements, importer/manufacturer details, photos, and supplier evidence;
- platform-specific response pack and appeal text;
- missing-evidence supplier request templates in Polish/English;
- paid fixed-fee rescue contract before work starts;
- seller revenue-loss context and post-case result.

The hard control is not a generic memo. It is live blocked platform revenue plus seller-authorized case workflow.
