Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
ButtonCellBlocked Amazon Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A white-label evidence-response desk for Amazon account-health and marketplace-compliance agencies serving sellers whose consumer products are suppressed, rejected, or at risk because Amazon asks for button/coin-cell battery safety evidence under Reese's Law, 16 CFR Part 1263, UL 4200A, GCC/CPC, warning-label, or test-report requirements.

Exact buyer:
Amazon account-health agencies, reinstatement consultants, marketplace-compliance agencies, and EU/CEE ecommerce agencies serving private-label sellers of consumer products that contain or are designed to use button or coin-cell batteries: LED candles, remotes, key finders, small electronics, bike lights, greeting cards, thermometers, scales, novelty items, calculators, trackers, alarm devices, pet products, household devices, and similar SKUs. The economic payer is the seller whose listing, ASIN, FBA/inventory movement, or account-health status is blocked.

Acute trigger:
Amazon requests a UL 4200A / Reese's Law / 16 CFR Part 1263 / button-cell evidence package, rejects a submitted test report or certificate, asks for a GCC/CPC or warning-label proof, flags battery-compartment safety, suppresses an ASIN, blocks inventory movement, or threatens account-health action. The seller has revenue-producing inventory but messy supplier documents, unclear battery type, missing product photos, incomplete warnings, mismatched model numbers, wrong lab scope, or no clear submit/cannot-submit/lab-needed decision.

Control point:
Signed agency-routed live-case mandate with the exact Amazon request/rejection text, ASIN and SKU list, product photos, battery type and location, age/children/toy exposure screen, packaging and warning-label photos, supplier declarations, test reports, GCC/CPC or certificate drafts where available, model-number match table, lab/accreditation evidence, prior Seller Central submissions, response log, supplier RFI, lab-needed/no-go matrix, and Amazon response wording. The startup controls the live evidence-response file, not product safety certification.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three Amazon/account-health agencies route at least one live button/coin-cell case each; eight prepaid live cases at 4,000-12,000 PLN each; each case has an actual Amazon request, rejection, suppression, inventory block, or account-health warning tied to button/coin-cell evidence; at least five response dossiers delivered within 72 hours; at least three cases produce accepted evidence, Amazon progress, lab-routing with seller approval, corrected supplier evidence, or clean no-go; one product-safety/lab reviewer available on paid per-case terms; gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs and agency margin.

6-month POC:
Handle 25-60 paid cases through 5-8 agencies; collect 150,000-450,000 PLN in gross fees; keep 72-hour turnaround for evidence-ready cases; get at least three agencies to send 5+ cases; document 15+ outcomes as accepted evidence, ASIN/account progress, lab escalation, supplier correction, or clean no-go; build a private request/outcome taxonomy by product type, battery type, Amazon wording, test-report gap, warning-label issue, model mismatch, lab-scope problem, and response result.

First acquisition mechanism:
Build a list of 80 Amazon account-health, reinstatement, marketplace-compliance, and ecommerce agencies. Offer a white-label escalation desk: "When Amazon blocks a product because of button/coin-cell battery evidence, send the exact request, ASIN, product photos, packaging/warning images, supplier docs, and test reports. I return a 72-hour dossier: submit now, cannot submit, supplier RFI, lab-needed path, Amazon response wording, and no-go warnings. You keep the client and 25-35% margin."

Economics:
Charge 4,000-6,000 PLN for request interpretation and submit/cannot-submit triage, 7,000-12,000 PLN for a full ASIN response dossier, and 15,000-25,000 PLN for multi-ASIN seller sprints or repeated rejection cases. Agency margin is 25-35%. Reviewer cost is 1,000-5,000 PLN per case for product-safety/lab-scope review. The seller pays because Amazon revenue or FBA inventory is blocked and because a wrong submission can lead to repeated rejection or account-health damage.

Copy risk:
Product-safety labs, CPSC consultants, Amazon agencies, compliance platforms, and testing companies can copy pieces. The defense is agency distribution, narrow button-cell case memory, exact Amazon request/rejection wording, model/test-report mismatch patterns, warning-label/photo checklist, supplier RFI templates, lab-routing outcomes, and accepted/rejected response history. Copy risk is high if the work becomes generic lab brokering or broad product-safety consulting.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once an agency routes a live Seller Central case, shares the Amazon request, ASIN, product photos, supplier docs, test reports, warning-label evidence, and seller authorization, the startup controls the active response file and submission package for that case. A generic lab or consultant would need to displace the agency relationship, request interpretation, model-match table, supplier RFI, response wording, and Amazon response log under listing/inventory pressure.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live Amazon listing, inventory, or account-health blockers tied to button/coin-cell battery evidence. The output is a case artifact used in Seller Central: request interpretation, evidence matrix, product photo/warning checklist, model/test-report match table, GCC/CPC evidence review, supplier RFI, lab-needed/no-go matrix, response wording, and response log. It is not broad product-safety consulting, lab testing, legal advice, certification, SaaS, a dashboard, a database, marketplace software, or brokered lab introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, product-safety certification, CPSC compliance opinions, lab testing, GCC/CPC issuance as manufacturer/importer, child-product certification, recall advice, product-design approval, or guarantee of Amazon acceptance. Sellers, manufacturers, importers, CPSC-accepted or ISO 17025 labs, product-safety advisers, lawyers, and Amazon remain responsible for testing, certificates, warnings, product design, legal compliance, and listing decisions. Cases involving missing required tests, unsafe battery compartments, forged reports, mismatched models, child-product uncertainty, counterfeit goods, or suspected safety risk are declined or routed to qualified labs/advisers.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked. REDBlocked handles connected baby monitors and indoor cameras under EU RED/CE/GPSR/cybersecurity evidence requests. ButtonCellBlocked handles button/coin-cell product-safety evidence for Amazon cases involving UL 4200A, Reese's Law, 16 CFR Part 1263, GCC/CPC, warning labels, battery-compartment photos, and test-report/model matching. The buyer channel is similar marketplace agencies, but the product category, regulation, evidence artifacts, reviewer skill, and proof of progress are materially different. It is not DORA, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, EPRBlocked, or any health/Rx route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Labs and product-safety consultants may already handle serious cases. Hard cases require real UL 4200A/16 CFR Part 1263 testing, design changes, warning-label fixes, or new supplier documents outside the founder's control. Amazon may reject evidence opaquely. Agencies may handle simple document collection themselves. Product-safety liability boundaries are serious. Case volume may be narrower than expected. A solo unknown founder needs a named product-safety/lab reviewer immediately. The idea may be seen as another Amazon evidence desk unless live button-cell case memory proves a distinct wedge.
