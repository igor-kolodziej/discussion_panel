## Scored Wording

Adult Diabetes Group R E-Order Mandate Rescue options a retiring/closing NFZ-contracted medical-device shop's adult diabetes Group R reorder book and executes monthly CGM/FGM/pump-consumable e-zlecenie fulfillment through a legally contracted NFZ realizator partner.

The founder does not prescribe, issue e-zlecenia, diagnose, provide clinical product advice, sell prescription medicines, advertise medicines, or claim healthcare-provider status. Authorized clinicians issue e-zlecenia. A lawfully contracted NFZ realizator/shop/apteka pulls and realizes the orders in eZWM and remains the regulated transaction party. The startup controls owner handoff, patient supply mandate/admin, SKU/reorder history, phone/domain/mailbox, supplier coordination, delivery/admin workflow, and payment/revenue-share economics.

This is a diabetes medical-device transaction-flow business, not an Ozempic/Rx drug business.

## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator considered this a strong regulated transaction-flow idea, but capped it below 85 because the founder does not directly own the NFZ realizator contract. The control point becomes credible only if the POC reaches actual order execution: patient/caregiver mandate, contracted NFZ realizator, eZWM-pulled adult Group R order, fulfilled shipment, and settlement proof.

## Main Objections

- The NFZ realizator partner can capture the value because it owns the regulated transaction capability, settlement relationship, supplier terms, and actual order execution.
- Patient mandates are not enough; the POC must prove pulled and fulfilled orders plus settlement evidence.
- Margins may be thin after product cost, courier, returns, call handling, seller earnout, realizator revenue share, stockouts, and settlement timing.
- Large online medical-device shops, pharmacies, and distributors can compete on stock, price, online ordering, brand trust, and product support.
- Mail-order realization appears possible for Group R, but only under precise legal and operational conditions through the proper NFZ-contracted place of service.
- GDPR/health-data handling and product-support drift are serious operating risks.

## Fatal Gate Reason

The idea has recurring demand and a plausible 2-month proof path, but the startup's strongest control point is still contractual/admin custody around a transaction substrate owned by a partner. The evaluator wanted direct ownership of the regulated transaction capability or a materially harder payment/title/control point.

## Pivot Implication

Next candidates should avoid partner-owned healthcare transaction rails unless the founder can own the contracting entity, title, receivable, device fleet, or payment account directly.
