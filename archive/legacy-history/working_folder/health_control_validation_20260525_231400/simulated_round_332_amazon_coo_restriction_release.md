## Why This Is Not A Duplicate

This is not REDBlocked because the trigger is not RED/CE/GPSR connected-device evidence, EN 18031, product safety, or cyber evidence. It is SKU-level country-of-origin listing restriction and batch source-proof reconciliation for cross-border Amazon EU/UK listings.

It is not Amazon Vendor Central Recovery because the payment event is not 1P deductions or remittance recovery. The buyer is a Seller Central account-health/compliance agency or seller with listing/inventory sales at risk, not a Vendor Central 1P brand recovering deducted cash.

It is not generic marketplace KYBC or product-safety evidence because the proof artifact is SKU origin support tied to invoices, manufacturing declarations, customs/shipping records, and Seller Central COO update status.

## Internal Objections To Watch In Real Validation

- Routine cases may be simple bulk attribute updates that a VA or agency can do cheaply.

- Amazon acceptance may be opaque or inconsistent.
- Customs brokers, freight forwarders, VAT firms, and Amazon agencies may already own the seller relationship.
- The enforcement spike may fade after sellers update catalogs.
- Low-value sellers create low-margin batch cleanup.
- Direct acquisition can attract desperate or non-compliant sellers seeking false origin shortcuts.
