## Lead Candidate: GrantPay Payment-Claim Release Desk

### Buyer

Polish/CEE SMEs, grant-funded project owners, accountants, CFOs, and grant advisers with already-awarded EU/PARP/NCBR/FENG/regional/innovation grants where a reimbursement or payment claim is rejected, corrected, paused, or not ready because the evidence file is messy.

### Acute Trigger

The beneficiary has already spent money or is near a payment claim, but reimbursement/current cash is blocked by a correction request or missing evidence: invoices, bank proofs, procurement comparisons, contracts, fixed-asset protocols, delivery/acceptance documents, photos, publicity markings, timesheets, payroll proofs, deliverables, indicators, VAT/split-payment details, or portal attachments.

### Control Point

Signed payment-claim mandate with:

- grant agreement and payment schedule;
- current payment claim/correction request;
- eligible-cost ledger;
- invoice and bank-payment proof;
- procurement/evaluation documents where applicable;
- delivery/acceptance/fixed-asset protocols;
- deliverables, photos, indicators, publicity proofs, timesheets, payroll documents where applicable;
- portal attachment checklist;
- correction response pack;
- no-go/escalation log for legal/accounting/procurement questions.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three prepaid payment-claim release cases at 5,000-25,000 PLN each.
2. At least one claim or correction pack used in a submitted payment request/correction response.
3. At least one accepted correction, payment-claim progress, reimbursement release, or clean no-go before wasting more time.
4. One grant-accounting/compliance reviewer on paid per-case terms.
5. Repeat taxonomy across missing invoices, bank proofs, procurement, delivery protocols, indicators, publicity, payroll/timesheets, VAT, and portal attachment gaps.

### Internal Simulated Score

**88 / 100**

Reason for advancing: the first proof is current cash movement or accepted correction, not a report; buyer pain is tied to money already awarded and spent.
