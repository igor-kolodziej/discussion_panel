## Lead Candidate: LeaseClear Machinery Payoff Closing Desk

### Buyer

Polish/CEE SME equipment sellers, lessees, search funds, used-equipment buyers, machine dealers, insolvency asset managers, and brokers trying to close a named sale of a serial-numbered industrial machine or equipment lot where the buyer is ready but the asset is still leased, financed, pledged, or title-controlled by a lessor/lender.

### Acute Trigger

A used-equipment sale is ready to close, but cash cannot move because the seller does not hold clean title or cannot prove lien-free transfer. The buyer will not wire until the lessor/lender confirms payoff, release, VAT/invoice chain, title transfer, pickup consent, and no duplicate sale. The seller needs the sale proceeds or wants to retire/close/upgrade, and the buyer has a production or resale deadline.

### Control Point

The startup controls the live closing file, not the buyer introduction:

- signed seller/lessee mandate for one named machine or lot;
- signed buyer reservation or deposit subject to clean payoff/release;
- lessor/lender payoff letter or balance statement;
- law-firm escrow or direct buyer-to-lessor payoff instruction;
- release/consent letter from lessor/lender;
- serial-number/asset-identification packet;
- invoice/VAT and handover checklist;
- pickup/release authorization;
- closing statement and founder fee paid from closing or upfront.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three live equipment-sale closing mandates where buyer and seller already exist and the blocked value is above 150,000 PLN.
2. One lawyer/accountant escrow or direct-payoff structure memo confirming the founder is not holding client funds, providing legal advice, or acting as unlicensed lender/broker.
3. Two lessor/lender payoff or release paths obtained in writing.
4. One transaction closes with clean payoff, release, buyer handover, and founder fee paid.
5. At least 25,000 PLN in collected fees or escrow-approved closing fees before total spend exceeds 100,000 PLN.

### Economics

Charge a fixed closing/admin fee of 8,000-18,000 PLN for clean single-machine deals and 1-3% of transaction value for larger lots, capped or split between seller and buyer. The fee is small relative to a blocked 150,000-1,000,000 PLN sale, lessor payoff, buyer production deadline, or seller closure need. No claim purchase or inventory purchase is needed.

### Copy Risk

Equipment dealers, leasing companies, machine brokers, insolvency firms, accountants, and lawyers can all solve parts of this. The wedge is not legal theory or machinery brokerage. It is a narrow paid closing desk for already-matched buyer/seller/lessor transactions, with repeated payoff/release patterns, buyer-safe closing checklists, and fast tri-party coordination.

### Why Incumbents Cannot Copy Before Control

Once the seller/lessee signs the closing mandate, the buyer signs a conditional reservation or deposit, and the lessor/lender provides payoff/release instructions for the exact serial-numbered asset, the startup controls that active closing workflow. A dealer or lawyer can offer a similar process later, but cannot instantly displace the already-authorized closing file without the parties restarting the transaction.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup accepts only current equipment sales with named buyer, named seller, identified serial-numbered asset, blocked payoff/title issue, and imminent cash movement. It does not list equipment, introduce buyers, appraise machinery, provide legal advice, finance purchases, hold escrow funds, build a marketplace, or sell generic title reports. The output is a completed payoff/release/handover closing for a specific transaction.

### Boundaries

Use law-firm escrow, not founder custody of client funds. Reject disputed ownership, litigation, insolvency without appointed authority, stolen-risk flags, sanctions, export controls, unsafe equipment, missing serial identity, buyer/seller mismatch, tax fraud risk, anti-assignment restrictions that cannot be cleared, or deals requiring legal advice beyond qualified counsel. Lawyers, accountants, lessors, lenders, equipment inspectors, and the parties remain responsible for legal, tax, financing, technical, and safety decisions.

### Duplicate Risk

This is not the failed forklift lease-end option buyout because the founder is not speculating on residual purchase options or buying used forklifts. The buyer and seller already exist; the control point is the cash/title/payoff closing file. It is not the failed ShowComms or hotel FF&E lot because the startup does not own inventory. It is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, 3PL recovery, LC discrepancy, retention release, or PFRON because the payment event is not a disputed deduction or public offset; it is a private equipment sale blocked by title/payoff mechanics.
