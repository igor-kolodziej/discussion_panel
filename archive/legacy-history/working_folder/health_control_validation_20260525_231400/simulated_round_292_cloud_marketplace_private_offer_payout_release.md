## Round Objective

The recent failures show that "cash stuck behind documents" is still too weak when normal incumbents already own the workflow: tenant reps, project managers, accountants, lawyers, lease auditors, carrier auditors, and platform agencies can absorb many cases.

This round searched for payment streams where:

- the buyer can sign a mandate over a named current transaction;
- the first proof can be case acknowledgement, credit, payout, scheduled disbursement, or a paid fee;
- the founder does not need to practice law, tax, insurance adjustment, clinical work, or regulated logistics;
- no bulky inventory or preventive retainer is required;
- the asset is a live monetary platform or counterparty file, not a report or dashboard.

## Finalists

### 1. Cloud Marketplace Private-Offer Payout Release Mandate

Buyer:

Small and mid-sized B2B SaaS vendors/ISVs selling through AWS Marketplace, Microsoft commercial marketplace/Azure Marketplace, or Google Cloud Marketplace.

Acute trigger:

An enterprise buyer accepted or paid a private offer, channel private offer, or marketplace transaction, but the seller's expected payout is missing, delayed, short, offset, unreconciled, or trapped in a support/reporting mismatch.

Transferable control point:

Signed recovery mandate for a named marketplace transaction plus the offer/order IDs, buyer PO or contract evidence, marketplace reports, invoices, payout IDs, bank deposits, tax/fee/partner margin table, and support case route.

Proof inside 60 days:

Three signed mandates over named private-offer payout files, at least two support cases acknowledged by marketplace or channel counterparty, and one corrected payout, credit, scheduled disbursement, or paid no-go fee.

Economics:

4,000-8,000 PLN intake/no-go plus 6-12% success fee on recovered or scheduled payout, capped by transaction size; or 10,000-25,000 PLN fixed for high-value non-disputed files. High line values and remote document work support >70% gross margin after accounting/tax reviewer costs.

Why it can beat the cap:

This is not a generic cloud marketplace launch or GTM advisory. The proof is current cash movement on a named transaction that the seller already won. The buyer pain is acute because the sale is already booked or expected, commissions/revenue recognition/month close may be blocked, and the founder can qualify only files with transaction IDs and report mismatches. The case memory is specific: marketplace, private-offer type, fee/tax/partner path, disbursement report, support wording, rejection reason, and corrected payout evidence.

Internal simulated score: **88.6 / 100**.

Main internal risks:

Marketplace GTM agencies, Tackle/Clazar/Labra-style tooling providers, channel partners, fractional CFOs, RevOps teams, and marketplace operations consultants can copy the workflow. Some apparent discrepancies are normal fees, taxes, timing, revenue-recognition misunderstanding, or contract terms. Support outcomes can be slow and opaque. Deal volume is narrower than broad SaaS finance recovery. The idea only clears the simulated gate if it rejects generic reconciliation and accepts only live, named private-offer payout discrepancies above a meaningful value threshold.
