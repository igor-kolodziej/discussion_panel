Positive points:

Main objections:

- founder avoids regulated audioprosthetist work, but that leaves lower-margin logistics, reminders, consumables, and repair coordination;

## Decision

Do not fresh-validate. Pivot away from B2C elderly device-continuity books unless the founder can control a harder payment stream, legal title, or institution-owned slot/route.
