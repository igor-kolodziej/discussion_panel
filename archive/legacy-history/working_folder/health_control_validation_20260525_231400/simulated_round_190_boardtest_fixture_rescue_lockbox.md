## Lead Candidate: BoardTest Fixture Rescue Lockbox

### Why It Advances

- It is not a continuity support book. The asset is a specific titled/custodied production fixture package: ICT bed-of-nails fixture, functional-test jig, adapters, harnesses, fixture CAD/BOM where available, test scripts, golden unit/control board, prior pass/fail logs, and handoff notes.
- The buyer has current cash at risk: a board repeat build, production transfer, warranty/service run, customer shipment, or new EMS onboarding cannot proceed cheaply without either the old fixture or a slow rebuild.
- First proof is not an expression of interest. It is seller-side fixture custody/option plus OEM/new-EMS buyer deposit and at least one accepted test run or handoff.
- It reduces the OrphanMold weakness by avoiding production tooling ownership ambiguity where the tool made parts for years. The buyer is usually the electronics OEM or its authorized new EMS, and the case is rejected unless ownership/authorization is written.
- It reduces the CMM continuity weakness by avoiding ongoing measurement-liability support. The startup transfers a controlled asset package; test engineers and EMS teams remain responsible for production validation.

### Internal Objections

- Many fixtures are already owned by the OEM or contractually returnable, making a paid intermediary unnecessary.
- Ownership, IP, firmware images, licenses, and customer data may be messy.
- Test fixture validity can be safety- or quality-critical; the startup must not certify that a board is good.
- The old EMS/test engineer may not have clean custody or may refuse release.
- Recreating a fixture may sometimes be cheaper than rescue.
- Case supply may be irregular; the founder needs liquidators, EMS exits, retiring test engineers, and OEM transfer pain channels.
