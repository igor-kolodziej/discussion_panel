## Search Frame

Round 253 failed because the cash claim was real but episodic, partly automated by FIFA, and likely to drift into sports-law disputes. This round tests a current official portal deadline with batchable data operations, but avoids clinical/patient/Rx scope by routing through authorised representatives, PRRC/regulatory consultants, and manufacturers who retain all regulatory responsibility.

Source checks used:

- The European Commission EUDAMED page says EUDAMED modules include actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies/certificates, investigations/studies, vigilance/PMS, and market surveillance.
- European Commission/EUDAMED materials and competent-authority notices state that the first four modules become mandatory from 28 May 2026.
- The Commission Q&A says UDI/Device module obligations apply around placing devices/systems/procedure packs on the market, with transition rules for devices placed before mandatory use and continuing after.
- Competent-authority notices state that manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, and system/procedure-pack producers must register in the Actor module.

## Lead Candidate

**EUDAMED UDI Batch Release Desk**

### Internal Simulated Score

88.1 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The deadline is current: mandatory use began 2026-05-28, two days before this round.
- The work is batchable and data-heavy, fitting a Warsaw solo founder with contractors.
- Trust is borrowed from authorised representatives, PRRC/regulatory consultants, and manufacturers; the startup is not the regulatory decision-maker.
- The proof artifact is concrete: actor/SRN status, Basic UDI-DI/UDI-DI data, EUDAMED draft/submission log, AR/regulatory review, and PO/shipment/new-SKU progress.
- The founder can reject cases involving classification, conformity assessment, clinical evidence, PMS/vigilance, safety judgment, or legal interpretation.
- The budget fits: secure intake, data cleanup, AR review, contractor data ops, and batch delivery do not require inventory or software build.

### Internal Objections

- Regulatory affairs consultants, authorised representatives, and QMS software vendors are strong incumbents.
- The founder must avoid claiming to classify, certify, validate, or approve medical devices.
- Many cases will lack source truth: Basic UDI-DI logic, certificates, DoC, manufacturer data, risk class, legacy/regulation status, issuing-agency identifiers, importer/AR relationships, or intended-purpose data.
- Upload/data acceptance is not the same as legal compliance.
- The window may compress after ARs and manufacturers standardize EUDAMED workflows.
- Handling medical-device technical files creates confidentiality and liability risk even without patient data.
- Direct-to-manufacturer CAC is weak; AR/regulatory channel batches are mandatory.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the candidate has current official timing plus a concrete portal/submission/payment-progress proof path.
