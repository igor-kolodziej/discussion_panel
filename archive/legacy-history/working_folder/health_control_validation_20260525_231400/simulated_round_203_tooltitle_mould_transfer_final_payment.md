## Selected Candidate

**ToolTitle Supplier-Held Mould Transfer & Final-Payment Release Desk**

### Why This Advanced

This is not a tooling marketplace or orphan mold resale. The buyer already paid for dedicated tooling and needs a binary commercial event: supplier possession/title acknowledgement, release authorization, new producer acceptance, or final payment release. The founder controls the file, not the mold itself, and rejects disputed/lien/insolvency cases.

### Control Point

- Signed mandate over named tool/mold/die and supplier.
- Purchase order, tooling invoice, payment record, ownership clause, and supplier correspondence.
- Photos, serials, cavity count, maintenance/shot-count notes, storage location, and condition declaration.
- Supplier possession and release/no-lien acknowledgement.
- New producer acceptance checklist where moving production.
- Final payment, pickup, transfer, or no-go tracker.

### 60-Day Proof

- 5 signed mandates.
- 8 named tool files reconstructed.
- 5 supplier acknowledgements, release letters, new-producer acceptances, final-payment releases, or clean no-go outcomes.
- 500,000 PLN+ production/final-payment value affected.
- 50,000 PLN+ collected in fees.

### Economics

- 8,000-18,000 PLN for title/release reconstruction.
- 18,000-45,000 PLN for urgent transfer/new-producer acceptance file.
- Optional 3-8% success fee on final payment released or avoided duplicate tooling cost, only where attribution is contractually defined.
- High gross margin if cases are screened for clean ownership, cooperative buyer, no active lien, no insolvency, and no quality dispute.

### Internal Caps Applied

- Capped below 93 because many real cases turn into lien, ownership, quality, supplier-payment, or legal disputes.
- Capped below 90 because procurement/engineering/lawyers can handle clean cases if focused.
- Not capped below 87 because the artifact is stronger than a receivable: title trail plus supplier possession/release acknowledgement tied to production transfer or final payment.
