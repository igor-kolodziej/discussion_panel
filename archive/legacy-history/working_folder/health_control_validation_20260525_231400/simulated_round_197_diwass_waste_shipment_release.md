## Search Frame

Round 196 failed because even clean construction cash release did not control the beneficiary, contract wording, or guarantee underwriter. Round 197 returns to current commercial movement: a named shipment cannot move or be invoiced unless a digital regulatory workflow is accepted.

Official/current timing:

- The European Commission states that most provisions of the new Waste Shipments Regulation apply from 21 May 2026.
- The Commission also announced that the revised Waste Shipment Regulation and DIWASS went live on 21 May 2026.
- The regulation governs transboundary waste shipments and replaces the old Regulation (EC) No 1013/2006 framework for most provisions.

This is not a broad environmental-compliance service. The candidate only accepts live shipment-release cases where a named waste load, pickup, customer delivery, recycling/recovery slot, sale invoice, or transport payment is blocked by DIWASS notification/consignment, Annex VII, prior-consent, routing, financial-guarantee, contract, or authority workflow after the 21 May 2026 go-live.

## Why It Clears Simulation

- Timing is current: DIWASS and most WSR provisions went live on 21 May 2026, so the first buyer is dealing with live operational change rather than future readiness.
- The first proof can be shipment movement: DIWASS accepted submission, consignment/Annex VII file accepted by route parties, authority response, carrier pickup, recovery-facility acceptance, invoice release, or clean no-go.
- The buyer has current cash or inventory movement at stake: truck slots, customer deliveries, recycler feedstock, sale invoices, and recovery-facility capacity.
- The startup does not decide waste classification, legal status, hazardous handling, consent law, or environmental obligations. Qualified waste consultants, licensed waste brokers/dealers, lawyers, carriers, recovery facilities, and responsible exporters/importers remain provider of record.
- The wedge can start with lower-risk repeated green-list paper/plastic/metal/recycled-feedstock lanes where the customer already has waste codes, route parties, contracts, and recurring shipment history.

## Internal Cap Check

- Cap below 82 if cases require the founder to decide waste code, hazardous status, consent requirement, Basel classification, legality of export, or treatment standard.
- Cap below 85 if the buyer lacks a current blocked shipment, pickup, customer delivery, sale invoice, or route-party deadline.
- Cap below 87 if qualified waste-adviser and broker/dealer channels cannot be secured before case intake.
- Cap below 87 if proof is only a readiness memo rather than accepted digital submission, route-party acceptance, authority response, pickup, invoice release, or clean no-go.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

Zero To One may view the workflow as regulated waste-compliance coordination owned by waste brokers, environmental consultants, recovery facilities, carriers, and DIWASS software vendors. The candidate only deserves a high score if the live shipment/cash trigger and narrow low-risk recurring-lane filter overcome that incumbent trust problem.
