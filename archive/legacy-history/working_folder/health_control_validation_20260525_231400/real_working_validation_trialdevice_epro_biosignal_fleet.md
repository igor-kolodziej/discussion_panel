- Biosignal devices add validation, pairing, battery, export, firmware, and data-quality support complexity.

- Two paid pilots and `30,000 PLN` deposits in 60 days are hard without warm access.
- Support load can destroy gross margin.
- The therapeutic focus was too broad; a real version would need one narrow device family.
