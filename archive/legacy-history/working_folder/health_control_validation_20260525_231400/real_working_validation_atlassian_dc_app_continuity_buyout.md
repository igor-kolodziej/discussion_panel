## Strongest Objections

1. The best app assets may not be cheap enough. Profitable, low-maintenance apps will command a real multiple or keep harvesting; cheap assets may hide technical debt, low renewals, security issues, angry customers, or a winning cloud replacement.
2. Enterprise customers may not prepay an unknown new owner. They may prefer to freeze and replace rather than onboard a new tiny vendor through procurement, DPA, security, and legal review.
3. The 60-day proof target is aggressive because enterprise procurement, security review, legal review, and vendor onboarding can exceed the timeline.
4. Atlassian transfer is possible but not frictionless. The official path still depends on partner-account details, proof of sale, acquired-admin approval, documentation quality, and Marketplace support processing.
5. Security obligations can destroy margins. Old Data Center apps may have dependency and vulnerability debt, and security bug-fix timelines can force urgent remediation.
6. Every new release creates Marketplace scan/release risk, especially for an old inherited codebase.
7. Continuity support can become Jira administration, migration consulting, customer-specific patches, or incident response.
8. The finite Data Center horizon caps valuation; the asset base declines toward 2029 unless the company becomes a repeatable acquisition machine or migration-adjacent business.
9. Seller sourcing is the real moat, and the founder does not yet have it. Atlassian Solution Partners, consolidators, and larger app vendors can outbid and look safer.
10. The exact-app control point is strong but narrow; it matters only where customers still depend on the app and cannot replace it cheaply.

## Evaluator's Narrow Version

Only target Jira/Confluence Data Center apps that are technically boring, security-light, externally low-risk, already paid by enterprise customers, and important enough to merit continuity.

Avoid apps involving secrets, identity, privileged automation, external egress, unsafe rendering, complex data migration, regulated workflows, or critical incident expectations.

Minimum proof before meaningful spend:

- one seller option or management handoff with admin-approved transfer path;
- at least three existing customers confirming named dependency and budget owner;
- at least 80,000 PLN committed through PO, paid pilot, current-vendor-routed renewal, or conditional order;
- independent contractor review confirming maintainability without major rewrite;
- no critical/high security remediation backlog that exceeds budget.
