## Candidate Advanced To Real Validation

Idea name: MDF Claim Lockbox for Cybersecurity VARs and MSPs

One-sentence thesis: recover expiring, pre-approved vendor MDF/co-op funds for cybersecurity VARs and MSPs by taking portal-authorized claim control over completed campaigns and turning proof-of-performance into vendor approvals, credit memos, or cash reimbursements.

Exact buyer: 10-150 person cybersecurity VARs, MSPs, MSSPs, and cloud/security resellers with partner status at vendors such as endpoint, firewall, identity, backup, MDR, email-security, cloud-security, or SIEM vendors, where allocated MDF/co-op funds or approved campaign budgets expire unused or get rejected for proof gaps.

Acute trigger: A vendor MDF/co-op allocation, webinar/event/campaign reimbursement, not-for-resale/demo subsidy, or approved market-development budget is expiring in the current quarter, has been rejected for missing proof, or is stuck in a partner portal while the reseller has already paid for the eligible activity.

Control point: Signed claim mandate plus partner-portal user role or screen-share filing authority, vendor/program allocation proof, campaign approval, proof-of-performance bundle, invoice/payment proof, attendee/lead/export evidence where allowed, submitted claim, vendor acknowledgement, and resulting approval, credit memo, payment, or clean no-go.

60-day proof:

- Five signed partner mandates.
- At least 250,000 PLN equivalent in identified allocated or rejected MDF/co-op claims.
- Three complete claims filed through vendor portals or vendor channel contacts.
- One vendor acknowledgement, approval, credit memo, or payment.
- 25,000-75,000 PLN in success/setup fees collected or invoiced after accepted claims.

6-month POC:

- 25-50 partner accounts screened.
- 1,500,000-4,000,000 PLN equivalent in allocated/rejected/near-expiry funds reviewed.
- 40-90 claims filed.
- 15-35 approvals, credit memos, or payments.
- 250,000-700,000 PLN revenue from setup fees and success fees.
- Gross margin above 65% after claim ops, partner portal QA, and subcontractor review.

First acquisition mechanism: Target cybersecurity VARs/MSPs/MSSPs listed in public vendor partner directories and conference exhibitor lists. Use a concrete offer: "If your current-quarter MDF/co-op allocation is expiring, rejected, or sitting unclaimed after a webinar, lunch-and-learn, trade show, demo day, or demand-gen campaign, we file the portal claim under your authorization and get paid only from accepted value plus a small screening fee."

Economics: Charge 1,500-4,000 PLN screening/setup per partner account plus 18-30% of vendor-approved cash, credit, or reimbursement value. Minimum claim value 15,000 PLN, preferred 40,000+ PLN. The buyer pays because the spend is already incurred, the vendor money is time-limited, and internal sales/marketing ops often treat the claim as annoying admin.

Copy risk: Vendor channel managers, distributors, marketing agencies, accountants, partner operations consultants, and the VAR/MSP's own staff can copy. The defense is control of current claim authority, vendor-specific proof checklists, rejection/approval memory by program, and quarter-end urgency before funds expire.

Why this is not a service/report/app/dashboard/database/marketplace/broker: The unit is a current vendor claim filed under partner authorization against a named allocation or rejected reimbursement. It is not a dashboard, audit report, marketplace, introduction, marketing agency, or generic channel consulting. If there is no named vendor allocation/rejection, proof bundle, filing authority, and monetary outcome, there is no case.

Boundaries:

- No fabrication, backdating, fake attendees, fake invoices, or false proof-of-performance.
- No legal, tax, accounting, vendor-contract, or channel-program interpretation beyond operational claim assembly and routing.
- Vendor, partner, and their advisors remain responsible for eligibility, tax/accounting treatment, program compliance, marketing claims, and final submissions.
- Reject claims involving sanctions-sensitive customers, public-sector procurement restrictions, bribery/anti-corruption risk, personal-data exports outside allowed portal evidence, channel conflict, or suspicious proof.

Duplicate risk:

- Not PromoLeak because it does not audit retailer trade promotion deductions or broad promo leakage.
- Not Amazon Vendor Central recovery, Retail OTIF, PartnerCenter incentive recovery, 3PL overbilling, or DRS settlement; this controls allocated vendor MDF/co-op campaign reimbursement in cybersecurity channel partner portals.
- Not an evidence desk because the output is vendor acknowledgement, approval, credit memo, or payment.

- Recovered/approved value per hour is too low after portal and proof work.
- Partners bypass after seeing the checklist and do not pay setup or success fees.
- Fraud pressure appears in more than isolated cases.
