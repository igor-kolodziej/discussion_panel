Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
MoCRABlocked Amazon Cosmetics Listing Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
Build a white-label live-case desk for Amazon/account-health and marketplace-compliance agencies serving EU/CEE cosmetics sellers whose US Amazon listings, FBA inventory movement, retailer onboarding, 3PL/importer handoff, or wholesale POs are blocked by FDA MoCRA-related facility-registration, product-listing, responsible-person, label, ingredient, or adverse-event evidence requests.

Exact buyer:
The first buyer is an Amazon account-health, reinstatement, marketplace-compliance, or cross-border e-commerce agency that already serves EU/CEE cosmetics sellers and does not want to build a specialist FDA MoCRA response workflow in-house. The economic buyer behind the agency is a private-label skincare, beauty, haircare, fragrance, soap, or cosmetics brand with US Amazon revenue, US retail onboarding, FBA inventory, distributor orders, or wholesale POs blocked by a named MoCRA/FDA evidence request. The first cases exclude drug, SPF, acne-treatment, anti-dandruff, medical-device, ingestible supplement, prescription, controlled-substance, and clinical-claim products.

Acute trigger:
A named ASIN, SKU, product family, retail onboarding item, FBA shipment, 3PL/importer handoff, or wholesale PO is blocked because Amazon, a retailer, a distributor, an importer, a 3PL, or an agency asks for FDA MoCRA facility registration, cosmetic product listing, responsible-person/contact information, manufacturer/filler/facility status, label or ingredient evidence, adverse-event process, safety substantiation pointer, or proof that the seller can truthfully map the exact product to its FDA/MoCRA evidence. The seller is not buying broad regulatory readiness; it is paying because US listing revenue, inventory movement, or a buyer order is stuck now.

Control point:
A signed seller or agency mandate over the exact ASIN/SKU case; agency resale or referral terms; seller authorization; Amazon/retailer/importer/3PL request text; case ID or portal screenshot; exact ASIN/SKU/EAN/UPC list; label artwork and live product-page claims; ingredient and INCI files; manufacturer, filler, packer, distributor, and facility information; FDA Cosmetics Direct account or submission-status evidence supplied by the responsible party; facility-registration/product-listing confirmation where already available; responsible-person/contact details supplied by the seller or qualified provider; adverse-event contact/process evidence supplied by the seller; source-document inconsistency log; draft response wording; and a submit/cannot-submit/escalate decision. The startup controls the live case file, response packet, agency channel, and fee right for that blocker. It does not control FDA legal status, Amazon acceptance, product safety, label legality, or cosmetic/drug classification.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign three Amazon/account-health or marketplace-compliance agencies on referral or resale terms; close six paid live ASIN/SKU cases under written seller authorization; collect at least 30,000 PLN in triage, case, or multi-SKU sprint fees; produce at least three concrete outcomes such as FDA submission confirmation supplied by the responsible party, corrected facility/product-listing evidence, Amazon or retailer progress, FBA/listing progress, retail onboarding progress, 3PL/importer acceptance of the evidence path, or a paid clean no-go escalation. No case counts without a live written blocker, named product identifiers, seller authorization, available source truth, and a cash-linked listing/order/inventory consequence.

6-month POC:
Process 30-50 paid live ASIN/SKU cases through 5-8 agency channels; collect 180,000-450,000 PLN in case fees and multi-SKU sprint fees; document 20+ Amazon, retailer, importer, 3PL, or clean no-go outcomes; build a private FDA/MoCRA-to-Amazon request taxonomy by product type, request wording, missing source truth, facility/product-listing status, label/claim issue, supplier gap, reviewer escalation, and accepted response wording; keep gross margin above 60%; keep average evidence-ready case turnaround under five business days; reject at least 25% of risky cases rather than stretching into legal, FDA, drug/cosmetic, label, or safety determinations; and continue only if at least two agencies each send five or more paid cases.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with Amazon account-health, reinstatement, and marketplace-compliance agencies serving EU/CEE beauty and private-label sellers. The pitch is: "Your next US cosmetics ASIN blocked by MoCRA/FDA evidence comes to us. We interpret the request, map the exact SKU to source evidence, assemble the response packet, flag cannot-submit items, and route legal/regulatory/safety issues to qualified advisers. You keep the client relationship and margin." Build a list of 50 agencies and cross-border e-commerce operators; ask each for one live or recent cosmetics MoCRA/FDA evidence blocker; offer a paid first-case triage, not a free compliance audit.

Economics:
Charge 2,000-3,500 PLN for triage and a submit/cannot-submit/escalate decision, 6,000-12,000 PLN for one ASIN/SKU release dossier, and 15,000-40,000 PLN for a multi-SKU brand or retail-onboarding sprint. Agencies keep a 25-35% resale or referral margin. The seller pays because a blocked US cosmetics listing, FBA shipment, retailer onboarding, or wholesale PO can be worth far more than the case fee. Costs are secure document handling, founder case assembly, contractor admin, and paid specialist review only for edge cases. The founder does not spend the 100,000 PLN budget on inventory, advances, paid media, or speculative software.

Copy risk:
FDA/MoCRA consultants, US responsible-person providers, cosmetics regulatory advisers, product-safety consultants, labeling consultants, Amazon agencies, and cross-border e-commerce consultants can copy pieces. The defense is not generic MoCRA knowledge. It is live agency case flow, exact ASIN/SKU request memory, FDA Cosmetics Direct/product-listing artifact patterns, Amazon/retailer response wording, CEE seller/supplier communication, reviewer escalation boundaries, and a case database of what can be submitted, what cannot be submitted, and what produces listing or buyer progress.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once an agency or seller signs a paid case mandate for a named ASIN/SKU blocker, provides the request text and source files, and receives the case packet or escalation path, the startup controls that live fee right, case memory, response draft, and agency relationship for that blocker. A MoCRA consultant or Amazon agency can compete for future cases, but cannot instantly take over the already-authorized case file, submitted evidence map, agency resale agreement, portal case ID, and paid work product without the seller or agency switching mid-case.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell broad MoCRA readiness, FDA legal advice, cosmetic safety substantiation, label approval, a compliance dashboard, a product database, a seller marketplace, or introductions to consultants. It accepts only named live ASIN/SKU, retailer, importer, 3PL, or PO blockers with written requests and source evidence. The deliverable is a bounded release packet: request interpretation, SKU-to-source evidence map, FDA/MoCRA artifact list, inconsistency log, supplier questions, draft platform/retailer response, and submit/cannot-submit/escalate decision. Proof is Amazon, retailer, FBA, importer, 3PL, or clean no-go progress on the named commercial blocker.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not act as FDA counsel, US responsible person, FDA agent, manufacturer, distributor of record, importer of record, cosmetics safety assessor, toxicologist, laboratory, legal adviser, tax adviser, drug-labeling adviser, or Amazon appeal guarantor. Qualified regulatory, legal, label, toxicology, or responsible-person providers handle issues requiring professional judgment. The seller remains responsible for product truth, facility identity, product listing, adverse-event records, labels, claims, safety substantiation, FDA submissions, Amazon account actions, and US law compliance. Reject products with drug/SPF/acne/anti-dandruff/medical/ingestible claims, missing ingredient truth, fake facility data, unsafe claims, pressure to alter records, unclear responsible party, or requests to certify legality or safety.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, CBAM Import Continuity Desk, Data Act Machine Data Liberation Desk, GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, KaucjaSet, KaucjaMark, ButtonCellBlocked, EPRBlocked, HazmatBlocked FBA, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, 3PL recovery, OTA recovery, KSeF payment release, AIQ, RAGProbe, or AppExchange retest. The buyer trigger, control object, and proof artifact are materially different: live US cosmetics MoCRA/FDA evidence blockers for named ASINs/SKUs and marketplace/retailer release packets, not connected-device RED evidence, generic product safety, EPR registration, retailer deductions, invoice acceptance, accessibility, AI/appsec, or buyer diligence.

Strongest anticipated objections:
FDA/MoCRA consultants and Amazon agencies may already solve easy cases. Hard cases may require truthful facility and product data, qualified US responsible-person support, regulatory/legal review, label correction, safety substantiation, or claim cleanup outside founder control. Amazon, retailers, importers, and 3PLs may reject the packet even if the source evidence is organized. Cosmetics claims can drift into drug, SPF, acne, anti-dandruff, medical, wellness, safety, allergen, state-law, and labeling issues. Seller files may be fake, incomplete, mismatched, outdated, or impossible to reconcile. Case values may be too small unless the seller has meaningful US revenue or multi-SKU retail onboarding. Agencies may internalize the workflow after seeing the checklist. The MoCRA transition pain may standardize as sellers and cosmetics consultants learn the process. The founder needs a credible reviewer/provider bench and strict rejection rules from day one.
