# Driver OSA Fast Lane Research Notes

Date: 2026-05-25

## Regulatory Basis

- EU Commission Directive 2014/85/EU amended driving-licence medical fitness rules to include obstructive sleep apnoea syndrome. It states that suspected moderate or severe OSA cases should be referred for further authorised medical advice before a licence is issued or renewed, and that treated moderate/severe cases should have periodic medical review.
  - Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32014L0085

- The current Polish driver medical-exam regulation is the Minister of Health Regulation of 5 December 2022 on medical examinations of applicants for driving authorisations and drivers.
  - It defines the scope of driver medical exams as including obstructive sleep apnea.
  - It allows an authorised doctor to order specialist consultation or auxiliary diagnostic tests.
  - Annex 7 defines moderate and severe obstructive sleep apnea using AHI and daytime sleepiness.
  - When moderate/severe OSA is suspected in listed driver groups, including professional-road-transport categories, the person is referred for specialist medical examination; until confirmation or exclusion, contraindications to driving may be stated.
  - Treated moderate/severe OSA drivers can be assessed as having no contraindications if they undergo annual control medical examinations to check compliance and continuing need for treatment.
  - Source: https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/2503/text.pdf

## Medical Workflow Basis

- Polish occupational-medicine / pulmonary / sleep societies published driver-OSA management recommendations. They identify OSA as an important road-accident risk factor and propose an algorithm using the driver health declaration, BMI, EuroSAS, Epworth Sleepiness Scale, poligraphy/polysomnography, and pulmonary/sleep-center consultation.
  - Source: https://ppm.edu.pl/seam/resource/rest/download/IMP3f629159d1e3471f9ffd96d2c857c12d?cid=73108&entityId=IMPa70b3e2cf0204add8fd7c2a29faeec27&entityType=article

## Existing Provider Evidence / Competition

- Home sleep testing is already available in Poland, so the idea cannot be a generic consumer HSAT reseller.
  - DrSen offers home sleep testing with physician interpretation and treatment planning: https://drsen.pl/
  - WatchPAT Poland advertises convenient home OSA testing and cooperation in therapy/care: https://watchpat.pl/
  - ENEL-MED offers home poligraphy for OSA diagnosis: https://leczeniebezdechu.enel.pl/

- Driver-medical exam providers already discuss OSA screening, including the two-stage flow of risk assessment by an authorised doctor followed by specialist sleep diagnostics if needed.
  - Example source: https://www.badania-kierowcow.warszawa.pl/tag/lekarz-medycyny-pracy-badania-kierowcow/

- Institutional Warsaw sleep centers offer PSG and driver-relevant OSA workups.
  - WIML states that Poland had to implement the EU directive requiring professional-driver sleep breathing disorder assessment and offers risk consultation, PSG, sleepiness testing, pulmonary consultation, CPAP qualification, and related services: https://www.wiml.waw.pl/pl/badania-polisomnograficzne

## Implications For Scoring

The opportunity is real only if it is framed as an occupational/fleet medical-decision bottleneck, not wellness, longevity, or direct-to-consumer testing.

Required control proof:

- signed occupational-medicine clinic or fleet routing agreement for suspected professional-driver OSA cases;
- owned/leased HSAT kit logistics and device availability;
- contracted sleep physician/pulmonologist interpretation slots with 48-72h SLA;
- partner clinic/authorised doctor remains responsible for medical certificate and driving-fitness decision;
- first paid routed tests and at least one accepted report in the medical decision workflow.

Fatal risks:

- occupational-medicine clinics and sleep centers already own clinical authority and trust;
- positive OSA cases may need treatment/CPAP compliance proof, not just fast diagnosis;
- fleets and drivers may resist discovery because a positive finding can remove a driver from work;
- generic HSAT supply is copyable unless the routed clinic/fleet flow and physician slots are signed.
