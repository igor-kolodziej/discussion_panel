## Search Frame

Round 157 failed because tiled stoves are one-off focal objects with heavy logistics and safety dependency. This round keeps the physical-suite control pattern but moves to a less safety-bound, more commercially reusable asset: complete apothecary/pharmacy/bookshop/shopfit cabinet interiors from one property or business, controlled before sellers split them into individual cabinets or antique dealers cherry-pick pieces.

The hypothesis is that complete walls of drawers, glass-front cabinets, counters, ladders, labels, and shelving have broader premium demand than tiled stoves: boutique hotels, restaurants, cocktail bars, cafes, wellness studios, luxury retail, offices, libraries, and HNW homes can use them as functional decorative interiors without chimney/fire-safety dependencies.

Source notes checked before simulation:

- Polish listings show antique pharmacy/apothecary furniture and full furniture sets appear in public classifieds.
- Polish news has covered historic pharmacy interiors and furniture as protected/heritage-worthy objects, showing cultural value but also conservation/title risk.
- European antique marketplaces show apothecary cabinets sold as premium decorative/functional furniture.
- Reproduction and newly built cabinets exist, which requires the wedge to focus on complete original fitted suites with provenance and buyer deposits.

- Custom reproduction and ordinary antique cabinets are substitutes.
- Large suites require transport/storage, condition review, and buyer-specific fit.
