## Lead Candidate

**Issued White-Certificate Cashout Options**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It starts after the difficult technical/regulatory creation step: the right has already been issued or registered.
- The controlled unit is a specific property right or sale mandate, not an audit report or consulting project.
- First proof is cash settlement from sale of the rights, not buyer interest.
- The founder can stay out of licensed trading by using a broker/TGE member while controlling the signed option/mandate and seller relationship.
- The narrow beachhead is orphaned or under-monetized certificates from hotels, factories, housing cooperatives, public-lighting retrofits, ESCO projects, and facility managers where the original energy auditor/ESCO did not complete monetization.

### Internal Objections

- ESCOs, energy auditors, energy brokers, and TGE members are natural incumbents.
- Legal structure must avoid unlicensed brokerage or investment advice.
- Right validity, registry status, title, expiry, liens, prior pledges, and sale mechanics must be verified.
- Deal sizes may be too small unless targeting larger issued rights.
- Sellers may not be aware of the asset, which helps acquisition but increases education burden.
- Energy-certificate prices and liquidity can move, so spreads must be conservative.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point is a tradable issued right with cash settlement, not another service/report/dashboard/marketplace.
