## Lead Candidate

**FacadePatch Matched Cladding Repair Lots**

### Internal Simulated Score

88.4 / 100

### Why It Advances

- It accepts only active repair, insurance, tenant-opening, warranty, or facade-maintenance cases where mismatch is expensive.
- The founder does not buy random facade salvage. Final purchase requires buyer deposit, conditional PO, or installer/property-manager written interest for a named lot.
- The controlled unit is an exact batch/profile/color lot: terracotta/rainscreen tiles, stone cladding, ceramic facade tile, brick slips, special facade brick, spandrel/panel inserts, or original spare crates from a completed project.
- Logistics are bounded compared with hotel FF&E: palletized crates and panels, not whole-room furniture, and buyer/installer handles final installation.
- The buyer is paying for avoided full-elevation replacement, tenant delay, insurance dispute, visible mismatch, or long OEM lead time, not decorative salvage browsing.

### Control Point

- Seller option or title over exact lot with location, quantity, dimensions, profile, finish/color, batch/code markings where available, condition photos, and pickup terms.
- Buyer-side deposit, conditional PO, or written reservation from property manager, facade contractor, insurer-side repair coordinator, boutique developer, or building owner.
- Installation remains with buyer-selected facade contractor/glazier/stone mason; startup does not certify structural, fire, weatherproofing, anchoring, or code compliance.
- Handoff file includes measured lot card, photos, condition notes, source/seller chain, pickup terms, and explicit no-certification boundary.

### 60-Day Proof

1. Three seller options over exact matched facade/cladding repair lots.
2. Three buyer deposits or conditional POs for named active repair cases before final lot purchase.
3. One paid handoff of a lot to a buyer-selected facade contractor, property manager, or building owner.
4. At least 50,000 PLN of buyer deposits/conditional orders.
5. Lot documentation for every controlled lot: quantity, dimensions, profile, color/finish, batch/markings, condition, photos, storage/pickup terms, source chain, and installation boundary.
6. No speculative purchase above 5,000 PLN without buyer deposit.

### 6-Month POC

- Control 12-20 matched repair lots.
- Secure 10 buyer deposits or conditional POs.
- Complete 5 paid handoffs.
- Collect 150,000-450,000 PLN revenue with gross margin above 45% before founder salary.
- Build a private map of demolition/facade contractor/stone yard/overstock sources and buyer demand by building type, facade system, material, dimensions, color/finish, and urgency.
- Kill if fewer than 25% of controlled lots receive buyer deposits within 45 days or if installation/warranty/code objections dominate.

### Economics

Option small lots for 500-3,000 PLN, buy only after buyer deposit or conditional PO. Typical acquisition plus pickup/staging cost: 2,000-20,000 PLN. Resale or reservation price: 8,000-60,000 PLN depending on material, scarcity, quantity, active repair value, and avoided mismatch/full-elevation cost. Use 30-60% gross margin targets. Buyer deposit funds pickup and prevents the founder from becoming a warehouse.

### Copy Risk

Facade contractors, property managers, demolition firms, stone yards, tile distributors, glaziers, and salvage dealers can copy the general idea. They cannot copy a specific optioned lot, buyer reservation, pickup window, source-chain file, and deposit once controlled. The longer-term advantage is source timing and exact-match memory: which buildings/materials are being stripped, which lots have batch markings, and which property managers or contractors pay for visible continuity.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Database, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup sells controlled title/options to exact repair lots with buyer deposits and paid handoffs. It does not provide facade design, engineering, installation, certification, insurance adjustment, renovation management, a searchable marketplace, or generic introductions.

### Duplicate Risk

- Not HeritageDoor: this is commercial/premium building exterior repair supply for active facade/cladding repair, not matched pre-war internal door suites for interior heritage renovations.
- Not hotel FF&E: palletized exact repair lots with buyer deposits, not bulky generic furniture liquidation.
- Not StormTile roof lots: the trigger is facade/elevation mismatch and active property-manager/facade-contractor repair, not generic roof-tile salvage.
- Not GreenTender, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, or health/Rx/clinical routes.
