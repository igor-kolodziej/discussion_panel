## Strongest Objections Captured

1. **The official process already contains batching.** PSE FAQ/process mechanics allow multiple applications in one file, including for multiple generators when the required codes are included. This makes the startup an execution layer around a public mechanism, not the creator of the batching advantage.
2. **O&M firms and asset managers are closer to the data.** The hard parts are mandates, SCADA/meter exports, installation details, OSD evidence, PB/MWE codes, and deficiency response history.
3. **Unknown founder trust is weak.** The claimant must share claim data, access paths, bank/tax details, SPV information, and possibly WOZE/PPB workflows.
4. **Success fees may be resisted.** Owners may see compensation as an administrative entitlement and accept fixed fees but reject 6-12% of compensation.
5. **Revenue proof mixed cash with receivables.** The validator rejected "documented success-fee receivables" as too soft. POC cash should separate prepaid fees from actually received success fees.
6. **Legal and technical boundaries are harder than written.** Eligibility, support-system treatment, negative prices, missing data, ownership, and calculation disputes can quickly become legal/tax/technical advice.
7. **The market may be temporary or lumpy.** The opportunity depends on curtailment frequency, PSE process friction, and owners not internalizing the workflow.
8. **Durability is weak.** Mandates, workpapers, WOZE memory, and reviewer checklists help execution but do not stop O&M firms, RES accountants, or energy advisers from copying.

## Lesson

Public official workflows with visible forms, portal access, FAQs, and clear incumbent data holders are not enough. Even current cash and official compensation rights fail if the core advantage is operational execution around a public process that O&M/accounting/asset-management incumbents can learn and absorb.

Next round should require a stronger founder-control object than public forms and submitted applications: direct payment direction, assigned/mandated cash where incumbents are not naturally next to the data, or a scarce titled asset/payment stream that cannot be internalized after observing the workflow.
