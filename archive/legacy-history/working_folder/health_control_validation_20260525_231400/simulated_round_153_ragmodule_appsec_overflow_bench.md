## Search Frame

The control object is a signed overflow module agreement with credible partners plus current client engagements, partner report integration, and prepaid AI-module tickets. This uses incumbent trust while keeping the scope narrower than a formal pentest or broad red team.

## Lead Candidate

**RAGModule AppSec Overflow Bench**

### Internal Simulated Score

88 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It addresses the biggest Round 152 objection by turning AI security/appsec incumbents into buyers and channels.
- It does not promise formal certification; it is a bounded report annex or procurement evidence module inside the partner's engagement.
- Partner credibility reduces buyer trust friction and allows work inside partner-controlled workspaces.
- The artifact remains harder than a questionnaire: test plan, traces, source-isolation checks, tool-permission tests, logs, rework/no-go table, buyer-safe annex.
- The proof is concrete: signed module agreements, paid current partner engagements, annex accepted into partner reports, and repeat partner tickets.

### Internal Objections

- Appsec partners can internalize after a few modules.
- Wholesale margins may compress.
- If buyers demand formal pentest/red-team certification, the module is insufficient.
- The startup must avoid being a hidden unqualified subcontractor; partner contracts and reviewer credentials matter.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and this is the strongest hard-control variant of the near-passing Round 152 idea.
