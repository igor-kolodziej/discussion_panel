## Current Source Notes

Official European Commission ICS2 materials show current operational pressure:

- ICS2 is the EU's advance cargo information system for goods entering or transiting the EU, Northern Ireland, Norway, and Switzerland: `https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs-4/customs-security/import-control-system-2-ics2-0_en`
- The Commission states that accurate and complete Entry Summary Declaration data is required prior to arrival and that ICS2 Release 3 extends requirements to all transport modes, including road and rail: `https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/eu-import-control-system-2-ics2-extends-rail-and-road-transportation-april-2025-2025-02-03_en`
- Commission guidance for freight forwarders says house-level data filing is critical and that forwarders must obtain required data from parties issuing house transport documents or identify parties still required to file.
- Commission reminders stress that accurate and complete ENS data at master and house levels is needed to avoid delays and penalties.

This round avoids CBAM/EUDR duplication by focusing on ICS2 pre-arrival safety/security data quality, house-level ENS exception packets, and current shipment delays rather than emissions, deforestation, or customs-duty classification advisory.

## Search Implication

The lead candidate is deliberately broker/forwarder-first:

- partner already holds shipment documents;
- current ENS exception or missing data blocks a shipment milestone;
- founder does not act as declarant/filer/customs adviser;
- output is a data-completion and exception packet the partner can use in its filing workflow;
- no broad customs consulting, SaaS dashboard, tariff classification opinion, or legal representation.
