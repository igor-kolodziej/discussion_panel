- **Concrete current event:** live enterprise buyer request blocking procurement, renewal, deployment, or go-live.
- **Harder artifact than questionnaire text:** attack traces, source-isolation checks, tenant/source permission checks, logging/human-review screenshots, rework/no-go table, and buyer-safe summary.
- **Risk reduction:** read-only RAG only; tool-agent side effects removed; no formal pentest/certification; no legal AI Act/DPIA/high-risk classification.
- **Channel:** SOC2, appsec, fCISO, and privacy partners already receive buyer questions and can route narrow modules.

### Internal Objections

1. Appsec firms, AI-security firms, pentesters, GRC platforms, and SOC2 consultants can copy or bundle the work.
2. Buyers may still demand formal pentesting, ISO 42001, SOC2, DPIA, legal AI Act classification, or remediation.
3. Vendors may lack real evidence; no packet fixes missing controls.
4. Sensitive prompts, corpora, access-control maps, logs, and customer documents create trust friction.
5. Partners can internalize after a few cases unless delivery uses accepted/rejected case memory and disciplined trace tooling.
6. A short sprint can create false assurance if scope language is careless.
7. It is still an evidence/release product, not current-cash recovery.
