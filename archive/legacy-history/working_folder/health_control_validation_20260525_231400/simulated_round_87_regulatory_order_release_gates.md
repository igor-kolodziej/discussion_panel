## Current Timing And Source Anchors

Official European Commission EUDR implementation pages state:

- EUDR applies to large and medium enterprises from `2026-12-30`, and to most micro and small enterprises from `2027-06-30`; micro/small operators already covered by the EU Timber Regulation enter on `2026-12-30`.
- Upstream operators must carry out due diligence before placing relevant products on the EU market or exporting them.
- Article 9-style information includes quantity, supplier, country of production, geolocation of production plots, and verifiable evidence of deforestation-free and legal production.
- Before relevant imports, the DDS reference number or simplified declaration identifier must be available for the customs declaration.
- Operators must pass DDS/reference identifiers to direct downstream buyers; first downstream operators/traders must collect and keep those identifiers.

## Lead Candidate: EUDR Wood Buyer-Release Evidence Lane

### Buyer

Polish/CEE wood and furniture suppliers with EU B2B buyer relationships:

- furniture makers exporting to German, Dutch, Nordic, Austrian, French, or marketplace/retail buyers;
- timber, plywood, board, flooring, doors, joinery, pallet, and packaging suppliers;
- wood-product traders whose customers ask for EUDR DDS/reference-number traceability;
- boutique/SME exporters too small for enterprise EUDR software but large enough to lose meaningful buyer orders.

### Acute Trigger

A named EU buyer, marketplace, distributor, retailer, contractor, or importer asks the Polish supplier for EUDR evidence before confirming a PO, listing, onboarding, 2027 framework supply, tender, or shipment schedule.

The buyer request is not generic "be ready for EUDR." It asks for one or more current order-release artifacts:

- supplier chain and wood species/product mapping;
- DDS reference number or simplified declaration identifier from upstream sources where applicable;
- proof of role in the supply chain and whether the supplier is upstream/downstream/trader for the product;
- batch/SKU/order mapping to upstream reference numbers;
- country of production and supplier details;
- geolocation or source documentation where the Polish supplier is upstream/operator for imported or produced material;
- recordkeeping file for first downstream buyer obligations;
- statement of what is known, missing, not applicable, or must remain the operator's responsibility.

### Transferable Control Point

Control exists only when the founder has:

- a current written buyer request, tender question, onboarding checklist, PO condition, or supplier-portal evidence request;
- supplier/customer authorization to assemble the evidence pack for that named case;
- access to product/SKU/order/batch/supplier files;
- upstream supplier mandate or email thread requesting DDS/reference identifiers and product/source facts;
- prepaid fixed case fee from the Polish supplier before delivery;
- agreement that the customer/operator remains legally responsible for due diligence and any DDS/simplified declaration submission;
- right to retain anonymized case taxonomy: buyer wording, product type, missing evidence, supplier response, reference-number handoff, and final buyer acceptance or pushback.

The founder does not act as operator, customs declarant, legal adviser, auditor, certifier, deforestation-risk assessor, or EUDR Information System submitter unless separately lawfully appointed and reviewed. The initial wedge avoids submission and focuses on order-release evidence organization.

### Sixty-Day Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

1. Build a target list of 120 Polish furniture, wood-product, timber, board, flooring, doors, pallet, packaging, and joinery exporters with visible EU B2B/retail/marketplace buyers.
2. Build a redacted sample buyer-release pack using official Commission roles/due-diligence fields and dummy supplier data.
3. Contact 80 exporters and 20 export/accounting/customs/quality advisors with a narrow prompt: "Send only live buyer requests, not readiness curiosity."
4. Close at least 4 prepaid current buyer-release cases at 6,000-18,000 PLN each.
5. Collect at least 30,000 PLN before delivery.
6. For each case, assemble product/SKU/batch/supplier maps, role assumptions, upstream reference-number request trail, missing-evidence register, buyer response draft, and escalation boundaries.
7. Obtain at least one written buyer acceptance, buyer correction, or supplier-portal progress event showing the pack moved the order/listing/tender forward.
8. Reject any case where the buyer only wants generic policy text, no current order is affected, or the supplier expects the founder to certify legality or submit DDS as responsible operator.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Exporter pitch:

"If a German/Dutch/Nordic buyer is asking for EUDR traceability before confirming your order, send the exact buyer request and one SKU/batch. I turn your existing supplier, product, and DDS/reference-number evidence into a buyer-ready response pack, clearly separating known facts, missing upstream data, non-applicable fields, and items your legal/compliance adviser must approve."

Advisor/channel pitch:

"Your client is not ready for full EUDR software, but has one buyer request blocking a PO. Send the live request. I handle document assembly and supplier RFI drafting so you keep advisory/accounting/customs work."

### Economics

Initial pricing:

- 6,000-9,000 PLN for one SKU/order/buyer request with limited supplier chain;
- 10,000-18,000 PLN for multi-SKU or multi-supplier buyer-release pack;
- 3,000-6,000 PLN annual/order refresh once prior case memory exists;
- no free readiness audits; only paid current buyer cases.

Costs:

- founder evidence mapping and drafting;
- optional forestry/EUDR reviewer on flagged cases;
- translation where needed;
- secure document storage.

Payback:

- two prepaid cases can cover setup/reviewer costs;
- one blocked EU order or framework buyer relationship can justify the fee if the order value is material.

### Copy-Risk Defense

EUDR consultants and software vendors can copy generic templates. They cannot instantly copy:

- the named buyer request;
- current SKU/batch/supplier file;
- upstream reference-number email trail;
- order-specific buyer response;
- accepted/pushed-back wording memory by buyer type and product class;
- channel relationships with Polish SME wood/furniture exporters that do not want enterprise EUDR software.

The compounding asset is not a regulation summary. It is the map of buyer wording, supplier evidence gaps, product/SKU role questions, reference-number handoff patterns, and what specific EU buyers accept or reject.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

The startup only works on live buyer/order/tender/listing requests with a prepaid fee and named product files. It does not sell broad EUDR readiness, a dashboard, a supplier database, legal opinions, audits, certification, or marketplace matching. The controlled unit is a current buyer-release case plus case-memory taxonomy.

### Safety And Legal Boundaries

- The customer/operator remains responsible for EUDR compliance, due diligence, DDS or simplified declaration submission, and legal conclusions.
- Lawyers, EUDR consultants, auditors, customs advisers, and forestry experts handle legal/technical approval where needed.
- The founder does not state that a product is deforestation-free or legally produced unless the customer/adviser provides that statement.
- The founder does not submit into the EUDR Information System in the initial wedge.
- Pack wording separates known evidence, missing evidence, non-applicable fields, and adviser/operator decisions.
- Sensitive supplier data is handled under NDA/DPA where needed.

### Duplicate-Risk Check

- Not CBAM Import Continuity Desk: no steel/aluminium CBAM, no customs broker CBAM authorisation/default-value/certificate workflow, and no CBAM declaration preparation. This is wood/furniture EUDR buyer-release evidence.
- Not DORA: no ICT supplier risk evidence for financial-sector renewals.
- Not REDBlocked: no Amazon account-health or connected-device compliance.
- Not BioSignal: no healthtech/investor claim diligence.
- Not PromoLeak: no retailer deduction recovery.
- Not GreenTender: no EPREL public lighting bid pack.
- Not TraceFaktura: no municipal event invoice routing.
- Not HeritageDoor: no physical salvage inventory.

## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- EUDR application is still months away for many actors, so current buyer pressure must be proven, not assumed.
- EUDR consultants and software platforms are already active.
- Supplier geolocation/reference-number data may not exist or may be commercially sensitive.
- If the customer expects the founder to certify legality, risk, or DDS correctness, the idea becomes unsafe.
- Buyer acceptance is outside founder control.
- The model can collapse into generic regulatory consulting unless every case has a current buyer/order blocker and prepaid fee.
