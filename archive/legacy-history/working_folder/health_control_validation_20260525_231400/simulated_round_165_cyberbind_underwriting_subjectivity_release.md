## Search Frame

The last failures showed that "urgent evidence" only works when the gatekeeper has a current commercial decision and the founder can control a live packet before a natural incumbent absorbs it. This round searches for external gatekeepers that withhold bind, renewal, contract approval, or cash until a narrow evidence packet is accepted.

The strongest candidate is a cyber-insurance broker-routed bind/renewal subjectivity release pack. The live gate is not abstract cyber readiness: an insurer, MGA, or underwriter has issued a conditional quote, renewal hold, decline, exclusion, sublimit, surcharge, or follow-up that blocks policy bind until the SME proves specific controls. The broker owns the client and underwriter relationship, while the startup owns the technical evidence assembly sprint.

## Lead Candidate

**CyberBind Underwriting Subjectivity Release Pack**

### Internal Simulated Score

90 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The trigger is a current policy bind, renewal, exclusion, sublimit, surcharge, or decline, not vague readiness.
- The buyer channel already exists: cyber/commercial insurance brokers and MSPs see insurer follow-ups and client panic before policy expiry.
- The proof artifact is operational: underwriter subjectivity matrix, evidence index, approved response wording, exception/no-go memo, and broker-ready evidence room.
- The economic value is concrete: coverage can be required for customer contracts, lending, board risk tolerance, or basic business continuity.
- The startup does not sell implementation or false compliance. It only accepts evidence-ready or near-evidence-ready cases and marks missing-control cases as remediation/no-go.
- It is materially different from DORA because the external gatekeeper is an insurance underwriter, the proof is a binder/renewal subjectivity response, and the channel is brokers/MSPs rather than regulated-finance customers.

### Internal Objections

- Brokers, MSPs, MSSPs, cyber insurers, and GRC tools can copy pieces.
- Many cases require actual control implementation, not evidence packaging.
- Misstatements on insurance applications can create coverage and legal risk, so client and broker approval boundaries must be strict.
- An unknown founder needs credibility and likely a cyber reviewer or insurance-side reviewer.
- The market may be crowded with broker support tools and MSP checklist content.
- Some SMEs may be too price-sensitive if policy premiums are small.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point is a live broker-authorized policy subjectivity with a concrete bind/renewal outcome.
