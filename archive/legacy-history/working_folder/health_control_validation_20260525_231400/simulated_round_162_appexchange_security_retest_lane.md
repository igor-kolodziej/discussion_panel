## Lead Candidate

**AppExchange Security Retest Lane**

### Why It Clears Simulation

- It is tied to a formal marketplace review with failed findings and retest submission, not vague buyer anxiety.
- The buyer's commercial path is blocked: listing, managed-package update, AppExchange launch, or enterprise prospect approval.
- The founder can control the active ticket, failed scanner output, source/package scope, patch evidence, false-positive writeups, retest packet, and submission calendar.
- It is narrower than generic appsec and less legally loaded than regulated compliance evidence.

### Internal Objections

- Salesforce security consultancies and appsec firms are credible incumbents.
- Some failures require real engineering fixes, not only evidence.
- Unknown founder needs a Salesforce/appsec reviewer bench immediately.
- Salesforce final review remains outside founder control.
- It can drift into generic Salesforce development or appsec remediation.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point is a live failed platform-review queue with a retest outcome.
