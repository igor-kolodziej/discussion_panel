Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
SBA EIDL Collateral Release Closing Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A broker- and closing-attorney-routed back-office desk for US small-business sales, refinances, equipment sales, and ownership transfers blocked because a COVID EIDL or SBA disaster-loan collateral/UCC lien needs a complete servicing-action, payoff, release-of-collateral, change-of-ownership, or UCC release package before escrow can close.

Exact buyer:
US business brokers, small M&A advisors, closing attorneys, escrow/title-adjacent providers, SBA lenders, equipment buyers, and small-business sellers with a live transaction delayed by an SBA COVID EIDL or disaster-loan UCC/collateral issue. The end payor is the borrower/seller or transaction party with sale, refinance, equipment-sale, or escrow proceeds at stake.

Acute trigger:
A signed LOI, purchase agreement, refinance condition, equipment sale, ownership transfer, lender condition, or escrow closing is blocked because SBA has a UCC-1 or collateral interest; the buyer/lender/closing attorney requires release comfort; the borrower needs an SBA release of collateral or ownership-change action; payoff/UCC-3 termination path is unclear; senior-lien evidence is missing; or SBA servicing asks for more documentation.

Control point:
Signed borrower and closing-team authorization for a live transaction file; SBA loan number and borrower authority; UCC search/lien record; purchase agreement or asset schedule; payoff/use-of-proceeds plan; escrow instructions or closing statement draft; senior lien payoff/release evidence where relevant; SBA servicing-action checklist; portal/email submission record; deficiency list; status log; and post-payoff UCC-3 release/termination checklist. The startup controls the packet and status workflow while brokers, attorneys, lenders, SBA, escrow parties, and US counsel remain responsible parties.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three business brokers, closing attorneys, SBA lenders, or escrow-adjacent partners sign routing terms. Five live EIDL/SBA collateral-release closing cases are prepaid, escrow-authorized, or partner-guaranteed at 8,000-45,000 PLN each. Every case has a signed transaction document or refinance/equipment-sale condition, borrower authorization, SBA loan/UCC evidence, deadline, and closing-party approval path. At least two cases produce SBA acknowledgement of a complete package, accepted deficiency cure, payoff/release instruction, UCC release/termination progress, escrow progress, or clean no-go. At least one fee is paid from closing or escrowed proceeds.

6-month POC:
Handle 25-60 live cases through 8-15 broker/attorney/lender partners; collect 300,000-1,200,000 PLN in case fees, urgent fees, escrow-paid closing fees, and partner-wholesale fees; document 15+ SBA acknowledgement, payoff/release, UCC release/termination, escrow-progress, close, or no-go outcomes; get 5 repeat partners; build a private taxonomy of SBA servicing defects, collateral-release packets, change-of-ownership packets, UCC issues, senior-lien evidence gaps, escrow instruction problems, and default/Treasury no-go cases.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target US Main Street and lower-middle-market business brokers, small M&A advisors, SBA lenders, closing attorneys, and escrow-adjacent providers. Pitch only live blockers: "If a business sale, refinance, equipment sale, or ownership transfer is stuck because a COVID EIDL/SBA collateral lien or UCC release must be handled before closing, route the file. I assemble the release packet, deficiency tracker, payoff/UCC checklist, and status memo under your closing workflow. I do not give legal advice, negotiate debt, modify loans, or authorize collateral sales."

Economics:
Charge 8,000-15,000 PLN for a payoff/UCC termination packet, 15,000-35,000 PLN for a full release-of-collateral or ownership-change transaction packet, and 35,000-60,000 PLN for urgent multi-lien, multi-asset, or near-closing cases. Fees can be paid by seller, broker, buyer, lender, or from escrow/closing proceeds where allowed. The fee is small relative to a 500,000-5,000,000 PLN equivalent sale/refinance/equipment transaction that fails because the SBA/UCC issue is unresolved.

Copy risk:
Closing attorneys, business brokers, SBA loan consultants, accountants, paralegals, lenders, and debt-workout advisers can copy pieces. The defense is live broker/attorney routing, narrow packet memory, speed on complete files, escrow-linked fee collection, repeat closing-party trust, and a defect/no-go taxonomy for SBA servicing and UCC release cases.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the broker or closing attorney routes the live file, the borrower signs authorization, and the startup receives SBA loan/UCC evidence, transaction documents, release checklist, escrow context, and fee authorization, the startup controls the active release packet and deficiency/status workflow for that closing event. Another provider can be hired later, but cannot instantly control the same in-flight packet unless the transaction team switches.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live transaction blockers with a signed LOI, purchase agreement, refinance condition, equipment sale, or escrow file and a specific SBA/EIDL/UCC release issue. The output is an operational closing artifact: servicing-action packet, payoff/UCC checklist, deficiency tracker, submission record, status memo, no-go note, and post-release filing checklist. It is not generic SBA consulting, debt settlement, loan modification, business brokerage, legal memo, dashboard, database, marketplace, or expert-intro service.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, loan modification, debt settlement, bankruptcy advice, tax advice, UCC legal opinions, authority-to-sell opinions, escrow services, or guarantees of SBA approval, lien release, payoff, assumption, or closing. Borrowers, buyers, brokers, closing attorneys, lenders, escrow parties, US counsel, accountants, SBA, and state filing offices remain responsible for legal advice, payoff decisions, lien releases, UCC filings, closing authority, collateral disposition, and final approvals.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, BESSGuard, RAGProbe, CyberBind, CleanRIPE, KSeF, or any health/Rx/clinical route. The buyer, trigger, control point, payment event, and proof artifact are materially different: US small-business transaction cash blocked by a government collateral/UCC release process.

Strongest anticipated objections:
US SBA/EIDL lien work is not a natural Warsaw-founder wedge and may require US paralegal, attorney, or SBA-servicing contractor credibility. Closing attorneys, brokers, lenders, accountants, and SBA consultants may already solve many cases. SBA timing and approval remain outside founder control. Some cases require payoff, assumption, settlement, default/Treasury handling, bankruptcy, or legal authority analysis outside scope. UCC and closing documents are state-specific and legal-boundary sensitive. Deal parties may not trust a remote unknown founder. Case volume may decline as COVID EIDL issues mature. Small transactions may not support the fee.
