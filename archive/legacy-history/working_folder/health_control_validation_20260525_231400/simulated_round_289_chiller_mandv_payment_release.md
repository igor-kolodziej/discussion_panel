### Why It Clears Simulation

- The payment event is already live: an ESCO, energy auditor, refrigeration/chiller service firm, or facility-service partner has an invoice, savings bonus, success fee, rebate, or stage payment blocked by missing or disputed measured evidence.
- The control point is specific: customer authorization, controller/BMS/meter export request, Data Act data-access wording where relevant, temporary logger fallback, raw/pre-processed data folder, baseline/post-period calculation workpaper, issue log, and buyer-safe payment-release wording.
- It is materially different from the confirmed compressed-air Data Act memo because the proof artifact is a payment-release M&V data room for chiller/refrigeration/BMS energy projects, not a service-partner prospecting memo for compressed-air savings.
- It has clear no-go outcomes: missing baseline, missing authorization, data gap, unstable operating conditions, weather/load mismatch, changed production hours, unresolved controls, or safety/comfort risk.
- The founder does not need to certify savings; the service partner/customer remains responsible for final commercial claims, and qualified M&V/energy reviewers handle flagged cases.

### Internal Objections

- ESCOs, energy auditors, BMS integrators, chiller service firms, and IPMVP/M&V specialists can internalize this work.
- Many payment disputes are not data-room problems; they involve poor savings, bad baselines, changed load, comfort complaints, equipment faults, control issues, or contract ambiguity.
- Handling BMS/controller access and facility energy data creates trust and security friction.
- Chiller/refrigeration performance can involve safety, comfort, food-temperature, downtime, and warranty boundaries.
- The idea risks duplicate perception with the confirmed Data Act machine-data desk unless the prompt strongly distinguishes payment-release M&V from compressed-air service-partner lead/savings memos.
- Gross margin depends on accepting evidence-ready files and rejecting broader engineering/remediation/M&V disputes.
