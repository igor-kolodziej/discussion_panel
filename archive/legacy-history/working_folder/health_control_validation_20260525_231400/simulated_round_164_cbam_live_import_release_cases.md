## Lead Candidate

**CBAM Live Import-Release Case Desk**

### Internal Simulated Score

89 / 100

### Why It Clears Simulation

- The trigger is a current import-release problem, not a readiness memo.
- The founder accepts only broker-routed live files with documents already in the customs workflow.
- Cases where the importer failed to apply and is above threshold are marked no-go immediately; the desk does not pretend to create authorisation.
- Proof is accepted customs declaration/release/progress, accepted TARIC/document-code evidence, or a clean no-go that saves broker/importer time.
- The older CBAM idea showed the domain can clear if anchored in broker workflows; this version uses a sharper payment/release event.

### Internal Objections

- It is close to the existing confirmed CBAM idea and must stay live-release-only.
- Customs brokers and CBAM consultants can absorb the workflow.
- Hard cases require authorised declarant status, portal access, legal/customs decisions, or supplier data outside the founder's control.
- The window may shrink once brokers standardize document-code workflows.
- The founder must avoid customs/legal representation or guarantee of release.

### Gate Decision

Advance to working Zero To One validation because the simulated score is strictly above 87 and the control point, trigger, and proof artifact are materially different from the old CBAM continuity memo.
