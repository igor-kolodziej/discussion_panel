2. Official eu-LISA/Frontex support, COBST, FAQs, web/mobile access, and onboarding materials reduce willingness to pay.
3. The opportunity may normalize quickly after carriers complete the first few departures.
4. Small carriers may be price-sensitive unless a boarding disruption or partner pressure has already occurred.
5. Mature airlines, ferry operators, and larger coach firms already have IT/compliance teams or system-to-system vendors.
6. Data-protection and passenger-boundary risk requires strict no-personal-data discipline and carrier-owned workflows.
7. Mistakes can create passenger disputes, missed travel, reputational damage, and legal escalation.
8. Channel partners can internalize the workflow after seeing it.
9. The proposed 60-day proof was aggressive for an unknown founder without established carrier channels.
10. The work can degrade into low-margin training or login support unless tied to named routes and live departure outcomes.
