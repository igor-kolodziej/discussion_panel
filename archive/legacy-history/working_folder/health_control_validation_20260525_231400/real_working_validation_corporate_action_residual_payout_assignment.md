## Zero To One Summary

The evaluator agreed that stranded corporate-action cash, dividends, redemption proceeds, squeeze-out proceeds, and other securities-related entitlements can exist when a holder's broker account, entity status, bank details, KYC, tax forms, or signatory records are stale.

It rejected the idea as a strong startup because the actual control point may not be transferable. Paying agents, brokers, trustees, issuers, depositaries, and transfer agents may deal only with the registered holder, broker participant, legal successor, or properly authorized representative. The founder may be able to organize a file, but may not be allowed to route the payout except through the holder's own account, broker, counsel, or regulated escrow.

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Payors may reject assignments or mandates and require registered holder, broker participant, legal successor, original corporate documents, tax forms, notarized/apostilled evidence, court or registry proof, counsel, or regulated escrow.
2. The best cases are already handled by corporate-law firms, fund administrators, brokers, accountants, transfer agents, trustees, and issuer-service providers.
3. Fraud and identity risk are high because stale cash entitlements require strict checks on beneficial ownership, signatory authority, entity existence, bank matching, assignment validity, sanctions, tax documentation, dissolved entities, and successor status.
4. Timelines are likely slower than the pitch; payors and brokers may take weeks just to confirm the instruction path.
5. The work drifts quickly into legal, tax, securities, escheatment, authority, withholding, and successor questions.
6. Sourcing is the hardest part because named payable amounts are hidden inside old broker statements, closing documents, fund-admin files, issuer notices, trustee letters, stale dividend checks, SPV records, and audit cleanup notes.
7. Ticket sizes may fall into a bad middle band: small cases cannot support the work, while large cases are routed to lawyers, brokers, or administrators.
8. Escheatment and unclaimed-property pathways may move the recovery away from the issuer or paying agent and into a different claims regime.

## Evaluator's Best Narrowing

The evaluator said the better version would be a counsel-backed **Professional-Channel Corporate-Action Payout Cleanup Desk** that accepts only files from corporate-law firms, M&A accountants, fund administrators, family-office accountants, ESOP administrators, boutique brokers, and issuer-service providers.

The narrowed intake would require:

- named and evidenced payout amount;
- corporate or professional holder, not consumer/heir;
- identifiable payor or broker;
- clean holder authority;
- funds paid directly to holder account or regulated escrow;
- counsel-reviewed mandate/assignment route;
- expected payout at least 100,000 PLN.

Even this narrowed version was framed as a small professional-services desk, not a high-scoring startup.

## Lesson

Do not continue corporate-action payout or stale securities entitlement variants unless the founder already has a referral channel and a payor-accepted instruction path. The hard-control object looked attractive, but Zero To One treated payor acceptance, legal authority, sourcing, and fraud/KYC as dominant constraints.
