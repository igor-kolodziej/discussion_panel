## Why This Is Not A Duplicate

This is not BorrowingBase Certificate Book Buyout. That failed fresh because it assumed a transferable third-party reporting book. This candidate does not buy a seller book; it starts from lender/factor/broker-routed current deficiency tickets and borrower prepayment.

It is not the one-off ABL Draw Release Desk because distribution and quality control come from lender/factor routing agreements and written deficiency queues rather than cold-selling distressed borrowers.

It is not KSeF/AP/factor invoice acceptance because the payment event is borrowing-base availability under an ABL/factoring facility, not invoice acceptance, KSeF status, service-entry, AP exceptions, or buyer payment release.

## Internal Objections To Watch In Real Validation

- The evaluator may still treat this as an ABL/factoring consultant workflow rather than a hard-control startup.
- Lenders/factors may refuse to recommend an unknown third party to borrowers.
- Borrowers may prefer their accountant, CFO, field exam firm, or internal controller.
- Broken books, fraud, ineligible collateral, covenant disputes, or bad inventory records cannot be fixed by packet work.
- Borrower-certified collateral facts create liability if the startup appears to certify truth.
- Lender templates and reserve logic vary enough to destroy standardization.
- Part-time month-end/week-end deadlines may be too intense.
- Channel partners can internalize the playbook after seeing accepted packages.
