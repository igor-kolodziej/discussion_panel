Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: ICS2 House-Level ENS Exception Desk.

One-sentence thesis:
Build a broker/forwarder-routed exception desk for current EU ICS2 house-level Entry Summary Declaration data problems, controlling prepaid shipment exception batches, freight-forwarder/customs-agent routing, shipper/importer authorization, commercial-document packets, and data-completion workpapers before any customs filing or regulated declaration action.

Exact buyer:
Small and mid-sized freight forwarders, customs agencies, NVOCCs, road/rail forwarders, air/ocean consolidators, ecommerce import 3PLs, and customs-document teams that now need accurate and complete ICS2 / ENS data at master and house level for goods entering or transiting the EU, Northern Ireland, Norway, or Switzerland.

Acute trigger:
ICS2 Release 3 extended pre-arrival safety/security data obligations across transport modes, including maritime, road, and rail. Forwarders and customs agents now need accurate house-level data, 6-digit HS codes, buyer/seller/consignee/consignor details, goods descriptions, routing, parties, and commercial-document consistency before arrival or loading milestones. Missing, vague, inconsistent, or late data can create ENS warnings, rejected filings, customer pressure, shipment delays, carrier escalation, or manual rework.

Transferable control point:
- Signed priority routing agreement with freight forwarders/customs agents/NVOCCs for current ICS2/ENS data exception batches.
- Partner-branded intake for current shipment files: house bills/waybills, commercial invoices, packing lists, product descriptions, shipper/consignee details, EORI/party data where available, routing, container/truck/rail/air details, and prior warning/rejection messages.
- Customer authorization for the founder/new desk to review documents, request missing facts, and prepare data-completion workpapers for the partner.
- Prepaid current exception batches before work begins.
- Data-completion workpaper per shipment/batch: missing fields, inconsistent fields, evidence source, proposed non-legal wording, customer questions, partner approval, unresolved no-go items, and change log.
- Outcome memory by mode, origin, commodity type, document source, forwarder process, common missing fields, rejection pattern, and fix/no-go outcome.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- 2-3 partner routing agreements with freight forwarders, customs agencies, NVOCCs, ecommerce import 3PLs, or road/rail forwarders.
- 10-25 prepaid current shipment exception batches, each tied to an actual missing-data, warning, rejection, delay-risk, or filing-readiness issue.
- Shipper/importer/customer authorization or partner-authorized document access for each batch.
- Data-processing/confidentiality terms limiting the desk to document extraction, consistency checks, missing-field requests, and partner-facing data-completion packets.
- First 5-10 completed batches with partner sign-off, corrected/complete data packet, or documented no-go reason.

6-month proof:
1. Build a target list of 50 Polish/CEE freight forwarders, customs agents, NVOCCs, ecommerce import 3PLs, road/rail forwarders, and consolidators exposed to ICS2 house-level data work.
2. Sign 2-3 routing agreements where the partner already receives shipment data gaps, carrier/ENS warnings, or customer document-quality problems.
3. Accept 25-60 prepaid current exception batches.
4. Collect 50,000-180,000 PLN in prepaid batch fees and urgent-shipment surcharges.
5. Complete 300+ line/shipment data checks across house-level shipments, with resolved/no-go status and partner sign-off.
6. Prove at least 70% of accepted exceptions are closed with complete partner-approved data packets or clear no-go escalation.
7. Prove gross margin above 60% after contractor document extraction, translation, partner share, QA, secure storage, and admin.
8. Show repeatability: at least one partner sends 5+ batches and one second partner sends 3+ batches without cold outreach.

First acquisition mechanism:
Partner pitch: "ICS2 house-level data now creates repetitive document-quality work for forwarders and customs teams. We do not file declarations or give customs advice. We take your current missing-data/warning batches, extract the commercial facts, identify gaps, ask approved questions, and return a partner-ready data-completion packet so your team can file or escalate faster under your workflow."

Importer/shipper pitch through partner: "Your forwarder needs accurate shipment data before filing. We review only the documents and facts you authorize, identify missing or inconsistent fields, prepare follow-up questions, and return a structured packet to your forwarder. Your forwarder/customs agent remains responsible for filing and customs decisions."

Economics:
- Prepaid batch fee: 1,500-4,000 PLN for small shipment/document batches; 4,000-12,000 PLN for urgent or high-volume house-level batches; higher only for clearly time-critical shipment releases.
- Partner share: 15-30% or white-label resale markup.
- First 6-month target: 25-60 accepted batches, 50,000-180,000 PLN collected, 60%+ gross margin, and 25,000-90,000 PLN owner contribution if scope stays bounded.
- Payback for partner/customer: fewer shipment delays, less senior broker rework, faster customer follow-up, cleaner ENS data, and better retention during a new customs-data burden.
- No unpaid generic audits. No tariff classification opinion. No customs declaration filing. No work without a current exception batch and prepaid fee.

Copy risk:
Large forwarders, customs software vendors, and brokers can build internal ICS2 workflows, but they cannot copy a signed partner lane, current shipment exception batches, customer-authorized commercial-document packets, and batch-level outcome memory before those shipments are processed. The first defensible wedge is partner trust plus repeated current exceptions, not a generic ICS2 checklist.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder starts with small and mid-sized partners whose staff are already overloaded by current shipment data quality. Once the partner routes a batch and the desk begins document extraction and missing-field work, a competing provider would have to interrupt an active shipment exception workflow. Over time, the desk owns a local map of common missing fields, document-source quality, origin/mode patterns, and partner-specific filing readiness.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls prepaid current shipment exception batches routed by forwarders/customs agents and returns operational workpapers tied to real filing readiness. It does not sell a dashboard, broad customs advice, lead brokerage, generic compliance reports, or software before manual partner-routed cases prove repeated value.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not act as customs declarant, freight forwarder, carrier, importer of record, indirect representative, legal adviser, tariff-classification adviser, sanctions adviser, or official filer. The partner remains responsible for ENS filing, customs declarations, tariff decisions, legal/customs advice, and customer relationship. The desk only extracts and reconciles authorized commercial facts, identifies missing data, drafts factual follow-up questions, and prepares partner-facing completion packets. Cases involving sanctions, dual-use goods, dangerous goods, restricted goods, suspected fraud, disputed parties, or classification/legal uncertainty are escalated back to the partner as no-go/specialist cases.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not CBAM Import Continuity Desk: there is no carbon-emissions, authorised CBAM declarant, certificate-price, default-value, or CBAM threshold workflow. ICS2 is pre-arrival safety/security data and house-level ENS document quality.

This is not EUDR buyer-release evidence: there is no deforestation due diligence, geolocation, DDS/reference-number, or operator/trader role mapping.

This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: there is no bank-vendor ICT third-party-risk questionnaire or DORA contract-gap request.

This is not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: there are no marketplace listings or Amazon compliance cases.

This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack, TraceFaktura, or HeritageDoor Suites.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether small forwarders already solve ICS2 data gaps internally or with existing customs software.
- Whether enough current exceptions are painful enough to pay for, rather than ordinary document admin.
- Whether an unknown founder can earn trust with shipment/commercial documents and time-sensitive freight workflows.
- Whether the work becomes low-margin manual data entry and customer chasing.
- Whether HS-code/goods-description cleanup drifts into tariff classification or legal advice.
- Whether current ICS2 pain is concentrated in large forwarders with internal systems rather than small/mid-sized partners reachable by a solo founder.
