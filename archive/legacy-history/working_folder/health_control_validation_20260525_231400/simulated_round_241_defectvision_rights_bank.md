## Pivot Logic

Recovery mandates keep failing because recoverability is ambiguous. Evidence desks keep failing because incumbents own formal authority. Physical lots keep failing because logistics, substitutes, and specialist trust eat the spread. This round tests a hard intangible-control asset: exclusive rights to a named non-health, non-personal-data industrial image archive, with buyer inspection deposits before cleanup. The control point is not advice or a report. It is an option/license over rare defect examples that buyers cannot immediately recreate.

## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- Buyers may prefer collecting data from their own customers because defects are process-specific.
- Source factories may refuse release due trade secrets, customer confidentiality, brand/IP, or fear of exposing quality problems.
- Data rights can be contractually messy even without personal data.
- Archive quality may collapse after inspection: missing labels, weak metadata, inconsistent lighting, camera changes, duplicates, non-representative defects, and no pass/fail truth.
- Buyer deposits are the truth serum; without deposits before cleanup, this becomes speculative data brokerage.
- Incumbent machine-vision vendors, integrators, and inspection firms can capture future source archives.
- The founder must avoid regulated safety certification and health/defense/medical categories.
