Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Please cap the score below 85 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, assigned, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Cap below 87 if CAC, payback, gross margin, cash conversion, or owner earnings are not credible. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality. Penalize generic clinics, apps, reports, dashboards, marketplaces, coaching, patient-data businesses, and prescription-drug businesses without asset/payment-flow control.

Scored idea:

Adult Diabetes Group R E-Order Mandate Rescue options a retiring/closing NFZ-contracted medical-device shop's adult diabetes Group R reorder book and executes monthly CGM/FGM/pump-consumable e-zlecenie fulfillment through a legally contracted NFZ realizator partner.

The founder does not prescribe, issue e-zlecenia, diagnose, provide clinical product advice, sell prescription medicines, advertise medicines, or claim healthcare-provider status. Authorized clinicians issue e-zlecenia. A lawfully contracted NFZ realizator/shop/apteka pulls and realizes the orders in eZWM and remains the regulated transaction party. The startup controls owner handoff, patient supply mandate/admin, SKU/reorder history, phone/domain/mailbox, supplier coordination, delivery/admin workflow, and payment/revenue-share economics.

This is a diabetes medical-device transaction-flow business, not an Ozempic/Rx drug business.

Buyer / payer:
- NFZ-contracted medical-device shop/apteka that wants recurring adult diabetes Group R order flow;
- adult insulin/CGM/FGM/pump patients, especially 50-60+ users and caregivers, who need continuity and monthly fulfillment;
- retiring or closing small diabetes medical-device shop owner who wants continuity and earnout rather than losing the book.

Acute trigger:
An adult patient with insulin therapy, CGM/FGM sensor use, or pump consumables needs monthly reimbursed device supply. A retiring/closing shop, stockout, weak online workflow, or missed renewal can break continuity. The patient can choose realizator, but once a realizator pulls the e-zlecenie, that order is assigned for realization and not freely fulfilled elsewhere.

Control point:
The acquired/optioned asset is:
- signed retiring-shop/book source and owner handoff;
- patient/caregiver supply mandates and contact authorizations;
- exact SKU/device/reorder history for adult Group R devices;
- phone/domain/mailbox/Google profile and old-owner call routing;
- exclusive contracted realizator agreement with an NFZ-contracted partner;
- eZWM pull workflow for named orders;
- supplier accounts/terms and starter stock where useful;
- fulfillment calendar and shipment/admin workflow where mail-order is legally permitted;
- revenue-share/payment custody with the realizator;
- first settlement file or NFZ/accounting settlement proof.

The hard control is not patient interest. It is patient mandate + partner realizator + actual eZWM-pulled order + fulfilled shipment + settlement evidence.

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 5,000-15,000 PLN option/revenue-share agreement with retiring/closing small diabetes-device shop/order-book source;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN legal/GDPR/NFZ/eZWM/medical-device advertising and realizator-contract review;
- 10,000-25,000 PLN starter stock / SKU buffer / supplier deposits for high-frequency adult Group R products, only after mandates and realizator contract;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN phone/mailbox/CRM/caregiver reorder workflow;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN logistics/packaging/returns/admin;
- no large upfront acquisition until actual pulled orders and settlement proof exist.

Required proof:
1. Signed retiring/closing shop or order-book source option with owner handoff.
2. Exclusive contracted realizator agreement with one NFZ-contracted medical-device shop/apteka covering revenue share, patient choice, eZWM process, supplier workflow, and settlement reporting.
3. 50 patient/caregiver supply mandates or service authorizations, with clear patient choice and GDPR language.
4. 35 actual eZWM-pulled adult Group R e-zlecenia through the contracted realizator.
5. 30 fulfilled/delivered Group R orders.
6. First NFZ settlement, settlement file, or accountant/NFZ process proof for the realized orders.
7. Contribution margin above 25% after product cost, co-pay/NFZ settlement timing, courier, returns, call handling, supplier terms, and seller/realizator revenue share.
8. No prescription medicine sale/advertising, no medical advice, no issuing orders, no lock-in misrepresentation; patients can choose.

Economics:
- supplier spread and retained fulfillment margin on recurring adult Group R reimbursed devices;
- patient co-pay/top-up where lawful;
- private accessories only where compliant and not medical advice;
- revenue share with realizator and seller earnout from retained gross margin.

Payback logic:
The startup does not buy a shop outright. It proves recurring transaction flow with mandates, pulled orders, fulfilled shipments, and settlement evidence. A retained group of 100 adult diabetes device users can generate recurring monthly gross merchandise flow, but the real proof is contribution margin after supplier terms, courier, returns, and call handling.

Why this is not a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace:
The foundation is an acquired/order-book source plus patient mandates plus actual eZWM-pulled orders through a contracted NFZ realizator. It is not a caregiver app, advice platform, or generic online store. If a competitor wants the same order flow, they must win the patient mandates, the old-owner handoff, and the pulled monthly order execution.

Copy risk:
Large online medical-device shops can compete and patients can switch. The wedge is already-pulled recurring monthly orders, old-owner phone handoff, SKU history, caregiver reorder administration, and continuity for adults whose exact sensor/pump-consumable supply matters. Copy risk is high if the founder has only leads/consents; lower if actual monthly orders are pulled and retained.

Regulatory and data guardrails:
- Authorized clinicians issue e-zlecenia; startup does not.
- NFZ-contracted realizator pulls and realizes orders; startup is admin/order-flow partner only.
- No prescription medicine sale, advertising, reservation, delivery, or clinical advice.
- No false lock-in: patient choice remains clear.
- GDPR/health-data handling around PESEL/order/device data must be reviewed.
- Mail-order realization only where legally permitted for the exact product and executed from the proper service location.
- Product support questions go to clinicians, manufacturers, or the realizator, not the founder.

Main risks acknowledged:
- Partner realizator owns the NFZ contract and may capture the value.
- Patients can switch realizators next month.
- GDPR/health-data handling around PESEL/order/device data is serious.
- Margins may collapse after supplier spread, courier, returns, support, and revenue shares.
- Mail-order realization must be legally permitted for the exact Group R product and executed from the proper service location.
- Product support can drift into medical advice and must be routed to clinicians/manufacturers.
- If the proof is only patient consents/leads and not actual pulled orders plus settlement evidence, cap at 82 or below.
