## Selected Candidate

**CDR Delivery Payment Release Desk**

One-sentence thesis:

> Corporate carbon-removal buyers and CDR resellers increasingly have signed purchases, prepayments, invoice holdbacks, or reporting deadlines where cash cannot move until a specific registry/retirement/chain-of-custody delivery packet is accepted; the startup turns only named, paid CDR delivery exceptions into buyer/supplier-approved release or no-go files.

## Internal Gate Review

### Concrete 6-month control proof

Pass. The proof is cash movement or a written no-go over named tonnes/serials, not a generic report:

- buyer/supplier or reseller signed release mandate;
- named contract, invoice, retirement request, prepayment, escrow, holdback, or reporting deadline;
- exact tonnes/serials/project/batch;
- registry, certificate, invoice, chain-of-custody, lab, audit, and retirement evidence;
- buyer-approved release/no-go log;
- first fee collected and first payment/retirement/accepted no-go outcome.

### CAC, payback, margin, cash conversion

Pass if the entry threshold stays high. Only accept cases where at least 150,000 PLN equivalent is blocked or where repeated reseller delivery files exist. Pricing can be 12,000-35,000 PLN fixed plus 1-3% of released holdback or a batch retainer. The buyer's pain is immediate because a delivery, payment, retirement claim, ESG report, or customer resale is blocked.

### Copy risk

Conditional pass. Carbon marketplaces, ratings providers, MRV consultants, brokers, lawyers, and ESG advisers can copy a generic evidence packet. They cannot copy a signed mandate over a specific blocked delivery, exact serials, buyer acceptance trail, and payment-release sequence once the startup controls it. Case memory can compound around accepted/rejected evidence patterns by CDR type, registry, reseller, and buyer.

### Founder constraints

Pass with boundaries. The founder does not certify credits, give legal/ESG assurance, rate projects, broker offsets, verify MRV, or opine on net-zero claims. Qualified carbon/MRV reviewers are used only for issue spotting and no-go routing.

### Duplicate-risk distinction

Not PromoLeak or other recovery desks: this is not retailer deductions, Amazon claims, or operational invoice leakage. Not CBAM: there is no customs/import release. Not BioSignal/PFAS claim-risk diligence: this is a named CDR delivery/payment-release queue, not investor memo work.

### Simulated strongest objections

1. Carbon credit buyers may already use brokers, marketplaces, registries, ratings firms, MRV consultants, carbon lawyers, and ESG advisers.
2. Missing delivery evidence may be impossible to create; many cases become no-go.
3. The buyer may need legal/accounting/assurance decisions, not a packet.
4. CDR standards, registry rules, reversal risk, permanence claims, and retirement language are high-trust and reputationally risky.
5. Case flow may be too sparse for an unknown Warsaw solo founder.
6. Suppliers may resist sharing lab data, chain of custody, feedstock/source records, or registry details.
7. Payment release attribution can be disputed.
8. The business could drift into carbon brokerage, project rating, ESG reporting, or legal claims.
