## Internal Simulation For Lead

### DefectLot AOI Rights Bank - 88.4

One-sentence thesis: Acquire or option rights-cleared industrial visual-inspection defect image lots from factories, QA labs, and machine-vision integrators only after machine-vision AI vendors or inspection integrators reserve named defect categories with deposits, then license bounded data lots with metadata for model evaluation, sales proof, and POC preparation.

Why it clears simulation:

- The controlled object is not a case file, report, or dashboard. It is a rights-cleared data lot: source license, metadata, buyer reservation, and delivery acceptance.
- The first cash proof is direct: buyer deposits and paid licenses before the founder pays meaningful source money.
- Incumbents can copy the search behavior, but not the exact source-rights lot once assigned.
- The buyer does not need legal, clinical, tax, customs, insurance, or regulated-provider trust. It needs rare, real, labeled examples that are expensive to generate from scratch.
- The first beachhead can be non-personal-data, non-safety-critical industrial images: surface defects, solder defects, print defects, molding defects, assembly defects, coating defects, and packaging defects.
- Founder fit is better than field service: source normalization, metadata QA, deduplication, rights-file hygiene, sample pack assembly, and buyer matching are data-heavy and asynchronous.

Internal risks:

- Buyers may insist on their own customer-domain data rather than third-party defect examples.
- Factories may not have clean rights or may be unwilling to license images.
- Data quality can be poor without defect taxonomy, image provenance, pass/fail labels, process context, and non-defect controls.
- Integrators and AI vendors may try to source similar data directly.
- Dataset sales can become a marketplace/database unless every lot is rights-controlled, buyer-reserved, and acceptance-tested.
- Some source archives contain confidential drawings, customer logos, product designs, personal data, regulated parts, defense/medical/automotive-safety content, or trade secrets that must be rejected.

Internal gate notes:

- Not capped below 82: the 60-day proof contains assignable source rights, buyer deposits, and paid delivery, not interviews or interest.
- Not capped below 85: a generic consultant cannot copy a signed source-rights lot and buyer reservation before delivery; copying future sourcing remains a risk.
- Barely not capped below 87: CAC and cash conversion are credible only because the buyer-deposit rule avoids speculative data acquisition and keeps gross margin above 70%. The model fails if buyer deposits are not real.
- No clinical/legal/patient/regulated-data authority is needed if source filters reject personal data, defense, medical-device, automotive-safety, and regulated production evidence.
