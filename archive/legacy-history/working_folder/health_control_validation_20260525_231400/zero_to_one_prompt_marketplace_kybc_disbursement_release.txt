Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
Marketplace KYBC Disbursement Release Mandate

One-sentence thesis:
EU/CEE marketplace sellers will pay for a narrow disbursement-release mandate when a marketplace holds payouts, suppresses selling privileges, or blocks EU offers because trader identity, bank-account, VAT, trade-register, address, or beneficial-owner evidence does not match the platform's KYBC / DSA trader-traceability workflow.

Exact buyer:
Marketplace sellers, marketplace aggregators, and Amazon/account-health or e-commerce agencies serving sellers on Amazon, Kaufland, bol, Zalando, Booking, TikTok Shop, or similar EU consumer marketplaces. The first seller must have at least 50,000 PLN equivalent of held disbursements or at least 100,000 PLN monthly GMV at risk from a written platform trader-verification, KYBC, identity, bank-account, VAT, trade-register, address, UBO, or business-information mismatch.

Acute trigger:
The seller's marketplace account has live held cash, blocked payouts, suspended selling privileges, suppressed EU offers, failed re-verification, or blocked onboarding because the platform cannot reconcile trader identity, legal entity, bank account, VAT/VIES data, trade-register information, address, phone/email, beneficial owner, or supporting documents. The seller or agency has already received a platform request, rejection, warning, review notice, support-ticket trail, or disbursement hold tied to identity / KYBC / trader traceability rather than product safety, tax filing, advertising, counterfeit, fulfilment, or policy evasion.

Control point:
A seller-signed release mandate over one live marketplace account case; agency or seller authorization; platform request/rejection text; disbursement or GMV-at-risk amount; exact mismatch matrix; government-register extracts, VAT/VIES evidence, bank-account ownership proof, company certificate, trade-register ID, address and contact evidence, UBO evidence where supplied by counsel/accountant, prior support tickets, submitted-document log, platform response chronology, secure evidence room, seller-approved platform response wording, and success-fee trigger tied to platform acceptance, payout release, selling-privilege restoration, offer reactivation, or paid clean no-go.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign two agency routing agreements or one aggregator case-source agreement, close at least 15 prepaid current cases, accept only accounts with written platform KYBC/trader-verification text and meaningful held cash or GMV-at-risk, produce 10 seller-approved mismatch files, submit or support five platform response packages, and document at least five measurable outcomes: released payout, restored selling privilege, offer reactivation, platform acceptance of corrected trader data, or paid no-go because source facts are inconsistent. The first proof is current account cash or selling privileges moving, not a readiness report.

6-month POC:
Handle 60-100 prepaid cases, release or progress at least 2,000,000 PLN equivalent of held payouts or GMV-at-risk, document 25+ platform acceptance/release/progress/no-go outcomes, retain three agencies or aggregators that each route at least five cases, keep accepted-case rejection below 30%, keep gross margin above 60% after agency split, accountant/legal review, secure intake, and founder time, and build a private mismatch taxonomy by platform, entity type, country, document type, rejection wording, response path, and outcome.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target Amazon account-health agencies, e-commerce agencies, marketplace aggregators, seller accountants, VAT advisers, and CEE sellers already discussing marketplace payout holds or verification failures. The outreach is narrow: "If your seller account has money held or EU selling blocked by trader identity, bank-account, VAT, company-register, address, or UBO mismatch, I will run the evidence-room and platform-response loop for that live case. I do not touch product compliance, tax advice, account sales, ad/PPC, counterfeit, fulfilment, or policy evasion."

Economics:
Charge 2,000-4,000 PLN prepaid triage for an exact mismatch map and go/no-go, 6,000-15,000 PLN for a standard release case, 15,000-35,000 PLN for urgent multi-marketplace or aggregator cases, and 5-12% success fee on released held cash where attribution is clean and seller-approved. Agency partners keep 20-35% of the case fee. The buyer pays because held cash, account suspension, or blocked EU offers are immediately more expensive than the case fee. The 100,000 PLN budget covers secure intake, platform-case operations, accountant/legal reviewer checks for flagged entity/UBO/tax boundaries, agency sourcing, and templates.

Copy risk:
Marketplace agencies, seller accountants, VAT advisers, PSP/KYB consultants, and platform operators can copy pieces of the workflow. The defense is not generic DSA knowledge. It is control of specific seller mandates, live platform case trails, held-cash success triggers, agency-routed intake, and a private taxonomy of which mismatch patterns each platform accepts, rejects, escalates, or times out.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the seller signs a mandate, the evidence room, platform ticket chronology, mismatch file, response sequence, and success-fee trigger are tied to that account and held-cash event. Competitors can offer future KYBC help, but they cannot copy the already-controlled case file without the seller restarting the release process during a payout or selling-privilege hold.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business accepts only live marketplace account cases with held cash, blocked payouts, suppressed selling privileges, failed verification, or blocked EU offers. It is paid for a release mandate over a named account and platform ticket, with outcome tracking to payout release, selling restoration, platform acceptance, or clean no-go. It does not sell software, a compliance dashboard, generic marketplace consulting, product listing help, ads, tax filing, or referrals.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide tax advice, legal advice, beneficial-ownership opinions, address-office services, account sales, payment-service activity, product compliance, counterfeit appeals, identity-document creation, nominee services, false documentation, VAT registration, fiscal representation, or platform policy-evasion work. Sellers, their accountants, lawyers, tax advisers, banks, marketplaces, and payment providers remain responsible for legal status, tax positions, UBO determinations, platform terms, and account ownership. Cases involving fake documents, nominee ownership, sanctions flags, counterfeit goods, unsafe products, tax fraud, account sales, or platform evasion are rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not REDBlocked because the trigger is DSA/KYBC trader-identity and payout/selling-privilege release, not connected-device RED/CE/GPSR product evidence for baby monitors or cameras. It is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retail deductions, promotions, rebates, or commercial leakage. It is not Amazon Vendor Central recovery because it does not dispute 1P deductions or shortage claims. It is not DORA, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, or Supplement Stack. The buyer trigger, control object, payment event, and proof artifact are a live marketplace identity-verification case and held-cash release.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Marketplace agencies may already solve identity-verification cases internally. Platforms control final acceptance and may stay slow or opaque even with correct evidence. Many cases may be impossible because seller facts are inconsistent, tax data is wrong, bank accounts do not match, UBO evidence is unavailable, address proof is weak, or the account has broader fraud/policy problems. Seller willingness to pay may be low if held cash is small. The founder may look like another account-health consultant unless strict intake requires held cash or blocked selling privileges. Legal, tax, UBO, address, sanctions, and identity-document boundaries are sensitive. Agencies can internalize the checklist after seeing a few cases. Case memory may decay if platform verification workflows change.
