Idea: BioSignal Buyer-Block Evidence Rescue Desk.

This is a 7-business-day paid response service for EU/CEE wearable, EEG, sleep, remote-monitoring, digital-biomarker, and medical-AI vendors whose live hospital, insurer, pharma/CRO, employer-health, or enterprise pilot/procurement/renewal is blocked by a buyer evidence request.

It activates only after a named buyer has sent a questionnaire, evidence checklist, DPA/security request, AI/MDR/GDPR question set, clinical-validation request, integration review, or safety-claim challenge that is delaying a pilot, renewal, procurement approval, or commercial rollout.

The startup does not certify, practice medicine, validate clinical claims, run trials, give legal advice, act as a notified body, or guarantee procurement approval. It organizes the vendor's existing evidence into a buyer-ready response pack and flags what must go to counsel, security, regulatory, or clinical reviewers.

Buyer:
- 10-150 person EU/CEE healthtech vendors with wearable, EEG, sleep, remote-monitoring, digital-biomarker, or medical-AI products;
- vendors trying to close hospital, insurer, pharma/CRO, employer-health, or regulated-enterprise pilots;
- founders, COOs, commercial leads, security leads, quality/regulatory leads, and CTOs facing a live buyer block.

Acute trigger:
A buyer says the procurement, renewal, or pilot cannot proceed until the vendor answers evidence questions around clinical validation, intended use, wellness vs medical-device boundary, MDR/MDCG software classification, AI Act high-risk exposure, human oversight, GDPR/DPA and health-data handling, cybersecurity, uptime, access control, incident response, subprocessors, hospital/enterprise integration, safety claims, and buyer-risk language.

Control point:
- live buyer questionnaire or evidence request;
- named buyer deadline and blocked commercial process;
- vendor-paid fixed-fee rescue contract;
- temporary custody of the vendor's evidence room under NDA;
- access to product, security, validation, DPA, and integration documents;
- buyer-ready response pack submitted into the actual procurement/pilot workflow;
- case memory of accepted answers, rejected claims, missing-evidence patterns, and reviewer escalations.

No live buyer block means no project.

Deliverable:
- buyer-request interpretation;
- existing-evidence index;
- questionnaire answer pack;
- claim boundary table: safe to answer, needs reviewer, unsafe to claim, evidence missing;
- MDR/AI/GDPR/security/integration issue map;
- buyer-safe response wording;
- missing-evidence request list for the vendor;
- escalation map for counsel/security/regulatory/clinical signoff;
- executive memo for the buyer-facing sales/procurement owner.

2-month proof:
1. 30 interviews with EU/CEE biosignal, wearable, EEG, remote-monitoring, or medical-AI vendors that have live or recent buyer evidence blocks.
2. 3 paid live cases at 8,000-18,000 PLN each.
3. At least 25,000 PLN collected or prepaid.
4. One response submitted to a real buyer and either accepted/unblocked or converted into a clear buyer-facing gap/remediation path.
5. One regulatory/quality reviewer, one cyber/security reviewer, and one clinical-methodology reviewer available on paid per-section terms.
6. 70%+ gross margin after reviewer costs.
7. Less than 7 business days from document intake to response pack for the first full case.

First acquisition:
- Build a list of 80 EU/CEE healthtech vendors from public hospital pilots, insurer pilots, pharma/CRO digital-endpoint programs, accelerators, EU digital-health competitions, and remote-monitoring vendor pages.
- Send precise outbound asking only for live or recent blocked buyer questionnaires.
- Build a partner-referral list of boutique medtech regulatory consultants, cyber/DPA advisors, healthtech accelerators, and CRO digital-endpoint teams. They keep formal advisory/remediation work; the desk handles urgent evidence collation and response packaging.
- Publish one redacted sample buyer-block response pack using public claims and a synthetic buyer checklist.

Economics:
- First paid pack: 8,000-18,000 PLN.
- Urgent multi-buyer sprint: 18,000-35,000 PLN.
- Annual evidence refresh before next buyer review: 6,000-12,000 PLN.
- Reviewer costs should stay below 20-30% of revenue because reviewers only handle flagged sections.
- Cash conversion is credible only when the vendor is protecting a pilot, renewal, enterprise sale, insurer rollout, or pharma/CRO contract that is worth far more than the pack.

Why this is not a generic report:
Reject if there is no named buyer, no blocked procurement/renewal/pilot, or no output submitted to a real buyer. The business is a paid transaction-unblock workflow, not a dashboard, marketplace, broad advisory retainer, generic diligence memo, certification, legal opinion, medical-device validation, or SaaS.

Copy risk:
Regulatory firms, privacy lawyers, cyber consultants, and CROs can offer parts of the work. The proposed wedge is narrower: only live blocked buyer evidence requests; biosignal/EEG/wearable/remote-monitoring specificity; 7-business-day response; buyer-safe wording; and compounding case memory across recurring buyer objections, accepted answer structures, rejected claims, and reviewer escalations.
