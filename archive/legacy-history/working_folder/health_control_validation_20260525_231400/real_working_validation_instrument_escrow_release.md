## Zero To One Judgment

## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Used equipment dealers, liquidators, auction houses, OEM pre-owned teams, and independent service firms already handle service records, deinstallation facts, accessories, software/dongles, buyer questions, and sometimes testing or refurbishment.
2. Missing records cannot be recreated. Calibration/PM history, deinstallation notes, decontamination paperwork, service records, software entitlements, and accessory lists may simply not exist.
3. Sellers may resist paying for negative clarity when the correct output is a gap disclosure or no-go.
4. Decontamination is a hard boundary. If contamination is uncertain, a qualified decon provider is needed, not a paperwork response.
5. Buyer acceptance remains outside the founder's control because the buyer may still require OEM inspection, PM, calibration, IQ/OQ, installation support, repair, warranty, or recertification.
6. Software and license transfer can kill sales when dongles, service contracts, acquisition PCs, methods, firmware, vendor accounts, or entitlements are missing or non-transferable.
7. Clinical, GMP, IVD, patient-use, and medical-device statements must stay excluded.
8. Seller title, leases, grant-funded assets, liens, consignments, and asset-sale restrictions may block transactions.
9. The 60-day proof target is aggressive without warm broker or liquidator access.
10. Each instrument can become bespoke document archaeology, weakening margin and repeatability.
11. Part-time execution may clash with escrow/payment deadlines unless intake is tightly limited or contractors handle operations.

## Useful Narrowing

The evaluator's best version is still narrow:

- used analytical-instrument brokers and lab-liquidation sellers;
- named non-clinical HPLC, GC, ICP, FTIR, UV-Vis, or similar instruments;
- buyer paperwork condition already exists;
- source documents already exist;
- seller title/consignment statement is clear;
- qualified service/deinstallation reviewer and decon route are available;
- no clinical/IVD/GMP/radioactive/biohazard/lien/export-control ambiguity.

Even then, the evaluator judged it as a practical narrow wedge, not a company strong enough for the gate.

## Decision

Reject. Do not continue used-instrument sale-release variants unless the founder controls something stronger than document organization, such as buyer-paid title/deposit over a scarce lot or a signed payment stream with repeatable release outcomes.
