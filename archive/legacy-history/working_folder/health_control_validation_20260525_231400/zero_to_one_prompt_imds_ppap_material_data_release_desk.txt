Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: IMDS PPAP Material-Data Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A prepaid live-case desk for Polish and CEE automotive suppliers whose PPAP, ISIR, launch, engineering-change, or customer part approval is blocked by missing or rejected IMDS material data, producing source-evidence-backed IMDS submissions and accepted MDS IDs for named part numbers.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE Tier 2/3 automotive component suppliers with live OEM or Tier 1 customer requests or rejections around IMDS material data sheet submissions. Best first buyers are too small to have a full material-compliance team but large enough that delayed PPAP, SOP, shipment, nomination, customer approval, or payment costs real money. Likely first segments: plastics, rubber, stamped metal, castings, fasteners, cable harnesses, coatings, electronics housings, and simple assemblies.

Acute trigger:
A customer launch, PPAP/ISIR submission, engineering change, requalification, or part approval is blocked because an IMDS material data sheet is missing, rejected, incomplete, incorrectly structured, inconsistent with BOM/material evidence, or unsupported by supplier material data. The quality manager needs a customer-actionable MDS submission or a clean no-go/escalation path before the customer deadline.

Control point:
Prepaid current case with customer request or rejection text, supplier authorization, named part numbers, BOM/material/SDS/certificate evidence, supplier declaration chain, IMDS account/submission path, expert review, live rejection/acceptance tracking, and resulting MDS ID or customer progress. The desk controls the source evidence workflow and case status for the named part-number blocker.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three prepaid live IMDS/PPAP blocker cases from at least two suppliers or one quality/IATF consultant channel.
2. One IMDS/material-compliance reviewer on paid per-case terms.
3. Supplier authorization and data-handling terms.
4. Source evidence folders for at least 10 part numbers or 3 part families.
5. At least 5 MDS submissions, revisions, or customer-response packets created from real source evidence.
6. At least 2 accepted MDS IDs, customer-narrowed rejection reasons, or PPAP/ISIR progress outcomes.
7. First 20,000-50,000 PLN collected in setup, urgent case, or per-part-family fees.

6-month POC:
Month 1: recruit one experienced IMDS/material-compliance reviewer, build intake templates, and contact 40-60 Polish/CEE automotive suppliers plus 20 quality/IATF/PPAP consultants.
Month 2: close three prepaid live cases, ingest source evidence, produce the first MDS submissions or rejection responses, and document all missing-source-truth cases as no-go or escalation.
Month 3: secure at least two accepted MDS IDs, customer-narrowed rejection reasons, or PPAP/ISIR progress outcomes.
Months 4-6: complete 12-20 paid cases, process 50-150 part numbers, collect 80,000-180,000 PLN in fees, keep gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs, and build case memory by customer/OEM, material type, rejection pattern, part family, source-evidence gap, reviewer note, and acceptance outcome.

First acquisition mechanism:
Target Polish/CEE automotive quality consultants, IATF 16949 consultants, PPAP/APQP freelancers, and Tier 2/3 suppliers. Ask for one live IMDS rejection or request tied to customer approval, not general compliance curiosity. Consultants can refer cases they do not want to staff internally and keep larger IATF, PPAP, audit, and quality-system work.

Economics:
Charge 2,000-4,000 PLN for triage, 4,000-12,000 PLN per part family or accepted submission package, and 15,000-30,000 PLN for urgent launch sprints with multiple related parts. Reviewer cost is scoped per case. AI/data leverage helps parse BOMs, SDSs, supplier declarations, GADSL/IMDS structure, rejection reasons, and reusable evidence checklists, while the expert validates boundaries. The buyer pays because one blocked customer approval or launch can be worth far more than the case fee.

Copy risk:
IMDS consultants, quality consultants, compliance firms, and internal quality teams can do the work. The defense is narrow Polish/CEE mid-market focus, accepted/rejected customer-specific case memory, fast launch-block response, source-evidence workflow, and relationships with quality consultants that do not want to staff IMDS specialist work. It is not defensible as generic IMDS entry; it must specialize in live blocked PPAP/ISIR cases and customer rejection patterns.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup controls the paid live case, customer rejection trail, named part numbers, source evidence folders, supplier authorization, reviewer notes, and submission status, competitors cannot act on that exact case without the supplier switching. The startup's useful asset compounds through case memory by customer/OEM, material type, rejection pattern, part family, supplier-data gap, and acceptance outcome.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The output is an accepted or customer-actionable IMDS MDS submission for named part numbers, not a compliance report, spreadsheet, dashboard, or marketplace. The proof is portal/customer progress: MDS ID accepted, rejection reason resolved or narrowed, PPAP/ISIR package progressed, or no-go due missing source truth.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The desk does not falsify material composition, invent supplier declarations, certify legal compliance, provide legal advice, perform lab analysis, or guarantee customer acceptance. Suppliers remain responsible for product truth, material composition, customer commitments, and PPAP/quality-system obligations. Missing source truth is routed to supplier, material provider, lab, or no-go. The desk organizes evidence, prepares submissions/responses, flags contradictions, and uses an experienced reviewer for IMDS/material-data boundaries.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, TraceFaktura, PromoLeak, CBAM, GreenTender EPREL, or Data Act. It is closest in shape to evidence-response ideas, but the control event and proof artifact are different: automotive IMDS/PPAP customer release, named part numbers, source material evidence chain, and accepted MDS IDs. It is not a generic compliance readiness pack or buyer questionnaire desk.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Good IMDS consultants and automotive quality firms already exist.
Missing supplier material truth cannot be invented.
Automotive suppliers may expect their quality team or Tier 1 customer to guide them.
Some cases require lab/material analysis, supplier renegotiation, or legal/compliance decisions outside scope.
Data quality is often poor; BOM, SDS, and material declarations can be incomplete or inconsistent.
Part-time founder needs a credible reviewer and strict delivery boundaries.
The first cases may be lumpy launch emergencies rather than recurring retainers.
If it becomes generic IMDS entry, price and defensibility collapse.
