## Why It Passed Simulation

- Hard control: titled legacy EEG/EMG/PSG inventory plus signed first-look/consignment rights over closing/upgrading lab stock.
- Urgent buyer pain: failed headbox, stimulator, dongle, cable loom, workstation, PSG module, or amplifier can cancel paid tests.
- No medical-service authority problem: the founder does not diagnose, test, interpret, prescribe, or touch patient workflow.
- Concrete two-month proof: 2-3 donor systems/lots, QA records, 3 first-look/standby agreements, and 1 paid emergency rental/resale/deposit.
- Copy risk is reduced by exact locked lots, compatibility records, and speed; a vendor can buy equipment generally but cannot instantly copy titled obsolete lots and signed first-look rights.

## Fatal Caveats To Test

1. Demand frequency may be too low.
   - One paid line-down transaction proves pain but not yet recurring owner earnings.

2. MDR/distributor compliance matters.
   - Resale/rental of clinical devices needs traceability, CE status, documentation, serial records, complaint handling, and careful avoidance of unauthorized refurbishment or relabeling.

3. Software/dongles are a trap.
   - Legacy systems may depend on licenses, old Windows workstations, drivers, service passwords, and proprietary accessories.

4. Authorized incumbents exist.
   - Existing distributors and biomed service firms can serve supported systems. NeuroSpare must win on obsolete exact-SKU availability and emergency speed.

5. Wrong stock kills payback.
   - Inventory must be purchased from a compatibility heat map, not from random used-equipment listings.

## Best Truthful Wording

NeuroSpare is a neurodiagnostic continuity-inventory company, not a medical testing provider. It signs first-look or consignment rights with closing, retiring, or upgrading EEG/EMG/PSG labs and biomed service shops, then owns titled, tested legacy modules, headboxes, stimulators, cables, dongles, workstations, and accessories for systems still used by Polish private labs and small hospitals.

The control point is not "used equipment resale"; it is locked access to exact legacy lots plus QA/test records, compatibility mapping, and emergency loaner/resale flow. The 60-day proof is: two signed first-look/consignment agreements over named lots, 2-3 acquired donor systems under 100,000 PLN, electrical-safety/biomed QA records, three standby buyer agreements, and one paid emergency rental/resale/deposit from a lab whose scheduled tests were blocked by device failure.
