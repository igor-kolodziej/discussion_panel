Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
Public Contract Guarantee Line Release Mandate

One-sentence thesis:
Free cash deposits, bid bonds, bank guarantees, insurance guarantees, standby letters of credit, and surety capacity that remain blocked after a public or enterprise contract event has expired, been lost, accepted, closed, or reached a documented step-down date, using signed mandates over named guarantee instruments and proof of release, cancellation, refund, reduction, or written no-go.

Exact buyer:
Polish and CEE contractors, IT integrators, facility-management suppliers, equipment suppliers, engineering firms, security/cleaning providers, construction subcontractors, public-framework suppliers, and multi-contract SMEs that regularly post bid security, performance guarantees, warranty guarantees, cash deposits, insurance guarantees, bank guarantees, standby letters of credit, or surety bonds for public-sector or enterprise customers, and have at least 300,000 PLN equivalent of blocked guarantee capacity or deposits tied to completed, expired, lost, accepted, or reducible contracts.

Acute trigger:
The company needs guarantee capacity or cash for a new tender, contract, working-capital line, surety renewal, or bank covenant, but old bid deposits, performance guarantees, warranty guarantees, standby letters of credit, or insurance/bank guarantee limits remain active even though the tender was lost, the bid validity expired, the contract was accepted, the defect-liability period ended or stepped down, the framework call-off expired, the beneficiary issued no-claim language, or the customer/authority has acknowledged that release or reduction can be processed.

Control point:
Signed release mandate over named guarantee instruments or deposits; tender or contract file; guarantee text, bank/insurer/surety number, amount, expiry, beneficiary, and reduction schedule; acceptance protocol, no-claim letter, lost-tender notice, call-off expiry, step-down date, or beneficiary email; bank/insurer/surety contact route; beneficiary release request; release tracker; bank or insurer cancellation, guarantee-limit release, cash refund, scheduled refund, reduction confirmation, or written no-go.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Five signed mandates over named guarantees or deposits; 1,500,000-6,000,000 PLN equivalent in blocked guarantee capacity or deposits screened; at least 750,000-3,000,000 PLN accepted into the release workflow after exclusions; three beneficiary, authority, bank, insurer, or surety acknowledgements of release, refund, cancellation, reduction, or required no-go; two cancellations, line releases, deposit refunds, scheduled refunds, or guarantee reductions above 150,000 PLN each; 40,000-120,000 PLN startup fees collected or invoiced.

6-month POC:
25-60 signed mandates; 15,000,000-60,000,000 PLN equivalent in blocked guarantee capacity or deposits screened; 7,500,000-30,000,000 PLN accepted into release workflow; 4,000,000-18,000,000 PLN released, refunded, cancelled, reduced, scheduled, or cleanly no-go'd; 300,000-900,000 PLN startup revenue; three repeat channels such as surety brokers, bid-writing agencies, accountants, public-procurement consultants, bank relationship managers, contractor CFOs, or project-management offices; gross margin above 65% after finance-ops contractors, document handling, channel commissions, and limited legal boundary review.

First acquisition mechanism:
Source through surety brokers, bank relationship managers, bid-writing agencies, accountants, public-procurement consultants, construction/facility/IT contractor CFOs, and project-management offices. Ask for lists of old guarantee instruments, bid deposits, framework call-offs, and warranty guarantees where a release, refund, cancellation, or step-down event should already exist. Accept only named instruments with contract/tender files and a clear beneficiary/bank/insurer/surety route.

Economics:
Charge 4,000-12,000 PLN per accepted guarantee release file plus 1-4% of cash refunded, guarantee line released, surety capacity freed, scheduled refund, or documented guarantee reduction. Minimum accepted file is 150,000 PLN in guarantee capacity or 50,000 PLN in cash deposit, with a preferred mandate above 300,000 PLN across multiple instruments. Success fees trigger only on documented cancellation, refund, scheduled transfer, line release, reduction confirmation, beneficiary approval, bank/insurer/surety acknowledgement, or agreed no-go classification.

Copy risk:
Contractor CFOs, procurement admins, lawyers, surety brokers, banks, insurers, public-procurement consultants, accountants, and project managers can copy parts of the workflow. The defense is signed current mandates over named instruments, before-state guarantee schedules, beneficiary/bank release trails, repeat release wording by authority and guarantee type, channel relationships with brokers and bid agencies that see stale guarantee capacity but do not want to run the cleanup, and a case library of release/no-go patterns.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
They can copy the general workflow, but not an already-signed mandate over a named guarantee instrument or deposit, the timestamped before-state blocked line or cash balance, the tender/contract acceptance or expiry file, the beneficiary release request, the bank/insurer/surety cancellation route, and the success-fee right attached to that specific release event. The wedge depends on capturing current releaseable capacity through surety, bid, accounting, and contractor channels before the company gives the same file to internal procurement, counsel, or its bank.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The unit is a named guarantee or deposit release file converted into bank cancellation, surety cancellation, insurance guarantee cancellation, standby letter of credit cancellation, cash refund, scheduled refund, guarantee-limit release, reduction confirmation, beneficiary approval, or written no-go. The startup is not selling procurement consulting, tender advice, legal opinions, claim drafting, spend analytics, dashboards, databases, software, financing, factoring, bond brokerage, bank introductions, or generic document review.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, public-procurement advice, tender appeals, claim drafting, contract interpretation, debt collection, litigation support, insurance brokerage, surety brokerage, banking services, factoring, guarantee issuance, payment processing, or custody of client funds. The client CFO/counsel decides entitlement and relationship posture. Banks, insurers, sureties, and beneficiaries remain decision makers. Accept only cases with clear expiry, loss notice, accepted work, signed handover, documented no-claim position, release letter, or contractual step-down date. Reject active defects, penalties, unresolved acceptance, insolvency, litigation, arbitration, disputed performance, missing instruments, unclear beneficiary identity, ambiguous release rights, premature requests, and low-value files.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
Not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central, retailer chargeback recovery, factoring reserve, PSP reserve, AP credit cash-out, construction retention collection, grant reimbursement, customs/import continuity, DORA, REDBlocked, GreenTender, TraceFaktura, HeritageDoor, CBAM, Data Act, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, or BioSignal. The distinctive control point is a signed mandate over a named bid, performance, warranty, bank, insurance, surety, standby-letter-of-credit, or cash-deposit guarantee where release or reduction creates immediate cash or financing-capacity movement.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Competent contractor CFOs, procurement admins, banks, brokers, and lawyers should already track guarantee expiries and reductions. Many guarantees remain active for valid reasons such as defects, claims, penalties, unresolved acceptance, warranty obligations, beneficiary discretion, or missing handover documents. Public authorities and enterprise beneficiaries may move slowly or ignore outside coordinators. Success-fee attribution can be disputed if cancellation would have happened automatically. Some files are small cash deposits, automatically returned bid securities, or low-value administrative cleanups. Legal and procurement boundaries are dangerous if the startup drifts into arguing entitlement, interpreting contracts, or pressuring beneficiaries. Surety brokers and banks can internalize the workflow after seeing the release packet.
