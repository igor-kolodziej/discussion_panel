Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:

Bank AI Model-Risk Renewal Evidence Pack

One-sentence thesis:

AI workflow vendors selling to banks and insurers need a fast vendor-side evidence response when a live renewal, procurement, rollout, or production-use approval is blocked by bank AI governance and model-risk questions; the product is an AI-system behavior evidence pack, not generic DORA evidence or broad AI consulting.

Exact buyer:

Polish/CEE and EU-adjacent AI workflow vendors with 20-200 employees and 2-10 regulated-finance customers, selling products such as customer-service copilots, document extraction, AML alert copilots, claims workflow assistance, call summarization, knowledge assistants, underwriting support tools that do not make final decisions, internal productivity copilots, agent-assist tools, and analytics assistants to banks, insurers, payment institutions, leasing groups, fintechs, or regulated financial infrastructure companies.

Acute trigger:

A named bank or insurer customer sends a live AI governance, model-risk, procurement, risk, security, compliance, or production-approval request before renewal, rollout, expansion, access, or procurement can proceed. The request asks for evidence around AI use-case boundary, model/system inventory, input/output data, training or fine-tuning posture, no-training terms, retrieval sources, evals, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, escalation/fallback, logging, monitoring, access control, model-change process, incident handling, explainability limits, and customer-specific risk controls.

Control point:

Prepaid live customer-request case with exact bank/insurer request text, vendor evidence room, product/use-case map, AI system inventory, data-flow map, model/provider configuration, eval artifacts where available, logging and monitoring evidence, human-review and fallback workflow, risk-limit wording, change-control notes, unsafe-answer list, reviewer notes, customer-facing response matrix, and gap split into answerable now, technical/security remediation needed, legal/privacy review needed, management-approved statement needed, and unsafe to answer.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:

Three prepaid live customer-request cases at 18,000-45,000 PLN each from at least two vendors. At least two different regulated-finance customers represented by incoming requests. One AI governance/model-risk reviewer or senior AI security reviewer on paid per-case terms. Two customer-progress outcomes: renewal/procurement moves to next step, buyer narrows follow-up, rollout blocker is downgraded, production approval continues, risk team accepts the evidence scope, or the vendor reaches a clean no-go/remediation route before overpromising. First 70,000-120,000 PLN collected.

6-month POC:

Complete 10-18 paid live-request packs, collect 250,000-650,000 PLN, document 6+ customer-progress/no-go outcomes, get at least two repeat channels from AI governance advisers, financial-sector cyber firms, fractional CISOs, privacy lawyers, or VC/PE portfolio operators, keep gross margin above 60% after reviewer costs, reject at least 35% of cases that require legal classification, formal model validation, regulated decisioning, raw customer data, clinical/HR/credit final-decision support, or product remediation before useful evidence can be produced.

First acquisition mechanism:

Build a target list of Polish/CEE AI workflow vendors with public bank, insurer, payments, leasing, fintech, or financial-infrastructure customers. Send precise outbound asking only for live bank/insurer AI governance or model-risk blockers, not generic AI readiness. In parallel, approach AI governance advisers, financial-sector cyber firms, privacy lawyers, fractional CISOs, and boutique DORA/GRC firms with an overflow offer: they keep legal, security, remediation, and strategic advisory work while the startup handles urgent vendor-side evidence collation and response packaging for live AI customer requests.

Economics:

Charge 18,000-28,000 PLN for a single live bank/insurer AI evidence request, 30,000-45,000 PLN for a 7-business-day renewal/procurement rescue with reviewer pass, and 8,000-15,000 PLN for annual refresh or changed-since-last-review update. The fee is justified only when a material renewal, procurement, rollout, expansion, or strategic regulated-finance customer is at risk.

Copy risk:

AI governance consultants, Big Four teams, cyber/security firms, privacy lawyers, DORA/GRC advisers, model-risk consultants, ISO 42001 advisers, and internal bank risk teams can copy pieces. The defense is narrow vendor-side live-request focus, local Polish/CEE regulated-finance vendor outreach, fast response packaging, reusable bank-question wording, and case memory by AI use case, evidence artifact, unsafe claim, reviewer note, buyer pushback, and accepted response pattern.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:

Once the vendor routes a live bank/insurer AI evidence request, signs the prepaid case, grants evidence-room access, and uses the startup's response matrix under the renewal or procurement clock, another provider must displace an active evidence workflow. The controlled unit is the exact customer request, vendor evidence folder, AI use-case map, reviewer notes, response wording, gap split, and customer progress or pushback outcome.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:

The startup accepts only live bank/insurer customer-request cases with prepaid fees and named evidence folders. The output is a buyer-ready response pack for a specific renewal, procurement, rollout, production-approval, or access blocker. It is not an AI governance readiness report, AI Act legal memo, model validation, dashboard, database, marketplace, GRC app, or brokered expert call.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:

The startup does not give legal advice, classify AI systems under law, certify model safety, validate model performance, perform formal model-risk approval, handle raw bank customer data, make credit/insurance/HR/clinical decisions, negotiate contract clauses, or represent the vendor to regulators. The vendor remains responsible for truthful statements, management approval, product remediation, customer communication, legal/privacy decisions, and contractual commitments. Legal, privacy, security, model-validation, and remediation specialists handle flagged issues where needed.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:

Closest confirmed idea is DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack. This is materially different because DORA is ICT operational-resilience and third-party-risk evidence for regulated-finance supplier reviews. This idea is AI-system behavior and model-risk evidence for bank/insurer AI governance requests: model/system inventory, evals, hallucination/error controls, human oversight, logging, monitoring, fallback, change control, and AI-specific unsafe-answer boundaries. It should be killed if buyers treat it as generic DORA/GRC questionnaire work rather than a repeatable AI model-risk evidence blocker.

Strongest anticipated objections:

The market may be crowded with AI governance consultants, cyber firms, lawyers, and Big Four teams. Unknown-founder credibility is weak without a named senior AI governance or model-risk reviewer. Some bank requests require real model validation, legal classification, privacy review, remediation, or product changes rather than evidence packaging. Bank acceptance remains outside founder control. The idea could collapse into generic AI questionnaire support or look like a DORA line extension unless AI-specific request patterns repeat.
