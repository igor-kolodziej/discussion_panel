# Simulated Round 83: Statutory Document Rights And Settlement Assets

Created: 2026-05-27

## Round Premise

After the round 81 and 82 failures, this round avoids evidence-only workflows and care/health trust surfaces. It starts from the question: what can a solo founder legally control, designate, assign, reserve, title, or route within 60 days before incumbents can copy the specific asset?

The strongest category in this round is a named statutory document stream or settlement right where:

- the seller/source can sign exclusivity or direct payment instructions before the document/cash is released;
- the buyer can place a deposit against named mass, lots, contracts, accounts, or credits;
- the first proof is a live paid allocation or cash movement, not a report.

## Source Anchors For The Lead Candidate

- BDO announced that from 2026-01-01 applications and issuance of packaging-waste DPR and EDPR documents for periods from 2026 must be handled through individual BDO accounts in the Confirmation Module: https://bdo.mos.gov.pl/news/modul-potwierdzen-w-zakresie-dpr-i-edpr/
- Interzero publicly offers repurchase of DPR and EDPR documents and says recovery organisations and producers use the documents to fulfil packaging recovery/recycling obligations and avoid product-fee exposure: https://interzero.pl/en/uslugi/gospodarka-odpadami/odkup-dpr-i-edpr/
- Public market pages from IOOO and SKELV show that DPR/EDPR purchase and sale exists as a private transaction market: https://iooo.pl/en/purchase-sale-of-dpr-edpr-packaging/ and https://skelv.pl/en/purchase-sale-of-dpr-edpr/
- Eko Cykl describes DPR as confirming packaging-waste recycling by material and mass and notes the 2026 shift into BDO's Confirmation Module, with thousands of documents and historical error/invalidity risk in the old process: https://ekocykl.pl/dokumenty-potwierdzajace-recykling-dpr-edpr-w-bdo-uporzadkowanie-systemu-od-2026-roku/

## Lead Candidate: BDO e-DPR Allocation Lockbox

### Buyer

Packaging recovery organisations, environmental-compliance operators, and producers/importers that need material-specific 2026 DPR/EDPR documents to satisfy packaging recycling obligations or reduce product-fee exposure.

### Acute Trigger

From 2026-01-01, DPR/EDPR applications and issuance for packaging-waste periods from 2026 run through the BDO Confirmation Module. Buyers and recovery organisations need valid material-specific documents, while small recyclers, exporters, collectors, or waste generators may have document rights but weak monetisation/admin capacity.

### Transferable Control Point

The founder controls a named document allocation only when all of the following are signed before serious work:

- source-side exclusive allocation mandate for named material, mass range, quarter, BDO source entity, and recipient-designation workflow;
- buyer-side deposit or take-or-pay reservation for the same material, mass, and quarter;
- direct payment direction into a lockbox/escrow account, with release to the source after buyer verification and BDO recipient acceptance;
- anti-circumvention period covering the named document stream;
- environmental-law/compliance reviewer boundaries confirming the founder is not certifying recycling, issuing documents, or advising on product-fee law.

The source entity remains the registered BDO account holder, applicant, recycler/exporter, and document issuer where applicable. The founder does not own the waste, create recycling proof, certify mass, or use another party's BDO account unlawfully.

### Sixty-Day Signed/Titled/Assigned/Prepaid Proof

Within 60 days and under 100,000 PLN:

- sign one source-side exclusive mandate over 50-300 tonnes of Q2 2026 packaging-waste DPR/EDPR document potential for one material stream;
- verify the source entity's BDO registration, material category, KPO/quarter data availability, and authority to request or issue the relevant document;
- sign one buyer deposit or take-or-pay reservation for at least part of the named mass;
- have buyer and source both acknowledge the lockbox/payment-direction terms;
- submit or support the first BDO Confirmation Module application/designation flow where permitted;
- close at least one paid allocation or record a written failure reason from buyer/source/reviewer.

### First Acquisition Mechanism

Source-side outreach:

"Your 2026 DPR/EDPR documents are now handled in BDO. If you have surplus packaging-waste document potential, I will not ask for a general brokerage listing. I want a short exclusive mandate over one named material/quarter stream, then I bring a deposit-backed buyer and route payment through a lockbox."

Buyer-side outreach:

"I have a named 2026 DPR/EDPR source stream with BDO-registration, material, quarter, mass range, and source-side exclusivity. If it fits your material obligation, reserve it with a deposit and payment releases only after your verification and BDO recipient acceptance."

### Economics

The first proof should be deposit-funded. The founder should not speculate on documents without buyer reservation.

Revenue options:

- allocation fee paid by buyer;
- percentage spread between source payout and buyer price;
- fixed minimum per-stream fee for admin custody, lockbox, and allocation coordination.

The model only advances if one 50-300 tonne stream can create at least 8,000-25,000 PLN gross profit or fee potential with limited founder labor, and if two or three streams can plausibly cover a 6-month POC without paid ads or inventory capital.

### Copy-Risk Defense

Recovery organisations and waste brokers can copy the category. They cannot copy a specific stream once the source signs exclusivity, buyer deposit is paid, and payment direction is acknowledged. The defensible asset is not knowledge of DPR/EDPR. It is the portfolio of named, source-authorised, buyer-reserved document streams and the acceptance history by material/source/buyer.

### Why It Is Not A Service, Report, App, Dashboard, Marketplace, Or Generic Broker

No value is counted unless the founder controls a named document stream and payment route. The startup is not selling BDO training, product-fee advice, environmental consulting, a document database, or introductions. It is an allocation lockbox over a statutory document stream where both source and buyer have signed around a specific mass/material/quarter.

### Safety, Legal, And Boundary Notes

- Registered recyclers/exporters/waste entities remain responsible for legal recycling, waste records, applications, issuance, and BDO account actions.
- Buyers and their compliance advisers remain responsible for obligation calculation and acceptance of documents.
- The founder must not falsify mass, recycling proof, KPO data, waste origin, recipient data, or product-fee calculations.
- Any transaction touching BDO access, powers of attorney, payment lockbox, or environmental obligations needs Polish legal/compliance review before execution.
- Streams with unclear entitlement, suspicious KPO records, invalid BDO status, disputed mass, or pressure to "manufacture" documents are rejected.

### Duplicate-Risk Check

- Not DORA: the asset is a statutory recycling-document allocation and payment stream, not an ICT supplier evidence pack.
- Not REDBlocked: there is no Amazon account-health channel or marketplace evidence request.
- Not BioSignal: no health data, patient trust, clinical claims, or investor memo.
- Not TraceFaktura: no municipal invoice event ledger; the control point is a named document/payment allocation.
- Not HeritageDoor: no reclaimed building inventory; the asset is a regulatory document stream, not a physical matched lot.

## Internal Objections Before Real Gate

- Incumbent recovery organisations already buy DPR/EDPR directly and may have superior trust, compliance, and price discovery.
- Legal transferability is subtle: the founder must confirm that exclusive commercial allocation, payment direction, and recipient designation are lawful without improperly using a BDO account or acting as an unauthorised environmental consultant.
- Buyer deposits may be hard for an unknown founder unless the first source stream is unusually clean and the payment path protects both sides.
- The first 60-day proof depends on Q2 2026 timing and source readiness; if documents can only be issued after quarter close, proof may be an accepted reservation/application rather than final cash.
- The market may compress spreads if document supply is transparent inside the BDO module.
