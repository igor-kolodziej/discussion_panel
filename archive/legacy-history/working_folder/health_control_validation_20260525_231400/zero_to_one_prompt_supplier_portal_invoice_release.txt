Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
SupplierPortal Invoice Release Desk

One-sentence thesis:
A prepaid and success-tied payment-release desk for CEE B2B suppliers whose approved enterprise invoices or POs are stuck because Ariba, Coupa, Oracle, SAP Business Network, vendor-master, EDI, bank-validation, tax-form, PO-flip, goods-receipt, or customer AP portal workflows have not accepted the supplier or invoice state.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE manufacturers, industrial distributors, component suppliers, SaaS vendors, professional-services firms, defense-prime suppliers, laboratory/scientific suppliers, equipment sellers, and other B2B vendors with named approved invoices, approved POs, delivered goods/services, or buyer-confirmed payables owed by large enterprise, public-sector-adjacent, university, hospital, prime-contractor, retail, telecom, energy, industrial, or multinational customers. The economic buyer is the owner, CFO, controller, AR lead, export lead, customer-success lead, or operations lead responsible for releasing cash already earned from the large customer.

Acute trigger:
The supplier has a written buyer AP/procurement/vendor-master/portal blocker saying payment cannot release until a specific process state clears: supplier registration incomplete, bank account verification pending, tax or residency form rejected, legal name/address mismatch, PO-flip failed, invoice line does not match the PO or goods receipt, EDI invoice rejected, vendor record inactive, portal administrator missing, payment method not accepted, AP ticket stalled, customer requires anti-fraud revalidation, or remittance is blocked by a portal state even though the buyer does not dispute the commercial obligation.

Control point:
Signed limited mandate over one named customer and invoice queue; buyer AP/procurement blocker text; approved PO or buyer acknowledgement; invoice list and invoice PDFs/XML/EDI logs; delivery or service acceptance evidence where available; portal administrator access where authorized; vendor-master profile; bank verification package; tax/business registration evidence; PO-flip or EDI correction trail; AP ticket log; customer contact map; remittance status; and no-go rules for actual debtor disputes, missing delivery, insolvency, legal/tax advice, fraud review, sanctions, fake bank data, or cases where the buyer does not acknowledge a payable.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign eight prepaid cases tied to named customers and approved invoices or POs; put at least 1,000,000 PLN of unpaid invoice value under mandate; collect at least 80,000 PLN in prepaid or success-tied fees; obtain five buyer-accepted portal, vendor-master, invoice, PO-flip, EDI, bank-validation, or AP-ticket outcomes or clean no-go decisions; obtain three paid invoice outcomes or buyer remittance confirmations; and keep a rejection log above 40% for commercial disputes, missing delivery, buyer insolvency, legal/tax questions, weak invoice value, generic onboarding, unsupported portals, or cases where the buyer has not acknowledged a payable.

6-month POC:
Handle 35-80 payment-release cases across 20-45 suppliers; put 8,000,000-30,000,000 PLN of invoice value under mandate; collect 350,000-1,200,000 PLN in fees; achieve 20+ paid, remittance-confirmed, portal-accepted, vendor-master-accepted, AP-progress, or clean no-go outcomes; build a private case taxonomy by customer, portal, blocker type, country, evidence item, bank-validation path, PO/invoice mismatch, EDI failure, buyer AP queue, elapsed time, and acceptance outcome; source repeat channels from fractional CFOs, export advisers, AR outsourcing firms, ERP implementers, EDI consultants, accountants, and industrial sales agencies; and maintain gross margin above 60% after AP-ops contractor, EDI/portal specialist, document QA, and secure workflow costs.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound to CEE suppliers that visibly sell to large enterprises, primes, universities, telecoms, energy companies, industrial groups, retailers, hospitals, or multinational customers. The ask is only for live cash blockers: "If a large customer has approved the PO or invoice but will not release payment because Ariba, Coupa, Oracle, SAP Business Network, vendor-master, EDI, PO-flip, bank validation, tax form, or AP ticket status is stuck, send the blocker text and invoice list. I turn it into a payment-release packet, portal correction trail, AP escalation file, and paid/remittance/no-go outcome." Partner outreach goes to fractional CFOs, accountants, AR outsourcing firms, ERP/EDI consultants, and export advisers that see trapped enterprise invoices but do not want to chase buyer portals.

Economics:
Charge 6,000-12,000 PLN prepaid for a narrow portal/vendor-master/invoice release case under 250,000 PLN of invoice value, 12,000-30,000 PLN for urgent or multi-invoice cases, and 3-8% success fee on released cash only where the customer has acknowledged the payable and the case does not create collection-agency or legal issues. Fees are justified only when named unpaid invoice value is materially larger than the fee. Contractors are paid for portal/EDI/AP operations, not for legal claims or debtor negotiation.

Copy risk:
Supplier finance teams, accountants, AR outsourcing firms, ERP consultants, EDI consultants, customer-success teams, collections agencies, procurement consultants, and buyer AP teams can solve parts of this workflow. The defense is narrow cash-release qualification, CEE cross-border supplier focus, repeated portal/vendor-master/AP blocker memory, fast evidence-room assembly, and active control over the named invoice queue, AP ticket chronology, portal state, and remittance proof once the mandate is signed.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the supplier signs the mandate, prepays, grants authorized portal visibility, provides the approved invoice queue, buyer blocker text, AP ticket trail, bank/tax/company evidence, EDI logs where relevant, and customer contact map, the startup controls the active payment-release file and response chronology for that named customer and invoice set. A consultant can compete for future cases, but cannot control the same buyer AP ticket, portal state, and paid-invoice proof without the supplier switching mid-blocker and restarting the evidence trail.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell generic supplier onboarding, collections, AR outsourcing, an AP dashboard, an EDI tool, a vendor database, or introductions to customer AP contacts. It accepts only named invoice or PO blockers where a large customer has acknowledged a payable or process path, then produces operational artifacts: corrected portal/vendor-master state, bank-validation package, PO-flip or EDI correction trail, AP escalation file, payment-release evidence room, remittance confirmation, paid invoice, or clean no-go.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
Do not act as a collection agency, lawyer, tax adviser, accountant of record, payment institution, bank account custodian, sanctions adviser, fraud investigator, or buyer representative. Do not pressure disputed debtors, negotiate commercial disputes, provide legal or tax opinions, change bank details without supplier officer authorization, certify delivery, fabricate documents, or handle cases involving insolvency, fraud, sanctions, fake bank data, false company information, missing delivery, unresolved goods-receipt disputes, or customer refusal to acknowledge a payable. Suppliers remain responsible for invoice truth, delivery facts, tax forms, bank details, company identity, and customer relationship. Buyers remain responsible for AP approval and payment.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not PromoLeak because it does not recover retailer promotional leakage or deductions. It is not KSeF, PEPPOL, e-invoicing, credit-memo recovery, unapplied-cash cleanup, factor reserve release, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, freight audit, SAM/NCAGE, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, HeatQuiet, or a generic evidence desk. The controlled object is a named approved unpaid invoice queue stuck in a buyer AP/vendor-master/portal/payment process, and the proof is buyer acceptance, remittance, paid invoice, or clean no-go.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Many suppliers already have finance teams, accountants, AR staff, ERP/EDI consultants, or customer-success people who can chase AP portals. Large-customer AP teams may ignore third parties. Some blockers are actually commercial disputes, missing delivery, goods-receipt mismatches, tax/legal problems, fraud reviews, or buyer insolvency outside founder control. Portal processes vary by customer and may not compound quickly. Small invoices cannot support the fee. Success fees may create collection-agency or legal boundary issues if not scoped tightly. The founder needs trust to handle bank, tax, invoice, and enterprise customer data. The first cases may become messy AR outsourcing unless the desk accepts only written buyer-process blockers on material approved invoices.
