## Internal Simulation For Lead

### Customer Concentration Price-Retention Pack - 88.6

One-sentence thesis: Lower-middle-market sellers with an active LOI or buyer diligence process will pay for a fast customer-concentration price-retention pack when a buyer, lender, or QoE adviser uses top-customer risk to demand a price cut, escrow, earnout, delayed close, or financing condition.

Why it clears simulation:

- The payment event is large and current: a single concentration objection can move purchase price, escrow, earnout, or financing by hundreds of thousands or millions of PLN.
- The trigger is externally visible inside the deal: buyer, lender, QoE, IC, or legal diligence has written the objection.
- The first proof is not interest: prepaid mandate, live LOI/diligence process, customer evidence room, submitted concentration packet, and buyer/lender progress or clean no-go.
- The founder does not value the company, give legal advice, opine on fairness, or negotiate terms. The artifact is a fact pattern that lets existing banker, CFO, counsel, or management answer a narrow objection.
- The work can be repeatable across B2B services, niche manufacturing, distribution, MSPs, vertical software, industrial services, and project-based businesses where concentration looks worse than the underlying renewal or switching-cost facts.
- Case memory can compound around accepted evidence objects: customer tenure, renewal rhythm, multi-site use, named sponsor depth, gross margin by account, pipeline replacement, contract term, purchase-order history, expansion record, switching cost, reference-call prep, and risk-mitigation commitments.

Internal risks:

- Bankers, sell-side QoE firms, fractional CFOs, and M&A advisers already own many seller preparation workflows.
- Access to live deals is relationship-heavy.
- If the top customer is genuinely leaving or contracts are weak, the correct output is no-go.
- Success-fee attribution is messy; fixed fees should dominate.
- Buyers may not change terms even with a better packet because they have negotiating leverage.
- The artifact can look like a report/service unless it is tied to a live written retrade/holdback/financing condition and submitted through the existing deal owner.

Internal gate notes:

- Not capped below 82: concrete 6-month proof exists through prepaid live deal mandates, buyer objection text, and deal-progress outcomes.
- Not capped below 85: generic consultants can copy future workflows but cannot instantly control a signed current-deal evidence room and submitted buyer-objection response once the seller mandates the file.
- Barely not capped below 87: CAC is the weak point, but the economics can tolerate high-touch referral acquisition because each paid case can be 30,000-90,000 PLN against a much larger price-retention decision.
- No clinical, tax, securities, legal, valuation, audit, or regulated-advice authority is assumed; all formal opinions remain with the seller's existing advisers.
