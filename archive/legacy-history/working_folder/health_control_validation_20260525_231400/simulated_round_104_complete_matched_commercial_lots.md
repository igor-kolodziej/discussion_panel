## Search Implication

The lead candidate must be stricter than generic liquidation:

- exact hotel-room package lot, not random furniture;
- seller option/title and removal window;
- buyer deposits before major capital/storage exposure;
- spec cards and room-count mapping;
- no mattresses/soft goods unless buyer explicitly accepts condition/hygiene;
- no broad auction marketplace or furnishing consultancy.
