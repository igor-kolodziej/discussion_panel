## Strongest Objections Captured

1. Many apparent credits are not collectible because claims may be expired, technically rejected, missing return proof, missing product registration, tied to wrong serials, or based on weak installation evidence.
2. Labor reimbursement is not universal; some OEM warranties exclude labor, travel, uninstalling, reinstalling, or contractor costs unless separately agreed.
3. Claim windows can kill old ledgers, so apparent face value may be dead value.
4. Per-line economics may collapse if many recoveries are only a few hundred PLN and require repeated follow-up.
5. Installers may not trust an unknown founder with portal/account evidence, distributor statements, invoices, job photos, and account balances.
6. OEMs and distributors may refuse third-party follow-up or require the installer to co-sign every escalation.
7. The workflow is copyable once the checklist is visible to installers, accountants, OEMs, distributors, or warranty admins.
8. Broad vertical coverage weakens execution because PV, heat pumps, refrigeration, EV chargers, and security have different warranty rules and payor behaviors.
9. The business can drift into warranty-dispute handling if most cases require arguing technical eligibility.

## Useful Narrowing

For a future non-gate pilot, the evaluator would accept only cases with:

- RMA number;
- serial number;
- payor acknowledgement or portal/account evidence;
- shipment/return proof where required;
- invoice or service report;
- claim still inside the relevant window;
- installer mandate for follow-up.

Suggested pricing was a 1,500-3,000 PLN screened-ledger fee plus 15-25% of actual recovered credit, not claimed value.

## Search Lesson

Even debtor/payor-acknowledged recovery ideas fail if the recoverable pool is ambiguous, low-ticket, or easy for the customer to internalize after seeing the checklist. Next candidates need a stronger current-cash control point, a larger and more binary payment event, or a live external buyer/payment gate that cannot be solved by ordinary admin follow-up.
