Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CO2TollClass Refund Mandate

One-sentence thesis:
CEE truck fleets may be overpaying German tolls when eligible vehicles remain in the default or wrong CO2 emission class, and the startup takes signed fleet mandates to assemble vehicle evidence, submit or guide Toll Collect class corrections, and recover refunds, accepted offsets, or verified future toll savings.

Exact buyer:
Polish, Czech, Slovak, Lithuanian, Romanian, Hungarian, and other CEE road-freight fleets, leasing fleets, and fleet-heavy transport groups with Euro VI trucks first registered after 1 July 2019, meaningful German mileage, Toll Collect or EETS statements, and weak internal ownership of CO2-class evidence.

Acute trigger:
German truck tolls include CO2 emission class as a tariff feature. Toll Collect assigns vehicles to CO2 emission class 1 by default, while higher classes require evidence such as registration documents, manufacturer customer information files, certificates of conformity, or other accepted vehicle evidence. Fleets discover that trucks with large German mileage may have paid class-1 rates even when a better class or corrected vehicle data may apply, or Toll Collect requests proof/correction for unconfirmed vehicle data.

Control point:
The startup accepts only named fleet batches with toll statements, vehicle registration/VIN list, Toll Collect or EETS account evidence, possible CO2 class delta, customer-portal authority or guided-submission mandate, document checklist, application/refund log, before/after rate calculation, and fee right tied to approved refund, accepted offset, confirmed class correction, verified future toll saving, or clean no-go.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Within 60 days, sign three fleet mandates covering at least 100 vehicles and 1,500,000 PLN equivalent annual German toll spend; identify at least 30 vehicles with plausible CO2 class or vehicle-data correction potential; file or guide at least 20 customer-portal correction applications with registration/CoC/CIF or other accepted evidence; obtain at least five Toll Collect class-change confirmations, refund/offset acknowledgements, or official clean no-go responses; document at least 150,000 PLN equivalent of refunds, accepted offsets, or first-year verified toll savings; and collect or invoice at least 25,000 PLN in fixed and success fees.

6-month POC:
Close 12-25 fleet mandates or leasing/fleet-accountant portfolio batches; screen 1,000-3,000 vehicles and 15-60 million PLN equivalent annual German toll spend; submit or guide 250-800 correction/refund applications; obtain 75-250 class changes, refunds, offsets, or official no-go decisions; document 1-5 million PLN equivalent in refunds, offsets, or first-year verified savings; collect 200,000-800,000 PLN in fees; secure three repeat channels from fleet accountants, truck dealers, leasing firms, EETS/fleet-card advisers, or transport associations; keep gross margin above 65%; and keep rejected/no-go vehicles above 40% if evidence or eligibility is weak.

First acquisition mechanism:
Start with channels that already see toll statements but do not want vehicle-evidence reconstruction: fleet accountants, transport-company bookkeepers, leasing-fleet managers, truck dealers, EETS/fleet-card advisers, and Polish/CEE transport associations. The direct written hook is: "If your German toll statements still show CO2 class 1 or unconfirmed vehicle data for newer Euro VI trucks, I run a no-win/no-go evidence check and handle only named vehicles where the rate delta is measurable."

Economics:
Charge 4,000-15,000 PLN fixed setup for a named fleet batch, plus 10-20% of refunds or accepted offsets and 10-20% of first-year verified toll savings for corrected vehicles, capped per vehicle if needed. Do not advance cash, hold client funds, appeal fines, or provide tax/legal advice. Costs are toll-statement parsing, vehicle-document collection, CoC/CIF requests where customer-authorized, portal workflow tracking, German/Polish support, and quality review.

Copy risk:
Toll-card providers, EETS providers, truck dealers, leasing companies, accountants, fleet managers, and Toll Collect self-service can all solve parts of this. The wedge exists where nobody owns the evidence reconstruction across toll statements, VINs, registrations, CoC/CIF documents, portal screenshots, and refund/savings attribution. The model is copyable if it becomes broad toll audit, so it must stay tied to named vehicle corrections and documented cash/savings outcomes.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
The startup controls only a named fleet batch after the fleet signs the mandate and shares toll statements, vehicle lists, evidence files, portal authority or guided-submission process, before/after rate calculations, application IDs, action logs, and fee terms. A toll-card provider or accountant can copy future work, but it cannot instantly own the current correction ledger, evidence room, application sequence, and fee right without the fleet replacing the mandate mid-case.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup does not sell a toll dashboard, fleet audit report, freight bill audit, brokered consultant introduction, generic EETS support, route optimization, or compliance software. It accepts named vehicles and produces one of four operational outcomes: class correction, refund/offset acknowledgement, verified future toll saving, or official clean no-go.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide legal advice, tax/VAT advice, appeal fines, dispute violations, falsify or alter vehicle documents, decide legal eligibility, operate as payment provider, hold client funds, or submit anything without fleet authorization and source evidence. Fleet owners, Toll Collect/EETS providers, vehicle manufacturers, accountants, and legal advisers remain responsible for their own decisions. Reject cases involving forged documents, disputed ownership, violation appeals, sanctions issues, unclear vehicle authority, tax-only invoice corrections, fines, or vehicles without source evidence.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not PromoLeak, Amazon Vendor Central recovery, carrier-credit recovery, 3PL overbilling, credit-memo cash-out, insurance return-premium cash-out, KSeF/VoP payment release, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, BatteryFit, Supplement Stack Safety, or HeatQuiet. It is a toll-portal vehicle-data correction and refund/savings mandate, not retailer deductions, customs import continuity, software evidence, product compliance, or generic freight audit.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Toll Collect self-service, EETS providers, toll-card advisers, truck dealers, leasing companies, accountants, and internal fleet managers can handle many corrections. Many vehicles are correctly class 1 and will produce no refund. Retroactive refunds may be limited. CoC/CIF documents may be unavailable or expensive. Future savings are less compelling than cash refunds. Fleets may resist sharing toll statements and vehicle documents with an unknown founder. Cold outreach may be noisy unless channel-routed. The business can degrade into generic toll audit or fines/appeals unless boundaries stay strict.
