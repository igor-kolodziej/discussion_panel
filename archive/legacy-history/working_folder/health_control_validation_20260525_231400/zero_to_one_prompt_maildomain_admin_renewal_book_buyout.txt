Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name: MailDomain Admin Renewal Book Buyout.

One-sentence thesis:
Acquire or option a retiring small web agency, hoster, MSP, or webmaster's SME domain/email/hosting renewal book, controlling specific domain lists, DNS/mail admin access, registrar/reseller relationships, renewal calendars, customer authorizations, customer-approved payment direction, and first current renewal cash before seller payout.

Exact buyer / customer:
Polish SMEs whose domains, DNS, business email, basic website hosting, SSL certificates, and sometimes Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace billing are managed by a small local web agency, freelancer, hoster, or MSP. Typical customers are clinics, restaurants, service firms, local retailers, schools, associations, small manufacturers, accountants, lawyers, repair shops, and local chains that cannot tolerate email/domain downtime.

Source sellers:
Retiring or overloaded small web agencies, freelance webmasters, cPanel/Plesk hosters, tiny MSPs, local domain resellers, and one-person "IT guy" operators with renewal calendars and customer invoices but poor succession.

Acute trigger:
Domain, DNS, SSL, hosting, and email failures are immediate business disruptions. A missed domain renewal, expired card, lapsed SSL certificate, DNS misconfiguration, lost admin contact, blocked mailbox, or email deliverability failure can stop inbound leads, invoicing, logins, and customer communication. Many SME owners do not know who controls the registrar account, DNS zone, hosting panel, or mail tenant; they just pay the old web agency when renewal invoices arrive.

Transferable control point:
- Seller option or earnout over a defined customer/domain/email/hosting renewal book.
- Device/account appendix listing each customer, domain, registrar, registrant, DNS provider, hosting server, mail platform, SSL status, renewal dates, admin contacts, support history, pricing, and open risks.
- Customer authorization naming the founder/new entity as renewal billing and continuity manager while the customer remains the domain registrant and tenant owner.
- Registrar, hosting, DNS, Microsoft/Google reseller or partner-of-record transfers where applicable and customer-approved.
- Authinfo/EPP codes or registrar account movement only with customer authorization.
- Support mailbox, phone number, documentation, billing templates, and historical invoices transferred from seller.
- Current-month or current-quarter renewal/support cash collected into the founder/new entity or lockbox before seller payout.

Exactly what is signed/titled/assigned/prepaid inside 60 days:
- Conditional seller option or renewal-book purchase/earnout agreement tied to retained customers and collected transferred revenue.
- Customer/domain appendix with renewal dates, account owners, admin contacts, access method, service scope, pricing, and known risks.
- Customer authorization forms approving new billing/payment direction and admin/continuity management for named domains, DNS, hosting, mail, SSL, and reseller subscriptions.
- Registrar/hosting/mail partner transfer actions or documented admin delegation for the first transferred customers.
- First batch of prepaid renewals or monthly/quarterly continuity retainers billed by the founder/new entity before final seller payout.

6-month proof:
1. Identify 40 retiring, overloaded, or succession-limited web agencies, hosters, local MSPs, and freelance webmasters in Poland.
2. Inspect 4 seller books with customer lists, domain/hosting/mail renewal calendars, 12-month invoices, gross margin, support burden, server/vendor dependencies, and customer concentration.
3. Sign one conditional option/earnout with upfront exposure below 50,000 PLN and total founder exposure below 100,000 PLN.
4. Obtain 40+ customer authorizations or 80+ domains/mail tenants under transferred renewal/admin management.
5. Transfer or delegate the first 20 customer accounts across registrar, DNS, hosting, SSL, Microsoft 365, or Google Workspace where applicable.
6. Collect 15,000-35,000 PLN current-month or current-quarter renewal/support cash under the founder/new entity.
7. Prove 55-75% gross margin after hosting/license costs, seller earnout, contractor support, backups, security tooling, accounting, and admin.
8. Document support load and reject any customer requiring bespoke website rebuilds, custom development, or open-ended helpdesk work unless separately prepaid.
9. Source a second seller book through the same acquisition channel.

First acquisition mechanism:
Seller pitch: "Your domain/email/hosting customers have real renewal value, but the book is hard to sell as a full agency because it includes old systems and personal trust. I can option the renewal book, keep customers stable, pay you an earnout only on retained paid customers, and let you step back without forcing a full business sale."

Customer pitch: "Your domain, DNS, email, SSL, and hosting continuity stay under your control. You remain the registrant and tenant owner. We only take over renewal billing, admin continuity, monitoring, and documented handoff from the previous provider so your email and domain do not break during succession."

Economics:
- Typical managed continuity pricing: 100-300 PLN/month for domain/DNS/email/hosting continuity for simple SMEs, or 1,200-3,000 PLN/year for basic annual continuity and renewal management, excluding large migrations or custom development.
- Additional pass-through revenue from domain renewals, hosting, SSL, Microsoft 365/Google Workspace licenses, backups, security monitoring, deliverability setup, mailbox migration, and emergency recovery.
- Target first book: 80-250 paying SMEs, 150-500 domains, 50-200 mail tenants or hosted sites, 120,000-500,000 PLN annualized managed renewal/support revenue.
- Upfront payment: 0-50,000 PLN, with seller earnout over 12-24 months tied to retained customers and collected revenue.
- Expected first 6-month cash: 15,000-35,000 PLN collected from current renewals/retainers before or at close; target monthly owner contribution after stabilization of 10,000-25,000 PLN if support scope is tightly bounded.
- Gross margin target: 55-75% after direct hosting/license/vendor costs and contractor support.
- No close unless customer-approved payment transfer and current renewal cash prove that the book is live.

Copy risk:
Large MSPs, registrars, and agencies exist, but they cannot copy a signed seller handoff, customer authorization, registrar/DNS/mail admin delegation, renewal calendar, and payment direction for the specific domains/accounts already transferred. The defensible asset is the customer-approved admin/payment book and continuity documentation, not generic hosting knowledge.

Why incumbents cannot copy before control is secured:
The founder targets small succession-limited operators where the seller introduces customers and transfers documentation before competitors know the book is in motion. Once customers authorize billing/admin transfer and current renewals are prepaid, another MSP would need to displace an already authorized continuity manager rather than merely pitch generic IT support.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, marketplace, or generic broker:
The business controls an acquired renewal/admin/payment book: customer authorizations, domain and DNS admin access, hosting/mail reseller or partner relationships, renewal calendars, and cash collection. Software may track expiries and access, but the value is the transferred book and payment stream.

Boundaries and legal/provider-of-record limits:
The founder does not transfer domain ownership, change registrants, access mailboxes, migrate tenants, or alter DNS without explicit customer authorization. Customers remain registrants and tenant owners. GDPR, data-processing, password custody, MFA, backup, incident-response, and liability terms are documented. Microsoft/Google/registrar/reseller transfers follow the relevant platform process. Bespoke development, legal disputes over domain ownership, hacked-account recovery, and regulated data systems are excluded unless handled under separate specialist engagement.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack: there is no bank-regulatory evidence renewal; the control point is a customer-approved domain/email/hosting admin and payment book.

This is not REDBlocked Amazon Connected-Device Evidence Rescue Desk: it does not route marketplace compliance tickets or Amazon evidence cases.

This is not BioSignal Claim-Risk Diligence Pack: it has no health-claim or investor-diligence component.

This is not TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot: it is not municipal event documentation or public-sector incident routing.

This is not HeritageDoor Suites: it is not a hospitality property or renovation asset play.

This is not OneStack Critical Connector Lockbox: OneStack was support around one critical connector/account. MailDomain controls a broader renewal/admin/payment book across domains, DNS, hosting, email, and customer authorizations, with cash-gated acquisition of an existing portfolio rather than a single integration rescue desk.

Strongest likely objections to test:
- Whether customer authorizations can be obtained quickly enough before close.
- Whether seller records are accurate enough to avoid hidden downtime/security liabilities.
- Whether support burden from old websites, mailboxes, DNS, and hosting destroys part-time economics.
- Whether managed continuity pricing is high enough beyond low-margin pass-through renewals.
- Whether customers perceive the handoff as a risk and switch to a known MSP or registrar.
- Whether large hosters/MSPs or the seller's existing platform can preempt the book.
