Official EU sources show EUDR application dates after the 2025 revision: 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and traders, and 30 June 2027 for most micro and small operators, with some timber/EUTR-covered micro/small operators applying from 30 December 2026. ## Why It Clears Internal Simulation

- It avoids future-readiness consulting by accepting only live customer/retailer/order blockers.
- The strongest first segment is Polish/CEE wood and furniture suppliers with EU retailer/private-label customers already asking for EUDR geolocation/origin evidence for 2027 collections and Q4 2026 shipments.
- Control proof is commercial: customer request, affected SKU/order/forecast value, supplier geolocation/origin documents, evidence room, no-go/substitution path, and customer acceptance/order progress.
- It is materially different from CBAM because the evidence objects are geolocation, deforestation-free status, supplier plots, chain of custody, country/commodity risk, and EUDR due diligence statement support, not embedded emissions/import carbon reporting.
