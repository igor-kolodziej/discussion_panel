## Why It Clears The Simulated Gate

- Timing is current: the tariff-preference opportunity began on 1 May 2026.
- The buyer trigger is commercial, not educational: a named order, customer landed-cost request, shipment, distributor onboarding, or first preference claim is blocked.
- The hard proof is external: broker-reviewed origin file, customer acceptance, import preference claim, order release, shipment progress, or clean no-go.
- The founder does not act as customs broker or legal adviser; qualified customs brokers and responsible exporters/importers remain provider of record.

## Internal Caps Applied

- Cap below 82 if the buyer wants generic Mercosur export strategy or market-entry research.
- Cap below 85 if the case lacks a named order, shipment, tariff-preference value, or customer/importer request.
- Cap below 87 if customs brokers can handle all useful cases without operational friction.
- Cap below 82 if the work requires legal origin rulings, controlled goods, sanctions, quotas, veterinary certificates, or regulated pharma decisions.

## Main Risk Before Real Validation

Zero To One may treat rules-of-origin files as normal customs-broker work. The candidate only deserves real validation if the current go-live, first-order tariff savings, SME document disorder, and customer/importer acceptance artifact overcome broker incumbent risk.
