Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
HeritageApothecary Complete Shopfit Suites

One-sentence thesis:
A controlled-inventory business that buys or options complete antique apothecary, pharmacy, herbalist, bookstore, and historic shopfit cabinet suites before closures or renovations split them, then sells measured, documented, full-interior suites to boutique hotels, restaurants, cocktail bars, luxury retail, offices, HNW homes, and heritage interior architects.

Exact buyer:
Boutique hotel owners, restaurateurs, cocktail-bar operators, luxury retail founders, wellness or concept-store owners, premium office designers, HNW homeowners, private-library clients, interior architects, heritage renovation firms, and boutique developers in Poland/CEE and EU-adjacent markets who want an authentic full-wall cabinet/counter/drawer suite as a functional decorative interior. The first buyer wants a coherent original apothecary or shop interior, not a single antique cabinet.

Acute trigger:
An old pharmacy, herbalist shop, bookstore, haberdashery, optical shop, watchmaker, library, institutional interior, or family business is closing, modernizing, selling premises, inheriting a fitted interior, or replacing antique cabinets. The complete suite is about to be split into individual cabinets, cherry-picked by antique dealers, damaged during fit-out demolition, or sold as ordinary furniture. On the demand side, a premium venue or home project needs a striking original interior feature with many drawers, glass-front cabinets, counters, shelving, ladders, labels, provenance, and matched proportions, while reproduction millwork or loose cabinets lack the same coherence and story.

Control point:
Written first-refusal, option, or purchase agreement over a complete fitted shop/apothecary cabinet suite before removal; title or clear title path from the owner; site photos and video of the intact interior; measured wall/counter layout; numbered disassembly map; titled possession of cabinets, counters, drawer banks, glass-front display units, shelves, ladders, plinths, cornices, hardware, labels, signage, and matching trim where recoverable; condition records for veneer, cracks, glass, locks, missing drawers, woodworm, moisture, paint, prior modifications, and hardware; palletized and wrapped storage by numbered section; buyer reservation deposits for full suites rather than individual pieces.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
Three written first-refusal or option agreements with pharmacy owners, old shop owners, estate executors, renovation firms, antique dealers, building administrators, or fit-out demolition crews; two complete shop/apothecary cabinet suites physically controlled or optioned with title path, site photos, measured layout, numbered disassembly map, and condition records; one furniture restorer or heritage carpenter willing to inspect and quote handoffs on paid terms; two buyer deposits of 5,000-20,000 PLN for full suites from hospitality, retail, HNW, office, or architecture buyers; one paid handoff or reservation contract; no restoration, storage-heavy purchase, or international transport started before buyer deposit.

6-month POC:
Control 5-10 complete shop/apothecary/library/shopfit suites; secure 6-10 buyer deposits; complete 3-6 paid handoffs; collect 250,000-1,000,000 PLN in raw-suite sales, reservation deposits, and restorer-handoff fees; maintain gross margin above 45% after acquisition, removal, transport, cleaning, condition recording, storage, restoration triage, insurance, and breakage allowance; build a private catalog by city, business type, period, wood, drawer count, cabinet run length, counter layout, glass condition, hardware completeness, provenance, fit constraints, buyer type, and sale outcome; get at least three architects/designers/hospitality buyers to ask for future suites before commissioning reproduction millwork.

First acquisition mechanism:
Supply side: build a list of 200 old pharmacies, herbalist shops, bookstores, antique dealers, estate clearers, renovation crews, fit-out demolition firms, building administrators, boutique developers, family-business brokers, and furniture restorers in Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan, Wroclaw, Gdansk, Sopot, and other historic towns. Pitch: "Call before a complete old shop or pharmacy interior is split or ripped out. I pay for full suites, document them on site, and arrange careful removal."

Demand side: build a list of 150 interior architects, boutique hotel designers, cocktail-bar and restaurant groups, luxury retail designers, boutique developers, premium office designers, HNW project managers, and heritage renovators. Pitch only controlled suites: "Reserve a complete measured apothecary/shopfit cabinet suite from one property, with site photos, disassembly map, condition notes, and restorer handoff. Full-suite deposits only; no loose-cabinet sourcing."

Economics:
Acquire or option complete suites for 10,000-60,000 PLN depending on rarity, access, size, condition, and seller urgency. Spend 5,000-25,000 PLN on careful removal, transport, cleaning, condition recording, wrapping, storage, insurance, and restoration triage. Sell raw documented suites for 50,000-200,000+ PLN and restorer-ready or high-provenance suites for 120,000-400,000+ PLN through buyer-selected carpenters/restorers. The startup captures spread by preserving and documenting the complete coherent interior before the demolition/antique market fragments it into lower-value individual cabinets.

Copy risk:
Antique dealers, architectural salvage yards, furniture restorers, interior architects, boutique developers, and demolition-connected resellers can copy pieces. The defense is upstream first-look density, fast cash/pickup reliability, full-suite documentation discipline, measured disassembly maps, buyer deposits before expensive removal, provenance records, gatekeeper memory of which suite types sell, and controlled inventory of exact interiors before competitors can access those properties.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once the startup signs the option or purchase agreement, documents the intact interior, maps and numbers the suite, controls title or possession, and takes a full-suite buyer deposit, competitors cannot sell that exact coherent shopfit suite. They can offer loose antique cabinets or reproduction millwork, but they cannot recreate the original property-level set, provenance, wall/counter layout, condition record, and reservation contract already controlled for that buyer.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup controls titled or optioned physical interiors and sells full, measured, documented shopfit suites with buyer deposits. It does not sell interior design, renovation management, furniture restoration, installation, software, a salvage marketplace, generic sourcing, a database, or brokered introductions. Cases without full-suite control, clear title path, measured disassembly map, and buyer deposit discipline are rejected.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not provide conservation-law advice, construction supervision, interior design, furniture restoration as provider of record, installation, building permissions, pharmacy licensing, antique authentication guarantees, pest-treatment certification, or structural/safety advice. Qualified restorers, carpenters, architects, conservation officers, engineers, owners, and contractors remain responsible for restoration, installation, permissions, fire/egress/code constraints, and final design decisions. Material with unclear title, protected heritage restrictions, disputed ownership, suspected theft, active museum/conservation claim, unsafe removal conditions, or incomplete provenance is rejected.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not HeritageDoor Suites because it does not control internal doors, frames, casings, hinges, handles, locks, or door-suite rhythm for apartments. The controlled unit is a complete shop/apothecary/library fitted cabinet interior with counters, drawers, shelves, glass, hardware, and measured wall layout, sold as a functional decorative interior feature. It is not HeritageKachel, HeritageWindow, HeritageRadiator, StormTile, hotel FF&E, DORA, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
Complete high-quality interiors may be rare, expensive, protected, or already controlled by antique dealers and restorers. Demand may be lumpy because each suite must fit a specific venue size, style, and budget. Reproduction millwork and individual antique cabinets are substitutes. Removal, transport, storage, insurance, woodworm/moisture treatment, glass breakage, missing drawers, and restoration triage can eat margin. Buyer deposits may be hard before physical inspection. Conservation/title risk is serious for historically important pharmacy interiors. The 100,000 PLN budget may only control one or two good suites. A slow sale can trap capital in bulky inventory. The business can drift into generic antique furniture dealing unless it only buys complete, provenance-backed suites with deposits.
