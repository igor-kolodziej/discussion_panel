## Strongest Objections

1. Supplier return windows and acceptance criteria may kill many old claims.
2. Missing RMA numbers, core tags, serials, job cards, shipment proof, portal records, pickup notes, branch return logs, or credit memo trails destroy recoverability.
3. Condition disputes are outside scope; damaged, incomplete, contaminated, late, wrong, or untagged cores may be denied.
4. Large dealer groups may already have parts directors, AP teams, branch controllers, and supplier reps handling this.
5. The first ledger rebuild can be labor-heavy and needs a minimum open value, likely 100,000 PLN per supplier account and preferably 250,000+ PLN.
6. Suppliers may resist old or ambiguous credits, claim credits were already issued, applied elsewhere, expired, denied, or offset.
7. Some categories involve regulated or hazardous physical handling, so the startup must remain documentation-only.
8. Success-fee attribution can be contested unless before-state supplier statements, claim logs, and after-state credit memos are clean.
9. Supplier/OEM fragmentation may slow learning because each has different core groups, windows, return paths, portals, labels, and denial reasons.
10. The desk can drift into warranty filing, physical returns, inventory consulting, legal disputes, or AP audit sprawl.

## Evaluator Advice

Best beachhead: multi-branch truck repair chains, agricultural machinery dealers, construction-equipment service firms, hydraulic service groups, HVAC compressor wholesalers, and parts distributors with supplier statements showing 100,000+ PLN in open core/exchange charges.

Accept only cases with a signed recovery mandate, named supplier account, supplier statement showing open core/exchange/deposit lines, AP invoice lines, part numbers and core charge lines, RMA/tag/job-card/shipment/portal evidence path, credit memo register, supplier policy/time-window screen, and prepaid ledger-rebuild fee.

Reject cases with no supplier statement, no return evidence, stale claims beyond supplier policy, condition dispute as main issue, physical return still needed, warranty adjudication, hazardous handling, legal dispute, low-value accounts, or "we think we returned it" with no proof trail.
