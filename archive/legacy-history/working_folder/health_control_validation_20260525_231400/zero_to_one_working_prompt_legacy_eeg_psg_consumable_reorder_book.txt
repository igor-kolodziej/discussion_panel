Evaluate this business idea from 0-100 and include the strongest objections or caveats behind the score.

Founder constraints: Warsaw-based data scientist with neuroscience/data/business background; unknown solo founder, possibly with a few contractors; maximum 100,000 PLN starting capital; 2-month POC/POV; low personal-brand/camera fit; part-time start.

Please cap the score below 85 if the idea lacks a concrete control point that can be secured, reserved, owned, routed, prepaid, assigned, or credibly proven during a 2-month POC/POV cycle by an unknown solo founder under these constraints. Cap below 87 if CAC, payback, gross margin, cash conversion, or owner earnings are not credible. Weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, margins, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and defensibility more heavily than originality. Penalize generic clinics, apps, reports, dashboards, marketplaces, coaching, patient-data businesses, and prescription-drug businesses without asset/payment-flow control.

Scored idea:

Legacy EEG/PSG Consumable + Reorder Book Buyout buys or options a retiring/closing micro-distributor, service technician, sleep-lab supplier, or neurodiagnostic lab's exact-SKU consumable/accessory stock and active reorder book for EEG/PSG/EMG/sleep-study labs. The startup controls titled stock, compatibility map, phone/domain/mailbox, customer reorder history, supplier contacts, and prepaid emergency/reorder packs.

The founder does not run EEG/PSG tests, diagnose, interpret signals, handle reports, access patient recordings, provide clinical advice, or market to patients. Clinics/labs remain providers of record. The business is a B2B spare/consumable continuity bank for professional buyers.

Buyer / payer:
- private EEG/EMG/PSG/sleep labs;
- neurology, psychiatry, sleep-medicine, ENT, pulmonology, occupational-medicine, and hospital labs with older neurodiagnostic systems;
- service technicians that need exact replacement cables/connectors/electrodes/sensors;
- small labs that lose revenue when a study is cancelled because one cap, belt, cable, airflow sensor, electrode, paste/gel, cannula, adapter, or headbox lead is missing.

Acute trigger:
A lab has paid tests scheduled but an old-system accessory fails or goes missing. OEM lead times, discontinued connectors, small order value, procurement friction, and poor compatibility knowledge can cancel private EEG/PSG nights or delay reports. The urgent buyer pays for exact compatible stock now, not a generic catalogue.

Control point:
The acquired/optioned asset is:
- titled exact-SKU inventory: EEG electrodes/leads/caps, PSG belts, airflow sensors/cannulas, oximeter probes, headbox cables, EMG leads, gels/pastes, adapters, chargers, dongles, and legacy connectors;
- SKU/serial/compatibility map by device model and lab;
- retiring supplier/service-technician customer list and reorder history;
- phone/domain/mailbox/WhatsApp handoff;
- supplier/distributor accounts and first-look rights to closing/upgrading lab accessory lots;
- condition, expiry, CE/instructions, lot, warranty, and storage records where relevant;
- prepaid emergency kits or monthly reorder packs from named labs;
- no patient data, no recordings, no test reports.

The hard control is: titled scarce stock + inherited reorder book + compatibility knowledge + prepaid B2B lab orders.

Two-month POC under 100,000 PLN:
- 5,000-10,000 PLN refundable/creditable option over a retiring reseller/service-technician reorder book;
- 20,000-45,000 PLN first exact-SKU stock lot, bought only after inspection and lab preorders;
- 5,000-10,000 PLN legal/medical-device distributor/advertising review and insurance;
- 5,000-10,000 PLN inventory system, labels, compatibility database, and shipment workflow;
- 5,000-15,000 PLN supplier deposits and emergency courier reserve.

Required proof:
1. Signed option/handoff over one reseller/service-technician/lab accessory reorder book with at least 20 active B2B lab contacts and recent order history.
2. Titled inspected inventory lot under 45,000 PLN, excluding expired, damaged, or legally questionable stock.
3. SKU/compatibility map covering at least 80% of the first lot by device/lab model.
4. 8-12 labs sign prepaid emergency-kit or monthly reorder-pack orders before final close.
5. 25,000+ PLN prepaid or invoiced first-month B2B orders.
6. First 20 paid shipments delivered with zero patient data and zero clinical claims.
7. Gross margin above 35% after stock cost, shipping, returns, damaged/obsolete reserve, seller earnout, and admin time.
8. Legal review confirms B2B professional-only medical-device supply boundaries and advertising/distributor obligations.

Economics:
Revenue comes from emergency replacement kits priced at a premium for exact compatibility, monthly/quarterly reorder packs for common electrodes/gels/pastes/cannulas/belts/probes/adapters, tested legacy cable/connector resale, and small retainers for reserved emergency stock for high-value labs.

Payback logic:
The founder does not buy broad catalogue inventory. The founder options an inherited reorder book, gets prepaid lab orders, then buys the exact stock lot needed to fulfill. Payback can happen in 1-3 inventory turns if the stock is scarce, clean, and matched to named labs.

Why this is not a service/report/dashboard/database/marketplace:
The business is not testing, interpretation, software, or a marketplace. It owns physical inventory and inherited order flow. A competitor can sell generic accessories, but cannot copy the specific titled stock lot, compatibility notes, old supplier phone trust, and first-month prepaid lab demand.

Copy risk:
OEMs, distributors, and online shops can sell common accessories. The defensible wedge is older systems, exact connectors, emergency availability, inherited reorder history, first-look rights to closing lab stock, and lab-specific compatibility notes. If the first lot is commodity stock, the score falls below 82.

Regulatory and data guardrails:
- No diagnosis, no interpretation, no testing, no patient recordings, no reports, no clinical advice.
- Clinics/labs remain providers of record and use accessories under their own professional process.
- Founder sells B2B professional supplies only and does not advertise patient-facing medical claims.
- Legal review covers distributor obligations, advertising limits, product documentation, CE/instructions/expiry/condition records, warranty/returns, and no questionable stock.

Main risks acknowledged:
- Commodity accessories and large distributors can compress margins.
- Some stock may be expired, damaged, counterfeit, noncompliant, or unsupported.
- Device compatibility errors can cause returns and trust loss.
- The reorder book may be stale or not transferable.
- Small labs may not prepay unless the stock is genuinely scarce.
- Legal/device distributor obligations must be checked before selling.
