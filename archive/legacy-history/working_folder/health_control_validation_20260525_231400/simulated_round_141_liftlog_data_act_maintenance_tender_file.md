## Why It Can Clear Simulation

The narrowest wedge is a live renewal/rebid file where the building owner already has a commercial reason to obtain data: the OEM/current maintenance provider is raising prices, uptime is poor, or an independent UDT-qualified firm cannot bid responsibly without fault/service history. The founder does not touch safety, maintenance, inspections, modernization, or lift-control systems. The founder controls the data-access workflow and tender artifact only.

The first proof is not "interest"; it is signed owner authorization, a Data Act access request, a data-holder response or portal export, a fault/service-history file, and at least one independent maintenance bid, price concession, or clean no-go.

- UDT/safety boundaries are serious; any drift into technical judgment or repair advice kills the idea.
- The confirmed Data Act compressed-air idea creates duplicate risk. The distinction must stay concrete: maintenance tender file for lifts, not energy-savings telemetry memo for compressors.
