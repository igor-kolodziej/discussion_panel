Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections.

Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time.

Idea name:
CableTender CPR Fire-Class Bid Packs

One-sentence thesis:
A 24-72 hour bid evidence pack for Polish/CEE cable distributors, electrical wholesalers, cable importers, and electrical contractors that maps a live public tender's cable fire-reaction requirements to the distributor's exact SKUs, Declarations of Performance, CE/CPR evidence, fire class, smoke/droplet/acidity class, datasheets, and missing supplier documents so the tender team can bid, no-bid, or correct evidence before disqualification.

Exact buyer:
Polish/CEE cable distributors, electrical wholesalers, low-voltage/telecom cable importers, and electrical contractors bidding public-sector, hospital, school, rail, municipal, office, and industrial tenders where cable fire-reaction class, CPR/CE evidence, and Declaration of Performance documents are required.

Best first buyers have 500-10,000 cable SKUs, messy brand/private-label aliases, public tender exposure, and a tender team that currently asks manufacturers or salespeople manually for DoPs, datasheets, and fire-class evidence under deadline pressure.

Acute trigger:
A live tender asks for exact cable evidence such as CPR Euroclass, smoke class, flaming droplets, acidity class, CE marking, Declaration of Performance, AVCP/certification evidence, manufacturer datasheet, installation/environmental restrictions, and exact SKU equivalence. The tender team has 24-72 hours to decide which catalog SKUs can be bid, which are disqualified, and which require supplier documents or clarification.

Control point:
One live tender plus one distributor catalog/SKU export turned into a bid evidence pack:
- tender requirement extraction;
- eligible SKU shortlist and rejected SKU list;
- CPR class and DoP match matrix;
- CE/DoP/certificate/datasheet links or attachment index;
- supplier/brand/private-label/part-number alias map;
- missing evidence and supplier RFI;
- bid/no-bid risk memo;
- strict "evidence only, no design/spec substitution" boundary.

60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof:
1. Three paid bid packs at 2,000-6,000 PLN each for live or recently closed tenders.
2. At least two buyers provide catalog/SKU exports or price lists.
3. At least one tender team uses the pack to bid, no-bid, request supplier evidence, correct a submission, or avoid a noncompliant SKU.
4. One electrical/cable standards reviewer available per pack for edge-case issue spotting.
5. A reusable matrix across tender language, CPR class, DoP, SKU aliases, supplier evidence, and buyer outcome.
6. One buyer agrees to paid monitoring for the next relevant cable tender or a second bid pack.

6-month POC:
Month 1: collect 40 live or recently closed Polish/CEE public tenders involving cables, low-smoke/halogen-free cables, telecom/data cables, fire-class requirements, hospitals, schools, rail, municipal buildings, offices, and industrial facilities. Build a target list of 50 cable/electrical distributors and contractors. Create a sample CPR/DoP bid matrix using public tender and public product documents.

Month 2: send evidence-led outbound to targets with one specific tender and one likely SKU/evidence gap. Close three paid bid packs. For each pack, ingest tender text, catalog/SKU export, datasheets, DoPs, CE/CPR evidence, aliases, and supplier documents. Deliver eligible/rejected SKU matrix, evidence index, missing-document RFI, and bid/no-bid summary.

Month 3: document whether at least one pack changes a commercial workflow: bid submitted, no-bid decision made, supplier documents requested, noncompliant SKU removed, clarification sent, or tender evidence corrected.

Months 4-6: complete 12-25 paid packs, collect 50,000-180,000 PLN, keep gross margin above 65% after reviewer costs, sign 2-4 recurring monitoring customers, and build a private tender-language-to-SKU-to-DoP evidence graph.

First acquisition mechanism:
Outbound pitch:
"This public tender appears to require cable CPR fire class / DoP evidence that may or may not match your catalog. Send the SKU list you would consider. I return a 24-72h bid pack: eligible SKUs, rejected SKUs, DoP/CE evidence links, missing supplier documents, and bid/no-bid risks. I do not design the system, substitute products, or give procurement/legal advice."

Partner pitch:
"You keep the supplier relationship and tender submission. I do the evidence matching when CPR fire class / DoP / cable SKU aliases are slowing the bid."

Economics:
Charge 2,000-3,500 PLN for a narrow one-tender/one-category pack, 3,500-6,000 PLN for a multi-lot or multi-brand cable pack, and 1,000-3,000 PLN/month for recurring tender monitoring once the buyer catalog is normalized. Reviewer cost is 300-1,500 PLN per pack only for edge cases. The buyer pays because a modest public cable/electrical tender can justify a few thousand PLN to avoid disqualification, bad no-bid decisions, or repeated manual supplier chasing.

Copy risk:
Electrical wholesalers, manufacturers, cable reps, tender consultants, standards consultants, and generic tender-alert tools can copy pieces. The defense is speed, specific tender-led outbound, normalized distributor catalogs, supplier alias memory, recurring DoP/evidence links, buyer-specific rejected-SKU history, and a local Polish/CEE tender-language-to-SKU-to-DoP graph.

Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream:
Once a distributor assigns a live tender, shares a SKU export, prepays the bid pack, and routes the evidence matching through the startup under a submission deadline, another provider must displace the active bid workflow. The controlled unit is the live tender, normalized SKU list, evidence matrix, supplier RFI, reviewer notes, and bid/no-bid decision record.

Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker:
The startup accepts only live or recently closed cable tenders with a named distributor/contractor catalog. The output is a bid artifact used by a tender team: eligible/rejected SKU matrix, CPR/DoP evidence index, supplier RFI, missing-document list, and bid/no-bid risk memo. It is not procurement representation, design engineering, legal advice, a dashboard, a database, a generic tender-alert service, or a brokered supplier introduction.

Boundaries and legal/provider limits:
The startup does not design electrical installations, select cables for safety-critical systems, certify products, provide legal/procurement advice, substitute specifications, guarantee tender success, approve installation methods, or make fire-safety engineering decisions. The distributor, contractor, designer, manufacturer, reviewer, and tender team remain responsible for product selection, compliance, submission, installation, and legal/procurement decisions. Edge cases are escalated to a qualified electrical/cable standards reviewer or manufacturer.

Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas:
This is not GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs. GreenTender maps public lighting tenders to lighting SKUs and EPREL energy-label/product-information evidence. CableTender maps cable/electrical tenders to CPR construction-product cable fire-reaction evidence, Declarations of Performance, smoke/droplet/acidity classes, CE/CPR certificates, and cable SKU aliases. The buyer, product regime, source documents, evidence artifacts, and technical objections are different.

It is not DORA, REDBlocked, CBAM, Data Act, BioSignal, PromoLeak, TraceFaktura, HeritageDoor, BatteryFit, HeatQuiet, Supplement Stack Safety, or any health/Rx/clinical route.

Strongest anticipated objections:
- Cable distributors and manufacturers may already handle DoP/fire-class evidence internally.
- Many tenders may rely on manufacturer-specified products, leaving little room for SKU matching.
- Incorrect evidence matching can cause disqualification or safety/compliance risk.
- Tender requirements can drift into design, substitution, legal/procurement interpretation, or fire-safety engineering.
- Manufacturers and cable reps may provide evidence for free to protect channel sales.
- Public tender volume in this specific cable evidence niche may be too small or lumpy.
- Once a distributor catalog is normalized, the buyer may internalize the process.
- The idea is structurally adjacent to GreenTender and must prove that CPR cable evidence has an independent repeated tender pain, not just another EPREL-like expansion.
