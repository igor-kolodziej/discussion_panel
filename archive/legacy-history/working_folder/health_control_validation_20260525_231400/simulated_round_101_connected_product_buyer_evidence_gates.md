## Current Source Notes

Active connected-product regulatory pressure:

- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 adds cybersecurity-related RED essential requirements for relevant radio equipment, applying from 1 August 2025: `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2022/30/oj`
- Commission communications and OJEU citations have made the EN 18031 series the practical harmonised-standard path for RED cybersecurity, with restrictions.
- The Cyber Resilience Act reporting obligations begin on 11 September 2026 for manufacturers of products with digital elements; the European Commission states the Single Reporting Platform will be operational by then: `https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting`

This round avoids duplicating the confirmed Amazon connected-device desk by leaving Amazon/Seller Central out of scope. The control point is a live B2B buyer, distributor, OEM, installer, public procurement, or CE/lab partner request blocking a non-marketplace sale or onboarding.
