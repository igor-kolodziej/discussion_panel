Score this business idea 0-100 and include strongest objections. Founder context: unknown Warsaw solo founder, contractors allowed, max 100,000 PLN, 6-month POC, part-time. Idea name: BrokerLock Cyber Subjectivity Case-Book Option. One-sentence thesis: Option or sign exclusive overflow terms with small cyber/commercial brokers and MSPs that already receive repeated cyber-insurance bind and renewal subjectivities, then monetize the authorized live case book through prepaid evidence-room and underwriter-response packets. Exact buyer: Polish/CEE cyber-insurance brokers, commercial brokers with cyber lines, MGAs/coverholder partners, and MSPs supporting 50-500 employee SMEs. The end payor is an SME whose cyber quote, renewal, conditional binder, ransomware coverage, funds-transfer coverage, surcharge, exclusion, sublimit, or customer-contract insurance certificate is blocked by a live underwriter request. Acquisition targets are small brokers/MSPs that see these cases but do not want to build a specialist evidence desk. Acute trigger: A policy bind or renewal deadline is blocked because the insurer, MGA, or underwriter asks for concrete proof around MFA scope, EDR/MDR coverage, backup isolation, restore testing, privileged access, patching, security awareness, incident response, funds-transfer controls, external scan findings, or control exceptions. The deadline is a policy expiry, binder deadline, lender/customer-contract requirement, board deadline, renewal quote expiry, ransomware/funds-transfer exclusion, premium surcharge, or sublimit decision. Control point: Signed exclusive or priority overflow routing terms with brokers/MSPs; direct client authorization for each policy event; underwriter subjectivity wording; policy deadline; quote/decline/exclusion/surcharge context; payment direction or broker guarantee; evidence-room custody; broker notes; cyber application answers; MSP/admin-console exports; backup restore-test evidence; EDR/MDR coverage reports; MFA configuration evidence; patch/vulnerability records; training logs; incident response materials; exception list; reviewer queue; and a private case-memory taxonomy of accepted evidence, rejected evidence, exception wording, and remediation/no-go outcomes. 60-day signed/titled/assigned/prepaid proof: Two brokers/MSPs sign exclusive or priority overflow terms for cyber subjectivity cases; 8-12 live bind/renewal cases are prepaid or broker-guaranteed at 5,000-25,000 PLN each; every case includes a real insurer/MGA/underwriter request, policy deadline, client authorization, existing technical evidence or named MSP source, and broker approval path; at least five cases produce bind, renewal, cleaner quote, exclusion/sublimit reduction, underwriter progress, accepted exception wording, or clean remediation/no-go decision; one broker routes a second case; gross margin stays above 60% after cyber reviewer and insurance-side reviewer costs. 6-month POC: Handle 40-80 broker/MSP-routed cyber-insurance subjectivity cases; collect 400,000-1,200,000 PLN in wholesale, direct-bill, urgent, and renewal-refresh fees; document 25+ bind/renewal/progress outcomes, 10 accepted exception/no-go decisions, and 8 repeat brokers/MSPs; build a private taxonomy of insurer wording, control evidence types, acceptable screenshots/exports, MFA scope disputes, EDR coverage thresholds, backup-restore evidence, external scan fixes, exception language, and underwriter follow-up patterns; keep turnaround under five business days for evidence-ready cases; decline implementation-heavy cases where controls are missing. First acquisition mechanism: Build a list of 80 Polish/CEE cyber/commercial brokers and MSPs that sell or support cyber insurance for SMEs. Pitch only live subjectivities: "When an underwriter holds bind or renewal because MFA, EDR, backups, patching, training, incident response, or scan evidence is unclear, route the case to me. I turn the client's existing evidence and MSP exports into a broker-ready subjectivity response pack in five business days. I do not sell insurance, answer on behalf of the insured, implement controls, or guarantee coverage." Offer the first broker an exclusive overflow option for a defined case type and pay them a margin or admin fee on routed cases. Economics: Charge brokers/MSPs 5,000-8,000 PLN wholesale for narrow evidence-ready control packets, 10,000-18,000 PLN for full renewal/bind subjectivity packs, and 18,000-35,000 PLN for urgent multi-entity or contract-critical cases. Brokers can resell, pass through, or bundle the pack as a paid emergency underwriting support product. The SME pays because the fee is small relative to losing cyber coverage, accepting a ransomware/funds-transfer exclusion, delaying a customer contract, missing lender/board requirements, or derailing a renewal close to expiry. Copy risk: Insurance brokers, MSPs, MSSPs, cyber consultants, GRC platforms, cyber insurers, MGAs, and underwriting support tools can copy pieces. The defense is the specific routed case book: signed overflow terms, client authorization, current policy deadlines, broker payment direction, evidence-room control, fast evidence-format memory across insurers, strict evidence-ready versus remediation-needed triage, accepted exception wording, repeat broker trust, and a private library of underwriter subjectivity patterns and evidence formats. Why incumbents cannot copy before the founder controls the specific asset/account/case/claim/lot/payment stream: Once the broker/MSP signs overflow terms, the insured authorizes the case, and the startup receives the live underwriter subjectivity, quote/renewal context, policy deadline, MSP/admin exports, evidence artifacts, and payment guarantee, the startup controls the active evidence room and response packet for that policy event. A broker, MSP, insurer, or cyber consultant can build a similar workflow later, but cannot instantly own the specific authorized evidence packet already being assembled for a pending bind or renewal deadline under signed routing terms. Why it is not a service, report, app, dashboard, database, marketplace, or generic broker: The startup accepts only live cyber-insurance bind or renewal blockers with an actual insurer/MGA/underwriter subjectivity, follow-up, exclusion, sublimit, decline, surcharge, or deadline. The output is an operational underwriting artifact: subjectivity matrix, evidence index, underwriter response draft, approved exception/no-go memo, and broker-ready evidence room tied to a named policy event. It is not generic cyber readiness consulting, MSP implementation, insurance brokerage, a GRC dashboard, a checklist database, a policy marketplace, a security audit, or a brokered introduction. Boundaries and legal/provider limits: The startup does not sell insurance, act as insurance broker, provide legal advice, provide security certification, perform formal audits, implement controls, sign insurance applications, make coverage representations, advise on policy terms, handle claims, or guarantee bind/renewal. Licensed brokers, insurers, MGAs, underwriters, insured companies, MSPs, MSSPs, lawyers, auditors, and security officers remain responsible for insurance advice, application answers, legal review, control implementation, security decisions, policy wording, and underwriting outcomes. The startup marks cases as remediation/no-go when evidence would be misleading or controls are missing. Duplicate risk against existing confirmed ideas: This is not DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack because it does not handle regulated-finance third-party risk, DORA Article 28/30 evidence, bank questionnaires, exit plans, subcontractor oversight, or ICT supplier renewals. The gatekeeper is a cyber insurer/MGA/underwriter, the channel is brokers/MSPs, the payment event is cyber policy bind/renewal or coverage improvement, and the proof artifact is an underwriting subjectivity response. It is not the prior CyberBind prompt because the control point is an optioned broker/MSP overflow case book with payment direction and repeated policy-event routing, not a cold-sold one-off evidence pack. It is not RAGProbe, REDBlocked, BioSignal, CBAM, Data Act, GreenTender, Supplement Stack, HeatQuiet, BatteryFit, PromoLeak, HeritageDoor, TraceFaktura, BESSGuard, or any health/Rx/clinical route. Strongest anticipated objections: Brokers and MSPs may already handle these questionnaires and may not pay an outside specialist. Many blocked cases need actual control implementation, not evidence assembly. Underwriters may still decline, surcharge, exclude, or ask for more evidence outside founder control. Misstatements on insurance applications can create severe coverage, legal, and trust risk, so the startup must never answer or sign for the insured. Cyber insurers and underwriting platforms may standardize evidence collection. Unknown founder credibility is a problem without a cyber reviewer and insurance-side reviewer. SMEs with low premiums may not tolerate 5,000-25,000 PLN fees. Brokers may resist exclusive routing if they fear loss of client control. The work can drift into generic MSP/cyber consulting unless it accepts only live bind/renewal subjectivity cases.
