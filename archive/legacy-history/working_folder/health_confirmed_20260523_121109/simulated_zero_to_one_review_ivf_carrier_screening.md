# Simulated Zero To One Review: IVF Carrier-Screening Consent & Routing Layer

## Exact Idea Evaluated

IVF Carrier-Screening Consent & Routing Layer for mid-sized Polish IVF clinics participating in the national IVF program. The hidden insight is that rising IVF demand and state-funded cycles do not mainly create a DNA-test supply problem; they create an emotionally sensitive clinic workflow problem around explaining carrier screening, PGT, donor/partner genetics, consent, and escalation without making the clinic look like it is upselling anxious couples. The product is a clinic-routed, neutral decision and documentation workflow, not a consumer DNA report and not test affiliate sales. The clinic routes IVF couples before genetic add-ons are chosen into a structured intake, family-history capture, plain-language option explanation, consent documentation, escalation criteria, and geneticist-reviewed exception path. Pricing: 300-600 PLN per couple paid by clinic or patient, reviewer cost only for escalations; later per-cycle retainer. Control point proof in 2 months: signed workflow pilot with one clinic for 20 couples, one reserved geneticist reviewer contract, measured doctor/admin time saved, consent completion, number of escalations, and at least 5 paid patient/clinic fees. Initial acquisition: build list of IVF program clinics, send a written "20-couple genetic add-on decision audit" offer to clinic managers; no software integration.

## Verdict

Score: **91/100**

Decision: **Pursue, but narrow to a paid 20-couple clinic-routed pilot before building software.**

## Diagnosis

This is one of the stronger health/genetics candidates because it attaches to an existing, state-funded patient flow instead of trying to create consumer demand from scratch. The pain is not abstract "genetics education"; it is a commercial and ethical workflow problem inside IVF clinics: high-anxiety couples, optional genetic add-ons, limited physician time, and risk of appearing to upsell. The unit economics can work if the work is templated and reviewer involvement is reserved for exceptions. The idea is not yet defensible as "content" or "forms"; it becomes defensible only when the founder controls routed pre-add-on decision workflow across clinics and builds a clinic-neutral consent/routing standard.

## Control Point Tier

Current tier: **B** as a designed workflow.

Tier after 2-month proof: **A-/B+** if one clinic signs a pilot that routes all carrier-screening/PGT decision packets for 20 couples through the founder's intake and documentation workflow before genetic add-ons are chosen, with a contracted reviewer and paid fees.

## Strongest Copy Risk

IVF clinics, genetic testing labs, or clinic software vendors can internalize the forms and educational materials. The strongest defense is not content; it is becoming the neutral, clinic-routed pre-add-on consent and escalation workflow across multiple mid-sized clinics before labs standardize their own affiliated version.

## Strongest Objections And Caveats

- Unknown founder trust is the main obstacle; the geneticist reviewer contract must be real and visible in the pilot offer.
- If clinics see this as "outsourced sales support for genetic tests," it loses the neutrality that makes it valuable.
- If the payer is only the patient, CAC and conversion could become B2C health trust friction; clinic-routed flow is required.
- If reviewer cost is needed for most couples, margins compress quickly.
- If the workflow does not save measurable doctor/admin time, clinic managers may treat it as extra bureaucracy.
- If labs bundle the same consent pathway for free with test sales, the startup must compete on neutrality, documentation quality, and multi-lab optionality.

## Profitability Veto

No veto. The gross margin, payback, and cash conversion are plausible if the first sale is a paid clinic-routed pilot and the founder avoids building software before proving routed flow. The idea would fail the veto if it became a generic educational report, clinic consulting project, or consumer genetics advice service.
