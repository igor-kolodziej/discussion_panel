# Simulated Zero To One Review: Pediatric Sleep-EEG Slot Yield Rail

## Exact Idea Evaluated

Pediatric Sleep-EEG Slot Yield Rail for private Polish EEG labs. The hidden insight is that pediatric sleep EEG failure is mostly an operational yield problem, not an interpretation problem: parents mis-handle sleep deprivation, hair/meal/caffeine/screens/transport timing, anxious children fail to sleep, and labs lose scarce evening or long-duration child EEG capacity. The product is a lab-routed slot-yield workflow: every child sleep or sleep-deprived EEG booking in a pilot is routed through a founder-operated readiness flow 72h/48h/12h before the exam, with parent readiness scoring, rescue calls for high-risk cases, age-specific sleep schedule instructions approved by the lab, transport reminders, cancellation triage, and a prepared standby list of families willing to take recovered slots. It does not diagnose, interpret EEG, recommend treatment, or change medical indications.

Buyer: private EEG labs and neurodiagnostic clinics doing pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived EEG.

Pricing: 80-140 PLN per routed pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived exam, or 20-30% of recovered slot value for rescued/cancellation-filled cases; after pilot, 2,000-6,000 PLN/month/lab depending on volume.

Control point proof in 2 months: one signed lab pilot where the lab routes all pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived bookings through the workflow for 30-50 cases; founder also builds a prepared standby list of at least 25 families from lab referrals and high-intent search. Proof: lower late-cancel/no-show/failed-prep rate versus baseline, number of high-risk cases rescued, number of recovered slots filled, parent completion rate, lab renewal or written refusal reason, and at least 10 paid routed cases.

Initial acquisition: build a list of private EEG labs from public search and clinic directories; call/email with a "30-day sleep-EEG slot-yield sprint" offer tied to recovered revenue, not generic reminders. The founder's neuroscience/EEG background helps credibility, while the offer avoids regulated diagnosis.

## Verdict

Score: **92/100**

Decision: **Pursue as a narrow EEG operational-yield business, but only if the pilot contract includes mandatory routing of booked cases and the founder owns the standby list.**

## Diagnosis

This is stronger than generic EEG AI, IVF genetic routing, or care-home evidence software because the first buyer pain is operational, measurable, and low-regulatory: private labs lose money and schedule quality when pediatric sleep EEGs fail or cancel late. The control point is concrete enough for a 2-month proof: routed booking flow plus a prepared standby list. The idea is not massive as a single category, but it has excellent founder fit, cheap validation, fast payback, and a plausible expansion path into pediatric neurodiagnostic appointment yield, mobile/home sleep EEG routing, and scarce neurophysiology capacity management.

## Control Point Tier

Current tier: **B+** as a designed operational rail.

Tier after 2-month proof: **A-** if a lab contractually routes all pediatric sleep/sleep-deprived bookings through the founder workflow and the founder owns a reusable prepared standby list of families that can fill cancelled slots.

## Strongest Copy Risk

Individual labs can copy basic reminders or assign staff to call parents. The defense is not reminders; it is the cross-lab readiness model, parent standby demand, failure-risk taxonomy, rescue playbook, and demonstrated recovered-slot economics.

## Strongest Objections And Caveats

- The single beachhead may be too small unless expanded beyond pediatric sleep EEG.
- If labs will not route all bookings, the founder only has a reminder service and should kill it.
- Parent standby acquisition must be real; lab-only routing weakens the control point.
- Pricing must tie to slot economics, not "education content."
- Avoid medical claims: no EEG interpretation, no seizure-risk advice, no treatment guidance.

## Profitability Veto

No veto. Gross margins are plausible because delivery is mostly templated messaging, readiness triage, and exception calls. Payback is credible if one recovered 800-1,100 PLN slot covers several routed cases. Retention is plausible if monthly reports show lower failure/cancellation and faster slot refill.
