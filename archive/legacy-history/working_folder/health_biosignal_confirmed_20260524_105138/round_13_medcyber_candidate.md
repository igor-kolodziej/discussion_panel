# Round 13 Candidate: MedCyber Biosignal Procurement Evidence Rail

## Candidate

**MedCyber Biosignal Procurement Evidence Rail** - a live-deal cybersecurity and MDR evidence-response rail for EU/CEE connected biosignal and neurodiagnostic-device vendors/distributors whose hospital, clinic, insurer, or enterprise sales are blocked by security questionnaires and medical-device cybersecurity evidence requests.

## Scored Wording Draft

MedCyber Biosignal Procurement Evidence Rail is a vendor-paid evidence-response service for EU/CEE distributors and manufacturers of connected EEG, EMG, Holter/patch ECG, sleep-testing, remote monitoring, neurofeedback, wearable medical, and diagnostic software products. It handles active procurement, renewal, tender, distributor onboarding, or hospital IT/security objections where the buyer asks for cybersecurity, MDR software, NIS2 supply-chain, GDPR, SBOM, vulnerability handling, network architecture, update/patch, incident-response, or data-flow evidence. The founder takes a signed paid mandate for one active deal, routes the vendor's technical documentation, product architecture, data flows, device/network requirements, clinical-risk claims, software lifecycle evidence, and security questionnaire through a fixed MedCyber Evidence Pack reviewed by a medical-device cybersecurity or MDR reviewer. Pricing is 8,000-25,000 EUR per blocked-deal rescue or 3,000-8,000 EUR for pre-tender readiness. The primary control point is the paid active-deal evidence mandate plus access to real buyer security questionnaires and device technical files, not a generic report, dashboard, or compliance template.

## Hidden Insight

Connected biosignal devices now sit at the intersection of patient safety, hospital IT, sensitive health data, and supply-chain cyber risk. Small medical-device vendors often have CE/MDR paperwork and good clinical claims, but they lose or delay hospital deals when IT/security asks for practical evidence: SBOM, update process, vulnerability disclosure, data-flow diagrams, minimum IT requirements, network segmentation assumptions, GDPR processor roles, incident contacts, and safety impact of cyber failure. Hospitals do not want a philosophy of cybersecurity; they need procurement-ready answers that let IT, clinical engineering, and purchasing continue the transaction.

## Why Now

- Healthcare is a high-criticality sector under NIS2, creating supply-chain cybersecurity pressure on hospitals, labs, and medical-device manufacturers.
- MDCG cybersecurity guidance under MDR/IVDR treats cybersecurity as part of safety/performance and covers device lifecycle, IT environment, unauthorised access, minimum IT requirements, risk control, and post-market considerations.
- Hospitals and health buyers increasingly use security questionnaires and vendor-risk processes.
- Connected EEG/Holter/sleep/remote-monitoring devices are exactly the kind of products that cross clinical safety, network access, and health-data risk.

## Beachhead

EU/CEE vendors and distributors selling:

- connected EEG / EMG / neurodiagnostic systems;
- Holter, patch ECG, ambulatory BP, sleep testing, CPAP telemonitoring, remote patient monitoring;
- wearable medical devices and diagnostic software;
- connected clinic devices with cloud dashboards, mobile apps, USB/network interfaces, or remote updates.

Only accept first customers with an active blocked or high-risk deal: public tender question, hospital IT/security questionnaire, distributor onboarding blocker, renewal risk, insurer enterprise buyer, or clinic chain procurement.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed paid active-deal mandate.
- Access to real buyer cybersecurity questionnaire, tender clarification, procurement request, or blocked-deal email.
- Access to vendor technical documentation: architecture, data flows, interfaces, update/patch process, vulnerability policy, cloud hosting, minimum IT requirements, risk management, post-market process, and instructions for use.
- Fixed evidence pack reviewed by a qualified medical-device cybersecurity/MDR reviewer.
- Outcome memory: buyer segment, question taxonomy, accepted answer patterns, missing evidence, security objections, and device category.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Build a target list of 100 EU/CEE connected biosignal vendors/distributors from public product pages, tender awards, hospital procurement pages, and distributor catalogs.
- Recruit one medical-device cybersecurity or MDR software reviewer for 10-20 prepaid hours.
- Prepare a public-page "deal-kill audit" for 30 vendors showing likely security/procurement gaps.
- Cold outbound to founders, sales, regulatory, distributor, and tender leads with a concrete offer: "I turn your live hospital security questionnaire into a MedCyber Evidence Pack so the deal can continue."
- Secure one prepaid active-deal mandate of at least 8,000 EUR or two pre-tender readiness packs of at least 3,000 EUR each.
- Deliver one pack and obtain buyer-side signal: questionnaire accepted, procurement continues, tender clarification answered, distributor onboarding continues, or buyer narrows objections.

## Why This Is Not Merely A Report

The POC only counts if tied to a live revenue event. A static cybersecurity template, general MDR audit, or generic compliance memo does not qualify. The asset is the routed live buyer evidence request plus the accepted-response memory for connected biosignal device categories.

## Unit Economics

- Active-deal pack: 8,000-25,000 EUR.
- Pre-tender/readiness pack: 3,000-8,000 EUR.
- Reviewer cost: 1,000-5,000 EUR per matter, depending on complexity.
- Target gross margin: 55-80%.
- Payback can be immediate if buyer prepays before deep work begins.

## Copy Risk

Medical-device cybersecurity consultancies, MDR consultants, and general cyber firms can copy. The defense must be narrow connected-biosignal specialization, a repeatable fixed evidence format, buyer-objection memory, fast founder-led delivery, and a reviewer bench. If the work becomes bespoke consulting without routed live deals, it fails.
