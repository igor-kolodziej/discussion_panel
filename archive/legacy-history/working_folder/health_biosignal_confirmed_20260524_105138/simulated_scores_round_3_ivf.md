# Simulated Score Round 3: IVF Genetic Add-On Consent Rail

## Candidate Tested

**IVF Genetic Add-On Consent Rail** - a clinic-paid, mandatory pre-add-on routing layer for Polish IVF clinics before carrier screening / PGT add-on discussions, with neutral consent capture, red-flag triage, and clinical-geneticist escalation.

## Result

Simulated Zero To One score: **74/100**.

## Reviewer Diagnosis

The reviewer treated the insight and timing as real: Poland's public IVF program is driving volume through 58 providers, and ESHRE expanded-carrier-screening guidance makes consent, counselling, reporting, and governance a live issue. The wedge is strongest only if it becomes mandatory routing inside a clinic before genetic add-ons are discussed.

## Fatal Objections

- **Trust and authority:** an unknown non-physician founder must persuade IVF clinics to route sensitive reproductive genetics decisions through an outside workflow.
- **Distribution mismatch:** this still requires repeated founder-led sales to clinic managers, doctors, and coordinators.
- **Vendor copy risk:** genetic-test vendors or clinic chains can bundle a similar consent questionnaire and patient packet for free.
- **Quasi-clinical boundary:** the line between consent workflow and genetic counselling must be legally bright, and clinical-geneticist review is part of the product rather than an optional extra.

## Control Point Tier

Current tier: **Tier 3 potential**.

It could become Tier 2 only if one clinic signs mandatory routing for a defined patient segment. Without that, it remains a compliance/service workflow.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate, so it cannot be sent to the real Zero To One chatbot. The next pivot should control a more concrete resource than clinic trust: inventory, removal rights, installer capacity, reimbursable transaction flow, or another asset that an unknown founder can secure with cash and disciplined outbound inside two months.
