## Takeaway

The strongest recurring failure pattern was not lack of creativity. It was that health, genetics, EEG, biosignal, and pre-elderly ideas repeatedly needed one of these hard-to-get assets:

- mandatory clinic/provider routing;
- trusted medical/legal authority;
- exclusive inventory or referral flow;
- enough transaction/event volume for retention;
- a resource incumbents cannot copy after one vendor contract or internal process change.

The late-run pivots strengthened the control points, but the strict simulated reviewers still rejected them. The recurring blocker was not only lack of a resource; it was that the resource was either easy for the nearest incumbent to package, too trust-heavy for an unknown founder, too episodic, or too slow to prove cash conversion inside two months.

The next promising search direction should start from a controllable transaction or inventory flow where the nearest incumbent is structurally disincentivized to hold the resource, not merely slow to notice it.
