## Conclusion From Late Rounds

The repeated cap is now explicit:

- if the idea relies on consumer urgency, it is easy to copy and CAC/retention are weak;
- if it relies on B2B procurement rescue, it is profitable but copyable unless the founder controls a channel;
- if it relies on channel partners, those partners often lack power to force routing;
- if it relies on data assets, B2B data sales are slow and buyers can replicate with labs/CROs;
- if it relies on health workflows, medical trust and authority dominate.

The remaining viable search space must start from assigned claims, regulated payout rights, legally exclusive custody, pre-paid inventory options, or a hard transaction where the founder can own the right to act before incumbents can copy.
