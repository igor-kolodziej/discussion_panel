# Round 7 Candidate: Last Sample DNA Custody Rail

## Candidate

**Last Sample DNA Custody Rail** - a legally consented, logistics-first DNA preservation service for 50-60 adult children whose elderly parent or close relative has advanced cancer, sudden cardiac death risk, neurodegenerative disease, unexplained early disease, or is near death / recently deceased. The company does not sell interpretation, ancestry, or health recommendations. It preserves usable DNA/material and consent provenance so the family can later pursue clinical genetics, targeted variant testing, genealogy, identity, or research donation through licensed providers.

## Hidden Insight

Families do not usually regret missing one more report. They regret losing the one biological sample that made future questions answerable. Adult children often realize too late that the most informative person in the family is dying, cognitively impaired, cremated, or buried before any genetic pathway is started. Formal genetic counselling and clinical testing can take weeks or months; sample preservation is a time-sensitive logistics problem that can happen now.

## Scored Wording Draft

Last Sample DNA Custody Rail sells an urgent DNA preservation and custody workflow to 50-60 adult children of seriously ill, dying, or recently deceased relatives. It provides consent paperwork, chain-of-custody, courier / phlebotomy or funeral-home sampling logistics, lab DNA extraction or biological-material storage, and a family-controlled release instruction for future clinical genetics or identity use. It explicitly does not diagnose, interpret variants, recommend tests, sell ancestry matching, or make medical claims. The control point is physical sample custody plus legally documented consent/release rights, secured by prepaid lab processing/storage capacity and signed referral/reseller agreements with 2-3 funeral homes, estate-clearout/end-of-life planners, or private nurse/phlebotomy operators.

## Primary Customer

The first buyer is not the elderly person directly. It is the 50-60 adult child who is managing a parent crisis and wants to avoid irreversible regret. Beachhead triggers:

- parent with advanced cancer before treatment/death;
- sudden unexplained cardiac death / family arrhythmia concern;
- early-onset dementia or neurodegenerative disease;
- rare unexplained disease in an older relative;
- cremation/funeral planning where no sample has been saved;
- family-history uncertainty where the oldest living relative is the last informative sample.

## Primary Control Point

- Physical DNA/sample custody under explicit consent and release instructions.
- Prepaid lab extraction/storage slots or a white-label processing order with an existing Polish/EU lab/biobank.
- 2-3 signed "last sample" referral agreements with funeral homes, private nurses/phlebotomists, estate/end-of-life planners, or hospice-adjacent service providers.
- Chain-of-custody and family authorization record.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Secure written processing/storage terms with one existing Polish/EU lab or biobank, prepaid for the first 20 samples.
- Secure 2-3 signed referral agreements with funeral homes or private nurse/phlebotomy operators, paying only per converted case.
- Build the consent, GDPR, chain-of-custody, and release paperwork with a lawyer.
- Run search ads and direct partner referral tests around "save DNA before cremation / before parent dies / family genetic sample".
- Sell 5 paid custody packages before serious capital:
  - 1,200-2,500 PLN setup/extraction/custody fee;
  - 150-400 PLN/year storage/admin renewal;
  - optional later release/clinical handoff fee.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Test It

The founder does not need hospitals, famous doctors, or clinic integration for the first proof. Labs/biobanks already sell storage or DNA extraction capacity. Funeral homes and private nurses have practical end-of-life logistics and may accept a commission-based add-on if legal paperwork is clean. The first buyer is motivated by urgency and regret avoidance, not by founder status.

## Key Risks

- Genetic data is sensitive GDPR data; explicit consent, purpose limitation, security, and deletion/release terms must be clean.
- Near-death and postmortem consent rights are legally sensitive; the POC should start with living consenting individuals and only later test postmortem workflows with legal review.
- Existing banks and labs can copy storage; the wedge must be urgent logistics, chain-of-custody, consent provenance, and partner capture at the last-sample moment.
- Funeral-home distribution may feel uncomfortable or predatory if framed poorly.
- Demand may be emotionally real but low-volume unless trigger channels convert.
