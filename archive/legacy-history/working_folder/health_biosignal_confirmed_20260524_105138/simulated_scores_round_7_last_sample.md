# Simulated Score Round 7: Last Sample DNA Custody Rail

## Candidate Tested

**Last Sample DNA Custody Rail** - a legally consented, logistics-first DNA preservation service for 50-60 adult children whose elderly parent or close relative has advanced cancer, sudden cardiac death risk, neurodegenerative disease, unexplained early disease, or is near death / recently deceased. The company does not sell interpretation, ancestry, or health recommendations. It preserves usable DNA/material and consent provenance so the family can later pursue clinical genetics, targeted variant testing, genealogy, identity, or research donation through licensed providers.

## Result

Simulated Zero To One score: **73/100**.

## Reviewer Diagnosis

The reviewer accepted that physical sample custody, consent provenance, prepaid lab slots, and referral agreements are concrete resources that can be secured in two months. However, the control point is operationally fragile and not deeply proprietary.

## Fatal Objections

- **Demand timing:** the need is urgent but rare and narrow; repeatable acquisition may be difficult.
- **Referral-channel risk:** funeral homes, private nurses, hospices, and doctors may avoid genetic-material handling because of legal and reputation risk.
- **Clinical usefulness uncertainty:** later providers may reject samples if collection/storage/consent documentation is not accepted.
- **GDPR and consent sensitivity:** genetic data is special-category data, and near-death/postmortem workflows are legally sensitive.
- **Lab/biobank copy risk:** existing DNA banks and biobanks already own the infrastructure and credibility; they could partner with the same channels.

## Control Point Tier

Tier 2: real but operationally fragile physical-sample custody and consent-control point.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate, so it cannot proceed to real Zero To One validation.
