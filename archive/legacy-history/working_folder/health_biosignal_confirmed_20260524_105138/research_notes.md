# Research Notes

## EEG Slot-Yield Sources

- EEG-GRAF price list: adult sleep-deprived EEG is 400 PLN; child sleep EEG is 800 PLN until 23:00, +400 PLN for each later hour, and a 400 PLN unsuccessful-exam charge may apply when the patient resigns on site or the child does not fall asleep due to inadequate preparation. Source: https://eeg.med.pl/prices-of-eeg/
- NEURODIAGNOSTYKA price list: clinic sleep EEG for children is 350-380 PLN; home sleep EEG for children is from 900-1,000 PLN; 12-hour home sleep EEG is 2,500 PLN; 24-hour home video EEG is from 6,000 PLN. Source: https://neurodiagnostyka.com/en/price-list/
- ACNS pediatric EEG guideline: sleep recordings should be obtained whenever possible; natural sleep is preferred, with sleep deprivation or melatonin potentially helpful; recording drowsiness, sleep initiation, and arousal is important. Source: https://www.acns.org/UserFiles/file/EEGGuideline5Ped_finaltrackchangesreformat_v1.pdf
- Sleep-deprived EEG feasibility in children: in a 261-child study, 14% slept less than 15 minutes and 7% did not sleep during recording. Source: https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/feasibility-of-sleep-deprived-eeg-in-children/

## Strategic Interpretation

The strongest current health candidate is not a regulated diagnostic tool, not a clinical interpretation product, not a generic evidence memo, and not a medical-device resale business. It is an operational-yield layer around an expensive, failure-prone, parent-dependent EEG appointment class.

The control point only exists if the lab contractually routes all relevant sleep/sleep-deprived booking flow and cancellation flow through the founder, while the founder separately builds a prepared standby-family list. If either side is missing, the idea collapses into a reminder service or a lab-dependent admin contractor.
