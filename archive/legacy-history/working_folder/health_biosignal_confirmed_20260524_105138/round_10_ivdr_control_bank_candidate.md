# Round 10 Candidate: IVDR Genetic Control Bank

## Candidate

**IVDR Genetic Control Bank** - a Polish/CEE validation-control inventory and access service for small genetic and molecular pathology labs that need characterized positive/reference materials for NGS panel validation, verification, lot checks, and ISO/IVDR evidence.

## Scored Wording Draft

IVDR Genetic Control Bank buys or obtains reseller/rental rights to commercially available, well-characterized genetic reference materials and positive controls for one narrow test family first, such as hereditary cancer/BRCA-Lynch panels or PGx controls. It holds the physical inventory, documents chain-of-custody and storage, translates use instructions into Polish SOP packs, and sells prepaid validation-control access kits to Polish/CEE labs preparing or maintaining genetic assays under IVDR/ISO 15189 pressure. The first product is not consulting and not a dashboard: it is a controlled physical reference-material pack plus usage record, with optional reviewer help only around documentation. The 2-month POC is to buy/control the first 10-20 control materials, secure written redistribution/reseller/rental terms where required, and get two labs to prepay for validation-control access before serious capital is spent.

## Hidden Insight

IVDR pressure does not stop at paperwork. Genetic labs need actual positive and reference materials to prove that assays detect known variants, difficult variant types, and clinically relevant edge cases. The public-health problem is known: characterized reference materials are essential for QC, proficiency testing, test development, and validation, yet for many genetic tests publicly available materials do not exist or are fragmented. Small labs cannot each efficiently hunt, buy, store, track, and justify every rare positive control. A local control bank can own the boring scarce materials and sell access.

## Beachhead

Start with one family where labs already run or maintain panels and controls are commercially obtainable:

- hereditary cancer / BRCA-Lynch NGS controls;
- pharmacogenomics controls such as CYP2C19/CYP2D6/VKORC1/UGT1A1;
- targeted reproductive-carrier-screening controls if reference materials are obtainable.

Do not start with bespoke patient samples or custom variant banking.

## Primary Control Point

- Owned or contract-controlled reference-material inventory for named variants/panels.
- Written rights/terms for resale, rental, aliquoting, or controlled access if required by supplier terms.
- Polish SOP, storage, chain-of-custody, and usage-record layer.
- Paid lab preorders for a validation-control access pack.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Research supplier terms for 30 candidate reference materials from public/reference-material programs and commercial suppliers.
- Buy or reserve the first 10-20 controls under 60,000 PLN, only after at least one paid lab preorder or signed LOI with deposit.
- Secure two paid preorders from Polish/CEE labs: 5,000-15,000 PLN each for validation-control access pack, depending on scope.
- Deliver one control pack or supervised access workflow with chain-of-custody and usage record.
- Prove gross margin, lab acceptance, and which controls are hard enough to source that buyers value local access.

## Unit Economics

- Initial packs: 5,000-15,000 PLN setup/access per lab.
- Later model: annual control-access subscription 12,000-40,000 PLN per lab for a defined panel family, plus one-off kit/access charges.
- Cost: physical controls, storage, QC/logging, shipping/courier, documentation support.
- Target: 50-70% gross margin if materials are reusable/rentable or bundled across multiple labs; lower if single-use resale only.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Test It

The founder does not need clinic patient trust, hospital integration, or medical interpretation. Reference materials and controls are sold commercially or distributed through known programs. Labs already understand why positive controls matter; the founder's job is narrow commercial packaging, local access, storage discipline, and prepaid proof.

## Key Risks

- Supplier terms may prohibit resale/aliquot/rental.
- Existing diagnostic suppliers may already offer enough controls.
- Small labs may prefer to buy directly rather than pay an access premium.
- If materials are single-use and expensive, margins may be weak.
- If the first panel family has no urgent IVDR/ISO validation deadline, demand may be slow.

## Initial Sources

- CDC GeT-RM program states reference and QC materials are essential for genetic testing QC, proficiency testing, test development, and validation, and notes that for most genetic tests characterized reference or QC materials are not publicly available.
- SeraCare advertises NGS reference materials covering somatic cancer, clinically relevant variants, SNVs, indels, CNVs, and fusions for validation/confidence in clinical genomics assays.
- IVDR / molecular pathology implementation sources emphasize validation, performance evaluation, control mechanisms, and appropriate reference materials.
