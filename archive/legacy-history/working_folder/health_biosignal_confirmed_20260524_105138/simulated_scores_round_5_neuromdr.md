# Simulated Score Round 5: NeuroMDR Field Evidence Rail

## Candidate Tested

**NeuroMDR Field Evidence Rail** - a mandatory service/complaint/event-routing layer for Polish importers and distributors of niche EEG, EMG, Holter, sleep-diagnostics, neurofeedback, and other biosignal medical devices. It turns every service call, complaint, suspected incident, user error, cable/electrode/accessory failure, software bug, training issue, and safety notice for one named device line into structured EU MDR / Polish URPL-ready field evidence.

## Result

Simulated Zero To One score: **78/100**.

## Reviewer Diagnosis

The reviewer classified this as stronger than dashboards or consultancy because it tries to capture the field-event moment. However, the score stayed below the gate because the route is contractual rather than structurally locked, event volume may be low, and small importers may underpay until a serious audit, incident, or manufacturer pressure appears.

## Fatal Objections

- **Mandatory routing is everything:** without it, the idea collapses into compliance admin, ticketing, or consultant paperwork.
- **Importer willingness to pay is uncertain:** MDR pressure is real, but the pain may be episodic.
- **Manufacturer ownership risk:** foreign manufacturers remain responsible for PMS/vigilance, so importer budget may not be strong unless manufacturers explicitly value the export.
- **Trust barrier:** an unknown founder handling safety-adjacent medical-device evidence needs credibility.
- **Low event volume:** one device line may not generate enough monthly volume unless bundled across several lines.
- **Copy risk:** MDR/QMS consultants or ticketing vendors can copy classified intake and monthly exports.

## Control Point Tier

Tier 2: contract-routed workflow control point.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate, so it cannot proceed to real Zero To One validation.
