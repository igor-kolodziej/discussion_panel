# Simulated Score Round 28: StomaRoute Recurring Order Book Acquisition

## Candidate Tested

**StomaRoute Recurring Order Book Acquisition** - option/acquire recurring ostomy supply customer books from small Polish NFZ-contracted shops or stoma nurse/shop pairs.

## Result

Simulated Zero To One score: **76/100**.

## Main Failure

The acquired recurring order book is a real control point, but not hard enough. Patients retain choice, e-zlecenie is portable, NFZ fulfillment requires a contracted shop route, and larger NFZ-contracted suppliers, manufacturers, wholesalers, or online shops can copy the roll-up playbook. GDPR/health-data consent and real contribution margin after delivery, nurse support, rev share, stock, and support costs are substantial constraints.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate and should not advance to the real Zero To One chatbot.
