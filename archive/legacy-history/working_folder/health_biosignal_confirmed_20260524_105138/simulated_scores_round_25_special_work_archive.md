# Simulated Score Round 25: SpecialWork Archive Rights Rail

## Candidate Tested

**SpecialWork Archive Rights Rail** - archive-control and claimant-intake workflow for Polish 50-60 workers needing certified old wage/employment/special-conditions records from liquidated employers for ZUS pension, initial-capital, bridge-pension, or rekompensata workflows.

## Result

Two simulated reviewers disagreed on strength but neither cleared the simulated `>90` gate:

- Reviewer 1: **76/100**
- Reviewer 2: **62/100**

## Main Failure

The control point is softer than it first appears. A custodian mandate over named employer records is useful, but former workers still have direct official request routes, public ZUS/State Archive databases often identify record locations, and official copy/odpis fees are regulated/capped. The service risks becoming document-retrieval and packet assembly rather than a durable control-point company.

## Specific Caveats

- Special-conditions proof may require employer/successor certificates or confirmations, not merely archive copies.
- The custodian can internalize demand-generation once paid demand is proven.
- GDPR and worker-data minimization constrain proactive indexing/outreach.
- Actual ZUS acceptance/outcome proof may not arrive within a 2-month POC.
- Pricing is vulnerable unless the pension/rekompensata upside is visibly large.

## Decision

Rejected for this run. The score is below the required simulated `>90` gate and should not advance to the real Zero To One chatbot.
