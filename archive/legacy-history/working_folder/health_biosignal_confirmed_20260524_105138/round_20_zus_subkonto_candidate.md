# Round 20 Candidate: SubkontoClaim Funeral-Channel Recovery Rail

## Candidate

**SubkontoClaim Funeral-Channel Recovery Rail** - a regulated-money recovery workflow for 50-60 adult children and surviving spouses who may be entitled to inherited ZUS subkonto / OFE funds after a parent or spouse dies.

## Scored Wording Draft

SubkontoClaim Funeral-Channel Recovery Rail is not generic pension advice. It signs funeral homes, probate notaries, and estate-document preparers to a white-label referral/routing agreement for one narrow overlooked payout: inherited ZUS subkonto/OFE funds after death. When a family arranges a funeral or inheritance documents, the channel gives a one-page eligibility screen and routes interested heirs/spouses to SubkontoClaim. The founder obtains signed claimant authorization, checks whether the deceased had a ZUS subkonto/OFE path, maps spouse/uposażony/spadkobierca status, prepares USS/OFE request packets and probate-document checklist, and tracks the payout/transfer process. Revenue is a prepaid 300-700 PLN diagnostic plus 900-2,500 PLN fixed recovery packet or legally compliant success/referral economics where allowed. The control point is exclusive funeral/probate channel routing plus signed claimant authority over a regulated-money recovery process, not a blog or dashboard.

## Hidden Insight

The pain is not that the form is hard. The hidden bottleneck is that families do not know the money exists at the exact moment when they are already handling death paperwork. ZUS materials say subkonto/OFE funds can be divided or paid to spouses, beneficiaries, or heirs, but press reports in 2026 describe hundreds of millions of PLN waiting unclaimed. The best acquisition moment is not search ads months later; it is the funeral/probate channel before the family closes the estate paperwork.

- Signed claimant authorization and document mandate.
- Standardized ZUS/OFE request workflow and status tracker.
- Channel-specific intake and conversion data.
