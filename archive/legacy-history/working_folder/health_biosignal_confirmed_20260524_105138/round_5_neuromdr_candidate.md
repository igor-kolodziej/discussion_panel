# Round 5 Candidate: NeuroMDR Field Evidence Rail

## Candidate

**NeuroMDR Field Evidence Rail** - a mandatory service/complaint/event-routing layer for Polish importers and distributors of niche EEG, EMG, Holter, sleep-diagnostics, neurofeedback, and other biosignal medical devices. It turns every service call, complaint, suspected incident, user error, cable/electrode/accessory failure, software bug, training issue, and safety notice for one named device line into structured EU MDR / Polish URPL-ready field evidence.

## Scored Wording Draft

NeuroMDR Field Evidence Rail sells a 60-day paid pilot to small Polish/EU importers or distributors of biosignal medical devices whose field feedback currently lives in email, WhatsApp, service calls, Excel, and technician memory. The pilot requires the importer to route all new service/complaint/suspected-incident events for one named EEG/EMG/Holter/sleep-diagnostic device line through NeuroMDR first. NeuroMDR classifies each event into service issue, complaint, use-error, potential serious incident, FSCA/safety-note relevance, manufacturer escalation, missing documentation, or trend signal; creates a manufacturer-ready monthly evidence export; and maintains a device-line field history. It is not a regulatory-law opinion, QMS consultancy report, dashboard, or generic ticketing system: the control point is the routed field-event flow.

## Hidden Insight

Small medical-device importers do not fail EU MDR because they lack another consultant-written policy. They fail because the real post-market evidence is born in messy field service: a technician call about an EEG lead, a clinic complaint about software freezing, a sleep-lab user error, a recurring accessory failure, or a question about whether an issue must be escalated. Generic QMS consultants write procedures after the fact; generic help desks capture tickets without MDR meaning. Whoever controls the field-event moment can become the evidence memory for the device line.

## Underused Momentum

- EU MDR and IVDR assign responsibilities to medical-device economic operators and emphasize safety, effectiveness, and traceability across the supply chain.
- URPL maintains Polish medical-device incident / MIR / FSCA reporting routes.
- Small importers and distributors often represent foreign manufacturers while lacking an internal regulatory operations team.
- Biosignal device categories are narrow enough to understand field patterns but commercially important enough that lost manufacturer authorization, audit failure, or unresolved serious incident exposure hurts.

## Primary Control Point

The control point is a **paid, signed, mandatory 60-day routing mandate** for one named device line:

- importer forwards or logs all new service, complaint, suspected incident, safety notice, and manufacturer feedback events through NeuroMDR first;
- existing historical service/complaint artifacts for the prior 6-12 months are imported;
- a named external MDR/QMS reviewer is retained for edge-case review, not daily operation;
- the importer sends the monthly evidence export to the foreign manufacturer or gets manufacturer-format feedback.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Source 40-60 target importers/distributors from public medical-device distributor lists, product pages, hospital tender records, URPL references, and biosignal equipment websites.
- Offer a 6,000-12,000 PLN pilot for one named device line, with no software build before payment.
- Secure one signed paid pilot and a mandatory routing clause.
- Import at least 30 historical service/complaint artifacts.
- Route 10+ new field events through the workflow.
- Retain an MDR/QMS reviewer for 5-10 hours.
- Produce one manufacturer-ready monthly export and obtain manufacturer acknowledgement, correction, or usable format feedback.

## Unit Economics

- Onboarding / historical import: 4,000-10,000 PLN.
- 60-day pilot: 6,000-12,000 PLN.
- Ongoing: 2,000-6,000 PLN/month per device line plus 500-1,500 PLN per serious escalation packet or FSCA support packet.
- Cost: founder operations, LLM/OCR/extraction, secure storage, reviewer hours only for edge cases. Target gross margin after reviewer cost: 65-80%.

## Why It Is Not Merely A Service Or Dashboard

The pilot is not valuable if it produces a retrospective report from selected data. It only qualifies if the importer routes live field events through it before they become fragmented emails or memories. The durable asset is the device-line field history, manufacturer-accepted evidence format, complaint-trend memory, and importer habit around escalation clocks.

## Key Risks

- Importers may underweight compliance until an audit, serious incident, or manufacturer pressure happens.
- Regulatory/QMS consultants can copy if the founder stays at report level.
- Generic ticketing tools can capture events, but they do not own MDR classification, manufacturer-ready evidence, or biosignal device-line memory.
- The founder must avoid giving legal/regulatory advice without qualified reviewer boundaries.
