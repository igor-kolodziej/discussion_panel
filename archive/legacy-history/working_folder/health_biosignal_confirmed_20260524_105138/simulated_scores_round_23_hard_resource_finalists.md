## Pattern Learned

Physical custody alone is not enough. Case-level custody, one-off claim mandates, or one transaction escrow repeatedly score in the high 70s unless the business controls **standing pre-event routing** before the acute event appears.

## Required Next Pivot

Only continue candidates where the 2-month proof can secure at least one of:

- prepaid recurring protocol placement;
- all-event routing for a named buyer/channel;
- legally assigned recurring claim/payment authority;
- exclusive option/control of scarce inventory that buyers repeatedly need;
- transaction release embedded before counterparties can bypass the founder.
