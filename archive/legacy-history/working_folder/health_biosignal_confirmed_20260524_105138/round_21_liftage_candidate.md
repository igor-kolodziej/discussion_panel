# Round 21 Candidate: LiftAge Obsolete Elevator Continuity Bank

## Candidate

**LiftAge Obsolete Elevator Continuity Bank** - a Warsaw/Łódź-first spare-part and emergency repair-capacity bank for aging residential lifts in buildings with high 50+ / elderly resident concentration.

## Scored Wording Draft

LiftAge Obsolete Elevator Continuity Bank buys or options parts removed during elevator modernizations and decommissionings, focusing on old residential passenger lifts in Warsaw, Łódź, Katowice, and other dense Polish housing blocks. It signs 60-90 day right-of-first-refusal agreements with modernization contractors and small lift-service firms for control boards, door operators, relays, motors, buttons, landing fixtures, controllers, cabin parts, documentation, and compatible donor assemblies before they are scrapped. It then sells emergency part sourcing, compatibility confirmation, and reserved UDT-qualified service capacity to property managers, housing cooperatives, and independent lift-maintenance companies when a single obsolete component keeps an elevator down for days or weeks. The beachhead is buildings where elevator downtime strands older and soon-elderly residents and creates administrator/political pressure. The control point is owned/optioned obsolete lift-part inventory plus compatibility records plus reserved UDT-qualified repair/modernization technician slots, not a generic maintenance service or marketplace.

## Hidden Insight

Aging-in-place does not only fail inside the apartment. It fails when the elevator in a 1970s/1980s block waits weeks for one obsolete component. For a 50-60 resident with a soon-frail spouse, an older parent upstairs, or their own emerging mobility limits, lift downtime converts a normal flat into a trap. Housing administrators do not need another modernization proposal; during a breakdown they need a credible part and UDT-qualified repair path faster than the incumbent maintenance contract can source it.

## Primary Control Point

- Written ROFR/option agreements with 5-10 elevator modernization and service firms to inspect and buy removed parts before scrap.
- Owned initial inventory of high-failure obsolete components from 10-20 lift models.
- Compatibility database: model, controller, door operator, component number, building type, service history, and tested donor part.
- Reserved UDT-qualified technician/service partner capacity for emergency confirmation and installation handoff.

## 2-Month Proof Under 100,000 PLN

- Call 80 modernization/service firms and housing administrators.
- Sign 5 ROFR agreements for removed parts from modernizations.
- Buy/control 50-100 parts under 50,000 PLN, prioritizing non-safety-critical replaceable components and documentation.
- Sign one UDT-qualified service partner for paid emergency part-validation slots.
- Get 3 paid emergency searches or 2 property-manager/service-company retainers.
- Complete one paid part handoff or compatibility-confirmed rescue quote.

## Unit Economics

- Buy removed/scrap component lots: 500-5,000 PLN per lot.
- Sell emergency compatible part: 1,500-15,000 PLN depending component and urgency.
- Retainer for lift-service firms/property managers: 1,000-4,000 PLN/month for search/reserve priority.
- Part-validation/service partner cost: passed through or 300-1,500 PLN per case.
- Target gross margin on parts: 50-80%; on searches/retainers: 70%+.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Test It

The supply side is fragmented and operational. Modernization crews and small service firms have bulky removed parts and little reason to build a searchable obsolete inventory. The founder offers cash, fast pickup, and a resale channel. The buyer side already has urgent failures and administrator pressure. The founder does not need to perform regulated repair personally; UDT-qualified partners handle technical validation and installation.

## Key Risks

- Safety-critical and certification-sensitive parts may not be legally reusable without proper documentation/validation.
- Compatibility errors can create liability.
- Large OEMs and established service firms can source proprietary parts.
- Demand may be sporadic unless service firms use the bank repeatedly.
- Topic fit is aging-in-place infrastructure, not direct health care.

## Initial Sources

- UDT passenger lifts are subject to technical inspection.
- UDT offers guidance around modernization and adapting lifts for people with disabilities.
- Polish lift service companies advertise UDT/SEP-qualified maintenance and repair.
- OECD housing-reform materials discuss housing adaptation for older residents and lift/accessibility requirements.
