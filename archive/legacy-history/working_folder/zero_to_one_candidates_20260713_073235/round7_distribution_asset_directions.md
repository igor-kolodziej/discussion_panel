# Round 7: Directly Creatable Distribution-Asset Directions

Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, panelled, chatbot-reviewed, confirmed, approved, or validated. Research date: 2026-07-13. This memo uses current official, regulatory, industry-association, and incumbent-primary sources. Every price, margin, conversion, supply yield, customer behaviour, eligible share, and valuation assumption not directly supported by a source is a hypothesis to test.

## Search Boundary

The round starts from an insider customer gap and admits only a route an unknown founder can directly buy, build, contract, or pre-sell. The foundational asset must exist within 12 months and 100,000 PLN; a paid pilot, deposit, preorder, purchase order, or other budget-backed commitment must be reachable within 180 days and 40,000 PLN while the founder remains employed.

The asset cannot be a reputation, audience, hoped-for referral, unsigned partnership, prospective data corpus, serial site-acquisition program, lead-generation list, marketplace, generic software, agency desk, field-service network, or collection of small acquisitions. A supplier agreement or buyer contract counts only when it grants a named supply right, call-off commitment, installed workflow, formulation/SKU position, or prepaid cohort.

## Direction 1 — BrewForm: Brewery-Yeast Ingredient Reorder Rail

**Funnel decision: retain for finalist-grade research.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

The first buyer is a Polish or nearby CEE dry-pet-food producer with an existing inactivated-yeast or palatability/protein ingredient line item. The paid event is not waste consulting: it is approval of a defined dried inactivated brewer's-yeast ingredient, followed by a paid trial lot and an annual call-off purchase order.

### Insider Customer Truth

The formulation and procurement teams do not primarily want a circularity story. They want a feed material that arrives inside moisture, microbiology, protein, odour, colour, traceability, and price tolerances every time, because an unstable secondary ingredient can cause batch holds, palatability drift, rework, or supplier-audit burden. Regional breweries want a predictable, documented outlet for wet surplus yeast without becoming ingredient processors. Both sides value a party that absorbs the specification and scheduling mismatch.

### Wedge

Acquire segregated wet yeast under named brewery contracts; stabilize, inactivate, dry, mill, test, and pack through paid toll processors; sell one narrow declared feed-material specification. Do not make probiotic, health, immune, or therapeutic claims. The EU Catalogue already contains an entry for inactivated yeasts, including inactivated brewer's yeast, but product compliance, feed hygiene, labelling, registration, and customer approval remain mandatory work.

### Resource and Securing Mechanism

The foundational asset is a two-sided contracted rail:

- exclusive or first-refusal rights to a defined minimum annual quantity of segregated wet yeast from named breweries, with contamination, collection-window, price-band, and rejection clauses;
- founder-owned specification, sampling plan, toll-process recipe, batch-release file, brand, and customer dossier; and
- at least two buyer-approved ingredient codes backed by annual minimum call-offs, deposits, or take-or-pay bands.

The founder creates this asset directly. Breweries are paid suppliers and pet-food plants are direct customers; no association, celebrity, distributor, university, or strategic partner must endorse the offer. By month 12, advance only if contracts reserve at least 2,000 wet tonnes/year and buyer orders cover at least 300 dry tonnes/year. Those quantities are hypotheses, not current evidence.

### Source-Backed Denominator

Eurostat reports **34.7 billion litres of EU beer production in 2024**, including 3.4 billion litres in Poland. FEDIAF's June 2026 statistics report about **8.6 million tonnes of pet food sold annually in Europe**, worth €29.4 billion; the 2025 report identified more than 400 companies and 500 production facilities. These figures prove large source and buyer industries, not recoverable yeast yield, eligible plants, or attainable share.

### Cold Start

Build a direct list of 40 Polish/Czech/German breweries and 50 regional pet-food plants from public producer lists and company sites. Ask breweries for representative yeast samples and a conditional first-refusal term sheet; ask formulation/procurement teams for their current acceptance specification before producing material. Use one paid feed-technology consultant, accredited labs, and two toll-drying quotes. No production equipment is purchased.

### Economics and Founder-Wealth Bridge

Hypothesized steady-state price is €2,000 per dry tonne, delivered COGS €1,360, and gross contribution €640/t. A conservative Year-12 shape of **12,000 dry t/year** produces €24.0m revenue, 32% gross margin, and 14% operating margin (€3.36m). At 6.0× operating profit, less €1.5m net debt/long-term obligations, equity value is €18.66m; 70% founder ownership equals **€13.06m**. No distributions are added. During corporate employment, 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested; outside capital and an employee pool together dilute 30%. Cumulative growth capital of roughly €2.0m is a hypothesis financed only after repeat orders, using customer prepayments, working-capital debt, and/or minority equity.

The 12,000 tonnes equal about 0.14% of the sourced 8.6m-tonne European pet-food volume, but that comparison is not an inclusion-rate proof. The case dies if a credible eligible formulation denominator cannot support the volume without more than 15% share of the addressable inactivated-yeast category.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

The dry powder itself is copyable. The potential control point is an approved ingredient code embedded in buyer formulations and supplier audits, combined with reserved wet-feedstock windows and a release history that reduces buyer qualification cost. A large yeast processor can copy or underprice the product; the defence works only if regional collection economics, batch consistency, and already-approved call-off positions are measurably better.

### 180-Day Paid Proof and 12-Month Resource

- Days 1–45, maximum 7,000 PLN: obtain 10 brewery samples, three conditional supply terms, 15 buyer interviews, current buyer specifications, and two toll-processing quotes.
- Days 46–100, maximum 25,000 PLN cumulative: make three lab-scale/toll batches and obtain moisture, protein, microbiology, stability, contaminant, odour, and colour evidence appropriate to the declared feed material.
- Days 101–150, maximum 38,000 PLN cumulative: two unrelated pet-food manufacturers each pay at least 8,000 PLN for a defined trial lot and qualification package. A free formulation test or distributor letter does not count.
- Days 151–180: deliver both paid lots using the same process; collect written accept/reject results and a priced annual call-off proposal.
- By month 12 and within 100,000 PLN original founder capital: secure the 2,000-wet-tonne named supply rights, two approved ingredient codes, and 300 dry tonnes of contracted annual demand before any dedicated dryer or inventory build.

### Failure Conditions

Reject if two independent buyers will not prepay; toll-processed COGS exceeds €1,500/t at the market-clearing price; variability requires brewery-specific bespoke processing; lawful feed placement needs an additive authorization rather than the intended feed-material route; customer qualification exceeds 12 months; annual contracted demand cannot cover a 30% gross margin; or the only scale path is constructing a dryer before multi-customer call-offs.

## Direction 2 — WhiteStream: Contracted Institutional-Linen Fibre

**Funnel decision: retain for finalist-grade research.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

The source-side counterparty is a commercial laundry, hospital-linen operator, or hotel group with recurring retired white cotton/polycotton flat linen. The paying customer is a European nonwoven, acoustic-felt, insulation, moulded-fibre, or technical-paper converter. The first paid event is a converter's prepaid 20-tonne qualification lot meeting a named fibre specification, not a waste audit or brokerage fee.

### Insider Customer Truth

Converters do not want “mixed textile waste.” They want predictable fibre composition, colour, contamination, fibre length, moisture, metal content, bale density, and delivery cadence. Institutional linen is commercially interesting only if it can arrive as a homogeneous industrial input. Laundries want scheduled bulk removal with clear title and rejection rules, not manual resale of individual sheets.

### Wedge

Contract title or first-refusal to pre-sorted retired white flat linen, remove incompatible items at the source under a simple specification, and use toll cutting/shredding/metal detection/baling to create one converter-approved grade. Haulage and toll processing are ordinary paid vendors. The company does not operate donation bins, consumer collection, a marketplace, resale shops, or a route-by-route field-service fleet.

### Resource and Securing Mechanism

The asset is a matched portfolio of multi-year, named-tonnage feedstock rights and output call-offs:

- exclusive title or first refusal to a minimum annual tonnage at a price formula from each source;
- source-side segregation instructions and rejection/contamination charges;
- founder-owned output specification, test method, bale identity, processing recipe, and second-source toll route; and
- converter approval plus minimum annual call-off for that grade.

By month 12, require rights to at least 3,000 t/year and paid buyer call-offs for at least 500 t/year. The same contracts should renew and expand without the founder inspecting every load.

### Source-Backed Denominator

The Commission reports **12.6m tonnes of textile waste in the EU in 2019**, with only one-fifth separately collected; the 2025 Waste Framework revision introduces textile EPR and uniform sorting requirements. Eurostat reports more than **1.9bn hotel nights in 2024**. These are broad flow indicators. The clean institutional white-linen subset is not publicly counted and is the first denominator to establish contractually.

### Cold Start

Directly approach 30 commercial laundries/hospitality-linen operators in Poland, Czechia, and Germany and 25 fibre converters. Get source samples before signing logistics. Ask buyers for a specification and a paid qualification-lot contract. Use one toll shredder, one independent textile lab, and one bulk haulier for the first lots; obtain a second toll quote before committing the post-proof capital.

### Economics and Founder-Wealth Bridge

Hypothesized average output price is €950/t, delivered COGS €684/t, gross contribution €266/t, and 28% gross margin. A conservative Year-13 shape of **30,000 t/year** produces €28.5m revenue and 13% operating margin (€3.71m). At 5.5× operating profit, less €1.8m net debt/obligations, equity value is €18.60m; 70% founder ownership equals **€13.02m**. No distributions are added. Ninety-five percent of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested during employment. Cumulative growth capital of roughly €3m and 30% total dilution are hypotheses.

Thirty thousand tonnes equal 0.24% of the sourced EU textile-waste total, but the relevant institutional grade could be far smaller. Kill the wealth case if verified eligible material is below 150,000 t/year or if 30,000 tonnes requires more than 20% share, owned truck routes, or founder-managed sorting.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

Waste traders and textile recyclers can copy spot aggregation. The only defensible version is contracted homogeneous title plus an output grade already approved in converter procurement. Each additional source should strengthen fulfilment reliability for the same SKU rather than create a new bespoke product. Copy risk remains high if laundries refuse minimum-tonnage rights or converters dual-source solely on spot price.

### 180-Day Paid Proof and 12-Month Resource

- Days 1–45, 6,000 PLN cap: sample at least 10 source streams and obtain three conditional exclusive/first-refusal offers with annual tonnage records.
- Days 46–100, 24,000 PLN cumulative: run composition, contamination, fibre-length, moisture, and processing trials; obtain two toll quotes and two buyer specifications.
- Days 101–140, 36,000 PLN cumulative: two unrelated converters each pay at least 6,000 PLN deposit for a 20-tonne qualification lot; buyer payments fund the remaining material, haulage, and toll processing.
- Days 141–180: deliver both lots and obtain written acceptance, repeat pricing, and a call-off proposal.
- By month 12 and 100,000 PLN: secure 3,000 t/year of transferable source rights, 500 t/year paid call-offs, founder-owned specification/test files, and two qualified toll routes. Do not buy shredding equipment.

### Failure Conditions

Reject if sources will sell only spot loads; feedstock is too contaminated or variable for one repeatable grade; buyer deposits cannot cover positive first-lot contribution; gross contribution remains below €200/t; haulage radius makes each source uneconomic; converter approval resets by source rather than output specification; or scaling requires owned collection routes, manual item sorting, or a processing plant before contracted demand.

## Direction 3 — QuietRoom Sentinel: Prepaid Hotel Bed-Bug Monitoring Cohort

**Funnel decision: retain conditionally, only behind a blind physical-performance gate.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

The buyer is a hotel operator with at least 100 rooms and centralized housekeeping. The paid event is a prepaid 60-day monitored-room cohort, followed by an annual per-room cartridge/replacement contract. The hotel controls the rooms, housekeeping procedure, and purchase; no guest, pest-control chain, insurer, booking platform, or regulator must authorize the first use.

### Insider Customer Truth

The manager wants silent early detection before a guest complaint or spread to adjacent rooms. They do not want visible equipment, a public “bed-bug program,” another dashboard, or weekly visits from a founder. Housekeeping needs a device that can be inspected or replaced during normal room turnover without identifying ambiguous bite marks. ANSES notes that hotel practices are heterogeneous and that public infestation data are scarce because operators protect reputation.

### Wedge

Create a non-pesticidal, tamper-evident passive monitor/harbourage cartridge positioned behind the headboard or at another validated furniture path, with a housekeeping-safe visual inspection and sealed positive sample. The first product makes no prevention, eradication, or disease claim. An independent entomologist runs blinded low-density detection comparisons against cheap passive interceptors and current inspection practice.

### Resource and Securing Mechanism

The asset is a prepaid room cohort plus an installed and repeatedly reordered physical workflow:

- founder-owned CAD/tool, material specification, monitor geometry, optional non-biocidal attractant formulation, packaging, blind-test protocol, and batch QA;
- a prepaid annual room schedule under which housekeeping replaces cartridges and sends only suspected positives; and
- a room-level installed base that produces reorder demand without founder visits.

By month 12, require at least 3,000 prepaid rooms across ten unrelated hotel operators, owned tooling, two manufacturers, and blind evidence that the device materially improves early detection. Hotel records stay hotel-owned; the control point is the prepaid installed workflow, not a prospective data moat.

### Source-Backed Denominator

Eurostat reports **1.9bn hotel and similar-accommodation nights in 2024**. That equals at least 5.2m occupied guest-bed equivalents per day before allowing for seasonality, empty rooms, and multiple guests. ANSES cites 16,850 hotels in France in 2023, reports that hotels are a relevant exposure setting, and says early detection can reduce spread, action cost, and reputation risk. It also states that no quantitative hotel-infestation dataset exists. That missing incidence and budget evidence is a first-test issue, not permission to invent demand.

### Cold Start

Build a list of 150 independent hotels and small chains in Poland, Germany, Czechia, and France. Sell to operations/housekeeping leaders with a discreet physical pilot and a fixed false-alarm/escalation protocol. Contract one entomologist and one small plastics/converted-paper manufacturer. Do not recruit pest-control partners or field technicians.

### Economics and Founder-Wealth Bridge

Hypothesized annual revenue is €75 per active room: €15 hardware/tool recovery and €60 cartridge/QA replenishment, with 66% gross margin. A conservative Year-11 installed base of **300,000 active rooms** produces €22.5m revenue and 20% operating margin (€4.5m). At 5.5× operating profit, less €0.8m obligations, equity value is €23.95m; 70% founder ownership equals **€16.77m**. No distributions are added; 95% reinvestment applies while employed. The volume equals less than 5.8% of the lower-bound 5.2m daily occupied guest-bed equivalents, but rooms and guest-beds are not interchangeable. A country-by-country eligible room count must replace that proxy before finalist status.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

EPA describes cheap do-it-yourself interceptors, and ANSES describes passive traps as inexpensive and easy to manage. An ordinary plastic trap is not a control point. The direction survives only if blind tests show a substantial detection/inspection advantage and hotels prepay annual room cohorts. The installed housekeeping procedure, replenishment schedule, and proprietary performance-tested geometry/formulation may create switching cost, but established pest-control vendors can reproduce the concept.

### 180-Day Paid Proof and 12-Month Resource

- Days 1–40, 7,000 PLN: 25 hotel interviews, current incident/cost evidence from at least five, entomologist protocol, competitor purchase, and two manufacturing quotes.
- Days 41–90, 22,000 PLN cumulative: fabricate three variants and run blinded low-density comparisons against a cheap interceptor and visual inspection.
- Days 91–130, 36,000 PLN cumulative: only if the physical test wins, two unrelated hotels each prepay at least 6,000 PLN for 100-room/60-day operational pilots.
- Days 131–180: deliver by mail, train housekeeping remotely, measure completion, damage, guest visibility, false escalations, and reorder intent. Natural catches are informative but not required to claim detection performance; blind test data carries that burden.
- By month 12 and 100,000 PLN: 3,000 prepaid annual rooms, owned production tool, second-source manufacturer, and a repeatable remote onboarding/replenishment method.

### Failure Conditions

Reject if the monitor does not materially outperform a cheap interceptor in blinded tests; hotels will pay only after a real infestation; natural low incidence makes annual willingness to pay below €50/room; housekeeping completion is below 90%; guest visibility or false alarms cause removals; annual gross margin is below 55%; or scaling requires on-site inspections or pest-controller endorsements.

## Direction 4 — RefillRail Hotels: Installed Bulk-Amenity Replenishment

**Funnel decision: reject.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

Hotel operations/procurement buys wall-mounted dispensers and a standing soap/shampoo concentrate replenishment contract. The event is every room replenishment after occupied nights.

### Customer Truth

Hotels need a reliable, tamper-resistant replacement for single-booking miniature toiletry packaging before 2030. They value guest acceptance, brand fit, housekeeping time, leakage control, and total cost more than abstract packaging compliance.

### Wedge and Resource

The proposed asset would be a prepaid room cohort with installed dispensers and an exclusive concentrate cartridge. Two hotels could directly prepay a 100-room pilot; contract manufacturers could supply product and dispensers. This is reachable without a public personality or field network.

### Denominator, Economics, and Wealth Bridge

Regulation (EU) 2025/40 prohibits specified single-use accommodation-sector cosmetic, hygiene, and toiletry packaging from 1 January 2030. Eurostat records 1.9bn hotel nights in 2024. A hypothetical 200,000-room base at €100 annual revenue would produce €20m revenue; at 18% operating margin, 5× profit, €1m obligations, and 70% ownership, founder equity would be €11.9m.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

The arithmetic is not the problem. Dispensers, bulk toiletries, private-label formulas, cartridges, and hotel procurement relationships are already ordinary capabilities of established hospitality-amenity vendors. A hotel replenishment contract is revocable purchasing, not a scarce founder-controlled rail, and the regulation pushes every incumbent toward the same solution.

### Proof and Failure Condition

A two-hotel 20,000 PLN prepaid pilot could fit 180 days and 40,000 PLN, but even success would prove only a commodity supplier relationship. Reject without an already owned patented closure/formula with independently demonstrated lifecycle advantage and prepaid multi-chain room access. Do not return through fragrance, branding, dispenser count, or revised price.

## Direction 5 — Allergen Release Rail: Direct-to-Line Test Reorders

**Funnel decision: reject.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

The buyer is a food manufacturer's quality manager; the event is release of a cleaned shared line before an allergen-sensitive production run. The proposed access asset is a prepaid site cohort with a leased reader and standing swab/lateral-flow reorder.

### Customer Truth

Quality teams want a fast actionable release result, matrix-appropriate sensitivity, few invalid tests, lot traceability, and evidence that survives customer/auditor review. They do not need another generic dashboard.

### Wedge and Resource

The apparent wedge is a one-allergen, one-line validated kit bundled into the buyer's SOP. A paid line study could be sold without a distributor. The resource would be the buyer-approved test method and annual kit call-off.

### Denominator, Economics, and Wealth Bridge

The Commission states that 14 allergens require declaration and that precautionary statements cannot replace good manufacturing practice and risk assessment. EU food, beverage, and tobacco manufacturing sold production exceeded €1tn in 2023. A hypothetical 8m tests/year at €4 revenue and 25% operating margin could support founder wealth, but no current official source establishes the eligible recurring line-release events or attainable share.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

Neogen already sells qualitative lateral-flow devices, surface swabs, CIP-rinse tests, quantitative ELISAs, and qualification support. Hygiena sells line-side allergen tests and an end-to-end data workflow; 3M sells rapid protein kits. These incumbents already own assay validation, installed readers, procurement trust, and reorders. An unknown founder would need a new assay and validation advantage, not merely a better pack or workflow.

### Proof and Failure Condition

A paid factory feasibility study might fit the capital/time cap, but it would be a service and would not create the required assay resource within 100,000 PLN. Reject. Do not return through QR records, a different allergen, contract-manufactured strips, or a lower test price.

## Direction 6 — CareWear ID: Resident-Clothing Reorder Cohort

**Funnel decision: reject.**

### Buyer and Paid Event

The buyer is a residential long-term-care facility. The event is intake of a resident's wardrobe and every subsequent laundry return. The proposed asset is a prepaid resident cohort, tagged wardrobe, table reader, and recurring replacement-label order embedded in the facility laundry workflow.

### Customer Truth

Facility staff and families want the correct clothing returned without hand-sorting, handwritten labels, repeated complaints, or an expensive enterprise laundry installation. The facility directly controls intake and laundry, so no outside partner must authorize the event.

### Wedge and Resource

The proposed wedge is a low-capital, table-reader kit for smaller homes, with facility staff attaching washable tags. A two-facility prepaid pilot could fit inside 180 days and 30,000 PLN. The apparent access asset is the tagged resident cohort and annual tag/replacement contract.

### Denominator, Economics, and Wealth Bridge

Eurostat's 2023 long-term-care dataset covers residential-care beds in 26 countries; published rates range from 20 to 1,400 beds per 100,000, with Poland at 222. A hypothetical one million active residents at €30/year produces €30m revenue, but the source does not establish the narrow unserved-facility subset, and the required share cannot be defended.

### Defensibility and Copy Risk

Datamars states that its textile-identification business already serves industrial laundry, healthcare, hospitality, and elderly care, with more than 10,000 installations and hundreds of millions of tags. Dedicated resident-clothing RFID workflows also exist. The tagged cohort would be locked to an established tag/read standard, not to a founder-owned scarce resource. Smaller-home pricing is a segment choice, not a durable control point.

### Proof and Failure Condition

The paid proof is reachable, but the wealth and control paths are not. Reject without an already controlled nationwide care-home procurement contract or a physical identification method that beats RFID on measured lifecycle economics and cannot be purchased from a tag supplier. Do not return through cheaper readers, label design, or a simplified app.

## Round-Level Rejection Notes

- Do not convert BrewForm or WhiteStream into two-sided marketplaces. Each survives only through named supply rights and named buyer call-offs held on the company's own balance sheet.
- Do not convert QuietRoom Sentinel into a pest-control referral or inspection network. Its only admissible form is mail-supplied physical monitoring run by hotel housekeeping and supported by blind product evidence.
- Do not revive hotel refills, allergen kits, or care-laundry tags through cosmetic segmentation. Current regulation or a large market denominator does not overcome incumbent control.
- None of the retained directions may spend the post-proof 60,000 PLN until two unrelated buyers have paid and accepted or explicitly rejected delivered proof. Family capital is excluded.
- No retained direction is ready for evaluation as a finalist until its decisive denominator and first-unit contribution are replaced by written supplier records, buyer specifications, and paid contracts.
