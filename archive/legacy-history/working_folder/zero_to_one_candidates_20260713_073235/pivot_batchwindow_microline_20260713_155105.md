# Zero-to-One Pivot: MicroLine Franchise

## Preserved Insight

Several small food brands become economically useful to a plant only when one counterparty makes their packaging, process, documents and money behave like one technically homogeneous funded production program.

## Structural Change

Stop buying isolated short-dated windows. MicroLine signs a 36-month exclusive **sub-MOQ intake franchise** for one named plant line and product envelope. The plant assigns MicroLine every inbound lead/order below a defined plant MOQ in that envelope, gives it first refusal over at least twelve named dates a year, and cannot sell that micro-program directly. MicroLine owns brand contracts, readiness decisions, deposits, transaction data and the program brand; the plant remains manufacturer of record.

This changes the scarce resource from a copyable booking feature to exclusive distribution/workflow access over an existing plant's unwanted small-client demand and recurring dates.

## Target Niche and Buyer

The supply-side buyer is one certified Polish ambient-sauce/condiment plant whose standard MOQ excludes profitable small brands and whose sales/quality team wastes time on incomplete sub-MOQ inquiries. Economic end customers are brands ordering 500–5,000 units per SKU under one jar/process/allergen envelope.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

The plant loses or mishandles sub-MOQ leads and wants incremental line contribution without adding a micro-client sales/quality department. It grants the franchise only if MicroLine funds dates, absorbs its own coordination work and meets rejection/default thresholds. Brands pay because the franchise offers a named plant, repeat dates and a standard product-development path rather than a one-off introduction.

## Value and Profit Wedge

Three unrelated brand deposits fund the first program date. MicroLine charges 10–14% of routed production value, pays the plant its fixed manufacturing price and bears only expressly defined coordination failures. The plant receives minimum annual funded production or the exclusivity terminates. Gross margin must exceed 55% after food technologist, payment, insurance and reserve; founder cash never finances ingredients or plant receivables.

## Domination Wedge

Dominate all sub-MOQ demand in one envelope at one plant, then become the plant's default micro-brand front end. Leads, reorders, readiness failures, yield and plant dates accumulate inside the exclusive franchise. A marketplace can copy the workflow but cannot access the assigned lead flow or dates during the contract.

## Acquisition Channel

Screen 30 plants and approach ten with a quantified outsourced-program proposal. The founder offers to process the plant's last 12 months of rejected/sub-MOQ inquiry metadata under NDA, qualify the reachable pipeline, and present three funded brands before exclusivity becomes fully effective. Brand acquisition starts with assigned rejected leads plus 100 precisely matched independent brands; it does not depend on media or an audience.

## Primary Control Point

Tier-1 hypothesis after execution:

- 36-month exclusive assignment of all sub-MOQ inbound demand for the named envelope;
- first refusal on at least twelve production dates/year at pre-agreed economics;
- direct brand contracts/deposits and control of residual slot allocation;
- rights to the program brand and aggregated run/outcome data;
- renewal/assignability and plant-change protections if SLA fails.

An option on one day, referral fee or non-exclusive lead-sharing agreement is rejected.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Food technologist defines the envelope and plant audit.
2. Obtain one 120-day conditional franchise option for <=10,000 PLN plus anonymised historic rejected-lead metadata.
3. Require evidence of at least 50 relevant rejected/sub-MOQ inquiries in the prior year or comparable idle capacity.
4. Contact assigned plus external brands; obtain three deposits totalling >=30,000 PLN and covering 110% of the first non-cancellable plant obligation by day 180.
5. Franchise becomes effective only when deposits, technical files and plant approval exist.
6. Execute one date within month 12 under total founder capital <=100,000 PLN.

An unknown founder can secure it because the plant gives up demand it currently rejects, receives a minimum-performance escape, and funds nothing. If the plant already serves these brands efficiently, it is not a target.

## Expansion Path

First add recurring dates and reorders at the same plant. Add a second plant only for geographic resilience or an adjacent envelope, using proven lead conversion and readiness data. Long-term scale is a network of exclusive outsourced micro-brand programs, not a public marketplace. Plants can later pay a minimum programme fee plus revenue share.

## Minimum People Stack

Founder for commercial/data/payment design, one food technologist/quality lead, one food/commercial lawyer and plant operations. A programme manager is hired only after repeat dates. Founder time stays 15–20 hours/week through first proof.

## Principal Risk

Plants with enough small-brand demand may already monetise it or refuse to assign customer relationships, while plants willing to grant exclusivity may lack demand or operational quality. The franchise can therefore select adverse-quality supply. A large co-packer network can offer its own program at other plants.

## First Disconfirming Test

After ten qualified plant negotiations, kill if no plant both (a) documents at least 50 relevant rejected/sub-MOQ inquiries or equivalent idle dates and (b) signs the 36-month lead/date franchise option for <=10,000 PLN. After 100 qualified brand approaches including assigned leads, kill without three unrelated deposits by day 180.

## Evidence Preserved

Company sources establish low-MOQ manufacturing and crowded discovery/brokerage. They support the existence of micro-brand demand and reject a directory. BatchWindow's technical-envelope and deposit logic remains the operating core.

## New Hypotheses

- A capable plant has valuable rejected leads yet will exclusively assign them to an unknown operator.
- Assigned leads materially lower CAC and make the first program fundable.
- A 36-month contract is enforceable and valuable without founder-funded minimum guarantees.
- The plant does not reclaim successful brands at renewal or use SLA leverage to compress margins.
- Twenty to thirty such franchises can support a non-heroic founder-wealth bridge.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The original candidate controlled one short-lived date and could be copied by adding booking/deposit features. MicroLine controls a plant's entire defined small-client distribution channel, assigned inbound leads, recurring first-refusal dates and brand payment flow for three years. It changes the supplier contract, acquisition channel and durability rather than renaming the booking workflow.

Return MicroLine to the evaluator first only if the evaluator accepts that adverse-selection risk is testable inside the option/deposit gates. This is a prioritisation judgment, not validation.
