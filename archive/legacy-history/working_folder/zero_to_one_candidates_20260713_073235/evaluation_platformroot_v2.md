# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PlatformRoot — V2 After Decision-Critical Research

Advisory evaluation only. This is not an independent panel score, chatbot score, validation result, or confirmation. PlatformRoot's wording and fixed economics are evaluated unchanged; this document does not design a revision.

## Diagnosis

PlatformRoot still contains a commercially useful insight: post-warranty private-label heat-pump repairs may require a provenance-linked platform/revision/outcome graph and an accountable basket, not another manual search tool. The completed research does not establish the exact addressable stock, annual eligible repair events, average basket, 20-order account frequency, wrong-fit rate, insurability, or a financeable cash cycle; it also finds adverse low-repair-cost evidence from non-matched Swiss systems and confirms large adjacent data, inventory, logistics, and installer-distribution incumbents. The fixed conservative wealth case therefore remains arithmetic without a credible denominator or operating bridge, while the proposed control point remains unbuilt and nonexclusive. The 180-day three-firm paid test is narrowly reachable, but the exact business cannot proceed to an independent panel because the Founder Wealth Gate fails and further public research has been exhausted.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### What the completed research establishes

- Poland has a substantial broad heat-pump stock and a fragmented importer/model environment. NFOŚiGW reports about 264,164 requested air-heat-pump sources through April 2026 within the Clean Air programme, while UOKiK/KAS import controls show multiple models and importers. Neither source counts completed post-warranty hydronic private-label units or repair events. [NFOŚiGW heat-source data](https://czystepowietrze.gov.pl/efekty-programu/zrodla-ciepla) and [UOKiK 2025 controls](https://uokik.gov.pl/pompy-ciepla-wspolne-dzialania-uokik-ih-i-kas)
- No current public bottom-up count exists for the candidate's exact Polish/CEE post-warranty private-label hydronic stock, annual eligible repair/part events, or annual eligible baskets per independent service firm. The broad 16,000+ F-gas-enterprise register still mixes refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat-pump activity and cannot substitute for that denominator. [UDT register](https://www.udt.gov.pl/uslugi-udt/szwo-i-f-gazy/rejestry)
- The only public longitudinal repair evidence found is not market-matched: Swiss monitoring of quality-selected small heat pumps reported high availability and low average repair cost. It does not prove Polish private-label units behave the same way, but it is adverse evidence against assuming frequent high-value failures without a matched dataset. [Swiss monitoring](https://www.aramis.admin.ch/Texte/?ProjectID=18166&Sprache=en-US) and [IEA HPT summary](https://heatpumpingtechnologies.org/publications/long-term-behaviour-and-performance-of-residential-heat-pumps-in-dwellings/)
- Public original-part prices range from hundreds of PLN to more than 13,000 PLN. That makes a €1,200 basket possible for some repairs but supplies no mean, median, mix, repair frequency, or private-label transaction distribution. A catalogue proves SKU availability, not completed-basket economics. [CNC parts catalogue](https://cncpompyciepla.pl/czesci-do-pomp-ciepla/)
- EPREL and Heat Pump KEYMARK can seed brand, supplier, certificate, and some OEM/private-label relationships. Their public layers stop before serial/date/revision-to-part, connector, firmware, supersession, and outcome mapping. This supports the existence of a data gap, while also showing that part of the ancestry layer is public rather than proprietary. [EPREL supplier guidance](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/suppliers_en), [Heat Pump KEYMARK database](https://www.heatpumpkeymark.com/en/nc/ps-keymark/certificate-holders/), and [KEYMARK private-label rules](https://www.heatpumpkeymark.com/fileadmin/user_upload/documents/HPK0029_Annex-G_V2-1.pdf)
- Independent observation, study, disassembly, or testing of a lawfully possessed product can be lawful where no valid restriction applies, but confidentiality, contract, trade-secret, database, and privacy rules remain source-specific. Contributor terms cannot blanket-clear OEM manuals, supplier portals, employer records, or indirectly identifiable homeowner data. [Directive (EU) 2016/943](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/943/oj/eng/) and [EDPB anonymisation guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/contact/frequently-asked-questions_en)
- No matched wrong-part, wrong-revision, fitment-return, or second-order rate is public. Daikin's public terms show that special-order parts may be non-returnable, so PlatformRoot's full customer refund may not be recoverable upstream. Bluon and CNC publish compatibility claims/processes but not audited realised rates for this beachhead. [Daikin price-list terms](https://www.daikin.pl/content/dam/DAPO/document-library/cenniki-2024/DAIKIN%20Cennik%20rozwi%C4%85za%C5%84%20grzewczych%20do%20zastosowa%C5%84%20rezydencyjnych_08.04.2024.pdf)
- Public Polish liability wording shows that relevant product/public-liability cover and optional extensions exist, but standard exclusions can affect removal/reinstallation cost, pure financial loss, design, advice, control, and opinion work. No bound quote covers PlatformRoot's exact combination of merchant sale, cross-brand equivalent recommendation, technical dossier, wrong-fit refund, and CEE territory. [Warta SME policy wording](https://www.warta.pl/documents/oferta_dla_firmy/WARTA_OWU_Ekstrabiznes_Plus_C7905_int.pdf)
- Public supplier terms generally support prepayment and individually negotiated or secured trade credit, not an unsecured scale facility for an unknown merchant. Professional portals prove ordering and delivery mechanisms, not PlatformRoot's gross margin, neutral drop-ship, supplier credit, returns, or title allocation. [Sofiterm terms](https://www.sofiterm.pl/ows), [Daikin professional parts](https://www.daikin.pl/content/dam/DAPO/document-library/2021/Cze%C5%9Bci%20zamienne%20i%20gwarancje%20Daikin.pdf), and [Mitsubishi B2B parts](https://mitsubishi-les.pl/czesci-zamienne/)
- Global and local competitors possess substantial adjacent assets. Bluon claims tens of millions of models, extensive parts cross-references, manuals, distributor inventory, APIs/plugins, and compatible-part routing. CNC already combines local fitment help, inventory, and sales; Thermosilesia claims 3,500 installer/trade customers, technical service, large warehousing, and parts; Onninen claims more than 100,000 Polish customers, 34 branches, 500,000 products, and eight-country reach. None proves complete coverage of PlatformRoot's exact cases, but the startup does not enter an empty channel. [Bluon developer documentation](https://docs.bluon.com/), [CNC](https://cncpompyciepla.pl/czesci-do-pomp-ciepla/), [Thermosilesia](https://thermosilesia.pl/pl/page/wspolpraca), and [Onninen](https://onninen.pl/o-firmie)

### Fixed scenario arithmetic that reconciles

- 2,000 accounts × 20 baskets = 40,000 annual orders.
- 40,000 × (€1,200 merchandise + €60 fee) = €50.4 million reported principal revenue.
- Merchandise COGS at 80% is €38.4 million; fee direct cost at 20% is €0.48 million.
- Gross profit is €11.52 million, €288 per order, or 22.9%.
- Fixed operating profit is €5.6 million, €140 per order, or 11.1%, leaving a €5.92 million/€148-per-order operating-expense envelope.
- A 4.0x operating-profit value is €22.4 million; 70% founder ownership is about €15.68 million. The table does not double-count distributions and uses the required reinvestment assumptions.

### Why the arithmetic is not a credible conservative case

- Two thousand accounts are 12.5% of the broad 16,000-company F-gas ceiling. If only 10,000 firms are eligible, required penetration is 20%; at 6,000 it is 33.3%; at 4,000 it is 50%. The actual eligible count is unavailable and necessarily below the broad ceiling.
- Forty thousand annual orders equal 5.37% of the entire cited 745,000-unit all-heat-pump stock. The targeted post-warranty private-label hydronic subset is smaller, so the required annual order-to-stock ratio is necessarily higher before accounting for events won by OEMs, distributors, CNC, other service centers, repair avoidance, or replacement instead of repair.
- Each account must buy 1.67 eligible baskets per month and €24,000 of parts per year from PlatformRoot. No matched benchmark supports this frequency or wallet share.
- The €148-per-order operating-expense envelope must cover acquisition, expert review, data maintenance, fulfillment labor, support, payment costs not treated as direct cost, insurance, compliance, software, management, bad debt, and financing. No cost stack demonstrates €140 operating profit after those items.
- Annual merchandise COGS is about €105,205 per calendar day. A €2 million line covers roughly 19 days before VAT timing, payment-processor reserves, freight, refunds, chargebacks, or safety stock; 30 days requires about €3.16 million and 60 days about €6.31 million. Full customer prepayment could reduce the cycle, but customer, supplier, VAT, processor, and refund timing is uncontracted.
- At a 5% full-refund incidence, gross customer refund outflow is €2.52 million annually. Even if all value is eventually recovered, timing consumes liquidity; if recovery is incomplete, the €288 gross profit erodes. The actual rate and recovery are unavailable.

### Remaining hypotheses after prescribed research

- Unrelated service firms will pay 750 PLN, prepay the part basket, accept the unknown merchant's evidence and report the outcome.
- The family records contain the required repeated platform families and can lawfully seed ten dossiers.
- Twenty annual eligible orders per mature account, €1,200 average merchandise value, 20% product gross margin, <5% wrong-fit refunds, <€300 CAC, and three-order payback coexist.
- A bound insurance package covers the product and fitment/advice promise with a premium, deductible, exclusions, and limit compatible with the 39,000 PLN proof budget.
- Customer-cleared prepayment, direct shipping, supplier payment timing, processor reserves, VAT, and refund recovery sustain a zero/negative cash cycle at 40,000 orders.
- After 250 cases, 70% of accepted cases can be resolved from existing evidence; 100 outcome mappings and 1,000 provenance-controlled nodes are lawfully commercialisable and meaningfully harder to reproduce.
- Local incumbents leave the exact private-label gap large enough, and Bluon cannot localise quickly enough, for PlatformRoot to build a compounding advantage.

## Overall Score

**51/100.** Weighted points are 102.5 across 20.0 total weight, giving a weighted average of 5.125/10 and an overall score of 51 after multiplying by 10 and rounding. The score declines from V1 because targeted research did not merely leave generic uncertainty: it confirmed that the broad denominators cannot support the fixed case, found adverse low-repair-cost evidence, exposed standard insurance exclusions, quantified the €2 million line at only about 19 days of COGS, and established stronger local/global adjacent capabilities.

## Control Point

**Current: Tier 3. Attainable: Tier 2 only after evidence not present.** PlatformRoot currently owns no unrelated repair outcomes, contributor rights, routed transactions, reserved inventory, supplier terms, exclusive mappings, or bound insurance/finance position. Public EPREL/KEYMARK data, ordinary professional parts, contracted expert work, software, and the UDT list are accessible inputs rather than controlled resources.

The proposed month-12 graph could become Tier 2 if 250 closed cases from 25 unrelated firms actually yield 100 accurate outcome-confirmed mappings, 1,000 provenance-controlled nodes, and 70% evidence reuse under enforceable commercialisation rights. It is not Tier 1: contributor rights are nonexclusive, source rights remain record-specific, physical supply is uncontrolled, public layers expose some ancestry, and competitors can independently observe the same machines and outcomes.

**Strongest copy risk:** CNC, Thermosilesia, an OEM portal, or another distributor can add structured case intake, revision/outcome logging, and a fitment guarantee atop existing inventory, technical staff, installer trust, and logistics; Bluon can localise its data engine and compatible-part routing through APIs/plugins and local distributor inventory. A competitor needs to cover the same few high-frequency platform families, not reproduce PlatformRoot's entire aspirational graph.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The candidate includes all required scenario fields, follows 95%/100% reinvestment rules, assumes no employment-period distribution, avoids double-counting, and uses a non-heroic 4.0x operating-profit multiple. Those presentation strengths do not make the conservative operating profit credible.

The fixed 40,000 annual baskets require more than 5.37% annual order density even against the entire broad Polish heat-pump stock; the actual post-warranty private-label hydronic subset is smaller. The required 2,000 accounts represent 12.5% of the broadest F-gas ceiling and a much larger share of any eligible account base. Each account must produce 20 annual high-value orders, but no matched public or private evidence establishes the stock, event rate, basket mix, order frequency, wallet share, or incumbent leakage.

The €5.6 million operating profit also assumes the €148/order expense envelope can absorb technical work, acquisition, fulfillment, support, insurance, compliance, data operations, bad debt, and finance. Exact insurance is unquoted, upstream return recovery is unproved, and the €2 million line covers about 19 days of merchandise COGS rather than an ordinary 30–60-day operating cycle. The founder's 70% ownership is mathematically possible only if customer prepayment, retained cash, supplier terms, and debt solve scale finance without additional dilution; none is contracted.

Expected and strong cases multiply the same unproved event density, basket economics, cash conversion, and data advantage. Because prescribed public research is exhausted and the fixed scenario cannot be supported without private transaction evidence that does not yet exist, this is no longer a “research further and assume neutral” gate. The exact founder-wealth bridge is structurally too assumption-heavy to pass.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS narrowly—for paid proof only.** The founder can retain employment and attempt three unrelated paid cases within the stated 39,000 PLN: audit only lawfully shareable family records, prepare ten dossiers, contact 100 named firms, charge 750 PLN, buy only after customer prepayment, use an independent F-gas/electronics expert, and require installed outcomes plus a repeat. The family business is correctly excluded from payment evidence, no warehouse/OEM partnership is required, and three cases fit the founder's time.

This pass depends on release gates rather than assumptions. Before the first basket, the exact SKU/source must have a documented manufacturer/importer/distributor role, conformity and traceability evidence, permissible fitment basis, data provenance, return position, and bound liability cover for both the product and technical advice/fitment promise. Public research found that standard policy wording can exclude precisely relevant removal/reinstallation, pure financial loss, design, advice, control, and opinion exposures. The budget also does not contain a known premium/deductible. If a binding quote and low-risk SKU path cannot fit the 39,000 PLN maximum, Reachability fails before accepting money.

Even if three firms pay, the gate proves a paid repair transaction, not the conservative business. It does not establish 20 annual orders per account, average basket, mature CAC, return rate, 70% evidence reuse, 11.1% operating margin, or scale financing.

## Expansion Check

The initial niche is specific enough to test but not proven large enough to dominate profitably. The family record audit may reveal repeated private-label families, and public EPREL/KEYMARK layers can accelerate initial mapping. Those are speed advantages, not exclusion rights.

Moving from three Polish platform families into CEE is not asymmetric. PlatformRoot must repeatedly acquire outcomes, clear provenance, establish supplier/return terms, localise technical support, and obtain country/territory coverage. CNC and Thermosilesia can deepen within their installed trade relationships; Onninen can use broad CEE distribution; OEMs can route their own parts; Bluon can add local datasets and supplier connections to an existing engine. Local execution may win isolated cases but does not yet show a durable path from beachhead to founder-wealth scale.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The fixed conservative volume lacks a serviceable denominator after targeted research.** Neither exact stock nor eligible events exist publicly, and 40,000 orders require an annual density above 5.37% even against the entire broad stock.
2. **Twenty annual orders per mature account is unsupported.** It requires 1.67 eligible baskets and €24,000 of parts spend per account per month/year respectively, with no matched benchmark or paid repeat behavior.
3. **The merchant model's profit and cash conversion are unproved.** The €148/order operating envelope omits a validated insurance/compliance/support/finance stack, while €2 million covers only about 19 days of €38.4 million annual COGS.
4. **Liability cannot currently be bounded.** Standard policy exclusions can hit the wrong-fit replacement and technical-advice promise, and no quote, premium, deductible, recall cover, or professional-liability endorsement exists.
5. **The control point is empty and nonexclusive.** Public mapping seeds exist; source rights are record-specific; 250 cases/100 mappings/70% reuse are targets rather than assets; contributors and facts can flow to rivals.
6. **Incumbents flank both sides of the wedge.** Bluon possesses an enormous adjacent data/API/parts-routing engine; CNC, Thermosilesia, Onninen, OEM portals, and service centers possess local inventory, installer reach, technical help, and logistics.
7. **The scale operation conflicts with the founder's part-time advantage.** Three cases fit employment; 250 first-year cases and a material unresolved-case share require technical review, QA, returns, compliance, and service operations before the graph proves automation.
8. **A small paid test cannot repair the fixed wealth case.** It can show willingness to pay and one transaction's contribution, but not the market-wide event density, mature order frequency, funding, margins, or competitive durability required for €15.68 million of founder equity.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest insight is that a repair-data asset becomes commercially useful when it links badge/OEM ancestry, serial and revision constraints, provenance, shipped parts, installed outcomes, callbacks, and returns to an accountable transaction. The family installer can lawfully seed vocabulary and first observations without being counted as customer proof, while unrelated paid cases can create the first real outcome records. That insight is worth preserving, but it does not validate the exact high-volume merchant-of-record shape.

## Recommendation

**pivot**

Do not repeat public research or send the unchanged candidate to an independent panel. The fixed business requires high event density, high account frequency, thinly financed principal sales, unbound liability, and a prospective nonexclusive graph to align simultaneously; the targeted evidence pass could not substantiate those dependencies. A structural revision must preserve only the strongest transaction-linked ancestry/outcome insight while materially changing the broken wealth, control, liability, or operating shape. Designing that revision belongs to the pivot role, not this evaluation.
