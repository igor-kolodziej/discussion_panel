# Zero-to-One Candidate: BlindProof

Candidate generation only — not validated. All prices, conversion rates, margins, retention, effect sizes, customer counts, legal classifications and valuation multiples are hypotheses unless a source is cited.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell wearable-owning supplement users a prepaid, manufactured, placebo-matched personal test that tells them whether a common supplement produces an individually detectable benefit, then compound the paid relationship and consented response data into the neutral cross-brand testing layer that supplement sellers are structurally disinclined to build.

## Founder Fit

The founder's neuroscience, biosignal, data-science, business-analysis and quantified-self background fits experimental design, wearable-signal analysis, uncertainty communication, software and cohort economics. AI can accelerate protocol review, software, support triage and analysis without becoming the product's authority.

The founder is not naturally suited to clinician-heavy sales, influencer content or manual coaching. BlindProof is deliberately direct-to-consumer, asynchronous and camera-independent. Food law, supplement safety, manufacturing, privacy and insurance are purchased from named professional vendors. The founder remains responsible for the product, analysis and customer relationship; contractors do not supply market access or validate efficacy.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first customer is a healthy adult in Poland who owns an Oura Ring, has at least 60 usable nights of data, and is considering continuing a legal, common sleep supplement. This user already spends on health optimization and is comfortable with tracked experiments.

The desired outcome is not another recommendation. It is a personal purchasing decision: “Does this improve my sleep enough that I should keep taking and buying it?” The user wants to avoid wasting money, swallowing an ineffective product, mistaking expectation for effect, and spending months reading correlations that cannot separate the supplement from training, alcohol, illness, season or regression to the mean.

Current workarounds are weak:

- take the supplement and watch the wearable score, fully unblinded;
- stop and restart it informally, without randomized periods or matched placebo;
- use N-of-1 apps that organize tags but do not manufacture a blind sequence;
- ask a clinician or online community for population advice that does not answer individual response; or
- trust a supplement brand whose business benefits from continued purchase.

A direct Blueprint-community post asks for almost the exact missing product: weekly blinded placebo/supplement delivery followed by analysis of Garmin history. It is one demand anecdote, not validation.

## Product Wedge

The first SKU is a fixed 10–12 week **1 mg melatonin versus matched cellulose control sleep self-test** for adults already considering melatonin, using coded daily capsules, randomized crossover blocks, a predeclared primary outcome, Oura import, a short daily confounder/adherence check and a final result.

The customer-facing output has only three defensible conclusions:

1. individually detectable benefit under the completed protocol;
2. no individually detectable benefit under the completed protocol; or
3. inconclusive because effect, adherence or noise did not support a decision.

The result is explicitly not a diagnosis, treatment instruction, population efficacy claim or recommendation to stop prescribed care. The first SKU excludes diagnosed/urgent sleep problems, medicines or conditions identified by counsel as incompatible, pregnancy, minors, unsupported devices and users unwilling to accept a null or inconclusive answer. The active/control outer package, daily coding, permitted food claims, warnings and sequence disclosure require written Polish legal approval before sale.

Do not launch an open protocol builder, supplement store, lab-test bundle, medical service, multi-ingredient stack or AI health adviser in the wedge.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

Wearables have made high-frequency personal outcomes cheap to collect, but observation is not experimentation. Existing trackers remove logging friction while leaving the user's expectation and the physical placebo problem intact. Academic personalized trials prove that matched capsules, randomized periods, wearable monitoring and individual reports can be delivered remotely; they do not package this as a repeat consumer purchasing product.

Supplement brands can contract the same capsules, but an honest test will often tell a customer not to repurchase the brand's product. BlindProof's neutral value proposition is commercially adverse to the seller and valuable to the buyer. The gap is therefore not an unavailable algorithm; it is an integrated neutral product whose revenue comes from the decision, including null results, rather than from proving that the active ingredient worked.

The direction survives only if consumers actually pay for that honesty and repeat it. Stated interest is insufficient.

## Underused Momentum

- Oura reports more than 5.5 million rings sold; in May 2026 it said it was on pace to exceed five million paid members. This is a counted high-intent wearable population before Garmin, Apple and other devices.
- AESGP's 2025 European work surveyed 6,000 consumers and manufacturer data representing 65% of the EEA market; its 2026 summary says 62% of supplement users take supplements daily.
- A published 2025 series delivered 60 remote personalized crossover trials of 0.5 mg and 3 mg melatonin versus color-matched cellulose placebo, with Fitbit data and an individual report. This supports protocol feasibility, not commercial willingness to pay or the exact proposed 1 mg design.
- Current apps including N of 1, Health OS and Vitalix show active interest in wearable-supported personal experiments but visibly emphasize software rather than a manufactured blind sequence.
- Polish supplement rules provide an accessible notification route: GIS states that supplements are food, require food-business registration and first-market notification, and may enter the market on notification while the operator remains responsible for safety, labeling and advertising.
- Polish manufacturers currently advertise pilot or small-batch supplement production, including one claim of batches from 250 units. Only signed quotes for matched active/control sequences count as resource evidence.

## Beachhead Market

Polish adults who:

- own Oura and have at least 60 recent nights;
- currently use or are seriously considering melatonin for sleep onset;
- purchase supplements online;
- accept a 10–12 week protocol and the possibility of “no detectable benefit” or “inconclusive”; and
- are reachable through exact-intent search and open quantified-self/Oura/Blueprint communities.

The beachhead is not “everyone with insomnia,” all supplement users or all wearable owners. The first 100 tests stay in Poland, one language, one device family, one ingredient, one dose and one outcome protocol.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Founding price hypothesis: 699 PLN including VAT for one full test. No revenue from supplement upsells, referrals, user-data resale or a favorable result.

Initial variable-cost hypothesis per paid test:

| Item | PLN |
|---|---:|
| Active/control capsules and allocated batch QA | 75 |
| Randomized sequence packaging and labels | 35 |
| Fulfilment and domestic shipping | 25 |
| Payment, support, refund/adverse-event reserve | 20 |
| Wearable import, compute and result delivery | 20 |
| Total before acquisition | 175 |

Gross contribution before acquisition is 524 PLN, or 75% of the consumer price. Target blended CAC is <=175 PLN and contribution after CAC is >=349 PLN, or about 50% of price. These costs require signed quotes and first-cohort actuals.

At scale, an active annual customer buys 2.2 tests at an average €149 ex-VAT-equivalent recognized price plus €39 of annual membership/analysis value, producing about €367 annual revenue. Membership cannot be required to read an already purchased result. Mature gross-margin target is 68–72%; operating-margin target is 22–30% after acquisition, safety, support, software and product development.

Prepayment funds manufacturing. Kill if the model needs large finished-goods inventory, individualized clinician time, an Oura commercial contract, or sale of user data to produce positive contribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10:

1. Build a free “Is your sleep supplement working, or are you reading noise?” analyser that accepts a voluntary 30-day Oura CSV and returns within-person variance, likely trial length and major confounders without claiming treatment effect.
2. Interview 20 qualified Polish Oura-plus-supplement users using two legally reviewed mock reports: one null and one inconclusive.
3. Require at least five deposits of 199 PLN from those 20. The deposit defines ingredient, duration, price, manufacturing deadline, exclusions and refund trigger.
4. Obtain the remaining five through exact-intent Polish search ads and transparent posts in open communities, not influencer access.

First 100:

- sell 100 numbered founding slots from the analyser and exact-intent pages;
- cap paid acquisition at 12,000 PLN until 30 deposits or 20 full prepayments exist;
- publish protocol, exclusions, data handling, batch certificate and the fraction of prior results that were null/inconclusive, without publishing medical testimonials;
- ask completers to buy a second different fixed protocol, not refer friends for a health claim; and
- retain only channels with CAC payback on the first test.

No manufacture occurs merely because 100 people joined a free waitlist.

## Primary Control Point

Current tier is Tier 3: a protocol, landing page, manufacturer conversation and analysis notebook are replaceable.

The attainable 12-month control point is Tier 2:

- legally cleared paired active/control product specification and matched-capsule acceptance method;
- owned sequence design, serial namespace, allocation keys, software and result engine;
- contractually assigned batch, packaging and QA documentation with multi-source production rights;
- direct prepaid customer cohort and repeat-test reservations;
- consented longitudinal mapping between protocol, baseline phenotype/wearable variance, adherence, individual detectability and repeat choice; and
- validated rules that reduce trial length and inconclusive rates without overstating certainty.

The physical capsule is not the moat. The compounder is the paid cross-protocol relationship and the response graph that helps BlindProof select a test a specific user can actually resolve. Data use is limited to the promised service and explicit de-identified protocol improvement; customers retain export/deletion rights. No claim is made that BlindProof owns medical records or population truth.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

The foundational resource must be secured in this order:

1. Written Polish food/consumer/medical-device/privacy opinion on the exact package, placebo disclosure, self-test language, outcome and exclusions.
2. Two manufacturer quotes for one matched active/control SKU, including MOQ, stability/batch tests, packaging, randomization, lead time, product notification support, failure terms and rights to the specification/records.
3. Physical matching check and a blinded distinguishability test before customer use.
4. Sequence/key system, Oura CSV import, preregistered analysis and plain-language report tested on synthetic and founder-owned historical data.
5. Paid cohort manufacture only after 30 deposits or 20 full prepayments and positive first-batch contribution are plausible.
6. Two completed paid cohorts inside 12 months, with adherence, detectable/no-effect/inconclusive, complaints, adverse events, support minutes, CAC and repeat reservations recorded.

An unknown founder can buy legal, registered manufacturing, packaging, insurance and nutrition/safety work as vendor services. None must endorse efficacy, provide customers, grant closed distribution or accept commercial risk. The founder directly owns the product relationship and software/data resource.

The first-year resource proof is: 250 legally saleable sequences or smaller signed pilot batch if available; two qualified manufacturers; 100 paid starts; >=75 usable completions; >=35 paid second-test reservations; no serious product/privacy event; and a measured CAC/payback cohort.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial route captures an exact moment of doubt: a wearable user has abundant data but cannot tell whether a recurring supplement purchase works. A free variance analyser can answer a useful part of that question before asking for trust or money, then price the necessary experiment length.

The route is:

`exact-intent query/open community -> owned variance analyser -> qualified mock result -> prepaid fixed test -> owned result relationship -> next fixed test`

This is a directly creatable distribution asset. It needs no doctor, employer, supplement brand, retailer, wearable partnership, celebrity or closed group. Manual CSV upload remains a fallback if APIs change.

Its advantage is only proven if paid cohorts show low CAC and repeat purchase. An SEO page and an email list alone are not a control point.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Visible alternatives include app-only N-of-1 trackers, Oura's own health guidance, supplement testing/review publishers, personalized-health services, academic trial programs and supplement brands. BlindProof differs by manufacturing the matched blind sequence and earning equally from positive, null and inconclusive results.

Expansion is deliberately sequential:

1. one Polish Oura/melatonin protocol;
2. second sleep/recovery supplement only after the first cohort proves null-result value and repeat purchase;
3. Garmin/Apple import only after independent export paths and cross-device measurement qualification;
4. Germany and another EU market using country-specific food/claims review and the same manufacturer/process;
5. a library of fast, reversible, safe protocols selected by baseline detectability;
6. optional employer/wellness bulk purchase only after DTC economics, without employer access to individual results.

Do not expand into medications, disease treatment, diagnostics, blood testing, clinician prescribing, bespoke protocols or brand-funded favorable studies. Each new ingestible SKU must reuse the sequence/analysis/manufacturing rail and repay qualification cost within 12 months.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland offers lower-cost software, regulatory and contract-manufacturing work, a national supplement-notification path, a founder who can communicate locally, and an EU base for later multilingual expansion. Starting in Polish avoids competing first for US wellness media and lets the company freeze one legal/product regime.

This is an execution advantage, not a legal monopoly. Food rules and permitted claims vary across EU states, and notification may reset. A US product can enter Europe after doing the same work. Defense must come from European manufacturing files, localized acquisition, response history and repeat relationship—not from being Polish.

## Compounding Advantage

Every completed test should improve four things:

- eligibility: which users/outcomes are measurable enough to sell responsibly;
- design: block length, washout, confounder handling and adherence thresholds;
- economics: expected support, inconclusive/refund reserve, CAC and second-test selection; and
- trust: transparent rates of benefit, no detectable benefit and inconclusive results.

The company becomes harder to replace if it can predict that a specific user should not buy a test because their data cannot resolve it. That sacrifices short-term revenue but strengthens neutral positioning and reduces poor outcomes.

The moat fails if the data do not reduce inconclusive rates or CAC, users will not repeat, or one wearable platform can reproduce the physical/operational layer through a single manufacturer contract.

### Wealth-gate sensitivities

The conservative bridge is sensitive to repeat frequency, CAC, operating service load, trust and valuation. Kill the wealth thesis if mature repeat purchase is below 1.5 tests/year, contribution after CAC is below 35%, support/safety work prevents 20% operating margin, or a five-million-plus paid wearable population cannot yield 45,000 active customers without privileged distribution. A 5.5× operating-profit value is a hypothesis, not evidence.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

During corporate employment, 95% of after-tax business cash available for distribution is reinvested in conservative and expected scenarios; founder distributions are optional and capped at 5%. The strong scenario reinvests 100%. Retained cash funds additional batch qualification, safety/legal work, software, customer acquisition with measured payback and small inventory buffers.

Prepayment should keep working capital neutral or positive. Do not use retained cash to subsidize inconclusive tests indefinitely or to launch multiple unproved SKUs. A new protocol receives capital only after the prior cohort has positive contribution, acceptable support burden and paid repeat demand.

The model avoids double counting: founder wealth is scenario equity value after debt/obligations; retained earnings are reflected in operating capacity/value and are not added again as personal distributions.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder remains employed until all are true:

- at least three independent paid cohorts and 300 paid tests;
- contracted recurring or repeat demand from multiple independent customers, with no single source >20% of revenue;
- positive contribution after manufacturing, refunds, CAC and paid support;
- repeatable acquisition over at least three monthly cohorts;
- >=35% paid second-test signal among usable completers;
- manufacturer redundancy and legal/safety operations not dependent on founder availability;
- at least 12 months of personal runway outside business working capital; and
- a forecast showing that full-time effort accelerates a working system rather than substitutes for unpaid support.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If ingestible blind kits fail, retain only assets with independent lawful value:

- wearable variance analyser and outcome-quality engine;
- synthetic/test harnesses and uncertainty reporting;
- customer research about null-result value;
- manufacturer, food-law, privacy and packaging diligence;
- acquisition creative and measured keyword economics; and
- non-medical fixed-protocol software that could test user-controlled lifestyle behaviors without placebo ingestion.

Inventory is capped to prepaid cohorts. No lab, equipment or long lease remains. Do not “recover” by becoming a generic supplement shop, health coach, CRO, influencer or medical adviser.

- At least 60% of qualified prospects value a truthful null/inconclusive result at >=599 PLN.
- Twenty full payments or 30 deposits can be acquired within 60 days and 12,000 PLN.
- Active/control capsules can be legally labeled, manufactured and made perceptually matched inside the first-capital cap.

## Kill Criteria

Kill the business shape if any occur:

- legal opinion requires a clinical-trial, pharmacy, medical-device or clinician-supervised route for the first product;
- no two manufacturers quote the exact paired sequence within the resource cap;
- active/control cannot be made perceptually matched and lawfully disclosed;
- fewer than five of 20 qualified interviewees deposit after seeing null and inconclusive reports;
- fewer than 30 deposits or 20 full payments arrive within 60 days and 12,000 PLN acquisition spend;
- first-batch net original-capital exposure exceeds 40,000 PLN;
- fewer than 15 of the first 20 completers produce usable results;
- fewer than 12 of 20 completers value the result at the paid price;
- fewer than seven completers place a paid second-test reservation;
- first-test contribution after acquisition is non-positive;
- a serious safety, misleading-claim, labeling or privacy event reveals an uncontrolled risk;
- delivery requires individualized medical/coach time or a wearable/platform partnership;
- mature tests remain mostly inconclusive, repeat purchase trends below 1.5/year, or no response-data effect improves protocol economics; or
