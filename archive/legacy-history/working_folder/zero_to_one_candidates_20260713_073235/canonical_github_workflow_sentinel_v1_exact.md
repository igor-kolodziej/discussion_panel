# Canonical Candidate — GitHub Workflow Sentinel v1

Status: frozen for advisory evaluation; not panel-ready.  
Success definition: 1,000,000 PLN of recognised annual revenue by Year 5 and at least 25% fully loaded operating margin. This replaces the original founder-wealth gate for this candidate.

## Thesis And Customer

Acquire a paid GitHub App that organisations already install for one narrow, recurring repository-control workflow, then deepen it into an evidence-producing policy system. The exact beachhead is a software organisation that must prove a release followed its own repository rules across branches, pull requests, required checks and exceptions. The product produces a signed release-policy record and exception history without claiming legal or regulatory certification.

The transaction must transfer the GitHub App registration, Marketplace listing and billing relationship, current installations, source/IP, domain, brand, documentation, support history, permissible customer records and backend. GitHub officially allows a GitHub App registration to transfer to another account. A code repository without the app registration, installs and billing is worthless.

## Control Point And Copy Risk

Current control is Tier 3 because no exact app or seller is identified. After a complete transfer, the app registration, installed permissions and customer billing relationship could be Tier 1; policy templates and accumulated exception histories may become Tier 2 switching costs. The strongest copy risks are GitHub adding the workflow to native rulesets and audit logs, or an open-source GitHub Action reproducing the evidence output.

No public set of three affordable qualifying sellers was found. GitHub's transfer mechanics prove technical assignability, not the continuity of every Marketplace subscription or an acquisition price. No seller was contacted.

## Atomic Target Specification

The target must have at least 20 independent paying organisations, at least 70% annual logo renewal, at least 100 active installations, 60,000–180,000 PLN trailing recognised annual revenue, clean security and permission history, no material customer concentration, and less than eight support hours per week. Cash at closing is capped at 20,000 PLN, diligence at 10,000 PLN and continuity reserve at 10,000 PLN. Additional consideration is capped at 60,000 PLN and paid only from 20% of post-transfer receipts. GitHub and seller evidence must confirm that installations, listing and paid subscriptions remain continuous.

## Product And Distribution

The inherited installations and Marketplace listing solve the first distribution problem. The first product release adds a policy-as-code definition, deterministic release snapshot, exception approval and exportable evidence record. New organisations discover and purchase in GitHub Marketplace; no enterprise partnership is existential. Over time, repository/provider adapters could reduce platform dependence, but the five-year model does not assume another platform.

The product avoids becoming generic security consulting. Support, policy onboarding and evidence generation must be self-serve or fixed-scope. Customer repository content is not pooled; only opt-in de-identified product telemetry may improve compatibility.

## Reachability And Kill Rules

By Day 180 after transfer, at least three independent organisations must pay a renewal and at least three must pay for the new policy-evidence tier. Contribution margin must be positive. The founder remains employed and works no more than five hours per day. Pre-proof exposure is capped at 40,000 PLN; further founder capital requires successful delivery and positive contribution; family capital is excluded.

Kill if three plausible qualifying sellers cannot be documented, if registration/listing/installations/billing do not remain continuous, if the purchase exceeds the cash cap, if required permissions make security assurance unaffordable, if native GitHub rulesets already supply the paid outcome, if fewer than six qualifying post-transfer payments occur in 180 days, or if the Year-5 target requires more than 200 customers at 5,000 PLN or less than 25% margin.

## Current Evidence Verdict Embedded In The Candidate

Technical app-registration transfer and a platform billing mechanism are real. Affordable seller supply, subscription continuity, a qualifying installed cohort, differentiated value and the 20-to-200 customer bridge are not proved. The candidate does not presently claim that the revenue-quality or Reachability Gate has passed.
