# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**MicroRouteFort Bronze Conversion** leases 20-bay sections of already paved, HGV-accessible and fully serviced yards near congested Polish freight corridors, adds only the EU Bronze security layer, and sells prepaid recurring bay blocks to fleets before upgrades.

The first site must already provide every expensive mandatory service through the host lease: separate toilets and showers, hot water and daily cleaning, 24/7 snacks/drinks, dining, free internet, personal power, reefer power, waste handling, emergency procedures, legal heavy-vehicle access, paving, drainage and lighting infrastructure. MicroRouteFort adds a quoted movable layer—visual perimeter, required lux, entry/exit CCTV, remote checks, signage, security plan, booking/access and certification. It opens and delivers inside 12 months using no more than the founder's original 100,000 PLN, and only after two unrelated fleets prepay at least 15,000 PLN for at least 12 bays across 90 nights.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Europe does not need every missing truck bay built from bare land: an unknown founder can convert underused capacity inside existing serviced yards into scarce certified corridor inventory by controlling the lease and fleet bookings before buying security equipment.

## Founder Fit

The founder is a Warsaw data scientist who can map corridors and yards, model site yield, automate lane-specific fleet outbound, price capacity, and build remote monitoring/booking workflows. His data/finance discipline suits option screening and staged capital. He does not need to become a truck-stop operator.

The POC people stack is the founder at 15–20 hours per week, a transport-property lawyer, an independent SSPA pre-auditor, a security/lighting integrator and the host's existing site-service staff. A remote monitoring contractor operates checks. Specialised work is contracted; the founder remains employed and never exceeds five hours per day.

## Customer And Pain

The buyer is a Polish/CEE fleet operator or freight forwarder running a recurring international lane, especially high-value or theft-sensitive cargo. The purchase event is a new lane, contract/insurance renewal, peak season or recurring inability to secure legal rest at the correct node.

Drivers currently search ad hoc, detour, lose driving time, park insecurely or overflow onto ramps. Fleets absorb theft exposure, missed rest schedules, driver dissatisfaction and uncertain arrival. The European Commission reported a current shortage of 390,057 safe and secure spaces, equivalent to about 3,250 parking areas, increasing to 483,000 spaces by 2040.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

One 20-bay Bronze-certified micro-site within roughly 5 km of an A1/A2/S8 freight node, carved from an existing truck-service, fuel, logistics or hospitality property. The host signs a 120-day option and then a 10–15-year assignable lease over the exact bay section, with explicit HGV parking, security-fit-out, signage, data, access and sub-booking rights plus the complete mandatory service schedule.

Before upgrades, an independent pre-auditor confirms the service bundle and a <=60,000 PLN Bronze gap. Two unrelated fleets reserve at least 12 bays/90 nights and prepay at least 15,000 PLN total. After that proof, MicroRouteFort installs the movable security/access layer, obtains certification, opens within 12 months and sells remaining capacity digitally at €16–20 per night.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

The Commission's December 2025 report does not only call for greenfield capacity; it recommends quickly upgrading insecure existing parking to the lowest security standard. Large developers favour 100–300-bay projects. Many service/logistics yards already carry the expensive amenities and HGV access but do not monetise spare overnight capacity as a separately certified product.

The valuable object is not cameras. It is a long, service-backed location right on the driver's actual corridor plus prepaid fleet inventory. The model rejects any site requiring toilets, showers, paving, drainage, grid works, food facilities or buildings; that is the structural difference from a capital-heavy truck stop.

## Underused Momentum

- The Commission reported the 390,057-space current gap in 2025 and called for rapid upgrades of existing insecure areas, inclusion in national transport plans and more EU/national funding.
- Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1012 makes the minimum services, Bronze security controls and independent certification explicit.
- CEF already funds safe-parking projects; a Netherlands precedent received 20% support, and 2024 selections included further SSPA capacity.
- Core TEN-T policy aims for appropriate safe parking roughly every 100 km.

These facts prove urgency and a recognised asset class. They do not prove that a particular Polish host, upgrade quote or fleet deposit exists.

## Beachhead Market

Start at one congested Polish international-freight node with repeat A1/A2/S8 lane traffic, no comparable certified capacity inside the practical driver radius, and at least 200 host properties screened. The host must already operate all mandatory services 24/7 and have a physically separable 20-bay section. The first fleet segment is high-value cargo carriers with weekly recurring lanes and a security/driver-retention reason to reserve.

The wedge is small enough to open with founder capital, but large enough to produce real operating data: 20 bays, two anchor fleets, certification, 90-night delivery and six months of utilisation/contribution.

## Primary Control Point

The Tier-1 control point is the combination of:

- a 10–15-year assignable lease/option over the named HGV bay section;
- contractual inclusion/SLA for every mandatory driver service and legal access;
- express security-fit-out, signage, data, booking and sub-use rights;
- independent Bronze certification after conversion;
- prepaid or take-or-pay fleet bay blocks; and
- control of the residual booking inventory and payment flow.

A host introduction, revocable service promise or generic camera purchase is Tier 3 and rejected. A nearby property may compete, but cannot copy the exact lease, certification history and fleet blocks through one equipment purchase.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping

1. Build a corridor heat map from TEN-T, parking data, driver reviews, theft/security reports, logistics clusters and route schedules.
2. Screen 200 host yards within the selected node. Require an existing-service photo/document checklist before a visit.
3. Contact 40 lane-matched fleets with a specific offer: 6–12 reserved Bronze bays at the named node, opening condition, price and 90-night block.
4. Spend no more than 8,000 PLN for a 120-day conditional option only after HGV access/use and every mandatory host service are documented.
5. Spend no more than 8,000 PLN on independent pre-audit and no more than 5,000 PLN on lease/service, insurance and integrator quotes.
6. Require a written <=60,000 PLN quote covering only visual perimeter/lighting/CCTV/access/signage/monitoring/security-plan/certification work.
7. By day 180, obtain two unrelated fleet deposits totalling >=15,000 PLN for >=12 bays/90 nights. No hardware is purchased before both deposits.
8. Use at most the remaining original founder capital to execute the quoted conversion, rent/security deposits, certification, insurance and working capital. Open and deliver within month 12.

Why an unknown founder can secure it: the host earns from stranded paved capacity without funding the conversion; the fleet reserves a precise corridor/date product with conditional refund if certification/opening fails; the founder controls downside through an option and independent quote rather than status. Proof before serious capital is the site option, service SLA, pre-audit, fixed quote and two fleet payments.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture

At 20 bays, 65% utilisation and €18/night, annual parking revenue is about €85,400/370,000 PLN. A conservative first-site operating hypothesis is:

- host lease and service allocation: 120,000 PLN;
- remote monitoring/security/host cleaning allocation: 55,000 PLN;
- utilities, insurance and maintenance: 45,000 PLN;
- booking, payment and other overhead: 25,000 PLN;
- site EBITDA: about 125,000 PLN/34% before central founder replacement cost.

Charge €16–20/night, with anchored 90-night blocks and residual daily booking. Ancillary food/showers remain mainly host revenue; MicroRouteFort may take only a contracted booking/service share. Reject the site if fully loaded EBITDA is below 25%, host rent/services exceed 45% of conservative revenue, conversion payback exceeds 30 months, or six-month occupancy is below 50% without contracted coverage.

### Distribution Moat

The first twelve bays are sold directly to two lane-specific fleets before fit-out. Those fleets generate the first drivers/transactions and can anchor adjacent corridor nodes. Residual demand comes from corridor signage, public truck-parking data, booking channels and direct fleet sales. The first 100 users are vehicles/drivers from contracted fleets, not an influencer audience or two-sided marketplace launch.

Each site adds route/yield data, a fleet reference, security performance and host credibility. A competitor can open nearby capacity, but must secure a host/lease, services, upgrades, certification and fleets. MicroRouteFort must renew leases/blocks and keep security performance high to retain pricing.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

1. Open one 20-bay site and deliver 90-night fleet blocks inside the 12-month POC.
2. Require three independent fleets, six months positive contribution, 65% utilisation/contracted equivalent and a second compliant host option before full-time transition.
3. Move to 60–120-bay serviced sections on adjacent nodes used by the same fleets.
4. Reuse the lease/SLA, pre-audit, movable security kit, monitoring, certification, fleet block and booking system.
5. Build a CEE corridor portfolio whose same fleets reserve multiple nodes.
6. Use site debt/leases, infrastructure equity and optional CEF only after operating proof; the base POC requires none.

The company expands outward from contracted routes. Broad parking developers must move inward by finding and converting the same overlooked micro-locations; booking apps have no inventory without leases.

## Local Adaptation Defense

Poland is a major European freight corridor with fragmented local yards and lower operating costs than Western freight nodes. Polish-language landlord sourcing, local planning/access knowledge and proximity enable the first conversions. The defence is not national regulation; it is a dense host pipeline, specific long leases, certified operating history and fleet route coverage.

## Why This Could Compound

Every site contributes certified bay inventory, fleet contracts, route demand, security/incident data, price/yield history, host references, and a reusable conversion template. Fleets on cross-border routes create demand for multiple nodes. Standard contracts and movable systems lower next-site diligence and fit-out cost. Central booking and remote monitoring improve utilisation without founder labour scaling with every bay.

## Reinvestment And Compounding Model

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax business cash available for distribution while the founder stays employed; optional founder distributions are <=5%. Strong reinvests 100%. The original 100,000 PLN funds the first site only after deposits. Later sites use retained cash, site leases/debt, infrastructure equity and grants after fleet anchors; all related liabilities and dilution are deducted. Family capital is excluded until operating proof.

The deposits satisfy the Reachability Gate; delivered reserved nights and site contribution release later capital. The founder stays employed and no external finance, family capital, bathroom build, paving or grid project is needed.

3. Up to 30,000 PLN total only for independent pre-audit, lease/service, insurance and fixed upgrade quote. Stop without two fleet deposits by day 180.
4. Up to 100,000 PLN original founder capital only after >=15,000 PLN deposits and a <=60,000 PLN upgrade quote; fund conversion, certification, rent/security deposits and working capital.
5. Possible family 100,000 PLN remains excluded until one operating certified site, three independent fleets, six months positive contribution, 65% utilisation/contracted equivalent, and a second compliant host option.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Remain employed until one operating certified site has three independent paying fleet customers, six months positive contribution, 65% utilisation or contracted equivalent, a second compliant host option, repeatable lane-specific fleet acquisition and at least 12 months of personal runway. One paid deposit or one site does not justify leaving employment.

## Failure Recovery And Reusable Assets

If no host passes, retain only corridor/yard/fleet maps, the service checklist, pre-audit template, quotes, lease/SLA terms and rejection record. If conversion fails after proof, refund conditional fleet deposits under contract and redeploy only movable hardware where lawful. No land, building or stranded bathroom/grid asset is purchased in the POC.

## Validation Plan

The first 180 days validate host availability, control rights, Bronze cost and fleet budget. Months 7–12 validate certification, opening, reserved-night delivery, occupancy, incident/security performance and positive contribution. A second host option and third independent fleet test repeatability. Only operating data can validate site EBITDA, later capital costs and founder ownership.

Evidence is separated: official EU sources establish shortage, standards and funding precedent; executed host/fleet contracts, audit reports, invoices, bookings and contribution statements establish this company.

## Kill Criteria

- No compliant host option after 200 screened properties.
- Any required toilet/shower/food/paving/drainage/grid/building work, or host refuses contractual service SLA.
- Option >8,000 PLN, pre-proof founder spend >30,000 PLN, or fixed Bronze upgrade quote >60,000 PLN.
- No two unrelated fleet deposits after 40 qualified fleet approaches, deposits total <15,000 PLN, or reserved coverage <12 bays/90 nights.
- Total founder cash to certification/opening >100,000 PLN before successful delivery.
- Certification/insurance is denied or requires non-movable capital beyond the quote.
- Six-month utilisation <50%, fully loaded site EBITDA <25%, host rent/services >45% of conservative revenue, or conversion payback >30 months.
- No third independent fleet or second compliant host option after operating proof.
- Real debt/dilution/EBITDA data removes a path to 2,800 bays, >=60% conservative founder ownership and €10m net founder value.
