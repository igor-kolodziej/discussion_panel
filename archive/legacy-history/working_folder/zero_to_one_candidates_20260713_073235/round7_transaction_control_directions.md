# Round 7 — Payer-Controlled Transaction and Release-Event Directions

Candidate generation only — not validated. This pass deliberately excludes generic software, compliance desks, advisory services, and products whose use must be approved again by an unrelated party at every customer site. Evidence and hypotheses are separated. No numerical score is assigned.

## Funnel Result

- **Retain for a complete finalist brief:** CoreBid PL.
- **Reject after research:** PalletClaim Clearing, AshKey Cremation Chain, TachoSeal PL, and EggMark Compatible Ink.
- The retained direction is materially different from the rejected spare-parts pools in the overlap ledger: inventory is bought only against two written, SKU-specific standing buyer bids; no obsolete or speculative stock is accumulated.

## 1. CoreBid PL — Demand-Backed Automotive Core Aggregator

### Insider customer insight

An independent workshop does not want a marketplace or another inventory app. After fitting an alternator, starter, turbocharger, steering rack, injector, compressor, or brake caliper, it wants the greasy removed unit off the floor and converted into certain cash. A remanufacturer does not want undifferentiated scrap; it wants the exact original-equipment core, intact enough for a named remanufacturing program. The commercially valuable gap is between those two preferences: instant local liquidity for a verified core, but only where downstream demand and rejection criteria are already known.

### Exact buyer and recurring event

- **Economic payer:** an automotive remanufacturer or established core broker with a written standing buy list, inspection rules, floor price, and payment term.
- **Supply-side customer:** independent workshops and small repair chains in Poland, initially within one dense Warsaw–Łódź–Poznań corridor.
- **Recurring event:** removal of a high-value eligible OE core during a paid repair, followed by title transfer to CoreBid and resale in a buyer-specific batch.

### Wedge

CoreBid offers a guaranteed photo-and-part-number quote, weekly pickup, and fast payment for only 30–50 pre-bid high-value core families. It buys title; it does not merely list parts. A quote is issued only when at least two downstream buyers have supplied written acceptance criteria and prices. The initial product is physical aggregation, inspection, and resale, with AI used only to reduce identification and pricing errors.

### Current resource and control point

The directly creatable control point is a four-part asset:

1. two signed, SKU-specific standing buyer bid books with acceptance rules and payment terms;
2. owned, paid-for batches of eligible cores;
3. 12-month first-call or exclusive pickup rights earned from workshops after a successful first batch; and
4. a proprietary observed-price and rejection graph linking casting/OE numbers, condition, buyer, realised price, and logistics cost.

No stock is purchased before item 1 exists. Within 12 months and 100,000 PLN, the founder can own inventory, the inspection dataset, and signed workshop supply rights; these are not prospective cooperation claims.

### Counted or evidenced denominator

- A Polish aftermarket study published in April 2025 estimates a **7.0 billion PLN potential market for common remanufactured product groups and 1.8 billion PLN of associated core value**; it breaks out 1.3 billion PLN for brake-caliper cores, 1.1 billion PLN for alternator/starter cores, 1.0 billion PLN for turbocharger cores, and 750 million PLN for power-steering racks. This is potential value, not proven annual accessible turnover, so it is an upper denominator rather than a sales forecast. [MotoFocus, *Rynek regeneracji części*, April 2025](https://old.motofocus.pl/media/file/2025/pdf/Rynek-regeneracji2025.pdf)
- BORG says it remanufactures **more than two million units annually**, covers more than 13,700 part numbers, and has warehouse capacity above one million cores. This proves industrial buyer throughput and the importance of core inventory, while also proving formidable incumbent scale. [BORG Automotive Reman](https://borgautomotive-reman.com/what-we-do)
- CoremanNet explicitly accepts cores according to defined material-number and technical criteria and operates credit, core-bank, and pickup functions. This confirms the customer behaviour and transaction structure, but also shows that the problem is not undiscovered. [CoremanNet FAQ](https://www.coremannet.com/frequently-asked-questions)
- APRA Europe describes cores as the essential raw material of remanufacturing and core dealers as an established supply-chain role. [APRA Europe](https://www.apraeurope.org/remanufacturing/)

### Cold start and paid proof

1. Obtain written price books and acceptance tests from two existing buyers that already publicly buy cores; no fabrication or garage recruitment starts before this.
2. Recruit 20 workshops from public directories using a concrete offer: a fixed quote on the named eligible families, payment at pickup after a photo pre-check, and no marketplace work.
3. Spend at most 25,000 PLN buying the first 40–70 units; reserve 8,000 PLN for crates, courier/pallet freight, inspection, and rejection loss; keep total pre-proof exposure below 38,000 PLN.
4. Sell two independent batches within 180 days. Gate-eligible evidence is cash received from downstream buyers, with item-level purchase price, rejection rate, freight, days held, and realised contribution.
5. Only after two profitable batches are paid does the founder offer 12-month first-call pickup agreements and expand the SKU set.

An unknown founder can run this alongside employment because pickup can be consolidated weekly, inspection can be contracted per batch, and the founder's high-leverage work is pricing, matching, loss control, and route density rather than daily workshop labour.

### Unit economics hypothesis

- Initial selected-core hypothesis: 350 PLN average purchase, 80 PLN handling/freight, 70 PLN rejection and price-risk reserve, 650 PLN resale, leaving **150 PLN contribution per accepted transaction**.
- Proof threshold: at least 100 PLN realised contribution per acquired core after all rejected items, freight, packaging, payment fees, and founder-paid inspection labour; cash conversion under 30 days.
- Conservative scale illustration, not evidence: 250,000 cores/year at 600 PLN recognised resale revenue equals 150 million PLN revenue. At 18% gross margin and 8% operating margin, operating profit is 12 million PLN. At a 5× operating-profit business value, 75% founder ownership produces 45 million PLN of founder equity, approximately €10.5 million before liabilities. The volume is about 12.5% of BORG's reported annual unit throughput and approximately 8% of the unit count implied by dividing the Polish 1.8 billion PLN potential core-value estimate by 600 PLN. This is demanding national-leader scale, but it is a bounded denominator rather than an invented site count.
- Expected illustration: 400,000 cores/year, 700 PLN average resale, 20% gross margin, 10% operating margin, 28 million PLN operating profit, 6× value, 70% founder ownership, and 117.6 million PLN founder equity (about €27 million).
- Strong illustration: 750,000 cores/year across Poland and adjacent CEE, 750 PLN resale, 22% gross margin, 12% operating margin, 67.5 million PLN operating profit, 6× value, and 65% ownership after growth capital, producing about 263 million PLN of founder equity (about €61 million).
- Conservative and expected cases assume 95% reinvestment of after-tax distributable cash while the founder is employed, optional distributions capped at 5%, and inventory finance only after profitable turns. The strong case assumes 100% reinvestment during the build-out.
- Capital beyond the initial 100,000 PLN must be short-duration inventory credit or buyer-backed receivables finance released only after repeatable turns. Equity is not required by the model, but the ownership assumptions allow for later dilution.

### Why an incumbent cannot copy it with one purchase

The physical activity is copyable. The possible advantage is a dense Polish pickup supply, item-level realised acceptance data across several buyers, and workshop first-call rights. No single equipment purchase or vendor contract recreates that history and route density. However, CoremanNet, BORG, and existing core brokers already have major data and logistics advantages; CoreBid must win an underserved geography or buyer category rather than claim a blank market.

### Proof test and fatal risks

- **First disconfirming test:** two unrelated downstream buyers must provide written SKU lists, floor prices, objective rejection rules, and payment within 30 days before more than 5,000 PLN is spent.
- Kill if no two buyers contract, if fewer than 8 of 20 workshops sell an eligible core within 90 days, if batch rejection exceeds 15%, if contribution falls below 100 PLN per acquired core, or if cash conversion exceeds 45 days.
- Fatal risks: buyer concentration; misidentified or damaged cores; theft/fraud; waste-versus-product classification and VAT treatment; working-capital intensity; established collectors responding with better prices; and the possibility that the 1.8 billion PLN figure is theoretical rather than accessible annual flow.

### Research decision

**Retain for a complete brief, conditional on buyer-side price-book evidence.** It is the only direction in this pass with a directly ownable transaction asset, a published Polish value denominator, one-sided proof that can be completed within the cash cap, and a wealth path that does not require millions of unrelated site approvals.

## 2. PalletClaim Clearing — Assigned EPAL Exchange Claims

### Insider customer insight and exact event

Small cross-border carriers do not mainly want pallet-account software; they want acknowledged pallet balances converted into cash or usable pallets without sending an empty truck across Europe. The proposed event was the assignment of a signed, undisputed EPAL pallet claim from a Polish carrier to PalletClaim, followed by physical or cash settlement with the named debtor.

### Proposed control point, cold start, and economics

- Buy only acknowledged claims carrying the original exchange note, named debtor, quantity, quality class, due date, and assignment permission.
- First proof would use less than 30,000 PLN to acquire 100–200 claims and realise settlement within 180 days.
- EPAL reports approximately **670 million EPAL Euro pallets and 20 million box pallets in circulation**, providing a real underlying asset population. [EPAL](https://www.epal-pallets.org/eu-en/the-success-system/about-epal)
- A contribution hypothesis of €3–5 per settled pallet would require roughly 1.5–2.5 million annual settlements to support a €10 million founder-equity outcome. That scale is not absurd relative to the pool, but circulation is not annual disputed-claim volume.

### Strongest copy and dependency risks

This market already has network incumbents. PAKi offers European exchange and account credit; PGS RLS reports more than 250 storage and repair centres across 14 countries; DPL explicitly manages pallet receivables and liabilities; inter.PAL reports more than 11,000 accounts and up to 100,000 pallets delivered per day. [PAKi](https://www.paki-logistics.com/services/exchanging-used-load-carriers/), [PGS RLS](https://www.pgsreverse.com/en/logistic-solutions/pgs-rls), [DPL](https://www.dpl-pooling.com/leistungen/serviceleistungen/ladungstraeger-konten-clearing/), [inter.PAL](https://interpal.de/en/unternehmen/)

Each purchased claim still depends on the named debtor accepting the balance, assignment, exchange quality, location, and settlement method. Assignment is therefore not equivalent to immediately controllable inventory. The founder lacks the depot network that makes low-value physical settlement economical.

### Research decision

**Reject.** The transaction is real and counted, but one-sided monetisation is not. The value sits in an existing European depot and netting network that cannot be recreated under 100,000 PLN, while every new debtor can reopen acceptance and quality disputes.

## 3. AshKey Cremation Chain — Per-Cremation Physical Identity Kit

### Insider customer insight and exact event

A crematorium customer is not buying a metal tag; the family is buying confidence that identity did not break between intake, chamber, processing, and urn sealing. The proposed consumable was a matched, sequential three-piece kit: a heat-stable disc travelling with the remains, a twin control token attached to the case record, and a tamper-evident urn seal plus family verification card. The crematorium controls each cremation event and can use the kit without another site's approval.

### Resource bootstrap, denominator, and proof

- The founder could own a registered design, laser-marked sequence, brand namespace, and initial 2,000-kit inventory for less than 35,000 PLN, then seek two prepaid 500-kit orders within 180 days.
- Poland's government already requires crematorium operators to maintain a record of bodies and remains accepted for cremation, proving an existing identity workflow but not a statutory need for this kit. [Gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/cyfryzacjamiejscpamieci/ewidencja-zwlok-i-szczatkow-przekazanych-do-spopielenia)
- The Cremation Society's 2023 statistics list 52 crematoria and an estimated 24% cremation rate in Poland; reported annual events include 775,200 in Germany, 535,750 in the British Islands, 252,075 in Italy, 115,757 in the Netherlands, 86,593 in Czechia, and 75,679 in Belgium. [International Cremation Statistics 2023](https://effs.eu/info-corner.html?file=files%2Feffs%2Fcontent%2Fdownloads%2Finfocorner%2Fmembers%2F02+Cremation+Statistics+%28source%3A+Pharos+Magazine%29%2F2024+Issue+of+International+Cremation+Statistics+2023.pdf)
- Sequential stainless-steel cremation tags are already ordinary catalogue products. [Starmark](https://starmarkcp.com/products/cremation-identification-tags)

### Unit economics and wealth problem

At a hypothesised 40 PLN kit price, 60% gross margin, and 25% operating margin, one million annual kits would generate 40 million PLN revenue and 10 million PLN operating profit. At a 7× value and 75% founder ownership, founder equity would be 52.5 million PLN (about €12.2 million). Reaching one million European cases would require a very large share of a concentrated crematorium market, while an incumbent tag supplier can add a twin tag and seal through one product-development cycle.

### Research decision

**Reject.** Reachable paid proof is plausible, but the €10 million wealth path requires heroic European penetration and the control point is a design/serial system that an established funeral-supply distributor can copy cheaply.

## 4. TachoSeal PL — Approved Smart-Tachograph Calibration Seals

### Customer insight, event, and denominator

Approved tachograph workshops must use traceable seals during calibration and inspection. Regulation (EU) 2016/799 requires approved workshops to use seals only from listed manufacturers, requires manufacturers and distributors to maintain full traceability, and requires unique seal identifiers. The calibration event is recurring and fully buyer-controlled. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2016/799](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2016/799/2016-05-26/eng)

The Commission states that the smart-tachograph retrofit for heavy vehicles concluded in August 2025 and that international light commercial vehicles above 2.5 tonnes entered scope from 1 July 2026, providing real recurring calibration momentum. [European Commission](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/stronger-and-more-unified-enforcement-eu-rules-in-international-road-transport-smart-tachograph-2025-08-19_en)

### Resource and cold-start problem

The apparent resource would be an approved seal design, tooling, traceability ledger, and serialized inventory. But the approval is not directly creatable just by commissioning a mould. UK enforcement guidance reported only six formally approved 1C seal providers and prohibited other providers; named suppliers include Continental, Royal Pack, Precintia, Phoenix, ACME, Skillray, and Unisto combinations. [UK approved-centre notice](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/approved-tachograph-centre-special-notice-02-22--2/approved-tachograph-centre-special-notice-02-22)

### Research decision

**Reject.** The product is a genuine mandatory consumable, but legal paid proof cannot precede approval, and approval/certification plus incumbent competition is an uncontrolled existential dependency. Reselling an existing approved seal would create only a copyable distributor contract, not a control point.

## 5. EggMark Compatible Ink — Food-Grade Egg-Coding Consumables

### Customer insight, event, and denominator

Egg packers must mark producer codes, but view ink as a necessary nuisance: they want compliant, fast-drying consumables that do not clog or lock them into OEM pricing. EU rules require producer-code marking before eggs leave a production site in relevant cross-border flows, with defined exemptions. [EUR-Lex, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2465](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R2465)

The Commission's July 2026 dashboard reports **6.664 million tonnes of EU egg production in 2024**, including 670,000 tonnes in Poland. [European Commission egg dashboard](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bdf9842-1eb6-41a2-8845-49738b812b2b_en?filename=eggs-dashboard_en.pdf&prefLang=et)

### Wedge, bootstrap, and copy risk

The proposed wedge was a certified food-grade red ink supplied in compatible cartridges for the most common Polish packer printer families, with batch certificates and a prepaid line trial. A contract formulator and cartridge inventory could fit below 100,000 PLN; two packers could prepay a trial within 180 days.

However, Domino already supplies compliant egg inks integrated with grader lines; CMS lists compatible 0.825-litre Domino egg-coding cartridges; an independent Dutch supplier advertises alternative inks up to 50% cheaper; and generic compliant egg ink sells openly for roughly €50–€125 per litre. [Domino](https://www.domino-printing.com/en/industries/food/egg-shell/eggs-shells-printing-coding), [CMS](https://www.cmsink.com/compatible-fluids/domino/), [FN Coding](https://www.fn-coding.nl/), [Sudhaus](https://sudhausinkjet.eu/gb/food/2475-1000-ml-marking-ink-for-egg-color-of-your-choice.html)

The egg denominator is enormous, but ink value per marked egg is extremely small. No evidenced cartridge consumption or gross-profit denominator supports a non-heroic founder-wealth bridge, and one incumbent formulation or distributor contract reproduces the offer.

### Research decision

**Reject.** Paid proof is reachable, but the direction is a price-led consumable substitute with existing compatible suppliers, low value capture per event, and no compounding control point.

- Prices, margins, contribution, conversion, acquisition, rejection, and founder-wealth cases are explicitly hypotheses for falsification; they are not market facts.
- CoreBid should not proceed to any downstream screen until two buyer price books make its inventory demand assigned rather than speculative.
