# Zero-to-One Evaluation: WooRenewal PriceOps EU v1

## Diagnosis

PriceOps moves WooRenewal toward a higher-value customer event and a more plausible 422-account Year-5 target, but research removes the claimed differentiation. The EU rule and bulk-change pain are real, while official-marketplace, premium, freemium, and free competitors already provide history, audit, scheduling, snapshots, rollback, and reference-price tracking. Acquiring an installed channel could reduce cold start, yet the only affordable target is anonymous and unverified, and a competent incumbent can add the remaining approval/export features through routine development. The pivot is a crowded feature bundle with acquisition risk, not a durable control point.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence:

- EUR-Lex guidance confirms the 30-day prior-price obligation.
- UOKiK publishes active Polish guidance and enforcement.
- WooCommerce Marketplace's Bulk Product Editor already provides detailed history, snapshots, one-click rollback, and who/when audit.
- Product Editor Pro provides scheduled price edits, activity logs, and undo history.
- Lavo provides free before/after snapshots and rollback.
- Omnibus and WC Price History have 10,000+ and 4,000+ active installations respectively; newer tools capture price changes from APIs/imports.
- The public USD 6,000 acquisition post claims an installed organic WooCommerce editor but does not identify or independently verify the seller/product.

Hypotheses:

- The anonymous target exists, remains available, is secure, has a credible paid cohort, and transfers atomically.
- Agencies will pay 4,900–9,900 PLN yearly for approval and evidence instead of combining cheaper existing tools and process.
- Store-local history and policies create switching costs greater than ordinary plugin migration.
- An unknown founder can reach and retain 422 accounts within five years.

## Overall Score

**62/100.** Weighted points: `123 ÷ 20 × 10 = 61.5`, rounded to 62. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 for the acquired update channel, but Tier 2 at best for the proposed PriceOps workflow and Tier 3 before a signed acquisition.** The strongest copy risk is an incumbent bulk editor adding maker-checker approval and evidence export to its existing history/rollback product through routine development. Because this requires neither exclusive data nor a scarce contract, the product advantage does not justify a score above 80.

## Founder Wealth Gate / User-Defined Revenue Quality Gate

The default wealth gate is superseded by the user's 1m PLN revenue/25% margin gate. **Fail.** The expected model reaches 1,004,800 PLN and 30% operating margin, but only by assuming 422 accounts at untested annual prices. Conservative revenue is 236,600 PLN at 15% operating margin. Indicative founder value is arithmetically consistent but not evidence-backed.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail as written.** The USD 6,000 public ask fits below the acquisition-cash cap, but the candidate requires an anonymous unverified target, atomic transfer, legal/security review, and ten paid PriceOps beta upgrades within 180 days. No primary target, installed cohort, seller trust, or customer demand exists. Too many existential facts must resolve before the pivot-specific paid proof.

## Expansion Check

Poland/EU is the strongest part of the pivot, and the merchant/agency niche is coherent. Expansion to other catalogue operations is technically natural but moves into the densest part of the WooCommerce admin-tool market. Broad competitors already operate inside the exact workflow and can move inward faster than PriceOps can establish customer history.

## Fatal Objections

1. The proposed history/audit/rollback/reference-price bundle already exists across several credible competitors, including free alternatives.
2. The affordable acquisition target is anonymous and unverified.
3. The 422-account pricing model has no willingness-to-pay or conversion evidence.
4. A routine incumbent feature release can copy the remaining maker-checker/evidence distinction.
5. Both the user-defined Revenue Quality Gate and Reachability Gate fail.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Use acquired installed distribution only when it delivers customers into a workflow whose valuable control point is not already available as an ordinary plugin feature.
