# Zero-to-One Evaluation: AgencyRail Transaction Plugin Buyout

## Diagnosis

AgencyRail correctly changes value capture from direct-site subscriptions to existing transaction flow, which could reduce the customer-count burden. It introduces a worse resource problem: an affordable plugin must already have installed merchants, 2,000+ monthly transactions, profitable take-rate economics, official update control, and assignable vendor/processor contracts. Such an asset is unlikely to sell for <=28,000 PLN cash plus a speculative note, and one non-assignable carrier/vendor agreement destroys the control point. The pivot is not ready for a panel and the plugin-acquisition family is exhausted under the current capital limit.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official WordPress transfer evidence supports the update channel only. No source proves an affordable transaction-routing target or assignable vendor/payment contracts. All GMV, fee, margin, seller-finance and target-supply claims are hypotheses. The v1 panel's first-asset control verdict does not validate this new target class.

## Overall Score

**61/100.** Weighted points `121.5 ÷ 20 × 10 = 60.75`, rounded to 61. Advisory only.

## Control Point

Tier 1 only after atomic assignment of update, merchants, transaction history, payout and vendor contracts; otherwise Tier 3. Strongest copy risk is vendor disintermediation or a better-capitalised platform buying the channel.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The pivot intentionally changes economics but supplies no conservative, expected and strong scenario with GMV, take rate, margin, capital, ownership, liabilities and timing. More importantly, no target proves that transaction flow can be acquired cheaply enough to retain founder ownership.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** The proof requires a rare asset with multiple assignable third-party contracts before the first payout. This is several uncontrolled dependencies and the likely asset value exceeds the cash cap.

## Expansion Check

Potentially attractive if an asset existed, but every later plugin and vendor contract resets sourcing and assignment. Poland offers no sourcing barrier.

## Fatal Objections

1. No affordable qualifying target evidence.
2. Vendor/payment contracts may not assign and are existential.
3. Wealth model absent and likely capital-intensive.
4. Platform/vendor disintermediation weakens profit retention.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

An installed payment/transaction rail is materially stronger than code or direct licences, but it must already be owned and contractually transferable.

## Recommendation

kill
