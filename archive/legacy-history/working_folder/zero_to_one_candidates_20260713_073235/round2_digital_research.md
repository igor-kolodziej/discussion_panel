# Round 2 Digital, Distribution, Payment-Flow, Public-Data, and Transaction-Control Directions

Research date: 2026-07-13. Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, selected, or validated.

The eight directions below are deliberately unlike generic SaaS, AI wrappers, consulting, dashboards, compliance-evidence services, and prospective data banks. Each starts with a presently transferable listing, installed base, payment stream, receivable, or exclusive transaction mandate. Marketplace listing claims are seller-reported unless the linked page explicitly says that Flippa or another marketplace verified the data; they are evidence that the transaction shape exists, not due diligence on a target.

For comparability, the NBP mid-rates on 2026-07-13 were USD/PLN 3.7846 and EUR/PLN 4.3281. Thus EUR 10 million was about PLN 43.28 million, and USD 10,000 was about PLN 37,846. Source: [NBP USD API](https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/usd/2026-07-13/?format=json) and [NBP EUR API](https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/eur/2026-07-13/?format=json).

All wealth shapes below are hypotheses, not forecasts. While the founder remains employed, the assumed default is to reinvest 95% of after-tax distributable business cash and take no more than 5% optionally.

## 1. ProUtility AppID Roll-Up — acquire narrow paid mobile utilities, not code projects

- **Secret / insight:** In a mobile-app acquisition, the scarce asset can be the AppID with its installed users, reviews, ratings, keyword position, and live subscriptions. Apple says a transferred app keeps its reviews, ratings, Bundle ID, availability, and updates; Google says users, download statistics, ratings, reviews, listing information, data, and subscriptions transfer. This is a specific transferable distribution and payment asset, so it is materially different from buying retiring software code.
- **Buyer / event:** A tradesperson, field worker, technician, or serious hobbyist renews a EUR 20-100 annual subscription to a low-support, non-regulated utility used in a repeated task. The acquisition filter would exclude social, medical, banking-data, regulated, and API-dependent apps; examples of acceptable shapes are an offline cut-list, measurement, inspection-photo, or specialist reference utility.
- **Exact first paid proof:** Buy one already-paid iOS/Android utility only after read-only App Store Connect / Play Console verification of at least 50 active subscribers, 12 months of cohort renewals, refunds, and organic downloads. Cap cash at close at PLN 30,000 and make any remaining price a seller note paid only from platform receipts. Complete the official app transfer and receive the first Apple/Google payout into the buyer entity within 180 days. A recent Stripe-verified Flippa precedent was an iOS eSIM app at USD 9,000 (about PLN 34,061) reporting USD 300 monthly profit; it proves that cash-flowing app assets can appear inside the proof budget, although that particular API-dependent app is not the desired target.
- **Control point:** Transferred AppID/Bundle ID, store listing, reviews and ratings, installed users and subscriptions, source code, signing materials, domain, support identity, and payout rights. Escrow releases the purchase price only after both platform transfers and repository/IP delivery complete.
- **Wealth shape:** A portfolio or consolidated professional-utility category reaching PLN 30 million net platform revenue, 35% operating margin, 6x operating-profit value, 75% founder ownership would imply about PLN 47.3 million founder equity. A current Acquire listing shows that a subscription-app portfolio can reach roughly USD 1.03 million revenue and USD 538,000 profit, supporting the existence of the roll-up shape rather than the assumed scale.
- **Fatal unknown:** Whether a founder can repeatedly find small apps whose organic discovery and renewal retention survive transfer, while avoiding hidden code, policy, privacy, refund, or paid-acquisition liabilities. A cheap app whose customers came from the seller's personal audience is not a distribution asset.
- **Direct sources:** [Apple app-transfer overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer), [Google Play app transfer](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247?hl=en-EN), [recent USD 9,000 revenue-producing app precedent](https://flippa.com/12193313-zapesim-esim-for-travel), [scaled subscription-app portfolio listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/jsdboyih9x-subscription-mobile-apps-with-1m-revenue-542k-profit).

## 2. WooRenewal — acquire a WordPress plugin listing plus its licence-renewal rail

- **Secret / insight:** A WordPress plugin's free repository listing is a persistent in-product distribution surface, while paid licences create an existing renewal flow. WordPress explicitly supports transfer of official plugin ownership; for fewer than 10,000 users the current owner can transfer it from the plugin's Advanced page, while larger transfers receive manual review. Freemius explicitly suggests sale or adoption instead of retirement and supports automatic licence renewals. The asset is therefore the listing + installed base + licence/payment rail, not merely GPL code.
- **Buyer / event:** A WooCommerce merchant renews a plugin because removing it would disrupt a live checkout, shipping, catalogue, or order workflow. Start with one operational, non-AI, non-compliance plugin with a narrow merchant job and no human service hidden inside the subscription.
- **Exact first paid proof:** Acquire a target shaped like the linked Flippa precedent: three WooCommerce plugins, 140+ sales, 190+ active sites, reported USD 194 monthly profit, and USD 5,000 asking price (about PLN 18,923). Before escrow release, require the WordPress.org ownership transfer, source/domain/IP assignment, licence-server export, and transfer of Stripe/Freemius ownership or payout instructions. Proof is the first new licence or automatic renewal paid to the founder's entity within 180 days.
- **Control point:** Official WordPress.org plugin ownership, update channel to installed sites, domain/brand, paid source, licence keys and customer contracts, support history, and recurring billing account. The acquisition closes only when these transfer together.
- **Wealth shape:** A suite reaching PLN 30 million ARR at 45% operating margin, valued at 5x operating profit with 75% founder ownership, would imply roughly PLN 50.6 million founder equity. A current verified WordPress-plugin listing reports 10,000+ installs and about USD 30,000 annual profit; another current portfolio listing reports about USD 166,000 annual profit and 5,000+ customers, showing that the ecosystem can support meaningful high-margin assets.
- **Fatal unknown:** GPL code can be forked, and a security or trust failure in the acquired update channel can destroy the installed base. The decisive unknown is whether renewal retention comes from the controlled update/distribution position rather than founder support, affiliates, or a temporarily ranking keyword.
- **Direct sources:** [WordPress official ownership-transfer process](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/transferring-your-plugin-to-a-new-owner/), [Freemius product-retirement alternatives](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/product-retirement/), [Stripe ownership change after a business sale](https://support.stripe.com/questions/transfer-a-stripe-account-to-a-different-entity-due-to-a-business-sale-or-acquisition?locale=en-GB), [USD 5,000 / 140-sale plugin precedent](https://flippa.com/11992701-monetized-wordpress-plugin-bundle-woocommerce-one-page-checkout-plugins-powerful-license-manager-140-sales-190-active-sites), [verified 10,000-install plugin listing](https://flippa.com/12897765-wordpress-plugin-with-10k-installs-recurring-revenue-30-ttm-profit-near-zero-costs-and-strong-upside-via-seo-upsells-and-feature-expansion), [scaled plugin portfolio listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/4w6k65z5vj-profitable-wordpress-pseo-social-auto-publish-plugins-172k-ttm-profit-5k-customers).

## 3. SteamBacklist — acquire one released simulation AppID and compound through DLC

- **Secret / insight:** Steam explicitly transfers management and payment rights, while the AppID keeps its store page, community hub, reviews, followers, wishlists, sales history, and customer ownership. The useful asset is an already-released niche game with organic platform demand and an expandable engine/content base, not an unreleased game or serial speculative title rights.
- **Buyer / event:** A player buys during a Steam visibility event, seasonal sale, major update, or DLC release. The beachhead is a narrow simulation/management game whose buyers demonstrably purchase expansions, not a hit-driven multiplayer game requiring constant community labour.
- **Exact first paid proof:** Offer no more than PLN 35,000 cash for a neglected, released title with at least 1,000 lifetime paid units, positive recent reviews, and non-zero trailing-90-day sales; make the remaining price a seller earn-out. Require seller-granted financial view access before signing and use Steam's official transfer tool with the founder entity as the new payee. The first paid proof is the first Steam remittance after the transfer effective date, before funding any DLC. A verified sold listing documents a one-year Steam simulator with 9,605 units, USD 25,531 trailing revenue, 70% margin, organic platform sales, and full IP rights.
- **Control point:** Steam AppID, store page, reviews, followers, wishlists, customer ownership, payment rights, full IP/source/assets, and the reusable engine. The acquisition contract must cover music, art, middleware, and contractor IP; a mere source-code licence is insufficient.
- **Wealth shape:** A focused publisher with a backlist and DLC/sequels reaching PLN 60 million annual net receipts at 25% operating margin, 5x operating-profit value, and 70% founder ownership would imply PLN 52.5 million founder equity. The expansion thesis depends on reusing controlled AppIDs, audiences, engines, and franchises rather than restarting distribution for every title.
- **Fatal unknown:** Whether a small acquired game's wishlist/review position can support profitable DLC and adjacent releases. If every new title resets discovery and revenue, the direction collapses back into the historically rejected serial cultural-rights shape.
- **Direct sources:** [Steamworks application-transfer documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/managing_apps/transfer?language=english), [Steamworks management/financial sharing](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/managing_apps/sharing?l=english&language=english), [verified sold cash-flowing Steam simulator](https://flippa.com/12207492-profitable-steam-simulator-with-10k-units-sold-70-margins-33k-net-revenue-zero-maintenance-and-full-ip-rights-strong-passive-income-asset), [cross-platform game with verified revenue](https://flippa.com/12230185-cross-platform-puzzle-rpg-with-8k-ttm-revenue-98-profit-margin-and-unity-source-code-runs-on-ios-android-steam-with-15-monthly-overhead).

## 4. OwnedCheckout Brand Transfer — buy a small EU DTC store whose checkout and customer list transfer

- **Secret / insight:** Marketplace sellers rent the customer relationship; a transferred Shopify store can carry an owned domain, checkout, first-party order history, consented email list, tracking data, and payout configuration. Shopify explicitly supports selling and transferring a store to an external new owner, including changing payout and domain details. The first acquisition is existing order flow, not a new dropshipping experiment.
- **Buyer / event:** A consumer buys a proven, simple, non-regulated product at a recurring or seasonal occasion. The acquisition filter excludes cosmetics, supplements, children's safety products, electrical goods, batteries, medical claims, and stores dependent on the seller as an influencer; fulfilment must already be outsourced.
- **Exact first paid proof:** A current Acquire listing offers a profitable Portuguese-made Shopify swimwear brand with 251-500 customers, USD 10,000 trailing revenue, USD 5,000 profit, and a USD 10,000 asking price—about PLN 37,846. A proof acquisition of that price class stays inside the budget. Escrow releases only after store/domain/trademark/customer-consent review and assignment of the existing manufacturer and fulfilment contracts. Proof is the first post-transfer Shopify order settled to the founder entity, not the asset purchase itself.
- **Control point:** Shopify ownership, domain, customer/order history and lawful marketing consents, checkout/payout account, pixels, creative library, trademark, inventory, and assigned supplier/3PL contracts. If the vendor and fulfilment agreements are not assignable, there is no controlled operating asset.
- **Wealth shape:** An EU portfolio or one expanded brand reaching PLN 120 million revenue at 15% operating margin, 5x operating-profit value, and 65% founder ownership would imply about PLN 58.5 million founder equity. A current all-digital Shopify business reports USD 3.0 million revenue and USD 1.37 million profit, showing that an owned-checkout model can reach high scale without inventory, although the proposed beachhead should be chosen on transfer quality rather than category fashion.
- **Fatal unknown:** Whether the acquired order flow is economically repeatable once the seller's creatives, season, and founder attention disappear. Paid-ad history without durable customer repeat, search demand, or assignable supply is not a distribution asset.
- **Direct sources:** [Shopify store sale and ownership-transfer procedure](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-orgs-and-stores/change-transfer-ownership), [current sub-PLN-40,000 profitable store listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/h6ds2dzxqx-shopify-dtc-swimwear-accessories-brand-50-margin-30-growth-portugal-made), [scaled fully digital Shopify precedent](https://app.acquire.com/startup/f25903rnju-delivers-premium-ready-to-use-rpg-content-instantly-for-immersive-gaming-experiences).

## 5. BrandASIN Continuity — acquire a trademark and live Amazon product position, not a seller account

- **Secret / insight:** Amazon seller accounts generally are not transferable when business ownership changes, but a buyer can acquire the product business—trademark, inventory, product IP, and Brand Registry authority—and operate through its own verified seller account. Amazon explicitly provides an "Update brand ownership" route and lets a trademark owner assign Brand Representative roles. The control thesis is the brand/ASIN position and inventory, not an illicit account sale.
- **Buyer / event:** A customer searches Amazon for a simple, non-regulated household organiser and buys the ranked, reviewed product. The first niche should avoid safety, health, electrical, food-contact, child, battery, and claims-heavy products.
- **Exact first paid proof:** One current verified/data-verified FBA listing reports about USD 2,034 monthly profit, 3,000+ reviews, USD 8,000 asking price (about PLN 30,277), and USD 3,000 inventory excluded. Keep cash risk below PLN 40,000 by paying the USD 8,000 IP/business price and taking inventory on consignment or a payout-funded seller note. The founder opens its own seller account, receives the trademark and Brand Registry administrator role, relists inventory against the existing ASIN where Amazon permits, and counts the first Amazon disbursement as proof.
- **Control point:** Registered trademark, Brand Registry administrator role, product design/copyright and packaging files, listing content, FBA inventory, supplier specifications, and the authorised seller offer on the ASIN. The seller account itself is expressly excluded.
- **Wealth shape:** A focused Amazon-native brand portfolio reaching PLN 100 million sales at 14% operating margin, 5x operating-profit value, and 70% founder ownership would imply about PLN 49 million founder equity. A current scaled Amazon-native brand listing reports USD 14.35 million annual sales and USD 2.17 million owner earnings with a four-person remote team, demonstrating the possible operating scale.
- **Fatal unknown:** Whether Amazon will preserve discoverability, Brand Registry control, product reviews, Buy Box eligibility, and inventory continuity through the compliant new-account/brand transfer. The platform can reverify, suppress, or strand the position; this must be tested before inventory capital is released.
- **Direct sources:** [Amazon seller registration and Brand Registry requirement](https://sell.amazon.com/sell/registration-guide), [Amazon Brand Registry roles](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/brand-registry-roles), [Amazon Brand Registry ownership-update route](https://sell.amazon.com/brand-registry/), [Amazon forum reproducing the official non-transferability rule](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/36e2742b-e684-4a03-9af9-998a9f3b8cea), [current USD 8,000 verified FBA listing](https://flippa.com/12204690-amazon-fba-brand-with-20-yoy-growth-3-000-reviews-at-4-74-8-stars-lean-operations-low-cogs-and-strong-profit-stability-with-clear-expansion-upside), [scaled Amazon brand listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/pmpo64x6xv-premium-portable-electric-food-warming-for-professionals-travelers-on-the-go).

## 6. RoyaltyRoute CEE — exclusive sale mandates over existing royalty payment streams

- **Secret / insight:** A creator's historical royalty stream is already an income-producing assignable asset. The commercial layer is an exclusive listing agreement, verified statements, buyer competition, escrow, and direct assignment of payment—not predicting a future hit. Royalty Exchange reports more than 2,300 completed deals, over USD 200 million raised for rightsholders, 30,000 registered investors, and assets cleared for assignment. Its closed page shows an international K-pop catalog with USD 1,375 last-12-month royalties sold for USD 6,608 (about PLN 25,006), proving that a first cash-flow asset can fit the budget.
- **Buyer / event:** A CEE indie label, producer, songwriter, game composer, or publisher wants a lump sum against three or more years of existing statements; global income-asset buyers want verified, directly assigned cash flow. Only multi-title catalogs or diversified royalty pools qualify.
- **Exact first paid proof:** Sign one 120-day exclusive listing mandate with a Polish/CEE rights holder whose catalog has at least PLN 20,000 annual royalties and three years of payor statements. No catalog is purchased first. Assemble an English data room, obtain written confirmation that the payor/CMO/publisher accepts the proposed assignment or payment direction, solicit binding bids, close through escrow, and collect a contractually agreed 5-10% success fee. The exclusive mandate and escrowed buyer deposit are the control proof; the fee receipt is the paid proof.
- **Control point:** Exclusive listing mandate over a named catalog/royalty share, verified historical statements, chain of title, payor-cleared assignment or irrevocable payment direction, binding bid, and escrow. This is materially different from the rejected serial one-title cultural-rights shape because it trades diversified historical payment flows and controls the sale transaction; it does not finance new releases.
- **Wealth shape:** A pan-European exchange at PLN 1 billion annual closed volume, 4% take rate, 40% operating margin, 6x operating-profit value, and 65% founder ownership would imply about PLN 62.4 million founder equity. Those figures require institutional buyer depth and repeat seller origination; they are hypotheses.
- **Fatal unknown:** Whether enough CEE royalty streams are both large and clean enough to assign, and whether their payors accept payment-direction changes. If each catalog requires bespoke title litigation, buyer discovery, and rights reconstruction, the marketplace will not compound.
- **Direct sources:** [Royalty Exchange deal and investor statistics](https://royaltyexchange.com/), [closed sub-PLN-40,000 royalty transactions](https://auctions.royaltyexchange.com/bin_overview/), [exclusive listing, binding auction, and escrow process](https://royaltyexchange.com/how-auctions-work-sellers), [standard life-of-rights purchase agreement](https://hs.royaltyexchange.com/hubfs/Terms%20of%20Use%20Documents/Purchase%20Agreement%20-%20Life%20of%20Rights%20%5BU122025%5D.pdf).

## 7. DelayCash — purchase an EU261 receivable immediately after a public flight disruption

- **Secret / insight:** EU flight-compensation claims have standardized face values of EUR 250, 400, or 600, while passengers often prefer immediate certainty to a disputed collection process. A 2025 CJEU judgment describes AirHelp acquiring a delayed-flight claim from a passenger on a Kraków-Nice itinerary, giving direct evidence that a trader can hold an assigned claim in this Polish fact pattern. Public flight events reveal where claims may exist even though passenger identities remain private.
- **Buyer / event:** The first customer is a Polish passenger whose eligible flight has just arrived sufficiently late or been cancelled. The economic payer is the operating airline. The offer is an immediate fixed purchase price for the claim, not a recovery consultation or evidence desk.
- **Exact first paid proof:** After one documented high-confidence disruption, run route/flight-number search ads and landing pages, verify tickets and arrival data, and buy up to ten claims through lawyer-reviewed written assignments for 35-50% of face value, keeping total claim purchases, ads, and legal templates below PLN 25,000. Submit as claim owner and obtain the first airline/settlement payment to the founder company within 180 days. Do not scale until one assignment is accepted and one cash recovery lands.
- **Control point:** Executed assignment of the exact receivable, booking and disruption evidence, passenger identity verification, notice to the airline, and payout account. Unlike a generic recovery desk, the company owns the claim and its payment flow after purchase.
- **Wealth shape:** At pan-EU scale, 150,000 claims annually with average PLN 1,600 recovery would mean PLN 240 million collections. If acquisition, legal, CAC, and losses leave PLN 75 million gross contribution and PLN 25 million operating profit, 5x operating profit and 60% founder ownership would imply PLN 75 million founder equity. This is an operating-scale hypothesis, not an assumption that every delayed flight is payable.
- **Fatal unknown:** Claim-acquisition CAC plus airline rejection/litigation time may consume the entire spread. The June-July 2026 EU agreement preserves compensation levels but adds mandatory electronic passenger information, which may reduce passenger ignorance and agency margins; extraordinary-circumstance disputes remain case-specific.
- **Direct sources:** [EU passenger rights and current compensation levels](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), [Regulation 261/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32004R0261), [CJEU case describing AirHelp's acquired Kraków-Nice claim](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A62024CJ0551), [European Parliament's 2026 agreement update](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20260703IPR46273/).

## 8. KRZ ClaimBook — exclusive transaction mandates for admitted Polish insolvency claims

- **Secret / insight:** Polish insolvency claims are fragmented, slow, and hard for a small creditor to price, but KRZ makes proceedings and claim-list events public. The Polish Civil Code generally permits assignment without debtor consent unless law, contract, or the obligation's nature prevents it. A May 2026 public sale notice offered a PLN 1.822 million nominal insolvency claim with a PLN 9,112.30 estimated value, showing extreme pricing gaps and a real, budget-sized transaction event. Existing generic debt exchanges list unpaid invoices; the narrower asset here is an admitted or documented insolvency/restructuring claim with a standardized proceeding data room.
- **Buyer / event:** An SME creditor wants immediate liquidity and administrative exit after its claim is admitted or its restructuring recovery becomes long-dated; a distressed investor wants a documented discounted claim. The triggering event is a KRZ notice, approved claim list, arrangement proposal, or distribution plan—not ordinary invoice delinquency.
- **Exact first paid proof:** Monitor KRZ and public sale notices, then obtain one 90-day exclusive sale mandate over a named, assignable business claim with the claim document, contract, anti-assignment check, KRZ case number, status, security, and recovery history. Send a standardized data room to a fixed list of verified claims investors, obtain a binding bid and escrowed deposit, close the assignment, and charge a 3-8% transaction fee. Spend at most PLN 15,000 on legal templates, data extraction, and outreach; do not buy the claim. The first success fee is the paid proof.
- **Control point:** Signed exclusive mandate over the named claim, complete assignability-checked data room, binding purchase agreement, and escrowed settlement. Public data sources the event; the mandate controls the actual transaction. This is not a dashboard, debt-collection service, or prospective corpus.
- **Wealth shape:** A Polish-to-CEE claims exchange at PLN 1 billion annual closed volume, 3% take rate, 40% operating margin, 6x operating-profit value, and 65% founder ownership would imply about PLN 46.8 million founder equity. Reaching it would require standardized document access and repeat institutional demand, both still hypotheses.
- **Fatal unknown:** KRZ's public layer may not provide enough claim-level documentation or lawful creditor contact data to originate clean mandates efficiently, and professional buyers may only want large portfolios. If every claim needs bespoke legal reconstruction, the transaction cost overwhelms the spread.
- **Direct sources:** [official KRZ purpose and public system](https://www.gov.pl/web/power/krajowy-rejestr-zadluzonych-krz), [KRZ public portal](https://prs.ms.gov.pl/krz), [current Polish Civil Code text; Articles 509-510 govern assignment](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/1964/93), [May 2026 sale of a PLN 1.822 million nominal claim at PLN 9,112.30 estimated value](https://monitorurzedowy.pl/announcement/1702315/konkurs-ofert-na-zbycie-wierzytelnosci), [existing Polish debt-exchange transaction evidence](https://vindicat.pl/uslugi-gielda-dlugow/).

## Structural Boundaries Applied

- No direction counts source code, a dashboard, an unbuilt dataset, an unsigned partnership, or manual customer cooperation as the control point.
- The software directions require formal transfer of an existing listing/installed base and payment rail; buying code alone fails the thesis.
- The cultural-rights and claim directions require a named, signed, assignable payment stream or exclusive mandate before work begins; generic brokerage or recovery work fails the thesis.
- The e-commerce directions require platform-compliant ownership/brand transfer and assigned operating contracts; buying or sharing a prohibited marketplace account fails the thesis.
- A target is rejected before escrow release if read-only primary payment data, IP chain, platform standing, assignability, or a first-payout path cannot be demonstrated.
