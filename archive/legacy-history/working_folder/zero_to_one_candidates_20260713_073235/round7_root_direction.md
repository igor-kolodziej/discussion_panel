# Round Seven Root Direction — BlindProof Personal Supplement Tests

Candidate generation only. This file does not score, validate, or claim that the concept passes either hard gate.

## Customer Truth

Committed supplement and wearable users do not primarily want another recommendation list or correlation dashboard. They want to know whether a product they already spend money and attention on causes a useful change **in them**, rather than reflecting expectation, regression to the mean, season, alcohol, training load, illness, or ordinary sleep variation.

The missing commercial layer is execution: software can suggest an N-of-1 protocol, but the user still knows which treatment is active, sources an unmatched placebo, labels sequences, maintains adherence, imports wearable data and interprets an underpowered result. A direct user request for a weekly blind placebo/supplement service tied to Garmin data appears almost verbatim in the Blueprint community. That is anecdotal demand evidence, not market validation, but it describes the job precisely.

## Direction: BlindProof

BlindProof sells a prepaid, fixed-duration, placebo-matched personal supplement test to an adult consumer who already owns a supported wearable and is considering continuing a common legal food supplement. The first SKU is one reversible, short-response sleep intervention with a published personalized-trial precedent; it is not a general supplement store or an AI health adviser.

The buyer receives coded active/placebo periods, a tamper-evident daily sequence, adherence prompts, a predeclared outcome and analysis plan, wearable import, confounder exclusions and a plain-language result: individually detectable benefit, no individually detectable benefit, or inconclusive. The business does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, promise benefit, or tell a customer to stop medical care. It sells a transparent self-observation product for healthy adults and excludes medicines, urgent conditions, pregnancy, contraindications and unsupported wearables.

## Why Now

- Oura reports more than 5.5 million rings sold and millions of users; in May 2026 it said it was on pace to exceed five million paid members. This provides a directly counted high-intent wearable population before Garmin, Apple and other devices.
- AESGP's 2025 European study surveyed 6,000 consumers and manufacturer data representing 65% of the EEA market; it reports that 62% of supplement users consume them daily and expects continued sector growth.
- A 2025 published series ran 60 digital personalized crossover trials comparing two melatonin doses with placebo. It used color-matched cellulose placebo, wearable data and an individual report, proving the physical/digital protocol is technically executable. It does not prove commercial willingness to pay.
- Research and several current apps show that phones and wearables reduce N-of-1 delivery burden. Current visible products largely stop at tracking/protocol software; BlindProof's proposed gap is the consumer-paid, manufactured blind sequence and decision-grade closeout.
- The Polish GIS states that supplements are food rather than medicines, require food-business registration and notification before first market placement, and need compliant composition, labeling, warnings and advertising. This creates a concrete compliance path, but a Polish food/regulatory opinion must confirm whether the coded placebo/active presentation and self-test language remain food/wellness rather than research or a medical device.
- At least one current Polish contract manufacturer advertises startup-oriented production from 250 units, while another advertises small pilot batches and regulatory documentation. This makes a paid-cohort production quote plausible, but only signed quotes for the exact paired active/control sequence count as evidence.

## Exact Wedge

Start in Poland with one 10–12 week “is this improving my sleep?” SKU for adults who already wear Oura and are already considering the permitted ingredient. Freeze ingredient, dose, capsule appearance, sequence, supported device, exclusions and primary endpoint. Do not begin with an open-ended platform, blood tests, medications, multi-ingredient stacks or medical claims.

The first paid offer is a 599–799 PLN founding test. The consumer prepays; a registered small-batch food-supplement manufacturer produces active and matched control capsules under a founder-owned specification, assigned batch records and randomized serial plan. BlindProof owns the brand, test design, code/analysis, customer relationship, result format, batch/sequence keys and consented longitudinal response data. The manufacturer is a replaceable paid vendor, not a distribution or validation partner.

## Initial Acquisition Mechanism

1. Publish a free “Is your sleep supplement working, or are you reading noise?” analyser that accepts a voluntarily uploaded 30-day Oura CSV and shows within-person variance, required trial length and confounders without claiming treatment effect.
2. Buy exact-intent search ads in Polish and English around Oura sleep experiments, supplement effectiveness and placebo self-tests; post the transparent protocol to open Quantified Self, Oura and Blueprint communities without requiring an influencer relationship.
3. Offer 100 numbered founding slots and require at least 30 non-refundable deposits of 199 PLN or 20 full prepayments before ordering active/placebo product. The customer-facing offer states manufacturing date, refund trigger and exclusions.
4. Track paid acquisition cost by source. Kill if 30 payments require more than 12,000 PLN of ads/community work, if fewer than 60% of qualified buyers accept blinding/inconclusive results, or if a legal opinion requires a clinical-trial, pharmacy or medical-device route for the first SKU.

This is a specific cold start: an owned variance calculator converts existing wearable owners at the exact moment they question a recurring supplement purchase. It does not depend on a celebrity, closed community, clinician referral or wearable partnership.

## Resource Bootstrap Within Twelve Months

Required foundational resource:

- one legally cleared, placebo-matched active/control SKU and founder-owned product specification;
- purchase order and manufacturing/quality agreement assigning batch documentation, randomization/packaging specification and the right to multi-source production;
- serialized sequence inventory and key management;
- supported wearable import, preregistered analysis, adherence logic and result engine;
- explicit consent/data rights for individual service and de-identified protocol improvement, with deletion/export controls;
- at least two completed paid cohorts producing protocol adherence, detectable-effect, inconclusive, refund, adverse-event and repeat-purchase data; and
- a prepaid next-test waitlist that proves the relationship is not one-and-done.

Why an unknown solo founder can secure it: these components can be purchased from food-regulatory counsel, a registered contract manufacturer, packaging/fulfilment vendors and ordinary software infrastructure. No vendor must endorse efficacy, recruit customers, grant market access or accept speculative outcome risk. The founder's neuroscience/data background directly covers experimental design and analysis; licensed legal, nutrition/safety and manufacturing work is contracted.

Pre-proof spend ceiling hypothesis: 8,000 PLN regulatory/food-law and label review; 5,000 PLN prototype/placebo-match and manufacturer samples; 6,000 PLN privacy/security and insurance review; 6,000 PLN landing page, analyser and creative; 12,000 PLN acquisition tests; 3,000 PLN contingency = 40,000 PLN. No production batch is ordered before deposits/full payments make its contribution plausibly positive.

Proof before serious capital: 30 independent payments, compliant manufacturing quote, blinded-identical sample acceptance, >=75% sequence completion among the first 20 starters, >=60% of completers calling the result worth the price even when null/inconclusive, CAC payback on the first test, and >=35% paid reservation for a second test within 60 days.

## Control Point And Copy Risk

Current control is Tier 3: a protocol, landing page and manufacturer quote are replaceable. Within 12 months it can become Tier 2 if BlindProof owns paid cohorts, validated kit specifications, sequence/analysis infrastructure, longitudinal individual-response records and repeat demand across several tests.

The compounding asset is not the capsule. It is a catalogue of manufactured blind sequences plus the consented mapping from protocol, adherence, phenotype/wearable baseline and individual detectability to the next best experiment. Every completed test should lower inconclusive rates, improve eligibility and trial length, and increase repeat conversion.

Strongest copy risk: Oura, a supplement brand or a large personalized-health company can add experiment software and contract identical capsules. Supplement brands have a conflict because an honest test will often tell buyers not to repurchase; however, a neutral company has no technical exclusivity. BlindProof must establish cross-brand trust, a multi-test response graph, low-inconclusive protocol design and direct acquisition economics before a platform internalizes the feature. If one vendor contract plus a wearable API reproduces retention and CAC, cap the direction below panel readiness.

## Unit Economics Hypothesis

First test at 699 PLN including VAT (about €162 gross consumer price). Initial variable costs: 75 PLN active/control capsules and batch allocation; 35 PLN sequence packaging; 25 PLN fulfilment/shipping; 20 PLN payment/support/refund reserve; 20 PLN wearable/API/compute and result delivery. Gross contribution before paid acquisition is about 524 PLN, or 75% of consumer price. Target blended CAC is <=175 PLN and first-test contribution after CAC >=45%.

At scale, an annual member purchases 2.2 tests at €149 ex-VAT equivalent average revenue plus €39 membership/analysis value, about €367 annual revenue. Hypothesized gross margin 68–72%; operating margin must reach at least 24% after customer acquisition, support, safety, product and cohort operations. These figures require paid proof.

## Founder-Wealth Hypothesis To Be Evaluated

Use Oura's directly counted five-million-plus paid-member population only as an initial reachable audience reference, not as the total supplement market.

- Conservative: 35,000 annual active customers × €367 = €12.85m revenue; 68% gross margin; 22% operating margin = €2.83m operating profit. At 6× operating profit, €17.0m EV; less €1.5m obligations = €15.5m equity; 70% founder ownership = €10.85m. This barely clears the gate and therefore requires proof that 35,000 retained customers (under 0.7% of Oura's stated paid members, before other wearables) and 2.2 tests/year are attainable without heroic CAC. No distributions are added; 95% of distributable after-tax cash is reinvested while employed.
- Expected: 80,000 active customers × €390 = €31.2m revenue; 70% gross margin; 27% operating margin = €8.42m operating profit. At 6×, €50.5m EV; less €3m obligations = €47.5m; 65% founder ownership = €30.9m. Use 95% reinvestment while employed and model any dilution/capital explicitly.
- Strong: 180,000 active customers × €420 = €75.6m revenue; 72% gross margin; 30% operating margin = €22.7m operating profit. At 7×, €158.8m EV; less €8m obligations = €150.8m; 55% founder ownership = €82.9m. Reinvest 100%; this is upside, not the gate case.

The direction fails Founder Wealth if repeat purchase is below 1.5 tests/year, mature CAC exceeds 35% of first-test revenue, individualized support prevents >20% operating margin, or the reachable wearable/supplement overlap is too small to obtain 35,000 active customers without celebrity distribution.

## Reachability Hypothesis

Potential pass, conditional on legal/manufacturing facts. One founder can build the variance analyser and sequence system part-time. Counsel and a registered manufacturer can be paid vendors. Deposits can be collected before a batch; the first cohort does not require a doctor, lab, wearable platform agreement, retailer or employer.

The exact 180-day gates are:

1. Days 1–21: written Polish food/consumer/medical-device boundary opinion; two small-batch quotes; 20 structured buyer interviews; analyser live. Spend <=12,000 PLN.
2. Days 22–60: 30 deposits or 20 full payments from unrelated consumers at CAC <=400 PLN/payment; no product manufacture before this. Cumulative founder risk <=24,000 PLN.
3. Days 61–100: manufacture, QC and ship the first 30–50 sequences only after positive contribution is plausible. Cumulative original-capital commitment <=40,000 PLN net of customer cash.
4. Days 101–180: at least 20 starters complete; >=15 usable results; >=12 buyers state the result was worth the price; >=7 pay to reserve a second test; no serious safety/privacy event. Only then release the next original-capital tranche.

Reachability fails if regulators classify the consumer sequence as research/medical care, matched control cannot be labeled and sold lawfully, MOQ forces >40,000 PLN before payment, an Oura partnership is needed for data access, or buyers will not prepay for a result that may say “inconclusive.”

## Fatal Risks

1. The legally careful description may still constitute a clinical investigation, medical-device function or misleading health claim.
2. Fast, reversible, safe outcomes are rare; many supplements have long carryover or effects smaller than wearable noise, producing too many inconclusive reports.
3. Users may say they want truth but pay supplement brands for hope and novelty; null-result value and repeat purchase are unproved.
4. Consumer trust, screening, adverse-event handling, privacy and support may require clinical authority or founder labour that the model excludes.
5. Wearable APIs can change; CSV/manual imports must preserve an independent path.
6. Oura or another health platform can copy the digital layer, and contract manufacturing is not exclusive.
7. The conservative wealth case has little cushion and stacks repeat rate, CAC, operating margin and retention hypotheses.

## First Disconfirming Test

Before manufacturing or collecting health intervention data, present a legally reviewed mock report containing an honest null and an inconclusive result to 20 qualified wearable-plus-supplement users. Require at least 12 to say either outcome would be worth >=599 PLN and at least five to place a deposit. Then require 30 unrelated deposits or 20 full prepayments within 60 days at <=12,000 PLN acquisition spend. Failure kills the shape; it is not cured by adding coaching, generic recommendations or supplement-store margin.
