## Concise Diagnosis

This is a disciplined deep-tech commercialisation thesis rather than a founder-built treatment-plant gamble. Its best feature is the sequencing: obtain a bounded option, require a live-stream integrator prepayment, and place technical and installation work with the inventor, laboratory, and integrator. However, the core control point is still a negotiation hypothesis, the product advantage has no real-effluent proof, and distribution begins with an unknown founder persuading an integrator that must itself bring a budgeted client case. The candidate is credible enough for bounded research and a paid-proof attempt, but not credible enough as written to clear the founder-wealth gate or justify a pursue verdict.

## Strongest Objections And Caveats

1. **The control point does not yet exist — currently fatal to `pursue`, and permanently fatal if the complete option cannot be signed.** The universities, inventors, know-how, pending European family, improvement rights, sublicensing rights, and retained data permissions are uncontrolled dependencies. A Polish patent or a research arrangement alone does not support the proposed company.

2. **Real-effluent superiority is wholly unproved — fatal if the system loses on activity destruction, transformation products, safety, energy, consumables, or lifecycle cost.** The commercial case depends on beating mature alternatives in difficult matrices, not on laboratory removal percentages or regulatory momentum.

3. **Integrator willingness to pay is a hypothesis — fatal if no qualified prepayment appears, but not fatal before the bounded test.** The integrator must trust an unknown founder enough to expose a live client stream and prepay despite thin early-study economics and an incomplete industrial performance record.

4. **The founder-wealth bridge is too fragile — fatal to the Founder Wealth Gate now.** The conservative case reaches about €11.3 million only with 110 sites, 180 active modules, 70 annual package sales, €2 million of royalties, 32% operating margin, 55% founder ownership, and a 7× multiple. The candidate supplies no bottom-up eligible-site denominator, demonstrated service attachment or retention, validated royalty denominator, acquisition cost, sales-cycle evidence, or dilution bridge sufficient to call that conservative.

5. **European defensibility is unresolved — fatal to the wealth thesis if valid exclusive European control is unavailable or narrow.** Polish rights alone cannot protect the European volume assumed, while the later family is described as pending and claim scope/FTO are unverified.

6. **Execution depends on a minimum coalition the founder does not control — conditionally fatal.** A named technical lead, co-owner cooperation, accredited laboratory, integrator process team, and live pharmaceutical client must align. The staged plan limits capital loss, but it does not remove dependency risk or the founder's lack of water-industry credibility and privileged access.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The candidate does the accounting correctly in important respects: it separates three scenarios, deducts net obligations, models dilution, follows the 95%/100% reinvestment rule, and does not add distributions on top of retained value. The failure is credibility rather than arithmetic. The conservative case has only a 13% cushion over €10 million and fails under ordinary deviations in operating margin, founder ownership, valuation multiple, European rights, site count, recurring attachment, or capital need. Until a bottom-up addressable-stream bridge and transaction-backed unit economics support the 110-site/180-module case, the €10 million path is possible but not yet credible enough for a hard-gate pass.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, narrowly and conditionally.**

The 180-day proof is a genuine external payment: one unrelated integrator prepays at least 20,000 PLN for a qualification tied to a live client's stream. Pre-proof founder exposure is capped at 38,000 PLN, hardware is excluded, licence exercise waits until after payment, and the stated 15–20 founder hours per week fit the five-hour-per-day ceiling while employment continues. The dependencies are significant, but they are staged and testable rather than requiring a full product or simultaneous technical scale-up before payment. This pass covers reachability of paid evidence only; it does not imply technical validation, repeatable demand, or investability.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 at present; capable of becoming Tier 1 only if the specified comprehensive option/licence is actually signed.**

Today the patents are university-controlled, the later European family is pending, and the know-how, inventor hours, improvement ownership, channel registration, sublicensing, and retained data rights are proposed contract terms. Under the governing classification, a speculative partnership-dependent resource is Tier 3. A signed exclusive field package covering both families, enforceable know-how access, improvements, and channel/data rights would create a genuine Tier-1 control point, but that future state cannot be scored as already owned.

## Strongest Copy Risk

An established integrator or AOP vendor can deploy a **non-infringing ozone, electrochemical, UV/oxidant, or alternative plasma system that delivers better lifecycle economics and accepted effluent outcomes**. Customers buy treatment performance and risk reduction, not the label “cold plasma.” If the patent claims are narrow, the know-how is non-exclusive, or field data cannot be retained, an incumbent can combine routine equipment, its existing client trust, and a competing process without needing this portfolio.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The commercially valuable layer is the combination of protected implementation, repeatable real-stream qualification, comparable field evidence, and integrator distribution—not ownership of treatment plants and not a standalone patent.

## Final Verdict

**RESEARCH FURTHER.**

The bounded option-and-prepayment experiment is rational and the Reachability Gate passes, but the current Tier-3 control position and failed Founder Wealth Gate prohibit `pursue`. The exact candidate should be rejected if the comprehensive rights package, named technical commitment, integrator prepayment, real-effluent superiority, European protection, or bottom-up wealth bridge fails its own stated tests.
