# Zero-to-One Evaluation: PelletLock v1

## Diagnosis

PelletLock is a real physical-control thesis, not a pellet-compliance desk: the founder can own the enclosure/latch/cassette/tooling/test corpus, demonstrate weighed effect, and sell two paid bay pilots without regulator or OEM permission. The enacted 2025/2365 regulation creates a timed operating context but does not mandate this product, while a cheap correctly used catch pan and better purging are strong substitutes. The current wealth case is the main veto: €10.40m founder value has almost no cushion and depends on 6,500 cumulative systems/5,000 recurring plans from a 27,000-point denominator built entirely from assumptions. The relevant physical-point count and current equivalent-product search need research before a panel.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official evidence supports the regulation, five-tonne scope, prevention/containment/cleanup/record duties, known application timing and the fact that installations will declare/register. OCS and vendor material supports catch pans, purging, self-closing valves, downstream containment and coupling fragmentation. It does not support the 27,000-point denominator, top-two 60% interface coverage, 15,000 PLN pilot willingness, €4,500 price, 90% incremental capture, <=10-second added time, 40% recurring attachment or current lack of an equivalent. No panel or chatbot verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**69.5/100.** Weighted points are 139 across 20 weight units; `10 × 139 / 20 = 69.5`. Advisory only; not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 path; Tier 3 today.** Founder-owned CAD, latch/cassette mechanism, removable tools, compatibility schema, 500-cycle corpus, technical file and reserved capacity are real resources if executed. Strongest copy risk: a coupling/containment supplier adds a comparable shroud/interlock/cassette with its installed distributor and safety authority, or a self-closing valve/better blow-off removes the event.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail pending physical-point evidence.** The conservative result is only €10.40m after obligations. It requires 6,500 cumulative systems, 1,200 hardware sales/year, 5,000 care plans, 24% operating margin, 75% ownership and a 6.5× multiple. The provisional 27,000-point denominator is calculated from three unsupported percentages/averages; the candidate would need 24% cumulative penetration. Modest misses in points, care attachment, dilution, margin or obligations break the gate.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** An external post-purge cell, surrogate rig and two reversible beta units can be built with paid vendors; pre-payment risk is <=32,000 PLN and day-180 exposure <=40,000 PLN. Two unrelated 6,000 PLN deposits plus 9,000 PLN acceptance balances are real evidence, work remains part-time, and no regulator/coupling OEM/distributor endorsement is assumed.

## Expansion Check

The first point is good. Expansion becomes a profitable catalogue only if one or two interfaces cover most events and the same safe external architecture repeats. Coupling fragmentation, ATEX/site classification and cleaning can turn every install into engineering. The regulation is EU-wide but local evidence is not a barrier to incumbent entry.

## Fatal Objections

1. The conservative wealth path has almost no resilience and no sourced physical-point denominator.
2. A correctly used pan/purge/self-closing valve may achieve most value at far lower lifecycle cost.
3. A broad coupling/containment vendor can reproduce the shell through ordinary product extension.
4. Safe post-purge state, static/dust/zone and operator bypass may create site-specific installation burden.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Use an enacted physical-loss duty to sell a weighed, hard-to-bypass control at one recurring industrial handoff rather than advice or reporting.

## Recommendation

**research further**

Research the Commission's operator/installation evidence, current equivalent products and any usable physical-point proxy. Re-evaluate the wealth/control shape before paneling.
