# RatioSnap Advisory Re-evaluation After Research

## Diagnosis

The additional evidence does not prove RatioSnap demand, but it resolves the order-of-magnitude veto: one meter-mix vendor alone claims more than 10,000 installed machines, several global peers serve the same categories, and current manufacturer procedures still require manual last-point checks. A 40,000 total-cell hypothesis is now plausible, while the candidate explicitly narrows to the subset without equivalent inline physical evidence. The 6,000-dock conservative case remains ambitious but can represent profitable category leadership rather than facially heroic global share.

## Gates

- **Founder Wealth:** pass narrowly. The conservative case reaches €15.2m after obligations; it has more cushion than prior finalists, and the denominator is now directionally supported. Kill before scale capital if account mapping cannot substantiate at least 25,000 relevant cells.
- **Reachability:** pass. Owner-created reference asset, <=28,000 PLN preproof, two unrelated payments and no strategic partner dependency.
- **Control:** Tier 2. Real cassette/reader/tooling/keys/compatibility assets, but vulnerable to OEM bundling.

## Residual Fatal Risk

The business collapses if plants value the procedure only as an occasional cheap cup-and-scale check. The paid pilot must prove check frequency, time/material saving and signed-record value together; reference-method agreement alone is insufficient.

## Recommendation

**pursue**

RatioSnap may enter an independent simulated panel using exact final wording, provided the denominator evidence and explicit OEM/ordinary-dual-scale kill criteria remain in that wording. This advisory recommendation is not validation.
