# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ChairArmor Flight Kit

## Diagnosis

ChairArmor is built around a real and unusually severe customer outcome: damage to a complex power chair can remove weeks of autonomy, so the relevant value is continuity of life rather than the replacement price of a plastic guard. The exact-fit, protrusion-specific wedge is more useful than a generic soft bag and more portable than a whole-chair carrier. However, the candidate has not established the two facts on which the business shape depends: how many **unique, eligible, premium power-chair owners** fly in a purchase year, and whether they will pay €1,100 directly for a non-OEM, non-airline-approved accessory from an unknown founder. The current three-configuration fitment/test corpus is not scarce enough to be a durable control point, while OEMs hold exact configuration data and existing mobility-product firms hold stronger lived-experience trust. Both hard gates fail as written: the wealth path depends on an unproved 8,400–12,000-unit annual floor and a favorable 6× multiple, while the 180-day plan spends the full 40,000 PLN allowance before proving access to three configurations or a repeatable way to reach qualified owners.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Evidence map

This supports real risk, but an enplanement is a checked event, not a unique device, power chair, complex chair, or buyer: [November 2025 Air Travel Consumer Report](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2025-12/November%202025%20ATCR.pdf). |

There is no sourced conversion from events to unique complex power-chair owners with a flight in the next year. |

A £60 electric-wheelchair bag is a much weaker substitute but shows the low end of the category: [AEGIS ARMOR](https://www.aegisadaptive.com/store/p1/aegisarmor.html), [Lith-Tech travel bag](https://lith-tech.com/shop/wheelchair-travel-bag/). Neither proves or disproves €1,100 for a complex power-chair kit. |

But the candidate has no evidence that handlers will leave the modules attached or not mistake them for lift points. Permobil itself advises moving the joystick inward and using bubble wrap, while a RESNA configuration card tells users to remove the joystick and store it in the cabin: [Permobil travel checklist](https://hub.permobil.com/blog/wheelchair-traveling-checklist), [RESNA Air Travel Configuration Card](https://downloads.regulations.gov/DOT-OST-2022-0014-0020/attachment_1.pdf). Bench non-interference is necessary but does not prove operational acceptance or protection during actual baggage handling. |
| GPSR can be handled with 4,000 PLN for legal/product-liability review and insurance quotes. | Unsupported cost hypothesis; regulatory obligations are sourced | The EU GPSR requires a safe product, internal risk analysis, technical documentation, traceability, safety information, and assessment of foreseeable interaction with other products. A QR record does not replace required physical information. The candidate budgets for review and quotes, not the policy premium, translations, technical-file work, recall readiness, or additional testing: [Regulation (EU) 2023/988, Articles 5, 6 and 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32023R0988). EU product-liability rules also preserve producer exposure for defective products; the revised directive applies to products placed on the market after 8 December 2026: [Directive (EU) 2024/2853](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/2853/2024-11-18/eng). A formal classification opinion is still needed on whether the intended use creates any medical-device-accessory implications. |
| A tested fitment and failure corpus will compound into defensibility. | Founder hypothesis | The corpus can reduce fit errors and speed later configurations, but three configurations are reproducible through public order forms, owner measurement, contract CAD, and routine fabrication. AEGIS says its second phase will cover power chairs, and Wheel Handi markets a patent-pending electric-wheelchair carrier. Both already frame the same air-travel damage problem: [AEGIS Adaptive](https://www.aegisadaptive.com/), [Wheel Handi AeroPod](https://wheelhandi.com/). |
| Owners can be acquired directly at CAC below €120. | Unsupported hypothesis | No search-volume, click-cost, landing-page, community-permission, email-list, conversion, or payback evidence is supplied. The first plan requires 150 qualified owner conversations/visits, but there is no controlled audience. AEGIS is explicitly “created by a wheelchair user for wheelchair users,” and Wheel Handi tells a family-origin story; an unknown founder without lived chair experience starts with a trust disadvantage rather than an acquisition advantage. |
| The founder can execute part-time. | Mixed | Data science and structured experimentation fit the configuration corpus and acquisition measurement. Mechanical impact design, assistive-technology fitment, product safety, accessible user research, and liability are outside the stated founder background. The 12,000 PLN contract-engineering allowance and 5,000 PLN component/access allowance are unverified against the work and the prices of specialized controls. The minimum credible people stack is not contracted. |

### Consequential missing facts

1. A counted annual denominator split by unique owner, complex power-chair type, exact configuration, trip frequency, geography, and self-pay ability.
2. Real price evidence at €1,100: model-specific €200 deposits from unrelated owners, refund/commitment behavior, and reasons for rejecting $495-or-less alternatives or improvised wrapping/removal.
3. Signed or prepaid access to at least two physical examples of each of the three exact configurations, including the adjustment and specialty-control variants the guard must cover.
4. An engineering test protocol tied to foreseeable airport loads and misuse, actual-flight evidence, failure thresholds, and a documented non-interference review against each OEM manual.
5. A written regulatory classification, GPSR technical-file plan, product-liability policy quote, coverage exclusions, and the fully loaded insured cost per unit.
6. A repeatable first-100-customer channel with measured qualified traffic, conversion, CAC, refund rate, and accessible support burden.
7. Price treatment for VAT, international freight, duties, returns, fit errors, field replacements, and actual warranty frequency. If €1,100 is EU VAT-inclusive, the stated €13.2m revenue and 62% gross margin overstate net revenue unless the table is explicitly ex-VAT.

No prior panel score, chatbot verdict, or market validation is used here. The generator's statement that both gates are “supportable as hypotheses” is a prior internal claim, not evidence and not a gate result.

## Overall Score

## Control Point

**Current classification: Tier 3; aspirational Tier 2 only after broad, paid, field-proven coverage.**

The three-configuration CAD/test package, owned photos, BOMs, and supplier terms are provable within 12 months, but they are neither exclusive nor scarce. Public option matrices, owner measurements, contract engineering, CNC/thermoforming, and sewing are all repeatably purchasable by competitors. Reserved small-batch capacity is operationally helpful, not a distribution or resource lock. The serial pre-flight record is a feature; users and OEM apps can reproduce photos and configuration instructions without routing a transaction through ChairArmor.

**Strongest copy risk:** an OEM such as Permobil, Quantum, or Sunrise adds an exact travel-protection accessory for its own installed base, using CAD, option codes, dealer fitment, warranty trust, and checkout/bundling access that ChairArmor lacks. The startup's cross-brand breadth does not neutralise this because each owner buys for one chair. The next strongest risk is AEGIS executing its announced power-chair phase as modular armor, or Wheel Handi shrinking its carrier into guards; both already have category narrative, patent claims, and lived-experience trust. At three configurations, catch-up requires routine product development, not a hard-to-obtain resource.

## Founder Wealth Gate

The failure is not arithmetic; it is that every case begins after the missing market proof and treats global annual unit volume, direct price, margins, and valuation multiples as established.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL as written, although the loss is capped and the deposit mechanism is directionally appropriate.**

The plan seeks 20 refundable €200 deposits at a €1,100 target price and stays nominally within 180 days and 40,000 PLN. A deposit is eligible evidence under the user’s rule, and the founder can keep employment if specialist and fabrication work is genuinely delegated. However, the plan spends exactly the entire allowed tranche—with no overrun buffer—before it has proven the following sequential dependencies:

1. access to two physical examples of each of three exact option-and-adjustment configurations;
2. affordable donor components despite controls and mounts costing hundreds or thousands of euros;
3. successful CAD and a functional guard under a 12,000 PLN engineering budget;
4. a relevant impact/compression/snag/non-interference protocol and pass result;
5. a GPSR classification/technical-file path and insurable product at a cost consistent with the margin;
6. direct access to 150 qualified owners who both match the exact configuration and intend to fly; and
7. 20 €200 commitments from a founder with no lived-experience status, owned audience, dealer channel, or measured search funnel.

The deposit is also fully refundable until technical release, so it is stronger than an email but weaker than a non-refundable preorder or successful delivery. The candidate must build most of the minimum product for at least one configuration and complete meaningful safety work before deposits convert. Real airport handling remains untested, so initial delivery could still fail contribution economics through refunds, field replacements, or liability exclusions. The 150-conversation plan plus physical measurements, contractor management, compliance, supplier coordination, and customer support is possible within 5 hours/day only with a reliable contractor stack that has not been secured.

Using conservative assumptions for missing access and channel facts, paid proof is not yet **credible**, even though it is conceivable. Further founder capital and all family capital remain correctly excluded until successful initial delivery, positive contribution margin, multiple independent customers, and repeatable acquisition evidence.

## Expansion Check

- **Beachhead:** exact-configuration complex power-chair owners with a flight inside 12 months have urgent pain and can be targeted by intent, but the beachhead is fragmented into option-level subsegments. Dominating three configurations may yield very few annual purchases.
- **Adjacency:** rail/coach, vehicle loading, temporary control-pod cases, and replacement modules reuse some fit data. No evidence shows these uses have €1,100-equivalent willingness to pay or sufficient repeat frequency.
- **Competitor entry:** OEMs can move inward using exact CAD and their dealer relationship; AEGIS has announced power-chair protection; Wheel Handi already targets electric chairs. All can copy the modular guard concept with routine engineering.
- **Local advantage:** Polish fabrication may improve prototype and small-batch economics. It does not create unique access to U.S./EU owners, airport handling data, OEM fitment, insurance, or distribution. Selling globally also adds language, tax, freight, returns, and product-liability complexity.
- **Asymmetry:** a true multi-brand failure/fit corpus could eventually let ChairArmor expand faster across models than one OEM, but the candidate has not shown how it obtains enough field failures and configurations before incumbents react. Three tested configurations are not yet asymmetric.

## Fatal Objections

1. **Founder Wealth Gate veto:** there is no counted unique-buyer denominator supporting the roughly 8,400 annual kits needed to cross €10m founder equity at the candidate's own conservative margin, ownership, and multiple, let alone the stated 12,000-unit case.
2. **Willingness-to-pay veto:** €1,100 and €120 CAC have no deposits, search data, sales comps, or buyer-budget evidence. Severe replacement cost does not prove self-pay accessory demand, particularly where the chair itself may have been reimbursed.
3. **Control-point veto:** three configurations of CAD, tests, and supplier terms are current Tier 3. OEMs possess exact geometry, option codes, installed-base access, dealer trust, and warranty context; AEGIS and Wheel Handi already occupy the category narrative.
4. **Configuration-access veto:** there is no signed, reserved, prepaid, or otherwise controlled access to the first three exact chairs. OEM option matrices imply substantial within-model variation, and specialized donor parts can consume the access budget.
5. **Reachability veto:** the candidate consumes the complete 40,000 PLN allowance before proving configuration access or customer reach and depends on at least six sequential technical, regulatory, access, and distribution assumptions before a deposit becomes a delivered positive-margin order.
6. **Safety/liability veto:** “no guarantee” language does not remove GPSR, product-liability, foreseeable-misuse, or non-interference obligations. Bench testing without a named standard and real-flight evidence cannot establish that handlers will leave, understand, or safely use the modules.
7. **Founder/channel trust veto:** the founder's analytical strengths do not supply mechanical engineering, assistive-technology fitment, lived wheelchair experience, or an owner audience. Existing entrants explicitly use lived-experience stories, while the proposed direct funnel has no measured source of qualified owners.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest insight is that a complex power chair is autonomy infrastructure and that users may prefer **portable protection for a few fragile protrusions while the chair remains visible and rollable**, rather than a generic bag or full enclosure. This insight is worth preserving if the missing denominator, price, access, and control facts can be proven; it does not by itself establish the current business shape.

## Recommendation

**research further**

Do not send this exact candidate to an independent panel. Re-evaluate only after decision-grade evidence resolves the unique eligible-owner denominator, unrelated €1,100 price commitments, exact-configuration access, fully loaded insured unit economics, and a measured direct-acquisition path. If those facts do not support at least roughly 8,400 annual units at the stated conservative economics or credible paid proof below 40,000 PLN, the current business shape fails rather than merely needing better presentation.
