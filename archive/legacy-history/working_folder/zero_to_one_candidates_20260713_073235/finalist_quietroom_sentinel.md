# Zero-to-One Candidate: QuietRoom Sentinel

Candidate generation only — not evaluated, scored, panelled, chatbot-reviewed, confirmed, approved, or validated. Research date: 2026-07-13. All efficacy, hotel-incidence, willingness-to-pay, pricing, margin, conversion, renewal, eligible-position, and valuation assumptions not explicitly supported by a linked source are hypotheses to test.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Sell hotels a prepaid, mail-replenished bed-bug monitoring position for each guest bed place, using a founder-owned reusable holder and sealed passive cartridge that advances only if an independent blinded insectary test detects low-density introductions materially better than current passive monitors without creating pest-control visits, software dependence, or guest-visible work.

## Founder Fit

The founder can own experimental design, preregistration, blinded-data analysis, product requirements, hotel qualification, direct outbound, pricing, supplier bidding, and the evidence package while keeping his data-science job. A 180-day proof fits 15–20 founder hours per week and no more than 40,000 PLN because insectary work, CAD, fabrication, material advice, and product-safety/FTO review are capped paid services.

The founder is not an entomologist, pest controller, chemist, biocide specialist, or hotel authority. He must not identify insects, advise treatment, certify a room as pest-free, or claim prevention/eradication. An independent entomologist designs and runs the blinded gate. Hotels route suspected positives through their existing escalation procedure. No cofounder, pest-control referral, insurer, hotel association, booking platform, or distributor is required for first proof.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first buyer is the operations, housekeeping, risk, or general manager of an independent hotel or small hotel group with:

- at least 100 Eurostat-style bed places under centralized housekeeping;
- fixed bed frames/headboards compatible with a low-profile holder;
- a documented bed-bug incident, room closure, adjacent-room response, contractor callout, guest refund/compensation, or monitoring expense in the last 24 months;
- no prepaid continuous-monitor contract already covering the cohort; and
- authority to install a non-pesticidal removable device and change cartridges during housekeeping.

The buyer's desired outcome is silent early evidence before a guest complaint or spread, with no public “bed-bug program,” electronics, dashboard, recurring technician visit, or ambiguous bite diagnosis. Housekeeping needs a 15-second inspection, a cartridge it can close without touching an insect, and an unambiguous route to the hotel's existing escalation procedure.

ANSES reports that 11% of surveyed French households experienced infestation between 2017 and 2022, that nearly 40% of reported introductions were associated with a hotel or other vacation stay, and that household control cost averaged roughly €900. Those are household survey findings, not a hotel incidence or hotel cost denominator. ANSES's hotel analysis says practices are heterogeneous, reputational sensitivity suppresses disclosure, and quantitative hotel-infestation data are unavailable. Each prospect must therefore disclose its own redacted 24-month incident/cost history before ROI is asserted.

Current workarounds include visual housekeeping inspection, responding to guest reports, periodic pest-contractor inspections, canine detection, cheap unbaited interceptors, lure-based passive monitors, and connected monitoring services. EPA describes inexpensive do-it-yourself interceptors. SenSci, Nattaro, Spotta, and Rentokil already market early-detection products or services. QuietRoom must beat an incumbent physical product, not a fictional absence of monitoring.

## Product Wedge

The beta is a non-electronic, non-pesticidal, low-profile **dual-path passive cartridge** in a reusable tamper-evident holder fixed behind the headboard or at another independently validated travel/harbourage location.

The cartridge tests two physical hypotheses in one sealed insert:

1. a dark micro-crevice harbourage path with replaceable textured surfaces; and
2. a high-grip entry/escape-resistant pitfall path positioned along likely bed access.

One monitoring position maps to one Eurostat bed place. A double bed, which Eurostat counts as two bed places, receives two positions, one on each sleeping side or other independently validated equivalent placement. This preserves a clean commercial denominator and avoids calling bed places “rooms” or physical bed frames.

Housekeeping inspects a shielded viewing window weekly, records the check on the hotel's own paper or existing system, and replaces the sealed cartridge at a target 90-day interval. A suspected positive is closed in place and passed to the hotel's existing pest professional. The product provides no insect identification, monitoring certificate, room-clearance statement, treatment, referral, mobile app, or cloud record.

The first proof uses geometry and materials only. Do not add pheromones, semiochemicals, pesticides, desiccants, or kill claims. Nattaro already markets a patented lure, and ECHA notes that some products intended to attract pests can enter biocidal-product rules. If the passive physical design cannot win, kill it rather than cross into a new regulatory/technical dependency during the first cycle.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The category is real but not empty. The proposed gap is narrower: hotels may prefer an evidence-backed monitor that their existing housekeeping team can inspect and replenish by mail, without paying for electronics or repeated pest-controller visits. The trust mechanism is a preregistered blinded comparison against products the hotel can buy, followed by a paid operational cohort—not founder authority or a regulation claim.

This is commercially useful only if QuietRoom produces both:

- a meaningful low-density detection advantage over the best purchased passive comparator; and
- lower all-in inspection/replenishment burden than current lure cartridges, electronics, or technician-led monitoring.

Convenient packaging alone is insufficient. Nattaro Scout already combines a reusable trap, replaceable pheromone lure, hotel use, and international pest-control resellers. SenSci Volcano/Activ targets hospitality with a discreet passive monitor and lure. Spotta offers connected monitoring as a service. Rentokil can bundle monitors with an existing service relationship. The candidate is product-first precisely because only physical superiority can open direct procurement against those channels.

## Underused Momentum

The useful momentum is not a legal mandate:

- Eurostat records 29.74m EU tourist bed places and more than 3bn tourist nights in 2024, so the recurring sleeping event and public account population are large;
- ANSES explicitly emphasizes early detection and non-chemical approaches while documenting fragmented hotel practice and poor incidence transparency;
- established monitors prove budget category and buyer awareness, but also create purchasable comparators for an unknown founder's blind test;
- Polish small-tooling, converted-material, and plastics suppliers can produce a non-electronic proof cheaply; and
- mail replenishment lets the product scale without field technicians.

The same momentum strengthens incumbents. Tourism volume is not proof that hotels will monitor continuously, and no EU rule requires QuietRoom or any bed-bug monitor.

## Beachhead Market

Start with independent and small-group hotels in Poland, Czechia, and eastern Germany that operate 100–600 bed places, use fixed beds/headboards, have centralized housekeeping, and can show a recent direct cost. The first cohort excludes campsites, private short-stay apartments, hostels without fixed placement, active infestations requiring treatment, hotels already under a bundled monitoring contract, and any property that expects the founder to inspect rooms or identify insects.

Eurostat's July 2026 dataset reports for 2024:

- **29,742,108 bed places** across hotels, holiday/short-stay accommodation, and campsites;
- **12,899,728 bed places in hotels and similar accommodation**;
- **162,375 hotel and similar-accommodation establishments**; and
- **6,173,288 hotel bedrooms**.

A bed place is capacity for one person; double beds count as two. These counts are not room counts. The commercial unit is deliberately one monitoring position per bed place. Eurostat also warns that national thresholds omit some small establishments; those omissions are not needed for the model.

It includes the reusable holder, four sealed quarterly cartridges, printed placement/inspection materials, batch traceability, freight in one annual pack, and replacement of defective holders. The hotel owns all incident and inspection records. There is no software subscription.

### First hundred

Add Polish, Czech, German, French, and English materials. Use remote qualification, mailed samples, and video training. Hire the first inside salesperson only after ten renewals and positive account contribution. Target 100 hotel customers and roughly 30,000 active positions through direct selling; distributors and pest companies are not part of the model.

Acquisition must remain below €15 per activated position after the first ten accounts, including samples, travel, salesperson cost, and failed pilots. Annual prepayment and €25–€30 first-year gross contribution then give sub-12-month contribution payback.

## Primary Control Point

**Control tier: Tier 3 today; a plausible Tier 2 only after the blind gate, owned production assets, and prepaid installed cohort. It is not a Tier 1 resource.**

Within 12 months and 100,000 PLN original founder capital, the founder must own and possess:

- editable CAD, tolerance stack, material and surface specifications, cartridge/holder drawings, and packaging files;
- the founder-owned test fixtures and coded blind-test protocol, raw results, and independent report;
- written FTO/design-search and product-safety route for the exact non-biocidal claims and geometry;
- a founder-owned small production tool or forming/cutting dies with relocation rights and editable supplier files;
- two qualified manufacturers using standard second-sourceable materials;
- batch QA, escape/closure, ageing, adhesive, housekeeping, and shipping test methods;
- brand, domain, manuals, contracts, product file, and liability-insurance quote; and
- prepaid contracts covering at least 3,000 bed-place positions across ten unrelated hotels.

The intended control point is the combination of a demonstrably better physical cartridge and a prepaid installed-holder/replenishment workflow. A holder alone is ordinary plastic. A prospective data corpus is not counted. Hotel data stays with the hotel.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. **Buy the category:** purchase SenSci Volcano with its available lure, Nattaro Scout, a cheap unbaited interceptor, and any locally available hotel passive monitor.
2. **Observe housekeeping:** time 30 mock inspections with hotel staff before freezing geometry. Record visibility, removal, closure, glove need, and placement failure.
3. **Screen several physical variants:** use an entomologist's insectary and a contract product engineer; do not test in occupied hotel rooms or seed hotels.
4. **Run the independent blind gate:** preregister endpoints, comparator products, placement, host cues, life-stage balance, exclusions, analysis, and go/kill rule before coded trials.
5. **Sell only after physical proof:** show the independent result and a physical sample to qualified hotels; collect two 8,000 PLN prepaid cohorts.
6. **Use hotels only for operational proof:** no deliberate insects; measure staff completion, time, damage, guest visibility, suspected-positive containment, and annual prepayment.
7. **Release post-proof capital on contracts:** owned tooling and a 3,000-position batch follow annual prepayments, not optimistic interest.

### Exact blinded physical-performance gate

The final design is compared with SenSci Volcano plus its standard Activ lure, Nattaro Scout used per instructions, and a cheap unbaited interceptor. An independent entomologist specifies a powered sample size; **120 coded low-density arenas is a planning hypothesis**, not permission to run an underpowered test. Arenas balance adult/nymph stage and use identical standardized host-cue, placement, temperature, humidity, and inspection conditions. Staff reading captures are blind to product identity.

Advance only if all are met:

- by day 14, QuietRoom detects at least 70% of one-bug/low-density arenas;
- its day-14 absolute detection rate is at least 15 percentage points above the best purchased passive comparator and the preregistered confidence interval excludes no advantage;
- by day 28, it is not more than five percentage points worse than any comparator;
- no captured insect escapes during at least 100 observed capture/closure challenges;
- median coded inspection plus safe closure is 15 seconds or less; and
- the independent result survives one replication batch made by the intended supplier.

Failing any primary threshold ends this business shape before hotel pilots. Do not reinterpret a usability win as efficacy.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial trust asset is independent physical evidence, not brand status. The acquisition unit is a hotel-controlled bed-place cohort; the buyer can authorize installation, inspection, and replacement without another party. Annual prepayment funds stock, and one annual parcel avoids route density or technicians.

Once installed, the reusable holder, quarterly housekeeping routine, known placement, stored cartridge stock, and staff familiarity reduce renewal friction. Each cohort improves manufacturing yield, placement instructions, damage forecasts, and inspection ergonomics. The company does not need customer incident data to retain the account.

This advantage is weak if hotels buy only through pest contractors, if annual replacement is forgotten, or if an incumbent bundles a monitor free with treatment. Direct demand must exist before any channel is considered.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

### Incumbent response

- **Nattaro** can lower lure/monitor price, use its patented lure and Anticimex/Rentokil relationships, or copy housekeeping packaging.
- **SenSci** can emphasize its research provenance, hospitality testimonials, 90-day lure, pest-professional distribution, and price.
- **Spotta** can sell automatic alerts and bundle the labour saved by electronics.
- **Rentokil and other pest companies** can include monitors inside existing service contracts and use technician trust.
- A plastics/converted-paper supplier can reproduce an unprotected holder through one tool.

QuietRoom therefore advances only with a physical result incumbents cannot dismiss, lower all-in hotel labour/cost, owned tooling, fast multilingual direct sales, and a prepaid cohort lead. If an incumbent matches the blind result and annual cost before 10,000 active positions, the proposed control point has failed.

### Expansion

1. **Phase 1:** 100–600-bed-place independent hotels in Poland/Czechia/eastern Germany.
2. **Phase 2:** hotel groups across DACH, France, Benelux, and southern Europe using the same cartridge and localized housekeeping materials.
3. **Phase 3:** hostels, student residences, and residential care only after separate placement, procurement, and operational paid proof; their beds are not silently included in the hotel model.
4. **No phase converts into treatment, inspection, software, referrals, or a field-service network.**

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides lower-cost design, tooling, production, and fast access to CEE hotel pilots. Multilingual EU documentation and mail fulfilment can beat imported North American monitors on landed cost and make direct adoption easier.

This is not a formal barrier. Nattaro is Swedish, Rentokil is established across Europe, and Spotta is European. A Polish base supplies speed and cost, not protected demand. The defence exists only if QuietRoom accumulates an 18–24 month performance, tooling, and prepaid-cohort lead.

## Compounding Advantage

The intended loop is:

1. blind physical superiority makes direct hotel proof credible;
2. prepaid cohorts finance holders/cartridges;
3. installed holders make annual replenishment simpler than replacement evaluation;
4. manufacturing and housekeeping evidence lowers COGS, damage, and acquisition cost;
5. lower cost and multilingual proof win more cohorts; and
6. volume funds better geometry and controlled replication without adding service labour.

The loop does not compound if renewal depends on a recent infestation, hotels remove monitors, or efficacy is equivalent to cheap interceptors. The company may never describe installed positions as pest-free rooms.

The strong case reinvests 100% through Year 8. Reinvestment funds tooling, annual-pack inventory, quality systems, multilingual direct sales, product liability, and second-source manufacturing.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

The exact paid proof is **two unrelated hotels each paying 8,000 PLN before shipment for a 100-bed-place, 60-day operational cohort, after QuietRoom passes the independent blind gate; at least one hotel prepays a 12-month 100-position conversion by day 180**. Work remains 15–20 founder hours/week. Original founder capital is capped at 40,000 PLN.

- **Days 1–30 — buyer and category evidence (5,000 PLN cumulative):** 25 hotel calls, five redacted incident/cost histories, 30 mock housekeeping inspections, competitor purchases, entomologist scope, two manufacturing quotes, and preliminary FTO/product-safety advice.
- **Days 31–65 — physical prototypes (15,000 PLN cumulative):** engineer/fabricate multiple geometry variants, run closure/escape/cleaning tests, and select one only on preregistered screening evidence.
- **Days 66–100 — independent blind gate (28,000 PLN cumulative):** coded powered comparison, independent report, supplier-batch replication, and hard go/kill decision.
- **Days 101–120 — pilot batch and selling (40,000 PLN cumulative):** manufacture 250 beta positions, send evidence/sample packs, contact at least 80 qualified accounts, and collect two 8,000 PLN payments.
- **Days 121–180 — two 60-day operational cohorts:** customer payments cover freight and bounded replacement. No live insects are introduced. Measure completion, time, damage, visibility, removals, handling, objections, and annual price acceptance. Secure one prepaid annual conversion by day 180.

Letters of interest, free trials, pest-company referrals, a natural hotel catch, or a paid inspection do not count.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder may leave corporate employment only when all are true:

- at least 20 unrelated hotels and 15,000 prepaid annual positions, with no group above 25% of revenue;
- two renewal cohorts with at least 75% position renewal and positive contribution;
- blind performance replicated on three independent production batches;
- blended CAC below €15 per activated position and sub-12-month contribution payback;
- first-year delivered COGS at or below €20 and mature gross margin at or above 55%;
- a second manufacturer can build without founder presence;
- no critical escape, guest safety, false claim, or product-liability event;
- inside sales and fulfilment operate without hotel visits or pest referrals; and
- at least 12 months of personal runway outside the 100,000 PLN business capital.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If the blind gate fails, stop at or below 28,000 PLN. Retain CAD, competitor teardowns, fixtures, protocol, raw results, hotel interviews, and supplier quotes; do not turn them into pest-control consulting, inspection, software, referrals, or an efficacy claim.

If operational pilots fail, stop at 40,000 PLN, disclose limitations to pilot hotels, recover unused holders, and recycle/scrap beta material. If demand fails after tooling, the small removable tool/dies and standard material choices limit stranded capital. Suspected positives and hotel records remain hotel property and never become a data asset.

- Eurostat reports 162,375 hotel establishments, 6.17m hotel bedrooms, more than 3bn total tourist nights, 62.8% of nights in hotels, and 49% hotel bed-place occupancy in 2024.

## Critical Hypotheses

1. The passive dual-path cartridge clears every blinded physical threshold.
2. It avoids Nattaro/SenSci patents and does not require an added lure.
3. One position per bed place is operationally acceptable, including two on a double bed.
4. Weekly inspection takes no more than 15 seconds and completion exceeds 90%.
5. At least two unrelated hotels prepay 8,000 PLN and one prepays an annual conversion by day 180.
6. A qualified hotel's documented annual pain supports €44 per position with at least 3× value coverage.
7. Four mailed cartridges per year create real renewal value without technician visits.
8. First-year COGS reaches €19 and mature blended gross margin reaches 65%.
9. Direct CAC falls below €15 per active position after the first ten accounts.
10. At least 5m economically eligible EU hotel bed-place positions exist.
11. Annual position renewal stays at or above 75% without a recent infestation.
12. An incumbent cannot match the result/cost before the company establishes a meaningful prepaid cohort.

## Kill Criteria

Kill or abandon QuietRoom if any occurs:

1. any primary blinded physical-performance threshold fails, including the 15-point day-14 advantage;
2. the independent test is underpowered, cannot be replicated, or changes after results are seen;
3. a blocking patent/design right prevents the exact geometry and an affordable written route is unavailable;
4. the product needs pheromones, pesticides, desiccants, a kill claim, or a biocidal route to compete;
5. captured-insect escape occurs after one redesign or safe closure cannot be performed by housekeeping;
6. two unrelated hotels do not each prepay 8,000 PLN by day 120, or neither prepays an annual conversion by day 180;
7. weekly inspection completion is below 90%, median time exceeds 15 seconds, missing/damaged positions exceed 5%, or guest-visible complaints/removals exceed 1%;
8. hotels will pay only during an active infestation or less than €35 per annual position;
9. documented annualized customer value is below three times plan cost and no named budget owner supplies another rationale;
10. delivered first-year COGS exceeds €20, blended gross margin is below 55%, or CAC payback exceeds 12 months;
11. country research yields fewer than 5m eligible hotel bed-place positions or 400,000 positions require more than 8% conservative eligible share;
12. annual position renewal is below 75% across two cohorts;
13. Nattaro, SenSci, Spotta, Rentokil, or another incumbent matches the blind result and lifecycle cost before 10,000 active positions;
14. hotels insist on pest-controller endorsement, site inspections, manual founder support, software, or referrals; or
15. product-liability cover is unavailable or a serious safety/false-clearance event occurs.
