# Round Four — Resource-Control Directions

**Research date:** 2026-07-13  
**Scope:** generator-only exploration; no scores, panel verdicts, validation claims, pivots, or chatbot contact.

## Boundary Conditions Applied

- Founder: Warsaw data scientist, employed full-time, maximum five hours per day, 100,000 PLN original capital.
- Paid proof must be possible within 180 days while founder cash at risk remains at or below 40,000 PLN.
- A foundational resource must be owned, optioned, exclusively licensed, reserved, or routed within 12 months and the original 100,000 PLN. A friendly conversation or unsigned “partnership” does not count.
- These directions avoid the confirmed compliance-desk families, the killed aftermarket/software/location roll-ups, serial cultural or cultivar rights, dashboards, CRMs, generic AI wrappers, influencer/SEO acquisition, and assets that reset for every local site.
- A platform patent appears only where the same patent family and know-how can be used across many customers; the model does **not** require acquiring one new right per customer.
- Wealth figures below are threshold shapes, not forecasts. They assume 95% reinvestment of distributable after-tax cash while the founder remains employed, optional employment-phase distributions of no more than 5%, no double-counting of retained earnings, and 60–65% founder ownership after later financing. Each shape dies if early transactions cannot support its stated spread and capital intensity.

## Shared Evidence About Acquireability

The University of Gdańsk Technology Transfer Office (TTO) explicitly offers exclusive and non-exclusive licences, outright IP sales, and know-how agreements across more than 280 patent applications. Its public process allows an entrepreneur to submit an offer; this is a named acquisition channel rather than an unspecified introduction. Licence price, exclusivity, inventor support, patent territory, freedom to operate, and milestone terms remain unproved until a written term sheet exists. [UG technology licensing and sales](https://biznes.ug.edu.pl/en/technology-licensing-sales/) and [UG transfer process](https://ctt.ug.edu.pl/en/technology-transfer-process-at-ug/).

For every patent direction below, the pre-proof instrument should be a 90–180 day field-of-use option costing no more than 10,000–15,000 PLN, with a pre-agreed licence formula and access to samples/know-how. A vague promise to negotiate later is a kill condition.

## 1. AntibioticQuench — Exclusive Industrial-Wastewater Field-of-Use Licence

**Customer-inside insight.** A pharmaceutical plant or specialist wastewater operator does not primarily want a “micropollutant dashboard.” It wants a treatment step that makes a difficult antibiotic-bearing stream safer without buying a full municipal-plant redesign or assuming that conventional biological treatment destroys antibacterial activity.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Option the named UG patent application and lab know-how for a narrow field such as high-concentration pharmaceutical or veterinary-production side streams. Spend at most 20,000 PLN on IP/engineering diligence and a customer-effluent bench protocol. Require one generator or treatment operator to prepay at least 12,000 PLN for a blinded before/after destruction trial on its real effluent. Do not construct a field skid before the paid test meets agreed removal, energy, and by-product criteria.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** An exclusive Poland/CEE field-of-use licence to P.431823, inventor know-how, reactor drawings, and the right to sublicense qualified fabrication. UG describes a continuous dc-APGD process, no plasma-forming gas, laboratory validation at TRL 4, and licensing or sale availability. [UG Antibiotic Inactivation offer 160/2025](https://ctt.ug.edu.pl/en/baza_ofert/method-for-inactivating-antibiotics-in-aqueous-solutions/).

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** The right is publicly offered for commercialisation; the founder can propose a cheap option, milestones, royalties, and inventor-funded development rather than asking a plant to trust an unowned invention. Data-science skill is useful for dose-response, energy-per-gram-destroyed, by-product, and trial-design work, while a contracted plasma engineer handles physical design.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** The same licensed reactor architecture and performance library can serve every installation. A mature shape of 120 modules at an average €180,000 system price plus €4 million of recurring electrodes/service would be about €25.6 million revenue. At 20% operating margin, 6× operating profit, €5 million net liabilities, and 65% founder ownership, founder equity would be about €16.7 million in years 10–14. Customer deposits, equipment leasing, and project debt—not original founder cash—must fund post-proof fabrication.

**Strongest copy risk.** Ozone, activated carbon, UV/AOP, or another plasma configuration achieves sufficient destruction at lower lifecycle cost without infringing the patent.

**Fatal dependencies.** The licence is unavailable or only Polish with no defensible CEE extension; real effluent defeats lab performance; toxic transformation products appear; energy/electrode cost is uncompetitive; or industrial buyers refuse paid bench work before a full reference plant. The EU Urban Wastewater Treatment Directive creates real micropollutant spending pressure and requires pharmaceutical/cosmetics producers to cover at least 80% of quaternary-treatment costs by the relevant implementation timetable, but it does not guarantee this technology will win. [Directive (EU) 2024/3019, Articles 8–9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L3019).

## 2. AirCatalyst Media — European-Patent HVAC Replacement Consumable

**Customer-inside insight.** A cold-store, food plant, animal facility, or commercial-building operator does not want an experimental air purifier. It may pay for a replacement filter insert or coated porous panel that fits the existing maintenance route and has independently measured VOC/microbial reduction without adding a new operator workflow.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Option the patent for one narrow non-medical HVAC field. Have the university or a toll coater make 20–50 coupons/inserts. Require an HVAC filter OEM or cold-chain integrator to prepay at least 10,000 PLN for a chamber test and a defined small-site trial. Founder spend is capped at 35,000 PLN across option, coatings, independent test design, and insurance review.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** Exclusive field-of-use rights to EP4110520/PCT know-how, the paste recipe, regeneration protocol, and qualified toll-manufacturing instructions. UG states that the patented paste uses low-cost components, can coat porous materials, works under low-intensity UV, and is offered for licence or sale at TRL 4. [UG Photocatalytic Air-Purification Materials offer](https://ctt.ug.edu.pl/en/baza_ofert/photocatalytic-materials-for-air-purification-from-organic-inorganic-compounds-and-microorganisms/).

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** This is a public licence offer with European protection, and a narrow HVAC field leaves the university free to license unrelated uses. The founder buys an option and a measurable trial, not the credibility of a medical claim.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** One paste formula, coating process, test library, and OEM qualification can support many filter models and customers. At 750,000 inserts/panels per year and €35 net revenue per unit, revenue would be €26.3 million. At 22% operating margin, 6× operating profit, €4 million net liabilities, 65% ownership, founder equity would be about €20 million in years 9–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** Incumbent filter and catalyst makers pair commodity TiO2 or another photocatalyst with an existing distribution channel and obtain equivalent real-world results.

**Fatal dependencies.** The patent claims do not cover the chosen insert; low-intensity moving-air performance is weak; by-products or nanoparticle shedding fail safety tests; a useful UV source is not already present; or no filter OEM prepays a controlled trial. No antimicrobial or health claim may be used before the applicable evidence and approvals exist.

## 3. QuantumSeed Rail — Exclusive Gaming/Simulation Field Licence to a Quantum Randomness System

**Customer-inside insight.** A regulated game platform, lottery supplier, security-appliance maker, or high-stakes simulation vendor does not buy “quantum.” It buys independently auditable entropy, replay-resistant seed provenance, and a hardware/software boundary that can survive a certification or customer-security review.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Secure a 180-day option for one commercial field, plus remote access to the existing TRL-4 demonstration. Sell a 15,000 PLN paid integration and evidence sprint to one B2B platform or certification lab: the deliverable is a working entropy feed, raw-test corpus, failover behaviour, and certification-gap report—not a promised production certification. Founder exposure stays below 35,000 PLN for option, optical-engineering support, and test hardware.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** An exclusive EU field-of-use licence to the EPO/PCT application EP22723834.2 / PCT/PL2022/050022, access to the lab design and inventor know-how, and sublicensing rights to a contract hardware maker. UG publicly offers licence, sale, or further commercialisation of the TRL-4 system. [UG quantum correlated random-number system offer](https://ctt.ug.edu.pl/baza_ofert/system-kwantowy-do-generowania-i-certyfikacji-nieklasycznie-skorelowanych-liczb-losowych/).

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** A narrow field option does not require the TTO to trust the founder with all quantum-security markets. The founder's quantitative background fits statistical test design and evidence automation; optical engineering remains a paid inventor/contractor role.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** One hardware design, certification dossier, monitoring rail, and patent family can be sold repeatedly. At 300 appliances/subscriptions averaging €50,000 recurring annual revenue, revenue would be €15 million. At 35% operating margin, 7× operating profit, €3 million net liabilities, and 60% founder ownership, founder equity would be about €20 million in years 9–12.

**Strongest copy risk.** Certified classical hardware RNGs or cheaper quantum entropy chips already satisfy buyers; the patent adds no economically meaningful assurance.

**Fatal dependencies.** The university will not grant the chosen field; production hardware cannot be built inside the first 100,000 PLN after paid proof; a relevant lab will not state a certifiable path; platform buyers will not pay before certification; or the patent claims are easy to design around. This direction is about a licensable hardware root, not a generic gambling analytics or crypto wrapper.

## 4. MOFGuard — Licensed Replaceable CO2-Sorbent Cartridges for Small Industrial Streams

**Customer-inside insight.** A small biogas upgrader, controlled-atmosphere operator, or process-gas integrator cares about regeneration cycles, impurity tolerance, pressure drop, and euros per tonne removed—not impressive gram-scale uptake. A replaceable cartridge gives it a bounded trial and shifts adoption from a plant redesign to one treatment stage.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Option the named patent application and obtain a reproducible kilogram-scale sample route. Sell a prepaid 12,000–20,000 PLN gas-stream screening campaign to one integrator with explicit cycle-life and impurity thresholds. Spend no more than 35,000 PLN before payment and do not build a pressure vessel; use an accredited lab or the customer's safe test loop.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** Exclusive small-stream field rights to P.452638, formula/process know-how, sample access, and the right to have granules toll-manufactured. UG describes a TRL-4 ionic-liquid-modified MOF sorbent intended for columns and offers licence or sale. [UG MOF CO2 Sorbent offer 193/2025](https://ctt.ug.edu.pl/en/baza_ofert/sorbent-based-on-metal-organic-frameworks-mofs-modified-with-ionic-liquids-for-co2-sorption-and-method-for-its-preparation/).

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** The public TTO route supports a milestone option, while a deliberately narrow segment avoids asking for all carbon-capture rights. A contract chemical engineer and accredited gas lab cover the founder's technical gap.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** The formula, production recipe, cartridge geometry, impurity data, and regeneration history improve across every customer. At 1,500 tonnes per year of granules/cartridges and €12/kg net revenue plus €4 million service/replacement revenue, total revenue would be about €22 million. At 22% operating margin, 6× operating profit, €6 million net liabilities, and 60% ownership, founder equity would be about €13.8 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** Mature amines, membranes, activated carbon, or another MOF wins on total installed cost and cycle life.

**Fatal dependencies.** No defensible protection outside Poland, kilogram scale changes the material, water/contaminants destroy capacity, regeneration energy is poor, or no integrator prepays a test. Industrial-scale manufacturing must be financed after validated orders; it cannot consume the founder's first 100,000 PLN.

## 5. CarbonFibreFlow — Contracted Clean CFRP Scrap Allocation Into Qualified Compounds

**Customer-inside insight.** A moulding compound or non-critical component producer wants predictable fibre length, resin residue, sizing, and lot traceability—not an environmental story or mixed composite waste. The scarce resource is a recurring, specification-controlled stream of clean production trim that can be reserved before buying recycling machinery.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Sign a 12-month exclusive purchase option over at least 20 tonnes of named, clean CFRP/prepreg offcuts from one multi-line producer, conditional on samples. Obtain a fixed toll-processing quote. Require a compounder or component maker to prepay at least 20,000 PLN for a one-tonne qualified trial lot. Founder exposure is capped at 35,000 PLN for samples, waste classification, insured transport, and toll conversion.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** Executed title/option to the annual scrap allocation, lot-specification and rejection rights, plus a paid toll-processing slot; no owned pyrolysis line. The resource feeds multiple downstream customers and does not reset per installation site.

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** A generator can grant a short option without changing its operations if the founder pays sampling/logistics and provides an auditable outlet. The offer becomes credible only when the supplier names tonnes, composition, title, price, and duration in the contract. EU projects have already demonstrated cross-sector routes from aeronautic composite waste to new parts, so the technical category is real even though this exact Polish stream is not yet proved. [EU MANIFICA project](https://trimis.ec.europa.eu/project/manufacture-et-innovation-avec-fibres-de-carbone-recyclees-manufacturing-and-innovation) and [EU FiberEUse summary](https://projects.research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/en/projects/success-stories/all/bringing-circular-economy-practices-glass-and-carbon-fibre-composites).

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** Multi-year feedstock allocations, qualification data, recipes, and customer approvals form the asset. At 5,000 tonnes/year and €6/kg net product revenue, revenue would be €30 million. At 15% operating margin, 5.5× operating profit, €5 million net liabilities, and 60% ownership, founder equity would be about €11.9 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** An established recycler offers the same generator a better gate fee and already-qualified product.

**Fatal dependencies.** The first stream is mixed/contaminated or too small; title/waste codes prevent cross-border tolling; mechanical properties do not meet a paid customer's threshold; or the required working capital exceeds customer deposits and trade finance. Manual spot sourcing is not a control point and kills the direction.

## 6. MagnetLoop — Optioned NdFeB Component Flow, Not a General Scrap Marketplace

**Customer-inside insight.** A magnet recycler or motor maker does not want “e-waste leads.” It wants lots with known motor/device origin, recoverable magnet type, coating/adhesive information, safe disassembly instructions, and sufficient tonnes to schedule processing.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Option the right to purchase at least five tonnes of named magnet-bearing components from one national service, remanufacturing, or dismantling organisation; the contract must cover all of that organisation's Polish depots for the selected component family. Characterise a sample lot and require a recycler/magnet buyer to prepay at least 15,000 PLN for the first specification-compliant shipment. Founder exposure stays below 35,000 PLN; certified subcontractors perform handling and waste transport.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** A two-to-three-year, multi-depot exclusive purchase right with volume, index price, title, contamination, and termination terms, plus a reserved toll-disassembly route. It compounds because one inbound contract can supply many magnet/material customers rather than requiring a lease at every site.

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** The founder offers the component owner a single auditable buyer and indexed upside without asking it to finance a plant. The contract is only plausible for an awkward, currently low-value component family; valuable clean magnet lots already sold competitively are excluded.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** At 1,200 tonnes/year of sorted magnets/material sold at €35/kg blended revenue, revenue would be €42 million. At 15% operating margin, 5.5× operating profit, €10 million net liabilities, and 60% ownership, founder equity would be about €14.8 million in years 10–14. Later plant finance is released only after paid lots prove recoverable kilograms and margin.

**Strongest copy risk.** Existing metal recyclers or OEM take-back programs bid away the same multi-depot stream once its value is visible.

**Fatal dependencies.** Magnet mass per component is too low; safe adhesive/coating removal is expensive; the supplier refuses cross-depot exclusivity; buyers will not prepay without full recycled-content certification; or waste/ADR rules make the first flow uneconomic. EU law provides real momentum: the Critical Raw Materials Act prioritises permanent-magnet recycling and adds labelling/data-carrier and recycled-content disclosure obligations, and a May 2026 implementing regulation lists permanent-magnet traction motors among relevant recovery streams. [Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, Articles 28–29](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1252) and [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1116](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32026R1116).

## 7. ReclaimGas Flow — Title to Homogeneous Recovered HFC Lots

**Customer-inside insight.** A refrigeration contractor maintaining an installed base needs legal, correctly labelled reclaimed refrigerant of known composition when virgin high-GWP gas use is restricted. It does not need another F-gas reporting consultant; it needs cylinders it can actually buy and trust.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Use the family installation network only to locate the first homogeneous recoverable batch, not as the sole scale channel. Contract a certified recovery operator, take conditional title to the batch, obtain a fixed reclamation/assay quote, and require a certified contractor or distributor to prepay for the first labelled reclaimed lot. Founder risk is capped at 30,000 PLN across cylinders, assay, transport, and optioned gas; no reclamation plant is built.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** A multi-branch distributor or service group's two-year first-purchase/exclusive right over specified recovered HFC grades, with title, chain of custody, minimum annual kilograms, and index pricing, plus a reserved reclamation slot. A single installer introduction is proof material, not the control point.

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** Recovery owners may prefer a documented purchase route that removes cylinders and shares recovered value. The founder can demonstrate the workflow through the family business, then seek the multi-branch right. The direction is invalid until the rights and kilograms are signed.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** Contracts, cylinder logistics, composition history, and buyer reservations can expand across grades and CEE. At 600 tonnes/year and €50/kg blended revenue, revenue would be €30 million. At 16% operating margin, 5.5× operating profit, €5 million net liabilities, and 60% ownership, founder equity would be about €12.8 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** Existing gas distributors vertically integrate collection and reclamation or lock contractors with cylinder-credit programs.

**Fatal dependencies.** A trader cannot legally take title without registrations/certification; batches are mixed or contaminated; reclaim yield/spread is weak; the multi-branch supplier will not grant a right; or working capital cannot be covered by customer prepayment. Regulation (EU) 2024/573 already restricts high-GWP virgin gas in specified servicing uses from 2026 while allowing labelled reclaimed gas during transition periods; UDT confirms that recovery work requires the relevant certification. [F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R0573), [European Commission bulk F-gas obligations](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases/stakeholder-obligations/f-gases-bulk_en), and [UDT F-gas guidance](https://www.udt.gov.pl/rozwiazania-dla-sektorow/srodowisko/o-f-gazach-i-szwo).

## 8. UniformMonoLoop — Multi-Site Workwear Take-Back Right Into One Qualified Material

**Customer-inside insight.** A fibre/insulation/compound buyer does not want random post-consumer clothes. It wants a stable monomaterial or tightly specified workwear stream with known fibre composition, contamination exclusions, and predictable collection volumes. An industrial laundry or uniform issuer wants one accountable end route, not another bin provider.

**Paid proof within 180 days / 40,000 PLN.** Sign a 12-month purchase/collection option covering all Polish depots of one laundry, workwear lessor, or multi-site employer for one composition class such as high-polyester uniforms. Obtain a toll-sort/shred quote and require an insulation, nonwoven, or compound maker to prepay at least 15,000 PLN for a 3–5 tonne specification trial. Founder risk remains below 35,000 PLN.

**Foundational resource within 12 months / 100,000 PLN.** A two-to-three-year multi-depot right specifying annual tonnes, composition, title, gate fee or purchase price, rejected contaminants, and audit access, plus a reserved toll-processing slot. The same inbound resource supports many product buyers; the company does not reopen a local-site lease for each customer.

**Why an unknown founder can secure it.** The initial supplier can grant an option without equipment capex if the founder pays sample logistics and provides a documented downstream route. EU textile EPR makes predictable sorting and fibre-to-fibre routes more valuable, but the founder must win on economics, not on a generic sustainability promise.

**Compounding resource and founder-wealth shape — hypothesis.** At 15,000 tonnes/year and €1,700/tonne blended product plus gate-fee revenue, revenue would be €25.5 million before any additional processing revenue. At 18% operating margin, 5.5× operating profit, €5 million net liabilities, and 60% ownership, founder equity would be about €12.1 million in years 10–14.

**Strongest copy risk.** Producer-responsibility organisations or established textile sorters secure the same clean institutional streams with lower logistics cost.

**Fatal dependencies.** Real garments are too blended/contaminated; the multi-depot supplier refuses exclusivity; toll processing destroys fibre value; downstream buyers will not prepay or qualify the output; or gate fees disappear as competition rises. Directive (EU) 2025/1892 requires producer-funded collection, sorting, reuse and recycling systems and explicitly seeks fibre-to-fibre recycling, with national schemes due by the directive's timetable. [Directive (EU) 2025/1892](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2025/1892/oj).

## Cross-Direction Kill Test Before Any Advisory Evaluation

For an IP direction, obtain a written option term sheet, patent-family/territory schedule, freedom-to-operate red flags, inventor-support commitment, reproducible sample access, and a customer-paid test. If any one is absent, the “resource” is still speculative.

For a material-flow direction, obtain a signed multi-site or multi-line right with named tonnes and title, a fixed toll-processing route, an independent assay/specification, and a downstream deposit. If supply or offtake remains a friendly conversation, it is coordination rather than control.

No wealth shape survives if it requires founder-funded production assets before paid proof, if the early gross spread cannot plausibly finance later working capital, or if expansion consists of signing an unrelated local contract from zero each time.

## Evidence Versus Hypothesis Summary

**Evidence:** the named UG technologies are publicly offered for licence/sale at stated TRL 4; EP4110520 and the listed patent/application numbers exist as described by the TTO; current EU rules create documented pressure around micropollutants, permanent-magnet circularity, F-gases, and textile recovery; EU-funded projects have demonstrated technical routes for composite recycling.

**Unproved hypotheses:** every licence/option price and exclusivity term; availability of the exact waste streams; all tonne, price, margin, conversion, customer-prepayment, and valuation assumptions; real-world performance of the TRL-4 technologies; and the founder's ability to obtain multi-depot supply rights. These facts must be resolved before any direction becomes a finalist.
