# Zero-to-One Pivot: QuenchPortfolio Integrator Licence

## Preserved Insight

Customer-funded real-effluent destruction evidence should precede hardware, and measured stream outcomes become valuable only when tied to protected process rights and a repeatable product.

## Structural Change

Replace a single-application direct skid seller with an inventor-backed IP/product spinout that options the complete relevant portfolio: granted Polish Pat.242412/P.431823 plus the later high-throughput plasma-brush family P.440185/PCT/PL2022/050027/EP4466234A1, documented know-how and company-funded improvement rights. The first economic buyer changes from an antibiotic plant to an established industrial-water integrator that prepays a field-of-use evaluation and receives a project-registered regional commercial option only after the result.

The integrator supplies plant access, process engineering, installation and service capacity; the spinout controls the plasma reactor/control package, study protocol, licence, improvements and performance corpus.

## Target Niche and Buyer

CEE/EU industrial-water integrators already serving pharmaceutical or veterinary-production plants with segregated antibiotic-bearing streams. Select integrators with existing clients, hazardous-sample processes and the ability to finance/install a pilot. The ultimate plant remains the beneficiary, but the integrator owns the treatment budget and trust relationship.

## Painful Event and Willingness to Pay

An integrator risks losing a treatment project when its existing biological/AOP design cannot economically handle a concentrated antibiotic stream or prove residual antibacterial-activity destruction. It pays for a protected differentiator only when one named client stream creates a live bid or retrofit opportunity. A prepaid €5,000–€10,000 evaluation is credited against a later project licence; generic innovation scouting does not count.

## Value and Profit Wedge

The spinout sells a standard real-effluent qualification, then licences/sells the protected reactor/control cartridge and receives an 8–12% technology royalty or 30–40% gross-margin hardware/control package. Integrators finance and install the balance of plant. Recurring value comes from electrodes/control recipes, verification and new-compound qualification, reducing founder capital and field-service burden.

## Domination Wedge

Control one difficult compound/matrix segment through the combined patent families, undisclosed know-how, inventor participation, company-owned scale-up improvements and comparable real-effluent performance. Project registration prevents channel conflict. The company expands by compound/stream evidence, not by offering general wastewater consulting.

## Acquisition Channel

The TTO/inventor route is public. Build a list of 40 industrial-water integrators and approach technical directors with the named patents, published multi-antibiotic results and a specific paid client-stream protocol. No plant status is borrowed; the integrator brings the live project and pays before external analysis.

## Primary Control Point

The foundational resource must be one signed option covering:

- granted Pat.242412 and the later P.440185/PCT/EP family in the chosen industrial field;
- patent-territory/status schedule and sublicensing/project-registration rights;
- exclusive access to undisclosed reactor/control know-how and samples;
- named inventor engineering hours or equity/contract commitment;
- ownership or exclusive field rights to company-funded improvements;
- rights to contractually retained anonymised performance data.

Publications or one Polish patent without the international family are rejected.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Obtain an IP status/FTO review across both families and co-owner authority.
2. Sign a 180-day portfolio option <=15,000 PLN with a pre-agreed <=50,000 PLN licence entry, royalties and inventor support.
3. Require one of 40 integrators to prepay >=20,000 PLN by day 180 for a named client-stream evaluation; founder pre-proof risk <=38,000 PLN.
4. Accredited lab/inventor team executes activity, transformation-product, energy and comparison protocol.
5. Convert the option only after the paid result and integrator's written budget-backed pilot option; total founder capital <=100,000 PLN through month 12.

## Expansion Path

First one integrator/one client stream, then a second independent integrator and adjacent compound supported by the same brush. Grant only project-registered or performance-bound territory rights. Build reference modules through integrators, then hire a technical product lead and expand European coverage as EP status/improvement IP allows.

## Minimum People Stack

Founder for data/product/commercial control; one inventor or plasma engineer with contracted/equity commitment; analytical lab; IP lawyer; integrator's process/installation team. No founder-owned lab or fabrication plant in POC.

## Principal Risk

The later PCT/EP brush uses helium and remains pending/TRL 4; real effluent, energy, electrode/gas cost or transformation products may make it inferior to ozone/electrochemical alternatives. The university may prefer to license directly to an integrator, leaving the founder without a reason to exist.

## First Disconfirming Test

Kill if the co-owning institutions will not offer the complete portfolio/improvement package on the stated option terms, if no qualified integrator prepays after 40 approaches, or if one real-stream result does not show a credible total-cost or activity-destruction advantage.

## Evidence Preserved

- Polish Pat.242412/P.431823 is granted, in force and currently paid through year six, with expiry listed as 2039-11-15.
- The research lab lists PCT/PL2022/050027 and EPO application EP22750924.7; EP4466234A1 is published/pending.
- Published work covers a high-throughput multi-cone brush and multiple antibiotic classes in laboratory solutions.
- WHO/EU policy creates spending pressure but does not select the technology.

## New Hypotheses

- Both co-owners can grant one coherent exclusive field package and improvement rights.
- An integrator values the portfolio enough to prepay a live-stream evaluation rather than approach the TTO directly.
- Helium/energy/electrode economics survive real effluent and scale.
- Project registration and protected control recipes retain 8–12% value capture.
- Integrator distribution lowers capital/sales burden enough to create a resilient €10m founder path after inventor/team dilution.

## Why This Is Not the Original Failure Shape

The original relied on a single Polish-family interpretation, direct plant trust, founder-coordinated skid sales and a thin 120-site wealth case. QuenchPortfolio changes the buyer/channel to an existing integrator, expands the resource to a coherent granted-plus-international patent/know-how package, requires inventor commitment, shifts balance-of-plant capital/service away from the founder and captures value through protected product/royalty economics.

Return QuenchPortfolio to the evaluator only after the public IP facts are incorporated; the option price, co-owner authority and integrator payment remain hypotheses. This is a prioritisation judgment, not validation.
