# Zero-to-One Candidate: RouteFort Safe Truck-Parking Site Options

## One-Sentence Thesis

Option underused paved yards on CEE freight corridors and exercise only after an independent certification plan and a fleet's prepaid/take-or-pay bay block prove that a specific location can become scarce, bookable safe-and-secure truck-parking capacity.

## Founder Fit

The founder can analyse TEN-T/traffic/security data, price corridors, automate outbound, and structure staged investment. A transport-property lawyer, SSPA certification adviser, security integrator, and later site operator cover operational gaps. The POC is part-time; construction and 24/7 operation begin only after contracts and finance.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Fleet operators and freight forwarders on recurring lanes need legal driver rest, cargo security, toilets/showers, and predictable arrival capacity. Drivers search ad hoc, park insecurely, waste time, or detour. Cargo theft, insurance requirements, and missed rest rules create willingness to pay for a guaranteed lane position. The Commission reports an EU shortage of 390,057 safe/secure spaces.

## Product Wedge

One 80–150-bay underused paved yard within roughly 5 km of an A1/A2/S8 freight node. Secure a six-month conditional site option, certification/security gap plan, and fleet deposit for at least 10 bays for 90 nights. Exercise into a 10–15 year lease only after access, planning, utilities, security, certification, and per-bay economics pass.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The scarce product is not barriers, CCTV, or an app; it is a certified overnight bay on the driver's actual corridor and date. Large developers target big sites, while underused private yards can be optioned and contracted before fit-out. Location rights and prepaid fleet allocation form the control point.

## Underused Momentum

The Commission documents a large present shortage and projected 483,000-space gap by 2040. EU rules define security/service levels and independent certification, making the product legible to fleets. This proves system need, not that a particular yard works.

## Beachhead Market

One Polish freight node with dense recurring international flows, high overnight search pressure, an existing paved yard, legal heavy-vehicle access, and no certified competitor within the practical lane radius. Start with high-value cargo fleets needing Silver/Gold-standard security.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Charge €16–22 per occupied night plus showers, laundry, food/vending, charging, and reserved blocks. A 120-bay site at 70% utilisation and €18 yields about €552,000 annual parking revenue; ancillary net revenue may add 10–20%. Target 55–65% site gross margin and 25–35% EBITDA after rent, staff, utilities, security, insurance, and maintenance. Reject fit-out above debt-serviceable levels or site EBITDA below 25%.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First ten are bays reserved by one fleet contract. First hundred are drivers/vehicles from three to five recurring fleet allocations, then booking-platform traffic and corridor signage. Fleet take-or-pay blocks reduce consumer CAC; booking history helps price capacity.

## Primary Control Point

Tier 1: assignable long lease/purchase option with express parking/security/fit-out rights over a named corridor site, followed by prepaid or take-or-pay bay allocations and independent certification. Cameras or equipment alone are not a control point.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Screen 100 yards; negotiate one <=12,000 PLN six-month option; spend <=10,000 PLN on planning/access/certification/security concept; obtain 5,000–15,000 PLN fleet deposit for 10 bays/90 nights; keep legal/outbound total pre-proof spend <=40,000 PLN. An unknown founder offers landlord optionality and the fleet a conditional reservation; no build occurs before both contracts.

## Distribution Advantage

Fleet allocations arrive from lane-specific outbound; corridor signage and booking platforms fill residual capacity. Each site adds a proven landlord/finance/certification playbook and fleet network. Strongest copy risk is a logistics-property owner developing a nearby yard; it cannot reproduce the exact lease/location but can add competing capacity.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Open one site, reach 65% utilisation and two fleet renewals, then option adjacent corridor nodes used by the same fleets. Build a CEE network of location leases, contracted bay allocations, security certifications, and route data. Expand site-by-site with debt/leases after anchor contracts; never rely on one national land purchase.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland is a major European freight corridor and has lower site/staff costs than western nodes. Local land sourcing and permitting matter. The defence is location-specific rights and fleet route density, not a regulatory exemption.

## Compounding Advantage

Every site adds scarce bay inventory, fleet contracts, route demand, security performance, price/yield data, landlord references, and booking liquidity. Shared fleets can anchor the next site; shared procurement lowers cost. Capital intensity is financed at site level after demand proof.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% of distributable after-tax cash while employed, optional distributions <=5%; strong reinvests 100%. Founder capital funds options/concepts only. Site leases, security fit-out, and construction require anchor contracts and non-founder debt/equity; liability is deducted in value.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–60: map corridors/100 yards/30 fleets. Days 61–100: sign one conditional site option and commission written gap plan. Days 101–150: sell a conditional 10-bay/90-night fleet block. By day 180: deposit received, landlord rights verified, certification/fit-out finance plan documented. Pre-proof <=40,000 PLN and employment retained. Delivery/opening is a later tranche funded only after multiple fleet commitments.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

5,000 PLN mapping/outbound; up to 12,000 PLN option only after access/planning screen; up to 30,000 PLN with certification/legal concept; 40,000 PLN ceiling before fleet deposit. Remaining founder 60,000 PLN only after two independent fleet commitments and financeable site contribution. Family capital excluded until certified opening plan, multiple paying fleets, and positive projected contribution.

## Critical Dependency Map

Site rights, planning/use, heavy access, utilities, certification, security fit-out, fleet deposit, and site finance are sequential. Any site requiring simultaneous speculative planning and construction is rejected. One fleet deposit is proof but not permission to build; multiple customers and financeable economics release more capital.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

One operating site, three independent fleet customers, positive site contribution, 65%+ utilisation or contracted equivalent, second-site option, repeatable fleet acquisition, and 12 months personal runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

An expired option leaves corridor demand maps, fleet interviews, certification gap template, yard scorecard, landlord terms, and finance quotes. Deposits are conditionally refundable if the named opening condition fails. No stranded security hardware is purchased before exercise.

## Critical Evidence

Commission shortage study; EU SSPA standards; TEN-T corridor maps. Target rent, fleet willingness, certification cost, and utilisation are hypotheses.

## Critical Hypotheses

Suitable paved yards can be optioned cheaply; local use/access/certification is feasible; fleets will reserve before opening; €16–22/night and 65–75% utilisation support debt; founder can raise site capital after anchors.

## Kill Criteria

No credible yard option after 100; option >12,000 PLN; no fleet deposit after 30 qualified fleet approaches; certification/access/planning path unclear; required founder cash >100,000 PLN before multiple customers; site EBITDA <25%; capex payback >8 years; no second independent fleet commitment before exercise.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; transport-property lawyer; certification/security adviser; local planning engineer; outsourced site operator only after financing. Pre-proof <=40,000 PLN; founder POC cap <=100,000 PLN.
