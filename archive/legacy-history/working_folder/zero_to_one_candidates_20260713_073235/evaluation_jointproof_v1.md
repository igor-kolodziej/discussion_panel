# Zero-to-One Evaluation: JointProof v1

## Diagnosis

JointProof is a genuine product thesis rather than a PV consultancy: the founder can build and own the fixture, firmware, calibration method, specimen bank and connector-specific cassette workflow without an OEM, distributor or first customer granting access. The narrow de-energized use case, external prepaid-pilot gate and explicit non-claims are unusually disciplined. The unresolved fact is also existential: there is no cited evidence that the proposed proof load, temporary test mate and resistance measurement can distinguish consequential field-assembly faults repeatably without changing or contaminating a qualified connector. Until a blind engineer-reviewed bench result exists, the supposed control point is a buildable design hypothesis, not a proved resource.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Manufacturer and industry material supports connector-specific tooling, correct assembly and the importance of contact resistance/mismatch. It also establishes that test-equipment incumbents already serve PV installers. It does not validate JointProof's measurement method, a non-damaging force limit, the meaning of a milliohm reading through a temporary mate, manufacturer-instruction compatibility, buyer willingness to add a 20–30-second step or the €4,000–€6,000 production price. The three wealth cases are internally stated scenarios, not market evidence. No panel or chatbot verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**70.5/100.** Weighted points are 141 across 20 weight units; `10 × 141 / 20 = 70.5`. This is advisory only and is not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 path; currently unproved.** The founder can directly create and own the fixture, firmware, calibration method, cassette geometry, specimen bank and measured recipe thresholds. Those assets require no exclusive partner or licence. They become a meaningful control point only after the bench method works and repeated field use produces recipe/calibration history. Strongest copy risk: Stäubli, Fluke or another trusted instrument company combines controlled pull, resistance measurement and an approved connector workflow, using existing distribution and authority to erase JointProof's trust deficit.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** The conservative case yields about €11.8m founder equity in year 12 after stated obligations, with no distributions double-counted and 95% reinvestment while employed. Selling roughly 83 devices/month across Europe and servicing 6,000 installed devices is not facially heroic, but the cushion is modest: lower recurring attachment, <20% operating margin, more dilution or product-liability reserves can break the gate. The expected and strong cases are expansion cases, not proof of the conservative one.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, conditional on the bench signal.** The founder can build the alpha with commodity parts and replaceable specialist contractors, risk no more than 35,000 PLN, remain employed and request two unrelated 4,000–6,000 PLN prepayments after an unedited blind benchmark. Family-company usage is correctly excluded as paid proof. If an engineer-approved benchmark cannot be produced inside that cap, the gate fails before outreach.

## Expansion Check

The one-connector/two-cable beachhead is appropriately narrow. Expansion to another family is not a pure software toggle: each cassette, force limit and measurement recipe needs independent evidence, and OEM warranty rules may vary. The compounding path is credible only if common electronics/firmware/calibration infrastructure makes each added recipe cheaper and field outcome history improves thresholds faster than incumbents can bundle the feature.

## Fatal Objections

1. The central measurement may not separate meaningful defects, or the proof load/test mate may itself change the connector.
2. Connector instructions, warranty positions or conformity/liability work may make an unofficial verifier commercially unusable even if technically informative.
3. Trusted tool makers can copy the function and start with much stronger channel authority.
4. The conservative wealth result loses its €10m clearance if recurring attachment, ownership or operating margin misses modestly.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

A founder-owned, connector-specific physical quality gate can turn one hidden field-made termination into a measurable, traceable production step before energisation.

## Recommendation

**research further**

The next evidence must be technical, not additional market prose: build the cheap single-recipe bench path, pre-register blinded defect/damage thresholds, and obtain an independent electrical/product-safety review of the exact test. Re-evaluate only if the method separates consequential faults without changing qualified good samples and without relying on an OEM endorsement.
