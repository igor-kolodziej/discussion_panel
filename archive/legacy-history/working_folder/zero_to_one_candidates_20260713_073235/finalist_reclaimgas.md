# Zero-to-One Candidate: ReclaimGas Flow

## One-Sentence Thesis

ReclaimGas secures multi-branch first-purchase rights to single-grade recovered HFC cylinders, routes them through certified recovery, transport, assay, and reclamation partners, and resells the labelled reclaimed refrigerant under prepaid offtake agreements to certified refrigeration contractors that need legal service gas for installed equipment during the EU HFC phase-down.

## Founder Fit

The founder can begin as the designer and commercial owner of a documented material flow rather than as a refrigerant technician or plant operator. His data-science background fits cylinder-level chain-of-custody records, composition and yield histories, price/yield forecasting, route density, supplier scoring, and inventory allocation. AI can accelerate source-account research, contract comparison, compliance checklists, outreach personalisation, and exception monitoring without turning the product into a generic software wrapper.

The founder's parents' installation company can help locate the first certified heat-pump, refrigeration, or HVAC contractors and identify how recovered cylinders are currently handled. This is an initial proof mechanism only. It does not count as regulated recovery authority, guaranteed feedstock, or a scale moat; no recovery occurs through the family company unless the exact undertaking and technician hold the required certificates.

The founder remains employed during paid proof, limits work to at most five hours per day, and hires certified parties for every regulated physical step. The business is a poor fit if it requires the founder to perform recovery, drive hazardous loads, operate a depot, or continuously broker one-off lots by hand.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first paying customer is a Polish certified commercial-refrigeration service contractor or specialist refrigerant distributor maintaining an installed base that cannot be economically replaced immediately. Its desired outcome is not an ESG report. It needs a legal, correctly labelled, specification-conforming reclaimed refrigerant lot, delivered when a repair is scheduled, so its customer's cold room, food retail cabinet, warehouse, or process refrigeration does not remain offline.

The customer is trying to avoid four losses: emergency equipment replacement, spoilage or downtime, an unavailable or volatile service-gas supply, and compliance exposure from an uncertain cylinder. Its current workarounds are buying available virgin or reclaimed material from incumbent distributors, reusing recycled gas only within the legally permitted recovery chain, cannibalising customer-owned gas, accelerating a full system conversion, or declining work. ReclaimGas is relevant only where a documented reclaimed lot is cheaper and faster than forced replacement and where the buyer will reserve it with cash.

The source-side counterparty is a certified refrigeration/HVAC contractor, decommissioning company, equipment remanufacturer, or multi-branch service group that accumulates recovered refrigerant. Its desired outcome is a predictable, auditable route that removes compliant cylinders, pays an indexed share for usable material, and eliminates the effort of finding a reclamation buyer for every small lot. A source is not a customer unless it pays a service fee; the base model treats it as a supplier.

## Product Wedge

The first product is one prepaid, fully traceable lot of reclaimed R404A or R507A for existing commercial-refrigeration equipment:

1. A certified technician recovers one declared refrigerant grade into dedicated refillable recovery cylinders and records equipment origin, grade, kilograms, date, technician, and cylinder identifier.
2. ReclaimGas holds a conditional first-purchase and routing right but does not physically recover or decant the gas.
3. An authorised transporter moves the cylinders directly to a registered reclamation undertaking; no unapproved storage occurs at the founder's home or office.
4. The reclaimer assays the feed, reclaims acceptable material to the applicable specification, rejects or destroys unsuitable material, and applies the required reclaimed-gas label and documentation.
5. A certified buyer takes the finished lot under a prepaid, specification-contingent order. Refillables remain in a documented return loop.

The wedge is the controlled, contracted flow and finished labelled inventory, not consulting, a compliance dashboard, a lead marketplace, or ownership of a reclamation plant.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The overlooked unit of supply is not "used refrigerant" in general. It is a separated cylinder with one declared grade, known equipment origin, valid recovery records, predictable contamination risk, and a buyer-ready route. Small contractors may each produce too little material or too much variability for efficient reclamation, while large distributors prefer standard stock and may not organise every fragmented recovery source. Aggregating only specification-compatible cylinders can turn an awkward disposal or dormant asset into usable service inventory.

The economic hypothesis is that source owners will accept an indexed revenue share plus reliable collection, reclamation facilities will quote reserved toll capacity, and certified buyers will prepay because a traceable reclaimed lot avoids a more expensive or slower retrofit. The company must prove all three sides in one transaction. Regulatory urgency alone is not willingness to pay.

## Underused Momentum

Official evidence:

- Regulation (EU) 2024/573 requires recovered Annex I and specified Annex II gases to be recycled or reclaimed before they are used to fill equipment. It also requires equipment operators to arrange recovery by appropriately certified people and then recycling, reclamation, or destruction.
- From 1 January 2025, the Regulation prohibits HFCs with GWP of at least 2,500 for servicing refrigeration equipment, subject to a temporary exception for correctly labelled reclaimed gas used in existing equipment until 1 January 2030. From 1 January 2026, a parallel rule applies to air-conditioning and heat pumps, with a reclaimed-gas exception until 1 January 2032. From 1 January 2032, the rule broadens to gases with GWP of at least 750 in existing stationary refrigeration, excluding chillers, while correctly labelled reclaimed material remains an expressly listed exception.
- Annex VII reduces the maximum HFC quantity placed on the EU market from 42.87 million tonnes CO2e in 2025-2026 to 21.67 million in 2027-2029 and 9.13 million in 2030-2032. This establishes supply pressure but does not prove any particular kilogram price.
- European Commission guidance says a bulk-gas distributor must avoid emissions, retain evidence of refillable-container return arrangements, and sell only to appropriately certified undertakings. It also says reclamation undertakings must register in the F-gas Portal, and report above specified thresholds.
- Poland's Ministry of Climate states that entities conducting recovery, recycling, reclamation, or destruction have national BDS reporting obligations. UDT identifies certification requirements for recovery work.
- The EEA reported that reclaimed HFCs represented 3% of total EU HFC supply in its 2021 publication. This is historical timing evidence that reclamation exists but remained a minority flow; it is not a current market-size estimate.

Hypothesis: the combination of falling quota, service restrictions, installed equipment that cannot all be replaced immediately, and fragmented recovered cylinders creates a profitable aggregation window. The size, duration, and grade mix of that window still require primary market validation.

## Beachhead Market

The beachhead is Polish certified commercial-refrigeration contractors servicing existing food retail, cold-room, food-processing, and small cold-logistics equipment that still uses R404A or R507A and can legally use labelled reclaimed gas. The initial source zone is Wielkopolskie, Lodzkie, and Mazowieckie, close enough for route testing and connected to the founder's initial installer access. The demand side is Poland-wide because finished cylinders can be delivered through authorised logistics.

The company deliberately excludes residential consumers, direct DIY sales, mixed unknown cylinders, refrigerant recovered without a certified chain, imported virgin HFC quota trading, and any application whose legal service window or technical specification is unclear.

After proving the high-urgency grade, expansion moves into other legally usable reclaimed HFC grades and existing stationary-refrigeration use cases, then into Czechia, Slovakia, Lithuania, and Germany. Expansion must follow written legal review for each grade, application, and country.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

ReclaimGas earns the spread between finished reclaimed-gas sales and the complete landed cost of recovered feedstock, yield loss, reclamation, assay, cylinders, compliant transport, insurance, rejects, and source revenue share. Larger buyers may also pay a reservation fee for take-or-pay seasonal inventory. The company does not assume revenue from environmental certificates or carbon credits.

Illustrative first-lot economics — all values are hypotheses to be replaced by written quotes:

|---|---:|
| Acceptable reclaimed output | 85 kg |
| Finished sale price | EUR 50/kg |
| Revenue | EUR 4,250 |
| Source payment / option exercise | EUR 500 |
| Refillable cylinders and compliant transport | EUR 700 |
| Assay and toll reclamation | EUR 1,400 |
| Reject handling, insurance, documentation, and loss | EUR 350 |
| Total direct cost | EUR 2,950 |

The first paid-proof order requires at least PLN 15,000 prepaid against a specification-contingent invoice. At the illustrative exchange assumption of EUR 1 = PLN 4.3, this covers most direct lot cost before ReclaimGas commits to processing. The order is refunded if the reclaimer cannot produce the contracted specification; it is not treated as revenue until delivery.

At maturity, the model assumes EUR 45-50/kg blended revenue, 28-32% gross margin, and 15-19% operating margin. These are not market facts. They depend on contracted source price, at least 80-85% reclaim yield, dense cylinder routes, prepaid or short-dated receivables, low reject rates, and no need to build a reclamation plant. The business is rejected if the first three paid lots do not show at least 25% gross margin after every physical and compliance cost.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First 10 buyers:

1. Build a named list from public UDT certification records, refrigeration-industry directories, commercial cold-service searches, and equipment-service references.
2. Prioritise contractors that publicly service supermarkets, cold rooms, food processors, or refrigerated logistics rather than general residential installers.
3. Contact the service manager or owner with a lot-specific offer: grade, expected kilograms, reclaimer, documentation pack, delivery window, price, and refund condition.
4. Ask for a small paid reservation or specification-contingent preorder, not a survey response or letter of intent.
5. Convert the first buyer into a quarterly reservation account only after delivery and cylinder return work.

The family installation network can provide the first five to ten introductions to certified contractors and help the founder learn cylinder handling and decommissioning events. At least eight of the first ten buyers must come from independent outbound, distributor referrals, reclaimer referrals, or trade directories so family access is not mistaken for a scalable channel.

First 100 buyers:

- Use completed-lot documentation and delivery reliability to sell branch-level quarterly allocations to contractor groups.
- Offer an API-free inventory feed and fixed reservation calendar to buyers before building software.
- Add reclaimer and cylinder-logistics referrals where permitted, but never rely on one reclaimer for demand.
- Expand by refrigerant/application playbook and country, with a separate legal memo and source contract for each.
- Target 100 buying branches across Poland and nearby CEE, with no single buyer above 15% of revenue.

The acquisition hypothesis is event-triggered: buyers pay when they have a service backlog, annual maintenance season, known installed-base exposure, or a pending retrofit delay. Generic sustainability outreach is not the channel.

## Primary Control Point

The initial control point is a 24-month, multi-branch exclusive first-purchase and routing agreement covering at least 2,500 kg per year of one declared HFC grade. It must specify:

- dedicated, uniquely identified refillable cylinders and no cross-grade mixing;
- equipment/source record, technician certificate reference, date, gross/net kilograms, and custody events;
- minimum annual kilograms across at least three branches;
- an indexed source payment or transparent revenue share;
- ReclaimGas's first-purchase or exclusive routing right before the material is offered elsewhere;
- quality exclusions, reject responsibility, permitted transport and storage, audit rights, and termination remedies;
- lawful transfer of the finished reclaimed output and a documented route for any feed that legally remains waste;
- no at-will termination after ReclaimGas pays for cylinder deployment or source onboarding.

Within 12 months, this right is paired with a written toll-reclamation reservation of at least 500 kg per month and at least one buyer's annual take-or-pay or prepaid reservation for 1,000 kg. The rights, not the family relationship, are the foundational resource.

This control point is still vulnerable to incumbent distributors bidding up the source at renewal. Its defensibility therefore depends on accumulating many small, staggered, multi-year source rights, lower collection cost from route density, trusted yield history, and matching buyer reservations—not on one supplier contract.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

The cold start is solved by sequencing one exact source lot and one exact buyer before building inventory:

1. Obtain a Polish legal/compliance memo covering ownership, waste status, brokerage/distribution, transport, cylinder return, insurance, labelling, BDS, F-gas Portal, and invoicing. ReclaimGas does not touch a cylinder until the lawful role is documented.
2. Use the family company only to identify certified contractors with upcoming decommissionings or cylinder backlogs. Confirm certificates independently.
3. Sign conditional source options covering at least 300 kg of one declared grade. The source retains regulated handling responsibility until the authorised chain accepts the cylinder.
4. Secure a fixed written assay, reject, toll-reclamation, and turnaround quote from a registered reclaimer, plus a compliant transporter/cylinder quote.
5. Show the documented proposed lot to at least 40 certified buyers and require at least one PLN 15,000 prepaid, specification-contingent order by day 180.
6. Process and deliver the first 100-150 kg feed lot only after the deposit covers most direct cost.
7. After successful delivery and plausible positive contribution margin, release the next founder-capital tranche to sign the 2,500 kg/year multi-branch source right, deploy dedicated cylinders, and reserve monthly toll capacity.

Why an unknown founder can attempt this: the first source is offered a documented removal route and indexed upside without buying equipment; the reclaimer receives paid feed and a reserved-capacity customer; the buyer sees a specific refundable lot rather than an unproven marketplace. No party is asked to believe in the founder's status. Each is asked to sign a narrow commercial contract whose value can be measured in kilograms, price, yield, turnaround, and cash.

Proof before serious capital: signed source options, certificate checks, the legal memo, a fixed reclaimer quote, a prepaid buyer order, assay/yield output, a labelled delivered lot, cylinder return evidence, and a first-lot contribution statement. If those do not exist, no inventory platform, depot, plant, or foreign expansion is funded.

## Distribution Advantage

Demand distribution begins with public certification and installed-base signals, not social media or a founder brand. Every sale creates a reusable grade/application objection log, price/urgency history, and timing signal for maintenance seasons. Supply distribution begins with multi-branch contractors and decommissioning events; dedicated cylinders make repeat recovery easier than restarting the paperwork for each lot.

The compounding distribution advantage is a two-sided reservation calendar backed by controlled supply: source branches know which dedicated cylinder and pickup slot to use, while buyers reserve upcoming kilograms before the finished lot exists. Route density lowers collection cost, yield history makes source pricing more accurate, and delivered-lot documentation reduces buyer diligence. None of this is defensible after one transaction; it becomes meaningful only across dozens of contracted branches and repeat buyers.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Incumbent refrigerant distributors already have trust, cylinders, logistics, and buyers. ReclaimGas does not win by claiming they ignore reclamation. It wins only if it can acquire fragmented single-grade feed at lower all-in collection cost and turn it faster through reserved reclamation capacity than a broad distributor finds worthwhile.

The first expansion path is horizontal across source branches for the same grade, then across legally usable grades, then across neighbouring CEE jurisdictions. The second is vertical: dedicated cylinder fleet, cross-reclaimer quality scoring, demand reservations, and eventually regional consolidation operated by licensed third parties. The third is data-driven: cylinder-level contamination and yield histories support better source pricing and rejection prevention than a spot trader can offer.

The company does not build a reclamation plant until multiple independent source portfolios and buyers demonstrate throughput sufficient to keep one utilised. It may never need to own a plant. Owning fragmented contract rights and transaction flow is preferable to premature equipment ownership.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

EU law supplies the common regulatory frame, but Poland provides the initial operating advantage: lower-cost route testing, a geographically reachable CEE source base, UDT certification records, national BDS processes that can be encoded into a standard operating procedure, and the founder's limited installer access for the first learning loop. The offer is adapted to Polish contracts, certified-party verification, cylinder logistics, BDS records, and buyer payment practice.

This is not a legal barrier against foreign entrants. A large distributor can enter or copy the workflow. The local defense must be a dense contracted source network with low pickup cost, Polish-language operating records, known cylinder yield by supplier, and prepaid local buyers. The company expands out of Poland only after this density works; a superficial EU-wide marketplace would destroy the advantage.

## Compounding Advantage

Each controlled kilogram can create five reusable assets:

1. A multi-year source right and a more accurate estimate of that branch's annual recoverable kilograms.
2. Cylinder-level contamination, composition, and reclaim-yield history.
3. A certified buyer's grade, timing, price, and reservation behaviour.
4. A proven transport/reclaimer route with actual turnaround and reject economics.
5. A compliance and chain-of-custody record that lowers diligence on the next lot.

Over time, many staggered source contracts plus buyer take-or-pay reservations reduce spot-price exposure and working-capital demand. The niche can compound only if repeat supply and repeat buying replace manual one-off brokering. A database without contractual flow is not a moat.

## Founder Wealth Model

All three scenarios are hypotheses, use EUR 1 = PLN 4.3 for rough planning, and value a profitable going concern rather than assuming an acquisition. Revenue is kilograms of finished reclaimed refrigerant sold multiplied by blended realised price. Retained earnings are not added separately to business value. Net liabilities are deducted before applying founder ownership. Personal distributions during employment are capped at 5% of after-tax cash available for distribution and are excluded from the stated founder-net-worth totals, making the totals equity-only and avoiding double counting.

### Conservative scale case — year 15

- **Customers/volume:** 100 buying branches, no buyer above 15% of revenue; 500,000 kg/year finished output from roughly 140 contracted source branches across Poland and nearby CEE.
- **Pricing/revenue:** EUR 50/kg blended realised price; EUR 25.0 million annual revenue.
- **Margins:** 28% gross margin and 15% operating margin; EUR 7.0 million gross profit and EUR 3.75 million operating profit.
- **Capital:** approximately EUR 5 million cumulative growth capital, principally retained cash plus cylinder, inventory, receivables, and equipment lines; EUR 3 million net liabilities at the valuation date. Original founder capital never exceeds PLN 100,000 before institutional finance is considered.
- **Ownership/dilution:** founder retains 70% after 30% cumulative employee, technical-partner, and growth-capital dilution.
- **Reinvestment:** at least 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution is reinvested while the founder remains employed; optional founder cash withdrawals stay at or below 5% and are not counted here.
- **Value:** 5.0 times EUR 3.75 million operating profit gives EUR 18.75 million enterprise value; less EUR 3 million net liabilities gives EUR 15.75 million equity value. Founder equity is approximately EUR 11.0 million.
- **Timing/milestones:** paid lot by month 6; 2,500 kg/year source right by month 12; 20 tonnes/approximately EUR 1 million revenue by year 3; 100 tonnes/EUR 5 million by year 6; 250 tonnes/EUR 12.5 million by year 10; 500 tonnes by year 15.

### Expected-success scale case — year 12

- **Customers/volume:** 180 buying branches and distributors; 1.2 million kg/year finished output across Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Lithuania, and Germany.
- **Pricing/revenue:** EUR 48/kg blended realised price; EUR 57.6 million annual revenue.
- **Margins:** 30% gross margin and 17% operating margin; EUR 17.28 million gross profit and EUR 9.79 million operating profit.
- **Capital:** approximately EUR 12 million cumulative growth capital from retained cash, supplier/customer working-capital structures, asset finance, and debt; EUR 8 million net liabilities at valuation.
- **Ownership/dilution:** founder retains 65% after 35% cumulative dilution for team and later growth capital.
- **Reinvestment:** 95% of after-tax distributable cash is reinvested while employed; optional founder distributions remain at or below 5% and are excluded from the value below.
- **Value:** 6.0 times EUR 9.79 million operating profit gives approximately EUR 58.75 million enterprise value; less EUR 8 million net liabilities gives EUR 50.75 million equity value. Founder equity is approximately EUR 33.0 million.
- **Timing/milestones:** first repeat buyer and two independent sources by year 1; 50 tonnes/year by year 3; 250 tonnes by year 6; 700 tonnes by year 9; 1.2 million kg by year 12.

### Strong-success scale case — year 9

- **Customers/volume:** 300 buying branches and distributors; 2.5 million kg/year finished output across a wider EU source and buyer network, with no single country above 35% of revenue.
- **Pricing/revenue:** EUR 45/kg blended realised price; EUR 112.5 million annual revenue.
- **Margins:** 32% gross margin and 19% operating margin; EUR 36.0 million gross profit and EUR 21.38 million operating profit.
- **Capital:** approximately EUR 28 million cumulative growth capital for cylinder pools, hubs, inventory, compliance teams, and selective processing capacity; EUR 18 million net liabilities at valuation.
- **Ownership/dilution:** founder retains 55% after 45% cumulative dilution for management, technical leadership, and growth capital.
- **Reinvestment:** 100% of after-tax cash available for distribution is reinvested while the founder remains employed; no employment-period distribution is assumed.
- **Value:** 6.5 times EUR 21.38 million operating profit gives approximately EUR 138.94 million enterprise value; less EUR 18 million net liabilities gives EUR 120.94 million equity value. Founder equity is approximately EUR 66.5 million.

The conservative case reaches the founder-wealth target only narrowly. Before treating the model as credible, independent market sizing must show that 500 tonnes/year of legally usable reclaimed grades is attainable without requiring more than 10% of the realistically reachable CEE/EU flow, and first-lot evidence must support the margin. Otherwise the wealth thesis is rejected.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

The order of reinvestment is controlled supply before software, then working-capital efficiency before owned processing:

1. Dedicated refillable cylinders and supplier onboarding that increase contracted homogeneous kilograms.
2. Reserved toll-reclamation capacity and a second reclaimer to reduce a single-point dependency.
3. Buyer deposits, take-or-pay reservations, and faster cylinder returns that shorten the cash cycle.
4. Route density, supplier quality controls, and assay/yield data that lift gross profit per collected kilogram.
5. Compliance and operations hires that let throughput grow without founder labour scaling one-for-one.
6. Only after sustained utilisation: licensed consolidation hubs or selective reclamation equipment with project finance.

In conservative and expected cases, 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution is reinvested while the founder remains in corporate employment, with no more than 5% optionally distributed. The strong case reinvests 100% during employment. After the full-time transition, the board may set distributions, but the wealth model above uses equity value only and does not add retained cash twice.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

The paid-proof target is not a free audit or memorandum of understanding. By day 180, one unrelated certified buyer must transfer at least PLN 15,000 against a specification-contingent order for the first 100-150 kg recovered-feed lot, after ReclaimGas has a signed source option and fixed reclaimer/logistics quotes.

### Days 1-30

- Commission the Polish legal/compliance memo and map every party's role.
- Verify at least two reclamation undertakings, two transport/cylinder providers, and the relevant registration/certification status.
- Define one accepted grade, source record, cylinder protocol, assay/reclaim specification, rejection rule, and buyer document pack.
- Build a list of 40 qualified buyers and 30 potential source branches.

### Days 31-75

- Use family access for learning and at most the first source introductions; pursue independent sources in parallel.
- Sign conditional source options covering at least 300 kg of one declared grade across at least two independent certified undertakings.
- Obtain fixed written quotes for cylinder rental, direct transport, assay, reject treatment, reclamation, labelling, and turnaround.
- Obtain a reclaimer pre-acceptance decision on a sample before promising finished inventory.

### Days 76-135

- Present the lot-specific pack to 40 certified buyers.
- Seek one PLN 15,000 specification-contingent prepayment plus two written backup orders; do not count non-binding interest.
- Reject any buyer expecting undocumented gas, open-ended credit, or a price that leaves less than 25% expected gross margin.

### Days 136-180

- After prepayment, move the first cylinders directly through the authorised chain, reclaim, label, deliver, and document return.
- Produce the first-lot contribution statement including source payment, yield, reject cost, transport, cylinders, insurance, reclaimer, refund risk, and founder hours.
- Paid proof exists only when money has arrived from an unrelated buyer. Successful delivery and positive contribution margin are separate conditions for releasing the next tranche.

Founder time is capped at roughly 15-20 hours per week during proof. Physical recovery, transport, reclamation, and regulated technical decisions remain with certified contractors. The founder does commercial coordination, data controls, contracts, and buyer acquisition alongside employment.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder does not leave corporate employment until all of the following are true:

- at least five independent paying buyers and no buyer above 25% of trailing revenue;
- at least two contracted source groups covering 12 months of expected throughput;
- at least two qualified reclamation routes;
- six months of completed lots with at least 25% gross margin and positive contribution after founder-replacement labour;
- repeat or take-or-pay demand covering at least six months;
- a documented acquisition channel with measurable conversion and payback;
- no unresolved legal/waste/certification dependency;
- at least 12 months of personal runway outside the founder's original business capital.

Until then, a paid part-time operations contractor handles weekday logistics exceptions and certified parties perform all technical work.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

If supply trading fails before inventory is purchased, reusable assets are the legal chain map, certified-party database, grade-specific source protocol, cylinder/transport quotes, buyer interviews, and source-quality schema. They can support a narrower sourcing service for an existing licensed reclaimer only if it is genuinely prepaid and does not become generic compliance consulting.

If the first lot delivers but multi-branch rights fail, the founder can complete contracted orders and stop rather than building a spot marketplace. Dedicated cylinders are rented or returnable where possible. Contracts must prevent take-or-pay exposure beyond buyer-backed quantities. The founder does not buy a depot or vehicle, so failure does not strand major fixed assets.

If high-GWP demand sunsets faster than expected, the company can apply the verified recovery-routing workflow to other legally usable reclaimed grades only after new buyer prepayments. It may not claim a broad circular-gas platform without proving each grade's regulation, yield, price, and demand.

## Critical Evidence

- Regulation (EU) 2024/573 establishes recovery, certification, service-use, labelling, reporting, refillable-container, and HFC quota rules. It expressly distinguishes recovery, recycling, and reclamation and prohibits using merely recovered gas to refill equipment. It is the binding base text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj/eng
- Article 13 of that Regulation contains the 2025 refrigeration, 2026 air-conditioning/heat-pump, and 2032 stationary-refrigeration service restrictions and reclaimed-gas exceptions: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/2024-02-20/eng/
- The European Commission's bulk-F-gas guidance lists distributor duties, reclamation-undertaking registration/reporting, and the lack of an Article 26 registration/reporting obligation for a distributor as such. The Commission warns that its guidance is not a substitute for the Regulation: https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases/stakeholder-obligations/f-gases-bulk_en
- UDT's Polish F-gas guidance confirms national certification administration and that regulated recovery work requires the relevant certified personnel/undertaking: https://www.udt.gov.pl/rozwiazania-dla-sektorow/srodowisko/o-f-gazach-i-szwo
- Poland's Ministry of Climate states that entities conducting recovery, recycling, reclamation, or destruction report through BDS: https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/wymogi-sprawozdawcze-dotyczace-fgazow-oraz-substancji-zubozajacych-warstwe-ozonowa-za-rok-2025
- The EEA's 2021 report states that reclaimed HFCs then represented about 3% of total EU HFC supply; this is dated context, not a 2026 market size: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/publications/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases-2021

## Critical Hypotheses

1. At least two independent Polish certified undertakings will option 300 kg of one declared grade within 75 days and one multi-branch group will later contract at least 2,500 kg/year for 24 months.
