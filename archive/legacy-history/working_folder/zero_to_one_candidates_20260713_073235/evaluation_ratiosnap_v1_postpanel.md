# RatioSnap Advisory Evaluation After Independent Panel

## Diagnosis

The exact candidate received 60 and 54. The objections are not a request for better explanation: both independent judges failed the Founder Wealth Gate on the same 5,000–6,000-installation/recurring-use premise, and both found that ordinary dual-scale or OEM accessories can remove the proprietary cassette economics. The prior advisory `pursue` decision over-weighted one supplier's 10,000-machine statement; that evidence establishes a broad equipment category, not the valuable manual-check subset. The current shape is a reachable experiment but not a resilient founder-wealth company.

## Evidence and Assumptions

The panel preserved the candidate's strongest evidence: current OEM procedures do use manual last-point checks, and some manuals suggest daily/shift checks. It added adverse evidence that DOPAG already offers automatic ratio checking with scales on some systems and that ratio caps/blocks are already controlled by equipment vendors. No judge found a technical impossibility. Both found the customer ROI, manual-check subset, proprietary consumable, global channel and conservative penetration assumptions unevidenced.

## Gates

- **Founder Wealth:** fail. At the candidate's own minimum 25,000-cell denominator, 6,000 active docks means 24% adoption, while 350 paid cassettes/year, 24% operating margin and 68% ownership remain unproved.
- **Reachability:** pass/narrow pass. A 28,000 PLN benign-fluid prototype and two prepaid pilots are attemptable while employed.
- **Control:** current Tier 3, potential Tier 2. No scarce right, channel, data source or transaction flow; OEM/reusable-cup substitution is routine.

## Shared Fatal Objection

Founder wealth depends on a high recurring-use installed base in an unevidenced “hollow middle” between cheap manual checking and high-value inline/OEM assurance. Two judges reached this independently, so the business shape must be abandoned rather than cosmetically repriced or reworded.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Manufacturer-prescribed last-point checks can be attractive product wedges when the physical evidence event controls a sufficiently valuable recurring transaction—not merely a convenience consumable.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Do not invoke the pivoter within 2K ratio-check accessories, dual-scale fixtures, reusable cups or OEM monitoring. A future candidate may reuse the last-point-evidence principle only with a different buyer/economic event and a materially stronger control point.
