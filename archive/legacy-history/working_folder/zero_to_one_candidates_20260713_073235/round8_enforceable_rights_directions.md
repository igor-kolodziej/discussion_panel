## Direction 1 — Peppol Relay PL

**Generation decision: retain conditionally as a finalist direction.**

### Customer Point Of View And Commercial Insight

The initial customer is a 50–500 employee Polish exporter or cross-border supplier whose finance team can send a domestic invoice through KSeF but still coordinates foreign purchase orders, dispatch advice, invoice status, and counterpart-specific formats by email, portals, or one-off EDI integrations. The outcome it wants is not “e-invoicing compliance.” It wants one dependable rail to exchange the commercial document chain with European counterparties, prove delivery, and stop rebuilding mappings for each buyer.

KSeF creates mass domestic digitisation, but it does not make ordinary cross-border counterparties join one interoperable order-to-cash network. Peppol does. The neglected wedge is therefore a Polish/CEE-focused access point for mid-market exporters, sold as reliable cross-border transaction routing and document operations, not as a generic KSeF connector.

### Present Resource That Controls The Event

The foundational resource is a signed Peppol Service Provider Agreement, production PKI certificate, certified Access Point status, optional SMP capability, assigned participant identifiers, and explicit customer authority to publish/reroute their Peppol endpoints. Once a customer grants that authority, inbound and outbound Peppol documents for its published identifiers pass through the provider. This is a present transaction-routing control point, not a promise that data or workflow gravity may emerge later.

OpenPeppol's official onboarding sequence is concrete: membership, the Service Provider Agreement with the relevant Peppol Authority, test PKI certificate, AS4 implementation, mandatory testbed, production PKI certificate, and listing as a certified provider. Open-source, commercial, and hosted AS4 implementations are permitted. The current small-company AP+SMP fees are €1,800 sign-up, €2,750 annual membership, and €2,500 certification; AP-only fees are lower. Official sources:

- [OpenPeppol Access Point setup guide](https://peppol.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/how_to_set-up_a__post-award__peppol_access_point_v2.0.pdf)
- [OpenPeppol membership and certification fees](https://peppol.org/join/fees/)
- [Polish Peppol Authority and certified providers](https://efaktura.gov.pl/openpeppol/)

The Polish authority currently lists five Poland-based certified providers: All For One Poland, Anachron Technology Poland, Comarch, EDITEL Polska, and Infinite. This is real competition, but not a market with hundreds of indistinguishable Polish issuers.

### Underused Momentum And Counted Denominator

Poland has made KSeF receipt mandatory from 1 February 2026 and is phasing mandatory issuance through 2026–2027. The official KSeF material also allows a taxpayer to authorise a registered Peppol provider. This gives finance teams a current reason to revisit document architecture, even though Peppol itself is not required for ordinary KSeF use:

- [Official KSeF implementation stages](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/etapy-wdrozenia-ksef/)
- [Official scope of mandatory KSeF](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/informacje-ogolne-ksef-20/zakres-obowiazkowego-ksef/)

The broad official denominator is the EU's roughly 31 million enterprises. That is not an eligible-customer count and must not be used as the sales denominator. The decision-critical next research step is a bottom-up count of Polish and CEE mid-market exporters/importers whose counterparties already accept Peppol order and invoice messages. [Eurostat enterprise population](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/wdn-20240719-1)

### Cold Start And 180-Day Paid Proof

The founder does not build the complete network first. The initial acquisition mechanism is an outbound “counterparty route audit” using public exporter directories, trade-fair exhibitor lists, and company procurement pages:

1. Select 150 Polish exporters in two document-heavy verticals, initially industrial components and contract manufacturing.
2. For each account, identify one named European buyer or supplier already using Peppol, and show the exporter the actual document route that could replace its portal/email handoff.
3. Sell a 60-day paid discovery-and-testbed engagement: counterpart route map, document-field mapping, test messages, exception log, and a reserved production migration slot once certification is complete.
4. Ask for 4,000 PLN prepaid from each of three unrelated customers. The payment is for delivered integration discovery and testbed work, not a vague letter of intent. Production routing is conditional on certification.

The founder can deliver this nights/weekends with one integration contractor. The paid-proof budget ceiling is 38,000 PLN: approximately 19,000–20,000 PLN for official AP+SMP fees at current published euro prices, up to 8,000 PLN for hosted AS4/cloud/security tooling, 6,000 PLN for contractor help, and 4,000 PLN for legal/accounting and outbound tests. Spending stops before certification if fewer than three independent customers prepay or no prospect provides a real counterparty/document sample.

### Twelve-Month Resource Bootstrap

The founder secures the control point in stages:

- **Days 1–30:** obtain written confirmation from the Polish Peppol Authority of the applicable agreement, test scope, evidence requirements, and expected onboarding timetable; choose a hosted or open-source AS4 base.
- **Days 31–90:** join OpenPeppol, obtain the test certificate, implement the smallest compliant AP/SMP stack, and complete customer-specific test mappings.
- **Days 91–180:** complete mandatory testbed work and the production-readiness evidence while delivering the paid discovery engagements.
- **Months 7–12:** obtain production PKI/certified status, publish the first authorised customer endpoints, and route live documents.

The initial resource budget remains below 100,000 PLN even with a 25,000–35,000 PLN second contractor/security tranche after paid proof. Proof before serious capital consists of the signed provider paperwork, test and production certificates, public provider listing, three prepaid customers, successful test messages, and at least one live customer-authorised route.

**Immediate kill condition:** reject this direction if the Polish Peppol Authority will not provide a written onboarding path compatible with a 12-month/100,000 PLN cap, if production access requires an unbudgeted audit or guarantee, or if prospects only value a generic KSeF connector that the free KSeF API or accounting suites already provide.

### Competitive Advantage, Copy Risk, And Expansion

The certificate alone is copyable by a sufficiently motivated software provider. The compounding niche must be the combination of customer-authorised endpoint control, reusable Polish/CEE-to-European document mappings, counterparty activation history, exception-resolution data, and embedded operating procedures in finance teams. Each activated counterparty makes the next customer in that corridor cheaper to onboard.

The strongest copy risk is an existing certified AP or ERP/accounting vendor launching the same exporter package. The only credible defence is speed in two narrow industrial corridors, materially better activation time, transparent exception ownership, and customer-permitted migration tooling. Public procurement is deliberately excluded from the wedge because Poland already provides a free PEF broker and public-procurement dependence would weaken both pricing and reachability.

Expansion sequence: Polish industrial exporters → Czech/Slovak and Baltic corridors → order/dispatch/invoice bundles → supplier onboarding and payment-status services → adjacent EU corridors. Expansion should use the same Peppol membership and accumulated maps, not reset the core resource country by country.

### Facts Required Before Advisory Evaluation

1. Written Polish Peppol Authority timeline, applicant prerequisites, security evidence, and total unavoidable cost.
2. Ten interviews with finance/EDI owners at 50–500 employee exporters, including actual counterpart and document examples.
3. At least three prospects willing to prepay the discovery/testbed offer.
4. A bottom-up eligible-account denominator for the first two corridors, not the 31 million-enterprise headline.
5. Competitive quotations from at least three listed providers to prove the wedge can command €4,800 annual revenue without becoming bespoke consulting.

## Direction 2 — Certified eFTI Platform For Cross-Border Freight Evidence

**Generation decision: reject for this run.**

The attractive control point is certification as an eFTI platform through which transport operators provide regulatory freight information. From 9 July 2027, EU authorities must accept electronically supplied information when operators use certified eFTI platforms. That could create a recurring freight-document rail rather than a dashboard. [European Commission eFTI overview](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-themes/logistics-and-multimodal-transport/efti-regulation_en)

The approval resource is not yet decision-ready. The Commission's official timeline says the remaining detailed specifications for platforms, service providers, and certification are planned only by December 2026. In July 2026, an unknown founder cannot yet obtain a binding certification quotation, identify the final evidence burden, or prove issuance inside the 12-month/100,000 PLN cap. A paid pilot would therefore sell work against an incompletely specified future approval, and large transport-management providers can prepare in parallel.

The conservative wealth case also lacks an official count of addressable freight operators or controlled shipments that would pay a new platform rather than use an existing TMS. Any €10m path would currently depend on invented penetration and pricing. Reject now; reconsider only after final certification rules, named certifiers, and quoted cost/timing exist.

## Direction 3 — ISPM 15 Export-Packaging Marking Operator

**Generation decision: reject.**

Poland's plant-health authority authorises registered operators to apply the IPPC/ISPM 15 mark to compliant wood packaging for exports to countries that require it. The assigned plant number and mark are real; authorisation includes an inspection before assignment and annual supervision. The Polish procedure can permit an operator to make packaging from wood treated by another registered entity, so a founder could avoid buying a kiln. The administrative decision fee is only 10 PLN. [PIORiN wood-packaging rules, procedure, and current authorised-operator list](https://www.gov.pl/web/piorin/opakowania-drewniane)

This is obtainable under the resource cap, but it does not support the required company. The mark is nonexclusive, the official list already contains many authorised plants, ordinary intra-EU packaging is generally outside the requirement, and the underlying product is a bulky, price-compared crate or pallet. Buying treated wood avoids kiln capital but not workshop space, inventory, transport, labour, and working capital.

A conservative path to €10m founder wealth would require industrial-scale unit volume and facilities rather than a high-margin compounding control point. Even an illustrative 500,000 units a year at 40 PLN contribution creates 20m PLN contribution before facilities, logistics, staff, breakage, and working capital; reaching that scale from nights-and-weekends custom packaging is the heroic dependency. The assigned mark therefore controls compliance on each unit but does not create sufficient scarcity or economics. Reject rather than disguise a physical packaging company as a licensing business.

## Direction 4 — UN-Approved P911 Kit For Damaged Lithium Batteries

**Generation decision: reject.**

ADR packing instruction P911 covers damaged or defective lithium cells and batteries liable to rapid disassembly, dangerous reaction, flame, dangerous heat, or toxic gas. The packaging must prevent external flame, maintain structural integrity, limit external surface temperature, manage gas, and use a verification method accepted by the competent authority. A UN-approved packaging design and manufacturer's marking right would directly control shipment of a dangerous recurring event. Primary sources:

- [UNECE ADR 2025 publication](https://unece.org/info/Transport/pub/395786)
- [Official Polish ADR 2025 text](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/b7292c03-ac9d-4f80-9a29-c22f53d847b8)
- [TDT approval and UN-marking process for dangerous-goods packaging](https://www.tdt.gov.pl/dzialalnosc/dzialalnosc-dozorowa/urzadzenia-podlegajace-dozorowi-technicznemu/urzadzenia-do-transportu-towarow-niebezpiecznych/duze-pojemniki-do-przewozu-luzem-dppl/)

The underlying battery stock is large—Eurostat reports 231,000 tonnes of portable batteries placed on the EU market in 2023, and the EU had 5.87 million battery-only electric cars at the end of 2024—but those figures are not a denominator for P911 shipments. [Eurostat portable-battery statistics](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/66807.pdf) and [Eurostat battery-electric car stock](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250730-1)

The fatal issue is that the resource is still a speculative safety-performance project. Public sources do not provide a decision-ready quotation for P911 thermal-event verification, competent-authority acceptance, destructive prototypes, product liability, or production quality evidence inside 100,000 PLN and 12 months. The founder cannot sell the approved unit before proving the hard technical result, and a carrier or receiver may still refuse it. A conservative founder-wealth case would require tens of thousands of high-margin incident kits each year without an officially counted incident/shipment denominator. Reject until a qualified laboratory and TDT provide a written fixed-scope test-and-approval path below the cap and paid buyers precommit against that exact design.

## Direction 5 — GLEIF-Accredited LEI Issuer

**Generation decision: reject.**

A Legal Entity Identifier issuer receives GLEIF accreditation, a Master Agreement, and authority to issue and renew LEIs in approved jurisdictions. The official process can be completed on paper within the 12-month window: GLEIF describes an applicant preparation phase of up to six months and a review period of up to 90 business days. The market is formally counted: on 1 July 2026, the Global LEI Index reported 3,357,672 total records, including 3,105,487 active LEIs. Sources:

- [GLEIF accreditation process](https://www.gleif.org/en/organizational-identity/become-an-lei-issuer/accreditation-process)
- [GLEIF required documents](https://www.gleif.org/en/organizational-identity/become-an-lei-issuer/required-documents)
- [GLEIF LEI statistics](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/global-lei-index/lei-statistics/)
- [GLEIF Master Agreement](https://www.gleif.org/organizational-identity/become-an-lei-issuer/required-documents/2024-06-26_ma-master-agreement-1.4.1final.pdf)

The right fails the founder-wealth gate structurally. The Master Agreement requires LOU operations to be sustainably financed on an efficient **not-for-profit cost-recovery** basis and prevents forced bundling with unrelated paid services. Therefore high-margin LEI issuance cannot be the engine for €10m founder wealth. Becoming a registration agent instead would avoid accreditation but makes the business dependent on a partner LOU, while more than 100 registration agents already exist and the LOU retains legal responsibility. [GLEIF registration-agent model](https://www.gleif.org/en/organizational-identity/registration-agents/)

This is a good example of a real, issued, event-controlling identifier with a counted market whose governing economics explicitly prevent the desired company. Reject without further work.
