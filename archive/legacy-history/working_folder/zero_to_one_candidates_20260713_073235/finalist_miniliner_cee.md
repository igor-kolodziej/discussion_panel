# Zero-to-One Candidate: MiniLiner CEE

Candidate generation only — not validated. Every uncontracted right, price, margin, customer count, performance claim, and forecast below is a hypothesis until the stated evidence is obtained.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Secure a protected Polish or compact-indoor-pipe customer-group right for an existing EU-made pipe-cleaning and epoxy-lining system, then turn small drain and plumbing contractors into a dense installed cohort whose training, spares, job recipes, and consumable reorders route through MiniLiner CEE—without MiniLiner performing or warranting their end-customer jobs.

## Founder Fit

The founder can use data-science and AI leverage to build the named-account denominator, quantify contractor job economics, manage serialized equipment and reorder data, and automate low-social-energy follow-up. Warsaw and family-business experience provide practical familiarity with small installation contractors, but the family renewable-energy company is neither a presumed buyer nor a substitute for trenchless expertise.

The fit gap is material: the founder is not a pipe-rehabilitation technician, product-compliance lawyer, or established equipment dealer. The minimum remedy is manufacturer certification, a paid contract technical demonstrator, external EU-product counsel, and a model in which the founder owns distribution and replenishment while trained contractors own site assessment and execution. The idea is killed if customers require the founder personally to supervise or certify projects.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The direct payer is the owner of a small drain-cleaning, plumbing, sanitary-installation, or property-maintenance contractor. The qualifying owner already receives small-diameter pipe problems but currently clears the blockage, replaces the pipe invasively, rents or buys a broader incumbent system, or refers the rehabilitation work away.

The desired outcome is not another lead-generation or estimating tool. It is the ability to quote and perform a higher-ticket, low-disruption pipe-renewal job with the existing van and crew, supported by one teachable configuration, dependable material replenishment, critical spares, and a known payback threshold. The feared losses are buying an underused machine, applying resin incorrectly, accepting a job outside the method's limits, waiting for a spare, or becoming responsible for a failed lining.

The tradeoff is explicit: the contractor accepts formal training, documented preparation and CCTV quality control, use of supported consumables, and responsibility for its workmanship in return for a compact launch package and local support. MiniLiner does not promise that every pipe is suitable.

## Product Wedge

Sell a fixed **MiniLiner launch pack** for one narrow use case: indoor and property-side DN32–DN150 pipe cleaning, preparation, and supported epoxy coating. The exact pack is selected only after conformity and application diligence and may combine an existing NoDig Mini6 or V1 cleaning unit, ProLight-class coating equipment, starter BSE or other documented compatible consumables, Polish instructions, manufacturer training, two local demonstration sessions, critical spares, standard inspection/preparation/application/QC recipe cards, and a twelve-month reorder-price schedule.

The first paid event is a 30% contractor deposit for a named machine configuration and training date. The deposit must cover the supplier deposit and attributable freight. The balance is collected before delivery or against documented supplier terms, so speculative inventory is not the financing model.

MiniLiner sells products, documented training, and replenishment. The contractor separately contracts with the property owner, judges project suitability, performs the work, documents acceptance, and gives the end-customer workmanship warranty. MiniLiner retains all mandatory obligations for products it distributes and cannot contract away liability for a defective product or its own misleading instruction.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The possible gap is not “trenchless equipment is unavailable in Poland.” PICOTE has an official Polish dealer with demonstration equipment, sales, and service; BRAWO SYSTEMS offers a complete small-diameter rehabilitation system; Fixaline markets a broad one-stop indoor pipe-lining catalogue. NoDig's apparent absence from its published Poland channel list therefore indicates only a possible supplier-route opening, not category whitespace.

The narrower commercial hypothesis is that a subset of small contractors is underserved by broad catalogues and larger systems: they will buy a tightly configured compact entry package when the seller converts an uncertain equipment purchase into a bounded contractor business launch with local-language recipes, job-economics proof, fast spares, and scheduled consumables. That hypothesis must be proved by deposits and repeat purchases, not interviews alone.

## Underused Momentum

- NoDig publicly invites distributors, describes protected territories, manufacturer training, spare-parts support, recurring consumables, and direct European supply. This is a current contracting route, not evidence that Poland or any customer group is still available.
- The Croatian-to-Polish route stays inside the EU single market, reducing import complexity, while MiniLiner still bears distributor diligence, Polish-language, chemical-supply, and market-surveillance obligations.
- Compact mechanical cleaning and epoxy systems let an existing contractor add a new job type without a civil-works fleet. Manufacturer claims about speed, savings, structural performance, or service life are sales claims until the exact supporting technical file is reviewed.
- The Machinery Regulation transition creates a practical documentation deadline: products placed before 20 January 2027 remain under Directive 2006/42/EC; Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies thereafter. A distributor that builds the transition pack early may remove friction, but compliance is not a moat.

## Beachhead Market

Start only with owner-operated Polish drain-cleaning and plumbing contractors that:

1. serve Warsaw–Łódź–Poznań or can attend a clustered demonstration there;
2. already own or routinely use CCTV inspection and perform at least two plausible small-pipe replacement/referral cases per month;
3. have at least one technician who can complete manufacturer training;
4. can finance a 30% deposit without MiniLiner credit;
5. accept sole responsibility and appropriate insurance for site assessment and workmanship; and
6. can show a real sample pipe or recurring lost job before receiving a proposal.

This deliberately excludes municipalities, main-sewer contractors, potable-water claims, large civil CIPP projects, consumers, and contractors seeking a turnkey subcontractor. Expansion to Czechia, Slovakia, the Baltics, or another adjacent market occurs only after Poland proof and only under signed rights and country-specific product/chemical documentation.

The 30% pack deposit is €7,500. It must cover the supplier's order deposit and attributable freight; otherwise the configuration is not offered. The remaining balance is due before handover. Target first-order CAC is below €1,500 and is recovered from the initial contribution. No economics rely on financing contractor purchases, taking a share of the contractor's job invoice, or performing jobs.

The recurring model depends on an installed consumable path, not a contractual fiction. Each serialized machine account receives a supported bill of materials, reorder thresholds, fixed delivery cadence, warranty-compatible consumables, and an optional prepaid replenishment balance. If generic resin is fully interchangeable, the OEM does not protect consumable availability, or contractors do not reorder at least €4,000 annually at 50% gross margin, the recurring economics weaken to ordinary equipment distribution.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

**First 10:** build a 250-company named Polish list from business registries, maps, trade exhibitor/member lists, equipment-service directories, and visible drain-cleaning offers. Qualify 80 by phone or email. Run 50 owner conversations and 20 hands-on demonstrations across two Warsaw/Poznań days. Ask each attendee to bring a real recurring case or sample pipe; provide the exact configuration, documented scope limits, training date, and payback worksheet on the day. Obtain three unrelated deposits by day 180 and ten delivered accounts by month 12. No founder content channel, influencer, municipality, or hoped referral is required.

**First 100:** after positive first deliveries, expand the named list to at least 1,500 qualifying accounts across territory already covered by the signed agreement. Use quarterly clustered demo days, trained-user case evidence, manufacturer-routed leads only if guaranteed in the contract, direct outreach to adjacent plumbing/drain firms, and account referrals as a bonus rather than a dependency. Add a country only after its protected right, instructions, chemical documents, trainer, and spare route are live. Target 100 installed payers by months 30–36, with at least 60% six-month consumable reorder and first-order CAC below 8% of launch-pack revenue.

## Primary Control Point

The month-zero control point is an **effective protected Poland territory or compact-indoor-pipe customer-group right**, not an informal reseller letter. It must state the covered products and customers, minimum term, objective order-backed targets, cure period, lead routing, no direct OEM selling into the protected segment, treatment of online/passive sales consistent with EU competition law, warranty and spare obligations, training/content rights, consumable supply and pricing logic, termination inventory, and assignability or change-of-control treatment.

The month-twelve control point must be stronger than the agreement: at least 20 unrelated installed payers whose serial records, trained operators, job-type recipes, warranty/spare history, reorder timing, and prepaid or scheduled replenishment route through MiniLiner. Data is collected with contractual consent; customer-specific job information is not appropriated without permission.

The right remains conditional until signed. If only revocable non-exclusive resale is offered, or protection requires speculative minimum inventory above the proof budget, there is no foundational control point.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. Request NoDig's current Poland/customer-group status, wholesale list, targets, model-level Declarations of Conformity, manuals, test references, warranty data, spare schedule, resin SDS/CLP/REACH file, and training terms before paying for stock.
2. Use counsel to convert any commercial offer into the effective protected-right clauses above. EU vertical-agreement rules constrain how exclusivity and active/passive sales may be structured; “exclusive” in a brochure is insufficient.
3. Secure a manufacturer-loaned demo, a refundable demonstrator, or a demo paid from customer deposits. The founder does not buy a fleet; cash at risk on a demonstrator is capped at 25,000 PLN before deposits.
4. Complete manufacturer certification and contract one experienced technical demonstrator for clustered days. Training establishes product competence, not authority to approve customer jobs.
5. Localize only verified manufacturer materials. For machinery sold before 20 January 2027, verify the CE mark, Declaration of Conformity, technical-file availability, traceability, and Polish instructions under the applicable Machinery Directive; prepare for Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 thereafter.
6. For each resin/chemical, obtain Polish SDS and labels, confirm the contractor's use is covered by the REACH exposure scenario, confirm CLP handling, and determine with product counsel whether CPR, a harmonised standard, ETA/Declaration of Performance, potable-water approval, or national use conditions apply. No potable-water or structural-life claim is made without exact evidence.
7. Convert the first 20 installations into a serialized lifecycle register and scheduled reorder calendar. Keep only critical spares and starter consumables after paid demand proves turns.

## Distribution Advantage

The initial acquisition mechanism is a named-owner list plus hands-on demonstration and same-day economics, which an unknown founder can execute without status. The intended advantage is higher relevance and lower sales friction than a broad catalogue: one compact use case, one documented launch configuration, one training path, and one replenishment promise.

The channel compounds only if the installed register predicts consumable and spare demand, enables same-day critical availability, produces credible payback cases, and reduces later CAC. Supplier lead routing helps only if written into the protected agreement. Local associations, installer referrals, and trade shows may accelerate the funnel but are not required control points.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

**Direct incumbent trench:** PICOTE's official Polish dealer says it has sold and serviced PICOTE equipment since 2016, holds demonstration machines, and offers cleaning, coating, lining, and rehabilitation tools. BRAWO SYSTEMS supplies liners, resins, machinery, rental equipment, advice, and delivery in more than 30 countries. Fixaline presents a broad one-stop indoor pipe-lining equipment, tool, and material offer. These firms can discount, bundle training, source a different compact system, or add scheduled replenishment. NoDig's missing Poland link is therefore not a monopoly.

MiniLiner can become preferred only if it proves a measurable operational edge: lower entry capex for the target job, faster time to first competent job, fewer incompatible purchases, same-day critical spares, predictable supported resin, and better contractor payback. The manufacturer agreement alone is copyable and terminable.

Expansion sequence:

1. Poland compact indoor/property-side cleaning and coating package.
2. Additional supported pipe diameters and compatible cleaning/coating modules sold to the same accounts.
3. Technician refreshers, second-machine packs, critical-spare membership, and consumable replenishment.
4. Only then, adjacent CEE countries already protected in the agreement, using the same data and supply system with localized compliance.
5. Potential private-label consumables or OEM co-developed compact modules only after installed demand gives negotiating leverage; neither is assumed in the proof case.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

The local layer is Polish-language operator and safety material, explicit product-versus-workmanship contracts, recipes for locally encountered pipe material/diameter/job classes, clustered demonstrations, local critical spares, PLN invoicing, and predictable EU delivery. For each additional country, the same layer must be rebuilt lawfully rather than merely translated by AI.

This defense is modest. A Polish equipment dealer can purchase another machine, translate a manual, hire a trainer, and stock resin. The harder-to-copy asset must become the dense installed cohort and its consented lifecycle data. If that data does not lower downtime, stockouts, CAC, or contractor failure rates, local adaptation is packaging rather than defense.

## Compounding Advantage

- Every installed serial creates forecastable spare, brush, hose, resin, retraining, and upgrade demand.
- Aggregated, consented job-class and consumption data can improve configuration and reorder timing without making MiniLiner the project engineer.
- Volume can improve OEM terms and justify fast-moving local inventory funded from retained gross profit.
- Trained-user evidence and local case economics can shorten later sales cycles.
- The same accounts can buy second machines and adjacent modules more cheaply than an unrelated dealer can acquire them.

There is no assumed network effect. Compounding fails if contractors multi-source consumables freely, machine reliability eliminates meaningful lifecycle spend, the OEM sells direct, or incumbents match the complete offer within 10% of price and delivery.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

While the founder retains corporate employment, the conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax business cash otherwise available for distribution and cap optional founder distributions at 5%. The strong case reinvests 100%. The model uses that cash for certified training capacity, fast-moving consumables, critical spares, localized documentation, account acquisition, and supplier deposits tied to paid orders.

No personal distributions are included in the wealth table, so company value and cash are not double-counted. After the full-time transition gate, management may lower reinvestment only when inventory turns, territory expansion, and 12-month runway remain funded. Outside capital is optional growth leverage after proof, not necessary to reach the first deposits.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

The founder does not leave employment until all are true: at least 20 unrelated installed paying contractors; contracted or demonstrated recurring demand across multiple customers; positive contribution on ten deliveries; at least 50% six-month reorder among matured accounts; first-order CAC below 8% of pack revenue with payback on first order; a signed right with at least 18 months remaining or objective renewal; two trained delivery resources who do not require founder attendance; no unresolved product-compliance issue; and at least 12 months of personal runway outside the business working-capital requirement.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

The low-inventory design preserves a named and qualified contractor database, interview evidence, job-economics worksheets, a Polish machinery/chemical diligence checklist, localized training and sales materials that may be reused only with permission, supplier-agreement learning, and a repeatable demonstration funnel. A loaned or refundable demo prevents a stranded fleet; any customer-backed unit can be delivered or resold under agreed terms.

If the NoDig right fails, the founder does not shop the same generic distribution proposition among OEMs and call it a new control point. The assets may support research for a genuinely protected compact product or a buyer-side equipment-selection service, but the latter would be a smaller business and is not the founder-wealth thesis here.

## Kill Criteria

Stop this exact candidate if any of the following occurs:

- Poland or the protected compact customer group is assigned, or the OEM offers only revocable/non-protected resale.
- Protection requires more than 40,000 PLN of speculative inventory or several separate country contracts before proof.
- A model lacks valid CE/Declaration/technical-file support, Polish instructions, traceability, or a workable 2027 transition.
- Resin lacks Polish SDS/CLP/REACH coverage or required construction-product/use documentation; any key performance claim cannot be substantiated.
- MiniLiner must inspect, approve, supervise, certify, or co-warrant each contractor job.
- Product-liability insurance is unavailable at a viable price, or counsel cannot separate product/training obligations from contractor workmanship.
- Landed launch-pack gross margin is below 38%, recurring consumable gross margin is below 50%, or the customer deposit does not cover supplier deposit and attributable freight.
- Fewer than three unrelated contractors pay deposits by day 180 or the first delivery is not contribution-positive.
- Fewer than half of matured installed accounts reorder within six months, or annual recurring spend trends below €4,000 per active account.
- PICOTE, Fixaline, BRAWO, or another Polish dealer supplies a comparable compact package, training, spares, and replenishment within 10% of MiniLiner's price and delivery and buyers show no preference.
- The 250-account Poland list cannot be built by day 30, or the 12,000-account named eligible denominator cannot be evidenced before the wealth case is used.
- Founder involvement exceeds the five-hour daily cap or deliveries depend on indefinite founder technical labor.
