# Zero-to-One Evaluation: AntibioticQuench v1

## Diagnosis

AntibioticQuench has a real technical secret candidate, a public acquisition channel and a paid real-effluent decision product that can precede hardware. Its patent/know-how and measured performance corpus could be substantially more defensible than a treatment consultancy. The present record, however, proves only a Polish patent application at TRL 4, while the wealth path needs protected European scale, successful real matrices, repeat modules and 120 sites. The cheap paid test is credible enough to attempt only after decision-critical IP and technical facts are researched.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official evidence supports the public TTO offer, application number, TRL 4 description, WHO concern and EU treatment-spending momentum. It does not establish patent grant/scope, freedom to operate, export protection, option price, inventor support, real-effluent performance, transformation-product safety or cost advantage. Module pricing/margins, 120-site scale and sales cycles are hypotheses. No external verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**68/100.** Weighted points are 136 across 20 weight units; `10 × 136 / 20 = 68`. This is advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 only after execution; currently Tier 3/speculative.** An exclusive field licence, undisclosed know-how, improvement rights and retained performance data would directly control the product. The official page alone provides none of those terms. Strongest copy risk: ozone/electrochemical/other plasma systems deliver equivalent outcomes, or competitors operate outside a Polish filing.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The conservative case reaches only €10.26m in year 15 while assuming 120 sites/200 modules, 20% operating margin, 75% ownership and 6× EBITDA. A small miss in margin, liabilities, IP geography or ownership removes the target. More importantly, European control and repeat real-effluent performance—the premises enabling 120 sites—are not evidenced.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** A non-refundable >=20,000 PLN study payment by day 150 is real paid proof; founder exposure is <=36,000 PLN and work can remain part-time. The test is sequential, but it depends on a usable option/know-how package, accredited methods and a buyer willing to fund unfamiliar TRL-4 technology.

## Expansion Check

The Polish segregated-stream beachhead is good. New compounds/matrices and foreign markets add technical/IP work, although the same reactor/performance system could compound. Mature treatment vendors can enter unless measured lifecycle advantage and improvement IP become strong.

## Fatal Objections

1. No evidence of enforceable export-scale IP or improvement ownership—fatal to the current wealth thesis.
2. TRL-4 clean/lab results may fail on real effluent or create toxic transformation products.
3. The conservative founder outcome has essentially no resilience.
4. Founder fit requires several specialist vendors and later technical leadership.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Sell a customer-funded real-stream destruction decision before equipment, then let protected process improvements and operating data compound.
