# Zero-to-One Evaluation: MicroRouteFort Bronze Conversion

## Diagnosis

MicroRouteFort fixes the original infrastructure veto by forbidding greenfield facilities and requiring every expensive driver service to pre-exist in the host lease. The first 20-bay conversion can now be optioned, presold, upgraded, certified and opened inside 12 months/100,000 PLN, while official EU evidence establishes a large shortage and specifically recommends quick upgrades of insecure existing parking. The line-level control point is a long location right plus fleet blocks, not commodity security equipment. The remaining uncertainty is whether any host actually has the complete service bundle and a <=60,000 PLN Bronze gap.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence: Commission 390,057-space gap/3,250-area equivalent, December 2025 quick-upgrade recommendation, EU Bronze and mandatory-service specifications, CEF funding precedents. Hypotheses: compliant host availability, option/lease price, upgrade quote, fleet deposits, €16–20 price, utilisation, site EBITDA and future financing. No named host or fleet contract exists.

## Overall Score

**84/100.** Weighted points are `167.5 ÷ 20 = 8.375`; multiplying by 10 gives 84 after rounding. Advisory only; not a simulated or real gate score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 conditional.** A 10–15-year assignable lease over the exact HGV bay section with included services and fit-out rights, Bronze certification, and prepaid/take-or-pay fleet blocks directly controls scarce location inventory and transaction flow. Strongest copy risk: a nearby logistics/fuel-property owner adds certified capacity and uses existing fleet relationships. It cannot copy the exact lease/blocks, but may cap pricing.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass narrowly.** Scenarios state sites/bays, price, utilisation, milestones, revenue, EBITDA, capital, founder ownership/dilution, reinvestment, enterprise value, obligations and timing. Conservative 2,800 bays are 0.72% of the official current gap; founder net equity reaches €10.5m after €9m obligations at 65% ownership. The cushion is thin and relies on 30% EBITDA, 7× multiple and site finance preserving ownership; first-site proof must validate 25%+ EBITDA and a repeat host option.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** Paid proof is two unrelated fleet deposits totalling >=15,000 PLN by day 180 with founder cash risk <=30,000 PLN. The foundational resource is a signed site option/lease whose host already supplies every required amenity. After deposits, <=70,000 PLN remaining founder cash funds only a quoted <=60,000 PLN movable Bronze layer, certification/rent/working capital; the site opens and delivers inside 12 months. No external project finance, bathroom construction, paving or grid build is needed in POC, and work remains compatible with employment through host/contractors.

## Expansion Check

The 20-bay wedge is small and measurable. Larger adjacent sites use the same host/service schedule, security kit, certification process, fleet routes and booking. Competitors can build nearby but face location/lease and certification time. Poland is a strong corridor start, then CEE.

## Fatal Objections

None identified in the written structure. The practical veto is site availability: if 200 hosts yield no complete service bundle and <=60,000 PLN Bronze quote, the material improvement is false and the original capital-heavy shape returns. Later wealth also fails if infrastructure investors/debt reduce founder ownership below the conservative bridge.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Lease and certify already serviced HGV capacity; never build bathrooms, paving or grid before demand.
