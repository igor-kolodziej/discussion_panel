# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CareChoice Book — Re-evaluation After Decision-Critical Research

## Diagnosis

The unchanged CareChoice Book candidate still has one strong conceptual feature—acquiring an intact reimbursed operating entity instead of starting with zero customers—but the exact live target cannot support that structure on the evidence available. Exhaustive public-source research found neither a share-capable legal form/share sale nor any seller-finance willingness, while the transaction needs more than 90.71% seller financing even at an artificial €50,000 floor. If the transaction is an asset or sole-proprietor transfer, the explicit GKV rule requiring new case-by-case cost-coverage applications breaks the claimed immediate recurring-payment rail. Corrected CAC, ownership, and debt math also removes the apparent conservatism from the founder-wealth case.

The exact candidate now merits **kill**, not another `research further` loop. The prescribed no-contact research is exhausted, and both hard gates fail on decision-critical facts that cannot be inferred. This does not kill the broader insight of buying an unchanged provider entity; a future target with disclosed share form, seller terms, operating cash flow, and written continuity would be a new evidence state requiring a fresh evaluation.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Sourced evidence

- [Nexxt-change B-NA-A-4505](https://www.nexxt-change.org/DE/Verkaufsangebot/Detailseite/detailseite_jsp?adId=592029) states Berlin, more than 1,000 subscription customers, up to five employees, €250,000–€500,000 last annual revenue, and an asking band above €50,000 through €250,000. These are listing claims, not independently audited facts.
- The listing does **not** disclose the target name, register number, legal form, share-sale or asset-sale structure, EBITDA, free cash flow, debt, working capital, prequalification, IK, exact provider contract, seller note, or retained operator. “Privatperson/Firma” describes the advertiser, not the target's legal form.
- Exact-ID searches reported in the appended `Decision-Critical Research Addendum` found no separate target-specific exposé or indexed legal-form, share-sale, instalment, financing, or seller-note disclosure.
- The [GKV P50 individual-provider contract](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/phm_vertraege/PHM_Vertrag_Einzelleistungserbringer_AC_TK_19-00-P50.pdf) explicitly says that when one provider takes over another provider's cases, the receiving provider must submit a new cost-coverage application for each case. An ordinary asset transfer therefore does not move the book as an uninterrupted payment rail.
- [GKV provider guidance](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/krankenversicherung/hilfsmittel/praequalifizierung/hinweise_fuer_leistungserbringer/hinweise_fuer_leistungserbringer.jsp) requires continued eligibility and notification/evidence after material changes, including owner change for a sole proprietorship, legal-form change, responsible technical-manager change, location change, or scope change.
- [IHK Stuttgart's acquisition guidance](https://www.ihk.de/stuttgart/fuer-unternehmen/nachfolge/hinweise-zum-unternehmenskauf-2744640) supports only a general-law inference: in a pure GmbH share deal, the legal entity, contracts, employees, debts, and liabilities remain with the company. Public GKV material does not guarantee continuity for this unidentified target, and no target documents are available.
- Using the candidate's NBP rate and its 20,000 PLN purchase-price cash cap, the founder contributes about €4,644 of price. At exactly €50,000, the minimum seller note is about €45,356, or 90.71%; because the published lower bound is above €50,000, the actual minimum percentage is slightly higher. At €250,000, the note is about €245,356, or 98.14%.
- Even at a zero-interest €45,356 note, principal service is about €1,260 monthly over 36 months or €756 over 60 months. The listing provides no earnings, cash, inventory, claim-loss, tax, liability, or working-capital data from which debt capacity can be judged.
- [GKV's current contract/FAQ framework](https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/phm_vertraege/2026-04-01_PHM_Fragen_und_Antworten.pdf) requires needs-based individual configuration and personal advice by trained staff; AI-based video chat does not satisfy the personal-advice requirement. Individual configuration is therefore a compliance baseline, while retained German staff are an operating dependency.
- Current providers such as [desbox](https://www.desbox.de/pflegebox-beantragen), [Sanubi](https://sanubi.de/produkte/), and [proSenio Pflegebox](https://pflegebox.de/) already advertise individual configuration and recurring payer-funded delivery. These company materials do not prove market share, but they disprove exclusivity of the proposed interface wedge.

### Corrected candidate arithmetic

- At €38 collected monthly revenue and 42% gross margin, twelve-month gross profit before additional variable support is €191.52.
- At 2% monthly churn, expected active months in year one are about 10.764, giving €171.79 first-year contribution before additional variable support.
- A 1.5× first-year contribution/CAC hurdle therefore allows maximum CAC of €114.53 with zero additional variable support, €100.18 with €2 per active month, and €85.83 with €4 per active month. The original €140 ceiling is disproved.
- A 100% share acquisition followed only by 12% outside-equity dilution leaves 88%, not 78%. Reaching 78% also requires an explicit additional 10% issued management/employee pool.
- A seller note is debt, not dilution. Founder value must use equity value after deducting outstanding seller-note and other net debt from enterprise value; retained cash cannot also be counted separately if included in that equity value.

### Consequential unresolved facts after authorized research

The target's legal form, share-sale availability, seller willingness, note terms, free cash flow, working-capital floor, active accounts, churn, margin, denials, prequalification, IK, provider-contract route, responsible technical manager, and change-of-control treatment all remain unavailable. Their absence is not proof of refusal or poor economics. It is decisive evidence insufficiency for this exact candidate and these gates.

## Overall Score

**36/100.** Weighted points are 71 over total weight 20; `10 × (71 ÷ 20) = 35.5`, rounded to 36. This is an advisory evaluator score, not a simulated-panel, working-chat, fresh-chat, confirmation, or validation score.

## Control Point

**Current control point: Tier 3.**

The founder has no owned shares, signed option, exclusivity, reserved transaction, seller-note agreement, target identity, verified provider documents, or continuity confirmation. The public listing is a sale lead, not control. A completed 100% share purchase of the same qualified legal provider with its IK, approvals, book, history, staff, supplier terms, and billing flow could become **Tier 1 over existing cash flow**, but that is precisely the target-specific package public research could not establish.

The strongest copy risk remains an established provider copying the proposed workflow while using a larger purchasing book, recognized brand, reviews, service staff, and paid-search budget. The UX itself can be copied through routine product work and is already widely visible. Historical claims data may improve operations, but no evidence shows a durable performance advantage.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The conservative endpoint is arithmetically correct: 40,000 accounts × €38 × 12 = €18.24 million revenue; 15% operating margin = €2.736 million operating profit; 7× = €19.152 million enterprise/business value; 78% = about €14.94 million before any required debt deduction. Credibility fails for five independent reasons:

1. Forty thousand accounts require a 40-fold expansion from the listing claim. The 4.9 million home-care population is an upper bound, not a reachable switching-and-acquisition denominator.
2. A 15% operating margin is unverified in a price-capped model where goods, trained human advice, packing, shipping, administration, claims loss, and customer service all fit the contracted price. At a 10% operating margin, 7× and 78% yield only about €9.96 million before net-debt deduction.
3. Corrected maximum CAC is approximately €85.83–€114.53 depending on variable support cost, not €140. At 2% monthly churn, a 40,000-account book loses about 800 accounts monthly and must fund replacement acquisition before it grows.
4. The stated 78% ownership is unexplained without issuing both 12% investor equity and an additional 10% pool. Dilution, management incentives, and acquisition financing are not modeled consistently.
5. Terminal founder equity must deduct seller-note and working-capital debt. The table neither schedules note repayment nor states terminal net debt.

The 95% reinvestment policy is stated, but there is no after-tax cash waterfall among working capital, customer acquisition, seller-note service, management build, and optional founder distributions. Expected and strong cases merely scale the same unproved economics. There is no conservative founder-wealth path with genuine headroom.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL.**

The cash schedule nominally limits founder cash to 38,000 PLN before successor-controlled payer receipts, but cash outlay is not the only existential exposure. The exact proof path requires, before closing:

1. a share-capable legal entity offered as a 100% share deal;
2. unchanged provider identity, IK, prequalification, contracts, approvals, responsible technical manager, and operations;
3. a seller willing to finance more than 90.71% of even the theoretical minimum price, non-recourse and paid only from collected receipts or contribution;
4. enough normalized free cash flow and working capital to service the note while funding stock, payroll, denials, and payer timing; and
5. a retained German operator and trained advice staff compatible with a five-hour-per-day remote founder.

None is publicly established. If the target is not an unchanged corporate share deal, new applications for every transferred case create payer/customer re-papering and attrition before paid proof. If it is a share deal, the buyer also inherits liabilities, employment obligations, historic claims exposure, and managing-director duties; the 38,000 PLN cash figure does not prove a 40,000 PLN maximum economic loss.

The seller-finance need is one speculative external acceptance, but it is not a normal minor condition: it supplies virtually all acquisition consideration and must coexist with an unusually buyer-protective lack of personal recourse. With no authority to contact the seller and public research exhausted, there is no credible 180-day paid-proof route for the exact candidate. It cannot enter an independent panel.

## Expansion Check

The advertised 1,000-account book would be a valid beachhead only after an intact share transfer is documented. The proposed next 300 search-acquired caregivers are not a distinct protected niche; they are the mainstream audience pursued by existing national providers. Germany is the necessary operating market, but the Poland-based founder has no demonstrated local regulatory, sourcing, trust, or distribution advantage there.

Expansion to 40,000 accounts is not asymmetric. Broad competitors already have the contract eligibility, products, staff, configurators, brand, and search presence needed to enter the proposed experience. The only potential compounding asset is claims/account history, and the listing gives no proof that it creates lower churn, denial cost, service cost, or CAC than competitors.

## Fatal Objections

1. **No target-specific share-transfer evidence:** legal form and transaction form remain unavailable after exact-ID research.
2. **The alternative destroys the cold-start claim:** an asset/new-provider takeover requires new case-by-case cost-coverage applications.
3. **Exceptional seller dependency:** the transaction requires more than 90.71% seller financing at the theoretical floor and up to about 98.14% at the top of the band, with no disclosed willingness.
4. **No debt-capacity evidence:** revenue cannot establish free cash flow, note service, working capital, or inherited-liability tolerance.
5. **Founder Wealth is not conservative:** 40× scale, unproved 15% margin, corrected lower CAC ceiling, unexplained dilution, and missing net-debt subtraction leave no robust €10 million path.
6. **No repeatable distribution proof:** the only founder-controlled growth channel is crowded search, and no paid cohort supports €85.83–€114.53 maximum CAC.
7. **The UX is not a control point:** individual configuration and human advice are contract requirements and competitor features.
8. **The operating stack is unproved:** a part-time Warsaw founder needs a retained German technical manager, trained advice, fulfilment, payer operations, accounting, privacy, and employment coverage.
9. **Maximum loss is not demonstrated:** a share deal imports debts, claims exposure, employment obligations, and director duties beyond the visible cash payment.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

An unchanged legal provider entity with an already-paying reimbursed book, valid eligibility, payer-accepted approvals, billing history, and retained operator can solve cold start. Preserve this insight only for a target that discloses or privately documents share form, share-sale availability, realistic acquisition financing, normalized free cash flow, provider continuity, and the operating team. The exact B-NA-A-4505 candidate does not provide those facts.

## Recommendation

kill
