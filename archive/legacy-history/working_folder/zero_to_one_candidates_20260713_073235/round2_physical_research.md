# Round 2 Physical / Contractual-Control Research

Research date: 2026-07-13

This is candidate-generation research only. It does not score, rank, select, validate, or recommend any direction. Commercial prices, margins, conversion rates, contract willingness, financing availability, and scale cases below are hypotheses unless a source says otherwise. Every paid-proof construct is designed to stay below 40,000 PLN and to be executable by an unknown Warsaw founder alongside employment; no direction assumes that a future partner will appear. Where another operator is required, an executed conditional contract with a named counterparty is part of the proof, not an unstated dependency.

The eight directions deliberately avoid loose-part pools, salvage, exact-match inventory, generic desks, equipment-led inspection, service-route aggregation, dashboards, prospective data assets, one-title media rights, and positions that require an authority to confer a scarce quota. The intended control is an executed site lease/option, a recorded licence, a matched take-or-pay allocation, or title to a location-bound resource stream.

## 1. RouteFort — optioned safe-and-secure truck-parking sites

**Secret / insight.** The scarce product is not asphalt or CCTV; it is a certified, bookable overnight position on the driver's actual corridor. The European Commission reported a current EU shortfall of 390,057 safe and secure truck-parking spaces, rising to 483,000 by 2040. A founder can option an already paved, underused private yard near an A1/A2/S8 freight node and sell guaranteed nightly blocks before committing to the security build-out. The location lease and fleet allocations, rather than purchased barriers, are the resource.

**Buyer / event.** Polish and CEE fleet operators, high-value cargo carriers, and freight forwarders when they assign recurring lanes, renew cargo-insurance/security conditions, or need compliant daily/weekly rest positions on a new customer route. The event is a named lane starting on a named date, not general interest in safer parking.

**Exact first paid proof.** Within 180 days: (1) pay no more than 12,000 PLN for a six-month conditional option over one paved private yard within roughly 5 km of a freight interchange; (2) spend no more than 10,000 PLN on a security/certification gap plan and site concept; (3) obtain a 5,000–15,000 PLN reservation deposit from one fleet for at least 10 bays for 90 nights, refundable only if the site fails the stated opening condition. Do not build before the option, gap plan, and paid fleet reservation all exist. Remaining spend is legal work, mapping, and targeted outbound, keeping the total below 40,000 PLN.

**Control point.** A registrable long lease or purchase option over the named yard, including an express right to operate truck parking and install security; followed by prepaid or take-or-pay bay blocks. Inside 12 months, exercise only if planning, access, utilities, and EU certification economics work. The moat at scale is a corridor portfolio of location-specific leases plus recurring fleet allocations and booking history; a competitor cannot reproduce it by buying one camera system.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** Twenty-five CEE sites averaging 120 bays, €18 per occupied night, and 70% utilisation imply about €13.8 million annual parking revenue before ancillary services. At a hypothesised 30% site-level EBITDA margin, that is about €4.1 million EBITDA. If expansion is financed mainly with site debt/leases after anchor contracts and the founder retains 65–70% of the operating holdco, ordinary infrastructure/parking cash flow or a non-heroic operating-business value could put founder equity above €10 million. The 3,000-bay network would still be below 1% of the Commission's stated current EU gap.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether an unknown entrant can find yards whose owner, local plan, access geometry, utilities, and certification path all work at a rent that still leaves attractive bay economics—and then win a genuinely take-or-pay fleet reservation rather than a non-binding expression of interest.

**Direct evidence.**

- [European Commission, April 2025: current 390,057-space SSPA gap and 483,000 expected by 2040](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/more-safe-and-secure-parking-professional-drivers-needed-eu-study-reveals-2025-04-11_en)
- [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1012: minimum service/security levels and independent certification](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32022R1012)
- [TEN-T map library, including rail freight, terminals, and European transport corridors](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-themes/infrastructure-and-investment/trans-european-transport-network-ten-t/tentec-information-system-and-ten-t-map-library/ten-t-eu-overview-maps_en)

## 2. CultivarHold — exclusive EU commercialisation rights to an underused protected plant variety

**Secret / insight.** A protected cultivar is self-reproducing product IP: one assigned or exclusively licensed genotype can yield royalties on every propagated plant for up to 25–30 years. The CPVO Gazette explicitly records contractual exploitation rights and licensees. Small Polish/CEE breeders and institutes may have technically good, protected ornamental, berry, vegetable, or resilience traits without EU commercial distribution. Unlike a cultural title, the same nursery/propagator channel, mother-stock controls, testing package, and territorial license architecture compound across varieties.

**Buyer / event.** Commercial nurseries, young-plant propagators, seed merchants, and specialist growers when they lock the next spring/autumn catalogue and reserve propagation material. The first beachhead should be one crop where the buyer can inspect the trait quickly—for example an ornamental with visible colour/shelf-life differentiation—rather than a multi-year tree yield claim.

**Exact first paid proof.** Search the public CPVO register and contact holders of in-force but weakly distributed Polish/CEE rights. Spend up to 8,000 PLN on title/chain-of-rights diligence and up to 8,000 PLN on a 12-month exclusive commercialisation option for one named variety, expressly including the right to show material and solicit conditional orders. Secure one propagator's paid 5,000–10,000 PLN preorder for a defined number of rooted cuttings/liners for its next catalogue, conditional on the licence and agreed plant-quality test. Use the balance for a small mother-stock/sample batch and phytosanitary counsel; do not finance mass multiplication before the preorder.

**Control point.** A recorded exclusive contractual exploitation licence for the EU or a defined multi-country territory, plus access to authenticated mother stock and contractual control over multiplication. CPVO says both applications and granted rights may be licensed; the Gazette records holders and licensees. The right—not a supply partnership—must be signed before selling.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** A portfolio of 15 cultivars averaging 1.5 million licensed propagations a year at a €0.40 net royalty would yield €9 million high-gross-margin annual revenue. Shared testing, mother-stock, nursery distribution, enforcement, and catalogue placement make this a portfolio platform rather than 15 unrelated titles. At a hypothesised €4–5 million operating profit and 70–80% founder ownership, retained distributions or strategic IP value can support more than €10 million founder wealth without requiring a dominant share of EU planting material.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether any affordable right combines a trait that buyers will pay for now, clean title, viable mother stock, and enough remaining protection—and whether a nursery will place a paid order without multi-season performance data.

**Direct evidence.**

- [European Commission: one Community plant variety right is valid throughout the EU, generally for 25 years](https://food.ec.europa.eu/plants/plant-variety-property-rights_en)
- [CPVO note: granted rights and even pending CPVR applications can be licensed](https://cpvo.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/note_for_cpvr_holders_and_applicants_on_licences.pdf)
- [CPVO Official Gazette structure: Chapter VIII records contractual exploitation rights, holders, and licensees](https://cpvo.europa.eu/en/applications-and-examinations/official-publications)
- [CPVO 2024 statistics: 31,317 rights in force and 67,705 granted since 1996](https://cpvo.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/2025-02/a4-cpvo-statistics-version-1-2025.pdf)

## 3. SidingGate — exclusive lease/operating option over a dormant private rail siding

**Secret / insight.** Many industrial properties have a physical rail connection whose replacement cost and location cannot be replicated by buying handling equipment. The commercial product is a shipper's reserved transload window at a connected site, backed by an operator that already has the necessary safety coverage. Poland produced 56.7 billion tonne-kilometres of rail freight in 2024, second in the EU after Germany. The founder does not become a speculative freight forwarder: the asset is a named siding option plus an anchor shipper's take-or-pay handling block.

**Buyer / event.** Mid-sized producers/importers of aggregates, metals, timber, fertiliser, packaged food inputs, or other rail-suitable freight when a plant changes supplier, a road-only contract becomes uneconomic, or a new annual bulk-flow contract starts. The trigger is a documented lane and minimum monthly tonnage.

**Exact first paid proof.** (1) Obtain a six-month, no more than 12,000 PLN exclusive option to lease one named dormant/underused private siding and adjacent hardstand, conditional on technical and safety feasibility; (2) obtain, for no more than 8,000 PLN, a written conditional operating and tariff offer from one named certified rail carrier or siding operator whose safety system can legally cover the movements; (3) collect a 10,000–20,000 PLN reservation fee from one shipper for a defined first train/month or tonnes/month, creditable against handling and refundable if the named siding cannot reopen. No track rehabilitation or rolling stock purchase occurs before all three documents.

**Control point.** The long lease/management right to the connected siding and hardstand, the carrier's executed operating agreement, and the shipper's reserved transload window. Polish UTK guidance says the legal basis to manage a siding may be ownership, perpetual usufruct, or an agreement, while operations require the appropriate safety documentation. The contract must allocate responsibility clearly; an unspecified future carrier is not acceptable.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** Ten regional sidings handling 100,000 tonnes a year each at €7 net contribution per tonne imply €7 million contribution. If central overhead and maintenance leave about €4 million operating profit, a founder retaining 60–70% through site SPVs and an operating holdco could exceed €10 million in equity or cumulative distributions. One million tonnes is a small fraction of Polish rail freight; the difficult part is profitable local flows, not theoretical national volume.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether a seemingly dormant siding is still physically connected, economically repairable, and coverable by an operator's safety system—and whether one anchor flow has enough margin and duration to finance rehabilitation.

**Direct evidence.**

- [Eurostat: Poland recorded 56.713 billion rail-freight tonne-kilometres in 2024](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251031-1)
- [Polish UTK guide to a siding user's safety certificate and required operating documents](https://utk.gov.pl/pl/uslugi/operatorzy-ocznic/uslugi-dla-operatorow-b/17962%2CWydanie-swiadectwa-bezpieczenstwa-dla-uzytkownika-bocznicy-kolejowej.pdf)
- [UTK reminder: a management basis can be ownership, perpetual usufruct, or an agreement; safety and operating documents remain mandatory](https://utk.gov.pl/pl/aktualnosci/22134%2CPrawidlowe-opracowanie-regulaminu-pracy-bocznicy-przypominamy-podstawowe-zasady.pdf)

## 4. CellarBox — long-lease micro self-storage in dense, awkward urban spaces

**Secret / insight.** Standard self-storage developers prefer large roadside boxes. Dense Warsaw catchments also contain dry basements, former retail back rooms, podium parking areas, and small industrial floors that are awkward for conventional tenants but close enough for walking/short-drive storage. The scarce asset is a long, use-permitted lease at a low all-in occupancy cost, signed before fit-out. FEDESSA's 2025 report estimates Poland at 225,000 m², 78.1% average occupancy, and €182 average annual rent per square metre; it also highlights growth in remotely managed stores.

**Buyer / event.** Apartment residents during a move, renovation, inheritance, new child, or household merger; and local ecommerce/trade micro-businesses when stock no longer fits at home. The event is immediate possession on a specific date within a 10–15 minute catchment.

**Exact first paid proof.** Secure a three-month conditional option (maximum 10,000 PLN) on one 400–800 m² dry site, expressly conditional on fire, access, and storage use. Spend up to 10,000 PLN on a fire/building/access concept and 5,000 PLN on catchment ads and a simple floor-plan booking page. Obtain at least 20 paid first-month reservations or deposits totalling at least 10,000 PLN for specified unit sizes and opening date, refundable only if the project does not open. Do not buy partitions or access hardware before the option, technical concept, and deposits.

**Control point.** A 10–15-year assignable/master lease over a use-permitted dense-catchment site, with rent review limits and fit-out rights. Scale compounds through a portfolio of scarce local leases, standardised modular fit-out, remote operations, pricing history, and a citywide brand; it is not defended by purchasing lockers.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** Fifty micro-sites averaging 1,200 rentable m² across Poland/CEE would create 60,000 m². At the 2025 Polish industry reference of €182/m²/year, that is about €10.9 million revenue; a hypothesised 35–40% mature operating margin gives €3.8–4.4 million EBITDA. With property/fit-out debt released only after site proof and 60–70% founder holdco ownership, operating equity or distributions could exceed €10 million. The CEE expansion premise avoids requiring 27% of today's Polish floor area indefinitely.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether fire separation, ventilation, moisture, access, security, insurance, VAT, and landlord rent turn an apparently cheap basement into an uneconomic conversion before sufficient local demand appears.

**Direct evidence.**

- [FEDESSA/CBRE European Self Storage Industry Report 2025: Poland 225,000 m², 78.1% occupancy, €182/m²; growth in remote management](https://www.fedessa.org/static/f6762ae6-c097-4bdd-84ac37c2b12e3cb7/70e08602-eab5-407b-98f4a1c839f22677/FEDESSA-European-Industry-Report-2025.pdf)
- [CBRE European Self Storage Industry Report 2024: 9,575 European stores and 16.5 million m²; continuing capital deployment](https://www.cbre.pl/en-gb/insights/reports/european-self-storage-industry-report-2024)

## 5. ResinReserve — matched long-dated allocation rights to verified packaging recyclate

**Secret / insight.** PPWR creates plant-year recycled-content obligations, while European recycling capacity has begun contracting. The valuable object is not a spot pile of pellets; it is a matched pair of contracts: a multi-year allocation of verified, specification-bound recyclate from a named plant and a converter/brand's take-or-pay drawdown. Regulation (EU) 2025/40 sets 2030 minimums including 30% for contact-sensitive PET packaging, 30% for single-use beverage bottles, and 35% for other plastic packaging. Plastics Recyclers Europe reported 13.5 million tonnes of installed capacity in 2024 and nearly one million tonnes lost over three years.

**Buyer / event.** Mid-sized packaging converters and brand manufacturing plants when they set the next annual resin recipe/budget, qualify a new material, or contract 2027–2030 supply against PPWR targets. Start with one polymer/grade and one specification, not a generic materials brokerage.

**Exact first paid proof.** Negotiate a 12–24-month exclusive allocation option for a named grade and monthly tonnage from one recycler, capped at 8,000 PLN option cost and exercisable only against a buyer contract. Spend up to 12,000 PLN on legal/specification/chain-of-custody review and sample testing. Obtain a 10,000–20,000 PLN capacity-reservation payment from one converter for a defined 2027 delivery allocation, creditable against the first invoices. The recycler ships the qualification lot directly or against buyer prepayment; the founder does not speculate on inventory or use the 40,000 PLN as working capital.

**Control point.** Transferable or back-to-back multi-year allocation rights at named recycling plants, with specifications, certificates, minimum volumes, pricing formula, non-circumvention, and buyer take-or-pay. Durability comes only after the portfolio spans several plants and buyers and builds qualification history, credit, and renewal priority. A one-off vendor introduction or spot purchase is explicitly not the business.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** 80,000 tonnes a year across CEE at €150 net contribution per tonne implies €12 million gross contribution; if credit, quality, hedging, and overhead leave €4–5 million operating profit, 65–75% retained founder ownership could support more than €10 million in equity or cumulative distributions. Trade finance/factoring must fund matched receivables after proof; the founder cannot bankroll tonnage personally. The volume is under 0.6% of PRE's reported European installed recycling capacity.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether recyclers will grant bankable, transferable allocations to an unknown company, converters will pay before full supplier qualification, and the spread survives index pricing, imports, quality claims, and working-capital costs.

**Direct evidence.**

- [Regulation (EU) 2025/40, Article 7: plant-year minimum recycled-content percentages for 2030 and 2040](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32025R0040)
- [European Commission PPWR overview: the regulation applies from mid-2026 and recycled-content targets rise in 2030 and 2040](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/packaging-waste/packaging-packaging-waste-regulation_en)
- [Plastics Recyclers Europe, November 2025: 13.5 Mt installed capacity, record contraction, nearly 1 Mt lost in three years](https://www.plasticsrecyclers.eu/news/2024-data-reveals-a-deepening-crisis-of-the-european-plastics-recycling-industry/)
- [PETCORE Europe 2024: about 1 Mt rPET recycling capacity needed for the 2030 bottle target and major country disparities](https://www.petcore-europe.org/images/2024/PET_report_V5.pdf)

## 6. ThermalStandby — prepaid industrial process-temperature continuity capacity

**Secret / insight.** A temporary chiller or boiler is generic equipment; guaranteed availability after a failure is not. Carrier already sells contingency plans whose upper levels guarantee equipment availability, demonstrating that buyers pay for preparedness before failure. A narrower CEE entrant would start with food/cold-storage plants dependent on roughly 300 kW–1 MW process cooling or hot water, contract a named standby unit, pre-engineer the customer's tie-in, and collect an annual retainer. The defensible resource would be the installed base of paid site contracts, ready connection kits, and regionally staged fleet—not ownership of one machine.

**Buyer / event.** Food manufacturers, refrigerated warehouses, beverage plants, hotels, and facility managers when an ageing chiller/boiler fails inspection, a replacement project begins, insurance/business-continuity planning is renewed, or the summer/winter risk season approaches. Start with one temperature band and connection standard.

**Exact first paid proof.** Before approaching customers, execute a conditional 90-day reservation/sub-hire contract over one specifically identified rental unit from one named owner, with the option payment capped at 7,000 PLN and dispatch terms written. Spend no more than 10,000 PLN on an engineer's site survey, load calculation, tie-in plan, and response procedure for one customer. Collect a 12,000–20,000 PLN non-refundable annual availability retainer that guarantees the named capacity and response window, with rental/installation charged only on call-off. Legal, insurance, and direct outbound keep total pre-proof spend below 40,000 PLN. If the equipment-owner contract is not signed, there is no offer.

**Control point.** Multi-year prepaid availability contracts, site-specific engineered tie-in plans and hardware stored at the customer, plus contractually reserved and later owned/financed regional fleet capacity. A competitor buying one chiller cannot instantly reproduce the response density and installed tie-ins, but the first contract alone is not yet durable.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** A CEE network of 250 financed units averaging €7,000 rental revenue per active month for six months a year produces about €10.5 million rental revenue before retainers, installation, and fuel. At a hypothesised €4 million mature EBITDA after engineering and logistics, a founder retaining 60–70% of the operating holdco while asset SPVs use debt/leases could exceed €10 million equity. Scale must come from fleet utilisation and renewal retainers, not founder-delivered emergency labour.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether the Polish niche is actually underserved despite Carrier and other rental incumbents, and whether an equipment owner will reserve hard capacity cheaply enough for the founder to sell a credible SLA before owning a fleet.

**Direct evidence.**

- [Carrier Temperature Control Emergency Planning Services: plans protect against downtime and Silver/Gold levels guarantee rental-equipment availability](https://www.carrier.com/rental/en/uk/about-us/emergency-planning-services/tceps/)
- [Carrier Poland: emergency planning for HVAC failure and process cooling in manufacturing](https://www.carrier.com/wynajem/pl/pl/dlaczego-wynajem/nasze-doswiadczenie-w-reagowaniu/sytuacje-awaryjne/)
- [Carrier UK: temporary boilers for breakdowns, planned maintenance, and process production; 24/7 emergency service](https://www.carrier.uk/en/rental/products/boilers.html)
- [Carrier case: a contingency plan was contracted before failure and activated two months later](https://www.carrier.com/rental/en/uk/about-us/case-studies/event-ice-rink-ice-rink-contingency-plan-slough-900kw-chiller-hire/)

## 7. FermentCarbon — long-term takeoff rights to fermentation CO2 at breweries/distilleries

**Secret / insight.** Fermentation already creates a concentrated, valuable gas stream. GEA states that it can be recovered to 99.998% food-grade purity, reused in the brewery, or sold to food and beverage users; the European Commission's JRC says beer fermentation produces about 4 kg CO2 per hectolitre. The company would not sell capture hardware. It would option long-term, location-specific rights to install and take the CO2 at a named brewery/distillery, then sell contracted local supply. Foreign companies such as RevolutionCO2 publicly use essentially this asset-owning/offtake model, but a CEE source-and-customer network remains a local execution hypothesis.

**Buyer / event.** On the source side, a brewery, distillery, or ethanol producer when it budgets a sustainability/capex programme or wants lower purchased-CO2 cost. On the demand side, a beverage bottler, greenhouse, food processor, fire-extinguisher filler, or industrial user when its annual bulk-CO2 supply contract renews or supply continuity is threatened.

**Exact first paid proof.** Secure for no more than 10,000 PLN a 12-month exclusive option from one named fermenter granting site access, sampling, and a conditional 10–15-year CO2 takeoff/installation right, exercisable only after purity/volume and financing. Spend up to 12,000 PLN on third-party gas sampling, mass-balance, and a budgetary engineering design. Obtain a 10,000–20,000 PLN conditional reservation deposit from one local CO2 user for a defined monthly tonnage and price band, refundable only if the source fails the disclosed purity/commissioning conditions. No capture equipment is purchased inside the first 40,000 PLN.

**Control point.** Exclusive long-term title/takeoff rights to the raw fermentation stream at each named site, the right to install equipment, and matched customer offtake. After financing, the installed capture/liquefaction asset makes the source relationship physically sticky. The right must survive a brewery ownership change and specify gas quality, interruptions, energy, space, maintenance, and by-product ownership.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** Twenty CEE source sites averaging 5,000 saleable tonnes a year create 100,000 tonnes. At €150 net contribution per tonne, annual contribution is €15 million; a hypothesised 30% operating margin yields €4.5 million. With project finance/equipment leases at each proven site and 60–70% founder ownership of the developer/operator, equity or distributions can exceed €10 million without owning breweries. The scale assumption must be checked against actual Polish source volumes and customer delivered prices.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether accessible Polish fermentation sites vent enough consistent, clean gas and will grant durable takeoff rights, while local delivered CO2 prices cover capture, power, testing, storage, transport, seasonality, and equipment financing.

**Direct evidence.**

- [GEA: fermentation CO2 can be purified to 99.998%, reused, or sold to food/beverage users](https://www.gea.com/en/products/brewery-systems/cold-block-processing/cold-block-process-units/gea-co2-recovery/)
- [European Commission JRC best-practice reference: about 4 kg CO2 produced per hectolitre of beer](https://greenbestpractice.jrc.ec.europa.eu/print/pdf/node/245)
- [Krones case, 2024: one brewery recovery installation saves up to 800 tonnes of CO2 per year](https://www.krones.com/en/company/press/magazine/innovation/co2-sourced-in-house-gives-independence.php)
- [RevolutionCO2 company model: operator installs, owns/operates recovery and connects distillery sources to contracted users](https://www.revolutionco2.com/)

## 8. ArchiveWorks — exclusive manufacturing rights to a complete dormant design catalogue

**Secret / insight.** Historical industrial designs can be relaunched as authentic products without acquiring a factory. EUIPO says registered designs can be sold or licensed; registered EU designs can last up to 25 years, and copyright/trademark/contract rights may last longer. The proof that a catalogue-level licence is commercially used is current: in 2026 Zanotta obtained an exclusive licence from the Italian state to produce 30 Carlo Mollino works. The Polish/CEE wedge is not buying loose vintage furniture; it is optioning a complete, coherent archive—drawings, prototypes, name/trademark rights, provenance, and manufacturing rights—then using paid limited-edition preorders before tooling.

**Buyer / event.** Design-led hospitality groups, architects, collectors, premium furniture dealers, and affluent consumers when specifying a hotel/restaurant/residential project, preparing a design fair launch, or buying a numbered authentic edition. The first product should be manufacturable by an existing workshop without dedicated mould capex.

**Exact first paid proof.** Spend up to 10,000 PLN on chain-of-title/copyright/trademark/design diligence for one coherent Polish or CEE designer/maker archive and up to 10,000 PLN on a 12-month exclusive production option covering at least five related products, images, story, and seller-authorised marketing. Produce one engineering prototype/render/sample for no more than 12,000 PLN. Collect at least three 30% customer deposits totalling at least 10,000 PLN for a numbered first edition, conditional only on a stated delivery date and the licence exercise. Tooling/production starts only after the option and deposits.

**Control point.** An exclusive, transferable multi-product manufacturing and sales licence or assignment covering drawings, prototypes, trademarks/signature use, archive imagery, authenticity marks, territories, channels, and enforcement. A catalogue and authenticity system create shared brand/distribution; this is materially different from buying vintage stock or licensing unrelated one-off titles.

**Wealth shape (hypothesis, not forecast).** Six thousand units a year across a multi-product European catalogue at €3,500 average ex-VAT revenue imply €21 million annual sales. A hypothesised 45% gross margin and 15% operating margin yield about €3.2 million operating profit. If contract manufacturing limits fixed capital and the founder retains 75–80%, cumulative distributions or an ordinary branded-product value can exceed €10 million. This requires international dealer/DTC reach and several repeatable products, not scarcity pricing on a single chair.

**Fatal unknown.** Whether an affordable archive has clean, enforceable rights and genuine latent demand, and whether authentic production quality, liability, working capital, and dealer acquisition can work without an already prestigious founder or brand.

**Direct evidence.**

- [EUIPO: registered designs can be sold or licensed and can be renewed to 25 years](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/designs/after-applying/manage-my-application)
- [Regulation (EU) 2026/715: written assignment of a registered EU design and registration of the transfer](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/715/oj/eng)
- [Zanotta, 2026: exclusive state-granted licence to produce 30 works from the Carlo Mollino archive](https://www.zanotta.com/en-us/magazine/from-design-to-product/from-one-off-to-collection-zanotta-and-the-legacy-of-carlo-mollino)

## Cross-direction discipline

- Each first proof requires cash from the exact buyer before full asset exercise or build-out; an LOI alone is not counted.
- Each seller/operator dependency is converted into a signed option, lease, licence, allocation, reservation, or operating agreement before it is presented as controlled.
- Initial spending does not buy speculative stock, production equipment, or a full fit-out.
- The wealth shapes are order-of-magnitude hypotheses showing how a control portfolio could become large enough; they are not evaluations, forecasts, or validation results.
- The fatal unknown named for each direction is the fact the next research/evaluation stage would have to resolve first.
