# Zero-to-One Evaluation: HoseWitness v1

## Overall Score

**66/100** — weighted score **131.5 / 20.0 = 6.575**, rounded to 66.

## Concise Diagnosis

HoseWitness identifies a real conceptual gap between recording that a cleaning operation occurred and obtaining physical evidence from the exact finished assembly. The proposed paid-proof experiment is unusually disciplined and reachable, but the business case is still built on three stacked hypotheses: a buyer will pay for per-item evidence, enough sites have thousands of eligible premium assemblies, and a new vendor can retain pricing against incumbents that already own the cleaning, imaging, extraction, traceability, or distribution layers. The idea may support a useful niche product, but the submitted evidence does not yet establish a credible €10 million founder-wealth path or a durable control point. It is a **research-further** candidate, not a pursue candidate.

## Evidence Boundary and Assumptions

- The product performance, cycle time, paid-pilot conversion, €15 downstream value, cassette cost, reorder rate, eligible-site denominator, margins, and founder ownership are hypotheses in the candidate, not demonstrated evidence.
- The external evidence supports the technical category, not demand for HoseWitness. [ISO/TS 18409:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/66733.html) covers collecting solid-particle samples from hose assemblies, excludes contamination analysis, and says the methods are recommended for statistical validation of cleaning methods suited to systematic batch production. That makes the proposed correlation concept technically legible, but also confirms that process validation is a strong substitute for per-item sampling.
- Existing suppliers already possess important building blocks. [Ultra Clean's production system](https://ultracleantech.com/product/uc-cal-ms-carousel-autoloader/) verifies projectile exit and logs production-cleaning events; [MP Filtri sells membrane patch imaging](https://www.mpfiltri.com/product/patch-imaging-kit/); and [HYDAC sells component-extraction and visual particle-analysis systems](https://www.hydac.com/download/components-and-systems-for-agricultural-machinery-1000443497-en.pdf).
- Public network scale does not establish the eligible denominator. Parker reports [more than 3,000 ParkerStore locations worldwide](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/parker/na/united-states/about-parker/Parker%20Hannifin%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf), but the candidate excludes commodity, emergency, low-use, and non-premium production. No sourced bottom-up count shows 5,000 sites/cells with both the required throughput and economic reason to screen each assembly.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.** The candidate supplies conservative, expected, and strong scenarios and avoids double-counting distributions, but its conservative clearance is not yet credible enough. It requires 800 active sites, 3.2 million annual cassette events, €5.20 consumable pricing, 20% operating margin, 60% founder ownership after outside growth capital, and a 7× operating-profit value, while reaching only about €13.96 million of founder value. Small adverse movements in eligible-site count, utilization, pricing, service cost, ownership, or multiple erase much of that margin of safety.

The decisive weakness is the missing bottom-up population of sites that simultaneously have at least roughly 2,500–4,000 eligible assemblies, a binding specification/premium/avoided cost, and willingness to add fluid handling and operator time per item. Generic workshop counts cannot substitute for that denominator, and expected or strong scenarios cannot repair a weak conservative case.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, with execution risk.** The exact plan seeks two unrelated, non-refundable 6,000–9,000 PLN comparison pilots within 180 days, caps pre-proof founder exposure at 35,000 PLN, keeps work at 15–20 hours/week, and buys bounded engineering and laboratory help. A full commercial reader, distributor, accreditation, production mold, or departure from employment is not required before payment. The day-60 technical schedule and conversion of 150 cold accounts are ambitious, but failure can occur inside the capital and employment constraints; therefore this is a valid paid-proof path, not proof that demand exists.

## Control Point

**Current: Tier 3. Potential: Tier 2. Not Tier 1.**

At present the cassette/tooling, validated recipes, reference corpus, lot history, installed specifications, and reorder behavior are plans rather than owned, proven assets. That makes current control speculative and built from commercially available fluid handling, membranes, optics, imaging, and contract engineering. It could become Tier 2 if the founder actually owns validated recipe performance, reproducible low-blank behavior, a specimen/reference corpus, economical cassette tooling, meaningful installed workflows, and recurring reorders across independent customers. It would still lack exclusive supply, standards ownership, contracted distribution, or a uniquely controlled transaction flow required for Tier 1.

**Strongest copy risk:** Ultra Clean, Parker, HYDAC, or MP Filtri can bundle debris capture/imaging and item records into an installed cleaning or contamination-control workflow. The most dangerous version is an incumbent cleaning supplier adding a disposable membrane and imaging step to an already trusted, logged, high-volume production system; customer trust, service access, and bundled pricing could outweigh HoseWitness's recipe lead.

## Strongest Objections and Caveats

1. **The economic denominator is unproved.** The idea needs premium/specification-driven production sites, not merely hose workshops, and the founder-wealth model depends on hundreds of high-utilization sites.
2. **Process validation may beat per-item screening.** ISO's own framing points toward statistical validation of systematic cleaning; customers may prefer a validated cleaning process plus periodic audit over four minutes, test fluid, purge, waste, and a cassette on every hose.
3. **The control point is copyable before installation compounds.** Incumbents already sell adjacent extraction, patch imaging, projectile verification, data logging, sealing, and traceability capabilities.
4. **Technical correlation may be envelope-specific and operationally fragile.** Blanks, extraction efficiency, hose geometry/material, debris morphology, cassette lots, operators, residual test oil, and purge efficacy can impair false-pass/false-reject performance or create warranty risk.
5. **Scale economics are target economics.** A €2 fully allocated cassette cost, 60–69% gross margin, low field-support burden, direct CAC below €3,000, and 20% operating margin across hundreds of international industrial sites are not yet substantiated.
6. **The expansion path compounds validation and liability as well as data.** Every new fluid, material, diameter, length, geometry, adapter, and customer specification adds evidence and support burden; cross-recipe reuse cannot be assumed.
7. **Founder fit declines after the first proof.** The technical validation fits well, but an 800-site industrial hardware/consumables business eventually needs channel, field-service, quality, and liability infrastructure not represented in the early people stack.

## Fatal Objection

**No immutable fatal technical objection is proven at this stage. The current fatal-to-pursue objection is the unverified paid denominator.** If a substantial cohort cannot demonstrate a binding specification, premium, or avoided cost sufficient to justify per-item screening at thousands of units per year, the beachhead, consumable recurrence, distribution economics, and founder-wealth case all collapse together. The candidate's proposed pilots can test this, but until they do, the Founder Wealth Gate remains failed.

## Recommendation

**Research further.** This judgment does not authorize a pursue decision or serious capital deployment.
