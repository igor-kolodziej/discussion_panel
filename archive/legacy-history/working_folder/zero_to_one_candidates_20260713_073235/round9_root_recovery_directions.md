## Direction 1 — MarginLedger EU: Retail-Deduction Recovery For Mid-Market Food Suppliers

**Generation decision: retain conditionally for finalist drafting.**

### Customer Truth And Commercial Gap

A €5m–€100m food or drink supplier selling to large grocers does not mainly want another accounts-receivable dashboard. It wants the retailer to release money already earned without damaging the commercial relationship. Deductions arrive as invoice discrepancies, delivery shortages, promotional adjustments, listing/marketing charges or alleged non-compliance. The files needed to challenge them are split across remittances, contracts, proof of delivery, EDI, transport records and retailer portals; small finance teams let low-value claims expire and are reluctant to confront a powerful buyer.

The gap is a confidential, outcome-priced European recovery operator that begins with one retailer/country lane, converts the supplier's own records into evidence-complete dispute packages, files only authorised claims, tracks actual repayment rather than approval, and turns resolved cases into a reusable retailer-rule/evidence graph. It is not generic bookkeeping or an AI summary tool. The first paid outcome is money restored to the supplier's bank/remittance flow.

The problem is legally and operationally real. The EU's Unfair Trading Practices Directive protects weaker agri-food suppliers up to €350m turnover and bans or conditions 16 buyer practices, including late payment, unilateral contract changes, payments unrelated to a transaction, and certain listing, promotion, marketing and advertising charges. The UK Groceries Code separately requires prompt payment and evidence/notice around deductions. In the UK regulator's 2025 supplier survey, 17% of respondents still reported inadequate processes for resolving invoice discrepancies promptly and 11% reported payment delays. Sources:

- [European Commission — unfair trading practices in the food chain](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/common-agricultural-policy/agri-food-supply-chain/unfair-trading-practices_en)
- [UK Groceries Supply Code of Practice](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/groceries-supply-code-of-practice/groceries-supply-code-of-practice)
- [UK Groceries Code Adjudicator 2025 survey results](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/gca-survey-shows-continued-improved-treatment-of-grocery-suppliers)

US incumbents prove the economic activity but also define the copy risk. SPS Commerce says its recovery product connects retailer portals, carrier/3PL systems and file stores to validate and file claims; HRG claims more than $1bn recovered. Their named retailer coverage and case material are heavily North-American. This does not prove an open European market, but it proves that suppliers pay for recovery and that software alone is not enough. Sources:

- [SPS Commerce Revenue Recovery](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/revenue-recovery/)
- [HRG retail deduction recovery](https://www.hrg-audit.com/)
- [SPS case study showing $677,000/year written off below a small-dollar threshold](https://go.supplypike.com/hubfs/Marketing%20Assets/One-Pagers/Case%20Studies%20%2B%20Social%20Proof/WM-GetBetter-Anon-CaseStudy.pdf)

### Beachhead, Direct Payer And Cold Start

The beachhead is Polish and CEE food/drink manufacturers with €5m–€100m turnover that directly supply one of 14 UK designated grocers or one selected EU grocer and have at least 200 deduction lines or €100,000 disputed value in the preceding 12 months. UK is attractive for the first evidence grammar because the Groceries Code and regulator guidance are public; CEE suppliers are the reachable seller pool. The founder must exclude the current employer, its affiliates, its direct competitors and any confidential employer knowledge, and obtain written employer/conflict clearance before outreach.

The acquisition mechanism is evidence-led outbound from public exhibitor/exporter lists. The founder offers a fixed-scope, paid 30-day backlog triage for one retailer and one legal entity:

1. supplier uploads redacted remittance/deduction exports plus its own contracts and delivery evidence into a segregated workspace;
2. MarginLedger classifies no more than 100 lines and produces an evidence/missing-document map;
3. the supplier prepays 4,000 PLN for the audit, creditable against a recovery mandate;
4. only after written authority, MarginLedger files selected disputes and earns 15%–22% of cash actually repaid or deductions actually prevented, with a monthly floor after the first recovery.

The 180-day test is three unrelated prepaid audits, two signed limited agency/data-rights mandates, at least 50 submitted claims, one actual repayment and positive contribution margin, with no more than 36,000 PLN at risk. A free audit, LOI, claimed approval or unverified receivable is not paid proof.

### Foundational Resource And Twelve-Month Bootstrap

The first control point is not a retailer partnership. It is a supplier-signed, retailer-specific mandate granting limited portal/workflow access, claim-submission authority, a success fee over actual recovered cash, and permission to retain anonymised non-customer-identifying features of claim type, evidence, retailer response, timing and resolution. The founder can contract this directly with the payer. Each additional resolved case improves evidence selection, expected recovery, deadline logic and prevention recommendations for the same retailer lane.

Within 12 months and 100,000 PLN the founder can secure: three to ten signed mandates; a lawyer-reviewed processing/agency agreement; a segregated evidence store; an auditable claim ledger; one retailer-country outcome taxonomy; and paid outcome records. The founder uses manual review plus simple extraction first, one part-time AR/retail contractor, and outside counsel only for fixed-scope legal interpretation. No retailer, law-firm, data-vendor or ERP partnership is required to prove the wedge.

Immediate resource kill: a supplier's retailer terms prohibit delegated portal access or claim filing and no compliant client-operated submission workflow exists; customers refuse outcome-metadata rights; or the first 100-line audit cannot distinguish a recoverable class without bespoke legal work per line.

### Compounding Advantage, Copy Risk And Expansion

Current control is only prospective until the first mandate; after a valid mandate it is Tier 1 for that client's claim flow and Tier 2 at company level if reusable outcome rights are contractual and the case library reduces work and raises recovery. The strongest copy risk is SPS Commerce, a European EDI/AP vendor, a retailer consultant or an accounting BPO localising the same offer. The defence cannot be “AI.” It must be the fastest evidence-complete recovery in one narrow retailer-country lane, a case-level record of what was repaid, reusable supplier-side evidence mappings, and a success-priced acquisition engine that produces proprietary labels.

Expansion is deliberately sequential: one UK grocer lane for CEE suppliers → three UK grocers using the same evidence objects → one Polish or German lane only after legal/data transfer is demonstrated → deduction prevention and trade-spend reconciliation → supplier margin intelligence. Kill if every retailer-country pair requires fresh bespoke legal analysis, security approval and portal engineering, or if incumbents already cover the exact CEE-to-UK wedge at comparable outcome pricing.

### Facts Required Before Advisory Evaluation

1. Conflict-of-interest and employer approval that permits the founder to operate this business without using employer data or relationships.
2. A bottom-up list of at least 12,000 named eligible European suppliers, with a separately counted first 500-account reachable list.
3. Fifteen supplier-finance interviews using actual anonymised deduction exports; at least five show both €100,000+ annual disputed value and inability to process it internally.
4. Written retailer portal/contract review showing a legal delegated or client-operated filing path.
5. Three 4,000 PLN prepayments and at least two success-fee mandates with anonymised outcome-data rights.
6. Competitive calls proving that SPS/other recovery operators do not already serve the exact first lane economically.
7. Actual recovery cycle time, win rate, human minutes per line, realised annual value, and contribution margin.

## Direction 2 — Freight Invoice Recovery For Polish Exporters

**Generation decision: reject.** Freight invoice audit and recovery is a mature contingency-fee service, and the founder would need client freight data but would not obtain a distinctive transaction right.
