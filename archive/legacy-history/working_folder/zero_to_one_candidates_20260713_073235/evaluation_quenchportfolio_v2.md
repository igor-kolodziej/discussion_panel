# Zero-to-One Evaluation: QuenchPortfolio Integrator Licence v2

## Diagnosis

QuenchPortfolio materially improves the original by controlling a coherent granted-plus-European patent/know-how package, making a technical inventor commitment mandatory, and selling first to an integrator with a live plant project rather than asking an unknown founder to win plant trust and build the whole treatment system. The €20,000+ payment tests willingness before licence exercise or hardware, and integrator finance contains capital. Real-effluent economics remain a hard technical unknown, but that is exactly what the small paid proof tests. The protected product/royalty model and 110-site conservative scale are sufficient to pursue a bounded proof, not to claim validation.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official/public evidence establishes the granted Polish patent in force, a related pending European application/PCT family, a public TTO commercialisation route, TRL-4 multi-antibiotic laboratory work, WHO concern and EU treatment momentum. Option/licence price, co-owner authority, inventor participation, integrator payment, real-effluent performance, module economics and eligible site count remain hypotheses. The original 68-point advisory review is diagnosis, not market evidence; no panel or chatbot verdict exists for v2.

## Overall Score

**77/100.** Weighted points are 154.5 across 20 weight units; `10 × 154.5 / 20 = 77.25`, rounded to 77. This is advisory only and is not a simulated score.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 after the signed portfolio option/licence; presently conditional.** The complete field licence, know-how, inventor commitment, improvement rights, project registration and retained performance data control scarce rights rather than equipment alone. Strongest copy risk is a non-infringing ozone/electrochemical/plasma design with better real-effluent lifecycle economics. The control cannot be copied through one vendor purchase if the option covers the claims and improvements as written.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** The conservative case reaches about €11.3m founder equity in year 15 after €3m obligations and 45% dilution, without adding distributions. Fifteen integrators, 110 sites and 180 modules across Europe over 15 years are not obviously heroic, but the case has only 13% cushion and fails below 30% operating margin, 50% founder ownership or valid European control. Expected/strong cases include greater capital and dilution. Bottom-up market proof remains a kill gate rather than an assumed fact.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass, narrowly.** A >=20,000 PLN integrator prepayment tied to a named client stream is budget-backed proof inside 180 days. Founder loss is <=38,000 PLN, work remains part-time, and no field skid/full product precedes payment. IP owners, inventor/lab and integrator are sequential controlled dependencies; the signed option/protocol must exist before outbound and payment before licence exercise.

## Expansion Check

The first stream is narrow and an accepted module gives adjacent integrators a credible precedent. Integrators supply installed trust and project finance, while the company retains the technology layer. Poland is an effective proof base; European defensibility depends on pending claims and new improvements. Mature AOP vendors can enter the customer problem but cannot use the exact protected package if control is properly secured.

## Fatal Objections

None identified before the bounded paid-proof attempt. The following become fatal if observed: no coherent co-owner option, no integrator prepayment after 40 approaches, no inventor commitment, unsafe/inferior real-effluent results, or a conservative site/ownership/margin bridge that loses the €10m cushion.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Make the trusted integrator pay for a live-client real-stream decision while the company retains the protected reactor/control and learning layer.
