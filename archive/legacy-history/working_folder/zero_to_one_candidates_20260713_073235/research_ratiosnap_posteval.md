# RatioSnap Decision-Critical Research After Advisory Evaluation

## Findings

- Glenmarc publicly states it has more than 10,000 meter-mix/dispense machines installed worldwide. This is one supplier, not the market total.
- Graco, Nordson, METER MIX, Fluid Research, Polymer-G and multiple regional integrators actively sell 2K systems across bonding, sealing, potting and encapsulation. Polymer-G alone states more than 100 automatic installations. No reliable aggregate count was found.
- Graco's current guidance still prescribes routine last-point two-container ratio checks even while it sells advanced systems with downloadable process data. Other current manuals continue to use two cups/containers. Manual last-point verification therefore persists; it is not merely obsolete-equipment folklore.
- Higher-end systems such as Graco ProMix expose ratio alarms, and equipment vendors emphasize accurate on-ratio dispense. The addressable subset is not every installed 2K system; it is cells where an independent physical check is still performed and valuable.
- No current search found a keyed disposable dual-stream cassette with simultaneous automated tare/mass, recipe conversion and signed record. This is negative search evidence, not freedom-to-operate proof.

## Denominator Interpretation

One supplier's 10,000 installed machines plus several global peers makes a 40,000 total global 2K meter-mix-cell hypothesis plausible in order of magnitude. It does not prove that 25,000 are compatible or that 6,000 will buy. The conservative case therefore requires RatioSnap to become a category leader in a cross-brand manual-check subset, not merely a minor accessory.
