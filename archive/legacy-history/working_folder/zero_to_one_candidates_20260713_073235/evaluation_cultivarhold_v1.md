# Zero-to-One Evaluation: CultivarHold EU Plant-Variety Licence

## Diagnosis

CultivarHold can own real reproductive IP and uses a paid propagator order to gate multiplication. The problem is that visible ornamental traits are easy to design around with competing cultivars, and buyer qualification, mother-stock health, propagation performance, and catalogue timing may exceed one season. The wealth cases require 18–40 successful cultivars and millions of propagations each without market evidence. The shape is too rights- and biology-dependent for the founder and should not be panelled.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official CPVO evidence proves licence mechanics, term and asset-class size. Everything commercial—option price, underdistribution, trait premium, buyer order, units, royalty, mother stock, qualification, enforcement and portfolio scale—is hypothetical.

## Overall Score

**65/100.** Weighted points `130 ÷ 20 × 10 = 65`. Advisory only.

## Control Point

**Tier 1 after recorded exclusive licence and authenticated stock.** Strongest copy risk is a nursery substitutes a similar unlicensed cultivar or competitor variety, avoiding the right without copying the genotype.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** Required scenario fields exist, but 18 cultivars each producing 1.8m annual licensed units in the conservative case is unsupported and heroically assumes repeated rights, buyer placement and enforcement.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass narrowly.** One option and paid propagator order can fit 34,000 PLN/180 days while employed if the trait is immediately visible. Seasonal availability and qualification can make the schedule fail.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is not sufficiently specific. Related cultivar portfolios can share channels, but each variety resets biological and catalogue proof. EU-wide rights help expansion; design-around remains easy.

## Fatal Objections

1. No named right or crop/trait with buyer evidence.
2. Wealth requires heroic repeated catalogue success.
3. Founder lacks horticultural credibility and depends on specialists.
4. Competing cultivars can bypass the control point.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Exercise reproductive IP only after authenticated stock and a paid propagator allocation exist.

kill
