# Zero-to-One Evaluation: BlindProof

Advisory evaluation only. This is not a simulated-panel score, real-chat score, validation result, or confirmation. The candidate is judged as written and is not rewritten here.

## Diagnosis

BlindProof contains a real product insight: software-only tracking cannot create a blind comparison, and a seller paid for the decision can be more neutral than a supplement brand paid for continued consumption. The exact first SKU is nevertheless undermined by its own strongest cited study: a 60-person, 12-week melatonin/placebo series found no significant improvement, low heterogeneity of treatment effects, and concluded that melatonin may not be an appropriate personalized N-of-1 target. The business also asks an unknown consumer brand to earn ingestible-product, privacy, statistical, and health-communication trust while charging 699 PLN to decide whether to continue a comparatively cheap supplement; neither willingness to pay for a null/inconclusive result nor paid repeat use is evidenced. Reachable deposits are possible under the staged plan, but the conservative founder-wealth case depends on heroic acquisition, repeat-frequency, protocol-breadth and margin assumptions, while the proposed data asset does not yet create switching costs.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Sourced evidence

- EU rules allow the health claim that 1 mg melatonin taken close to bedtime contributes to reducing the time taken to fall asleep. They do **not** establish that a commercial active/placebo sequence plus personalized inference is an ordinary supplement product/service, or authorize broader individualized “benefit” claims across Oura outcomes. [EU Register consolidation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?qid=1773248154746&uri=CELEX%3A02012R0432-20250820)
- EU guidance defines a food supplement as a concentrated source of nutrients or other substances with a nutritional or physiological effect. A cellulose-only control capsule does not obviously satisfy that definition on its own; the legal treatment of the combined, coded active/control sequence and required disclosures is therefore an unresolved, product-specific question, not an established notification path. [European Commission food-supplement guidance](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/food-supplements_en)
- Under EU MDR Rule 11, software intended to inform diagnostic or therapeutic decisions can be a medical device. Intended purpose and claims are decisive. Calling the output a purchasing decision and excluding disease may keep it outside that boundary, but the exact copy, report, user selection and use of physiological data require written specialist advice; the candidate cannot treat non-device status as already secured. [Regulation (EU) 2017/745](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj?locale=en)
- The cited personalized melatonin trial demonstrates operational feasibility and participant acceptability, but it is adverse evidence for effect detectability and personalization. It compared 0.5 mg and 3 mg melatonin with placebo over 12 intervention weeks, found no significant improvement in device-measured sleep duration or self-reported sleep quality, found low treatment-effect heterogeneity, and stated that future personalized N-of-1 use of melatonin may not be needed. Participants' high satisfaction and recommendation rates were recorded in a research setting; they do not demonstrate payment of 699 PLN or purchase of a second test. [Published trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12468169/)
- Oura has reasonable population-level validation against polysomnography for several global sleep measures, including sleep-onset latency in one Gen3 study, but this does not establish the minimum detectable **within-person causal effect** for the proposed 1 mg protocol or protect the experiment from proprietary algorithm changes. [Gen3 validation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382312/)
- One Polish manufacturer advertises a minimum of 250 units **per product**, not 30–50 serialized, randomized active/placebo sequences; another advertises pilot batches but publishes no MOQ, price, matching method or serial-randomization capability. These pages support vendor availability, not the candidate's 5,000 PLN sample budget, 175 PLN variable cost, or <=40,000 PLN delivered-cohort exposure. [OSSEA](https://ossea.pl/) and [Global Pharma](https://globalpharma.pl/en/offer/hard-capsules/)

### Candidate hypotheses, not evidence

- At least 60% of qualified prospects value a truthful null or inconclusive result at >=599 PLN.
- Twenty full payments or 30 deposits can be acquired with <=12,000 PLN, despite the founder lacking an established health brand, clinician channel, influencer reach or wearable partnership.
- The specific 1 mg protocol yields at least 75% usable results and an acceptable inconclusive rate.
- At least 35% of usable completers reserve a different second protocol and mature customers buy 2.2 tests per year.
- A sufficiently large library of safe, reversible, perceptually matchable interventions exists with effects detectable by consumer wearables and qualification costs repayable inside 12 months.
- Mature CAC is <=25% of first-test revenue, even though the initial acquisition release gate permits up to 12,000 PLN for only 20 full payments (up to 600 PLN per payer, 86% of the 699 PLN price) or 30 deposits (up to 400 PLN per deposit).
- The first 75 usable completions create a response graph that materially reduces future inconclusive rates or CAC. One protocol does not establish cross-protocol prediction.
- Forty-five thousand annual active customers can be reached without privileged distribution, and 2.2 annual tests can be sustained despite each user's finite set of eligible interventions.

### Consequential missing facts

1. A written Polish opinion classifying the exact outer package, control capsules, coded daily presentation, personalized result, permitted language, liability model and data use.
2. Signed quotes for two exactly matched formulations, blind-integrity testing, randomized serial packing, stability/QA, notification support, lead time and a 30–50-sequence or other affordable first batch.
3. A predeclared primary outcome and power/minimum-detectable-effect analysis using real Oura within-person variance, with an explicit threshold for a result that changes a 699 PLN purchasing decision.
4. Paid behavior after seeing null and inconclusive mock results, not broad supplement interest or free-study satisfaction.
5. A second eligible protocol proving repeat demand; a reservation made before that protocol's legal, manufacturing and detectability qualification is weak evidence.
6. Exact-intent Polish search volume, analyser-to-deposit conversion, refund rate, full-payment conversion, support minutes and cohort contribution after CAC.
7. A bottom-up annual-customer model by country and channel. Five million Oura members are a device-installed base, not a qualified buyer denominator.

## Overall Score

**43/100.** Weighted points are 85 out of a possible 200: `10 × (85 / 20) = 42.5`, rounded to 43. This is an advisory score only. The failed Founder Wealth Gate independently prohibits a `pursue` recommendation.

## Control Point

**Current tier: Tier 3. Attainable tier: weak Tier 2 only after evidence not yet present.**

Today the protocol, software, manufacturer access, legal work and analyser are purchasable or reproducible. A prepaid cohort and consented response data would be owned/controlled, but one protocol with 75 usable completions does not establish a cross-protocol response graph, lower inconclusive rates, switching cost or durable distribution. Tier 2 becomes plausible only if multiple paid protocols show that accumulated data measurably improves eligibility, effect detectability, repeat selection or CAC and customers actually return.

**Strongest copy risk:** Oura or another wearable/app company can add a fixed N-of-1 workflow and contract one capsule manufacturer, while entering with an installed user relationship, device data access and greater trust. A supplement or clinical-trial operator can also supply the same physical sequence. No exclusive ingredient, rights, distribution, manufacturing capacity or transaction flow prevents this.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The scenarios correctly avoid double-counting retained earnings and state pricing, revenue, margins, capital, ownership, dilution, reinvestment, value and timing. The failure is in the commercial bridge:

- The “conservative” case requires 45,000 annual active customers and 99,000 annual tests at 2.2 tests per customer. The cited five-million-member Oura trajectory does not establish how many users take the specific supplement, distrust their own observation enough to want a blinded experiment, accept 10–12 weeks, qualify on baseline data, pay about 699 PLN, accept null/inconclusive output and repeat.
- The first test decides whether to continue an inexpensive commodity supplement. Avoided supplement spend alone is unlikely to justify the price, so value must come from health certainty and scientific identity—benefits that require substantially more trust and may create stricter claim/classification risk.
- Annual repeat use is structurally constrained. After testing a finite set of fast, reversible, safe and wearable-detectable interventions, customers can lapse. The candidate has no evidence that 2.2 tests per year persists rather than occurring in one short experimentation phase.
- The response-data moat is asserted at the same time as 68% gross margin, 22% operating margin, low CAC and limited support. Qualification, country review, safety, refunds, customer education and inconclusive results can all erode those margins.
- The 5.5x operating-profit value is not inherently extreme, but it only produces €12.94m founder equity after the other optimistic assumptions align. It is therefore a thin, assumption-stacked clearance rather than a conservative wealth case.

The expected and strong cases scale the same unproven acquisition and repeat mechanism to 100,000 and 250,000 annual active customers; they do not repair the conservative case. The wealth gate can only be reconsidered after paid repeat behavior, a proven second/third protocol, channel-level CAC and a bottom-up multi-country eligible-buyer model exist. Those facts are absent from the exact candidate.

## Reachability Gate

**PASS, narrowly and only for paid deposit/preorder evidence—not for successful product delivery.**

The founder can keep employment and spend at most 12,000 PLN first on a written boundary opinion, manufacturer feasibility, interviews and a lightweight analyser. Five explicit 199 PLN deposits from 20 qualified users can count as paid evidence; the plan can stop before manufacture. The next tranche remains within 24,000 PLN until 30 deposits or 20 full prepayments, and the stated net-exposure ceiling through first delivery is 40,000 PLN.

This pass is fragile:

- Non-device/non-trial/food classification for the exact combined offer is unresolved.
- The only quantified manufacturer minimum found is 250 units per product, while the first delivery assumes 30–50 exact randomized sequences; a second vendor says pilot batches are possible without publishing MOQ or price.
- An unknown founder must earn trust simultaneously for ingestible manufacture, exclusion/safety rules, wearable-data handling and statistical inference.
- Spending the full 12,000 PLN acquisition cap for 20 full payments implies up to 600 PLN CAC per payer, leaving negative contribution against the candidate's stated 524 PLN pre-acquisition contribution. The production release gate must therefore be read together with positive projected cohort contribution, even though the candidate does not require that until delivery.
- A paid second-test reservation does not prove repeat economics unless a lawful, detectable and manufacturable second protocol is already concrete.

The gate fails immediately if counsel requires a medical-device, medicinal-product, clinical-trial, pharmacy or clinician-supervised route; if exact paired production exceeds the capital ceiling; or if payment requires promising a favorable outcome.

## Expansion Check

The beachhead is narrow enough to test but not yet economically meaningful enough to dominate: Polish Oura-owning melatonin users who accept a long blind protocol and an uncertain conclusion are a subset of a subset, with no measured search or payment denominator. Moving to another supplement is not merely adding a SKU; it resets effect size, reversibility, carryover/washout, safe exclusions, perceptual matching, claims, outcome validity, inconclusive-rate and manufacturing qualification. Moving to Germany or another EU state can reset notification and legal review.

The manufactured sequence gives a better experiment than app-only competitors, but broad wearable platforms have the installed relationship and data access, while contract research and supplement manufacturers have operational credibility. Poland lowers execution cost but does not block entry. The candidate has not shown asymmetric outward expansion: every new protocol requires work the broad entrant can also buy, while the founder's data only compounds if cross-protocol prediction is empirically demonstrated.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The first scientific wedge is contradicted by the closest trial.** The cited 60-person series found no meaningful melatonin/placebo differences, low individual heterogeneity and concluded melatonin may not need personalized N-of-1 testing. This attacks both result detectability and the rationale for personalization.
2. **Founder Wealth fails on heroic recurring demand.** Forty-five thousand annual active customers at 2.2 paid tests each is not supported by a qualified denominator, repeat evidence or a bottom-up acquisition model. Oura's installed base cannot substitute for these facts.
3. **Willingness to pay is economically weak and unproved.** A 699 PLN experiment to decide whether to continue a cheap supplement has poor direct financial payback. Free research-study satisfaction and one community request do not prove payment, null-result value or repeat purchase.
4. **Legal classification is existential and exact-product-specific.** The active supplement has an authorized narrow claim, but the cellulose control, coded presentation and personalized result may cross food-information, health-claim or medical-device boundaries. A generic notification path is not proof.
5. **The first-batch feasibility and unit costs are unsupported.** Advertised standard MOQs/pilot capability do not establish affordable, serial-randomized, perceptually matched active/control production and QA inside the cap.
6. **The purported data moat is prospective and easy to pre-empt.** One cohort cannot show that response data reduces inconclusive rates or predicts another protocol; there is no switching cost or exclusive access, and an installed wearable platform can copy the product through one manufacturer contract.
7. **Trust and CAC stack rather than offset.** The founder must acquire a small exact-intent audience and convince it to ingest a novel coded product, upload sensitive data and accept an uncertain statistical answer. The acquisition test permits economics far worse than the mature model.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

The strongest insight is the incentive alignment: a neutral provider paid for a purchasing decision can credibly value a null result, whereas a supplement seller benefits from continued consumption. The physical matched sequence also solves a real limitation that software-only N-of-1 trackers cannot solve. This insight is worth preserving in future structural work, but it does not rescue the exact melatonin DTC testing business as written.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Kill this exact business shape rather than cosmetically changing dose, wearable, report wording, CAC target or customer-count arithmetic. The first intervention's closest evidence undermines individual detectability, while the DTC null-result value, repeat frequency and response-data moat are all unproved and jointly required for founder wealth. Any future use of the salvageable neutrality/blinding insight should be handled by the pivot skill as a material structural change and then returned for a new advisory evaluation.
