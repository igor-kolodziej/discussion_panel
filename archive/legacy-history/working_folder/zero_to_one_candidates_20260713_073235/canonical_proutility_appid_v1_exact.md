# Canonical Candidate — ProUtility AppID v1

Status: frozen for advisory evaluation; not panel-ready.  
Success definition: 1,000,000 PLN of recognised annual revenue by Year 5 and at least 25% fully loaded operating margin. This replaces the original founder-wealth gate for this candidate.

## Thesis And Control

Acquire one professionally useful Android app whose Google Play App ID, store listing, users, ratings, reviews and subscriptions transfer formally. The purchase must include source/IP, backend, domain, analytics history, support records, brand and payment configuration. Improve one repeated high-intent workflow and sell an annual Pro subscription through the inherited listing. Code without the transferred App ID and subscriber relationship is worthless.

Google's official transfer process preserves users, statistics, comments, ratings and subscriptions, but historical payout/earnings reports and pre-transfer orders do not move. The seller must therefore provide a complete financial export and remain contractually responsible for pre-transfer refunds and reporting. Current control is Tier 3 until an exact transfer completes; after transfer, the App ID/update route and subscriber relationship are Tier 1. The strongest copy risk is a better-funded app buying traffic against the same keywords or adding the feature set in one release.

## Target Specification And Public Evidence

The target must have at least 200 verified paying subscribers, at least 75% twelve-month paid retention, at least 10,000 monthly organic store visits or downloads, a rating of at least 4.4 from at least 500 reviews, no policy strikes or content/IP risk, positive contribution margin, and less than eight support hours per week. Cash at closing is capped at 22,000 PLN; diligence and transfer at 8,000 PLN; a 10,000 PLN continuity reserve completes the pre-proof 40,000 PLN cap. Additional purchase consideration is capped at 60,000 PLN and paid only from 20% of collected post-transfer receipts.

The public sell-side market is real, but inspected sub-cap listings do not meet this specification. Jawsi asks USD 10,000 and reports roughly USD 1,296 monthly profit and 4,000 monthly downloads, but has only a few public reviews and no verified cohort disclosure. ELO AI asks USD 10,000 and reports about USD 8,000 ARR from 48 subscribers and 866 monthly downloads, with inconsistent profit presentation. Lyrix asks USD 10,000, has more than 100,000 installs and 36,000 users, but only about USD 150 monthly profit and content-rights risk. Pharmacy Connect asks USD 8,000 and has more than 100 installs but no revenue. KPass has a strong 100,000-install/10,400-review store asset and about EUR 898 monthly revenue, but asks EUR 30,000—above total founder capital before diligence—and competes in a high-trust security category.

No seller was contacted. No three public targets meet the entry specification.

## Product, Customer And Distribution

The acceptable customer is a professional who repeatedly performs a measurable mobile workflow and already searches the app store for it: field measurement, inspection capture, specialised calculation, offline document preparation or another non-regulated job. Health diagnosis, password/security, children's content, dating, entertainment and copyright-dependent media are excluded.

The first distribution is the transferred App ID, organic search history, ratings and subscriber cohort. The wedge is a paid reliability/offline/export workflow, not a generic AI chat feature. Later compounding can come from workflow templates, user-created artefacts and compatibility knowledge, but only with explicit rights and without selling personal data.

Store-wide install counts are not a reachable paid market.

## Reachability And Kill Rules

By Day 180 after transfer, at least 20 independent subscribers must renew and at least 20 must buy the improved Pro offer; contribution margin must be positive after all store fees, refunds, support and contractor labour. The founder remains employed and works no more than five hours per day.

No more than 40,000 PLN can be committed before this proof. Further founder capital requires successful delivery and positive contribution. The final tranche requires repeat demand or a measured acquisition channel. Family capital is excluded.

Kill if fewer than three exact targets meet the specification, if the App ID/subscriptions/source/backend do not transfer atomically, if price plus diligence exceeds the pre-proof cap, if the app depends on policy-unsafe content or high-trust claims, if fewer than 40 qualifying post-transfer payments occur in 180 days, or if the expected case needs more than 2,100 subscribers at 480 PLN or less than 25% operating margin.

## Current Evidence Verdict Embedded In The Candidate

Formal transfer and public sell-side inventory are real. A qualifying target, affordable complete transaction, retained cohort and credible bridge to 2,100 subscribers are not. The candidate does not presently claim that the revenue-quality or Reachability Gate has passed.
