# Zero-to-One Candidate: QuenchPortfolio Integrator Licence

## One-Sentence Thesis

Create an inventor-backed spinout around the complete Polish-plus-European cold-plasma antibiotic-degradation portfolio, require an industrial-water integrator to prepay a real-client effluent qualification before licence exercise, and scale through protected reactor/control packages and project royalties rather than founder-built treatment plants.

## Founder Fit

The Warsaw data-scientist founder can map integrators and pharmaceutical/veterinary sites, design multivariate experiments, analyse energy/activity/transformation data, automate account research and build the performance corpus. He cannot credibly own plasma engineering, environmental chemistry or field safety alone.

The non-negotiable POC stack is the founder, one named inventor/plasma engineer under equity or deliverable contract, the university owners/TTO, an accredited analytical lab and one industrial-water integrator's process team. The founder remains at 15–20 hours/week. Inventor commitment and integrator field capability replace founder technical theatre; if neither signs, the idea stops.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

The first economic buyer is a CEE/EU industrial-water integrator with a live pharmaceutical or veterinary-production client whose segregated antibiotic stream is expensive to dispose of or difficult to treat. The integrator wants to win or retain the plant project with documented destruction of parent compounds and antibacterial activity, not a plasma demonstration.

Current alternatives include off-site hazardous-liquid destruction, dilution into a larger biological plant, carbon, ozone, UV/oxidant, membranes, electrochemical oxidation and other advanced oxidation. Any can win. QuenchPortfolio matters only where the licensed brush/reactor produces a lower lifecycle cost, lower secondary waste, better activity destruction or more controllable response in the named matrix.

## Product Wedge

The first sale is a €5,000–€10,000 prepaid **Integrator Client-Stream Qualification**. The integrator names the client opportunity and sample, signs data/confidentiality terms and funds independent testing. The licensed inventor/lab team measures parent antibiotics, residual antibacterial activity, relevant COD/TOC/matrix effects, targeted transformation products/ecotoxicity, helium/energy/electrode use and comparison with the integrator's best feasible alternative.

A passed result opens a customer-funded €50,000–€100,000 engineered field pilot. At maturity QuenchPortfolio sells/licences the protected plasma brush/reactor/control package at roughly €70,000–€80,000 per module, earns 8–12% on the integrator's project value attributable to the technology, and sells €15,000–€25,000 annual electrode/control/verification support. The integrator engineers, finances, installs and services the balance of plant.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

The lab-to-market gap is not lack of antibiotic-removal papers. It is that no plant or integrator can price a process from clean-water removal percentages. Each real stream changes activity, energy, by-products, electrode/gas use and pretreatment needs.

The founder's company is useful only if it controls a coherent patent/know-how/improvement package and turns paid client streams into a comparable performance system. This lets integrators quote a protected module rather than run an unfunded science project. The integrator—not an unknown founder—carries the existing plant trust, installation and service relationship.

- WHO's 2024 manufacturing guidance and Directive (EU) 2024/3019 create AMR/treatment attention and budgets. Neither selects cold plasma or compels an integrator to pay.

## Beachhead Market

One Polish/Czech/Slovak/German industrial-water integrator, one live antibiotic API/veterinary/formulation client, one recurring segregated aqueous stream and one antibiotic family within or close to the published portfolio. Exclude municipal tenders, dilute hospital sewage, general wastewater, streams without a budget owner and buyers seeking a free demo.

The first 12-month proof is the complete portfolio option, a named inventor commitment, one integrator prepayment, a real-effluent result and a budget-backed field-pilot option. It does not require a certified production module.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

The first ten customers are integrators, not plants. Build a 40-integrator list from industrial-water project references and a parallel map of 60 likely antibiotic-production sites. Each approach names the patent portfolio and asks for one live difficult stream; no generic innovation pitch. One prepayment among 40 is the reachability test. Customers two and three must be unrelated integrators or distinct plant groups.

The first 100 plant installations arrive through 15–30 project-registered integrators after two accepted field modules. Direct account intelligence remains centrally owned; integrators receive project/territory protection only while meeting paid-study, module and service performance milestones.

## Primary Control Point

The Tier-1 resource exists only if one signed option/licence package covers:

- Pat.242412/P.431823 and the later P.440185/PCT/EP brush family for the defined industrial field;
- co-owner authority, territory/status schedule and rights to subcontract, sublicense and sell packages;
- exclusive access to undisclosed reactor/control know-how, samples and named inventor hours;
- ownership or exclusive field rights to company-funded scale-up/control/electrode improvements;
- project-registration/channel rights; and
- customer contracts permitting retention of specified anonymised performance features.

One public patent, a research collaboration or a promise to negotiate is Tier 3 and rejected. The strongest copy risk is a mature AOP vendor or non-infringing plasma design outperforming the portfolio in real effluent.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

1. IP counsel verifies the granted Polish right, pending EP family, claims/ownership, FTO red flags and whether both co-owners can grant one package.
2. Sign a 180-day portfolio option <=15,000 PLN with pre-agreed licence entry <=50,000 PLN, royalties, improvement rights and inventor support.
3. Contract one named inventor/plasma engineer for protocol delivery and a later equity/technical-lead path.
4. Fix accredited-lab prices and sample-safety rules before outbound.
5. Approach 40 qualified integrators with the exact named-client-stream qualification.
6. By day 180, require one unrelated integrator to prepay >=20,000 PLN (about €4,600) for a real-client qualification. No free pilot counts.
7. Convert the licence only after payment and only if the study can be completed inside the remaining original capital. By month 12 require a delivered result and integrator's written budget-backed field-pilot option.

Pre-proof founder risk is <=38,000 PLN. Total original founder capital through month 12 is <=100,000 PLN. No founder-owned lab, field skid or fabrication plant is built.

## Distribution Advantage

The integrator owns plant trust and engineering access; QuenchPortfolio owns the protected reactor/control product, study method and comparable evidence. A live project creates urgency, and study credit against a pilot reduces buyer risk. Project registration prevents channel conflict. Each result lets another integrator see a closer compound/matrix reference and reduces protocol uncertainty.

The channel is invalid if integrators merely ask the university directly, refuse technology royalties or demand free studies. The founder must add productisation, commercial speed, improvement IP, performance comparison and channel governance the TTO does not supply.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

1. One integrator/one stream/one paid real-effluent result.
2. One customer-funded field pilot and accepted performance protocol.
3. Two independent integrators and three modules in the same compound/matrix family.
4. Standard reactor/control package, toll fabrication and recurring verification.
5. Adjacent antibiotic families supported by paid studies.
6. European integrator network under project-registration and minimum-performance rights.
7. Municipal/hospital applications only after industrial reference economics and regulatory procurement evidence.

The company does not become a general water integrator. It retains the protected technology layer and data while partners finance site-specific civil/process work.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

Poland provides the granted right, inventors, lower-cost lab/engineering, founder proximity and the first integrator/site map. The later PCT/EP family offers a possible European protection path but is pending and must be counsel-verified. Export defence requires valid claims plus undisclosed know-how, improvement filings, reference modules and service data; Polish rights alone cannot support the wealth model.

## Compounding Advantage

Each paid study adds matrix composition, compound/activity destruction, by-products, energy, helium, electrode wear, control settings and lifecycle-cost comparison. Modules add BOM, safety, factory/field acceptance, service and improvement inventions. Integrators bring live projects; better precedents shorten qualification and improve guarantees; more modules fund improvements and make the licence harder to route around.

Conservative and expected cases reinvest 95% of after-tax cash available for distribution while the founder stays employed; optional distributions remain <=5%. Strong reinvests 100%. Reinvestment funds technical leadership, field evidence, improvement patents, control/electrode productisation, QA and integrator support. Customer deposits, integrator project finance, grants and later equipment debt fund modules; equity dilution/obligations are deducted above.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Remain employed until two accepted field modules, three independent paying integrators, contracted recurring support, six months positive contribution including founder replacement, a repeatable integrator acquisition signal, signed technical leadership and 12 months personal runway. One paid study or patent option never justifies leaving.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

Before licence exercise, reusable assets are the IP/FTO map, integrator/site account graph, protocol, lab quotes and rejection record. Conditional customer payment is refunded only under disclosed lab-safety/company-failure terms. No hardware is stranded. If real effluent fails, do not sell a compliance service; return or let the option expire. If one compound fails but another documented paid stream exists, require a new payment before testing it.

## Critical Hypotheses

- Both co-owners will grant the complete narrow field package <=50,000 PLN entry and accept founder/inventor economics.
- A live-project integrator will prepay an unknown spinout instead of approaching the TTO or using mature AOP.
- Helium/energy/electrode costs and transformation products permit a lifecycle advantage in real effluent.
- Project royalties and protected product packages retain 55%+ gross margin.
- The integrator channel supports at least 110 sites without heroic share or founder labour.

## Kill Criteria

- No complete portfolio/improvement/inventor option after one formal offer to both co-owners.
- Option >15,000 PLN, licence entry >50,000 PLN or pre-proof spend >38,000 PLN.
- No inventor/plasma engineer commitment before customer protocol.
- No >=20,000 PLN integrator prepayment after 40 qualified approaches by day 180.
- No live client stream or only generic innovation-scouting interest.
- Real effluent retains antibacterial activity, produces unacceptable by-products, or loses to the best alternative on lifecycle cost/risk.
- No budget-backed field pilot after the paid result, or field pilot requires founder cash beyond 100,000 PLN.
- No second unrelated integrator or third module after the first field acceptance.
- European claims/improvement rights cannot support export, operating margin <30%, founder ownership <50% in conservative bridge, or 110 sites is a heroic share.
