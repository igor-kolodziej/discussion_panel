# Zero-to-One Evaluation: WooRenewal v1 After Independent Panel

## Diagnosis

WooRenewal's atomic update-and-renewal asset is real, but both judges expose the same non-compensating wealth failure. The first acquisition can be a good small cash-flow business, yet the text calls a 600× paying-site increase with little acquisition capital “conservative” and supplies no evidence that cross-sell or organic growth bridges it. The stringent target filter also risks producing no affordable deal. The exact candidate cannot proceed unchanged.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official transfer mechanics and marketplace precedents are evidence of the asset class. Panel scores 71 and 76 are external judgments, not market evidence. No named qualifying target, seller terms, data room, cohort, transfer or payout exists. The wealth bridge and target funnel are hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**70/100.** Weighted points `140.5 ÷ 20 × 10 = 70.25`, rounded to 70. Advisory only.

## Control Point

Tier 1 after atomic transfer; Tier 3 before acquisition. Strongest copy risk is an incumbent suite bundles the feature through a larger installed base.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail.** The arithmetic deducts liabilities, but customer growth, asset count, pricing, capital and ownership do not form a credible conservative bridge. Both independent judges identify this as fatal.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass narrowly.** An escrowed <=40,000 PLN acquisition and first unrelated receipt can occur within 180 days while employed. Target scarcity and seller-note acceptance remain serious but sequenced.

## Expansion Check

The first niche is valid; the 60,000-site portfolio is not supported by repeated distribution or acquisition evidence. Global market access does not cure the capital/customer bridge.

## Fatal Objections

1. Founder Wealth Gate fails.
2. Affordable qualifying target may not exist.
3. Every later acquisition can reset sourcing and distribution.
4. First receipt is too weak to validate the wealth engine.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Acquire the atomic update, installed workflow and renewal rail—not code—and require escrowed transfer before cash release.
