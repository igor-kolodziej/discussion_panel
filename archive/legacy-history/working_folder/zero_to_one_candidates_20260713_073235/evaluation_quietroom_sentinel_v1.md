# Zero-to-One Evaluation: QuietRoom Sentinel

## Diagnosis

QuietRoom is unusually honest about incumbents and proposes a rigorous blinded technical gate, but the business as written depends on a speculative passive-geometry breakthrough before the first customer can pay. Even if the cartridge beats purchased comparators in an insectary, the candidate does not establish that hotel incidence, avoided loss, specificity, housekeeping adherence, or annual willingness to pay supports €44 per bed place, four replacements per year, and 400,000 active positions. The reusable holder, tooling, protocol, published evidence, and prepaid cohort could become an operating asset, but not a scarce control point: passive-monitor vendors and pest-service incumbents can copy the geometry, improve a lure, or bundle monitoring into an existing trusted contract. Both the Founder Wealth Gate and Reachability Gate fail, so the exact candidate should not enter an independent panel.

## Evidence and Assumptions

### Evidence supported by the candidate's sources

- Eurostat provides a large, correctly labelled capacity denominator: 12.90m EU hotel bed places and 6.17m hotel bedrooms in 2024. It does **not** establish that every bed place needs a separate monitor, that every hotel room is physically compatible, or that the hotel has sufficient bed-bug risk and budget.
- ANSES supports material household exposure and cost and the value of early/non-chemical action. The candidate correctly acknowledges that ANSES lacks a quantitative hotel-infestation denominator and that household attribution to travel is not hotel incidence.
- EPA's inexpensive interceptor guidance and the identified SenSci, Nattaro, Spotta, and Rentokil offers prove both a real monitoring category and strong substitutes ranging from cheap passive devices to lure-based and service-bundled products.
- Existing 90-day lure products support recurring replacement where an attractant depletes. They do not support quarterly replacement of a geometry-and-material-only passive cartridge.
- A non-electronic, non-pesticidal product can avoid some biocidal complexity, but exact General Product Safety Regulation, claims, product-liability, design-right, and patent treatment still requires written review.

### Candidate hypotheses, not evidence

- A passive dual-path geometry without lure detects at least 70% of one-bug/low-density arenas by day 14, beats the best purchased passive/lure comparator by at least 15 percentage points, remains competitive by day 28, prevents escape, and replicates in a supplier batch.
- That artificial-arena advantage transfers to occupied rooms where harbourage, host cues, furniture geometry, cleaning, placement, guest belongings, temperature, competing refuges, and introduction timing vary.
- Two monitoring positions on a double bed detect meaningfully more than one correctly placed position and justify charging by bed place rather than room, bed frame, or risk zone.
- A qualifying hotel's annual avoided cost is at least three times €44 per position and the hotel attributes enough of that value to continuous detection rather than treatment response, staff training, or an existing pest contract.
- Hotels prepay 8,000 PLN for a 100-position, 60-day operational beta after no more than roughly 20 days of post-efficacy selling, and one converts to an annual prepayment by day 180.
- Housekeeping completes more than 90% of weekly checks in 15 seconds per position despite staff turnover, hidden placement, two positions on double beds, paper/existing-system recording, closure, cartridge storage, and escalation.
- Suspected positives are sufficiently specific. No false-alarm rate, non-target capture threshold, debris/visibility standard, or pest-professional confirmation cost is included, even though housekeeping is expressly not allowed to identify insects.
- Four passive cartridges per year create durable renewal value, position renewal remains at least 75% without a recent infestation, and hotels do not simply clean/reuse the holder, extend replacement intervals, or switch to a cheaper monitor.
- Delivered first-year COGS reaches €19, mature gross margin reaches 65%, direct CAC falls below €15 per activated position, and the company preserves 20% operating margin while supporting multilingual direct sales, liability, QA, replacements, complaints, and account renewal.

### Consequential missing facts

1. No independent efficacy result exists, and no evidence supports the key claim that unbaited passive geometry can materially outperform both lure products and cheap interceptors at low density.
2. No hotel-specific incidence distribution, expected avoided cost, monitoring budget, procurement cycle, or price evidence supports annual whole-property deployment.
3. The eligible denominator is not known. The raw 12.90m bed places include rooms that lack compatible placement, properties without a recent incident, accounts already served by a pest contractor, and hotels unwilling to run a weekly housekeeping workflow.
4. One position per bed place is a commercial convention, not a validated biological or operational requirement. Eurostat's statistical definition does not prove that two positions are needed on a double bed.
5. Quarterly passive-cartridge consumption lacks a physical depletion mechanism or demonstrated customer need.
6. There is no natural-room sensitivity, time-to-detection, false-negative, false-positive/non-target, or avoided-spread result. An insectary detection rate cannot alone quantify hotel ROI.
7. No buyer evidence shows hotels will purchase directly from an unknown product founder without pest-controller endorsement, site inspection, treatment integration, or a known liability-bearing supplier.
8. The 13,000 PLN insectary budget, powered arena count, 28-day duration, supplier-batch replication, entomologist independence, and day-100 delivery are unquoted hypotheses.

## Overall Score

**41/100.** Weighted points are **82.5 out of 200** (`10 × 82.5 / 20 = 41.25`, rounded to 41). This is an advisory evaluation score only. Strong experimental discipline cannot compensate for failed willingness-to-pay, recurrence, defensibility, wealth, and reachability evidence.

## Control Point

**Current tier: Tier 3. Attainable within 12 months: weak Tier 2, not Tier 1.**

Today the founder controls no working geometry, test result, IP position, tool, hotel contract, installed cohort, or replenishment flow. If the exact design passes independent replicated testing, clears FTO/product safety, is made on founder-owned relocatable tooling, and wins ten unrelated prepaid hotel contracts, the company could possess a repeatably accessible operating asset and modest installed-base switching friction. That would support weak Tier 2. It would not support Tier 1 because standard materials and tooling are purchasable, hotel contracts are annual rather than exclusive, hotel workflow is not mandatory, incident data is not owned, and the passive cartridge can be reverse engineered.

**Strongest copy risk:** Nattaro or SenSci can study the published method, alter holder/harbourage geometry, combine it with an existing lure, and sell through established pest-control channels; Rentokil can bundle an adequate monitor at a low explicit price inside a treatment/inspection relationship. One design cycle or vendor contract may remove QuietRoom's advantage before its direct-sales cohort becomes large.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**FAIL.**

The scenario presentation is mechanically complete and avoids double counting. In the conservative case, €3.52m operating profit at 5.5× gives €19.36m enterprise value; deducting €0.80m obligations and applying 72% ownership gives €13.36m founder equity. Reinvestment assumptions comply with the founder rules, and zero distributions are separately counted.

The bridge is not credible enough to pass:

1. **The raw denominator is not eligible demand.** Four hundred thousand positions are 3.1% of 12.90m hotel bed places, but the buyer definition excludes properties without a recent direct cost, incompatible furniture, decentralized housekeeping, bundled monitoring, insufficient authority, and weak ROI. The candidate supplies no evidence that 5m economically eligible positions exist.
2. **Bed places are not validated monitoring units.** Eurostat's one-person-capacity definition does not establish one device per sleeping side. Using the EU ratio of 12.90m hotel bed places to 6.17m hotel bedrooms, 400,000 positions correspond to roughly **191,000 average hotel bedrooms**. Depending on eligible-room occupancy, the commercial burden could approach the roughly **200,000–250,000 active-room** scale highlighted for review. That is a major direct-procurement footprint, not a small 3.1% share.
3. **Account count is large.** At the candidate's first-100 average of 300 positions per hotel, 400,000 positions require about 1,333 active hotel accounts. Each must accept monitoring risk, weekly staff work, direct procurement, annual prepayment, and continuing cartridge replacement.
4. **Recurrence is manufactured rather than evidenced.** A lure-free passive geometry has no stated 90-day depletion mechanism. Four annual cartridges and 75% renewal are essential to the valuation but unsupported by product physics or customer behaviour.
5. **Rare-event economics are unknown.** A 300-position hotel pays about €13,200 annually and must document roughly €39,600 annualized value for 3× coverage. The source evidence does not establish that qualifying hotels incur this level of recurring avoidable loss or that the monitor reduces it.
6. **Margin and ownership stack several successes.** The case needs 63% gross margin, 20% operating margin, €1.8m growth capital, 72% founder ownership, a 5.5× multiple, and low long-term obligations while building direct multilingual sales, QA, liability, and renewal operations. None is individually impossible, but together they are not conservative without paid cohort and renewal evidence.

The expected 900,000 and strong 1.8m position cases use 7% and 14% of the unfiltered raw denominator and are plainly upside cases. They do not repair the conservative gate.

## Reachability Gate

**FAIL.**

The maximum founder loss is clearly capped at 40,000 PLN, work is intended to remain within 15–20 hours per week, and the candidate uses staged stop conditions. Those are strengths. However, paid proof is not reachable on conservative evidence because payment is postponed until after all of the following succeed: several physical prototypes, entomologist access, a powered 14/28-day low-density study, a 15-point win over established comparators, escape/closure tests, intended-supplier replication, preliminary legal/FTO clearance, manufacture of 250 positions, and direct hotel procurement.

The timeline leaves roughly days 101–120 to contact at least 80 qualified hotels and obtain two 8,000 PLN prepayments, followed by two complete 60-day cohorts and one annual prepayment by day 180. It has no meaningful buffer for a 28-day trial, replication, product iteration, hotel legal/procurement review, staff approval, sample evaluation, or delayed operating cohort. More importantly, the blind superiority is a presently unproved technical invention, not an ordinary build step. The candidate therefore requires a substantially complete, independently proven product and several uncontrolled scientific, supplier, legal, and buyer dependencies before the first paid evidence. That conflicts with the Reachability Gate despite disciplined downside control.

## Expansion Check

The first buyer is well specified, and mail fulfilment avoids technician-route economics. Yet Poland, Czechia, and eastern Germany already create multilingual sales and procurement complexity before a single market is dominated. Moving west exposes the company more directly to Nattaro, SenSci, Spotta, Rentokil, local pest firms, and hotel-group procurement. Incumbents can move inward more easily than QuietRoom can move outward because they already control treatment relationships, technical trust, and escalation; QuietRoom expressly refuses the service/referral layer that could otherwise anchor adoption. Poland offers production cost and founder proximity, not exclusive demand or a difficult-to-copy local adaptation.

## Fatal Objections

1. **The first payment requires an unproved technical breakthrough.** Passive, lure-free geometry must beat the best purchased monitor by 15 points at day 14 and replicate before any hotel pays.
2. **Insectary detection is not hotel loss prevention.** Natural-room sensitivity, introduction-to-detection time, false negatives, non-target/false alarms, downstream confirmation cost, and avoided spread are not established.
3. **The recurring unit is unsupported.** Eurostat bed places do not prove one monitor per sleeping side, and a passive cartridge has no demonstrated reason for quarterly replacement.
4. **The founder-wealth adoption case is heroic.** Four hundred thousand positions mean roughly 191,000 average EU hotel bedrooms and around 1,333 accounts at the candidate's own 300-position/customer planning average, drawn from an unknown eligible subset.
5. **Willingness to pay is unevidenced.** A typical 300-position account costs €13,200 per year, while quantitative hotel incidence and recurring avoided cost are explicitly unavailable.
6. **Housekeeping execution can destroy efficacy.** Weekly checks behind headboards, dual placement, paper/existing-system logging, closure, quarterly replacement, staff turnover, missing units, and escalation create real labour and adherence risk.
7. **False-alarm and trust mechanics are incomplete.** Housekeeping may not identify insects, yet every suspected positive can trigger a pest-professional callout and room concern; no specificity or non-target threshold exists.
8. **Hotel distribution is trust-heavy.** Reputational sensitivity suppresses disclosure, procurement may prefer a known pest controller, and an unknown founder has only about 20 post-test days to secure two paid pilots.
9. **The control point is copyable.** Owned tooling protects production access, not design exclusivity; published results and standard materials can accelerate incumbent response.
10. **Incumbent economics are stronger.** Cheap interceptors constrain price, lure products have a genuine replenishment mechanism, electronics remove weekly inspection labour, and pest firms can bundle adequate monitoring with treatment.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

**Require a preregistered, independently blinded, powered comparison against the best purchasable alternatives before making efficacy claims or releasing production capital.** This is excellent technical and capital discipline. It does not establish that the resulting physical detector has recurring hotel demand, a defensible control point, or a non-heroic founder-wealth path.

## Recommendation

**kill**

Kill the exact QuietRoom passive hotel-monitor and quarterly-cartridge shape for this run. Its hard gates depend on the same unresolved pattern that invalidated prior per-item physical accessories: a prospective technical advantage, an unproven eligible denominator, assumed recurring consumption, and easy incumbent substitution. The experimental gate would answer whether a device works in a controlled setting, but it would not by itself validate hotel incidence, avoided economics, housekeeping adherence, direct acquisition, renewal, or defensibility at the required scale.
