# Zero-to-One Evaluation: ProUtility AppID v1

## Diagnosis

Google Play provides a cleaner transferable distribution asset than WordPress: users, ratings, reviews and subscriptions formally move with the app. The candidate also has explicit public sell-side inventory. It fails as written because none of the inspected affordable listings combines the target paid cohort, professional intent, retention, policy safety and revenue bridge, while the expected model requires 2,100 subscribers from a minimum starting cohort of 200. The direction is a speculative acquisition filter, not an evidenced business.

The user-defined revenue-quality gate replaces the original Founder Wealth Gate for this evaluation.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- Sourced: Google Play app transfer preserves users, statistics, ratings, comments and subscriptions but not historical payout reports or pre-transfer orders.
- Sourced: current public listings exist around USD 8,000–10,000, but Jawsi, ELO AI, Lyrix and Pharmacy Connect each fail core target conditions; KPass is above total capital and adds security trust.
- Candidate claim: a professional utility can grow from at least 200 to 2,100 subscribers at 480 PLN recognised annual value with 20% churn and 30% operating margin.
- Missing: one qualifying target, three plausible targets, paid retention cohorts, traffic source, conversion, CAC, support/security history, policy standing and exact seller-note willingness.

## Overall Score

**43/100 advisory**. Weighted total: `85.5 ÷ 20 × 10 = 42.75`, rounded to 43. This is not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 3 now; Tier 1 App ID only after a complete transfer.** The strongest copy risk is a competitor with better reviews or paid acquisition duplicating the workflow and displacing the store position. The App ID does not make customer value scarce.

## Founder Wealth Gate / User-Defined Revenue Quality Gate

**Fail.** Conservative revenue is 288,000 PLN. Expected reaches 1,008,000 PLN at 30%, but the 2,100 subscribers, 480 PLN recognised annual revenue, 20% churn and acquisition rate are not supported by an exact asset. Strong is more speculative. Ownership is stated but does not repair demand evidence.

## Reachability Gate

**Fail.** A USD 10,000 purchase is approximately the entire 40,000 PLN first tranche before diligence, transfer, policy audit or maintenance. The proposed 22,000 PLN cash target has no public match. There is no exact cohort from which 20 renewals and 20 Pro purchases can be tested.

## Expansion Check

The candidate has no exact beachhead or local advantage. An inherited app can expand through the store, but generic professional utilities face global copy, ranking and paid-acquisition competition. Templates and user artefacts are hypotheses, not current switching costs.

## Fatal Objections

1. Zero qualifying targets and fewer than three plausible targets under the complete specification.
2. Expected subscriber growth, ARPU, churn and margin are invented rather than target-derived.
3. Affordable public listings either lack revenue/retention, have content or policy risk, or consume the pre-proof cap.
4. The acquired control point does not stop feature or keyword competition.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Google Play's formal transfer of subscriptions, ratings and users is a genuinely atomic distribution mechanism when the exact paid cohort is verified.

## Recommendation

kill
