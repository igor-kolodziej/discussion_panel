# Confirmed Business Idea

## Idea

**RatioSnap** is a keyed disposable cassette and reusable reader that automates the existing last-point ratio check on two-component (2K) adhesive dispensing equipment. It simultaneously captures the separate A/B streams, verifies tare and identity, measures both masses, applies only authorized density/temperature conversion, and issues a signed result tied to the material, valve, recipe, operator and cassette lot. It is a process check—not a cure, bond-strength or whole-line compliance claim.

## One-Sentence Thesis

Turn the manufacturer's slow two-cup ratio-check ritual into a 90-second signed consumable event for high-value potting and bonding cells that lack equivalent independent last-point assurance.

## Founder Fit

The founder's data-science background fits measurement design, error analysis, calibration, signed records, finite-account research and AI-assisted compatibility documentation. Safe viscosity-matched fluids allow most alpha work without plant access. A fixed-scope mechanical/product engineer designs the cassette/dock, an electronics contractor builds the dual-scale reader, and a chemical-safety specialist defines material and waste boundaries under full foreground-IP assignment. The founder remains employed, works 15–20 hours/week and does not claim to be a dispensing OEM, adhesive formulator or plant quality authority.

## Customer And Pain

The first buyer is the quality or process engineer at a Polish/Czech/Slovak electronics-potting, battery/power-electronics, lighting, appliance or industrial contract manufacturer operating one to ten semi-automatic 2K cells without trustworthy independent last-point ratio evidence.

Wrong base/catalyst ratio can produce incomplete cure, weak bonds, scrap and latent field failures. Upstream machine values do not necessarily catch trapped air, pressure instability, blockage, worn pumps/check valves or error at the dispense end. [Graco's current procedure](https://www.graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/solutions/articles/ratio-check-guidelines-for-dispensing-2k-materials.html) therefore checks at the dispense valve: stabilize pressure, tare and identify two containers, capture separate streams at production flow/shot size, weigh both, subtract tare, convert density if necessary, repeat and transcribe. The method is valid but slow and error-prone enough to postpone.

The buyer wants the existing check completed and recorded reliably. RatioSnap is valuable only if it saves paid time or expensive sample material, prevents identity/calculation errors, and becomes part of a work instruction.

## Product/Project/Idea Wedge

The POC supports one ratio-check adapter family, one shot-size band and three customer-approved chemistries:

- the customer's ratio nozzle keeps A and B separated at the true last point;
- a disposable keyed cassette presents two pre-identified compatible wells and closes for normal adhesive-waste disposal;
- a dock verifies cassette ID/tare, reads two masses simultaneously, records temperature and rejects unstable/purge shots;
- recipe logic converts mass to volume ratio only with approved density data; and
- a signed export reports ratio, tolerance, recipe/material lots, operator, time and cassette lot.

Plants with an already equivalent independent inline record are excluded. RatioSnap never predicts cure or replaces the equipment/material instructions.

## Commercial Insight Or Market Gap

High-end Graco/Nordson systems can monitor ratio, yet current OEM literature still prescribes manual last-point physical checks. The neglected band is legacy and semi-automatic equipment for which a controls replacement is uneconomic but a traceable check still matters.

The product is not “two scales.” The wedge combines simultaneous capture, immutable A/B identity, known tare, recipe conversion, acceptance logic and a signed consumable record across brands. A current search found no equivalent keyed cassette/reader. One supplier, Glenmarc, [claims more than 10,000 installed meter-mix machines](https://www.glenmarc.com/meter.htm), and Graco, Nordson, METER MIX, Fluid Research and regional integrators add substantial installed fleets. The conservative global 40,000-cell hypothesis is plausible in order of magnitude but unproved; only the subset still performing valuable manual checks is addressable.

## Underused Momentum

- Manufacturers themselves document routine last-point checking and failure modes.
- Electrification, batteries, power electronics and thermal management create more high-value potting/bonding work, without guaranteeing demand.
- Small precision load cells, secure elements, temperature sensors and short-run compatible molding are cash-purchasable.
- Plants increasingly retain electronic batch records; RatioSnap supplies one evidence artifact without replacing MES/QMS.

## Beachhead Market

CEE plants that perform at least one manual last-point check per operating day on semi-automatic 2K cells, use costly material or produce costly-to-rework parts, can expose separate A/B streams safely, and lack an equivalent independent record. Every lead must disclose its present check frequency, shot, material cost, density basis, tolerance, waste route and scrap consequence before a demo. Spray foam, hand-mixed epoxy, fully monitored lines and unsupported hazardous materials are out.

## Primary Control Point

Within 12 months the founder can own the cassette geometry/keys/closure and tool, valve adapters, reader electronics/firmware/test fixtures, tare-temperature-density logic, signing keys/result schema, compatibility recipes, cassette-lot drift history and reference/failure corpus. Contractors transfer editable files and foreground IP. Two production routes are required for existential components.

This is Tier 2: a real product and recurring format that requires no customer licence or donated data, but can be copied. It compounds only if cross-brand compatibility, installed work instructions, signed history and cassette/calibration reliability accumulate before OEMs bundle an equivalent.

### Cold Start & Resource Bootstrapping 

1. Reproduce the OEM two-cup method with water/glycerol and safe viscosity-matched fluids.
2. Build a founder-owned dual-scale dock, three cassette iterations and one generic adapter for <=28,000 PLN.
3. Pre-register agreement, repeatability, cross-contamination, temperature/tare drift and cycle-time limits; compare blindly with calibrated separate containers/scales.
4. Obtain safety/material, FTO and applicable-product-rule reviews before customer chemistry.
5. Show the same reference comparison to 50 qualified plants and collect two unrelated 6,000–8,000 PLN prepaid 50-check pilots by day 180.
6. Only after payment, spend up to the 100,000 PLN founder cap on a rugged dock, first cassette tool, calibration fixture and two paid deliveries.

The base asset needs no equipment code, formulator endorsement, distributor or plant data. Customer-material trials occur under the host's SDS/PPE/ventilation/waste procedure after benign-fluid proof.

### Unit Economics & Value Capture 

Target dock price €3,500; fully loaded dock cost €1,500; 57% gross margin. Target cassette price €5.50; fully loaded cost €1.70; 69% gross margin. Optional annual calibration/care is €900 at €300 direct cost.

The external benchmark includes all taring, identity, weighing, calculation, transcription, cleanup and sample waste; common purge/setup time is excluded. Proceed only if RatioSnap saves at least four paid minutes after a stable shot, reduces sample/handling waste by at least 30% or prevents a documented record error, completes in <90 seconds, and buyers accept at least €4/cassette. Kill if dock gross margin <45%, cassette gross margin <60%, use <200 checks/year, support >15% of revenue or mature direct CAC >€5,000.

### Distribution Moat 

First acquisition is a finite, evidence-led comparison: “Bring your ratio-check nozzle and recent record; this cassette will run beside the same calibrated-cup method.” Public exhibitor lists, certifications, case studies, job descriptions and integrator references identify 80 initial CEE accounts. Two unrelated payments are the gate; ten customers must span three corporate groups and two valve families.

Compatibility and time/waste evidence make later targeting more precise. Adapters are added only after paid commitments. Direct sales preserve learning; distributors become optional after ten accepted installations. There is no influencer or speculative partnership.

## Competitive Advantage And Expansion Path

First win one valve family, three chemistries, two plants and 100 paid checks. Then reach ten plants/three equipment families, add cassette sizes only against paid demand, export signed files/API to common QMS/MES, and later enter rail/aerospace/medical suppliers only after their additional quality requirements are met.

The advantage is neutral cross-brand physical evidence for installed cells, deployed faster than each OEM retrofits its fleet. Strongest copy risk: Graco, Nordson or an integrator adds a keyed dual-container/scale accessory to trusted controls. The company fails if compatibility stays bespoke, an ordinary dual scale/reusable cups capture the value, or plants upgrade away the target segment.

## Local Adaptation Defense

CEE offers lower-cost engineering, short trips and dense electronics/automotive/appliance contract manufacturing. Polish/Czech/Slovak language and application support reduce early CAC. EU chemical, waste, electrical/EMC and machinery rules are costs, not barriers. Global OEMs already serve the region; local origin alone is no defence.

## Why This Could Compound

Each accepted installation adds a reusable adapter/recipe, shot-size boundary, cassette-lot drift observation, temperature effect, operator-error pattern and signed work-instruction history. The loop is: more compatible cells -> more precise accounts -> more paid checks -> better compatibility/failure evidence -> faster qualification -> more compatible cells. Installed docks generate recurring cassette/calibration revenue. No network effect or compulsory customer data contribution is assumed.

Resell load cells/fixtures/electronics and preserve assigned CAD/firmware, safe-fluid corpus, adapter measurements, safety/FTO work, buyer map and acquisition evidence. Do not become a generic adhesive-process consultant. Reuse the dock only after another last-point industrial sample has separate paid evidence.

## Validation Plan

Technical: pre-registered blind agreement, repeatability, false-pass, temperature/tare drift, cross-contamination and closure tests against calibrated cups/scales. Commercial: 50 qualified approaches, six demos, two prepaid pilots, observed check frequency, time/material/error value, paid repeat and first production deposit. Scale: construct a named 200-account sample and obtain non-confidential installed-base/manual-check evidence from multiple distributors before second tooling. Product: independent safety/material/FTO review and fully loaded supplier quotes.

## Kill Criteria

- Equivalent keyed simultaneous last-point product already exists at comparable cost.
- Reference agreement misses tolerance or drift can create false passes.
- Handling/waste is worse than two cups.
- No two unrelated payments after 50 qualified approaches/six demos by day 180.
- <4 minutes saved, no meaningful waste/error value, or willingness below €4/cassette.
- <200 checks/dock/year, dock GM <45%, cassette GM <60% or support >15%.
- >5 engineering days/new tool per cell or proprietary OEM approval/code required.
- Verified relevant denominator <25,000 or 6,000-dock conservative scale is heroic.
- OEM-bundled equivalent arrives before installed workflow switching cost.
