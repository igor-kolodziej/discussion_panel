# Candidate Funnel

All prices, margins, conversion rates, sales cycles, adoption rates, and valuation multiples are hypotheses until contracts and transactions prove them. This generator artifact assigns no numeric score and makes no validation claim.

## Divergent Directions

### 1. Afterline Installed-Base Succession Buyout — finalist

- **Commercial insight:** The valuable asset of a retiring niche machine builder is often not the factory; it is the captive installed base, drawings, tooling, service history, supplier knowledge, and permission to contact customers who cannot tolerate orphaned equipment.
- **Buyer/event:** A factory with a maintenance-critical machine whose small OEM is retiring, shrinking, or abandoning the product line.
- **Cold start:** Sign a low-fee exclusive option with one owner-led OEM, then use seller-authorised outreach to pre-sell continuity plans and spare/retrofit orders before closing.
- **Control point:** Assigned or exclusively licensed aftermarket rights, service mark, technical files, tools, spares, customer records, and transferable contracts.

### 2. MouldRelay Dormant-Tool Reorder Rights — finalist

- **Commercial insight:** Retiring converters and OEMs hold paid-off injection moulds with intermittent profitable reorder demand that neither side manages well.
- **Buyer/event:** An industrial customer needs another batch of a legacy plastic component but the original converter is exiting or refuses small runs.
- **Cold start:** Option ten named moulds with seller-authorised customer outreach; exercise only when one customer prepays a production run.
- **Control point:** Title or exclusive production licence to named moulds, CAD/process files, and assigned reorder rights.

### 3. PlantExit Exclusive MRO Liquidation Mandates — finalist

- **Commercial insight:** Closing factories often liquidate maintenance inventory as undifferentiated scrap even though nearby plants pay for exact, documented spares and fixtures.
- **Buyer/event:** A restructuring adviser, insolvency practitioner, or owner needs to clear a plant while preserving value; maintenance buyers need exact parts fast.
- **Cold start:** Obtain one exclusive sale mandate over a photographed and serialised inventory, then run buyer-direct sales before paying for stock.
- **Control point:** Contracted exclusivity over the named inventory and transaction flow, with custody only after a sale.

### 4. FirmwareEscrow Building-Control Continuity — rejected

- **Insight:** Property owners fear abandoned proprietary BMS and access-control systems.
- **Reason rejected:** Credential transfer, cybersecurity liability, IP, and OEM licences create several simultaneous uncontrolled dependencies; most integrators can be replaced without acquiring source rights.

### 5. ClinicDevice Service-Line Carveout — rejected

- **Insight:** Retiring distributors leave diagnostic-device users without service.
- **Reason rejected:** Medical-device authorisation, parts traceability, regulatory liability, and manufacturer consent make the first control point authority-dependent.

### 6. PV Inverter Exchange-Core Network — rejected

- **Insight:** A tested exchange unit can restore a legacy PV site faster than a full repower.
- **Reason rejected:** It repeats blacklisted obsolete energy-equipment pools; safe testing, firmware access, and warranty liability require capital before demand is proven.

### 7. Orphan Chemical Formulation Rights — rejected

- **Insight:** A closing specialty-chemical maker may own qualified niche formulations with recurring buyers.
- **Reason rejected:** REACH, batch QA, environmental obligations, and customer requalification make a low-cost proof implausible.

### 8. Retiring Calibration-Lab Method Buyout — rejected

- **Insight:** Accreditation scope and methods can outlive a retiring lab owner.
- **Reason rejected:** Accreditation is not simply assignable; the authority and specialist team are existential dependencies.

### 9. Legacy Food-Machine Wear-Kit Licence — rejected as narrower duplicate

- **Insight:** Wear kits are the highest-frequency aftermarket cash flow.
- **Reason rejected:** This is a subset of Afterline. Keeping the full installed-base rights makes customer access and expansion materially stronger.

### 10. Insolvency Software Customer-Book Buyout — rejected

- **Insight:** Existing subscriptions are more valuable than abandoned code.
- **Reason rejected:** It repeats the retiring-software family, and no transferable listing/payment asset or target was identified.

### 11. Municipal Pump Service-Territory Assignment — rejected

- **Insight:** Installed municipal equipment creates recurring parts and service.
- **Reason rejected:** Public procurement, OEM authorisation, and local contractor trust cannot be secured through one low-cost option.

### 12. Discontinued Packaging-Format Tooling Bank — rejected

- **Insight:** Format parts and change parts are essential for old packaging lines.
- **Reason rejected:** It overlaps the blacklisted packaging-test/changeover family and can be copied by machine shops from samples.

### 13. Shutdown Data-Room Diligence Product — rejected

- **Insight:** Buyers need structured technical files before buying industrial assets.
- **Reason rejected:** This is generic diligence with no compounding transaction control.

### 14. OEM Manual Translation Rights Library — rejected

- **Insight:** CEE buyers need usable local service documentation.
- **Reason rejected:** Translation is a copyable service and customer urgency is weak without the underlying aftermarket rights.

### 15. Retired Engineer Knowledge Royalties — rejected

- **Insight:** Tacit repair knowledge is scarce after an owner retires.
- **Reason rejected:** A talent network alone is partner-dependent and scales labour roughly with revenue; the knowledge becomes valuable only inside an acquired installed base.

## Round Seven — Direct Payers, Counted Events, and Owned Test Assets

This generator invocation began only after the four selected round-six shapes and the PelletLock-to-LaneSeal structural pivot were exhausted. Seventeen researched directions are documented in `round7_root_direction.md`, `round7_transaction_control_directions.md`, `round7_distribution_asset_directions.md`, `round7_owned_resource_directions.md`, and `round7_brewform_reassessment.md`.

### 74. BlindProof placebo-matched personal supplement tests — finalist

The user truth is unusually exact: wearable-owning supplement buyers want to know whether a recurring product produces a benefit **in them**, but app-only tracking leaves expectation bias and placebo manufacture unsolved. The first product is a prepaid fixed melatonin-versus-matched-control sequence with Oura import and a result that may honestly be positive, no detectable benefit, or inconclusive. The founder can directly secure the legal/manufacturing specification, sequence keys, analysis, prepaid cohort and consented response graph. A free wearable-variance analyser is the specific cold-start mechanism; no clinician, influencer, supplement brand or wearable partnership supplies access. The conservative wealth bridge reaches €12.94m of founder equity at 45,000 annual active customers, subject to paid proof that users value null results, repeat 2.2 times/year and can be acquired economically.

### 75. CoreBid PL demand-backed automotive core ownership — finalist

CoreBid pays workshops for only named high-value OE cores that two downstream remanufacturers/core brokers have already priced under written acceptance rules. It takes title rather than listing parts, then builds workshop first-call rights and an observed price/rejection graph from paid turns. A Polish 2025 study estimates 1.8bn PLN of associated core value, while BORG reports more than two million remanufactured units per year; these bound the opportunity and expose formidable incumbents. The conservative wealth bridge is thin and requires 275,000 cores/year, but the first two paid batches can be completed inside 39,000 PLN without speculative obsolete inventory. Two contract-grade buyer bid books are a pre-spend requirement.

### 76. QuietRoom Sentinel passive hotel bed-bug monitoring — finalist, conditional on blind product superiority

Hotel operators want discreet early detection before a guest complaint, not pest-control visits or a public dashboard. The proposed mail-supplied passive monitor fits normal housekeeping turnover and is sold as a prepaid room cohort with recurring cartridges. The founder can own the geometry/tool, performance protocol, production specification and installed replenishment workflow without a pest-controller partnership. Eurostat counts 29.7m EU tourist bed places in 2024; this is a broad capacity bound, not a hotel-room demand count. A conservative wealth path uses 250,000 active rooms and therefore needs a bottom-up eligible-room census. The direction advances only if a blinded low-density test materially beats cheap interceptors before hotel deposits; otherwise it is a copyable plastic trap.

### 77. BrewForm brewery-yeast ingredient rail — rejected after direct incumbent research

Translis, AgroYeast, Foodcom, TBESBY, Leiber and larger yeast/feed groups already run the proposed brewery-feedstock-to-standardized-dried-ingredient chain. Buyer SKU approval is non-exclusive qualification, feedstock rights are ordinary procurement, and the 12,000-tonne wealth case exceeds named incumbent output without an owned dryer or category denominator.

### 78. WhiteStream institutional-linen fibre — rejected as blacklist overlap

The homogeneous source and converter call-off improve ordinary textile collection, but the business repeats the explicitly rejected Textile-Bale Yield family. Each source still adds transport/segregation dependence, output is a price-led bale grade, and no material new control point resolves the prior objection.

### 79. RefillRail Hotels — rejected

The 2030 accommodation-packaging rule creates demand, but established amenity vendors already own dispenser, formula, procurement and refill capabilities. Installed dispensers are revocable purchasing rather than a scarce rail.

### 80. Allergen Release Rail — rejected

Neogen, Hygiena and 3M already provide the rapid line-side tests, qualification support and consumable workflows. A new pack, record or lower price does not create an assay asset inside the capital cap.

### 81. CareWear ID — rejected

Datamars reports more than 10,000 textile-identification installations and hundreds of millions of tags. A smaller-home workflow would depend on the incumbent identification standard rather than founder control.

### 82. PalletClaim Clearing — rejected

Assigned balances still reset debtor acceptance, location and quality disputes, while PAKi, PGS RLS, DPL and inter.PAL own the European depot/netting networks needed to monetize low-value pallet claims.

### 83. AshKey cremation identity kit — rejected

The crematorium controls the event and paid proof is reachable, but ordinary sequential cremation tags already exist. The €10m path needs roughly one million annual kits and an implausible share of a concentrated buyer market for a serial design that funeral suppliers can copy.

### 84. TachoSeal / TachoTrace inspection packs — rejected from finalist set

EN 16882 certification and a JRC manufacturer code form a real EU-wide asset around a counted statutory event. However, the under-100,000 PLN certification/tool path is unquoted, existing security-seal companies are already approved, and the conservative economics reach only about €4.25m founder equity. A plaque/serial bundle does not repair the non-compensating wealth failure.

### 85. EggMark compatible coding ink — rejected

Mandatory producer codes create enormous event volume, but Domino and multiple compatible-fluid suppliers already sell compliant ink. Value per egg is tiny and one formulation/distributor contract reproduces the price-led offer.

### 86. FrySafe closed-loop fryer filtration dock — reserve only

Avoiding exposed hot-oil pouring and dirty filter cleaning is a credible human-behavior insight, and a dock/cassette asset can be prototyped directly. The eligible fry-heavy, non-filtering account census is missing, the conservative wealth case fails as written, and VITO/OEM filtration creates a strong copy/performance bar.

### 87. CopperGate scrap-intake classifier — hold behind blind technical proof

The founder can autonomously build a physical sample bank, multimodal fixture and labelled dataset, but no evidence shows that cheap imaging/conductivity sensors can classify whole-lot copper/brass value accurately enough to replace expertise or XRF. It is an experiment, not a finalist.

### 88. HerdTag Direct — rejected

ARiMR already lists 19 identification suppliers; listing/API access is shared entry rather than scarcity, and national approval resets make EU herd counts unusable as one market.

### 89. ReturnCell battery-pack pool — rejected

E-bike sales do not count return shipments. Every rental still depends on carrier and receiver acceptance, hazardous-goods conditions and reverse logistics, repeating the rejected two-party module-pool failure.

### 71. PalletCincher reusable mixed-load wrap — rejected

The 2026 pallet-wrap exemption weakens urgency, the asset is readily copied through a textile supplier, and the closed-loop lane denominator is missing.

### 72. PatchKey offline update module — rejected from finalist set

Founder fit is high, but chip/OTA vendors already provide secure-update building blocks and ten-year key/support liabilities make the low-volume retrofit wedge difficult to dominate.

### 73. ClampWitness PV small-joint preload indicator — finalist

Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's February 2026 report explicitly identifies small-diameter direct-tension indication for PV mounting/module joints as a product opportunity. The founder can own a joint-specific washer/clamp geometry, tooling and load/corrosion corpus, while family access supplies alpha only. Generic metric DTIs and fastener incumbents make the exact PV-specific gap, mounting-system compatibility and volume bridge the decisive facts.
