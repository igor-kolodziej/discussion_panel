# Zero-to-One Candidate: CultivarHold EU Plant-Variety Licence

## One-Sentence Thesis

Option an underdistributed but protected Polish/CEE ornamental cultivar, obtain a recorded exclusive EU commercialisation licence and authenticated mother stock, and exercise only after a propagator prepays a catalogue allocation whose visible trait can be checked in one season.

## Founder Fit

The founder can screen CPVO rights, build licence/economic models, automate catalogue outreach, and manage data; a plant-variety lawyer and contract horticulturist cover title and biology. The first proof is part-time and capital-light; multiplication is subcontracted only against orders.

## Customer, Pain, and Current Workaround

Nurseries and young-plant propagators need differentiated catalogue products with legal exclusivity and reliable mother stock. Small breeders/institutes may own technically attractive varieties but lack EU distribution. Buyers currently license from established breeders, sell commodity varieties, or conduct slow trials.

## Product Wedge

One ornamental with an immediately visible, non-medical trait—colour, form, shelf life, compactness, or flowering window—covered by an in-force CPVR with at least 12 years remaining. Secure a 12-month exclusive option, authenticated mother stock, demonstration/material rights, and a paid propagator preorder before exercising a multi-country licence.

## Commercial Insight or Market Gap

A protected genotype is self-reproducing product IP; the same mother-stock, enforcement, catalogue and propagator channel can earn per propagated unit. The ignored assets are rights held by CEE breeders with weak commercial distribution, not new unprotected plant discovery.

## Underused Momentum

One CPVR covers the EU and generally lasts 25 years. CPVO explicitly permits licensing of applications/rights and records exploitation rights/licensees. More than 31,000 rights were in force in 2024. This proves the asset class, not buyer demand for a target variety.

## Beachhead Market

One fast-cycle ornamental category sold through Polish/German/Dutch young-plant propagators where the trait is visible in one season and the licence has clear remaining term. Exclude food/feed/medical claims, multi-year tree yield, patented production equipment, or phytosanitary dependency that cannot be contracted.

## Business Model and Unit Economics

Charge €0.25–0.70 net royalty per propagated plant plus optional minimum annual guarantees. Gross margin can exceed 80% after breeder royalty, mother-stock QA, legal/enforcement, and account management. Reject if breeder/stock/QA consumes >45% of net royalty, buyer qualification exceeds one season, or fewer than three propagators can reach meaningful scale.

## First 10 and 100 Customers

First customer is the prepaid propagator allocation. First ten are propagators/nurseries contacted from catalogues and trade directories with live material. First hundred are downstream licensed propagators/distributors across varieties; end-consumer marketing is not the wedge.

## Primary Control Point

Tier 1: recorded exclusive contractual exploitation licence for named territories/channels, authenticated mother stock, contractual multiplication control, audit/enforcement rights, and variety denomination/marketing rights. A nonexclusive introduction is Tier 3.

## Cold Start and Resource Bootstrap

Screen CPVO Gazette/registries for 200 in-force CEE rights with weak EU distribution. Spend <=8,000 PLN on chain/title/remaining-term diligence and <=8,000 PLN on one exclusive option. Spend <=10,000 PLN on mother stock/sample/QA and phytosanitary advice. Obtain a 5,000–10,000 PLN paid propagator preorder within 180 days. Do not finance mass multiplication first.

## Distribution Advantage

The exclusive licence restricts lawful commercial propagation in its territory; authenticated stock and catalogue placement build distributor habit. Copy risk is design-around through competing cultivars with similar traits or illegal propagation; enforcement and a genuine performance difference are essential.

## Competitive Advantage and Expansion Path

Win one variety, then add 10–15 cultivars sharing propagators, trial protocols, mother-stock QA, enforcement, and catalogue placement. Expand EU territories only after one season's sell-through/royalty evidence. Avoid unrelated crops.

## Poland/EU or Local Adaptation Defense

CEE breeders may be commercially under-distributed, while EU-wide rights make one licence scalable. Poland offers lower trial/mother-stock cost and founder access to CEE rights holders. The defence is exclusive rights and channel performance, not nationality.

## Compounding Advantage

Each cultivar adds royalty stream, propagator relationships, trial data, enforcement know-how, and catalogue credibility. A portfolio shares channels unlike one-off cultural titles; still, each genotype must earn placement.

## Reinvestment and Compounding Model

Conservative/expected reinvest 95% and limit optional employment-phase distributions to 5%; strong reinvests 100%. Cash funds options, trials, mother-stock QA, enforcement and adjacent licences; no mass inventory before paid orders.

## Reachability and 180-Day Paid-Proof Plan

Days 1–45: screen 200 rights and interview ten propagators. Days 46–90: option one right with stock access. Days 91–150: show live/sample material, obtain buyer QA and price. By day 180: one 5,000–10,000 PLN prepaid catalogue order. Pre-proof <=34,000 PLN and employment retained.

## Capital Tranches and Maximum Loss

8,000 PLN screening/legal; up to 16,000 PLN after title/stock evidence; up to 34,000 PLN before preorder; remaining founder capital only after paid order and successful small batch; family capital excluded until multiple propagators, repeat order and positive contribution.

## Critical Dependency Map

Clean/remaining rights, licence availability, authenticated stock, phytosanitary movement, visible trait, propagation performance, buyer preorder and enforcement. Resolve sequentially; no licence exercise until stock and paid demand.

## Full-Time Transition Gate

Three licensed varieties, five independent propagators, repeat catalogue orders, positive contribution, a repeatable rights-sourcing channel and 12 months runway.

## Failure Recovery and Reusable Assets

An expired option leaves the rights screen, title checklist, buyer interviews and trial protocol. Mother stock remains limited and contract-governed; no speculative commercial inventory.

## Critical Evidence

EU/CPVO right term and licensing mechanics are official. Licence affordability, trait quality, nursery WTP and unit volumes are hypotheses.

## Critical Hypotheses

An affordable underdistributed right has a trait buyers value now; remaining term is sufficient; a propagator prepays before multi-season evidence; 15+ related cultivars can share the same channel.

## Kill Criteria

No licensable option after 200 rights; option >8,000 PLN; less than 12 years term; no authenticated stock; no paid preorder after 30 propagator approaches; qualification >one season; royalty contribution <55%; design-around eliminates premium; no second related right within 12 months.

## Budget, Time, and Minimum People Stack

Founder 15–20 hours/week; plant-variety lawyer; contract horticulturist/trial nursery; phytosanitary adviser. Pre-proof <=34,000 PLN; first-year founder cap <=100,000 PLN.
