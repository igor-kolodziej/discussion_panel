# Zero-to-One Evaluation: RatioSnap v1

## Diagnosis

RatioSnap begins from a manufacturer-prescribed, economically important workflow rather than inventing a new safety claim. The founder can build and own the cassette, reader, adapters, calibration logic and reference corpus with benign fluids, and the external pilot compares directly against calibrated cups/scales. Its strongest business weakness is not technical feasibility but scale evidence: the conservative wealth case assumes 6,000 active docks and 350 paid checks/year without a public denominator for cells that still perform manual last-point checks. Before paneling, the installed-base and manual-check fractions need decision-critical research.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Official/current equipment material confirms routine last-point ratio checking, two separately tared cups, repeated stable shots, weighing, density conversion, ratio alarms on some higher-end systems and serious incumbent capability. It does not establish check frequency, operator time, sample-material cost, cassette willingness to pay, the number of compatible legacy/semi-automatic cells, or how many already have equivalent inline records. No panel or chatbot verdict was used.

## Overall Score

**71.25/100.** Weighted points are 142.5 across 20 weight units; `10 × 142.5 / 20 = 71.25`. Advisory only; not a validation score.

## Control Point

**Tier 2 path, unproved.** The founder can directly own cassette tooling, dock/firmware, adapter drawings, recipe conversions, test fixtures, signing keys and compatibility/failure history. Strongest copy risk: Graco, Nordson or another meter-mix supplier adds a keyed dual-container/scale accessory and exports the result through its existing controls, eliminating the neutral accessory's reason to exist.

## Founder Wealth Gate

**Fail pending denominator research.** The conservative arithmetic reaches about €15.2m founder equity after €3m obligations and avoids distribution double-counting. But 6,000 active docks represents 15% of an unsupported 40,000-cell hypothesis. Without evidence that tens of thousands of relevant cells exist and a meaningful fraction still use manual last-point checks, scale may be heroic.

## Reachability Gate

**Pass.** The first reader/cassettes can be built with safe fluids and ordinary paid vendors inside 28,000 PLN, while the founder remains employed. Two unrelated 6,000–8,000 PLN prepaid pilots after a reference-method demonstration are genuine paid proof. Customer chemistry and production equipment are needed only for paid delivery, not creation of the underlying asset.

## Expansion Check

The first niche is narrow and the cross-brand installed-base thesis is sensible. Expansion becomes service-like if every valve/material pair needs bespoke engineering or customer-specific safety work. Broad vendors can move inward quickly, so only a genuinely reusable compatibility pack, low test cost and work-instruction embedding support asymmetry.

## Fatal Objections

1. The relevant installed-base/manual-check denominator is missing.
2. Plants may use the manual procedure too infrequently for recurring cassette economics.
3. A dual scale and ordinary cups may capture most value without a proprietary consumable.
4. OEM ratio alarms or a bundled accessory can erase the wedge.

## Strongest Salvageable Insight

Automate a manufacturer-prescribed last-point physical check into a signed consumable event rather than trying to infer quality from upstream machine data.

## Recommendation

**research further**

Research installed-base evidence, manual-check persistence and current equivalent accessories. Re-evaluate before any panel.
