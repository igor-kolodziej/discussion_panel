# Canonical Candidate — Atlassian LegacyBridge v1

Status: frozen for advisory evaluation; not panel-ready.  
Success definition: 1,000,000 PLN of recognised annual revenue by Year 5 and at least 25% fully loaded operating margin. This user-defined gate replaces the original founder-wealth gate for this candidate.

## Thesis

Acquire one small, paid Jira Data Center Marketplace app that stores customer-specific workflow configuration, has no Cloud version, and would otherwise strand its customers during Atlassian's 2026–2029 Data Center wind-down. Obtain the complete Marketplace position—formal app transfer, code and IP, listing, installed licences, support history, documentation, brand/domain, permissible customer records and obligations—then build the Forge Cloud successor and a tested migration path. The business sells continuity to the inherited cohort and the same narrow workflow to new Jira Cloud organisations.

This is not a source-code purchase, a generic app roll-up or a migration consultancy. The asset is worthless unless the entire position transfers atomically through Atlassian and the seller contractually transfers everything needed to support, bill and migrate customers.

## Customer And Pain

The initial buyer is the Jira administrator or platform owner at an organisation using the acquired Data Center app. The buyer is not trying to buy another feature. They are trying to avoid losing a configured workflow, historical state and user behaviour when the underlying Jira deployment moves to Cloud. Atlassian says a missing reliable app migration path can block a customer's Cloud migration. A small incumbent vendor may rationally sell when the Data Center revenue tail no longer justifies learning Forge, meeting Cloud security expectations and supporting two architectures.

The candidate excludes authentication, encryption, PII-protection, medical, financial and other high-liability app classes. The acceptable target is a narrow configuration-bearing project, notification, planning, approval or workflow-control app whose customer configuration can be exported and deterministically recreated in Cloud.

## Public Supply Evidence And Missing Proof

The official Atlassian Marketplace API screen covered 995 Jira Data Center apps. It found 457 paid-via-Atlassian apps with 5–500 public installs, 226 with no Cloud version detected under the same app key, and 137 in the 5–60-install band. Public price tiers are available. Atlassian formally supports transfers after a sale or acquisition, requiring proof of transfer/purchase and approval from the selling partner.

This establishes a public legacy-app pool, not affordable supply. Public install counts are not paid licences. No screened owner has publicly stated a willingness to sell; no price, paid-customer count, renewal, revenue, support burden, code quality, liability history or transfer package has been verified. No vendor was contacted because this run does not authorise seller outreach.

Three illustrative low-install leads exist, but they are not qualifying targets: Idea Hub has about 11 public installs and a USD 750 minimum annual tier; SLA Timer has about 17 installs and a USD 500 minimum tier; Better Comments with Reply has about 55 installs and a USD 1,000 minimum tier. Their different workflows, active owners, unknown paid cohorts and visible substitutes prevent treating them as affordable sellers.

## Atomic Target Specification

No acquisition may close unless one exact target meets all of these conditions in a data room and contract:

- 10–60 verified paying organisations, not public installs, with at least 75% trailing-twelve-month logo renewal;
- at least 60,000 PLN trailing recognised annual revenue and positive contribution margin after Atlassian share, refunds, support and infrastructure;
- less than eight founder/contractor support hours per week and no critical unresolved security issue, threatened delisting, IP dispute or abnormal refund cohort;
- customer configuration that is valuable to preserve and technically exportable;
- no Cloud version under seller control and a Cloud competitive set that does not already make the acquired workflow economically irrelevant;
- simultaneous assignment of app listing, app key, source/IP, brand/domain, documentation, support system and history, reporting, permissible customer contacts, contracts and obligations;
- written Atlassian transfer acceptance or a closing condition tied to Atlassian approval;
- no more than 20,000 PLN acquisition cash at closing, 8,000 PLN legal/security diligence and 12,000 PLN Cloud prototype/continuity reserve before paid post-transfer proof;
- any additional consideration is capped at 60,000 PLN, paid only as 20% of collected post-transfer receipts, and stops if contribution margin or transfer continuity fails.

An app with more revenue can qualify only if the seller note preserves the same cash-at-risk limits. A seller promise, handover of code without the listing, or non-assignable customer relationship is a kill.

## Product Wedge

Days 0–30 preserve service and ship a stewardship/security release. In parallel, the company publishes a feature-parity and migration document and creates a deterministic configuration exporter. Days 31–120 produce a thin Forge successor for the top inherited configuration patterns. The first commercial offer is an annual Cloud continuity subscription with included migration, not bespoke consulting. The migration tooling is repeatable and becomes product functionality.

The product records source configuration, transformation rules, validation output and post-migration exceptions. With explicit contractual rights, these de-identified compatibility cases improve automated migration and regression testing. Customer content is not pooled or reused.

## Cold Start, Distribution And Control Point

The initial acquisition mechanism, after separate outreach authorisation, is a transparent buyer page plus direct approaches to owners from the 137-app public pool, starting with low-liability apps that have 10–60 installs and no detected Cloud version. The offer uses escrow, a standard asset schedule, Atlassian's formal transfer process, a small cash close and receipt-capped upside. This is a finite founder-led acquisition sprint, not a permanent high-touch sales model.

After closing, cold start is the inherited customer cohort, the transferred Marketplace listing and the Cloud Migration Assistant path. New customers can discover and buy through Marketplace without an enterprise partnership. Solution-partner referrals may help later but are not required for viability.

Before a signed and Atlassian-approved transaction, the current control point is Tier 3: the founder owns no app, users or billing. After atomic transfer it becomes Tier 1 for the app key/listing, code/IP, brand and inherited customer obligations. The migration rules, regression fixtures and configuration history can become Tier 2 know-how. The strongest copy risk is a larger Cloud app importing the same configuration or Atlassian adding the narrow workflow natively.

## Reachability And Capital Tranches

The paid-proof clock starts only after atomic closing. By Day 180, at least three independent inherited customers must have paid a renewal, Cloud continuity subscription or non-refundable migration reservation. At least one must be a Cloud payment; merely collecting old Data Center renewals is insufficient product proof. Contribution margin on those receipts must be positive after support and migration labour.

Pre-proof maximum loss is 40,000 PLN: 20,000 PLN purchase cash, 8,000 PLN legal/security diligence and 12,000 PLN continuity/Cloud prototype reserve. No seller-note payment is due before receipts. The next 30,000 PLN of founder capital is released only after three independent payments and successful first delivery. The final 30,000 PLN is released only after five paying organisations, positive contribution economics and either repeat demand or a measured Marketplace acquisition signal. Family capital is excluded until repeatability.

The founder remains employed and caps personal time at five hours per day. A fixed-scope Forge contractor is used only after code review and only within the staged budget. Full-time transition requires multiple independent Cloud customers or contracted recurring demand, positive contribution economics, a repeatable acquisition signal and 12 months of personal runway.

## Compounding And Expansion

The acquired listing and customer cohort provide the first distribution advantage. Each successful migration adds regression fixtures, configuration mappings and support knowledge that shorten the next migration and reduce risk. The outward expansion is adjacent Jira workflow modules sold to the same administrators, then a vendor-neutral export/evidence layer that reduces dependence on one deployment architecture. No second acquisition is required in the five-year model.

This compounding claim is conditional. If customer configurations are simple, incumbents can import them through one release and the control point remains a finite installed base rather than a moat.

## Validation Sequence And Kill Criteria

1. Public screen already completed; no seller contact has occurred.
2. With separate authorisation, request a standard ten-field seller packet from a finite target list: paid organisations, revenue by month, renewals, price tiers, support hours, security history, code/IP, configuration exportability, Cloud plans and transfer willingness.
3. Supply is real only after three owners provide enough data to show a plausible transaction inside the cash and seller-note caps.
4. Select one target only after competitive and code/security diligence; close atomically through escrow and Atlassian approval.
5. Obtain three independent post-transfer payments within 180 days, including one Cloud payment.

Kill before closing if fewer than three plausible sellers exist, if no exact target meets every atomic-transfer condition, or if the purchase requires more than 40,000 PLN before proof. Kill after closing if fewer than three independent customers pay within 180 days, if no Cloud customer pays, if contribution margin is non-positive, if the migration path cannot be productised, or if the expected case requires more than 100 organisations or less than 25% fully loaded operating margin to reach 1,000,000 PLN.

## Current Evidence Verdict Embedded In The Candidate

The platform mechanics and public target pool are real. Affordable seller access, an exact configuration-bearing target, the 10-to-100 customer bridge and copy-resistant Cloud value are not yet proved. The candidate therefore does not presently claim that the revenue-quality or Reachability Gate has passed.
