# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-006

Lead: `RAW-FPE-01`, reserved textile-recovery capacity. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Municipalities have separately collected textiles since 1 January 2025. Observed 2025–2026 contracts and tariffs span about 499–2,149 PLN/Mg; Elbląg budgeted 704,291 PLN for 616 Mg. EU textile EPR must be established by April 2028, but Poland’s exact PRO timing is unsettled. The core contradiction is general scarcity: published Polish sorting capacity is about 300,000 t/year, while Wtórpol claims large spare capacity and an existing 60,000-container network.

## Proposed Transaction

Secure a 6–12 month, 50-tonne facility option for a named code/composition, delivery window and gate-fee formula, then sell a matching prepaid reservation. Customer prepays transport/treatment; founder funds only option/sampling/legal work. Test assumptions: 50–150 PLN/t option premium and 150–400 PLN/t coordination margin. No waste custody or transport without the required authorization.

## Acquisition Route

Use BIP/e-Zamówienia to find known tonnes and approach below-threshold municipal procurements or regional apparel operations through their waste managers. Cheapest proof: two comparable facility quotes, one binding composition-specific option and one customer reservation with no founder-financed processing.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Whether facilities sell options; scarcity for sampled composition; contamination/yield; legal transferability; transport/rejection costs; whether buyers separate capacity from bundled waste contracts; future PRO timing; direct incumbent route-around. General sorter scarcity is contradicted; only a narrow difficult-stream bottleneck remains plausible.
