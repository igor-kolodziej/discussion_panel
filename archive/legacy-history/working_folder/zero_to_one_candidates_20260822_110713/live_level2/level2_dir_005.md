# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-005

Lead: `RAW-FPE-04`, sealed used-cooking-oil route with quality-linked offtake. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Poland had roughly 101,500 catering establishments in 2024, but only fry-intensive sites matter. UCO requires correct BDO scope, records and licensed custody. Competition is strong: incumbents supply drums, pick up, manage records, deliver fresh oil and pay sources; SARIA/ReFood reports 6,000 customers and 70 vehicles. A February 2026 Northwest-European bulk proxy was about €1,030/t, not a Polish restaurant-gate price. One 120 L drum has only about €112 gross commodity value before collection/testing/losses.

## Proposed Transaction

A licensed collector/processor signs a short floor-price offtake first. Restaurants grant time-limited exclusive pickup rights; numbered drums and samples create source-quality history; processor grades FFA/MIU/water/sulfur and settles a transparent formula. Founder revenue is a route/data fee plus a share of clean-quality uplift. All waste custody, BDO, transport and sustainability documentation remain with the licensed partner.

## Acquisition Route

Recruit dense fried-food/canteen sources through fresh-oil wholesalers, fryer service and BDO advisers. Cheapest proof: binding 60-day processor quote, ten-source samples, measured weekly liters and conditional exclusivity letters for 6–15k PLN.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Local processor netback, incumbent source payments and free-container value, whether quality uplift reaches the collector, source volume/contamination, stop economics, licensed-partner liability, sustainability audit scope, commodity sensitivity, churn/counteroffers and working-capital timing. Legal collection is not differentiation.
