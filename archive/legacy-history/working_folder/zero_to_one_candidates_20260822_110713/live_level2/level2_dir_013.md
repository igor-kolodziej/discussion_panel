# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-013

Lead: `RAW-IHK-03`, DSR enrollment/control pod for sub-1 MW sites. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

PSE requires 1 MW for direct IRP participation; smaller sites can enter through a registered aggregator. Enel X advertises sites with several hundred kW and about 100–200k+ PLN/year per 1 MW of customer reduction availability. Auction prices vary widely. The incumbent contradiction is strong: market leaders advertise free onboarding, have hundreds of customers, and already develop reduction plans. A paid generic audit duplicates pre-sales.

## Proposed Transaction

Sell process-specific commissioning under an existing aggregator: safety envelope, temporary metering, witnessed flexibility test, control narrative, contractor quote and comparison of two aggregator terms. Customer signs with aggregator, which owns PSE certification/dispatch/settlement; site retains safety override. Test ranges: 2–5k PLN audit and 10–30k PLN simple metering/control pod.

## Acquisition Route

Use refrigeration, compressed-air, PLC and power-quality contractors to identify cold storage, food processing, pumps, furnaces, crushers and other controllable processes. Cheapest proof: five paid audit deposits, temporary metering and one accepted site.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Aggregator minimum/revenue share/exclusivity/data/penalties; willingness to accept a third-party pod; customer payment despite free onboarding; dependable versus connected kW; telemetry/cybersecurity cost; baseline/rebound risk; automation need; post-2027 revenue. No direct balancing-market revenue should be assumed.
