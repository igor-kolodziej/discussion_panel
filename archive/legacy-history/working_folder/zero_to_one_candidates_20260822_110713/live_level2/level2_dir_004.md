# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-004

Lead: `RAW-TSE-03`, dental-amalgam cartridge exchange route. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

New dental-amalgam use is generally prohibited from 1 January 2025, but practices removing legacy fillings must still use ≥95% separators, maintain them and transfer waste to an authorized undertaking. Poland has at least 2,508 publicly funded dental practices and 46,564 registered dentists, but no public waste-volume or fill-cadence series. Visible inputs are roughly 574 PLN for one common cartridge and 500 PLN/kg for advertised downstream handling. Existing equipment-service firms already combine maintenance, parts and disposal.

## Proposed Transaction

A technician-led, prepaid model-specific exchange records separator model/serial, fill status, cartridge SKU and service history. Only clean cartridges are stocked. A qualified technician swaps; an authorized waste undertaking collects the sealed full cartridge directly. A 1,200–1,900 PLN completed exchange or 100–180 PLN/month allowance is a quote hypothesis. The coordinator never possesses full cartridges unless separately authorized.

## Acquisition Route

Sign a dental-equipment technician first, build an exact compatibility sheet and screen 30 serviced practices for recent 75–95% alerts or overdue maintenance. Cheapest proof: serial confirmation, historic fill intervals and ten conditional deposits for 5–12k PLN.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Installed models/SKUs; post-2025 removal-derived volume; replacement intervals; downstream minimums; technician authority under manufacturer instructions; clinic prepayment; margin after cartridge, disposal, travel and support; incumbent alert capture. The business may be a declining installed-base service with too-slow cadence.
