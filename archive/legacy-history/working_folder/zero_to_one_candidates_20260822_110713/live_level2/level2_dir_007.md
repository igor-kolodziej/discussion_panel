# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-007

Lead: `RAW-TSE-01`, asbestos-roof removal slots bundled with replacement. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

Gmina Ostrów Wielkopolski funds licensed removal/transport/disposal, while replacement roofing is usually owner-funded. A 2026 local procurement around 200 Mg drew five offers and a roughly 124.5k PLN contract; nearby work shows meaningful route density. Public removal/disposal cost per typical roof is modest, while commercial dismantling and replacement can put a straightforward 150 m² roof at tens of thousands of PLN. A stale 2022 capacity figure suggests local landfill pressure, but five bids contradict a broad contractor shortage.

## Proposed Transaction

Sell a verified date joining a trained asbestos contractor, authorized landfill and roofer/weatherproofing commitment. Customer or municipality funds materials and contractor mobilization; 20–50k PLN founder capital cannot finance five roofs. The safer model is disclosed coordination with direct customer contracts; prime contracting would assume full statutory/subcontractor liability.

## Acquisition Route

Use the family renewable-installer route to identify PV jobs blocked by asbestos roofs, and municipal opt-in outreach for already approved owners. Cheapest proof: five refundable scheduling deposits, written contractor/landfill/roofer slots and full customer material funding before orders.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Current landfill capacity/reservability; clustered rates; subsidy cash timing; owner ability to coordinate replacement; structural exclusions; notification/weather liability; insurance; lawful lead-list access; incumbent response. Current disposal-slot scarcity and fixed-date reliability remain private-proof questions.
