# Level-2 Dossier — DIR-009

Lead: `RAW-FPE-05`, shared mobile leak-detection asset. Development status: `complete`.

## Problem And Payer Evidence

GUS exposes gmina-level loss volume, percentage, network length and failures. Some utilities report 14.5–46% losses, but these include non-physical causes. NIK found direct variable lost-water cost of only 0.47–0.51 PLN/m³ at two utilities, far below retail tariffs. Incumbent diagnostics are cheap: public offers around 366–524 PLN for the first labor hour plus follow-on time. Need exists, but equipment access alone is not differentiated.

## Proposed Transaction

Sell a fixed isolated-zone baseline using temporary flow/pressure logging plus acoustic instruments, then a tightly capped fee on verified avoided variable cost. Utility staff retain valves, SCADA and repairs. A small-zone test may be 3–8k PLN fixed; renting/subcontracting for 5–15k PLN is safer than buying 50–80k PLN of equipment before utilization proof.

## Acquisition Route

Filter GUS data for high absolute and percentage loss, exclude utilities already selling their own diagnostics, and approach technical directors with a zone-feasibility protocol below simplified-procurement limits. Neighbouring utilities with underused equipment are both substitutes and partners.

## Decision-Critical Unknowns

Isolatable zones; meter accuracy; physical versus accounting loss; performance on plastic/low-pressure networks; incumbent price; equipment quotes; attribution; repair capacity; repeat frequency/utilization; data rights. The avoided-cost denominator may be too low for outcome economics.
