## Diversity And Stopping Finding

The strongest candidates across both live waves did not fail because discovery stayed in one sector or one business-model family. They failed at the next empirical layer: an indispensable buyer, owner, operator, processor or debtor had to grant authority, pay an advance/reservation, acknowledge a claim, or accept a complete price that survives an incumbent route-around.

No materially untested mechanism/control class is named by the accumulated live record. A third wave would therefore repeat public hypothesis generation without resolving the private acceptance bottleneck. The stopping rule is satisfied without using shadow omissions, candidate content, rankings, evaluator scores or validator feedback as a regeneration input.
