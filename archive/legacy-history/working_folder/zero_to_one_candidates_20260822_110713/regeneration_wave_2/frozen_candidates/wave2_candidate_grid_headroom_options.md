## Exact Candidate

Create a founder-controlled Polish SPV that obtains a counsel-reviewed, 60–90-day conditional option over one existing energy-site owner's cooperation, exclusivity, data access, land nomination and participation in a lawful cable-pooling or complementary-storage application. A licensed grid engineer first screens the connection documents and interval data. The SPV then sells a qualified storage developer a non-refundable reservation for exclusive diligence and nomination rights. The developer—not the founder—funds all operator application fees, connection advances, securities, detailed engineering, construction and operating risk. The SPV never represents that it owns or can transfer grid capacity; it owns only the contractual option and transaction work product.

The first transaction is deliberately thin: one verified owner option, one screened data room, and one developer reservation. No speculative portfolio is acquired before that paid proof.

## Customer, Payer And Trigger

- Immediate payer: a qualified storage developer, IPP, utility or infrastructure investor.
- Supply counterparty: an existing connected owner with interval data, cooperation authority and usable land rights.
- Paid trigger: the developer accepts the redacted data room and pays for exclusive diligence before the owner identity and full documents are released.
- Owner consideration: a modest option premium plus a fixed, per-MW or minority closing participation agreed site by site.

## Contract And Risk Boundary

1. Owner signs NDA and limited data-access letter.
2. Engineer screens 12–24 months of interval data, connection capacity, protection, outages and complementary profiles.
3. SPV pays for a time-limited option covering exclusivity, owner cooperation, land arrangements and nomination of a qualified developer.
4. Developer pays a non-refundable reservation and funds further diligence.
5. Definitive fees are staged against signed documents and operator or lender conditions.
6. Long-stop and unwind clauses expire the option if approvals do not arrive.

The network operator, owner, secured lender, landowner and developer retain their actual statutory and contractual powers. Counsel must confirm that the option preserves meaningful exclusivity without purporting to transfer a connection right.

## First Paid Proof And Maximum Loss

- Use the family installer relationship to seek one owner with complete connection documents and interval data.
- Spend 5,000–10,000 PLN on the engineer screen, 5,000–10,000 PLN on documents and data-room terms, and 5,000–10,000 PLN on owner exclusivity.
- Present the resulting teaser to at least 15 qualified developers.
- Seek a 15,000–30,000 PLN non-refundable reservation within eight weeks.
- Founder-funded proof ceiling: 30,000 PLN. No founder-funded connection advance, security or construction.

## Economics — Planning Assumptions, Not Observed Prices

- Owner option premium: 10,000–25,000 PLN.
- Developer reservation: 15,000–35,000 PLN.
- Later assignment/development fee: 50,000–100,000 PLN per usable MW, minimum 100,000–150,000 PLN.
- Illustrative three-MW transaction: 225,000 PLN revenue and 145,000 PLN gross profit after owner, engineering, legal, referral and diligence costs.

The modeled path to founder wealth exceeds 5 million PLN only in the expected case: 24 annual transactions at 225,000 PLN each by year 7, 31.1% operating margin and 85% founder ownership imply roughly 7.24 million PLN of founder wealth. This is highly sensitive to observed reservation prices, close rates, option failures, buyer count and transaction duration.

## Acquisition And Compounding Control

- Supply: installer, O&M, land-agent, renewable-adviser and project-finance-counsel referrals.
- Demand: named Polish storage developers and infrastructure buyers reached with standardized redacted site briefs.
- Compounding asset: enforceable options, repeat buyer diligence standards, owner/referrer channels, operator-specific process knowledge and realized conversion data.
- Incumbent route-around: developers can originate internally or buy complete ready-to-build projects, so the thin option must be materially faster or cheaper than those routes.

## Closed Facts And Indispensable Unknown

Polish cable-pooling rules support multiparty storage structures, public evidence shows institutional acquisition of a 250 MW / 1 GWh grid-secured ready-to-build BESS project, and named developer prospects are accessible. Public evidence does not show an owner granting this exact thin option or a developer paying this exact reservation. The indispensable premise is therefore:

> One technically credible owner will grant an enforceable short option, and one qualified developer will pay a non-refundable reservation while funding all operator-facing costs.

Stop if ten suitable owners yield no option at 25,000 PLN or less, three complete sites yield no credible complementary capacity, twenty qualified developers yield no 15,000 PLN reservation, counsel rejects meaningful exclusivity, or a successful transaction cannot produce at least 100,000 PLN gross profit.
