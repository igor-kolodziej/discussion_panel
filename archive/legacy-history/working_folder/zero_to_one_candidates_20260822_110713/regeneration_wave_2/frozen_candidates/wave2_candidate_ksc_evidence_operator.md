## Exact Candidate

Provide already-classified Polish medium entities with a counsel- and MSP-supported managed operation for KSC registry submissions, 14-day data updates, named-role administration, recurring evidence custody, incident intake and regulator-ready incident packages. The company sells a fixed paid onboarding dossier followed by prepaid quarterly custody. It uses an external qualified 24/7 MSSP for out-of-hours intake before promising deadline coverage. The client retains statutory management responsibility and the authority to classify and submit incidents; the operator acts only under explicit roles and powers.

This is not a generic cyber consultancy or a claim that registration transfers responsibility. The wedge is the operational gap between a one-time filing and continuously retrievable evidence across the client's counsel, MSP, executives and incident responders.

## Customer, Payer And Trigger

- Customer and payer: an already-classified medium entity subject to the applicable Polish KSC duties.
- Acquisition controller: its MSP, counsel, auditor or insurer/broker.
- First paid trigger: acceptance and payment for a registry/evidence-readiness dossier.
- Recurring trigger: quarterly prepayment for evidence custody, role updates, source attestations and incident-package readiness.

## Contract And Risk Boundary

1. Counsel confirms scope, proxy limits, GDPR roles, referral arrangements and responsibility language.
2. Client appoints named internal decision owners and grants the minimum external account roles.
3. Operator records source, owner, date and attestation for every evidence item.
4. Client approves classifications and submissions unless a specific lawful proxy applies.
5. A contracted MSSP supplies explicit out-of-hours incident intake and availability terms.
6. Cyber/E&O cover, exclusions, liability caps, subprocessors, encryption, retention and exit exports are agreed before live incident custody.

The service cannot represent itself as the client's statutory management, CSIRT, regulator, certification body or full SOC.
