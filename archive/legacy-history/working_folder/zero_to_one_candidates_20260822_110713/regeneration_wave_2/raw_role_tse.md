## W2-TSE-01 — Principal buyer of separated demolition fractions

- **One-sentence transaction:** Contract with small demolition contractors as the principal accepting specified, pre-sorted mineral, gypsum, wood, metal, glass and plastic batches, reserve downstream processing capacity, and retain the spread between the contractor’s gate fee and processors’ prices or recovered-material proceeds.
- **Sector / mechanism:** Construction waste / owned acceptance capacity plus material title.
- **Loss bearer:** Contractor paying for containers, rejected loads, re-sorting and idle crews.
- **Payer:** Demolition or refurbishment contractor.
- **Beneficiary:** Contractor, licensed processor and recovered-material buyer.
- **Authority holder:** Waste holder, permitted processor, relevant environmental authority and BDO system.
- **Distributor:** Skip suppliers, demolition subcontractors, construction estimators and the family installation firm’s builder relationships.
- **Information holder:** Contractor knows upcoming fractions; processor knows acceptance specifications and available capacity.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant after accepting contractual contamination, rejection and price risk.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant contracting in its own name for each accepted batch and its downstream capacity.
- **First paid event:** Contractor pays the first invoice for an accepted, specification-compliant load.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Exclusive contractor supply agreements, processor slots, load-composition history and repeatable acceptance specifications.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Obtain written specifications and conditional capacity from two permitted processors, then contract one small roof or refurbishment batch without owning vehicles; **PLN 8,000–25,000** for testing, deposits, sampling, insurance and licensed transport.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q01/S01:** Since 1 January 2025, covered construction and demolition waste must be collected separately into at least seven listed material groups; unsorted loads must subsequently be sorted. [Official Waste Act text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/1587/text.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Local gate prices by fraction; minimum loads; rejection frequency; whether processors will reserve capacity for a new principal; working-capital delay; exact permit and BDO position when title passes without founder-operated storage.
- **Constraint class:** Binding waste law, permit scope, contamination technology and unit economics.
- **Lawful structure:** Begin with direct transfer between the contractor and permitted processor under documented KPO/BDO movements; take title or possession only where registration, permits and insurance expressly allow it.
- **Prohibited reliance:** False waste codes, concealed contamination, sham “product” declarations, undocumented transport or use of an adviser’s permit as if it belonged to the entrant.

## W2-TSE-02 — Clean excavation-material title and matching desk

- **One-sentence transaction:** Acquire documented clean excavated material or a contractually defined recovery batch from constrained sites, then resell or place it into pre-contracted lawful earthworks instead of leaving the generator to buy disposal.
- **Sector / mechanism:** Civil works / inventory title and destination rights.
- **Loss bearer:** Excavation contractor or developer facing haulage, storage, rejection and disposal costs.
- **Payer:** Receiving earthworks contractor for qualifying material, or the generating site for a guaranteed lawful destination.
- **Beneficiary:** Both sites: one clears material and the other avoids buying virgin fill.
- **Authority holder:** Original holder, receiving-site owner, permitted recovery operator where applicable, laboratory and environmental/building authorities.
- **Distributor:** Geotechnical laboratories, excavation firms, surveyors, road contractors and renewable-installation trenching contacts.
- **Information holder:** Site manager holds schedules and volumes; laboratory holds composition results; receiving contractor holds the fill specification.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant for misclassification, failed tests, schedule mismatch and rejected loads once it becomes principal.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant owning the matched batch or an exclusive purchase-and-placement option.
- **First paid event:** Receiving contractor pays for the first accepted batch, or the generator pays the contracted placement charge.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Exclusive forward rights to qualifying batches, receiving-site capacity reservations and a dataset linking soil parameters to acceptance outcomes.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Match one small trenching or foundation job to a nearby permitted destination using independent sampling and third-party haulage; **PLN 10,000–35,000**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q01/S01:** Polish waste law imposes defined sorting duties and record-related exceptions for construction and demolition streams, showing that treatment depends on the material’s legal status and documentation rather than colloquial labels such as “clean soil.” [Official Waste Act text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/1587/text.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Frequency of genuinely compatible site pairs; testing cost and turnaround; liability allocation after placement; local interpretation of product, by-product and waste status; seasonal demand for fill.
- **Constraint class:** Binding waste classification, technical specification, timing and transport economics.
- **Lawful structure:** Use independent test results and a written legal classification for every batch; where it remains waste, place it only through authorised recovery and transport arrangements.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Relabelling waste as soil, fabricated test results, dilution, dumping, backdated documents or placement inconsistent with planning and environmental conditions.

## W2-TSE-03 — Non-recourse purchase of accepted OZE curtailment receivables

- **One-sentence transaction:** After an eligible renewable owner has documented a PSE-ordered reduction and submitted a conforming application, purchase the accepted or sufficiently mature compensation receivable at a discount and collect the eventual payment.
- **Sector / mechanism:** Renewable energy / receivable acquisition.
- **Loss bearer:** OZE owner carrying lost production and a delayed, document-heavy compensation process.
- **Payer:** PSE under the applicable non-market redispatch compensation process.
- **Beneficiary:** OZE owner receives earlier, certain cash; entrant receives the purchased receivable’s proceeds.
- **Authority holder:** Installation owner, PSE, relevant DSO and any secured lender or contractual counterparty whose consent is required.
- **Distributor:** Renewable accountants, asset managers, installers and the family firm’s commercial-PV customers.
- **Information holder:** PSE and DSO hold dispatch and connection records; owner holds settlement, support-scheme and production data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant for timing, evidentiary defects and non-payment after a disclosed non-recourse purchase.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as assignee or purchaser of the receivable, subject to assignability and required acknowledgements.
- **First paid event:** PSE settles the first purchased receivable; alternatively, the owner pays a separately contracted preparation fee before any purchase.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Historical claim files, eligibility rules encoded into underwriting, payment-duration data and repeat seller agreements.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Review archived curtailment files for several commercial installations, have counsel produce one assignment form, and buy one small post-submission receivable; **PLN 15,000–50,000**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q02/Q03/S02:** PSE states that owners complying with identified PSE curtailment instructions may apply for compensation; it also states that compensation is unavailable where connection terms do not guarantee reliable delivery for the relevant balancing causes. [PSE non-market redispatch page](https://www.pse.pl/pl_PL/redysponowanie-nierynkowe), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Assignability before determination; lender consent; claim-processing duration; minimum economic claim size; incidence of exclusions; treatment of auction, PPA and balancing revenues.
- **Constraint class:** Contract law, EU electricity rules, process evidence and underwriting economics.
- **Lawful structure:** Purchase only disclosed receivables supported by owner authority, legal review and any required PSE/DSO or lender acknowledgement; keep application facts attributable to the owner.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Fabricated baselines, duplicate recovery, impersonation, guaranteed approval, interference with dispatch instructions or concealment of non-guaranteed connection terms.

## W2-TSE-04 — Exclusive options over underused grid-connection headroom

- **One-sentence transaction:** Pay existing renewable owners for time-limited exclusive options over demonstrably complementary connection headroom, then sell or exercise those rights with a qualified co-located generator or storage developer.
- **Sector / mechanism:** Energy infrastructure / capacity option.
- **Loss bearer:** Existing owner with a costly connection that is underused during predictable periods, and developers blocked from ordinary connection capacity.
- **Payer:** Storage or complementary-generation developer buying the option, development right or capacity participation.
- **Beneficiary:** Existing connection holder, incoming project and ultimately the shared infrastructure.
- **Authority holder:** Existing installation owner, incoming developer, network operator, landowner and relevant secured lenders.
- **Distributor:** Commercial-PV installers, land agents, renewable advisers and storage developers.
- **Information holder:** Connection holder has operational and contractual data; operator controls connection approval; developer knows equipment economics.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant for option premiums and failed operator or lender consent; technical-development risk remains with the qualified developer.
- **Transaction owner:** An entrant-controlled option SPV holding exclusivity and conditional assignment rights.
- **First paid event:** Developer pays a reservation or option-assignment fee for a site-specific connection package.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Exclusive site options, consent-ready contract templates, interval-generation profiles and a proprietary pipeline of compatible sites.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Through installer relationships, secure one low-cost conditional option over a commercial PV connection and package its documents for a storage developer; **PLN 10,000–30,000**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q04/S03:** URE’s monitoring found that cable pooling was most often used where one installation was already connected, while simultaneous or secondary connections were less common; the regulator identified legal-support interactions as a practical barrier. [URE cable-pooling monitoring](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/12985%2Cdok.html), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Current eligibility of independent storage; operator-specific procedures; assignability; lender consent; actual spare headroom after clipping and outages; option duration needed to reach consent.
- **Constraint class:** Binding energy law, network-code limits, contract control and development economics.
- **Lawful structure:** Make every option conditional on operator, owner, land and lender approvals; use licensed engineers for grid studies and keep the SPV’s role to rights ownership and commercial development.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Selling nonexistent capacity, bypassing the operator, approval guarantees, undisclosed double options or exceeding registered export limits.

## W2-TSE-05 — Municipality-paid textile acceptance blocks

- **Sector / mechanism:** Municipal textiles / mandatory acceptance capacity plus recovered-output rights.
- **Loss bearer:** Municipality funding obligatory collection amid volatile treatment prices and weak second-hand demand.
- **Payer:** Municipality, municipal waste company or incumbent system contractor.
- **Beneficiary:** Municipality receives known capacity; sorter receives predictable feedstock; reuse and fibre buyers receive graded fractions.
- **Authority holder:** Municipality, PSZOK operator, licensed collector/sorter and procurement authority.
- **Distributor:** Municipal procurement notices, PSZOK operators and regional waste companies.
- **Information holder:** Municipality holds tonnage and seasonal data; sorter holds yield and contamination data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant for unused reserved capacity, grade mix and downstream price changes.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as principal holder of contracted acceptance blocks and output rights, with physical handling by permitted operators.
- **First paid event:** Municipality pays for the first reserved block or accepted tonne.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Multi-period capacity rights, grade-yield history, municipal contracting references and reusable-fraction offtakes.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Reserve a small block at one licensed sorter and bid as a disclosed consortium or subcontracted principal for a limited collection period; **PLN 20,000–60,000**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q06/S04:** Municipalities must accept separately collected textiles through at least PSZOK infrastructure, while the ministry identifies weak second-hand values, export restrictions and rising labour, energy and transport costs. [Ministry textile guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/tekstylia), accessed 2026-08-22.

- **Constraint class:** Municipal procurement, waste permits, commodity economics and quality risk.
- **Lawful structure:** Contract permitted operators for every physical step, disclose consortium roles and price acceptance separately from any recovered-output allocation.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Charity branding for waste, exporting unusable material as reusable clothing, hidden subcontractors, false recovery records or off-permit storage.

## W2-TSE-06 — Take-title service for decommissioned PV modules

- **Payer:** Installation owner, insurer or repowering contractor.
- **Beneficiary:** Owner receives compliant clearance; secondary buyers receive tested modules; recycler receives consolidated failures.
- **Authority holder:** Equipment owner, waste holder, permitted recycler and programme administrator where public funding conditions apply.
- **Distributor:** The family renewable-installation firm, insurers, roofers and commercial repowering contractors.
- **Information holder:** Installer holds serial numbers and failure history; owner holds funding records; test shop holds electrical condition data.
- **Risk bearer:** Entrant for title, testing, breakage, warranty disclaimers and negative-value failures.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as purchaser of each documented batch and seller of passed modules or components.
- **First paid event:** Owner pays the first removal invoice or a secondary buyer pays for the first tested batch.
- **Control / compounding asset:** Installer supply agreements, serial-level test histories, secondary-buyer channels and recycler pricing.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Use the family firm to source one small repowering batch; contract testing, pallets and licensed transport; **PLN 12,000–40,000**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **Q07/Q08/S06:** Mój Prąd guidance states that, following the 2025 amendment it describes, end users bear disposal costs for covered used equipment including PV panels, and improper handling can breach programme conditions. [Mój Prąd recycling guidance](https://mojprad.gov.pl/obowiazek-recyklingu/), accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Precise responsibility by installation date and producer; reusable yield; insurability; secondary-market prices; treatment cost; required evidence for funded systems; transport-breakage rate.
- **Constraint class:** WEEE law, grant conditions, electrical safety and salvage economics.
- **Lawful structure:** Distinguish functioning used equipment from waste through documented testing and intent; transfer failed equipment directly through authorised WEEE channels.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Exporting waste as working equipment, fake test certificates, stripping serial numbers, unsafe resale, concealed defects or misrepresenting public-funding obligations.

- **Constraint class:** EU battery law, dangerous-goods transport, fire safety, product liability and residual-value uncertainty.

- **Prohibited reliance:** Opening energised packs without competence, ordinary warehousing, false state-of-health reports, disguising waste as a product, unauthorised transport or exporting hazardous packs to evade treatment costs.
