# Regeneration Wave 2 — Incentive-Hacker Report

## W2-IHK-01 — White-certificate proceeds for energy-efficiency projects

- **One-sentence transaction:** Before an eligible efficiency project starts, contract for an explicit share of the proceeds from its energy-efficiency certificates in exchange for assembling the audit, application and sale workflow.
- **Loss bearer:** Building or industrial owner that completes eligible work without monetising documented final-energy savings.
- **Payer:** Buyer of the certificate’s property rights, ultimately serving an obligated energy-market participant.
- **Beneficiary:** Project owner, with the renewable-installation firm benefiting from a higher-value sales package.
- **Authority holder:** Project owner or another entity legally entitled to apply to URE and dispose of the resulting rights.
- **Distributor:** Family renewable installer, HVAC and electrical contractors, energy auditors and commercial-property accountants.
- **Information holder:** Project owner and installer holding invoices, designs, baseline consumption and commissioning records.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears agreed preparation costs; owner bears project-performance risk; qualified auditor bears responsibility for the audit.
- **Transaction owner:** Written servicing agreement plus a disclosed assignment or proceeds-sharing clause covering the identified project.
- **First paid event:** Certificate rights are sold and the agreed proceeds share becomes payable.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Structured database of equipment changes, savings calculations, application status, certificate issuance and realised proceeds.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Use the family firm to identify one not-yet-started heat-pump, control-system or commercial PV-adjacent efficiency project; fund eligibility counsel and a qualified audit partner for **5,000–20,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q01/S01]** URE describes fulfilment through final-customer efficiency projects producing final-energy savings, efficiency certificates or the substitute fee. [URE mechanics](https://ure.gov.pl/pl/oze/umarzanie-swiadectw/6738%2CZasady-realizacji-obowiazku-w-zakresie-umarzania-swiadectw-efektywnosci-energety.pdf) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Current pre-commencement rules; qualifying project types; audit cost; certificate issuance time; current property-right price; assignment mechanics; tax treatment.
- **Constraint class:** Regulated incentive; technical evidence; timing-dependent eligibility.
- **Lawful structure:** Qualified energy auditor, customer-authorised URE filing, transparent proceeds clause and licensed brokerage where certificate trading requires it.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Post-dating project commencement, fabricated baselines, double counting savings, undisclosed assignment or impersonating URE or an auditor.

## W2-IHK-02 — Construction payment-guarantee completion desk

- **One-sentence transaction:** For a fixed or delivery-contingent fee, assemble the contract evidence and coordinate a bank or insurer so a contractor receives the statutory payment guarantee requested from its investor.
- **Loss bearer:** Contractor exposed to investor non-payment while continuing to finance labour and materials.
- **Payer:** Contractor pays the service fee; investor and contractor share documented guarantee costs as prescribed by the cited Civil Code provisions.
- **Beneficiary:** Contractor receiving secured payment coverage.
- **Authority holder:** Contractor decides whether to demand the guarantee; investor commissions the bank or insurance instrument.
- **Distributor:** Renewable and electrical contractors, construction accountants, trade associations and contract-law advisers.
- **Information holder:** Contractor holding the works contract, approved extras, invoices, schedules and correspondence.
- **Risk bearer:** Bank or insurer bears instrument credit risk; contractor bears uncovered payment risk; founder bears only scoped preparation costs.
- **Transaction owner:** Engagement covering one named contract, defined document package and guarantee-delivery event.
- **First paid event:** A compliant guarantee is delivered and accepted under the contractor’s engagement.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Clause library, evidence checklist, guarantee-provider routing data and anonymised timelines by investor and project type.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Commission a Polish construction lawyer to create the workflow and use contractor contacts around the family firm for one live contract; **3,000–12,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q03/S03]** Polish Civil Code arts. 649¹–649⁴ establish a contractor’s payment-guarantee right, identify bank and insurance instruments, split documented costs and provide a response period of at least 45 days. [Official consolidated Civil Code](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/1360/text.html) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Applicability to the specific contracting tier; guarantee amount; provider underwriting appetite; treatment of disputed extras; professional-advice boundary.
- **Constraint class:** Contract-specific statutory right; banking or insurance dependency.
- **Lawful structure:** Founder supplies document operations while Polish counsel handles legal interpretation and the regulated institution issues the instrument.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Acting as a bank, insurer or lawyer; misstating debt; threatening parties without authority; altering contracts or correspondence.

## W2-IHK-03 — Customs-duty repayment recovery

- **One-sentence transaction:** Recover overpaid import or export duties for Polish importers under an authorised, case-specific mandate and receive a disclosed percentage only after repayment.
- **Loss bearer:** Importer that leaves recoverable customs duty with the treasury.
- **Payer:** Customs authority repays the importer; importer pays the agreed recovery fee.
- **Beneficiary:** Importer or exporter named in the customs record.
- **Authority holder:** Claimant or declarant, subject to customs-representation rules.
- **Distributor:** Customs agencies, freight forwarders, import accountants and e-commerce operators.
- **Information holder:** Importer, declarant and broker holding declarations, classification, valuation, contracts and defect evidence.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears preparation cost under a contingent engagement; claimant bears disclosure accuracy; authorised representative bears filing responsibility.
- **Transaction owner:** Servicing contract tied to specified customs entries and repayment grounds.
- **First paid event:** Customs repayment reaches the claimant’s controlled bank account.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Entry-level anomaly dataset linking commodity codes, declared values, duty paid, correction grounds, evidence and outcomes.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Partner with a licensed customs agency and inspect an importer’s historical entries for overvaluation, incorrect rates or defective returned goods; **5,000–15,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q04/S04]** Poland’s tax portal lists repayment grounds including overcharged duty, invalidated declarations, defective or non-conforming goods, authority error and equity. [Polish customs repayment guidance](https://www.podatki.gov.pl/informacje-o-cle/podstawowe-informacje-o-cle/zabezpieczenie-i-pobor-naleznosci-celnych-oraz-zwrot-i-umorzenia-naleznosci-celnych/zwroty-i-umorzenia-naleznosci-celnych) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Limitation periods; representation requirements; recoverable entry volume; access to source documents; repayment duration; treatment of VAT and interest.
- **Constraint class:** Regulated filing; document completeness; statutory deadlines.
- **Lawful structure:** Read-only entry analysis followed by claimant authorisation and filing through an authorised customs representative where required.
- **Prohibited reliance:** False classification, concealed relationships, fabricated defect evidence, unauthorised portal access or routing refunds through the founder.

## W2-IHK-04 — Foreign business-VAT recovery

- **One-sentence transaction:** Identify recoverable foreign VAT in Polish companies’ travel and supplier ledgers, coordinate compliant filings and charge an agreed share only when the foreign authority pays.
- **Loss bearer:** Business writing off eligible foreign VAT as an expense.
- **Payer:** Foreign tax authority repays the claimant; claimant pays the recovery fee.
- **Beneficiary:** VAT-registered business incurring eligible cross-border expenses.
- **Authority holder:** Claimant’s authorised finance or tax representative.
- **Distributor:** Travel-management firms, event agencies, accountants, transport associations and expense-software advisers.
- **Information holder:** Finance team holding invoices, receipts, expense records and VAT-registration evidence.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears screening and preparation cost; claimant bears invoice authenticity; qualified tax partner bears filing responsibility.
- **Transaction owner:** Country- and period-specific recovery engagement with payment triggered by received refunds.
- **First paid event:** A foreign tax authority deposits an approved refund into the client’s account.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Jurisdictional eligibility rules, invoice classification model, deadline calendar and refund-outcome history.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Process a read-only export from one Warsaw company with recurring trade-fair or foreign-travel expenditure alongside a VAT adviser; **4,000–15,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q05/S05]** A live substitute offers EU and UK foreign-VAT recovery with no upfront fee and payment as an agreed percentage after the overseas authority processes the refund. [Global Tax Reclaim](https://globaltaxreclaim.com/reclaim-foreign-vat/) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Country-specific expense eligibility; invoice defects; filing deadlines; minimum claim amounts; adviser authorisation; expected refund timing.
- **Constraint class:** Multi-jurisdiction tax procedure; sensitive financial records.
- **Lawful structure:** Read-only extraction, written authority, client-controlled refunds and filings reviewed or submitted by qualified tax professionals.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Invented invoices, altered expense purposes, duplicate claims, false VAT-registration statements or holding client refunds without proper authority.

## W2-IHK-05 — Cloud-SLA credit recovery

- **One-sentence transaction:** Reconcile customer observability logs against cloud SLA terms, submit authorised credit requests and receive a percentage of credits posted to future bills.
- **Loss bearer:** Cloud customer that experiences qualifying downtime but misses the provider’s evidence or notice requirements.
- **Payer:** Cloud provider through account credits; customer pays the founder from the realised credit under the engagement.
- **Beneficiary:** Customer whose future cloud bill is reduced.
- **Authority holder:** Customer’s cloud-contract and support-account administrator.
- **Distributor:** Managed-service providers, FinOps consultants, cloud accountants and software houses.
- **Information holder:** Customer holding billing exports, architecture data, monitoring logs and support records.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears claim-preparation cost; customer retains operational risk; provider decides contractual eligibility.
- **Transaction owner:** Read-only monitoring and claims mandate covering identified projects, services and billing accounts.
- **First paid event:** Provider posts an approved financial credit to the customer’s account.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Machine-readable SLA library, outage-to-project attribution engine, evidence bundles and provider-response history.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Connect one cooperative company’s Google Cloud billing and monitoring exports to a narrow reconciliation script; **2,000–8,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q07/S06]** Google Compute Engine provides bill credits of 10%, 25% or 100% for specified uptime bands and requires the customer to request credit with logs within 60 days. [Google Compute Engine SLA](https://cloud.google.com/compute/sla) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Frequency and value of qualifying events; log sufficiency; reseller-account ownership; claim friction; treatment of exclusions; procurement resistance to contingent fees.
- **Constraint class:** Contract credit; short evidence window; technical attribution.
- **Lawful structure:** Customer-authorised, read-only access; claims submitted in the customer’s name; no changes to workloads or monitoring history.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Creating outages, fabricating logs, concealing customer-caused incidents, unauthorised support access or overstating covered spend.

## W2-IHK-06 — Late-parcel guarantee recovery

- **One-sentence transaction:** Detect eligible late time-definite shipments, file authorised requests inside carrier deadlines and retain a disclosed share of refunded shipping charges.
- **Loss bearer:** High-volume shipper that pays for guaranteed service but does not request available refunds.
- **Payer:** Carrier credits or refunds the party that paid for carriage.
- **Beneficiary:** Merchant, marketplace seller or 3PL account owner.
- **Authority holder:** Shipper or other contracting party entitled under the carrier account.
- **Distributor:** E-commerce accountants, fulfilment centres, shipping-software integrators and merchant associations.
- **Information holder:** Shipper and carrier holding labels, tracking events, service commitments, weights and account charges.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears detection and filing cost; shipper bears documentation accuracy; carrier applies exclusions.
- **Transaction owner:** Carrier-account-specific recovery mandate and claim ledger.
- **First paid event:** Refund or credit appears on the shipper’s carrier statement.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Lane-level commitment history, exclusion classifier, tracking reconciliation and realised-credit dataset.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Analyse one merchant’s historical UPS export and submit only customer-approved claims still inside the contractual window; **2,000–10,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q08/S07]** UPS’s 2026 guide provides an on-request refund of qualifying shipping charges, requires notice within 15 calendar days and excludes documentation failures, force majeure and specified handling classes. [UPS 2026 Service Guide](https://assets.ups.com/adobe/assets/urn%3Aaaid%3Aaem%3A29892e1f-11d8-4d8f-8008-f31bf6593dbc/original/as/service-guide-base-ie-gb-en.pdf) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Polish-account guarantee coverage; eligible service mix; contractual waiver language; data-export access; credit frequency; carrier automation limits.
- **Constraint class:** Contractual refund; very short claim window; shipment-level exclusions.
- **Lawful structure:** Written shipper mandate, account-scoped API access and fees based only on posted credits.
- **Prohibited reliance:** False lateness records, claims for excluded services, credential sharing outside carrier rules, duplicate filing or disrupting deliveries.

## W2-IHK-07 — Commercial service-charge recovery

- **One-sentence transaction:** Compare a commercial tenant’s lease with service-charge statements, pursue documented corrections under explicit authority and receive a percentage of recovered or credited overcharges.
- **Loss bearer:** Tenant paying misallocated, duplicated or contractually unrecoverable building costs.
- **Payer:** Landlord or managing agent through refund, credit or corrected balance.
- **Beneficiary:** Commercial tenant.
- **Authority holder:** Tenant under its lease and audit provisions.
- **Distributor:** Tenant brokers, multi-site retailers, franchise accountants and property advisers.
- **Information holder:** Tenant holds the lease and statements; landlord or manager holds invoices, allocation schedules and service accounts.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears preliminary review cost; tenant controls any dispute; regulated advisers handle legal or accounting opinions.
- **Transaction owner:** Property- and accounting-period-specific audit engagement with documented authority limits.
- **First paid event:** Landlord issues a refund, credit note or accepted reduction.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Lease-clause parser, charge taxonomy, apportionment benchmarks and anonymised correction history.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Engage a Polish property lawyer and surveyor to review one multi-year retail or office tenancy; **8,000–25,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q09/S08]** A current commercial substitute identifies mis-apportionment, landlord-only costs and fee-cap issues, uses human approval before external action and charges 25% of documented recovery. [Chargewise](https://getchargewise.com/) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Polish lease audit rights; statement limitation periods; access to underlying invoices; regulated-advice boundaries; landlord set-off rights; local fee norms.
- **Constraint class:** Contract interpretation; property-specific evidence; professional-advice boundary.
- **Lawful structure:** Evidence review under recorded tenant authority, with Polish counsel or qualified property professionals handling reserved work and disputes.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Manufacturing disputes, withholding undisputed rent, impersonating the tenant, making unsupported allegations or contacting landlords without approval.

## W2-IHK-08 — Solar warranty deployment-fee capture

- **One-sentence transaction:** Administer eligible inverter and component replacements for installers, retain an agreed share of manufacturer deployment fees and pass through pre-agreed transport reimbursements.
- **Loss bearer:** Installer absorbing diagnosis, removal, return and replacement labour because warranty proceeds are not claimed correctly.
- **Payer:** Manufacturer or its appointed service company.
- **Beneficiary:** Installer receives reimbursement; system owner receives completed warranty service.
- **Authority holder:** Eligible warranty customer and authorised or qualified installer.
- **Distributor:** Family renewable firm, regional solar installers, wholesalers and inverter service partners.
- **Information holder:** Installer and owner holding serial numbers, purchase evidence, commissioning data, fault records and return tracking.
- **Risk bearer:** Installer bears rejected-job labour unless otherwise contracted; founder bears administration cost; manufacturer applies warranty exclusions.
- **Transaction owner:** Installer-authorised RMA and proceeds-administration agreement for specified equipment.
- **First paid event:** Manufacturer pays the deployment fee after receiving the matching defective product.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Installed-base warranty registry, serial-to-policy rules, fault evidence, return deadlines and reimbursement history.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Use the family firm’s installed base to process one genuine covered failure and document every portal and logistics step; **1,000–5,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q10/S09]** Huawei’s EU terms provide a **€110** installer deployment fee for listed products, **€25** for later defective optimisers and payment within 60 days after receipt, subject to matching returns and installation requirements. [Huawei FusionSolar EU warranty conditions](https://ske-solar.com/en/support-contact/warranty-terms) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Which brands offer comparable labour payments; portal access; authorised-installer conditions; installed-base failure incidence; VAT treatment; customer-consent wording.
- **Constraint class:** Manufacturer warranty; serial-level evidence; return-dependent payment.
- **Lawful structure:** Installer remains service provider and warranty claimant; founder supplies authorised administration and never controls customer warranty rights invisibly.
- **Prohibited reliance:** False fault reports, swapping serial numbers, unauthorised repairs, claiming non-covered damage or hiding reimbursement from the installer or customer.

## W2-IHK-09 — In-store deposit-return station concession

- **One-sentence transaction:** Operate a retailer-authorised manual packaging-acceptance station inside the shop and receive a contracted portion of the handling fee for each correctly recorded return.
- **Loss bearer:** Retailer carrying labour, sorting and reporting costs or forgoing voluntary return traffic and its handling fee.
- **Payer:** Retailer pays the concession fee from handling-fee proceeds settled by its selected DRS operator.
- **Beneficiary:** Retailer, returning consumer and DRS operator.
- **Authority holder:** Retailer controls the collection point and operator agreement; operator controls settlement rules.
- **Distributor:** Independent grocers, small retail chains, wholesalers and retail cooperatives.
- **Information holder:** Retailer and operator hold deposit sales, return counts, settlement data and packaging eligibility rules.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears staffing and sorting cost; retailer retains deposit-settlement responsibility; operator applies acceptance rules.
- **Transaction owner:** Store-level concession covering a defined station, operating hours, records and handling-fee split.
- **First paid event:** Retailer receives operator settlement for recorded returns and pays the contractual station share.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Packaging-recognition data, store-level throughput, staffing curves, contamination records and operator reconciliation history.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Equip one consenting small shop with bins, scanner, scales and operating procedures while its cashier retains cash-refund control; **10,000–30,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q11/S10]** Poland’s ministry states that shops settle deposits with an operator, agreements should specify a handling fee payable to the outlet, and collection may be manual or machine-based. [Ministry of Climate and Environment](https://www.gov.pl/web/climate/the-deposit-refund-system-starts-in-october--how-to-prepare) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Actual operator fee schedules; subcontracting permission; packaging rejection rates; cash-flow timing; hygiene and fire rules; retailer willingness to share point-of-return proceeds.
- **Constraint class:** Regulated retail operation; operator contract; physical handling.
- **Lawful structure:** Retailer remains the statutory collection point and deposit counterparty; founder performs disclosed station services without independently issuing or withholding deposits.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Deposit skimming, accepting unmarked packaging as eligible, double counting returns, misleading vouchers or transporting regulated waste without required authority.

## W2-IHK-10 — Refused-flight-claim evidence desk

- **One-sentence transaction:** After a passenger has filed directly and received a refusal or no response, assemble the flight and expense evidence under a transparent mandate and collect a success fee only from compensation actually received.
- **Loss bearer:** Passenger abandoning a supportable compensation or expense claim after airline resistance.
- **Payer:** Airline pays compensation to the passenger; passenger pays the contracted fee.
- **Beneficiary:** Passenger.
- **Authority holder:** Passenger, or a legal representative under a disclosed mandate or assignment.
- **Distributor:** Travel agencies, corporate travel managers, airport-transfer firms and consumer-law partners.
- **Information holder:** Passenger, airline, airport and flight-data providers.
- **Risk bearer:** Founder bears non-legal preparation cost; passenger bears truthful disclosure; qualified counsel bears litigation responsibility.
- **Transaction owner:** Claim-specific mandate covering one passenger, itinerary and disruption, with any assignment separately and explicitly consented to.
- **First paid event:** Airline compensation reaches the passenger or an authorised client-money channel.
- **Control/compounding asset:** Airline-response corpus, evidence completeness model, disruption records and jurisdiction-specific handoff rules.
- **Founder-accessible first proof with capital range:** Work with a consumer-law adviser on one already-refused claim whose passenger supplies the boarding pass, correspondence and expense evidence; **5,000–20,000 PLN**.
- **Evidence atoms:**
  - **[Q12/S11]** A current fee comparison reports consumer claim-service fees of 25%–44%, rising to 41%–50% for court cases, while direct initial filing is free; statutory compensation amounts cited are €250, €400 and €600. [FlightFund fee comparison](https://flightfund.net/en/guides/service-fees) — accessed **2026-08-22**.
- **Decision-critical unknowns:** Polish legal-services restrictions; assignment enforceability by jurisdiction; access to reliable flight-cause evidence; airline payment time; litigation handoff economics; current legislative changes.
- **Constraint class:** Consumer claim; jurisdiction-dependent law; crowded substitute set.
- **Lawful structure:** Passenger-controlled proceeds, explicit authority, accurate evidence and referral to qualified counsel for legal opinions or proceedings.
- **Prohibited reliance:** Hidden assignment, invented delay causes or expenses, misleading passengers about free direct filing, unauthorised legal practice or holding client money improperly.
