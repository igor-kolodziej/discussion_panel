## Closure finding

**Status: `unknown`**

Public evidence confirms that Polish law supports cable-pooling arrangements involving storage, that institutional buyers acquire grid-secured Polish BESS projects, and that named developers constitute a founder-accessible prospect pool.

It does **not** establish either half of the indispensable commercial premise:

- No public evidence shows an existing owner granting the proposed short-term, nominee-capable option over cooperation, data and land rights.
- No public evidence shows a developer paying a non-refundable reservation specifically for an option over screened connection headroom while separately funding operator-facing costs.

The closest observed transaction was ENGIE’s completed acquisition of a **250 MW / 1 GWh ready-to-build BESS project** from R.Power. That purchase involved a complete, grid-secured RTB project; its price was not disclosed. It therefore proves institutional acquisition appetite, but not willingness to pay for the much thinner proposed product. ## Public facts closed

### Contract, authority, title and operator access

- The 2026 amendment expressly accommodates multiple installations or storage facilities sharing a connection and requires the relevant agreement to identify its parties, associate each installation or storage facility with a party, identify the applicant and include proof of representative authority. This supports a multiparty structure but does not make connection headroom transferable property. [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 516](https://dziennikustaw.gov.pl/D2026000051601.pdf)
- Each owner settles its own imported or exported energy. The parties’ agreement allocates costs associated with metering differences at the common connection point. [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 516](https://dziennikustaw.gov.pl/D2026000051601.pdf)
- The connecting entity or a party to the statutory agreement bears the purchase, installation and maintenance cost of equipment preventing the registered connection limit from being exceeded. The system operator may stop import or export if that equipment malfunctions and the limit is exceeded. [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 516](https://dziennikustaw.gov.pl/D2026000051601.pdf)
- PSE’s May 2026 briefing describes one application covering two or more installations or storage facilities, proof of payment of the application fee, current operator forms and an initial 60-day verification period. It also indicates that the simplified land-title declaration does not replace the underlying title documentation in a cable-pooling application. An operator may request additional documents. [PSE connection briefing](https://www.pse.pl/documents/20182/30599281/Przy%C5%82%C4%85czenia_spotkanie%2Binformacyjne%2B21%2Bmaja%2B2026.pdf/70142b47-a064-4cdc-bc32-dd2ff92b02a6?safeargs=76657273696f6e3d312e32)
- None of these provisions confirms that an outside option SPV can replace the owners or applicants required in the operator-facing arrangement. Its enforceable asset must remain a private option over cooperation, exclusivity, data, land rights and nomination—not a purported ownership interest in grid capacity.

**Status:** `counsel-required`

The declarations are non-binding, units are not unique companies, and the registry does not identify which developers would purchase externally originated sites. [PSE capacity-market figures](https://www.pse.pl/documents/20182/98611984/Rynek_mocy_w_liczbach_CO_2025.pdf)

Membership does not prove qualification for a specific site or willingness to pay a reservation. [PSME members](https://psme.org.pl/czlonkowie/)

[R.Power and ENGIE transaction](https://r-power-s-a_1.prowly.com/463304-rpower-and-engie-complete-largest-utility-scale-bess-transaction-in-poland?preview=true)

### Closest substitutes and incumbent route-around

- R.Power originates and develops grid-secured BESS projects through RTB and sells the complete project. A developer can therefore buy a mature project instead of paying a separate originator for conditional headroom. [R.Power and ENGIE transaction](https://r-power-s-a_1.prowly.com/463304-rpower-and-engie-complete-largest-utility-scale-bess-transaction-in-poland?preview=true)

## Remaining private premises

- A suitable owner will sign enforceable exclusivity at an affordable premium.
- Existing connection, land, financing, PPA and support documents permit the required cooperation and nomination rights.
- The secured lender will consent within the option period.
- Interval data reveals technically usable complementary capacity, including any charging-import requirement.
- The relevant operator will accept the proposed parties, controls and land documentation.
- A qualified developer values the packaged opportunity enough to pay before detailed operator work.
- The developer will fund application fees, engineering, protection equipment, advances and security without requiring founder balance-sheet support.
- Reservation and assignment proceeds exceed owner consideration, professional costs and expired-option losses.
- The named public prospect pool contains enough repeat buyers; the number of unique qualified payers remains unknown.
- Owner and developer documents prevent bypass while remaining commercially acceptable.

## Cheapest resolving test

1. Have Polish energy counsel mark up one short-form owner option and the proposed developer reservation, expressly covering exclusivity, data, land cooperation, nomination, operator-party requirements, lender consent and expiry.
2. Use the existing installer introduction to obtain one real owner’s complete connection documents and interval data.
3. Pay only for a limited engineer screen sufficient to support a redacted data room.
4. Obtain the owner’s executed 60-day conditional option.
5. Present that single site to named storage developers from the public PSME pool.
6. Require one developer to execute the reservation, transfer a genuinely non-refundable payment and commit separate ring-fenced funds for operator-facing work.

An executed owner option plus cleared developer money would close the commercial premise. A legal memo or expressions of interest alone would not.

## Lawful-structure boundary

- The SPV may option contractual cooperation, exclusivity, data access, nomination and conditional land or project rights.
- It must not represent that it owns, reserves or guarantees operator-controlled connection capacity.
- Actual installation owners and properly authorized applicants must remain visible in the statutory multiparty arrangement.
- Nomination or assignment must be conditional on operator, landowner, corporate and lender approvals.
- The developer should pay statutory and operator-facing amounts directly or through ring-fenced escrow. Whether the named applicant can contractually shift each cost requires Polish counsel.
- Approved metering, protection and control must enforce both import and export limits.
- The structure should not depend on the option SPV displacing a party whom statute, an existing connection agreement or the operator requires.
