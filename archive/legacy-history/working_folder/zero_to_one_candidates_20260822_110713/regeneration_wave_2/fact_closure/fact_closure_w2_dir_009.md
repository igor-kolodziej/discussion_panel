## Exact public facts closed

1. **The earlier “local” GZUK evidence is geographically inapplicable.** Its own page identifies the operator at **39-103 Ostrów, Ostrów 225**, with a Rzeszów-area telephone prefix—not Ostrów Wielkopolski. Its 2026 schedules cannot establish beachhead pricing or capacity. [GZUK page](https://www.gzukostrow.pl/index.php/uslugi/2014-11-07-07-47-20)

2. **Orli Staw historically maintained separate prices for outside-member waste and accepted relevant construction codes.** Its tariff, explicitly effective from 1 May 2020, listed outside-member net prices including:

   - clean concrete, code 17 01 01: PLN 11.50/Mg;
   - contaminated concrete: PLN 169.05/Mg;
   - mixed mineral material, code 17 01 07: PLN 224.25/Mg;
   - contaminated mixed mineral material: PLN 349.60/Mg;
   - mixed mineral material with substantial insulation: PLN 920/Mg;
   - clean construction wood: PLN 350.75/Mg;
   - contaminated construction wood: PLN 621/Mg;
   - gypsum, code 17 08 02: PLN 1,150/Mg;
   - mixed construction and demolition waste, code 17 09 04: PLN 1,150/Mg.

   These are historical observations, not current offers or capacity commitments. [Orli Staw tariff](https://www.orlistaw.pl/orlistaw/pliki/Cennik_01-05-2020.pdf)

3. **The historic tariff demonstrates material contamination sensitivity.** For a ten-tonne load, the listed outside-member gate amount mechanically ranges from PLN 115 for clean concrete to PLN 1,690.50 for contaminated concrete, while insulation-heavy mixed mineral material reaches PLN 9,200. A current contribution estimate is not defensible without live specifications and prices.

4. **A local 2026 public-works notice assigns construction-waste removal cost to the contractor.** The Ostrów Wielkopolski road reconstruction includes demolition work and states that removal of construction waste is at the contractor’s expense. This verifies a payer role, but not prepayment or demand for an independent principal. [Procurement notice](https://ezamowienia.gov.pl/mo-client-board/bzp/notice-details/id/08deeeca-3981-42c9-962b-12000107b72b)

5. **The local incumbent route is already simple.** A container operator serving Ostrów Wielkopolski offers companies with BDO numbers individual terms and rapid quotations; price depends on container size, waste type and rental duration. It excludes specified contaminating and hazardous materials. [Gruz Assistance](https://gruz-assistance.pl/lokalizacje/wielkopolskie/ostrowski/ostrow-wielkopolski/)

6. **Local waste incumbents already transact at meaningful contract scale.** A 2025 award to Ostrów Wielkopolski’s MZO covered twelve months and PLN 540,496.80, with subcontracted handling of part of the waste. Its published description does not isolate professional construction-waste generators or their willingness to prepay. [Award notice](https://ezamowienia.gov.pl/mo-client-board/bzp/notice-details/id/08de3bc3-eb17-5e4a-d6d3-f50001ceb3fe)

7. **The official statutory text preserves the possession distinction.** It defines intermediaries as potentially operating without physical possession and states that sellers and intermediaries do not assume waste-management responsibility when they are not waste holders. Storage, by contrast, is tied to waste generation, collection or processing. [Waste Act text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/699/text/U/D20220699Lj.pdf)

8. **No actual payer denominator was closed.** GUS cautions that monthly REGON tables include registered entities regardless of legal-economic activity, while quarterly tables use active entities. From 2025, new or changed entries use PKD 2025 while some unchanged records retain PKD 2007. Neither dataset identifies annual compatible batches or economical processor routes. [GUS REGON tables](https://bip.stat.gov.pl/dzialalnosc-statystyki-publicznej/rejestr-regon/liczba-podmiotow-w-rejestrze-regon-tablice/)

## Cheapest resolving test

Use one already scheduled, photographed, non-hazardous batch—preferably clean concrete or brick—and obtain:

1. Two written processor offers covering code, contamination, current gate price, slot, minimum load, rejection and payment terms.
2. One primary and one backup direct-haul carrier quotation.
3. A contractor-signed conditional order that prepays the quoted transport and processor exposure plus a separately stated coordination fee.
4. Delivery to the primary processor without entrant possession or storage.
5. Final reconciliation using the scale ticket, processor invoice, carrier invoice, contamination adjustment and contractor payment.

The premise is closed only by the combination of two usable processor routes, actual contractor prepayment and positive reconciled contribution. Merely receiving quotations does not close it.

## Lawful-structure boundary

The initial transaction must remain contractor site → BDO-registered carrier → permitted processor.

The entrant should:

- hold the required BDO dealer/broker registration before trading;
- avoid unloading, storage, consolidation, sorting, sampling that changes possession, or reclassification;
- condition commercial acceptance and final price on processor inspection;
- document the actual holder, carrier, receiver and dealer/broker roles correctly;
- avoid representing itself as assuming the contractor’s Article 101a segregation duty;
- obtain written Polish waste-law advice on title, KPO entries, responsibility, VAT, invoicing and insurance before the first load.

Any entrant-operated yard, interim unloading or physical re-sorting crosses into a materially different permission boundary.
