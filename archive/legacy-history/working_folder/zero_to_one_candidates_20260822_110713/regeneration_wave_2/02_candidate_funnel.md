# Regeneration Wave 2 — Economic-Fingerprint Clustering

## W2-DIR-001 — Acquired post-event payment rights

**Thesis:** Purchase an evidenced payment right after the underlying event and earn the discount-to-collection spread.

**Members:** `W2-TSE-03`, `W2-TSE-09`, `W2-IAT-09`, `W2-FPE-05`, `W2-FPE-06`

**Economic fingerprint**

- **Customer/loss:** Renewable owner awaiting curtailment compensation; supplier carrying an overdue invoice; merchant carrying a card dispute; carrier with unpaid detention; subcontractor waiting for retention release.
- **Payer/budget:** PSE, commercial invoice debtor, acquirer or issuer, detention debtor, or construction counterparty.
- **First paid event:** The underlying obligor or payment system settles the assigned right.
- **Transaction owner:** Entrant as disclosed assignee and legal owner; `W2-IAT-09` alternatively permits an exclusive recovery-principal structure where assignment is unavailable.
- **Acquisition route:** Renewable accountants and installers; SME accountants; payment consultants; carrier accountants and fleet associations; construction subcontractor networks.
- **Compounding asset:** Claim book, document-quality rules, obligor payment curves, dispute outcomes and repeat seller agreements.

**Merge rationale:** All five transfer post-event recovery risk and collection economics to the entrant. They remain siblings because payer identity, maturity conditions, evidence type, recovery duration and distribution route are not interchangeable.

**Unresolved sibling tension:** Public compensation, ordinary invoices, card-network proceeds, transport accessorials and construction retentions may have materially different assignment restrictions and servicing requirements.

**Inherited evidence**

- `W2-IAT-09` — Q10/S11, [Stripe Poland pricing](https://stripe.com/en-pl/pricing).
- `W2-FPE-05` — Q05/S04, [UOKiK payment-delay enforcement](https://uokik.gov.pl/public/zatory-platnicze-dzialania-prezesa-uokik); Q04/S03, [Eurostat transport figures](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/15216629/20875401/KS-01-24-021-EN-N.pdf).

## W2-DIR-002 — Authorized contingent recovery administration

**Thesis:** Find and document an existing recovery opportunity under client authority, with compensation triggered by realized proceeds or credits.

**Members:** `W2-IHK-01`, `W2-IHK-03`, `W2-IHK-04`, `W2-IHK-05`, `W2-IHK-06`, `W2-IHK-07`, `W2-IHK-08`, `W2-IHK-10`

**Economic fingerprint**

- **Customer/loss:** Project owner, importer, cross-border business, cloud customer, shipper, commercial tenant, solar installer or passenger failing to monetize an available contractual, regulatory or warranty recovery.
- **Payer/budget:** The client pays from certificate proceeds, treasury refunds, bill credits, freight refunds, property corrections, manufacturer reimbursement or airline compensation.
- **First paid event:** External proceeds or credits reach the client, except where the engagement defines payment immediately after the corresponding manufacturer reimbursement.
- **Transaction owner:** Client or claimant retains the underlying right; entrant operates under a project-, account-, property-, warranty- or claim-specific mandate.
- **Acquisition route:** Installers, auditors, customs and tax advisers, managed-service providers, fulfilment firms, property advisers, wholesalers and travel partners.
- **Compounding asset:** Eligibility rules, deadline calendars, evidence templates, counterparty-response history and realized-recovery data.

**Merge rationale:** `W2-IHK-03` and `W2-IHK-04` share a recovery-administration family but remain preserved siblings because the authority, accounting records, mandate, acquisition route and learned eligibility history differ. The other members likewise preserve distinct contractual counterparties, triggers and acquisition channels.

**Unresolved sibling tension:** Certificate monetization and warranty administration can involve proceeds-sharing rather than a conventional recovery fee; cloud and parcel credits may never create cash; passenger claims introduce consumer-law constraints.

## W2-DIR-006 — Long-lived product evidence custody

**Thesis:** Create and preserve durable product-level records, controlled updates and continuity exports for responsible economic operators.

**Members:** `W2-RSA-04`, `W2-RSA-06`, `W2-RSA-10`
