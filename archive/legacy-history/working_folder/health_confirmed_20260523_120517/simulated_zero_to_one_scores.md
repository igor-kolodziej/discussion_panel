# Simulated Zero To One Gate

## Scoring Frame

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist, max 100,000 PLN, part-time start, no privileged introductions, low camera/personal-brand fit, can use AI/data leverage. Scores weight profitability, willingness to pay, CAC, gross margin, payback, retention, repeatability, cash conversion, and concrete control-point proof more heavily than originality.

## Senior Discharge Bridge Review

Idea reviewed: 14-Day Senior Discharge Bridge for adult children of Polish elderly patients discharged after fall, surgery, stroke, infection, or sudden mobility decline.

Score: **86/100**.

Control point tier: **Tier 2 - reserved operating capacity plus prepaid demand**.

Strongest copy risk: home-care agencies, rehab clinics, and equipment-rental companies can bundle the same discharge-start package once demand is visible.

Decision: **narrow, not confirmed**.

Main caveats:

- Strong acute pain and cash conversion, but the business initially looks like an operational concierge service.
- Contractor coordination, quality control, and family trust consume founder time and reduce data/software leverage.
- The control point is not yet structural; small retainers for caregiver/nurse/physio/equipment slots are useful but not hard for others to replicate.
- The first wedge can be profitable, but the path to domination depends on controlling scarce discharge capacity and institutional referrals, neither proven for an unknown part-time founder.

## Selected Idea Exact Scored Wording

**CycleGuard Genetics is a clinic-routed IVF carrier-screening consent and sample-completion rail for Polish fertility clinics participating in or adjacent to the 2024-2028 government IVF program. The hidden insight is that clinics are not mainly missing genetic-test supply; they are missing a neutral, low-friction way to explain carrier screening and PGT-related genetic choices without looking like they are upselling anxious couples or consuming doctor time. The product is a fixed-scope workflow inserted before the carrier-screening decision: couple intake, family-history/risk flags, plain-language consent/decline documentation, sample-kit/courier coordination, status chasing, and escalation to a contracted clinical genetics reviewer only when predefined red flags or positive/complex results require it. It does not diagnose, interpret treatment options independently, sell tests as an affiliate, or replace physician/geneticist advice.**

**Initial acquisition: build a list of Polish IVF-program providers from official public sources; target clinic managers with a 14-day "genetic add-on decision audit" and a paid 20-couple pilot that requires no software integration. The pilot is clinic-branded or neutral, fixed-price, and explicitly no-commission. Pricing: 350-600 PLN per couple decision packet, or 5,000-9,000 PLN for a 20-couple pilot; escalation reviewer cost 150-300 PLN only for flagged cases. Primary control point proof in 2 months: one signed clinic pilot routing all eligible self-pay carrier-screening decision moments for at least 20 couples through CycleGuard before test order/decline/escalation, one contracted clinical genetics reviewer, lab/courier sample logistics arranged through ordinary commercial channels, at least 10 completed paid couple packets, measured sample-completion rate, and measured reduction in doctor/admin explanation time.**

## Why The Simulated Gate Passed

- Profit quality is plausible: high-ticket fertility context, self-pay genetic add-ons, low variable cost for non-escalated cases, upfront clinic/patient payment, and clear doctor-time/sample-completion value.
- CAC is plausible for a narrow B2B pilot: official provider lists expose targets, and the pitch is operational rather than status-based.
- Control point is concrete: a signed clinic workflow that routes the decision moment, not a vague partnership or future data asset.
- Retention can exist if the rail becomes the clinic's default pre-test consent, sample, and escalation workflow.
- The founder can build the workflow with forms, document automation, AI-assisted summarization, status tracking, and reviewer routing before any software platform.

## Caveats Carried Into Real Validation

- Clinic sales can still be slow if managers view this as medical trust work rather than operational relief.
- Labs or larger IVF clinics can copy the workflow if CycleGuard does not own neutral consent templates, completion benchmarks, and multi-clinic decision data.
- Regulatory and ethical boundaries must be conservative: no diagnosis, no independent treatment advice, no hidden lab commissions, no automated genetic interpretation.
- The first two months must prove routed workflow access, not just interest from clinics or couples.
