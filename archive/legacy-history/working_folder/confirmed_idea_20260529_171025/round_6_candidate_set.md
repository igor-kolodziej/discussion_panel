# Round 6 Candidate Set: Rights, Acknowledged Money, And Low-Liability Control

Created: 2026-05-29 Europe/Warsaw

## Premise

Earlier rounds failed because the founder controlled only a case workflow, expert judgment, a commodity service book, or a physical lot with hidden logistics/condition risk. Round 6 tests candidates where the founder can secure a signed, titled, assigned, reserved, or payment-directed object before serious capital is spent.

Known failed or duplicate shapes are excluded: heat-pump commissioning, PV battery retrofit, FMCG deduction recovery, CBAM, machine data liberation, lighting tender evidence, supplement safety, heat-pump noise, biosignal diligence, Amazon connected-device evidence rescue, DORA evidence packs, municipal animal-care routing, heritage doors, 3PL parcel claims, BioWear listing-ticket buyout, collector VAT, DPR/EDPR lockbox, FBA stranded inventory, hotel FF&E, OrphanMold tooling, CMM continuity, stairlift kits, KRS/e-Doreczenia address books, and cyber/accessibility/evidence sprints.

## Candidate A: ReturnPack Industrial Deposit Rights Bank

`ReturnPack` buys or options neglected industrial returnable-packaging deposits and physical return rights from SME manufacturers, warehouses, food/chemical producers, and distributors that have old DPPL/KLT/IBC/container/pallet deposit ledgers but no one owns the cleanup.

Control point: signed assignment of deposit receivable or recovery mandate for named supplier/account/packaging IDs, invoice/WZ/ledger proof, photos/serials where available, pickup protocol, supplier notice/payment direction, and fallback title to usable containers where documents fail.

Cold start: call accounting offices and warehouse managers in Warsaw/Lodz/Radom/Poznan industrial zones with one ask: "Export AP/AR lines containing kaucja, opakowania zwrotne, DPPL, KLT, IBC, palety; I buy documented stale deposit claims and remove the containers." Pay only after ledger proof and supplier return terms are reviewed.

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 5 signed assignments/mandates, 100,000 PLN face value, 40,000 PLN collected/refunded/resold, at least 2 suppliers acknowledging payment direction or return acceptance, and a repeatable supplier-term/outcome database.

Economics: buy clean deposits at 25-45% of face or success fee 20-35%; fallback resale/scrap of titled packaging. Target 45%+ gross margin after transport, cleaning, rejection, and legal/admin checks.

Main risk: pallet/IBC traders or accounting firms copy the paperwork path; many deposits are expired, non-assignable, undocumented, or cheaper for the owner to ignore.

## Candidate B: ReclaimGas Emergency Refrigerant Reservation Bank

`ReclaimGas` secures legal recovered/reclaimed high-GWP refrigerant supply and emergency reservation rights for refrigeration contractors serving cold rooms, small supermarkets, food producers, and hospitality operators that cannot wait during a line-down refrigerant shortage.

Control point: signed first-refusal pickup from certified F-gas contractors for recovered R404A/R507/R410A-class gas, serialized cylinders, weigh tickets, chain-of-custody, reclaimer/refiller agreement, testing/reclamation certificate, and prepaid emergency reservation from contractors or operators.

Cold start: call 50 certified refrigeration contractors with "I pay/revenue-share for recovered gas you do not want to monetize and handle cylinder logistics through a compliant reclaimer." Then pre-sell a reserve to 10 contractors who need emergency legal supply for existing systems.

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 300-800 kg documented recovered/reclaimed gas routed through certified partners, 5 contractor pickup agreements, 5 paid reservation customers, first paid emergency release, and no founder handling outside legal cylinder/logistics scope.

Economics: spread on reclaimed gas plus reservation fees; target 50-150 PLN/kg gross spread only where legal supply scarcity is real. Capital goes to cylinders/deposits, scale/logistics, compliance review, and reclaimer capacity.

Main risk: hazardous-goods/F-gas compliance, incumbent wholesalers/reclaimers, unclear scarcity, and legal handling complexity.

## Candidate C: OEM Warranty Approved Credit Lockbox

`WarrantyCredit` buys or collects already-approved OEM warranty credits, rebates, or claim payments owed to authorized service companies after the OEM portal has approved the claim but cash/credit remains unapplied, delayed, or trapped as account credit.

Control point: warranty portal approval or credit note, dealer/service authorization, invoice/repair order, claim ID, OEM/AP acknowledgement where possible, signed assignment or collection mandate, and payment direction or offset execution plan.

Cold start: target authorized service firms in commercial kitchen, refrigeration, cleaning machines, forklifts, HVAC, and industrial equipment. Offer: "Send only approved-but-unpaid warranty credits older than 30 days; I advance a small fraction or collect for success fee."

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 10 approved credits from 3 service firms, 100,000 PLN face value, 50,000 PLN collected or offset, one OEM/AP written status acknowledgment, and a repeatable map of portal claim states, payout timing, and credit usage.

Economics: 10-25% success fee or purchase at 60-75% of expected payout where assignable. Target payback from first 2-3 credits; reject disputed, under-review, safety/legal, or customer-complaint claims.

Main risk: OEMs may block assignment/payment direction; credits may be offset-only, small, already reconciled, or tied to dealer account terms.

## Candidate D: SupplierPortal Payment-Blocker Release Lane

`PortalPayRelease` handles named payment blocks in enterprise supplier portals only after an SME supplier has a live portal status showing blocked invoice, missing PO/ASN/GRN match, tax data issue, bank validation issue, or stuck dispute code.

Control point: supplier mandate, delegated portal access or screen-share workflow, live blocked invoice list, PO/ASN/GRN/invoice evidence, AP ticket thread, approved bank/tax master data, and success fee on released cash.

Cold start: target Polish suppliers to large retailers, logistics groups, manufacturers, and enterprise buyers using Ariba/Coupa/SupplyOn/SAP-like portals. Offer: "I only take live blocked invoices above 30,000 PLN with a portal status and AP ticket; fixed triage fee plus success fee on release."

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 5 paid suppliers, 20 blocked invoices, 500,000 PLN released or formally reclassified, 3 repeat portal/buyer workflows, and a private blockage-code/outcome library.

Economics: 2,000-6,000 PLN triage plus 2-6% of released cash above a threshold; gross margin depends on fast portal repeatability and strict rejection of legal disputes.

Main risk: becomes AP admin consulting; accountants/AR teams/BPOs can copy; enterprise AP controls payment timing.

## Candidate E: PublicBuyer Late-Payment Side-Claim Assignment

`PublicPaySidecar` buys or collects matured statutory late-payment interest and compensation side-claims from SMEs after public-sector invoices have already been accepted and paid late, focusing on ex-customers or low-relationship-risk public debtors.

Control point: paid invoice, contract/PO, acceptance/protocol if available, due date, actual payment date, statutory compensation/interest calculation, signed assignment/collection mandate, and debtor notice.

Cold start: scrape public procurement award lists and contact contractors through accountants: "Your principal invoice was eventually paid; I buy or recover only the late-payment side claim you are not pursuing."

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 200 assigned side-claims, 150,000 PLN face value, 50,000 PLN collected, at least 20 public buyers paying without litigation, and a debtor/tender-body payment behavior database.

Economics: average claims must be large enough for batch processing; target acquisition at 20-35% of face or success fee 30-40% after automated calculation and notices.

Main risk: legal/debt-collection boundary, public buyers resist small claims, claim values too small, debt collectors/law firms copy.

## Candidate F: TrialSite Payment Query Mandate

`TrialSitePay` handles only finance/admin payment queries for independent clinical-research sites when sponsor/CRO payments are delayed by screen-fail rules, visit-count mismatch, startup fees, holdbacks, pass-throughs, invoice evidence, or portal status. It excludes patient data and clinical interpretation.

Control point: site mandate, CTA/budget/payment schedule, redacted visit/event counts or invoiceable event export, sponsor/CRO payment portal status, query thread, AP contact, and released cash evidence.

Cold start: target independent Polish/CEE research sites and SMOs with "send one payment query older than 45 days; if no billable event path exists, no success fee."

Proof in 12 months under 100,000 PLN: 5 sites, 20 live payment queries, 250,000 PLN released or formally denied with reasons, one repeat site retainer, and a private sponsor/CRO payment query taxonomy.

Economics: 3,000-8,000 PLN fixed fee plus 3-8% of released cash for cases above 25,000 PLN. Reject patient-data-heavy, disputed, protocol-interpretation, or legal cases.

Main risk: clinical-trial payment admin already failed adjacent validation as too trust-heavy and rare; included only because the remaining scout suggested payment-query specificity.
