- **PalletMark**: buyer pain can be real, but pallet suppliers and freight forwarders already own most release paths; likely turns into ordinary packaging coordination.
- **UFI/CLP** and **Retailer Barcode Fix Pack**: useful but too close to prior evidence-desk patterns and legal/compliance scope creep.
- **Food QA Lab Recovery**: too close to recovery-service ideas that real Zero To One repeatedly scored below threshold.

- **Sewing Pattern / Allergen Matrix**: control point is real but market size and willingness to pay look weak without privileged industry access.
