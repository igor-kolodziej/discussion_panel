## Pivot From Round 3

Rounds 1-3 failed because they controlled service books, operational workflows, physical resources, or inventory without enough current cash, repeat urgency, or compounding data. Round 4 returns to the structure that has historically survived real validation: a live commercial blocker, current documents, routed access, prepaid case fees, and a case-memory control point.

## Lead Candidate: BindProof Cyber Insurance Evidence Bind Lane

### Scored Wording For Simulated Review

**BindProof Cyber Insurance Evidence Bind Lane** is a Poland/CEE broker-routed evidence-response product for SMEs whose cyber-insurance quote, renewal, binder, customer-required insurance certificate, or premium/exclusion review is blocked by live underwriter evidence requests.

The first customer is not a random SME. The first channel is a commercial insurance broker, cyber-insurance broker, MGA, or broker-adjacent risk consultant who already receives client panic around MFA, EDR/MDR, backup, restore-test, admin-access, patching, remote-access, incident-response, phishing-training, and compensating-control questions.

The startup does not sell insurance, advise on coverage, certify security controls, perform pentesting, implement remediation, act as broker of record, interpret policy wording, or promise policy approval. Licensed brokers remain broker of record. MSPs/security vendors remain provider of record for technical attestations and fixes. Underwriters decide whether evidence is sufficient.

The controlled asset is a signed broker routing agreement over named live blocked cases plus insured authorization, the actual underwriter questionnaire/request, prepaid case fee, broker-approved response channel, scoped reviewer bench, and anonymized case-memory rights. The product turns existing client/MSP artifacts into a broker-ready underwriter evidence pack, separates answerable controls from real gaps, and routes remediation back to the MSP.

Cold start: build a list of 80 Polish/CEE brokers and cyber brokers; send a sample redacted pack and pitch only live blocked cases. Sign 2 broker routing agreements, receive 6 named live cases, close 4 prepaid cases at 5,000-15,000 PLN, collect at least 30,000 PLN before delivery, and obtain at least 2 broker/underwriter acknowledgements that the pack moved underwriting forward within 60 days.

Economics: 5,000-7,500 PLN for a single-control/narrow evidence rescue; 8,000-15,000 PLN for full renewal/binder evidence pack; 2,500-5,000 PLN annual refresh. Broker keeps 20-30%. Gross margin is plausible if the average full case stays below 10-14 founder hours plus scoped cyber-reviewer cost.

Compounding control point: underwriter question wording, evidence object, source system, gap type, MSP action needed, follow-up request, and final disposition by broker/carrier/MGA. This is not a dashboard or generic cyber-readiness report; it only works on prepaid live bind/renewal blockers.

### Duplicate-Risk Check

This is intentionally checked against the confirmed DORA idea.

- DORA Bank Renewal Evidence Pack serves software vendors whose regulated-finance customers ask for DORA/ICT supplier procurement evidence.
- BindProof serves insured SMEs and commercial insurance brokers whose cyber-insurance underwriting or binder is blocked by carrier/MGA evidence requests.
- The buyer, channel, timing, economic trigger, evidence taxonomy, and distribution path are different.

The idea should be rejected if it collapses into generic cyber consulting, MSP overflow, readiness audits, or security-questionnaire services without broker-owned live case routing and prepaid current cases.
