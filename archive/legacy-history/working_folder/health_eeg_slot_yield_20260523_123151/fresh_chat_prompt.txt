Idea: Recovered Sleep-EEG Slot Guarantee for Polish private neurodiagnostic labs.

This is not reminders, EEG interpretation, or diagnostic software. It sells recovered slot yield. Target private EEG labs and neurodiagnostic clinics that perform child sleep EEG, home sleep EEG, sleep-deprived EEG, or long EEG and experience late cancellations, failed child sleep attempts, parent-prep mistakes, and parent conflict. Polish private EEG price lists show meaningful slot value: child sleep EEG can be priced around 800 PLN until 23:00; mobile/home sleep EEG can be around 900-1,100 PLN; long/video EEG can be several thousand PLN. Failed sleep attempts and late cancellations therefore cost real money and schedule quality.

Hidden insight: labs do not mainly need AI interpretation. They need better yield from scarce sleep/sleep-deprived slots. Parents often mis-handle sleep deprivation, naps, meals, screens, hair preparation, transport, and anxiety, and labs lose long evening/home slots or repeat work.

Product wedge: a lab-routed slot-yield workflow. In the pilot, the lab routes all sleep/sleep-deprived EEG bookings and late cancellations through a founder-operated preparation + rescue + standby workflow. The workflow includes lab-approved age-specific prep instructions, 72h/48h/12h readiness checks, risk scoring, rescue calls for high-risk parents, cancellation triage, and a founder-owned prepared-family standby list gathered from lab referrals and high-intent search pages such as "fast child sleep EEG slot" or "earlier child EEG appointment." It does not diagnose, interpret EEG, recommend treatment, change medical indications, or replace technician/neurologist instructions.

Initial acquisition: build a list of 40-80 private EEG labs from public search and clinic directories. Cold email/call owners with a "30-day sleep-EEG slot-yield sprint": route 30-50 sleep/sleep-deprived bookings through the founder workflow; no software integration; pricing tied to recovered revenue. The founder's EEG/neuroscience background gives enough domain credibility without celebrity or high-status introductions.

Primary control point and 2-month proof: one lab signs a semi-exclusive slot-yield pilot for all sleep/sleep-deprived bookings during the sprint. Resource/control point: mandatory routed parent booking flow plus a founder-owned 40-family prepared standby list. Proof before serious capital: 30-50 routed bookings, at least 10 paid routed cases, parent readiness completion above 75%, at least 5 otherwise-empty/failed slots saved or refilled, measured no-show/late-cancel/failed-prep rate versus lab baseline, and lab renewal or written refusal reason. Kill if the lab only wants generic reminder templates or will not route bookings.

Pricing and economics: 2,000 PLN fixed pilot plus 120-200 PLN per saved/recovered/high-risk completed slot, or 10-15% of recovered slot value. After pilot: 2,000-6,000 PLN/month/lab depending on volume plus success fees. Delivery cost is mostly templated messaging, one founder review queue, and exception calls. Expected gross margin 70-85% after the initial playbook stabilizes. A single saved/refilled 800-1,100 PLN sleep EEG slot can justify several routed cases; long/home/video EEG slots improve economics further.
