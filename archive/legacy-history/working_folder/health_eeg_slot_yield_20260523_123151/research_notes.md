# Research Notes

## Market / Operational Anchors

- EEG-GRAF lists child sleep EEG at 800 PLN until 23:00, an extra 400 PLN per later hour, and a 400 PLN charge for unsuccessful exams caused by on-site resignation or failure to bring the child to sleep. It also lists mobile sleep EEG at 1,100 PLN until 23:00 plus later-hour charges. Source: https://eeg.med.pl/prices-of-eeg/
- Neurodiagnostyka lists in-clinic child sleep EEG at 350-380 PLN, home child sleep EEG from 900-1,000 PLN, 12-hour sleep EEG at 2,500 PLN, 12-hour video EEG from 2,800 PLN, and 24-hour video EEG from 6,000 PLN. Source: https://neurodiagnostyka.com/en/price-list/
- eegdomowe.pl / Klinika Neuron presents Polish home EEG, sleep/deprivation EEG, and video EEG services, with sleep/deprivation EEG for children from 900-1,000 PLN, 12-hour sleep EEG from 2,500 PLN, and 24-hour video EEG at 6,000 PLN. Source: https://eegdomowe.pl/
- A pediatric sleep-deprived EEG feasibility study of 261 children reported 14% sleeping less than 15 minutes and 7% not sleeping during recording. Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1090379815002238
- ACNS pediatric EEG guidance says sleep recordings should be obtained whenever possible, and that sleep deprivation or melatonin may help. Source: https://www.acns.org/UserFiles/file/EEGGuideline5Ped_finaltrackchangesreformat_v1.pdf

## Compliance / Boundary Anchors

- EU GDPR treats health data as a special category and allows processing only in specific cases such as explicit consent. Source: https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/dealing-with-customers/data-protection/data-protection-gdpr/index_en.htm
- European Commission consent guidance requires consent to be freely given, informed, specific, explicit, clear, and withdrawable. Source: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/rules-business-and-organisations/legal-grounds-processing-data/grounds-processing/when-consent-valid_en
- To reduce MDR/medical-device exposure, the product must stay outside diagnosis, interpretation, treatment recommendation, triage of medical indication, or patient-specific clinical advice. It should route bookings, preparedness, parent communication, cancellation rescue, and standby demand only.
