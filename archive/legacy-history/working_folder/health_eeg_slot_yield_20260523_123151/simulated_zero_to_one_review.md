# Simulated Zero To One Review

## Exact Pivot Reviewed

Recovered Sleep-EEG Slot Guarantee for Polish private neurodiagnostic labs. This is not reminders, EEG interpretation, or diagnostic software. It sells recovered slot yield. Target private labs that charge meaningful prices for child sleep EEG, home sleep EEG, sleep-deprived EEG, or long EEG and have late cancellations, failed child sleep attempts, and parent conflict. The founder signs a pilot where the lab routes all sleep/sleep-deprived EEG bookings and cancellations through a preparation + rescue + standby workflow. The founder also owns a direct prepared-family waitlist from search landing pages such as "fast child sleep EEG slot" and parent communities.

Pricing: 2,000 PLN fixed pilot plus 120-200 PLN per saved/recovered/high-risk completed slot or 10-15% of recovered slot value.

Control point proof in 2 months: one lab signs semi-exclusive slot-yield pilot for all sleep/sleep-deprived bookings for 30-50 exams; founder builds 40-family standby list; pilot recovers at least 5 otherwise-empty/failed slots and produces before/after metrics.

Expansion: cross-lab standby list and failure-risk benchmark for sleep EEG, home EEG, long EEG, and other neurodiagnostic slots.

## Score

92/100.

## Control Point Tier

Tier 2 now: routed operational workflow access plus founder-owned standby demand.

Can become Tier 1 within the beachhead if the first pilot makes routing mandatory, the standby list is owned outside the lab, and cross-lab failure-risk benchmarks become the renewal reason.

## Strongest Copy Risk

Labs can copy reminder scripts or basic parent calls internally. EEG vendors or clinic scheduling systems can eventually add prep automation. The hard-to-copy part must be the prepared-family replacement demand, cross-lab slot-yield data, failure-risk taxonomy, and recovered-slot proof.

## Objections

1. The initial beachhead is narrow; it must expand from child sleep EEG to home EEG, long EEG, and other high-failure neurodiagnostic capacity.
2. The idea fails if labs route only optional cases; mandatory workflow access is the control point.
3. Parent demand acquisition must be proven independently, otherwise the founder is dependent on one lab's flow.
4. Pricing must be tied to recovered revenue, not education or reminders.
5. Retention may weaken after the lab learns the scripts unless the founder keeps controlling standby fills and benchmark reporting.

## Profitability Veto

No veto. Gross margins of 70-85% are plausible after templates stabilize because most delivery is messaging, readiness scoring, exception calls, and scheduling. Cash conversion can be fast with a fixed pilot fee and success fees. Payback is clear if one recovered 800-1,100 PLN sleep EEG slot covers several routed cases.

## Verdict

Passes the simulated `>90` gate. The idea is strong enough for real Zero to One validation only if described as a routed booking-flow and standby-demand control point, not a reminder service.
