# Real Zero To One Working-Chat Validation 5

## Idea

TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot for per-event Polish municipal animal-care contractors.

## Score

84/100.

## Result

Passes the revised user threshold of 82.

## Control-Point Tier

Tier 2.5 / 5.

The chatbot judged that the revised control point is stronger than the previous live-event version because it combines a paid, signed all-event routing mandate for specific gminas with early gmina-format acknowledgement. It still capped the idea below 85 because the control point is contractual and behavioral, not statutory, exclusive, a payment rail, or an official registry.

## Strongest Copy Risk

Good-enough internal workflow cloning. A contractor could imitate much of the MVP with Google Forms, WhatsApp, Drive folders, timestamps, photo naming conventions, Excel, and a PDF template. A shelter-management vendor, vet-software vendor, accounting office, or gmina-issued template could also copy the surface.

The defensible asset must become accepted gmina packet rules, event histories, missing-field logic, staff habit, contractor renewal/audit evidence, and proof that packets reduce questions or speed acceptance.

## Main Caveats And Objections

- The qualified market may be smaller than it looks because excluding lump-sum readiness contracts may remove many easy targets.
- Gmina acknowledgement may be harder than expected; wording should ask whether the packet scope is sufficient for invoice verification or whether fields should be changed, not request formal invoice pre-approval.
- All-event routing may break where the workflow spans cooperating vets, shelters, drivers, or emergency responders outside direct contractor control.
- Payment acceleration may be hard to prove within 60 days, so early proof should emphasize invoice-prep time, missing fields, fewer follow-up questions, and renewal/audit defensibility.
- KROPiK is both tailwind and threat; durable positioning must be invoice-event evidence and municipal packet workflow, not animal ID alone.
- The prepaid deposit is realistic only for contractors already feeling pain.
- RODO/GDPR controls are required because photos, location, staff names, vet documents, and citizen reports may include personal data.

## Exact 30-Day Kill Gates

- By day 7, produce at least 30 qualified contractors from public sources with active animal-care work for at least 2 gminas, per-event or animal-level billing exposure, recurring documentation, and identifiable owner/manager contact.
- By day 14, complete at least 8 serious conversations and have at least 3 contractors confirm invoice-prep time, gmina follow-up questions, scattered evidence, audit/renewal pressure, or animal-level evidence affecting acceptance/payment.
- By day 14, secure one signed paid pilot with 3,000-5,000 PLN prepaid.
- By day 14, secure written commitment that all eligible new billable events for at least 2 named gminas go through TraceFaktura first.
- By day 14-21, receive written acknowledgement, correction, or usable feedback from at least one gmina accountant, contract owner, or relevant official on the proposed packet format.
- By day 30, capture at least 20 live events, not historical uploads.
- By day 30, at least 3 non-founder users capture events with median normal-event capture time under 90 seconds.
- By day 30, at least 5 invoice lines are generated from TraceFaktura event objects.
- By day 30, contractor confirms one measurable economic signal in writing: invoice-prep time down around 50%, materially fewer missing fields, fewer internal follow-ups, clearer renewal/audit evidence, or fewer gmina clarification questions.

## Final Judgment From Chatbot

Pursue only under strict gates. The revised idea clears 82 because it creates a concrete POC control point: prepaid contractor mandate, all-event routing, early gmina-format feedback, and live ledger creation before invoices exist. It remains below 85 until qualified per-event contractor density and real enforcement are proven.
