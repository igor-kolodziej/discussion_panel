Revised scored idea wording:

TraceFaktura All-Event Routing Pilot for per-event Polish municipal animal-care contractors.

Narrow beachhead:
Only private animal-care contractors with existing active contracts for at least 2-3 gminas where the economics are case/event/line-item driven: stray-animal pickup, transport, shelter intake/day, veterinary treatment, sterilization, quarantine, adoption/return/death documentation, or similar billable animal-level events. The first wedge explicitly excludes mostly lump-sum readiness contracts because invoice-line evidence is weaker there.

Customer pain:
These contractors do not primarily need "shelter software." They need a way to make each future invoice line defensible at the operational moment, before staff memory, WhatsApp photos, paper protocols, vet notes, chip numbers, and gmina attachment expectations fragment. The pain is sharp only when prior gmina questions, delayed acceptance, renewal/audit anxiety, or month-end invoice-prep labor exist.

Product wedge:
TraceFaktura is a mobile-first event-stamping rail plus accepted invoice-attachment standard. Contractor field staff, dispatchers, shelter workers, or cooperating vets capture every eligible billable animal event through a lightweight QR/mobile/WhatsApp-entry flow: animal/provisional ID, service type, time/date, location, photo/video, chip/tag when available, counterparty, handoff/protocol fields, vet/shelter document, and responsible person. TraceFaktura turns those events into invoice-line evidence objects, flags missing fields before month-end, and generates gmina-specific packet attachments. AI handles OCR, extraction, duplicate detection, missing-field checks, photo/document parsing, and Polish packet wording; the controlled asset is the live event ledger plus outcome memory about what each gmina accepts.

Primary control point:
The first resource is not "better reports"; it is a signed, paid, all-event routing mandate inside one contractor's workflow for specific gminas. The pilot contract requires all new eligible billable events for at least 2 gminas during the pilot to be captured through TraceFaktura first. The founder therefore uniquely creates the source ledger before invoice creation. Selective capture is not allowed; if the contractor will not mandate usage, the POC is rejected.

Second control-point proof:
Before building beyond the no-code/mobile-form MVP, the contractor must route a one-page "invoice attachment standard" to an existing gmina accountant/contract owner as an operational format acknowledgement under the current contract. This is not a new public procurement or partnership. It asks: "For upcoming invoices, we plan to attach this animal-event packet format. Is this acceptable or what fields should be changed?" The POC proceeds only if at least one gmina gives written acceptance, correction, or usable feedback within the first 14 days.

Cold start:
The founder builds a target list from public BIP notices, Platforma Zakupowa tenders, gmina animal-care program documents, contract award notices, price schedules, and contractor/shelter/vet pages. Filter for per-event price schedules and contractors serving multiple gminas. Outbound offer:
