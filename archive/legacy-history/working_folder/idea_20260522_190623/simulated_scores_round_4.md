## Passing Candidate: Damaged Lithium Battery Quarantine Evidence Rail

Strongest insight: the valuable record is created before damaged lithium batteries are mixed, moved, burned, quietly dumped, or transferred to a licensed collector. Serial, source, condition, weight, tamper seal, packaging state, and shop handoff cannot be reconstructed later.

Primary control point:

- signed collection-point mandates;
- controlled quarantine/evidence workflow;
- fire-safe quarantine containers;
- tamper seals;
- serial/photo/weigh-in record;
- collector handoff evidence;
- BDO/KPO support pack;
- monthly importer/PRO evidence report.

Control-point caveat: the founder must not become an unauthorized waste collector, transporter, or handler. The startup should provide quarantine/evidence infrastructure and assist the waste-holder / licensed collector process, while licensed parties retain legal waste roles.

Strongest objections:

- Legal boundary risk around waste collection, transport, ADR, BDO/KPO, and liability.
- Licensed collector dependency.
- Shops may not pay enough; importer, PRO, rental chain, or insurer-adjacent buyer is stronger.
- Fire-safe logistics must be real; a box plus QR form is weak.
- Incident liability must be explicit if a battery catches fire in a startup-provided container.

Strongest copy risk: recycler, producer-responsibility operator, or battery importer places its own branded quarantine boxes at shops. Defense is signing collection-point mandates first, owning the first-mile condition dataset, and becoming the neutral evidence layer before recyclers see the battery.

## Passing Candidate: MV Cable Fault Repair Slot Bank

Strongest insight: private MV network emergency response is often blocked by specific kit/cable compatibility and certified response capacity, not generic electrician availability.

Primary control point:

- stocked 12/20 kV kits for a narrow installed base;
- pre-surveyed customer cable specs and one-line diagrams;
- paid retainers with named kit reservation;
- reserved certified jointer/fault-location provider hours.

Strongest objections:

- Kit universe can fragment rapidly.
- The hardest asset may be certified jointer/fault-location capacity, not the kit.
- Trust barrier is high for an unknown founder.
- Liability is serious; licensed contractors must perform diagnosis/repair.
- Founder fit is weaker for emergency operations without technician-led triage.

Strongest copy risk: large electrical contractors, DSO service arms, or MV maintenance firms copy the bundle. Defense is customer-specific pre-survey files, prepaid reserved kits, and priority subcontractor capacity for neglected private 15/20 kV networks before failure.
