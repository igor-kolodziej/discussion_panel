# Real Validation Failed: MV Cable Fault Repair Slot Bank

## Gate

Real Zero To One working-chat validation.

## Idea Sent

MV Cable Fault Repair Slot Bank: a preparedness retainer for neglected private 15/20 kV owners. The proposed control point was stocked 12/20 kV joint, termination, and transition kits for a narrow installed base; paid customer retainers with named kit reservation; pre-surveyed cable specs and one-line diagrams; priority agreements with certified jointers and fault-location providers; and an emergency file that lets licensed contractors act faster. The startup would not perform energized electrical work.

## Score

`68/100`

## Verdict

Failed the real `>=85` gate. Do not create a confirmed idea file and do not proceed to fresh-chat validation for this candidate.

## Key Chatbot Diagnosis

The idea targets a real high-pain operational niche, and the reserved-kit plus pre-surveyed emergency-file control point is stronger than generic electrician coordination. However, the strongest scarce asset is probably trusted MV repair capacity rather than kit inventory. If contractor priority is soft, the business becomes emergency electrical-service brokerage.

The chatbot said the business could be worth testing only as a narrow regional preparedness-retainer for neglected private MV owners with high downtime cost, not as a broad electrical repair or slot-bank platform.

## Strongest Objections

- The real control point is certified jointer, fault-location, isolation, excavation, testing, re-energization, and trust, not merely owning 30-60k PLN of kits.
- Kit compatibility fragments quickly by voltage, cable type, cross-section, insulation, age, termination, transition need, route condition, and switchgear interface.
- Customers may prefer direct contractor retainers and ask why they should pay an unknown data-scientist intermediary.
- The trust barrier is severe because MV work is high-consequence and requires qualified electrical personnel.
- Fault location is already a specialist market, so the startup can only integrate it into response readiness; it cannot claim fault location as the innovation.
- Preparedness is a rare-event sale: many sites may agree intellectually but refuse to pay before failure.

## Useful Feedback To Preserve

The stronger narrowed version would be:

**MV Emergency Readiness Retainer for cold stores, waterworks, and single-feed private MV sites in Wielkopolskie/Lodzkie.**

The chatbot said a better POC could raise the score only to roughly `76-80` if it proves:

- 3 paid retainers, not free surveys;
- each retainer names a kit family or reserved emergency material;
- one MV engineer signs the survey template;
- two contractors sign actual priority terms, not vague cooperation;
- one fault-location provider agrees to response terms;
- one mock dispatch proves who is called, what kit is released, and how the contractor acts;
- contracts clearly state the startup does not perform energized work or regulated electrical repair.

Weak proof with 10 surveys and contractor interest but no paid retainers or only soft cooperation keeps the score around `64-70`. If customers will not pay before failure or contractors refuse priority terms, the score drops below `55`.

## Pivot Instruction

Pivot away from contractor-capacity emergency retainers unless the founder can hard-reserve scarce technician capacity and sell paid readiness before failure. Future candidates need a control point the founder can own, reserve, or prove directly, not a coordination wrapper around technical labor that incumbents already own.
