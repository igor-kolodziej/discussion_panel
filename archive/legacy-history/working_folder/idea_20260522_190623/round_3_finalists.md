# Round 3 Finalists

Round 2 failed because the best candidates still looked like useful services with conditional control points. Round 3 only includes ideas where the two-month POC can lock a hard bottleneck: signed mandates, reserved inventory/capacity, routed workflow, physical custody, or legally sensitive recurring evidence.

## 1. Remote-Read Heat Meter Slot Bank

Target buyer: small and mid-size Polish housing communities, cooperatives, and property managers with 40-300 flats facing the 2027 remote-read heat/hot-water meter deadline.

Hidden insight: the scarce asset is not metering software. It is building approval, resident access, device inventory, and installer calendar before the deadline.

Primary control point:

- signed building-level mandate or exclusive resolution;
- resident-access schedule and device register;
- reserved meter/cost-allocator inventory;
- reserved installer calendar;
- optional recurring readout/billing data contract after installation.

Two-month proof: 3 signed building mandates covering 300-800 flats, one supplier allocation letter, and one installer calendar reservation with customer deposits funding device purchases.

Main copy risk: Ista, Techem, Brunata, Kamstrup, utilities, and local metering firms can copy the service. Defense must be early building mandates plus resident-access calendar control, not the devices.

## 2. PFAS-Free Food-Packaging Slot Bank

Target buyer: small/mid food brands, bakery chains, frozen/ready-meal brands, fast-casual chains, and private-label food suppliers using grease-resistant paper, molded fiber, wraps, liners, trays, labels, or coated cartons.

Hidden insight: the 2026 PPWR/PFAS pressure point is not a legal-advice problem. It is a capacity and evidence problem: find compliant substitutes, get PFAS evidence, secure converter MOQs/slots, and avoid stranded packaging inventory.

Primary control point:

- reserved PFAS testing slots;
- reserved converter production/MOQ capacity for selected replacement formats;
- supplier declarations and SKU-level evidence custody;
- ideally Polish/CEE priority access or exclusivity for 3-5 high-risk substitute formats.

Two-month proof: deposits with 1-2 labs/converters, 5 standard replacement formats, 10 paid SKU-migration deposits from food SMEs, and at least one signed priority/exclusivity letter.

Main copy risk: packaging distributors, converters, and compliance firms can copy advisory/service parts. Defense must be actual reserved production/testing capacity plus SKU outcome data.

## 3. HPLC / GC Module Loaner Bank For Accredited Labs

Target buyer: small ISO 17025 testing labs, food/environmental labs, pharma QC subcontractors, university commercialization labs, and private analytical labs using aging Agilent/Waters/Shimadzu HPLC or GC systems.

Hidden insight: small labs do not merely fear repair cost. They fear losing accredited throughput and customer trust when one module fails and they cannot prove audit-ready continuity.

Primary control point:

- owned tested inventory of narrow high-failure modules;
- reserved service engineer hours;
- qualification/calibration evidence packet for each loaner;
- customer instrument registry and failure history.

Two-month proof: choose one brand/generation, buy 5-10 tested modules under 80k PLN, reserve a freelance service engineer, get 3 paid emergency memberships/deposits, and execute 1-2 real swaps with documented serial/test/return records.

Main copy risk: OEMs, used-equipment dealers, and independent service engineers. Defense must be local same/next-day inventory plus audit-ready documentation and recurring membership.

## 4. White Certificate Pre-Start Rights Router

Target buyer: installers and vendors selling energy-efficiency projects to SMEs, farms, warehouses, workshops, cold rooms, small hotels, and building owners.

Hidden insight: white-certificate value can be destroyed before anyone notices because a project starts before the application/evidence path is secured. The asset is the pre-start legal/evidence position.

Primary control point:

- installer-side routed-flow agreement for eligible quotes before work starts;
- customer mandate assigning or sharing certificate proceeds;
- no-start covenant and evidence custody;
- reserved auditor capacity;
- ledger of project types, baseline evidence, refusal reasons, and realized certificate value.

Two-month proof: 3 installer routing agreements, 5 signed pre-start customer mandates, 2 filed or ready-to-file applications, and at least one paid retainer/success-fee right.

Main copy risk: ESCOs, energy auditors, utilities, and engineering consultancies. Defense must be quote-flow routing before work starts, not white-certificate expertise.

## 5. Driver Sleep-Apnea Fitness-To-Drive Fast Lane

Target buyer: occupational-medicine clinics, fleet/logistics employers, bus/coach operators, and driver-medical exam providers.

Hidden insight: this is not wellness sleep tracking. EU driving-licence rules make suspected moderate/severe OSA a licensing/workflow bottleneck, so the scarce asset is fast defensible evidence.

Primary control point:

- leased or owned validated home sleep-apnea test devices;
- reserved sleep physician / pulmonologist report slots;
- routed clinic/fleet suspected-driver workflow;
- archived reports and compliance evidence;
- repeat compliance checks where required.

Two-month proof: 5-10 HSAT devices, one contracted sleep physician, one occupational clinic or fleet routing suspected drivers, and 10 paid tests through the channel.

Main copy risk: sleep labs, occupational-medicine networks, and telemedicine clinics. Defense must be routed clinic/fleet flow plus device/doctor capacity.

## 6. Lead Exposure Surveillance Rail

Target buyer: Polish SME employers with lead exposure risk and their BHP/occupational-medicine providers: battery service/recycling, metal foundries, renovation/paint removal, shooting ranges, stained glass, metalwork, scrap yards.

Hidden insight: lowered EU lead limits make informal monitoring weaker. The hard asset is a legally sensitive worker-health evidence chain, not another BHP checklist.

Primary control point:

- employer-signed worker monitoring roster;
- lab agreement for blood lead testing;
- occupational physician interpretation capacity;
- chain-of-custody evidence archive;
- recurring test schedule controlled by the startup.

Two-month proof: prepaid 50-100 lab tests/reviews, 2-3 employer rosters, physician/lab workflow, and a recurring surveillance calendar.

Main copy risk: occupational-medicine clinics, BHP consultants, or labs. Defense must be employer rosters plus recurring evidence workflow.

## Research Pointers

- EU Energy Efficiency Directive 2023/1791 requires non-remotely-readable heat meters and heat cost allocators already installed to be rendered remotely readable or replaced by 2027-01-01 where cost-efficient.
- PPWR Regulation (EU) 2025/40 generally applies from 2026-08-12 and restricts PFAS in food-contact packaging above thresholds.
- URE white-certificate materials confirm the energy-efficiency certificate pathway; commercial commentary highlights refusal risk if work starts before application.
- Directive 2014/85/EU refers suspected moderate/severe obstructive sleep apnea drivers for authorized medical advice before licence issue/renewal.
- Directive (EU) 2024/869 lowers occupational lead limits and tightens medical surveillance triggers.
- ISO 17025-style equipment record requirements support the HPLC/GC loaner bank as an audit-continuity problem, not only repair logistics.
