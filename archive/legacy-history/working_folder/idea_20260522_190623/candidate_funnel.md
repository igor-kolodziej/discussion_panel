- GreenTender EPREL Bid Packs: lighting tender evidence packs.
- Supplement Stack Safety Passport: supplement/lab safety reports.
- HeatQuiet: heat-pump noise risk packets.

## Divergent Candidates

1. **OZE Orphan Portfolio Rescue**
   - Buy/option service handoff rights, technical files, and customer-transition workflows from retiring, distressed, overloaded, or closing PV/heat-pump installers.
   - Control point: signed installed-base transition rights plus customer opt-in and technical file custody.
   - Initial buyer/resource owner: small installers with 100-2,000 installed systems and weak aftercare economics.
   - Monetization: paid inspections, annual service plans, inverter/heat-pump service, battery/EMS retrofit pipeline, manufacturer-warranty handling.
   - Cap risk: GDPR/consent, service-capacity burden, local operations. Strong if the first POC gets real handoff rights and paid customer conversion.

2. **Passive Fire Substitution Lock**
   - Quote/procurement interlock for passive fire products that blocks unsupported substitutions against ETA/DoP/application conditions.
   - Control point: routed quote lines and normalized approval corpus.
   - Cap risk: strong incumbents such as Hilti/Firepin/documentation tools; founder lacks fire engineering credibility.

3. **ColdRoom Reclaim Bank**
   - Cylinder routing and recovered/reclaimed high-GWP refrigerant allocation for small refrigeration service firms.
   - Control point: cylinder pool, recovered gas stream, reclaimer allocation, service-firm ledger.
   - Cap risk: regulated handling, existing Prozon/reclaimer infrastructure, dependence on licensed partners.

4. **PV Inverter Emergency Exchange Pool**
   - Tested used/reconditioned inverter exchange stock for small PV installers handling failed/orphaned systems.
   - Control point: acquired/tested inverter stock plus compatibility corpus.
   - Cap risk: existing used-equipment/repair players; inventory dead-stock risk.

5. **Industrial PLC/HMI Emergency Exchange Pool**
   - Local emergency pool of obsolete Siemens/Allen-Bradley HMIs, PLC modules, drives for Polish packaging/food plants.
   - Control point: owned emergency stock and compatibility/testing data.
   - Cap risk: strong global surplus-spares competitors; founder cannot easily beat Radwell/EU Automation without privileged supply.

6. **Lift Obsolete Parts Donor Bank**
   - Donor components from elevator modernizations routed to independent lift-maintenance firms.
   - Control point: modernization pickup rights and tested component inventory.
   - Cap risk: safety/regulatory liability and existing Polish lift spare suppliers.

7. **KSeF Rejection Atlas**
   - Rare invoice XML rejection debugging layer for small ERP vendors and accounting offices.
   - Control point: anonymized rejection fingerprints and fixes.
   - Cap risk: very crowded KSeF tool market, easy for ERP vendors to internalize.

8. **EUDR Supplier Coordinate Workflow**
   - Supplier-response and plot-coordinate workflow for Polish wood/furniture/coffee/cocoa importers.
   - Control point: supplier-response data and repeated purchase-order workflows.
   - Cap risk: generic compliance-service shape unless tied to live PO blocks and supplier data custody.

9. **Hotel Safety-Critical Spare Suite Bank**
   - Exact discontinued door hardware, access-control readers, panic bars, and room fixtures from hotel renovations.
   - Control point: matched operational spare lots.
   - Cap risk: too close to generic hospitality stock, slow inventory.

10. **Clinic Autoclave Evidence & Backup Slot Desk**
    - Sterilization log review plus backup autoclave/sterilization slots for dental/vet clinics after failed validation.
    - Control point: reserved backup sterilization slots.
    - Cap risk: regulated trust and local operational burden.

11. **Battery Fire-Evidence Quarantine Desk**
    - For e-bike/e-scooter sellers and repair shops: controlled quarantine, incident evidence, supplier claim packet, and compliant disposal routing after battery failures.
    - Control point: safe storage slots and incident evidence workflow.
    - Cap risk: hazardous goods handling, insurance, low frequency.

12. **School Canteen Allergen Substitution Gate**
    - Blocks supplier/product substitutions in school/kindergarten menus when allergen/evidence data is incomplete.
    - Control point: procurement substitution flow.
    - Cap risk: low willingness to pay; municipal sales slow.

13. **Medical Practice Vaccine-Fridge Excursion Rescue**
    - Temperature excursion evidence, viability escalation, and replacement-cost claim packets for clinics.
    - Control point: logger data and pharmacy/clinic workflow.
    - Cap risk: clinical liability and narrow buyer urgency.

14. **HVAC Legionella Documentation Gate**
    - Water-system monitoring evidence and remedial-work trigger packets for small hotels/spas/gyms.
    - Control point: recurring sampling schedule and lab relationship.
    - Cap risk: generic compliance maintenance with weak data moat.

15. **Insurer-Ready Roof Leak Evidence Desk**
    - Drone/photo/document evidence packets for PV roof leaks and storm-damage disputes.
    - Control point: routed insurer/installer claim flow.
    - Cap risk: looks like claims admin unless insurer or installer mandates are signed.

16. **Construction Product Recall Install-Base Mapper**
    - Maps recalled/problematic construction components across contractor/installer historical jobs.
    - Control point: contractor install records and product-batch mapping.
    - Cap risk: recall frequency uncertain and customer trust difficult.

6. Lift Obsolete Parts Donor Bank

## Leading Thesis To Test

**OZE Orphan Portfolio Rescue** is the best finalist because it changes the control point from analysis or repair skill to **installed-base custody**: signed handoff rights, customer transition consent, technical files, and routed aftercare demand from installers whose economics no longer support long-tail service.

The idea must be killed if it cannot secure a real installer-initiated customer handoff. It is not enough to cold-call prosumers or advertise PV service.
