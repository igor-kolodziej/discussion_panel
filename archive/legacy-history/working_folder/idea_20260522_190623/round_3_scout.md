Date: 2026-05-22

This is scouting only. No confirmed idea file should be written from this document. The objective is to find candidates with a two-month hard control point: signed exclusivity, routed transaction flow, prepaid/reserved scarce capacity, legal evidence custody, physical inventory, specialized talent reservation, or a proprietary data loop.

Current facts used:
- EU/Polish heat and hot-water metering rules create a deadline: installed heat meters and heat cost allocators without remote-read capability must be made remotely readable or replaced by 2027-01-01, unless uneconomic. Sources: EU EED text hosted by URE, Fortum explainer, WBJ.

## 1. Remote-Read Heat Meter Slot Bank

Target buyer:
Small and mid-size housing communities, cooperatives, and property managers with district heating or central heating/hot water systems, especially 40-300 flat buildings that are too small or too late for the largest metering firms to prioritize.

Hidden insight:
The 2027 remote-read deadline turns a boring metering replacement into a calendar-capacity market. The scarce asset is not software. It is board approval plus resident access plus device inventory plus an installation crew before the deadline. Once a building resolution, resident access schedule, device map, and supplier/installer slot are locked, switching becomes operationally painful.

Specific control point:
- Signed building-level mandate or exclusive resolution to execute the remote-read replacement.
- Device register and resident-access schedule held by the startup.
- Reserved inventory allocation from one or two meter/cost-allocator vendors.
- Reserved installer calendar with cancellation penalties.
- Optional recurring billing/readout data contract for the post-install period.

Why an unknown founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:
- The sale is local and operational, not brand-led. A founder can target neglected Wielkopolska buildings through zarzadcy, administrators, and local heating contractors.
- 100k PLN is enough for legal templates, demo devices, deposits on inventory, and retainer payments to one or two small installation crews. Customer deposits should finance actual device purchases.
- A convincing POC is concrete: 3 signed building mandates, 300-800 flats under exclusive schedule, one supplier allocation letter, and one crew calendar reservation.

Copy risk:
High from Ista/Techem/Brunata/Kamstrup/Fortum/local metering firms. Defense is not the meter. Defense is capturing small-building decisions early, controlling the access calendar, and owning the follow-on reading/billing data relationship.

Likely Zero-To-One score range:
88-94. It can clear 90 if the POC has signed mandates plus real inventory/crew reservations. It falls to low 80s if it is only a lead-gen broker.

## 2. White Certificate Pre-Start Rights Router

Target buyer:
Installers and vendors selling energy-efficiency projects to SMEs, farms, warehouses, workshops, cold rooms, small hotels, and public/private building owners: heat pumps, heat recovery, compressed-air upgrades, insulation, controls, lighting, boiler-room upgrades, and refrigeration modernization.

Hidden insight:
White-certificate value is often destroyed before anyone notices because the project starts before the application/evidence path is secured. The hard asset is the pre-start legal/evidence position: owner authorization, baseline data, no-start clause, assignment or success-fee right, and auditor slot before contract execution.

Specific control point:
- Installer-side routed-flow agreement: every eligible quote above a threshold is routed to the desk before work starts.
- Customer mandate assigning or sharing future certificate proceeds, with evidence custody and a no-start covenant.
- Reserved certified/auditor capacity for fast baseline packets.
- Proprietary ledger of project types, baseline evidence, refusal reasons, and realized certificate value.

Why an unknown founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:
- Parents' installer business can provide the first quote flow and credibility with adjacent installers.
- The founder does not need to become a full ESCO. The POC can use a contracted energy auditor and a standard legal mandate.
- 100k PLN covers legal review, 5-10 subsidized packet preparations, auditor retainers, and outreach. No project capex should be financed by the startup.
- POC gate: 3 installer routing agreements, 5 signed pre-start customer mandates, 2 filed applications or ready-to-file packets, and at least one paid success-fee/retainer.

Copy risk:
Medium-high from ESCOs, energy auditors, utilities, and engineering consultancies. Defense is the installer quote-flow lock and time-stamped evidence custody before work begins, not expertise in the statute.

Likely Zero-To-One score range:
86-92. It crosses 90 only if the POC secures routed installer flow and signed assignment/mandate rights before work starts. Without routed flow, it is a consulting shop.

## 3. Flammable-Refrigerant Service Capacity Bank

Target buyer:
Small grocery chains, restaurants, cold-room owners, farms with refrigeration, HVAC/refrigeration contractors, and local heat-pump distributors exposed to R290/R32/A2L/A3 service gaps.

Hidden insight:
The low-GWP refrigerant transition is not just a product transition. It is a service-capacity transition. Owners can buy equipment, but they cannot easily buy guaranteed emergency response from technicians trained and willing to handle flammable refrigerants, with the right recovery/detection gear and spare parts. Downtime creates immediate food-loss or heating-loss pain.

Specific control point:
- Reserved callout capacity from 2-4 trained refrigeration/HVAC technicians with non-circumvention terms.
- Physical pool of gas detectors, recovery tools, adapters, and critical parts for selected equipment families.
- Paid customer retainers for emergency response windows.
- Incident/service ledger that compounds into failure-pattern data by model and installer.

Why an unknown founder can secure it in 2 months under 100k PLN:
- The founder can start narrow in Wielkopolska with a few technician subcontractors and a few equipment families rather than trying to cover all HVACR.
- 100k PLN is enough for technician reservation fees, insurance/legal review, basic tools/spares, and emergency-retainer sales. It is not enough for a broad service network, so the POC must be very narrow.
- POC gate: 3 paid emergency retainers, 2 technician reservation contracts, one stocked parts/tool list, and one manufacturer/distributor referral agreement.

Copy risk:
High from existing refrigeration service firms. Defense is hard only if the startup has prepaid technician slots, parts on hand, and customer retainers. A website saying "R290 service" is worthless.

Likely Zero-To-One score range:
84-91. It can reach 90 if the POC proves prepaid emergency retainers and true reserved technician capacity. It is capped below 85 if technicians will not sign reservation/non-circumvention terms.

## Recommendation For Round 3 Panel

Send these to the simulated panel in this order:

1. Remote-Read Heat Meter Slot Bank
2. White Certificate Pre-Start Rights Router
3. Flammable-Refrigerant Service Capacity Bank

The first has the cleanest hard-control path because the POC can show signed building mandates, inventory, and installation calendar control. The second is attractive because the legal timing creates an actual right that can be captured. The third is a useful backup but needs harsher proof because incumbent service firms can copy it quickly.
