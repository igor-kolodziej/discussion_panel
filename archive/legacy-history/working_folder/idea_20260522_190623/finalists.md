- signed installed-base transition agreement;
- installer-initiated customer handoff;
- customer opt-in/consent;
- technical file custody;
- routed aftercare demand before generic service firms reach the customer.

Strict 2-month proof:

- 1-2 installer transition agreements covering 200+ historical systems;
- 50+ customer opt-ins through original-installer communication;
- 20 paid diagnostics or 10 annual care plans;
- transferred technical files for opted-in customers.
