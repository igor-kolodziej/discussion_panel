# Simulated Scores Round 7

## Candidate

Orphan Casting Pattern Vault.

## Score

`49/100`

## Verdict

Failed the simulated `>90` gate. Do not send to real Zero To One validation.

## Key Diagnosis

The hidden insight is strong: old physical casting patterns can be the scarce root key for obsolete pump, valve, and fluid-handling repairs. However, the business quality is weak unless the founder controls legally usable, demand-linked patterns rather than interesting old pattern inventory.

## Strongest Objections

- Demand matching is sparse; a large pattern vault is worthless unless named buyers need named pattern families.
- Owning a physical pattern may not equal legal permission to manufacture the corresponding part.
- Foundries, model shops, and pump-service firms can often work from drawings, samples, supplied models, or recreate tooling.
- The founder lacks technical/foundry-sector trust and may struggle to secure pattern lots plus paid demand in two months.
- Buyers may choose replacement pumps or standard service rather than one-off reproduction.

## Control-Point Tier

Tier 3 with Tier 2 upside only if the founder secures enforceable exclusive/first-refusal rights over patterns that map to recurring obsolete-equipment demand.

## Pivot Instruction

Stop treating upstream industrial artifacts as strong by default. A physical artifact clears the gate only if it is tied to known demand, legally usable, and controlled before incumbents can capture it.
