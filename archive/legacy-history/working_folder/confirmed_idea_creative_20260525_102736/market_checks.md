# Market And Competition Checks

## F1: Critical Controls Spare Custody Vault

Competitors/substitutes:

- Radwell Poland, ASM Automatyka, Omega Electronics, EFIXLAB, ATT-Automation, INEE, Siemens service/spares, GOHR-SPS, PLCStock, Allegro/eBay.

Key findings:

- Generic spare-parts trading is crowded.
- Polish and EU repair/stock players already claim fast repair, large spare bases, and Siemens S5/S7/HMI expertise.
- The stronger wedge is not "we sell used PLC parts"; it is customer-specific spare custody, tested spares, program backups, swap SOPs, and paid standby rights.

## F2: Senior Scam Handset Custody Rail

Competitors/substitutes:

- Telco anti-spoofing and anti-smishing obligations, Orange Phone/CyberTarcza, Truecaller, Hiya, tellows, iOS/Android unknown-caller blocking, police education campaigns, family call-screening workflows, CallNinja-like tools.

Key findings:

- True number custody or VoIP routing can create telecom/GDPR/liability issues.
- Poland has active anti-spoofing regulation, but family-level protection remains incomplete.
- The securable POC control point is handset policy and family escalation, not caller-ID truth.

## F3: HardLock Owned-Device Doomscroll Custody

Competitors/substitutes:

- Apple Screen Time, Google Family Link, Opal, Freedom, One Sec, AppBlock, Qustodio, Norton Family, Jamf, Intune, Scalefusion, Hexnode, ManageEngine, lockboxes, Brick/NFC devices.

Key findings:

- BYOD app blockers are weak because the user remains admin.
- Apple and Android stronger controls require supervised, fully managed, or reset devices.
- The POC must use owned/leased managed devices and external admin custody.

## F4: CPAP Mask Trial + OSCAR Rescue Bank

Competitors/substitutes:

- ResMed Poland, Philips, Fisher & Paykel, Lowenstein/prisma, CPAP.pl, RespiCare, ZAKMED, Orteo, Enel-Med, medical shops, OSCAR/SleepHQ communities.

Key findings:

- Feasible under 100,000 PLN only with new sealed masks/cushions and accessories.
- Do not redistribute used patient-contact masks.
- Avoid clinical therapy-setting advice; position outputs as education and clinician-ready evidence.

## F5: Lithium Battery Quarantine Intake Rail

Competitors/substitutes:

- Elemental / AE Elemental, Eneris B&R, MB Recycling / Eko Hybres, municipal PSZOK points, battery producers' EPR systems, bike/scooter repair shops.

Key findings:

- Not feasible as a licensed storage/recycling operation under 100,000 PLN.
- Feasible only as a compliant intake/broker/chain-of-custody layer with licensed recycler/collector handoff.
- Damaged lithium packs create ADR, fire, insurance, landlord, and waste-permit issues.

## F6: Authorized Deadstock De-Branding Vault

Competitors/substitutes:

- Stock Polska, S&A Textile, StradiModa/TopHurt, Outlet Store, StockPoint, Poliness, IFS Poland, Wiltex, Premium Outlet, Stock-Hurt, Allegro, Vinted, OLX, liquidators, recyclers.

Key findings:

- The EU unsold apparel destruction ban creates urgency, but many incumbents can capture it.
- Feasible as a service vault with written authorization, not as inventory buyout.
- Removing labels can remove legally required fiber/care/importer information, so authorization and relabeling matter.
