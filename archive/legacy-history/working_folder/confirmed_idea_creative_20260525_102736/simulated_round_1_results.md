## Repeated Fatal Objections

- The pain is real across all six, but the control points are mostly operational rather than proprietary.
- Incumbents can copy the stronger versions with one vendor contract, inventory purchase, service SOP, or internal process.
- Trust and CAC are weak for an unknown solo founder in senior, health, and industrial-trust categories.
- B2C ideas risk high support load and fragile retention.
- Operational asset ideas risk liability, labor intensity, and thin owner earnings.

## Decision

No candidate cleared the simulated `>87` gate. Do not contact real Zero To One with this round.

## Required Pivot

The next pivot must not merely add more inventory, more support, or more documentation. It needs a harder asset/control point such as:

- exclusive archive/source-code access;
- signed first-look rights from a supplier whose asset cannot be copied;
- regulatory or insurance-triggered transaction flow that can be controlled in the POC;
- customer-owned operational data or spares held in escrow with switching costs;
- prepaid routing through a channel whose economics are proven by deposits.
