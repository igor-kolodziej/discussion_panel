## Strongest Learning

The creative search improved the quality of ideas, but it also revealed a recurring pattern: many niche pains become plausible paid POCs, yet fail the gate because the control point is not strong enough to create domination.

The next search should start from assets that are already legally transferable and scarce:

- retiring owner/operator customer books where the buyer can sign an enforceable transition option;
- paid exclusive rights to an inventory stream with proven buyer demand;
- high-value receivables or claims that can be assigned and collected quickly;
- transaction flow already routed through a small operator that can be optioned or acquired;
- records, rights, or access that an incumbent cannot recreate without buying the same source.
