## First Hard Filter

Strongest control-point candidates:

- #1 / #16: physical owned/reserved exact-match repair inventory plus image/SKU/demand data. Good cold start, low trust barrier on supply, urgent paid demand if exact match exists.
- #2: physical owned/tested obsolete industrial controls inventory. High willingness to pay but has strong incumbents and requires testing credibility.
- #4: routed supply rights over used cooking oil. Real resource control possible but environmental/logistics permitting and incumbent collectors are serious barriers.
- #7: narrower version of #2 with building systems; less clear buyer urgency than line-down industrial plants.
- #9: physical old roof-tile inventory. Strong exact-match logic, but bulky inventory and narrower than surface materials.

## Current Leading Hypothesis

The best candidate is **Insurance Restoration Exact-Match Materials Desk / Discontinued Surface Match Bank**.

Secret: repair economics are often decided by a tiny missing physical match, not by repair labor. Insurers, landlords, property managers, and restoration firms may prefer a 700 PLN exact-match tile/panel/trim solution over a 12,000 PLN full-room replacement, but existing supply is scattered across tile shops, basements, leftover contractor stock, OLX/Allegro, and obsolete showroom stock.

Potential control point: owned/reserved inventory of exact-match discontinued surfaces, photographed and indexed by dimensions, pattern, color, manufacturer marks, texture, batch clues, and installation context; plus supplier first-look rights from tile shops, renovation crews, property cleanouts, and restoration contractors.

Need research: restoration/insurance repair economics, flooring/tile deadstock substitutes, Polish/EU property insurance restoration channel, competitors in discontinued tile matching, and whether first POC can prove paid exact-match searches.
