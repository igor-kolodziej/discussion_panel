# Round 2: Rights-Control Candidates

## Candidate R2-1: PIWIRail CEE Fungus-Resistant Grape Rights

Exclusive Poland/CEE propagation and distribution rights for one named protected fungus-resistant PIWI wine grape variety from a breeder/rightsholder. The business sublicenses propagation to one certified nursery and sells preorder-backed vine allocation to Polish/CEE vineyards planning climate-resilient, lower-spray plantings.

Control point: signed exclusive option/master-license for one named protected variety in Poland/CEE, plus nursery sublicense and grower deposits.

2-month proof: identify 20 candidate varieties; sign one conditional exclusive; get nursery propagation quote; collect 10 vineyard LOIs or deposits linked to hectares/vine counts.

## Candidate R2-2: BerryRight Under-Monetized Cultivar Royalty Rail

Acquire or exclusively administer Poland/CEE propagation rights and royalty streams for one named protected berry cultivar from a breeder or small rightsholder whose variety is commercially useful but under-distributed. Start with haskap, blackcurrant, raspberry, strawberry, aronia, or gooseberry.

Control point: exclusive 12-36 month commercialization/royalty-administration right for a named protected cultivar plus nursery sublicense contracts.

2-month proof: map 30 cultivars; sign one exclusive option; secure 2-3 nursery conditional sublicenses; collect grower purchase intent for 50k-200k plants.

## Candidate R2-3: OHMSeed Organic Heterogeneous Seed Population Rights

Exclusive commercialization rights to a named organic heterogeneous wheat/rye/oat/spelt population bred by an organic farmer-breeder, small breeding group, or university, using EU organic heterogeneous material notification route rather than classic variety registration.

Control point: exclusive commercialization/source-material control over one named seed population plus multiplier agreements and buyer reservations.

2-month proof: identify 5-10 populations; sign one exclusive option; confirm notification route; get multiplier quotes; collect 20 organic-farm reservations.

## Candidate R2-4: Orphan CE-IVD Genotyping Kit Rights

Exclusive Poland/CEE distribution and forward allocation rights for a low-volume CE-IVD genotyping kit whose manufacturer is rationally under-serving CEE under IVDR pressure, such as pharmacogenetics or founder-mutation assays.

Control point: exclusive territory rights plus named inventory/forward allocation for a specific CE-IVD kit that labs cannot replace without validation and supplier qualification.

2-month proof: contact 30 kit makers; sign one exclusive option; secure 3 paid lab trials/LOIs; buy limited inventory only after deposits.

## Candidate R2-5: CareBed Priority Rights

Paid, assignable priority rights or reservation credits on named rooms/beds in specific private care homes, sold to 50-60 adult children or self-planning buyers.

Control point: contractual option/priority right over named beds/room categories with refund/transfer rules.

2-month proof: contract 2-3 homes for 10-20 rights; sell 10 deposits to 50-60 buyers; prove one exercise/renewal.

## Candidate R2-6: DataAct 50-60 Biosignal Rights Pool

Delegated access and commercial licensing rights from 150-250 50-60-year-old EU wearable owners with longitudinal sleep/HRV/activity/SpO2/BP-linked data, using EU Data Act/user-access rights.

Control point: participant-signed data delegation and licensing rights plus actual successful export/access requests from device makers.

2-month proof: recruit 150 participants; execute consents; submit data access requests; pre-sell a feasibility dataset to one CRO/insurer/device maker.
