## Decision

No candidate passed the simulated `>85` gate. Round 2 improved physical/resource control but still produced low scores because most ideas were either commodity logistics, slow inventory, incumbent-copyable, or too local. Round 3 must start from a stronger workflow or transaction-control wedge where the founder can become the default operating rail for a recurring, high-stakes process, not merely owner of scarce inventory.
