# Round 7 - Asset-Control Candidate Set

## Selection Premise

The first six rounds mostly failed because the strongest-looking ideas still depended on incumbent labor trust, technician availability, broad inventory guesses, or slow institution-side decisions. This round shifts toward control points the founder can directly secure under 100,000 PLN: owned/optioned physical lots, customer sample custody, equipment test benches, and narrow emergency assets.

## Candidate A - TileMatch Claim Salvage

**Exact idea wording:** Build a Polish exact-match construction-material salvage bank for property-damage claims, starting with discontinued roof tiles, facade tiles, and premium paving slabs in Wielkopolskie/Lower Silesia. The company buys or options photographed, measured, batch-marked lots from roofers, wholesalers, demolition contractors, and renovation leftovers, then sells high-margin exact-match repair packs to loss adjusters, insurers, property managers, and contractors who otherwise face full-roof/full-facade replacement disputes. The control point is owned/optioned matched inventory plus a claim-photo matching dataset and supplier-removal flow, not a generic marketplace.

## Candidate B - BatchVault Reference Sample Custody

**Exact idea wording:** Create a third-party reference-sample custody and retrieval service for Polish private-label supplement, functional-food, and cosmetics brands. Brands ship two tamper-evident samples from every production batch into a founder-operated ambient/cold retention vault with chain-of-custody, photo/lot records, expiry logic, and fast retrieval for retailer complaints, consumer claims, audits, or manufacturer disputes. The control point is physical custody of batch evidence and longitudinal complaint/sample records, not a report or dashboard.

## Candidate C - DepositMark RVM Acceptance Bench

**Exact idea wording:** Build a deposit-return packaging acceptance preflight bench for Polish small beverage producers: test barcode placement, deposit marking, container geometry, and scan readability on owned or rented reverse-vending-machine scanner hardware and representative retail scanners before a SKU is printed at scale or listed. The wedge is urgent SKU rescue and launch preflight for craft beverage, juice, cider, dairy-drink, and private-label producers under Poland's deposit system. The control point is physical test capability, failed-label corpus, and producer launch workflow access.

## Candidate D - PatternReserve HoReCa

**Exact idea wording:** Build a contract-grade hospitality pattern reserve for Polish wedding venues, boutique hotels, and caterers using discontinued or hard-to-source tableware, glassware, cutlery, and buffet pieces. The company buys/consigns matched lots from closures, renovations, wholesalers, and liquidators; creates exact pattern files for venues; and sells paid reserve boxes plus high-margin replacement packs after breakage or expansion. The control point is owned/optioned matched pattern inventory and named-venue pattern files, not a general resale shop.

## Candidate E - ColdChain Rescue Locker

**Exact idea wording:** Sell paid emergency cold-chain rescue retainers to small Polish clinics, pharmacies, fertility clinics, and aesthetic/dermatology clinics holding high-value vaccines, biologics, injectables, or lab materials. The company owns validated portable fridges/freezers, calibrated loggers, transport boxes, and documented handoff SOPs, then provides rapid temporary custody/backup cooling when a clinic fridge alarm or power failure threatens stock loss. The control point is owned validated equipment plus customer-specific stock-risk files and incident documentation.

## Candidate F - ToolingAfterlife Spare-Part Rights

**Exact idea wording:** Acquire low-cost rights, drawings, jigs, molds, or leftover stock for discontinued industrial plastic/rubber/metal parts from retiring Polish toolmakers and small manufacturers, starting with food-processing and packaging-line format parts that keep older lines running. The company identifies recurring orphan parts, secures the production artifacts before they are scrapped, and sells emergency replacement runs or reserve packs to factories and maintenance firms. The control point is owned production artifacts and compatibility files, not generic machining brokerage.

## Non-Redundancy Check

- Not another PV/heat-pump installer product.
- Not CBAM/Data Act/EPREL/DORA/RED/municipal-animal evidence.
- Not supplement safety diligence; BatchVault is operational physical custody, not safety opinion or lab report.
- Not HeritageDoor unless a subagent decides material-matching salvage is too adjacent. TileMatch and PatternReserve must be judged specifically for buyer/control-point difference.
