# Round 10 - Option And Resource-Control Candidates

## Selection Premise

After repeated failures around services, inventory, delegated workflows, and acquired books, this round tests option-like control points: signed flexibility rights, receivable assignments, hazardous-return logistics capacity, reserved care beds, and feedstock options. The question is whether a founder can control a resource that is valuable before broad competitors notice it.

## Candidate A - FlexCold Load Option Portfolio

**Exact idea wording:** Build a portfolio of exclusive dispatch options over small Polish cold-storage and refrigeration loads that are too fragmented for large DSR aggregators to acquire directly. Start with fruit/vegetable cold rooms, meat processors, frozen-food warehouses, and refrigerated logistics sites in Wielkopolskie/Lower Silesia with 50-300 kW flexible compressor load. The founder signs no-upfront or small-option-fee contracts granting exclusive rights to measure, pre-cool, curtail, and dispatch defined loads for demand-response/flexibility monetization; installs low-cost metering/control on 5-10 sites; and resells the aggregated verified flexibility to an existing DSR/VPP aggregator or energy supplier. The control point is signed dispatch rights plus telemetry/performance history for a clustered MW-scale portfolio, not an energy dashboard or consulting.

## Candidate B - PalletBalance Receivables Market-Maker

**Exact idea wording:** Buy, option, or take collection assignment over aged EUR/EPAL pallet receivables from one Polish FMCG, beauty, or household-goods manufacturer with repeated retail/DC shipments. Instead of merely reconciling balances, the founder controls a legally assigned tranche of pallet debt: documents, WZ/PZ, CMR/POD evidence, counterparty balances, settlement authority, and right to collect cash, offsets, or physical pallet returns. Monetization is spread on recovered cash/pallet value plus optional ongoing monthly assignment of new aged balances. The control point is assigned returnable-packaging receivables and counterparty recovery data, not pallet-management software.

## Candidate C - LithiumSafe Damaged Battery Return Kits

**Exact idea wording:** Build a controlled damaged-lithium-battery return and quarantine kit service for Polish e-bike, e-scooter, power-tool, laptop, and small electronics repair shops whose couriers refuse swollen, damaged, recalled, or fire-risk batteries. The founder owns certified/ADR-compliant packaging kits, fire-safe temporary storage boxes, calibrated temperature/photo evidence workflow, and contracted licensed waste-battery recycler intake; shops pay per kit and monthly retainer for compliant removal, documentation, and emergency quarantine. The control point is owned packaging/box fleet plus signed recycler intake and shop-level waste-flow mandates, not generic waste brokerage.

## Candidate D - RespiteBed Option Desk

**Exact idea wording:** Reserve short-stay dementia and post-hospital respite-care beds in small private care homes within Warsaw/Poznan/Wielkopolskie for families facing discharge pressure and no time to shop. The founder pays small option fees or guaranteed minimum occupancy to vetted homes for specific two-week bed windows, publishes only controlled availability, and sells placement packages to families and hospital-adjacent social workers. The control point is prepaid/reserved bed inventory plus quality/vacancy history, not a directory or lead-generation marketplace.

## Candidate E - BiogasFeedstock Option Cluster

**Exact idea wording:** Originate a de-risked biogas/biomethane feedstock cluster around Ostrów Wielkopolski by signing exclusive 12-24 month options over manure, poultry litter, food-processing waste, and glycerin/fats residues from farms and processors within a tight logistics radius. The founder does lab tests, GIS routing, seasonality estimates, contamination screening, and basic contract terms, then sells the option package to a biogas developer, municipal energy company, or strategic investor. The control point is signed feedstock supply options plus tested quantity/quality data, not a consulting report.

## Candidate F - Mobile Smoke/Fire Restoration Contents Vault

**Exact idea wording:** Build a mobile emergency contents-removal and ozone/thermal-drying vault for small fire/smoke/water-damage restoration contractors and insurers after apartment or small-business incidents. The founder owns/leases a small set of sealable transport crates, ozone/air-treatment capacity, moisture/odor documentation tools, and temporary storage space, then sells fast controlled custody for valuable textiles, books, art, electronics, and business records when contractors lack clean overflow capacity. The control point is physical custody/logging/restoration capacity during claims, not a claims report or contractor marketplace.

## Non-Redundancy Notes

- FlexCold is energy flexibility asset origination, not PV/heat-pump installer tooling or grid-connection consulting.
- PalletBalance is returnable-packaging receivable assignment, not parcel claims or FMCG promotion deduction recovery.
- LithiumSafe is hazardous logistics capacity, not compliance evidence or generic recycling.
- RespiteBed is reserved care-bed inventory, not a directory.
- BiogasFeedstock is feedstock-rights origination, not renewable installer workflow.
- Contents Vault touches insurance claims but uses physical custody/restoration capacity, not paperwork-only claim recovery.
