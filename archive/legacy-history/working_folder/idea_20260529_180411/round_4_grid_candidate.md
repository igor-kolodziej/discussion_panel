# Round 4 Candidate - CablePool Option Bank

## Candidate

**CablePool Option Bank** secures exclusive owner-side mandates over underused Polish medium-voltage grid-connection capacity at existing small RES, biogas, industrial, agricultural, and commercial sites, then sells developer-funded data-room access, co-development rights, and success-fee transactions for cable-pooling, BESS, hybrid RES, or behind-the-meter expansion projects.

## Hidden Insight

In Polish RES development, the scarce asset is increasingly not land, panels, turbines, or software. It is grid access. Capable developers can finance and build projects, but they cannot easily discover and control underused owner-held connection capacity before competitors, OSD procedures, or local relationships block them.

Most observers treat connection refusals as a macro grid problem. The commercial secret is narrower: many small owners already hold some form of connection agreement, export history, transformer/site infrastructure, or industrial demand profile that could support a second asset under cable pooling or hybridization, but these rights are not packaged as financeable projects.

## Product Wedge

The founder signs an exclusive 6-12 month owner-side mandate or option over a named site before approaching developers. The founder then builds a developer-grade connection-right data room:

- connection agreement / technical conditions / OSD correspondence;
- metering/export/import history;
- transformer and MV/LV one-line notes;
- land and access notes;
- current asset status and support-regime caveats;
- cable-pooling / BESS / hybridization feasibility screen by energy lawyer and grid engineer;
- owner-approved commercial terms and exclusivity window.

The startup does not guarantee OSD approval and does not act as a licensed energy company. It controls origination, owner exclusivity, data-room packaging, developer matching, and transaction rights.

## Primary Control Point

- Signed exclusive owner mandate/option over a named underused connection asset.
- Owner authorization to assemble and share the data room with developers.
- Developer paid review fee or success-fee/co-development mandate.
- Proprietary map of underused connection capacity, refusal/approval outcomes, and owner willingness.

## Beachhead

Wielkopolskie/Lodzkie/Mazowieckie agricultural and SME energy sites:

- old biogas plants;
- small PV/wind projects with unused export headroom or weak operations;
- industrial/agro sites with MV connection and predictable load;
- farms or SMEs with connection agreements, transformer capacity, or stalled RES plans.

## 90-Day POC Under 100,000 PLN

- Build a target list of 150 sites from URE/OSD/public RES registers, satellite imagery, old project announcements, tender records, farm/biogas/PV databases, and local installer knowledge.
- Pay an energy lawyer and grid engineer for mandate templates and first 5 feasibility screens.
- Sign 3 exclusive owner mandates/options with clear data-room authority and no parallel developer marketing during the option.
- Build one complete data room for a technically plausible site.
- Get 3 developer reviews and at least one paid review fee, LOI, or success-fee term sheet.
- Kill if mandates are not legally bankable, developers will not pay/review, or OSD feasibility is too speculative.

## Why Unknown Founder Can Secure It

The founder is not asking high-status developers for trust first. The first hard work is local owner origination: find owners whose existing rights are not monetized, offer free/cheap feasibility packaging, and take success economics only if a developer engages. Parents' renewable-installation background helps with local energy-owner credibility and technical conversation; the founder's data skills help build the owner/site map cheaply.

## Why Incumbents Cannot Copy With One Contract

Developers can copy a category, but not a signed owner option over a named site. OSDs control approval, but they are not project-originators. Large developers often chase greenfield pipelines, while small owners are fragmented, relationship-heavy, and not packaged for financeable review. The compounding asset is the map of owner-held capacity plus outcomes, not a generic spreadsheet.
