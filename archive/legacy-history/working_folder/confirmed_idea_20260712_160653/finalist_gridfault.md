# Exact Candidate: GridFault Attribution

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist with AI/data skills, 100,000 PLN maximum POC capital, five hours/day, no privileged introductions or status. He can use paid electrical-engineering contractors and his family’s small renewable-energy installation company for practical field context, but not as a guaranteed customer channel.

**GridFault Attribution** obtains a 6–12-month exclusive field-of-use option and then a Poland/CEE commercial licence to Warsaw University of Technology patent PL 229627, currently listed by the university as technology #212 for commercialization. The method uses harmonic-frequency impedance/admittance spectra to classify whether a harmonic disturbance source lies inside or outside a measured electrical network.

The first buyer is not a factory and not the grid operator. It is an independent Polish power-quality measurement/engineering firm already called into industrial plants, renewable projects, data centres, or BESS/PV commissioning disputes where standard Class A recording proves distortion but does not resolve who caused it. GridFault sells a 15,000–25,000 PLN blind back-test on the firm’s historical resolved cases, followed by a paid live attribution pilot. It does not sell generic energy audits, filters, meters, dashboards, or legal opinions.

The controlled resource is the signed exclusive field option/licence, inventor handoff and benchmark protocol, followed by proprietary labelled cases linking spectra, inside/outside source, remediation, and outcome. The 12-month proof is: executed option, verified patent status and freedom to license, an external blind benchmark showing incremental decision value, three paid cases, one documented remediation decision changed, and one integration or licence with an analyzer/service firm. Spend is capped at 90,000 PLN: up to 35,000 licence/option/legal diligence, 25,000 inventor/electrical-engineer work, 15,000 rented measurement hardware/site work, and 15,000 software/insurance/sales.

Cold start uses the university’s open commercialization office, public Polish power-quality service-provider lists, OPA-style dispute measurement providers, URE industrial-customer lists, and BESS/PV commissioning firms. No serious build occurs before written option terms and two paid or deposit-backed benchmark commitments.

Initial revenue is per-case; the intended company is a licensed attribution layer for multiple measurement firms and analyzer OEMs. The compounding niche is the exclusive Polish patent position plus a cross-site labelled source/remediation corpus that broad meter vendors do not possess. Kill if exclusivity is unavailable, blind accuracy does not beat existing practice, firms will not prepay, or each case requires irreplaceable inventor consulting.
