# Simulated Round 2 Result

## SpanBank Portal-Frame Options

- Fresh reviewer: **67/100**.
- Tier: Tier 2, strong transaction-level control but weak market-level control.
- Fatal issues: uncertain legal/engineering route for reused frames, a reservation structure that asks both seller and buyer to accept hidden-condition risk, and margins vulnerable to failed surveys, coating, storage, and logistics.
- Strongest copy risk: demolition contractors control the source and hall builders control the buyer/permitting path; either can internalize the dossier after the spread is proved.
- Decision: kill the structural-building shape. Do not pivot into documentation services.

## Round 2 Lesson

Whole-system options are insufficient when every transaction carries engineering liability and multi-party installation risk. The next resource must be directly movable and independently valuable without a certification chain.
