# Exact Candidate: EarLock

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist, five hours/day, 100,000 PLN POC capital, no status-based distribution. Paid audiology/PPE contractors and compliant manufacturers are allowed.

**EarLock** sells noisy factories a 24–36-month workforce hearing-fit and rapid replacement programme. It captures employee-authorized bilateral ear scans/impressions, job/noise profile, attenuation choice, fit-verification result, product specification, and replacement authorization, then fulfils lost/damaged custom hearing protection from the archived record through interchangeable compliant manufacturers where technically possible. It is not a generic PPE distributor, hearing test, compliance report, wellness app, or reminder CRM.

The first buyer is the BHP/EHS manager at a 100–1,000-worker metal, wood, food, logistics, or manufacturing site after a noise survey, poor PPE adherence, or workforce PPE renewal. Cold start is a paid 25-worker cohort for 7,500–12,500 PLN from a list of plants in Wielkopolskie, Łódzkie, and Mazowieckie.

The control point is the employer term contract plus consented reusable fit archive and recurring replacement authorization, not the outsourced earplug. The 12-month proof is three employer contracts, 250 enrolled workers, 200 manufacturing-usable records, 50 replacements without rescanning, one renewal, and positive contribution margin under a 70,000 PLN budget.

Revenue is 250–500 PLN enrollment, paid replacement/refresh, and multi-site contract fees. The intended moat is employer tenure, vendor-neutral continuity, replacement speed, and longitudinal fit/failure data. Kill if records are not portable across suppliers, employers refuse term contracts, employee consent is weak, or PPE manufacturers can bundle equivalent continuity at materially lower CAC.
