# Exact Candidate: CasingReserve

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist, five hours/day, 100,000 PLN POC capital, no privileged fleet or retreader access. Licensed transport/waste contractors may be paid, but supply and demand must be contractually proven.

**CasingReserve** controls known-origin commercial truck-tyre casings before removal and mixing. It gives 20–150-truck regional fleets a guaranteed conditional credit and free serial/photo/history capture in return for a 12-month first-refusal and title-transfer agreement over named removed casings. Before collecting, it obtains written grade/price schedules and deposits or purchase orders from at least two UN R109-approved retreaders. Rejected tyres remain in the fleet’s licensed waste route. It is not a tyre shop, waste collector, generic broker, or retread manufacturer.

The controlled resource is the fleet-side first-refusal/title agreement plus casing serial, vehicle, axle, mileage, maintenance, removal, and condition record. The hidden insight is that provenance and pre-removal control determine retread yield, but casing value is destroyed when mixed in dealer/waste piles.

Cold start targets regional fleets at scheduled tyre-change windows using public fleet registers/directories and service calendars. The 12-month proof is five fleet agreements, 300 mapped tyres, 100 controlled casings, two retreader schedules, 50 accepted/paid transfers, rejection below 25%, and positive margin after pickup/inspection under 70,000 PLN.

Initial economics are a 50–250 PLN spread per accepted casing plus later retread procurement for the same fleet. Compounding comes from contracted supply, multi-retreader price discovery, and casing-yield/life data. Kill if fleet first refusal is unavailable, the product/waste boundary cannot be made clean, retreaders already capture nearly all value, or route density cannot cover logistics.
