# Divergent Candidate Directions

All economics are hypotheses until buyer quotes, paid deposits, or completed transactions prove them.

## 1. GridFault Attribution

- **Insider outcome:** an industrial plant, BESS/PV project, or power-quality engineer needs to prove whether harmonic pollution is generated inside the site or arrives from the grid before paying for filters, accepting blame, or fighting a connection dispute.
- **Hidden insight:** ordinary power-quality measurement shows that distortion exists; the valuable scarce output is defensible source attribution.
- **Momentum:** inverter-heavy loads, BESS, PV, variable-speed drives, and formal grid-code harmonic limits.
- **Beachhead/cold start:** independent Polish power-quality measurement firms with unresolved historical cases; sell a 15,000–25,000 PLN blind back-test and one live attribution pilot.
- **Resource/control:** a 6–12 month exclusive field-of-use option followed by a Poland/CEE licence to Warsaw University of Technology patent PL 229627, inventor handoff hours, benchmark cases, and labelled remediation outcomes.
- **12-month proof/budget:** signed option, external benchmark, three paid cases, and one analyzer integration under 90,000 PLN.
- **Economics/compounding:** initial 15,000–30,000 PLN cases; later annual software/OEM licences. Patent exclusivity plus labelled source/remediation data compounds.
- **Copy risk:** analyzer OEMs and power-quality consultants; kill if the patent does not improve decisions beyond standard direction-of-harmonic-power methods.

## 2. EcotypeBank Mazovia

- **Insider outcome:** restoration contractors need seed that can survive scrutiny as genuinely local and habitat-appropriate, not a colorful generic “native” mix.
- **Hidden insight:** the scarce unit is not a species list; it is a finite, permitted annual harvest from an eligible donor meadow with defensible provenance.
- **Momentum:** the EU Nature Restoration Regulation, public biodiversity projects, and Polish law governing region-bound environmental conservation mixtures.
- **Beachhead/cold start:** Mazowieckie road, utility, solar, industrial, and municipal restoration contractors named in public environmental decisions and tenders; pre-sell project lots subject to permit and germination results.
- **Resource/control:** exclusive 3-year harvest rights over eligible source meadows, botanist survey, ministerial marketing decision for a defined origin region, sealed tested inventory, and provenance chain.
- **12-month proof/budget:** one eligible meadow, one complete application/decision path, 30–50 kg controlled inventory, and three conditional deposits under 90,000 PLN.
- **Economics/compounding:** sell complete provenance lots per project rather than commodity packets; regional donor rights, germination history, seed-zone knowledge, and contractor specifications compound.
- **Copy risk:** established meadow-seed vendors can seek other donor sites; kill unless buyers pay a material premium for auditable local origin.

## 3. EarLock

- **Insider outcome:** noisy factories want workers to use correctly fitted hearing protection and replace lost pieces without repeating impressions and administration.
- **Hidden insight:** the valuable asset is the employer-authorized, reusable fit record and replacement flow, not the earplug itself.
- **Momentum:** binding 80/85 dB workplace action levels and increasing digitization of custom ear impressions.
- **Beachhead/cold start:** 100–1,000-worker metal, wood, food, and logistics sites in Wielkopolskie/Łódzkie/Mazowieckie; sell a paid 25-worker cohort for 7,500–12,500 PLN.
- **Resource/control:** 24–36-month employer supply contract, employee consent, bilateral scans, attenuation selection, fit verification, and replacement authorization; use manufacturers as paid suppliers.
- **12-month proof/budget:** three employer contracts, 250 enrolled workers, 200 reusable records, 50 replacements without rescanning, and one renewal under 70,000 PLN.
- **Economics/compounding:** 250–500 PLN enrollment plus replacements/refreshes; multi-site archives and fit/failure history compound.
- **Copy risk:** PPE manufacturers and distributors can vertically integrate; kill if employers will not sign term contracts or portable archive custody is not valued.

## 4. CasingReserve

- **Insider outcome:** truck fleets want transparent value for worn tyres while retreaders need known-origin casings that have not been mixed, damaged, or misclassified.
- **Hidden insight:** a casing’s operating history and pre-removal title are more valuable than the worn tyre after it enters a waste/dealer pile.
- **Momentum:** UN R109 traceability and the economic pressure to extend commercial-tyre life.
- **Beachhead/cold start:** regional 20–150-truck fleets at scheduled tyre changes; offer guaranteed conditional credits and sign 12-month first-refusal/title terms after obtaining two retreader grade schedules.
- **Resource/control:** named casing first-refusal/title agreements, vehicle/axle/mileage history, serial photos, removal events, two downstream grade schedules, and buyer deposits.
- **12-month proof/budget:** five fleet agreements, 300 mapped tyres, 100 controlled casings, 50 paid acceptances, and rejection below 25% under 70,000 PLN.
- **Economics/compounding:** 50–250 PLN spread per accepted casing plus retread procurement; casing-yield data and contracted supply compound.
- **Copy risk:** tyre dealers and retreaders are adjacent; kill unless fleets sign first refusal and multi-buyer pricing improves credits.

## 5. Forklift Battery Exit Exchange

- **Secret:** known-use history and state-of-health matter more than cheap cells, but retirement lots lose history when handed to a recycler.
- **Control:** 30-day options over serial-numbered industrial batteries, BMS/maintenance data, buyer deposit, and licensed transport/testing.
- **Proof:** three fleet options covering 30 batteries and ten completed transfers. Main risk is becoming ordinary brokerage or accidentally handling waste.

## 6. RetainOnce

- **Secret:** orthodontic treatment creates a lifelong replacement need, while patients lose access to production-grade final scans when clinics, platforms, or manufacturers change.
- **Control:** clinic contract, patient consent, reusable production file, prescription workflow, and prepaid replacement entitlement.
- **Proof:** one clinic, 100 archived cases, 40 prepayments, and 15 replacements. Main risks are medical-device trust and clinic dependence.

## 7. ChipDefect Tail

- **Secret:** small semiconductor/MEMS pilot lines pay to classify failed runs, not for another generic vision model.
- **Control:** exclusive SME-lab field licence to Warsaw University of Technology technology #192 plus labelled process-outcome data.
- **Proof:** exclusive option, three external image sets, a blind benchmark, and one annual lab licence. Main risk is insufficient external performance.

## 8. ChargeSteward

- **Secret:** small property owners with installed EV chargers want to surrender operating responsibility and payment administration, not buy a new backend.
- **Control:** five-year operating concession, OCPP/admin credentials, tariff authority, and charging revenue assignment.
- **Proof:** two sites/eight charge points, 300 paid sessions, and positive site contribution. Main risk is low utilization and CPO competition.

## 9. PFAS Foam Transition Skid

- **Secret:** facilities need physical foam removal, containment, decontamination, and waste custody without disabling fire protection, not PFAS advice.
- **Control:** owned mobile skid plus prepaid waste-acceptance capacity and system-specific cleaning data.
- **Proof:** two completed transitions and accepted waste manifests. Main risk is immediate copy by fire/waste incumbents and liability.

## 10. No-Drain Fire-Water Tank Inspection

- **Secret:** facility managers postpone inspections because draining creates impairment; a clean submersible inspection avoids downtime.
- **Control:** owned ROV and repeat corrosion maps.
- **Proof:** eight tanks and two annual contracts. Main risk is one-equipment-purchase copyability.

## 11. Radon Measurement Route

- **Secret:** designated-county employers need detector custody and annual closure, not radiation education.
- **Control:** prepaid detector analysis, calibrated monitors, facility history, and renewal contracts.
- **Proof:** 100 buildings and 60% renewal intent. Main risk is low-ticket route economics and laboratory adjacency.

## 12. Roof Membrane Baseline Before PV

- **Secret:** a roof owner’s hidden fear is losing proof of whether damage predated the PV crew.
- **Control:** owned electronic leak-location equipment plus pre/post independent baseline custody.
- **Proof:** 20 roofs and ten before/after pairs. Main risk is easy equipment purchase by roofers.

## 13. FramePassport

- **Secret:** the reusable unit in a demolished bolted warehouse is the complete engineered frame kit, not tonnes of scrap steel.
- **Control:** pre-demolition purchase option over an intact frame, component map, testing, and buyer deposit.
- **Proof:** one controlled frame and one paid reinstallation. Main risks are engineering liability, logistics, and adjacency to matched salvage.

## 14. TreeMove Canopy Bank

- **Secret:** developers may pay to remove a transplantable mature tree while other projects pay for immediate canopy; the market destroys value by treating each side separately.
- **Control:** exclusive pre-construction transplant rights over surveyed trees, permits, and buyer reservations.
- **Proof:** ten optioned trees, three deposits, and one successful move. Main risks are survival, seasonality, and landscaping-incumbent copy.

## 15. Geomembrane Defect-Location Fleet

- **Secret:** a pinhole is cheap before cover and expensive after; contractors pay for exact repair coordinates at the closing window.
- **Control:** owned electric leak-location equipment, calibrated method, and defect/installer benchmark.
- **Proof:** ten cells/ponds and three repeat contractors. Main risk is equipment-led service copyability.
