# Non-Duplication Blacklist

## Already Confirmed

- PV/battery retrofit evidence for installers.
- FMCG retailer-deduction and promotion recovery.
- CBAM customs-broker import continuity.
- EU Data Act compressed-air telemetry access.
- EPREL/public-tender lighting evidence.
- Supplement-stack safety rationalization.
- Heat-pump noise-risk and complaint packets.
- Biosignal/wearable investment diligence.
- Marketplace connected-device compliance rescue.
- DORA vendor renewal evidence.
- Municipal animal-care event-to-invoice routing.
- Matched pre-war door-suite salvage.

## Repeatedly Failed Or Saturated Shapes

- Generic evidence, compliance, release, claim, discrepancy, refund, or recovery desks.
- Agency-routed case books, referral lanes, and white-label partner desks without an owned asset.
- Customer or service route-book acquisitions that remain operator-heavy commodity services.
- Retiring-webmaster, connector, mobile-app, or orphaned-software succession without transferable durable rights.
- Used/obsolete spare-part pools for PLC, HPLC, lab, HVAC, elevator, PV, medical, dental, refrigeration, or industrial equipment.
- Exact-match construction salvage adjacent to heritage doors, tiles, brick, stone, roofs, and discontinued surface materials.
- Keg, cylinder, pallet, returnable-packaging, consumables, waste-oil, rag-bin, and other recovery/route variants.
- Grid connection, EV charging, rooftop, meter, energy-curtailment, white-certificate, and F-gas quota variants already explored.
- Reserved medical/care/diagnostic capacity, clinic slots, biosamples, genetic samples, wearables, or health workflow rails.
- Tender, DORA/NIS2/AI Act/CRA, KSeF, EUDR, CBAM, PFAS, EcoVadis, marketplace, or procurement evidence variants.
- Invoice/payment unblock, portal release, LC discrepancy, customs refund, chargeback, core-credit, receivable, and statutory-compensation variants.
- Scarce-capacity options that depend on a lab, clinic, care home, certified expert, or platform accepting an unknown intermediary.

## Required Material Difference

A candidate may touch an adjacent sector only if all three differ materially:

1. The buyer and urgent trigger.
2. The acquisition mechanism for the first paid proof.
3. The hard resource or control point, secured by ownership, option, prepayment, exclusive contract, assigned right, or custody rather than cooperation alone.

The final business must become more than a manual service and must not rely on an incumbent platform, regulator, buyer, lab, or generic partner to create the decisive value.
