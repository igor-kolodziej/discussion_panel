# Exact Candidate: EcotypeBank Mazovia

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist, five hours/day, 100,000 PLN maximum POC capital, no privileged introductions or status. He may contract a field botanist, seed-harvest operator, laboratory, and regulatory counsel.

**EcotypeBank Mazovia** is a regional native-restoration seed-rights and inventory company, not a consumer flower-seed brand, landscaper, ecology consultancy, or generic seed marketplace. It signs exclusive three-year harvest rights with owners of eligible old species-rich donor meadows in one defined Mazovian origin region, pays for botanical/source-site surveys, applies for the Polish ministerial decision needed to market an environmental conservation mixture in that origin region, harvests and stores sealed tested lots, and records source site, date, habitat, species composition, germination, and buyer project.

The first buyer is a road, utility, solar, industrial, or municipal restoration contractor whose environmental decision, tender, or ecologist requires native/local-origin meadow restoration. Cold acquisition starts from public environmental decisions and awarded contracts naming restoration obligations, then offers a reserved provenance lot priced per project with a refundable deposit contingent on permit and germination. The first ten buyers come from 100 named Mazovian contractors/designers, not broad consumer marketing.

The controlled resource is exclusive harvest rights over specific eligible donor meadows, the origin-region marketing decision, finite annual physical inventory, and the resulting provenance/germination record. Polish/EU rules make origin and source-site facts legally meaningful: conservation mixtures are region-bound, officially authorized/monitored, and directly harvested source sites must not have been sown during the prior 40 years.

The 12-month POC is one contracted meadow, completed legal/permit path, 30–50 kg of controlled tested inventory, and three conditional buyer deposits before scaling. Budget is capped at 90,000 PLN: 12,000 landowner options, 18,000 botanist/surveys, 12,000 regulatory/legal/testing, 20,000 harvest/cleaning/storage, 8,000 insurance/logistics, and 20,000 buyer discovery/contingency.

The hidden insight is that restoration buyers are currently sold species lists, while the scarce defensible product is a finite local-provenance harvest attached to an eligible source habitat and annual window. Expansion adds adjacent Polish origin regions and habitat types. Kill if buyers do not pay a meaningful provenance premium, a first decision cannot fit 12 months, donor sites are not contractable, or established vendors can supply equivalent permitted lots immediately.
