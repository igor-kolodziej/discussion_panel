# Exact Candidate: GridFault Commissioning OS

Evaluate this exact business shape.

Founder constraints: Warsaw data scientist with AI/data skills, maximum 100,000 PLN founder capital for a 12-month POC, five hours per day, no privileged introductions or status. Certified electrical engineers and commissioning contractors may be hired per project. The founder does not perform switching or issue a grid operator's formal acceptance decision.

**GridFault Commissioning OS** is a portable diagnostic system for utility-scale BESS and large PV commissioning when harmonic non-compliance threatens energization, commercial-operation acceptance, retained EPC payment, or warranty allocation. Its first user is the independent commissioning lead or owner's engineer, not the grid operator and not a generic factory buyer. It identifies which inverter, firmware/configuration state, site topology, or upstream grid condition most likely caused each material harmonic; shows confidence and contrary evidence; proposes a controlled retest; and compares the post-intervention result with the acceptance baseline.

This is not a generic power-quality survey, meter, dashboard, filter reseller, expert-witness practice, or permanent engineering consultancy. Existing analyzers such as the Fluke 1777 already use harmonic-power direction to suggest upstream versus downstream origin. GridFault must outperform that baseline on cases where simple direction is ambiguous. The technical product combines synchronized recordings at the point of common coupling and selected inverter feeders, topology and switching logs, controlled operating-state perturbations executed by the commissioning engineer, uncertainty-aware causal ranking, and matching against inverter/firmware/topology fingerprints learned from verified interventions.

The original Warsaw University of Technology patent PL229627/technology #212 is optional background IP. Before technical disclosure, the company seeks a 90-day exclusive evaluation option capped at 15,000 PLN, covering inventor handoff and a defined field while leaving the company free to continue independently if the option is unavailable. All founder, contractor, and university improvement work begins under written invention-assignment and confidentiality terms. A patent attorney then searches prior art and drafts a new first filing only if the active multi-point method is novel and non-obvious; an EPO/PCT decision is made within the priority year. The company never represents the old Polish patent as worldwide protection.

The initial control point is the combination of: (1) owned or exclusively licensed new improvement claims if patentable; (2) customer contracts allowing GridFault to retain de-identified spectra, topology class, inverter/firmware state, intervention, and verified outcome while the customer retains raw site data; (3) a proprietary blind benchmark and fingerprint corpus; and (4) a repeatable portable test protocol embedded in commissioning firms' work. Patentability is a hypothesis, not a fact. If no defensible improvement can be filed, the business must still prove that its contractually controlled outcome corpus produces a repeatable advantage; otherwise it is killed.

Cold start uses the quarterly public PSE list of planned generation and storage connections plus public project/EPC announcements to identify named projects 60–180 days before expected energization. The founder contacts 50 commissioning leads, owner's engineers, EPC electrical leads, and independent power-quality firms with a fixed offer: an 8,000–15,000 PLN blind back-test on a difficult resolved case, credited against a 20,000–40,000 PLN live Harmonic Acceptance Sprint. The conversion test is 50 targeted contacts → 12 problem interviews → five historical-case offers → two paid or deposit-backed benchmarks → one paid live sprint. No field hardware is built and no contractor is reserved before two deposits or paid benchmark commitments.

The first ten customers come from project-triggered outbound and two independent commissioning firms that can resell the sprint. The first 100 are reached through a portable software/recorder kit licensed to commissioning firms, not by the founder personally performing 100 studies. A live sprint includes baseline import, test design, contractor-executed switching/measurement, ranked attribution, intervention replay, and acceptance comparison. The commissioning firm remains responsible for electrical safety and any formal engineering sign-off.

Illustrative economics to test, not facts: a 30,000 PLN sprint has no more than 12,000 PLN direct engineering, rental, travel, and insurance cost, producing at least 60% contribution before fixed product development; a repeat commissioning-firm licence/rental is 60,000–120,000 PLN annually. By month 12, a trained commissioning engineer must be able to run a site with no more than eight hours of founder/expert support. The company keeps a 50% minimum contribution-margin kill threshold after all per-site contractors and acquisition costs.

The 12-month proof is: verified PL229627 family/status and option outcome; signed assignment/data-rights templates; ten usable historical cases; a preregistered blind benchmark against harmonic-power direction and manual feeder isolation; either a 20-percentage-point improvement in correct top-ranked cause, a 30% reduction in investigation time, or correct resolution of at least three previously ambiguous cases; three paid projects; two documented decisions changed; one repeat customer; and one annual kit licence, rental, or paid integration. All performance claims must report case count, exclusions, uncertainty, and failures.

Budget is capped at 95,000 PLN: 15,000 patent diligence/university option/contracts; 20,000 patent attorney and first filing; 20,000 electrical engineer/inventor work; 15,000 instrument rental and field execution; 10,000 software/data infrastructure; 10,000 sales/travel/insurance; and 5,000 contingency. The founder schedules no more than 25 hours per week and outsources live electrical work.

The domination path starts with Polish BESS/PV acceptance cases, expands to a CEE fingerprint corpus and commissioning-firm kits, then to OEM-neutral SDK licensing for analyzer vendors and inverter manufacturers. Warranty, lender, insurer, or specification adoption is upside, not required for the POC. The compounding advantage is that every verified intervention adds a labelled inverter/firmware/topology response that broad analyzers and single EPCs do not see across vendors. The strongest copy risk is an analyzer OEM or global commissioning firm adding active tests and pooling its installed-base data.

Kill if the method cannot work without PL229627 on acceptable terms; no patentable improvement and no corpus-based repeatable advantage exists; it does not beat the declared baseline; fewer than two buyers pay or place deposits; customers refuse derived-data rights; three paid projects do not close within nine months; every case needs bespoke inventor consulting; no buyer accepts a reusable kit/licence; or contribution margin stays below 50%.
