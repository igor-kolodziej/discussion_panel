# Decision Summary

## Outcome

No candidate in this run qualified for confirmation. No real Zero to One validation was attempted and no `ideas/CONFIRMED_IDEA_*.md` file was created.

## Best Original Shape

GridFault Attribution scored 72/100 in an early diagnostic review. Its Polish patent position, public disclosure, narrow buyer volume, and consulting risk prevented promotion.

## Implemented Pivot

GridFault Commissioning OS reframed the concept around BESS/PV energization and acceptance, active multi-point causal testing, newly filed improvement IP, derived-data rights, a cross-vendor outcome corpus, and a portable commissioning kit.

Two fresh independent simulated reviewers scored the exact wording **64/100** and **68/100**. Both classified the current control point as Tier 3 and independently found the same fatal flaw: the patent, contracts, corpus, productization, and channel are prospective, while established analyzer OEMs and commissioning firms already control trust, installed hardware, workflow access, and larger datasets.

## Decision

The repeated fatal objection kills this business shape before real-chat validation. It may be reconsidered only after executed external resource proof: a university option/independent-IP decision, two paid benchmark commitments, signed case-data rights, a demonstrated baseline advantage, a credible improvement claim, and one commissioning-firm kit commitment.
